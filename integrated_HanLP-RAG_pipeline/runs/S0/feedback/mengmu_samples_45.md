## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子。他们住在墓地的旁边，所以孟子老是祭拜。孟子不是祭拜，就是跟朋友聊天。他很喜欢他的城市，但是城市有才两个学校，都很小。

**Grammar**
- 古代的时候 → 在古代: 古代 is the time/location setting, so use 在+Place before the action: 在古代。
**Vocabulary**
- (suggestion) ∅ → 只: It would be more natural to use 只 to mean “only” for the small number of schools.
- (suggestion) 才 → (delete): It would be more natural to delete 才 here; 只 already expresses “only.”
**Coherence: reference**
- ⟦他⟧: This character appears for the first time, so please introduce the boy briefly with a noun phrase before using 他.
**Content**
- 祭拜 → 模仿别人办丧事: Here, 孟子 is copying funeral rituals, not actually worshipping. Use 模仿别人办丧事.
- 祭拜 → 模仿别人办丧事: Here, 孟子 is copying funeral rituals, not actually worshipping. Use 模仿别人办丧事.

**Vocabulary suggestions**
- 喜欢 (“他很喜欢他的城市”) → 喜爱: A slightly more literary word for liking something very much.
- 聊天 (“跟朋友聊天”) → 闲聊: Means chatting casually with no serious topic.

**Expressiveness**
- At “他们住在墓地的旁边，所以孟子老是祭拜。”: Add that he saw many people coming to worship and thought it was interesting. For example: 他看到很多人来祭拜，觉得很好玩。
- At “孟子不是祭拜，就是跟朋友聊天。”: Describe that he and his friends copied the adults, and he laughed while doing it. For example: 他跟朋友学别人祭拜，一边笑一边祭拜。
- At “孟子不是祭拜，就是跟朋友聊天。”: You could add a short line of speech from an angry visitor at the cemetery. For example: 一个人说：“你们不要笑！”

**Encouragement** You clearly introduce Mengzi, his mother, and where they live. Your “不是……就是……” sentence is a good way to show what Mengzi does often.

## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈不是不喜欢他们的城市，而是想孟子有很好的教育。他们搬家到有很多学校的城市。他们从早到晚走路，孟子一直抱怨。

**Vocabulary**
- ∅ → 让: 想 cannot be followed directly by a new subject and verb. Use 想让孟子… to show what the mother wants him to do.
- 有 → 接受: 接受 normally collocates with 教育. Say 让孟子接受很好的教育.
- 他难怪 → 难怪他: 难怪 comes before the subject. Say 难怪他抱怨.
**Coherence: 了 / 着 / 在**
- (suggestion) 家 → (delete): It would be more natural to say 搬到…, not 搬家到…. Here 搬 already means “move house.”
- (suggestion) ∅ → 了: It would be more natural to add 了completed because moving to the city is a completed action in the story: 搬到了….

**Vocabulary suggestions**
- 很多 (“有很多学校的城市”) → 许多: A slightly more formal way to say “many.”
- 很好 (“有很好的教育”) → 良好: Means “good” or “sound” and is often used for education or an environment.
- 抱怨 (“孟子一直抱怨”) → 埋怨: Means to complain with a stronger feeling of dissatisfaction.; 不停地抱怨: Emphasizes that he complains again and again without stopping.

**Expressiveness**
- At “而是想孟子有很好的教育。”: You could add what his mother says to show why she wants to move. For example: 妈妈说：“我想让孟子好好学习。”
- At “他们搬家到有很多学校的城市。”: You could describe the new place by showing students studying near the schools. For example: 学校旁边有很多学生在看书。
- At “他们从早到晚走路，孟子一直抱怨。”: You could add that his mother feels worried but still hopes the new environment will help him learn. For example: 妈妈很着急，可是她希望孟子以后喜欢学习。

**Encouragement** You clearly explain the mother’s reason for moving: she wants Mengzi to have a good education. The pattern “不是……而是……” makes this contrast especially clear.

## Scene 3

> 他难怪抱怨，他想他的朋友，因为他不要离开他的城市。他的妈妈找到孟子一个老师。跟老师孟子学最重要的文学，所以他成为哲学家。

**Grammar**
- 不要 → 不想: 不想 means “does not want to.” Use 不想离开他的城市 here.
- (suggestion) 一个 → 的: It would be more natural to use 的 because 孟子 describes the noun 老师: 孟子的老师.
**Vocabulary**
- 跟老师孟子 → 孟子跟老师: Put the subject 孟子 before 跟老师. Say 孟子跟老师学最重要的文学.
**Coherence: reference**
- 他 → (delete): 他 is already clear from the close continuation, so delete it; Chinese uses zero form here.
- 他 → (delete): 他 is already clear from the previous clause, so it is redundant. Chinese uses zero form here.
**Coherence: 了 / 着 / 在**
- (suggestion) ∅ → 了: Becoming a philosopher is a completed change in the story, so use 了completed: 成为了哲学家.
**Other**
- ∅ → 给孟子找了一个老师: Use 给 to mark 孟子 as the person receiving the teacher. Say 给孟子找了一个老师.

**Vocabulary suggestions**
- 抱怨 (“他难怪抱怨”) → 埋怨: This means to complain with a slightly stronger sense of dissatisfaction.
- 朋友 (“他的朋友”) → 同伴: This means companions or friends, often people who do activities together.

**Expressiveness**
- At “他难怪抱怨”: You could add what Mengzi says to show that he misses his friends. For example: 他说：“我想我的朋友。”
- At “他的妈妈找到孟子一个老师。”: You could show that his mother feels happy because the new place helps him learn. For example: 他的妈妈很高兴，说：“这儿很好。”
- At “跟老师孟子学最重要的文学”: You could add a small detail about how Mengzi studies with his teacher. For example: 孟子每天看书学习。

**Encouragement** You clearly explain why Mengzi complains: he misses his friends and does not want to leave his city. You also connect learning with a teacher to his later success as a philosopher.
