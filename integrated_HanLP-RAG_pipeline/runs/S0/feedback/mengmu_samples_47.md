# Feedback: mengmu_samples#47

## Scene 1

> 这个男孩子叫孟子，孟子是古代的人。孟子的妈妈觉得住在哪儿对童年很重要，所以他们常常搬了家。她望子成龙，所以孟子的妈妈安排他的生活。

**Grammar**
- 了 → (delete): 常常 shows a habitual action, so do not use completed-action 了. Say 常常搬家.

**Vocabulary suggestions**
- 重要 (“对童年很重要”) → 关键: This means especially important or decisive.; 有影响: This focuses on how a place can affect a child.
- 安排 (“安排他的生活”) → 规划: This means to plan something carefully for the future.; 照顾: This emphasizes a mother's care for her child.
- 常常 (“他们常常搬了家”) → 多次: This clearly shows that something happened several times.; 不断: This suggests the moving kept happening.

**Expressiveness**
- At “这个男孩子叫孟子，孟子是古代的人。”: You could add that Mencius was still a young boy, so the places around him easily influenced what he learned. For example: 孟子还是一个小男孩。
- At “孟子的妈妈觉得住在哪儿对童年很重要，所以他们常常搬了家。”: You could add the mother's thought about wanting to find a place that was good for her son's learning. For example: 她想：“我要给孟子找一个适合学习的地方。”
- At “她望子成龙，所以孟子的妈妈安排他的生活。”: You could add that she hoped Mencius would study and become successful in the future. For example: 她希望孟子好好学习，长大以后有出息。

**Encouragement** You clearly introduce Mencius and explain his mother's main reason for moving. Using “望子成龙” is a strong, story-appropriate way to show her hopes for him.

## Scene 2

> 因为他们常常搬了家，中国人说，孟母三迁。孟子没有爸爸，所以，他的妈妈更重要。从这儿到那儿，妈妈教孟子很多技能。先她让孟子跟朋友玩儿，然后教他怎么买又便宜又好质量的东西。

**Grammar**
- 了 → (delete): With 常常, this is a habitual action, so do not use 了completed.
- 先她 → 她先: Put the subject before 先: 她先让孟子跟朋友玩儿.
- 好质量 → 质量好: Say 质量好, with the noun 质量 before 好.
**Vocabulary**
- (suggestion) 技能 → 东西: It would be more natural to say 东西 here. 技能 is less natural for the things a mother teaches him to buy.
- (suggestion) ∅ → 质量: It would be more natural to add 质量: 又便宜又质量好 describes both the price and the quality.
**Coherence: reference**
- ⟦孟母⟧: 孟母 appears for the first time here, so please briefly introduce her, for example as 孟子的妈妈.

**Vocabulary suggestions**
- 重要 (“妈妈更重要”) → 关键: means “key” or “crucial”; it gives stronger emphasis.
- 朋友 (“跟朋友玩儿”) → 同伴: means “companion” or “playmate”; it is a slightly more story-like word.

**Expressiveness**
- At “先她让孟子跟朋友玩儿”: You could say that Mengzi saw many people visiting the cemetery and thought copying their worship was interesting. For example: 孟子每天看见很多人来祭拜，觉得很有意思。
- At “然后教他怎么买又便宜又好质量的东西。”: Add a detail about the busy market, with many merchants selling things all day. For example: 每天有很多商人卖东西，从早到晚很热闹。
- At “然后教他怎么买又便宜又好质量的东西。”: You could add Mengzi’s mother’s worry and a short comment about his learning. For example: 妈妈很着急，说：“住在这儿会影响学习。”

**Encouragement** You clearly connect this scene to the saying “孟母三迁.” You also show that Mengzi’s mother played an important role in guiding his life.

## Scene 3

> 最后，她让孟子学写作。他不是写作就是看书，难怪他很聪明。现在在中国，孟子很有名。

**Coherence: reference**
- 他 → (delete): 孟子 is already clear from the previous clause, so 他 is redundant. Delete it; Chinese uses zero form here.

**Vocabulary suggestions**
- 聪明 (“他很聪明”) → 聪慧: A slightly more literary word for intelligent and wise.; 优秀: Means excellent; it can describe his strong ability as a student.
- 有名 (“孟子很有名”) → 著名: A more formal word for well-known or famous.

**Expressiveness**
- At “最后，她让孟子学写作。”: Add that they are living near a school, so Mengzi can study in a quiet place. For example: 这一次，他们搬到学校旁边。
- At “他不是写作就是看书，难怪他很聪明。”: Add how Mengzi studies every day and show his mother’s happiness with a short quote. For example: 孟子每天安静地看书学习。妈妈很高兴，说：“这儿很适合孩子学习。”
- At “现在在中国，孟子很有名。”: Add that he later became a philosopher, which explains why people still know him. For example: 后来，孟子成为了一个哲学家。

**Encouragement** You clearly show the result of Mengzi’s learning: he reads, writes, becomes smart, and is famous in China. Your phrase “不是写作就是看书” gives a strong sense that he studies often.
