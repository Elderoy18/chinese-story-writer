## Scene 1

> 一天，很久很久以前，孟子和他妈妈住在中国。孟子妈妈的工作是祭拜，孟子不高兴，因为他说我们老是搬家，为什么？他妈妈说，从早到晚，我一直祭拜，很多墓地得我祭拜。所以我们得去很多不同的地方。

**Grammar**
- …的工作是祭拜 → …的工作是祭拜祖先: 祭拜 needs an object here. Add 祖先: 祭拜祖先.
- 很多墓地得我祭拜 → 很多墓地需要我祭拜: 很多墓地需要祭拜 is more natural here. 得 is not used this way in this statement.
**Vocabulary**
- 一天，很久很久以前 → 很久很久以前的一天: use the established time expression 很久很久以前的一天, not this word order.
- 所以我们得去很多不同的地… → 所以我们得搬到很多不同的地…: It would be more natural to say 搬到很多不同的地方, because this means moving house rather than simply going somewhere.
**Coherence: reference**
- ⟦孟子⟧和他妈妈住在…: 孟子 appears for the first time, so please introduce him briefly, for example as 一个叫孟子的孩子.
- 很多墓地得我祭拜 → 很多墓地得祭拜: 我 is already clear from the previous clause, so delete it; Chinese uses zero form here.

**Vocabulary suggestions**
- 不高兴 (“孟子不高兴”) → 难过: This means sad or upset.; 生气: This is stronger and means angry.
- 很多 (“很多不同的地方”) → 许多: This also means “many,” with a slightly more story-like tone.; 各种各样的: This emphasizes that there are many different kinds.
- 一直 (“我一直祭拜”) → 不停地: This emphasizes doing something without stopping.; 总是: This means doing something again and again.

**Expressiveness**
- At “孟子和他妈妈住在中国。”: You could add where their home was, so the setting is clearer. For example: 他们的家在墓地旁边。
- At “孟子不高兴，因为他说我们老是搬家，为什么？”: You could make Mengzi’s question into direct speech to show that he feels confused. For example: 孟子说：“妈妈，我们为什么老是搬家？”
- At “他妈妈说，从早到晚，我一直祭拜，很多墓地得我祭拜。”: You could add that his mother felt worried because she thought this place was not good for Mengzi. For example: 妈妈很担心孟子。

**Encouragement** You clearly show that Mengzi is unhappy about moving so often. You also give his mother a reason, which makes the scene easy to follow.

## Scene 2

> 孟子想，难怪我们常常搬家，原来我妈妈很忙。可是常常搬家害得孟子很难找到朋友。每次搬家孟子也觉得新的地方不是太热就是太冷，不是太大就是太小。在哪的地方，他都不高兴。孟子想帮助妈妈祭拜，可是他六岁，所以不能祭拜。

**Vocabulary**
- 在哪的地方 → 在哪个的地方: 哪 does not normally collocate directly with 地方 here; use 哪个地方 instead.
**Coherence: reference**
- (suggestion) 每次搬家孟子也觉得新的地方… → 每次搬家孟子都觉得新的地方…: It would be more natural to use 都 with 每次搬家 to show that this was true on every move.
- (suggestion) 不是太大就是太小 → 不是大就是太小: It would be more natural to say 不是大就是小; the repeated 太 is unnecessary here.
- (suggestion) 不是太大就是太小 → 不是太大就是小: It would be more natural to say 不是大就是小; the repeated 太 is unnecessary here.
**Other**
- (suggestion) 可是他六岁 → 可是他只有六岁: It would be more natural to say 只有六岁 to emphasize that he was too young to take part in the ritual.

**Vocabulary suggestions**
- 常常 (“我们常常搬家”) → 经常: A common alternative meaning “often,” with a slightly more neutral tone.
- 不高兴 (“他都不高兴”) → 难过: More specifically shows that he feels sad.; 不开心: A natural, everyday way to say he is unhappy.
- 忙 (“我妈妈很忙”) → 辛苦: Shows that someone works hard and has a difficult job.

**Expressiveness**
- At “可是常常搬家害得孟子很难找到朋友。”: You could show that he feels lonely and add a short line of speech about wanting a friend. For example: 孟子很难过，说：“我想有一个好朋友。”
- At “孟子想帮助妈妈祭拜，可是他六岁，所以不能祭拜。”: You could add what Mengzi says to his mother when he wants to help her. For example: 孟子说：“妈妈，我也想帮你祭拜。”

**Encouragement** You clearly explain why moving often is hard for Mengzi, especially because it makes finding friends difficult. Your “不是……就是……” sentence also gives a clear contrast between the different new places.

## Scene 3

> 四年后，孟子开始帮妈妈祭拜。他画画很厉害，他越来越喜欢祭拜，所以他祭拜不是想帮妈妈工作，而是他很喜欢画画。孟子成为很著名的古代画家。今天很多中国人很喜欢孟子。

**Grammar**
- (suggestion) 孟子成为很著名的古代… → 孟子成为了很著名的古代…: It would be more natural to add 了 after 成为 to show that this change was completed.
**Coherence: reference**
- 他越来越喜欢祭… → 越来越喜欢祭…: 他 is already clear from the previous clause, so it is redundant. Delete it; Chinese uses zero form here.
- 所以他祭拜不是想帮… → 所以祭拜不是想帮…: 他 is already clear from the previous clause, so it is redundant. Delete it; Chinese uses zero form here.
- 而是他很喜欢画画 → 而是很喜欢画画: In 不是……而是……, put the subject before 不是 and do not repeat it after 而是. 他 is redundant here, so use zero form.
**Content**
- 而是他很喜欢画画 → 而是他很喜欢祭拜: The reason for 祭拜 should match the action being explained. Say 很喜欢祭拜, not 很喜欢画画.

**Vocabulary suggestions**
- 厉害 (“他画画很厉害”) → 出色: It means excellent and sounds a little more formal.; 有天分: It means having natural talent.
- 喜欢 (“他越来越喜欢祭拜”) → 感兴趣: It means to be interested in something.; 热爱: It shows a stronger, deeper kind of liking.
- 著名 (“孟子成为很著名的古代画家”) → 闻名: It means well-known, often for a particular achievement.; 杰出: It emphasizes that someone is outstanding, not only famous.

**Expressiveness**
- At “孟子开始帮妈妈祭拜。”: Add that he watched the people at the cemetery carefully and found the ceremonies interesting. For example: 他觉得大家祭拜很有意思。
- At “他越来越喜欢祭拜，”: Add a short thought to show why he enjoyed it, such as wanting to learn what the adults did. For example: 他想：“我也想学他们。”
- At “所以他祭拜不是想帮妈妈工作，”: Add a little detail about how he acted during the ceremony, for example that he smiled while copying the adults. For example: 他一边笑，一边学别人祭拜。

**Encouragement** You used “四年后” clearly to move the story forward in time. You also explained Mengzi’s interest and gave him an achievement at the end of the scene.
