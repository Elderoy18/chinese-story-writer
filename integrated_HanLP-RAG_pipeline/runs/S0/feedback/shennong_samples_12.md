# Feedback: shennong_samples#12

## Scene 1

> 很久以前，古代的人很不知道什么植物能吃，也什么植物不能吃。所以很多人常常吃不安全的植物，吃不安全的植物对你的身体有坏处，很麻烦的情况。但是一个人，神农，要解决这个问题。

**Grammar**
- ∅ → 不知道: The two parts are parallel: people do not know what plants can be eaten or what plants cannot be eaten. Add 不知道 after 也.
- (suggestion) 么 → 麼: It would be more natural to use the traditional character 麼 if you are writing in traditional Chinese.
- 不安全 → 有毒: 有毒的植物 is the established phrase for plants that harm people when eaten, not 不安全的植物.
- (suggestion) 植物 → 食品: It would be more natural to say 食品 for harmful things people eat in this general statement.
- 你的 → 人的: This is a general statement, so use 人的身体, not 你的身体.
- ∅ → 有: Use 有 to introduce a new person: 但是有一个人。
**Vocabulary**
- 很 → (delete): 很不知道 is not an established expression. Say 不知道 here.
- (suggestion) 不安全 → 有毒: It would be more specific to say 有毒的植物. 不安全的 is vague here.
- (suggestion) 植 → 食: It would be more specific to say 食物 here, because this means food people eat.
- ∅ → 这是一个: 很麻烦的情况 is only a noun phrase. Add 这是一个 to make a complete sentence: 这是一个很麻烦的情况.
**Coherence: reference**
- ⟦神农⟧: This character appears for the first time, so please introduce 神农 briefly, for example 有一个叫神农的人.

**Vocabulary suggestions**
- 很多 (“所以很多人常常吃不安全的植物”) → 许多: A slightly more written-style word for “many.”; 不少: Means “quite a few” and sounds natural in narration.
- 解决 (“神农，要解决这个问题”) → 弄清楚: Means “find out clearly,” which fits discovering which plants are safe.; 找出: Means “find out” or “identify,” especially useful for identifying plants.

**Expressiveness**
- At “很多人常常吃不安全的植物，吃不安全的植物对你的身体有坏处，很麻烦的情况。”: You could add that people often became ill, to show why this problem was serious. For example: 很多人生病了。
- At “但是一个人，神农，要解决这个问题。”: Add that Shennong felt worried about the sick people and wanted to find out which plants could be medicine. For example: 神农觉得很担心，想找可以做药的植物。
- At “但是一个人，神农，要解决这个问题。”: You could include a short sentence from Shennong to make his decision more lively. For example: 神农说：“我要找出哪些植物可以吃。”

**Encouragement** You clearly explain the main problem at the start: people did not know which plants were safe to eat. You also introduce Shennong and his goal, so the scene has a clear direction.

## Scene 2

> 他又聪明又努力，旅行很远找到很多植物。比方说，灵芝和夹竹桃。他一吃各种各样的植物就知道能不能吃这些植物。然后，神农写他的主意。他喜欢植物的味道，特别是花。神农遇到了一个黄色的花，一吃就觉得不舒服。

**Grammar**
- 找到 → 找到了: This is a completed discovery, so use 找到了 with 了.
**Vocabulary**
- 旅行很远 → 走了很远: 旅行很远 does not normally collocate with the action of going a long distance. Use 走了很远 instead.
- 写 → 写下了: When Shennong records his ideas, use 写下了, meaning “wrote down,” rather than just 写.
- 个 → 朵: Use 朵 as the measure word for flowers, not 个.

**Vocabulary suggestions**
- 聪明 (“他又聪明又努力”) → 了不起: means very admirable, and is good for praising Shennong’s work.; 勇敢: emphasizes that he is not afraid of danger.
- 努力 (“他又聪明又努力”) → 勤劳: describes someone who works hard over a long time.
- 不舒服 (“一吃就觉得不舒服”) → 难受: is a stronger word for feeling physically bad.; 痛苦: shows very strong physical suffering.

**Expressiveness**
- At “他又聪明又努力，旅行很远找到很多植物。”: You could add that he went to many high mountains, showing how hard he worked. For example: 他走了很多高山，找植物。
- At “他喜欢植物的味道，特别是花。”: You could describe the yellow flower as big and beautiful, like a star. For example: 黄色的花像星星一样，又大又漂亮。
- At “神农遇到了一个黄色的花，一吃就觉得不舒服。”: You could show his worry by adding what he thinks when his stomach starts to hurt. For example: 他的肚子疼起来。他想：“我会死吗？”

**Encouragement** You clearly explain that Shennong tests many plants and then records what he learns. You also include his reaction after eating the yellow flower, which helps the scene move toward the danger.

## Scene 3

> 发现了夹竹桃有毒。别人看到他，很快给他吃一个灵芝。神农一吃就觉得比较好。然后，他继续写他发现的信息。会告诉他的朋友哪一个植物能不能吃。

**Grammar**
- 能不能吃 → 能吃，哪一种不能吃: Do not combine 哪一种 with the yes/no form 能不能吃. List the two Wh-questions: 哪一种能吃，哪一种不能吃.
**Coherence: 了 / 着 / 在**
- 了 → (delete): 发现 is a mental state verb here, so use no了.
- (suggestion) ∅ → 了: It would be more natural to use 了 because giving him the 灵芝 is completed in the story (了completed).
- (suggestion) ∅ → 吃: It would be more natural to say 吃了: eating the 灵芝 is completed before he feels better (了completed).
**Content**
- 个 → 种: Use 种 for a type of plant: 哪一种植物.

**Vocabulary suggestions**
- 很快 (“别人看到他，很快给他吃一个灵芝。”) → 马上: means immediately, with a stronger sense of quick action.; 立刻: means at once and sounds a little more vivid in a story.
- 信息 (“然后，他继续写他发现的信息。”) → 资料: means information or material collected for a book or study.; 内容: means the specific things he discovered and wrote down.

**Expressiveness**
- At “发现了夹竹桃有毒。”: Add how badly Shennong’s stomach hurt after he ate the flower. For example: 他的肚子很疼。
- At “别人看到他，很快给他吃一个灵芝。”: Describe the helper as a man in blue clothes and show that he helped quickly. For example: 一个穿蓝衣服的男人马上帮助他。
- At “神农一吃就觉得比较好。”: Add Shennong’s thankful words and his question about the plant. For example: 他说：“谢谢你！这是什么植物？”

**Encouragement** You clearly included the important rescue: someone gives Shennong 灵芝 and he feels better. You also showed that he keeps writing down what he learns to help his friends know which plants are safe.
