## Scene 1

> 有一天孟子去了墓地玩儿，但是他妈妈觉得这是不合适的地方让孩子玩，所以她决定了他们会搬家找更好的地方。她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福，而且住在这儿教育不太好。因为她望子成龙，所以她会一直搬家才能找到质量好的教育。然后他们搬到了离城市更近的地方。

**Grammar**
- …妈妈觉得这是不合适的地方让孩子玩 → …妈妈觉得这是不适合让孩子玩的地方: 不适合 must take the action phrase 让孩子玩 as its complement before 地方.
- 而且住在这儿教育不太好 → 而且这儿的教育不太好: use 这儿的 to modify 教育.
**Vocabulary**
- 所以她会一直搬家才能… → 所以她要一直搬家才能…: 会 does not naturally form the conditional pattern with 才能 here; use 要...才能 instead.
**Coherence: reference**
- 有一天⟦孟子⟧去了墓地玩儿: 孟子 appears for the first time, so please introduce him briefly, for example 孟子这个孩子.
- 所以她会一直搬家才… → 所以会一直搬家才…: 她 is already clear from the previous clause, so delete it; Chinese uses zero form here.
**Coherence: 了 / 着 / 在**
- 所以她决定了他们会搬家找… → 所以她决定他们会搬家找…: 决定 is a mental-state verb here, so use no 了.
**Coherence: connectors**
- (suggestion) 有一天孟子去了墓地玩儿 → 有一天孟子去墓地玩儿: It would be more natural to delete 了 and use a sequence phrase such as 后来 to show how this sentence connects to the narrative.
**Other**
- (suggestion) …搬家找更好的地方 → …搬家找更好的: It would be more natural to delete 地方 because 更好的 already expresses the thing she wants to find.

**Vocabulary suggestions**
- 觉得 (“他妈妈觉得这是不合适的地方让孩子玩”) → 认为: a slightly more formal word for “think” or “believe”.; 感到: use this when emphasizing her feeling or sense of worry.
- 更好 (“找更好的地方”) → 更合适: more precise for a place that suits a child better.; 更理想: means more ideal and sounds a little stronger.
- 搬家 (“然后他们搬到了离城市更近的地方。”) → 迁居: a more formal word for moving home.

**Expressiveness**
- At “有一天孟子去了墓地玩儿”: Add that he watched people worship at the graves and found it interesting, so the reader can understand why his mother worried. For example: 他看见别人祭拜，觉得很有趣。
- At “但是他妈妈觉得这是不合适的地方让孩子玩，所以她决定了他们会搬家找更好的地方。”: Add a short thought or quote from the mother to show her worry and make her decision feel more personal. For example: 妈妈想：“这里不适合孩子，我得搬家。”

**Encouragement** You clearly explain why Mengzi’s mother wants to move, using her hopes for her son’s education. You also connect this scene well to the next move closer to the city.

## Scene 2

> 他们住才一月就又搬家。古代的时候，一般人不喜欢卖东西的人。但是孟子老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿，害得妈妈着急得很，因为她恐怕儿子会成为跟他们很像。她告诉了孟子：他们不是朋友，而是不好的人。

**Grammar**
- …一月就又搬家 → …一月就又搬家了: Add sentence-final 了 because the move was completed: 又搬家了.
- 古代的时候 → 在古代: 古代 is the time/location background, so use 在+Place before the action: 在古代.
**Vocabulary**
- 他们住才一月就又搬家 → 他们才住一月就又搬家: use 才住 because 才 goes before the verb to show the short duration “only lived for one month.”
- (suggestion) 因为她恐怕儿子会成为跟… → 因为她担心儿子会成为跟…: It would be more natural to say 担心 here, because it directly expresses that the mother worries about her son.
- …她恐怕儿子会成为跟他们很像 → …她恐怕儿子会变得跟他们很像: use 变得 before the adjective phrase 很像. 成为 is used for becoming a person, role, or thing.
**Coherence: 了 / 着 / 在**
- 她告诉了孟子：他们不… → 她告诉孟子：他们不…: 告诉 introduces quoted speech, so use no 了 for the speaking word: 她告诉孟子：……
**Other**
- 但是孟子老是跟这些卖东西… → 但是孟子跟这些卖东西…: 老是 is redundant because 常常 already expresses that Mengzi played with them often.

**Vocabulary suggestions**
- 卖东西的人 (“跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿”) → 商人: a common word for people who buy and sell things, especially in a market or city; 小贩: a more specific word for a street seller or small vendor
- 着急 (“害得妈妈着急得很”) → 焦急: stronger than 着急; it shows that someone is very worried and impatient; 担心: focuses more on worrying about what may happen
- 不好 (“而是不好的人。”) → 不合适: means not suitable; it is softer and can describe people or influences that are not good for a child; 不正经: means not serious or proper; it gives a stronger negative tone

**Expressiveness**
- At “但是孟子老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿”: You could add that he copied what the sellers did, such as taking money or talking about prices. For example: 他还学他们收钱、说价钱。
- At “害得妈妈着急得很”: You could add the mother’s thought that this place might affect her son’s learning. For example: 妈妈想：住在这儿会影响他的学习。
- At “她告诉了孟子：他们不是朋友，而是不好的人。”: You could make the mother’s words more direct and show that she wants Mengzi to study instead. For example: 妈妈说：“你不要老跟他们玩儿，要好好学习。”

**Encouragement** You clearly show the cause of the mother’s worry: Mengzi often spends time with the sellers. You also include the mother’s concern about her son’s future, which makes this scene feel connected to the story.

## Scene 3

> 你不是当数学家，而是当哲学家，不能当卖东西的人。所以他们又搬家。在他们第三个家，孟子学了怎么祭拜。妈妈感觉这个地方适合他们，告诉了孟子：我们找到了最后的家。在这儿妈妈让儿子从早到晚学习，他难怪成为有名的哲学家。

**Vocabulary**
- 而是当哲学家 → 而要当哲学家: Use the matching contrast 不要……而要…… here. It is more natural than pairing 不要 with 而是.
- (suggestion) 而是当哲学家 → 而是当哲学家的: It would be more natural to add 的 here to make the description sound more complete.
- (suggestion) …他们第三个家 → …他们第三个家里: A modified location with 家 normally uses 里. Say 在他们第三个家里.
- 他难怪成为有名的哲… → 难怪他成为有名的哲…: Put 难怪 before the subject it explains. Say 难怪他成为有名的哲学家.
**Coherence: 了 / 着 / 在**
- (suggestion) 孟子学了怎么祭拜 → 孟子学会了怎么祭拜: It would be more natural to say 学会了怎么祭拜. 学会 shows that Mencius learned this skill successfully.
- 告诉了孟子：我们找… → 告诉孟子：我们找…: 告诉 introduces quoted speech, so use no 了 after it.
**Coherence: connectors**
- 你不是当数学家 → 你不要当数学家: For advice or prohibition, use 不要, not 不是. 不是……而是…… is used to correct a mistaken idea, not to give this instruction.
- (suggestion) …以他们又搬家 → …以他们又搬家了: It would be more natural to add 了 because this move was completed. 又 shows that they moved again.

**Vocabulary suggestions**
- 学习 (“从早到晚学习”) → 用功: This emphasizes studying with effort and diligence.; 读书: This specifically focuses on reading and studying books.
- 有名 (“有名的哲学家”) → 著名: This is a more formal word for widely known.; 闻名: This means famous or well-known, often for a good reason.

**Expressiveness**
- At “妈妈感觉这个地方适合他们，告诉了孟子：我们找到了最后的家。”: Show that the mother feels happy and relieved because she thinks this environment is good for her son. For example: 妈妈很高兴地说：“这儿的环境很适合你。”
- At “在这儿妈妈让儿子从早到晚学习”: Add a small detail about Mengzi studying quietly and seriously. For example: 孟子每天安静地看书，很认真地学习。
- At “他难怪成为有名的哲学家。”: Add that Mengzi’s hard work helped him become successful later. For example: 因为他很用功，所以后来成了有名的哲学家。

**Encouragement** You clearly explain that the third home is the final and suitable home for Mengzi. You also connect his long hours of study with his later success as a philosopher.
