# Feedback: mengmu_samples#18

## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个男孩名字叫孟子。孟子和孟子的妈妈两个人住在很穷的农村。他们住的地方旁边是个墓地，墓地老是有很多人祭拜。因为人们在祭拜，他们一直在哭。孟子和孟子的妈妈住在这里才一个星期就觉得受不了了，而且害得孟子的学习。

**Grammar**
- 古代的时候 → 在古代: 古代 is the location/time setting, so use 在+Place: 在古代.
**Vocabulary**
- 害得 → 影响了: 害得 needs a harmful result after the person or noun. 影响了 fits here because 学习 is the noun affected.
- (suggestion) 的 → (delete): It would be more natural to say 孟子学习 without 的 here.
**Coherence: connectors**
- (suggestion) ∅ → 所以: It would be more natural to add 所以 to show that the people’s crying is the result of their worshipping.

**Vocabulary suggestions**
- 很多 (“有很多人祭拜”) → 许多: A slightly more varied way to say “many.”; 不少: Means “quite a few” and sounds natural in narration.
- 哭 (“他们一直在哭”) → 哭泣: A more literary word for “cry.”; 痛哭: Means to cry deeply or bitterly.
- 穷 (“很穷的农村”) → 贫穷: A more formal word for “poor.”

**Expressiveness**
- At “墓地老是有很多人祭拜。”: Add that Mencius found the ceremonies interesting and copied the people with his friends. For example: 孟子觉得祭拜很好玩，就和朋友学他们祭拜。
- At “因为人们在祭拜，他们一直在哭。”: Add that the visitors became angry because Mencius laughed while copying the ceremony. For example: 孟子一边笑一边祭拜，人们很生气。
- At “孟子和孟子的妈妈住在这里才一个星期就觉得受不了了”: Add the mother’s thought that staying there would bring more trouble. For example: 孟子的妈妈想：“住在这里，麻烦会越来越多。”

**Encouragement** You clearly set the scene by introducing Mencius, his mother, and the cemetery. You also explain what the people at the cemetery were doing, which helps the reader understand the atmosphere.

## Scene 2

> 孟子的妈妈觉得孟子取得良好的教育是很重要的，所以孟子的妈妈认为这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好，就打算跟孟子搬家。孟子的妈妈带孟子不是搬到城市，而是搬到了孟子老师的家。

**Grammar**
- (suggestion) ∅ → 不是: It would be more natural to use 不是……而是…… to contrast the two places.
- 不是 → 没有: For a completed action that did not happen, use 没有, not 不是.
**Vocabulary**
- 取得 → 接受: 取得 does not normally collocate with 教育; use 接受 instead.
- (suggestion) 跟 → 让: It would be more natural to use 让 when the mother makes Mengzi move. 跟 means “with,” while 让 shows that she tells him to do the action.
**Coherence: connectors**
- (suggestion) 的 → (delete): It would be more natural to omit 的 here and say 孟子妈妈.
- (suggestion) 的教育 → (delete): It would be more natural to say 这样的地方对儿子不太好; 的教育 is not needed here.

**Vocabulary suggestions**
- 重要 (“取得良好的教育是很重要的”) → 关键: This means “key” or “crucial” and makes the importance stronger.
- 打算 (“就打算跟孟子搬家”) → 决定: This shows that she has made a firmer choice.
- 地方 (“这样的地方”) → 环境: This refers more specifically to the surroundings and their effect on a child.

**Expressiveness**
- At “所以孟子的妈妈认为这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好，就打算跟孟子搬家。”: You could add that his mother feels worried because she wants him to study well. For example: 妈妈很着急，她想让孟子好好学习。
- At “就打算跟孟子搬家。”: You could add a short quote to show her decision. For example: 妈妈说：“我们再搬一次家吧。”
- At “孟子的妈妈带孟子不是搬到城市，而是搬到了孟子老师的家。”: You could describe that the new place is quieter and better for studying. For example: 那里很安静，孟子可以看书学习。

**Encouragement** You clearly explain Mengzi’s mother’s reason for moving: she cares about his education. You also use “所以” and “而是” well to connect her thinking and her action.

## Scene 3

> 孟子跟他的老师从早到晚学习，难怪他的成绩很快就进步了很多。孟子学习的时候，孟子的妈妈不是待在家里就是编织衣服赚钱。以后孟子成为了中国有名的哲学家。

**Grammar**
- 待在家里就是 → 在家里就是待: 家里 is the location, so use 在+Place before the action: 在家里待.
**Vocabulary**
- (suggestion) 编织衣服 → 织布: It would be more natural to say 织布 for the activity of making cloth to earn money.

**Vocabulary suggestions**
- 学习 (“从早到晚学习”) → 认真学习: shows that he studies carefully and takes it seriously; 专心学习: shows that he concentrates and is not distracted
- 进步 (“进步了很多”) → 提高: is often used for an improvement in grades or ability; 进展: emphasizes progress over a period of time
- 有名 (“中国有名的哲学家”) → 著名: is a more formal word for well-known; 闻名: means known by many people, often for achievements

**Expressiveness**
- At “孟子跟他的老师从早到晚学习”: Show that Mengzi is serious and focused when he studies. For example: 孟子很认真地看书学习。
- At “难怪他的成绩很快就进步了很多。”: Add his mother’s happy feeling when she sees his progress. For example: 孟子的妈妈看到他的成绩进步，很高兴。
- At “孟子学习的时候，孟子的妈妈不是待在家里就是编织衣服赚钱。”: You could add a short sentence from his mother to show that she thinks this place is good for his learning. For example: 孟子的妈妈高兴地说：“这儿很适合孟子学习！”

**Encouragement** You clearly show the result of Mengzi’s hard work: his grades improve and he later becomes a philosopher. Using “从早到晚” also gives a strong sense that he studies every day for a long time.
