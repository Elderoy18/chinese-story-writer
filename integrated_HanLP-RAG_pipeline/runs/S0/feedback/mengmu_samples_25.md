# Feedback: mengmu_samples#25

## Scene 1

> 这是古代的故事。孟子小的时候，他的爸爸很早就去世了。从他的家到墓地很近，所以他每天老是从早到晚去墓地跟朋友们玩玩儿。

**Coherence: reference**
- ⟦孟子⟧小的时候: This character appears for the first time, so please introduce 孟子 briefly, for example with an identifying noun phrase.
**Coherence: connectors**
- (suggestion) 所以他每天老是从早到晚去墓… → 所以他每天从早到晚去墓…: It would be more natural to say 每天从早到晚, because 每天 already shows that this happened repeatedly.
- …老是从早到晚去墓地跟朋友们… → …老是从早到晚在墓地跟朋友们…: From early to late, the playing takes place at the cemetery, so use 在墓地 rather than 去墓地.
- (suggestion) …地跟朋友们玩玩儿 → …地跟朋友们玩儿: It would be more natural to say 玩儿 here. Delete the extra 玩 to avoid repetition.

**Vocabulary suggestions**
- 很近 (“到墓地很近”) → 不远: This is a natural, slightly more varied way to say that a place is close.
- 朋友们 (“跟朋友们玩玩儿”) → 小伙伴: This sounds warm and natural for childhood friends.

**Expressiveness**
- At “从他的家到墓地很近，所以他每天老是从早到晚去墓地跟朋友们玩玩儿。”: Add that he saw people worshipping at the cemetery and found it interesting. For example: 他看见很多人来祭拜，觉得很有意思。
- At “从他的家到墓地很近，所以他每天老是从早到晚去墓地跟朋友们玩玩儿。”: You could add a short line of speech to show that he wanted to copy what the adults did. For example: 他对朋友们说：“我们也来学他们吧！”

**Encouragement** You clearly set the story in ancient times and introduce an important part of Mengzi’s childhood. You also use time details such as “很早” and “从早到晚” to show the situation clearly.

## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈觉得在墓地玩儿，害得，所以他们搬家。搬家对孟子有好的影响，他才五岁，可是每天不是写字就是帮妈妈的工作。

**Grammar**
- 他的妈妈觉得在墓地玩儿 → 他的妈妈觉得在墓地玩儿玩儿: Here 在墓地 describes 玩儿, not 觉得. Say 觉得在墓地玩儿: 在+Place comes before the action it modifies.
- …字就是帮妈妈的工作 → …字就是帮妈妈工作: Use 帮妈妈工作 here, without 的. 帮妈妈工作 means “help Mom work.”
**Vocabulary**
- 害得 → 不好: 害得 needs 害得 + N/sb. + harmful result. Here you are saying that playing in a cemetery is not good, so use 不好.
**Coherence: connectors**
- (suggestion) 害得，所以他们搬家 → 害得他们搬家: It would be more natural to delete 所以 here; the reason for moving is already clear from the previous words.

**Vocabulary suggestions**
- 妈妈 (“他的妈妈觉得在墓地玩儿”) → 母亲: A more formal word for “mother,” often used in stories.
- 搬家 (“所以他们搬家。”) → 迁居: A more formal word for moving home.

**Expressiveness**
- At “所以他们搬家。”: Add that the new home was very busy, with many people selling things every day. For example: 新家很热闹，有很多人卖东西。
- At “他的妈妈觉得在墓地玩儿，害得，”: You could add what his mother says to show that she is worried about his learning. For example: 妈妈说：“这儿会影响你的学习。”
- At “可是每天不是写字就是帮妈妈的工作。”: Show what Mengzi does when he watches the sellers, such as copying how they take money and bargain. For example: 孟子学商人收钱，还说：“便宜一点吧！”

**Encouragement** You clearly show that the family moves and that the new environment affects Mengzi. Your use of “每天不是……就是……” is also a good story pattern for describing his daily actions.

## Scene 3

> 他很聪明的人，难怪他成为有名的人。

**Grammar**
- 他很聪明的人 → 他是个很聪明的人: Use 是个 before 很聪明的人 because this is a noun phrase describing what he is.
**Coherence: reference**
- 难怪他成为有名的人 → 难怪成为有名的人: 他 is already clear from the previous clause, so delete it; Chinese uses zero form here.

**Vocabulary suggestions**
- 聪明 (“他很聪明的人”) → 优秀: means excellent; it describes someone who does very well.
- 有名 (“成为有名的人”) → 著名: means famous or well-known; it is a slightly more formal word.

**Expressiveness**
- At “他很聪明的人，难怪他成为有名的人。”: You could add what people say about Mengzi to make the ending feel more lively. For example: 大家都说：“孟子真聪明！”
- At “他很聪明的人，难怪他成为有名的人。”: You could add that he later became a philosopher after studying hard. For example: 后来，他成了一个有名的哲学家。

**Encouragement** You clearly show the final result: Mengzi became a well-known person. Your use of “难怪” also gives the ending a clear reason and conclusion.
