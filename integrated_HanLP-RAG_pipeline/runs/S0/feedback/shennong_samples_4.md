## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个男人，他的名字是神农。他看到了有很多人不知道哪个植物好吃，也不知道什么植物对你的身体有好处。这个问题神农觉得很糟糕，所以神农去了吃每种植物。他吃了一种植物以后，就写了这个植物对你有没有好处。有的时候，神农吃了很好吃的植物。有的时候，他吃了很危险的植物。他吃完了每种植物以后，会来跟他的朋友聊到他吃的植物了。

**Grammar**
- 古代的时候 → 在古代: 古代 is the location/time setting, so use 在+Place before the action: 在古代。
- (suggestion) 也不知道什么植物对你… → 也不了解什么植物对你…: It would be more natural to say 了解什么植物对人的身体有好处.
- …道什么植物对你的身体有好处 → …道什么植物对人的身体有好处: This means people in general, not the listener. Use 人 instead of 你.
- …了这个植物对你有没有好处 → …了这个植物对人有没有好处: This refers to people in general, not the listener. Use 人 instead of 你.
**Vocabulary**
- 所以神农去了吃每种植物 → 所以神农去吃每种植物: 了 does not go between 去 and 吃. Use 去吃, without 了 in the middle.
- (suggestion) 所以神农去了吃每种植物 → 所以神农去了每种植物: It would be more natural to omit 吃 here.
**Coherence: reference**
- ⟦他⟧的名字是神农: 神农 appears for the first time here, so please introduce him briefly with an identifying noun phrase before using his name or a pronoun.
- 所以神农去了吃每种植… → 所以去了吃每种植…: 神农 is already clear from the previous clause, so delete it; Chinese uses zero form here.
- …跟他的朋友聊到他吃的植物了 → …跟他的朋友聊他吃的植物了: 聊 takes its topic directly. Use 聊他吃的植物, without 到.
- …到他吃的植物了 → …到他吃的植物: 会 already describes a future or habitual action here, so do not add 了 at the end.
**Content**
- …多人不知道哪个植物好吃 → …多人不知道哪种植物好吃: Use 种 for kinds of plants. 个 does not classify plants naturally here.

**Vocabulary suggestions**
- 好吃 (“很好吃的植物”) → 可口: means tasty and pleasant to eat; it is a little more descriptive.; 美味: means delicious; it gives stronger praise for food.
- 危险 (“很危险的植物”) → 有毒: means poisonous, so it is more precise for a harmful plant.
- 很多 (“有很多人”) → 许多: means many; it is a slightly more formal story word.

**Expressiveness**
- At “这个问题神农觉得很糟糕”: Add what Shennong decides to do, using his own words. For example: 他想：“我要找一找什么植物可以做药。”
- At “有的时候，他吃了很危险的植物。”: Add a detail about how his body feels after he eats a poisonous plant. For example: 他吃了有毒的植物，肚子很疼。
- At “他吃完了每种植物以后，会来跟他的朋友聊到他吃的植物了。”: Add a short sentence showing what he tells his friends about a plant. For example: 他对朋友说：“这个植物可以做药。”

**Encouragement** You clearly explain the problem that many people did not know which plants were good for the body. You also use “有的时候” to show that Shennong had different experiences with different plants.

## Scene 2

> 一个人问神农，哪个植物好吃。神农说，这就要看你的口味了。要是你喜欢吃甜的，有很多甜的水果，比方说苹果、西瓜和梨。一个别的人问神农，哪个植物对你的身体有好处。神农说，有很多种植物对你的身体有好处，比方说小白菜、黄瓜、茶和菠菜。一个别的人问神农，哪个植物对你的身体有坏处。

**Vocabulary**
- 哪个植物好吃 → 哪种植物好吃: Use 种 when asking about kinds of plants: 哪种植物好吃?
- 这就要看你的口味了 → 这就要看你的口味了: 看 means “look,” while 看到 means “see.” Here you are asking what the taste depends on, not describing a result of seeing, so use 看, not 看到.
- 一个别的人问神农 → 一个另外一个人问神农: This means an additional person, so use 另外, not 别的: 另外一个人。
- 哪个植物对你的身… → 哪种植物对你的身…: Use 种 when asking about kinds of plants: 哪种植物对你的身体有好处?
- 一个别的人问神农 → 一个另外一个人问神农: This means an additional person, so use 另外, not 别的: 另外一个人。
- (suggestion) 哪个植物对你的身… → 哪种植物对你的身…: It would be more natural to use 种 when asking about kinds of plants: 哪种植物对你的身体有坏处?

**Vocabulary suggestions**
- 好吃 (“哪个植物好吃”) → 美味: means delicious; it is a slightly more vivid and formal way to describe food.; 可口: means tasty and pleasant to eat; it often describes food or fruit.
- 有好处 (“对你的身体有好处”) → 有益: means beneficial; it is a concise word often used for health.
- 很多 (“有很多甜的水果”) → 各种各样: means many different kinds, emphasizing variety.

**Expressiveness**
- At “神农说，这就要看你的口味了。”: You could add a short quote to make Shennong sound friendly and explain that different people like different tastes. For example: 神农笑着说：“每个人喜欢的味道不一样。”
- At “一个别的人问神农，哪个植物对你的身体有好处。”: You could add that this person wants to stay healthy, so the question has a clear reason. For example: 他想让身体更健康。
- At “一个别的人问神农，哪个植物对你的身体有坏处。”: You could continue with Shennong’s warning that some plants are poisonous and can make people very sick. For example: 神农说：“有的植物有毒，吃了会肚子疼。”

**Encouragement** You clearly organize the scene as a question-and-answer conversation, and you give useful examples of fruits, vegetables, and tea. Your explanation of “口味” also makes Shennong’s answer sound sensible and connected to daily life.

## Scene 3

> 神农说，有一天，我吃了一个黄色的花，名字是夹竹桃。我一吃夹竹桃，就觉得这种植物对我的脑有坏处。可是，一个灵芝和一个男人正好在我的旁边，我说，麻烦你把那个植物给我吃一下。我一吃这个灵芝就好了。所以，你得小心吃植物，因为有的植物对你的健康有坏处，特别是夹竹桃。因为夹竹桃有毒。

**Vocabulary**
- 我吃了一个黄色的花 → 我吃了一朵黄色的花: Use the classifier 朵 for flowers: 一朵黄色的花.
- 名字是夹竹桃 → 它的名字是夹竹桃: 名字 needs a possessive subject here. Use 它的名字是夹竹桃.
- …种植物对我的脑有坏处 → …种植物对我的大脑有坏处: Use 大脑, not 脑 alone, when you mean the brain.
- 一个灵芝和一个男… → 一株灵芝和一个男…: Use the classifier 株 for 灵芝: 一株灵芝.
**Other**
- 因为夹竹桃有毒 → 夹竹桃有毒: 因为 is redundant here because this is a separate statement, not a full reason clause. Say 夹竹桃有毒.

**Vocabulary suggestions**
- 黄色 (“黄色的花”) → 金黄色: a bright, golden yellow; 淡黄色: a light, pale yellow
- 旁边 (“我的旁边”) → 身边: means close beside someone; it sounds natural for a person nearby
- 小心 (“小心吃植物”) → 当心: a common, slightly stronger warning word; 谨慎: means careful and cautious, with a more serious tone

**Expressiveness**
- At “有一天，我吃了一个黄色的花，名字是夹竹桃。”: You could describe the flower as big and beautiful, like a star, before Shennong eats it. For example: 那个花又大又漂亮，像星星一样。
- At “我一吃夹竹桃，就觉得这种植物对我的脑有坏处。”: Add how badly Shennong feels after eating it, such as a stomachache and lying on the ground. For example: 我肚子很疼，难受得不得了，躺在地上。
- At “我说，麻烦你把那个植物给我吃一下。”: You could add the man’s reply to make this moment sound more like a story. For example: 那个男人说：“快吃这个！”

**Encouragement** You clearly explain the danger of 夹竹桃 and give an important warning at the end. You also include how 灵芝 helps Shennong feel better, so the scene has a clear problem and solution.
