# Feedback: mengmu_samples#12

## Scene 1

> 古代，孟子跟孟母住在墓地的旁边。可是孟母注意，住在墓地的旁边影响孟子。他常常跟朋友们祭拜玩，所以孟母决定他们会搬家。这次，他们住在城市里的商店的旁边，从早到晚很吵。

**Grammar**
- …常常跟朋友们祭拜玩 → …常常跟朋友们玩祭拜的游戏: Put 玩 before the activity being played. Say 玩祭拜的游戏.
**Vocabulary**
- (suggestion) …在墓地的旁边影响孟子 → …在墓地的旁边会影响孟子: It would be more natural to say 会影响孟子 to show that living beside a cemetery may have this result.
- 所以孟母决定他们会搬家 → 所以孟母决定搬家: After 决定, use the action directly: 决定搬家. Do not add 他们会 here.
**Coherence: reference**
- ⟦孟子⟧跟孟母住在墓…: This character appears for the first time, so please introduce 孟子 briefly with an identifying noun phrase, not only his name.
- 孟子跟⟦孟母⟧住在墓地的旁…: This character appears for the first time, so please introduce 孟母 briefly, for example as 孟子的妈妈.
**Coherence: connectors**
- 可是孟母注意 → 可是孟母注意到: Use 注意到 when you mean “notice” or “realize” a fact.

**Vocabulary suggestions**
- 常常 (“他常常跟朋友们祭拜玩”) → 总是: shows that he does it again and again, often with a slightly stronger feeling.; 经常: is a natural word for doing something frequently.
- 商店 (“城市里的商店的旁边”) → 店铺: means shops and sounds a little more varied in a story.; 市场: means a marketplace, with many sellers and shops together.
- 吵 (“从早到晚很吵”) → 热闹: describes a lively, busy place in a more positive way.; 嘈杂: focuses on the loud, mixed noises of a busy place.

**Expressiveness**
- At “他常常跟朋友们祭拜玩，”: Add that Mengzi thinks copying the worshippers is fun, to show why he keeps doing it. For example: 他觉得祭拜很好玩。
- At “所以孟母决定他们会搬家。”: Add Mengmu’s worried thought or speech about this place not being good for Mengzi. For example: 孟母想：“这里不适合孟子。”
- At “这次，他们住在城市里的商店的旁边，从早到晚很吵。”: Add a detail about the many merchants selling things, so the new neighbourhood feels busy and lively. For example: 每天有很多商人卖东西。

**Encouragement** You clearly show the first move from the cemetery to the city. You also give a reason for the move by explaining that Mengzi’s behaviour affects his mother’s decision.

## Scene 2

> 城市也有点儿危险。害得孟母只好一直陪孟子。孟子也没有朋友，难怪他老是看起来不高兴。住在商店的旁边不是适合孟子，而是让他觉得很伤心。

**Grammar**
- (suggestion) …母只好一直陪孟子 → …母只好一直陪着孟子: It would be more natural to say 一直陪着孟子. 着 shows that 陪 is a continuing action.
**Vocabulary**
- …在商店的旁边不是适合孟子 → …在商店的旁边不但不适合孟子: this sentence needs the paired structure 不但……而且…… to add a second negative result.
- 而是让他觉得很伤… → 而且让他觉得很伤…: 不但 should pair with 而且, not 而是.

**Vocabulary suggestions**
- 城市 (“城市也有点儿危险。”) → 市中心: This is more precise for the busy central part of a city.
- 不高兴 (“他老是看起来不高兴。”) → 难过: This more clearly shows that someone feels sad.; 孤单: This means lonely, which fits a child without friends.
- 伤心 (“让他觉得很伤心。”) → 难受: This describes feeling upset or uncomfortable inside.; 失望: This means disappointed, when something is not as hoped.

**Expressiveness**
- At “城市也有点儿危险。”: You could describe the busy shops and sellers, so the city setting feels more vivid. For example: 每天有很多商人卖东西，很热闹。
- At “害得孟母只好一直陪孟子。”: Add what Meng’s mother says to show that she is worried about his learning in this environment. For example: 孟母说：“这儿会影响孟子学习。”
- At “孟子也没有朋友，难怪他老是看起来不高兴。”: Add a small detail about Meng’s feelings, such as missing friends or feeling lonely. For example: 他觉得很孤单，也很想朋友。

**Encouragement** You clearly show Meng’s unhappy feelings and explain how the environment affects him. Your use of “难怪” gives a natural reason for why he looks unhappy.

## Scene 3

> 所以，孟母和孟子搬到学校的旁边住。才五岁孟子开始学哲学。在学校，孟子不是看书，就是练习写字。将来，他成为中国的一个非常有名的哲学家。

**Vocabulary**
- (suggestion) 他成为中国的一… → 他会成为中国的一…: It would be more natural to add 会 to show that becoming a famous philosopher was his future result. 成为 is correctly followed by the profession noun 哲学家.
**Coherence: 了 / 着 / 在**
- 将来 → 后来: 将来 means “in the future,” but this is a later event in a past story. Use 后来 for the next story event.
**Coherence: connectors**
- 才五岁孟子开始学哲… → 才五岁时孟子开始学哲…: Use 时 after an age phrase to show the time when an action happened: 才五岁时。
- (suggestion) 才五岁孟子开始学哲学 → 才五岁孟子就开始学哲学: It would be more natural to use 才Num.就V. to emphasize that he started learning at the early age of five.

**Vocabulary suggestions**
- 看书 (“孟子不是看书，就是练习写字。”) → 阅读: A more formal word for reading books or written materials.
- 有名 (“一个非常有名的哲学家”) → 著名: A slightly more formal word meaning well-known or famous.
- 非常 (“一个非常有名的哲学家”) → 十分: A natural, slightly more formal intensifier meaning very.; 格外: It means especially or particularly, with a stronger emphasis.

**Expressiveness**
- At “所以，孟母和孟子搬到学校的旁边住。”: Add that Meng’s mother feels happy because she thinks the school environment is good for her child. For example: 孟母很高兴，觉得这儿很适合孟子。
- At “在学校，孟子不是看书，就是练习写字。”: Add a small detail about Mengzi studying seriously every day. For example: 孟子每天认真看书，也认真写字。
- At “将来，他成为中国的一个非常有名的哲学家。”: You could add Meng’s mother’s happy words when she sees him begin to study. For example: 孟母说：“这次搬家太对了！”

**Encouragement** You clearly show the final move to the school and Mengzi’s new study routine. The pattern “不是……，就是……” makes his daily activities sound clear and natural.
