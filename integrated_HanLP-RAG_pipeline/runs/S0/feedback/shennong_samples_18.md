# Feedback: shennong_samples#18

## Scene 1

> 神农和他的家人住在古代，所以不知道什么植物能吃。因为他的家人常常生病了，所以神农和他的哥哥一起去旅行找可以吃的植物。他们去很多不一样的地方，比方说高山、山谷和沙漠。

**Grammar**
- …农和他的家人住在古代 → …农和他的家人生活在古代: 住 is used for living in a specific home or place. For living in a historical era, say 生活在古代.
- 他们去很多不一样的… → 他们去了很多不一样的…: This is a completed action in the narrative, so add 了 after 去.
**Coherence: reference**
- ⟦神农⟧和他的家人住…: This character appears for the first time, so please introduce 神农 briefly, for example with an identifying noun phrase such as 一个叫神农的人.
**Coherence: 了 / 着 / 在**
- …家人常常生病了 → …家人常常生病: This describes a repeated condition with 常常, so use no 了 for routine/habit.

**Vocabulary suggestions**
- 不一样 (“很多不一样的地方”) → 不同: This is a slightly more formal way to say “not the same.”; 各种各样: This emphasizes that there are many varied kinds of places.
- 旅行 (“一起去旅行找可以吃的植物”) → 探险: This suggests an adventurous journey into unfamiliar or difficult places.
- 找 (“找可以吃的植物”) → 寻找: This is a more formal and purposeful word for “look for.”

**Expressiveness**
- At “所以不知道什么植物能吃。”: You could explain that some plants are dangerous, so people can become ill after eating them. For example: 有的植物有毒，吃了以后会生病。
- At “因为他的家人常常生病了，所以神农和他的哥哥一起去旅行找可以吃的植物。”: Add Shen Nong’s worry and a short line of speech to show why he wants to go. For example: 神农很担心，说：“我们去找可以让人好起来的植物吧。”
- At “他们去很多不一样的地方，比方说高山、山谷和沙漠。”: Describe the high mountains and show that the journey is difficult. For example: 高山又高又大，他们走得很累。

**Encouragement** You clearly explain why Shen Nong begins his journey: his family is often ill. Your list of places, including mountains, valleys, and deserts, also makes the journey feel broad and adventurous.

## Scene 2

> 他们在很漂亮的河找夹竹桃。神农问他的哥哥：“我应该吃这种植物吗？”哥哥说：“这就要看你想要安全还是接近知道了。“神农一吃夹竹桃就生病了。哥哥说：“太麻烦了。

**Vocabulary**
- …说：“这就要看你想要安全还… → …说：“这就要看到你想要安全还…: Use 看到, not 看, because this refers to seeing the situation or result.
**Coherence: reference**
- …想要安全还是接近知道了 → …想要安全还是想知道了: 接近 cannot modify 知道. Use 想知道 to mean “want to know.”
- (suggestion) …安全还是接近知道了 → …安全还是接近知道: It would be more natural to say 想知道 without 了, because this is a general choice, not a completed action.
**Content**
- …在很漂亮的河找夹竹桃 → …在很漂亮的河边找夹竹桃: Use 河边 here: a river needs a locative word when it is the place where the action happens.

**Vocabulary suggestions**
- 漂亮 (“很漂亮的河”) → 美丽: A slightly more literary way to say “beautiful.”; 清澈: Means “clear” and is especially useful for describing river water.
- 吃 (“我应该吃这种植物吗？”) → 尝: Means “taste/try” and fits when someone tries an unknown plant.; 尝一尝: A natural, softer way to say “try a little.”

**Expressiveness**
- At “他们在很漂亮的河找夹竹桃。”: You could describe the flowers they find: they are yellow, big, and look like stars. For example: 河边有黄色的花，像星星一样，又大又漂亮。
- At “神农一吃夹竹桃就生病了。”: You could show how badly Shennong feels, such as his stomach hurting and needing to lie down. For example: 他的肚子很疼，躺在地上，难受得不得了。

**Encouragement** You clearly show the important cause and result: Shennong eats the oleander and becomes ill. You also use dialogue to show that he is thinking carefully about whether the plant is safe.

## Scene 3

> 夹竹桃是毒的，我想知道灵芝会帮他。”哥哥给神农灵芝。神农吃完了以后开始痊愈了。神农说：“谢谢哥哥，你帮得真好。灵芝我们可以吃，特别是如果我们已经吃了夹竹桃。“

**Vocabulary**
- 夹竹桃是毒的 → 夹竹桃有毒的: Here the structure needs an Adj., but 毒 is a noun. Use 有毒 to mean “poisonous.”
- 我想知道灵芝会帮他 → 我想灵芝会帮他: 知道 means “know,” but here you are stating what you think will happen. Delete it: 想灵芝会帮他 means “think Lingzhi will help him.”
- (suggestion) …想知道灵芝会帮他 → …想知道灵芝会不会帮他: It would be more natural to say 不会帮他 if you mean that Lingzhi will not help him.
**Coherence: reference**
- 特别是如果我们已经吃了夹竹… → 特别是如果已经吃了夹竹…: 我们 is already clear from the previous clause, so delete it; Chinese uses zero form here.
**Coherence: 了 / 着 / 在**
- ”哥哥给神农灵芝 → ”哥哥给了神农灵芝: Giving the 灵芝 is completed in the story, so it takes 了completed: 给了.
**Other**
- (suggestion) …别是如果我们已经吃了夹竹桃 → …别是如果我们吃了夹竹桃: It would be more natural to delete 已经 because 吃了 already shows the completed action.

**Vocabulary suggestions**
- 给 (“哥哥给神农灵芝。”) → 递给: means to hand something to someone, so it shows the action more clearly.
- 吃 (“神农吃完了以后开始痊愈了。”) → 服用: means to take medicine; it is especially suitable for lingzhi as a healing plant.

**Expressiveness**
- At “哥哥给神农灵芝。”: Add what the lingzhi looks like and show that the brother acts quickly to help him. For example: 哥哥赶快拿起像伞一样的灵芝给神农吃。
- At “神农吃完了以后开始痊愈了。”: Add how Shen Nong feels when his stomach pain becomes better. For example: 神农很快觉得好多了，肚子也不那么疼了。

**Encouragement** You clearly show the cause and result: the brother gives Shen Nong lingzhi, and Shen Nong gets better. Your direct speech also makes the scene sound warm and thankful.
