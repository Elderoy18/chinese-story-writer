# Feedback: mengmu_samples#10

## Scene 1

> 孟子小的时候，从早到晚都跟朋友玩。孟子和他的朋友喜欢玩在墓地里，装祭拜。孟子五岁的时候，跟妈妈搬家。他们搬家不是因为孟子玩得太多，而是因为妈妈认为他们的家离墓地太近。

**Grammar**
- …他的朋友喜欢玩在墓地里 → …他的朋友喜欢在墓地里玩: 墓地里 is the location, so use 在+Place before the action: 在墓地里玩.
- (suggestion) 而是因为妈妈认为他们… → 而是妈妈认为他们…: It would be more natural to say 而是妈妈认为…… because 而是 already introduces the contrasting reason, so 因为 is unnecessary.

**Vocabulary**
- 装祭拜 → 装作祭拜: Use 装作 before a verb phrase. Here, say 装作祭拜.
- (suggestion) 玩 (“从早到晚都跟朋友玩”) → 玩耍: A slightly more story-like word for children playing.; 嬉戏: Means playing happily and noisily; it is more vivid.
- (suggestion) 朋友 (“孟子和他的朋友”) → 伙伴: A natural word for companions who play or do things together.; 小伙伴: A warm, child-like word for young friends.
- (suggestion) 近 (“离墓地太近”) → 靠近: Means “close to” and works well when describing a location.; 邻近: Means “nearby” or “next to”; it sounds a little more descriptive.

**Coherence**
- **Reference** ⟦孟子⟧小的时候: This character appears for the first time, so please introduce 孟子 briefly with an identifying noun phrase, not only his name.

**Other**
- (suggestion) 跟妈妈搬家 → 跟妈妈搬家了: It would be more natural to add 了 after 搬家 to show the move was completed.

**Expressiveness**
- At “孟子和他的朋友喜欢玩在墓地里，装祭拜。”: Add that Mengzi watched people come to worship and found it interesting. For example: 他每天看见很多人来祭拜，觉得很好玩。
- At “孟子和他的朋友喜欢玩在墓地里，装祭拜。”: Add a short line of speech to show the children copying the adults. For example: 孟子说：“我们也来祭拜吧！”
- At “孟子和他的朋友喜欢玩在墓地里，装祭拜。”: Add that the children laughed while playing, which made the real visitors unhappy. For example: 他们一边笑一边玩，来祭拜的人很生气。

**Encouragement** You clearly explain Mengzi’s daily habit of playing with his friends and give a reason for the move. Using “不是……而是……” is also a good way to make the reason clear.

## Scene 2

> 妈妈认为，住离墓地太近，害得孟子的心气。孟子和孟母搬到子思老师的家，跟子思老师住。孟母帮子思打扫房间和做饭。子思帮孟母教她的儿子。因为孟子现在没有朋友，他老是读书。

**Grammar**
- 住离墓地太近 → 住得离墓地太近: Use 得 after 住 to introduce the degree result: 住得离墓地太近.
- (suggestion) …子思老师的家，跟子思老师住 → …子思老师的家住: It would be more natural to delete 跟子思老师 because 搬到子思老师的家 already shows where they will live.

**Vocabulary**
- 害得孟子的心气 → 伤了孟子的心气: 害得 needs a harmful result after 孟子的心气. Here, 伤了孟子的心气 is the natural expression.
- (suggestion) 打扫 (“孟母帮子思打扫房间”) → 收拾: This means to tidy up and put things in order.
- (suggestion) 做饭 (“孟母帮子思打扫房间和做饭”) → 下厨: This is a slightly more vivid way to say “cook,” especially when someone cooks for others.
- (suggestion) 读书 (“他老是读书”) → 学习: This is broader and can include reading, writing, and other school work.; 用功: This emphasizes studying hard and making a real effort.

**Coherence**
- **Reference** 孟子和⟦孟母⟧搬到子思老师…: 孟母 appears for the first time, so please introduce her briefly, for example 孟子的妈妈孟母.
- **Connectors** (suggestion) 他老是读书 → 所以他老是读书: It would be more natural to add 所以 to show that having no friends is the reason for reading books often.

**Expressiveness**
- At “孟子和孟母搬到子思老师的家，跟子思老师住。”: Add what Meng’s mother hopes for in the new place, using a short sentence of speech. For example: 孟母说：“这里很适合孩子学习。”
- At “因为孟子现在没有朋友，他老是读书。”: Add how Meng feels about reading, to show that he is beginning to enjoy studying. For example: 孟子觉得读书很有意思。
- At “子思帮孟母教她的儿子。”: Add a small detail about what Meng does while learning, such as reading books carefully. For example: 孟子每天认真看书学习。

**Encouragement** You clearly show the change after the move: Meng has no friends there, so he reads often. You also include how Meng’s mother and the teacher help each other, which makes the scene feel connected.

## Scene 3

> 子思认为，对孩子来说，读书最重要，特别是学哲学。孟子不是练习写文章，就是看哲学书。孟子从五岁一直学哲学。他才五岁就学哲学，难怪他成为很有名的哲学家。

**Grammar**
- 孟子从五岁一直学哲学 → 孟子从五岁起一直学哲学: After 从五岁, add 起 to show the starting point: 从五岁起一直学哲学.
- 难怪他成为很有名的哲学… → 难怪他成为了很有名的哲学…: Becoming a famous philosopher is a completed event, so use 了 after 成为.

**Vocabulary**
- 就是看哲学书 → 就是看到哲学书: The person actually sees the philosophy books, so use 看到see, not 看look.
- (suggestion) 有名 (“成为很有名的哲学家”) → 著名: Means “well-known” and is a little more formal.; 出名: Means “famous” and is common in everyday speech.
- (suggestion) 读书 (“读书最重要”) → 阅读: Means “to read” and is more formal, often used for reading books or texts.

**Coherence**
- **Reference** 难怪他成为很有名的… → 难怪成为很有名的…: 孟子 is already clear from the previous sentence, so 他 is redundant. Chinese uses zero form here.

**Expressiveness**
- At “孟子不是练习写文章，就是看哲学书。”: Add that Mengzi studies very seriously and is interested in philosophy. For example: 他很喜欢哲学，读得很认真。
- At “孟子从五岁一直学哲学。”: Add a short quote from Mengzi’s mother to show that she feels happy about his learning environment. For example: 孟母很高兴，说：“这儿很适合孩子学习。”
- At “他才五岁就学哲学，难怪他成为很有名的哲学家。”: Add that his mother feels proud when she sees him become successful. For example: 孟母看到他很高兴，也很骄傲。

**Encouragement** You clearly show that Mengzi studied philosophy from a very young age. Your final sentence also connects his childhood learning to his later success as a philosopher.
