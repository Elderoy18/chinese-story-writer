# Feedback: mengmu_samples#39

## Scene 1

> 古代的时候人觉得祭拜非常重要，孟子很小的时候，从早到晚一直祭拜，因为他妈妈告诉他祭拜很重要。虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜。他的同学们老是对他开玩笑了。孟子妈妈看了他的同学们对孟子不好的影响，所以孟子和孟子妈妈搬家了。

**Grammar**
- 古代的时候人觉得祭拜非… → 在古代人觉得祭拜非…: 古代 is the setting, so use 在+Place before the action: 在古代人觉得祭拜非常重要。
- (suggestion) …同学们对孟子不好的影响 → …同学们对孟子有不好的影响: It would be more natural to say 对孟子有不好的影响. Use 有 to show that the classmates caused a bad influence.

**Vocabulary**
- 孟子妈妈看了他的同学们… → 孟子妈妈看到了他的同学们…: 孟子妈妈 actually sees the bad influence, so use 看到see, not 看look.
- (suggestion) 重要 (“祭拜非常重要”) → 神圣: This describes something solemn and worthy of deep respect.; 格外重要: This means especially important and adds a little emphasis.
- (suggestion) 开玩笑 (“对他开玩笑”) → 嘲笑: This is more negative: it means making fun of someone in an unkind way.
- (suggestion) 搬家 (“孟子和孟子妈妈搬家了”) → 迁居: This is a more formal word for moving home.

**Coherence**
- **Reference** ⟦孟子⟧很小的时候: This character appears for the first time, so please introduce 孟子 briefly, for example 孟子是一个小男孩。
- **Reference** (suggestion) 因为每天只祭拜 → 因为每天只是祭拜: It would be more natural to say 因为每天只是祭拜. 是 emphasizes that worshipping was the only activity.
- **了 / 着 / 在** (suggestion) …以孟子和孟子妈妈搬家了 → …以孟子和孟子的妈妈搬家了: It would be more natural to say 孟子和孟子的妈妈. Use 的 to show that the mother is Mengzi's mother.
- **Connectors** 虽然他没有朋友 → 他没有朋友: 虽然 needs a matching contrast, such as 但是. There is no contrast here, so delete 虽然.
- **Connectors** …是对他开玩笑了 → …是对他开玩笑: 老是 describes a habitual action, so do not use sentence-final 了 here.

**Expressiveness**
- At “从早到晚一直祭拜”: You could add that he saw people coming to the cemetery and thought their worshipping was interesting. For example: 他看见很多人来祭拜，觉得很好玩。
- At “孟子妈妈看了他的同学们对孟子不好的影响”: Add Mengzi’s mother’s worried thought to show why she decided to move. For example: 孟子妈妈很着急，心想：“不能再住在这儿了。”
- At “所以孟子和孟子妈妈搬家了。”: You could describe how quickly they moved to make the decision feel more important. For example: 才住了一个星期，他们就搬家了。

**Encouragement** You clearly show a cause-and-effect link: the bad influence on Mengzi leads to the family moving. You also set the scene in ancient times and explain why worshipping matters.

## Scene 2

> 孟子才十一岁，成为一个哲学家。孟子不是祭拜，而是看政治文章，难怪他成为了哲学家。不过，看过很多政治文章对他害得，他有很多问题。

**Grammar**
- *No corrections.*

**Vocabulary**
- 而是看政治文章 → 而是看到政治文章: 孟子 actually sees the political articles here, so use 看到see, not 看look.
- …政治文章对他害得 → …政治文章对他有害: Use 对他有害 for something that is harmful to him. 害得 needs a person plus a following result or action.
- (suggestion) 成为 (“成为一个哲学家”) → 成长为: means to grow up and become someone over time.
- (suggestion) 很多 (“很多政治文章”) → 许多: a slightly more formal word for “many.”; 大量: emphasizes a large quantity.
- (suggestion) 问题 (“很多问题”) → 疑问: means questions or doubts that he wants to understand.; 难题: means difficult problems to solve.

**Coherence**
- **了 / 着 / 在** 成为一个哲学家 → 成为了一个哲学家: 成为 is a change of state completed in the story, so it takes 了completed: 成为了。

**Expressiveness**
- At “孟子不是祭拜，而是看政治文章，”: Describe how he studies quietly and seriously near the school. For example: 他每天安静地看书学习。
- At “孟子才十一岁，成为一个哲学家。”: Add his mother’s proud reaction and a short comment about the new place being good for learning. For example: 孟子妈妈很高兴，说：“这里很好，他喜欢学习。”
- At “不过，看过很多政治文章对他害得，他有很多问题。”: Show that he likes thinking about the questions he finds in the books. For example: 他常常想：“为什么会这样？”

**Encouragement** You clearly show the important change from copying rituals to reading and learning. You also connect his reading with his later success as a philosopher.

## Scene 3

> 他一直问问题，但是不能解决那些问题。孟子长大，他开始能解决问题。他常常建议你不是学金融，而是学政治，因为如果你懂政治，你会成为高薪。后来孟子成为教授，他教学生很厉害。

**Grammar**
- 孟子长大 → 孟子长大了: Add 了 after 长大 because this is a completed change of state.

**Vocabulary**
- 他常常建议你不是学金融 → 他常常建议你不要学金融: use 不要 for a recommendation not to do something; 不是 is used to say something is not the case.
- 而是学政治 → 而要学政治: with 不要, use 而要 to contrast the recommended action.
- (suggestion) …如果你懂政治 → …如果你懂政治的话: It would be more natural to add 的话 after 如果你懂政治 to mark the condition clearly.
- 你会成为高薪 → 你会获得高薪: use 获得 because a salary is received; 成为 is used for becoming a person or identity.
- (suggestion) 问题 (“一直问问题”) → 疑问: This means a question or doubt in the mind, and sounds a little more thoughtful.; 难题: This means a difficult problem that needs careful thinking to solve.
- (suggestion) 解决 (“能解决问题”) → 处理: This means to deal with a problem or situation.; 克服: This means to overcome a difficulty or challenge.
- (suggestion) 厉害 (“教学生很厉害”) → 出色: This means excellent and is a more formal way to praise teaching.; 擅长: This means to be especially good at something; for example, 擅长教学生.

**Coherence**
- **Reference** (suggestion) 他常常建议你不是学金融 → 他常常建议人们不是学金融: It would be more natural to say 人们 because 你 has no clear referent in this narrative.
- **Reference** 你会成为高薪 → 会成为高薪: 你 is already clear from the previous clause, so delete it; Chinese uses zero form here.

**Expressiveness**
- At “他一直问问题”: Add that he felt curious and wanted to understand the answers. For example: 他很好奇，想知道答案。
- At “孟子长大，他开始能解决问题。”: Add a short thought or quote to show his confidence when he could finally solve problems. For example: 孟子很高兴：“我明白了！”
- At “后来孟子成为教授，他教学生很厉害。”: Add that his students enjoyed listening to him because he explained things clearly. For example: 学生们都喜欢听他讲课，因为他说得很清楚。

**Encouragement** You clearly show Mengzi growing from someone with many questions into someone who can solve them. You also add his later work as a teacher, which gives this scene a strong ending.
