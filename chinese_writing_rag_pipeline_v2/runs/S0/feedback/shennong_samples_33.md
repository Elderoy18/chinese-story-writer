## Scene 1

> 古代时候，人不知道他们能吃什么植物，常常一吃就死。但是不知道为什么，没有办法。所以有一天，神农出去试一试很多种植物，告诉人什么植物是安全什么是危险。

**Grammar**
- …么植物是安全什么是危险 → …么植物是安全的什么是危险: 安全 describes 植物, so use 的: 安全的植物.
- …全什么是危险 → …全什么是危险的: 危险 describes 植物, so use 的: 危险的植物.

**Vocabulary**
- 所以有一天 → 于是有一天: Use 于是 to introduce the resulting action naturally here, not 所以.
- 神农出去试一试很多种植物 → 神农出去尝一尝很多种植物: 试一试 does not naturally collocate with plants in this situation. Use 尝一尝 to mean tasting plants to find out whether they are edible.
- (suggestion) 死 (“一吃就死”) → 生病: means “become ill”; it is less final and fits what often happened to people.; 中毒: means “be poisoned”; it describes the result of eating a poisonous plant more precisely.
- (suggestion) 危险 (“什么是危险”) → 有毒: means “poisonous”; it is more specific for plants that should not be eaten.

**Coherence**
- **Reference** 人不知道他们能吃什么植物 → 人不知道自己能吃什么植物: With generic 人, use 自己 to refer back to the same people, not 他们.
- **Reference** ⟦神农⟧出去试一试很…: 神农 appears for the first time, so please briefly introduce him, for example as a person who tried plants.

**Other**
- 古代时候 → 古代: 时候 is redundant because 古代 already expresses the time.

**Expressiveness**
- At “常常一吃就死。”: You could add that people often became ill after eating poisonous plants, to show why this was a serious problem. For example: 很多人吃了有毒的植物，常常生病。
- At “但是不知道为什么，没有办法。”: You could add that people did not know which plants were safe and which were poisonous. For example: 人们不知道什么植物能吃，什么植物有毒。
- At “神农出去试一试很多种植物，告诉人什么植物是安全什么是危险。”: You could add Shen Nong’s decision or purpose: he wanted to find plants that could be medicine. For example: 神农决定找可以做药的植物。

**Encouragement** You clearly explain the problem at the beginning of the story: people did not know which plants they could eat. You also give Shen Nong a clear purpose in helping people find safe plants.

## Scene 2

> 发现有的植物对身体非常好，特别是茶，让肚子感觉又暖和又清楚。他告诉朋友：“你吃哪种植物，这就要看你的口味。比方说，你爱吃甜的，可能喜欢糖。”他最喜欢吃花，除了夹竹桃以外，什么都花想吃。

**Grammar**
- 什么都花想吃 → 什么花都想吃: Use the word order 什么花都想吃: 什么花 is the object, and 都 comes before 想吃.

**Vocabulary**
- …感觉又暖和又清楚 → …感觉又暖和又舒服: 清楚 describes whether something is clear, not how the stomach feels. Use 舒服 for a warm, comfortable stomach feeling.
- 这就要看你的口味 → 这就要看你的口味: Here 看 means “depend on,” in the phrase 看你的口味. Do not change it to 看到, which means “see/catch sight of.”
- (suggestion) 发现 (“发现有的植物对身体非常好”) → 找到: This emphasizes that he searched for and found the plants.; 发觉: This means to notice or realize something after observing it.
- (suggestion) 好 (“对身体非常好”) → 有益: This is a more precise word for “good for health.”},{; 有好处: This is a natural, common phrase meaning “beneficial.”{; 暖和: This is slightly more descriptive for a warm, comfortable feeling.

**Coherence**
- **Connectors** 可能喜欢糖 → ，这样可以吃糖喜欢糖: Use ，这样可以 to show how the example connects to the previous clause: if someone likes sweet food, they can eat candy.

**Expressiveness**
- At “发现有的植物对身体非常好，特别是茶，让肚子感觉又暖和又清楚。”: You could add how Shen Nong uses the tea: he puts it in water and finds that it is both a nice drink and a useful medicine. For example: 他把茶放在水里，觉得很好喝，也可以做药。
- At “他告诉朋友：“你吃哪种植物，这就要看你的口味。比方说，你爱吃甜的，可能喜欢糖。””: You could make his speech connect more clearly to his discovery by having him tell his friend that tea is good for the body. For example: 他告诉朋友：“茶不但好喝，而且对身体有好处。”
- At “发现有的植物对身体非常好，特别是茶，让肚子感觉又暖和又清楚。”: You could add that Shen Nong writes down this discovery, because he wants other people to know which plants can help them. For example: 他把茶可以做药的事写在书上。

**Encouragement** You clearly introduce tea as an important plant and explain its effect on the body. You also tried using direct speech, which makes the scene feel more like a story.

## Scene 3

> 他发现这个口味，一百岁时候，不小心吃了毒的花。一吃就开始死。但是生病时候告诉人什么植物是药，什么植物是毒。

**Grammar**
- 一百岁时候 → 的一百岁时候: Use the established time expression 一百岁的时候, with 的 before 时候.
- 一吃就开始死 → 一吃就死了: Death is a completed event here, so use 死了 rather than 开始死.
- 但是生病时候告诉人什… → 但是生病的时候告诉人什…: Use 的 before 时候 in this time phrase: 生病的时候.

**Vocabulary**
- 不小心吃了毒的花 → 不小心吃了有毒的花: Here the noun 花 needs an adjective phrase. 毒 is a noun, so use 有毒的花.
- (suggestion) 什么植物是毒 → 什么是毒: It would be more natural to say 什么是毒 after 什么植物是药; the omitted noun 植物 is already clear.
- 什么植物是毒 → 什么植物有毒: Here 毒 needs the adjective form 有毒. Say 什么植物有毒.
- (suggestion) 告诉 (“告诉人什么植物是药”) → 提醒: This means to warn or remind someone, so it is especially useful when Shennong talks about danger.; 说明: This means to explain clearly and suits giving information about plants.
- (suggestion) 不小心 (“不小心吃了毒的花”) → 误食: This means to eat something by mistake; it is a precise word for accidentally eating a harmful plant.

**Coherence**
- *No corrections.*

**Expressiveness**
- At “不小心吃了毒的花。”: Add what the flower looked like, such as its bright yellow, star-like flowers. For example: 那种花是黄色的，像星星一样。
- At “一吃就开始死。”: Add how Shennong feels after eating it, especially his stomach pain and discomfort. For example: 他的肚子很疼，难受得不得了。
- At “但是生病时候告诉人什么植物是药，什么植物是毒。”: Add a short warning that Shennong says to other people. For example: 他说：“这种花不能吃。”

**Encouragement** You included the important turning point where Shennong accidentally eats a poisonous flower. You also clearly showed the useful contrast between plants that are medicine and plants that are harmful.
