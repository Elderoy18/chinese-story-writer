# Feedback: shennong_samples#42

## Scene 1

> 古代的时候有一个人叫神农。他需要当上医，一看别的人就想帮助他们。但是，他有一个问题，他不知道什么东西能做药。神农听说一个远远的地方有一种植物，什么病人吃都会觉得身体更好。神农三个月走去，找到有植物的地方，他看到一个很漂亮的植物，又香又好看。

**Grammar**
- 古代的时候有一个人叫神… → 在古代有一个人叫神…: 古代 is the location/time setting, so use 在+Place before the action: 在古代有一个人叫神农。
- (suggestion) 神农听说一个远远的地… → 神农听说在一个远远的地…: It would be more natural to say 在一个很远的地方有一种植物, using 在+Place for the location.

**Vocabulary**
- 一看别的人就想帮… → 一看到别的人就想帮…: He actually sees the other people, so use 看到see, not 看look.
- (suggestion) 神农听说一个远远的地方有一种… → 神农听说一个很远的地方有一种…: It would be more natural to say 很远的地方. 很远 is the standard expression here.
- 他看到一个很漂亮的植物 → 他看到一株很漂亮的植物: Use 株 as the measure word for plants.
- (suggestion) 漂亮 (“一个很漂亮的植物”) → 美丽: A slightly more literary word for “beautiful.”; 艳丽: Describes flowers with especially bright, striking beauty.
- (suggestion) 香 (“又香又好看”) → 芬芳: A richer word for a pleasant flower scent.

**Coherence**
- **Reference** 他不知道什么东… → 不知道什么东…: 他 is already clear from the close continuation, so delete it; Chinese uses zero form here.
- **Reference** 找到有植物的地方 → 找到有这种植物的地方: This refers to the specific plant mentioned earlier, so add 这种: 有这种植物的地方。
- **了 / 着 / 在** (suggestion) 什么病人吃都会觉得身体… → 什么病人吃了都会觉得身体…: It would be more natural to add 了: 吃了都会觉得身体更好. 了 shows the eating action is completed before they feel better.
- **了 / 着 / 在** 神农三个月走去 → 神农三个月走着: Walking is a durative action while he finds the place, so use 着durative: 走着。

**Other**
- (suggestion) 他需要当上医 → 他需要当医: It would be more natural to delete 上. 当 already expresses becoming a doctor here.
- 他需要当上医 → 他需要当上医生: Use 医生 for “doctor”; 医 alone is not the usual word for a doctor here.

**Expressiveness**
- At “他看到一个很漂亮的植物，又香又好看。”: You could describe the flower’s colour and shape, like little stars, to help the reader picture it. For example: 那儿有黄色的花，像星星一样。
- At “他看到一个很漂亮的植物，又香又好看。”: You could add what Shennong thinks when he sees this new plant. For example: 神农想：“这是什么植物呢？”

**Encouragement** You clearly introduce Shennong’s wish to help sick people and explain his problem with finding medicine. The description “又香又好看” already makes the plant feel special.

## Scene 2

> 他觉得，这种一定是做药的花。原来他想错了。吃了以后，神农快生病。 这时一个穿着蓝色衣服的人帮神农，快给他一种红的灵芝。神农吃了以后，他没有病，想起来以前一个人告诉他，灵芝对身体有好处。

**Grammar**
- *No corrections.*

**Vocabulary**
- (suggestion) 这种一定是做药的… → 这种花一定是做药的…: It would be more natural to say 这种花一定是做药的花. 这种 needs a noun here.
- 神农快生病 → 神农很快生病: 快 alone is not natural before 生病 here; use 很快 to mean “soon.”
- 快给他一种红的… → 赶快给他一种红的…: Use 赶快 here to mean “quickly” or “hurriedly.”
- (suggestion) 快给他一种红的灵芝 → 快给他一朵红的灵芝: It would be more natural to say 一朵红的灵芝. 一种 means a type, while 一朵 counts one individual lingzhi.
- (suggestion) 帮 (“人帮神农”) → 救: This is stronger: it shows that the person helps Shennong in a dangerous situation.
- (suggestion) 好处 (“灵芝对身体有好处”) → 作用: This means a specific effect or function, often used when talking about medicine.; 用处: This means usefulness and is a little more specific than 好处.

**Coherence**
- **了 / 着 / 在** 神农快生病 → 神农快生病了: The change to being ill is completed in the story, so it takes 了 (了completed).
- **了 / 着 / 在** 快给他一种红的灵… → 快给了他一种红的灵…: Giving the lingzhi is a completed action in the story, so it takes 了 (了completed).
- **了 / 着 / 在** 他没有病 → 他的病好了: Here Shennong was ill and then recovered, so say 他的病好了, not 他没有病.

**Expressiveness**
- At “原来他想错了。”: Add that he feels surprised or worried when he realizes the flower is not medicine. For example: 他很害怕。
- At “吃了以后，神农快生病。”: Describe his stomach hurting badly after he eats it. For example: 他的肚子很疼，很难受。
- At “这时一个穿着蓝色衣服的人帮神农，快给他一种红的灵芝。”: Add a short line of speech from the blue-clothed person to make the rescue more lively. For example: 他说：“快吃这个药！”

**Encouragement** You clearly show the important change from Shennong choosing the wrong flower to someone helping him. You also connect lingzhi to its benefit for the body, which helps the scene make sense.

## Scene 3

> 啊，他怎么忘记了？他把灵芝放在包里。一回家就告诉别人。他找到药以后他写下怎么做药在书上。所以人们都知道，那红灵芝对身体好处。

**Grammar**
- …找到药以后他写下怎么做药在书上 → …找到药以后他在书上写下怎么做药: Put the location phrase 在书上 before the action 写下.
- …红灵芝对身体好处 → …红灵芝对身体有好处: Use the fixed phrase 对身体有好处. 有 is needed before 好处.

**Vocabulary**
- (suggestion) 包 (“他把灵芝放在包里。”) → 背包: a more specific word for a bag carried on a journey.
- (suggestion) 告诉 (“一回家就告诉别人。”) → 提醒: means to remind or warn people about something important.; 说明: means to explain something clearly to others.
- (suggestion) 找到 (“他找到药以后”) → 发现: means to discover something, often after looking carefully.

**Coherence**
- **Reference** (suggestion) 一回家就告诉别人 → 一回家就告诉了别人: It would be more natural to use 了completed because telling the other people is a specific completed action in the story.
- **Reference** 那红灵芝对身体… → 那种红灵芝对身体…: Use a classifier after 那 when you mean one type: 那种红灵芝.
- **了 / 着 / 在** (suggestion) …？他把灵芝放在包里 → …？他把灵芝放把灵芝放在包里了在包里: It would be more natural to use 了completed because putting the 灵芝 in the bag is a completed action in the story.

**Expressiveness**
- At “啊，他怎么忘记了？”: Show that Shennong suddenly remembers the lingzhi and feels surprised at himself. For example: 他想：“灵芝对身体有好处，我怎么忘记了？”
- At “一回家就告诉别人。”: Add what he tells the other people, so this moment has direct speech. For example: 他告诉大家：“灵芝可以做药，对身体很好。”
- At “他找到药以后他写下怎么做药在书上。”: Add that he is happy to write it down because he can help more people. For example: 他很高兴，因为他可以帮助很多人。

**Encouragement** You clearly show that Shennong remembers the lingzhi and takes it home. You also connect his discovery to writing a book, which helps explain how other people learn about the medicine.
