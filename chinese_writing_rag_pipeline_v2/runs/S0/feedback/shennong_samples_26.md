# Feedback: shennong_samples#26

## Scene 1

> 很久以前，有一个老男人叫神农。神农在古代每一天看到了别的小镇人生病还是不舒服，所以他决定开始找新的药。他下一天开始爬当地的山脉，找了各种各样的植物。神农试一试每个植物，知道有什么好处。

**Grammar**
- 神农试一试每个植物 → 神农试了试每个植物: This is a completed action in the past story, so use 试了试 with 了completed.
- 知道有什么好处 → 知道了有什么好处: Knowing the benefits is a completed change in the story, so it takes 了completed.

**Vocabulary**
- 有一个老男人叫神农 → 有一个老人叫神农: 老人 is the normal word for an elderly man. It also gives a brief introduction when Shennong first appears.
- …在古代每一天看到了别的小镇人生… → …在古代每一天看到别的小镇人生…: With 每一天, this is a habitual action, so do not use completed 了. Use 看到see because Shennong actually sees the people’s condition.
- …天看到了别的小镇人生病还是不舒… → …天看到了别的镇上的人生病还是不舒…: Use 镇上的人 for people who live in the town. 小镇人 is not a usual Chinese expression.
- (suggestion) …开始爬当地的山脉 → …开始爬当地的山: It would be more natural to say 爬山. 山脉 is a mountain range, which people do not normally 爬 as one whole object.
- 找了各种各样的植… → 找到了各种各样的植…: He successfully finds the plants, so use 找到find, not 找look for.
- 神农试一试每个植物 → 神农试一试每种植物: Use 种 as the measure word for types of plants. 个 is not the usual measure word here.
- (suggestion) 新的药 (“决定开始找新的药”) → 草药: This means herbal medicine and fits a story about useful plants.
- (suggestion) 好处 (“知道有什么好处”) → 作用: This means a plant's function or effect, especially in a medicine context.; 用处: This means a practical use and is a little more specific than 好处.
- (suggestion) 生病 (“别的小镇人生病还是不舒服”) → 患病: This is a more formal word for becoming ill.

**Coherence**
- **了 / 着 / 在** (suggestion) 他下一天开始爬当… → 他第一天开始爬当…: It would be more natural to say 第一天 when you mean “the first day.” 第 marks the order of a day.
- **Connectors** …的小镇人生病还是不舒服 → …的小镇人生病或者不舒服: In a statement that gives alternatives, use 或者, not 还是. 还是 is usually used in questions.

**Expressiveness**
- At “神农在古代每一天看到了别的小镇人生病还是不舒服，所以他决定开始找新的药。”: Add that he felt worried for the sick people and wanted to help them. For example: 他很担心他们，想帮他们。
- At “神农在古代每一天看到了别的小镇人生病还是不舒服，所以他决定开始找新的药。”: Add his thought to show why he made this decision. For example: 他想：“我要找出什么植物可以做药。”
- At “他下一天开始爬当地的山脉，找了各种各样的植物。”: Add that he walked for a long time and visited many high mountains. For example: 他走啊走啊，去了很多高山。

**Encouragement** You clearly explain Shen Nong’s reason for searching for plants: he sees that people are ill. You also use “各种各样的植物” well to show that he searches widely.

## Scene 2

> 比方说，他一次试一试有些植物的叶，吃得又新鲜又甜。煮这些叶以后，他一喝就知道他发现新的茶叶。虽然他找到了很多的新的植物，但是他的旅程有的时候特别是危险。比方说，他一吃灵芝就开始生病。

**Grammar**
- 他一次试一试有些植物的叶 → 他一次试了试有些植物的叶: this trying action is completed in the story, so use 了completed.
- 吃得又新鲜又甜 → 吃起来又新鲜又甜: use 吃起来 before a description of how something tastes, such as 又新鲜又甜.
- …他找到了很多的新的植物 → …他找到了很多新的植物: 很多 does not need 的 before 新的植物, so omit 的 here.
- …有的时候特别是危险 → …有的时候特别危险: 特别 directly modifies 危险, so omit 是: 特别危险.

**Vocabulary**
- 他一次试一试有些植… → 他有一次试一试有些植…: use 有一次 to mean “on one occasion” in the story. 一次 alone usually means “one time” as a number of times.
- …试有些植物的叶 → …试有些植物的叶子: use 叶子 as the usual noun for leaves.
- 煮这些叶以后 → 煮这些叶子以后: use 叶子 as the usual noun for leaves.
- (suggestion) 甜 (“又新鲜又甜”) → 清甜: a light, fresh sweetness, often used for drinks or tea; 甘甜: a pleasant, rich sweetness
- (suggestion) 危险 (“特别是危险”) → 艰险: hard and dangerous, especially for a journey; 可怕: emphasizes that something feels frightening
- (suggestion) 很多 (“很多的新的植物”) → 许多: a slightly more written and varied way to say “many”; 各种各样: shows that the plants are of many different kinds

**Coherence**
- **了 / 着 / 在** 煮这些叶以后 → 煮了这些叶以后: 煮 is completed before 以后, so it takes 了completed: 煮了……以后.
- **了 / 着 / 在** …就知道他发现新的茶叶 → …就知道他发现了新的茶叶: 发现 is a completed discovery in the story, so it takes 了completed: 发现了.

**Expressiveness**
- At “煮这些叶以后，他一喝就知道他发现新的茶叶。”: Add that the drink tastes good and can help people, so his discovery feels more important. For example: 他觉得这个茶很好喝，也可以做药。
- At “虽然他找到了很多的新的植物，但是他的旅程有的时候特别是危险。”: Add how Shen Nong feels about the danger, but show that he still wants to continue. For example: 他有一点害怕，可是他还是想帮助别人。
- At “比方说，他一吃灵芝就开始生病。”: Add Shen Nong’s worried thought when his stomach starts to hurt. For example: 他想：“我会死吗？”

**Encouragement** You clearly show both Shen Nong’s useful tea discovery and the danger in his journey. The contrast with “虽然……但是……” makes the scene more interesting.

## Scene 3

> 一个爬山的男人正好看到神农的麻烦，然后给了他一些药。神农最终他的小镇回到，跟别的人分享他的发现。你认为神农的故事真的吗？这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处。

**Grammar**
- 神农最终他的小镇回到 → 神农最终回到他的小镇: Put the place after the directional complement: 回到他的小镇. This follows V. + directional complement + Place + 来/去.
- …为神农的故事真的吗？这就… → …为神农的故事是真的吗？这就…: Use 是 before the adjective question: 神农的故事是真的吗？
- (suggestion) …？这就要看你相信中药对身… → …？这就要看你是否相信中药对身…: It would be more natural to say 看你是否相信, because 是否 clearly introduces the two possibilities.

**Vocabulary**
- 跟别的人分享他的发现 → 跟别人分享他的发现: Use 别人 here for other people in general: 跟别人分享他的发现.
- …的吗？这就要看你相信中药对… → …的吗？这就要看你相信中药对…: Here 看 means “depends on,” not 看look. Keep 看; 看到see does not fit this expression.
- (suggestion) 麻烦 (“神农的麻烦”) → 困难: a common word for a difficult situation; 困境: a stronger word for a serious difficult situation
- (suggestion) 药 (“一些药”) → 草药: medicine made from plants; this fits the story especially well; 药物: a more formal general word for medicine

**Coherence**
- *No corrections.*

**Expressiveness**
- At “一个爬山的男人正好看到神农的麻烦，然后给了他一些药。”: Add what the man looked like and what the medicine plant looked like, so the rescue is easier to imagine. For example: 他穿着蓝色衣服，拿了一种像伞一样的植物给神农吃。
- At “一个爬山的男人正好看到神农的麻烦，然后给了他一些药。”: Add a short question from Shennong after he feels better. For example: 神农说：“这是什么药？”
- At “神农最终他的小镇回到，跟别的人分享他的发现。”: Show one thing Shennong tells the other people about the two plants. For example: 他说：“灵芝可以做药，可是夹竹桃有毒，不能吃。”

**Encouragement** You included the important rescue by the man on the mountain and Shennong’s return home. Your final question also connects the story to Chinese medicine and invites the reader to think.
