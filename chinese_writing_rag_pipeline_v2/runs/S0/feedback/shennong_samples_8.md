## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个人叫神农。一天，神农正在路上走，一看很多人正在吃一些植物。一些人一吃植物就生病了，神农想帮别人。所以想知道哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃。

**Grammar**
- 古代的时候 → 在古代: 古代 is the location/time setting, so use 在+Place before the action: 在古代。
- (suggestion) 哪些植物不能吃 → 哪些不能吃: It would be more natural to omit the repeated 植物: 哪些植物能吃，哪些不能吃。

**Vocabulary**
- 一看很多人正在吃… → 一看到很多人正在吃…: Shennong actually sees the people eating plants, so use 看到see, not 看look.
- (suggestion) 路上 (“神农正在路上走”) → 山路: a mountain path; it gives a clearer picture of where he is walking.
- (suggestion) 生病 (“一些人一吃植物就生病了”) → 中毒: to be poisoned; this is more precise when illness comes from harmful plants.
- (suggestion) 知道 (“想知道哪些植物能吃”) → 了解: to find out or understand; it sounds a little more formal.; 弄清楚: to work something out clearly; it shows careful investigation.

**Coherence**
- **了 / 着 / 在** (suggestion) 神农想帮别人 → 神农想帮助别人: It would be more natural to say 帮助别人 here.

**Expressiveness**
- At “一些人一吃植物就生病了”: Add that the plants were poisonous and that the people felt very unwell. For example: 他们吃了有毒的植物，身体很不舒服。
- At “神农想帮别人。”: You could add what Shennong says to show his decision to help. For example: 神农说：“我要找出能吃的植物。”
- At “一天，神农正在路上走”: Add a small detail about where he is walking, such as the mountains he visits to look for plants. For example: 神农在高山上走啊走啊。

**Encouragement** You clearly introduce the problem: people eat plants and become ill. You also explain Shennong’s helpful goal, which gives this scene a strong beginning.

## Scene 2

> 然后，神农开始尝很多植物，比方说他吃过生姜、芥兰和菠菜。一些植物吃真好，特别是黄瓜。但是，一些植物毒得很，特别是夹竹桃。

**Grammar**
- 一些植物吃真好 → 一些植物真好吃: Use 真好吃, with 真 before the adjective 好吃. It describes how the plants taste.

**Vocabulary**
- (suggestion) 一些植物毒得很 → 一些植物有毒得很: It would be more natural to say 有毒. Here you need the adjective form 有毒 to describe the plants.
- (suggestion) 一些植物毒得很 → 一些植物毒: It would be more natural to say 有毒 without 得很. 有毒 is already the adjective that describes the plants.
- (suggestion) 尝 (“神农开始尝很多植物”) → 品尝: This means to taste or try food, with a slightly more careful tone.; 试吃: This means to try eating something to see what it is like.
- (suggestion) 很多 (“尝很多植物”) → 各种各样: This emphasizes that there were many different kinds.; 许多: This is a slightly more formal way to say “many.”

**Coherence**
- *No corrections.*

**Expressiveness**
- At “然后，神农开始尝很多植物”: You could add that he was willing to try them even though this was dangerous, to show his courage. For example: 他不怕危险。
- At “一些植物毒得很，特别是夹竹桃。”: You could add what happened to Shennong after he ate the poisonous flower, such as a bad stomachache. For example: 他吃了以后，肚子很疼。
- At “一些植物毒得很，特别是夹竹桃。”: You could add Shennong’s worried thought when he became ill. For example: 他想：“我会死吗？”

**Encouragement** You clearly show that Shennong tried both safe and poisonous plants. Your examples of ginger, Chinese kale, spinach, cucumber, and oleander make this scene more specific and interesting.

## Scene 3

> 一个人对神农说，麻烦我们可以吃什么植物？神农说，这就要看你想不想生病了。以后，神农告诉别人一些植物能吃，一些植物不能吃。

**Grammar**
- (suggestion) 一些植物不能吃 → 一些不能吃: It would be more natural to omit the second 植物 because it is already understood from the first phrase.

**Vocabulary**
- 这就要看你想不想生病… → 这就要看你想不想生病…: Here 看 means “depends on” in 要看……, so do not change it to 看到.
- (suggestion) 一个人 (“一个人对神农说”) → 男人: This is more specific when the person is male.; 胖男人: This adds a simple visual detail about the character.
- (suggestion) 告诉 (“神农告诉别人”) → 说明: This means to explain something clearly.; 提醒: This is useful when Shennong warns people about dangerous plants.

**Coherence**
- *No corrections.*

**Other**
- 麻烦我们可以吃什… → 请问我们可以吃什…: Use 请问 to politely introduce a question. 麻烦 cannot directly introduce this question.

**Expressiveness**
- At “一个人对神农说，麻烦我们可以吃什么植物？”: You could describe the person a little, such as his clothes or body shape, so the scene is easier to imagine. For example: 一个穿着咖啡色衣服的胖男人问神农。
- At “神农说，这就要看你想不想生病了。”: You could add Shennong’s direct explanation about one safe plant and one poisonous plant. For example: 神农说：“像伞一样的灵芝可以吃，是药。夹竹桃有毒，不能吃。”
- At “以后，神农告诉别人一些植物能吃，一些植物不能吃。”: You could add that Shennong wrote these plants in his book, showing how he helped more people. For example: 他把这些植物都写在书上，帮助了很多人。

**Encouragement** You clearly show that Shennong teaches other people which plants are safe and which are dangerous. Your ending also shows the helpful result of his work.
