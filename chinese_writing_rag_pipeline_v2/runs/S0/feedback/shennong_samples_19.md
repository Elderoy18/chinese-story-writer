# Feedback: shennong_samples#19

## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男人觉得植物很有意思。他叫神农。有的植物吃了以后他的肚子疼死了，可是有的吃了以后很舒服。他找很多植物。

**Grammar**
- 古代的时候 → 在古代: 古代 is the setting for the action, so use 在+Place before the action: 在古代。
- 他找很多植物 → 他找了很多植物: This is a completed past action, so add 了 after 找: 找了很多植物。

**Vocabulary**
- (suggestion) 可是有的吃了以后很舒服 → 可是有的吃了之后很舒服: It would be more natural to say 之后 here for the time after eating: 吃了之后。
- (suggestion) 有意思 (“植物很有意思”) → 有趣: A common alternative for something that is interesting.; 神奇: Means amazing or mysterious; it suits plants with surprising effects.

**Coherence**
- **Reference** ⟦他⟧叫神农: This character appears for the first time, so please introduce 神农 briefly with an identifying noun phrase, not only a pronoun or name.

**Expressiveness**
- At “有的植物吃了以后他的肚子疼死了”: Add that Shennong feels very scared and wonders if he might die. For example: 他想：“我会死吗？”
- At “他找很多植物。”: Describe his journey by saying that he keeps walking and visits many high mountains. For example: 他走啊走啊，去了很多高山。
- At “他找很多植物。”: Add his purpose: he wants to learn which plants can be medicine and which ones are poisonous. For example: 他想找可以做药的植物。

**Encouragement** You clearly show the important contrast between plants that make Shennong’s stomach hurt and plants that make him feel comfortable. You also introduce Shennong early, so the scene has a clear main character.

## Scene 2

> 凉山很大的山，这也找植物。一天，他找到黄色的花。他听说你跟黄色的花做茶很有用。比方说，你的头发疼死，这冰茶是药。他一喝茶就很不舒服。

**Grammar**
- 凉山很大的山 → 凉山是很大的山: This clause needs 是 to link 凉山 with 很大的山.
- 他找到黄色的花 → 他找到一朵黄色的花: A single flower needs a measure word. Say 一朵黄色的花.
- (suggestion) …跟黄色的花做茶很有用 → …跟黄色的花做的茶很有用: It would be more natural to say 用黄色的花做的茶, using 的 to link 做 to 茶.
- 你的头发疼死 → 你的头发疼死了: Use 了 for this change of state: 头疼死了.
- (suggestion) 他一喝茶就很不舒服 → 他一喝茶就觉得很不舒服: It would be more natural to say 他一喝茶就觉得很不舒服.

**Vocabulary**
- 他听说你跟黄色的花做茶… → 他听说用黄色的花做茶…: Use 用 to show the material used to make tea: 用黄色的花做茶.
- 你的头发疼死 → 你的头疼死: 头发 means “hair,” but a headache is 头疼. Delete 发.
- 这冰茶是药 → 这杯茶是药: Use 杯 as the measure word for a cup of tea: 这杯茶.
- (suggestion) 不舒服 (“就很不舒服”) → 难受: stronger; it shows that he feels quite unwell; 痛苦: stronger and more serious; it emphasizes suffering

**Coherence**
- **Reference** 这也找植物 → 他也找植物: 这 refers to a thing, but the person finding plants is 神农. Use 他.

**Expressiveness**
- At “一天，他找到黄色的花。”: Describe the flowers as big, pretty, and like little stars so the place feels more vivid. For example: 黄色的花又大又漂亮，像星星一样。
- At “他一喝茶就很不舒服。”: Add what he says when he starts to feel ill, to show his pain and surprise. For example: 他说：“我的肚子怎么这么疼？”

**Encouragement** You clearly introduce the yellow flower and connect it with tea and medicine. You also show that Shennong feels unwell after trying it, which gives the scene a clear problem.

## Scene 3

> 糟糕，这黄色的花是夹竹桃，特别是毒。正好在这儿，灵芝药的夹竹桃他吃完了，也写一个课文的植物。谢谢神农。

**Grammar**
- 也写一个课文的植物 → 也写一个关于植物的书: 课文的植物 does not mean “a book about plants.” Use 关于植物的书 for a book whose topic is plants.

**Vocabulary**
- 特别是毒 → 特别有毒: Use 有毒 to describe that the oleander has poison. 是毒 treats 毒 as a noun after 是, but this description needs the adjective form 有毒.
- 也写一个课文的植… → 也写了一个课文的植…: Use 了 after 写 for this completed past action: 写了.
- 也写一个课文的植物 → 也写一本课文的植物: Use 本 for a book. 个 does not normally collocate with 课文 here.
- (suggestion) 黄色 (“黄色的花”) → 金黄色: a bright, golden yellow; 鲜黄色: a vivid, fresh-looking yellow
- (suggestion) 正好 (“正好在这儿”) → 刚好: a common word meaning just at that moment; 恰好: a slightly more formal word meaning exactly by chance

**Coherence**
- **Reference** (suggestion) …芝药的夹竹桃他吃完了 → …芝药的夹竹桃他把它吃完了: It would be more natural to say 他把它吃完了. 把它 clearly marks 灵芝 as the object that he finished eating.
- **Connectors** 灵芝药的夹竹桃他吃完了 → 有灵芝他吃完了: 灵芝药的夹竹桃 does not say that lingzhi is present. Use 有灵芝 to introduce that there is lingzhi here.

**Expressiveness**
- At “糟糕，这黄色的花是夹竹桃，特别是毒。”: Add how Shennong feels after eating the poisonous flower, and show that he is worried. For example: 他肚子疼得不得了，想：我会死吗？
- At “正好在这儿，灵芝药的夹竹桃他吃完了，也写一个课文的植物。”: Add a short line of speech from Shennong to thank the man who helps him and ask about the medicine. For example: 神农说：“谢谢你！这是什么药？”

**Encouragement** You clearly name the flower as 夹竹桃 and show that it is dangerous. Starting with 糟糕 also gives this moment a strong sense of trouble.
