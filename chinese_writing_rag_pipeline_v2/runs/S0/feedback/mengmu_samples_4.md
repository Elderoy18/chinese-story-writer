# Feedback: mengmu_samples#4

## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，孟子和孟母住了在靠墓地的家。孩子才五岁就每天在墓地祭拜了。在那个城市，孟母从早到晚都找工作，可是找不到，害得孟母没有钱。难怪她跟孩子搬家，原来买不了房。他们搬了到别一个城市找工作。

**Grammar**
- 古代的时候 → 在古代: Use 在古代 for this time setting. Put 在 before the time/place phrase.
- 孟子和孟母住了在靠墓地的家 → 孟子和孟母住在靠墓地的家: 住在 describes a state here, so do not put 了 before 在. Use 住在+Place.
- (suggestion) …在靠墓地的家 → …在靠墓地的家里: It would be more natural to add 里 after 家, making the location clearer: 家里.
- …天在墓地祭拜了 → …天在墓地祭拜: 每天 shows a habitual action, so sentence-final 了 is not needed here.
- (suggestion) …母从早到晚都找工作 → …母从早到晚都在找工作: It would be more natural to use 在 for an action continuing from morning to night: 从早到晚都在找工作.
- 他们搬了到别一个城市… → 他们搬到了到别一个城市…: With 搬到, the directional complement 到 comes before 了. Use 搬到了+Place.
- (suggestion) 他们搬了到别一个城市找… → 他们搬了别一个城市找…: It would be more natural to use 搬到了+Place rather than adding a separate 到 after 搬了.

**Vocabulary**
- …和孟母住了在靠墓地的家 → …和孟母住了在靠近墓地的家: Use 靠近 to mean “near” a place. 靠近墓地 means “near the cemetery.”
- 他们搬了到别一个城市找工… → 他们搬了到另一个城市找工…: Use 另一个 for “another one.” 别一个 is not the standard expression here.
- (suggestion) 墓地 (“在墓地祭拜”) → 墓园: a slightly more formal word for a cemetery; 坟地: a common, more everyday word for a burial place
- (suggestion) 每天 (“每天在墓地祭拜了”) → 天天: a natural, conversational alternative meaning every day; 每日: a more formal alternative meaning every day

**Coherence**
- **Reference** ⟦孟子⟧和孟母住了在…: 孟子 appears for the first time here, so please introduce him briefly, not only with his name.
- **Reference** 孟子和⟦孟母⟧住了在靠墓地…: 孟母 appears for the first time here, so please introduce her briefly, for example as 孟子的妈妈.
- **Reference** (suggestion) 在那个城市 → 在他们住的城市: It would be clearer to say 他们住的城市 because no city has been introduced for 那个城市 to refer to.
- **Connectors** (suggestion) 原来买不了房 → 原来是因为买不了房: It would be more natural to use 原来是因为 to clearly introduce the reason.

**Expressiveness**
- At “孩子才五岁就每天在墓地祭拜了。”: Add that Mengzi found the rituals interesting and copied them playfully, so the reader can picture why this worried his mother. For example: 孟子觉得祭拜很好玩，常常一边笑一边学别人祭拜。
- At “难怪她跟孩子搬家，原来买不了房。”: Add Mengmu’s worried thought to show how she felt before deciding to move. For example: 孟母很着急，心想：“不能一直住在这儿。”

**Encouragement** You clearly set the story in ancient times and introduced Mengzi and his mother. You also gave a clear reason for why they decided to move.

## Scene 2

> 这个城市老是很吵，不是人吵架，就是都叫，害得孟子一直不能睡觉。在那儿，孟母也找不到工作。孟子和妈妈要又一个次搬了家。在第三个城市，孟母找到工作，在商店做衣服。

**Grammar**
- 就是都叫 → 就是有人叫: 都 needs a clear plural referent. Use 有人 here to say that people are shouting.
- …要又一个次搬了家 → …要又一个次搬家: After 要, do not use completed-action 了. 要搬家 describes a planned action, not a completed one.

**Vocabulary**
- (suggestion) 孟母找到工作 → 孟母找到了工作: It would be more natural to say 找到了工作, because finding the job is a completed action.
- (suggestion) 吵 (“这个城市老是很吵”) → 吵闹: means noisy and disorderly; it is a little more descriptive for a city or street.
- (suggestion) 商店 (“在商店做衣服”) → 店铺: a common, slightly richer word for a shop or small store.

**Coherence**
- **Connectors** 孟子和妈妈要又一个次搬了家 → 孟子和妈妈要再一次搬了家: use 再一次 here because it means “one more time.”

**Expressiveness**
- At “这个城市老是很吵，不是人吵架，就是都叫，害得孟子一直不能睡觉。”: Add a small detail about the noise and Mengzi’s tired feeling. For example: 从早到晚，街上很吵，孟子很累。
- At “在那儿，孟母也找不到工作。”: Show Mengmu’s worry and let her say why she wants to move again. For example: 孟母很着急，说：“这里太吵了，我们再搬家吧。”

**Encouragement** You clearly explain the problem in this city and its effect on Mengzi’s sleep. Your use of “不是……就是……” makes the noisy situation easy to picture.

## Scene 3

> 孟子在那个商店也找了到工作，可是不是做衣服的工作，而是喜欢的工作。孟子学习馆学了十五年，他的学习和他的旅行经验很重要，对他很多好处。他是大人的时候，他成为很有名的哲人。

**Grammar**
- …做衣服的工作，而是喜欢的工作 → …做衣服的工作: The phrase 而是喜欢的工作 does not fit the meaning here, so delete it.
- 孟子学习馆学了十… → 孟子在学习馆学了十…: Use 在 before a place where an action happens: 在学习馆学了十五年.
- 对他很多好处 → 对他有很多好处: Use 有 in 对他有很多好处 to say something has many benefits for him.

**Vocabulary**
- …在那个商店也找了到工作 → …在那个商店也找到了到工作: The person successfully finds a job, so use 找到find, not 找look for.
- 他是大人的时候 → 他长大后: Use the established time expression 长大后, not the literal combination 是大人的时候.
- (suggestion) 他成为很有名的哲人 → 他成为了很有名的哲人: It would be more natural to add 了 after 成为 to show the completed change: 成为了很有名的哲人.
- (suggestion) 重要 (“他的学习和他的旅行经验很重要”) → 关键: This means “key” or “crucial,” stronger than important.
- (suggestion) 有名 (“很有名的哲人”) → 著名: This is a more formal and common word for “famous.”; 好处: This means “benefit,” and can make the sentence more concise.

**Coherence**
- **Reference** 他成为很有名的… → 成为很有名的…: The subject is already clear from the previous clause, so delete 他. Chinese uses zero form here.

**Expressiveness**
- At “孟子学习馆学了十五年”: Add that Mengzi enjoyed studying there and felt that the school was a good place for him. For example: 孟子很喜欢在那儿学习，觉得学校很好。
- At “他的学习和他的旅行经验很重要，对他很多好处。”: Add Mengmu’s happy words about the new place being suitable for her child. For example: 孟母很高兴，说：“这儿很适合孩子学习！”
- At “他成为很有名的哲人。”: Add a short comment from other people about why he became successful. For example: 人们都说：“孟子的成功离不开妈妈搬家。”

**Encouragement** You clearly show that Mengzi studied for a long time and later became a famous philosopher. You also explain that his learning and experience helped him, which gives the ending a clear reason.
