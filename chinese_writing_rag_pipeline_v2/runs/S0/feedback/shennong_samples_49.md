# Feedback: shennong_samples#49

## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有很多人不知道什么植物能吃，什么植物不能吃。有很多人吃植物以后就吃坏肚子了，因为他们吃了毒。那个时候，有一个老人叫神农，他什么植物都觉得好吃，不怕生病，不怕毒。他希望别人也可以吃好吃的植物。可是他知道，如果他们还怕吃毒，不会吃他喜欢的植物。

**Grammar**
- 古代的时候 → 在古代: 古代 is the time/location setting, so use 在+Place before the statement: 在古代。
- (suggestion) 什么植物不能吃 → 什麼植物不能吃: It would be more natural to use 麼 if you are writing this word in traditional characters.
- (suggestion) 不怕毒 → 也不怕毒: It would be more natural to add 也 to connect the two similar ideas: 不怕生病，也不怕毒。
- (suggestion) …别人也可以吃好吃的植物 → …别人也可以吃到好吃的植物: It would be more natural to use 吃到result here, because people successfully get to eat the tasty plants.
- 如果他们还怕吃毒 → 如果他们还怕吃到有毒的植物: Use 吃到result when the action reaches something. Here, say 吃到有毒的植物, not 吃毒.
- (suggestion) 不会吃他喜欢… → 就不会吃他喜欢…: It would be more natural to add 就 in this conditional pattern: 如果……，就……。

**Vocabulary**
- 因为他们吃了毒 → 因为他们吃了有毒的植物: Here you mean the plants people ate, so use the noun phrase 有毒的植物, not 毒 by itself.
- (suggestion) 很多 (“有很多人”) → 许多: A slightly more written and varied way to say “many.”; 不少: Means “quite a few” and sounds natural in a story.
- (suggestion) 好吃 (“好吃的植物”) → 美味: Means “delicious” and is a little more descriptive.
- (suggestion) 希望 (“他希望别人也可以吃好吃的植物”) → 盼望: Shows a stronger, warmer hope.

**Coherence**
- *No corrections.*

**Other**
- 他什么植物都觉得好吃 → 他觉得什么植物都好吃: 觉得 should come before the clause it describes. Say 觉得什么植物都好吃.

**Expressiveness**
- At “有很多人吃植物以后就吃坏肚子了”: Add that they often became ill after eating poisonous plants, to show why this was a serious problem. For example: 很多人生病了。
- At “有一个老人叫神农”: You could add what Shennong decided to do, showing that he wanted to find out which plants could help people. For example: 他决定找一找哪些植物可以做药。
- At “他希望别人也可以吃好吃的植物”: Add a short thought or direct speech to show his wish to protect people from dangerous plants. For example: 他说：“我要找出哪些植物不能吃。”

**Encouragement** You clearly explain the problem people had with plants at the beginning of the story. You also give Shennong a caring reason for helping other people.

## Scene 2

> 所以，他上山下山找植物，吃了很多样子的植物。他是第一人，找到了茶。他把所有的植物写下来了。他一直都吃了，他连毒都能吃。可是他还没吃了一个叫夹竹桃的植物。他找到了那个像星星的植物，觉得很漂亮，马上就吃下了。

**Grammar**
- 他是第一人 → 他是第一个人: Use the measure word 个 here: 第一个人. 个 is the MW for 人 in this phrase.
- (suggestion) 他是第一人，找到了茶 → 他是第一个找到了茶: It would be more natural to say 第一个, using 个 before the following description of the person.
- (suggestion) 找到了茶 → 找到茶: It would be more natural to use 找到 before 的人 here, rather than 找到了.
- (suggestion) 找到了茶 → 找到了茶的人: It would be more natural to add 的人: 第一个找到茶的人. This turns 找到茶 into a description of the person.
- (suggestion) 他一直都吃了 → 他一直都在吃了: It would be more natural to use 在 for an ongoing action: 一直都在吃.
- 他一直都吃了 → 他一直都吃: 一直 describes an ongoing habit, so do not use 了 here. Say 一直都在吃.
- …他还没吃了一个叫夹竹桃的植… → …他还没吃了一种叫夹竹桃的植…: Use 种 to count plants: 一个叫夹竹桃的植物 becomes 一种叫夹竹桃的植物.

**Vocabulary**
- 吃了很多样子的植物 → 吃了很多种的植物: Use 种 to count types of plants. Say 很多种植物, not 很多样子的植物.
- …把所有的植物写下来了 → …把所有的植物的名字写下来了: Write down the plants' names, not the plants themselves. Say 把所有植物的名字写下来.
- (suggestion) 漂亮 (“觉得很漂亮”) → 美丽: a slightly more literary word for beautiful.; 好看: a common, natural word for something that looks nice.
- (suggestion) 吃 (“马上就吃下了”) → 尝: means to taste something, which fits when he is testing a plant.; 品尝: means to taste carefully; it sounds a little more deliberate.

**Coherence**
- **Reference** 他连毒都能吃 → 连毒都能吃: 他 is already clear from the previous clause, so delete it. Chinese uses zero form here to avoid too many 他.
- **了 / 着 / 在** 可是他还没吃了一个叫夹竹桃… → 可是他还没吃一个叫夹竹桃…: This action did not happen, so use 没 + V. with no 了: 还没吃一个叫夹竹桃的植物.

**Expressiveness**
- At “他是第一人，找到了茶。”: You could add that tea was not only a drink but could also help people when they were ill. For example: 茶不但很好喝，还可以做药。
- At “他把所有的植物写下来了。”: You could show that writing down every plant was hard, but Shennong still wanted to do it. For example: 写这么多植物很麻烦，可是他还是愿意写。
- At “他找到了那个像星星的植物，觉得很漂亮，马上就吃下了。”: You could describe the flower’s yellow colour and add what Shennong says when he sees it. For example: 那是黄色的大花，像星星一样。他说：“这个花真漂亮！”

**Encouragement** You clearly show Shennong’s important work: he travels to find plants, discovers tea, and writes about plants. The star-like flower is also a nice detail that leads naturally to the next event.

## Scene 3

> 可是夹竹桃的毒很重，让他肚子很痛。他在地上躺着，觉得他快会死了。可是正好有别人找到他了，给了他一个像伞的植物，叫灵芝。他吃灵芝以后就觉得很好，不再生病了。他测量起来了，像星星的植物是毒，像伞的植物是药。

**Grammar**
- *No corrections.*

**Vocabulary**
- (suggestion) 可是正好有别人找到他了 → 可是正好有人找到他了: It would be more natural to say 有人 here for an unspecified person who found him.
- 给了他一个像伞的植物 → 给了他一株像伞的植物: Use 株 as the measure word for one individual plant, not 个.
- (suggestion) 不再生病了 → 肚子不痛了: It would be clearer to say 肚子不痛, because the symptom in the story is stomach pain, not a general illness.
- 他测量起来了 → 他明白了: 测量 means “to measure,” not “to realize.” Use 明白了 when he realizes which plants are poisonous or medicinal.
- 像星星的植物是毒 → 像星星的植物有毒: Use 有毒, not 是毒, to say that a plant is poisonous.
- (suggestion) 痛 (“让他肚子很痛”) → 疼: A very natural word for physical pain.; 难受: Shows that he feels generally uncomfortable or sick.
- (suggestion) 很好 (“觉得很好”) → 好多了: Shows that he feels better than before.; 舒服: Means his body feels comfortable again.

**Coherence**
- **Reference** 觉得他快会死了 → 觉得快会死了: 他 is already clear from the previous clause, so delete this redundant subject. Chinese uses zero form here.
- **Reference** (suggestion) 像伞的植物是药 → 像伞的是药: It would be more natural to omit 植物 because it is already understood: 像伞的是药.
- **了 / 着 / 在** 觉得他快会死了 → 觉得他快要死了: Use 快要 for something that is going to happen very soon: 快要死了.

**Expressiveness**
- At “他在地上躺着，觉得他快会死了。”: Add a short thought to show how scared Shennong feels. For example: 他想：“我会死吗？”
- At “可是正好有别人找到他了，给了他一个像伞的植物，叫灵芝。”: Add what the man says when he quickly gives Shennong the plant. For example: 他说：“快吃这个！”
- At “他测量起来了，像星星的植物是毒，像伞的植物是药。”: Add that Shennong warns people not to eat the star-shaped flower. For example: 他说：“这个有毒，不能吃。”

**Encouragement** You clearly show the cause and result: Shennong eats the poisonous plant, feels pain, and then gets better after eating lingzhi. You also compare the star-shaped and umbrella-shaped plants clearly.
