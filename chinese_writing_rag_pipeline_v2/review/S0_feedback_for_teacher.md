# Feedback from the new system (round S0) on 20 student stories

This document shows the complete feedback the new feedback system gives on 20 of the graded student
retellings (10 of 孟母三迁, 10 of 神农尝百草). Each story is split into three scenes, the way the app
gives feedback scene by scene; after the last scene comes the check on the story's content.

Round S0 uses only the teacher's original materials: her corrections on the graded samples, the model
stories, her required-scene lists, and her coherence rules. None of her later comments on earlier AI
feedback has been used yet. Her corrections of these 20 stories were NOT available to the system; they
are used only to measure it.

Per scene the feedback has three parts:

- **Corrections**, grouped as Grammar, Vocabulary, Coherence (each item labelled Reference, 了 / 着 / 在, or
  Connectors), and Content. Vocabulary also lists words used correctly, with richer alternatives.
  Items marked *(suggestion)* are less certain and are worded as a possible improvement.
  Each correction shows the student's words before → after the change (… marks a trimmed clause);
  ⟦X⟧ marks words a comment is about.
- **Expressiveness**: ideas for going beyond recounting events (feelings, speech, detail).
- **Encouragement**.

Her ratings per story go in `S0_story_ratings.csv`; comments can also be written directly into this
document.

## mengmu_samples#4

### Scene 1

> 古代的时候，孟子和孟母住了在靠墓地的家。孩子才五岁就每天在墓地祭拜了。在那个城市，孟母从早到晚都找工作，可是找不到，害得孟母没有钱。难怪她跟孩子搬家，原来买不了房。他们搬了到别一个城市找工作。

**Grammar**
- 古代的时候 → 在古代: Use 在古代 for this time setting. Put 在 before the time/place phrase.
- 孟子和孟母住了在靠墓地的家 → 孟子和孟母住在靠墓地的家: 住在 describes a state here, so do not put 了 before 在. Use 住在+Place.
- (suggestion) …在靠墓地的家 → …在靠墓地的家里: It would be more natural to add 里 after 家, making the location clearer: 家里.
- …天在墓地祭拜了 → …天在墓地祭拜: 每天 shows a habitual action, so sentence-final 了 is not needed here.
- (suggestion) …母从早到晚都找工作 → …母从早到晚都在找工作: It would be more natural to use 在 for an action continuing from morning to night: 从早到晚都在找工作.
- 他们搬了到别一个城市… → 他们搬到了到别一个城市…: With 搬到, the directional complement 到 comes before 了. Use 搬到了+Place.
- (suggestion) 他们搬了到别一个城市找… → 他们搬了别一个城市找…: It would be more natural to use 搬到了+Place rather than adding a separate 到 after 搬了.

**Vocabulary**
- …和孟母住了在靠墓地的家 → …和孟母住了在靠近墓地的家: Use 靠近 to mean “near” a place. 靠近墓地 means “near the cemetery.”
- 他们搬了到别一个城市找工… → 他们搬了到另一个城市找工…: Use 另一个 for “another one.” 别一个 is not the standard expression here.
- (suggestion) 墓地 (“在墓地祭拜”) → 墓园: a slightly more formal word for a cemetery; 坟地: a common, more everyday word for a burial place
- (suggestion) 每天 (“每天在墓地祭拜了”) → 天天: a natural, conversational alternative meaning every day; 每日: a more formal alternative meaning every day

**Coherence**
- **Reference** ⟦孟子⟧和孟母住了在…: 孟子 appears for the first time here, so please introduce him briefly, not only with his name.
- **Reference** 孟子和⟦孟母⟧住了在靠墓地…: 孟母 appears for the first time here, so please introduce her briefly, for example as 孟子的妈妈.
- **Reference** (suggestion) 在那个城市 → 在他们住的城市: It would be clearer to say 他们住的城市 because no city has been introduced for 那个城市 to refer to.
- **Connectors** (suggestion) 原来买不了房 → 原来是因为买不了房: It would be more natural to use 原来是因为 to clearly introduce the reason.

**Expressiveness**
- At “孩子才五岁就每天在墓地祭拜了。”: Add that Mengzi found the rituals interesting and copied them playfully, so the reader can picture why this worried his mother. For example: 孟子觉得祭拜很好玩，常常一边笑一边学别人祭拜。
- At “难怪她跟孩子搬家，原来买不了房。”: Add Mengmu’s worried thought to show how she felt before deciding to move. For example: 孟母很着急，心想：“不能一直住在这儿。”

**Encouragement** You clearly set the story in ancient times and introduced Mengzi and his mother. You also gave a clear reason for why they decided to move.

### Scene 2

> 这个城市老是很吵，不是人吵架，就是都叫，害得孟子一直不能睡觉。在那儿，孟母也找不到工作。孟子和妈妈要又一个次搬了家。在第三个城市，孟母找到工作，在商店做衣服。

**Grammar**
- 就是都叫 → 就是有人叫: 都 needs a clear plural referent. Use 有人 here to say that people are shouting.
- …要又一个次搬了家 → …要又一个次搬家: After 要, do not use completed-action 了. 要搬家 describes a planned action, not a completed one.

**Vocabulary**
- (suggestion) 孟母找到工作 → 孟母找到了工作: It would be more natural to say 找到了工作, because finding the job is a completed action.
- (suggestion) 吵 (“这个城市老是很吵”) → 吵闹: means noisy and disorderly; it is a little more descriptive for a city or street.
- (suggestion) 商店 (“在商店做衣服”) → 店铺: a common, slightly richer word for a shop or small store.

**Coherence**
- **Connectors** 孟子和妈妈要又一个次搬了家 → 孟子和妈妈要再一次搬了家: use 再一次 here because it means “one more time.”

**Expressiveness**
- At “这个城市老是很吵，不是人吵架，就是都叫，害得孟子一直不能睡觉。”: Add a small detail about the noise and Mengzi’s tired feeling. For example: 从早到晚，街上很吵，孟子很累。
- At “在那儿，孟母也找不到工作。”: Show Mengmu’s worry and let her say why she wants to move again. For example: 孟母很着急，说：“这里太吵了，我们再搬家吧。”

**Encouragement** You clearly explain the problem in this city and its effect on Mengzi’s sleep. Your use of “不是……就是……” makes the noisy situation easy to picture.

### Scene 3

> 孟子在那个商店也找了到工作，可是不是做衣服的工作，而是喜欢的工作。孟子学习馆学了十五年，他的学习和他的旅行经验很重要，对他很多好处。他是大人的时候，他成为很有名的哲人。

**Grammar**
- …做衣服的工作，而是喜欢的工作 → …做衣服的工作: The phrase 而是喜欢的工作 does not fit the meaning here, so delete it.
- 孟子学习馆学了十… → 孟子在学习馆学了十…: Use 在 before a place where an action happens: 在学习馆学了十五年.
- 对他很多好处 → 对他有很多好处: Use 有 in 对他有很多好处 to say something has many benefits for him.

**Vocabulary**
- …在那个商店也找了到工作 → …在那个商店也找到了到工作: The person successfully finds a job, so use 找到find, not 找look for.
- 他是大人的时候 → 他长大后: Use the established time expression 长大后, not the literal combination 是大人的时候.
- (suggestion) 他成为很有名的哲人 → 他成为了很有名的哲人: It would be more natural to add 了 after 成为 to show the completed change: 成为了很有名的哲人.
- (suggestion) 重要 (“他的学习和他的旅行经验很重要”) → 关键: This means “key” or “crucial,” stronger than important.
- (suggestion) 有名 (“很有名的哲人”) → 著名: This is a more formal and common word for “famous.”; 好处: This means “benefit,” and can make the sentence more concise.

**Coherence**
- **Reference** 他成为很有名的… → 成为很有名的…: The subject is already clear from the previous clause, so delete 他. Chinese uses zero form here.

**Expressiveness**
- At “孟子学习馆学了十五年”: Add that Mengzi enjoyed studying there and felt that the school was a good place for him. For example: 孟子很喜欢在那儿学习，觉得学校很好。
- At “他的学习和他的旅行经验很重要，对他很多好处。”: Add Mengmu’s happy words about the new place being suitable for her child. For example: 孟母很高兴，说：“这儿很适合孩子学习！”
- At “他成为很有名的哲人。”: Add a short comment from other people about why he became successful. For example: 人们都说：“孟子的成功离不开妈妈搬家。”

**Encouragement** You clearly show that Mengzi studied for a long time and later became a famous philosopher. You also explain that his learning and experience helped him, which gives the ending a clear reason.

### Content & completeness (after the whole story)

- After they leave the graveyard area, add that they live near the market and 孟子 copies buying and selling.
- Wrong information: “难怪她跟孩子搬家，原来买不了房。” — 孟母 moves because she worries that copying sacrifices will negatively affect 孟子, not because she cannot afford a house.
- Wrong information: “他们搬了到别一个城市找工作。” — The move away from the graveyard is prompted by 孟母's concern about 孟子 copying sacrifices, not by looking for work.

## mengmu_samples#10

### Scene 1

> 孟子小的时候，从早到晚都跟朋友玩。孟子和他的朋友喜欢玩在墓地里，装祭拜。孟子五岁的时候，跟妈妈搬家。他们搬家不是因为孟子玩得太多，而是因为妈妈认为他们的家离墓地太近。

**Grammar**
- …他的朋友喜欢玩在墓地里 → …他的朋友喜欢在墓地里玩: 墓地里 is the location, so use 在+Place before the action: 在墓地里玩.
- (suggestion) 而是因为妈妈认为他们… → 而是妈妈认为他们…: It would be more natural to say 而是妈妈认为…… because 而是 already introduces the contrasting reason, so 因为 is unnecessary.

**Vocabulary**
- 装祭拜 → 装作祭拜: Use 装作 before a verb phrase. Here, say 装作祭拜.
- (suggestion) 玩 (“从早到晚都跟朋友玩”) → 玩耍: A slightly more story-like word for children playing.; 嬉戏: Means playing happily and noisily; it is more vivid.
- (suggestion) 朋友 (“孟子和他的朋友”) → 伙伴: A natural word for companions who play or do things together.; 小伙伴: A warm, child-like word for young friends.
- (suggestion) 近 (“离墓地太近”) → 靠近: Means “close to” and works well when describing a location.; 邻近: Means “nearby” or “next to”; it sounds a little more descriptive.

**Coherence**
- **Reference** ⟦孟子⟧小的时候: This character appears for the first time, so please introduce 孟子 briefly with an identifying noun phrase, not only his name.

**Other**
- (suggestion) 跟妈妈搬家 → 跟妈妈搬家了: It would be more natural to add 了 after 搬家 to show the move was completed.

**Expressiveness**
- At “孟子和他的朋友喜欢玩在墓地里，装祭拜。”: Add that Mengzi watched people come to worship and found it interesting. For example: 他每天看见很多人来祭拜，觉得很好玩。
- At “孟子和他的朋友喜欢玩在墓地里，装祭拜。”: Add a short line of speech to show the children copying the adults. For example: 孟子说：“我们也来祭拜吧！”
- At “孟子和他的朋友喜欢玩在墓地里，装祭拜。”: Add that the children laughed while playing, which made the real visitors unhappy. For example: 他们一边笑一边玩，来祭拜的人很生气。

**Encouragement** You clearly explain Mengzi’s daily habit of playing with his friends and give a reason for the move. Using “不是……而是……” is also a good way to make the reason clear.

### Scene 2

> 妈妈认为，住离墓地太近，害得孟子的心气。孟子和孟母搬到子思老师的家，跟子思老师住。孟母帮子思打扫房间和做饭。子思帮孟母教她的儿子。因为孟子现在没有朋友，他老是读书。

**Grammar**
- 住离墓地太近 → 住得离墓地太近: Use 得 after 住 to introduce the degree result: 住得离墓地太近.
- (suggestion) …子思老师的家，跟子思老师住 → …子思老师的家住: It would be more natural to delete 跟子思老师 because 搬到子思老师的家 already shows where they will live.

**Vocabulary**
- 害得孟子的心气 → 伤了孟子的心气: 害得 needs a harmful result after 孟子的心气. Here, 伤了孟子的心气 is the natural expression.
- (suggestion) 打扫 (“孟母帮子思打扫房间”) → 收拾: This means to tidy up and put things in order.
- (suggestion) 做饭 (“孟母帮子思打扫房间和做饭”) → 下厨: This is a slightly more vivid way to say “cook,” especially when someone cooks for others.
- (suggestion) 读书 (“他老是读书”) → 学习: This is broader and can include reading, writing, and other school work.; 用功: This emphasizes studying hard and making a real effort.

**Coherence**
- **Reference** 孟子和⟦孟母⟧搬到子思老师…: 孟母 appears for the first time, so please introduce her briefly, for example 孟子的妈妈孟母.
- **Connectors** (suggestion) 他老是读书 → 所以他老是读书: It would be more natural to add 所以 to show that having no friends is the reason for reading books often.

**Expressiveness**
- At “孟子和孟母搬到子思老师的家，跟子思老师住。”: Add what Meng’s mother hopes for in the new place, using a short sentence of speech. For example: 孟母说：“这里很适合孩子学习。”
- At “因为孟子现在没有朋友，他老是读书。”: Add how Meng feels about reading, to show that he is beginning to enjoy studying. For example: 孟子觉得读书很有意思。
- At “子思帮孟母教她的儿子。”: Add a small detail about what Meng does while learning, such as reading books carefully. For example: 孟子每天认真看书学习。

**Encouragement** You clearly show the change after the move: Meng has no friends there, so he reads often. You also include how Meng’s mother and the teacher help each other, which makes the scene feel connected.

### Scene 3

> 子思认为，对孩子来说，读书最重要，特别是学哲学。孟子不是练习写文章，就是看哲学书。孟子从五岁一直学哲学。他才五岁就学哲学，难怪他成为很有名的哲学家。

**Grammar**
- 孟子从五岁一直学哲学 → 孟子从五岁起一直学哲学: After 从五岁, add 起 to show the starting point: 从五岁起一直学哲学.
- 难怪他成为很有名的哲学… → 难怪他成为了很有名的哲学…: Becoming a famous philosopher is a completed event, so use 了 after 成为.

**Vocabulary**
- 就是看哲学书 → 就是看到哲学书: The person actually sees the philosophy books, so use 看到see, not 看look.
- (suggestion) 有名 (“成为很有名的哲学家”) → 著名: Means “well-known” and is a little more formal.; 出名: Means “famous” and is common in everyday speech.
- (suggestion) 读书 (“读书最重要”) → 阅读: Means “to read” and is more formal, often used for reading books or texts.

**Coherence**
- **Reference** 难怪他成为很有名的… → 难怪成为很有名的…: 孟子 is already clear from the previous sentence, so 他 is redundant. Chinese uses zero form here.

**Expressiveness**
- At “孟子不是练习写文章，就是看哲学书。”: Add that Mengzi studies very seriously and is interested in philosophy. For example: 他很喜欢哲学，读得很认真。
- At “孟子从五岁一直学哲学。”: Add a short quote from Mengzi’s mother to show that she feels happy about his learning environment. For example: 孟母很高兴，说：“这儿很适合孩子学习。”
- At “他才五岁就学哲学，难怪他成为很有名的哲学家。”: Add that his mother feels proud when she sees him become successful. For example: 孟母看到他很高兴，也很骄傲。

**Encouragement** You clearly show that Mengzi studied philosophy from a very young age. Your final sentence also connects his childhood learning to his later success as a philosopher.

### Content & completeness (after the whole story)

- After they leave the graveyard, add that 孟母 moves them near the market and 孟子 copies buying and selling.
- After the market events, add that 孟母 moves them near a school before 孟子 studies every day.
- Wrong information: “孟子和孟母搬到子思老师的家，跟子思老师住。” — The story requires them to move first to a neighborhood near the market, and later from the market to a neighborhood near a school; it does not say they move to 子思老师's home.

### Scene 1

> 古代，孟子跟孟母住在墓地的旁边。可是孟母注意，住在墓地的旁边影响孟子。他常常跟朋友们祭拜玩，所以孟母决定他们会搬家。这次，他们住在城市里的商店的旁边，从早到晚很吵。

- 所以孟母决定他们会搬家 → 所以孟母决定搬家: After 决定, use the action directly: 决定搬家. Do not add 他们会 here.
- (suggestion) 常常 (“他常常跟朋友们祭拜玩”) → 总是: shows that he does it again and again, often with a slightly stronger feeling.; 经常: is a natural word for doing something frequently.
- (suggestion) 商店 (“城市里的商店的旁边”) → 店铺: means shops and sounds a little more varied in a story.; 市场: means a marketplace, with many sellers and shops together.
- (suggestion) 吵 (“从早到晚很吵”) → 热闹: describes a lively, busy place in a more positive way.; 嘈杂: focuses on the loud, mixed noises of a busy place.

**Coherence**
- **Reference** ⟦孟子⟧跟孟母住在墓…: This character appears for the first time, so please introduce 孟子 briefly with an identifying noun phrase, not only his name.
- **Reference** 孟子跟⟦孟母⟧住在墓地的旁…: This character appears for the first time, so please introduce 孟母 briefly, for example as 孟子的妈妈.
- **Connectors** 可是孟母注意 → 可是孟母注意到: Use 注意到 when you mean “notice” or “realize” a fact.

**Encouragement** You clearly show the first move from the cemetery to the city. You also give a reason for the move by explaining that Mengzi’s behaviour affects his mother’s decision.

### Scene 3

> 所以，孟母和孟子搬到学校的旁边住。才五岁孟子开始学哲学。在学校，孟子不是看书，就是练习写字。将来，他成为中国的一个非常有名的哲学家。

**Grammar**
- *No corrections.*

**Vocabulary**
- (suggestion) 他成为中国的一… → 他会成为中国的一…: It would be more natural to add 会 to show that becoming a famous philosopher was his future result. 成为 is correctly followed by the profession noun 哲学家.
- (suggestion) 看书 (“孟子不是看书，就是练习写字。”) → 阅读: A more formal word for reading books or written materials.
- (suggestion) 有名 (“一个非常有名的哲学家”) → 著名: A slightly more formal word meaning well-known or famous.
- (suggestion) 非常 (“一个非常有名的哲学家”) → 十分: A natural, slightly more formal intensifier meaning very.; 格外: It means especially or particularly, with a stronger emphasis.

**Coherence**

- 他测量起来了 → 他明白了: 测量 means “to measure,” not “to realize.” Use 明白了 when he realizes which plants are poisonous or medicinal.
- 像星星的植物是毒 → 像星星的植物有毒: Use 有毒, not 是毒, to say that a plant is poisonous.
- (suggestion) 痛 (“让他肚子很痛”) → 疼: A very natural word for physical pain.; 难受: Shows that he feels generally uncomfortable or sick.
- (suggestion) 很好 (“觉得很好”) → 好多了: Shows that he feels better than before.; 舒服: Means his body feels comfortable again.

**Coherence**
- **Reference** 觉得他快会死了 → 觉得快会死了: 他 is already clear from the previous clause, so delete this redundant subject. Chinese uses zero form here.
- **Reference** (suggestion) 像伞的植物是药 → 像伞的是药: It would be more natural to omit 植物 because it is already understood: 像伞的是药.
- **了 / 着 / 在** 觉得他快会死了 → 觉得他快要死了: Use 快要 for something that is going to happen very soon: 快要死了.
