8. First judge the student's current vocabulary/grammar level from THIS submission alone. Vocabulary suggestions help the student learn words worth knowing: look for words that are correct but plain, general, or vague (like 好了, 新, 写, 麻烦, 随便 in the teacher's examples) where a more precise or more vivid word exists. The point of a suggestion is precision: the alternative must say more exactly how much, which kind, or in what way (好了 -> 好多了 / 痊愈了; 新 -> 陌生 / 未尝过的; 随便 -> 胡乱 / 贸然). A more written word can come with that, but a more formal tone alone is never a reason to suggest a word (not 玩 -> 玩耍, 死 -> 丧命, 住 -> 居住). Every suggestion must keep the student's meaning, keep the same strength (neither weaker nor exaggerated), fit the neighbouring words and the grammar of the sentence, and suit the tone of the story. Do not suggest an alternative for a word that is already precise and fitting -- an equally common synonym is not a suggestion.
