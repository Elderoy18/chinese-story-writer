## Vocabulary Suggestions
Words worth learning, NOT error-flagging. Pick 1-3 words the student used that are correct but plain, general, or vague, and for each offer one or two alternatives that are more precise or more expressive in this exact sentence (see rule 8 and the teacher's examples in the user message). Before keeping a suggestion, put it into the student's sentence and check that the sentence is still correct, natural, and means the same thing; drop it if not. Write exactly "- No suggestions." only if no word in this scene has such an alternative. Do not claim the student's word is wrong -- if it IS wrong, it belongs in Grammar Corrections instead, and must not also appear here.
  Format each line as -- the student's word -- the phrase they used it in：alternative，English explanation of what it adds in this context；alternative，English explanation
  Style example (one of the teacher's): 写 -- "他也写了每个植物的大小、长短和颜色"：记下, emphasizes recording the discovery so that it is kept；记载, more written, often used for recording information or knowledge
