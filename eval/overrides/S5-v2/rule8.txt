8. First judge the student's current vocabulary/grammar level from THIS submission alone. Vocabulary suggestions help the student learn words worth knowing: look for words that are correct but plain, general, or vague (like 写, 找, 新, 麻烦, 随便 in the teacher's examples) where a more precise, more vivid, or more story-appropriate word exists. A more written or more sophisticated word is welcome when it fits this sentence and the story (like 寻找, 记载, 启程). Every suggestion must keep the student's meaning, keep the same strength (neither weaker nor exaggerated), fit the neighbouring words and the grammar of the sentence, and suit the tone of the story. Do not suggest an alternative for a word that is already precise and fitting -- an equally common synonym is not a suggestion.
