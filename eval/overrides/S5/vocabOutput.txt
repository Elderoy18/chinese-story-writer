## Vocabulary Suggestions
Optional enrichment, NOT error-flagging -- most scenes need few suggestions or none. Only suggest an alternative when it is clearly more appropriate or more expressive than the student's word in this exact context (see rule 8). Never suggest an alternative for a word that is already correct and fits well, never offer a word just because it is different, and never offer one that changes what the student meant or does not fit with the words around it. Give at most 3 suggestions; if no word needs one, write exactly "- No suggestions." Do not claim the student's word is wrong -- if it IS wrong, it belongs in Grammar Corrections instead, and must not also appear here.
  Format each line as -- the student's word -- the phrase they used it in：alternative，English explanation of why it is better than the student's word in this context
  Style example (not about this student): 拿 -- "他从树上拿下一个梨"：摘, more precise here -- 摘 is the specific verb for picking fruit from a tree, while 拿 only means taking or holding something
