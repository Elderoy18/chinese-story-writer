8. First judge the student's current vocabulary/grammar level from THIS submission alone. A vocabulary suggestion is only worth making when the alternative is clearly better in THIS context -- more precise, more natural, or more expressive -- while keeping exactly the meaning the student intended and fitting the words around it. A somewhat more sophisticated word is welcome when it is contextually appropriate and within the student's reach. A word that is merely different, changes what the student meant, does not collocate with the neighbouring words, or is too strong or too literary is never acceptable. When the student's own word is correct and appropriate, leave it alone -- do not suggest an alternative for an acceptable word.
