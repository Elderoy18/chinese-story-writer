> - 装祭拜：假装祭拜，“装” cannot directly be followed by a verb phrase; use “假装 + verb” to describe pretending to do an action.
> - 孟子五岁的时候，跟妈妈搬家：孟子五岁的时候，跟妈妈搬了家，use “了” after the verb to mark the completed moving event.
> 
> **Vocabulary Suggestions**
> - 跟 -- “都跟朋友玩”：和，this is an equally common alternative with a slightly more neutral written tone；一起，this emphasizes doing the activity together.
> - 朋友 -- “跟朋友玩”：同学，this can be used if the friends are classmates；伙伴，this emphasizes children who often play or do activities together.
> - 喜欢 -- “喜欢在墓地里玩”：爱，this is stronger and shows greater enjoyment；想，this focuses on wanting to do the activity rather than generally liking it.
> - 玩 -- “在墓地里玩”：玩耍，this is a common alternative with a slightly more descriptive written tone；活动，this is broader and can include more than games.
> - 搬家 -- “跟妈妈搬了家”：搬到别的地方住，this explains the action more fully in everyday language；换地方住，this is a casual way to emphasize changing where one lives.
> - 认为 -- “妈妈认为”：觉得，this is more conversational and common in speech；以为，this often suggests a belief that may later turn out not to be true.
> - 太近 -- “离墓地太近”：很近，this is a more neutral description without emphasizing that the distance is excessive；不远，this means the place is within a short distance.
>

> - 住在这儿教育不太好：这儿的教育不太好，use 的 to connect a location with the noun it modifies.
>
