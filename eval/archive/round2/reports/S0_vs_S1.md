### Scene 1

> 孟子小的时候，从早到晚都跟朋友玩。孟子和他的朋友喜欢玩在墓地里，装祭拜。孟子五岁的时候，跟妈妈搬家。他们搬家不是因为孟子玩得太多，而是因为妈妈认为他们的家离墓地太近。

#### S0 (before)

> **Grammar Corrections**
> 
> Incorrect phrase: “孟子小的时候” / Formula: Person + 小时候 / Correct: “孟子小时候”  
> “小的时候” is understandable, but “小时候” is more natural and concise when talking about someone’s childhood.
> 
> Incorrect phrase: “孟子和他的朋友喜欢玩在墓地里” / Formula: Subject + 在 + place + verb / Correct: “孟子和他的朋友喜欢在墓地里玩。”  
> In Chinese, the location usually comes before the main verb: “在墓地里玩,” not “玩在墓地里.”
> 
> Incorrect phrase: “装祭拜” / Formula: Subject + 假装/装作 + verb / Correct: “假装祭拜” or “装作祭拜。”  
> “装” alone cannot directly mean “pretend to do something” here. Use “假装” or “装作.”
> 
> Incorrect phrase: “孟子小的时候” as the first introduction of the character / Formula: 有一个 + 叫……的 + noun / Correct: “从前，有一个叫孟子的小孩子。”  
> When a character first appears in a story, it is clearer to briefly introduce who they are. For example: “从前，有一个叫孟子的小孩子。孟子小时候……”
> 
> **Vocabulary Suggestions**
> 
> Word: “玩” / Context: “从早到晚都跟朋友玩” / Alternatives: “玩耍” or “嬉戏”  
> “玩” is very common and simple. “玩耍” is slightly more descriptive and works well in storytelling. “嬉戏” is more literary and suggests children playing happily and actively.
> 
> Word: “从早到晚” / Context: “从早到晚都跟朋友玩” / Alternatives: “整天” or “一天到晚”  
> “从早到晚” clearly emphasizes the long duration from morning until evening. “整天” is shorter and natural in everyday Chinese. “一天到晚” often suggests that someone does something too much, so it would fit well if you want to show the mother’s concern.
> 
> Word: “装” / Context: “装祭拜” / Alternatives: “假装” or “模仿”  
> “假装” means pretending to do something that is not real. “模仿” means copying someone’s actions or behavior. If the children copied adults at funerals, “模仿大人祭拜” would be especially vivid.
> 
> Word: “搬家” / Context: “跟妈妈搬家” / Alternatives: “搬到别的地方” or “迁居”  
> “搬家” is the most common everyday word for moving homes. “搬到别的地方” makes the destination change clearer. “迁居” is more formal and literary, so it may suit a historical story.
> 
> Word: “认为” / Context: “妈妈认为他们的家离墓地太近” / Alternatives: “觉得” or “担心”  
> “认为” is neutral and somewhat formal. “觉得” is more conversational and natural for a mother’s personal opinion. “担心” adds the feeling that the mother was worried: “妈妈担心他们的家离墓地太近。”
> 
> **Encouragement and Next Steps**
> 
> You did a good job using “不是……而是……” to explain the real reason for the family’s move, which makes the story clear and logical.  
> Next time, focus on word order with places: remember that “在 + place” usually comes before the verb.
