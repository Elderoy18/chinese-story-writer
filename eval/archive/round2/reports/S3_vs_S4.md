### Scene 1

> 孟子小的时候，从早到晚都跟朋友玩。孟子和他的朋友喜欢玩在墓地里，装祭拜。孟子五岁的时候，跟妈妈搬家。他们搬家不是因为孟子玩得太多，而是因为妈妈认为他们的家离墓地太近。

#### S3 (before)

> **Grammar Corrections**
> - 孟子小的时候，从早到晚都跟朋友玩。：有一个叫孟子的小孩子，他从早到晚都跟朋友玩。A named character should be briefly introduced at the first mention.
> - 喜欢玩在墓地里：喜欢在墓地里玩，A location phrase such as this normally comes before the main verb as an adverbial.
> - 装祭拜：假装在祭拜，装 cannot directly precede this action. 假装 means “pretend” and can be followed by an ongoing action.
> 
> **Vocabulary Suggestions**
> - 从早到晚 -- “从早到晚都跟朋友玩”：整天, a shorter everyday expression meaning “all day”；一整天, emphasizes the complete length of the day
> - 跟 -- “跟朋友玩”：和, an equally common alternative with a slightly more neutral tone；跟着, emphasizes physically accompanying someone, so it has a different meaning
> - 玩 -- “跟朋友玩”：玩耍, a common slightly more written alternative for children playing；做游戏, specifically refers to playing games
> - 朋友 -- “他的朋友”：小伙伴, a warm, child-focused word for friends；同伴, a neutral word for companions or peers
> - 妈妈 -- “跟妈妈搬家”：母亲, a more formal alternative；妈妈, the original word, is warmer and more natural in a child’s narration
> - 认为 -- “妈妈认为”：觉得, a common everyday word for a personal opinion or feeling；以为, often suggests an assumption that may later be shown to be mistaken
> 
> **Encouragement**
> You used clear cause-and-effect structure with “不是……而是……,” and the explanation for the move is easy to understand. Your time expressions also help the scene move smoothly.

#### S4 (after)

> **Grammar Corrections**
> - 喜欢玩在墓地里：喜欢在墓地里玩，The place phrase should come before the main verb in this sentence.
> - 装祭拜：假装祭拜，Use 假装 before an action verb to mean pretending to do that action; 装 cannot directly modify 祭拜 in this meaning.
> - 跟妈妈：妈妈带着他，When a mother relocates with her child, use 带着 to show that the mother takes the child with her; 跟 expresses simply doing something together.
> 
> **Vocabulary Suggestions**
> - 从早到晚 -- “从早到晚都跟朋友玩”：整天，This is a common, shorter way to say that an activity lasts all day；一天到晚，This has a slightly stronger sense that the activity continues for the whole day.
> - 朋友 -- “孟子和他的朋友”：小伙伴，This is a warm, child-focused word for friends.
> - 喜欢 -- “喜欢在墓地里玩”：爱，This can show a stronger liking, but it is still very common in everyday speech.
> - 玩 -- “跟朋友玩”：玩儿，This is a common spoken alternative, especially when describing children.
> - 认为 -- “妈妈认为他们的家离墓地太近”：觉得，This is a more conversational word for a personal opinion or feeling.
> - 太近 -- “离墓地太近”：很近，This is less emphatic and simply states that the distance is short.
> 
> **Coherence & Expressiveness**
> - Coherence (Reference): 孟子小的时候：有一个小男孩叫孟子，他小的时候，On first mention, introduce the character with a full noun phrase before using the name or a pronoun.
> - Coherence (Reference): 孟子和他的朋友：他和朋友，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the same character was named in the previous sentence and no other character competes as the referent.
> - Coherence (Reference): 孟子五岁的时候：他五岁的时候，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence of the same character.
> - Coherence (Temporality): 搬家：搬家了，Use 了 for this completed, one-time moving event in the past narrative.
> - Expressiveness: At the cemetery-play moment, add a physical action to show what the children do, such as “他一边笑，一边假装祭拜。”
> - Expressiveness: When explaining the move, add the mother’s feeling, such as “妈妈觉得很麻烦。”
> - Expressiveness: Add a short line of the mother’s thoughts before the move, such as “我们不能住在这儿了。”
> 
> **Encouragement**
> - You clearly show the main reason for the family’s move, and the paired pattern “不是……而是……” is used effectively.
> - Your scene also uses simple time expressions well to show that the events happened when Mengzi was young.

### Scene 2

> 妈妈认为，住离墓地太近，害得孟子的心气。孟子和孟母搬到子思老师的家，跟子思老师住。孟母帮子思打扫房间和做饭。子思帮孟母教她的儿子。因为孟子现在没有朋友，他老是读书。

#### S3 (before)

> **Grammar Corrections**
> - 妈妈认为，住离墓地太近，害得孟子的心气。：妈妈认为，住在离墓地太近的地方会影响孟子的心情。，“住” needs “在” before a location, and “害得” must be followed by a result description rather than a bare noun phrase. “会影响” correctly expresses the effect on someone’s feelings.
> - 老是：一直，“老是” usually carries a negative or complaining tone. For neutral reading or studying over a period of time, “一直” is the appropriate word.
> 
> **Vocabulary Suggestions**
> - 妈妈 -- “妈妈认为”：母亲，this is a more formal word for a mother；家长，this refers more broadly to a parent or guardian.
> - 认为 -- “妈妈认为”：觉得，this is more conversational and often sounds less formal；想，this is a simple everyday choice for expressing an opinion.
> - 搬到 -- “搬到子思老师的家”：搬去，this emphasizes going to the new place；搬家，this states the move without naming the destination.
> - 帮 -- “孟母帮子思打扫房间”：帮助，this has the same meaning but sounds slightly more formal；给，this can sound more casual when introducing an action done for someone.
> - 打扫 -- “打扫房间”：收拾，this can include tidying and organizing, not only cleaning；清理，this emphasizes removing dirt or unwanted things.
> - 做饭 -- “打扫房间和做饭”：煮饭，this focuses more specifically on cooking a meal；做菜，this focuses on preparing dishes.
> - 读书 -- “他老是读书”：看书，this focuses on reading books；学习，this has a broader meaning and can include doing schoolwork.
> 
> **Encouragement**
> You used clear sentences to show what each character does, especially with “孟母帮子思打扫房间和做饭” and “子思帮孟母教她的儿子.” You also used “因为” well to connect a reason and an action.

> **Vocabulary Suggestions**
> - 认为 -- “妈妈认为”：觉得, a very common alternative that sounds slightly more personal or conversational
> - 搬到 -- “搬到子思老师的家”：搬去, a common choice that emphasizes moving toward the new place

> - 做饭 -- “做饭”：煮饭, a common alternative that focuses more specifically on cooking a meal
> - 教 -- “教她的儿子”：教导, a slightly more formal word for guiding and teaching a child
> - 读书 -- “他老是读书”：看书, a common alternative that focuses on the action of reading books

> **Coherence & Expressiveness**
> - Coherence (Reference): 跟子思老师住：跟他住，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence: 子思老师 was named earlier in the same sentence, and no other male person competes as the referent.
> - Coherence (Reference): 孟母帮子思打扫房间和做饭：孟母帮他打扫房间和做饭，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence: 子思老师 was the most recently mentioned person.
> - Coherence (Reference): 子思帮孟母教她的儿子：他帮孟母教她的儿子，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence: the previous sentence already identifies 子思.
> - Coherence (Temporality): 孟子和孟母搬到子思老师的家：孟子和孟母搬到了子思老师的家，Use 了 for a completed moving action in the narrative.
> - Coherence (Conjunction): 因为孟子现在没有朋友，他老是读书：因为孟子现在没有朋友，所以他一直读书，Use 因为……所以…… to show the cause-and-effect relation. Also, 一直 is neutral for a continuing activity, while 老是 often carries a negative, complaining tone.
> - Expressiveness: After the mother’s concern about the cemetery, add her feeling or thought: 孟母很担心孟子的学习。
> - Expressiveness: When the family moves in, add a simple detail about the new study environment: 子思老师的家很安静，房间里有很多书。
> - Expressiveness: At the end, show Mengzi’s new attitude through an action: 孟子坐在桌子前，认真地看书。

> **Encouragement**

> - You also used a cause-and-effect idea to explain why Mengzi reads, which is a strong way to connect events.

### Scene 1

> 有一天孟子去了墓地玩儿，但是他妈妈觉得这是不合适的地方让孩子玩，所以她决定了他们会搬家找更好的地方。她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福，而且住在这儿教育不太好。因为她望子成龙，所以她会一直搬家才能找到质量好的教育。然后他们搬到了离城市更近的地方。

#### S3 (before)

> **Grammar Corrections**
> - 决定了他们会搬家找更好的地方：决定带着孟子搬家，去找更好的地方，For an intended future action, use 决定 + verb phrase. The completed form 决定了 does not naturally take the predictive clause that follows here.
> - 住在这儿教育不太好：这儿的教育不太好，Use a place phrase with 的 to modify 教育 when describing the quality of education in that place. 住在这儿 should describe a person’s action, not directly modify 教育.
> - 会一直搬家才能找到质量好的教育：得一直搬家，才能找到质量好的教育，才能 expresses a result that requires a condition. 得 shows that the repeated moving is necessary for that result; 会 only expresses a future prediction.
> 
> **Vocabulary Suggestions**
> - 有一天 -- "有一天孟子去了墓地玩儿"：一天, a shorter, neutral way to introduce a day in a story
> - 玩儿 -- "去了墓地玩儿"：玩, the same everyday meaning in a more neutral written form；玩耍, a slightly more written-style word for playing
> - 觉得 -- "他妈妈觉得"：认为, a more deliberate opinion or judgment；想, a simpler and more casual way to express what someone thinks
> - 恐怕 -- "她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福"：担心, more directly emphasizes her worry；害怕, emphasizes stronger fear
> - 幸福 -- "童年不会幸福"：快乐, focuses more on feeling happy；开心, a very common, casual word for feeling happy
> - 质量好 -- "质量好的教育"：高质量, a common compact phrase for something with good quality
> - 更近 -- "离城市更近的地方"：近一点, a softer way to express a small difference in distance
> 
> **Encouragement**
> You used clear cause-and-effect connectors, especially 因为 and 所以, to show the mother’s reasons. The final location phrase, 搬到了离城市更近的地方, is also well formed and easy to understand.

#### S4 (after)

> **Grammar Corrections**
> - 不合适的地方让孩子玩：不适合孩子玩的地方，use 适合 when stating that a place is suitable or unsuitable for a person to do an action; the pattern is “适合 + person + verb phrase.”
> - 住在这儿教育不太好：住在这儿，孩子受到的教育不太好，when describing the education available to a child in a place, the sentence needs a clear noun phrase such as “孩子受到的教育.”
> - 她会一直搬家才能找到质量好的教育：她得一直搬家，才能找到质量好的教育，use 得 before a verb to show that an action is necessary for the result introduced by 才能; 会 mainly predicts a future action.
> 
> **Vocabulary Suggestions**
> - 墓地 -- “去了墓地玩儿”：坟地, a common, slightly more everyday word for a burial place；墓园, a common word that can suggest a more organized cemetery
> - 玩儿 -- “去了墓地玩儿”：玩, an equally common choice without the conversational 儿 sound；玩耍, a slightly more formal word for playing
> - 觉得 -- “妈妈觉得”：认为, a common choice that sounds more like an opinion or judgment；感到, a common choice for a personal feeling
> - 恐怕 -- “她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福”：担心, a more direct everyday word for feeling worried；害怕, a stronger word that emphasizes fear
> - 幸福 -- “童年不会幸福”：快乐, a common word focusing more on joy and happiness；开心, a more casual word for feeling happy
> - 一直 -- “会一直搬家”：总是, a common alternative emphasizing that something happens repeatedly；不断, a slightly stronger word emphasizing that something continues without stopping
> - 更近 -- “离城市更近的地方”：近一些, a common softer comparison；比较近, a common phrase meaning relatively close
> 
> **Coherence & Expressiveness**
> - Coherence (Reference): 有一天孟子：有一天，小男孩孟子，follow the first-mention rule by introducing the character with both a description and a name before using later pronouns.
> - Coherence (Temporality): 决定了：决定，use the zero particle with the mental-state verb 决定; it describes her decision rather than a completed physical action.
> - Expressiveness: After “去了墓地玩儿,” add one simple visual detail about the place, such as “他看到很多人拿着花来祭拜。”
> - Expressiveness: At the point where the mother worries about his childhood, add her inner thought: “她心想：‘这儿不适合孩子长大。’”
> - Expressiveness: When the family moves, add a feeling to show the mother’s hope, such as “孟子的妈妈希望新家会更安静。”
> 
> **Encouragement**
> - You clearly show the mother’s concern and connect it to her decision to move. You also use useful connectors such as “但是,” “所以,” “因为,” and “然后” to organize the events.

### Scene 2

> 他们住才一月就又搬家。古代的时候，一般人不喜欢卖东西的人。但是孟子老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿，害得妈妈着急得很，因为她恐怕儿子会成为跟他们很像。她告诉了孟子：他们不是朋友，而是不好的人。

#### S3 (before)

> **Grammar Corrections**
> - 他们住才一月就又搬家：他们才住了一个月，就又搬家了，A duration follows the verb, and a completed period of living needs 了 after the verb; use the measure word 个 in this duration phrase.
> - 古代的时候：在古代，Use 在古代 or 古代 for this historical time expression; 古代的时候 is not natural here.
> - 老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿：常常跟这些卖东西的人玩儿，老是 and 常常 both express repeated actions, so using both together is redundant.
> - 害得妈妈着急得很：让妈妈着急得很，害得 must introduce a clearly harmful result; ordinary worry is better expressed with 让.
> - 成为跟他们很像：变得跟他们很像，成为 normally needs a noun phrase after it, while 变得 can be followed directly by an adjective phrase.
> 
> **Vocabulary Suggestions**
> - 搬家 -- “又搬家”：换地方住，a common everyday phrase that emphasizes moving to a different place to live；搬到别的地方，more specific because it includes the destination change.
> - 一般人 -- “一般人不喜欢”：普通人，a close everyday synonym with a more neutral tone；大多数人，emphasizes that this opinion is held by most people.
> - 不喜欢 -- “不喜欢卖东西的人”：不爱，a slightly shorter and more conversational alternative；不太喜欢，softens the strength of the dislike.
> - 卖东西 -- “卖东西的人”：做买卖，an everyday alternative that refers to doing business or selling goods；卖货，a more casual term focused on selling products.
> - 恐怕 -- “她恐怕儿子会”：担心，more directly expresses the mother’s worry；怕，a simpler and more conversational word for concern.
> - 告诉 -- “她告诉了孟子”：对孟子说，focuses on the act of speaking directly to him；提醒孟子，suggests that she is warning him about something important.
> - 不好 -- “不好的人”：坏，a stronger and more direct everyday word for someone with bad behavior；不太好，makes the judgment less strong.
> 
> **Encouragement**
> You used several useful narrative connectors and cause-and-effect ideas, especially with “因为” and “害得.” You also clearly showed the mother’s concern through dialogue, which makes the scene easy to understand.

#### S4 (after)

> **Grammar Corrections**
> - 古代的时候：在古代，this is the standard concise time expression for an ancient-time setting.
> - 老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿：常常跟这些卖东西的人玩儿，do not stack two frequency adverbs with the same function; choose either “老是” or “常常.”
> - 害得妈妈着急得很：让妈妈很着急，“害得” should introduce a clearly harmful consequence; for an emotional reaction such as worry, “让” is more appropriate.
> - 会成为跟他们很像：会变得跟他们很像，“成为” normally takes a noun phrase identifying what someone becomes, while “变得” is used before an adjective phrase describing a change of state.
> 
> **Vocabulary Suggestions**
> - 一般人 -- “一般人不喜欢卖东西的人”：大多数人, this means most people and sounds more specific when discussing a broad group；很多人, this is a simple everyday alternative that is less exact than “大多数人.”
> - 不喜欢 -- “不喜欢卖东西的人”：不爱, this is a common, more casual alternative；不太喜欢, this makes the dislike sound softer and less absolute.
