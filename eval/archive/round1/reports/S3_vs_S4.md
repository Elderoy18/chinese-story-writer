### Scene 1

> 孟子小的时候，从早到晚都跟朋友玩。孟子和他的朋友喜欢玩在墓地里，装祭拜。孟子五岁的时候，跟妈妈搬家。他们搬家不是因为孟子玩得太多，而是因为妈妈认为他们的家离墓地太近。

#### S3 (before)

> **Grammar Corrections**
> - 孟子小的时候：有一个叫孟子的小孩子，他小的时候，Introduce a named character with a brief noun phrase at the first mention so the reader knows who the character is.
> - 喜欢玩在墓地里：喜欢在墓地里玩，A place phrase should normally come before the main verb.
> - 装祭拜：假装祭拜，The verb 装 needs a suitable structure such as 装作, while 假装 can directly mean “pretend to do something.”
> 
> **Vocabulary Suggestions**
> - 小的时候 -- “孟子小的时候”：小时候, a shorter and very common everyday way to express the same time period；年幼的时候, slightly more formal and emphasizes being very young
> - 从早到晚 -- “从早到晚都跟朋友玩”：整天, a simpler everyday word meaning “all day”；一天到晚, an informal alternative that can emphasize doing something for the whole day
> - 朋友 -- “跟朋友玩”：伙伴, a common word for companions, often used when children play or do activities together；小朋友, a natural choice when referring specifically to other children
> - 喜欢 -- “喜欢在墓地里玩”：爱, a more casual and direct word for liking an activity；很想, emphasizes wanting to do the activity rather than generally liking it
> - 搬家 -- “跟妈妈搬家”：搬到别的地方, makes the change of location more explicit；换个地方住, a casual phrase focusing on living in a different place
> - 认为 -- “妈妈认为”：觉得, a very common everyday word for an opinion or feeling；想, a simpler word for what someone thinks
> - 太近 -- “离墓地太近”：靠墓地太近, makes the physical closeness more concrete；离墓地很近, a softer description without the strong emphasis of 太
> 
> **Encouragement**
> You used clear time expressions and connected the reason for moving with a natural contrast structure using “不是……而是……”. Your sentences also stay focused and easy to follow.

#### S4 (after)

### Scene 2

> 妈妈认为，住离墓地太近，害得孟子的心气。孟子和孟母搬到子思老师的家，跟子思老师住。孟母帮子思打扫房间和做饭。子思帮孟母教她的儿子。因为孟子现在没有朋友，他老是读书。

#### S3 (before)

> **Grammar Corrections**
> - 住离墓地太近：住在离墓地太近的地方，The verb 住 needs the preposition 在 before a location, and the location phrase needs a noun such as 地方 after the adjective phrase.
> - 害得孟子的心气：影响孟子的心气，害得 must introduce a result clause with a verb or adjective predicate; 孟子的心气 is only a noun phrase, so it cannot be the result after 害得.
> - 老是读书：一直读书，老是 usually carries a negative, complaining tone. For a neutral description of continuing to study, use 一直.
> 
> **Vocabulary Suggestions**
> - 妈妈 -- "妈妈认为"：母亲，a slightly more formal word for a mother；妈妈觉得，a more conversational way to introduce her opinion
> - 搬到 -- "搬到子思老师的家"：搬去，an everyday alternative that emphasizes moving away to that destination；迁到，a somewhat more formal word often used for changing one’s residence
> - 打扫 -- "打扫房间"：收拾房间，this can include tidying up as well as cleaning；整理房间，this focuses more on putting things in order
> - 做饭 -- "做饭"：做菜，this specifically focuses on preparing dishes；煮饭，this more specifically refers to cooking a meal or rice
> - 帮 -- "子思帮孟母教她的儿子"：帮助，a slightly more formal alternative；帮忙，an everyday word that emphasizes offering help
> - 读书 -- "一直读书"：看书，this emphasizes the action of reading books；学习，this is broader and can include reading, writing, and other study activities
> 
> **Encouragement**
> You used clear, short sentences and included several everyday action words, such as 搬到, 打扫, 做饭, and 读书. You also used 因为 to show a reason, which helps connect the ideas in the scene.

#### S4 (after)

> **Grammar Corrections**
> - 住离墓地太近：住得离墓地太近，A verb followed by a degree phrase needs the complement marker 得: 住得 + adjective phrase.
> - 害得孟子的心气：影响了孟子的心气，害得 must be followed by a result clause describing what happened to someone; 影响了 means “affected” and can directly take the noun phrase 心气.
> - 老是读书：一直读书，老是 has a negative, complaining connotation. Use 一直 for neutral or positive continued reading.
> 
> **Vocabulary Suggestions**
> - 认为 -- “妈妈认为”：觉得，a very common and slightly more conversational way to express an opinion；想，a simpler everyday choice for a character’s thought.
> - 搬到 -- “搬到子思老师的家”：搬去，emphasizes moving toward that destination；搬家，focuses on the act of changing homes rather than the destination.
> - 打扫 -- “打扫房间”：收拾，an everyday word that can include tidying as well as cleaning；清理，focuses a little more on making a space clean.
> - 房间 -- “打扫房间”：屋子，a common, more casual word for a room or small home；家里，refers more broadly to the home rather than one particular room.
> - 做饭 -- “打扫房间和做饭”：做菜，specifically means preparing dishes；煮饭，focuses on cooking a meal, especially rice.
> - 教 -- “教她的儿子”：教导，sounds a little more like guiding a child’s learning and behavior；辅导，focuses on helping someone study.
> - 读书 -- “一直读书”：看书，a very common everyday phrase for reading books；学习，broader than reading and can include doing schoolwork.
> 
> **Coherence & Expressiveness**
> - Coherence (Reference): 跟子思老师住：跟他住，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence: 子思老师 was named earlier in the same sentence, and no other possible male referent has appeared.
> - Coherence (Temporality): 孟子和孟母搬到子思老师的家：孟子和孟母搬到了子思老师的家，Use 了 for the completed action of moving to a new home.
> - Coherence (Conjunction): 因为孟子现在没有朋友，他一直读书：因为孟子现在没有朋友，所以他一直读书，Use 因为……所以…… to clearly mark the cause-and-effect relation.
> - Expressiveness: After the move, add Mengzi’s feeling about studying in the new environment, for example: “孟子觉得这里很安静，能好好看书。”
> - Expressiveness: When Zisi teaches Mengzi, add one short line of dialogue to show the teaching, for example: “子思老师说：‘你要认真学习。’”
> - Expressiveness: Add a detail showing Mengmu’s reaction after seeing Mengzi read, for example: “孟母看见孟子在看书，很高兴。”
> 
> **Encouragement**
> - You clearly introduced the main people in this scene, including 子思老师, and you showed how each person helps another person.
> - Your final sentence gives a clear reason for Mengzi’s change in behavior, which helps the scene feel connected.

### Scene 1

> 有一天孟子去了墓地玩儿，但是他妈妈觉得这是不合适的地方让孩子玩，所以她决定了他们会搬家找更好的地方。她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福，而且住在这儿教育不太好。因为她望子成龙，所以她会一直搬家才能找到质量好的教育。然后他们搬到了离城市更近的地方。

#### S3 (before)

> **Grammar Corrections**
> - 她决定了他们会搬家找更好的地方：她决定带着孟子搬到更好的地方，A decision is normally followed directly by the action being decided, so use 决定 + verb phrase. Here, 搬到 + place expresses moving to a destination; 了 is not needed for the mental-state verb 决定 in this sentence.
> - 住在这儿教育不太好：这儿的教育不太好，When describing something belonging to or characteristic of a place, use the place phrase with 的 before the noun: 这儿的教育.
> - 她会一直搬家才能找到质量好的教育：她得一直搬家，才能找到质量好的教育，Use 得 + verb + 才能 to express that an action is necessary in order to achieve a result. 会 describes a future possibility or intention, not a necessary condition.
> 
> **Vocabulary Suggestions**
> - 玩儿 -- “去了墓地玩儿”：玩耍, a slightly more written-style word for playing；活动, a broader everyday word that can mean doing activities or moving around
> - 觉得 -- “他妈妈觉得”：认为, a slightly more formal word for holding an opinion；感到, better when emphasizing a personal feeling
> - 恐怕 -- “她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福”：担心, a more direct everyday word for being worried；害怕, stronger and more emotional than 恐怕
> - 幸福 -- “童年不会幸福”：快乐, a common word focusing on being happy；开心, a more casual word often used for a temporary happy feeling
> - 望子成龙 -- “因为她望子成龙”：希望儿子有出息, an everyday explanation of wanting one’s son to succeed；希望儿子成功, a simpler and more direct expression
> - 然后 -- “然后他们搬到了”：后来, useful when the next event happened after some time rather than immediately afterward；接着, useful when events happen one right after another
> 
> **Encouragement**
> You used several strong story-linking words, including 但是、因为、所以、然后, to connect the ideas clearly. You also used the idiom 望子成龙 appropriately to show the mother’s hopes for her child.

#### S4 (after)

> **Grammar Corrections**
> - 不合适的地方让孩子玩：不适合让孩子玩儿的地方，Use the pattern “适合 + someone + verb” or “适合让 + someone + verb” to describe whether a place is suitable for an activity.
> - 决定了他们会搬家找更好的地方：决定带着孟子搬家，找更好的地方，Mental-state verbs such as “决定” normally take the zero particle rather than “了”; this revision also gives the mother a clear action she can decide to do.
> - 住在这儿教育不太好：这儿的教育不太好，Use “Place + 的 + noun” when the place modifies the noun “教育.”
> 
> **Vocabulary Suggestions**
> - 有一天 -- “有一天孟子去了墓地玩儿”：某一天, this is slightly more specific and narrative in tone；一天, this is shorter and simple when the exact day is unimportant.
> - 墓地 -- “去了墓地玩儿”：坟地, a common everyday alternative that focuses more directly on graves；墓园, a common alternative with a slightly more formal tone.
> - 恐怕 -- “她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福”：担心, this directly expresses the mother’s worry；害怕, this is stronger and expresses fear rather than concern.
> - 幸福 -- “童年不会幸福”：快乐, this focuses on feeling happy day to day；开心, this is a more casual everyday word for happiness.
> - 望子成龙 -- “因为她望子成龙”：希望儿子有出息, this explains the idiom in a clear, everyday way；希望儿子成功, this is simpler and more direct.
> - 质量好的教育 -- “找到质量好的教育”：高质量的教育, this is a common collocation for education of good quality；更好的教育, this is simpler and compares the new education with the current one.
> - 更近 -- “离城市更近的地方”：近一点, this gives a softer sense of being somewhat closer；城市附近, this describes a place near the city without making a direct comparison.
> 
> **Coherence & Expressiveness**
> - Expressiveness: After “她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福,” add the mother’s feeling and thought, for example: “她很担心，心想：‘我不能让孟子在这儿长大。’”
> - Expressiveness: At “孟子去了墓地玩儿,” add a concrete detail about what he does there, for example: “他看见很多人来祭拜，也学着他们的样子。”
> - Expressiveness: At the new home near the city, describe the environment with a simple sensory detail, for example: “那儿每天都有很多人，街上很热闹。”
> 
> **Encouragement**
> You clearly show the mother’s reason for moving, and you use a cause-and-effect structure with “因为……所以……” well. You also connect this scene to the next place with “然后,” which makes the events easy to follow.

### Scene 2

> 他们住才一月就又搬家。古代的时候，一般人不喜欢卖东西的人。但是孟子老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿，害得妈妈着急得很，因为她恐怕儿子会成为跟他们很像。她告诉了孟子：他们不是朋友，而是不好的人。

#### S3 (before)

> **Grammar Corrections**
> - 他们住才一月就又搬家。：他们才住了一个月，就又搬家了。For a completed length of stay, 才 comes before the verb, and 了 marks the completed stay; the sentence-final 了 marks the completed move.
> - 古代的时候：在古代，This is the natural time expression for a general historical setting.
> - 害得妈妈着急得很：让妈妈着急得很，害得 should introduce a clearly harmful result; 着急 is an emotional reaction, so 让 is the appropriate neutral causative verb.
> - 成为跟他们很像：变得跟他们很像，成为 normally takes a noun or identity after it, while 变得 is used before an adjective phrase such as 跟他们很像.
> 
> **Vocabulary Suggestions**
> - 搬家 -- “又搬家”：搬到别的地方，This makes the change of location more explicit.
> - 一般 -- “一般人”：大多数，This emphasizes that the statement applies to most people rather than people in general.
> - 喜欢 -- “不喜欢”：不爱，This is a common alternative with a slightly more direct, conversational tone.
> - 卖东西的人 -- “这些卖东西的人”：商人，This is a shorter common noun for people who sell goods, especially as a job.
> - 玩儿 -- “跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿”：一起玩，This stresses doing the activity together and is very natural in everyday speech.
> - 着急 -- “妈妈着急得很”：担心，This focuses more on the mother’s worry about a possible future result.
> - 告诉 -- “她告诉了孟子”：跟……说，This is a common spoken alternative when introducing someone’s direct speech.
> - 不好 -- “不好的人”：坏人，This is more direct and concise for a person with bad behavior.
> 
> **Encouragement**
> You used several useful story connectors and cause-and-effect ideas, including 又, 因为, and 恐怕. The description of the mother’s worry is also clear and easy to understand.

#### S4 (after)

> **Grammar Corrections**
> - 古代的时候：在古代，Use “在古代” for a general historical time setting; “古代的时候” is not the natural form here.
> - 老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿：老是跟这些卖东西的人玩儿，Both “老是” and “常常” express a repeated habit, so only one should modify the verb phrase.
> - 害得妈妈着急得很：让妈妈很着急，“害得” should introduce a clearly harmful result; for causing someone to feel worried, “让” is the appropriate verb.
