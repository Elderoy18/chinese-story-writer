#### S4 (before)

> **Grammar Corrections**
> - 不合适的地方让孩子玩：不适合孩子玩的地方，Use the pattern “适合 + person + verb phrase” to describe whether a place suits someone’s activity; the original word order does not form a natural modifier for “地方.”

> - 住在这儿教育不太好：这儿的教育不太好，Use “的” to link the location to the noun it describes: the education in this place.
>

> - 幸福 -- “童年不会幸福”：快乐，A very common word focusing more directly on feeling happy；开心，A casual everyday word for being happy.

### Scene 2

> 他们住才一月就又搬家。古代的时候，一般人不喜欢卖东西的人。但是孟子老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿，害得妈妈着急得很，因为她恐怕儿子会成为跟他们很像。她告诉了孟子：他们不是朋友，而是不好的人。

#### S4 (before)

> **Grammar Corrections**
> - 他们住才一月：他们才住一个月，The adverb 才 comes before the verb phrase, and a duration normally uses the measure word 个.
> - 古代的时候：在古代，This time expression is not natural here; use 在古代 or 古代 for a general historical setting.
> - 老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿：常常跟这些卖东西的人玩儿，Do not use two frequency adverbs, 老是 and 常常, to modify the same action in this way.
> - 害得妈妈着急得很：让妈妈很着急，害得 should introduce a clearly harmful consequence; for making someone worried, 让 is the natural verb.
> - 成为跟他们很像：变得跟他们很像，成为 needs a noun phrase after it, while 变得 can be followed by an adjective phrase such as 跟他们很像.
> 
> **Vocabulary Suggestions**
> - 搬家 -- “就又搬家”：搬到别的地方, this is a longer everyday phrase that makes the destination change more explicit；换地方住, a simple, casual way to describe moving home
> - 一般 -- “一般人”：大多数人, this means “most people” and is more specific than a general group；很多人, a very common and simple alternative
> - 卖东西 -- “卖东西的人”：商人, this is a common noun for people who sell goods, especially in a market；做生意的人, this highlights that selling is their job or business
> - 玩儿 -- “跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿”：一起玩, this emphasizes doing an activity together；学着他们做生意, this would specifically show that Mengzi is copying what the sellers do
> - 着急 -- “妈妈着急得很”：担心, this focuses on the mother’s worry about Mengzi’s future；不放心, this means she does not feel reassured about the situation
> - 恐怕 -- “她恐怕儿子会”：担心, this is a more direct and common word for being worried；怕, this is shorter and more conversational
> 
> **Coherence & Expressiveness**
> - Coherence (Temporality): 住一个月就又搬家：住了一个月，就又搬家了，Use 了 for the completed period of living there and for the completed moving action; this is a series of completed events.
> - Coherence (Temporality): 告诉了孟子：告诉孟子，Use the zero particle with a saying verb that directly introduces quoted speech.
> - Expressiveness: After the move, add one small detail about the busy market environment, such as “每天街上有很多商人卖东西，热闹极了。”
> - Expressiveness: Show Mengzi’s action more clearly with a detail, such as “孟子学着商人收钱，还跟他们讨价还价。”
> - Expressiveness: Make the mother’s feeling stronger before her speech, such as “孟母看到以后很担心，说：‘他们不是你的朋友。’”
> 
> **Encouragement**
> - You clearly show the mother’s concern and include direct speech, which helps the scene feel more personal. Your use of 又 also successfully shows that this is another move.
