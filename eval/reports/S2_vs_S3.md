> - 装祭拜：装作祭拜，The verb 装作 is needed before a verb phrase to express pretending to do an action.
> - 跟妈妈搬家：跟妈妈搬了家，Use 了 after the verb to show that this one-time move was completed in the past.
> 
> **Vocabulary Suggestions**
> - 小 -- “孟子小的时候”：小时候，a common shorter phrase for someone’s childhood；年纪小，this emphasizes that the person was young in age.
> - 跟 -- “跟朋友玩”：和，an equally common alternative meaning “with”；跟着，this means following someone, so it is useful when the focus is on movement rather than being together.
> - 喜欢 -- “喜欢玩”：爱，this expresses a stronger liking；想，this focuses on wanting to do something at a particular time.
> - 玩 -- “跟朋友玩”：玩儿，a common spoken-style version of the same verb；玩耍，a slightly more written-style word for children playing.
> - 墓地 -- “在墓地里”：坟地，a common everyday alternative with a similar meaning；墓园，this can suggest a more organized cemetery or burial park.
> - 搬家 -- “跟妈妈搬家”：搬到别的地方，this makes the change of location more explicit；换地方住，a more casual way to describe moving home.
> - 认为 -- “妈妈认为”：觉得，a more conversational word for expressing an opinion；以为，this often suggests that the belief may later turn out to be mistaken.
> 
> **Encouragement**
> You used time expressions clearly to organize the scene, especially “孟子五岁的时候.” The contrast pattern “不是因为……而是因为……” is also used very well.

### Scene 1

> **Grammar Corrections**
> - 决定了他们会搬家找更好的地方：决定搬家，找更好的地方，A mental-state verb such as “决定” does not take “了” here; when the same people will carry out both actions, the following verb phrases can be stated directly.
> - 住在这儿教育不太好：这儿的教育不太好，Use “Place + 的 + noun” when describing something that belongs to or is available in a place.
>

> - 幸福 -- “童年不会幸福”：快乐，This is more common for describing a child’s daily feelings；开心，This is a casual word for feeling happy.
> - 望子成龙 -- “因为她望子成龙”：希望儿子有出息，This explains the idiom in simple everyday language；希望儿子以后很好，This is a very simple way to express a similar hope.

#### S3 (after)

> **Grammar Corrections**
> - 不合适的地方让孩子玩：不适合孩子玩儿的地方，Use the verb pattern “适合 + person + verb” to say that a place is suitable or unsuitable for someone to do an activity.
> - 她决定了他们会搬家找更好的地方：她决定带着孟子搬家，找更好的地方，A mental-state verb such as “决定” does not take “了” here, and the decision should be followed by the action she decides to do rather than “他们会”.

>

> - 玩儿 -- "去了墓地玩儿"：玩, the standard written form without the northern-style ending；玩耍, a common alternative that focuses on the activity of playing
> - 觉得 -- "他妈妈觉得这是不合适的地方"：认为, a slightly more formal word for having an opinion；想, a simpler, everyday word for what someone thinks
> - 恐怕 -- "她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福"：担心, more directly expresses worry；害怕, expresses a stronger feeling of fear
> - 幸福 -- "童年不会幸福"：快乐, focuses more on being happy and joyful；开心, a very common, casual word for feeling happy
> - 一直 -- "她会一直搬家"：不断, emphasizes that something happens again and again；总是, an everyday word for something that happens frequently

### Scene 2

#### S2 (before)

> **Grammar Corrections**
> - 他们住才一月就又搬家：他们才住了一个月，就又搬家了，Place the duration after the verb and use 了 to show that the one-month stay was completed; use the measure word 个 with 月 in this expression.

> - 恐怕 -- “她恐怕儿子会”：担心，This directly expresses worry and is especially natural before an unwanted possibility；怕，This is a simpler, more everyday word for fear or concern.
> - 不好的人 -- “而是不好的人”：坏人，This is a common, direct word for a bad person；不太好的人，This is softer and less strong than 坏人.
>

> **Grammar Corrections**
> - 他们住才一月就又搬家。：他们才住了一个月，就又搬家了。，For a duration, place 才 before the verb and use 了 after the completed action. Use 一个月 for a length of time rather than 一月, which can also mean January.

> - 告诉 -- “她告诉了孟子”：对……说，this is especially natural before direct speech；说明，this means to explain or make something clear.
> - 朋友 -- “他们不是朋友”：伙伴，this means companions and can sound a little less personal；好朋友，this emphasizes a close friendship.
> - 不好 -- “不好的人”：坏，this is more direct and stronger when describing someone’s character；不太好，this gives a softer, less severe tone.
> 
> **Encouragement**
> - You used clear cause-and-effect reasoning with 因为, and you also included direct speech to show the mother’s response. Your sentences communicate the characters’ feelings and actions clearly.
