### Scene 2

> 他们住才一月就又搬家。古代的时候，一般人不喜欢卖东西的人。但是孟子老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿，害得妈妈着急得很，因为她恐怕儿子会成为跟他们很像。她告诉了孟子：他们不是朋友，而是不好的人。

#### S5-v2 (before)

> **Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections**
> - 住才一月：才住了一个月，Place the adverbial 才 before the verb, use the duration form “number + measure word + 月,” and use 了 for the completed stay.
> - 古代的时候：在古代，This is the standard time expression for a historical setting.
> - 老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿：常常跟这些卖东西的人玩儿，Do not use two frequency adverbs together when both express repeated action; keep one.
> - 害得妈妈着急得很：让妈妈很着急，害得 requires a clearly harmful consequence, while being worried is better expressed with 让.
> - 成为跟他们很像：变得跟他们很像，成为 is followed by an identity, role, or noun phrase; use 变得 before an adjective phrase describing a change of state.
> 
> **Vocabulary Suggestions**
> - 卖东西的人 -- “这些卖东西的人”：商人，A more precise noun for people whose work is buying and selling goods in this city setting.
> 
> **Coherence & Expressiveness**
> - Coherence (Temporality): 搬家：搬家了，Use 了 for the completed move in this past narrative event.
> - Coherence (Temporality): 告诉了孟子：告诉孟子，Use the zero particle with a saying verb that introduces direct speech.
> - Expressiveness: Before the mother speaks, add a brief inner thought to show why she is worried: “妈妈心想：‘孟子不能总是跟他们学做生意。’”
> - Expressiveness: Make the market setting more vivid when introducing the sellers: “街上从早到晚都很热闹，商人一直在卖各种东西。”
> - Expressiveness: Give the mother a more emotional line of speech: “她着急地说：‘孟子，你要好好学习，不要只想着玩儿。’”
> 
> **Encouragement**
> - You clearly show the mother’s concern and use a direct quotation to make the scene feel more like a story.
> - The contrast introduced with 但是 helps show that Mengzi behaves differently from other people.

#### S6 (after)

> **Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections**
> - 他们住才一月：他们才住了一个月，The time adverbial with 才 comes before the verb, and a completed period of living needs 了; use the measure word 个 with 月.
> - 古代的时候：在古代，Use 在古代 for this broad historical time setting; 古代的时候 is not the natural form here.
> - 老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿：老是跟这些卖东西的人玩儿，老是 and 常常 both mark a repeated habit, so using both together is redundant.

>

> - Coherence (Conjunction): 他们才住了一个月又搬家：他们才住了一个月，就又搬家了，Use 就 after 才住了一个月 to show the sequence and unexpected quick change.

> - Expressiveness: Add the mother’s internal reaction before her speech, such as “妈妈看见以后，心里更着急了。”
