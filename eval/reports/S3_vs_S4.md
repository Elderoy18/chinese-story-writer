### Scene 1

> 孟子小的时候，从早到晚都跟朋友玩。孟子和他的朋友喜欢玩在墓地里，装祭拜。孟子五岁的时候，跟妈妈搬家。他们搬家不是因为孟子玩得太多，而是因为妈妈认为他们的家离墓地太近。

#### S3 (before)

> **Grammar Corrections**
> - 玩在墓地里：在墓地里玩，A location phrase normally comes before the verb it modifies.
> - 装祭拜：假装祭拜，The verb “装” cannot directly take an action verb as its object here; “假装” can be followed by an action to mean pretending to do it.
> 
> **Vocabulary Suggestions**
> - 从早到晚 -- “从早到晚都跟朋友玩”：整天，This is a common, shorter everyday word for doing something throughout the day.
> - 朋友 -- “跟朋友玩”：小伙伴，This is a warm, child-friendly word for friends of a similar age.
> - 喜欢 -- “喜欢玩”：爱，This can show a slightly stronger feeling of liking in everyday speech.
> - 妈妈 -- “跟妈妈搬家”：母亲，This is a more formal word, suitable for narrative writing.
> - 认为 -- “妈妈认为”：觉得，This is a common, more conversational way to express a personal opinion.
> - 太近 -- “离墓地太近”：很近，This means simply “very close,” while the original emphasizes that the distance is excessively close.
> 
> **Encouragement**
> You used clear time expressions and connected the final sentence well with “不是……而是……”. Your cause-and-effect explanation about the move is also easy to understand.

#### S4 (after)

> **Grammar Corrections**
> - 玩在墓地里：在墓地里玩，A location phrase with 在 should normally come before the verb it modifies.
> - 装祭拜：假装祭拜，假装 is the verb used for pretending to do an action; 装 cannot directly introduce the action 祭拜 in this structure.

> **Vocabulary Suggestions**
> - 从早到晚 -- “从早到晚都跟朋友玩”：整天，This is a common, shorter way to say “all day,” with less emphasis on the start and end times.
> - 跟 -- “跟朋友玩”：和，This is an equally common connector meaning “with”; it has a slightly more neutral tone.
> - 朋友 -- “孟子和他的朋友”：小伙伴，This is a warm, child-focused word for friends and fits a young child’s activities.

> **Coherence & Expressiveness**
> - Coherence (Reference): 孟子小的时候，从早到晚都跟朋友玩。：有一个小男孩叫孟子。他小的时候，从早到晚都跟朋友玩。On first mention, introduce a character with a full noun phrase and name; after that, use the pronoun 他 for the nearby re-occurrence.
> - Coherence (Reference): 孟子和他的朋友喜欢在墓地里玩：他和他的朋友喜欢在墓地里玩，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when no competing male character makes the reference unclear.
> - Coherence (Temporality): 搬家：搬家了，Use 了 for the completed move, since this sentence recounts a finished event in the past.
> - Expressiveness: After “在墓地里玩,” add Mengzi’s feeling about what he sees, for example: “他觉得别人祭拜的样子很有趣。”
> - Expressiveness: At “假装祭拜,” add a simple action detail, for example: “他和朋友一边笑，一边假装祭拜。”
> - Expressiveness: When the mother decides to move, add her concern in quoted speech, for example: “妈妈说：‘这儿离墓地太近了，我们搬家吧。’”
> 
> **Encouragement**
> - You clearly show the reason for the move with the accurate contrast pattern “不是……而是……”. You also use time expressions such as “孟子小的时候” and “孟子五岁的时候” to organize the scene clearly.

### Scene 2

> **Coherence & Expressiveness**
> - Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟母搬到子思老师的家：他们搬到子思老师的家，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the two people were just mentioned and no other pair competes as the referent.

> - Expressiveness: After the move, add a simple description of the new learning environment: “子思老师的家很安静，桌子上有很多书。”
> - Expressiveness: Give 孟母 a short line of speech after seeing him study: “孟母说：‘在这儿读书很好。’”

> 有一天孟子去了墓地玩儿，但是他妈妈觉得这是不合适的地方让孩子玩，所以她决定了他们会搬家找更好的地方。她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福，而且住在这儿教育不太好。因为她望子成龙，所以她会一直搬家才能找到质量好的教育。然后他们搬到了离城市更近的地方。

#### S3 (before)

> **Grammar Corrections**
> - 不合适的地方让孩子玩：不适合孩子玩儿的地方，Use the verb pattern “适合 + person + verb” to say that a place is suitable or unsuitable for someone to do an activity.
> - 她决定了他们会搬家找更好的地方：她决定带着孟子搬家，找更好的地方，A mental-state verb such as “决定” does not take “了” here, and the decision should be followed by the action she decides to do rather than “他们会”.
> - 住在这儿教育不太好：这儿的教育不太好，Use “的” to make the place phrase modify the noun “教育”; “住在这儿” cannot directly describe “教育”.
> 
> **Vocabulary Suggestions**
> - 有一天 -- "有一天孟子去了墓地玩儿"：一天, a shorter and equally common way to introduce the time of an event；某一天, slightly more specific and neutral in tone
> - 玩儿 -- "去了墓地玩儿"：玩, the standard written form without the northern-style ending；玩耍, a common alternative that focuses on the activity of playing
> - 觉得 -- "他妈妈觉得这是不合适的地方"：认为, a slightly more formal word for having an opinion；想, a simpler, everyday word for what someone thinks
> - 恐怕 -- "她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福"：担心, more directly expresses worry；害怕, expresses a stronger feeling of fear
> - 幸福 -- "童年不会幸福"：快乐, focuses more on being happy and joyful；开心, a very common, casual word for feeling happy
> - 一直 -- "她会一直搬家"：不断, emphasizes that something happens again and again；总是, an everyday word for something that happens frequently
> - 更近 -- "离城市更近的地方"：附近, means “nearby” and can describe the general area；不远, means that the distance is not far
> 
> **Encouragement**
> You used several useful story connectors, including “但是”, “所以”, “因为”, and “然后”, to show clear relationships between ideas. You also used the idiom “望子成龙” appropriately to explain the mother’s hopes for her child.

#### S4 (after)

> **Grammar Corrections**
> - 不合适的地方让孩子玩：不适合孩子玩的地方，Use the pattern “适合 + person + verb phrase” to describe whether a place suits someone’s activity; the original word order does not form a natural modifier for “地方.”
> - 会一直搬家才能找到：得一直搬家，才能找到，“才能” introduces a necessary condition, so “得” appropriately expresses that this action is necessary before the result is possible.
> - 住在这儿教育不太好：这儿的教育不太好，Use “的” to link the location to the noun it describes: the education in this place.
> 
> **Vocabulary Suggestions**
> - 墓地 -- “去了墓地玩儿”：墓园，A common, slightly more neutral word for a cemetery；坟地，A more everyday word that focuses on graves.
> - 玩儿 -- “去了墓地玩儿”：玩，A neutral written form without the conversational ending；玩耍，A slightly more descriptive word for children’s play.
> - 幸福 -- “童年不会幸福”：快乐，A very common word focusing more directly on feeling happy；开心，A casual everyday word for being happy.
> - 童年 -- “儿子的童年”：小时候，A more conversational way to refer to the period when a child was young；成长，This focuses on the child’s process of growing up rather than only early childhood.

> - Expressiveness: At the point where Mengzi goes to the cemetery, add one concrete detail about what he sees: “孟子看到很多人拿着花来墓地。”
> - Expressiveness: When the mother thinks about the cemetery, show her feeling more clearly: “孟子的妈妈很担心，也有一点儿着急。”

### Scene 2

> 他们住才一月就又搬家。古代的时候，一般人不喜欢卖东西的人。但是孟子老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿，害得妈妈着急得很，因为她恐怕儿子会成为跟他们很像。她告诉了孟子：他们不是朋友，而是不好的人。

#### S3 (before)

> **Grammar Corrections**
> - 他们住才一月就又搬家。：他们才住了一个月，就又搬家了。，For a duration, place 才 before the verb and use 了 after the completed action. Use 一个月 for a length of time rather than 一月, which can also mean January.
> - 古代的时候：在古代，Use 在古代 for this general historical time expression; 古代的时候 is not the natural form here.
> - 孟子老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿：孟子常常跟这些卖东西的人玩儿，Do not use both 老是 and 常常 to modify the same action; choose one frequency adverb.
> - 害得妈妈着急得很：让妈妈很着急，害得 should introduce a clearly damaging or harmful result. For causing someone to feel worried, use 让.
> - 会成为跟他们很像：会变得跟他们很像，成为 needs a noun phrase expressing an identity or role after it. Use 变得 before an adjective phrase such as 跟他们很像.
> 
> **Vocabulary Suggestions**
> - 一般人 -- “一般人不喜欢卖东西的人”：普通人，this emphasizes ordinary people rather than people in general；大多数人，this emphasizes that most people have this opinion.
> - 喜欢 -- “不喜欢卖东西的人”：爱，this is a stronger feeling of liking；愿意，this means being willing to do something and would fit different sentence patterns.
> - 卖东西 -- “卖东西的人”：做买卖，this refers more broadly to doing business；卖货，this focuses specifically on selling goods.
> - 告诉 -- “她告诉了孟子”：对……说，this is especially natural before direct speech；说明，this means to explain or make something clear.
> - 朋友 -- “他们不是朋友”：伙伴，this means companions and can sound a little less personal；好朋友，this emphasizes a close friendship.
> - 不好 -- “不好的人”：坏，this is more direct and stronger when describing someone’s character；不太好，this gives a softer, less severe tone.
> 
> **Encouragement**
> - You used clear cause-and-effect reasoning with 因为, and you also included direct speech to show the mother’s response. Your sentences communicate the characters’ feelings and actions clearly.

#### S4 (after)

> **Grammar Corrections**
> - 他们住才一月：他们才住一个月，The adverb 才 comes before the verb phrase, and a duration normally uses the measure word 个.
> - 古代的时候：在古代，This time expression is not natural here; use 在古代 or 古代 for a general historical setting.
> - 老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿：常常跟这些卖东西的人玩儿，Do not use two frequency adverbs, 老是 and 常常, to modify the same action in this way.

> - 成为跟他们很像：变得跟他们很像，成为 needs a noun phrase after it, while 变得 can be followed by an adjective phrase such as 跟他们很像.
> 
> **Vocabulary Suggestions**
> - 搬家 -- “就又搬家”：搬到别的地方, this is a longer everyday phrase that makes the destination change more explicit；换地方住, a simple, casual way to describe moving home
