## Scene 1

> 很久以前，古代的人很不知道什么植物能吃，也什么植物不能吃。所以很多人常常吃不安全的植物，吃不安全的植物对你的身体有坏处，很麻烦的情况。但是一个人，神农，要解决这个问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很不知道：不知道，The degree adverb 很 normally modifies a gradable adjective, not the mental-state verb 不知道.
- 也什么植物不能吃：也不知道什么植物不能吃，After 也, use a verb phrase; repeating 知道 makes the two parallel clauses complete.
- 吃不安全的植物对你的身体有坏处，很麻烦的情况：吃不安全的植物对身体有坏处，这是一个很麻烦的情况，The final noun phrase needs a subject and a copula structure; this correction clearly names the whole situation.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不安全 -- “很多人常常吃不安全的植物”：有害的，identifies the plants more precisely as plants that can harm people’s health.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 但是一个人，神农，要解决这个问题：但是，有一个叫神农的人要解决这个问题，On first mention, introduce the character with a clear full noun phrase rather than placing the name after a vague noun.
- Expressiveness: After the sentence about harmful plants, add a concrete health result, such as “很多人吃了以后肚子疼，常常生病。”
- Expressiveness: Show Shennong’s purpose through an inner thought or a short quotation, such as “神农想：‘我要找出能吃的植物。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish the main problem—people cannot tell which plants are safe—and give Shennong a meaningful goal. Your use of 所以 also shows a good attempt to connect cause and result.

## Scene 2

> 他又聪明又努力，旅行很远找到很多植物。比方说，灵芝和夹竹桃。他一吃各种各样的植物就知道能不能吃这些植物。然后，神农写他的主意。他喜欢植物的味道，特别是花。神农遇到了一个黄色的花，一吃就觉得不舒服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 旅行很远：走了很远，Use a movement verb to describe covering a long distance; the original verb does not naturally take a distance complement in this meaning.
- 找到很多植物：找到了很多植物，The finding is a completed event in the narrative, so the verb needs 了.
- 写他的主意：把自己的主意写下来了，Use 写下 to show that ideas were recorded; in a completed narrative action, add 了. Because the writer and possessor are the same person, 自己的 makes the relationship clear.
- 遇到了：看到了，A flower is normally something the character sees or discovers, while 遇到 is more suitable for meeting a person or encountering an event.
- 一个黄色的花：一朵黄色的花，Flowers use the measure word 朵, not 个.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 知道 -- “一吃各种各样的植物就知道能不能吃这些植物”：判断出，emphasizes making a conclusion after testing the plants.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农写他的主意：他把自己的主意写下来了，The name was used in the immediately preceding sentence and no other character competes as the referent, so use a pronoun for close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农遇到了一个黄色的花：他看到了一朵黄色的花，The same character remains the clear subject after the previous sentence, so a pronoun is more natural than repeating the name.
- Expressiveness: After the long journey, add a small detail about the setting, such as: “高山上长着很多奇怪的植物。”
- Expressiveness: When the character feels ill, show a clearer physical reaction, such as: “他的肚子疼了起来，脸也白了。”
- Expressiveness: Add an internal thought at the moment of illness, such as: “这朵花是不是有毒？”

### Encouragement
- You used the pattern “一……就……” effectively to show an immediate result after eating a plant.
- You also introduced two important plants and showed that the main character tests them carefully.

## Scene 3

> 发现了夹竹桃有毒。别人看到他，很快给他吃一个灵芝。神农一吃就觉得比较好。然后，他继续写他发现的信息。会告诉他的朋友哪一个植物能不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别人：一个男人，This word refers to other people contrasted with a stated person or group; it cannot introduce an unspecified rescuer in this scene.
- 给他吃一个灵芝：给他吃了一个灵芝，The feeding action is completed, so the main verb needs 了; with this structure, 了 follows the verb 吃.
- 写他发现的信息：写下他发现的信息，Writing down information means recording it for later, while 写 alone does not clearly express that recording meaning here.
- 哪一个植物能不能吃：哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃，The sentence is about explaining the edible and inedible kinds of plants, so use plural reference and state the two alternatives in parallel.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 发现 -- “发现了夹竹桃有毒”：辨认出，emphasizes recognizing the plant’s poisonous nature after observing or trying it.
- 比较好 -- “一吃就觉得比较好”：舒服些，specifies that his physical discomfort became somewhat less severe.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the character eats the medicine, add a bodily reaction to show recovery more vividly: “他的肚子慢慢不疼了。”
- Expressiveness: When introducing the rescuer, add one clear visual detail: “一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人看到他。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where he plans to tell his friends, add a short line of speech: “这种植物有毒，不能吃！”

### Encouragement
You keep the main character’s actions easy to follow, especially the sequence of discovering danger, receiving help, and recording what he learned. You also used the pattern “一……就……” effectively to show an immediate result.
