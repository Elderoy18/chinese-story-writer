## Scene 1

> 古代时候，人不知道他们能吃什么植物，常常一吃就死。但是不知道为什么，没有办法。所以有一天，神农出去试一试很多种植物，告诉人什么植物是安全什么是危险。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代时候：在古代，use the standard time expression for a historical setting; the phrase with “古代时候” is not natural.
- 人：人们，this refers to a concrete group of people in the story, so use the plural noun rather than the generic noun.
- 他们：删除，after “人们不知道,” the possessor is already clear, so the pronoun is redundant.
- 试一试很多种植物：尝试各种植物，“试一试” describes a brief trial of one action, while “尝试各种植物” naturally expresses testing a range of plants.
- 什么植物是安全什么是危险：什么植物是安全的，什么植物是危险的，when an adjective follows “是” in this structure, use “的”; repeating “什么植物” also makes the two categories parallel and clear.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：一个叫神农的人，this is the character’s first mention, so introduce him with a full noun phrase rather than assuming the reader already knows who he is.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉：告诉了，this is the last action in a completed series of actions, so it takes “了”; earlier verbs in the series do not need it.
- Expressiveness: After “常常一吃就死,” add the people’s fear to show the consequence more vividly: “人们非常害怕，不知道该怎么办。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where Shennong begins his work, add his purpose or determination: “神农想：我要找出可以吃的植物。”
- Expressiveness: Add one concrete action to show how he tested plants: “他看到一种植物，就摘一点儿来尝。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the problem that motivates Shennong’s actions, and your use of “一吃就” effectively expresses an immediate result.

## Scene 2

> 发现有的植物对身体非常好，特别是茶，让肚子感觉又暖和又清楚。他告诉朋友：“你吃哪种植物，这就要看你的口味。比方说，你爱吃甜的，可能喜欢糖。”他最喜欢吃花，除了夹竹桃以外，什么都花想吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 清楚：舒服，This word describes clarity of thoughts or information, not a physical feeling in the stomach; this adjective correctly describes bodily comfort.
- 什么都花想吃：什么花都想吃，The noun being referred to must come before the distributive pattern with “都”.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 非常好 -- “有的植物对身体非常好”：非常有益，specifies that the plants benefit health rather than being generally good；很有好处，clearly states their positive effect on the body
- 甜的 -- “你爱吃甜的”：甜食，identifies the things being eaten as sweet foods rather than using a general nominalized description

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 这就要看你的口味：这就要看你的口味了，The final “了” is required as part of this fixed expression meaning that the answer depends on a condition; it does not mark a completed action.
- Expressiveness: After the tea is introduced, add one sensory detail about its taste or warmth, such as “茶水喝起来有一点香，肚子也暖暖的。”
- Expressiveness: Show the character’s satisfaction with a short thought after discovering tea, such as “他想：原来这种植物不但好喝，对身体也有好处！”

### Encouragement
You used a clear example with tea and included direct speech, which makes the scene more lively. The contrast between the dangerous flower and other flowers also shows that you are connecting this scene to the earlier events.

## Scene 3

> 他发现这个口味，一百岁时候，不小心吃了毒的花。一吃就开始死。但是生病时候告诉人什么植物是药，什么植物是毒。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一百岁时候：一百岁的时候，The structure ……的时候 needs 的 after a multi-syllable expression such as an age.
- 毒的花：有毒的花，Use 有毒的 to describe a flower that is poisonous; 毒的 does not fit this noun-modifying use.
- 生病时候：生病的时候，The structure ……的时候 needs 的 after the verb phrase.
- 什么植物是毒：什么植物有毒，Use 有毒 to state that a plant is poisonous; 是毒 is not the natural predicate here.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不小心 -- “不小心吃了毒的花”：误食了，specifically means eating something by mistake, which is especially useful for accidentally eating a poisonous flower.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 告诉人：告诉人们，Use 人们 for a concrete group of people; 人 by itself is too generic in this context.
- Expressiveness: At the poisonous-flower moment, add one visual detail: “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花。”
- Expressiveness: After the flower is eaten, show the character’s physical feeling and worry: “他肚子疼得不得了，心想：我会死吗？”

### Encouragement
You used the pattern 一……就…… effectively to show that the bad result happened immediately after eating the flower. Your scene also clearly contrasts plants that can be medicine with plants that are poisonous.
