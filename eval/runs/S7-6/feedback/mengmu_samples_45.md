## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子。他们住在墓地的旁边，所以孟子老是祭拜。孟子不是祭拜，就是跟朋友聊天。他很喜欢他的城市，但是城市有才两个学校，都很小。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 already expresses a time period, so adding 的时候 is redundant.
- 城市有才两个学校：城市才有两个学校，才 must come before the verb 有 to limit the number of schools.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 小 -- “都很小”：规模都不大，specifies that the schools are limited in scale rather than physically small.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子不是祭拜，就是跟朋友聊天：他不是祭拜，就是跟朋友聊天，孟子 was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other male character competes as the subject, so use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Reference): 他的城市：自己的城市，when the subject and the possessor are the same person, 自己的 makes that relationship clear.
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery setting, add a concrete action showing what Mengzi copies: “孟子学着大人弯腰祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: At “跟朋友聊天,” add a small feeling or spoken line to show why he enjoys the place: “他觉得这里很有趣。”
- Expressiveness: After “都很小,” add a detail about the city’s atmosphere to make the contrast clearer: “街上每天都有很多商人卖东西。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish the setting and introduce Mengzi and his mother at the beginning of the scene.
- The pattern “不是……就是……” works well to show Mengzi’s repeated daily activities.

## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈不是不喜欢他们的城市，而是想孟子有很好的教育。他们搬家到有很多学校的城市。他们从早到晚走路，孟子一直抱怨。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 想孟子有很好的教育：想让孟子受到很好的教育，When 想 expresses a wish for another person, adding 让 clearly introduces that person and avoids the meaning “think that he has.”
- 搬家到：搬到了，Use 搬到 before a destination; add 了 because the move to the city is a completed event.
- 从早到晚走路：从早到晚走了一整天的路，For one completed journey, use 了 and a duration phrase such as 一整天的路.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他们搬到了有很多学校的城市：所以，他们搬到了有很多学校的城市，The mother’s wish for better education is the cause of the move, so add 所以 to show the cause-and-effect connection.
- Expressiveness: At the walking sentence, add a physical detail to show that the journey is difficult: “他们走得脚都疼了。”
- Expressiveness: After the complaint, add a short line of speech to show Mengzi’s feelings: “孟子说：‘妈妈，我们什么时候才能到？’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the mother’s motivation for moving and Mengzi’s unhappy reaction during the journey. The contrast structure with 不是……而是…… helps explain her reason effectively.

## Scene 3

> 他难怪抱怨，他想他的朋友，因为他不要离开他的城市。他的妈妈找到孟子一个老师。跟老师孟子学最重要的文学，所以他成为哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他难怪抱怨：难怪他抱怨，原来，“难怪” means “no wonder” and should introduce the observation; “原来” then introduces the reason that follows.
- 跟老师孟子学最重要的文学：孟子跟老师学最重要的文学，The learner’s name should come before the phrase beginning with “跟老师”; otherwise, “老师孟子” can be read as a teacher named Mengzi rather than Mengzi studying with a teacher.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 他的妈妈找到孟子一个老师：他的妈妈给孟子找到了一个老师，This is a completed event in the narrative, so “找到了” needs “了”; “给” marks Mengzi as the person for whom the teacher was found.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为哲学家：成为了哲学家，Becoming something is a completed change of state in this narrative, so use “了.”
- Expressiveness: After the complaint, add a small action or a line of speech to show Mengzi’s feelings, such as “孟子叹了一口气，说：‘我想我的朋友。’”
- Expressiveness: When his mother finds a teacher, show her purpose with an internal thought, such as “妈妈心想：‘我要让孟子好好学习。’”
- Expressiveness: Add an evaluative detail to his later achievement, such as “后来，孟子成为了有名的哲学家。”

### Encouragement
You clearly connect Mengzi’s complaint with his reluctance to leave his city. You also give the scene a positive direction by showing that his mother helps him begin learning.
