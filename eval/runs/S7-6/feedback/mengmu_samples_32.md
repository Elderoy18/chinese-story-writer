## Scene 1

> 有一天孟子去了墓地玩儿，但是他妈妈觉得这是不合适的地方让孩子玩，所以她决定了他们会搬家找更好的地方。她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福，而且住在这儿教育不太好。因为她望子成龙，所以她会一直搬家才能找到质量好的教育。然后他们搬到了离城市更近的地方。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不合适的地方让孩子玩：不适合孩子玩的地方，Use the pattern “适合 + person + activity” to describe a place that suits a child’s activity; the original phrase does not connect the place naturally to the activity.
- 他们会搬家找更好的地方：搬家，找更好的地方，After “决定,” use the action phrase directly; do not repeat the people who will carry out the decision as a future clause.
- 恐怕：担心，“恐怕” means “I am afraid that” and cannot follow “她” to describe her personal worry; “担心” expresses that she is worried.
- 住在这儿教育不太好：这儿的教育不太好，Use a location possessive phrase to show that the education in this place is not good; the original wording does not clearly connect the education to the place.
- 会一直搬家才能：得一直搬家，才能，“才能” introduces a necessary condition, so “得” is needed to express that moving is necessary; add a comma before the result.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好 -- “找更好的地方”：教育环境更好的地方，specifies that the place is better because its educational environment is better for the child；更适合孩子成长的地方，makes clear how the place is better while matching the mother’s concern.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 决定了：决定，“决定” is a mental-state verb, so it takes the zero particle rather than “了”; the focus is the decision itself, not its completion.
- Expressiveness: At the cemetery scene, add a specific action showing what Mengzi finds interesting: “孟子一边笑，一边学着别人祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: When the mother worries, add a short inner thought to show her feelings: “孟母心想：‘住在这儿会影响孩子的学习。’”
- Expressiveness: After the move closer to the city, describe the new environment: “街上有很多商人卖东西，很热闹。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the mother’s reason for moving by using the cause-and-effect connector “因为……所以……”. You also use completed-action marking well in “搬到了,” which makes the move feel like a finished event.

## Scene 2

> 他们住才一月就又搬家。古代的时候，一般人不喜欢卖东西的人。但是孟子老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿，害得妈妈着急得很，因为她恐怕儿子会成为跟他们很像。她告诉了孟子：他们不是朋友，而是不好的人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，this expression redundantly combines two time expressions; use the shorter time phrase.
- 老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿：老是跟这些卖东西的人玩儿，using both frequency adverbs in the same verb phrase is redundant; keep one.
- 害得妈妈着急得很：让妈妈很着急，害得 must introduce a clearly harmful result, while being worried is better expressed with 让.
- 她恐怕儿子会成为跟他们很像：她担心儿子会变得跟他们很像，恐怕 cannot follow a subject other than 我; use 担心 for a mother’s fear, and use 变得 for becoming similar in a quality.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 卖东西的人 -- “这些卖东西的人”：商贩，specifies people who sell goods in a market-like setting and is more concise.
- 不好 -- “不好的人”：品行不好，makes clear that the problem is the people’s behaviour or character.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们住才一月就又搬家：他们才住了一个月，就又搬家了，place 才 before the completed duration, use a measure word in 一个月, and add 了 to show that both living there and moving happened.
- Coherence (Temporality): 她告诉了孟子：她告诉孟子：a speaking verb that introduces quoted speech normally takes the zero particle, not 了.
- Expressiveness: After the mother becomes worried, add a short internal thought to show her concern, such as “她心想：这样下去可不行。”
- Expressiveness: Describe what Mengzi does with the sellers so the reader can picture the behaviour, such as “他学着他们收钱，还大声叫卖。”
- Expressiveness: Make the mother’s warning more vivid with a feeling word before her dialogue, such as “妈妈皱着眉头说：‘他们不是朋友。’”

### Encouragement
You used 又 appropriately to show that the family moved again, and the dialogue gives the mother a clear voice in the scene. The contrast between ordinary people and Mengzi’s behaviour is also easy to understand.

## Scene 3

> 你不是当数学家，而是当哲学家，不能当卖东西的人。所以他们又搬家。在他们第三个家，孟子学了怎么祭拜。妈妈感觉这个地方适合他们，告诉了孟子：我们找到了最后的家。在这儿妈妈让儿子从早到晚学习，他难怪成为有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在他们第三个家：在他们的第三个家里，A possessive modifier before this ordinary noun needs 的, and 里 completes the location phrase after 在.
- 难怪：后来，难怪 introduces a conclusion that should be supported by an explanation with 原来; it cannot mean “later.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 又搬家：又搬家了，This move has already happened, so the completed action needs 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉了孟子：告诉孟子，A speaking verb that introduces quoted speech takes the zero particle, not 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为有名的哲学家：成为了有名的哲学家，This is a completed change of state in the retrospective ending, so it needs 了.
- Expressiveness: After the third-home sentence, add a concrete setting detail such as “他们的新家在学校旁边”。 This helps the reader picture why the new environment supports study.
- Expressiveness: At the mother’s realization, add a short line of thought or speech such as “这儿的环境很适合孩子”。 This shows her feelings more directly.
- Expressiveness: Make the study scene more vivid with a detail such as “孟子每天认真地看书学习”。 This shows how he behaves rather than only stating the routine.

### Encouragement
You use a clear contrast at the beginning and include a specific all-day time expression for the study routine. The ending also connects Mengzi’s learning to his later achievement.
