## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男人觉得植物很有意思。他叫神农。有的植物吃了以后他的肚子疼死了，可是有的吃了以后很舒服。他找很多植物。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，this phrase redundantly combines a time period with another “time of” expression.

### Vocabulary Suggestions

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一个男人觉得植物很有意思。他叫神农。：有一个叫神农的男人觉得植物很有意思。，On first mention, introduce the character with both the name and a full noun phrase instead of naming him only in the following sentence.
- Expressiveness: After the stomach-pain sentence, add the character’s physical reaction, such as “他在地上躺着，难受得不得了。” This shows how serious the pain feels.
- Expressiveness: At the moment of pain, add an inner question such as “他想：我会死吗？” This lets the reader hear the character’s fear.
- Expressiveness: When introducing one plant, add a simple visual detail such as “那儿开着黄色的像星星一样的花。” This makes the plants more vivid.

### Encouragement
You clearly show the contrast between plants that cause pain and plants that make Shennong feel well. The use of “吃了以后” also helps the reader understand the result of eating each kind of plant.

## Scene 2

> 凉山很大的山，这也找植物。一天，他找到黄色的花。他听说你跟黄色的花做茶很有用。比方说，你的头发疼死，这冰茶是药。他一喝茶就很不舒服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 凉山很大的山：凉山是一座很大的山，A noun used as the predicate needs the copula and an appropriate measure word here.
- 找到黄色的花：找到了黄色的花，This is a completed discovery in a past event, so the verb needs 了.
- 跟黄色的花做茶：用黄色的花做茶，Use 用 to introduce the material used to make something; 跟 means “with” in the sense of accompaniment and does not mark material.
- 头发疼死：肚子疼死，Hair itself cannot feel pain; 肚子 names a body part that can hurt and fits the physical-discomfort context.
- 这冰茶：这杯茶，A demonstrative used with a countable noun needs a measure word; 杯 is the suitable measure word for tea.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 这也找植物：神农也在凉山找植物，After the sentence shifts to the location, 这 does not clearly identify the person doing the searching; repeat the established character’s name after this shift.
- Coherence (Reference): 你跟黄色的花做茶：用黄色的花做茶，No listener or second-person character has been introduced, so 你 has no clear referent; an impersonal phrase is clearer here.
- Coherence (Reference): 你的肚子疼死：他的肚子疼死，The narration is about 神农, so the pronoun should refer back to him rather than introduce an unexplained second person.
- Expressiveness: After the flower appears, add a visual detail: “黄色的花又大又漂亮，像星星一样。”
- Expressiveness: When he feels ill, show his physical reaction: “他躺在地上，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Add his worried thought after the discomfort: “他想：我会死吗？”

### Encouragement
- You used a clear time marker to begin the new event, and the 一……就…… pattern effectively shows that the discomfort happened immediately after drinking the tea.

## Scene 3

> 糟糕，这黄色的花是夹竹桃，特别是毒。正好在这儿，灵芝药的夹竹桃他吃完了，也写一个课文的植物。谢谢神农。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 这黄色的花：这朵黄色的花，A demonstrative before a noun needs an appropriate measure word here.
- 特别是毒：特别有毒，Here the meaning is a high degree of toxicity, so use the adjective phrase with 有毒; 毒 cannot directly follow a degree adverb in this use.
- 灵芝药的夹竹桃：灵芝和夹竹桃，Use 和 to coordinate the two plant nouns; 的 cannot link 药 and 夹竹桃 in this noun phrase.
- 写一个课文的植物：写下了这些植物，When recording plants, 写下 expresses that the information was written down; 了 marks the completed action.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他：神农，After two plant names appear, 他 can be unclear; repeat the character’s name because the last human reference was in an earlier scene.
- Expressiveness: After “糟糕”, add a visual detail about the flower, such as “黄色的花像星星一样，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where 神农 eats the plant, show his physical reaction: “他一吃夹竹桃，肚子就疼了起来。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought to show his fear, for example: “他想：我会不会死？”

### Encouragement
- You clearly named the important plant 夹竹桃 and used 糟糕 to show that the situation is dangerous. You also tried to connect eating the plants with recording what was learned.
