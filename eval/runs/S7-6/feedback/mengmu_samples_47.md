### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 安排 -- “孟子的妈妈安排他的生活”：规划，emphasizes making an overall plan for his life and future.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 这个男孩子：一个男孩子，A first-mentioned character should use an indefinite full noun phrase rather than a demonstrative phrase that presents him as already known.
- Coherence (Reference): 所以孟子的妈妈安排他的生活：所以安排他的生活，When the same subject continues across linked clauses, use zero form instead of repeating the full noun phrase.
- Coherence (Temporality): 常常搬了家：常常搬家，A habitual action uses the zero particle, not completed-action 了.
- Expressiveness: After the mother’s belief about where to live, add a brief inner thought to show her concern, such as: 孟母心想：“住的地方会影响孩子的成长。”
- Expressiveness: At the point about arranging his life, add one concrete example of what she does, such as: 她每天陪孟子读书，还给他安排学习时间。

### Encouragement

## Scene 2

> 因为他们常常搬了家，中国人说，孟母三迁。孟子没有爸爸，所以，他的妈妈更重要。从这儿到那儿，妈妈教孟子很多技能。先她让孟子跟朋友玩儿，然后教他怎么买又便宜又好质量的东西。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 常常搬了家：常常搬家，A habitual action does not take 了 because it is not presented as a completed single event.
- 先她：她先，The subject comes before the time or sequence adverbial 先.
- 又便宜又好质量的东西：又便宜又质量好的东西，To describe an item’s quality, use the modifier phrase 质量好 before 的; 好质量 is not a natural noun phrase here.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 技能 -- “妈妈教孟子很多技能”：生活技能，specifies that these are useful everyday abilities, matching the examples about playing and shopping.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 妈妈教孟子很多技能：她教孟子很多技能，The mother was named in the immediately preceding sentence, so a pronoun is more natural for a close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 然后教他：然后教了他，This is the last action in a sequence introduced by 先 and 然后, so the final verb takes 了 to show the teaching action was completed.
- Expressiveness: After the shopping lesson, add a short line of advice from the mother, such as: “买东西的时候，要看价钱，也要看质量。”
- Expressiveness: Show Mengzi’s feeling while playing with his friends, such as: “孟子跟朋友玩儿的时候，笑得很开心。”
- Expressiveness: Add one concrete shopping detail, such as: “他学着比较两个苹果的价钱。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect the mother’s teaching with Mengzi’s daily activities, and the use of 先 and 然后 gives the scene a helpful sense of order.
