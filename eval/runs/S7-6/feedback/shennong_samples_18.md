## Scene 1

> 神农和他的家人住在古代，所以不知道什么植物能吃。因为他的家人常常生病了，所以神农和他的哥哥一起去旅行找可以吃的植物。他们去很多不一样的地方，比方说高山、山谷和沙漠。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 神农和他的家人住在古代：在古代，神农和他的家人住在一起，An era is a time setting rather than a place where someone can live; place the time phrase at the beginning and use a natural living relationship.
- 常常生病了：常常生病，A habitual action or state uses the zero particle, so it does not take 了.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 找 -- “一起去旅行找可以吃的植物”：探寻，emphasizes an ongoing search for an answer through travel and observation；寻找，emphasizes deliberately looking for the plants.
- 地方 -- “很多不一样的地方”：不同的地形，more exactly names the kinds of places shown by the following examples, such as mountains, valleys, and deserts.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：一位叫神农的老人，On first mention, introduce a character with both a name and a brief identity so the reader knows who the character is.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们去很多不一样的地方：他们去了很多不一样的地方，This travel action is presented as completed in the past, so it needs 了.
- Expressiveness: After the family’s illness, add Shennong’s feeling to show his motivation, for example: “神农很担心他们。”
- Expressiveness: Add one visual detail to make the travel locations easier to imagine, for example: “高山又高又陡，山谷很安静，沙漠一眼望不到边。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought to show Shennong’s determination before the journey, for example: “神农心想：我一定要找到可以吃的植物。”

### Encouragement
You used the cause-and-effect pattern with “因为……所以……” clearly, and the examples of mountains, valleys, and deserts make the journey feel varied.

## Scene 2

> 他们在很漂亮的河找夹竹桃。神农问他的哥哥：“我应该吃这种植物吗？”哥哥说：“这就要看你想要安全还是接近知道了。“神农一吃夹竹桃就生病了。哥哥说：“太麻烦了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在很漂亮的河：在很漂亮的河边，Use 河边 to name the location where the characters are searching; 河 alone does not provide the needed location in this sentence.
- 接近知道：想知道，接近 means “to get close to” and needs a noun target; it cannot mean “to come to know.” 想知道 also makes a parallel choice with 想要安全.
- 。“：。”，Direct speech must end with a closing quotation mark, not an opening quotation mark.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 找 -- “他们在很漂亮的河边找夹竹桃”：寻找, emphasizes the ongoing process of looking for a particular plant

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: At the river setting, add the flowers’ colour and shape to make the place easier to picture: “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花。”
- Expressiveness: When 神农 becomes ill, add a physical feeling and an inner thought: “他躺在地上，肚子疼得不得了，心想：我会死吗？”

### Encouragement
You used dialogue to show the relationship between 神农 and his brother. The 一……就…… pattern also clearly shows that he became ill immediately after eating the plant.

## Scene 3

> 夹竹桃是毒的，我想知道灵芝会帮他。”哥哥给神农灵芝。神农吃完了以后开始痊愈了。神农说：“谢谢哥哥，你帮得真好。灵芝我们可以吃，特别是如果我们已经吃了夹竹桃。“

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 夹竹桃是毒的：夹竹桃是有毒的，毒 is a noun here; 有毒 is the adjective meaning “poisonous.”
- 哥哥给神农灵芝：哥哥给了神农灵芝，this is a completed giving action, so the verb needs 了.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 吃完 -- “神农吃完了以后开始痊愈了”：服下，more precise for taking a medicinal plant and fits the healing context.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the healing begins, add a physical feeling to show the change: “神农觉得肚子没有那么疼了。”
- Expressiveness: Make the brother’s help feel more urgent with a brief action detail: “哥哥赶快把灵芝给了神农。”

### Encouragement
You used dialogue effectively to show both the brother’s help and Shen Nong’s gratitude. The time phrase 吃完了以后 and the completed action 已经吃了 are also used clearly.
