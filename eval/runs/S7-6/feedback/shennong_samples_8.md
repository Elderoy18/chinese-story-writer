## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个人叫神农。一天，神农正在路上走，一看很多人正在吃一些植物。一些人一吃植物就生病了，神农想帮别人。所以想知道哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，Both parts mean “in ancient times,” so using them together is redundant.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 知道 -- “所以想知道哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃”：弄清，emphasizes learning the answer clearly by investigating and distinguishing the plants.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一天，神农：一天，他，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the named character has just been introduced and no other male character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 一看很多人正在吃一些植物。一些人一吃植物就生病了：一看到很多人正在吃一些植物，就发现一些人一吃植物就生病了，The sequence pattern needs “一……就……”; use “看到” because the intended meaning is that he successfully sees the scene.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 神农想帮别人。所以想知道哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃：因为神农想帮别人，所以想知道哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃，Use “因为……所以……” to make the cause-and-effect relationship explicit: his wish to help others causes his desire to learn about the plants.
- Expressiveness: After the people become ill, add Shennong’s concern: “神农看见他们很难受，心里很担心。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short inner thought after he decides to investigate: “他想：‘我一定要弄清楚哪些植物能吃！’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduce Shennong and set up the problem that motivates him to act. You also use “正在” well to show the ongoing actions of walking and eating, and “一吃……就……” effectively shows how quickly the illness happens.

## Scene 2

> 然后，神农开始尝很多植物，比方说他吃过生姜、芥兰和菠菜。一些植物吃真好，特别是黄瓜。但是，一些植物毒得很，特别是夹竹桃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一些植物吃真好：一些植物真好吃，The verb 吃 cannot directly describe a plant’s taste in this pattern; 好吃 functions as the adjective meaning “tasty.”
- 一些植物毒得很：一些植物有毒，毒 cannot take a degree complement such as 得很; use 有毒 to describe a poisonous plant.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 吃过 -- “比方说他吃过生姜、芥兰和菠菜”：尝过，emphasizes sampling plants as part of an investigation rather than simply eating them

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 比方说他吃过：比方说，吃过，Since 神农 was named earlier in the same sentence and no other character competes as the subject, use zero form rather than repeating 他.
- Expressiveness: After mentioning 夹竹桃, add a simple visual detail to make the dangerous plant memorable: “黄色的花像星星一样，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: After the poisonous-plant sentence, add Shennong’s physical reaction to show the danger: “他一吃夹竹桃，肚子就疼了起来。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought after he feels ill to show his worry: “他想：这种花会不会有毒？”

### Encouragement
- You clearly contrast plants that are tasty with plants that are dangerous, and your examples of specific plants make the scene easy to understand. You also use 过 well to show that Shennong has already tried particular plants.

## Scene 3

> 一个人对神农说，麻烦我们可以吃什么植物？神农说，这就要看你想不想生病了。以后，神农告诉别人一些植物能吃，一些植物不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 麻烦我们可以吃什么植物？：请问，我们可以吃什么植物？，“请问” introduces a polite question; “麻烦” normally means troubling someone and needs a structure such as “麻烦你……”.
- 以后：后来，At the beginning of a sentence, “后来” means “afterwards”; “以后” usually means “after a specified event or time”.
- 别人：人们，“别人” distinguishes other people from a person already mentioned, while “人们” refers to people generally.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 说 -- “一个人对神农说”：问，identifies this speech specifically as a question rather than general speaking.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: In the first line of dialogue, add the speaker’s worried feeling to make the question more lively. Example: “一个人着急地问：‘请问，我们可以吃什么植物？’”
- Expressiveness: In Shen Nong’s answer, give one concrete example of a safe plant and a dangerous plant. Example: “神农说：‘灵芝可以吃，夹竹桃有毒，不能吃。’”

### Encouragement
You used dialogue effectively: the person’s question and Shen Nong’s reply make the scene easy to follow. You also clearly stated the important contrast between plants that can and cannot be eaten.
