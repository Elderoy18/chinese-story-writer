## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有很多人不知道什么植物能吃，什么植物不能吃。有很多人吃植物以后就吃坏肚子了，因为他们吃了毒。那个时候，有一个老人叫神农，他什么植物都觉得好吃，不怕生病，不怕毒。他希望别人也可以吃好吃的植物。可是他知道，如果他们还怕吃毒，不会吃他喜欢的植物。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，This time phrase is redundant because both parts express time; use the shorter form.
- 怕吃毒：怕吃到有毒的植物，Use `吃到` when the feared outcome is actually encountering and eating something; `有毒的植物` clearly names the toxic plants.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不知道 -- “有很多人不知道什么植物能吃，什么植物不能吃”：分不清，emphasizes that people could not distinguish edible plants from unsafe ones.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After “吃坏肚子了,” add a physical feeling such as “他们常常肚子疼，难受得不得了。” This makes the consequence more vivid.
- Expressiveness: After introducing 神农, add an internal thought such as “神农想：‘我要找出哪些植物能吃，哪些不能吃。’” This shows his purpose and character.

### Encouragement
You clearly explained the difference between plants that can be eaten and plants that cannot. You also introduced 神农 with both his identity and name, so the reader can follow the scene easily.

## Scene 2

> 所以，他上山下山找植物，吃了很多样子的植物。他是第一人，找到了茶。他把所有的植物写下来了。他一直都吃了，他连毒都能吃。可是他还没吃了一个叫夹竹桃的植物。他找到了那个像星星的植物，觉得很漂亮，马上就吃下了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很多样子的植物：很多种植物，样子 describes an appearance or shape; use 种 when referring to different types of plants.
- 第一人：第一个人，第一人 usually means a leading or outstanding person, while 第一个人 means the first person to do something.
- 一个叫夹竹桃的植物：一种叫夹竹桃的植物，种 is the appropriate measure word when referring to one type or species of plant.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 找 -- “他上山下山找植物”：寻找, emphasizes the ongoing process of looking for plants；探寻, highlights exploration in search of an answer.
- 所有 -- “他把所有的植物写下来了”：每一种, makes clear that he recorded the plants one type at a time.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 一直都吃了：一直都吃，了 marks a completed action, but 一直 describes an ongoing or habitual action, so it takes the zero particle.
- Coherence (Temporality): 还没吃了：还没吃过，还没 states that the action has not happened, so it cannot take completed-action 了; 过 appropriately expresses that he has not had this experience yet.
- Expressiveness: At the tea discovery, add what makes tea useful: “他发现，把茶放在水里，可以做成很好喝的饮料。”
- Expressiveness: When introducing the flower, add a concrete visual detail: “那儿开着黄色的花，像星星一样，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: Before he eats it, add his thought to show his confidence: “他想：这么漂亮的花，一定很好吃！”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show Shennong’s repeated testing of plants, and the sequence from finding tea to finding the star-like plant is easy to follow. Your use of “马上就吃下了” also gives the final action a quick, lively pace.

## Scene 3

> 可是夹竹桃的毒很重，让他肚子很痛。他在地上躺着，觉得他快会死了。可是正好有别人找到他了，给了他一个像伞的植物，叫灵芝。他吃灵芝以后就觉得很好，不再生病了。他测量起来了，像星星的植物是毒，像伞的植物是药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 快会死了：快要死了，Use the imminent marker “快要” for something that is about to happen; “会” is a modal verb and does not fit naturally with “快” in this construction.
- 是毒：有毒，“毒” is a noun, while “有毒” describes the plant as poisonous.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 痛 -- “让他肚子很痛”：疼得厉害，expresses the same level of pain in a more vivid, natural way for a physical symptom.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他：神农，After the other male character appears, “他” could refer to either person; repeat the established name to make clear who eats the plant.
- Coherence (Temporality): 找到他了，给了他：找到他，给了他，In a series of completed actions, only the last verb takes “了”.
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃灵芝以后：吃了灵芝以后，The eating is a completed action before the later result, so it needs “了”.
- Expressiveness: After the stomach pain, add a stronger physical reaction to show how serious it feels, such as “他疼得在地上打滚，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: When the helper arrives, add one visual detail and a quick action, such as “这时，一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人走过来，赶快给神农灵芝。”
- Expressiveness: At the end, add a short line of speech to make the discovery clearer, such as “神农说：‘像星星一样的花有毒，不能吃。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly present a strong cause-and-effect sequence: the poisonous plant causes pain, and the medicine helps the character recover. You also use action details such as lying on the ground and receiving a plant to make the scene easy to follow.
