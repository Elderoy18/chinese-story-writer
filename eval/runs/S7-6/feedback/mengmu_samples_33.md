## Scene 1

> 一天，很久很久以前，孟子和他妈妈住在中国。孟子妈妈的工作是祭拜，孟子不高兴，因为他说我们老是搬家，为什么？他妈妈说，从早到晚，我一直祭拜，很多墓地得我祭拜。所以我们得去很多不同的地方。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很多墓地得我祭拜：我得祭拜很多墓地，The subject must come before the modal verb, and the object follows the main verb.
- 一天，很久很久以前：很久很久以前，A single-day expression does not fit the continuing state expressed by “住”; use the general past-time expression instead.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不高兴 -- “孟子不高兴”：不满，more specifically shows that he is dissatisfied with moving repeatedly.
- 很多不同的地方 -- “我们得去很多不同的地方”：各地，more concisely indicates many places in different areas.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和他妈妈：有一个小男孩叫孟子，他和妈妈，On first mention, introduce the character with a name and a short identifying noun phrase before using a pronoun or family relationship.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为他说我们老是搬家，为什么？：，说：“我们为什么老是搬家？”，“因为” should introduce a cause, but this part is the character’s direct question rather than the cause of his unhappiness; use a speaking verb to introduce the quotation.
- Expressiveness: After “孟子不高兴,” add a small action such as “皱着眉头” to show his feeling instead of only naming it.
- Expressiveness: When describing the mother’s work, add a setting detail such as “墓地旁边” to help the reader picture where she works.
- Expressiveness: Add a reaction from the mother before her explanation, such as “叹了口气,” to make the conversation feel more alive.

### Encouragement
You clearly show why the family needs to move by connecting the mother’s work with visiting many places. The use of direct speech also gives both characters a voice in the scene.

## Scene 2

> 孟子想，难怪我们常常搬家，原来我妈妈很忙。可是常常搬家害得孟子很难找到朋友。每次搬家孟子也觉得新的地方不是太热就是太冷，不是太大就是太小。在哪的地方，他都不高兴。孟子想帮助妈妈祭拜，可是他六岁，所以不能祭拜。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在哪的地方，他都不高兴：在每个地方，他都不高兴，哪 cannot be used with 的 to modify 地方 in this location phrase; use 每个地方 to refer to all of the places after repeated moves.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 忙 -- “原来我妈妈很忙”：忙碌，emphasizes that she is continuously occupied with many duties；工作繁忙，specifies that it is her work schedule that keeps her busy.
- 找到朋友 -- “孟子很难找到朋友”：交到朋友，more precisely describes forming friendships rather than simply locating people.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After “孟子很难找到朋友,” add a small feeling or thought to show why moving is upsetting, such as “每次刚认识一个朋友，就要搬走了。”
- Expressiveness: At “孟子想帮助妈妈祭拜,” add a short line of inner speech that shows his wish, such as “他心想：‘我什么时候才能帮妈妈呢？’”

### Encouragement
- You used the pattern “难怪……，原来……” effectively to connect Mencius’s realization with the reason for the family’s frequent moves.
- The paired “不是……就是……” descriptions clearly show how uncomfortable Mencius feels in each new place.

## Scene 3

> 四年后，孟子开始帮妈妈祭拜。他画画很厉害，他越来越喜欢祭拜，所以他祭拜不是想帮妈妈工作，而是他很喜欢画画。孟子成为很著名的古代画家。今天很多中国人很喜欢孟子。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不是想帮妈妈工作，而是他很喜欢画画：不是为了帮妈妈工作，而是因为喜欢画画，Use 不是……而是…… to contrast the rejected purpose with the actual reason; 为了 marks a purpose, and 因为 introduces a reason.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 开始 -- “孟子开始帮妈妈祭拜”：着手，emphasizes that he begins taking practical action to help.
- 画画很厉害 -- “他画画很厉害”：擅长画画，states more precisely that drawing is a skill he is good at.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 孟子成为很著名的古代画家：孟子成为了很著名的古代画家，This is a completed change in the narrative, so 了 marks the completed result of becoming a painter.
- Expressiveness: After “孟子开始帮妈妈祭拜,” add a brief feeling to show his motivation, such as “孟子觉得终于能帮妈妈了，心里很高兴。”
- Expressiveness: At “他画画很厉害,” give one concrete detail about his drawings, such as “他把墓碑旁边的花和来祭拜的人都画得很像。”
- Expressiveness: Before the final sentence, add a short line of praise from others, such as “人们看了他的画，都说：‘孟子的画真有意思！’”

### Encouragement
You clearly show a time change with “四年后,” and the ending connects Mengzi’s childhood interest in drawing with his later achievement.
