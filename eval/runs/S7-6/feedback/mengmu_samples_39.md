### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，Both parts refer to time, so the original is redundant.
- 人：人们，This refers to the group of people living in that historical period, so the plural noun is needed.
- 看了：看到了，Here the mother notices or perceives the classmates’ influence; 看 means “look,” while 看到 expresses the result of seeing.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子很小的时候：有一个小男孩叫孟子。孟子很小的时候，This is Mengzi’s first appearance, so introduce him clearly as a child before continuing with his actions.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子妈妈搬家了：孟子和妈妈搬家了，The mother was just mentioned, so repeating Mengzi’s name inside the relationship phrase is unnecessary; use the shorter reference 妈妈.
- Coherence (Temporality): 老是对他开玩笑了：老是对他开玩笑，The adverb 老是 describes a repeated habit, so the verb takes the zero particle rather than completed-action 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜：因为他每天只祭拜，所以没有朋友，The intended relationship is cause and effect, so use 因为……所以…… rather than an unfinished 虽然 clause.
- Expressiveness: After the worship routine, add Mengzi’s feeling to show why he keeps doing it, for example: “孟子觉得祭拜很有趣，一边笑一边学着大人祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: At the classmates’ reaction, add a short line of speech to make the teasing more vivid, for example: “同学们笑着说：‘孟子，你又在学祭拜吗？’”
- Expressiveness: Before the move, show the mother’s worry through an inner thought, for example: “孟子的妈妈很着急，心想：‘这样会影响孟子的学习。’”

## Scene 3

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不是学金融，而是学政治：不要学金融，而要学政治，Use this pattern for advice or a recommendation; the original contrast pattern is for correcting or refuting an idea rather than telling someone what they should choose.
- 成为高薪：得到高薪，The word for a high salary is something a person receives, not something a person becomes.

- Coherence (Conjunction): 长大，开始能解决问题：长大以后，开始能解决问题，Use 以后 to show the sequence: first Mengzi grows up, and after that he begins to solve problems.
