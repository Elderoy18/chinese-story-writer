## Scene 1

> 这是古代的故事。孟子小的时候，他的爸爸很早就去世了。从他的家到墓地很近，所以他每天老是从早到晚去墓地跟朋友们玩玩儿。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 玩玩儿：玩儿，The reduplicated verb suggests a brief or casual action, which conflicts with an activity lasting from morning to night.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子小的时候，他的爸爸很早就去世了：有一个小男孩叫孟子；他小的时候，爸爸很早就去世了，This is the character’s first mention, so introduce him with both a description and his name before using a pronoun.
- Coherence (Reference): 他的家：孟子的家，After the father was mentioned, this pronoun could refer to either Mengzi or his father; repeat Mengzi’s name to make the possessor clear.
- Expressiveness: When describing the cemetery, add a small visual detail about the visitors: “每天，孟子看见来墓地祭拜的人，有的人低着头，有的人伤心地哭。”
- Expressiveness: Add Mengzi’s thought or reaction before he plays with his friends: “孟子觉得祭拜很有趣，就学着大人的样子祭拜。”

### Encouragement
You used a clear cause-and-effect connection between the home’s location and Mengzi’s daily activities. The time expression also helps the reader understand how often he went to the cemetery.

## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈觉得在墓地玩儿，害得，所以他们搬家。搬家对孟子有好的影响，他才五岁，可是每天不是写字就是帮妈妈的工作。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在墓地玩儿，害得：孟子在墓地玩儿会带来不好的影响，害得 must be followed by the person affected and a harmful result; it cannot end without that complement.
- 帮妈妈的工作：帮妈妈做事，帮 should be followed by a person plus an action; this wording incorrectly treats 工作 as the object after 妈妈.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好的 -- “搬家对孟子有好的影响”：积极的，specifies that the effect is positive rather than using a general description.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 所以他们搬家：所以他们搬家了，this move is a completed past event in the narrative, so the final action takes 了.
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery activity, add the mother’s feeling to show why she decides to move. For example: “孟子的妈妈很担心。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of the mother’s thoughts before the move. For example: “她想：‘这儿不适合孟子。’”
- Expressiveness: Make the child’s activities more vivid by adding a small detail. For example: “他每天拿着笔写字，还帮妈妈扫地。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show a cause-and-effect relationship with 所以, and the contrast introduced by 可是 fits the idea that the child is very young but already has daily responsibilities.

## Scene 3

> 他很聪明的人，难怪他成为有名的人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他很聪明的人：他是一个很聪明的人，A noun phrase used as the predicate needs the copula “是”; “一个” naturally introduces the description of him as a person.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 有名的人 -- “难怪他成为有名的人”：有名的哲学家，identifies the particular kind of famous person he became and fits this story’s ending.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为有名的人：成为了有名的人，This is a completed result in the narrative, so “了” marks the completed change of state.
- Expressiveness: At the ending, add a response from other people to show his achievement: “人们都很尊敬他。”
- Expressiveness: You could close with a reflection that connects his success to his mother’s choices: “人们都说，他的成功离不开妈妈三次搬家。”

### Encouragement
- You used “难怪” effectively to connect his intelligence with his later success. The sentence also gives the story a clear concluding idea.
