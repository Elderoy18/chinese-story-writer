## Scene 1

> 古代，孟子跟孟母住在墓地的旁边。可是孟母注意，住在墓地的旁边影响孟子。他常常跟朋友们祭拜玩，所以孟母决定他们会搬家。这次，他们住在城市里的商店的旁边，从早到晚很吵。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 孟母注意：孟母注意到，`到` is a result complement here: it shows that she became aware of the fact that living near the cemetery affects Mengzi.
- 跟朋友们祭拜玩：跟朋友们一起学别人祭拜，`祭拜` is a solemn ritual, so it cannot be followed by `玩` to express pretending or imitating; `学别人祭拜` expresses that meaning.
- 决定他们会搬家：决定搬家，`决定` takes a verb phrase directly, so the people who will move do not need to be repeated after it.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 吵 -- “从早到晚很吵”：嘈杂，emphasizes the many mixed, noisy sounds around the shops; 喧闹，adds the sense of a busy and lively commercial area.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 古代，孟子跟孟母住在墓地的旁边：古代，有一个小男孩叫孟子，他和妈妈住在墓地的旁边，at first mention, briefly introduce the main character with both a name and who he is.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 可是孟母注意到：孟母注意到，`可是` introduces contrast, but noticing the harmful effect is a natural result of living beside a cemetery, not a contrasting event.
- Expressiveness: When Mengzi imitates the ritual, add one visible action to show what he does: `孟子学着大人鞠躬，还假装哭。`
- Expressiveness: At the point where his mother notices the problem, add her worried thought or speech: `孟母心想：“住在这儿会影响孟子。”`
- Expressiveness: In the final sentence, describe one sound from the shopping area: `商人一直大声叫卖，街上嘈杂极了。`

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the change from the quiet cemetery setting to the noisy shopping area, and the reason for the family’s move is easy to understand.

## Scene 2

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- Nothing to flag.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不高兴 -- “难怪他老是看起来不高兴”：闷闷不乐，describes a continuing, visibly low mood rather than general unhappiness；愁眉苦脸，adds the detail that his sadness shows on his face
- 陪 -- “孟母只好一直陪孟子”：照看，makes clear that Meng’s mother stays with him to keep him safe because the area is dangerous

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 孟子也没有朋友，难怪他老是看起来不高兴：难怪他老是看起来不高兴，原来他没有朋友，the pattern introduces the surprising result after 难怪 and then gives its explanation with 原来.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 住在商店的旁边不是适合孟子，而是让他觉得很伤心：住在商店的旁边不适合孟子，让他觉得很伤心，the two ideas are cause and result, not a correction of one idea by another, so the paired connector 不是……而是…… does not fit.
- Expressiveness: After “城市也有点儿危险,” add a concrete detail about the busy street: “每天从早到晚，商店旁边人来人往，吵得很。”
- Expressiveness: Show Meng’s mother’s worry with a short thought or line of speech after “孟母只好一直陪孟子”: “孟母心想：‘这儿太乱了，我不能让孟子一个人出去。’”
- Expressiveness: Make Meng’s loneliness more vivid after “孟子也没有朋友”: “他常常一个人坐在窗边，看着别的孩子一起玩。”

### Encouragement

## Scene 3

> 所以，孟母和孟子搬到学校的旁边住。才五岁孟子开始学哲学。在学校，孟子不是看书，就是练习写字。将来，他成为中国的一个非常有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 才五岁孟子开始学哲学：孟子才五岁就开始学哲学，The pattern for an action that begins at an early age is “才 + number + 就 + verb.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬到学校的旁边住：搬到了学校的旁边住，This move is a completed event in the narrative; place 了 after the resultative complement 到.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为中国的一个非常有名的哲学家：成为了中国一个非常有名的哲学家，After using 后来 for a later completed event in the past narrative, 成为 needs 了 to mark the completed change of state.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 将来：后来，The story is recounting events that already happened, so 后来 clearly introduces the later event in the sequence.
- Expressiveness: At the school setting, add one concrete detail about the learning environment, such as “学校旁边很安静，常常能听到学生读书的声音。”
- Expressiveness: After the learning activities, show Meng Mu’s feelings with a short thought or quotation, such as “孟母看见孟子认真学习，心里很高兴。”

### Encouragement
- You used the “不是……就是……” pattern well to show Meng Zi’s two regular school activities. The scene also has a clear progression from moving near the school to studying and later becoming a philosopher.
