## Scene 1

> 古代的时候有一个人叫神农。他需要当上医，一看别的人就想帮助他们。但是，他有一个问题，他不知道什么东西能做药。神农听说一个远远的地方有一种植物，什么病人吃都会觉得身体更好。神农三个月走去，找到有植物的地方，他看到一个很漂亮的植物，又香又好看。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，The time noun already identifies an era, so adding a second “time of” expression is redundant.
- 一看：一看到，Use the resultative complement to show that he actually sees the people before wanting to help them.
- 别的人：别人，Use this word for people in general when there is no previously named person or group being contrasted.
- 一个很漂亮的植物：一株很漂亮的植物，Use the classifier 株 for one individual plant.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 远远的 -- “一个远远的地方”：遥远的，conveys a place at a great distance and makes the journey feel more vivid.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 有植物的地方：有这种植物的地方，Use 这种植物 to refer clearly back to the particular plant introduced in the previous sentence.
- Coherence (Reference): 他看到：看到，After 神农 has already been named in the same sentence and no other character competes as the subject, use zero form rather than repeating the pronoun.
- Coherence (Temporality): 神农三个月走去：神农走了三个月，Put the duration after the action, and use 了 to mark the completed three-month journey; 去 is not needed here without a destination.
- Expressiveness: When describing the journey, add a small physical detail to show its difficulty, such as “他走啊走啊，翻过了好几座山。”
- Expressiveness: After he sees the plant, describe its appearance more specifically, such as “黄色的花像星星一样，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: Add Shennong’s thought before he searches, such as “他想：我一定要找到能治病的植物。”

### Encouragement
You clearly introduce Shennong and explain his wish to help other people. The description “又香又好看” also gives the plant a pleasant sensory detail.

## Scene 2

> 他觉得，这种一定是做药的花。原来他想错了。吃了以后，神农快生病。 这时一个穿着蓝色衣服的人帮神农，快给他一种红的灵芝。神农吃了以后，他没有病，想起来以前一个人告诉他，灵芝对身体有好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 这种一定是做药的花：这一定是一种可以做药的花，Use a numeral-measure-word phrase to introduce one kind of flower; “这种” normally needs a following noun when it modifies that noun.
- 快给他一种红的灵芝：赶快给了他一种红的灵芝，This is a completed action in the narrative, so the verb needs 了; “赶快” naturally describes acting promptly in a past event.
- 想起来以前一个人告诉他：想起以前有一个人告诉过他，The earlier telling happened before the current scene, so 告诉过 marks it as a past experience; 有 introduces the previously unmentioned person naturally.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 觉得 -- “他觉得”：认定，shows that he formed a firm conclusion rather than only having a general feeling
- 有好处 -- “灵芝对身体有好处”：有益于身体健康，states more specifically that the benefit is for health

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农吃了以后，他没有病：神农吃了以后，没有病了，The subject has already been named in the same sentence, so use zero form rather than repeating it with a pronoun.
- Coherence (Temporality): 没有病：没有病了，Use 了 to show a change of state: he was ill earlier and is no longer ill after eating the medicine.
- Expressiveness: After the illness begins, add a bodily feeling to make the danger vivid, for example: “神农肚子疼得不得了，只能躺在地上。”
- Expressiveness: When the blue-clothed person helps, add a short line of speech to show urgency, for example: “快吃这个，也许能帮你！”
- Expressiveness: Describe the flower before the mistake is revealed, for example: “那朵花又黄又大，像天上的星星一样。”

### Encouragement
- You used “原来” effectively to show that the character’s first guess was wrong. You also included a helpful new character and connected the medicine to its benefit for the body.

## Scene 3

> 啊，他怎么忘记了？他把灵芝放在包里。一回家就告诉别人。他找到药以后他写下怎么做药在书上。所以人们都知道，那红灵芝对身体好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 写下怎么做药在书上：在书上写下怎么做药，The location phrase should come before the verb when it describes where the writing happens.
- 那红灵芝：那个红灵芝，This demonstrative needs a measure word before the noun.
- 对身体好处：对身体有好处，The phrase needs 有 to express that something benefits the body.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 以后他写下：以后写下，After the same subject has already appeared in this sentence, use zero form rather than repeating the pronoun.
- Coherence (Temporality): 把灵芝放在包里：把灵芝放在包里了，This is a completed action in the narrative, so it needs 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉别人：告诉了别人，The telling is a completed event after returning home, so use 了 after the verb.
- Coherence (Temporality): 写下：写下了，The writing is completed; with the resultative complement 下, 了 follows the full verb-complement phrase.
- Expressiveness: At the opening question, add Shennong’s remembered thought to show his feeling: “原来灵芝对身体有好处，我差点忘了！”
- Expressiveness: When he puts the herb in his bag, add a small action showing care: “他小心地把灵芝放进包里，怕它弄坏了。”
- Expressiveness: When he tells others, add direct speech to make the scene more lively: “这种红灵芝可以做药，对身体有好处！”

### Encouragement
You used useful story connectors such as “一……就……”, “以后”, and “所以” to show the order and result of events. The scene also clearly shows Shennong remembering the value of the herb and sharing what he knows.
