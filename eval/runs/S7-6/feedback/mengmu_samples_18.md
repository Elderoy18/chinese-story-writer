## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个男孩名字叫孟子。孟子和孟子的妈妈两个人住在很穷的农村。他们住的地方旁边是个墓地，墓地老是有很多人祭拜。因为人们在祭拜，他们一直在哭。孟子和孟子的妈妈住在这里才一个星期就觉得受不了了，而且害得孟子的学习。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，The original repeats two time expressions with the same meaning; use the shorter time phrase.
- 有一个男孩名字叫孟子：有一个叫孟子的男孩，Place the name phrase before the noun to introduce the character clearly as both a boy and the person named孟子.
- 而且害得孟子的学习：而且影响了孟子的学习，害得 must be followed by a person and a harmful result clause; it cannot directly take a noun phrase such as孟子的学习.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 穷 -- “很穷的农村”：贫困，identifies the rural area’s economic hardship more specifically.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子的妈妈两个人：孟子和妈妈两个人，Within the same clause, do not repeat the name inside the possessive phrase; zero form is natural when the relationship is already clear.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子的妈妈住在这里：孟子和妈妈住在这里，Within the same clause, omit the repeated name in the possessive phrase because the mother has already been identified.
- Coherence (Temporality): 住在这里才一个星期就觉得受不了了：才在这里住了一个星期，就觉得受不了了，The one-week stay is completed before the later feeling, so use了 after住; the duration follows the verb.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为人们在祭拜，他们一直在哭：因为人们在祭拜，所以他们一直在哭，A cause-and-effect relationship needs the paired connector因为……所以…… .
- Expressiveness: At the description of the cemetery, add a sensory detail about the sound: “墓地里不时传来伤心的哭声。”
- Expressiveness: After the crying, show孟子的 reaction or thought: “孟子觉得很奇怪，也有一点害怕。”
- Expressiveness: At the final sentence, add the mother’s feeling to make the problem more immediate: “妈妈叹了口气，觉得这里真的不适合孩子学习。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish the setting with the rural home and the nearby cemetery, so the reader can picture where the family lives.
- You also use a specific time length, “一个星期,” to show that the family quickly begins to find the situation difficult.

## Scene 2

> 孟子的妈妈觉得孟子取得良好的教育是很重要的，所以孟子的妈妈认为这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好，就打算跟孟子搬家。孟子的妈妈带孟子不是搬到城市，而是搬到了孟子老师的家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 取得良好的教育：接受良好的教育，The verb 取得 usually collocates with achievements or results, while 接受 is the natural verb for receiving an education.
- 孟子老师的家：孟子的老师的家，The possessive marker is needed to show that the teacher belongs to Mengzi, rather than suggesting that the teacher is named Mengzi.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 地方 -- “这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好”：环境，more specifically refers to the living and learning surroundings that affect a child；住处，more specifically refers to the place where the family lives.
- 不太好 -- “对儿子的教育不太好”：不利于儿子的教育，states more precisely that the place may negatively affect his education.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 所以孟子的妈妈认为：所以她认为，The mother was named earlier in the same sentence and no other female character competes as the referent, so a pronoun is more natural for a close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 带孟子不是搬到城市，而是搬到了：没有带孟子搬到城市，而是搬到了，This is an ordinary choice between two destinations, so use the negative form with 而是 rather than the corrective pattern 不是……而是…… .
- Expressiveness: At the point where the mother decides to move, add her worried feeling: “孟母很着急，担心这里会影响孟子的学习。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought or line of speech after her decision: “孟母心想：‘我得给孟子找一个更适合学习的地方。’”
- Expressiveness: When describing the teacher’s home, add one concrete detail about the learning environment: “老师的家很安静，书架上有很多书。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the mother’s reason for moving by using a cause-and-effect connection with “所以.”
- You also used “搬到了” well to show that the move was completed.

## Scene 3

### Encouragement
