## Scene 1

> 在古代，很多医生不知道怎么用药。所以他们找到了很多植物对药。神农先看到很多病人，他们吃很多植物对药。但是我觉得他们的吃的植物，让他们病人。神农觉得这个植物可能特别是毒。所以他想找一个植物对药，他们可以吃对药。正好，他找到了一把花，他想把花放在茶里，对药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很多植物对药：很多能做药的植物，The phrase “对药” cannot describe a plant’s medicinal use; “能做药的” correctly modifies “植物.”
- 很多植物对药：很多能做药的植物，The phrase “对药” cannot describe plants that are used as medicine; “能做药的” forms the correct modifier before “植物.”
- 他们的吃的植物：他们吃的植物，A verb phrase used to modify a noun takes the pattern “verb + 的 + noun”; the extra possessive “的” after “他们” does not belong in this relative clause.
- 让他们病人：让他们生病，“病人” is a noun meaning “patient,” so it cannot be the result after “让”; “生病” is the verb phrase meaning “become ill.”
- 特别是毒：特别有毒，“毒” is not used as a predicate adjective here; “有毒” means “poisonous” and can follow “特别.”
- 一个植物对药：一种能做药的植物，“对药” cannot express that a plant can be made into medicine; “能做药的植物” gives the plant this function, and “一种” is the suitable measure word for a kind of plant.
- 吃对药：吃药，“对药” does not fit as the object of “吃”; “吃药” means to take medicine.
- 对药：做药，“对药” cannot express the purpose of putting the flowers into tea; “做药” means “to make medicine.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很多 -- “找到了很多能做药的植物”：许多种，specifies that the plants belong to many different kinds；各类，emphasizes a range of plant categories.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：一个叫神农的人，This is the character’s first mention, so introduce the name together with a full noun phrase rather than assuming the reader already knows who he is.
- Coherence (Reference): 这个植物：这些植物，No particular single plant has been identified before this sentence, so the singular reference is unclear; the plural phrase refers clearly to the plants the patients ate.
- Coherence (Reference): 他们可以吃药：病人可以吃药，Several groups have appeared between the earlier mention of the patients and this sentence, including doctors and 神农, so repeat “病人” instead of using an unclear pronoun.
- Expressiveness: When 神农 sees the ill people, add one physical detail to show their condition, such as “他们捂着肚子，脸色很不好.”
- Expressiveness: When 神农 realizes the plants may be poisonous, add his worried thought: “神农想：这些植物不能随便吃.”
- Expressiveness: When he finds the flowers, describe their appearance to make the discovery vivid: “那些花是黄色的，像星星一样，又大又漂亮.”

### Encouragement
You create a clear problem-and-response pattern: people become ill, and then 神农 decides to look for a solution. You also use useful story connectors such as “所以,” “但是,” and “正好” to move the scene forward.

## Scene 2

> 他不知道如果这把花对你的身体好，这就要看如果这把花毒不毒了。神农想找别的植物，但是这很麻烦。正好，他找到了别的花。他一吃花，很快就生病了。他的肚子疼死了。神农不知道他吃不吃灵芝或者夹竹桃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 如果：删除，After a verb of knowing, the following clause should state the information or question known; a conditional introduced by 如果 does not fit here.
- 好：好不好，An indirect yes-or-no question after 不知道 uses the A-not-A form.
- 如果：删除，After 要看, the clause should give the criterion being checked, so 毒不毒 follows directly without 如果.
- 他吃不吃灵芝或者夹竹桃：自己吃的是灵芝还是夹竹桃，The original asks whether he eats the plants. To identify which plant he ate, use 吃的是…还是…; 还是 is used for alternatives in a question.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 找 -- “神农想找别的植物”：探寻，emphasizes exploring plants carefully to discover their properties；寻找，emphasizes an ongoing search rather than simply locating something.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 你的身体：自己的身体，The body belongs to the subject, so use 自己的 to make the possessor clearly refer back to 神农.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 一吃花，很快就生病了：一吃花，就很快生病了，The pattern 一…就… needs 就 to link the first action with its immediate result.
- Expressiveness: At “他的肚子疼死了,” add a physical detail to show how severe the pain is, such as “他疼得在地上打滚。”
- Expressiveness: After he becomes ill, add his worried thought to show his fear, such as “他想：我会死吗？”
- Expressiveness: When introducing the flower, describe its appearance so the reader can picture it, such as “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花。”

### Encouragement
You clearly connect eating the flower with becoming ill, and “疼死了” vividly communicates Shennong’s strong pain. You also use “找到了” correctly to show that his search had a result.

## Scene 3

> 别的人给了神农一个灵芝。这个植物可以让神农更好。比方说，他会觉得更健康。吃灵芝以后，他可以再走。现在他觉得更好。最后，神农找到了很多植物，他可以用对药。这些植物让神农很高兴。神农想跟很多人分享他的新药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别的人：一个人，This phrase refers to other people contrasted with a previously mentioned person or group. Here, an unspecified new person is being introduced.
- 最后，神农找到了很多植物，他可以用对药：最后，神农找到了很多可以用来做药的植物，The phrase “用对药” is not a valid way to express the purpose of plants. Use “用来做药,” and place this modifying phrase before “植物” with “的.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 更好 -- “现在他觉得更好”：好多了，shows more clearly that his condition has noticeably improved after taking the medicine
- 走 -- “他可以再走”：继续赶路，specifies that he is able to resume travelling on his journey
- 高兴 -- “这些植物让神农很高兴”：欣慰，shows satisfaction and relief after his hard work produces useful results

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 这个植物：它，Since the lingzhi was mentioned in the immediately previous sentence and no other object competes as the referent, use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃灵芝以后：吃了灵芝以后，The eating is completed before the later action of walking again, so the completed action takes 了.
- Expressiveness: After the medicine helps him, add a physical detail and a feeling: “他觉得肚子慢慢不疼了，心里很惊讶。”
- Expressiveness: When he finds useful plants, show how he records his discoveries: “他把这些植物的样子和作用记在书上。”
- Expressiveness: At the sharing point, add a short line of speech: “这种植物叫灵芝，可以做药！”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the recovery sequence from receiving the lingzhi to being able to travel again. You also use completed-action forms well when describing the person giving the medicine and Shen Nong finding plants.
