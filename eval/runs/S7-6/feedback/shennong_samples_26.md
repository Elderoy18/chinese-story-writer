## Scene 1

> 很久以前，有一个老男人叫神农。神农在古代每一天看到了别的小镇人生病还是不舒服，所以他决定开始找新的药。他下一天开始爬当地的山脉，找了各种各样的植物。神农试一试每个植物，知道有什么好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别的小镇人：小镇上的人们，别的 is used to distinguish people or things from one already mentioned; here there is no stated comparison group, so name the people in the town directly.
- 生病还是不舒服：生病或者感到不舒服，in this declarative sentence, 或者 connects two alternative conditions; 还是 is normally used for a question or a choice being asked about.
- 下一天：第二天，第二天 is the usual expression for the day after the previously described event; 下一天 is not natural here.
- 找了各种各样的植物：找到了各种各样的植物，找 means “to look for,” while 找到 means “to find”; the sentence describes the result of his search.
- 试一试：试了试，this is a completed action in a past narrative, so the verb needs 了; the reduplicated past form is 试了试.
- 每个植物：每一种植物，种 is the appropriate measure word when referring to each type of plant.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 找 -- “所以他决定开始找新的药”：寻找，emphasizes an ongoing search for medicine.
- 好处 -- “知道有什么好处”：药效，specifies the medicinal effects of each plant in this context.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农在古代每一天看到了：他在古代每一天看到了，after a character has just been introduced and no other person competes as the referent, use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农试一试每个植物：他试一试每个植物，after the previous sentence continues to describe the same person, use a pronoun rather than repeating the name.
- Coherence (Temporality): 每一天看到了：每一天看到，每一天 describes a routine, so use the zero aspect form rather than 了, which marks a completed action.
- Expressiveness: After the people become ill, add a concrete cause to make the problem clearer, such as “很多人吃了有毒的植物，常常生病。”
- Expressiveness: When Shennong decides to search, add his thought or determination, such as “他想：我要找出能治病的植物。”
- Expressiveness: While he climbs the mountains, add one visual detail about the journey, such as “山路又长又陡，但是他没有放弃。”

### Encouragement
You introduced Shennong clearly at the beginning and kept the scene focused on his goal. You also used a strong cause-and-effect connection with “所以,” which helps the reader understand why he begins his search.

## Scene 2

> 比方说，他一次试一试有些植物的叶，吃得又新鲜又甜。煮这些叶以后，他一喝就知道他发现新的茶叶。虽然他找到了很多的新的植物，但是他的旅程有的时候特别是危险。比方说，他一吃灵芝就开始生病。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 吃得又新鲜又甜：这些叶子吃起来又新鲜又甜，吃得 describes how a person performs an action, while 吃起来 describes the taste of the leaves.
- 特别是危险：特别危险，特别是 must introduce a typical example from a preceding general statement; here the intended meaning is “very dangerous.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 新鲜 -- “这些叶子吃起来又新鲜又甜”：爽口，describes a light, pleasant taste in the mouth more specifically；清香，adds the gentle fragrance that tea leaves may have
- 危险 -- “他的旅程有的时候特别危险”：充满危险，shows that the journey contains many risks rather than simply labelling it dangerous；凶险，describes danger on a difficult journey more vividly

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 试一试：试了试，This is a completed testing action in a past narrative, so it needs 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 煮这些叶以后：煮了这些叶以后，The boiling must be completed before the later action of drinking.
- Coherence (Temporality): 发现新的茶叶：发现了新的茶叶，The discovery is presented as completed when he realizes it after drinking.
- Expressiveness: After the tea is made, add a sensory detail about its smell or taste, such as “茶水闻起来清香，喝起来又甜又暖。”
- Expressiveness: At the moment he becomes ill, add a physical reaction and feeling, such as “他的肚子突然疼了起来，心里非常害怕。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought to show his concern during the dangerous journey, such as “他想：这种植物到底能不能吃呢？”

### Encouragement
- You use the contrast pattern “虽然……但是……” effectively to show that finding plants does not make the journey easy.
- Your use of “一……就……” clearly connects eating the plant with becoming sick.

## Scene 3

> 一个爬山的男人正好看到神农的麻烦，然后给了他一些药。神农最终他的小镇回到，跟别的人分享他的发现。你认为神农的故事真的吗？这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 你认为神农的故事真的吗？：你认为神农的故事是真的吗？，A yes-or-no question about whether a story is true needs the copula before the predicate adjective.
- 这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处。：这就要看你相不相信中药对身体有好处了。，The pattern requires a question form after 看, such as an A-not-A form, and ends with the fixed final particle.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 药 -- “给了他一些药”：草药，identifies the medicine as plant-based and fits the later reference to 中药.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 神农最终他的小镇回到：神农最终回到了小镇，The place object follows 回到, and this return is a completed narrative event, so 了 follows the resultative complement 到.
- Expressiveness: At the moment the man helps, add Shen Nong’s physical condition to show why he needs medicine: “神农躺在地上，肚子疼得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: When Shen Nong returns, add a short line of speech to show his wish to help others: “神农高兴地说：‘我要把这些发现告诉大家。’”
- Expressiveness: Before the final question, add an evaluative sentence about Shen Nong’s character: “神农不怕危险，一直帮助别人。”

### Encouragement
You clearly connected the helper’s action and Shen Nong’s return with a logical sequence. Your final question also gives the scene an interesting reflective ending.
