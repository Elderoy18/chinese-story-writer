## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，孟子和孟母住了在靠墓地的家。孩子才五岁就每天在墓地祭拜了。在那个城市，孟母从早到晚都找工作，可是找不到，害得孟母没有钱。难怪她跟孩子搬家，原来买不了房。他们搬了到别一个城市找工作。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，this phrase redundantly combines a historical time noun with a second “time of” expression.
- 住了在靠墓地的家：住在墓地旁边的家里，this is a continuing living situation, so it does not take completed `了`; the location follows `在`, and `家里` clearly marks the place of residence.
- 祭拜了：祭拜，this is a daily routine, so it uses the zero aspect form rather than completed `了`.
- 跟孩子搬家：带着孩子搬家，this expression means that the mother takes her child with her when moving; `跟` means “with” but does not express that relationship as precisely here.
- 搬了到：搬到了，`了` follows the verb-plus-direction/result expression, not the verb before `到`.
- 别一个城市：另一个城市，`另一个` is the standard expression for one additional or different city; `别的` is used to distinguish others from the one already discussed.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 找不到 -- “可是找不到”：一直没找到，emphasizes that the search continued but did not succeed during that time.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟母：有一个小男孩叫孟子，他和妈妈，this is the first mention of the main character, so introduce him as a child with his name before using the family title for his mother.
- Coherence (Reference): 孩子：他，Mengzi was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other male character competes with him, so a pronoun is the natural close re-occurrence form.
- Coherence (Reference): 那个城市：他们住的城市，no city has been introduced before this point, so `那个` has no clear earlier referent; use a full noun phrase to identify the place.
- Coherence (Reference): 害得孟母没有钱：害得她没有钱，Mengmu is already the subject of this sentence, so use a pronoun for the close re-occurrence rather than repeating her name.
- Expressiveness: At the cemetery action, add Mengzi’s reason for copying the ritual, for example: `孟子觉得祭拜很好玩，学着大人的样子鞠躬。`
- Expressiveness: At the unsuccessful job search, show Mengmu’s worry with an inner thought or speech line, for example: `孟母很着急，心想：“再这样下去，我们怎么办呢？”`

### Encouragement
You used a clear cause-and-result relationship with `害得`, and the `难怪……原来……` pattern effectively connects the family’s financial problem with the decision to move.

## Scene 2

> 这个城市老是很吵，不是人吵架，就是都叫，害得孟子一直不能睡觉。在那儿，孟母也找不到工作。孟子和妈妈要又一个次搬了家。在第三个城市，孟母找到工作，在商店做衣服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 孟子和妈妈要又一个次搬了家：孟子和妈妈要再搬一次家，A future repeat uses 再 after the modal verb, and the order is modal + 再 + verb + number + measure word + object; the future action does not take 了.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很吵 -- “这个城市老是很吵”：嘈杂，specifies that many mixed sounds make the place noisy；喧闹，suggests a busy, noisy atmosphere with many people.
- 做 -- “在商店做衣服”：缝制，specifies that she sews and makes the clothes.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 人：人们，This refers to a concrete group in the city, so 人们 is needed rather than the generic noun 人.
- Coherence (Temporality): 孟母找到工作：孟母找到了工作，Finding the job is a completed action, so it needs 了 after the resultative verb.
- Expressiveness: At the opening, add one specific sound detail to show why the city is noisy, such as “街上从早到晚都是叫卖声和吵闹声。”
- Expressiveness: After the sleeping problem, show Mengzi’s physical feeling, such as “孟子捂着耳朵，难受极了。”
- Expressiveness: After Mengmu finds work, add her feeling about the change, such as “孟母终于松了一口气。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show how the noisy environment affects Mengzi by using “害得孟子一直不能睡觉,” which creates a strong cause-and-effect link.
- You also move the scene forward clearly by introducing a new city and Mengmu’s new job.

## Scene 3

> 孟子在那个商店也找了到工作，可是不是做衣服的工作，而是喜欢的工作。孟子学习馆学了十五年，他的学习和他的旅行经验很重要，对他很多好处。他是大人的时候，他成为很有名的哲人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 学习馆学了：在学习馆学习了，A location should be introduced with 在 before the action; 学习 is the verb that can take the duration phrase 十五年.
- 对他很多好处：对他有很多好处，The fixed expression is 对……有好处; 有 is the verb that connects the person affected with the benefit.
- 他是大人的时候：他长大以后，长大以后 naturally introduces the time after someone becomes an adult; 是大人 is not the usual expression for this change of life stage.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很有名 -- “他成为很有名的哲人”：广为人知，specifies that many people know him and expresses fame more vividly in this context.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他的学习和他的旅行经验：他的学习和旅行经验，After the possessive pronoun has introduced Mengzi as the owner, omit the repeated pronoun in the same noun phrase.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为很有名的哲人：成为了很有名的哲人，Use 了 for the completed change of becoming a well-known philosopher.
- Expressiveness: After the study-duration sentence, add one concrete learning detail, such as “他每天认真看书，还常常思考人生的问题。” This shows what his long period of study looked like.
- Expressiveness: At the final sentence, add a brief reaction from other people, such as “人们都很敬佩他。” This makes his reputation feel more visible in the scene.

### Encouragement
- You clearly show Mengzi’s development over time, from finding work to studying for many years and becoming a respected thinker.
- Your use of the contrast pattern “不是……而是……” helps distinguish the two kinds of work clearly.
