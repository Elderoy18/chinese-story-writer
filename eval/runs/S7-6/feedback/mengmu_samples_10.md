## Scene 1

> 孟子小的时候，从早到晚都跟朋友玩。孟子和他的朋友喜欢玩在墓地里，装祭拜。孟子五岁的时候，跟妈妈搬家。他们搬家不是因为孟子玩得太多，而是因为妈妈认为他们的家离墓地太近。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 喜欢玩在墓地里：喜欢在墓地里玩，The location phrase should come before the action verb it modifies.
- 装祭拜：假装祭拜，装 cannot directly introduce an action in this meaning; 假装 + verb phrase expresses pretending to do that action.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 朋友 -- “孟子和他的朋友”：小伙伴，specifically suggests friends of a similar age and fits a childhood scene.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 跟妈妈搬家：跟妈妈搬了家，This is a completed move at a specific time in the past, so the action needs 了.
- Expressiveness: After “墓地里,” add a small detail about what Mengzi sees there, such as “常常有人来祭拜,” to make the setting easier to picture.
- Expressiveness: At “假装祭拜,” add Mengzi’s thought or feeling, such as “孟子觉得祭拜很有趣,” to show why he copies the adults.
- Expressiveness: Before the final sentence, add the mother’s concern in quoted speech, such as “住在这儿会影响孩子,” to make her reason for moving more vivid.

### Encouragement
You clearly show both Mengzi’s childhood behavior and his mother’s reason for moving. The contrast structure “不是……而是……” is used well to distinguish the real reason for the move.

## Scene 2

> 妈妈认为，住离墓地太近，害得孟子的心气。孟子和孟母搬到子思老师的家，跟子思老师住。孟母帮子思打扫房间和做饭。子思帮孟母教她的儿子。因为孟子现在没有朋友，他老是读书。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 住离墓地太近：住在离墓地太近的地方，The verb phrase “住在” needs a location after it; “离墓地太近的地方” is the complete location phrase.
- 害得孟子的心气：害得孟子心情不好，The structure “害得” must be followed by the affected person and a harmful result; “孟子的心气” is only a noun phrase and does not state a result.
- 老是读书：一直读书，“老是” often expresses an annoying or undesirable repeated action, while “一直” neutrally describes continuing to read.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 教 -- “子思帮孟母教她的儿子”：辅导，more specifically describes a teacher guiding a student’s learning.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟母：他们，Both characters were identified in the previous sentence, so the collective pronoun is more natural for this close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Reference): 跟子思老师住：跟他住，After “子思老师” has just been named, use a pronoun rather than repeating the name in the same sentence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬到：搬到了，Moving to the teacher’s home is a completed narrative action, so “搬到” needs “了.”
- Expressiveness: At “孟母帮子思打扫房间和做饭,” add a small action detail to show her work, such as “她从早到晚忙着扫地、做饭。”
- Expressiveness: At “因为孟子现在没有朋友,” add his feeling to make the reason more personal, such as “孟子觉得很孤单，只好安静地读书。”
- Expressiveness: After the final sentence, add Mengmu’s reaction through speech, such as “孟母看到孟子认真读书，高兴地说：‘这样才好！’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show how each person helps in the new home, especially through the actions of cleaning, cooking, and teaching.
- The final sentence also uses a clear cause-and-effect connection to explain why Mengzi reads.

## Scene 3

> 子思认为，对孩子来说，读书最重要，特别是学哲学。孟子不是练习写文章，就是看哲学书。孟子从五岁一直学哲学。他才五岁就学哲学，难怪他成为很有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 从五岁一直学哲学：从五岁开始一直学哲学，Use 开始 after a starting age to mark the point when the continuing activity began.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子从五岁开始一直学哲学：他从五岁开始一直学哲学，孟子 was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other male character competes here, so use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他成为很有名的哲学家：他成为了很有名的哲学家，Becoming a philosopher is a completed change of state, so add 了 after the verb.
- Expressiveness: After the sentence about studying from age five, add a small detail showing his concentration, such as: “他每天安静地坐在书桌前看哲学书。”
- Expressiveness: After the final sentence, add his teacher’s positive reaction to make the achievement feel more vivid, such as: “子思老师满意地点点头，说：‘孟子学得很认真。’”

### Encouragement
You used the paired structure “不是……就是……” correctly to show Mengzi’s two study activities. The final sentence also gives a clear connection between his early study and his later achievement.
