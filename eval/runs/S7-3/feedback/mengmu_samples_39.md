## Scene 1

> 古代的时候人觉得祭拜非常重要，孟子很小的时候，从早到晚一直祭拜，因为他妈妈告诉他祭拜很重要。虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜。他的同学们老是对他开玩笑了。孟子妈妈看了他的同学们对孟子不好的影响，所以孟子和孟子妈妈搬家了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，This phrase redundantly combines two time expressions; use the shorter time expression for an ancient-time setting.
- 人：人们，Use this plural noun when referring to a group of people rather than the generic noun.
- 看了：看到了，Here the mother has perceived an effect, so the verb must mean “see/notice,” not “look at.”
- 同学们对孟子不好的影响：同学们对孟子的不好的影响，The adjective phrase modifying “influence” needs 的 before the noun it modifies.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很小 -- “孟子很小的时候”：年幼，more specifically describes a child’s young age and fits narrative writing.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子很小的时候：一个叫孟子的小男孩从小就，On a character’s first mention, introduce the character with a full noun phrase rather than using only the name.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子妈妈：孟子和他的妈妈，After the name has appeared in the same clause, use a pronoun for the repeated possessor instead of repeating the name.
- Coherence (Temporality): 老是对他开玩笑了：老是对他开玩笑，A habitual action with 老是 takes the zero particle, not 了, because it is not presented as completed.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜：因为他每天只祭拜，所以没有朋友，The relationship is cause and effect, so use the paired connector because–therefore rather than 虽然.
- Expressiveness: After the worship routine, add a visual detail about the people he observes, such as “他看见不同的人低着头来祭拜”.
- Expressiveness: At the point where the classmates tease him, show Mengzi’s feeling with a short detail, such as “孟子觉得很难过，不知道自己做错了什么”.
- Expressiveness: When his mother notices the influence, add her worried thought or speech, such as “孟子的妈妈很着急，说：‘不能让他再这样下去了。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show a cause-and-effect chain: Mengzi worships often, his classmates react, and his mother decides to move. You also used useful story vocabulary such as “祭拜”, “同学们”, and “搬家”.

## Scene 2

> 孟子才十一岁，成为一个哲学家。孟子不是祭拜，而是看政治文章，难怪他成为了哲学家。不过，看过很多政治文章对他害得，他有很多问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 才十一岁，成为一个哲学家：才十一岁，就成为了一个哲学家，The pattern pairs 才 with 就 to show an unexpectedly early achievement, and a completed change into a profession takes 了.
- 不过，看过很多政治文章对他害得，他有很多问题：不过，他看过很多政治文章，因此有很多问题，害得 must follow the pattern “cause + 害得 + person + harmful result”; it cannot be used after 对他.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 问题 -- “他有很多问题”：疑问, specifies that these are questions he wants to understand or answer.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子不是祭拜：他不是祭拜，Use a pronoun when the same character was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other male character competes as the referent.
- Expressiveness: After the reading activity, add a small inner reaction to show why the articles interest him, for example: “他越看越好奇。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought or question after “有很多问题” to make the questions more concrete, for example: “为什么人们要这样做呢？”

### Encouragement
You clearly show a contrast between worshipping and reading, and the use of 难怪 helps the reader understand the connection between Mengzi’s interests and his later achievement.

## Scene 3

> 他一直问问题，但是不能解决那些问题。孟子长大，他开始能解决问题。他常常建议你不是学金融，而是学政治，因为如果你懂政治，你会成为高薪。后来孟子成为教授，他教学生很厉害。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不是学金融，而是学政治：不要学金融，而要学政治，After a verb of advice, use a negative proposal such as 不要……而要……; the original form sounds as if it is correcting a fact rather than giving advice.
- 成为高薪：获得高薪，高薪 refers to pay, not a role or identity that a person can become; 获得 expresses receiving that pay.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 问问题 -- “他一直问问题”：提出问题，emphasizes actively raising questions for discussion or thought.
- 厉害 -- “他教学生很厉害”：很有办法，specifies that he teaches effectively and knows how to help students learn.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 你……你：别人……他们，The listener has not been introduced, so 你 has no clear referent in this third-person narrative. Use 别人 for the people Mengzi advises, then 他们 for that same group.
- Coherence (Temporality): 长大：长大了，Growing up is a completed change of state here, so it needs 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 后来孟子成为教授：后来孟子成为了教授，Becoming a professor is a completed change in the later sequence, so add 了 after 成为.
- Expressiveness: At the point where he can solve questions, add his feeling of progress, for example: “孟子很高兴，觉得自己终于懂了很多道理。”
- Expressiveness: When he teaches students, add a small classroom detail, for example: “学生们认真地听孟子讲课，还不停地问问题。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of advice to show his teaching style, for example: “孟子说：‘学习的时候，要多想一想为什么。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly show Mengzi’s development from asking unanswered questions to being able to teach others. The use of 后来 also helps move the scene forward in time.
