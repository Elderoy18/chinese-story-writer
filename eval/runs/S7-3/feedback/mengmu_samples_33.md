## Scene 1

> 一天，很久很久以前，孟子和他妈妈住在中国。孟子妈妈的工作是祭拜，孟子不高兴，因为他说我们老是搬家，为什么？他妈妈说，从早到晚，我一直祭拜，很多墓地得我祭拜。所以我们得去很多不同的地方。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 孟子不高兴，因为他说我们老是搬家，为什么？：孟子不高兴，问：“我们为什么老是搬家？”，“因为” should introduce the reason for being unhappy, not the act of speaking; use “问” to introduce this question and place the direct speech in quotation marks.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不高兴 -- “孟子不高兴”：感到不满，specifies that he is dissatisfied with the repeated moves rather than generally unhappy.
- 很多不同的地方 -- “我们得去很多不同的地方”：各地，more concisely indicates many places in different areas.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: At the child’s question, add a small action to show his feeling: “孟子皱着眉头，问：‘我们为什么老是搬家？’”
- Expressiveness: Give the mother a tired or worried reaction before her explanation: “妈妈叹了口气，说：‘我从早到晚都在祭拜。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the cause of the family’s frequent moves with “所以,” and “从早到晚” gives the mother’s work a useful sense of duration.

## Scene 2

> 孟子想，难怪我们常常搬家，原来我妈妈很忙。可是常常搬家害得孟子很难找到朋友。每次搬家孟子也觉得新的地方不是太热就是太冷，不是太大就是太小。在哪的地方，他都不高兴。孟子想帮助妈妈祭拜，可是他六岁，所以不能祭拜。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在哪的地方：在哪个地方，哪 followed by a noun needs a measure word here: 哪个地方.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 新的 -- “孟子也觉得新的地方不是太热就是太冷”：陌生的, emphasizes that each place feels unfamiliar to him.
- 太大 -- “不是太大就是太小”：太宽敞, describes a place as overly spacious more specifically；太狭小, gives a more vivid opposite for a place that feels too small.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 常常搬家害得孟子很难找到朋友：常常搬家害得他很难找到朋友，孟子 was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other person competes as the referent, so a pronoun is more natural.
- Coherence (Reference): 每次搬家孟子也觉得：每次搬家，他也觉得，use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence of the same character.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子想帮助妈妈祭拜：他想帮助妈妈祭拜，use a pronoun because the same character remains the clear subject from the preceding sentence.
- Expressiveness: After “很难找到朋友,” add a small feeling detail to show why this is difficult for him, such as “他觉得很孤单。”
- Expressiveness: At “新的地方不是太热就是太冷,” add a concrete physical detail, such as “有的地方夏天热得满头大汗，有的地方冬天冷得发抖。”
- Expressiveness: At the end, add a short line of inner speech to make his wish clearer, such as “他心想：‘我什么时候才能帮妈妈呢？’”

### Encouragement
You used the paired structure “难怪……原来……” well to show Mengzi’s realization. You also gave clear reasons for his unhappiness about moving, including difficulty making friends and discomfort in each new place.

## Scene 3

> 四年后，孟子开始帮妈妈祭拜。他画画很厉害，他越来越喜欢祭拜，所以他祭拜不是想帮妈妈工作，而是他很喜欢画画。孟子成为很著名的古代画家。今天很多中国人很喜欢孟子。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- Nothing to flag.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 厉害 -- “他画画很厉害”：擅长，states more precisely that he has strong skill in drawing；画得很好，clearly describes the quality of his drawings.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 而是他很喜欢画画：而是很喜欢画画，The subject remains the same person in this paired clause, so the second pronoun is unnecessary; use zero form when the referent is clear in the same sentence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 孟子成为很著名的古代画家：孟子成为了很著名的古代画家，This describes a completed change of identity, so add 了 after the verb to mark completion.
- Expressiveness: After “他画画很厉害,” add one concrete detail about what he draws, such as: “他把花和树画得很像。”
- Expressiveness: At “他越来越喜欢祭拜,” add his feeling or thought to show why the activity attracts him, such as: “他觉得祭拜的仪式很特别。”
- Expressiveness: You could add a short line of speech from his mother after noticing his skill, such as: “妈妈说：‘你画得真好！’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show a change in Mengzi over time with “四年后” and “越来越喜欢,” which helps the scene move forward. Your sentences are also easy to follow and use several different actions and feelings.
