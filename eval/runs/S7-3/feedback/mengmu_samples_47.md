## Scene 1

> 这个男孩子叫孟子，孟子是古代的人。孟子的妈妈觉得住在哪儿对童年很重要，所以他们常常搬了家。她望子成龙，所以孟子的妈妈安排他的生活。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 对童年：对孩子的成长，童年 names a stage of life, while the sentence means that the living environment affects the child’s development. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 生活 -- “安排他的生活”：日常生活, specifies the child’s everyday routines；起居, focuses more specifically on daily living arrangements such as meals and sleep

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 这个男孩子叫孟子，孟子是古代的人：在古代，有一个男孩子叫孟子。On first mention, use an indefinite full noun phrase to introduce a new character; 这个 makes the boy sound already known. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Reference): 她望子成龙，所以孟子的妈妈安排他的生活：她望子成龙，所以安排他的生活。The subject is already clear in the same sentence, so use zero form rather than repeating the full noun phrase. [rule: none]
- Coherence (Temporality): 常常搬了家：常常搬家，A habitual action takes the zero particle; 了 marks a completed action rather than a routine. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: After the reason for moving, add the mother’s inner thought to show her concern: “孟母心想：‘住的地方会影响孟子的成长。’”
- Expressiveness: Make the mother’s “arrangements” concrete with one daily detail: “她让孟子每天读书，按时睡觉。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect the mother’s wish for her son’s future with her decisions about his life. The connector “所以” also gives the scene a clear cause-and-effect structure.

## Scene 2

> 因为他们常常搬了家，中国人说，孟母三迁。孟子没有爸爸，所以，他的妈妈更重要。从这儿到那儿，妈妈教孟子很多技能。先她让孟子跟朋友玩儿，然后教他怎么买又便宜又好质量的东西。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 先她让孟子跟朋友玩儿：她先让孟子跟朋友玩儿，The adverb 先 should come after the subject and before the verb phrase it modifies. [rule: none]
- 又便宜又好质量的东西：质量又好又便宜的东西，质量 is a noun, so it cannot follow 好 directly in this structure; use 质量好 to describe an item with good quality. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 技能 -- “妈妈教孟子很多技能”：生活技能，specifies that these are practical abilities for daily life, which fits learning to get along with friends and buy things.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 常常搬了家：常常搬家，A habitual action uses the zero particle; 了 marks a completed action rather than a repeated routine. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: At “孟子没有爸爸”, add the mother’s inner thought to show why she feels responsible, for example: “孟母觉得自己要更用心地照顾孟子。”
- Expressiveness: After the shopping lesson, add a short line of speech to make the teaching more vivid, for example: “买东西的时候，要看看价钱，也要看看质量。”
- Expressiveness: At “跟朋友玩儿”, add an evaluative detail about Mengzi’s behaviour, for example: “孟子跟朋友玩得很开心。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the mother’s important role in Mengzi’s life, and you use a useful learning sequence with 先 and 然后. Your details about playing with friends and buying things also make the scene easy to follow.
