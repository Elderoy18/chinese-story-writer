## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有很多人不知道什么植物能吃，什么植物不能吃。有很多人吃植物以后就吃坏肚子了，因为他们吃了毒。那个时候，有一个老人叫神农，他什么植物都觉得好吃，不怕生病，不怕毒。他希望别人也可以吃好吃的植物。可是他知道，如果他们还怕吃毒，不会吃他喜欢的植物。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，This time expression is redundant because 古代 already names a time period.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 喜欢 -- “他喜欢的植物”：爱吃，specifies that he likes eating these plants rather than liking them in a general way.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 有很多人吃植物以后就吃坏肚子了：他们吃植物以后就吃坏肚子了，Use a pronoun for the same group when it was mentioned in the immediately preceding sentence and no competing group has appeared.
- Expressiveness: After the stomachache event, add a concrete physical symptom, such as “他们的肚子疼得厉害。”
- Expressiveness: When introducing the old man, add an evaluative detail that shows his character, such as “神农是一个勇敢的老人。”
- Expressiveness: Show Shen Nong’s purpose through an inner thought, such as “神农想：‘我要找出能吃的植物。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly explain the central problem of not knowing which plants are safe to eat. You also use contrast effectively with “可是” to show the difference between Shen Nong’s hope and other people’s fear.

## Scene 2

> 所以，他上山下山找植物，吃了很多样子的植物。他是第一人，找到了茶。他把所有的植物写下来了。他一直都吃了，他连毒都能吃。可是他还没吃了一个叫夹竹桃的植物。他找到了那个像星星的植物，觉得很漂亮，马上就吃下了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很多样子的植物：很多种植物，样子 refers to an object’s appearance or shape, while 种 is the measure word used for different types of plants.
- 他是第一人，找到了茶：他是第一个找到茶的人，第一人 usually means a leading or foremost figure; 第一个找到茶的人 clearly identifies him as the first person who found tea.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 写下来 -- “他把所有的植物写下来了”：记下来了, emphasizes keeping a record of the plants he discovered；记录下来了, emphasizes recording information in an organized way.
- 上山下山 -- “他上山下山找植物”：翻山越岭, gives a clearer picture of travelling across many mountains while searching.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 那个像星星的植物：一个像星星的植物，this is the first mention of this particular plant, so it should be introduced with an indefinite noun phrase rather than 那个.
- Coherence (Temporality): 一直都吃了：一直都在吃，an action continuing over a period needs 在 for the progressive aspect; 了 marks completion and does not fit with 一直.
- Coherence (Temporality): 还没吃了：还没吃过，没 cannot be followed by 了; 吃过 expresses that he has not had the experience of eating this plant before.
- Coherence (Temporality): 找到了：找到，when closely connected actions are presented in a series, only the final action takes 了; the final 吃下了 already marks the completed sequence.
- Expressiveness: At the moment he sees the plant, add its colour and shape to make the setting more vivid: “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花。”
- Expressiveness: Before he eats it, add his thought to show why he acts so quickly: “这么漂亮的植物，一定很好吃吧！”
- Expressiveness: When he finds tea, add a small sensory detail: “茶叶泡在水里，闻起来清香。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show Shennong’s active exploration through the mountain-searching actions, and the quick action marked by 马上就 makes the final moment feel lively. You also use the 把 structure effectively to show that he records what he finds.

## Scene 3

> 可是夹竹桃的毒很重，让他肚子很痛。他在地上躺着，觉得他快会死了。可是正好有别人找到他了，给了他一个像伞的植物，叫灵芝。他吃灵芝以后就觉得很好，不再生病了。他测量起来了，像星星的植物是毒，像伞的植物是药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 快会：快要，Use this form for something that is about to happen; the modal verb does not express imminence naturally here.
- 别人：一个人，This word distinguishes other people from the speaker or a named group; a newly appearing helper should be introduced as an unspecified person instead.
- 一个像伞的植物：一种像伞一样的植物，Use the classifier for a kind of plant, and use the comparison pattern with both parts to describe its shape.
- 觉得很好：觉得好多了，This expression means that his physical condition has improved, while the original wording usually means that he thinks something is good.
- 他测量起来了，：他看了一下，发现，The original verb means to measure size or length and does not introduce the observation that follows; the corrected wording introduces what he notices.
- 像星星的植物：像星星一样的植物，Use the full comparison pattern to say that the plant resembles a star.
- 是毒：有毒，Use this adjective form to describe a poisonous plant.
- 像伞的植物：像伞一样的植物，Use the full comparison pattern to say that the plant resembles an umbrella.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 毒 -- “夹竹桃的毒很重”：毒性很强，more precisely describes the strength of the plant’s poisonous property in this context
- 很痛 -- “让他肚子很痛”：疼得厉害，shows the degree of his stomach pain more vividly

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他：自己，When the person who may die is the same person as the subject who feels this, use the reflexive form so the reader does not interpret it as another male character.
- Coherence (Temporality): 找到他了，给了他：找到他，给了他，In a series of completed actions, only the last verb takes 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃灵芝以后：吃了灵芝以后，The eating is completed before the later improvement, so use 了 with the completed action.
- Expressiveness: At the moment of pain, add a physical detail to show how serious it feels: “他在地上躺着，疼得满头大汗。”
- Expressiveness: After he worries about dying, add a short thought or question: “我会死吗？”
- Expressiveness: When the helper arrives, add one visual detail and a line of speech: “一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人走过来，说：‘快吃这个！’”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the change from stomach pain to relief after taking the medicine. The repeated star-shaped and umbrella-shaped plant descriptions also help the reader distinguish the two plants.
