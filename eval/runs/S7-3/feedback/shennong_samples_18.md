## Scene 1

> 神农和他的家人住在古代，所以不知道什么植物能吃。因为他的家人常常生病了，所以神农和他的哥哥一起去旅行找可以吃的植物。他们去很多不一样的地方，比方说高山、山谷和沙漠。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 住在古代：在古代住在一起，A historical period is a time expression, so it should frame the action rather than function as the place after the residence verb.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 找 -- “找可以吃的植物”：探寻, emphasizes exploring carefully for plants whose properties are still unknown.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：一个叫神农的男人，On a character’s first mention, use a name together with a full noun phrase to introduce who the character is.
- Coherence (Temporality): 常常生病了：常常生病，Remove 了 because this describes a habitual condition; routines use the zero particle.
- Coherence (Temporality): 去很多不一样的地方：去了很多不一样的地方，Add 了 because the trip to many places is presented as a completed narrative action.
- Expressiveness: After the family’s illness, add a brief description of their condition to make the reason for the journey more vivid, for example: “他们常常肚子疼，躺在床上休息。”
- Expressiveness: At the travel sentence, add a small detail about the difficult journey, for example: “他们走了很远的路，又累又渴。”

### Encouragement
You used clear cause-and-effect connectors to link the family’s illness with the journey. The list of mountains, valleys, and deserts also gives the scene a good sense of variety.

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在很漂亮的河：在一条很漂亮的河边，A location beside a river is expressed with 河边, and 一条 introduces the newly mentioned river with its appropriate measure word.
- “这就要看你想要安全还是接近知道了。“：“这就要看你想要安全还是想知道答案了。”，接近 cannot directly modify 知道 in this meaning; 想知道答案 expresses the second choice clearly. After 这就要看, the content should be a question or alternative such as A还是B, and the direct-speech quotation needs a closing quotation mark.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 麻烦 -- “太麻烦了”：棘手，means difficult to deal with and makes the brother’s reaction more specific.

- Expressiveness: After 神农 becomes ill, add a physical feeling to show how serious it is, for example: “他的肚子疼了起来，难受得不得了。”

### Encouragement

## Scene 3
