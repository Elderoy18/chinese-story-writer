## Scene 1

> 有一天孟子去了墓地玩儿，但是他妈妈觉得这是不合适的地方让孩子玩，所以她决定了他们会搬家找更好的地方。她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福，而且住在这儿教育不太好。因为她望子成龙，所以她会一直搬家才能找到质量好的教育。然后他们搬到了离城市更近的地方。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 这是不合适的地方让孩子玩：这是一个不适合让孩子玩的地方，适合 is used for whether something suits a person or activity; 合适 cannot directly link this place to the action of children playing in this structure.
- 他们会搬家找更好的地方：搬家，找更好的地方，after 决定, use the action phrase directly rather than repeating the people who will act or adding 会.
- 会一直搬家才能找到：得一直搬家，才能找到，才能 presents the earlier action as a necessary condition, so 得 expresses necessity more accurately than the predictive modal 会.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 更好的 -- “找更好的地方”：更适合孩子成长的，identifies exactly why the new place would be better for the child.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 决定了：决定，决定 is a mental-state verb, so it normally uses the zero particle rather than 了.
- Expressiveness: At the cemetery scene, add one concrete detail about what Mengzi sees, such as “孟子看见很多人在墓地祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: When the mother decides to move, add her feeling or a short thought, such as “妈妈很担心，心想：‘这儿不适合孩子成长。’”
- Expressiveness: After the move closer to the city, describe the new environment, such as “街上有很多商人，从早到晚都很热闹。”

### Encouragement
- You used 因为……所以…… clearly to explain the mother’s motivation, and the move in the final sentence is correctly marked as completed with 搬到了. Your use of 望子成龙 also gives the mother’s goal a strong, story-appropriate meaning.

## Scene 2

> 他们住才一月就又搬家。古代的时候，一般人不喜欢卖东西的人。但是孟子老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿，害得妈妈着急得很，因为她恐怕儿子会成为跟他们很像。她告诉了孟子：他们不是朋友，而是不好的人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他们住才一月就又搬家：他们才住了一个月，就又搬家了，With the pattern using 才 and a duration, 才 comes before the verb; both the completed stay and the later move need 了.
- 古代的时候：在古代，This expression redundantly combines two time expressions; use the shorter form for a historical setting.
- 老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿：老是跟这些卖东西的人玩儿，Using both frequency adverbs together is redundant; choose one.
- 她恐怕儿子会成为跟他们很像：她担心儿子会变得跟他们很像，恐怕 does not follow a third-person subject to mean that person is afraid; use 担心 here. Also, 成为 needs a noun phrase, while 变得 can introduce an adjective phrase describing a change.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 卖东西的人 -- “一般人不喜欢卖东西的人”：商人，identifies the people more specifically by their occupation.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 她告诉了孟子：她告诉孟子，A speaking verb that introduces direct speech takes the zero particle rather than 了.
- Expressiveness: After the sentence about the people selling things, add a sensory market detail to show why the place attracts Mengzi, such as “街上到处都是叫卖声，热闹极了。”
- Expressiveness: Show what Mengzi does with the sellers instead of only saying that he plays with them, such as “孟子学着商人收钱，还跟朋友讨价还价。”
- Expressiveness: Add the mother’s private worry before her speech, such as “妈妈心想：‘这样下去，孟子会不会只想做买卖？’”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the cause of the mother’s worry, and the direct speech at the end gives the scene a strong, understandable conflict.

### Vocabulary Suggestions

### Coherence & Expressiveness

- Expressiveness: At “妈妈感觉这个地方适合他们,” add the mother’s relief to show her emotion: 妈妈终于放心了，觉得这儿很适合孟子学习。

- Expressiveness: Add a small detail about Mengzi’s study routine after “从早到晚学习”: 他每天认真看书，还常常向老师请教问题。

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the family’s move, the mother’s decision, and Mengzi’s later achievement in a logical sequence.
- Your use of direct speech gives the mother an active role in the scene.
