## Scene 1

> 这是古代的故事。孟子小的时候，他的爸爸很早就去世了。从他的家到墓地很近，所以他每天老是从早到晚去墓地跟朋友们玩玩儿。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 从他的家到墓地很近：他的家离墓地很近，Use 离 to state that two places are close in distance; 从……到…… normally introduces a route or distance range rather than directly taking 很近 in this pattern.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 这是古代的故事。孟子小的时候：古代有一个小男孩叫孟子。他小的时候，Introduce the main character with a full noun phrase on first mention, then use a pronoun when he is mentioned again immediately afterward.
- Expressiveness: After the sentence about his father, add a simple feeling to show how this affected him: “孟子很想念爸爸。”
- Expressiveness: At the cemetery scene, add a concrete detail about what he sees: “他常常看见有人来墓地祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of dialogue with his friends to make the scene livelier: “朋友说：‘我们也学他们祭拜吧！’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish the time setting and explain the cause of Mengzi’s daily visits with 所以. Your use of 从早到晚 also gives the routine a strong sense of time.

## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈觉得在墓地玩儿，害得，所以他们搬家。搬家对孟子有好的影响，他才五岁，可是每天不是写字就是帮妈妈的工作。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 害得：会带来麻烦，害得 must be followed by the person affected and a harmful result; use this phrase when an action causes someone to experience a negative outcome.
- 帮妈妈的工作：帮妈妈做事，after 帮妈妈, use a verb phrase to state what help he gives; 妈妈的工作 is a noun phrase and cannot directly express the action here.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好的 -- “搬家对孟子有好的影响”：积极的，specifies that the move supports his development in a positive way；有益的，emphasizes that the move brings him benefit.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们搬家：他们搬家了，this move is a completed event in the narrative, so the final action needs 了.
- Expressiveness: After “他的妈妈觉得在墓地玩儿会带来麻烦,” add the mother’s concern or inner thought, such as “孟母心想：这样下去可不行。”
- Expressiveness: At “每天不是写字就是帮妈妈做事,” show Mengzi’s effort with a small detail, such as “孟子认真地一笔一画写字。”

### Encouragement
- You used the pattern “不是……就是……” well to show Mengzi’s daily activities.
- You also clearly connected the move with its positive effect on Mengzi.

## Scene 3

> 他很聪明的人，难怪他成为有名的人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他很聪明的人：他很聪明，An adjective can be the predicate directly with a degree adverb; adding 人 after the adjective creates an incomplete noun phrase without a copula.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 难怪他：难怪，Because the same person was named in the preceding clause of the same sentence, use zero form rather than repeating the pronoun.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为：成为了，This describes a completed change in the past, so the verb takes 了.
- Expressiveness: After the statement about intelligence, add a concrete study detail, such as “他每天认真看书，还常常问老师问题。”
- Expressiveness: Add a reaction from his mother to show her pride, such as “妈妈看着他，高兴地说：‘你越来越懂事了！’”

### Encouragement
- You used 难怪 effectively to connect the character’s intelligence with his later achievement. The sentence also clearly shows that the character develops into someone well known.
