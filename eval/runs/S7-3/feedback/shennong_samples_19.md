## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男人觉得植物很有意思。他叫神农。有的植物吃了以后他的肚子疼死了，可是有的吃了以后很舒服。他找很多植物。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，The original repeats the time meaning because both parts refer to a time period.
- 他找很多植物：他找到了很多植物，Here the intended meaning is that Shennong found the plants; 找 means “to look for,” while 找到 means “to find.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一个男人觉得植物很有意思。他叫神农。：一个叫神农的男人觉得植物很有意思。，A character should be introduced with both a name and a full noun phrase at the first mention, rather than receiving the name only in the next sentence.
- Expressiveness: After the first sentence, add one observable detail about the plants, such as “有的叶子又大又绿，有的花开得像星星一样。” This helps the reader understand why he finds them interesting.
- Expressiveness: After the stomach-pain sentence, show his physical reaction: “神农捂着肚子，难受得不得了。” This makes the consequence more vivid.
- Expressiveness: At the end, add his purpose in a short thought: “他想：我要找出哪些植物能吃！” This gives his searching action a clear motivation.

### Encouragement
- You created a clear contrast between plants that cause pain and plants that make Shennong feel well. The completion marker in the after-eating phrases also helps show that the physical results happen after he tries the plants.

## Scene 2

> 凉山很大的山，这也找植物。一天，他找到黄色的花。他听说你跟黄色的花做茶很有用。比方说，你的头发疼死，这冰茶是药。他一喝茶就很不舒服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 凉山很大的山：凉山是一座很大的山，A noun phrase needs a linking verb and an appropriate measure word when identifying a mountain.
- 一天：有一天，Use this phrase to introduce one particular day in a narrative.
- 找到：找到了，This is a completed discovery in the past, so the verb needs 了.
- 跟黄色的花做茶：用黄色的花做的茶，Use 用 to show the material used to make the tea; 跟 does not express this relationship.
- 头发疼死：头疼得要死，头发 means “hair,” which cannot feel pain; use 头疼 for a headache, with 得要死 as a degree complement.
- 这冰茶：这杯冰茶，A demonstrative before a countable noun needs a measure word.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 有用 -- “用黄色的花做的茶很有用”：有药用价值，specifies that the tea is useful because it has a medicinal function

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 黄色的花：一朵黄色的花，This flower is being introduced for the first time, so use an indefinite full noun phrase with a measure word.
- Coherence (Reference): 这：他，Use a pronoun for the already introduced character; 这 refers to an object or place, not to Shennong.
- Coherence (Temporality): 也找植物：在找植物，Use 在 for an action that is ongoing in this part of the scene; 也 requires a previous parallel action, which is not present here.
- Expressiveness: After the yellow flowers appear, add a visual detail: “那儿开着像星星一样的黄色花，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: At “他一喝茶就很不舒服,” show his physical reaction: “他捂着肚子，疼得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Add his worried thought after he feels ill: “他想：我会不会死？”

### Encouragement
You used the useful sequence pattern “一……就……” correctly to show that the discomfort happened immediately after drinking the tea. You also introduced a clear cause-and-effect moment in the scene: he tries the tea and then feels unwell.

## Scene 3

> 糟糕，这黄色的花是夹竹桃，特别是毒。正好在这儿，灵芝药的夹竹桃他吃完了，也写一个课文的植物。谢谢神农。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 这黄色的花：这朵黄色的花，A demonstrative such as 这 needs a matching measure word before a countable noun here.
- 特别是毒：特别有毒，To describe a plant’s poisonous property, use 有毒; 毒 alone is a noun and cannot follow 特别 in this structure.
- 灵芝药的夹竹桃他吃完了：灵芝是药；夹竹桃，他吃完了，Use 是 to state that 灵芝 is a medicine. 的 cannot connect two nouns to make this predicate meaning.
- 一个课文的植物：一篇关于这些植物的文章，Use 篇 for an article, and use 关于 to show that the article is about the plants; 课文 is usually a lesson text rather than something a narrator writes.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 写：写了，The writing is narrated as a completed action after eating, so it needs 了.
- Expressiveness: After identifying the flower, add a visual detail: “这朵花像星星一样，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: Show Shennong’s physical reaction to make the danger vivid: “神农捂着肚子，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Describe the appearance of the helpful plant: “灵芝像一把小伞。”

### Encouragement
You clearly identify 夹竹桃 and communicate that it is dangerous. You also try to include both 灵芝 and Shennong’s writing, which connects several important ideas in this scene.
