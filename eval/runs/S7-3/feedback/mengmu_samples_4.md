## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，孟子和孟母住了在靠墓地的家。孩子才五岁就每天在墓地祭拜了。在那个城市，孟母从早到晚都找工作，可是找不到，害得孟母没有钱。难怪她跟孩子搬家，原来买不了房。他们搬了到别一个城市找工作。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，this expression redundantly combines a historical time period with a “time of” phrase.
- 住了在靠墓地的家：住在靠近墓地的家里，use 在 before a location phrase with 住; this sentence describes a living situation, so 了 is not needed.
- 跟孩子搬家：带着孩子搬家，跟 means “together with,” while the mother is the person taking the child to a new home.
- 别一个城市：另一个城市，另一个 is the standard expression for one other city; 别一个 is not a natural noun phrase here.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 买不了 -- “原来买不了房”：买不起，specifies that the reason for being unable to buy a home is lack of money, matching the previous sentence.
- 找不到 -- “可是找不到”：始终找不到，emphasizes that her job search continues but has no result.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 害得孟母没有钱：害得她没有钱，孟母 was named earlier in the same sentence, so a pronoun is more natural for this close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 每天在墓地祭拜了：每天在墓地祭拜，remove 了 because 每天 marks a routine action, which uses the zero particle.
- Coherence (Temporality): 从早到晚都找工作：从早到晚都在找工作，add 在 to show the ongoing dynamic action across the whole day.
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬了到：搬到了，place 了 after the resultative element 到, not between the verb and it.
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery sentence, add a small sensory detail to make the setting clearer, such as “墓地里常常有人哭，气氛很安静。”
- Expressiveness: Show what Mengzi thinks about the activity, such as “孟子觉得祭拜很有趣，就学着别人祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: Give Mengmu a short line of inner speech before moving, such as “孟母心想：‘这样住下去不行，我得带孟子搬家。’”

### Encouragement
- You used the pattern 才五岁就 correctly to show that Mengzi was very young when he began this daily activity.
- Your scene has clear cause-and-effect ideas, especially the connection between not finding work, lacking money, and moving.

## Scene 2

> 这个城市老是很吵，不是人吵架，就是都叫，害得孟子一直不能睡觉。在那儿，孟母也找不到工作。孟子和妈妈要又一个次搬了家。在第三个城市，孟母找到工作，在商店做衣服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不是人吵架，就是都叫：不是有人吵架，就是有人大声叫喊，The second alternative needs an explicit actor: 都 is an adverb meaning “all” and cannot serve as the subject of 叫; 有人 supplies the person making the noise.
- 孟子和妈妈要又一个次搬了家：孟子和妈妈要再搬一次家，For an action that has not happened yet after 要, use the order modal verb + 再 + verb + number + measure word + object; 又 and completed 了 do not fit a planned repeat.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 吵 -- “这个城市老是很吵”：嘈杂，specifies that many mixed sounds make the city unpleasantly noisy.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 孟母找到工作：孟母找到了工作，Finding a job is a completed change of state in this past sequence, so add 了 after the resultative complement 到.
- Expressiveness: Add one or two specific sounds after the first sentence so the noisy setting feels more real, for example: “外面有人大声吵架，还有汽车一直按喇叭。”
- Expressiveness: Show Meng Mu’s worry before the move with an inner thought or quotation, for example: “孟母很着急，心想：‘不行，我们得再搬一次家。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly show how the noisy environment affects Meng Zi, and the use of the harmful-result structure with 害得 is effective. The movement to the third city and Meng Mu’s new job also make the scene easy to follow.

## Scene 3

> 孟子在那个商店也找了到工作，可是不是做衣服的工作，而是喜欢的工作。孟子学习馆学了十五年，他的学习和他的旅行经验很重要，对他很多好处。他是大人的时候，他成为很有名的哲人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 找了到：找到了，The completion marker must come after the resultative complement; the correct order is verb + resultative + 了.
- 学习馆学了十五年：在学校学习了十五年，学习馆 is not the usual name for an educational place here; use 学校, and use 在 before the location.
- 对他很多好处：对他有很多好处，The phrase needs 有 to state that something brings benefits to someone.
- 他是大人的时候：他长大以后，大人 is a noun for an adult; use 长大以后 to express the change into adulthood in a narrative.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子：他，After mentioning 孟子 in the immediately preceding sentence, use a pronoun because no other person competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Reference): 他的学习和他的旅行经验：自己的学习和旅行经验，Because these experiences belong to 孟子, 自己的 makes the possessor clear and avoids repeating the pronoun.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他成为很有名的哲人：他成为了很有名的哲人，This becoming is a completed event in the past, so it needs 了.
- Expressiveness: At the long learning period, add a small daily-study detail to show what the learning looked like, for example: “他每天安静地看书学习。”
- Expressiveness: When he becomes well known, add an evaluative reaction from others, for example: “人们都很尊敬他。”
- Expressiveness: Add Meng Mu’s feeling about his success through a short quotation, for example: “孟母高兴地说：‘这儿的环境很适合孩子！’”

### Encouragement
- You used the contrast structure 不是……而是…… effectively to distinguish the kind of job Mengzi found. You also gave the learning period a clear length with 十五年, which helps the reader understand his later success.
