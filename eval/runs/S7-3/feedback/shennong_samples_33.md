## Scene 1

> 古代时候，人不知道他们能吃什么植物，常常一吃就死。但是不知道为什么，没有办法。所以有一天，神农出去试一试很多种植物，告诉人什么植物是安全什么是危险。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代时候：古代的时候，The structure ……的时候 requires 的 after a multi-syllable time expression such as 古代.
- 人：人们，This refers to a concrete group of people in the story, so 人们 is needed rather than the generic noun 人.
- 试一试很多种植物：尝试很多种植物，试一试 usually means giving one particular thing a brief try; 尝试 can naturally describe trying many kinds of plants.
- 人什么植物是安全什么是危险：人们哪些植物安全，哪些植物危险，The two parts need parallel question phrases to distinguish the plants that are safe from those that are dangerous.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 没有办法 -- “但是不知道为什么，没有办法”：束手无策，emphasizes that the people cannot think of any way to deal with the problem.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：一个叫神农的人，This is the character’s first mention, so introduce him with a full noun phrase rather than assuming the reader already knows who he is.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉人们：告诉了人们，This is the final action in a completed series of events after 有一天, so the last verb takes 了 to mark completion.
- Expressiveness: After “没有办法,” add the people’s feelings to show why the problem matters. Example: “人们又害怕又着急。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where 神农 decides to act, add a short thought or line of speech. Example: “神农想：‘我一定要找出能吃的植物。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish the danger that people face and then connect it to 神农’s action with 所以. Your use of 一吃就 also effectively shows an immediate result.

## Scene 2

> 发现有的植物对身体非常好，特别是茶，让肚子感觉又暖和又清楚。他告诉朋友：“你吃哪种植物，这就要看你的口味。比方说，你爱吃甜的，可能喜欢糖。”他最喜欢吃花，除了夹竹桃以外，什么都花想吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 清楚：舒服，清楚 describes whether information is clear, not a physical feeling in the stomach; 舒服 describes a pleasant bodily sensation.
- “你吃哪种植物，这就要看你的口味。”：“你喜欢吃哪种植物？这就要看你的口味了。”，this pattern needs an uncertain question before it, and the final 了 belongs to the fixed response pattern.
- 什么都花想吃：什么花都想吃，when expressing “every kind of flower,” the noun must come before 都 in this pattern.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好 -- “有的植物对身体非常好”：有益，specifies that the plants benefit health；对身体有好处，states the health benefit more directly.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the sentence about tea, add a sensory detail about drinking it, such as “喝起来又香又暖和。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where he talks about flowers, describe their appearance to make the scene more vivid, such as “那些花又黄又大，像天上的星星。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought or spoken reaction after he discovers tea, such as “他高兴地想：‘这种植物不但好喝，还对身体有好处！’”

### Encouragement
You clearly connect tea with a positive effect on the body, and you also use an example to explain the idea of personal taste. The phrase “除了夹竹桃以外” is a good, clear way to show an exception.

## Scene 3

> 他发现这个口味，一百岁时候，不小心吃了毒的花。一吃就开始死。但是生病时候告诉人什么植物是药，什么植物是毒。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他发现这个口味，一百岁时候：他一百岁的时候发现这种味道，The age phrase should come before the action it modifies, and a multi-syllable time phrase before 时候 needs 的. Also, 口味 usually means someone’s preference in food, while 味道 is the taste that someone senses.
- 毒的花：有毒的花，Use 有毒的 to describe a plant that contains poison; 毒的植物 is not the natural adjective form here.
- 什么植物是毒：什么植物有毒，Use 有毒 to state that a plant is poisonous; 是毒 does not fit this meaning.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不小心 -- “不小心吃了有毒的花”：误食，specifies that he accidentally ate something that should not have been eaten

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 人：人们，This refers to a concrete group whom he informs, so use 人们 rather than the generic noun 人.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉：告诉了，This is a completed action in the narrative, so add 了 after the verb.
- Expressiveness: At “有毒的花,” add a visual detail so the dangerous plant is easier to imagine: “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花。”
- Expressiveness: At “一吃就开始死,” show his physical feeling: “他一吃，肚子就疼了起来，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought at the moment he becomes ill: “他想：‘我会死吗？’”

### Encouragement
You used the cause-and-result pattern effectively in the sentence about eating the flower and becoming very ill. You also clearly tried to explain the useful knowledge the character shares with others.
