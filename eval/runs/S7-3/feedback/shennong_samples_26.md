## Scene 1

> 很久以前，有一个老男人叫神农。神农在古代每一天看到了别的小镇人生病还是不舒服，所以他决定开始找新的药。他下一天开始爬当地的山脉，找了各种各样的植物。神农试一试每个植物，知道有什么好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别的小镇人：小镇上的人，The word “别的” distinguishes other items from a stated one, but no particular town has been established for comparison. The location phrase also needs to show that the people are in the town. [teacher rule: bieren]
- 下一天：第二天，For the following day in a past narrative, this is the standard time expression. [rule: time_expression]
- 找了各种各样的植物：找到了各种各样的植物，找 means “to look for,” while 找到 means “to find.” The sentence says that he successfully found the plants. [vocab list]
- 每个植物：每一种植物，Plants are counted by types here, so the matching measure word is 种 rather than the general measure word 个. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好处 -- “知道有什么好处”：药用价值，specifies that he is learning about each plant’s possible medical use in this scene

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农在古代每一天看到：他在古代每一天看到，The name was used in the immediately previous sentence and no other character competes as the referent, so a pronoun is more natural for a close re-occurrence. [rule: redundancy_delete]
- Coherence (Reference): 神农试一试每个植物：他试一试每一种植物，The previous sentence already refers clearly to him as 他, so using a pronoun creates smoother reference between close sentences. [rule: redundancy_delete]
- Coherence (Temporality): 每一天看到了：每天看到，A habitual action uses the zero particle; 了 marks a completed event and does not fit a daily routine. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: After the people become ill, add one physical detail to show their condition, such as “有的人脸色很差，连路都走不动。”
- Expressiveness: When he begins climbing, show the effort of his journey with a short detail, such as “他走啊走啊，爬了好多山。”
- Expressiveness: After testing the plants, add his motivation or thought, such as “他想：我一定要找到能治病的植物。”

### Encouragement
You introduced the main character clearly at the beginning and kept the events in an easy-to-follow time order. The cause-and-effect connection with “所以” also clearly explains why he starts searching for medicine.

## Scene 2

> 比方说，他一次试一试有些植物的叶，吃得又新鲜又甜。煮这些叶以后，他一喝就知道他发现新的茶叶。虽然他找到了很多的新的植物，但是他的旅程有的时候特别是危险。比方说，他一吃灵芝就开始生病。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 试一试：试了一些，The reduplicated verb form is for a light, tentative plan or request; this sentence describes an action he actually carried out, so use the completed form instead. [rule: verb_reduplication]
- 吃得又新鲜又甜：吃起来又新鲜又甜，The complement marker 得 cannot introduce the taste qualities of the leaves; 吃起来 describes how the food tastes when eaten. [rule: none]
- 特别是危险：特别危险，特别是 introduces a particular item that is being emphasized, while 特别 directly modifies an adjective such as 危险. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 新的 -- “很多的新的植物”：未尝过的，specifies that these are plants he has not tasted before；陌生的，emphasizes that he does not yet know these plants.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 知道他发现了新的茶叶：知道发现了新的茶叶，The second pronoun refers to the same character within the same sentence, so Chinese naturally uses zero form rather than repeating the pronoun. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Temporality): 发现新的茶叶：发现了新的茶叶，Finding the tea leaves is presented as a completed discovery, so the verb needs 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: After “吃起来又新鲜又甜,” add a sensory detail about the drink, such as “煮出来的水有一股清香。”
- Expressiveness: After discovering the tea leaves, show his reaction with an internal thought, such as “他高兴地想：这种叶子也许可以做药！”
- Expressiveness: After “开始生病,” add a physical feeling to make the danger more vivid, such as “他捂着肚子，觉得很难受。”

### Encouragement
- You use useful story patterns such as 虽然……但是…… and 一……就…… to show contrast and a quick sequence of events. Your scene also gives a clear example of Shennong testing plants and discovering tea.

## Scene 3

> 一个爬山的男人正好看到神农的麻烦，然后给了他一些药。神农最终他的小镇回到，跟别的人分享他的发现。你认为神农的故事真的吗？这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 你认为神农的故事真的吗？：你认为神农的故事是真的吗？，A judgment about whether a story is true needs the copula before the adjective. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 麻烦 -- “看到神农的麻烦”：困境, makes clear that Shennong is in a difficult situation rather than referring to a minor inconvenience
- 药 -- “给了他一些药”：草药, specifies that the medicine is made from plants, fitting this story’s setting

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 神农最终他的小镇回到：神农最终回到了他的小镇，A completed return needs 了, and the destination follows 回到. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处。：这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处了，This fixed pattern ends with 了; here it is part of the expression rather than a marker of completed action. [teacher rule: zhe_jiu_yao_kan_le]
- Expressiveness: At the mountain scene, describe the helper so the reader can picture him, for example: “一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人正好走过来。”
- Expressiveness: After receiving the medicine, add Shennong’s physical feeling or relief, for example: “神农吃了药以后，觉得好多了，松了一口气。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech when Shennong returns, for example: “神农说：‘这些植物有的可以做药，有的不能吃。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly connected the helper’s action, Shennong’s return, and the final question with a sensible sequence. Your use of “正好” and “最终” also helps show timing in the scene.
