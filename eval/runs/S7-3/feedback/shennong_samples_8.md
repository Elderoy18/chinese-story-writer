## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个人叫神农。一天，神农正在路上走，一看很多人正在吃一些植物。一些人一吃植物就生病了，神农想帮别人。所以想知道哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 already names a time period, so adding 的时候 redundantly repeats the time meaning. [vocab list]
- 一看：看到，here the verb means “see” rather than “look at,” so it needs 看到. [vocab list]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 想知道 -- “所以想知道哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃”：弄清楚，emphasizes clarifying the difference between safe and unsafe plants.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 一看很多人正在吃一些植物。一些人一吃植物就生病了：一看到很多人正在吃一些植物，就发现一些人一吃植物就生病了，an 一V clause needs 就 before the following main action; this connector expresses an immediate sequence. [teacher rule: yi_jiu]
- Expressiveness: After the people become ill, add Shen Nong’s concern to show his motivation more vividly: 神农很着急，心想：“我一定要弄清楚哪些植物能吃。”
- Expressiveness: At the moment Shen Nong sees the people, add a small visual detail about the setting: 路边长着各种各样的植物。
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought to make his question feel more personal: “为什么他们吃了植物以后会生病呢？”

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduce the central problem of the scene: people eat plants and become ill. You also use 正在 and 一……就…… effectively to show actions in progress and a quick result.

## Scene 2

> 然后，神农开始尝很多植物，比方说他吃过生姜、芥兰和菠菜。一些植物吃真好，特别是黄瓜。但是，一些植物毒得很，特别是夹竹桃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一些植物吃真好：一些植物真好吃，Use the adjective phrase after the noun to describe that these plants taste good; “吃真好” is not a natural predicate structure. [rule: none]
- 一些植物毒得很：一些植物有毒，The adjective “毒” cannot be intensified with “得很”; use “有毒” to describe poisonous plants. [teacher rule: du_youdu]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 吃过 -- “他吃过生姜、芥兰和菠菜”：尝过，emphasizes tasting small amounts in order to test the plants’ effects.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the examples of edible plants, add one sensory detail to make the discovery more vivid, such as “生姜辣辣的，黄瓜吃起来很清爽。”
- Expressiveness: At the mention of the poisonous plant, show Shennong’s reaction to create tension, such as “他一吃夹竹桃，肚子就疼了起来。”
- Expressiveness: Add Shennong’s thought after he compares the plants, such as “神农想：哪些植物能吃，哪些不能吃呢？”

### Encouragement
You clearly contrast plants that are good to eat with plants that are dangerous, and the examples of vegetables make the scene easy to picture. The sequence word “然后” also connects this scene naturally to the earlier events.

## Scene 3

> 一个人对神农说，麻烦我们可以吃什么植物？神农说，这就要看你想不想生病了。以后，神农告诉别人一些植物能吃，一些植物不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 麻烦我们可以吃什么植物？：请问，我们可以吃什么植物？，麻烦 is a verb used when asking someone to do something; it cannot by itself introduce this question. [rule: none]
- 以后：后来，when this word begins a new sentence to mean “afterwards,” use 后来 rather than 以后. [vocab list]
- 告诉别人：告诉人们，别人 means “other people” in contrast with a particular person or group; use 人们 for people in general here. [teacher rule: bieren]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 说 -- “一个人对神农说”：问，identifies the speech specifically as a question.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: At the first question, add a small feeling or manner to show why the person needs help, such as “一个人着急地问：‘请问，我们可以吃什么植物？’”
- Expressiveness: After the explanation about plants, add a short reaction from the listeners, such as “人们听了以后，明白了哪些植物不能吃。”

### Encouragement
You created a clear question-and-answer exchange between the person and Shennong. The final contrast between edible and inedible plants is also easy for the reader to understand.
