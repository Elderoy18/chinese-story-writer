## Scene 1

> 古代，孟子跟孟母住在墓地的旁边。可是孟母注意，住在墓地的旁边影响孟子。他常常跟朋友们祭拜玩，所以孟母决定他们会搬家。这次，他们住在城市里的商店的旁边，从早到晚很吵。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 注意：注意到，When reporting that someone has realized or noticed a fact, the resultative complement shows that the noticing has reached that result.
- 祭拜玩：学着别人祭拜，祭拜 is an action and cannot be followed directly by 玩 to mean doing it playfully; this revision expresses copying the way other people perform the ritual.
- 决定他们会搬家：决定搬家，决定 takes a verb phrase for the agent’s intended action; the people moving are already clear, so a future clause with a repeated pronoun is not needed.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 吵 -- “从早到晚很吵”：嘈杂，specifies that the place has many mixed, disturbing sounds throughout the day

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 可是：∅，可是 introduces a contrast, but the mother noticing the problem follows naturally from living beside the cemetery rather than opposing the previous idea.
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery setting, add one concrete sight or sound to show why the place affects the child, such as “他看见有人在墓地哭，还学着他们祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: At the mother’s decision, add her worried thought or speech, such as “孟母心想：‘这里不适合孩子长大。’”
- Expressiveness: At the city-shop setting, describe the source of the noise more specifically, such as “商人一直叫卖，客人来来往往。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish the two settings and use the cause-and-effect connector “所以” to link the mother’s concern to her decision. Your final sentence also gives a clear atmosphere for the new neighbourhood.
