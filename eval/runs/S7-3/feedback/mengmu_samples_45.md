## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子。他们住在墓地的旁边，所以孟子老是祭拜。孟子不是祭拜，就是跟朋友聊天。他很喜欢他的城市，但是城市有才两个学校，都很小。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 有才两个学校：才有两个学校，Place + 才 + 有 + quantity expresses that the place has only that small number; 才 cannot follow 有 in this structure. [rule: none]
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 already names a time period, so adding 的时候 redundantly repeats the meaning of “time.” [vocab list]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 聊天 -- “跟朋友聊天”：闲聊，emphasizes relaxed, casual conversation between friends.
- 小 -- “两个学校，都很小”：规模都很小，specifies that the schools are small in size or capacity.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子：一个叫孟子的男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国，Introduce a character by name and role at the first mention rather than introducing him first with a general noun and naming him only afterward. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Reference): 他的城市：自己的城市，When the subject and the possessor are the same person, 自己的 makes that relationship clear. [teacher rule: ziji_possessor]
- Coherence (Reference): 但是城市才有两个学校：但是它才有两个学校，The city was mentioned immediately before, and no competing object appears, so use a pronoun for this close re-occurrence. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Expressiveness: After “他们住在墓地的旁边,” add a concrete daily detail about the setting, such as “每天都有不同的人来墓地祭拜。” This helps the reader picture why the place attracts Mengzi’s attention.
- Expressiveness: At “所以孟子老是祭拜,” add Mengzi’s feeling or reason, such as “他觉得祭拜很好玩。” This shows his thought rather than only listing his action.
- Expressiveness: Before the final sentence, add a short reaction from his mother, such as “妈妈看到以后，很担心。” This gives the scene an emotional response.

### Encouragement
- You use clear, short sentences to introduce Mengzi, his mother, their home, and his daily activities. The paired pattern “不是……就是……” is used well to show his repeated choices.

## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈不是不喜欢他们的城市，而是想孟子有很好的教育。他们搬家到有很多学校的城市。他们从早到晚走路，孟子一直抱怨。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 想孟子有很好的教育：想让孟子接受很好的教育，When the mother wants another person to do or receive something, use 想让 + person + verb phrase. “接受教育” is the natural verb-object phrase here. [rule: none]
- 他们搬家到有很多学校的城市：他们搬到了有很多学校的城市，Use 搬到 + place; 搬家 cannot directly take 到 + place in this pattern. Add 了 because the move is completed. [rule: preposition_dao_place] [rule: aspect_le_completed]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the complaint, add a small physical detail to show Mengzi’s tiredness, such as: 孟子累得满头大汗，脚也很疼。
- Expressiveness: Add a line of dialogue at the moment of complaining, such as: 孟子皱着眉头说：“妈妈，我们还要走多久？”
- Expressiveness: Show the mother’s hope more personally after the first sentence, such as: 妈妈希望孟子以后能在学校认真学习。

### Encouragement
You clearly explain the mother’s reason for moving and use the contrast structure to separate what she does not dislike from what she wants for Mengzi. The final sentence also gives Mengzi a believable reaction to the long journey.

## Scene 3

> 他难怪抱怨，他想他的朋友，因为他不要离开他的城市。他的妈妈找到孟子一个老师。跟老师孟子学最重要的文学，所以他成为哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不要离开：不想离开，In a statement about his own preference, 不想 expresses that he does not want to do something; 不要 usually expresses a prohibition or that something is unnecessary. [rule: none]
- 找到孟子一个老师：给孟子找了一个老师，Use 给 to introduce the person who receives the benefit of the action, and add 了 because finding the teacher is a completed action. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- 跟老师孟子学：孟子跟老师学，The subject should come before the phrase that identifies whom he studies with. [rule: none]
- 成为哲学家：成为了哲学家，Add 了 to show that becoming a philosopher is a completed change of state in the narrative. [rule: aspect_le_completed]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他的朋友：自己的朋友，Use 自己的 when the possessor is the same person as the subject, so it is immediately clear that these are Mencius’s own friends. [teacher rule: ziji_possessor]
- Coherence (Reference): 他的城市：自己的城市，Use 自己的 to make clear that the city belongs to the same person who does not want to leave it. [teacher rule: ziji_possessor]
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他难怪抱怨，他想他的朋友：难怪他抱怨，原来他想自己的朋友，Use 难怪 to introduce the result and 原来 to introduce the reason that explains it. [teacher rule: nanguai_yuanlai]
- Expressiveness: At the beginning, add a physical sign of Mencius’s unhappiness, such as: “孟子皱着眉头，一直抱怨。”
- Expressiveness: You could show his feelings through a short line of speech: “我不想离开这里，我会想我的朋友！”
- Expressiveness: When he studies with the teacher, add one concrete learning detail, such as: “老师教孟子读书，也教他思考问题。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show why Mencius is unhappy: he does not want to leave his friends and city. You also use a cause-and-result idea with “所以” to connect his learning to his later success.
