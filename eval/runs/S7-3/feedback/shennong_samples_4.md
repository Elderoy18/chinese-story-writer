## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个男人，他的名字是神农。他看到了有很多人不知道哪个植物好吃，也不知道什么植物对你的身体有好处。这个问题神农觉得很糟糕，所以神农去了吃每种植物。他吃了一种植物以后，就写了这个植物对你有没有好处。有的时候，神农吃了很好吃的植物。有的时候，他吃了很危险的植物。他吃完了每种植物以后，会来跟他的朋友聊到他吃的植物了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，Both parts refer to time, so using them together is redundant.
- 哪个植物：哪种植物，Plants are classified by type, so the measure word for a type or variety is needed here.
- 写了：写下了，Writing information about a plant’s effects requires the result complement 下, which emphasizes recording it; the completion marker follows the result complement.
- 聊到：聊，聊 means to talk about a topic, while 聊到 means that a topic happens to be mentioned during a conversation.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 糟糕 -- “这个问题神农觉得很糟糕”：严重，emphasizes that the problem has important harmful consequences
- 危险 -- “很危险的植物”：有害的，specifies that the plants can harm people’s bodies

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 所以神农去了吃每种植物：所以去吃了每种植物，Within the same sentence, the subject has already been named, so Chinese normally uses zero form rather than repeating the name.
- Coherence (Temporality): 有的时候，神农吃了很好吃的植物：有的时候，神农吃很好吃的植物，A habitual action introduced by 有的时候 uses the zero particle rather than 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 有的时候，他吃了很危险的植物：有的时候，他吃很危险的植物，A habitual action introduced by 有的时候 uses the zero particle rather than 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 去了吃：去吃了，With 去 followed by another verb, the completion marker comes after the action verb: 去 + V + 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 聊他吃的植物了：聊他吃的植物，The modal verb 会 describes a repeated or expected action, so the verb phrase takes the zero particle rather than sentence-final 了.
- Expressiveness: After the problem is introduced, add a concrete result to show why it matters, such as “很多人吃了有害的植物，常常生病。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where he decides to test plants, add Shen Nong’s purpose or thought: “神农想：我要弄清楚哪些植物能吃。”
- Expressiveness: When contrasting the plants, add a physical reaction to make the danger vivid: “他一吃那种植物，肚子就疼了起来。”

### Encouragement
You clearly introduce Shen Nong at the beginning and use time-ordering phrases such as “以后” effectively. Your contrast between beneficial and dangerous plants also gives the scene a clear focus.

- 哪个植物：哪种植物，Use the measure word for a type of plant when asking which plant is beneficial.
- 一个别的人：另一个人，This is another new questioner, so “另一个人” is the natural expression.
- 哪个植物：哪种植物，Use the measure word for a type of plant when asking which plant is harmful.

- 我的脑：我的大脑，“脑” is usually used inside compound words; “大脑” clearly names the body part affected.
- 一个灵芝：一朵灵芝，An individual lingzhi is normally counted with “朵,” rather than the general measure word “个.”

- 好 -- “我一吃这个灵芝就好了”：痊愈了，makes clear that the illness completely recovered.
- 旁边 -- “一个灵芝和一个男人正好在我的旁边”：身边，more specifically suggests that they were very close to the speaker.
- 有坏处 -- “有的植物对你的健康有坏处”：有害，states more directly that the plants damage health.

### Encouragement
