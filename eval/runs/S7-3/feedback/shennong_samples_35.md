## Scene 1

> 在古代，很多医生不知道怎么用药。所以他们找到了很多植物对药。神农先看到很多病人，他们吃很多植物对药。但是我觉得他们的吃的植物，让他们病人。神农觉得这个植物可能特别是毒。所以他想找一个植物对药，他们可以吃对药。正好，他找到了一把花，他想把花放在茶里，对药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很多植物对药：很多能做药的植物，The phrase “对药” does not describe a plant that can be used as medicine; “能做药的植物” clearly expresses that function. [rule: none]
- 植物对药：能做药的植物，Use “能做药的植物” for plants that patients eat as medicine; “对药” is not a suitable predicate here. [rule: none]
- 他们的吃的植物：他们吃的植物，When a verb phrase modifies a noun, the subject comes directly before the verb: “他们吃的植物”; the extra “的” after “他们” is not used. [rule: none]
- 让他们病人：让他们生病，After “让 + person,” use a verb phrase describing the result. “病人” is a noun meaning a patient, while “生病” is the needed verb phrase. [rule: none]
- 特别是毒：特别有毒，“毒” is a noun, so it cannot follow “特别” as an adjective. “有毒” means poisonous and can be modified by “特别.” [vocab list]
- 找一个植物对药：找一种能做药的植物，Use “一种” when referring to one kind of plant, and use “能做药的植物” rather than “植物对药.” [rule: none]
- 吃对药：吃，“对药” cannot follow “吃” in this meaning; “吃” already states that people can eat the plant. [rule: none]
- 把花放在茶里，对药：用花和茶做药，“对药” is not a verb phrase for making medicine. “做药” means to make or use something as medicine. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农先看到很多病人：一个叫神农的人先看到了很多病人，On a character’s first mention, introduce the person with a name and a brief noun phrase rather than assuming the reader already knows who he is. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Reference): 但是我觉得：但是他觉得，The narration suddenly shifts to an unintroduced first-person narrator. Since the immediately preceding character is 神农, use the pronoun “他” to refer back to him. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Reference): 神农觉得这个植物：他觉得这些植物，Use a pronoun for the recently mentioned character, and use “这些植物” because the previous sentence discusses several plants rather than one clearly identified plant. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Reference): 他们可以吃：人们可以吃，Both doctors and patients have been mentioned, so “他们” has an unclear referent. “人们” clearly means people in general. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Temporality): 神农先看到很多病人：神农先看到了很多病人，This is a completed event in the narrative, so “看到” needs the completion marker “了.” [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们吃很多能做药的植物：他们吃了很多能做药的植物，The patients’ eating is presented as a completed past action, so add “了” after the verb. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: At the point where 神农 sees the patients, add one visible symptom to show their condition, such as “有的病人捂着肚子，脸色很难看.”
- Expressiveness: When 神农 finds the flowers, describe their appearance so that the reader can picture why he notices them, such as “花是黄色的，像小星星一样，又大又漂亮.”
- Expressiveness: Add 神农’s worried thought after he realizes the plants may be poisonous, such as “这些植物会不会害人？”

### Encouragement
- You established the historical setting clearly with “在古代,” and you tried to connect events with cause-and-effect words such as “所以.”
- You also used several useful story actions, including “找到了” and “想把花放在茶里,” which helps move the scene forward.

## Scene 2

> 他不知道如果这把花对你的身体好，这就要看如果这把花毒不毒了。神农想找别的植物，但是这很麻烦。正好，他找到了别的花。他一吃花，很快就生病了。他的肚子疼死了。神农不知道他吃不吃灵芝或者夹竹桃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不知道如果这把花对你的身体好：不知道这把花对自己的身体好不好，An embedded yes/no question after a verb such as 知道 uses the A-not-A form, and 自己的 shows that the body belongs to the same person who is thinking. [rule: a_not_a_question] [teacher rule: ziji_possessor]
- 看如果这把花毒不毒：看这把花有没有毒，The part after 这就要看 must be a question form; 有没有毒 asks whether the flower is poisonous, while 如果 does not introduce this kind of embedded question. [rule: zhe_jiu_yao_kan] [teacher rule: du_youdu]
- 吃不吃灵芝或者夹竹桃：吃的是灵芝还是夹竹桃，When choosing between two possibilities in an embedded question, use 是……还是…… rather than A-not-A plus 或者. [rule: a_not_a_question]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 找 -- “神农想找别的植物”：寻找, emphasizes a deliberate, continuing search for plants；探寻, emphasizes exploring unknown plants to discover their properties.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 花：这种花，There are two different mentions of flowers nearby, so the general noun 花 does not clearly identify which flower made him ill; use 这种花 to refer back to the flower he has just found. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Reference): 他：zero form, In the same sentence, 神农 is already the subject, so omit the repeated pronoun: 神农不知道吃的是灵芝还是夹竹桃. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Expressiveness: After the illness, add a physical reaction to show how severe the pain feels: “他在地上躺着，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: When introducing the flower, add a simple visual detail so the setting is easier to imagine: “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought after the stomach pain to show the character’s fear: “他想：我会死吗？”

### Encouragement
- You used 找到了 well to show that the search was completed, and the pattern 一……就…… clearly connects eating the flower with becoming ill. Your scene also gives a strong physical result with 肚子疼死了.

## Scene 3

> 别的人给了神农一个灵芝。这个植物可以让神农更好。比方说，他会觉得更健康。吃灵芝以后，他可以再走。现在他觉得更好。最后，神农找到了很多植物，他可以用对药。这些植物让神农很高兴。神农想跟很多人分享他的新药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别的人给了神农一个灵芝：别人给了神农一个灵芝，别人 refers to other people as a group; 别的 is used before a noun, so 别的人 is not the natural subject form here. [teacher rule: bieren]
- 他可以用对药：可以用来做药，use 用来 + verb to state a purpose; 用对药 is not a valid verb phrase. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 更好 -- “现在他觉得更好”：好一些, specifies that his condition has improved by a modest amount.
- 高兴 -- “这些植物让神农很高兴”：欣慰, shows that he feels pleased because his discoveries can help people.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 这个植物可以让神农更好：它可以让神农更好，after 灵芝 was named in the previous sentence, use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Reference): 神农想跟很多人分享他的新药：他想跟很多人分享他的新药，神农 was mentioned in the immediately preceding sentence, so a pronoun is more natural for a close re-occurrence. [rule: redundancy_delete]
- Expressiveness: After “吃灵芝以后”, add a physical detail showing his recovery: “神农站起来，肚子不那么疼了。”
- Expressiveness: After “这些植物让神农很高兴”, add his positive thought: “神农想：这些植物可以帮助很多病人！”
- Expressiveness: Describe the medicinal plants more specifically when he finds them: “他找到了一些能让病人好起来的植物。”

### Encouragement
- You use time markers such as “吃灵芝以后”, “现在”, and “最后” to make the events easy to follow. You also clearly show that 神农 feels better and wants to help other people.
