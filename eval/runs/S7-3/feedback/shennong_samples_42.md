## Scene 1

> 古代的时候有一个人叫神农。他需要当上医，一看别的人就想帮助他们。但是，他有一个问题，他不知道什么东西能做药。神农听说一个远远的地方有一种植物，什么病人吃都会觉得身体更好。神农三个月走去，找到有植物的地方，他看到一个很漂亮的植物，又香又好看。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，The original repeats the time meaning because both parts refer to a time period. [vocab list]
- 当上医：当上医生，The occupation noun needs the full word for a medical doctor in this context. [rule: none]
- 别的人：别人，Use this form for other people in general when no specific person is being contrasted. [teacher rule: bieren]
- 看：看到，Here the meaning is “see,” so the resultative form is needed rather than the verb meaning “look at.” [vocab list]
- 一个很漂亮的植物：一株很漂亮的植物，Plants take the matching measure word rather than the general measure word. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 香 -- “又香又好看”：芬芳，describes a pleasant flower-like fragrance more vividly in this description.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他不知道：不知道，The same character has already been named in the immediately preceding clause, so use zero form rather than repeating the pronoun. [rule: redundancy_delete]
- Coherence (Reference): 有植物的地方：有这种植物的地方，Use the demonstrative noun phrase to refer clearly back to the particular plant introduced in the previous sentence. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Reference): ，他看到：，看到，The subject remains the same within this sentence, so zero form is more natural than repeating the pronoun. [rule: redundancy_delete]
- Coherence (Temporality): 神农三个月走去：神农走了三个月，A duration follows the verb, and the completed three-month journey takes 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 找到：找到了，Finding the place is a completed event in the narrative, so add 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 看到：看到了，The seeing event is completed; with this resultative verb, 了 follows the resultative part. [teacher rule: le_placement]
- Expressiveness: At the description of the plant, add its color and shape: “黄色的花像星星一样，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: After seeing the plant, add Shennong’s hopeful thought: “神农很高兴，觉得这种植物可能可以做药。”

### Encouragement
You introduced Shennong clearly at the beginning and gave him a meaningful goal. The plant description already uses two sensory details, which makes the setting easier to picture.

## Scene 2

> 他觉得，这种一定是做药的花。原来他想错了。吃了以后，神农快生病。 这时一个穿着蓝色衣服的人帮神农，快给他一种红的灵芝。神农吃了以后，他没有病，想起来以前一个人告诉他，灵芝对身体有好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 快生病：很快就生病了，In a past narrative, “很快” describes rapid occurrence, and the completed change into illness needs 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- 快给他一种红的灵芝：很快给了他一种红的灵芝，“快” alone usually means “soon” or functions as a command; “很快” describes the speed of a past action, and the completed giving action needs 给了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 没有病：没有病了，After the medicine is eaten, the state changes from being ill to no longer being ill, so sentence-final 了 marks that change of state. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: At the moment when the illness begins, add a physical symptom to make the danger clearer, for example: “他的肚子疼得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: When the blue-clothed person helps, describe the medicine’s appearance, for example: “他拿起了一种像伞一样的灵芝。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought when Shennong becomes ill to show his fear, for example: “神农想：我会死吗？”

### Encouragement
- You used “原来” well to show that Shennong’s first belief was mistaken, and “这时” introduces the helper at an effective moment.
- Your scene also has a clear action sequence: Shennong eats the flower, becomes ill, receives help, and remembers what he learned.
