## Scene 1

> 很久以前，古代的人很不知道什么植物能吃，也什么植物不能吃。所以很多人常常吃不安全的植物，吃不安全的植物对你的身体有坏处，很麻烦的情况。但是一个人，神农，要解决这个问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很不知道：不知道，A negated knowledge verb should not be modified by 很; use the plain negative form here. [rule: none]
- 古代的人：古代的人们，This refers to a concrete group of people in ancient times, so 人们 is needed rather than the generic noun 人. [teacher rule: ren_renmen]
- 也什么植物不能吃：也不知道什么植物不能吃，Also must be followed by a verb phrase; repeating 不知道 makes the second part parallel with the first. [teacher rule: ye_vp]
- 很麻烦的情况：是一种很麻烦的情况，The noun phrase needs 是 to function as the predicate after the preceding statement. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不安全 -- “不安全的植物”：有毒的，specifies that the plants are dangerous because they contain poison
- 有坏处 -- “对你的身体有坏处”：会伤害身体，states the physical harm more directly and vividly

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 吃不安全的植物对你的身体有坏处：这对你的身体有坏处，The action has just been mentioned, so use 这 for close re-occurrence instead of repeating the full phrase. [rule: none]
- Coherence (Reference): 一个人，神农：有一个叫神农的人，A character’s first mention should introduce the name clearly together with a full noun phrase. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Expressiveness: At the point where the plants are described as dangerous, make the result more concrete with a short detail, such as “很多人吃了有毒的植物以后，常常生病。”
- Expressiveness: When introducing 神农, add his determination through an inner thought or quotation, such as “神农想：我一定要找出能吃的植物。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish the main problem of the scene: people do not know which plants are safe to eat.
- Your use of 所以 and 但是 already helps show both the cause of the problem and the contrast introduced by 神农.

## Scene 2

> 他又聪明又努力，旅行很远找到很多植物。比方说，灵芝和夹竹桃。他一吃各种各样的植物就知道能不能吃这些植物。然后，神农写他的主意。他喜欢植物的味道，特别是花。神农遇到了一个黄色的花，一吃就觉得不舒服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 旅行很远找到很多植物：到很远的地方旅行，找到了很多植物，Use a place phrase with “旅行”; in a series of completed actions, only the final verb takes 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- 写他的主意：写下了自己的主意，写下 is the natural verb for recording an idea, and 自己的 clearly shows that the ideas belong to the subject; 了 marks the completed narrative action. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- 一个黄色的花：一朵黄色的花，朵 is the matching measure word for a flower; 个 is too generic here. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农写下了自己的主意：他写下了自己的主意，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the same character was named or referred to in the preceding sentence and no other character competes. [rule: redundancy_delete]
- Coherence (Reference): 神农遇到了一朵黄色的花：他遇到了一朵黄色的花，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence of the same character. [rule: redundancy_delete]
- Expressiveness: At the travel moment, add a small detail about the journey, such as “他走啊走啊，去了很多高山。”
- Expressiveness: When he becomes unwell, describe the physical feeling more vividly, such as “他的肚子疼了起来，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Add his worried thought after eating the flower, such as “他想：我会死吗？”

### Encouragement
- You used the “一……就……” pattern correctly to show an immediate result.
- You also chose clear plant names, which makes the scene easy for the reader to picture.

## Scene 3

> 发现了夹竹桃有毒。别人看到他，很快给他吃一个灵芝。神农一吃就觉得比较好。然后，他继续写他发现的信息。会告诉他的朋友哪一个植物能不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别人：一个男人，别人 distinguishes other people from a speaker or a specified person; here a new character should be introduced with a full noun phrase. [teacher rule: bieren]
- 写他发现的信息：记下他发现的信息，写 alone means “write,” while 记下 means to record information so it is kept. [vocab list]
- 哪一个植物能不能吃：哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃，哪一个 asks about one specific item, but the sentence needs to state the two categories of plants: edible and not edible. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 给他吃一个灵芝：给他吃了一个灵芝，this is a completed action, so 了 marks its completion. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: When the man notices him, add a visual detail to introduce the helper: “一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人走过来。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where the medicine works, describe Shen Nong’s physical feeling more clearly: “神农的肚子不那么疼了。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought to show his relief: “神农想：我终于好多了！”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the important sequence: Shen Nong learns that the flower is dangerous, receives help, feels better, and records what he learned.
- Your use of 然后 helps the events move forward in a clear order.
