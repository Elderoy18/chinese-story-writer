## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个男孩名字叫孟子。孟子和孟子的妈妈两个人住在很穷的农村。他们住的地方旁边是个墓地，墓地老是有很多人祭拜。因为人们在祭拜，他们一直在哭。孟子和孟子的妈妈住在这里才一个星期就觉得受不了了，而且害得孟子的学习。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 already functions as a time expression, so adding 的时候 repeats the time meaning unnecessarily.
- 害得孟子的学习：这里的环境还影响了孟子的学习，害得 must be followed by a person and a harmful result clause; it cannot directly take a noun phrase such as 孟子的学习.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 地方 -- “他们住的地方旁边是个墓地”：住处，more specifically means the place where the family lives

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子的妈妈两个人：孟子和妈妈两个人，Within the same noun phrase, use zero form rather than repeating Mengzi’s name to show that the mother belongs to the already-mentioned character.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子的妈妈住在这里：孟子和他妈妈住在这里，After naming Mengzi, use a pronoun for the close re-occurrence of the possessor instead of repeating his name.
- Coherence (Temporality): 住在这里才一个星期：在这里住了才一个星期，The living period is completed before the family reaches the point of feeling unable to endure the place, so the verb needs 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为人们在祭拜，他们一直在哭：因为人们在祭拜，所以他们一直在哭，Use 因为……所以…… to mark the cause-and-effect relationship explicitly.
- Expressiveness: At the cemetery description, add one atmosphere detail, such as “冷冷清清的墓地” or “树叶沙沙响,” to make the setting more vivid.
- Expressiveness: After the crying people, add Mengzi’s reaction with a phrase such as “觉得很好奇” to show what he thinks about what he sees.
- Expressiveness: At the ending, show the mother’s worry through short dialogue elements such as “这里太吵了” and “影响孟子学习.”

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduce the setting and the two main family members at the beginning. The cemetery and the crying worshippers also create a strong, serious atmosphere for this scene.

## Scene 2

> 孟子的妈妈觉得孟子取得良好的教育是很重要的，所以孟子的妈妈认为这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好，就打算跟孟子搬家。孟子的妈妈带孟子不是搬到城市，而是搬到了孟子老师的家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 取得良好的教育：受到良好的教育，取得 is used for obtaining an achievement or result, while education is something a person receives.
- 不是搬到城市，而是：，This contrast does not correct an earlier or conventional mistaken claim, so the “not X but Y” structure is not needed here.
- 跟孟子搬家：带着孟子搬家，跟孟子搬家 suggests that the mother and Mengzi move together as equal participants; 带着孟子搬家 correctly shows that the mother takes her child with her.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不太好 -- “这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好”：不利于儿子的教育，states more precisely that this environment has a negative effect on his education；不适合儿子学习，makes clear that the place is unsuitable for studying

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 所以孟子的妈妈认为：所以她认为，use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the mother was named earlier in the same sentence and no other female character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子的妈妈带孟子：她带孟子，use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence because the mother remains the clear subject from the previous sentence.
- Expressiveness: After the decision to move, add the mother’s worried feeling and inner thought, for example: “她很着急，心想：‘住在这里会影响孟子的学习。’”
- Expressiveness: Give the place a concrete detail to show why it is unsuitable for education, for example: “墓地旁边每天都有人哭，孟子很难安静地学习。”

### Encouragement
You clearly connect the mother’s belief about education to her decision to move by using a cause-and-effect structure. You also make the mother’s concern for Mengzi’s education easy for the reader to understand.
