## Scene 1

> 这是古代的故事。孟子小的时候，他的爸爸很早就去世了。从他的家到墓地很近，所以他每天老是从早到晚去墓地跟朋友们玩玩儿。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 从他的家到墓地很近：他的家离墓地很近，The distance pattern uses 离 + place + 很近; 从……到…… describes a route or range and does not fit this predicate. [vocab list]
- 每天老是从早到晚去墓地跟朋友们玩玩儿：每天都从早到晚在墓地跟朋友们玩儿，From early to late describes the continuing activity of playing, so use 在墓地 to locate that activity rather than making 去 the action that lasts all day. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子小的时候，他的爸爸很早就去世了：有一个小男孩叫孟子。他小的时候，爸爸很早就去世了，On a character’s first mention, introduce the character with a full noun phrase, such as name plus identity, before using pronouns. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Expressiveness: After the mention of the cemetery, add one sensory detail to make the setting clearer, such as “墓地里常常传来哭声，还有人带着花来祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where Mengzi plays with his friends, show what he thinks is interesting: “孟子觉得大人祭拜的样子很有趣，就跟朋友学着他们的动作。”

### Encouragement
- You used 所以 effectively to show why the nearby cemetery affects Mengzi’s daily life.
- The completed action marker in 去世了 is used well to describe his father’s death.

## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈觉得在墓地玩儿，害得，所以他们搬家。搬家对孟子有好的影响，他才五岁，可是每天不是写字就是帮妈妈的工作。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在墓地玩儿，害得：孟子在墓地玩儿，害得她很担心，害得 must be followed by the person affected and a harmful result; here it needs to state what the cemetery play caused for the mother. [teacher rule: haide_structure]
- 他们搬家：他们搬家了，the move is presented as a completed event, so add 了 after the verb. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- 帮妈妈的工作：帮妈妈干活，帮妈妈的工作 is not a complete action phrase here; after 帮妈妈, use a verb phrase such as 干活 so it can parallel 写字 in the 不是……就是…… structure. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好的 -- “搬家对孟子有好的影响”：积极的，specifies that the effect is beneficial rather than merely “good” in a general way.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: At the cemetery-play moment, add one concrete action to show what Mengzi does there, such as “孟子跟朋友学着别人祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: Show the mother’s concern before the move with a thought or feeling, such as “妈妈很担心，心想：‘不能让孟子一直在这儿玩。’”

### Encouragement
- You used the contrast between being very young and having daily responsibilities effectively with 才五岁 and 不是……就是…….
- Your scene also clearly connects the mother’s concern with the family’s decision to move.

## Scene 3

> 他很聪明的人，难怪他成为有名的人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他很聪明的人：他是一个很聪明的人，When the predicate is a noun phrase, use 是 before it; an adjective alone can be the predicate in 他很聪明. [rule: adjective_predicate]
- 成为有名的人：成为了有名的人，This final achievement is presented as completed, so add 了 after the verb. [rule: aspect_le_completed]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 难怪他成为：难怪成为，Since the same person is already the subject in this sentence and no other person competes, omit the repeated pronoun. [rule: none]
- Expressiveness: At the conclusion, add one concrete learning detail to show why he was intelligent, such as: “他认真读书，常常向老师请教。”
- Expressiveness: You could add a short public reaction after the achievement, such as: “人们都说：‘孟子真了不起！’”

### Encouragement
You used 难怪 to create a clear connection between his intelligence and later success. The final sentence also gives the scene a strong concluding idea.
