## Scene 1

> 孟子小的时候，从早到晚都跟朋友玩。孟子和他的朋友喜欢玩在墓地里，装祭拜。孟子五岁的时候，跟妈妈搬家。他们搬家不是因为孟子玩得太多，而是因为妈妈认为他们的家离墓地太近。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 喜欢玩在墓地里：喜欢在墓地里玩，The location phrase should normally come before the activity verb it describes. [rule: none]
- 装祭拜：假装祭拜，装 cannot directly introduce this action here; 假装 can be followed by an action to mean pretending to do it. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和他的朋友：他和朋友，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the referent was named in the preceding sentence and no competing person makes it unclear. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬家：搬家了，This is a completed moving event at a specific time in the past, so it needs 了 to mark completion. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery activity, add Mengzi’s feeling to show why he does it, for example: “孟子觉得祭拜很好玩。”
- Expressiveness: At the mention of the cemetery, add a reaction from the real visitors, for example: “来祭拜的人看见他们，很生气。”
- Expressiveness: After the mother’s reason for moving, add her inner thought or speech, for example: “妈妈心想：‘再住在这儿，麻烦会越来越多。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish Mengzi’s childhood habit and give a logical reason for the family’s move. The contrast structure “不是……而是……” is used effectively to explain the mother’s decision.

## Scene 2

> 妈妈认为，住离墓地太近，害得孟子的心气。孟子和孟母搬到子思老师的家，跟子思老师住。孟母帮子思打扫房间和做饭。子思帮孟母教她的儿子。因为孟子现在没有朋友，他老是读书。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 住离墓地太近：住得离墓地太近，Use 得 after the verb to introduce the degree/distance complement. [rule: none]
- 害得孟子的心气：害得孟子心情不好，The 害得 structure needs the affected person followed by a harmful result; 心气 is not a result describing how孟子 was harmed. [teacher rule: haide_structure]
- 老是读书：一直读书，老是 has a negative, complaining tone, while this sentence neutrally describes a routine. [rule: laoshi_negative_connotation]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 朋友 -- “孟子现在没有朋友”：玩伴，specifies that he has no companions to play with, which fits a young child’s situation.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 跟子思老师住：跟他住，After 子思老师 has just been named, use a pronoun for this close re-occurrence; 他 clearly refers to the teacher here. [rule: none]
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为孟子现在没有朋友，他：因为孟子现在没有朋友，所以他，Use the paired connector 因为……所以…… to make the cause-and-effect relationship explicit. [rule: coherence_connectives]
- Expressiveness: After the first sentence, add孟母’s worried reaction to show why she wants a different home, for example: “孟母皱着眉头，心里很着急。”
- Expressiveness: After mentioning that孟子 has no friends, add his feeling to make the situation more vivid, for example: “孟子觉得很孤单，只好一个人看书。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the new living arrangement and the ways the adults help each other. The final sentence also gives a clear reason for why孟子 spends time reading.

## Scene 3

> 子思认为，对孩子来说，读书最重要，特别是学哲学。孟子不是练习写文章，就是看哲学书。孟子从五岁一直学哲学。他才五岁就学哲学，难怪他成为很有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 从五岁一直学哲学：从五岁开始一直学哲学，A starting age introduced with 从 needs 开始 to mark when the ongoing study began. [teacher rule: cong_kaishi]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子从五岁一直学哲学：他从五岁开始一直学哲学，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the same character was named in the previous sentence and no competing referent is present. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为很有名的哲学家：成为了很有名的哲学家，This is a completed change of state in the narrative, so the verb needs 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: After the study routine, add the child’s feeling to show motivation: “孟子觉得哲学很有意思。”
- Expressiveness: You could add a short line of praise from the teacher after the first sentence: “子思说：‘你很爱学习。’”

### Encouragement
- You used the paired structure “不是……就是……” correctly to show the child’s regular study activities. The age pattern “才五岁就……” also clearly emphasizes how early the learning began.
