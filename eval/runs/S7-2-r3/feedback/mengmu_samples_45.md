## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子。他们住在墓地的旁边，所以孟子老是祭拜。孟子不是祭拜，就是跟朋友聊天。他很喜欢他的城市，但是城市有才两个学校，都很小。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，this phrase redundantly combines a time period with another “time of” expression. [vocab list]
- 城市有才两个学校：城市才有两个学校，才 must come before the verb 有 when it limits the number that exists. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 小 -- “都很小”：规模都很小，specifies that the schools are small in scale rather than only physically small.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子：一个叫孟子的男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国，at first mention, introduce the main character with both a name and a noun phrase rather than naming him only in the next sentence. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Reference): 他很喜欢他的城市：他很喜欢自己的城市，when the possessor is the same person as the subject, use 自己的 to make that relationship clear. [teacher rule: ziji_possessor]
- Expressiveness: After the sentence about the cemetery, add a concrete detail about what Mengzi sees there, such as “每天都有不同的人来墓地祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: After “孟子老是祭拜”, add his thought or feeling to explain his interest, such as “孟子觉得祭拜很有趣。”
- Expressiveness: After describing the small schools, add one contrasting detail about the setting, such as “学校不大，可是很安静。”

### Encouragement
You used both “所以” and “但是” to connect ideas, which helps the scene move clearly. The pattern “不是……就是……” is also a strong way to show Mengzi’s repeated activities.

## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈不是不喜欢他们的城市，而是想孟子有很好的教育。他们搬家到有很多学校的城市。他们从早到晚走路，孟子一直抱怨。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 想孟子有很好的教育：想让孟子接受很好的教育，When expressing a wish for another person to receive or do something, use 想让 + person + verb phrase; 想 cannot directly take this noun phrase in this meaning. [rule: none]
- 他们搬家到有很多学校的城市：他们搬到了有很多学校的城市，Use 搬到 + place for moving to a destination, and add 了 because the move is presented as completed. [rule: preposition_dao_place] [rule: aspect_le_completed]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他们从早到晚走路，孟子一直抱怨：因为他们从早到晚走路，所以孟子一直抱怨，Use 因为……所以…… to make the cause-and-effect relationship clear: the long walk causes the complaint. [rule: coherence_connectives]
- Expressiveness: After the move, add a concrete detail about the new city’s atmosphere, such as “城市里从早到晚都很热闹，街上有很多商人卖东西。”
- Expressiveness: Show Mengzi’s feeling through quoted speech at “孟子一直抱怨,” such as “孟子说：‘走路太累了，我的脚都疼了！’”
- Expressiveness: Add the mother’s inner worry after the complaint, such as “妈妈心想：‘这个城市的学校很多，希望孟子能好好学习。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly explain the mother’s reason for moving, and the final sentence gives Mengzi a believable reaction to the long journey.

## Scene 3

> 他难怪抱怨，他想他的朋友，因为他不要离开他的城市。他的妈妈找到孟子一个老师。跟老师孟子学最重要的文学，所以他成为哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不要离开：不想离开，Use the modal verb for “does not want to” when describing his wish; 不要 is normally used for an instruction or refusal. [rule: none]
- 找到孟子一个老师：给孟子找一个老师，Put the beneficiary after 给 and before the verb phrase: this means finding a teacher for Mencius. [rule: none]
- 跟老师孟子学：孟子跟老师学，The subject should come before the phrase beginning with 跟老师. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他的朋友：自己的朋友，Because the subject and the possessor are the same person, use 自己的 to make clear that Mencius misses his own friends. [teacher rule: ziji_possessor]
- Coherence (Reference): 他的城市：自己的城市，Because the city belongs to the subject of this clause, 自己的 makes the possession clear. [teacher rule: ziji_possessor]
- Coherence (Temporality): 找到：找到了，This reports a completed successful action, so add 了 after the resultative complement. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为：成为了，Becoming a philosopher is a completed change of state in this past narrative, so use 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他难怪抱怨：难怪他抱怨，原来，Place 难怪 before the result and use 原来 to introduce the explanation for his complaint. [teacher rule: nanguai_yuanlai]
- Expressiveness: After the complaint, add a small physical detail to show Mencius’s feelings, such as “孟子皱着眉头，低声说：‘我真想自己的朋友。’”
- Expressiveness: When he studies with the teacher, show how he learns rather than only stating the event, such as “孟子认真地听老师讲课，还常常问问题。”
- Expressiveness: After his achievement, add his mother’s reaction, such as “孟母看到孟子成为哲学家，高兴地笑了。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect Mencius’s learning with his later achievement by using 所以. You also give a reason for his complaint, which helps the reader understand his feelings.
