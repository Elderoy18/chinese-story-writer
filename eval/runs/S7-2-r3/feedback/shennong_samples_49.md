## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有很多人不知道什么植物能吃，什么植物不能吃。有很多人吃植物以后就吃坏肚子了，因为他们吃了毒。那个时候，有一个老人叫神农，他什么植物都觉得好吃，不怕生病，不怕毒。他希望别人也可以吃好吃的植物。可是他知道，如果他们还怕吃毒，不会吃他喜欢的植物。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 already functions as a time expression, so adding 的时候 redundantly repeats the meaning of “time.” [teacher rule: gudai_de_shihou_why]
- 吃了毒：吃了有毒的植物，毒 is a noun meaning “poison,” while this sentence needs to name the plants that caused the illness. [vocab list]
- 怕吃毒：怕吃有毒的植物，this wording makes clear that the people fear eating poisonous plants, rather than treating 毒 as the thing they would deliberately eat. [vocab list]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好吃 -- “他什么植物都觉得好吃”：可口，specifically describes food or plants as pleasant to eat；味道鲜美，adds a clearer detail about the taste.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: At the description of people becoming ill, add one physical symptom to make the result more vivid, such as “很多人肚子疼得很厉害。”
- Expressiveness: When introducing 神农, add his purpose or determination, such as “神农决定找出哪些植物能吃。”
- Expressiveness: After 神农 hopes to help others, add a short thought to show his caring attitude, such as “神农想：我要帮助大家！”

### Encouragement
You clearly introduce the central problem of not knowing which plants are safe to eat, and you use the contrast between edible and inedible plants effectively. Your introduction of 神农 also gives him a clear caring motivation.

## Scene 2

> 所以，他上山下山找植物，吃了很多样子的植物。他是第一人，找到了茶。他把所有的植物写下来了。他一直都吃了，他连毒都能吃。可是他还没吃了一个叫夹竹桃的植物。他找到了那个像星星的植物，觉得很漂亮，马上就吃下了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很多样子的植物：很多种植物，样子 describes an appearance, while 种 is the measure word used for different kinds of plants. [rule: none]
- 他是第一人，找到了茶：他是第一个找到茶的人，第一 needs the measure word 个 here, and the action that makes him “first” should modify 人. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 上山下山 -- “他上山下山找植物”：翻山越岭，describes travelling across many mountains and makes his search sound more strenuous and adventurous.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 他一直都吃了：他一直都吃，The habitual action marked by 一直 uses the zero particle; 了 would incorrectly present the ongoing habit as completed. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 还没吃了：还没吃过，After 没, do not use completion marker 了. The experiential marker 过 fits the meaning that he has not tried this plant before. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: At the point where he sees the plant, add its colour and shape to make the description more vivid: “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花。”
- Expressiveness: After he eats it, add an immediate physical feeling to create suspense: “他刚吃下去，肚子就疼了起来。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought to show his fear when the pain begins: “他想：我会不会生病？”

### Encouragement
You used several effective narrative actions, including “上山下山”, “找到了”, and “马上就吃下了”, which make the scene move forward clearly. The comparison “像星星的植物” is also a strong visual detail.

## Scene 3

> 可是夹竹桃的毒很重，让他肚子很痛。他在地上躺着，觉得他快会死了。可是正好有别人找到他了，给了他一个像伞的植物，叫灵芝。他吃灵芝以后就觉得很好，不再生病了。他测量起来了，像星星的植物是毒，像伞的植物是药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 快会死了：快要死了，快要 expresses that a change is imminent; 会 is not needed here. [rule: none]
- 别人：一个人，别人 distinguishes other people from a speaker or a previously identified group, but no such group has been established here. [teacher rule: bieren]
- 像伞的植物：像伞一样的植物，use 一样 to complete this comparison meaning “a plant that looks like an umbrella.” [rule: none]
- 测量起来了：明白了，测量 means measuring a physical size or amount, so it cannot introduce his conclusion about which plants are poisonous or medicinal. [rule: none]
- 像星星的：像星星一样的，use 一样 to complete the comparison. [rule: none]
- 是毒：有毒，有毒 describes that a plant contains poison; 是毒 does not work as the predicate here. [vocab list]
- 像伞的：像伞一样的，use 一样 to complete the comparison. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 毒很重 -- “夹竹桃的毒很重”：毒性很强，specifies that the plant’s toxicity is strong.
- 很痛 -- “让他肚子很痛”：疼得厉害，shows the severity of the pain more vividly.
- 很好 -- “就觉得很好”：好多了，more precisely shows that he has improved after eating the medicine.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他：自己，when the person who feels something and the person who may die are the same person, 自己 makes that reference clear. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃灵芝以后：吃了灵芝以后，eating the plant is a completed action before the later feeling of recovery, so it needs 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: At “让他肚子很痛,” add a visible reaction: “他疼得在地上打滚，脸色发白。”
- Expressiveness: At the helper’s arrival, describe the new character’s appearance and urgency: “这时，一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人快步走过来。”
- Expressiveness: After the final conclusion, add a short line of speech: “这种像伞一样的植物可以做药，叫灵芝。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the cause-and-effect sequence from eating the poisonous plant to receiving help. You also use completed-action forms well in “给了他一个像伞的植物” and “不再生病了.”

## End of story

### Content & Completeness
- After 神农 eats 夹竹桃, the story needs to show that he faints, not only that his stomach hurts and he lies down.
- When the helper finds 神农, the story needs to say that this is a person in blue who stops and gives him 灵芝.
- After 神农 feels better, the story still needs the visit from a person in brown and their conversation with 神农.
- At the end, add that 神农 writes down records specifically for both 灵芝 and 夹竹桃.

### Overall Encouragement
- You included a clear beginning about people getting sick from unknown plants, and you showed 神农 trying plants in the mountains. The tea discovery, the dangerous 夹竹桃, and the helpful 灵芝 make your retelling feel exciting.
