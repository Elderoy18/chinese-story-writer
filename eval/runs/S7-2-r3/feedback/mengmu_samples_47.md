## Scene 1

> 这个男孩子叫孟子，孟子是古代的人。孟子的妈妈觉得住在哪儿对童年很重要，所以他们常常搬了家。她望子成龙，所以孟子的妈妈安排他的生活。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- Nothing to flag.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 安排 -- “孟子的妈妈安排他的生活”：规划，emphasizes that she is thoughtfully making plans for his daily life and future.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 这个男孩子：有一个男孩子，A first-mentioned character should be introduced with an indefinite full noun phrase; 这个 suggests that the reader already knows which boy is meant.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子是古代的人：他是古代的人，After the name has just appeared, use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence rather than repeating the name.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子的妈妈安排他的生活：安排他的生活，The mother is already the subject of the first clause in the same sentence, so use zero form instead of repeating her full name.
- Coherence (Temporality): 常常搬了家：常常搬家，A habitual action uses the zero particle; 了 marks a completed event, not a repeated routine.
- Expressiveness: After “他们常常搬家,” add a short contrast between the places, such as “有的地方很安静，有的地方很热闹。” This makes the repeated moves easier to picture.
- Expressiveness: After “她望子成龙,” show the mother’s thought in quotation: “她心想：‘我要给孟子找一个适合学习的地方。’” This gives her motivation a more personal voice.

### Encouragement
You clearly introduce the main family relationship and use a cause-and-effect connector to explain the mother’s motivation. The idiom “望子成龙” is an especially fitting choice for this scene.

## Scene 2

> 因为他们常常搬了家，中国人说，孟母三迁。孟子没有爸爸，所以，他的妈妈更重要。从这儿到那儿，妈妈教孟子很多技能。先她让孟子跟朋友玩儿，然后教他怎么买又便宜又好质量的东西。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 先她让孟子跟朋友玩儿：她先让孟子跟朋友玩儿，The time adverb should follow the subject and directly modify the verb phrase.
- 又便宜又好质量的东西：又便宜又质量好的东西，To describe the quality of an object, use the modifier structure with the noun followed by the adjective; “好质量” is not a natural Chinese noun phrase.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 东西 -- “教他怎么买又便宜又好质量的东西”：商品，specifies that these are goods being bought and sold rather than unspecified objects；日用品，specifies everyday items, fitting practical skills taught by a parent
- 从这儿到那儿 -- “从这儿到那儿，妈妈教孟子很多技能”：搬来搬去，states more precisely that the repeated movement was moving house from place to place

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 妈妈教孟子很多技能：她教孟子很多技能，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence: the mother was named in the immediately preceding sentence, and no other female character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Temporality): 常常搬了家：常常搬家，Use the zero particle for a habitual action; 了 marks a completed event, while 常常 describes a routine.
- Expressiveness: After “孟子没有爸爸”, add the mother’s feeling to show why she takes responsibility, for example: “她觉得自己要更努力地照顾孟子。”
- Expressiveness: After the shopping lesson, add a short line of dialogue to make the teaching scene livelier, for example: “妈妈说：‘买东西要先看看价钱，也要看看质量。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly organized the last sentence with “先……然后……”, which makes the order of the mother’s teaching easy to follow. You also gave specific examples of what she taught, rather than only saying that she cared for Mengzi.

## Scene 3

> 最后，她让孟子学写作。他不是写作就是看书，难怪他很聪明。现在在中国，孟子很有名。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- Nothing to flag.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 有名 -- “孟子很有名”：广为人知，emphasizes that many people know him across China；家喻户晓，emphasizes that he is known by almost every household.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After “她让孟子学写作,” add a small detail about his study habits, such as “他每天认真地写文章，还常常读书。”
- Expressiveness: At “难怪他很聪明,” add the mother’s feeling with a short thought or quotation, such as “妈妈看着他，心里很高兴。”
- Expressiveness: Make “很有名” more vivid by naming what people remember him for, such as “后来，很多人都知道孟子是一个聪明的哲学家。”

### Encouragement
You used “最后” clearly to continue the earlier sequence of the mother teaching Mengzi different skills. The sentence “他不是写作就是看书” effectively shows that he spent much of his time studying.
