## Scene 1

> 在古代，很多医生不知道怎么用药。所以他们找到了很多植物对药。神农先看到很多病人，他们吃很多植物对药。但是我觉得他们的吃的植物，让他们病人。神农觉得这个植物可能特别是毒。所以他想找一个植物对药，他们可以吃对药。正好，他找到了一把花，他想把花放在茶里，对药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 植物对药：可以做药的植物，The phrase “对药” does not express that plants can be used as medicine; use “可以做药” to describe this function.
- 吃很多植物对药：吃了很多植物，The action is presented as completed before the patients become ill, so it needs “了”; “对药” cannot follow “吃” in this meaning.
- 他们的吃的植物，让他们病人：他们吃的植物让他们生病了，A relative clause before a noun is formed as “他们吃的植物,” not “他们的吃的植物”; after “让,” the result must be a predicate such as “生病了.”
- 特别是毒：有毒，“毒” is a noun and cannot be intensified in this predicate pattern; use “有毒” to describe a poisonous plant.
- 一个植物对药：一种可以做药的植物，Use the measure word “种” for a type of plant, and use “可以做药” rather than “对药.”
- 吃对药：吃这种植物，“对药” cannot be the object or complement of “吃”; “这种植物” clearly names what the patients can eat.
- 对药：做药，Use “做药” to mean using something as medicine.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 想 -- “他想把花放在茶里”：打算，makes his intended action more definite and planned in this moment；计划，emphasizes that he has formed a clear plan for what to do with the flower

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：一个叫神农的人，This is the character’s first mention, so introduce him with a name and a brief noun phrase rather than assuming the reader already knows him.
- Coherence (Reference): 这个植物：这些植物，The earlier sentence mentions many plants, but no single plant has been selected; use the plural noun phrase to make the reference clear.
- Coherence (Reference): 他们可以吃这种植物：病人可以吃这种植物，“他们” could refer to the doctors or the patients after several sentences and another character have appeared; repeat “病人” for a clear far re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 先看到：先看到了，Seeing the patients is a completed event in the narrative sequence, so add “了.”
- Expressiveness: At the mention of the patients, add a physical detail to show how ill they are: “他们脸色很差，肚子疼得直冒汗。”
- Expressiveness: When the character suspects the plants are dangerous, add his inner question: “这些植物是不是有毒？”
- Expressiveness: When the flower appears, describe its shape or colour: “花又大又漂亮，像星星一样。”

### Encouragement
You have a clear cause-and-effect idea: the patients eat plants, become ill, and then the character wants to find safer plants. You also used completed-action wording well in “找到了,” which helps move the story forward.

## Scene 2

> 他不知道如果这把花对你的身体好，这就要看如果这把花毒不毒了。神农想找别的植物，但是这很麻烦。正好，他找到了别的花。他一吃花，很快就生病了。他的肚子疼死了。神农不知道他吃不吃灵芝或者夹竹桃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不知道如果这把花对你的身体好：不知道这把花对你的身体好不好，An embedded yes/no question after 不知道 should use an A-not-A form rather than 如果; 好不好 asks whether the flower is beneficial.
- 看如果这把花毒不毒：看这把花毒不毒，After 看, an embedded yes/no question cannot begin with 如果; 毒不毒 is the correct A-not-A question form.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 麻烦 -- “但是这很麻烦”：费力，specifies that finding more plants requires effort；艰难，emphasizes that the task is difficult to carry out.
- 很快 -- “他一吃花，很快就生病了”：没过多久，more specifically shows that only a short time passed before he became ill.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 你的：他的，There is no listener introduced as “you”; this refers to Shen Nong’s own body, so use the close re-occurrence pronoun for Shen Nong.
- Coherence (Reference): 一吃花：一吃这种花，The flower has just been found in the previous sentence, so use a specific noun phrase to show that this is that particular flower rather than flowers in general.
- Expressiveness: After “正好，他找到了别的花,” add one visual detail about the flower, such as “那种花又黄又大，像天上的星星。”
- Expressiveness: After “他的肚子疼死了,” show Shen Nong’s physical reaction, such as “他躺在地上，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short inner thought after he becomes ill, such as “他想：‘我会不会死？’”

### Encouragement
- You used the completed form in “找到了” accurately to show that Shen Nong successfully found flowers.
- The sentence about his stomach pain is vivid, and the “one action, then result” structure makes the illness happen clearly and quickly.

## Scene 3

> 别的人给了神农一个灵芝。这个植物可以让神农更好。比方说，他会觉得更健康。吃灵芝以后，他可以再走。现在他觉得更好。最后，神农找到了很多植物，他可以用对药。这些植物让神农很高兴。神农想跟很多人分享他的新药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别的人给了神农一个灵芝：别人给了神农一个灵芝，Use 别人 for people other than the person being discussed; 别的人 normally needs a stated comparison group.
- 很多植物，他可以用对药：很多可以用来做药的植物，The phrase 用对药 is not a valid way to express a plant’s medicinal use. Use 可以用来做药 to show that the plants can serve as medicine.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 走 -- “他可以再走”：继续赶路，specifies that he is able to resume his journey after recovering.
- 新药 -- “分享他的新药”：新发现的药，makes clear that this is medicine Shennong has only recently discovered.
- 高兴 -- “这些植物让神农很高兴”：欣慰，expresses satisfaction and relief after finding plants that can help people.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃灵芝以后：吃了灵芝以后，The eating is completed before the later action of walking again, so use 了 to mark the completed action.
- Expressiveness: After receiving the medicine, add a simple visual detail: “灵芝像一把小伞一样，长在夹竹桃旁边。”
- Expressiveness: Show Shennong’s recovery with a physical action after “现在他觉得更好”: “他的肚子不那么疼了，慢慢站了起来。”
- Expressiveness: At “神农想跟很多人分享他的新药,” add his purpose in quoted speech: “神农说：‘我要告诉大家，什么植物可以做药。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the sequence from receiving the medicine, feeling better, and continuing the journey. You also use useful story words such as 灵芝、植物, and 分享.
