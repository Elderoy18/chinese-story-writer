## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，孟子和孟母住了在靠墓地的家。孩子才五岁就每天在墓地祭拜了。在那个城市，孟母从早到晚都找工作，可是找不到，害得孟母没有钱。难怪她跟孩子搬家，原来买不了房。他们搬了到别一个城市找工作。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，This expression redundantly combines a time period with a “time of” phrase. [vocab list]
- 住了在：住在，Residence is a continuing state here, so it does not take 了; use 在 directly before the place phrase. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- 跟孩子搬家：带着孩子搬家，跟 means “together with” as equal participants, while a mother relocates with her child in her care. [vocab list]
- 搬了到：搬到了，Place 了 after the directional/resultative part: 搬到 + 了. [teacher rule: le_placement]
- 别一个：另一个，Use 另一个 for one additional or different city; 别的 distinguishes other things from a specific one already mentioned. [teacher rule: bieren]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 买不了 -- “原来买不了房”：买不起，specifies that the family cannot afford a home because they lack money.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟母：有一个小男孩叫孟子，他和母亲，On first mention, introduce the character with a name and identifying description rather than assuming the reader already knows who he is. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Reference): 孩子：他，After a recent mention of Mengzi and with no competing male character, use a pronoun for close re-occurrence. [rule: none]
- Coherence (Reference): 在那个城市：在那里，The city has not been introduced, so the definite phrase is unclear; use a location reference to the previously mentioned home area. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Reference): 害得孟母没有钱：害得她没有钱，Mengmu was already named earlier in the same sentence, so a pronoun makes the reference smoother. [rule: none]
- Coherence (Temporality): 每天在墓地祭拜了：每天在墓地祭拜，A routine marked by 每天 uses the zero particle, not 了, because it is not one completed action. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: At the cemetery scene, add a concrete observation and Mengzi’s reaction, such as “孟子看见有人伤心地哭，就学着他们祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: Before the move, show Mengmu’s worry through an inner thought, such as “孟母心想：‘再住下去，日子会更难。’”

### Encouragement
- You used the pattern “才五岁就” correctly to show that Mengzi started the action at a very young age.
- The contrast between searching for work and not finding work is clear, and “害得” effectively shows the difficult result for Mengmu.

## Scene 2

> 这个城市老是很吵，不是人吵架，就是都叫，害得孟子一直不能睡觉。在那儿，孟母也找不到工作。孟子和妈妈要又一个次搬了家。在第三个城市，孟母找到工作，在商店做衣服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 人吵架，就是都叫：有人吵架，就是有人大叫，Each alternative should describe a possible event with an appropriate subject. Bare 人 is too generic here, and 都 does not fit an either-or alternative. [rule: none]
- 又一个次搬：再搬一次，With a modal verb such as 要, use the pattern modal verb + 再 + verb + number + measure word. 再 refers to a move that has not happened yet. [teacher rule: zai_structure]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 吵 -- “这个城市老是很吵”：吵闹，specifies that the city is full of noisy activity, not simply loud.
- 做 -- “在商店做衣服”：缝制，specifies that her job involves sewing and making clothes.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬了家：搬家，After the modal verb 要, the future action should use the zero particle rather than 了, because the move has not been completed yet. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 找到工作：找到了工作，Finding the job is a completed event in the narrative, so the completed action needs 了 after the resultative complement 到. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: After the opening sentence, add a sound detail to make the city’s noise more vivid, such as: “从早到晚，街上都是商人的叫卖声。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where sleep becomes difficult, show Mengzi’s reaction with an action and a short quotation, such as: “孟子捂着耳朵说：‘太吵了，我睡不着！’”
- Expressiveness: When Mengmu finds work, add her feeling of relief, such as: “孟母终于找到了工作，心里轻松多了。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show how the noisy environment affects Mengzi, and the result phrase with 害得 connects the problem and its effect well. You also use the city setting consistently to move the scene forward.

## Scene 3

> 孟子在那个商店也找了到工作，可是不是做衣服的工作，而是喜欢的工作。孟子学习馆学了十五年，他的学习和他的旅行经验很重要，对他很多好处。他是大人的时候，他成为很有名的哲人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 找了到：找到了，The resultative complement must come before the completed-action marker: use verb + resultative + 了. [teacher rule: le_placement]
- 学习馆学：在学习馆学习，A place where an action happens needs the preposition 在, and 学习 is the verb for studying. [rule: none]
- 对他很多好处：对他有很多好处，The verb 有 is needed to state that something brings benefits to someone. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 学习 -- “他的学习和他的旅行经验很重要”：求学经历，specifies that this refers to his years of education and learning experience.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子：他，Use a pronoun when the same character was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other character competes as the referent. [rule: none]
- Coherence (Reference): 他的学习和他的旅行经验：他的学习和旅行经验，Within one noun phrase, use zero form rather than repeating the same possessor pronoun. [rule: none]
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为：成为了，This is a completed life change in the narrative, so the verb needs 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Conjunction): 孟子学习馆学了十五年：后来，他在学习馆学习了十五年，Later introduces the next stage of the narrative and makes the time sequence clear. [rule: time_expression]
- Expressiveness: At the point where he studies, add a concrete detail about his effort, such as “他每天认真地看书，还常常向老师请教问题。”
- Expressiveness: When describing his later success, add an inner feeling or reaction, such as “他心里很高兴，觉得多年的努力没有白费。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech after he becomes well known, such as “孟子说：‘我希望用学到的知识帮助更多人。’”

### Encouragement
- You used the contrast pattern “不是……而是……” to distinguish two kinds of work clearly.
- You also included a long period of study and later achievement, which gives this scene a clear sense of development.
