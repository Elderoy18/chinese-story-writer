## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个男孩名字叫孟子。孟子和孟子的妈妈两个人住在很穷的农村。他们住的地方旁边是个墓地，墓地老是有很多人祭拜。因为人们在祭拜，他们一直在哭。孟子和孟子的妈妈住在这里才一个星期就觉得受不了了，而且害得孟子的学习。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，this expression redundantly combines a time period with a phrase meaning “at the time of”; use the shorter time expression. [vocab list]
- 害得孟子的学习：这里的环境害得孟子不能专心学习，害得 must be followed by the person affected and a harmful result clause, not only a noun phrase. [teacher rule: haide_structure]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子的妈妈两个人：孟子和他的妈妈两个人，after naming 孟子, use a pronoun for the closely repeated possessor; repeating the name in the same noun phrase is unnecessary. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子的妈妈住在这里：孟子和他的妈妈住在这里，use a pronoun for the closely repeated possessor after the character has already been named. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Temporality): 住在这里才一个星期就觉得受不了了：在这里才住了一个星期，就觉得受不了了，the one-week stay is a completed duration before the resulting feeling, so 住 takes 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为人们在祭拜，他们一直在哭：因为人们在祭拜，所以他们一直在哭，use 因为……所以…… to connect the cause with its result. [rule: none]
- Expressiveness: At the description of the cemetery, add a sound detail to show why the place feels disturbing, such as “墓地里常常传来大声的哭声。”
- Expressiveness: When the family cannot bear the environment, add Mengzi’s mother’s feeling or thought, such as “孟子的妈妈觉得这里太吵了，孟子没办法安心学习。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech after the final sentence to reveal the mother’s decision, such as “我们得搬到一个安静的地方去。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduce Mengzi, his mother, their home, and the nearby cemetery, so the setting is easy to understand. You also use the ongoing actions “在祭拜” and “一直在哭” effectively to create a sad, noisy atmosphere.

## Scene 2

> 孟子的妈妈觉得孟子取得良好的教育是很重要的，所以孟子的妈妈认为这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好，就打算跟孟子搬家。孟子的妈妈带孟子不是搬到城市，而是搬到了孟子老师的家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 取得良好的教育：接受良好的教育，取得 is used for obtaining an achievement or result through effort, while 接受 describes receiving schooling or education. [rule: none]
- 不是搬到城市，而是搬到了孟子的老师的家：搬到了孟子的老师的家，this construction is for correcting a conventional or previously assumed claim; here the sentence only needs to state the new destination. [rule: bushi_ershi_construction]
- 孟子老师：孟子的老师，without 的, this phrase means a teacher named Meng子; 的 is needed to show that the teacher is Meng子的 teacher. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 地方 -- “这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好”：环境，specifies that the concern is the surrounding setting and its influence on education
- 不太好 -- “对儿子的教育不太好”：不利，states more precisely that the setting negatively affects the child’s education

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子的妈妈认为：她认为，after the mother has just been named and no other possible female referent appears, use a pronoun for the close re-occurrence. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子的妈妈带孟子：她带孟子，begin the next sentence with a pronoun because the mother remains the clear, recently mentioned subject. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Expressiveness: At the point where the mother judges the place, add her worried inner thought: “孟母心想：‘这里不利于孟子的教育。’”
- Expressiveness: Before the moving decision, show what makes her concerned: “她看到孟子总是学着别人祭拜，心里很着急。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech to make the decision feel more immediate: “我们得搬到一个更适合学习的地方。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the mother’s reason for moving, using a cause-and-effect connection with “所以.” You also use “搬到了” well to show that the move to the new place was completed.
