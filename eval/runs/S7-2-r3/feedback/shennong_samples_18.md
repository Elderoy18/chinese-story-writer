## Scene 1

> 神农和他的家人住在古代，所以不知道什么植物能吃。因为他的家人常常生病了，所以神农和他的哥哥一起去旅行找可以吃的植物。他们去很多不一样的地方，比方说高山、山谷和沙漠。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 找 -- “去旅行找可以吃的植物”：寻找，emphasizes an active, continuing search during the journey；探寻，highlights exploring in order to discover an answer.
- 不一样 -- “很多不一样的地方”：地形各异，more specifically shows that the places have different kinds of landforms, matching the examples that follow.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：一个叫神农的人，A character’s first mention should briefly introduce who the person is, rather than assuming the reader already knows the name. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Temporality): 常常生病了：常常生病，A habitual action or repeated state uses the zero particle; the completion marker 了 is not used with 常常. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们去很多不一样的地方：他们去了很多不一样的地方，The visits to many places are presented as completed actions in the narrative, so the verb needs 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: After the family’s illness is mentioned, add Shennong’s feeling to show his motivation more clearly: “看着家人常常生病，神农心里很着急。”
- Expressiveness: When listing the places, add one visual detail to make the journey easier to imagine: “高山上云雾缭绕，山谷里长着许多植物。”
- Expressiveness: Before the journey, add a short line of speech showing the brothers’ purpose: “我们一定要找到能吃的植物！”

### Encouragement
You used the paired connector “因为……所以……” correctly to connect the family’s illness with the journey. The list of different places also gives the journey a clear sense of variety.

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在很漂亮的河找夹竹桃：在一条很漂亮的河边找夹竹桃，The location after 在 needs a place phrase; 河边 names the place beside the river, and 一条 is the suitable measure-word phrase for 河. [rule: none]
- 接近知道：想知道这种植物能不能吃，接近 cannot directly modify the verb 知道; it normally means “be close to” and needs a noun-like target. [rule: none]
- 了。“：了。”，In Chinese direct speech, the sentence-final punctuation comes before the closing quotation mark. [rule: none]
- “太麻烦了。：“太麻烦了。”，A spoken quotation needs a closing quotation mark, placed after the final punctuation. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农问他的哥哥：神农问自己的哥哥，When the possessor is the same person as the subject, 自己的 makes that relationship clear. [teacher rule: ziji_possessor]
- Expressiveness: At the river setting, add a visual detail about the flower: “河边开着黄色的、像星星一样的花。”
- Expressiveness: After the illness, show Shen Nong’s physical feeling: “他在地上躺着，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Make the brother’s reaction more personal through dialogue: “哥哥着急地说：‘你怎么样？肚子还疼吗？’”

### Encouragement
You used dialogue effectively to show the brothers discussing whether the plant is safe. The sentence showing that Shen Nong became ill immediately after eating clearly communicates a quick cause-and-effect event.

## Scene 3

> 夹竹桃是毒的，我想知道灵芝会帮他。”哥哥给神农灵芝。神农吃完了以后开始痊愈了。神农说：“谢谢哥哥，你帮得真好。灵芝我们可以吃，特别是如果我们已经吃了夹竹桃。“

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 毒的：有毒的，Use 有毒的 to describe a plant that contains poison; 毒 cannot directly modify a noun in this form. [vocab list]
- 夹竹桃是毒的，我想知道灵芝会帮他。”：“夹竹桃是有毒的，我想知道灵芝会帮神农。”，Direct speech needs a matching opening quotation mark before the spoken words and a closing quotation mark after them. [rule: none]
- 。“：。”，The final quotation mark must be a closing quotation mark, not an opening one. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 吃 -- “灵芝我们可以吃”：服用，specifically describes taking a medicinal plant or medicine.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他：神农，Both the brother and 神农 are male characters in the nearby context, so 他 could refer to either one; repeat 神农 to make the referent clear. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Temporality): 哥哥给神农灵芝：哥哥给了神农灵芝，This is a completed giving action, so it needs 了 after the verb. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: After 神农 eats the herb, add a physical detail showing that he is recovering, such as “他的肚子慢慢不疼了，脸色也好了一点。”
- Expressiveness: After the thanks, add a short question that shows 神农’s curiosity about the medicine, such as “神农问：‘这是什么植物？’”
- Expressiveness: Replace the broad praise after “你帮得真好” with a feeling of relief, such as “神农高兴地说：‘谢谢哥哥，你救了我！’”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the helpful action of the brother giving 神农 灵芝, and the final dialogue gives the scene a warm, grateful ending.
