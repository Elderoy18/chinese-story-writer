## Scene 1

> 古代，孟子跟孟母住在墓地的旁边。可是孟母注意，住在墓地的旁边影响孟子。他常常跟朋友们祭拜玩，所以孟母决定他们会搬家。这次，他们住在城市里的商店的旁边，从早到晚很吵。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代：在古代，A time expression needs the preposition shown in the standard narrative form.
- 注意：注意到，Use this resultative form when the mother notices that a fact is true; the original verb means “pay attention” and does not naturally introduce this conclusion.
- 跟朋友们祭拜玩：跟朋友们一起学着别人祭拜，The original pairing is not natural because the ritual verb does not combine with the play verb in this order; the revised phrase clearly expresses that he imitates others’ ritual actions.
- 决定他们会搬家：决定搬家，The verb takes a verb phrase directly, so the subject does not need to be repeated after it.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 吵 -- “从早到晚很吵”：嘈杂，specifies that the place is filled with unpleasant noise；喧闹，suggests a busy place with many noisy people and activities

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子跟孟母：有一个小男孩叫孟子，他跟孟母，On a character’s first mention, introduce the character with both a name and a brief identity; after that, a pronoun can refer back to him.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 可是孟母注意到：孟母注意到，The connector does not express contrast here: the mother’s observation follows from the setting, rather than going against an expectation.
- Expressiveness: At the cemetery scene, add what the child sees and copies, such as “孟子看到人们哭着祭拜，觉得很好玩。”
- Expressiveness: After the mother notices the problem, show her worry with an inner thought or quoted speech, such as “孟母心想：‘这样会影响孟子。’”
- Expressiveness: At the new home, make the noise concrete by describing the shops, such as “商人一直大声叫卖，路上人来人往。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish the two settings and use a cause-and-effect connector to show why the family moves. The final sentence also gives the new location a strong atmosphere through the all-day noise.

## Scene 2

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不高兴 -- “难怪他老是看起来不高兴”：闷闷不乐，describes a continuing, visibly low mood rather than general unhappiness.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 住在商店的旁边不是适合孟子，而是让他觉得很伤心：住在商店旁边不适合孟子，会让他觉得很伤心，the second part describes a consequence, not an unexpected replacement for the first idea; do not use the paired connector 不是……而是…… here.
- Expressiveness: After the sentence about danger, add one concrete detail showing why the place feels unsafe. Example: “街上人来人往，孟子不敢一个人出去玩。”
- Expressiveness: Show Mengzi’s feelings through a small action as well as naming the feeling. Example: “孟子常常一个人坐在窗边，看着外面的商店。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect Mengzi’s lack of friends with his unhappy mood, and the use of “难怪” makes that relationship easy to understand.

## Scene 3

> 所以，孟母和孟子搬到学校的旁边住。才五岁孟子开始学哲学。在学校，孟子不是看书，就是练习写字。将来，他成为中国的一个非常有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 才五岁孟子开始学哲学：孟子才五岁就开始学哲学，The pattern for an early action is “才 + age + 就 + verb”; place the character before the age phrase and add “就” before the action.
- 将来：后来，For a later event in a completed past narrative, use “后来”; “将来” refers to the future from the present viewpoint.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟母和孟子搬到学校的旁边住：他们搬到学校的旁边住，Both characters were mentioned in the immediately preceding scene, and no other pair competes as the referent, so the pronoun is more natural for a close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子不是看书，就是练习写字：他不是看书，就是练习写字，Use a pronoun when the same character was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no competing character has appeared.
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬到：搬到了，The move is a completed narrative event, so “了” follows “到” to mark completion.
- Expressiveness: At the school scene, add a small study detail to show how seriously he learns, for example: “孟子坐在窗边，认真地看书写字。”
- Expressiveness: After the school is introduced, add Mengmu’s positive feeling or a short thought, for example: “孟母看到孟子喜欢学习，心里很高兴。”

### Encouragement
You used the “不是……就是……” pattern correctly to show Mengzi’s regular school activities. The scene also has a clear movement from the new home to his learning and later achievement.
