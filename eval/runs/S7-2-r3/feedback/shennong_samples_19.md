## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男人觉得植物很有意思。他叫神农。有的植物吃了以后他的肚子疼死了，可是有的吃了以后很舒服。他找很多植物。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，this expression redundantly combines two time expressions; use the shorter time phrase instead. [vocab list]
- 他找很多植物：他找到了很多植物，找 means “to look for,” while 找到 means “to find.” Here the narration describes plants that he found, and 了 marks the completed finding action. [vocab list]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一个男人觉得植物很有意思。他叫神农：一个叫神农的男人觉得植物很有意思，at a character’s first mention, introduce the name together with a full noun phrase so the reader knows who the character is immediately. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Expressiveness: After the stomach pain, add a physical reaction to show how severe it feels: “他捂着肚子，疼得直冒冷汗。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought after he notices the different effects of plants: “为什么有的植物会让我的肚子疼呢？”
- Expressiveness: Describe one kind of plant to make the scene more visual: “有的植物开着黄色的小花，像星星一样。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the different effects of plants by using the contrast between “有的” and “可是有的.” The sentence about stomach pain is also vivid and helps the reader understand why Shennong becomes interested in plants.

## Scene 2

> 凉山很大的山，这也找植物。一天，他找到黄色的花。他听说你跟黄色的花做茶很有用。比方说，你的头发疼死，这冰茶是药。他一喝茶就很不舒服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 凉山很大的山：凉山是一座很大的山，A noun used as the subject needs the copula and an appropriate classifier before a noun predicate. [rule: none]
- 这也找植物：他也在那里找植物，A location needs a locative form, and the action needs an animate agent rather than the mountain as its subject. [rule: none]
- 跟黄色的花做茶：用黄色的花做茶，The preposition “用” introduces the material used to make something; “跟” expresses accompaniment and does not mean “using as material” here. [rule: none]
- 头发疼死：头疼得厉害，“头发” means hair, while “头疼” means having a headache; “疼得厉害” expresses severe pain naturally. [rule: none]
- 这冰茶：这杯冰茶，A demonstrative before a countable noun needs a matching measure word. [teacher rule: zhe_na_mw]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很不舒服 -- “他一喝茶就很不舒服”：很难受，gives a more vivid description of physical discomfort after drinking the tea.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 你：delete it，This pronoun has no established addressee, so the sentence should state the general action directly rather than suddenly addressing an unclear person. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Reference): 你的：有人，This pronoun has no clear referent in the story; “有人” introduces a general person clearly for the example. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Temporality): 找到：找到了，This is a completed, one-time action in the past, so the completed action needs “了” after the resultative complement. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: After the yellow flowers appear, add a visual detail such as “黄色的花像星星一样，又大又漂亮。” This helps the reader picture why the flowers attract attention.
- Expressiveness: After the tea makes him uncomfortable, show his physical reaction: “他捂着肚子，难受得不得了。” This makes the discomfort feel more immediate.
- Expressiveness: Add an inner thought after he feels ill: “他想：‘我会不会生病？’” This shows his worry instead of only stating the event.

### Encouragement
You use the pattern “一……就……” correctly to show that the discomfort happens immediately after drinking the tea. You also introduce a new setting and a new plant, which moves the scene forward clearly.

## Scene 3

> 糟糕，这黄色的花是夹竹桃，特别是毒。正好在这儿，灵芝药的夹竹桃他吃完了，也写一个课文的植物。谢谢神农。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 这黄色的花：这朵黄色的花，Demonstratives such as 这 need a matching measure word before a countable noun; 朵 is the measure word for a flower. [teacher rule: zhe_na_mw]
- 特别是毒：特别有毒，To describe a plant’s poisonous property, use 有毒; 毒 cannot follow a degree adverb in this way. [vocab list]
- 灵芝药的夹竹桃他吃完了：他吃了夹竹桃和灵芝，The particle 的 cannot link these unrelated nouns or state that one is medicine. Name the two objects with 和 and place the subject before the completed action. [rule: none]
- 一个课文的植物：一篇关于它们的文章，一篇 is the appropriate measure word for an article, and 关于 introduces the topic of the article. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 也写：也写了，The writing is presented as a completed action after the eating, so it needs 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: After the dangerous discovery, add a physical reaction to show why the plant is frightening: “神农的肚子疼了起来，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Turn the final thanks into a short line of speech so the reader knows who is speaking: “谢谢你，神农！你救了我。”
- Expressiveness: Add one visual detail when the flower appears: “黄色的花像星星一样，又大又漂亮。”

### Encouragement
You clearly name the important plant and show that it is dangerous. You also try to include several connected actions, including eating and writing.
