## Scene 1

> 一天，很久很久以前，孟子和他妈妈住在中国。孟子妈妈的工作是祭拜，孟子不高兴，因为他说我们老是搬家，为什么？他妈妈说，从早到晚，我一直祭拜，很多墓地得我祭拜。所以我们得去很多不同的地方。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一天，很久很久以前：很久很久以前的一天，A time phrase modifying a noun should come before that noun; this means “one day long, long ago.” [rule: time_expression]
- 孟子不高兴，因为他说我们老是搬家，为什么？：孟子不高兴，问：“我们为什么老是搬家？”，Use a question verb and quotation marks for direct speech; in this question, the question word comes before the action phrase. [rule: none]
- 很多墓地得我祭拜：我得去很多墓地祭拜，The person who has the obligation should be the subject before the modal verb 得; the places cannot be the subject of 得 in this meaning. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和他妈妈：有一个小男孩叫孟子，他和妈妈，On first mention, introduce the character clearly with a name and a short description; after naming him, use a pronoun for the closely mentioned character. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子妈妈的工作：他妈妈的工作，Use a pronoun for a character mentioned in the immediately previous sentence when no other possible referent competes. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Expressiveness: At the question, add Mengzi’s visible feeling, such as “孟子皱着眉头，问：‘我们为什么老是搬家？’” This shows that he is worried or confused rather than only stating that he is unhappy.
- Expressiveness: At the mother’s reply, add her tired emotion from working all day, such as “妈妈叹了口气，说：‘从早到晚，我都要去很多墓地祭拜。’” This makes her explanation feel more personal.

### Encouragement
You clearly show the reason for the family’s frequent moves, and the connector “所以” links the mother’s work to the result effectively. The dialogue also helps the reader understand Mengzi’s concern.

## Scene 2

> 孟子想，难怪我们常常搬家，原来我妈妈很忙。可是常常搬家害得孟子很难找到朋友。每次搬家孟子也觉得新的地方不是太热就是太冷，不是太大就是太小。在哪的地方，他都不高兴。孟子想帮助妈妈祭拜，可是他六岁，所以不能祭拜。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在哪的地方：在哪个地方，When 哪 modifies a noun, it needs a matching measure word; use 哪个地方. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 新的 -- “孟子也觉得新的地方不是太热就是太冷”：陌生的，emphasizes that each place is unfamiliar to Meng子；没住过的，states more exactly that he has not lived in the place before.
- 找到 -- “孟子很难找到朋友”：交到，more precisely describes making new friends rather than simply locating people.
- 不高兴 -- “他都不高兴”：闷闷不乐，shows a continuing unhappy mood in each new place.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the sentence about finding friends, add a small feeling detail to show why this is difficult for him, such as “他看着别的孩子一起玩儿，心里很孤单。”
- Expressiveness: At the final sentence, add a short thought or line of speech to make his wish to help more vivid, such as “孟子说：‘妈妈，我也想帮你。’”
- Expressiveness: When describing the different places, add one concrete sensory detail, such as “有的地方夏天太阳很大，热得他满头大汗。”

### Encouragement
You used the paired pattern “难怪……，原来……” naturally to show Meng子的 realization. You also clearly explained the cause of his unhappiness by connecting frequent moving with difficulty making friends.

## Scene 3

> 四年后，孟子开始帮妈妈祭拜。他画画很厉害，他越来越喜欢祭拜，所以他祭拜不是想帮妈妈工作，而是他很喜欢画画。孟子成为很著名的古代画家。今天很多中国人很喜欢孟子。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他祭拜不是想帮妈妈工作，而是他很喜欢画画：他祭拜，不是为了帮妈妈工作，而是因为他很喜欢画画，use “为了” to mark the purpose of an action; “因为” then introduces the reason. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 厉害 -- “他画画很厉害”：擅长，specifies that his ability is especially in drawing；画得很好，states the quality of his drawings more directly.
- 很著名 -- “很著名的古代画家”：享有盛名，expresses that he is widely known and respected as a painter.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他画画很厉害，他越来越喜欢祭拜，所以他祭拜：他画画很厉害，越来越喜欢祭拜，所以祭拜，after the first “他,” the same subject continues throughout the sentence, so zero form is more natural than repeating the pronoun. [rule: none]
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为：成为了，becoming a famous painter is presented as a completed later event, so the verb needs “了.” [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Conjunction): 孟子成为了很著名的古代画家：后来，孟子成为了很著名的古代画家，“后来” marks the sequence from his childhood actions to his later achievement. [rule: time_expression]
- Expressiveness: After the first sentence, add one visual detail about what he does during the ceremony, such as “孟子认真地画下墓碑旁的花和来祭拜的人。”
- Expressiveness: Add Mengzi’s thought when he discovers that he enjoys the activity, such as “孟子心想：‘祭拜的时候，我也可以画很多东西。’”
- Expressiveness: Add a brief reaction from his mother after seeing his drawing, such as “妈妈看了看他的画，笑着说：‘你画得真仔细！’”

### Encouragement
You clearly show a change in Mengzi’s interests by using “越来越喜欢,” and the final sentence successfully connects his early ability in drawing with his later reputation.
