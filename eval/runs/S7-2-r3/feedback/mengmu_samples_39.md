## Scene 1

> 古代的时候人觉得祭拜非常重要，孟子很小的时候，从早到晚一直祭拜，因为他妈妈告诉他祭拜很重要。虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜。他的同学们老是对他开玩笑了。孟子妈妈看了他的同学们对孟子不好的影响，所以孟子和孟子妈妈搬家了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，The original is redundant because both parts express a time period. [vocab list]
- 人：人们，This refers to people in general, so the plural noun is needed rather than the generic singular noun. [teacher rule: ren_renmen]
- 看了：看到了，Here the mother “sees/notices” the negative effect, so use the result verb rather than the verb meaning “look at.” [vocab list]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子很小的时候：有一个小男孩叫孟子。孟子很小的时候，Introduce the character with a full noun phrase when he first appears, rather than assuming the reader already knows who he is. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子妈妈：孟子和他的妈妈，Use a pronoun for the possessive relationship when the character has just been mentioned; repeating the name in the same phrase is unnecessary. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Temporality): 老是对他开玩笑了：老是对他开玩笑，This is a repeated habitual action, so it takes the zero particle rather than completed-action 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Conjunction): 虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜：因为他每天只祭拜，所以没有朋友，The relationship is cause and effect, so use the paired connector 因为……所以…… rather than 虽然 with a causal clause. [rule: coherence_connectives]
- Expressiveness: At the opening, add a small action to show what the ritual looks like: “孟子跟着大人低着头，认真地祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: When the classmates tease him, add a short line of speech: “一个同学笑着说：‘你怎么每天都在祭拜？’”
- Expressiveness: Show the mother’s concern before the move: “孟子妈妈皱着眉头，心想：‘这样下去会影响孟子。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show a cause-and-effect chain: the mother notices the classmates’ influence, and the family moves afterward. The use of “从早到晚一直” also gives a strong sense of how much time Mengzi spends on the activity.

## Scene 2

> 孟子才十一岁，成为一个哲学家。孟子不是祭拜，而是看政治文章，难怪他成为了哲学家。不过，看过很多政治文章对他害得，他有很多问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 才十一岁，成为：才十一岁，就成为，With 才 followed by an age or number, use 就 before the following action to show that it happened earlier than expected. [teacher rule: yi_jiu]
- 成为一个哲学家：成为了一个哲学家，Use 了 after a completed change of status. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- 看过很多政治文章对他害得，他有很多问题：看过很多政治文章害得他有很多问题，害得 must be followed by the affected person and then the harmful result; 对他 cannot appear before 害得 here. [teacher rule: haide_structure]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 问题 -- “他有很多问题”：疑问，specifically shows that these are intellectual questions arising from reading

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子不是祭拜：他不是祭拜，Since 孟子 was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other character competes as the referent, use a pronoun for close re-occurrence. [rule: none]
- Expressiveness: At the point where he reads, add a detail about his attitude, such as “他每天认真地看政治文章。”
- Expressiveness: When describing his questions, add an inner thought or a quoted question, such as “他心想：‘为什么有些国家会发生战争呢？’”
- Expressiveness: You could show how his reading changed him with a feeling, such as “他越看越感兴趣，也越想知道答案。”

### Encouragement
You clearly connect Mengzi’s reading to his later achievement, which gives the scene a strong central idea. You also used the contrast pattern with 不是……而是…… to show the change in his activities.

## Scene 3

> 他一直问问题，但是不能解决那些问题。孟子长大，他开始能解决问题。他常常建议你不是学金融，而是学政治，因为如果你懂政治，你会成为高薪。后来孟子成为教授，他教学生很厉害。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不是学金融，而是学政治：不要学金融，而要学政治，After a verb of recommendation, use a negative recommendation followed by the preferred action; this is not a correction of a mistaken fact. [rule: bushi_ershi]
- 成为高薪：拿到高薪，The word 高薪 means a high salary, which someone can earn or receive, not become. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 厉害 -- “他教学生很厉害”：很有方法，This specifies that he teaches effectively through good methods.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子长大：他长大，Because the same character was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other character intervenes, use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Reference): 你：人们，The narrative has not introduced an addressee, so this pronoun has an unclear referent; use a full generic noun phrase. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Reference): 后来孟子成为教授：后来他成为教授，Use a pronoun because the same character remains the clear subject from the nearby sentences. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Temporality): 长大：长大了，Growing up has reached a new state here, so use 了 to mark that change of state. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 开始能解决问题：开始能解决问题了，Use 了 to show that being able to solve problems is a newly achieved ability. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为教授：成为了教授，The later change into a professor is completed, so the verb needs 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Conjunction): 如果你懂政治，你会拿到高薪：如果你懂政治，你就会拿到高薪，A conditional sentence with 如果 needs 就 in the result clause. [teacher rule: yi_jiu]
- Expressiveness: At the point where he can solve problems, add a feeling to show his growth, for example: “他终于能解开这些难题，心里非常高兴。”
- Expressiveness: When describing his teaching, add one concrete classroom action, for example: “他耐心地听学生的问题，再一步一步地解释。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of advice after his recommendation, for example: “他说：‘学习政治可以帮助你了解国家和社会。’”

### Encouragement
- You used clear time progression from childhood to adulthood and then to his work as a professor. The contrast between asking questions and later solving them gives this scene a clear sense of development.

## End of story

### Content & Completeness
- At the beginning, the story needs to say that Mencius and his mother lived near a graveyard, and that he copied people offering sacrifices there every day.
- When his mother decides to move, the reason should be that she worries his imitation of sacrifices will negatively affect him, not that his classmates are unkind to him.
- After leaving the graveyard, the story needs the event where they live near a market and Mencius copies people buying and selling.
- The story also needs the next move from the market to a neighborhood near a school, where Mencius begins to study every day.
- The ending should show that, after growing up and studying, Mencius becomes a famous philosopher; he should not become a philosopher at age eleven before the moves and studying happen.

### Overall Encouragement
- You included Mencius, his mother, moving house, and his later interest in political ideas. You also gave him a clear future as a teacher, which helps show his growth.
