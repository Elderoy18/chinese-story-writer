## Scene 1

> 很久以前，古代的人很不知道什么植物能吃，也什么植物不能吃。所以很多人常常吃不安全的植物，吃不安全的植物对你的身体有坏处，很麻烦的情况。但是一个人，神农，要解决这个问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很不知道：不知道，The mental-state verb “知道” is not naturally modified by “很” in this negative form; use “不知道” to state that the ancient people lacked this knowledge.
- 也什么植物不能吃：也不知道什么植物不能吃，“也” must be followed by a verb phrase here, so repeat “不知道” before the second embedded question.
- 很麻烦的情况：是一种很麻烦的情况，This noun phrase needs the copular verb “是” and a measure word to function as the predicate after the previous clause.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不安全 -- “吃不安全的植物”：有害的，specifies that the plants can harm people’s bodies, which matches the following health consequence more closely.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一个人，神农：有一个叫神农的人，This is Shennong’s first mention, so introduce him clearly with a full noun phrase containing both his name and who he is.
- Expressiveness: At “不安全的植物,” add visible or sensory details such as “发黑” and “怪味” so readers can imagine why the plants are dangerous.
- Expressiveness: After “很麻烦的情况,” add people’s emotional response with a feeling word such as “担心” or “害怕.”
- Expressiveness: When introducing Shennong, add a word such as “坚定” to show his determination to solve the problem.

### Encouragement
You clearly establish the central problem: people do not know which plants are safe to eat. You also introduce Shennong at a useful moment, giving the scene a clear focus.

## Scene 2

> 他又聪明又努力，旅行很远找到很多植物。比方说，灵芝和夹竹桃。他一吃各种各样的植物就知道能不能吃这些植物。然后，神农写他的主意。他喜欢植物的味道，特别是花。神农遇到了一个黄色的花，一吃就觉得不舒服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 旅行很远：走很远，Use “走很远” to describe covering a long distance on foot; “旅行” does not directly take a distance phrase in this way.
- 一个黄色的花：一朵黄色的花，Flowers take the measure word “朵,” not the general measure word “个.”
- 特别是花：特别是花的味道，“特别是” should introduce the specific kind of taste being discussed; adding “花的味道” completes the noun phrase.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农写他的主意：他写他的主意，Use a pronoun because the same character was mentioned in the immediately preceding sentence and no other person competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农遇到了一个黄色的花：他遇到了一朵黄色的花，Use a pronoun for the same character after several nearby references to him.
- Coherence (Temporality): 找到很多植物：找到了很多植物，This is the final action in a completed sequence, so it needs “了” to mark completion.
- Coherence (Temporality): 写他的主意：写下了自己的主意，“然后” presents this as the next completed story action, so use “写下了”; “写下” means to record an idea in writing, and “了” marks the completed action.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 旅行很远找到很多植物：走了很远以后，找到了很多植物，Use “以后” to show the sequence: after travelling a long distance, he found many plants.
- Expressiveness: At the travelling spot, add a setting detail to show where he searches, for example: “他走进高山，仔细观察路边的植物。”
- Expressiveness: When he sees the flower, describe its appearance more vividly, for example: “那儿开着又大又漂亮的黄色花，像星星一样。”
- Expressiveness: After he feels unwell, add his immediate thought or feeling, for example: “他捂着肚子想：‘我会不会生病？’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show that the character is intelligent and hardworking, and you use “一……就……” correctly to show a quick result.  
- Naming “灵芝”和“夹竹桃” also gives your scene specific plant examples.

## Scene 3

> 发现了夹竹桃有毒。别人看到他，很快给他吃一个灵芝。神农一吃就觉得比较好。然后，他继续写他发现的信息。会告诉他的朋友哪一个植物能不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别人：有人，Use this when introducing an unspecified person who sees him; 别人 means “other people” and requires a contrast with a stated person or group.
- 给他吃一个灵芝：给了他一个灵芝吃，The giving action is completed, so the verb needs 了; this word order also clearly shows that the plant is for him to eat.
- 写他发现的信息：写下他发现的信息，When recording information or discoveries, 写下 means to write it down and preserve it as a record.
- 哪一个植物能不能吃：哪一种植物能吃，哪一种不能吃，哪一个 asks for an identified choice, while 能不能 forms a yes-or-no question; use two parallel clauses to explain which plants are edible and which are not.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 比较好 -- “神农一吃就觉得比较好”：好一些，more specifically shows that his condition has improved compared with before he ate the medicine.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: At the moment he feels better, add a physical detail to show the change: “神农的肚子不那么疼了。”
- Expressiveness: When the person gives him the medicine, add a short line of speech to make the rescue more vivid: “快吃这个灵芝！”
- Expressiveness: When he writes down the information, add what he learns about the two plants: “夹竹桃有毒，灵芝可以做药。”

### Encouragement
You used “然后” to move the events forward clearly, and the pattern “一……就……” effectively shows that the medicine worked immediately. You also kept 神农 as the clear main character of the scene.
