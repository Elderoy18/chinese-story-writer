## Scene 1

> 很久以前，有一个老男人叫神农。神农在古代每一天看到了别的小镇人生病还是不舒服，所以他决定开始找新的药。他下一天开始爬当地的山脉，找了各种各样的植物。神农试一试每个植物，知道有什么好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别的：删除，别的 distinguishes other items from one already mentioned; no town has been established for this comparison.
- 小镇人：小镇上的人们，this refers to a concrete group of people, so use 人们; 小镇上的 also clearly shows where they live.
- 生病还是不舒服：生病或者身体不舒服，还是 normally presents alternatives in a question or an indirect question; use 或者 for alternatives in this statement.
- 下一天：第二天，第二天 is the natural time expression for the day after the event just described.
- 找了各种各样的植物：找到了各种各样的植物，找 means “look for,” while 找到 means “find”; the sentence describes the plants he found.
- 每个植物：每种植物，种 is the suitable measure word when referring to each type of plant.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好处 -- “知道有什么好处”：对身体有什么好处，specifies that he is learning about each plant’s effects on the body；有什么作用，allows him to learn about a plant’s particular function rather than only a general benefit

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农在古代每一天看到了小镇上的人们生病或者身体不舒服：他在古代每一天看到了小镇上的人们生病或者身体不舒服，after the name appeared in the immediately preceding sentence and no other character competes with it, use a pronoun.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农试一试每种植物：他试一试每种植物，after the previous sentence already refers to the same person as 他, repeating the name is unnecessary; use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 每一天看到了：每天看到，this is a routine action, so use the zero particle rather than 了, which marks a completed event.
- Coherence (Expressiveness): When he begins climbing, add a simple description of the mountain setting, such as “高山上长着很多绿色的植物。”
- Coherence (Expressiveness): Add Shennong’s purpose as an internal thought before he searches, such as “神农想：我一定要找到能帮助人们的植物。”
- Coherence (Expressiveness): Make his testing action more vivid by showing his careful observation, such as “他仔细看了看植物的颜色和叶子。”

### Encouragement
You clearly introduce Shennong at the beginning and use a cause-and-effect connection with “所以” to explain why he begins searching. Your scene also has a logical sequence from seeing sick people, to climbing mountains, to studying plants.

## Scene 2

> 比方说，他一次试一试有些植物的叶，吃得又新鲜又甜。煮这些叶以后，他一喝就知道他发现新的茶叶。虽然他找到了很多的新的植物，但是他的旅程有的时候特别是危险。比方说，他一吃灵芝就开始生病。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一次试一试有些植物的叶：尝了尝有些植物的叶子，Verb reduplication such as “试一试” is for a light or tentative plan; for an action that happens in the narrative, use the completed form “尝了尝.”
- 吃得又新鲜又甜：觉得又新鲜又甜，“吃得” introduces a complement describing the manner or degree of eating, but “新鲜、甜” describe the leaves themselves; “觉得” introduces the character’s judgment about their taste.
- 特别是危险：特别危险，“特别是” introduces a particular example or item, while “特别” directly modifies the adjective “危险.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 新的 -- “很多的新的植物”：未尝过的，specifies that these were plants he had not tasted before；陌生的，emphasizes that the plants were unfamiliar to him.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 煮这些叶以后：煮了这些叶以后，The boiling must be completed before the later action introduced by “以后,” so it needs “了.”
- Coherence (Temporality): 发现新的茶叶：发现了新的茶叶，This discovery is presented as a completed result after he drinks the tea, so use “了.”
- Expressiveness: After the drinking action, add a sensory detail about the tea, such as “茶水闻起来很香，喝起来有一点苦，可是很舒服。”
- Expressiveness: At the dangerous moment, add Shen Nong’s feeling or thought, such as “他肚子很疼，心里很害怕。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of thought when he learns about tea, such as “他想：这种叶子也许可以帮助生病的人。”

### Encouragement
- You used the paired connector “虽然……但是……” correctly to show a contrast between the discoveries and the danger of the journey.
- The “一……就……” structure also clearly shows a quick cause-and-result sequence.

## Scene 3

> 一个爬山的男人正好看到神农的麻烦，然后给了他一些药。神农最终他的小镇回到，跟别的人分享他的发现。你认为神农的故事真的吗？这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他的小镇回到：回到他的小镇，The destination phrase must follow the verb; it cannot come before the verb in this structure.
- 跟别的人：跟小镇的人们，别的人 normally contrasts with a particular person or group already mentioned; here the people in the town are the intended audience.
- 神农的故事真的吗：神农的故事是真的吗，Use 是 to link the noun subject with the predicate in this yes-or-no question.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 药 -- “给了他一些药”：草药，specifies that the medicine is plant-based, which fits the setting of discovering useful plants.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 回到：回到了，Returning to the town is a completed event, so the verb needs 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处：这就要看你相不相信中药对身体有好处了，This structure asks what the result depends on, so use the yes-or-no form and the final 了 that belongs to this structure.
- Expressiveness: After the man gives medicine, add Shen Nong’s physical feeling to show why the help matters. Example: “神农吃了药以后，肚子慢慢不疼了。”
- Expressiveness: Describe the helpful man with one visual detail when he appears. Example: “一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人正好走过来。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech when the man offers the medicine. Example: “你先吃这个，可能会好一点。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the sequence of help, return, and sharing, and your final question gives the scene an engaging ending. The use of “最终” also helps show that Shen Nong’s journey has reached an important point.
