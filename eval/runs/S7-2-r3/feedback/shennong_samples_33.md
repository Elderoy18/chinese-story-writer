## Scene 1

> 古代时候，人不知道他们能吃什么植物，常常一吃就死。但是不知道为什么，没有办法。所以有一天，神农出去试一试很多种植物，告诉人什么植物是安全什么是危险。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代时候：在古代，a time noun followed by a time expression is redundant here; use the simpler historical-time phrase.
- 是安全什么是危险：是安全的，什么植物是危险的，adjectives used to describe which plants belong in each category need 的, and repeating the plant phrase makes the two categories parallel.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很多种 -- “试一试很多种植物”：各种各样的，emphasizes that the plants differ in many forms and types, not only that there are many of them.
- 没有办法 -- “没有办法”：找不到解决的办法，makes clear that the people cannot find a solution to the problem.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 人不知道他们能吃什么植物：人们不知道什么植物能吃，the sentence refers to a concrete group of people, so use 人们; removing 他们 also avoids an unnecessary pronoun whose referent is already the subject.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农出去：一个叫神农的人出去，at this character’s first mention, introduce the name together with a full noun phrase so the reader knows who the character is.
- Coherence (Reference): 告诉人：告诉人们，this refers to the group of people in the story, so 人们 is needed rather than the generic noun 人.
- Coherence (Temporality): 出去试一试很多种植物：出去试了很多种植物，this is a completed past event introduced by 有一天; in the action sequence, 了 goes on the final action 试.
- Expressiveness: After the deaths are mentioned, add a concrete consequence to show why the problem is serious, such as “很多人吃了有毒的植物，肚子疼得很厉害。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where 神农 acts, add his determined thought: “神农想：我一定要找出能吃的植物。”
- Expressiveness: Make the exploration more visual by adding a simple setting detail: “他走到高山上，仔细看各种各样的植物。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish the central problem first and then show how 神农 responds to it, which gives this short scene a logical direction.
- Your use of 一吃就 effectively shows an immediate result and makes the danger feel strong.

## Scene 2

> 发现有的植物对身体非常好，特别是茶，让肚子感觉又暖和又清楚。他告诉朋友：“你吃哪种植物，这就要看你的口味。比方说，你爱吃甜的，可能喜欢糖。”他最喜欢吃花，除了夹竹桃以外，什么都花想吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 清楚：舒服，清楚 describes clear information or thinking, not a physical feeling in the stomach; 舒服 describes physical comfort.
- 什么都花想吃：什么花都想吃，when a question word such as 什么 modifies a noun in an “all/every” meaning, the noun comes directly after it, followed by 都.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好 -- “有的植物对身体非常好”：有益，states more precisely that the plants benefit health
- 甜的 -- “你爱吃甜的”：甜食，specifies sweet foods rather than using a general descriptive phrase

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 你吃哪种植物，这就要看你的口味：你想吃哪种植物？这就要看你的口味了，this structure gives an answer to an uncertain question; the question should come before 这就要看……了.
- Expressiveness: At the tea discovery, add one concrete sensory detail, such as “神农喝了一口茶，觉得肚子暖暖的，很舒服。”
- Expressiveness: You could show his reaction to the discovery with a short thought, such as “他想：原来茶对身体有好处！”

### Encouragement
You clearly connect the plant discovery to a specific example, tea, and you try using direct speech to show the character’s ideas. The contrast with 夹竹桃 also shows that you are thinking about which plants are safe to eat.

## Scene 3

> 他发现这个口味，一百岁时候，不小心吃了毒的花。一吃就开始死。但是生病时候告诉人什么植物是药，什么植物是毒。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一百岁时候：一百岁的时候，The multi-syllable age expression needs 的 before 时候.
- 毒的花：有毒的花，Use 有毒的 to describe a plant that contains poison; 毒的 is not the natural modifier here.
- 死：生病，死 is a final event and does not naturally describe the beginning of illness after eating a plant; 生病 describes the onset of being ill.
- 生病时候：生病的时候，The multi-syllable verb phrase needs 的 before 时候.
- 什么植物是毒：什么植物有毒，Use 有毒 to describe the property of a plant; 是毒 would mean that the plant itself is poison.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不小心 -- “不小心吃了毒的花”：误食，specifically means accidentally eating something and is especially precise for eating a harmful plant.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 人：人们，This refers to a concrete group of listeners, so use 人们 rather than the generic noun 人.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉：告诉了，This is a completed telling action in the past narrative, so add 了 after the verb.
- Expressiveness: At the moment he eats the flower, add a visual detail to make the danger more memorable: “黄色的花像星星一样，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: After he becomes ill, show his physical feeling rather than only naming the illness: “他的肚子疼了起来，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: When he explains the plants, use a short line of dialogue: “这种像伞一样的植物是药，可以吃；这种花有毒，不能吃。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show a cause-and-effect sequence: eating the harmful flower leads to illness, and then the character shares what he has learned. You also use the useful pattern 一……就…… correctly to show an immediate result.
