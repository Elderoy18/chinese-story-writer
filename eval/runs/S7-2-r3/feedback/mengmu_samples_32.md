## Scene 1

> 有一天孟子去了墓地玩儿，但是他妈妈觉得这是不合适的地方让孩子玩，所以她决定了他们会搬家找更好的地方。她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福，而且住在这儿教育不太好。因为她望子成龙，所以她会一直搬家才能找到质量好的教育。然后他们搬到了离城市更近的地方。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不合适的地方让孩子玩：不适合孩子玩的地方，Use “适合” to say that a place suits a person or activity; the activity should modify the place with “的”.
- 他们会搬家找更好的地方：搬到更好的地方，After “决定”, use a verb phrase directly; the subject is already understood as the mother, and “搬到” clearly shows the destination of the move.
- 她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福：她担心儿子的童年不会幸福，“恐怕” means “I am afraid that” and cannot describe her personal worry in this structure; use “担心” for a person being worried.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 更好 -- “搬到更好的地方”：更适合孩子成长的，explains specifically why the new place would be better for the child.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 决定了：决定，A mental-state verb takes the zero particle here, not “了”; the sentence describes her decision rather than a completed physical action.
- Expressiveness: At the cemetery scene, add one concrete detail about what Mengzi notices, such as “墓地旁有人在哭，也有人来祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: After the mother worries about his childhood, add her inner thought or speech, such as “她心想：‘这儿不适合孩子长大，我得搬家。’”
- Expressiveness: At the new home near the city, describe the busy setting, such as “街上有很多商人，从早到晚都很热闹。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the mother’s reason for moving, and your cause-and-effect connection is easy to follow. The final sentence also gives the scene a clear next step in the story.

## Scene 2

> 他们住才一月就又搬家。古代的时候，一般人不喜欢卖东西的人。但是孟子老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿，害得妈妈着急得很，因为她恐怕儿子会成为跟他们很像。她告诉了孟子：他们不是朋友，而是不好的人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他们住才一月：他们才住了一个月，In a 才……就…… pattern, 才 comes before the verb phrase; the completed period of living there takes 了.
- 古代的时候：在古代，The two time expressions are redundant because 古代 already refers to a time period.
- 孟子老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿：孟子老是跟这些卖东西的人玩儿，Do not use both 老是 and 常常 together before the same habitual action; either one alone expresses repetition.
- 她恐怕儿子会：她担心儿子会，恐怕 means “I am afraid that” and cannot follow another subject in this structure; 担心 expresses that she is worried.
- 成为跟他们很像：变得跟他们很像，成为 introduces an identity or role, while 变得 is used for a change into a state or quality.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 卖东西的人 -- “这些卖东西的人”：商人，names their occupation more precisely and makes the sentence more concise.
- 不好的人 -- “他们不是朋友，而是不好的人”：坏人，gives a clearer, more natural noun for people with bad behaviour.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 又搬家：又搬家了，The move is a completed event, so the final verb needs 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉了孟子：告诉孟子，A saying verb that introduces quoted speech uses the zero particle rather than 了.
- Expressiveness: Before the sentence about the sellers, add a market detail to show why the place attracts Mengzi, for example: “街上人来人往，叫卖声不断。”
- Expressiveness: After the sentence about Mengzi playing with the sellers, show what he finds interesting, for example: “孟子觉得收钱和讨价还价很好玩儿。”
- Expressiveness: Before the mother speaks, show her feeling through an action, for example: “妈妈皱着眉头，心里很着急。”

### Encouragement
You used “但是” to set up a clear contrast between ordinary people and Mengzi. You also included a direct quote from the mother, which makes the scene feel more like a story rather than only a list of events.

## Scene 3

> 你不是当数学家，而是当哲学家，不能当卖东西的人。所以他们又搬家。在他们第三个家，孟子学了怎么祭拜。妈妈感觉这个地方适合他们，告诉了孟子：我们找到了最后的家。在这儿妈妈让儿子从早到晚学习，他难怪成为有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在他们第三个家：在他们的第三个家里，A location introduced by 在 should use a complete place phrase; 的 marks the possessive relationship, and 里 makes the home clearly function as the location.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 在这儿妈妈让儿子从早到晚学习：在这儿她让儿子从早到晚学习，Use a pronoun for the close re-occurrence of the mother, since 妈妈 was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other female character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Temporality): 所以他们又搬家：所以他们又搬家了，The move is a completed event in the narrative, so the final verb needs 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉了孟子：告诉孟子，A saying verb that introduces quoted speech takes the zero particle rather than 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他难怪成为有名的哲学家：难怪他成为了有名的哲学家，Becoming a philosopher is presented as a completed change of state, so 成为 takes 了.
- Expressiveness: At “妈妈感觉这个地方适合他们,” show her relief through an action and speech, for example: “妈妈松了一口气，说：‘这儿很安静，适合你学习。’”
- Expressiveness: At “孟子学了怎么祭拜,” add a small observed action to make the scene easier to picture, for example: “孟子学着大人的样子，低头祭拜。”

### Encouragement
- You used the contrast pattern “不是……而是……” appropriately to show what the mother wants for her son.
- Your scene also has a clear sequence of moving, finding a home, studying, and later becoming a philosopher.
