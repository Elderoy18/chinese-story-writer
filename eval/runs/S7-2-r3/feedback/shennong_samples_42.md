## Scene 1

> 古代的时候有一个人叫神农。他需要当上医，一看别的人就想帮助他们。但是，他有一个问题，他不知道什么东西能做药。神农听说一个远远的地方有一种植物，什么病人吃都会觉得身体更好。神农三个月走去，找到有植物的地方，他看到一个很漂亮的植物，又香又好看。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，The original expression marks time twice, because 古代 already refers to a time period. [vocab list]
- 医：医生，In modern Chinese, 医生 is the usual occupation noun; 上医 is a fixed term meaning a highly skilled physician, not the result complement intended after 当. [rule: none]
- 一看：一看到，Here the meaning is “see/perceive,” so use 看到 rather than 看, which means “look at.” [vocab list]
- 别的人：病人，别人 distinguishes other people from a previously mentioned person or group, but no such contrast exists here; 病人 matches the medical context. [teacher rule: bieren]
- 什么病人：病人，什么 introduces a question about identity or type and cannot by itself introduce a general group here; use the generic noun 病人. [rule: none]
- 一个很漂亮的植物：一株很漂亮的植物，株 is the measure word that specifically matches an individual plant; 个 is too generic in this context. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 帮助 -- “就想帮助他们”：医治，specifies that the help is medical treatment for patients；救治，emphasizes treating sick people and helping them recover

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农三个月走去：他三个月走去，After 神农 was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other person competes as the referent, use a pronoun for this close re-occurrence. [rule: redundancy_delete]
- Coherence (Reference): 有植物的地方：有那种植物的地方，The earlier sentence introduced a particular kind of plant, so repeating it with 那种植物 makes the destination clear rather than referring vaguely to any place with plants. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Temporality): 三个月走去，找到：走了三个月，找到了，Place the duration after the verb, and use 了 to show that the walking and finding were completed narrative actions. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Conjunction): 找到有植物的地方，他看到：找到了有那种植物的地方以后，他看到，Use 以后 to show the sequence: he saw the plant after he had found the place. [rule: time_expression]
- Expressiveness: At the point where he sees the plant, add visual detail about its flowers or leaves: “黄色的花像星星一样，又大又亮。”
- Expressiveness: Add Shen Nong’s reaction after he hears about the plant: “神农想：‘这种植物也许真的能治病。’”
- Expressiveness: Add a sensory detail after the plant appears: “他走近一闻，闻到了一股淡淡的香味。”

### Encouragement
You clearly establish Shen Nong’s wish to help others and give him a problem to solve, which creates a strong reason for his journey. The description “又香又好看” also gives the plant an appealing sensory detail.

## Scene 2

> 他觉得，这种一定是做药的花。原来他想错了。吃了以后，神农快生病。 这时一个穿着蓝色衣服的人帮神农，快给他一种红的灵芝。神农吃了以后，他没有病，想起来以前一个人告诉他，灵芝对身体有好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- Nothing to flag.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好处 -- “灵芝对身体有好处”：有益于身体，states the health benefit more directly and precisely in this context

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他（“神农吃了以后，他没有病”）：∅，After the same character has been named in the opening time clause, use zero form for the continuing subject in the same sentence. [rule: redundancy_delete]
- Coherence (Temporality): 快生病：很快生病了，Use 很快 for an action that happened quickly; add 了 because becoming sick is a completed change of state. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 快给他一种红的灵芝：赶快给了他一种红的灵芝，Use 赶快 for an action done urgently, and add 了 because giving the medicine is a completed narrative action. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 没有病：没有病了，Add 了 to show the change from being ill to no longer being ill. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 想起来以前一个人告诉他：想起了以前一个人告诉他的话，Use 了 after the completed act of remembering; adding 的话 makes clear what he remembered. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: After the illness begins, add a physical reaction to show how serious it feels: “神农肚子疼了起来，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: When the blue-clothed person helps, show the urgency of the action: “他赶快拿出灵芝，说：‘快吃这个！’”
- Expressiveness: At the moment of remembering, add Shen Nong’s thought: “神农心想：‘原来灵芝真的能帮我。’”

### Encouragement
- You used the time sequence “吃了以后” clearly to show what happened after Shen Nong ate the plant. You also introduced the helper with a concrete detail, “穿着蓝色衣服的人,” which helps the reader picture the scene.

## Scene 3

> 啊，他怎么忘记了？他把灵芝放在包里。一回家就告诉别人。他找到药以后他写下怎么做药在书上。所以人们都知道，那红灵芝对身体好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 写下怎么做药在书上：在书上写下做药的方法，The location phrase should come before the verb, and the thing written should be a noun phrase such as a method rather than an embedded question. [rule: none]
- 对身体好处：对身体有好处，The phrase requires the verb 有 between 对身体 and 好处. [vocab list]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 放 -- “他把灵芝放在包里”：装进，specifies that the lingzhi is put inside the bag.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 那红灵芝：那个红灵芝，A demonstrative such as 那 needs a measure word before a noun; 个 makes this previously mentioned lingzhi clear. [teacher rule: zhe_na_mw]
- Coherence (Temporality): 他把灵芝放在包里：他把灵芝放在包里了，This is a completed action in the story, so it needs 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 一回家就告诉别人：一回家就告诉了别人，Telling other people is a completed event here, so use 了 after the verb. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 写下：写下了，Writing the method down is completed before people know about it, so add 了 after the resultative complement 下. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: After putting the lingzhi in the bag, add a small feeling detail, such as “他摸了摸包里的灵芝，心里很高兴。”
- Expressiveness: When he tells others, add a short line of speech, such as “灵芝可以做药，对身体有好处！”
- Expressiveness: Describe the book more clearly at the writing moment, such as “他认真地把灵芝的样子和作用写在书上。”

### Encouragement
You connected the ending clearly: writing down the discovery leads naturally to people learning about it. You also used the useful resultative verb “写下,” which fits the idea of recording knowledge.
