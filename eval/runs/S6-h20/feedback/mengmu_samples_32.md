## Scene 1

> 有一天孟子去了墓地玩儿，但是他妈妈觉得这是不合适的地方让孩子玩，所以她决定了他们会搬家找更好的地方。她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福，而且住在这儿教育不太好。因为她望子成龙，所以她会一直搬家才能找到质量好的教育。然后他们搬到了离城市更近的地方。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 这是不合适的地方让孩子玩：这是一个不适合孩子玩儿的地方，Use a modifier ending in 的 before the noun; 不适合孩子玩儿 describes the place.
- 住在这儿教育不太好：这儿的教育不太好，When describing the education in a location, use the location as a modifier with 的 before the noun.
- 她会一直搬家才能找到质量好的教育：她得一直搬家，才能找到质量好的教育，The pattern 得……才能…… expresses that an action is necessary in order to achieve a result; 会 only predicts a future action.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 更好的 -- “找更好的地方”：更适合孩子成长的，specifies that the new place suits the child’s development rather than simply being generally better.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 有一天孟子去了墓地玩儿：古代，有一个小男孩叫孟子。一天，他去了墓地玩儿，On first mention, introduce the character with a name and a brief identifying noun phrase; after that, use a pronoun for the close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 她决定了：她决定，Decide is a mental-state verb, so it normally uses the zero particle rather than 了.
- Expressiveness: After the visit to the cemetery, add a concrete detail about what the child sees or does: “孟子看见很多人伤心地祭拜，也跟着学他们的样子。”
- Expressiveness: At the mother’s decision, add her inner feeling or a short thought: “孟母很担心，心想：‘这儿不适合孩子长大。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the mother’s concern for her child and connect her motivation with the move using the 因为……所以…… pattern. The final sentence also gives the scene a clear next step with 然后.

## Scene 2

> 他们住才一月就又搬家。古代的时候，一般人不喜欢卖东西的人。但是孟子老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿，害得妈妈着急得很，因为她恐怕儿子会成为跟他们很像。她告诉了孟子：他们不是朋友，而是不好的人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 住才一月：才住一月，In the pattern with a short duration, 才 comes before the verb: 才 + verb + duration.
- 古代的时候：在古代，This is the standard time expression for a historical setting; 古代的时候 is not natural here.
- 老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿：老是跟这些卖东西的人玩儿，Do not use two frequency adverbs that modify the same habitual action; 老是 already expresses repeated behaviour.
- 她恐怕儿子会成为跟他们很像：她担心儿子会变得跟他们很像，恐怕 cannot take another subject such as 她 in this meaning; 担心 means that she is worried. Also, 成为 normally takes a noun identity, while 变得 is used before an adjective or state such as 很像.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 着急 -- “害得妈妈着急得很”：焦急不安，adds the idea that she is worried and unable to settle down.
- 不好 -- “而是不好的人”：品行不好的人，specifies that the people have bad behaviour or character.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们住才一月就又搬家：他们才住了一个月，就又搬家了，Use 了 for completed events: the one-month stay ended, and the later move was also completed.
- Coherence (Temporality): 她告诉了孟子：她告诉孟子，A speaking verb that introduces quoted speech normally takes the zero particle rather than 了.
- Expressiveness: After the mention of the sellers, add a marketplace detail to make the setting more vivid, such as “街上从早到晚都很热闹，商人不停地叫卖。”
- Expressiveness: Show the mother’s worry through an action or an inner thought before her speech, such as “妈妈皱着眉头，心想：‘要是孟子一直跟他们玩儿，恐怕会学坏。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the cause of the mother’s worry with “害得妈妈着急得很，因为……”, and the final quoted speech makes her reaction easy to understand.

## Scene 3

> 你不是当数学家，而是当哲学家，不能当卖东西的人。所以他们又搬家。在他们第三个家，孟子学了怎么祭拜。妈妈感觉这个地方适合他们，告诉了孟子：我们找到了最后的家。在这儿妈妈让儿子从早到晚学习，他难怪成为有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他难怪：难怪他，The adverbial expression must come before the clause it comments on; here it introduces the reason why his later success is unsurprising.
- 在他们第三个家：在他们的第三个家里，A possessive modifier normally uses 的, and 里 marks the location after 在.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 卖东西的人 -- “不能当卖东西的人”：商人，names the specific occupation of a person who sells goods.

### Coherence & Expressiveness

- Coherence (Temporality): 成为：成为了，Use 了 to show that becoming a philosopher is a completed change of state.
- Expressiveness: At the third home, add a concrete learning detail, such as “第三个家在学校旁边，孟子常常听见学生读书的声音。”
- Expressiveness: Show the mother’s feelings when she sees her son studying, such as “妈妈看见孟子认真学习，心里很高兴。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech to make the mother’s decision more vivid, such as “妈妈说：‘这儿的环境很适合你学习。’”

### Encouragement
You used the contrast pattern “不是……而是……” effectively to show the mother’s hopes for her son. The phrase “从早到晚” also gives a clear sense of how diligently he studied.
