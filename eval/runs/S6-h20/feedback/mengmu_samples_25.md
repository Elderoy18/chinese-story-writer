## Scene 1

> 这是古代的故事。孟子小的时候，他的爸爸很早就去世了。从他的家到墓地很近，所以他每天老是从早到晚去墓地跟朋友们玩玩儿。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 从他的家到墓地很近：墓地离他的家很近，Use 离 to express that two places are close in distance; 从……到…… describes a route or range and does not fit directly with 很近 here.

- Expressiveness: After the father’s death, show Mengzi’s feeling with a short inner thought, such as “孟子很想爸爸，心里很难过。”
- Expressiveness: When describing his visits with friends, add a concrete action, such as “他们学着大人的样子，低头拜一拜。”

### Encouragement

- The phrase 从早到晚 gives the reader a strong sense that this was a regular part of his daily life.

## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈觉得在墓地玩儿，害得，所以他们搬家。搬家对孟子有好的影响，他才五岁，可是每天不是写字就是帮妈妈的工作。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他的妈妈觉得在墓地玩儿，害得：他的妈妈觉得孟子在墓地玩儿，害得来祭拜的人很生气，The verb phrase with 害得 must include the affected person and a result after it; adding 孟子 also makes clear who is playing.
- 他才五岁，可是每天不是写字就是：他才五岁，就每天不是写字，就是，Use the pattern 才 + age + 就 + verb to show that someone began doing something at an early age.
- 帮妈妈的工作：帮妈妈做事，After 帮 + person, use a verb phrase to state the help given. 妈妈的工作 is a noun phrase and cannot directly serve as the second action after 不是……就是…….

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好的 -- “搬家对孟子有好的影响”：积极的，specifies that the influence helps his development in a positive way.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 所以他们搬家：所以他们搬了家，Use 了 for the completed action of moving house in this finished event.
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery sentence, add the mother’s concern, such as “她怕孟子学坏了”.
- Expressiveness: Make the child’s effort more vivid after “写字”, such as “他认真地一笔一画地写字”.
- Expressiveness: Add the mother’s feeling when she sees his change, such as “妈妈看到以后，心里很高兴”.

### Encouragement
- You clearly show that the move changes Mengzi’s daily life, and you use the useful pattern “不是……就是……” to describe his routine.

## Scene 3

### Vocabulary Suggestions
