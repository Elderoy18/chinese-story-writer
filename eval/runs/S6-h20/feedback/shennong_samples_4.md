## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个男人，他的名字是神农。他看到了有很多人不知道哪个植物好吃，也不知道什么植物对你的身体有好处。这个问题神农觉得很糟糕，所以神农去了吃每种植物。他吃了一种植物以后，就写了这个植物对你有没有好处。有的时候，神农吃了很好吃的植物。有的时候，他吃了很危险的植物。他吃完了每种植物以后，会来跟他的朋友聊到他吃的植物了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，Use the established time expression for a historical setting.
- 写了这个植物对你有没有好处：记下了这种植物对身体有没有好处，Use 记下 for recording information; 这 must take a measure word before a noun, and a general statement about plants’ effects should not switch to the listener-specific pronoun 你.
- 去了吃每种植物：去吃了每种植物，In a series of actions, place 了 on the final completed action; 去吃了 expresses that the eating was completed.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 糟糕 -- “这个问题神农觉得很糟糕”：严重，specifies that the problem has important harmful consequences rather than merely being bad.
- 好吃 -- “很好吃的植物”：可口，describes a pleasant taste more precisely in this context.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 所以神农去了吃每种植物：所以去吃了每种植物，Use zero form when the same character continues as the subject within the same sentence; repeating the name is unnecessary.
- Coherence (Temporality): 会来跟他的朋友聊到他吃的植物了：会来跟他的朋友聊到他吃的植物，The modal verb 会 takes the zero particle; final 了 cannot mark a completed action after a modal expressing a habitual or possible future action.
- Expressiveness: After the sentence about people not knowing plants, add the consequence to show why the problem matters, such as “很多人吃了有毒的植物，常常生病。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where he decides to try plants, add an inner thought to show his motivation, such as “神农想：‘我要找到可以治病的植物。’”
- Expressiveness: When he records each plant, add one concrete detail about what he writes, such as “他记下了植物的颜色和吃了以后的感觉。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduce the main character and keep the scene focused on his effort to learn about plants. Your use of repeated time phrases also helps show that he tries different plants more than once.

- 这就要看你的口味了：这就要看你喜欢什么口味了，The pattern with “这就要看……了” must be followed by an embedded question, such as a wh-question; “你喜欢什么口味” provides that question form.
- 一个别的人：另一个人，“别人” refers to other people as a group distinct from the speaker or listener. When introducing one additional questioner, use “另一个人”.
- 一个别的人：另一个人，“别人” refers to other people as a group distinct from the speaker or listener. Use “另一个人” for one further person in the scene.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 坏处 -- “哪个植物对你的身体有坏处”：有害，describes more precisely that the plant itself can harm the body.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 要是你喜欢吃甜的，有很多甜的水果：要是你喜欢吃甜的，就有很多甜的水果，The conditional connector “要是” introduces a condition, and “就” marks the result.
- Expressiveness: After the examples of sweet fruit, add one sensory detail, such as “苹果又甜又脆。” This helps the reader imagine why the fruit suits someone who likes sweet food.
- Expressiveness: When the second questioner asks about healthy plants, add a short evaluative detail about tea, such as “茶不但好喝，对身体也有好处。” This makes the example more informative.
- Expressiveness: At the final question about harmful plants, show the questioner’s feeling with a brief action or tone, such as “他有点担心地问……”. This gives the dialogue more life.

### Encouragement
You used clear question-and-answer dialogue, and your examples of fruits and vegetables match each question well. You also correctly used “对你的身体有好处” to describe plants that benefit health.

## Scene 3

> 神农说，有一天，我吃了一个黄色的花，名字是夹竹桃。我一吃夹竹桃，就觉得这种植物对我的脑有坏处。可是，一个灵芝和一个男人正好在我的旁边，我说，麻烦你把那个植物给我吃一下。我一吃这个灵芝就好了。所以，你得小心吃植物，因为有的植物对你的健康有坏处，特别是夹竹桃。因为夹竹桃有毒。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一个黄色的花：一朵黄色的花，use the measure word “朵” for an individual flower; “个” is not the usual measure word here.
- 对我的脑有坏处：对我的脑子有害，“脑子” is the natural everyday noun for a person’s brain, and “有害” directly means “causes harm.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好了 -- “我一吃这个灵芝就好了”：痊愈了，specifically means that an illness has fully recovered；好转了，means the condition became better, without necessarily being completely cured.
- 旁边 -- “正好在我的旁边”：身边，more specifically describes a person or object being close to someone.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 那个植物：灵芝，there are two plants in the nearby context, so “那个植物” is ambiguous; repeat the noun “灵芝” to make the intended object clear.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 特别是夹竹桃。因为夹竹桃有毒。：特别是夹竹桃，因为它有毒。，a clause beginning with “因为” should connect to the result or statement it explains; here it gives the reason why this plant is especially dangerous.
- Expressiveness: After the yellow flower appears, add one visual detail to make it memorable, such as “那朵花又黄又大，像天上的星星一样。”
- Expressiveness: When the narrator feels ill, add an internal feeling or body reaction, such as “我的肚子突然疼了起来，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: The man could reply after being asked for help, such as “他马上把灵芝给我，说：‘快吃这个！’”

### Encouragement
- You used the “一……就……” pattern well to show that one action immediately led to another. Your final warning also clearly explains the danger of poisonous plants.
