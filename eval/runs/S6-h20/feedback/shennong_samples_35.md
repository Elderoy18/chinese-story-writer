## Scene 1

> 在古代，很多医生不知道怎么用药。所以他们找到了很多植物对药。神农先看到很多病人，他们吃很多植物对药。但是我觉得他们的吃的植物，让他们病人。神农觉得这个植物可能特别是毒。所以他想找一个植物对药，他们可以吃对药。正好，他找到了一把花，他想把花放在茶里，对药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 植物对药：可以做药的植物，"对药" is not a valid phrase for saying that plants can be used as medicine; the modifier phrase must come before "植物".
- 很多植物对药：很多有毒的植物，"对药" cannot describe poisonous plants; use "有毒的" before the noun.
- 他们的吃的植物：他们吃的植物，when a verb phrase modifies a noun, the subject goes directly before the verb; "他们的" cannot introduce this verb phrase.
- 让他们病人：让他们生病，"病人" is a noun meaning a sick person, while "生病" is the verb phrase needed after "让".
- 一个植物对药：一种可以做药的植物，use the measure word "种" for a type of plant, and replace the invalid phrase "对药" with "可以做药".
- 吃对药：吃这种植物，"对药" cannot be the object of "吃"; the edible object here is the plant.
- 对药：用来做药，"对药" is not a verb phrase; "用来做药" clearly expresses the purpose of putting the flower into the tea.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：一个叫神农的医生，first mention should introduce a new character with both a name and a brief identifying noun phrase.
- Coherence (Reference): 他们可以吃这种植物：人们可以吃这种植物，repeat the noun when a pronoun’s referent is far away or could refer to more than one earlier group, here the doctors or the patients.
- Coherence (Temporality): 神农先看到很多病人：神农先看到了很多病人，use "了" for the completed action of seeing the patients in a sequence of past events.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们吃很多有毒的植物：他们吃了很多有毒的植物，use "了" because this describes a completed past action that caused the patients to become ill.
- Expressiveness: When describing the patients, add a visible sign of illness to make the danger clearer. Example: “有的病人捂着肚子，脸色很难看。”
- Expressiveness: At the flower, add colour and shape so that the reader can picture what Shen Nong finds. Example: “那是一朵黄色的花，像星星一样，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: Add Shen Nong’s worried thought after he notices that the plant may be poisonous. Example: “这种植物有毒吗？我得小心一点。”

### Encouragement
- You used a clear historical opening with “在古代” and connected several ideas with “所以”. You also showed Shen Nong noticing sick people and trying to find a helpful plant, which gives this scene a clear direction.

## Scene 2

> 他不知道如果这把花对你的身体好，这就要看如果这把花毒不毒了。神农想找别的植物，但是这很麻烦。正好，他找到了别的花。他一吃花，很快就生病了。他的肚子疼死了。神农不知道他吃不吃灵芝或者夹竹桃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不知道如果这把花对你的身体好：不知道这把花对他的身体好不好，An embedded yes/no question after 知道/不知道 uses an A-not-A form rather than 如果; the possessive pronoun should match 神农, not an unexplained “you.”
- 要看如果这把花毒不毒：要看这把花有没有毒，After 看, use an embedded question such as 有没有毒; 如果 introduces a condition and cannot introduce this “whether” question.
- 他一吃花，很快就生病了：他一吃花，就很快生病了，The pattern 一… must be followed by 就… to show that the second event happens as soon as the first one occurs.
- 吃不吃灵芝或者夹竹桃：吃的是灵芝还是夹竹桃，When choosing between two possible things, use 是……还是……; 吃不吃 is a yes/no question and cannot be paired with 或者 in this meaning.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 麻烦 -- “但是这很麻烦”：艰难, emphasizes that finding the plants requires real effort and is not easy；费劲, highlights the amount of effort the task takes.
- 很快 -- “很快就生病了”：没过多久, states more specifically that only a short time passed before he became ill.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农不知道他：神农不知道，Use zero form for a character mentioned again in the same sentence when no other possible referent intervenes.
- Expressiveness: When 神农 finds the flower, add one visual detail to make it memorable, for example: “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花。”
- Expressiveness: After the stomach pain, describe his physical feeling more vividly, for example: “他躺在地上，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short worried thought after he becomes ill, for example: “他想：‘我会不会死？’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the sequence from finding a flower to eating it and becoming ill, and “肚子疼死了” gives the reader a strong sense of 神农’s pain. You also used completed-action forms such as “找到了” and “生病了” effectively.

## Scene 3

> 别的人给了神农一个灵芝。这个植物可以让神农更好。比方说，他会觉得更健康。吃灵芝以后，他可以再走。现在他觉得更好。最后，神农找到了很多植物，他可以用对药。这些植物让神农很高兴。神农想跟很多人分享他的新药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别的人给了神农一个灵芝：别人给了神农一个灵芝，Use “别人” for other people when they are not being contrasted with a specific person or group already mentioned.
- 这个植物可以让神农更好：这个植物可以让神农感觉好一些，“让 + person + 更好” does not naturally describe a person’s recovery; use “感觉好一些” to describe that his physical condition has improved.
- 他可以用对药：可以用来做药，“用对药” is not a valid verb-object combination. “用来做药” means that the plants can be used as medicine.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 更好 -- “现在他觉得更好”：好多了，shows a clearer improvement after being ill.
- 再走 -- “他可以再走”：继续走，states more precisely that he is able to resume his journey.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 这些植物让神农很高兴：这些植物让他很高兴，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence: “神农” was the subject in the immediately preceding sentence, and no competing person has appeared.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农想跟很多人分享他的新药：他想跟很多人分享他的新药，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence because the previous sentence is still about the same character.
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃灵芝以后：吃了灵芝以后，Use “了” for the completed action that happens before the later action of walking again.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 这些植物让神农很高兴。神农想跟很多人分享他的新药：这些植物让他很高兴，所以他想跟很多人分享他的新药，Use “所以” to show the cause-and-effect link between his happiness and his wish to share the medicine.
- Expressiveness: After “现在他觉得更好,” add a physical detail showing recovery, such as “他的肚子不怎么疼了，也能慢慢站起来了。”
- Expressiveness: When he decides to share the medicine, add his inner thought or speech, such as “他想：‘我要告诉大家，哪些植物可以做药。’”

### Encouragement
You used several useful time markers, including “吃灵芝以后”, “现在”, and “最后”, which help the reader follow the sequence of events. You also clearly showed that the medicine helps Shen Nong recover and gives him a reason to help other people.
