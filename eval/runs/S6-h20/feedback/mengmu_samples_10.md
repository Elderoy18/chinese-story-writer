## Scene 1

> 孟子小的时候，从早到晚都跟朋友玩。孟子和他的朋友喜欢玩在墓地里，装祭拜。孟子五岁的时候，跟妈妈搬家。他们搬家不是因为孟子玩得太多，而是因为妈妈认为他们的家离墓地太近。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 喜欢玩在墓地里：喜欢在墓地里玩，A location phrase such as “在墓地里” normally comes before the action verb it modifies.
- 装祭拜：假装祭拜，To express pretending to carry out an action, use “假装 + verb” or “装作 + verb”; “装” alone does not naturally take the verb “祭拜” here.
- 跟妈妈搬家：妈妈带着他搬家，“跟” means doing an action together with someone, but the intended meaning is that the mother took the child when moving, which requires “带着”.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和他的朋友喜欢玩在墓地里：他和朋友喜欢玩在墓地里，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence: “孟子” was the subject of the preceding sentence, and no competing male character has appeared.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子五岁的时候：他五岁的时候，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence of the same character.
- Coherence (Temporality): 妈妈带着他搬家：妈妈带着他搬了家，Use “了” for the completed moving action in this past narrative event.
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery scene, add what makes the children interested in the activity: “他觉得祭拜很有趣，就学着大人的样子祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: Before the final sentence, show the mother’s worry with an inner thought or short quotation: “妈妈心想：‘这儿离墓地太近了，不适合孩子长大。’”

### Encouragement

## Scene 2

> 妈妈认为，住离墓地太近，害得孟子的心气。孟子和孟母搬到子思老师的家，跟子思老师住。孟母帮子思打扫房间和做饭。子思帮孟母教她的儿子。因为孟子现在没有朋友，他老是读书。

- 住离墓地太近，害得孟子的心气：住得离墓地太近，会影响孟子的心情，A verb followed by an adjective phrase needs the complement marker 得 in this pattern. Also, 害得 must be followed by a result predicate, not only a noun phrase.
- 老是读书：一直读书，老是 usually carries a negative, complaining tone; 一直 neutrally describes continuing to study.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟母帮子思打扫房间和做饭：她帮子思打扫房间和做饭，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the referent is clear; 孟母 was named in the immediately preceding sentence, and 她 cannot be confused with the male characters.
- Coherence (Temporality): 孟子和孟母搬到子思老师的家：孟子和孟母搬到了子思老师的家，Use 了 with 搬到 to show that the move to the new place was completed.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为孟子现在没有朋友，他一直读书：因为孟子现在没有朋友，所以他一直读书，Use 因为……所以…… to connect the cause with its result.

- Expressiveness: When describing Mengzi’s studying, add his feeling to show why he changes: “孟子觉得很孤单，只好一个人坐在房间里读书。”
