## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个人叫神农。一天，神农正在路上走，一看很多人正在吃一些植物。一些人一吃植物就生病了，神农想帮别人。所以想知道哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，This time expression is not idiomatic here; use “在古代” or “古代” to introduce the historical setting.
- 一看很多人正在吃一些植物：看到很多人正在吃一些植物，“看” means to look, while “看到” means to see; this sentence describes what Shen Nong saw.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 想知道 -- “所以想知道哪些植物能吃”：想弄清楚，emphasizes wanting to find a clear answer after seeing people become ill.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After “一些人一吃植物就生病了”, add a visible sign of illness, such as “有的人捂着肚子，脸色很难看”, to make the danger more vivid.
- Expressiveness: After “神农想帮别人”, add Shen Nong’s inner thought, such as “神农心想：我一定要弄清楚哪些植物能吃。”, to show his motivation more clearly.

### Encouragement
You introduced Shen Nong clearly when he first appeared, and you used “正在” well to show that both Shen Nong and the people were in the middle of actions. The pattern “一……就……” is also used effectively to show that eating certain plants quickly caused illness.

## Scene 2

> 然后，神农开始尝很多植物，比方说他吃过生姜、芥兰和菠菜。一些植物吃真好，特别是黄瓜。但是，一些植物毒得很，特别是夹竹桃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一些植物吃真好：一些植物吃起来真好，When describing how food tastes, use the resultative pattern with “起来” after the verb; “吃起来真好” means that the plants taste very good.
- 毒得很：有毒，“毒” is not used with degree complements such as “得很”; use “有毒” to describe poisonous plants.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: At “特别是夹竹桃,” add a visual detail to make the dangerous plant memorable, such as “黄色的花像星星一样，又大又漂亮”.
- Expressiveness: After discovering the poisonous plants, add Shennong’s reaction or a short thought, such as “神农皱着眉头想：这种植物不能吃。”
- Expressiveness: You could show that he is carefully learning from the plants by adding “他把这些植物的样子和用处记下来”.

### Encouragement

## Scene 3

> 一个人对神农说，麻烦我们可以吃什么植物？神农说，这就要看你想不想生病了。以后，神农告诉别人一些植物能吃，一些植物不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 麻烦：请问，麻烦 means “trouble” or “to inconvenience someone”; it cannot by itself introduce a direct question here. Use 请问 as a polite question opener.
- 这就要看你想不想生病了：会不会生病，这就要看你吃什么植物了，this pattern needs an uncertain condition before 这就要看……了, and the part after 看 must be a question form. The revised version explains that becoming ill depends on which plants someone eats.
- 以后：后来，use 后来 to begin a new sentence meaning “afterwards”; 以后 usually means “after a particular time or event.”
- 别人：人们，别人 distinguishes other people from the speaker or a specified person. Here 神农 is explaining this knowledge to people in general, so 人们 fits.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉：告诉了，use 了 for the completed action of explaining the information later in the story.
- Expressiveness: At the question, add the person’s worried feeling: “他担心地问：‘我们可以吃什么植物？’”
- Expressiveness: After the explanation, add a concrete contrast from the scene: “神农说：‘黄瓜能吃，可是夹竹桃有毒，不能吃。’”

### Encouragement
- You used a clear question-and-answer exchange, which helps the scene feel like a real conversation.
- The contrast between plants that can and cannot be eaten connects well to the earlier part of the story.
