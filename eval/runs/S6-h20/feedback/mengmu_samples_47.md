### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 生活 -- “孟子的妈妈安排他的生活”：日常生活，specifies the everyday routines and activities she plans for him.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 这个男孩子叫孟子：有一个男孩子叫孟子，On a first mention, use an indefinite full noun phrase rather than “这个”, which normally refers back to someone already introduced.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子是古代的人：他是古代的人，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when no other male character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Reference): 她望子成龙，所以孟子的妈妈安排他的生活：她望子成龙，所以安排他的生活，Within the same sentence, use zero form rather than repeating the same character’s noun phrase.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们常常搬了家：他们常常搬家，A habitual action takes the zero aspect marker; “常常” describes a routine, so it should not take completed-action “了”.
- Expressiveness: After the mother’s reason for moving, add an inner thought to show her concern: “她心想：‘我一定要给孟子找一个适合学习的地方。’”
- Expressiveness: Make the mother’s planning more concrete by naming one daily activity: “她每天安排孟子看书、学习。”
- Expressiveness: Add an evaluative phrase to show how seriously she takes his upbringing: “她觉得孟子的成长非常重要。”

### Encouragement
You clearly introduce the central relationship between Mencius and his mother, and you use “望子成龙” appropriately to show the mother’s hopes for her son.

## Scene 2

> 因为他们常常搬了家，中国人说，孟母三迁。孟子没有爸爸，所以，他的妈妈更重要。从这儿到那儿，妈妈教孟子很多技能。先她让孟子跟朋友玩儿，然后教他怎么买又便宜又好质量的东西。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 先她：她先，The subject should come before the time-order adverb; use the order subject + 先 + verb phrase.
- 又便宜又好质量的东西：又便宜、质量又好的东西，质量好 is the correct predicate phrase: the noun 质量 must come before 好. 好质量 is not a natural noun phrase here.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 妈妈教孟子很多技能：她教孟子很多技能，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence: the mother was just mentioned in the previous sentence, and no other female character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Temporality): 常常搬了家：常常搬家，Use the zero aspect particle for a habitual action; 常常 describes a routine, so 了 should not mark it as completed.
- Coherence (Temporality): 然后教他怎么买又便宜、质量又好的东西：然后教了他怎么买又便宜、质量又好的东西，In a completed series of actions, only the last verb takes 了; here 让 is followed by the final action 教了.
- Expressiveness: After 孟子没有爸爸，所以，他的妈妈更重要, show the mother’s sense of responsibility with an inner thought, such as: 妈妈心想：“我要好好照顾孟子。”
- Expressiveness: At 教他怎么买又便宜、质量又好的东西, add a small action or line of speech to make the lesson visible, such as: 妈妈指着一件东西说：“买东西要看价格，也要看质量。”

### Encouragement
- You use 因为……所以…… to show a reason and result, and 先……然后…… gives the final sentence a clear sequence. Your detail about learning to choose inexpensive, good-quality items also makes the mother’s teaching feel practical.
