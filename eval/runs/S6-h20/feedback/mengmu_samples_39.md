## Scene 1

> 古代的时候人觉得祭拜非常重要，孟子很小的时候，从早到晚一直祭拜，因为他妈妈告诉他祭拜很重要。虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜。他的同学们老是对他开玩笑了。孟子妈妈看了他的同学们对孟子不好的影响，所以孟子和孟子妈妈搬家了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候人觉得：在古代，人们觉得，Use “在古代” for this time expression. For a general group of people, use “人们” rather than the generic noun “人”.
- 看了：看到了，“看” means to look at, while “看到” means to successfully see or notice something.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不好 -- “他的同学们对孟子不好的影响”：负面的，specifies that the influence harms him rather than merely being generally “not good”.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子很小的时候：有一个小男孩叫孟子。他很小的时候，Follow the first-mention rule: introduce a character with a full noun phrase before using the name alone in the narrative.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子妈妈搬家了：孟子和妈妈搬家了，Follow the zero-form/pronoun rule: once “孟子” has appeared in the same clause, do not repeat his name inside “孟子妈妈”; “妈妈” clearly refers to his mother.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他的同学们老是对他开玩笑了：他的同学们老是对他开玩笑，“老是” describes a repeated routine, so it takes the zero particle rather than completed-action “了”.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜：因为他每天只祭拜，所以他没有朋友，Use “因为……所以……” to connect a cause and its result. “虽然” needs a contrasting second clause, usually introduced by “但是” or “可是”.
- Expressiveness: After the opening, add a brief setting detail to show where the worship happens, such as “墓地旁边” or “来祭拜的人”.
- Expressiveness: Show Mengzi’s own reason or feeling about the activity with a short thought, such as “觉得祭拜很有趣”.
- Expressiveness: At the point where his mother notices the influence, add her worried reaction, such as “妈妈很着急”.

### Encouragement
- You clearly show a cause-and-effect chain: the mother sees the classmates’ influence, and then the family moves. You also used “从早到晚一直” effectively to show how often Mengzi worships.

## Scene 2

> 孟子才十一岁，成为一个哲学家。孟子不是祭拜，而是看政治文章，难怪他成为了哲学家。不过，看过很多政治文章对他害得，他有很多问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 孟子才十一岁，成为一个哲学家：孟子才十一岁，就成为了一个哲学家，Use the pattern 才 + number + 就 + verb to show that an action happened at an early age; 了 marks the completed change of becoming a philosopher.
- 看过很多政治文章对他害得，他有很多问题：看过很多政治文章，害得他有很多问题，The causative pattern is cause + 害得 + affected person + result; 对他 cannot be placed before 害得 here.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 问题 -- “他有很多问题”：疑问，specifies that these are unanswered questions in his mind rather than general difficulties.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子不是祭拜：他不是祭拜，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence: the previous sentence has just named 孟子, and no other male character competes as the referent.
- Expressiveness: At the point where he reads, add a small action showing concentration, such as “他每天坐在桌子前，认真地看政治文章。”
- Expressiveness: After he develops questions, add an inner thought or quoted question, such as “他心想：‘为什么有的人有钱，有的人没有钱呢？’”

### Encouragement
- You used the contrast pattern 不是……而是…… correctly to show how his interests changed. The phrase 难怪 also gives a clear link between his reading and his later achievement.

## Scene 3

> 他一直问问题，但是不能解决那些问题。孟子长大，他开始能解决问题。他常常建议你不是学金融，而是学政治，因为如果你懂政治，你会成为高薪。后来孟子成为教授，他教学生很厉害。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不是学金融，而是学政治：不要学金融，而要学政治，After the verb “suggest,” use a negative command such as “不要” to say what someone should not study; “不是……而是……” does not fit naturally as the content of this advice.
- 会成为高薪：会有高薪，“高薪” is a noun phrase meaning high pay, not a role that someone can become. Use “有” to express receiving high pay.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 问 -- “他一直问问题”：提出，emphasizes raising questions for discussion or thought；不断提出，emphasizes that he keeps raising questions over time.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子：他，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the same character was mentioned in the preceding sentence and no other character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Reference): 你：学生，This is the first mention of the people receiving the advice, so introduce them with a full noun phrase rather than using an unexplained pronoun.
- Coherence (Temporality): 长大：长大了，Use “了” for the completed change of state: the character has grown up before beginning to solve problems.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为教授：成为了教授，Use “了” to mark the completed event of becoming a professor in the later stage of the narrative.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 孟子长大，他开始能解决问题：他长大了以后，开始能解决问题，“以后” marks the sequence: first he grows up, and then he begins to be able to solve problems.
- Expressiveness: After the character starts solving problems, add an inner thought to show his confidence, such as “原来，认真想一想就能找到办法。”
- Expressiveness: At the teaching spot, add a short line of speech that shows how he helps students, such as “别着急，我们一步一步来想。”
- Expressiveness: Add one concrete detail about his teaching, such as “他在黑板上写下问题，还让学生先说自己的想法。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the character’s development from asking questions to solving them and later teaching others. The contrast between being unable to solve problems and becoming capable is a strong, easy-to-follow idea.
