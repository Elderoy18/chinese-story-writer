- Coherence (Reference): 神农和他的家人：一个叫神农的男人和家人，First mention should briefly introduce the character by giving the name and who he is, rather than assuming the reader already knows him.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他的家人常常生病了：他的家人常常生病，The zero particle is required for a habitual situation; 常常 shows that the illness happens repeatedly, so it does not take 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们去很多不一样的地方：他们去了很多不一样的地方，Use 了 for this completed series of travel actions in the narrative.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 住在古代，所以不知道什么植物能吃：在古代，神农和他的家人不知道什么植物能吃，The ancient period is a time setting, not a direct cause-and-effect relation, so remove 所以 and use the time expression at the beginning.

- Expressiveness: Show Shen Nong’s motivation before the trip with an inner thought or a short quote, such as “神农想：‘我一定要找到能治病的植物。’” This makes his decision feel more personal.
