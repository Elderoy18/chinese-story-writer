## Scene 1

> 古代的时候有一个人叫神农。他需要当上医，一看别的人就想帮助他们。但是，他有一个问题，他不知道什么东西能做药。神农听说一个远远的地方有一种植物，什么病人吃都会觉得身体更好。神农三个月走去，找到有植物的地方，他看到一个很漂亮的植物，又香又好看。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，use this time expression for a historical setting; the listed form is not natural here.
- 当上医：当上医生，the noun for a person who practises medicine is “医生”; “医” alone does not work as this job title.
- 别的人：别人，“别的人” distinguishes people from another stated group, while “别人” means other people generally.
- 一个远远的地方：一个很远的地方，“远远地” is normally an adverb describing an action; “很远的” naturally modifies “地方”.
- 什么病人：病人，“什么” is interrogative and cannot introduce a general group of patients in this statement.
- 神农三个月走去，找到有植物的地方：神农走了三个月，找到了有这种植物的地方，put the duration after the verb, use “了” for the completed journey and discovery, and use “这种植物” to mean the particular plant mentioned before.
- 一个很漂亮的植物：一株很漂亮的植物，plants use a classifier such as “株” rather than the general classifier “个”.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好看 -- “又香又好看”：艳丽，describes the plant’s visual beauty more specifically, especially its bright and striking appearance；秀丽，suggests a delicate, graceful kind of beauty.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 病人吃都会觉得：病人吃了都会觉得，“吃了” marks the completed action that leads to the resulting feeling of improved health.
- Expressiveness: At the description of the plant, add a specific visual detail: “黄色的花像星星一样”.
- Expressiveness: After hearing about the plant, add Shennong’s determination or feeling: “一定要去找这种植物”.
- Expressiveness: When he sees the plant, add his immediate reaction: “神农觉得非常惊奇”.

### Encouragement
- You introduce Shennong clearly at the beginning and keep him as the focus of the scene.
- Your use of “一……就……” effectively shows that he quickly wants to help people when he sees them.

## Scene 2

> 他觉得，这种一定是做药的花。原来他想错了。吃了以后，神农快生病。 这时一个穿着蓝色衣服的人帮神农，快给他一种红的灵芝。神农吃了以后，他没有病，想起来以前一个人告诉他，灵芝对身体有好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 快给他一种红的灵芝：赶快给了他一种红的灵芝，In a completed narrative event, use 赶快 to show that the action was done quickly and add 了 to mark the completed giving action.
- 以前一个人告诉他：以前有一个人告诉他，Use 有 to introduce an indefinite person in a past event; without 有, this phrase does not clearly form a complete clause.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 没有病 -- “神农吃了以后，他没有病”：病好了，more precisely shows that he recovered after becoming ill.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农吃了以后，他没有病：神农吃了以后，没有病，Use zero form for the same character in the same sentence; after naming 神农 in the first clause, repeating 他 is unnecessary.
- Coherence (Temporality): 神农快生病：神农就生病了，快 indicates that something will happen soon, but this event has already happened; use 了 to mark the completed change of state.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 吃了以后，神农快生病：神农一吃，就生病了，Use 一……就…… to connect an immediate cause and effect: eating the flower immediately led to illness.
- Expressiveness: After 神农 becomes ill, add a physical feeling to make the danger clearer, such as “他的肚子疼得很厉害，难受得躺在地上。”
- Expressiveness: When the blue-clothed person helps him, add a short line of speech, such as “那个人说：‘快吃这个，也许能帮你！’”
- Expressiveness: When 神农 remembers what he learned, show his realization, such as “神农一下子想起来了：‘原来灵芝可以做药！’”

### Encouragement
- You used 这时 effectively to introduce the blue-clothed person’s arrival as an interruption in the scene. You also connected the ending to 神农’s earlier knowledge about 灵芝, which gives the scene a clear idea.

## Scene 3

> 啊，他怎么忘记了？他把灵芝放在包里。一回家就告诉别人。他找到药以后他写下怎么做药在书上。所以人们都知道，那红灵芝对身体好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 那红灵芝：那种红灵芝，A demonstrative such as “那” needs a measure word before a noun in this structure.
- 对身体好处：对身体有好处，The fixed expression is “对……有好处”; “好处” is a noun and needs “有” here.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 包 -- “他把灵芝放在包里”：随身的包，shows that he is carrying the bag with him and makes the action more concrete.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他找到药以后他写下：他找到药以后，写下，Use zero form for the same subject within one sentence when no other character competes as the referent.

- Expressiveness: After he puts the lingzhi into the bag, add his feeling: “他高兴极了，觉得自己终于找到了有用的药。”
- Expressiveness: When he returns home, add a short line of speech: “他说：‘这种红灵芝对身体有好处！’”
