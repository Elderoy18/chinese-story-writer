## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个男孩名字叫孟子。孟子和孟子的妈妈两个人住在很穷的农村。他们住的地方旁边是个墓地，墓地老是有很多人祭拜。因为人们在祭拜，他们一直在哭。孟子和孟子的妈妈住在这里才一个星期就觉得受不了了，而且害得孟子的学习。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 名字叫孟子：叫孟子的男孩，Use an attributive phrase before the noun to introduce a named character naturally: “a boy called Mencius.”
- 害得孟子的学习：这也影响了孟子的学习，The pattern “害得” requires a person or thing followed by a result, not a noun phrase alone. “影响了” correctly expresses that the situation negatively affected his studies.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 穷 -- “住在很穷的农村”：贫困，specifies that the rural area lacks money and resources, rather than using the general word for “poor.”
- 受不了 -- “就觉得受不了了”：难以忍受，makes the discomfort more specific as something that is difficult to endure.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子的妈妈：孟子和他的妈妈，Use a pronoun for the second mention of the same character within one noun phrase; repeating the name is unnecessary.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子的妈妈：孟子和他的妈妈，Use a pronoun for the second mention of the same character in this phrase.
- Coherence (Temporality): 住在这里才一个星期：在这里才住了一个星期，Use 了 after a completed action with a duration complement: they had lived there for only one week before reaching this result.
- Expressiveness: After “很多人祭拜,” add a small sound detail to make the cemetery feel vivid: “墓地里常常传来伤心的哭声。”
- Expressiveness: At “觉得受不了了,” show the mother’s worry through her thoughts: “孟母心想：‘这里太吵了，会影响孟子学习。’”
- Expressiveness: Describe Mengzi’s reaction to the mourners to show his feelings: “孟子看到他们哭，心里觉得很害怕。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish the setting with the rural home and the nearby cemetery, so the reader can understand why the family feels uncomfortable.  
- You also used “因为” effectively to connect the mourners’ actions with their crying.

## Scene 2

> 孟子的妈妈觉得孟子取得良好的教育是很重要的，所以孟子的妈妈认为这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好，就打算跟孟子搬家。孟子的妈妈带孟子不是搬到城市，而是搬到了孟子老师的家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 跟孟子搬家：带着孟子搬家，Use 带着孟子搬家 to show that the mother takes her son with her; 跟孟子搬家 incorrectly makes them sound like equal people moving together.
- 带孟子不是搬到城市，而是搬到了：不是带孟子搬到城市，而是搬到了，When both parts share the same subject, the subject must come before the paired contrast structure 不是……而是…….
- 孟子老师的家：孟子的老师的家，Add 的 after 孟子 to show that the teacher belongs to or teaches 孟子.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 地方 -- “这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好”：环境，specifies that it is the surrounding living and learning setting that affects the child.
- 不太好 -- “对儿子的教育不太好”：不利于，states more precisely that the place has a negative effect on the child’s education.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 所以孟子的妈妈认为：所以她认为，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence: the mother was already the subject earlier in the same sentence, and no other female character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子的妈妈带孟子不是：她带孟子不是，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence: the mother remains the clear subject from the preceding sentence.
- Expressiveness: After the mother decides to move, add her worried feeling, for example: “孟子的妈妈很着急，担心这里会影响孟子的学习。”
- Expressiveness: At the decision point, add a short line of quoted speech to show her reason, for example: “她说：‘我得给孟子找一个更适合学习的地方。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect the mother’s concern about education with her decision to move by using 所以. You also correctly use 搬到了 to show that the move to the new destination was completed.

## Scene 3

> 孟子跟他的老师从早到晚学习，难怪他的成绩很快就进步了很多。孟子学习的时候，孟子的妈妈不是待在家里就是编织衣服赚钱。以后孟子成为了中国有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 以后孟子成为了中国有名的哲学家：后来孟子成为了中国有名的哲学家，At the start of a new sentence meaning “afterwards” in a narrative, use “后来”; “以后” normally follows a time expression or refers to a later time.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 进步 -- “他的成绩很快就进步了很多”：提高，specifically describes a score becoming higher.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After “从早到晚学习,” add a concrete study detail, such as “他认真地听老师讲课，也常常看书。”
- Expressiveness: In the sentence about his mother, add her hope or feeling to show why she works, such as “她希望孟子能受到更好的教育。”
- Expressiveness: Before the final result, add a short evaluative link, such as “孟子一直努力学习。” This makes his later success feel more meaningful.

### Encouragement
You use the completed-action marker “了” well in “进步了很多” and “成为了,” and the final sentence gives a clear long-term result. The paired pattern “不是……就是……” is also used correctly.
