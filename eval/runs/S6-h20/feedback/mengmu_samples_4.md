## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，孟子和孟母住了在靠墓地的家。孩子才五岁就每天在墓地祭拜了。在那个城市，孟母从早到晚都找工作，可是找不到，害得孟母没有钱。难怪她跟孩子搬家，原来买不了房。他们搬了到别一个城市找工作。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，this time expression is not natural in narrative Chinese; use the established form for a historical setting.
- 靠墓地的家：靠近墓地的房子里，use a location phrase with a noun such as “house,” and use “inside the house” after the verb for a natural place expression.
- 跟孩子搬家：带着孩子搬家，when a mother moves together with her child, this expression shows that she takes the child with her; the original wording incorrectly treats the child as an equal companion in the moving action.
- 别一个城市：另一个城市，use this word for “another city”; the original word is used to distinguish other people or things from a specifically mentioned one.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孩子才五岁：他才五岁，use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence because the boy was just introduced as “孟子” in the previous sentence.
- Coherence (Reference): 在那个城市：在一个城市里，this is the first mention of a city, so use an indefinite full noun phrase rather than a demonstrative that refers to an unintroduced place.
- Coherence (Reference): 害得孟母没有钱：害得她没有钱，use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence of the same character; the name has already appeared earlier in the sentence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 住了在：住在，use the zero particle for a background living state; “了” does not fit an ongoing state here.
- Coherence (Temporality): 每天在墓地祭拜了：每天在墓地祭拜，use the zero particle for a routine action marked by “每天.”
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬了到：搬到了，put “了” after the full verb-complement “搬到” to mark the completed move.
- Expressiveness: At the cemetery sentence, add what Mengzi sees and feels, such as “孟子觉得祭拜很有趣，还笑着学别人祭拜。” This makes his daily action more vivid.
- Expressiveness: After the unsuccessful job search, show Mengmu’s emotion with a detail such as “孟母又累又着急。” This helps the reader understand why moving feels necessary.
- Expressiveness: Before the move, add Mengmu’s thoughts or speech, such as “孟母心想：‘再这样下去，麻烦会越来越多。’” This gives her a clear voice.

## Scene 2

> 这个城市老是很吵，不是人吵架，就是都叫，害得孟子一直不能睡觉。在那儿，孟母也找不到工作。孟子和妈妈要又一个次搬了家。在第三个城市，孟母找到工作，在商店做衣服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 人吵架，就是都叫：有人吵架，就是有人大叫，\(人\) is a generic noun and cannot refer to a concrete group in this scene; \(都\) does not provide a subject, and \(大叫\) clearly means shouting loudly.
- 又：再，\(要\) shows that the move has not happened yet, so \(再\) is used for a future repeated action; \(又\) is for a repetition that has already happened.
- 一个次：一次，A number plus measure word uses the order number + measure word, so \(一次\), not \(一个次\).
- 搬了家：搬家，After the modal verb \(要\), use the zero aspect form; do not add \(了\) to an action that is still planned.
- 找到工作：找到了工作，\(找到\) means “find”; use \(找到了\) here because finding the job is a completed result.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很吵 -- “这个城市老是很吵”：吵闹，specifies that the city has frequent noisy activity and voices.
- 做 -- “在商店做衣服”：缝制，more precisely describes making clothes by sewing.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 孟母也找不到工作。孟子和妈妈要再搬一次家。：因为孟母也找不到工作，所以孟子和妈妈要再搬一次家，Use the paired connector \(因为……所以……\) to show the cause-and-effect relation between not finding work and moving again.
- Expressiveness: After the city is described as noisy, add one or two specific sounds, such as “晚上也有人在街上大叫，车子一直响。”
- Expressiveness: Add Mengzi’s feeling about not being able to sleep, such as “孟子很累，躺在床上也睡不着。”
- Expressiveness: At the moment Mengmu finds work, add a short line of speech, such as “孟母高兴地说：‘我终于找到工作了！’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show why the family needs to move again, and the final sentence gives the scene a hopeful change when Mengmu finds work.

## Scene 3

> 孟子在那个商店也找了到工作，可是不是做衣服的工作，而是喜欢的工作。孟子学习馆学了十五年，他的学习和他的旅行经验很重要，对他很多好处。他是大人的时候，他成为很有名的哲人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 学习馆学了十五年：在学习馆学习了十五年，A place noun needs the locative preposition 在 before it, and the action verb 学习 should be stated after the place.
- 对他很多好处：对他有很多好处，The pattern is 对 + person + 有 + 好处; 有 is required to state that something brings benefits.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 学习 -- “他的学习和他的旅行经验很重要”：所学知识，specifies that the learning refers to knowledge he has gained.
- 大人 -- “他是大人的时候”：成年以后，states more precisely that the event happened after he reached adulthood.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他的旅行经验：旅行经验，Use zero form for a repeated possessor within the same clause: the first 他的 already makes clear that both learning and travel experience belong to him.
- Coherence (Temporality): 找了到工作：找到了工作，With a result complement such as 到, the completion marker 了 follows the complement: 找到 + 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他成为很有名的哲人：他成为了很有名的哲人，Use 了 with 成为 to show that this change of status was completed in the past.
- Expressiveness: After he finds the job, add a feeling to show why it is meaningful to him: “孟子很高兴，觉得自己终于找到了喜欢的工作。”
- Expressiveness: When describing his achievement as an adult, add a detail about his reputation: “很多人都来听他讲道理，也很尊敬他。”

### Encouragement
You clearly used the contrast structure “不是……而是……” to distinguish two kinds of work. You also connected his learning and travel experience to his later success, which gives the scene a clear sense of development.
