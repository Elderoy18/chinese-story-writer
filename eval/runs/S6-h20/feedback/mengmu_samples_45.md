## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子。他们住在墓地的旁边，所以孟子老是祭拜。孟子不是祭拜，就是跟朋友聊天。他很喜欢他的城市，但是城市有才两个学校，都很小。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，Use the fixed time expression “在古代”; “古代的时候” is not the natural form for this meaning.
- 城市有才两个学校：城市里才有两个学校，For a small quantity, “才” must come before the verb “有”; add “里” to show that the schools are located in the city.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 小 -- “都很小”：规模都不大，specifies that the schools are small in scale rather than physically small.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子：一个叫孟子的男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国，Use the character’s name in the first mention rather than introducing him first with a vague noun phrase and naming him only in the next sentence.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子不是祭拜，就是跟朋友聊天：他不是祭拜，就是跟朋友聊天，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence because “孟子” was named in the immediately preceding sentence.
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery is introduced, add a detail about what he sees there, such as “他每天看到很多人来墓地祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: Show why he keeps copying the activity with a feeling, such as “他觉得祭拜很好玩。”
- Expressiveness: Add the mother’s reaction after his behaviour, such as “妈妈很生气，说：‘你不能这样玩。’”

### Encouragement
- You use clear, short sentences to introduce the setting, Mengzi, and his daily activities. The contrast with “但是” also helps show that he likes the city even though it has few small schools.

## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈不是不喜欢他们的城市，而是想孟子有很好的教育。他们搬家到有很多学校的城市。他们从早到晚走路，孟子一直抱怨。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 想孟子有很好的教育：想让孟子受到良好的教育，When expressing a wish for another person, use 想让 + person + verb phrase. 有教育 does not mean “to receive an education”; 受到教育 is the appropriate collocation.
- 搬家到有很多学校的城市：搬到有很多学校的城市，Use 搬到 + place for completing a move to a destination; 搬家到 is not the natural verb structure here.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 走路 -- “他们从早到晚走路”：赶路，specifies that they are travelling toward their new destination rather than simply walking.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 不是不喜欢他们的城市，而是：不是因为不喜欢他们的城市而搬家，而是因为，Use 不是因为……而是因为…… to connect two contrasting reasons for moving; the original contrasts “not disliking” with a wish without clearly marking both as reasons.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他们从早到晚走路：他们搬家的时候，从早到晚都在走路，Use ……的时候 to show that the walking happened during the move; this connector makes the time relationship between the two events clear.
- Expressiveness: After the complaint, add a small physical detail about the long journey, such as “孟子又累又渴，脚也疼了。”
- Expressiveness: Give Mengzi a short line of speech at the point where he complains, such as “妈妈，我们还要走多久？”
- Expressiveness: Show the mother’s concern with an internal thought, such as “孟母心想：为了孩子的学习，辛苦一点也值得。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the mother’s goal of finding better schooling for Mengzi, and the move followed by his complaint creates a clear sense of difficulty in this scene.

## Scene 3

> 他难怪抱怨，他想他的朋友，因为他不要离开他的城市。他的妈妈找到孟子一个老师。跟老师孟子学最重要的文学，所以他成为哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他难怪抱怨：难怪他抱怨，难怪 should come before the clause it evaluates, with the subject placed before the verb after it.
- 不要离开：不想离开，不要 is used for a prohibition, request, or refusal; 不想 expresses that he does not want to leave.
- 找到孟子一个老师：给孟子找到了一个老师，use 给 before the beneficiary and make the teacher the object of 找到; 了 marks the completed action.
- 跟老师孟子学：孟子跟老师学，孟子 is the subject and should come before the phrase 跟老师.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 想 -- “他想他的朋友”：想念，specifically expresses missing friends who are no longer nearby.
- 抱怨 -- “难怪他抱怨”：发牢骚，gives a more vivid sense of repeatedly voicing dissatisfaction.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 所以他成为哲学家：所以他成为了哲学家，use 了 with 成为 to mark the completed change of becoming a philosopher in the past narrative.
- Expressiveness: At the complaint, add a physical reaction or quoted speech to show Mengzi’s feelings: 孟子皱着眉头说：“我不想离开朋友。”
- Expressiveness: When the mother finds a teacher, add one detail about the teacher’s manner or the lesson: 老师耐心地教孟子读书。

### Encouragement
You clearly connect Mengzi’s complaint to his reluctance to leave his city and friends. You also show a meaningful change from complaining to studying with a teacher.
