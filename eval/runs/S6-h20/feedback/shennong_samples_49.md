## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有很多人不知道什么植物能吃，什么植物不能吃。有很多人吃植物以后就吃坏肚子了，因为他们吃了毒。那个时候，有一个老人叫神农，他什么植物都觉得好吃，不怕生病，不怕毒。他希望别人也可以吃好吃的植物。可是他知道，如果他们还怕吃毒，不会吃他喜欢的植物。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，Use 在古代 for this historical time setting; 古代的时候 is not the standard expression here.
- 吃植物以后：吃了植物以后，The eating happened before the stomach problem, so the completed action takes 了 before 以后.
- 吃了毒：吃了有毒的植物，毒 refers to poison itself; here the people are eating plants that are poisonous, so use 有毒的植物.
- 不怕毒：不怕中毒，中毒 means “to be poisoned”; 不怕毒 does not naturally express that he is not afraid of becoming ill from poison.
- 怕吃毒：怕吃有毒的植物，Use 有毒的植物 for poisonous plants; 吃毒 means directly consuming poison rather than eating a toxic plant.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不知道 -- “有很多人不知道什么植物能吃，什么植物不能吃”：分不清，emphasizes that people cannot tell edible and inedible plants apart.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): ，不会：，就不会，A conditional relation introduced by 如果 normally uses 就 in the result clause.
- Expressiveness: After the people become ill, add a physical detail such as “有的人肚子疼得直冒汗。” This makes the consequence of eating the plants more vivid.
- Expressiveness: When introducing Shennong’s courage, add an inner thought such as “他想：我一定要找出能吃的植物！” This shows his motivation more clearly.

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduce the problem of edible versus poisonous plants before bringing in Shennong. You also use 可是 effectively to show the contrast between his hope and the danger people feel.

## Scene 2

> 所以，他上山下山找植物，吃了很多样子的植物。他是第一人，找到了茶。他把所有的植物写下来了。他一直都吃了，他连毒都能吃。可是他还没吃了一个叫夹竹桃的植物。他找到了那个像星星的植物，觉得很漂亮，马上就吃下了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很多样子的植物：很多种植物，Use the measure word “种” to count different kinds of plants; “样子” describes an appearance, not a plant category.
- 他是第一人，找到了茶：他是第一个找到茶的人，An ordinal expression needs the measure word “个,” and the phrase needs “找到茶的人” to state what he was first to do.
- 把所有的植物写下来了：把所有植物的资料都写下来了，“写下来” records words, information, or notes, rather than the physical plants themselves.
- 连毒都能吃：连有毒的植物都能吃，“毒” is a noun referring to poison; use “有毒的植物” for plants that contain poison.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 漂亮 -- “觉得很漂亮”：鲜艳美丽，specifies that the flower’s colour is bright as well as attractive；格外美丽，emphasizes that it looks especially beautiful to him.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 一直都吃了：一直都吃，Use the zero particle for an ongoing habit or repeated action; “了” marks a completed action.
- Coherence (Temporality): 还没吃了一个叫夹竹桃的植物：还没吃一个叫夹竹桃的植物，Use “没” without “了” to negate a completed action; “没吃” means that the action has not happened yet.
- Expressiveness: After finding tea, add a brief reaction to show why the discovery matters, such as “他喝了一口，觉得又香又好喝。”
- Expressiveness: When he writes down his discoveries, add a concrete detail about what he records, such as “他把茶的样子和作用都记了下来。”
- Expressiveness: Before he eats the flower, show his curiosity or confidence with an inner thought, such as “他想：这么漂亮的植物，一定很好吃吧！”

### Encouragement
- You use a clear sequence of actions: searching in the mountains, finding tea, recording discoveries, and then encountering a new plant. The sentence “觉得很漂亮，马上就吃下了” also creates a lively sense of his quick decision.

## Scene 3

> 可是夹竹桃的毒很重，让他肚子很痛。他在地上躺着，觉得他快会死了。可是正好有别人找到他了，给了他一个像伞的植物，叫灵芝。他吃灵芝以后就觉得很好，不再生病了。他测量起来了，像星星的植物是毒，像伞的植物是药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他测量起来了，：他看了一下，说：，测量 means taking physical measurements, so it does not fit before identifying the plants and stating their properties; 看了一下 followed by 说 introduces this observation and speech naturally.
- 像星星的植物是毒：像星星一样的植物有毒，use 像……一样的 to describe resemblance; 有毒 is the adjective meaning “poisonous,” while 毒 is a noun and should not be used after 是 here.
- 像伞的植物是药：像伞一样的植物是药，use 像……一样的 when saying that the plant looks like an umbrella.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 毒很重 -- “夹竹桃的毒很重”：毒性很强，names the plant’s poisonous quality more precisely.
- 很好 -- “他吃灵芝以后就觉得很好”：好多了，more specifically shows that his condition improved after eating the medicine.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 觉得他快会死了：觉得自己快要死了，when the person who is thinking and the person who may die are the same, use the reflexive form 自己 rather than repeating 他; 快要 marks an event that is about to happen.
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃灵芝以后：吃了灵芝以后，use 了 because eating the medicine is a completed action before the later result.
- Expressiveness: When he is lying on the ground, add a physical detail to show how painful he feels, for example: “他疼得满头大汗，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Make the helper more vivid by adding one clear detail, for example: “这时，一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人走过来。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought or question when he fears death, for example: “他想：‘我会死吗？’”

### Encouragement
You clearly showed the cause-and-effect sequence: the poisonous plant hurts him, and the medicine helps him feel better. You also introduced the important contrast between the star-shaped plant and the umbrella-shaped plant.
