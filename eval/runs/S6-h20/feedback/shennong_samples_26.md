## Scene 1

> 很久以前，有一个老男人叫神农。神农在古代每一天看到了别的小镇人生病还是不舒服，所以他决定开始找新的药。他下一天开始爬当地的山脉，找了各种各样的植物。神农试一试每个植物，知道有什么好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别的小镇人：其他小镇的人，别的 is used to distinguish something from a previously mentioned one; here there is no specific small town for comparison, so 其他 is needed, and 的 links the town to its people.
- 生病还是不舒服：生病或者不舒服，或者 connects alternatives in a statement; 还是 is normally used for alternatives in a question or an indirect question.
- 下一天：第二天，第二天 is the usual narrative expression for the day after the event just mentioned.
- 爬当地的山脉：爬当地的山，山脉 is a chain of mountains; a person normally climbs a mountain, while a mountain range can be crossed or traversed.
- 找了各种各样的植物：找到了各种各样的植物，找 means “look for,” while 找到 means “find.” The later testing shows that the plants were found.
- 每个植物：每种植物，种 is the appropriate measure word when discussing different kinds of plants and their effects.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很久以前 -- “很久以前，有一个老男人叫神农”：在远古时代，gives a more exact sense of a very distant historical period that suits this traditional story.
- 药 -- “所以他决定开始找新的药”：草药，specifies that the remedies are plant-based, matching the plants he searches for.
- 好处 -- “知道有什么好处”：对身体有什么益处，makes the type of benefit more specific and connects naturally to the purpose of finding medicine.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农在古代每一天：他在古代每一天，under the pronoun rule, use a pronoun when the same character was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农试一试每种植物：他试一试每种植物，under the pronoun rule, use a pronoun for the same close, unambiguous referent.
- Coherence (Temporality): 看到了：看到，every day describes a routine, so it takes the zero aspect particle rather than completed-action 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 试一试每种植物：试了试每种植物，this scene recounts a completed round of testing after he found the plants, so the testing action takes completed-action 了; 知道 remains without 了 because it is a mental-state verb.
- Expressiveness: After the people become ill, add a concrete cause: “很多人吃了有毒的植物以后，常常生病。”
- Expressiveness: When describing the mountains, add a simple visual detail: “山上有很多从来没见过的花和草。”
- Expressiveness: Add Shen Nong’s determination before he begins climbing: “他想：我要找到能治病的植物。”

### Encouragement
- You introduced the main character clearly with both his identity and name in the opening sentence.
- You also used a clear cause-and-effect link with “所以,” and your scene follows Shen Nong’s actions in an easy-to-follow order.

## Scene 2

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一次试一试有些植物的叶，吃得又新鲜又甜：尝了一些植物的叶子，觉得叶子又新鲜又甜，An action that was actually carried out should use a completed verb such as “尝了”, not verb reduplication. Also, “吃得” describes the manner or degree of eating, so it cannot describe the taste of the leaves; use “觉得” to state what he thought they tasted like.
- 特别是危险：特别危险，“特别是” introduces a particular example or category, while “特别” directly modifies an adjective such as “危险”.

### Vocabulary Suggestions

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 知道他发现：知道自己发现，“他” could refer to another male character, so use the reflexive “自己” when the person who knows and the person who discovered are the same.
- Coherence (Temporality): 煮这些叶以后：煮了这些叶以后，Use “了” for the completed action that happens before “以后”.
- Coherence (Temporality): 发现新的茶叶：发现了新的茶叶，Use “了” because the discovery is a completed action.
- Coherence (Temporality): 开始生病：开始生病了，Use “了” after “开始生病” to mark the change into a new state.
- Expressiveness: At the point where he drinks the tea, add a sensory detail about its smell or taste, for example: “茶水有一股淡淡的香味，喝起来很清甜。”
- Expressiveness: When he becomes ill, show his physical feeling more clearly, for example: “他突然肚子疼得厉害，只好坐在地上休息。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought to show his worry, for example: “他想：‘这种植物到底能不能吃？’”

### Encouragement
