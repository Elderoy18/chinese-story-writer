## Scene 1

> 很久以前，古代的人很不知道什么植物能吃，也什么植物不能吃。所以很多人常常吃不安全的植物，吃不安全的植物对你的身体有坏处，很麻烦的情况。但是一个人，神农，要解决这个问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的人很不知道：古代的人们不知道，The adverb “很” does not normally modify the negated mental-state verb phrase “不知道” in this meaning; use the verb without “很.” “人们” is appropriate for the group of people in that time.
- 也什么植物不能吃：也不知道什么植物不能吃，The second part of a parallel “不知道” structure also needs the verb “不知道.”
- 很麻烦的情况：这是一种很麻烦的情况，A noun phrase cannot stand alone after a comma as the predicate here; add a subject and linking verb to form a complete clause.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不安全 -- “吃不安全的植物”：有毒的，identifies the specific danger of the plants in this story rather than describing them generally as unsafe；对身体有害的，states more precisely that the plants harm the body.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 你的身体：人们的身体，The pronoun “你” unexpectedly addresses the reader, while this sentence is about the ancient people; repeat the group noun for a clear reference.
- Coherence (Reference): 但是一个人，神农：但是，有一个叫神农的人，On a character’s first mention, introduce the character with an unambiguous full noun phrase rather than placing the name after a vague noun.
- Expressiveness: After the people eat the plants, add a physical result to show why the situation is serious: “很多人吃了有毒的植物以后，肚子疼得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where Shennong appears, add his determined thought: “神农想：我一定要找出能吃的植物。”
- Expressiveness: You could describe the danger more vividly with a consequence: “有的人吃了以后，马上生病了。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish the central problem: people do not know which plants are edible, and Shennong wants to help solve it.
- Your use of “所以” successfully shows the result of people lacking this knowledge.

## Scene 2

> 他又聪明又努力，旅行很远找到很多植物。比方说，灵芝和夹竹桃。他一吃各种各样的植物就知道能不能吃这些植物。然后，神农写他的主意。他喜欢植物的味道，特别是花。神农遇到了一个黄色的花，一吃就觉得不舒服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 旅行很远找到很多植物：去了很远的地方，找到了很多植物，The verb for travelling does not naturally take a bare distance phrase here; this version gives a clear destination phrase and marks the completed discovery with 了.
- 写他的主意：写下了自己的想法，The result complement 下 is needed when recording an idea in writing. Also, 主意 usually means a plan or solution, while 想法 fits personal thoughts or ideas.
- 一个黄色的花：一朵黄色的花，Flowers require the measure word 朵.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农遇到了一朵黄色的花：他遇到了一朵黄色的花，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence: the same character was named recently, and no other male character has been introduced.
- Coherence (Reference): 能不能吃这些植物：能不能吃，Use zero form for a re-occurring object within the same sentence when the object is already clear.
- Expressiveness: At the point where he finds the flower, add a visual comparison to make it memorable: 黄色的花像星星一样，又大又漂亮。
- Expressiveness: After he feels unwell, show a specific physical feeling and emotion: 他肚子疼了起来，难受得不得了。
- Expressiveness: Add a short inner thought after the pain begins: 他想：“我会不会生病了？”

### Encouragement
- You use the immediate-result pattern with the tasting action effectively, and you give concrete examples of plants rather than mentioning plants only in general.
- The yellow-flower detail also begins to create a clear image for the reader.

## Scene 3

> 发现了夹竹桃有毒。别人看到他，很快给他吃一个灵芝。神农一吃就觉得比较好。然后，他继续写他发现的信息。会告诉他的朋友哪一个植物能不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别人：一个人，别人 means people other than the speaker and is used to contrast with the speaker; it cannot introduce an unspecified new character here.
- 继续写他发现的信息：继续记下他发现的信息，in this context, 记下 means to record information that has been discovered; 写 does not naturally express recording plant information by itself.
- 哪一个植物能不能吃：哪一种植物能吃，哪一种植物不能吃，哪一个 selects a specific item, while 能不能 asks a yes-or-no question. To explain the two kinds of plants to friends, state both categories clearly.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 比较好 -- “神农一吃就觉得比较好”：好一些了，shows a specific, modest improvement from his earlier discomfort；好多了，shows that his condition improved clearly after eating the medicine.
- 很快 -- “很快给他吃一个灵芝”：赶快，adds the idea that the person acted promptly because the situation was urgent.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 给他吃一个灵芝：给他吃了一个灵芝，use 了 on the last verb in this completed action sequence to show that the person finished giving him the medicine.
- Expressiveness: When the helper appears, add one visual detail to make the new character easier to imagine, for example: “一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人看到他。”
- Expressiveness: After the medicine works, add a physical detail showing Shennong’s relief, for example: “神农的肚子不那么疼了，慢慢坐了起来。”
- Expressiveness: When Shennong explains the plants to his friends, add direct speech, for example: “夹竹桃有毒，不能吃；灵芝可以做药。”

### Encouragement
- You used the pattern “一……就……” well to show that Shennong felt better immediately after eating the medicine. The scene also has a clear action order, moving from discovering danger to receiving help and recording the result.
