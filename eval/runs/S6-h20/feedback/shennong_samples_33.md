## Scene 1

> 古代时候，人不知道他们能吃什么植物，常常一吃就死。但是不知道为什么，没有办法。所以有一天，神农出去试一试很多种植物，告诉人什么植物是安全什么是危险。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代时候：古代的时候，A multi-syllable time expression before “时候” needs the particle “的”.
- 人不知道他们能吃什么植物：人们不知道什么植物能吃，Use “人们” for a concrete group of people; after this subject is stated, “他们” is unnecessary here.
- 试一试很多种植物：尝试很多种植物，“试一试” suggests trying something briefly, which does not fit testing many different kinds; “尝试” fits systematic testing.
- 什么植物是安全什么是危险：哪些植物是安全的，哪些植物是危险的，When an adjective follows “是” to describe a category, use “的”; “哪些……，哪些……” also makes the two groups parallel.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：一个叫神农的人，This is the character’s first mention, so introduce the name with a full noun phrase rather than assuming the reader already knows who he is.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉人们：告诉了人们，This is the final completed action in a series of past events, so it takes “了”; the earlier action in the series does not need another “了”.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 但是不知道为什么，没有办法：因为不知道为什么，所以没有办法，Use “因为……所以……” for the cause-and-effect relation: not knowing the reason causes the lack of a solution.
- Expressiveness: After the people’s problem, add a feeling of worry to show why the situation matters, for example: “大家都很害怕，也不知道该怎么办。”
- Expressiveness: When introducing the journey, add a simple physical detail, for example: “他走啊走啊，去了很多高山。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought for the character before he begins testing plants, for example: “神农想：‘我一定要找出能吃的植物。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly set up the problem first and then introduced a character who wants to solve it. Your use of “一吃就死” also creates an immediate, dramatic danger for the scene.

## Scene 2

> 发现有的植物对身体非常好，特别是茶，让肚子感觉又暖和又清楚。他告诉朋友：“你吃哪种植物，这就要看你的口味。比方说，你爱吃甜的，可能喜欢糖。”他最喜欢吃花，除了夹竹桃以外，什么都花想吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 让肚子感觉又暖和又清楚：让肚子感觉暖和舒服，清楚 describes information, thoughts, or perception, so it cannot describe how a stomach feels; 暖和 and 舒服 can both describe this physical feeling.
- 你吃哪种植物，这就要看你的口味：你吃哪种植物？这就要看你喜欢吃什么了，this pattern requires an uncertain question before it and a question form after 看; 看你的口味 is only a noun phrase, not the needed question form.
- 什么都花想吃：什么花都想吃，什么 must directly modify the noun 花, and 都 follows the whole noun phrase.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 非常好 -- “有的植物对身体非常好”：非常有益，states more precisely that the plants benefit health.
- 甜的 -- “你爱吃甜的”：甜食，names the category of sweet foods more clearly in this context.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the discovery of tea, add a sensory detail such as “茶叶放进热水里，散发出淡淡的香味。”
- Expressiveness: Show Shen Nong’s positive feeling as he speaks, for example: “神农高兴地告诉朋友：‘茶不但好喝，对身体也有好处。’”
- Expressiveness: Near “除了夹竹桃以外,” add a thought that shows his curiosity, such as “他觉得每一种花都可能有用。”

### Encouragement
- You used 特别是 correctly to give tea as a specific example of healthy plants.
- The phrase “除了夹竹桃以外” clearly shows that Shen Nong knows this flower is different from the others.

## Scene 3

> 他发现这个口味，一百岁时候，不小心吃了毒的花。一吃就开始死。但是生病时候告诉人什么植物是药，什么植物是毒。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一百岁时候：一百岁的时候，A multi-syllable verb phrase before “时候” needs the structural particle “的”.
- 毒的花：有毒的花，“毒” is a noun here and cannot directly describe the flower in this form; “有毒的” means “poisonous”.
- 开始死：觉得自己快要死了，“死” describes an endpoint and does not naturally follow “开始”; “快要死了” expresses that he feels close to death.
- 生病时候：生病的时候，A multi-syllable verb phrase before “时候” needs the structural particle “的”.
- 告诉人：告诉了人们，“人” is a generic noun, while “人们” refers to the concrete group of people being told; “了” marks this telling action as completed.
- 什么植物是毒：什么植物有毒，“有毒” is the adjective phrase meaning “poisonous”; “是毒” does not describe a plant naturally in this context.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不小心 -- “不小心吃了有毒的花”：误食了，more precisely means accidentally eating something harmful or unwanted.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 这个口味：一朵花，Under the first-mention rule, “这个” needs a clear earlier antecedent. Introduce the newly found object with a full noun phrase instead.
- Coherence (Temporality): 发现：发现了，Use “了” for the completed discovery of the flower.
- Expressiveness: After the poisonous flower is eaten, add a physical reaction: “他肚子疼得不得了，只能躺在地上。”
- Expressiveness: At the moment he feels close to death, add an inner thought or short quotation: “他想：‘我会死吗？’”
- Expressiveness: When he tells people about the plants, add a direct warning: “他说：‘这种花有毒，不能吃。’”

### Encouragement
- You used the useful pattern “一……就……” to show an immediate result after eating the flower. You also clearly included the important contrast between medicinal plants and poisonous plants.
