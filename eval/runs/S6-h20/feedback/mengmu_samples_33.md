## Scene 1

> 一天，很久很久以前，孟子和他妈妈住在中国。孟子妈妈的工作是祭拜，孟子不高兴，因为他说我们老是搬家，为什么？他妈妈说，从早到晚，我一直祭拜，很多墓地得我祭拜。所以我们得去很多不同的地方。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一天，很久很久以前：很久很久以前的一天，when both expressions modify the same event, the broad time expression should come before the specific “one day.”
- 孟子妈妈：孟子的妈妈，use 的 to show that the mother belongs to or is related to 孟子; without it, the phrase can sound like a name or title.
- 很多墓地得我祭拜：我得祭拜很多墓地，when 得 means “must,” it normally comes before the action performed by its subject; make 我 the subject of the required action.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不高兴 -- “孟子不高兴”：感到不满，more specifically shows dissatisfaction with the repeated moves.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和他妈妈住在中国：有一个小男孩叫孟子，他和妈妈住在中国，at first mention, introduce a character with a name and a brief identity before using a pronoun or relationship term.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 孟子不高兴，因为他说我们老是搬家，为什么？：孟子不高兴，问：“我们为什么老是搬家？”，because 因为 should introduce the cause of an event, but this sentence presents Mengzi’s question instead; use quoted speech to show his question directly.
- Expressiveness: When Mengzi asks his question, add a small physical detail to show his feeling: “孟子皱着眉头，问：‘我们为什么老是搬家？’”
- Expressiveness: After the mother explains her work, add a feeling to make her voice more vivid: “妈妈叹了一口气，说：‘从早到晚，我一直祭拜。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show why Mengzi is unhappy by giving him a question to ask his mother. You also use 老是 appropriately here to communicate his complaint about moving repeatedly.

## Scene 2

> 孟子想，难怪我们常常搬家，原来我妈妈很忙。可是常常搬家害得孟子很难找到朋友。每次搬家孟子也觉得新的地方不是太热就是太冷，不是太大就是太小。在哪的地方，他都不高兴。孟子想帮助妈妈祭拜，可是他六岁，所以不能祭拜。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在哪的地方：不管在哪个地方，`哪个` directly modifies `地方`; use `不管……都……` to express that the statement applies to every location.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 常常 -- “我们常常搬家”：频繁地，emphasizes that the moves happen repeatedly and close together.
- 找到朋友 -- “很难找到朋友”：交到朋友，more precisely describes forming friendships after arriving in a new place.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After “很难找到朋友,” add a feeling to show how the repeated moves affect him, for example: “他觉得很孤单。”
- Expressiveness: At “新的地方不是太热就是太冷,” add one concrete detail about an unfamiliar home, for example: “有的房子又小又暗。”
- Expressiveness: After the final sentence, add a short thought to make his wish clearer, for example: “要是我能帮妈妈就好了。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show Mengzi’s growing frustration through repeated moving and his difficulty making friends. The paired structure `不是……就是……` is used well to describe the problems he notices in each new place.
