## Scene 1

> 古代的时候有一个人叫神农。他需要当上医，一看别的人就想帮助他们。但是，他有一个问题，他不知道什么东西能做药。神农听说一个远远的地方有一种植物，什么病人吃都会觉得身体更好。神农三个月走去，找到有植物的地方，他看到一个很漂亮的植物，又香又好看。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “古代的时候有一个人叫神农” / Formula: 在 + historical time +，有一个 + identity/name + … / Correct: “在古代，有一个人叫神农。” “在古代” is more natural than “古代的时候,” and introducing him as a person is a good start.

Incorrect phrase: “他需要当上医” / Formula: 谁 + 想/希望 + 成为 + 职业 / Correct: “他想成为医生。” “当上” usually emphasizes successfully obtaining a position after effort, while “成为医生” fits his goal here.

Incorrect phrase: “一看别的人就想帮助他们” / Formula: 谁 + 一看到 + 某类人 + 就 + 想帮助 + 他们 / Correct: “他一看到别人有困难，就想帮助他们。” “别人” is more natural than “别的人,” and adding “有困难” makes the reason for helping clearer.

Incorrect phrase: “他不知道什么东西能做药” / Formula: 谁 + 不知道 + 什么 + 名词 + 可以/能 + 用来 + 做 + 名词 / Correct: “他不知道什么植物可以用来做药。” “东西” is too vague here; he is specifically looking for plants.

Incorrect phrase: “一个远远的地方” / Formula: 一个 + adjective + 地方 / Correct: “一个很远的地方。” “远远的” is usually an adverbial expression (e.g., “远远地看着”), not a usual modifier for “地方.”

Incorrect phrase: “什么病人吃都会觉得身体更好” / Formula: 不管/无论 + 什么病人 + 吃了 + 都会/都能 + 感觉 + 身体好一些 / Correct: “不管什么病人吃了，都会觉得身体好一些。” Add “不管” to make the meaning of “any patient” clear, and use “好一些” rather than “更好” when comparing with the person’s previous condition.

Incorrect phrase: “神农三个月走去” / Formula: 谁 + 走了 + duration +，才/然后 + 到达 + place / Correct: “神农走了三个月，才到达那个地方。” Duration usually follows the verb with 了: “走了三个月.”

Incorrect phrase: “找到有植物的地方” / Formula: 找到 + 有 + specific noun + 的地方 / Correct: “找到了有这种植物的地方。” Since the plant has already been mentioned, “这种植物” is more specific than simply “植物.”

Incorrect phrase: “一个很漂亮的植物” / Formula: 一株/一种 + adjective + 植物 / Correct: “一株很漂亮的植物。” “植物” is usually measured with “株” for an individual plant. Also, because it is newly introduced, “一株” is appropriate.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “医” / Context: “他需要当上医” / Alternatives: “医生” is the normal modern word for a medical doctor. “医者” is more literary and historical, so it could work especially well in a story set in ancient times.

Word: “什么东西” / Context: “他不知道什么东西能做药” / Alternatives: “植物” is precise and directly matches Shennong’s search. “药材” means medicinal materials and is more advanced; it emphasizes plants or other natural materials used to make medicine.

Word: “远远的地方” / Context: “一个远远的地方” / Alternatives: “遥远的地方” is more literary and vivid than “很远的地方.” “偏远的地方” means a remote place that is far from cities or busy areas.

Word: “漂亮” / Context: “一个很漂亮的植物” / Alternatives: “美丽” is a slightly more literary word for visual beauty. “奇特” means unusual or strange-looking, which may be especially suitable for a magical or medicinal plant.

Word: “香” / Context: “又香又好看” / Alternatives: “芬芳” is more elegant and literary, often used for flowers or plants with a pleasant fragrance. “清香” describes a light, fresh, natural smell, while “香” is a general everyday word.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You created a clear story sequence—from Shennong’s wish to help people, to his journey and discovery—and your description “又香又好看” makes the plant easy to imagine.  
Next time, focus on using more specific nouns and on placing 了 correctly after completed actions or durations.

## Scene 2

> 他觉得，这种一定是做药的花。原来他想错了。吃了以后，神农快生病。 这时一个穿着蓝色衣服的人帮神农，快给他一种红的灵芝。神农吃了以后，他没有病，想起来以前一个人告诉他，灵芝对身体有好处。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “这种一定是做药的花” / Formula: 这种 + noun + 一定是 + 用来 + verb + 的 / Correct: “这种花一定是用来做药的。”

Incorrect phrase: “吃了以后，神农快生病” / Formula: subject + 吃了……以后 + 差点儿/快要 + verb + 了 / Correct: “吃了以后，神农差点儿生病了。”  
“快生病” is not natural here. Use “差点儿生病了” if he almost became ill.

Incorrect phrase: “一个穿着蓝色衣服的人帮神农” / Formula: someone + 帮 + someone + verb / Correct: “一个穿着蓝色衣服的人帮助神农。”  
If you mean that the person gave him medicine, you can say: “一个穿着蓝色衣服的人给神农灵芝，帮助了他。”

Incorrect phrase: “快给他一种红的灵芝” / Formula: subject + quickly + 给 + someone + noun / Correct: “赶快给了他一种红色的灵芝。”  
Use “赶快” for “quickly,” and “红色的” is more natural than “红的” before 灵芝.

Incorrect phrase: “神农吃了以后，他没有病” / Formula: subject + 吃了……以后 + 就/结果 + 没有 + illness / Correct: “神农吃了以后，病就好了。”  
“没有病” means “not ill,” but here the intended meaning is probably that his illness was cured.

Incorrect phrase: “想起来以前一个人告诉他” / Formula: subject + 想起 + earlier + someone + told + him/her + clause / Correct: “他想起以前有一个人告诉过他，灵芝对身体有好处。”  
“一个人” is vague, so “有一个人” makes the reference clearer. “告诉过” also shows this happened in the past.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “做药” / Context: “这种花一定是做药的。” / Alternatives: “入药” or “药材.” “做药” is understandable but simple and slightly informal. “可以入药” means “can be used medicinally” and is more natural in this context. “药材” refers to medicinal materials, especially traditional Chinese medicine ingredients.

Word: “快生病” / Context: “神农快生病。” / Alternatives: “差点儿生病” or “快要病倒了.” “差点儿生病” means he almost got sick. “快要病倒了” is stronger and suggests that he was becoming seriously ill.

Word: “帮” / Context: “一个穿着蓝色衣服的人帮神农。” / Alternatives: “帮助” or “救治.” “帮” is common in everyday speech. “帮助” is more complete and neutral in writing. “救治” means to treat and save someone who is ill or injured, so it fits especially well if the man gives Shennong medicine.

Word: “红的灵芝” / Context: “一种红的灵芝。” / Alternatives: “红色的灵芝” or “赤灵芝.” “红色的” is the standard descriptive form. “赤灵芝” is a specific, more advanced name for red reishi mushroom and gives the story a more traditional-medicine feeling.

Word: “对身体有好处” / Context: “灵芝对身体有好处。” / Alternatives: “有益于身体健康” or “能够强身健体.” “对身体有好处” is clear and natural but basic. “有益于身体健康” is more formal. “强身健体” means “strengthen the body and improve health,” and has a more literary, idiomatic tone.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You created a clear sequence of events—Shennong eats a flower, receives lingzhi, and remembers its health benefit—so the story is easy to follow.  
Next time, focus on using “了,” “过,” and result expressions such as “病好了” to make the timeline and outcomes clearer.

## Scene 3

> 啊，他怎么忘记了？他把灵芝放在包里。一回家就告诉别人。他找到药以后他写下怎么做药在书上。所以人们都知道，那红灵芝对身体好处。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “一回家就告诉别人。” / Formula: Subject + 一……就…… + 告诉 + someone + content / Correct: “他一回家就告诉别人这个发现。”  
The object of “告诉” should be stated: what did he tell people?

Incorrect phrase: “他找到药以后他写下怎么做药在书上。” / Formula: Subject + 找到……以后，+ Subject + 在……上 + 写下 + content / Correct: “他找到药以后，在书上写下了怎么用灵芝做药。”  
Use “然后/……以后” to show narrative sequence, and place “在书上” before the verb “写下”. “用灵芝做药” is also clearer.

Incorrect phrase: “所以人们都知道，那红灵芝对身体好处。” / Formula: Subject + 对 + noun + 有 + 好处 / Correct: “所以人们都知道，灵芝对身体有好处。”  
“对身体好处” needs the verb “有”. Also, use “灵芝” unless the story specifically says it was red lingzhi.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “忘记” / Context: “他怎么忘记了？” / Alternatives: “想起来” or “记起来”. “忘记” means to fail to remember, but “想起来/记起来” is more natural if the character suddenly recalls something: “啊，他想起来了！”

Word: “告诉” / Context: “告诉别人” / Alternatives: “分享” or “告诉大家”. “告诉” is neutral and common, while “分享” emphasizes sharing useful knowledge or an experience. “告诉大家” sounds more natural than the vague “告诉别人” in this context.

Word: “找到” / Context: “找到药” / Alternatives: “发现” or “辨认出”. “找到” means to locate something, while “发现” emphasizes making a new discovery. “辨认出” means recognizing what something is, which works well if he learns that lingzhi is medicinal.

Word: “做药” / Context: “怎么做药” / Alternatives: “入药” or “制成药材”. “做药” is understandable but basic. “入药” means “to be used as medicine,” while “制成药材” means “to make it into medicinal material” and is more precise.

Word: “对身体有好处” / Context: “灵芝对身体有好处” / Alternatives: “有益健康” or “有药用价值”. “对身体有好处” is a simple everyday expression. “有益健康” is more concise and natural, while “有药用价值” specifically emphasizes medicinal value.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You used a clear sequence of events—going home, telling people, writing it down, and spreading the knowledge—which makes your story easy to follow.  
Next time, focus on adding the object after verbs such as “告诉” and using “有” in phrases like “对身体有好处.”
