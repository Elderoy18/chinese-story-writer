## Scene 1

> 在古代，很多医生不知道怎么用药。所以他们找到了很多植物对药。神农先看到很多病人，他们吃很多植物对药。但是我觉得他们的吃的植物，让他们病人。神农觉得这个植物可能特别是毒。所以他想找一个植物对药，他们可以吃对药。正好，他找到了一把花，他想把花放在茶里，对药。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “他们找到了很多植物对药”  
Formula: Subject + 找到 + quantity + 可以/能 + 做药（or 入药）+ 的 + noun  
Correct: “他们找到了很多可以入药的植物。”

Incorrect phrase: “神农先看到很多病人，他们吃很多植物对药。”  
Formula: Subject + 看到/看到了 + object; people who take medicine = 病人 + 吃了 + 药 / 用植物做的药  
Correct: “神农先看到了很多病人；这些病人吃了很多用植物做的药。”  
“他们” is unclear here because it could refer to the doctors or the patients, so “这些病人” is more specific.

Incorrect phrase: “但是我觉得他们的吃的植物，让他们病人。”  
Formula: Subject + 觉得 + clause; X + 让 + person + verb phrase  
Correct: “但是神农觉得，他们吃的植物让他们生病了。”  
Use “神农觉得” rather than “我觉得” because this is a story narrated in the third person. “病人” is a noun; the verb is “生病.”

Incorrect phrase: “神农觉得这个植物可能特别是毒。”  
Formula: Subject + 觉得 + noun + 可能 + 有毒  
Correct: “神农觉得这些植物可能有毒。”  
“特别是” means “especially” and does not fit before “毒.” Also, “这些植物” matches the earlier reference to many plants.

Incorrect phrase: “所以他想找一个植物对药，他们可以吃对药。”  
Formula: 找 + 一种 + 可以入药的 + 植物; 让 + person + verb phrase  
Correct: “所以他想找一种可以入药的植物，让病人吃了以后能治病。”  
Use “一种植物,” not “一个植物.” “对药” is not a natural phrase; use “入药,” “做药,” or “吃药,” depending on the meaning.

Incorrect phrase: “正好，他找到了一把花，他想把花放在茶里，对药。”  
Formula: 正好 + subject + verb; 一束 + 花; 把 + object + 放进 + place + 里 + verb  
Correct: “正好，他找到了一束花，想把花放进茶里泡着喝。”  
“一束花” is the usual measure word for flowers. The final “对药” should be replaced by the action you mean, such as “泡着喝” or “用来做药.”

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “很多”  
Context: “很多医生”“很多植物”“很多病人”  
Alternatives: “许多” is slightly more formal and works well in written storytelling. “各种” means “many different kinds of” and is especially useful for plants: “各种植物.”

Word: “看到”  
Context: “神农先看到很多病人”  
Alternatives: “发现” means “to discover” and suggests that Shennong notices something important: “神农发现了很多病人。” “遇到” means “to encounter” and emphasizes meeting people by chance.

Word: “医生”  
Context: “很多医生不知道怎么用药”  
Alternatives: “医者” is a more literary, historical term for healers or doctors, so it fits an ancient setting well. “大夫” is also a traditional term for a doctor.

Word: “毒”  
Context: “这个植物可能特别是毒”  
Alternatives: “有毒” is the natural adjective meaning “poisonous.” “毒性很强” means “highly toxic” and is stronger and more specific.

Word: “花”  
Context: “他找到了一把花”  
Alternatives: “草药” means medicinal herbs and makes your meaning clear if Shennong plans to use the plant as medicine. “药材” means medicinal material and is a broader, more formal word often used for ingredients in traditional medicine.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You did well creating a clear story sequence: doctors and patients have a problem, Shennong notices it, and then he tries to find a solution.  
Next time, focus on using specific subjects such as “病人” or “神农” instead of unclear pronouns like “他们,” and use “做药 / 入药 / 吃药” rather than “对药.”

## Scene 2

> 他不知道如果这把花对你的身体好，这就要看如果这把花毒不毒了。神农想找别的植物，但是这很麻烦。正好，他找到了别的花。他一吃花，很快就生病了。他的肚子疼死了。神农不知道他吃不吃灵芝或者夹竹桃。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “他不知道如果这把花对你的身体好” / Formula: 谁 + 不知道 + embedded yes/no question (A-not-A), not “如果”; keep the person reference consistent / Correct: “神农不知道这束花对自己的身体有没有好处。”

Incorrect phrase: “这就要看如果这把花毒不毒了” / Formula: 这就要看 + question form (e.g. 有没有毒 / 能不能吃) + 了; do not use 如果 after 看 / Correct: “这就要看这束花有没有毒了。”  
A more natural full version is: “这束花对身体有没有好处？这就要看它有没有毒了。”

Incorrect phrase: “但是这很麻烦” / Formula: 但是/可是 + specific situation + 很困难/不容易 / Correct: “但是找到其他植物很困难。”  
“这” is too vague here; say exactly what is difficult.

Incorrect phrase: “正好，他找到了别的花” / Formula: 恰好/碰巧 + subject + 找到/发现 + 另一种/其他的 + noun / Correct: “恰好，他找到了一种别的花。”  
Use “了” because finding the flower is a completed event.

Incorrect phrase: “他一吃花，很快就生病了” / Formula: 一 + verb + object，subject + 就 + result / Correct: “他一吃这种花，很快就生病了。”  
“花” alone is too general; “这种花” clearly refers to the flower he found.

Incorrect phrase: “他的肚子疼死了” / Formula: 身体部位 + 疼得 + degree complement / Correct: “他的肚子疼得要命。”  
“疼死了” can sound like he literally died from the pain.

Incorrect phrase: “神农不知道他吃不吃灵芝或者夹竹桃” / Formula: 谁 + 不知道 + 该 + verb + A 还是 B / Correct: “神农不知道自己该吃灵芝还是夹竹桃。”  
Use “自己” to avoid an unclear “他,” and use “还是” for a choice between two options.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “这把花” / Context: “这把花对你的身体好” / Alternatives: “这束花” or “这种花.” “把” is usually used for objects with handles, such as 一把伞 or 一把椅子. “束” is the natural measure word for a bunch of flowers, while “这种花” is best when you mean a type of flower.

Word: “别的” / Context: “想找别的植物” / Alternatives: “其他的植物” or “另一种植物.” “别的” is common and conversational. “其他的” is slightly more neutral and natural in narration; “另一种” specifically means “another type.”

Word: “麻烦” / Context: “这很麻烦” / Alternatives: “困难” or “不容易.” “麻烦” can mean inconvenient, troublesome, or bothersome. “困难” emphasizes that something is hard to do, while “不容易” is a softer, very natural way to say something is not easy.

Word: “正好” / Context: “正好，他找到了别的花” / Alternatives: “恰好” or “碰巧.” “正好” often means “just right” or “exactly.” “恰好” and “碰巧” emphasize that something happened by chance, which fits finding a flower unexpectedly.

Word: “很快” / Context: “很快就生病了” / Alternatives: “不久” or “没过多久.” “很快” emphasizes speed. “不久” and “没过多久” are especially natural in storytelling and mean that only a short time passed.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You created a clear cause-and-effect story: Shennong finds a flower, eats it, and then becomes ill.  

Next time, focus on using A-not-A questions such as “有没有毒” and making pronoun references clear with names or “自己.”

## Scene 3

> 别的人给了神农一个灵芝。这个植物可以让神农更好。比方说，他会觉得更健康。吃灵芝以后，他可以再走。现在他觉得更好。最后，神农找到了很多植物，他可以用对药。这些植物让神农很高兴。神农想跟很多人分享他的新药。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “这个植物可以让神农更好。”  
Formula: 这/那 + 种 + 名词 + 可以让 + 人 + 身体/状态 + 更 + 形容词  
Correct: “这种植物可以让神农的身体更健康。”

Incorrect phrase: “比方说，他会觉得更健康。”  
Formula: 吃了 + 东西 + 以后，+ 人 + 觉得/感到 + 状态 + 了  
Correct: “吃了灵芝以后，神农觉得更健康了。”  
“比方说” should introduce a specific example, but here you are describing a result, so it is better to state the result directly.

Incorrect phrase: “吃灵芝以后，他可以再走。现在他觉得更好。”  
Formula: 吃了……以后，+ 人 + 觉得……，还可以 + 继续 + 动作  
Correct: “吃了灵芝以后，神农觉得好多了，还可以继续赶路。”  
This also avoids repeating the similar idea of “更好” twice.

Incorrect phrase: “神农找到了很多植物，他可以用对药。”  
Formula: 找到 + 很多 + 可以用来/用作 + 用途 + 的 + 名词  
Correct: “最后，神农找到了很多可以用来做药的植物。”  
“用对药” is not a natural verb-object combination. Say “用来做药” or “用作药材.”

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “别的人”  
Context: “别的人给了神农一个灵芝。”  
Alternatives: “别人” or “他人.” “别人” is the most common and natural everyday word for “other people.” “他人” is more formal and is often used in writing.

Word: “一个灵芝”  
Context: “别人给了神农一个灵芝。”  
Alternatives: “一株灵芝” or “一些灵芝.” “株” is a more precise measure word for plants, while “一些” works well if the exact number is not important.

Word: “更好”  
Context: “神农觉得更好。”  
Alternatives: “好多了,” “更健康,” or “身体好转了.” “好多了” is natural in conversation, “更健康” specifically emphasizes health, and “好转了” means that an illness or physical condition has improved.

Word: “再走”  
Context: “他可以再走。”  
Alternatives: “继续赶路” or “继续前行.” “继续赶路” emphasizes continuing a journey toward a destination. “继续前行” is slightly more literary and focuses on moving forward.

Word: “新药”  
Context: “神农想跟很多人分享他的新药。”  
Alternatives: “药材,” “草药,” or “药用植物.” “新药” usually means a newly developed medicine. “药材” means medicinal materials, “草药” refers to herbal medicine, and “药用植物” means plants that can be used as medicine.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You did well in presenting the events in a clear time order: receiving the lingzhi, feeling better, finding more plants, and sharing them with others.  
Next time, focus on using more precise health and medicine vocabulary, such as “更健康,” “药材,” and “药用植物.”

## End of story

- Scene 2 needs to explain what Shen Nong decides to eat when he is sick. The story mentions both 灵芝 and 夹竹桃, but the reader does not know why he chooses 灵芝 before Scene 3.
- Scene 3 needs a clearer final result of sharing the new medicine: for example, say whether the other sick people get better after Shen Nong shares the plants.

### Overall Encouragement
You have a clear beginning, middle, and ending: Shen Nong searches for medicine, gets sick, recovers, and wants to help others. The story also has an interesting problem and a happy ending.
