## Scene 1

> 孟子小的时候，从早到晚都跟朋友玩。孟子和他的朋友喜欢玩在墓地里，装祭拜。孟子五岁的时候，跟妈妈搬家。他们搬家不是因为孟子玩得太多，而是因为妈妈认为他们的家离墓地太近。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “孟子和他的朋友喜欢玩在墓地里，装祭拜。” / Formula: Subject + 喜欢 + 在 + place + verb; “pretend to do something” = 装作 + verb / Correct: “孟子和他的朋友喜欢在墓地里玩，装作祭拜。”

Incorrect phrase: “孟子五岁的时候，跟妈妈搬家。” / Formula: 才 + age + 就 + verb, for an action that happened unusually early / Correct: “孟子才五岁就跟妈妈搬家了。”

Incorrect phrase: “他们搬家不是因为孟子玩得太多，而是因为妈妈认为他们的家离墓地太近。” / Formula: 不是因为 A，而是因为 B; when referring to the mother’s own home, use 她们家 / Correct: “他们搬家不是因为孟子玩得太多，而是因为妈妈认为她们家离墓地太近。”

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “小的时候” / Context: “孟子小的时候” / Alternatives: “小时候” is the most natural and concise choice for “when he was little.” “年幼时” is more formal and literary, suitable for a story.

Word: “从早到晚都跟朋友玩” / Context: “从早到晚都跟朋友玩” / Alternatives: “整天跟朋友玩” is more concise and natural in everyday narration. “成天跟朋友玩” often suggests that someone plays all day and may carry a mildly critical tone.

Word: “玩” / Context: “喜欢在墓地里玩” / Alternatives: “玩耍” is slightly more literary and works well in a written story. “嬉戏” is more vivid and suggests children playing happily and noisily.

Word: “装祭拜” / Context: “装祭拜” / Alternatives: “模仿祭拜” means to imitate the actions of worshipping and is clear for children’s play. “装作祭拜” emphasizes pretending to be worshipping, even though they are not actually doing it seriously.

Word: “太近” / Context: “离墓地太近” / Alternatives: “非常近” is neutral and simply stresses a short distance. “紧挨着墓地” is more vivid: it means the home was right next to the cemetery.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You clearly explained both Mengzi’s childhood behavior and the real reason for the move, so the story has a logical flow.  
Next time, focus on word order with places—use “在 + place + verb”—and try the useful pattern “才 + age + 就 + verb.”

## Scene 2

> 妈妈认为，住离墓地太近，害得孟子的心气。孟子和孟母搬到子思老师的家，跟子思老师住。孟母帮子思打扫房间和做饭。子思帮孟母教她的儿子。因为孟子现在没有朋友，他老是读书。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “住离墓地太近”  
Formula: 住在 + 离 + place + adjective + 的地方  
Correct: “住在离墓地太近的地方” / More naturally: “住在离墓地很近的地方”

Incorrect phrase: “害得孟子的心气”  
Formula: A + 害得/影响 + person + result; or A + 不利于 + person + 的 + development  
Correct: “住在墓地附近会影响孟子的心情。” / “住在墓地附近不利于孟子的成长。”  
“害得” needs a clear negative result after it, such as “害得孟子没有心思读书.”

Incorrect phrase: “孟子和孟母搬到子思老师的家，跟子思老师住。”  
Formula: 因为/所以 + subject + 搬到 + place + 和 + person + 一起住  
Correct: “所以，孟子和孟母搬到子思老师家里，和子思老师一起住。”  
Adding “所以” makes the reason for moving clear, and “一起住” is more natural.

Incorrect phrase: “子思帮孟母教她的儿子。”  
Formula: person + 帮助 + person + 教 + object; or person + 教 + student  
Correct: “子思老师帮助孟母教孟子。” / “子思老师教孟子。”  
“她的儿子” can be unclear, while using “孟子” clearly identifies the child.

Incorrect phrase: “他老是读书。”  
Formula: 因为 + reason，person + 只好/一直 + verb  
Correct: “因为孟子现在没有朋友，他只好一直读书。”  
“老是” often expresses annoyance or complaint. “一直” is neutral, while “只好” clearly shows that he has no better option.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “认为”  
Context: “妈妈认为，住在离墓地很近的地方……”  
Alternatives: “担心” or “觉得.” “认为” is fairly formal and means “to hold an opinion.” “觉得” is more conversational and personal. “担心” is best here because the mother is worried about the effect of living near a cemetery.

Word: “太近”  
Context: “离墓地太近”  
Alternatives: “很近” or “过于接近.” “太近” means “too close” and implies a negative result. “很近” simply states the distance. “过于接近” is more formal and emphasizes that the distance is excessively close.

Word: “搬到”  
Context: “搬到子思老师的家”  
Alternatives: “搬去” or “迁居到.” “搬到” is a common, neutral word for moving house. “搬去” is especially natural when the destination has already been mentioned. “迁居到” is more formal and literary.

Word: “做饭”  
Context: “孟母帮子思打扫房间和做饭。”  
Alternatives: “准备饭菜” or “做菜.” “做饭” means preparing a meal in general. “准备饭菜” sounds a little more polite and emphasizes preparing food for others. “做菜” focuses specifically on cooking dishes.

Word: “读书”  
Context: “他只好一直读书。”  
Alternatives: “学习” or “用功读书.” “读书” can mean reading books or studying. “学习” is broader and includes all kinds of study. “用功读书” emphasizes that Mengzi studies diligently and works hard.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You did a good job creating a clear story with a logical sequence: moving house, helping each other, and Mengzi’s loneliness.

Next time, focus on using connectors such as “所以” and choosing “一直” rather than “老是” for neutral actions.

## Scene 3

> 子思认为，对孩子来说，读书最重要，特别是学哲学。孟子不是练习写文章，就是看哲学书。孟子从五岁一直学哲学。他才五岁就学哲学，难怪他成为很有名的哲学家。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “特别是学哲学”  
Formula: 特别是 / 尤其是 + verb phrase  
Correct: “特别是学习哲学” or “尤其是学习哲学。”

Incorrect phrase: “孟子从五岁一直学哲学。”  
Formula: 从 + time + 开始/起 + 一直 + verb + object  
Correct: “孟子从五岁开始一直学习哲学。”  

Incorrect phrase: “难怪他成为很有名的哲学家。”  
Formula: subject + 后来/就 + 成为（了）+ adjective + 的 + noun  
Correct: “难怪他后来成为了很有名的哲学家。”  

“孟子不是练习写文章，就是看哲学书” correctly uses the pattern **subject + 不是……，就是……**, meaning “either…or…”.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “认为”  
Context: “子思认为……”  
Alternatives: “主张” emphasizes publicly advocating an idea or position; it is stronger and more formal than “认为,” which simply means “to think” or “to believe.”  
Example: “子思主张，儿童应该重视读书。”

Word: “读书”  
Context: “对孩子来说，读书最重要。”  
Alternatives: “学习” is clearer if you mean gaining knowledge generally, while “接受教育” is more formal and emphasizes the process of being educated. “读书” can sometimes specifically mean reading books.  
Example: “对孩子来说，接受教育非常重要。”

Word: “练习写文章”  
Context: “孟子不是练习写文章，就是看哲学书。”  
Alternatives: “练笔” is concise and literary, usually referring to practising writing; “练习写作” is more natural and general than “练习写文章.”  
Example: “孟子不是练习写作，就是阅读哲学著作。”

Word: “看哲学书”  
Context: “就是看哲学书。”  
Alternatives: “阅读哲学著作” is more formal and suggests serious philosophical works; “研读哲学书籍” emphasizes careful, in-depth study rather than simply looking at or reading a book.  
Example: “孟子常常研读哲学书籍。”

Word: “很有名”  
Context: “成为很有名的哲学家。”  
Alternatives: “著名” is more concise and formal; “卓越” means outstanding and emphasizes ability and achievement, not just fame.  
Example: “他后来成为了一位著名的哲学家。”  

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You used the **才……就……** pattern accurately to show that Mencius began studying philosophy at a very young age.  
Next time, focus on adding **开始** after “从 + time” and choosing more precise verbs such as “阅读” and “研读.”
