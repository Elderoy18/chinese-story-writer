## Scene 1

> 古代时候，人不知道他们能吃什么植物，常常一吃就死。但是不知道为什么，没有办法。所以有一天，神农出去试一试很多种植物，告诉人什么植物是安全什么是危险。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “古代时候” / Formula: 在 + time period / Correct: “在古代的时候” or simply “在古代”

Incorrect phrase: “人不知道他们能吃什么植物” / Formula: 人们 + 不知道 + [embedded question: 什么 + noun + 能不能 + verb] / Correct: “人们不知道什么植物能吃。”  
“人” is too vague here; “人们” is more natural. Also, “他们” is unnecessary and slightly unclear.

Incorrect phrase: “常常一吃就死” / Formula: subject + 常常 + 一 + verb + 就 + result / Correct: “人们常常一吃有毒的植物就死。”  
Add “有毒的植物” to make clear what they eat.

Incorrect phrase: “但是不知道为什么，没有办法” / Formula: subject + 不知道 + question word + clause; subject + 没有 + 办法 / Correct: “人们不知道为什么，也没有办法。”  
The subject should be stated again because “不知道” without a subject sounds incomplete here.

Incorrect phrase: “神农出去试一试很多种植物” / Formula: subject + 去/出去 + 试 + many kinds of + noun / Correct: “神农出去试吃很多种植物。”  
When trying plants to see whether they are edible, “试吃” is the most natural verb.

Incorrect phrase: “告诉人什么植物是安全什么是危险” / Formula: 告诉 + 人们 + [什么……是……，什么……是……] / Correct: “告诉人们什么植物是安全的，什么植物是危险的。”  
Use “人们,” and repeat “什么植物” to make the two categories clear.

Also, introduce the character on first mention: “有一个人叫神农” or “神农是一个传说中的人物.” For example: “有一个人叫神农，他出去试吃很多种植物。”

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “死” / Context: “常常一吃就死” / Alternatives: “丧命” or “中毒身亡.” “死” is direct and basic. “丧命” is more formal and story-like, while “中毒身亡” specifically explains that people died from poisoning.

Word: “安全” / Context: “什么植物是安全的” / Alternatives: “无毒的” or “可以食用的.” “安全” is broad—it can describe many situations. “无毒的” specifically means “not poisonous,” while “可以食用的” means “edible.”

Word: “危险” / Context: “什么植物是危险的” / Alternatives: “有毒的” or “有害的.” “危险” means generally dangerous. “有毒的” is best for poisonous plants, and “有害的” means harmful, but does not always mean deadly.

Word: “试一试” / Context: “神农出去试一试很多种植物” / Alternatives: “试吃” or “尝试.” “试一试” is conversational and general. “试吃” clearly means trying food by eating it, while “尝试” is more formal and can be used for trying an action or method.

Word: “告诉” / Context: “告诉人们什么植物……” / Alternatives: “分辨” or “辨别.” “告诉” emphasizes giving people information. “分辨” and “辨别” emphasize recognizing the difference between edible and poisonous plants, which fits Shennong’s purpose especially well.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You clearly communicated the central problem and Shennong’s brave solution, so your short story already has a logical beginning and purpose.  
Next time, focus on making subjects specific (“人们,” “神农”) and using precise words such as “试吃” and “有毒的植物.”

## Scene 2

> 发现有的植物对身体非常好，特别是茶，让肚子感觉又暖和又清楚。他告诉朋友：“你吃哪种植物，这就要看你的口味。比方说，你爱吃甜的，可能喜欢糖。”他最喜欢吃花，除了夹竹桃以外，什么都花想吃。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “发现有的植物对身体非常好” / Formula: Subject + 发现 + clause / Correct: “他发现有的植物对身体非常好。”  
A clear subject is needed here because the following sentences refer to “他.”

Incorrect phrase: “你吃哪种植物，这就要看你的口味。” / Formula: Uncertain result/question + 这就要看 + A还是B / Correct: “你该吃哪种植物？这就要看你喜欢甜的还是咸的了。”  
“这就要看……” needs to be followed by a question form, such as “A还是B.” Also, “你吃哪种植物” should be a question or an uncertain result, not a statement.

Incorrect phrase: “你爱吃甜的，可能喜欢糖。” / Formula: 如果 + condition, subject + 可能会 + verb + object / Correct: “如果你爱吃甜食，你可能会喜欢糖果。”  
Adding “如果” makes the relationship clearer, and “会” sounds more natural after “可能.”

Incorrect phrase: “让肚子感觉又暖和又清楚” / Formula: 让 + person + 觉得 + body feeling / mental state / Correct: “喝茶让他觉得肚子很暖和，头脑也很清醒。”  
“清楚” does not usually describe a stomach. We can say “头脑清醒” for a clear, alert mind.

Incorrect phrase: “除了夹竹桃以外，什么都花想吃。” / Formula: Subject + 除了……以外 + 什么 + noun + 都 + 想 + verb / Correct: “除了夹竹桃以外，他什么花都想吃。”  
“什么花都想吃” is the correct word order.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “对身体非常好” / Context: “有的植物对身体非常好” / Alternatives: “有益健康” or “对身体很有好处.” “非常好” is simple and understandable; “有益健康” is more concise and formal, while “很有好处” is natural in everyday conversation.

Word: “暖和” / Context: “让肚子感觉又暖和……” / Alternatives: “温暖” or “暖胃.” “暖和” usually describes weather, rooms, or clothing. “温暖” has a softer, more descriptive feeling, while “暖胃” specifically means that a food or drink makes the stomach feel warm.

Word: “清楚” / Context: “让肚子感觉……清楚” / Alternatives: “清醒” or “舒服.” “清楚” means clear or understandable. “清醒” describes an alert mind, while “舒服” describes physical comfort.

Word: “告诉朋友” / Context: “他告诉朋友：……” / Alternatives: “他对朋友说” or “他跟朋友解释.” “告诉” focuses on giving information. “对朋友说” is more natural for introducing direct speech, while “解释” emphasizes explaining a reason or idea.

Word: “糖” / Context: “可能喜欢糖” / Alternatives: “糖果” or “甜食.” “糖” can mean sugar as an ingredient. “糖果” means candy, and “甜食” is a broader word for sweet foods such as cakes, candy, and desserts.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You did well using “特别是” correctly to give tea as a specific example of a healthy plant, and your ideas flow naturally from health to food preferences.  
Next time, focus on the word order of “什么……都……” sentences and on using the required question form after “这就要看……了.”

## Scene 3

> 他发现这个口味，一百岁时候，不小心吃了毒的花。一吃就开始死。但是生病时候告诉人什么植物是药，什么植物是毒。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “一百岁时候” / Formula: 在 + possessive subject + age + 的时候 / Correct: “在他一百岁的时候”

Incorrect phrase: “毒的花” / Formula: 有毒的 + noun / Correct: “有毒的花”

Incorrect phrase: “一吃就开始死” / Formula: 一 + verb phrase + 就 + result / Correct: “他一吃就中毒了，快要死了。”  
“开始死” is not natural in Chinese. Use “中毒了” for the immediate result and “快要死了” for being close to death.

Incorrect phrase: “但是生病时候告诉人” / Formula: 在 + situation/time + 的时候，subject + verb + object / Correct: “但是，在中毒、快要死的时候，他告诉人们什么植物是药，什么植物是毒。”  
Use “中毒” rather than “生病” here, because he became ill from eating poison.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “这个口味” / Context: “他发现这个口味” / Alternatives: “这种花” or “这种植物” would fit the story better. “口味” means the taste or flavor of food, while “花” and “植物” refer to the thing he ate.

Word: “毒的花” / Context: “不小心吃了毒的花” / Alternatives: “有毒的花” or “毒花.” “有毒的花” is the most clear and neutral expression; “毒花” is shorter but less common in everyday speech.

Word: “开始死” / Context: “一吃就开始死” / Alternatives: “中毒了,” “生命垂危,” or “奄奄一息.” “中毒了” is clear and appropriate for this level; “生命垂危” means one’s life is in critical danger; “奄奄一息” is more literary and means barely alive.

Word: “告诉人” / Context: “告诉人什么植物是药” / Alternatives: “告诉人们” or “告诉大家.” “人们” means people in general and sounds natural in narration; “大家” is friendlier and more conversational.

Word: “药” / Context: “什么植物是药” / Alternatives: “药材” or “有药用价值的植物.” “药” can mean medicine in general; “药材” specifically means medicinal materials, such as plants used to make medicine.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You clearly communicated the important sequence: he ate a poisonous flower and then taught others how to identify medicine and poison.  
Next time, focus on adding time expressions such as “在……的时候” and using more precise words for poisoning and serious illness.
