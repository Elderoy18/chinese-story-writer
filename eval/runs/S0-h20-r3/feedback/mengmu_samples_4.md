## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，孟子和孟母住了在靠墓地的家。孩子才五岁就每天在墓地祭拜了。在那个城市，孟母从早到晚都找工作，可是找不到，害得孟母没有钱。难怪她跟孩子搬家，原来买不了房。他们搬了到别一个城市找工作。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “古代的时候”  
Formula: 在 + historical time period  
Correct: “在古代”  
“古代的时候” is understandable, but “在古代” is more natural and concise.

Incorrect phrase: “孟子和孟母住了在靠墓地的家”  
Formula: Subject + 住在 + Place  
Correct: “孟子和孟母住在靠近墓地的房子里。”  
Use “住在” before a location. Also, “靠近墓地的房子里” is a clearer place phrase.

Incorrect phrase: “孩子才五岁就每天在墓地祭拜了”  
Formula: Subject + 才 + age/number + 就 + Verb  
Correct: “孟子才五岁，就每天在墓地祭拜祖先。”  
Use “才五岁就……” to show that he was very young when he began doing this. Do not use “了” for a habitual action such as “每天祭拜.”

Incorrect phrase: “他们搬了到别一个城市找工作”  
Formula: Subject + 搬到 + Place + 去 + Verb  
Correct: “他们搬到了另一个城市去找工作。”  
Use “搬到” before the destination, and say “另一个城市,” not “别一个城市.”

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “靠”  
Context: “靠墓地的家”  
Alternatives: “靠近” or “紧挨着”  
“靠” by itself can sound incomplete here. “靠近墓地” means “near a cemetery” and is neutral. “紧挨着墓地” means “right next to a cemetery,” so it creates a stronger and more vivid image.

Word: “家”  
Context: “靠墓地的家”  
Alternatives: “房子” or “住处”  
“家” often emphasizes family and the feeling of home. “房子” refers to the physical building, so it fits well when describing its location. “住处” means residence or lodging and sounds slightly more formal.

Word: “祭拜”  
Context: “在墓地祭拜”  
Alternatives: “祭祀” or “扫墓”  
“祭拜” means worshipping ancestors or gods. “祭祀” is more formal and literary, often referring to traditional rituals. “扫墓” specifically means visiting and cleaning ancestors’ graves, so use it if that is what Mengzi is doing.

Word: “没有钱”  
Context: “害得孟母没有钱”  
Alternatives: “没有收入” or “经济拮据”  
“没有钱” is simple and clear. “没有收入” specifically means having no income, which fits the situation of not finding work. “经济拮据” means being in financial difficulty and is more advanced and formal.

Word: “另一个城市”  
Context: “别一个城市”  
Alternative: “另一个城市”  
“别一个” is not a natural Chinese expression. “另一个” is the standard way to say “another one,” while “别的城市” means “other cities” in a more general sense.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You did well connecting the events logically: living near the cemetery, not finding work, and moving to another city creates a clear story flow.  

Next time, focus on using location patterns such as “住在 + place” and “搬到 + place.”

## Scene 2

> 这个城市老是很吵，不是人吵架，就是都叫，害得孟子一直不能睡觉。在那儿，孟母也找不到工作。孟子和妈妈要又一个次搬了家。在第三个城市，孟母找到工作，在商店做衣服。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “这个城市老是很吵” / Formula: Place + 总是/一直 + adjective; “老是” often expresses annoyance about repeated actions / Correct: “这个城市总是很吵” or “这个城市一直很吵。”

Incorrect phrase: “不是人吵架，就是都叫” / Formula: 不是 + A +，就是 + B; both parts should have clear, parallel subjects / Correct: “不是有人在吵架，就是有人在大叫。”

Incorrect phrase: “孟子和妈妈要又一个次搬了家” / Formula: Subject + 又 + V. + 了 + Num. + MW. + Object; for moving house, use “搬家” or “搬到 + place” / Correct: “孟子和妈妈又搬了一次家。”  
If you mean they *were going to* move: “孟子和妈妈又要搬家了。”

Incorrect phrase: “在第三个城市，孟母找到工作，在商店做衣服。” / Formula: 在 + Place + verb; use “找到了” for a completed result, and connect related actions clearly / Correct: “在第三个城市，孟母找到了工作，在一家商店里做衣服。”


**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “很吵” / Context: “这个城市老是很吵” / Alternatives: “嘈杂” is a more advanced word for a place full of mixed, unpleasant noises, such as traffic and crowds. “喧闹” describes a lively, noisy atmosphere and can sometimes sound less negative than “嘈杂.” “很吵” is simple and natural, but less descriptive.

Word: “都叫” / Context: “就是都叫” / Alternatives: “大叫” means to shout loudly, often because of anger, fear, or excitement. “吵闹” means to make noise or create a disturbance, especially in a group. Both are more natural and specific than “叫” here.

Word: “害得” / Context: “害得孟子一直不能睡觉” / Alternatives: “导致” means “lead to” and is more neutral and formal: “导致孟子无法入睡。” “使得” is also neutral and works well in writing. “害得” strongly emphasizes a negative consequence and is very suitable in this context.

Word: “找不到工作” / Context: “孟母也找不到工作” / Alternatives: “一直没找到工作” emphasizes that she has searched for some time without success. “很难找到工作” emphasizes that the job market or situation is difficult. “找不到工作” is correct, but these options add more detail.

Word: “做衣服” / Context: “在商店做衣服” / Alternatives: “做裁缝” means to work as a tailor, often making or altering clothes. “缝制衣服” specifically means sewing clothes and sounds more precise in writing. “做衣服” is understandable, but it is more general and conversational.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You did well using “不是……就是……” and “害得……” to show the city’s noise and its negative effect on Mencius clearly.  
Next time, focus on word order with 又、要、了 and on making the subject of each action explicit.

## Scene 3

> 孟子在那个商店也找了到工作，可是不是做衣服的工作，而是喜欢的工作。孟子学习馆学了十五年，他的学习和他的旅行经验很重要，对他很多好处。他是大人的时候，他成为很有名的哲人。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “孟子在那个商店也找了到工作” / Formula: Subject + 在 + place + 找到 + object / Correct: “孟子也在那家商店找到了工作。”

Incorrect phrase: “不是做衣服的工作，而是喜欢的工作” / Formula: 不是 + noun phrase， 而是 + noun phrase; use a modifier before 工作 / Correct: “不是做衣服的工作，而是他喜欢的工作。”

Incorrect phrase: “孟子学习馆学了十五年” / Formula: Subject + 在 + place + 学习了 + duration / Correct: “孟子在学校学习了十五年。”  
“学习馆” is not a natural word for a school in this context.

Incorrect phrase: “他的学习和他的旅行经验很重要，对他很多好处” / Formula: A + 对 + someone + 有 + benefit / Correct: “他的学习和旅行经验对他很有帮助。”  
Alternatively: “他的学习和旅行经验给他带来了很多好处。”

Incorrect phrase: “他是大人的时候” / Formula: Subject + 长大以后 / Correct: “他长大以后……”

Incorrect phrase: “他成为很有名的哲人” / Formula: Subject + 成为了 + description + profession / Correct: “他成为了一位很有名的哲学家。”

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “那个商店” / Context: “孟子在那个商店……” / Alternatives: “那家店” is more natural in everyday Chinese. “那间店铺” sounds slightly more descriptive and is useful when emphasizing the physical shop.

Word: “工作” / Context: “找到了工作” / Alternatives: “职位” emphasizes a specific position or post, while “职业” refers to a long-term profession or career. “工作” is the most general word and can mean either a job or work itself.

Word: “喜欢的工作” / Context: “而是他喜欢的工作” / Alternatives: “感兴趣的工作” means work that interests him, while “理想的工作” means an ideal or dream job. “喜欢” is simple and direct; these alternatives are more precise.

Word: “旅行经验” / Context: “他的学习和旅行经验” / Alternatives: “游历经历” is especially suitable for historical figures and suggests traveling to many places to learn from the world. “旅行经验” is more modern and usually refers to practical travel experience.

Word: “很有名的哲人” / Context: “成为了一位很有名的哲学家” / Alternatives: “著名的哲学家” is concise and natural in writing. “伟大的哲学家” gives stronger praise, suggesting that the person made an important contribution. “哲人” is literary and can mean a wise thinker, but “哲学家” is more specific.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You did well connecting Mengzi’s job, education, life experience, and later achievement into a clear short narrative.  
Next time, focus on using natural time expressions such as “长大以后” and complete patterns such as “对……有帮助.”
