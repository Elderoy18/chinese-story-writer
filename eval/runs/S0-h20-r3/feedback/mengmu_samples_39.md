## Scene 1

> 古代的时候人觉得祭拜非常重要，孟子很小的时候，从早到晚一直祭拜，因为他妈妈告诉他祭拜很重要。虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜。他的同学们老是对他开玩笑了。孟子妈妈看了他的同学们对孟子不好的影响，所以孟子和孟子妈妈搬家了。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “古代的时候人觉得祭拜非常重要” / Formula: 在 + historical time, 人们 + 认为/觉得 + statement / Correct: “在古代，人们认为祭拜非常重要。”

Incorrect phrase: “孟子很小的时候” / Formula: First mention: name + identity + background information / Correct: “有一个叫孟子的男孩，他很小的时候……”  
It is clearer to briefly introduce 孟子 when he first appears.

Incorrect phrase: “虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜。” / Formula: 因为 + reason，(所以) + result; or 虽然 + contrast， 但是 + result / Correct: “因为他每天只祭拜，所以他没有朋友。”  
“虽然” needs a contrasting result, but this sentence expresses a cause and result.

Incorrect phrase: “他的同学们老是对他开玩笑了” / Formula: Subject + 老是 + verb phrase (habitual action; no 了) / Correct: “他的同学们老是拿他开玩笑。”  
Here, 老是 is acceptable because the classmates’ behavior is negative and annoying. Do not use 了 for a repeated habit.

Incorrect phrase: “孟子妈妈看了他的同学们对孟子不好的影响” / Formula: 谁 + 看到/发现 + 谁 + 对 + 谁 + 的 + 负面影响 / Correct: “孟子的妈妈发现他的同学们对孟子有不好的影响。”

Incorrect phrase: “所以孟子和孟子妈妈搬家了” / Formula: Subject + 和 + possessive noun + 搬家了 / Correct: “所以，孟子和他的妈妈搬家了。”  
Use “他的妈妈” rather than repeating “孟子妈妈.”

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “觉得” / Context: “人们觉得祭拜非常重要” / Alternatives: “认为” is more formal and objective than 觉得. It is especially natural when describing a general social belief: “人们认为祭拜非常重要。”

Word: “非常重要” / Context: “祭拜非常重要” / Alternatives: “至关重要” means extremely important and has a stronger, more formal tone. “祭拜在人们的生活中至关重要” sounds more sophisticated.

Word: “祭拜” / Context: “从早到晚一直祭拜” / Alternatives: “祭祀” is a more formal and literary word for making ritual offerings, often to ancestors or gods. “祭拜” is common in everyday speech, while “祭祀祖先” fits a historical story particularly well.

Word: “开玩笑” / Context: “同学们老是对他开玩笑” / Alternatives: “取笑” or “嘲笑” makes the negative meaning clearer. 开玩笑 can be friendly, but 取笑 means making fun of someone, and 嘲笑 is stronger and more hurtful.

Word: “不好的影响” / Context: “对孟子不好的影响” / Alternatives: “负面影响” is a more concise and formal expression. It clearly means an effect that harms someone’s behavior, feelings, or development.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You did well connecting the events logically: Mengzi’s rituals lead to loneliness, teasing, and finally moving house.  
Next time, focus on introducing characters clearly and choosing between 因为…所以… and 虽然…但是….

## Scene 2

> 孟子才十一岁，成为一个哲学家。孟子不是祭拜，而是看政治文章，难怪他成为了哲学家。不过，看过很多政治文章对他害得，他有很多问题。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “孟子才十一岁，成为一个哲学家。”  
Formula: Subject + 才 + age + 就 + V + 了  
Correct: “孟子才十一岁，就成为了一位哲学家。”  
“才……就……” emphasizes that something happened unusually early.

Incorrect phrase: “孟子不是祭拜，而是看政治文章。”  
Formula: Subject + 不是 + 在 + V1， 而是 + 在 + V2  
Correct: “孟子不是在祭拜，而是在看政治方面的文章。”  
Use “不是……而是……” for a contrast between two actions. Adding “在” makes the two actions parallel.

Incorrect phrase: “看过很多政治文章对他害得，他有很多问题。”  
Formula: V + 了 + object + 以后，subject + 产生了 + many + 疑问 / A + 对 + person + 有害  
Correct: “不过，他读了很多政治方面的文章以后，产生了很多疑问。”  
“害得” needs a result after it, such as “害得他很担心.” Also, “看过” describes experience, while “读了……以后” connects more clearly to the result.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “成为一个哲学家”  
Context: “孟子才十一岁，就成为了一个哲学家。”  
Alternatives: “成为哲学家” is more natural because professions and identities often do not need “一个.” “成为一位哲学家” is also correct and sounds more respectful and formal.

Word: “看政治文章”  
Context: “而是看政治文章。”  
Alternatives: “读政治方面的文章” is more natural for reading written articles. “研读政治理论” is more advanced and suggests careful, serious study rather than casual reading.

Word: “政治文章”  
Context: “很多政治文章”  
Alternatives: “政治方面的文章” is broader and natural. “政治理论文章” specifically refers to articles about political ideas or theories, and sounds more academic.

Word: “问题”  
Context: “他有很多问题。”  
Alternatives: “疑问” means questions or uncertainties in his mind, which fits this context better. “思考” emphasizes that he reflected deeply on the ideas he read.

Word: “不过”  
Context: “不过，看过很多政治文章……”  
Alternatives: “后来” is better if you want to show a sequence of events. “因此” is better if reading the articles caused him to have many questions. “不过” should introduce a clear contrast or unexpected turn.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You used “才……就……” and “不是……而是……” to express sophisticated ideas, which shows that you are trying useful sentence patterns.  
Next time, focus on adding “就” after “才 + age” and choosing connectors such as “后来” or “因此” to make the story flow more clearly.

## Scene 3

> 他一直问问题，但是不能解决那些问题。孟子长大，他开始能解决问题。他常常建议你不是学金融，而是学政治，因为如果你懂政治，你会成为高薪。后来孟子成为教授，他教学生很厉害。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “孟子长大，他开始能解决问题。”  
Formula: Subject + 长大以后/后来 + 就/开始 + 能 + Verb + Object  
Correct: “孟子长大以后，就开始能解决这些问题了。”  
“长大” needs a time connection such as “以后,” and “这些问题” refers more clearly to the questions mentioned before.

Incorrect phrase: “他常常建议你不是学金融，而是学政治”  
Formula: 建议 + someone + 不要/别 + Verb A，+ 而要/而是 + Verb B  
Correct: “他常常建议你不要学金融，而要学政治。”  
After “建议你,” use “不要……而要……” for advice. “不是……而是……” is generally used to correct or contrast a statement, rather than directly give advice.

Incorrect phrase: “因为如果你懂政治”  
Formula: If + condition, + result / 因为 + reason, + result  
Correct: “因为他认为，如果你懂政治，你就会……”  
Do not normally put “因为” directly before “如果” in this structure. Add “他认为” to make the reason and logic clearer.

Incorrect phrase: “你会成为高薪。”  
Formula: Subject + 会 + 成为 + noun/person role; or Subject + 会 + 获得/找到 + 高薪的 + noun  
Correct: “你会找到一份高薪的工作。”  
“高薪” is an adjective-like word meaning “high salary,” so it cannot be the object of “成为.”

Incorrect phrase: “他教学生很厉害。”  
Formula: Subject + 教得 + adjective / Subject + 很会 + Verb + Object  
Correct: “他教得很好。” / “他很会教学生。”  
Use “教得很好” to describe how well someone teaches.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “问问题”  
Context: “他一直问问题。”  
Alternatives: “提出问题” is more formal and natural in academic contexts; it emphasizes raising questions for discussion. “不断提问” emphasizes that he asks repeatedly and is curious.

Word: “解决问题”  
Context: “不能解决那些问题。”  
Alternatives: “解答问题” is better when the questions are intellectual or academic questions. “克服困难” is better if the meaning is overcoming real-life difficulties rather than answering questions.

Word: “学金融”  
Context: “建议你不要学金融。”  
Alternatives: “学习金融” is slightly more complete and formal. “攻读金融专业” is more advanced and specifically means studying finance as a university major.

Word: “高薪”  
Context: “成为高薪。”  
Alternatives: “高薪工作” means a job with a high salary. “薪资优厚的工作” is more formal and emphasizes that the pay and benefits are generous.

Word: “很厉害”  
Context: “他教学生很厉害。”  
Alternatives: “教学能力很强” is formal and clearly describes strong teaching ability. “是一位优秀的教授” is a natural way to praise him as a professor overall.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You created a clear story of Mencius growing up, becoming able to solve problems, and eventually becoming a professor.  
Next time, focus on using “建议” patterns and choosing nouns after “成为.”
