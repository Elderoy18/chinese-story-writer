## Scene 1

> 神农和他的家人住在古代，所以不知道什么植物能吃。因为他的家人常常生病了，所以神农和他的哥哥一起去旅行找可以吃的植物。他们去很多不一样的地方，比方说高山、山谷和沙漠。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “神农和他的家人住在古代” / Formula: 在 + historical time, + character introduction / Correct: “在古代，神农和家人住在一起。”  
“古代” is a time period, so use “在古代.” Also, introduce 神农 as a character when he first appears.

Incorrect phrase: “所以不知道什么植物能吃” / Formula: Subject + 不知道 + what/which + noun + 可以/能 + verb / Correct: “人们不知道什么植物可以吃。”  
“神农和他的家人” is not necessarily the only group who did not know this, and “人们” is more natural for this general situation.

Incorrect phrase: “他的家人常常生病了” / Formula: Subject + 常常 + verb / Correct: “他的家人常常生病。”  
“常常” describes a repeated habit or situation, so do not use 了 here.

Incorrect phrase: “神农和他的哥哥一起去旅行找可以吃的植物” / Formula: 去 + place/verb phrase + 找 + object / Correct: “神农和哥哥一起去各地寻找可以吃的植物。”  
“去旅行” usually means travelling for leisure. Here, they travel with a clear purpose, so “去各地寻找” is more precise.

Incorrect phrase: “他们去很多不一样的地方” / Formula: 去 + 很多/各种 + adjective + places / Correct: “他们去了很多不同的地方。”  
Use 了 because the sentence describes a completed action in the story. “不同的地方” is also more natural than “不一样的地方” here.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “家人” / Context: “神农和他的家人住在古代” / Alternatives: “家人” is a general term for family members. “一家人” emphasizes the whole family as one unit and is especially natural when describing living together: “神农一家人住在一起.”

Word: “不知道” / Context: “不知道什么植物能吃” / Alternatives: “不知道” simply means “do not know.” “不了解” means “do not understand well” and sounds slightly more formal: “人们不了解哪些植物可以食用.”

Word: “找” / Context: “找可以吃的植物” / Alternatives: “找” is basic and means “look for.” “寻找” is more formal and fits an important, difficult search in a story. “辨别” means “identify or distinguish,” which works well if Shennong is learning which plants are edible.

Word: “不一样的地方” / Context: “很多不一样的地方” / Alternatives: “不一样” is conversational and broad. “不同的地方” is more natural in writing. “各种地方” emphasizes variety, while “各地” is concise and means “many regions/places.”

Word: “比方说” / Context: “比方说高山、山谷和沙漠” / Alternatives: “比方说” is common in speech. “例如” is more suitable for writing, and “包括” introduces a list of places that are included: “他们去了很多地方，包括高山、山谷和沙漠。”

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You clearly expressed a logical story sequence: the family gets sick, so Shennong begins searching for edible plants in many places.  
Next time, focus on using “了” only for completed events, not for repeated actions with words like “常常.”

## Scene 2

> 他们在很漂亮的河找夹竹桃。神农问他的哥哥：“我应该吃这种植物吗？”哥哥说：“这就要看你想要安全还是接近知道了。“神农一吃夹竹桃就生病了。哥哥说：“太麻烦了。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “他们在很漂亮的河找夹竹桃。”  
Formula: Subject + 在 + (modifier + 的) place + verb; “河边” is needed when the action happens beside a river.  
Correct: “神农和他的哥哥在一条很漂亮的河边找夹竹桃。”

Incorrect phrase: “这就要看你想要安全还是接近知道了。”  
Formula: 这就要看 + question form (A 还是 B / A不A / wh-word) + 了.  
Correct: “这就要看你是想要安全，还是想了解这种植物了。”  
“接近知道” is not a natural Chinese expression. Also, the two choices should be parallel: “想要……还是想……”.

Incorrect phrase: “哥哥说：‘太麻烦了。’”  
Formula: When describing a new problem caused by an event, use 这下 + 麻烦了 / 这可 + 麻烦了.  
Correct: “哥哥说：‘这下麻烦了！’”  
“太麻烦了” can mean “it is too troublesome,” but here “这下麻烦了” more naturally means “Now we have a problem!”

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “很漂亮”  
Context: “一条很漂亮的河边”  
Alternatives: “美丽” is a more literary and natural choice for scenery, while “清澈” emphasizes that the river water is clear. You could say “一条美丽的小河边” or “一条清澈的小河边.”

Word: “找”  
Context: “找夹竹桃”  
Alternatives: “寻找” is more formal and story-like than “找.” “采” means to pick flowers or plants, so use it if they are collecting the oleander: “寻找夹竹桃” or “采夹竹桃.”

Word: “这种植物”  
Context: “我应该吃这种植物吗？”  
Alternatives: “夹竹桃” is more specific and avoids repeating a vague noun. “这株夹竹桃” is especially suitable if they are pointing to one particular plant; 株 is a measure word for plants.

Word: “安全”  
Context: “想要安全”  
Alternatives: “平安” has a warmer, more personal meaning of being safe and unharmed. “避免危险” is more explicit and means “avoid danger.” In this scene, “想保证自己平安” sounds natural.

Word: “生病了”  
Context: “神农一吃夹竹桃就生病了。”  
Alternatives: “中毒了” is much more precise because oleander is poisonous; it means “was poisoned.” “感到不舒服” is softer and means “felt unwell.” Here, “神农一吃夹竹桃就中毒了” makes the cause clearer.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You did well using dialogue and the pattern “一……就……,” which makes the scene move quickly and clearly.  
Next time, focus on making the choices after “这就要看……” grammatical and parallel.

## Scene 3

> 夹竹桃是毒的，我想知道灵芝会帮他。”哥哥给神农灵芝。神农吃完了以后开始痊愈了。神农说：“谢谢哥哥，你帮得真好。灵芝我们可以吃，特别是如果我们已经吃了夹竹桃。“

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “我想知道灵芝会帮他。” / Formula: When the person is already known, use a specific noun or a clear pronoun + 帮 + person + solve/with + problem / Correct: “我想知道灵芝会不会帮助神农恢复健康。”  
“我” and “他” are unclear here. Also, 帮他 alone does not explain how lingzhi helps him.

Incorrect phrase: “神农吃完了以后开始痊愈了。” / Formula: 谁 + 吃完……以后 + 开始 + 恢复/好转; 痊愈 means “recover completely,” so it should not normally follow 开始 / Correct: “神农吃完灵芝以后，开始恢复健康了。”  
Alternatively: “神农吃完灵芝以后，痊愈了。”

Incorrect phrase: “谢谢哥哥，你帮得真好。” / Formula: 谢谢 + 人，+ 你 + 帮了我 + 大忙 / Correct: “谢谢哥哥，你帮了我大忙。”  
“帮得真好” is understandable, but it sounds incomplete because it does not specify what the brother did.

Incorrect phrase: “灵芝我们可以吃” / Formula: Noun + 是 + 可以 + verb + 的 / Correct: “灵芝是可以吃的。”  
This structure sounds more natural when making a general statement about whether something is edible.

Incorrect phrase: “特别是如果我们已经吃了夹竹桃。” / Formula: 特别是 + noun/clause (a typical special case); if expressing a condition, use 尤其是/如果 / Correct: “尤其是如果我们已经吃了夹竹桃，吃灵芝可能会帮助我们恢复健康。”  
“特别是” needs to introduce a special example or case more clearly. Here, “尤其是如果……” is more natural.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “毒的” / Context: “夹竹桃是毒的” / Alternatives: “夹竹桃有毒” or “夹竹桃是有毒植物。” “毒的” is understandable but somewhat informal and incomplete-sounding here. “有毒” is the standard, natural way to say that a plant is poisonous.

Word: “帮” / Context: “灵芝会帮他” / Alternatives: “帮助,” “治疗,” or “缓解。” “帮” is a very general everyday word. “帮助” is slightly more formal; “治疗” means to treat an illness; “缓解” means to lessen symptoms, such as stomach pain.

Word: “哥哥” / Context: “哥哥给神农灵芝” / Alternatives: “一位农民,” “一位好心人,” or “一位医生。” “哥哥” means an older brother, so it may be confusing if he has not been introduced as Shennong’s brother. “好心人” emphasizes kindness, while “农民” fits a countryside/herb-gathering story.

Word: “开始痊愈” / Context: “开始痊愈了” / Alternatives: “开始好转,” “逐渐恢复,” or “恢复健康。” “开始好转” emphasizes the first signs of improvement; “逐渐恢复” emphasizes a gradual process; “恢复健康” describes becoming healthy again.

Word: “吃” / Context: “灵芝我们可以吃” / Alternatives: “食用” or “服用。” “吃” is the most common everyday word. “食用” is more formal and often used for food or plants; “服用” is best when lingzhi is being used as medicine.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You clearly connected the events logically: Shennong eats lingzhi after eating poisonous oleander, and then he gets better.  
Next time, focus on making pronouns such as “我” and “他” clear, and choose more precise health-related verbs such as “恢复” or “好转.”
