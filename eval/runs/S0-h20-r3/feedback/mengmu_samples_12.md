## Scene 1

> 古代，孟子跟孟母住在墓地的旁边。可是孟母注意，住在墓地的旁边影响孟子。他常常跟朋友们祭拜玩，所以孟母决定他们会搬家。这次，他们住在城市里的商店的旁边，从早到晚很吵。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “古代” / Formula: 在 + time period / Correct: “在古代，孟子跟孟母住在墓地的旁边。”

Incorrect phrase: “孟母注意，住在墓地的旁边影响孟子” / Formula: Subject + 注意到/发现 + clause; living situation + 会 + 影响 + person / Correct: “孟母注意到住在墓地旁边会影响孟子。”

Incorrect phrase: “他常常跟朋友们祭拜玩” / Formula: Subject + 跟 + person + 玩 + object / Correct: “他常常跟朋友们玩祭拜的游戏。”  
“祭拜” is an action, so it should not come before “玩” in this sentence.

Incorrect phrase: “孟母决定他们会搬家” / Formula: 决定 + verb phrase / Correct: “所以孟母决定搬家。”  
You can also say: “所以孟母决定带孟子搬家。”

Incorrect phrase: “从早到晚很吵” / Formula: 从早到晚 + 都 + adjective / Correct: “从早到晚都很吵。”

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “可是” / Context: “可是孟母注意到……” / Alternatives: “然而” is more formal and suitable for written storytelling. “可是” is natural in both speech and writing, while “然而” creates a clearer contrast between the peaceful cemetery area and the noisy commercial area.

Word: “注意到” / Context: “孟母注意到住在墓地旁边会影响孟子。” / Alternatives: “发现” means “to discover/realize” and is more direct. “察觉到” emphasizes gradually becoming aware of a subtle problem. Here, “发现” would be very natural: “孟母发现住在墓地旁边会影响孟子。”

Word: “影响” / Context: “会影响孟子” / Alternatives: “不利于孟子的成长” is more specific and formal; it means that something is harmful to Mencius’s development. “影响” is broad and neutral, while “不利于成长” clearly explains why Meng Mu wants to move.

Word: “祭拜的游戏” / Context: “玩祭拜的游戏” / Alternatives: “模仿祭拜” means “to imitate worship rituals” and sounds more concise. It describes children copying what they see around them, which fits this story well: “他常常跟朋友们模仿祭拜。”

Word: “很吵” / Context: “从早到晚都很吵。” / Alternatives: “嘈杂” means noisy, bustling, and full of mixed sounds; it is more descriptive and literary. “很吵” is simple and conversational. You could write: “商店旁边从早到晚都很嘈杂。”

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You told the story in a clear sequence and successfully showed why Meng Mu wanted to move again.  
Next time, focus on using “决定 + verb phrase” and adding “都” after time phrases such as “从早到晚.”

## Scene 2

> 城市也有点儿危险。害得孟母只好一直陪孟子。孟子也没有朋友，难怪他老是看起来不高兴。住在商店的旁边不是适合孟子，而是让他觉得很伤心。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “害得孟母只好一直陪孟子”  
Formula: ……害得 + someone + harmful result  
Correct: “城市有点儿危险，让孟母只好一直陪着孟子。”  
“只好一直陪着孟子” is an understandable consequence, but it is not clearly a harmful result for 孟母, so 让 is more suitable here.

Incorrect phrase: “住在商店的旁边不是适合孟子”  
Formula: Place/Activity + 不适合 + someone  
Correct: “住在商店旁边不适合孟子。”  
You do not need 是 before 适合. Also, 商店旁边 is more natural than 商店的旁边 in this sentence.

Incorrect phrase: “住在商店旁边不适合孟子，而是让他觉得很伤心”  
Formula: A 不适合 B，因为/所以 + result; 不是 A，而是 B = “not A, but B”  
Correct: “住在商店旁边不适合孟子，所以让他觉得很伤心。”  
“不是……而是……” contrasts two nouns, actions, or descriptions; here, a cause-and-result connector such as 所以 makes the logic clearer.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “危险”  
Context: “城市也有点儿危险。”  
Alternatives: “不安全” or “治安不好.” 危险 is broad and can describe immediate physical danger. 不安全 is more natural when discussing whether a place is suitable for a child. 治安不好 specifically refers to poor public security or crime.

Word: “一直陪孟子”  
Context: “孟母只好一直陪孟子。”  
Alternatives: “时刻陪在孟子身边” or “寸步不离地照顾孟子.” 一直陪 is simple and clear. 时刻陪在……身边 emphasizes constant presence, while 寸步不离地照顾…… is stronger and suggests very close, protective care.

Word: “没有朋友”  
Context: “孟子也没有朋友。”  
Alternatives: “交不到朋友” or “缺少玩伴.” 没有朋友 states the situation. 交不到朋友 emphasizes that he cannot successfully make friends, while 缺少玩伴 is especially natural for a child who has no one to play with.

Word: “老是”  
Context: “他老是看起来不高兴。”  
Alternatives: “总是” or “常常.” 老是 often sounds slightly negative or impatient. 总是 is more neutral and natural in writing, while 常常 simply means “frequently.”

Word: “很伤心”  
Context: “让他觉得很伤心。”  
Alternatives: “很难过” or “感到孤单.” 很伤心 suggests deep sadness, often caused by a painful event. 很难过 is a common, natural word for feeling unhappy; 感到孤单 more specifically connects his sadness to having no friends.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You clearly explain the connection between the unsafe environment, Mengzi’s lack of friends, and his unhappiness, so your ideas flow logically.  
Next time, focus on choosing connectors such as 所以 and 因为 to show cause-and-effect relationships more precisely.

## Scene 3

> 所以，孟母和孟子搬到学校的旁边住。才五岁孟子开始学哲学。在学校，孟子不是看书，就是练习写字。将来，他成为中国的一个非常有名的哲学家。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “搬到学校的旁边住” / Formula: 搬到 + Place + （以后）+ 住 / Correct: “孟母和孟子搬到学校旁边以后住了下来。”  
“学校旁边” is more natural than “学校的旁边,” and adding “住了下来” clearly shows that they settled there.

Incorrect phrase: “才五岁孟子开始学哲学” / Formula: Subject + 才 + Num. + 就 + V. / Correct: “孟子才五岁就开始学哲学了。”  
Use 就 after 才五岁 to emphasize that he started unusually early.

Incorrect phrase: “将来，他成为中国的一个非常有名的哲学家” / Formula: In a past narrative, Subject + 后来 + 成为 + Noun / Correct: “后来，他成为了中国一位非常有名的哲学家。”  
将来 refers to the future from the present moment. Since this is a story about Mengzi’s life, 后来 is the appropriate narrative time word.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “学校的旁边” / Context: “搬到学校的旁边住” / Alternatives: “学校附近” or “学校旁边.” 学校旁边 means directly next to the school, while 学校附近 means “in the area near the school” and is slightly broader and more natural in everyday speech.

Word: “开始学哲学” / Context: “孟子才五岁就开始学哲学了” / Alternatives: “开始学习儒家思想” or “开始接受启蒙教育.” 学哲学 is understandable but broad. 学习儒家思想 is more historically specific for Mengzi, while 接受启蒙教育 emphasizes beginning a child’s formal early education.

Word: “练习写字” / Context: “孟子不是看书，就是练习写字” / Alternatives: “练字” or “学习书法.” 练字 is a concise, natural everyday expression. 学习书法 is more formal and emphasizes calligraphy as an art.

Word: “非常有名” / Context: “一个非常有名的哲学家” / Alternatives: “著名的” or “享有盛名的.” 非常有名 is clear but basic. 著名的 is more concise and formal; 享有盛名的 is more literary and emphasizes having a great reputation.

Word: “哲学家” / Context: “中国一位非常有名的哲学家” / Alternatives: “思想家” or “儒家思想家.” 哲学家 is correct, but 思想家 is often more natural for major historical figures whose ideas influenced society. 儒家思想家 is especially precise for Mengzi.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You used “不是……就是……” correctly, and your sentences create a clear, logical story about Mengzi’s early education and later success.  
Next time, focus on narrative time words such as 后来 and on using the complete 才……就…… structure.
