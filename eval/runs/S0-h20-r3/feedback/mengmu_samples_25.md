## Scene 1

> 这是古代的故事。孟子小的时候，他的爸爸很早就去世了。从他的家到墓地很近，所以他每天老是从早到晚去墓地跟朋友们玩玩儿。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “这是古代的故事。” / Formula: 在 + historical time / Correct: “这是一个发生在古代的故事。”  
“古代的故事” is understandable, but “发生在古代的故事” is more natural when introducing the setting.

Incorrect phrase: “孟子小的时候” / Formula: Character introduction + 小的时候 / Correct: “在古代，有一个叫孟子的小孩子。孟子小的时候……”  
Since 孟子 is mentioned for the first time, briefly introduce who he is before continuing the story.

Incorrect phrase: “从他的家到墓地很近” / Formula: A + 离 + B + 很近 / Correct: “他家离墓地很近。”  
Use 离 to express the distance between two places.

Incorrect phrase: “每天老是” / Formula: 每天 + verb / 老是 + verb / Correct: “所以他每天从早到晚都去墓地跟朋友们玩。”  
每天 and 老是 both express repetition, so using both is redundant. “每天” fits especially well here.

Incorrect phrase: “去墓地跟朋友们玩玩儿” / Formula: 去 + place + 跟 + person + verb / Correct: “去墓地跟朋友们玩。”  
“玩玩儿” can mean “play a little / casually play,” but it is less suitable with “从早到晚,” which describes a long duration. Use 玩 instead.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “很早就去世了” / Context: “他的爸爸很早就去世了。” / Alternatives: “很早便去世了” is slightly more literary and fits a historical story well. “早逝” is concise and formal, often used to say someone died young.

Word: “从早到晚” / Context: “从早到晚去墓地……” / Alternatives: “整天” is a simple, natural word for “all day.” “一整天” emphasizes the full length of the day. For example: “他整天去墓地跟朋友们玩。”

Word: “玩” / Context: “跟朋友们玩” / Alternatives: “嬉戏” has a more literary tone and describes children playing happily. “玩耍” is a slightly more formal written alternative to 玩.

Word: “墓地” / Context: “去墓地跟朋友们玩” / Alternatives: “坟地” is more colloquial and common in daily speech. “墓园” sounds more neutral and organized, like a cemetery or memorial park. “墓地” is correct, but it can sound somewhat formal.

Word: “故事” / Context: “这是一个发生在古代的故事。” / Alternatives: “传说” suggests a traditional tale that may not be historically factual. “典故” refers to a well-known historical or literary anecdote, and is suitable if you are retelling a famous story about Mencius.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You did well connecting the father’s death, the home’s location, and Mengzi’s behavior with a clear cause-and-effect flow.  
Next time, focus on introducing a character clearly on their first mention and using natural distance expressions such as “A离B很近.”

## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈觉得在墓地玩儿，害得，所以他们搬家。搬家对孟子有好的影响，他才五岁，可是每天不是写字就是帮妈妈的工作。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “在墓地玩儿，害得” / Formula: Subject + 觉得/认为 + Clause；“害得” needs a result after it: A + 害得 + B + Result / Correct: “他的妈妈觉得在墓地玩儿不好，所以他们搬家。”  
Alternatively: “他的妈妈觉得住在墓地附近会影响孟子，所以他们搬家。”

Incorrect phrase: “搬家对孟子有好的影响” / Formula: Modifier + 的 + Noun; use a more natural collocation: 对 + Person + 有 + positive/negative + 影响 / Correct: “搬家对孟子有好的影响。”  
This is understandable, but “积极的影响” is more natural in this context.

Incorrect phrase: “他才五岁，可是每天不是写字就是帮妈妈的工作” / Formula: 才 + Number + 就 + Verb; 不是 + V1 + 就是 + V2; 帮 + Person + Verb / Correct: “他才五岁，就每天不是写字，就是帮妈妈工作。”  

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “玩儿” / Context: “在墓地玩儿” / Alternatives: “玩耍” is slightly more formal and works well in writing: “在墓地附近玩耍。” “嬉戏” is more literary and emphasizes children playing happily and noisily. “玩儿” is natural in speech but more casual.

Word: “害得” / Context: “在墓地玩儿，害得” / Alternatives: “影响” is neutral and means to affect someone or something: “会影响孟子的成长。” “不利于” is more formal and means “is not beneficial to”: “不利于孟子的成长。” These fit the mother’s concern better than “害得,” which usually introduces a clear bad result.

Word: “好的影响” / Context: “搬家对孟子有好的影响” / Alternatives: “积极的影响” is a more natural written expression for a beneficial effect. “好处” is simpler and direct: “搬家对孟子有好处。” “好的影响” is understandable but less idiomatic.

Word: “写字” / Context: “每天不是写字就是……” / Alternatives: “练字” specifically means practising handwriting or calligraphy. “读书” means studying or reading books and may better express a child’s education. “写字” simply describes the physical act of writing characters.

Word: “帮妈妈的工作” / Context: “帮妈妈的工作” / Alternatives: “帮妈妈干活儿” is natural and means helping with chores or work. “帮妈妈做家务” specifically means helping with household chores. “帮妈妈的工作” is not natural because you need a verb after “帮妈妈.”

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You clearly expressed the connection between the environment, moving house, and Mencius’s early education, which gives your short scene a logical storyline.  
Next time, focus on completing result structures such as “害得……” and using “才……就……” for actions that happened at a young age.

## Scene 3

> 他很聪明的人，难怪他成为有名的人。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “他很聪明的人” / Formula: Subject + 很 + adjective; or Subject + 是 + adjective + 的 + noun / Correct: “他很聪明” or “他是一个很聪明的人。”

Incorrect phrase: “成为有名的人” / Formula: 成为 + specific noun/profession/identity / Correct: “难怪他后来成为了一位有名的哲学家。”  
“有名的人” is understandable, but “成为” sounds more natural with a specific identity, such as 哲学家、医生、作家, etc.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “聪明” / Context: “他很聪明” / Alternatives: “智慧” describes deeper wisdom, judgment, and insight, while “聪明” usually describes being quick to understand or learn. You could say “他非常有智慧。”

Word: “有名” / Context: “成为有名的人” / Alternatives: “著名” is more formal and is often used for people with broad recognition; “知名” emphasizes being well known within a particular field. For example: “他成为了一位著名的哲学家。”

Word: “人” / Context: “有名的人” / Alternatives: “哲学家” gives the reader specific information about his identity. “人物” can describe an important or notable person, often in history or a field, but it is less specific than a profession.

Word: “难怪” / Context: “难怪他成为……” / Alternatives: “因此” simply shows a logical result and is more neutral; “所以” is common and conversational. “难怪” means “no wonder,” so it works best when the result is unsurprising because of the previous information.

Word: “很” / Context: “很聪明” / Alternatives: “非常” is stronger and slightly more formal; “极其” is much stronger and more literary. For example: “他非常聪明。”

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You successfully connected his intelligence with his later success using “难怪,” which shows clear logical thinking.  
Next time, focus on using “是……的人” and “成为 + specific identity/profession” accurately.
