## Scene 1

> 一天，很久很久以前，孟子和他妈妈住在中国。孟子妈妈的工作是祭拜，孟子不高兴，因为他说我们老是搬家，为什么？他妈妈说，从早到晚，我一直祭拜，很多墓地得我祭拜。所以我们得去很多不同的地方。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “一天，很久很久以前”  
Formula: 很久很久以前 + 的一天 / 很久很久以前，有一天 + story event  
Correct: “很久很久以前的一天，孟子和妈妈住在中国。”  
“很久很久以前” should come before “一天,” or you can use the common story opening “很久很久以前，有一天……”.

Incorrect phrase: “孟子妈妈的工作是祭拜”  
Formula: Proper noun + 的 + family member / title; 工作是 + verb-object phrase  
Correct: “孟子的妈妈的工作是祭拜祖先。” / “孟母的工作是祭拜祖先。”  
With a name, use “孟子的妈妈” or the title “孟母.” Also, adding “祖先” makes “祭拜” clearer.

Incorrect phrase: “孟子不高兴，因为他说我们老是搬家，为什么？”  
Formula: Subject + emotional state + 说/问：“quotation”  
Correct: “孟子不高兴，问妈妈：‘我们老是搬家，为什么？’”  
Here, “因为” does not introduce the reason for his unhappiness. He is asking about the reason, so “问妈妈” is more natural. “老是” is appropriate here because he is complaining about moving repeatedly.

Incorrect phrase: “很多墓地得我祭拜”  
Formula: 很多 + noun + 需要 + person + 去 + verb  
Correct: “有很多墓地需要我去祭拜。”  
“得” can mean “must,” but “需要我去祭拜” is clearer and more natural in this context.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “工作”  
Context: “孟子妈妈的工作是祭拜。”  
Alternatives: “职责” or “任务.” “工作” is a general word for a job or employment. “职责” emphasizes a person’s duty or responsibility, while “任务” emphasizes a specific assigned duty. For this sentence, “孟母的职责是祭拜祖先” sounds more formal and story-like.

Word: “祭拜”  
Context: “祭拜祖先。”  
Alternatives: “祭祀” or “祭扫.” “祭拜” is a general word for worshipping or paying respects to ancestors or deities. “祭祀” is more formal and is often used for traditional ceremonies; “祭扫” specifically refers to visiting and cleaning a grave while paying respects.

Word: “不高兴”  
Context: “孟子不高兴。”  
Alternatives: “不满” or “烦恼.” “不高兴” is simple and means unhappy. “不满” emphasizes dissatisfaction with a situation, while “烦恼” emphasizes feeling worried or troubled. Since Mengzi is unhappy about moving often, “孟子对此感到不满” is especially fitting.

Word: “很多不同的地方”  
Context: “所以我们得去很多不同的地方。”  
Alternatives: “很多地方” or “各地.” “很多不同的地方” is understandable but slightly repetitive because “很多地方” already suggests different places. “各地” means “various places/regions” and is more concise: “所以我们得去各地祭拜。”

Word: “一直”  
Context: “从早到晚，我一直祭拜。”  
Alternatives: “不停地” or “整天.” “一直” emphasizes continuous action over a period of time. “不停地” emphasizes doing something without stopping, and “整天” simply means “all day.” “我从早到晚不停地祭拜” makes the mother’s tiring schedule more vivid.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You created a clear dialogue and gave Mengzi a believable complaint, which makes the scene easy to follow.  
Next time, focus on using natural story time expressions and on making quoted speech clear with “说” or “问” plus quotation marks.

## Scene 2

> 孟子想，难怪我们常常搬家，原来我妈妈很忙。可是常常搬家害得孟子很难找到朋友。每次搬家孟子也觉得新的地方不是太热就是太冷，不是太大就是太小。在哪的地方，他都不高兴。孟子想帮助妈妈祭拜，可是他六岁，所以不能祭拜。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “每次搬家孟子也觉得新的地方不是太热就是太冷，不是太大就是太小。” / Formula: 每次 + V. + 的时候，谁 + 都/也 + 觉得……; “不是……就是……” is used for two alternatives. / Correct: “每次搬家的时候，孟子都觉得新地方不是太热就是太冷，不是太大就是太小。”

Incorrect phrase: “在哪的地方，他都不高兴。” / Formula: 在 + 疑问词 + 的地方，谁 + 都 + V. / Correct: “在哪个地方，他都不高兴。”  
More naturally: “不管搬到哪里，他都不高兴。”

Incorrect phrase: “孟子想帮助妈妈祭拜，可是他六岁，所以不能祭拜。” / Formula: 谁 + 才 + Num. + 岁 + 就/还 + 不能 + V.; use “才” to emphasize that someone is young. / Correct: “孟子想帮助妈妈祭拜，可是他才六岁，所以还不能祭拜。”

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “很忙” / Context: “原来我妈妈很忙。” / Alternatives: “工作繁忙” is more formal and specifically means that someone has a heavy workload. “忙碌” describes a continually active, occupied state and sounds more descriptive than the simple “很忙.”

Word: “害得” / Context: “常常搬家害得孟子很难找到朋友。” / Alternatives: “导致” is more neutral and formal, while “害得” strongly emphasizes an undesirable result and can sound like blame. You could write: “常常搬家导致孟子很难交到朋友。”

Word: “找到朋友” / Context: “孟子很难找到朋友。” / Alternatives: “交到朋友” is the natural expression for successfully making friends. “结交朋友” is slightly more formal and emphasizes forming new friendships.

Word: “新的地方” / Context: “孟子也觉得新的地方……” / Alternatives: “新住处” specifically means a new place to live. “新环境” is broader and can include the people, atmosphere, school, and surroundings, not only the physical home.

Word: “不高兴” / Context: “他都不高兴。” / Alternatives: “不开心” is natural and conversational. “不适应” means “not able to adjust,” which better explains his reaction to each new place. “感到失落” is more emotional and suggests sadness from losing familiar friends and surroundings.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You expressed Mengzi’s frustration clearly, especially by using paired patterns such as “不是太热就是太冷.”  
Next time, focus on using **才 + age** and more natural phrases for making friends, such as “交到朋友.”

## Scene 3

> 四年后，孟子开始帮妈妈祭拜。他画画很厉害，他越来越喜欢祭拜，所以他祭拜不是想帮妈妈工作，而是他很喜欢画画。孟子成为很著名的古代画家。今天很多中国人很喜欢孟子。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “四年后，孟子开始帮妈妈祭拜。”  
Formula: Time + 后，Subject + 开始 + Verb; when saying someone is four years old, use “四岁” rather than “四年后.”  
Correct: “孟子四岁时就开始帮妈妈祭拜。”  
*(If you mean four years after a previous event, “四年后” is correct.)*

Incorrect phrase: “孟子开始帮妈妈祭拜”  
Formula: Subject + 帮 + Person + Verb + Object  
Correct: “孟子开始帮妈妈祭拜祖先。”  
“祭拜” usually needs an object, such as 祖先 (ancestors).

Incorrect phrase: “他越来越喜欢祭拜”  
Formula: Subject + 越来越 + Adjective / Verb phrase  
Correct: “他越来越喜欢画画。”  
“越来越喜欢祭拜” is grammatically possible, but it does not match the following sentence, which says that he likes painting.

Incorrect phrase: “所以他祭拜不是想帮妈妈工作，而是他很喜欢画画。”  
Formula: Subject + 做A，不是为了 + B，而是因为 + C  
Correct: “所以他祭拜不是为了帮妈妈，而是因为他喜欢画画。”  
However, this idea is still unclear: painting and worshipping are different actions. If he paints during the ceremony, you could write: “他帮妈妈祭拜时喜欢画画。”

Incorrect phrase: “孟子成为很著名的古代画家。”  
Formula: Subject + 成为 + 一个 + Modifier + Noun; “古代” should describe the historical setting, not usually the person’s job title.  
Correct: “后来，孟子成为了一位很著名的画家。”  
Note: In the traditional story, Mencius became a famous philosopher, not a painter: “后来，孟子成为了一位很著名的哲学家。”

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “很厉害”  
Context: “他画画很厉害。”  
Alternatives: “画得很好” is simple and natural for a beginner. “画得很出色” means he performs exceptionally well and sounds more polished. “很有绘画天赋” emphasizes natural talent rather than learned skill.

Word: “喜欢”  
Context: “他越来越喜欢祭拜。”  
Alternatives: “对……越来越感兴趣” means becoming increasingly interested in something and is useful for hobbies or subjects. “热爱” is stronger than 喜欢 and expresses deep passion.

Word: “帮妈妈工作”  
Context: “不是想帮妈妈工作。”  
Alternatives: “帮妈妈做事” is more natural for helping with general tasks. “帮妈妈分担家务” specifically means sharing household chores. “协助妈妈祭拜” is more formal and specifically fits the ceremony.

Word: “很著名”  
Context: “成为很著名的古代画家。”  
Alternatives: “有名” is common and conversational. “闻名” means “well-known” and is often used in writing. “卓越的” means outstanding or exceptional, emphasizing achievement and quality rather than fame.

Word: “很多中国人”  
Context: “今天很多中国人很喜欢孟子。”  
Alternatives: “许多中国人” is slightly more formal than 很多. “不少中国人” means “quite a few Chinese people” and sounds natural in writing. “人们至今仍然尊敬孟子” is especially suitable here because it emphasizes lasting respect, not only liking him.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You connected the ideas with “所以” and “而是,” which shows that you are starting to express reasons and contrasts in Chinese.  
Next time, focus on making sure that each action and reason match clearly—for example, explain how painting is connected to the worship ceremony.
