## Scene 1

> 这个男孩子叫孟子，孟子是古代的人。孟子的妈妈觉得住在哪儿对童年很重要，所以他们常常搬了家。她望子成龙，所以孟子的妈妈安排他的生活。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “孟子是古代的人” / Formula: 在 + historical time period, + person + lived/existed / Correct: “孟子是古代人” or, more naturally, “孟子生活在古代。”

Incorrect phrase: “他们常常搬了家” / Formula: Subject + 常常 + Verb; use 了 for a completed single action, not a habitual action / Correct: “他们常常搬家。”

Incorrect phrase: “她望子成龙，所以孟子的妈妈安排他的生活” / Formula: Subject + 希望/为了 + clause, so the subject should remain clear and consistent / Correct: “孟子的妈妈望子成龙，所以她安排孟子的生活。”


**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “觉得” / Context: “孟子的妈妈觉得住在哪儿对童年很重要” / Alternatives: “认为” is more formal and suitable for expressing a considered opinion. “意识到” emphasizes that she came to realize the importance of the living environment.

Word: “住在哪儿” / Context: “住在哪儿对童年很重要” / Alternatives: “居住环境” is more concise and advanced; it refers to the overall environment in which someone lives. You could say: “孟子的妈妈认为居住环境对孩子的成长很重要。”

Word: “童年” / Context: “对童年很重要” / Alternatives: “成长” focuses on a child’s overall development, including education and character, while “童年” mainly refers to the period of being a child. Here, “成长” fits the meaning better.

Word: “常常” / Context: “他们常常搬家” / Alternatives: “多次” emphasizes the number of completed moves, while “频繁地” stresses that the moves happened very often. For the story of Mencius, “他们多次搬家” is especially natural.

Word: “安排他的生活” / Context: “孟子的妈妈安排他的生活” / Alternatives: “重视他的教育” emphasizes her attention to his learning and moral development. “为他选择合适的环境” specifically connects her actions to moving house and choosing where to live.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You clearly explained the cause-and-effect relationship between Mencius’s mother valuing his upbringing and moving house, and you used the idiom “望子成龙” well.  
Next time, focus on distinguishing habitual actions from completed actions, especially when to use “了.”

## Scene 2

> 因为他们常常搬了家，中国人说，孟母三迁。孟子没有爸爸，所以，他的妈妈更重要。从这儿到那儿，妈妈教孟子很多技能。先她让孟子跟朋友玩儿，然后教他怎么买又便宜又好质量的东西。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “因为他们常常搬了家，中国人说，孟母三迁。” / Formula: 因为 + specific subject + 搬了 + number + 次 + 家，所以 + subject + 常说 + “phrase” / Correct: “因为孟母带着孟子搬了三次家，所以中国人常说‘孟母三迁’。”  
“他们” is unclear here, and 常常 usually does not go with 了 in this context. “孟母三迁” specifically refers to three moves.

Incorrect phrase: “孟子没有爸爸，所以，他的妈妈更重要。” / Formula: 对 + person + 来说，+ person + 很/尤其 + important / Correct: “孟子的爸爸不在身边，所以孟母对孟子来说尤其重要。”  
Do not put a comma directly after 所以. Also, “更重要” normally implies a comparison, but the comparison is not clear.

Incorrect phrase: “从这儿到那儿，妈妈教孟子很多技能。” / Formula: 搬到/住在 + different places + 后，+ subject + 教 + person + skills / Correct: “搬到不同的地方以后，孟母教孟子很多生活技能。”  
“从这儿到那儿” describes physical movement, but it does not clearly connect to the idea of teaching.

Incorrect phrase: “先她让孟子跟朋友玩儿，然后教他怎么买又便宜又好质量的东西。” / Formula: 先 + verb phrase， 然后 + verb phrase；又 + adjective + 又 + adjective + 的 + noun / Correct: “她先让孟子跟朋友一起玩儿，然后教他怎么买又便宜又质量好的东西。”  
“先” should come before the action. Say “质量好的东西,” not “好质量的东西.”

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “常常” / Context: “他们常常搬了家” / Alternatives: “多次” or “三次.” “常常” means “often” and emphasizes a repeated habit. “多次” is more formal and emphasizes repeated occasions, while “三次” is best here because it gives the exact number and connects directly to the idiom “孟母三迁.”

Word: “重要” / Context: “他的妈妈更重要” / Alternatives: “尤其重要” or “不可或缺.” “重要” is a general word meaning “important.” “尤其重要” means “especially important” and sounds natural when emphasizing the mother’s role; “不可或缺” means “indispensable” and is stronger and more literary.

Word: “技能” / Context: “教孟子很多技能” / Alternatives: “生活本领” or “处世之道.” “技能” often refers to practical, learnable abilities, such as computer or sports skills. “生活本领” is more natural for everyday abilities, while “处世之道” refers to wisdom about how to behave and interact with others in society.

Word: “跟朋友玩儿” / Context: “让孟子跟朋友玩儿” / Alternatives: “与朋友相处” or “和朋友交往.” “玩儿” is casual and focuses on having fun. “与朋友相处” emphasizes learning how to spend time harmoniously with others, while “交往” is slightly more formal and focuses on building social relationships.

Word: “东西” / Context: “买又便宜又质量好的东西” / Alternatives: “物品” or “日用品.” “东西” is very general and conversational. “物品” is more neutral and formal, while “日用品” specifically means everyday necessities, such as food, clothing, or household products.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You clearly organized the ideas in a logical order—moving house, the mother’s importance, and the lessons she taught Mengzi—and your use of “先……然后……” shows good awareness of sequence.

Next time, focus on making subjects specific and using precise phrases such as “搬了三次家” and “质量好的东西.”

## Scene 3

> 最后，她让孟子学写作。他不是写作就是看书，难怪他很聪明。现在在中国，孟子很有名。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “最后，她让孟子学写作。”  
Formula: 让 + 人 + 学/做 + 事情；for a final event in a narrative, 最后/后来 + 主语 + 动作  
Correct: “最后，孟母让孟子学习写作。”  
“她” is understandable, but restoring “孟母” makes the reference clearer. “学习写作” is also more natural than “学写作.”

Incorrect phrase: “他不是写作就是看书”  
Formula: 不是 + 动作/状态 + 就是 + 动作/状态 (“either…or…”)  
Correct: “他不是写作就是看书。”  
This sentence is grammatically correct. However, if you mean that he did both activities very often, “他每天不是写作就是看书” is clearer.

Incorrect phrase: “难怪他很聪明。”  
Formula: 难怪 + result/clause; “聪明的人” when modifying a noun  
Correct: “难怪他这么聪明。”  
“很聪明” is correct, but “这么聪明” connects more naturally to the reason given in the previous sentence.

Incorrect phrase: “现在在中国，孟子很有名。”  
Formula: 在 + place + time expression, or 现在 + 主语 + 在 + place + 很有名  
Correct: “现在，孟子在中国很有名。”  
You can also say: “直到现在，孟子在中国仍然很有名。” This emphasizes that he remains famous today.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “最后”  
Context: “最后，她让孟子学写作。”  
Alternatives: “后来” means “later” in a narrative and does not necessarily mean the final event. “最终” means “in the end/finally” and emphasizes the final outcome. Here, “后来” may fit better if the story continues after this event.

Word: “学写作”  
Context: “她让孟子学写作。”  
Alternatives: “学习写作” is more natural and neutral. “练习写作” emphasizes practising writing skills, while “学习写作” can include learning techniques, ideas, and style.

Word: “写作”  
Context: “他不是写作就是看书。”  
Alternatives: “写文章” is more concrete and beginner-friendly: it means writing articles or compositions. “读书” is a natural partner for “写文章,” so you could say “他不是写文章就是读书.”

Word: “看书”  
Context: “他不是写作就是看书。”  
Alternatives: “读书” is slightly more literary and often refers to reading as a regular intellectual activity. “阅读” is more formal and is common in educational contexts. For this sentence, “读书” sounds especially natural.

Word: “很有名”  
Context: “孟子很有名。”  
Alternatives: “著名” is more formal and is often used in writing, for example, “孟子是中国著名的思想家.” “家喻户晓” means “known to every household” and is stronger than “有名”; it suggests extremely broad fame.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You used “不是……就是……” correctly and created a clear connection between Mengzi’s study habits and his intelligence.  
Next time, focus on using “后来/最后/直到现在” precisely to make the timeline of your story even clearer.
