## Scene 1

> 有一天孟子去了墓地玩儿，但是他妈妈觉得这是不合适的地方让孩子玩，所以她决定了他们会搬家找更好的地方。她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福，而且住在这儿教育不太好。因为她望子成龙，所以她会一直搬家才能找到质量好的教育。然后他们搬到了离城市更近的地方。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “她决定了他们会搬家找更好的地方” / Formula: Subject + 决定 + Verb phrase; “决定” is a mental verb, so it does not usually take 了 here. / Correct: “她决定搬家，找一个更好的地方。”

Incorrect phrase: “住在这儿教育不太好” / Formula: Place + 的 + noun; when describing education in a place, use “这里的教育”. / Correct: “而且这里的教育不太好。”

Incorrect phrase: “她会一直搬家才能找到质量好的教育” / Formula: Subject + 不断/一直 + Verb +，直到 + result; “才能” usually follows a condition or amount of effort. / Correct: “为了让儿子接受更好的教育，她会不断搬家，直到找到合适的地方。”

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “不合适” / Context: “这是不合适的地方让孩子玩” / Alternatives: “不适合” is more natural when saying a place is unsuitable for a specific activity: “这不是适合孩子玩耍的地方。” “不合适” is broader and can describe something inappropriate or unsuitable in general.

Word: “恐怕” / Context: “她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福” / Alternatives: “担心” more directly expresses a mother’s worry: “她担心儿子的童年不幸福。” “恐怕” often means “I’m afraid that…” and can sound more like the speaker’s prediction.

Word: “望子成龙” / Context: “因为她望子成龙” / Alternatives: “希望儿子成才” is clearer and more neutral for a learner’s narrative. “望子成龙” is an idiom that emphasizes parents’ very high hopes that their son will become successful.

Word: “质量好的教育” / Context: “找到质量好的教育” / Alternatives: “高质量的教育” is the standard, natural collocation. “良好的教育” emphasizes that the education is sound and beneficial, not only high in quality.

Word: “离城市更近的地方” / Context: “他们搬到了离城市更近的地方” / Alternatives: “离市中心更近的地方” is more specific if you mean better schools and resources in the urban center. “城市附近” simply means near the city, while “市中心” suggests a more developed central area.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You clearly explained Mengzi’s mother’s motivation, and the ideas flow logically from the cemetery to moving for better education.  
Next time, focus on using natural verb patterns after mental verbs such as “决定” and on making the subject of each sentence clear.

## Scene 2

> 他们住才一月就又搬家。古代的时候，一般人不喜欢卖东西的人。但是孟子老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿，害得妈妈着急得很，因为她恐怕儿子会成为跟他们很像。她告诉了孟子：他们不是朋友，而是不好的人。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “他们住才一月就又搬家。”  
Formula: Subject + 才 + V. + 了 + duration + 就 + V.  
Correct: “他们才住了一个月，就又搬家了。”  
“才” should come before the verb, and “住了一个月” shows the completed duration.

Incorrect phrase: “古代的时候”  
Formula: 在 + historical time  
Correct: “在古代”  
“在古代” is a more natural and concise way to introduce a historical setting.

Incorrect phrase: “孟子老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿”  
Formula: Subject + 常常／老是 + 跟 + person + 玩儿  
Correct: “孟子常常跟这些卖东西的人玩儿。”  
“老是” and “常常” both express frequency, so use only one. “常常” is more neutral; “老是” can suggest that the speaker finds the action annoying.

Incorrect phrase: “害得妈妈着急得很”  
Formula: Subject + 害得 + specific person + adjective  
Correct: “害得孟母着急得很。”  
Using “孟母” makes the reference clearer, especially in a story with several people.

Incorrect phrase: “她恐怕儿子会成为跟他们很像。”  
Formula: Subject + 恐怕 + person + 会 + 变得 + 跟……一样／很像  
Correct: “她恐怕儿子会变得跟他们一样。”  
“成为” is usually followed by a noun, such as “成为商人.” For becoming similar in behavior or character, use “变得”.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “卖东西的人”  
Context: “这些卖东西的人”  
Alternatives: “商人” or “小贩.” “卖东西的人” is clear but general. “商人” means merchants or businesspeople and sounds more concise in a historical story. “小贩” refers specifically to street vendors or small-scale sellers.

Word: “一般人”  
Context: “一般人不喜欢卖东西的人”  
Alternatives: “当地人” or “人们.” “一般人” means ordinary people in general, but it can sound slightly vague here. “当地人” means the people living in that area, while “人们” is a broad, natural word for people in a narrative.

Word: “玩儿”  
Context: “跟这些卖东西的人玩儿”  
Alternatives: “来往” or “混在一起.” “玩儿” usually suggests playing for fun, often like children do. “来往” means to have social contact and is more neutral. “混在一起” suggests spending a lot of time with a group and can have a slightly negative tone, which fits the mother’s worry.

Word: “不好的人”  
Context: “而是不好的人”  
Alternatives: “坏人” or “不正经的人.” “不好的人” is understandable but vague. “坏人” means bad people or villains and is direct. “不正经的人” means people with improper behavior; it is less absolute and may better fit people the mother considers a bad influence.

Word: “着急得很”  
Context: “孟母着急得很”  
Alternatives: “担心” or “忧虑.” “着急” emphasizes immediate anxiety and impatience. “担心” means to worry and is very common. “忧虑” is more formal and literary, emphasizing deep or ongoing concern.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You did well using clear cause-and-effect language with “害得” and “因为,” which makes the mother’s concern easy to understand.  
Next time, focus on word order with “才” and on choosing precise verbs such as “变得” versus “成为.”

## Scene 3

> 你不是当数学家，而是当哲学家，不能当卖东西的人。所以他们又搬家。在他们第三个家，孟子学了怎么祭拜。妈妈感觉这个地方适合他们，告诉了孟子：我们找到了最后的家。在这儿妈妈让儿子从早到晚学习，他难怪成为有名的哲学家。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “你不是当数学家，而是当哲学家，不能当卖东西的人。”  
Formula: Subject + 不是 + A，+ 而是 + B；“卖东西的人” can be replaced by the more precise noun “商人.”  
Correct: “孟子不是数学家，而是哲学家，不能当商人。”  
Note: In a story about Mencius, use “孟子” rather than “你,” which addresses the reader directly.

Incorrect phrase: “在他们第三个家”  
Formula: 在 + place + 里/中  
Correct: “在他们的第三个家里”  
You can also say “在第三次搬家以后” if you mean “after their third move.”

Incorrect phrase: “孟子学了怎么祭拜。”  
Formula: 学习/学会 + 如何 + verb  
Correct: “孟子学习了如何祭拜。”  
“学会了如何祭拜” is better if he successfully mastered it.

Incorrect phrase: “妈妈感觉这个地方适合他们”  
Formula: Subject + 觉得 + clause  
Correct: “妈妈觉得这个地方很适合他们。”  
“感觉” is possible, but “觉得” is more natural for giving an opinion or making a judgment.

Incorrect phrase: “他难怪成为有名的哲学家。”  
Formula: Reason，+ 难怪 + subject + 后来 + 成为了 + result  
Correct: “妈妈让他从早到晚学习，难怪他后来成为了有名的哲学家。”  
“难怪” introduces a logical result, so the reason should come first. Adding “后来” also makes the story’s timeline clearer.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “最后的家”  
Context: “我们找到了最后的家。”  
Alternatives: “合适的家” or “理想的家.” “最后的家” literally means the final home and can sound as if they will never move again. “合适的家” emphasizes that the place suits their needs, while “理想的家” suggests it is the home they have been hoping for.

Word: “卖东西的人”  
Context: “不能当卖东西的人。”  
Alternatives: “商人” or “生意人.” “卖东西的人” is understandable but descriptive and informal. “商人” is the standard, neutral word for a merchant; “生意人” is slightly more conversational and emphasizes doing business.

Word: “感觉”  
Context: “妈妈感觉这个地方适合他们。”  
Alternatives: “觉得” or “认为.” “感觉” often emphasizes a personal feeling or physical sensation. “觉得” is natural for a personal opinion, while “认为” is more formal and sounds like a considered judgment.

Word: “学了怎么祭拜”  
Context: “孟子学了怎么祭拜。”  
Alternatives: “学习如何祭拜” or “学会了祭拜的礼仪.” “学习如何祭拜” is more natural and neutral. “学会了祭拜的礼仪” is more specific and emphasizes that he mastered the rituals and etiquette.

Word: “有名的”  
Context: “有名的哲学家。”  
Alternatives: “著名的” or “伟大的.” “有名的” simply means well-known. “著名的” is more formal and is common in writing, while “伟大的” praises the person’s major historical importance, not just their fame.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You used a clear story sequence and connected Mencius’s study environment to his later success effectively.  
Next time, focus on using precise location phrases and making cause-and-result relationships clearer with words such as “后来” and “难怪.”
