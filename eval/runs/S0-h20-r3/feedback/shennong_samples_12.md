## Scene 1

> 很久以前，古代的人很不知道什么植物能吃，也什么植物不能吃。所以很多人常常吃不安全的植物，吃不安全的植物对你的身体有坏处，很麻烦的情况。但是一个人，神农，要解决这个问题。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “古代的人很不知道什么植物能吃，也什么植物不能吃” / Formula: 人们 + 不知道 + 什么……能……，什么……不能…… / Correct: “古代的人不知道什么植物能吃，什么植物不能吃。”  
“很不知道” is not natural; 不知道 normally does not take 很 here. Also, use parallel structure rather than 也.

Incorrect phrase: “所以很多人常常吃不安全的植物” / Formula: 很多人 + 常常 + 吃 + 不安全的植物 / Correct: “所以很多人常常吃不安全的植物。”  
This sentence is grammatically correct, but it needs a full stop before the next idea.

Incorrect phrase: “吃不安全的植物对你的身体有坏处” / Formula: 吃……对 + 人们的/他们的 + 身体 + 有害 / Correct: “吃不安全的植物对人们的身体有害。”  
“你的身体” suddenly addresses the reader, while the story is about ancient people. Use “人们的身体” or “他们的身体.” “有害” is also more natural than “有坏处.”

Incorrect phrase: “很麻烦的情况” / Formula: 这是一个 + 很麻烦的情况 / Correct: “这是一个很麻烦的情况。”  
The phrase needs a verb or a complete sentence. It can also be combined with the previous sentence: “这是一种很麻烦的情况。”

Incorrect phrase: “但是一个人，神农，要解决这个问题” / Formula: 但是 + 人物（name + identity）+ 想要/决定 + 解决 + 问题 / Correct: “但是，有一个人叫神农，他想要解决这个问题。”  
Introduce the character clearly when he first appears. Adding “叫” makes it clear that 神农 is the person’s name.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “很久以前” / Context: “很久以前，古代的人……” / Alternatives: “在古代” is more direct and natural because the next phrase already talks about ancient people. “远古时期” is more literary and emphasizes a very distant historical time.

Word: “不安全的植物” / Context: “很多人常常吃不安全的植物” / Alternatives: “有毒的植物” is more precise if the plants can poison people. “不能吃的植物” is simpler and suitable when you mean plants that are not edible, whether or not they are poisonous.

Word: “有坏处” / Context: “对人们的身体有坏处” / Alternatives: “有害” is concise and formal, meaning harmful to health. “会伤害身体” is more vivid and emphasizes the result of eating the plants.

Word: “麻烦” / Context: “很麻烦的情况” / Alternatives: “危险” emphasizes physical risk, which fits poisonous plants especially well. “严重的问题” emphasizes that this is an important problem that needs to be solved.

Word: “要解决” / Context: “神农要解决这个问题” / Alternatives: “想要解决” shows Shennong’s intention. “决定解决” is stronger and shows that he has made a firm choice to act.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You created a clear problem-to-solution beginning, and introducing Shennong at the end gives your story a good direction.  
Next time, focus on keeping pronouns consistent and making every phrase into a complete sentence.

## Scene 2

> 他又聪明又努力，旅行很远找到很多植物。比方说，灵芝和夹竹桃。他一吃各种各样的植物就知道能不能吃这些植物。然后，神农写他的主意。他喜欢植物的味道，特别是花。神农遇到了一个黄色的花，一吃就觉得不舒服。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “他又聪明又努力” / Formula: First mention = name + identity/role; avoid an unclear pronoun / Correct: “神农是一个又聪明又努力的人。”

Incorrect phrase: “旅行很远找到很多植物” / Formula: Subject + 为了…… + 去/到 + place + verb + object / Correct: “他为了寻找植物，去了很远的地方，找到了很多植物。”

Incorrect phrase: “比方说，灵芝和夹竹桃。” / Formula: Full sentence: 例如 + object + 都是 + category / Correct: “例如，灵芝和夹竹桃都是植物。”

Incorrect phrase: “他一吃各种各样的植物就知道能不能吃这些植物” / Formula: Subject + 一 + verb phrase + 就 + result; use “有没有毒/能不能吃” for a plant’s edibility / Correct: “他一尝各种各样的植物，就知道这些植物有没有毒、能不能吃。”

Incorrect phrase: “然后，神农写他的主意。” / Formula: Subject + 把 + object + verb + result/complement; write down information, not “ideas” in this context / Correct: “然后，神农把这些植物的用处都写下来。”

Incorrect phrase: “他喜欢植物的味道，特别是花。” / Formula: 特别是 + a parallel category; distinguish “植物的味道” from “花的香味” / Correct: “他喜欢植物的味道，特别喜欢花的香味。”

Incorrect phrase: “神农遇到了一个黄色的花” / Formula: Number + classifier + adjective + noun; 花 uses the classifier “朵” / Correct: “神农遇到了一朵黄色的花。”

Incorrect phrase: “一吃就觉得不舒服。” / Formula: Subject + 一 + verb + 就 + result; repeat the subject when it may be unclear / Correct: “他一吃这朵花，就觉得不舒服。”

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “努力” / Context: “他又聪明又努力” / Alternatives: “勤奋” is a more natural written-word choice for someone who works hard consistently. “努力” can be a noun or verb and emphasizes making an effort; “勤奋” describes a person’s hardworking character.

Word: “旅行很远” / Context: “旅行很远找到很多植物” / Alternatives: “远行” means travelling a long distance and is concise and slightly literary. “跋山涉水” vividly emphasizes crossing mountains and rivers, so it suits a difficult journey.

Word: “找到” / Context: “找到很多植物” / Alternatives: “发现” emphasizes discovering plants that were previously unknown or unnoticed. “采集” means collecting plant specimens, which is especially suitable for Shennong’s study of plants.

Word: “主意” / Context: “神农写他的主意” / Alternatives: “用处” means practical use, such as whether a plant can be eaten or used as medicine. “记录” refers to written notes or documentation. “主意” means an idea or plan, so it does not fit this context well.

Word: “不舒服” / Context: “一吃就觉得不舒服” / Alternatives: “中毒” clearly means being poisoned and is suitable if the flower is harmful. “身体不适” is more formal and less specific; it means feeling physically unwell without stating the cause.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You created a clear story about Shennong and used a good example—lingzhi and oleander—to make his plant-testing work vivid.  
Next time, focus on introducing the character clearly at the beginning and using complete sentences with precise verbs and classifiers.

## Scene 3

> 发现了夹竹桃有毒。别人看到他，很快给他吃一个灵芝。神农一吃就觉得比较好。然后，他继续写他发现的信息。会告诉他的朋友哪一个植物能不能吃。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “发现了夹竹桃有毒。” / Formula: Subject + 发现 + object + 有毒 / Correct: “神农发现了夹竹桃有毒。”

Incorrect phrase: “别人看到他，很快给他吃一个灵芝。” / Formula: Specific person + 看到 + someone + condition, then 给 + someone + object / 让 + someone + verb / Correct: “朋友看到神农不舒服，很快给了他一株灵芝，让他吃。”

Incorrect phrase: “神农一吃就觉得比较好。” / Formula: Subject + 一 + verb + 就 + 觉得/感觉 + adjective + 了 / Correct: “神农一吃就觉得好多了。”  
“比较好” usually compares two things; here, use “好多了” to show that he felt better than before.

Incorrect phrase: “然后，他继续写他发现的信息。” / Formula: Subject + 继续 + verb + object; write down information = 记录 + information / Correct: “然后，他继续记录自己发现的信息。”

Incorrect phrase: “会告诉他的朋友哪一个植物能不能吃。” / Formula: Subject + 会 + 告诉 + someone + 哪一种植物 + 能/不能 + verb / Correct: “他会告诉朋友哪一种植物能吃，哪一种植物不能吃。”  
“哪一个植物能不能吃” incorrectly combines “哪一个” with the A-not-A question form “能不能.”

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “别人” / Context: “别人看到他” / Alternatives: “朋友” or “附近的人.” “别人” means other people in a very general way. “朋友” is clearer and more personal, while “附近的人” means people nearby and fits a rescue situation.

Word: “给他吃” / Context: “给他吃一个灵芝” / Alternatives: “给了他一株灵芝” or “让他吃灵芝.” “给他吃” can sound like someone is feeding him. “给了他……” emphasizes giving him the medicine, while “让他吃……” emphasizes advising or helping him to take it.

Word: “灵芝” / Context: “一个灵芝” / Alternatives: “一株灵芝” or “灵芝药.” “一个” is a general measure word. “一株” is more precise for a plant, and “灵芝药” emphasizes its role as medicine.

Word: “比较好” / Context: “觉得比较好” / Alternatives: “好多了,” “舒服多了,” or “精神好多了.” “比较好” usually needs a comparison. “好多了” is natural for recovery, “舒服多了” focuses on physical comfort, and “精神好多了” focuses on having more energy.

Word: “写他发现的信息” / Context: “继续写他发现的信息” / Alternatives: “记录自己的发现” or “记下这些发现.” “写信息” is understandable but slightly unnatural here. “记录自己的发现” is more formal and fits scientific exploration, while “记下这些发现” is natural and simple.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You correctly included the important plot point that oleander is poisonous and lingzhi helps Shennong recover.  
Next time, focus on making every sentence’s subject clear and using “哪一种……能/不能……” for two possible answers.
