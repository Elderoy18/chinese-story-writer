## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有很多人不知道什么植物能吃，什么植物不能吃。有很多人吃植物以后就吃坏肚子了，因为他们吃了毒。那个时候，有一个老人叫神农，他什么植物都觉得好吃，不怕生病，不怕毒。他希望别人也可以吃好吃的植物。可是他知道，如果他们还怕吃毒，不会吃他喜欢的植物。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，the time expression is redundant because 古代 already refers to a time period; use 在古代 or 古代.
- 吃了毒：吃了有毒的植物，毒 alone refers to poison, while 有毒的植物 precisely names the plants that made the people ill.
- 怕吃毒：害怕中毒，害怕中毒 is the natural expression for being afraid of becoming poisoned; 吃毒 normally needs a specific object such as 毒药.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 不会吃他喜欢的植物：就不会吃他喜欢的植物，an 如果 conditional normally uses 就 in the result clause to mark the cause-and-result relationship.
- Expressiveness: After the people become ill, add a physical result to make the danger clearer, for example: “有的人肚子疼得很厉害。”
- Expressiveness: When introducing 神农, add his inner purpose, for example: “他想找出哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought after his wish for others, for example: “他想：大家以后就不会随便生病了。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly explain the problem that people did not know which plants were safe, and you introduce 神农 with a full noun phrase before using 他. Your use of 吃了以后 and 吃坏肚子了 also shows a good sense of completed actions and results.

## Scene 2

> 所以，他上山下山找植物，吃了很多样子的植物。他是第一人，找到了茶。他把所有的植物写下来了。他一直都吃了，他连毒都能吃。可是他还没吃了一个叫夹竹桃的植物。他找到了那个像星星的植物，觉得很漂亮，马上就吃下了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很多样子的植物：很多种植物，Use the measure word “种” to count different kinds of plants; “样子” describes appearance rather than a category or variety.
- 他是第一人，找到了茶：他是第一个找到茶的人，Use “第一个 + verb phrase + 的人” for the first person to complete an action. “第一人” usually means a leading or most important person, not the first discoverer.
- 一个叫夹竹桃的植物：一种叫夹竹桃的植物，Use “种” as the measure word for a type of plant.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 吃 -- “吃了很多样子的植物”：尝试了很多种植物，emphasizes that he tested different plants to learn about them.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 他一直都吃了：他一直都吃，“一直” describes an ongoing or habitual action, so it takes the zero aspect form rather than completed-action “了”.
- Coherence (Temporality): 还没吃了一个叫夹竹桃的植物：还没吃过一种叫夹竹桃的植物，Use “没 + verb + 过” to say that someone has not had an experience before; “没” does not take completed-action “了” here.
- Expressiveness: At the discovery of the flower, add a visual detail to show why it attracts him, such as “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: After he sees the flower, add his thought before he eats it, such as “他想：这种花这么漂亮，一定很好吃吧！”

### Encouragement
- You use “找到了” correctly to show that the search was successful, and your scene has a clear action sequence from searching for plants to finding the flower.

## Scene 3

> 可是夹竹桃的毒很重，让他肚子很痛。他在地上躺着，觉得他快会死了。可是正好有别人找到他了，给了他一个像伞的植物，叫灵芝。他吃灵芝以后就觉得很好，不再生病了。他测量起来了，像星星的植物是毒，像伞的植物是药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 快会死了：快要死了，Use the imminent-change pattern with 快要……了; 会 is not needed when the meaning is that death seems very near.
- 是毒：有毒，Use 有毒 to describe a poisonous plant; 毒 is a noun and cannot directly serve as the predicate in this meaning.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很重 -- “夹竹桃的毒很重”：毒性很强，states more precisely that the plant’s poisonous effect is strong.
- 很痛 -- “让他肚子很痛”：疼得厉害，gives a more vivid sense of how severe the stomach pain is.
- 很好 -- “他吃灵芝以后就觉得很好”：舒服多了，more specifically describes feeling physically better after taking the medicine.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 觉得他快会死了：觉得自己快会死了，Use 自己 when the person who is thinking and the person who may die are the same; this makes the self-reference clear.
- Expressiveness: After “让他肚子很痛”, add a stronger physical reaction, such as “他疼得在地上打滚，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: After “觉得自己快要死了”, add an inner thought in quotation marks, such as “他想：‘我会不会死在这里？’”
- Expressiveness: When the helper appears, add one visual detail to make him easier to imagine, such as “这时，一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人走了过来。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the cause and result of eating the plant: the pain comes first, and the medicinal plant helps afterwards. You also use the contrast between the star-shaped and umbrella-shaped plants well.
