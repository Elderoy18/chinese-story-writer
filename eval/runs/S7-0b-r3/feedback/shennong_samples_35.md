## Scene 1

> 在古代，很多医生不知道怎么用药。所以他们找到了很多植物对药。神农先看到很多病人，他们吃很多植物对药。但是我觉得他们的吃的植物，让他们病人。神农觉得这个植物可能特别是毒。所以他想找一个植物对药，他们可以吃对药。正好，他找到了一把花，他想把花放在茶里，对药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很多植物对药：很多可以做药的植物，The modifier describing the plants’ use must come before the noun; 对药 is not a grammatical verb phrase.
- 吃很多植物对药：吃很多植物当药，Use 当药 to mean “treat the plants as medicine”; 对药 cannot express this meaning.
- 让他们病人：让他们生病了，病人 is a noun meaning “patient,” while 生病 is the verb phrase needed after 让 to describe becoming ill.
- 特别是毒：特别有毒，Use 有毒 to describe a plant’s poisonous property; 毒 cannot directly follow an intensifier such as 特别.
- 一个植物对药：一种可以做药的植物，Use the measure word 种 for a type of plant, and place the phrase describing its medicinal use before 植物.
- 吃对药：吃，The phrase 对药 is not grammatical here; after 可以, the verb 吃 is sufficient.
- 放在茶里，对药：放在茶里，做药，做药 means “make medicine” or “use something for medicine”; 对药 is not a valid verb phrase.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 看到 -- “神农先看到很多病人”：发现，emphasizes that he notices their situation through observation.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：一个叫神农的男人，This is the character’s first mention, so introduce the name together with a short noun phrase identifying who he is.
- Coherence (Reference): 他们吃很多植物对药：这些病人吃很多植物当药，Use the full noun phrase again because both 医生 and 病人 have appeared, so 他们 can have more than one possible referent.
- Coherence (Reference): 他们的吃的植物：那些病人吃的植物，Repeat the noun 病人 because the previous sentence also mentions 神农, making the pronoun reference less clear.
- Coherence (Reference): 这个植物：这些植物，Use a plural reference because the preceding discussion is about many plants, not one previously identified plant.
- Coherence (Reference): 他们可以吃：病人可以吃，Repeat 病人 because 医生 and 病人 are both possible referents for 他们 after several intervening ideas.
- Expressiveness: At the point where the patients become ill, add a concrete symptom to show the result: “很多病人吃了有毒的植物，肚子疼得厉害。”
- Expressiveness: When the flowers appear, add visual detail to make the place memorable: “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: After the character finds the flowers, add a short thought to show his uncertainty: “神农想：‘这种花能做药吗？’”

### Encouragement
- You have started the scene with a clear time setting and introduced the problem of people becoming ill after eating plants. You also use connectors such as 所以 and 但是 to show relationships between ideas.

## Scene 2

> 他不知道如果这把花对你的身体好，这就要看如果这把花毒不毒了。神农想找别的植物，但是这很麻烦。正好，他找到了别的花。他一吃花，很快就生病了。他的肚子疼死了。神农不知道他吃不吃灵芝或者夹竹桃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 如果这把花对你的身体好：这朵花对身体好不好，An embedded yes/no question cannot use 如果; use an A-not-A form such as 好不好. Also, a single flower takes the classifier 朵, while 一把花 refers to a bunch of flowers.
- 如果这把花毒不毒：这朵花有没有毒，After 看, an embedded yes/no question uses A-not-A or 有没有, not 如果. Use 有毒 to describe a poisonous plant.
- 他吃不吃灵芝或者夹竹桃：自己吃的是灵芝还是夹竹桃，When choosing between two possibilities, use 是……还是…… rather than an A-not-A question followed by 或者. 自己 also clearly shows that the person who ate the plant is 神农.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 麻烦 -- “但是这很麻烦”：费事，emphasizes that finding the plants takes a lot of effort and time；艰辛，emphasizes that the task is difficult and demanding.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 这很麻烦：找别的植物很麻烦，The demonstrative 这 has a vague referent here; repeat the action with a noun phrase so the reader knows exactly what is difficult.
- Coherence (Reference): 他一吃花：他一吃那朵花，The noun 花 is unclear because more than one flower has been mentioned. Use a demonstrative plus classifier to identify the flower being eaten.
- Expressiveness: When 神农 becomes ill, add a physical detail to show how painful it is: “他躺在地上，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: After the stomachache, add 神农’s worried thought: “神农想：‘我会不会死？’”
- Expressiveness: When he finds the flower, describe its appearance before he eats it: “那朵花又黄又大，像星星一样漂亮。”

### Encouragement
- You use the sequence pattern “一……就……” correctly in “他一吃花，很快就生病了,” and “疼死了” gives the illness a strong, vivid feeling.

## Scene 3

> 别的人给了神农一个灵芝。这个植物可以让神农更好。比方说，他会觉得更健康。吃灵芝以后，他可以再走。现在他觉得更好。最后，神农找到了很多植物，他可以用对药。这些植物让神农很高兴。神农想跟很多人分享他的新药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别的人：有人，别的人 means “other people” in contrast with a previously identified person or group; no such contrast has been established here, so 有人 introduces an unspecified giver naturally.
- 找到了很多植物，他可以用对药：找到了很多可以用来做药的植物，the phrase 用对药 is not a valid way to say that plants can serve as medicine. Use 用来 + verb phrase to express purpose, and 做药 means “to be used as medicine.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 高兴 -- “这些植物让神农很高兴”：欣慰，adds the specific feeling of being pleased because his efforts have produced a good result.
- 新药 -- “分享他的新药”：新发现的药，specifies that the medicine is new because Shen Nong has only just discovered it.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃灵芝以后：吃了灵芝以后，use 了 for the completed action that happens before the later action introduced by 以后.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农想跟很多人分享他的新药：他想跟很多人分享他的新药，use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when Shen Nong was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other person competes as the referent.
- Expressiveness: At the first sentence, describe the helper so the moment feels more vivid: “一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人走过来，给了神农一个灵芝。”
- Expressiveness: After Shen Nong eats the medicine, add a physical change to show his recovery: “他的肚子不那么疼了，觉得好多了。”
- Expressiveness: When he shares the medicine, add a short line of speech: “神农说：‘这种像伞一样的植物叫灵芝，可以做药。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the helpful effect of the lingzhi and use sequence words such as 吃……以后 and 最后 to move the scene forward.
- Your sentence about Shen Nong wanting to share his medicine gives the scene a positive ending and shows his helpful character.
