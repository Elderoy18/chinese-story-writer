## Scene 1

> 一天，很久很久以前，孟子和他妈妈住在中国。孟子妈妈的工作是祭拜，孟子不高兴，因为他说我们老是搬家，为什么？他妈妈说，从早到晚，我一直祭拜，很多墓地得我祭拜。所以我们得去很多不同的地方。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很多墓地得我祭拜：我得去很多墓地祭拜，A modal verb such as “得” follows its subject and is followed by a verb phrase. Because “墓地” is the place visited for the ritual, use “去” before the location rather than treating it as the direct object of “祭拜”.
- 一天，很久很久以前：在很久很久以前的一天，The time expression should be formed as one phrase; “一天” is a specific day within the earlier time period.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不高兴 -- “孟子不高兴”：不满，specifies that he is dissatisfied with the repeated moves；心烦，shows that the repeated moves are making him feel troubled and annoyed.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和他妈妈住在中国：有一个小男孩叫孟子。他和他的妈妈住在中国，For a first mention, introduce the character with a name and a brief identity before using a pronoun to refer back to him.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为他说我们老是搬家，为什么？：，说：“我们老是搬家，为什么？”，The connector “因为” should introduce the cause of his unhappiness, but this clause introduces his question instead. Use quoted speech to show what he says.
- Expressiveness: After “孟子不高兴”, add an internal reaction to show why the moves bother him, such as “他心想：“我们什么时候才能有一个自己的家？””
- Expressiveness: In the mother’s explanation, add a physical detail to make her hard work more vivid, such as “我每天从早到晚祭拜，累得腿都酸了。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show both characters’ reasons: Mengzi is unhappy about moving, while his mother explains why she must travel.
- The use of “从早到晚” and “所以” helps the reader understand the mother’s busy routine and its result.
