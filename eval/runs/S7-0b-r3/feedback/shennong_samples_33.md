## Scene 1

> 古代时候，人不知道他们能吃什么植物，常常一吃就死。但是不知道为什么，没有办法。所以有一天，神农出去试一试很多种植物，告诉人什么植物是安全什么是危险。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代时候：在古代，do not combine a time noun with a redundant time expression; “古代” already gives the time setting.
- 人不知道他们能吃什么植物：人们不知道什么植物能吃，use “人们” for the concrete group of people in this situation; “他们” is unnecessary because the people who may eat the plants are already understood.
- 试一试很多种植物：尝一尝很多种植物，“试一试” usually means testing whether something works or fits, while “尝一尝” specifically means tasting food or plants.
- 告诉人什么植物是安全什么是危险：告诉人们哪些植物是安全的，哪些植物是危险的，use “人们” for the group being told, and repeat the noun phrase in the two parallel parts; an adjective after “是” normally takes “的” here.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 安全 -- “什么植物是安全的”：无毒的，identifies more precisely that the plants do not contain poison.
- 危险 -- “什么植物是危险的”：有毒的，states the specific danger in this plant-testing context.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：一个叫神农的男人，follow the first-mention rule by briefly introducing a new character with a name and a noun phrase.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉人们：告诉了人们，use “了” for the completed action of telling people after he has tested the plants.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 但是不知道为什么，没有办法：人们不知道为什么，也没有办法，“但是” marks contrast, but these two ideas are not contrasting; “也” links two related problems.
- Expressiveness: After the plant testing, add a physical consequence to make the danger vivid, such as “有的人吃了有毒的植物以后，肚子很疼。”
- Expressiveness: At the introduction of the new character, add a simple thought to show his motivation, such as “神农想：‘我一定要找出能吃的植物。’”
- Expressiveness: Describe one kind of plant he finds, such as “有的花是黄色的，像天上的星星一样。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish the main problem at the beginning: people do not know which plants they can eat. Your use of “一吃就死” also creates a strong sense of danger.

## Scene 2

> 发现有的植物对身体非常好，特别是茶，让肚子感觉又暖和又清楚。他告诉朋友：“你吃哪种植物，这就要看你的口味。比方说，你爱吃甜的，可能喜欢糖。”他最喜欢吃花，除了夹竹桃以外，什么都花想吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 让肚子感觉又暖和又清楚：让肚子暖和，头脑也清醒，清楚 describes information or an explanation, not a physical feeling; use 清醒 for a clear, alert mental state.
- 什么都花想吃：什么花都想吃，when a question word such as 什么 is used with 都 to mean “every,” it comes before the noun: 什么花都…….

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好 -- “有的植物对身体非常好”：有益，states more precisely that the plants benefit health.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 朋友：他的一个朋友，this is the first mention of this person, so introduce the character with a full noun phrase rather than an unmarked bare noun.
- Expressiveness: After the discovery about tea, add a sensory detail such as “茶水冒着热气，喝起来还有一点香味。”
- Expressiveness: Show his reaction to finding a useful plant: “他觉得很高兴，因为他终于找到一种对身体有好处的植物。”
- Expressiveness: In the quoted speech, add a brief warning about the dangerous flower: “可是夹竹桃有毒，不能吃。”

### Encouragement
- You used direct speech to show the character explaining his ideas, which makes the scene more lively. You also contrasted tea with the dangerous flower, giving the scene a clear focus.

## Scene 3

> 他发现这个口味，一百岁时候，不小心吃了毒的花。一吃就开始死。但是生病时候告诉人什么植物是药，什么植物是毒。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他发现这个口味，一百岁时候，不小心吃了毒的花：他发现这个口味。一百岁的时候，不小心吃了有毒的花，The time phrase needs 的 after the multi-syllable age expression, and it should be attached to the action it modifies. Use 有毒的植物 or 有毒的花 for a poisonous plant; 毒的花 is not the standard modifier.
- 一吃就开始死：一吃就快要死了，Death is a change-of-state event rather than an action that can normally begin; 快要死了 expresses that he was close to dying.
- 但是生病时候告诉人：但是生病的时候告诉人们，The structure with 时候 needs 的 here. 人 is a generic singular noun and cannot refer to a particular group of listeners; use 人们 for “people.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不小心 -- “不小心吃了有毒的花”：误食了，specifically means accidentally ate or swallowed something and is especially useful for food or plants.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉人们什么植物是药，什么植物有毒：告诉了人们什么植物是药，什么植物有毒，Use 了 for this completed narrative action; the telling happened while he was ill.
- Coherence (Reference): 什么植物是毒：什么植物有毒，Use 有毒 to describe the property of a plant clearly; this makes the contrast with 药 easy for the reader to follow.
- Expressiveness: At the point where he eats the flower, add one visual detail about it, such as 黄色的花像星星一样，又大又漂亮.
- Expressiveness: After he becomes ill, show his physical feeling or a worried thought, such as 他肚子疼得厉害，躺在地上，心想：“我会死吗？”

### Encouragement
- You used the pattern 一……就…… effectively to show that the danger happened immediately after eating the flower. You also clearly set up an important contrast between plants that are medicine and plants that are poisonous.
