## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个男孩名字叫孟子。孟子和孟子的妈妈两个人住在很穷的农村。他们住的地方旁边是个墓地，墓地老是有很多人祭拜。因为人们在祭拜，他们一直在哭。孟子和孟子的妈妈住在这里才一个星期就觉得受不了了，而且害得孟子的学习。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，The phrase is redundant because 古代 already expresses a time period, while 的时候 also means “at the time of.”
- 有一个男孩名字叫孟子：有一个叫孟子的男孩，Use the modifier pattern with 的: the name phrase comes before the noun it describes.
- 而且害得孟子的学习：而且这里的环境影响了孟子的学习，害得 must be followed by a person and a harmful result clause; it cannot be followed only by a noun phrase.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很穷 -- “住在很穷的农村”：贫困，specifies that the area lacks economic resources and is more precise for describing the countryside.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子的妈妈两个人：孟子和他的妈妈两个人，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence: after naming 孟子, his mother should be introduced as his mother rather than repeating his name.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子的妈妈住在这里：孟子和他的妈妈住在这里，Use a pronoun for the possessor when the name has just identified whose mother is meant.
- Coherence (Temporality): 住在这里才一个星期就觉得受不了了：住在这里才住了一个星期就觉得受不了了，Use 了 after 住 to mark the completed duration of living there before the later reaction.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为人们在祭拜，他们一直在哭：因为人们在祭拜，所以他们一直在哭，Because this sentence presents a cause-and-effect relation, use the paired connector 因为……所以……
- Expressiveness: After introducing the cemetery, add a sound detail to make the setting more vivid, such as “墓地里不时传来伤心的哭声。”
- Expressiveness: At the final sentence, show the mother’s feeling with an internal thought or short speech, such as “孟子的妈妈叹了口气，说：‘这里太吵了，会影响孩子学习。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduce the main setting, the cemetery, and explain why it makes the family uncomfortable. Your use of “才……就……” effectively shows that they quickly became unable to tolerate the environment.

## Scene 2

> 孟子的妈妈觉得孟子取得良好的教育是很重要的，所以孟子的妈妈认为这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好，就打算跟孟子搬家。孟子的妈妈带孟子不是搬到城市，而是搬到了孟子老师的家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 取得良好的教育：接受良好的教育，Chinese uses “接受教育” for receiving an education; “取得” is used for achievements or results, not for education itself.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 地方 -- “这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好”：环境，more precisely refers to the surroundings and atmosphere that can affect a child’s education.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 所以孟子的妈妈认为：所以她认为，Use a pronoun when the same character was mentioned earlier in the same sentence and no other possible referent intervenes.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子的妈妈带孟子不是搬到城市：她带孟子不是搬到城市，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence of the same character from the previous sentence.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 不是搬到城市，而是搬到了孟子老师的家：搬到了孟子老师的家，The “不是……而是……” connector should correct or refute an expected or mistaken idea; here the sentence simply states the new destination.
- Expressiveness: After the mother decides to move, add her worried inner thought to show why education matters: “这样的环境会影响孟子的学习。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech before the move to show the mother’s determination: “我得给孟子找一个适合学习的地方。”

### Encouragement
You clearly connect the mother’s concern about education with her decision to move. You also use the action pattern of taking a child along naturally in the second sentence.
