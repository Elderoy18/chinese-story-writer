## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男人觉得植物很有意思。他叫神农。有的植物吃了以后他的肚子疼死了，可是有的吃了以后很舒服。他找很多植物。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，The phrase is redundant because 古代 already expresses a time period, while 的时候 also means “at the time of.”
- 他找很多植物：他找到了很多植物，找 means “to look for,” while 找到 means “to find”; this sentence describes plants that he has found and tried.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一个男人觉得植物很有意思。他叫神农：有一个叫神农的男人觉得植物很有意思，Use the character’s name together with a full noun phrase at first mention, rather than introducing him only as 一个男人 and naming him afterward.
- Expressiveness: After the stomachache, add a physical reaction to make the pain more vivid: 他疼得在地上躺着，难受得不得了。
- Expressiveness: Add an internal question after he becomes ill to show his curiosity: 他想：“这种植物有毒吗？”
- Expressiveness: At the end, add a short description of his purpose: 他想找出哪些植物可以吃，哪些植物不能吃。

### Encouragement
- You clearly show two different results of eating plants, and the contrast with 可是 makes the comparison easy to understand. Your phrase 肚子疼死了 is vivid and effectively shows how severe the pain is.

## Scene 2

> 凉山很大的山，这也找植物。一天，他找到黄色的花。他听说你跟黄色的花做茶很有用。比方说，你的头发疼死，这冰茶是药。他一喝茶就很不舒服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 凉山很大的山：凉山是一座很大的山，A noun used as the predicate needs the linking verb and an appropriate measure word here.
- 这也找植物：在这儿找植物，The character 也 is miswritten for 儿, and a location before an action is introduced with 在.
- 你跟黄色的花做茶：用黄色的花做茶，跟 means “with/together with” and cannot introduce the material used to make something; 用 marks the material or means.
- 你的头发疼死：头疼得要命，头发 means “hair,” not “head”; for an extremely painful condition, use a degree complement with 得.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不舒服 -- “他一喝茶就很不舒服”：难受，more specifically conveys physical distress or suffering in this situation.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 找到黄色的花：找到了黄色的花，The finding is a completed event in the past narrative, so it needs the perfective marker 了.
- Expressiveness: When the yellow flowers appear, add one visual detail to make the place easier to imagine, such as：那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花。
- Expressiveness: After he feels ill, describe a physical reaction, such as：他捂着肚子，难受得不得了。
- Expressiveness: Add his worried thought after drinking the tea, such as：他想：我会不会生病？

### Encouragement
- You clearly move the scene from finding flowers to making tea and then to the character’s reaction. Your use of the pattern 一……就…… also shows the immediate result of drinking the tea well.

## Scene 3

> 糟糕，这黄色的花是夹竹桃，特别是毒。正好在这儿，灵芝药的夹竹桃他吃完了，也写一个课文的植物。谢谢神农。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 这黄色的花：这朵黄色的花，A demonstrative referring to one flower needs a matching measure word: 这 + measure word + noun.
- 特别是毒：特别有毒，Use 有毒 to describe a poisonous plant; 毒 alone is a noun and cannot follow 特别 in this predicate.
- 灵芝药的夹竹桃：灵芝和夹竹桃，Two plant names should be linked with 和; 药 cannot be placed between them as an attributive modifier.
- 也写一个课文的植物：也把这些植物写进了一本书里，课文 is a lesson text, not a place for recording plants. To express recording the plants in a book, use 把 + object + 写进 + location, with 了 for the completed writing action.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the first sentence, add a visual detail about the flower, such as “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花”, to help the reader picture why the plant catches attention.
- Expressiveness: When the character eats the plant, show his physical reaction with a detail such as “他一吃，肚子就疼了起来”, making the danger of the plant clearer.
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought at the moment of pain, such as “他想：我会死吗？”, to show the character’s fear.

### Encouragement
- You clearly named the important plants, including “夹竹桃” and “灵芝”, and you tried to show that the character records what he learns. The opening “糟糕” also gives the scene an immediate feeling of danger.
