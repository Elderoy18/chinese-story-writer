## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个男人，他的名字是神农。他看到了有很多人不知道哪个植物好吃，也不知道什么植物对你的身体有好处。这个问题神农觉得很糟糕，所以神农去了吃每种植物。他吃了一种植物以后，就写了这个植物对你有没有好处。有的时候，神农吃了很好吃的植物。有的时候，他吃了很危险的植物。他吃完了每种植物以后，会来跟他的朋友聊到他吃的植物了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 already refers to a time period, so adding 的时候 is redundant.
- 哪个植物：哪种植物，use 种 as the measure word when asking about a type of plant.
- 去了吃每种植物：去吃了每种植物，when 去 is followed by another verb, the completion marker follows the main action: 去 + V. + 了.
- 写了：写下了，写下 means to record information; it fits recording whether a plant is beneficial.
- 聊到：聊，聊到 means that a topic happens to be mentioned, while 聊 + object means deliberately discussing that topic.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 看到 -- “他看到了有很多人不知道哪个植物好吃”：发现，emphasizes that he realized people had this problem, rather than only visually noticing them.
- 糟糕 -- “这个问题神农觉得很糟糕”：严重，states more precisely that this problem has harmful or important consequences.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 所以神农去了吃每种植物：所以他去吃了每种植物，use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when 神农 was named in the previous sentence and no other male character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Reference): 这个植物对你的身体有好处：这种植物对身体有好处，this narrative has no addressee, so 你的 has no clear referent; use a general expression instead.
- Coherence (Reference): 有的时候，神农吃了很好吃的植物：有的时候，他吃很好吃的植物，use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence because the previous sentence already refers to 神农.
- Coherence (Temporality): 有的时候，神农吃了很好吃的植物：有的时候，他吃很好吃的植物，zero particle is needed for a repeated/habitual action introduced by 有的时候; 了 marks one completed event.
- Coherence (Temporality): 有的时候，他吃了很危险的植物：有的时候，他吃很危险的植物，zero particle is needed for this habitual action.
- Coherence (Temporality): 会来跟他的朋友聊到他吃的植物了：会来跟他的朋友聊他吃的植物，after the modal verb 会, use the zero particle; the final 了 cannot mark a completed action here.
- Expressiveness: After the dangerous-plant sentence, add a physical reaction to show the risk more vividly, such as “他肚子疼了起来，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: When 神农 decides to test plants, add a brief thought to show his motivation, such as “他想：我一定要找出哪些植物能吃。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show Shennong’s purpose and use cause-and-effect with 所以 to connect his concern to his action.
- Your repeated use of 有的时候 creates a clear contrast between beneficial plants and dangerous plants.

## Scene 2

> 一个人问神农，哪个植物好吃。神农说，这就要看你的口味了。要是你喜欢吃甜的，有很多甜的水果，比方说苹果、西瓜和梨。一个别的人问神农，哪个植物对你的身体有好处。神农说，有很多种植物对你的身体有好处，比方说小白菜、黄瓜、茶和菠菜。一个别的人问神农，哪个植物对你的身体有坏处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一个别的人：另一个人，别的人 means “other people” in contrast with a specified person or group; when introducing the next individual in a sequence, use 另一个人.
- 一个别的人：另一个人，别的人 does not naturally introduce one new individual here; 另一个人 means “another person.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好吃 -- “哪个植物好吃”：合口味，emphasizes that the food suits a particular person’s taste in this question.
- 坏处 -- “哪个植物对你的身体有坏处”：有害，states more precisely that the plant is harmful to the body.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 这就要看你的口味了：这就要看你的口味怎么样了，within the 这就要看……了 pattern, the part after 看 should be a question form; 怎么样 makes the listener’s taste the uncertain condition.
- Expressiveness: After the list of fruits, add one sensory detail to make the answer livelier, such as “苹果又脆又甜，西瓜很多汁。”
- Expressiveness: In the health-benefit answer, add a short reason for one example, such as “茶对身体有好处，因为可以让人更有精神。”
- Expressiveness: Give the questioner a small reaction after receiving the answer, such as “那个人听了以后，高兴地说：‘我想吃西瓜！’”

### Encouragement
- You use clear question-and-answer dialogue to organize this scene, and your examples of fruits and vegetables make each answer easy to understand.

## Scene 3

> 神农说，有一天，我吃了一个黄色的花，名字是夹竹桃。我一吃夹竹桃，就觉得这种植物对我的脑有坏处。可是，一个灵芝和一个男人正好在我的旁边，我说，麻烦你把那个植物给我吃一下。我一吃这个灵芝就好了。所以，你得小心吃植物，因为有的植物对你的健康有坏处，特别是夹竹桃。因为夹竹桃有毒。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一个黄色的花：一朵黄色的花，花 takes the matching measure word 朵 rather than the general measure word 个.
- 我的脑：我的脑子，when referring to a person’s brain in ordinary narration, use 脑子; 脑 is mainly used in compounds such as 大脑.
- 一个灵芝：一株灵芝，灵芝 is a plant-like fungus, so 株 is a more appropriate measure word than the general measure word 个.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 旁边 -- “正好在我的旁边”：身边，fits especially well when a person is close to the narrator and makes the location feel more personal.
- 有坏处 -- “对你的健康有坏处”：会损害你的健康，states more specifically that the plants can cause harm to health.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 那个植物：灵芝，use the name again because 那个植物 could refer either to the earlier yellow flower or to the newly mentioned 灵芝; repeating the noun makes the referent clear.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 特别是夹竹桃。因为夹竹桃有毒。：特别是夹竹桃，因为它有毒。，the second 因为-clause cannot stand alone here; connect it to the statement about 夹竹桃 to express the cause.
- Expressiveness: After “就觉得这种植物对我的脑子有坏处”, add a physical reaction to show how the narrator feels, such as “我的肚子疼了起来，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: When the man appears, add one visual detail, such as “一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人正好在我的身边。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought when the narrator becomes ill, such as “我心想：我会不会死？”

### Encouragement
- You use the pattern “一……就……” effectively to show that eating the plant quickly led to a result. Your final warning about being careful with plants also gives the scene a clear lesson.
