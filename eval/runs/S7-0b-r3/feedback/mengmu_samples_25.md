## Scene 1

> 这是古代的故事。孟子小的时候，他的爸爸很早就去世了。从他的家到墓地很近，所以他每天老是从早到晚去墓地跟朋友们玩玩儿。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 从他的家到墓地很近：他的家离墓地很近，Use the distance structure “place + 离 + place + 很近”; “从……到……” normally introduces a route or distance, not this adjectival location description.
- 从早到晚去墓地跟朋友们玩玩儿：从早到晚去墓地跟朋友们玩，Verb reduplication in “玩玩儿” usually suggests a brief or casual action, which conflicts with the long duration expressed by “从早到晚”.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the father’s death, add Mengzi’s feeling to make the background more personal: “孟子很想念爸爸，常常觉得很难过。”
- Expressiveness: When he goes to the cemetery, add one sensory detail about what he sees or hears: “墓地里常常有人哭，声音听起来很伤心。”
- Expressiveness: Show why the cemetery interests him through an inner thought: “孟子觉得人们祭拜的样子很有意思。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduce Mengzi’s childhood background and use a daily routine to show what he often did. The time phrase “从早到晚” gives the scene a strong sense of how regularly he visited the cemetery.

## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈觉得在墓地玩儿，害得，所以他们搬家。搬家对孟子有好的影响，他才五岁，可是每天不是写字就是帮妈妈的工作。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 害得：会带来不好的影响，害得 must be followed by the person affected and a harmful result; it cannot end the clause by itself.
- 帮妈妈的工作：帮妈妈做事，after 帮妈妈, a verb phrase is needed; 工作 with 的 is a noun phrase, so it cannot parallel 写字 here.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好 -- “有好的影响”：积极的，specifies that the influence benefits Mengzi’s development rather than being merely “good.”

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 所以他们搬家：所以他们搬家了，use 了 for the completed action of moving in this past narrative.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他才五岁，可是每天不是写字就是帮妈妈的工作：他才五岁，就每天不是写字就是帮妈妈做事，use the 才……就…… construction to show that he began these daily activities at a very young age; 可是 is a contrast connector and does not express this relationship.
- Expressiveness: At the mother’s decision to move, add her worry or an inner thought, such as “孟母很担心，心想：‘这儿会影响孟子。’”
- Expressiveness: Make Mengzi’s daily activity more vivid with an evaluative detail, such as “孟子每天认真写字，也帮妈妈做事。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect the cemetery setting to the family’s move, and you use the “不是……就是……” pattern to show Mengzi’s daily routine.

## Scene 3

> 他很聪明的人，难怪他成为有名的人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他很聪明的人：他是一个很聪明的人，A noun phrase used as the predicate needs 是; 一个 introduces the noun phrase 人 naturally.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为有名的人：成为了有名的人，Use 了 after 成为 to mark the completed change of state in this biographical event.
- Expressiveness: After describing his intelligence, add a concrete learning habit, such as “他从小喜欢看书，也常常思考问题。”
- Expressiveness: You could make the result more personal by adding a short comment from others, such as “大家都很佩服他。”

### Encouragement
- You used 难怪 well to connect his intelligence with his later success. The sentence also gives a clear positive ending for the character.
