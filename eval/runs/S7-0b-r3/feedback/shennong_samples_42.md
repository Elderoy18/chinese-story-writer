## Scene 1

> 古代的时候有一个人叫神农。他需要当上医，一看别的人就想帮助他们。但是，他有一个问题，他不知道什么东西能做药。神农听说一个远远的地方有一种植物，什么病人吃都会觉得身体更好。神农三个月走去，找到有植物的地方，他看到一个很漂亮的植物，又香又好看。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，this phrase redundantly combines two time expressions; use the single time expression shown in the correction.
- 当上医：当上医生，医 is not normally used alone as the noun for a doctor; 医生 is the standard occupation noun.
- 一看别的人：一看到别的人，when the meaning is “see,” use 看到 rather than 看.
- 远远的地方：很远的地方，远远 cannot modify 地方 in this way; 很远 describes a distant place naturally.
- 什么病人：病人，什么 asks which kind of patient; it cannot mean every patient in this sentence.
- 一个很漂亮的植物：一株很漂亮的植物，株 is the matching measure word for an individual plant.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 香 -- “又香又好看”：芬芳，specifies that the plant gives off a pleasant fragrance；清香，specifies a light, fresh pleasant smell.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农听说：他听说，follow the pronoun rule: after 神农 was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other male character competes, use 他.
- Coherence (Reference): 有植物的地方：有这种植物的地方，repeat the noun with 这种 to refer clearly to the particular plant mentioned in the previous sentence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 病人吃都会觉得：病人吃了都会觉得，use 了 for the completed eating action that happens before the patients feel better.
- Coherence (Temporality): 三个月走去：走了三个月，put the duration after the verb, and use 了 because the three-month journey is presented as completed.
- Expressiveness: At the moment he sees the plant, add a visual detail to make the discovery vivid, for example: “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花。”
- Expressiveness: Add Shennong’s hopeful thought after hearing about the plant, for example: “他想：‘如果这是真的，我就能帮很多病人了。’”
- Expressiveness: Show his reaction to the plant’s smell, for example: “他闻了闻，觉得这股清香很特别。”

### Encouragement
- You introduce Shennong early and keep the scene focused on his wish to help sick people. The final description uses both smell and appearance, which already makes the plant feel more interesting.

## Scene 2

> 他觉得，这种一定是做药的花。原来他想错了。吃了以后，神农快生病。 这时一个穿着蓝色衣服的人帮神农，快给他一种红的灵芝。神农吃了以后，他没有病，想起来以前一个人告诉他，灵芝对身体有好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 以前一个人：以前有一个人，An indefinite person being introduced in this remembered event needs the existential verb 有.
- 没有病：病好了，After being ill, this phrase should express recovery; this means that the illness has recovered, rather than simply stating that he does not have an illness.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农吃了以后，他没有病：神农吃了以后，病好了，Use zero form for the same character within one sentence when no other character competes as the referent; repeating 他 is unnecessary.
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃了以后，神农快生病：吃了以后，神农很快生病了，Here the illness happens as a completed event, so it needs 了. 很快 describes that the event happened soon, while 快 + verb more often expresses an imminent action.
- Coherence (Temporality): 快给他一种红的灵芝：赶快给了他一种红的灵芝，This is a completed action in the narrative, so 给 needs 了. In a series of actions, the last action can take 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 想起来以前有一个人告诉他：想起来了以前有一个人告诉过他，The remembering is completed at this point in the story, so the resultative verb 想起来 takes 了 after its complement. 告诉过 marks that the earlier telling happened before this memory.
- Expressiveness: After the illness begins, add a physical feeling to show its effect, such as “他的肚子疼了起来，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: When the blue-clothed person helps, show urgency through an action, such as “他赶快拿起灵芝，递给神农。”
- Expressiveness: After the medicine works, add Shen Nong’s feeling or thought, such as “神农松了一口气，觉得灵芝真有用。”

### Encouragement
- You used “原来” effectively to show that Shen Nong’s first guess was wrong. You also introduced the blue-clothed helper clearly with a descriptive noun phrase.

## Scene 3

> 啊，他怎么忘记了？他把灵芝放在包里。一回家就告诉别人。他找到药以后他写下怎么做药在书上。所以人们都知道，那红灵芝对身体好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别人：人们，Use 人们 when the meaning is the general public; 别人 means other people in contrast with a particular person or group.
- 写下怎么做药在书上：在书上写下怎么做药，The location phrase should come before the verb here; it shows where the writing is recorded.
- 那红灵芝：那种红灵芝，A demonstrative such as 那 should be followed by a measure word before a noun; 种 identifies a kind of plant or medicine.
- 对身体好处：对身体有好处，The fixed pattern is 对 + noun + 有好处; 有 is required to state that something benefits the body.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他找到药以后他写下：他找到药以后写下，Use zero form when the subject remains the same within one sentence; repeating 他 after the time clause is unnecessary.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他把灵芝放在包里：他把灵芝放进了包里，This is a completed action in the narrative, so it needs 了; with the directional result 放进, 了 follows the resultative complement.
- Coherence (Temporality): 写下怎么做药：写下了怎么做药，Writing the method is presented as a completed action, so the verb needs the perfective marker 了.
- Expressiveness: After the opening question, add Shen Nong’s realization to show his feeling: “原来灵芝在我的包里！”
- Expressiveness: When he puts away the medicine, add one visual detail from the scene: “红灵芝像一把小伞。”
- Expressiveness: After he tells the public, add a short line of speech to make the moment livelier: “灵芝对身体有好处！”

### Encouragement
You used the immediate-sequence pattern 一…就… effectively to show that Shen Nong acted as soon as he arrived home. The final 所以 also clearly connects his writing and sharing to people learning about the medicine.
