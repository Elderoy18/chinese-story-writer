- 跟孩子搬家：带着孩子搬家，The verb phrase means taking the child along when moving; it should use 带着 rather than 跟.
- 搬了到别一个城市：搬到了另一个城市，了 follows the directional complement in 搬到; also, 另一个 is the standard form for “another one.”

- Coherence (Reference): 孩子才五岁：他才五岁，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the referent, 孟子, was named in the previous sentence.
- Coherence (Reference): 在那个城市：在一个城市里，This is the first mention of the city, so introduce it with an indefinite noun phrase rather than using 那个, which implies that the reader already knows it.
- Coherence (Reference): 害得孟母没有钱：害得自己没有钱，When the affected person is the same as the subject of the clause, use 自己 to make that relationship clear and avoid repeating the name in the same sentence.

- Coherence (Temporality): 每天在墓地祭拜了：每天在墓地祭拜，Use the zero particle for a routine marked by 每天; 了 is not used for habitual actions.
