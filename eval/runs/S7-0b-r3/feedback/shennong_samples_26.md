## Scene 1

> 很久以前，有一个老男人叫神农。神农在古代每一天看到了别的小镇人生病还是不舒服，所以他决定开始找新的药。他下一天开始爬当地的山脉，找了各种各样的植物。神农试一试每个植物，知道有什么好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别的小镇人：小镇上的人们，The generic noun 人 should be 人们 when referring to a concrete group of people, and 小镇上的 clearly shows where they live.
- 生病还是不舒服：生病或觉得不舒服，还是 is generally used in questions or after a choice has been introduced; use 或 to join two possible conditions in a statement.
- 爬当地的山脉：爬当地的山，爬 normally takes individual mountains as its object; 山脉 refers to a range of connected mountains and does not fit this action naturally.
- 找了各种各样的植物：找到了各种各样的植物，找 means “to look for,” while 找到 means “to find”; the sentence describes plants that he found.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 每一天看到了：每天看到，Every day describes a routine, so it takes the zero aspect marker rather than completed-action 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 试一试每一种植物：试了试每一种植物，This is a completed action in the past event sequence, so 了 is needed after the reduplicated verb.
- Expressiveness: After the people’s illness, add one concrete cause to make the problem more vivid: “很多人吃了有毒的植物以后，肚子疼得很厉害。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where he decides to search, add his thought or a short line of speech: “神农想：‘我一定要找到能治病的植物。’”
- Expressiveness: When he climbs the mountains, add a simple image of the setting: “山上有很多高高的树，也开着各种颜色的花。”

### Encouragement
- You introduce the main character clearly at the beginning and give him a reason to act. The scene also has a clear sequence from seeing sick people to searching in the mountains.

## Scene 2

> 比方说，他一次试一试有些植物的叶，吃得又新鲜又甜。煮这些叶以后，他一喝就知道他发现新的茶叶。虽然他找到了很多的新的植物，但是他的旅程有的时候特别是危险。比方说，他一吃灵芝就开始生病。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他一次试一试有些植物的叶，吃得又新鲜又甜：他尝了一些植物的叶子，叶子又新鲜又甜，verb reduplication in “试一试” is for a light or tentative action, not for an action that was actually completed; also, “又新鲜又甜” describes the leaves, not the person eating them.
- 特别是危险：特别危险，“特别” directly modifies an adjective; “特别是” must introduce a noun, phrase, or clause that is being singled out.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 新的 -- “他找到了很多的新的植物”：未尝过的，specifies that these were plants he had not tasted before；陌生的，emphasizes that the plants were unfamiliar to him.
- 旅程 -- “他的旅程有的时候特别危险”：探索过程，makes clear that this journey is a process of investigating plants rather than ordinary travel.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 知道他发现了新的茶叶：知道自己发现了新的茶叶，when the person in the embedded clause is the same as the main subject, use “自己” to make the reference clear.
- Coherence (Temporality): 煮这些叶以后：煮了这些叶以后，use “了” for the completed cooking action before the later action introduced by “以后”.
- Coherence (Temporality): 发现新的茶叶：发现了新的茶叶，use “了” to mark the discovery as completed after he drinks the tea.
- Expressiveness: After the tea is cooked, add a sensory detail about its smell or taste, such as “煮好以后，茶闻起来很香，喝起来也很甜。”
- Expressiveness: At the moment he becomes ill, show his physical feeling and worry, such as “他肚子疼了起来，难受得躺在地上，心想：我会不会生病很久？”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought when he discovers the tea, such as “他很高兴，心想：这种叶子也许可以做药。”

### Encouragement
- You used “虽然……但是……” correctly to show that finding many plants did not make the journey completely safe.
- Your scene includes clear examples of testing plants, cooking leaves, and becoming sick, which gives the narrative a strong sequence of events.

## Scene 3

> 一个爬山的男人正好看到神农的麻烦，然后给了他一些药。神农最终他的小镇回到，跟别的人分享他的发现。你认为神农的故事真的吗？这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 神农最终他的小镇回到：神农最终回到了自己的小镇，A directional verb needs the location after it, and completed return takes 了. Use 自己的 because the town belongs to the subject of the sentence.
- 你认为神农的故事真的吗？：你认为神农的故事是真的吗？，An adjective predicate in a yes/no question needs 是 before the adjective.
- 这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处。：这就要看你是否相信中药对身体有好处了。，After 看, use 是否 to form the indirect yes/no question here. The pattern 这就要看……了 ends with 了.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 最终 -- “神农最终回到了自己的小镇”：终于，emphasizes that the return came after a long journey or effort.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农的麻烦：神农遇到了麻烦，A vague noun phrase does not clearly show what the man saw; this version states that Shen Nong was experiencing difficulty.
- Coherence (Reference): 跟别的人分享他的发现：跟小镇的人们分享自己的发现，Use 人们 for a concrete group of people in the town; 别的人 is used to distinguish other people from a particular person or group already under discussion.
- Expressiveness: After the man gives medicine, add Shen Nong’s physical reaction, such as “神农吃了药以后，肚子慢慢不疼了。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech when Shen Nong shares his discovery, such as “神农说：‘这种植物可以做药，可是有些植物不能吃。’”
- Expressiveness: Describe the helper briefly to make the scene more vivid, such as “一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人走过来。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect the rescue, Shen Nong’s return, and his sharing of discoveries. The final question also gives the scene an interesting ending that invites the reader to think.
