## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个人叫神农。一天，神农正在路上走，一看很多人正在吃一些植物。一些人一吃植物就生病了，神农想帮别人。所以想知道哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 already names a time period, so adding 的时候 is redundant.
- 看：看到，看 means “look,” while 看到 means “see/perceive”; here Shennong sees people eating.
- 别人：人们，别人 means “other people” in contrast with a particular person, while this sentence refers generally to the people Shennong wants to help.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 神农正在路上走，一看到很多人正在吃一些植物。一些人一吃植物就生病了：神农正在路上走，一看到很多人正在吃一些植物，就发现一些人一吃植物就生病了，the pattern 一V就 needs 就 to link the first event to its immediate result.
- Expressiveness: After the illness is mentioned, add a short description of the people’s condition, such as “他们肚子疼得很厉害”, to make the danger more vivid.
- Expressiveness: Add Shennong’s inner thought before he decides to investigate, such as “这些植物到底能不能吃呢？”, to show his curiosity and concern.

### Encouragement
- You use 正在 well to create an ongoing background action, and the sentence with 一吃植物就生病了 clearly shows the quick result of eating the plants.

## Scene 2

> 然后，神农开始尝很多植物，比方说他吃过生姜、芥兰和菠菜。一些植物吃真好，特别是黄瓜。但是，一些植物毒得很，特别是夹竹桃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一些植物吃真好：一些植物很好吃，Use the compound adjective “好吃” to describe food or plants that taste good; “吃真好” does not form a correct predicate here.
- 一些植物毒得很：一些植物有毒，“毒” cannot be intensified with “得很” in this meaning. Use “有毒” to describe a poisonous plant.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很多 -- “神农开始尝很多植物”：各种各样的，emphasizes that he tried many different kinds of plants, not only a large number.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 然后，神农开始尝很多植物：然后，他开始尝很多植物，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence: “神农” was the subject in the immediately preceding scene, and no other character competes as the referent.
- Expressiveness: After the sentence about the cucumber, add a sensory detail about its taste, such as: “黄瓜吃起来又脆又爽口。”
- Expressiveness: After mentioning the poisonous plant, add Shennong’s physical reaction to make the danger vivid, such as: “神农吃了夹竹桃以后，肚子疼了起来。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly contrast safe, good-tasting plants with dangerous ones by using “但是,” and your examples of plants make the scene easy to understand.

## Scene 3

> 一个人对神农说，麻烦我们可以吃什么植物？神农说，这就要看你想不想生病了。以后，神农告诉别人一些植物能吃，一些植物不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 麻烦我们可以吃什么植物？：请问，我们可以吃什么植物？，麻烦 cannot introduce a polite question by itself; 请问 is the appropriate question opener.
- 这就要看你想不想生病了：这就要看你吃的是哪种植物了，this structure should make the answer depend on the relevant uncertain condition. Here, the question is about which plants are edible, so the condition must be the type of plant eaten.
- 以后：后来，when a new sentence means “afterwards,” use 后来 rather than 以后.
- 神农告诉别人一些植物能吃，一些植物不能吃。：神农告诉人们，一些植物能吃，一些植物不能吃。，“别人” distinguishes other people from a stated person or group; 人们 is needed here because the meaning is people in general.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 说 -- “一个人对神农说”：问，more precisely shows that the person is asking a question rather than simply speaking.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the person asks the question, add a feeling of worry to show why the question matters: “我怕吃了会生病。”
- Expressiveness: When explaining edible plants, add one concrete example to make the information more vivid: “像伞一样的灵芝可以吃。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short reaction from the listener after learning the answer: “那我以后要小心一点！”

### Encouragement
- You created a clear question-and-answer exchange between the person and Shennong. You also clearly stated the important contrast between plants that can be eaten and plants that cannot.
