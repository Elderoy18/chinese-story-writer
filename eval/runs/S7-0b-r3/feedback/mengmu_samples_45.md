## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子。他们住在墓地的旁边，所以孟子老是祭拜。孟子不是祭拜，就是跟朋友聊天。他很喜欢他的城市，但是城市有才两个学校，都很小。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，this expression is redundant because 古代 already identifies a time period.
- 城市有才两个学校：城市里只有两所学校，use 只有 rather than 有才 to mean “there are only,” add 里 for location, and use 所 as the measure word for schools.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子：一个叫孟子的男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国，under the first-mention rule, introduce the character’s name in the first full noun phrase instead of naming him only in the following sentence.
- Coherence (Reference): 他的城市：自己的城市，when the possessor is the same person as the subject, use 自己的 to make that relationship clear.
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery is introduced, add one sensory detail to make the setting more vivid, such as “墓地里常常传来哭声。”
- Expressiveness: After the sentence about the small schools, add Mengzi’s reaction to show his feelings, such as “孟子觉得学校太小了，有一点失望。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduce the setting and use the cause connector “所以” to link the home near the cemetery with Mengzi’s actions.
- Your pattern “不是……就是……” is used well to show Mengzi’s repeated activities.

## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈不是不喜欢他们的城市，而是想孟子有很好的教育。他们搬家到有很多学校的城市。他们从早到晚走路，孟子一直抱怨。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 搬家到：搬到，When a destination follows, use the verb 搬 with 到 + place; 搬家 does not take the destination in this structure.
- 他的妈妈不是不喜欢他们的城市，而是想孟子有很好的教育：他的妈妈不是因为不喜欢他们的城市才搬家，而是想让孟子受到很好的教育，The two parts of 不是……而是…… should present contrasting reasons. To express another person receiving an education after 想, use 想让 + person + verb phrase.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们搬到有很多学校的城市：他们搬到了有很多学校的城市，Use 了 after 搬到 because the move is a completed action before the following events.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们从早到晚走路：他们从早到晚走了一天的路，Use 了 for this completed journey; a duration phrase such as 一天 can show how long they walked.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他们搬到有很多学校的城市：所以，他们搬到了有很多学校的城市，Use 所以 to connect the mother’s wish for better education with the resulting move; this is a cause-and-effect relation.
- Expressiveness: After the walking sentence, add a physical detail to show that the journey is tiring, such as “孟子走得腿都酸了。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where Mengzi complains, add a short line of speech to show his feeling, such as “孟子说：‘妈妈，我们什么时候才能到？’”
- Expressiveness: Show the mother’s inner feeling while she hears the complaint, such as “妈妈虽然很累，但是还是安慰孟子。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect the mother’s wish for Mengzi’s education with the move to a city with many schools. The time phrase also gives the final sentence a strong sense of a long, difficult journey.

## Scene 3

> 他难怪抱怨，他想他的朋友，因为他不要离开他的城市。他的妈妈找到孟子一个老师。跟老师孟子学最重要的文学，所以他成为哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 找到孟子一个老师：给孟子找了一个老师，Use 给 before the person who receives the benefit; 找了 marks the completed action of finding a teacher.
- 跟老师孟子学最重要的文学：孟子跟老师学最重要的文学，The subject must come before the phrase beginning with 跟 when it means “learn from/with the teacher.”
- 成为哲学家：成为了哲学家，Use 了 for this completed change of state: Mencius eventually became a philosopher.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 想 -- “他想他的朋友”：想念，more precisely expresses that he misses his friends because he is leaving them.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他难怪抱怨，他想他的朋友，因为他不要离开他的城市：难怪他抱怨，原来他想自己的朋友，也不想离开自己的城市，Use 难怪……，原来…… to give the explanation for a result; 也 connects the two parallel reasons. 不想 expresses his own wish, while 不要 is normally used for a command or prohibition.
- Expressiveness: After the teacher is found, add Mencius’s feeling about being able to study: 孟子很高兴，觉得终于可以好好学习了。
- Expressiveness: Describe one concrete study activity before the final result: 孟子每天认真看书，还跟老师讨论问题。
- Expressiveness: Add a short line from his mother after seeing his progress: 孟母笑着说：“这次搬家太对了！”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show Mencius’s reason for complaining and connect his later learning with becoming a philosopher. The scene also moves smoothly from his mother’s action to Mencius’s education.
