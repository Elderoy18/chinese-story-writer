- 喜欢玩在墓地里：喜欢在墓地里玩，A location phrase such as “在墓地里” normally comes before the main verb “玩”.
- 装祭拜：装作祭拜，“装作” must be followed by the action someone is pretending to do.

- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和他的朋友：他和朋友，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the character was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other male character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Temporality): 跟妈妈搬家：跟妈妈搬了家，Use “了” after a completed single action in the past; this move happened when he was five.

## Scene 2

> 妈妈认为，住离墓地太近，害得孟子的心气。孟子和孟母搬到子思老师的家，跟子思老师住。孟母帮子思打扫房间和做饭。子思帮孟母教她的儿子。因为孟子现在没有朋友，他老是读书。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 住离墓地太近：住在离墓地太近的地方，A verb of living requires a location introduced with 在, and the phrase needs 地方 to complete the location.
- 害得孟子的心气：伤了孟子的心气，害得 must be followed by a person plus a harmful result clause; it cannot directly take a noun phrase as its result.
- 老是读书：一直读书，老是 usually conveys a negative, complaining attitude; 一直 describes the child’s continual reading neutrally.

- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为孟子现在没有朋友，他老是读书：因为孟子现在没有朋友，所以他老是读书，Because the lack of friends causes the reading, use the paired cause-and-effect connector 因为……所以…….
