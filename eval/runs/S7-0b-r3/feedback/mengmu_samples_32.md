## Scene 1

> 有一天孟子去了墓地玩儿，但是他妈妈觉得这是不合适的地方让孩子玩，所以她决定了他们会搬家找更好的地方。她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福，而且住在这儿教育不太好。因为她望子成龙，所以她会一直搬家才能找到质量好的教育。然后他们搬到了离城市更近的地方。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不合适的地方让孩子玩：不适合孩子玩的地方，Use the pattern “适合／不适合 + person + verb” to describe whether a place suits an activity.
- 她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福：她担心儿子的童年不会幸福，The subject before “恐怕” cannot be the person who feels afraid; use “担心” when describing her worry.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 更好 -- “找更好的地方”：更适合孩子成长的，specifies that the new place would support the child’s development rather than simply being generally better.
- 一直 -- “会一直搬家”：不断，more precisely shows that the moves would happen repeatedly.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子去了墓地玩儿：小男孩孟子去了墓地玩儿，On a first mention, introduce the character with a brief noun phrase as well as the name.
- Coherence (Temporality): 决定了他们会搬家找更好的地方：决定搬家，找更好的地方，“决定” is a mental-state verb and takes the zero particle, not “了”; it should be followed directly by the action phrase that was decided.
- Expressiveness: At the cemetery scene, add what the child notices and feels, such as “孟子看见有人在墓地祭拜，觉得很好玩。”
- Expressiveness: After the mother’s concern, add a short inner thought or quoted speech, such as “孟母心想：‘住在这儿会影响孩子的成长。’”
- Expressiveness: When the family reaches the new place, describe its atmosphere, such as “那里每天都有很多商人卖东西，很热闹。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the mother’s reason for moving, and the paired connector “因为……所以……” helps the reader follow that cause-and-effect relationship.
- You also use “搬到了” well to show that the move was completed.

## Scene 2

> 他们住才一月就又搬家。古代的时候，一般人不喜欢卖东西的人。但是孟子老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿，害得妈妈着急得很，因为她恐怕儿子会成为跟他们很像。她告诉了孟子：他们不是朋友，而是不好的人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿：老是跟这些卖东西的人玩儿，Do not stack two frequency adverbs with the same meaning; both “老是” and “常常” mark habitual repetition, so one is enough.
- 恐怕儿子会成为跟他们很像：担心儿子会变得跟他们很像，“恐怕” means “I am afraid that” and cannot describe another person’s fear with a separate subject; use “担心” for her worry. Also, “成为” is followed by a noun or role, while “变得” introduces a change into an adjective-like description.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 住才一月就又搬家：才住了一个月，就又搬家了，Use the pattern “才 + duration + 就 + action” to show that the move happened after only a short time. Add “了” after the completed period of living and after the completed move.
- Coherence (Temporality): 她告诉了孟子：她告诉孟子：A saying verb that introduces quoted speech normally takes the zero particle rather than “了”.
- Expressiveness: After the move, add a marketplace sound or sight to show why the place attracts孟子, for example: “街上到处都是叫卖声，热闹极了。”
- Expressiveness: Show what孟子 does when he plays with the sellers, for example: “他学着商人收钱，还跟他们讨价还价。”
- Expressiveness: Make the mother’s worry more vivid with an internal thought before her speech, for example: “妈妈心想：‘他天天这样玩，会不会不喜欢学习？’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the contrast between ordinary people’s opinion of the sellers and孟子’s interest in them. The use of “害得妈妈着急得很” also effectively connects his behaviour to his mother’s reaction.

## Scene 3

> 你不是当数学家，而是当哲学家，不能当卖东西的人。所以他们又搬家。在他们第三个家，孟子学了怎么祭拜。妈妈感觉这个地方适合他们，告诉了孟子：我们找到了最后的家。在这儿妈妈让儿子从早到晚学习，他难怪成为有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在他们第三个家：在他们第三个家里，A location phrase with a house normally needs a location word such as 里 to mean “in their third home.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉了孟子：我们找到了最后的家：告诉孟子：我们找到了最后的家，A saying verb that introduces quoted speech takes the zero particle; do not add 了 to 告诉 here.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为：成为了，Use 了 after 成为 to show that becoming a philosopher was a completed change of state.
- Coherence (Conjunction): ，他难怪成为：，所以他后来成为，Use 所以 to show the cause-and-effect relationship: studying from morning to night led to his later success. 难怪 is used when the narrator reaches an explanatory conclusion, often with 原来.
- Expressiveness: After the place is described as suitable, add one concrete detail about the learning environment, such as “第三个家旁边有一所学校，环境很安静。”
- Expressiveness: Show the mother’s happiness when she finds the right home, such as “妈妈高兴地说：‘这儿很适合你学习。’”
- Expressiveness: Add Mengzi’s feeling about studying to make his change clearer, such as “孟子觉得看书很有意思，每天都认真学习。”

### Encouragement
- You used direct speech to show the mother’s hopes for Mengzi, and the contrast structure “不是……而是……” makes her message clear. You also used “从早到晚” effectively to show that Mengzi studied for a long time.
