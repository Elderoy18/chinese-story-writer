## Scene 1

> 很久以前，古代的人很不知道什么植物能吃，也什么植物不能吃。所以很多人常常吃不安全的植物，吃不安全的植物对你的身体有坏处，很麻烦的情况。但是一个人，神农，要解决这个问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很不知道：不知道，The mental-state verb phrase does not take the degree adverb 很 when it is negated; use 不知道 here.
- 吃不安全的植物对你的身体有坏处，很麻烦的情况：吃不安全的植物对身体有坏处，是一种很麻烦的情况，The original has two noun/predicate ideas without a linking verb. Add 是一种 to make the second part a complete description; remove 你的 because this is a general statement about people’s bodies.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 有坏处 -- “吃不安全的植物对身体有坏处”：有害，more concise and specific for something that harms the body.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一个人人，神农：有一个叫神农的人，This is the character’s first mention, so introduce him clearly with a full noun phrase rather than using an unclear apposition.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 但是：于是，The relation is sequence and result: people do not know which plants are edible, so a person wants to solve the problem. 于是 introduces this result; 但是 would require a contrast.
- Expressiveness: After the people eat harmful plants, add a physical result to make the danger vivid: “很多人肚子疼，还常常生病。”
- Expressiveness: When introducing 神农, add a short thought to show his motivation: “神农想：‘我一定要找出能吃的植物。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish the main problem of the scene: people do not know which plants are safe to eat. You also use a cause-and-effect idea to connect their lack of knowledge with the danger to their bodies.

## Scene 2

> 他又聪明又努力，旅行很远找到很多植物。比方说，灵芝和夹竹桃。他一吃各种各样的植物就知道能不能吃这些植物。然后，神农写他的主意。他喜欢植物的味道，特别是花。神农遇到了一个黄色的花，一吃就觉得不舒服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 旅行很远找到很多植物：到很远的地方旅行，找到了很多植物，A destination should be expressed with a place phrase after 到; 很远 alone cannot naturally modify 旅行 to mean travelling a long distance.
- 一个黄色的花：一朵黄色的花，朵 is the matching measure word for flowers.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 然后，神农写他的主意：然后，他写了他的主意，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence: the previous sentence already identifies the subject as 神农, and no competing character has appeared.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农遇到了一个黄色的花：他遇到了一朵黄色的花，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence: 神农 is still the clear subject from the preceding sentences.
- Coherence (Temporality): 然后，他写他的主意：然后，他写了他的主意，Use 了 for this completed action in a sequence introduced by 然后.
- Expressiveness: When the flower first appears, add a visual detail to make it memorable, such as “黄色的、像星星一样的花”.
- Expressiveness: After the character feels ill, describe the physical feeling more specifically, such as “肚子疼了起来，难受得不得了”.
- Expressiveness: Add a brief inner thought at the moment of pain, such as “我会死吗？”.

### Encouragement
- You clearly show that the character learns by tasting plants, and the pattern “一……就……” works well to show an immediate result.
- You also introduce 灵芝 and 夹竹桃 early, which gives the scene clear focus.

## Scene 3

> 发现了夹竹桃有毒。别人看到他，很快给他吃一个灵芝。神农一吃就觉得比较好。然后，他继续写他发现的信息。会告诉他的朋友哪一个植物能不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别人看到他：一个人看到他，别人 means people other than the speaker or a previously specified person; it does not introduce one unknown person in this scene.
- 写他发现的信息：写下他发现的信息，写下 means to record information or discoveries; 写 cannot directly take the discovered information in this meaning.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 信息 -- “写下他发现的信息”：发现，this is more precise because it refers specifically to the things he learned from trying the plants.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 很快给他吃一个灵芝：很快给他吃了一个灵芝，this is a completed action, so it needs 了 after the verb 吃.
- Coherence (Temporality): 觉得比较好：觉得好多了，觉得 is a mental-state verb and takes the zero particle; 了 belongs after the adjective phrase to show that his condition changed for the better.
- Expressiveness: After the medicine helps, add one physical detail to show the change, such as: 神农的肚子慢慢不疼了。
- Expressiveness: Give the helpful person a short line of speech when offering the medicine, such as: “你快吃这个灵芝！”

### Encouragement
- You use the 一……就…… pattern well to show that the improvement happened immediately after Shennong ate the medicine. You also keep the scene focused on Shennong’s discoveries and his wish to share useful knowledge with his friends.
