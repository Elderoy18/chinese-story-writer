## Scene 1

> 神农和他的家人住在古代，所以不知道什么植物能吃。因为他的家人常常生病了，所以神农和他的哥哥一起去旅行找可以吃的植物。他们去很多不一样的地方，比方说高山、山谷和沙漠。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 住在古代：生活在古代，住 is used for living at a physical place, while an historical period needs a verb such as 生活.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不一样 -- “很多不一样的地方”：各不相同的，more clearly shows that the places differ from one another；地形不同的，specifies that the differences are in the kinds of land described next.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：一个叫神农的人，at first mention, introduce a character with a full noun phrase that gives both the name and who the person is.
- Coherence (Temporality): 常常生病了：常常生病，use the zero particle for a habitual action; 了 does not follow a routine marked by 常常.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们去很多不一样的地方：他们去了很多不一样的地方，use 了 for the completed action of visiting many places in this past narrative.
- Expressiveness: At the list of places, add one visual detail to make the journey easier to imagine, for example: “高山又高又陡，山谷里很安静。”
- Expressiveness: After the family’s illness, add Shennong’s feeling or motivation, for example: “神农看见家人常常生病，心里很难过。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech before the journey to show the brothers’ decision, for example: “哥哥说：‘我们去找能吃的植物吧！’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly explain the reason for the journey, and your list of different places gives the scene a good sense of a long trip.

## Scene 2

> 他们在很漂亮的河找夹竹桃。神农问他的哥哥：“我应该吃这种植物吗？”哥哥说：“这就要看你想要安全还是接近知道了。“神农一吃夹竹桃就生病了。哥哥说：“太麻烦了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在很漂亮的河找夹竹桃：在很漂亮的河边找夹竹桃，A location phrase with a river needs a place word such as 河边 or 河里; 河 by itself does not clearly state where the searching happens.
- 接近知道：想知道，接近 means “come close to” and needs a concrete target; it cannot be used before the mental-state verb 知道. The corrected phrase also makes the choices after 这就要看 a valid A-or-B question.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 麻烦 -- “太麻烦了”：严重，makes the problem of becoming ill more specific and shows that it has serious consequences；令人担忧，focuses on the brother’s worry about Shen Nong’s condition.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the search by the river, add one visual detail about the plant, such as “河边开着黄色的花，像星星一样，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: When Shen Nong becomes ill, show a physical feeling instead of only stating the event: “他的肚子疼了起来，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Give the brother a more specific worried response, such as “哥哥着急地说：‘你怎么样？肚子很疼吗？’”

### Encouragement
- You used a direct question from Shen Nong, which makes the scene feel active. You also used the pattern 一……就…… well to show that he became ill immediately after eating the plant.

## Scene 3

> 夹竹桃是毒的，我想知道灵芝会帮他。”哥哥给神农灵芝。神农吃完了以后开始痊愈了。神农说：“谢谢哥哥，你帮得真好。灵芝我们可以吃，特别是如果我们已经吃了夹竹桃。“

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 夹竹桃是毒的：夹竹桃有毒，毒 is a noun; use 有毒 to describe the property that a plant contains poison.
- 开始痊愈了：开始好起来了，痊愈 means fully recovering, so it does not fit naturally after 开始; 好起来 describes the beginning of recovery.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农吃完了以后开始痊愈了：他吃完了以后开始好起来了，use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the same character was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农说：他说，use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence of the same character.
- Coherence (Temporality): 哥哥给神农灵芝：哥哥给了神农灵芝，use 了 after 给 to mark the completed action of giving the medicine.
- Expressiveness: After the recovery, add one physical detail to show that the medicine is working, for example: 神农觉得肚子没有那么疼了。
- Expressiveness: Make the thanks more emotional with a short line of speech, for example: 哥哥，谢谢你救了我！

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the sequence of the medicine being given, eaten, and then helping Shennong recover. The final speech also gives the scene a warm interaction between the two brothers.
