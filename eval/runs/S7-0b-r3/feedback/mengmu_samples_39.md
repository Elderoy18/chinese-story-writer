## Scene 1

> 古代的时候人觉得祭拜非常重要，孟子很小的时候，从早到晚一直祭拜，因为他妈妈告诉他祭拜很重要。虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜。他的同学们老是对他开玩笑了。孟子妈妈看了他的同学们对孟子不好的影响，所以孟子和孟子妈妈搬家了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候人：在古代，人们，`古代` already expresses a time period, so adding `的时候` is redundant; `人` is too generic for a concrete group, so use `人们`.
- 看了：看到了，`看` means “look at,” while `看到` means “see/perceive”; the mother is perceiving the influence.
- 他的同学们对孟子不好的影响：孟子的同学们对他的不良影响，use `A 对 B 的 + noun` to mark the relationship clearly; `不良影响` precisely means a harmful influence.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子很小的时候：有一个小男孩叫孟子；他很小的时候，follow the first-mention rule by introducing the character with a noun phrase before using his name or a pronoun.
- Coherence (Temporality): 同学们老是对他开玩笑了：同学们老是对他开玩笑，`老是` describes a repeated habit, so use the zero particle rather than completed-action `了`.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜：因为他每天只祭拜，所以没有朋友，these ideas show cause and effect, so use the paired connector `因为……所以……`; `虽然` requires a contrasting result clause.
- Expressiveness: After the classmates’ teasing, add Mengzi’s feeling to make the effect more vivid: `孟子觉得很难过，不明白同学们为什么笑他。`
- Expressiveness: At the point where his mother notices the influence, add her inner reaction or speech: `孟子的妈妈很担心，心想：“这儿不适合孟子长大。”`
- Expressiveness: Describe the classmates’ behaviour more specifically instead of only saying they joke with him: `同学们一边笑，一边学着他祭拜的样子。`

### Encouragement
- You clearly show a cause for Mengzi’s behaviour and a reason for the family’s move. You also use time expressions such as `从早到晚` effectively to show how often he worships.

## Scene 2

> 孟子才十一岁，成为一个哲学家。孟子不是祭拜，而是看政治文章，难怪他成为了哲学家。不过，看过很多政治文章对他害得，他有很多问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 才十一岁，成为一个哲学家：才十一岁，就成为一个哲学家，The pattern for an unexpectedly early result is 才 + number/age + 就 + verb phrase.
- 对他害得，他有很多问题：害得他有很多问题，The structure is 害得 + person + harmful result; the affected person follows 害得 directly.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 问题 -- “他有很多问题”：疑问，specifies that these are questions in his mind after reading, rather than general difficulties.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子不是祭拜：他不是祭拜，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the referent is clear from the immediately preceding sentence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为一个哲学家：成为了一个哲学家，Use 了 after a completed change of state: becoming a philosopher is presented as an achieved result.
- Expressiveness: At the point where he reads the articles, add a quiet action detail: “他每天安静地坐在桌子旁边看政治文章。”
- Expressiveness: After “他有很多问题,” show one specific thought: “他心想：‘为什么有的人很有钱，有的人很穷？’”
- Expressiveness: Add a reaction from his mother to show his progress: “孟子的妈妈看到他认真学习，觉得很高兴。”

### Encouragement
- You used the contrast pattern “不是……而是……” effectively to show that Mengzi changed what he did. You also connected his reading to his later achievement with “难怪,” which gives the scene a clear cause-and-result idea.

## Scene 3

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不是学金融，而是学政治：不要学金融，而要学政治，After a verb of advice, use an instruction such as “do not X, but rather Y.” The pattern “不是……而是……” corrects a mistaken statement and does not fit this advice.
- 成为高薪：获得高薪，The verb “become” needs an identity or role as its complement, while “high salary” is payment that a person receives.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 问问题 -- “他一直问问题”：不断提出问题，emphasizes that he keeps raising questions one after another.
- 厉害 -- “他教学生很厉害”：很擅长教学生，states more specifically that he has strong teaching ability.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子长大：他长大，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence: the previous sentence already identifies the character as 孟子.
- Coherence (Temporality): 长大：长大了，Use 了 after this change-of-state verb to show that growing up has happened.
- Coherence (Reference): 你：人们，Use a full noun phrase when the referent is not an introduced character; “you” has no clear listener in this narration.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子成为教授：他成为教授，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence because the previous sentence is still about 孟子 and no competing character has been introduced.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为教授：成为了教授，Use 了 for the completed change of role.
- Expressiveness: After he grows up, add an inner thought to show how his ability makes him feel, for example: “他很高兴，因为终于能回答以前解决不了的问题了。”
- Expressiveness: Make his advice sound more like a real interaction by adding a short line of speech, for example: “他对人们说：‘学政治能帮助你们了解社会。’”
- Expressiveness: Give one concrete teaching detail after he becomes a professor, for example: “上课的时候，他常常耐心地回答学生的问题。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show a change from being unable to solve problems to becoming a professor, which gives this scene a strong sense of progress.
- You also use the contrast pattern to show the difference between studying finance and studying politics.
