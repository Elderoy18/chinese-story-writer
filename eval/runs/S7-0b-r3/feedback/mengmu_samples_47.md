- Coherence (Temporality): 常常搬了家：常常搬家，use the zero particle for a habitual action; “常常” describes a routine, so it does not take completed-action “了”.
- Expressiveness: After “住在哪儿”, add a concrete detail about the surroundings, such as “墓地旁边常常有人哭”, to show why the place matters.
- Expressiveness: At “她望子成龙”, add Meng’s mother’s inner thought or speech, such as “我要给孟子找一个更适合学习的地方。”, to show her motivation more vividly.
