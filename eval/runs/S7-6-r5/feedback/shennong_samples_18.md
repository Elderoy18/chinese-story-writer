## Scene 1

> 神农和他的家人住在古代，所以不知道什么植物能吃。因为他的家人常常生病了，所以神农和他的哥哥一起去旅行找可以吃的植物。他们去很多不一样的地方，比方说高山、山谷和沙漠。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 住在古代：生活在古代，住在 usually takes a place as its complement, while 生活在 can describe living in a historical period.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 找 -- “找可以吃的植物”：寻找, emphasizes a purposeful, ongoing search；探寻, highlights exploration to discover something unknown

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：有一位叫神农的人，This is the character’s first mention, so introduce him with a full noun phrase rather than assuming the reader already knows who he is.
- Coherence (Temporality): 常常生病了：常常生病，A habitual action or state with 常常 uses the zero particle; 了 is for a completed action or change of state.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们去很多不一样的地方：他们去了很多不一样的地方，The visits to these places are completed events in the narrative, so the verb needs 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 所以不知道：不知道，Living in an ancient period establishes the time setting; it does not by itself express a cause-and-effect relationship, so 所以 is not needed.
- Expressiveness: After the family’s illness, add Shen Nong’s concern to show his motivation, such as “神农很担心家人的身体”.
- Expressiveness: When listing the places, add one visual detail to make the journey easier to imagine, such as “高山上长着各种各样的植物”.
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech before the journey to show determination, such as “我们一定要找到能治病的植物！”

### Encouragement
You clearly connect the family’s illness with Shen Nong’s decision to search for plants, and your list of mountains, valleys, and deserts gives the journey a good sense of range.
