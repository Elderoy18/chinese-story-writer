## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子。他们住在墓地的旁边，所以孟子老是祭拜。孟子不是祭拜，就是跟朋友聊天。他很喜欢他的城市，但是城市有才两个学校，都很小。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，Both expressions refer to a time period, so using them together is redundant.
- 城市有才：城市里才有，In an existential sentence, the location comes before the verb, and 才 should directly modify 有.
- 两个学校：两所学校，Schools use the measure word 所, not 个.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 小 -- “都很小”：规模都不大，specifies that the schools are small in scale rather than only physically small.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子：有一个叫孟子的男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国，Introduce the main character with his name at first mention, rather than naming him only after an unclear pronoun.
- Coherence (Reference): 他很喜欢他的城市：他很喜欢自己的城市，When the city belongs to the same person as the subject, 自己的 makes the possessor clear and avoids a possible reading that it belongs to another male person.
- Expressiveness: After “孟子老是祭拜,” add what he observes at the cemetery and why the action interests him, for example: “他每天看到很多人来祭拜，觉得很有趣。”
- Expressiveness: At “孟子不是祭拜，就是跟朋友聊天,” show that he is imitating others rather than seriously taking part, for example: “他一边笑，一边学别人祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: Add the mother’s reaction before the move to the city, for example: “孟母很担心，常常要跟来祭拜的人道歉。”

### Encouragement
You used the connectors 所以 and 但是 effectively to show cause and contrast. The scene also clearly introduces the cemetery setting and then moves naturally to the city setting.

## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈不是不喜欢他们的城市，而是想孟子有很好的教育。他们搬家到有很多学校的城市。他们从早到晚走路，孟子一直抱怨。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 想孟子有很好的教育：想让孟子受到很好的教育，想 needs a verb phrase or a full clause after it; 想让……受到…… clearly expresses that the mother wants him to receive an education.
- 搬家到：搬到，搬家 is a verb-object phrase for changing residence; use 搬到 + place to state the destination.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很好的 -- “想孟子有很好的教育”：优质的，specifies that the education is of a high standard.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们搬到有很多学校的城市：他们搬到了有很多学校的城市，this move is a completed event in the narrative, so the verb needs 了.
- Expressiveness: After the walking sentence, add a physical detail to show Meng子的 tiredness, such as “孟子累得满头大汗，脚也很疼。”
- Expressiveness: Show the complaint through quoted speech, such as “孟子皱着眉头说：‘妈妈，我们还要走多久？’”
- Expressiveness: You could briefly show the mother’s reason or feeling during the move, such as “妈妈虽然很累，但是想到孟子能上好学校，就继续往前走。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the mother’s motivation for moving and connect it to Meng子的 education.
- The detail that 孟子 keeps complaining gives the scene an understandable character reaction.

## Scene 3

> 他难怪抱怨，他想他的朋友，因为他不要离开他的城市。他的妈妈找到孟子一个老师。跟老师孟子学最重要的文学，所以他成为哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 找到孟子一个老师：给孟子找到一个老师，The person who receives the teacher must be introduced with 给; the original order does not mark 孟子 as the recipient.
- 跟老师孟子学最重要的文学：孟子跟老师学最重要的文学，The learner, 孟子, needs to come before the phrase meaning “with the teacher”; the original order can wrongly make 孟子 sound like the teacher’s name.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 想 -- “他想他的朋友”：想念，specifies that he misses friends who are no longer nearby；挂念，adds the sense that he keeps worrying about or thinking of them.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他的朋友：自己的朋友，The subject and the possessor are both 孟子, so 自己的 makes the relationship clear.
- Coherence (Reference): 他的城市：自己的城市，The subject is referring to the city that belongs to or is familiar to himself, so 自己的 avoids an unclear possessive reference.
- Coherence (Temporality): 找到：找到了，Finding the teacher is a completed event in this past narrative, so the verb needs 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为：成为了，Becoming a philosopher is presented as a completed change of state, so use 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他难怪抱怨，他想他的朋友：难怪他抱怨，原来他想他的朋友，Use the paired explanation pattern 難怪……，原来…… to show that the later information explains the complaint.
- Expressiveness: At the point where he misses his friends, add an emotion to show how the move affects him, for example: “孟子觉得很难过。”
- Expressiveness: When the mother finds a teacher, add a short line of speech to make her goal clearer, for example: “孟子的妈妈说：‘你要认真学习。’”
- Expressiveness: Add one concrete learning activity after he meets the teacher, for example: “孟子每天跟老师读书、写字。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the connection between Mengzi’s unhappiness about moving and his later education. The final result also gives the scene a clear direction.
