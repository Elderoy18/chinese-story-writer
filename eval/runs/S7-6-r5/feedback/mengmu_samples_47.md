## Scene 1

> 这个男孩子叫孟子，孟子是古代的人。孟子的妈妈觉得住在哪儿对童年很重要，所以他们常常搬了家。她望子成龙，所以孟子的妈妈安排他的生活。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- Nothing to flag.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 生活 -- “安排他的生活”：日常生活，specifies that the mother organizes his everyday routines and activities.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 这个男孩子：一个男孩子，This is a first mention, so an indefinite noun phrase introduces the boy more naturally; “这个” normally points to someone already known to the reader.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子是古代的人：他是古代的人，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence after the boy has just been named.
- Coherence (Reference): 所以孟子的妈妈安排他的生活：所以她安排他的生活，Use a pronoun when the mother was the subject in the immediately preceding clause and no other female character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Temporality): 常常搬了家：常常搬家，A habitual action uses the zero particle rather than completed-action 了.
- Expressiveness: After the mother’s hope for her son, add a short inner thought to show her motivation, such as: 孟母心想：“我一定要给孟子找一个适合学习的地方。”
- Expressiveness: At “常常搬家,” add a small feeling detail to show that moving was difficult for the child, such as: 每次搬家，孟子都觉得很累，也有一点舍不得。

### Encouragement
You used a clear cause-and-effect connection to explain why the family moved. The idiom for a parent hoping for a child’s success is also a strong and fitting choice.

## Scene 2

> 因为他们常常搬了家，中国人说，孟母三迁。孟子没有爸爸，所以，他的妈妈更重要。从这儿到那儿，妈妈教孟子很多技能。先她让孟子跟朋友玩儿，然后教他怎么买又便宜又好质量的东西。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 常常搬了家：常常搬家，A habitual action takes no completed-action marker.
- 先她让：她先让，The subject comes before the time-order adverbial.
- 好质量的：质量好的，To describe an item's quality, use the noun phrase with the adjective after the noun: “quality is good,” then add the modifier marker.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 妈妈：她，The mother was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other female character competes as the referent, so use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 然后教他：然后教了他，In a completed series introduced by a first-then sequence, only the last verb takes the completed-action marker.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 中国人说：所以中国人说，The cause introduced with “because” needs “so” before the result clause.
- Expressiveness: After the sentence about the mother being important, add a short thought to show her motivation, such as “孟母心想：‘我要好好教育孟子。’”
- Expressiveness: In the final sentence, show Mengzi’s feeling while he plays, such as “孟子高兴地跟朋友跑来跑去。”
- Expressiveness: Add a line of speech when the mother teaches him to shop, such as “妈妈说：‘买东西要看价钱，也要看质量。’”

### Encouragement
You give a clear reason for the mother’s importance and use a first-then sequence to organize her teaching. The examples of playing with friends and buying inexpensive, good-quality goods make the scene concrete.
