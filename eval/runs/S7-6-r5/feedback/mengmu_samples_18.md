## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个男孩名字叫孟子。孟子和孟子的妈妈两个人住在很穷的农村。他们住的地方旁边是个墓地，墓地老是有很多人祭拜。因为人们在祭拜，他们一直在哭。孟子和孟子的妈妈住在这里才一个星期就觉得受不了了，而且害得孟子的学习。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，Ancient times is already a time expression, so adding this phrase makes the time reference redundant.
- 名字叫：叫，Use the verb directly to introduce a person’s name in this noun phrase.
- 害得孟子的学习：这里的环境影响了孟子的学习，This use of the harmful-result structure is incomplete: it needs a person followed by a harmful result clause. The verb shown can take the noun phrase about study directly.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 住的地方 -- “他们住的地方旁边是个墓地”：家，identifies the family’s residence more directly and makes the location sound more natural in this scene.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子的妈妈两个人：孟子和他的妈妈两个人，After the name has just been given, use a pronoun for the close re-occurrence; the possessor is clear here.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子的妈妈住在这里：孟子和他的妈妈住在这里，Use a pronoun for the repeated possessor when the referent remains clear.
- Coherence (Temporality): 住在这里才一个星期：才在这里住了一个星期，The one-week stay is completed before the later reaction, so this duration uses completed-action 了; this also forms the natural 才……就…… pattern.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为人们在祭拜，他们一直在哭：因为人们在祭拜，所以他们一直在哭，Use the paired connector to show that the crying is the result of the worship activity.
- Expressiveness: At the description of the cemetery, add a sensory detail about what Mengzi hears, such as: “墓地旁边常常传来哭声。”
- Expressiveness: At the final sentence, show the mother’s worry with an action or direct speech, such as: “孟母皱着眉头说：‘这里太吵了，孟子没法安心学习。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly establish the family, their rural home, and the nearby cemetery. You also use ongoing-action forms well when describing the people crying during the worship activity.

## Scene 2

> 孟子的妈妈觉得孟子取得良好的教育是很重要的，所以孟子的妈妈认为这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好，就打算跟孟子搬家。孟子的妈妈带孟子不是搬到城市，而是搬到了孟子老师的家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 取得良好的教育：接受良好的教育，接受教育 is the standard verb–object collocation; 取得 is used for achievements or results, not for receiving education.
- 带孟子不是搬到城市：带孟子搬家，不是搬到城市，Add 搬家 so that both parts of the paired contrast describe where the move was made.
- 孟子老师的家：孟子的老师的家，The first 的 is needed to show that the teacher belongs to 孟子; without it, the phrase normally means a teacher named 孟子.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 地方 -- “这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好”：环境，specifies the surrounding living and learning conditions that affect the child’s education.
- 不太好 -- “对儿子的教育不太好”：不太合适，more precisely shows that this setting does not suit the child’s education while keeping the mild meaning.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子的妈妈认为：她认为，After 孟子的妈妈 has just been named and no other female character competes as the referent, use a pronoun for close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子的妈妈带孟子搬家：她带孟子搬家，The mother is still the clear subject from the previous sentence, so a pronoun makes the connection smoother.
- Expressiveness: At the point where the mother judges the place unsuitable, add one concrete observation showing why she worries: “她看到孟子又跟着别人祭拜，心里很着急。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of the mother’s inner speech when she decides to move: “这儿不适合孟子学习，我们搬家吧。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the mother’s reason for moving, linking her concern about education to her decision. You also tried to use the paired contrast pattern with 不是……而是……, which is a useful structure for telling a story.

## Scene 3

> 孟子跟他的老师从早到晚学习，难怪他的成绩很快就进步了很多。孟子学习的时候，孟子的妈妈不是待在家里就是编织衣服赚钱。以后孟子成为了中国有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 以后孟子成为了中国有名的哲学家：后来孟子成为了中国有名的哲学家，At the beginning of a new narrative sentence, use 后来 to mean “afterwards”; 以后 normally follows a stated time or event to mean “after that.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 成绩 -- “他的成绩很快就进步了很多”：学习成绩，specifies that these are his results in school rather than another kind of achievement.

### Coherence & Expressiveness

- Expressiveness: In the sentence about his mother, add her feeling about his change, such as “孟子的妈妈看到儿子认真学习，心里很高兴。” This gives her a clearer emotional response.

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the connection between sustained study and improved results. The paired alternative structure in the second sentence is also used effectively to describe the mother’s daily life.
