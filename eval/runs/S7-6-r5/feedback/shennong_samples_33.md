## Scene 1

> 古代时候，人不知道他们能吃什么植物，常常一吃就死。但是不知道为什么，没有办法。所以有一天，神农出去试一试很多种植物，告诉人什么植物是安全什么是危险。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 人：人们，This refers to a concrete group of people, so the plural collective noun is needed rather than the generic noun.
- 他们：自己，The people who do not know are also the people who may eat the plants, so the reflexive possessive makes this same-subject relationship clear.
- 人：人们，This again refers to the group receiving Shennong’s information, so use the collective noun.
- 什么植物是安全什么是危险：什么植物是安全的，什么植物是危险的，When an adjective classifies a noun after 是, use 的 to complete the descriptive phrase; repeat the noun for a parallel comparison.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 试一试 -- “神农出去试一试很多种植物”：尝试，emphasizes systematically trying different plants to learn about them；品尝，emphasizes tasting the plants, which fits this particular story situation.
- 很多种 -- “试一试很多种植物”：各种各样的，highlights the wide variety of plants he examined.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：一个叫神农的人，This is the character’s first mention, so introduce him with both his name and a full noun phrase.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉：告诉了，This is the final completed action in a series of past narrative actions, so the last verb takes 了.
- Expressiveness: At the point where people die, add a physical reaction to make the danger vivid, for example: “很多人吃了有毒的植物以后，肚子疼得厉害。”
- Expressiveness: When Shennong begins his work, add his purpose as an inner thought or a short quotation, for example: “神农想：‘我一定要找出能吃的植物。’”
- Expressiveness: Describe how he tests the plants to show his careful work, for example: “他每找到一种植物，就先看一看，再尝一点儿。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish the problem at the beginning and then introduce Shennong as someone who will help solve it. The use of the pattern 一吃就 also makes the dangerous result immediate and easy to understand.

## Scene 2

> 发现有的植物对身体非常好，特别是茶，让肚子感觉又暖和又清楚。他告诉朋友：“你吃哪种植物，这就要看你的口味。比方说，你爱吃甜的，可能喜欢糖。”他最喜欢吃花，除了夹竹桃以外，什么都花想吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 清楚：舒服，This word describes clarity of thought or explanation, not a comfortable bodily feeling; the revised word naturally describes how the stomach feels after drinking tea.
- 什么都花想吃：什么花都想吃，The indefinite object phrase must be formed as “什么花” and placed before “都”; “都” then emphasizes that every kind is included.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好 -- “有的植物对身体非常好”：很有益，states more precisely that the plants benefit the body；很有好处，clearly emphasizes a health benefit.
- 甜的 -- “你爱吃甜的”：甜食，specifies sweet foods rather than using a general nominalized adjective.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他：神农，Because a friend has just been introduced and the quotation includes “你,” the pronoun can be unclear; repeat the name to identify the person who likes eating flowers.
- Coherence (Temporality): 这就要看你的口味：这就要看你的口味了，The final “了” is part of the fixed “这就要看……了” structure, which means that the answer depends on a condition.
- Expressiveness: After the tea discovery, add a sensory detail such as “他喝了一口茶，觉得全身暖暖的。” This shows the effect instead of only stating that it is good for the body.
- Expressiveness: Give the character a brief thought after the discovery, such as “神农想：这种植物也许能帮助生病的人。” This adds his motivation and curiosity.

### Encouragement
You clearly connect the plant discovery to a concrete example, tea, and you use direct speech to show the character explaining his idea. The contrast with “除了夹竹桃以外” also makes the last sentence easy to understand.

## Scene 3

> 他发现这个口味，一百岁时候，不小心吃了毒的花。一吃就开始死。但是生病时候告诉人什么植物是药，什么植物是毒。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一百岁时候：一百岁的时候，Multi-syllable age expressions need 的 before 时候.
- 毒的花：有毒的花，Use 有毒的 to describe a poisonous plant; 毒的 is not the normal modifier here.
- 开始死：快要死了，For one person, 死 is a change-of-state verb and does not naturally follow 开始 as an ongoing action; this expression shows that death was imminent.
- 人：人们，Use 人们 for a concrete group of people being told this information; 人 is a generic noun.
- 什么植物是毒：什么植物有毒，Use 有毒 to describe that a plant contains poison; 是毒 does not fit this predicate pattern.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不小心 -- “不小心吃了毒的花”：误食了，more precisely describes accidentally eating something that should not have been eaten.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 这个口味：一种味道，This is the first mention of this particular taste, so introduce it with an indefinite noun phrase rather than a demonstrative that has no clear antecedent.
- Coherence (Temporality): 发现：发现了，This discovery is a completed event in the narrative, so it needs 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉：告诉了，Telling people the information is a completed action in this past event sequence, so it needs 了.
- Expressiveness: After the accidental eating, add a physical reaction such as “他肚子疼了起来，难受得不得了。” This makes the danger more vivid.
- Expressiveness: Describe the flower before it is eaten, for example: “黄色的花像星星一样，又大又漂亮。” This helps the reader picture why it attracted him.
- Expressiveness: Add an anxious thought at the moment he becomes ill: “我会死吗？” This shows the character’s fear directly.

### Encouragement
You clearly show a cause-and-effect sequence with the 一…就… pattern, which makes the dangerous event easy to follow. You also include the useful contrast between plants that are medicine and plants that are poisonous.
