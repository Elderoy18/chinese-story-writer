## Scene 1

> 很久以前，古代的人很不知道什么植物能吃，也什么植物不能吃。所以很多人常常吃不安全的植物，吃不安全的植物对你的身体有坏处，很麻烦的情况。但是一个人，神农，要解决这个问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很不知道：不知道，A degree adverb cannot directly modify the negative mental-state verb phrase here; use the plain mental-state verb to state that people lacked this knowledge.
- 也什么植物不能吃：什么植物不能吃，the parallel question should list the two categories directly; 也 cannot be followed by a noun phrase or clause in this structure.
- 很麻烦的情况：是一件很麻烦的事，when identifying the whole preceding action as a noun phrase, use 是 before the noun phrase.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不安全 -- “吃不安全的植物”：有毒的，specifies that the plants harm people because they contain poison
- 坏处 -- “对你的身体有坏处”：危害，states more precisely that the plants cause harm to the body

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一个人，神农：有一个叫神农的人，On a character’s first mention, introduce the person naturally with both the name and a full noun phrase.
- Expressiveness: After the sentence about unsafe plants, add a concrete physical consequence, such as “很多人吃了以后，常常肚子疼。”
- Expressiveness: At the introduction of Shen Nong, show his determination through an inner thought, such as “神农想：我一定要找出能吃的植物。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish the main problem first: people do not know which plants are safe to eat. You also introduce Shen Nong’s goal, which gives this scene a clear direction.

## Scene 2

> 他又聪明又努力，旅行很远找到很多植物。比方说，灵芝和夹竹桃。他一吃各种各样的植物就知道能不能吃这些植物。然后，神农写他的主意。他喜欢植物的味道，特别是花。神农遇到了一个黄色的花，一吃就觉得不舒服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 旅行很远找到很多植物：到很远的地方去旅行，找到了很多植物，A distance cannot directly modify the verb phrase this way; use a destination phrase to show where the travel went, and use 了 for the completed finding action.
- 一个黄色的花：一朵黄色的花，朵 is the appropriate measure word for a flower.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 努力 -- “他又聪明又努力”：勤奋，emphasizes steady, sustained hard work.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 然后，神农写他的主意：然后，他写他的主意，After the recent reference to him as 他, use the pronoun because no other character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农遇到了：他遇到了，Use a pronoun for this close re-occurrence because the previous sentence already refers to 神农 as 他.
- Coherence (Temporality): 写他的主意：写下了他的主意，This is a completed action in the sequence introduced by 然后, so it needs 了; 写下 also shows the result that the idea was recorded.
- Expressiveness: When he finds the flower, add its appearance to make the setting more vivid: “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花。”
- Expressiveness: After he eats the flower, show a more specific physical reaction: “他一吃，肚子就疼了起来。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought to show his fear: “他想：我会不会生病？”

### Encouragement
- You used the pattern “一……就……” well to show an immediate result after eating a plant. You also introduced specific plants such as 灵芝 and 夹竹桃, which makes the scene clearer.

## Scene 3

> 发现了夹竹桃有毒。别人看到他，很快给他吃一个灵芝。神农一吃就觉得比较好。然后，他继续写他发现的信息。会告诉他的朋友哪一个植物能不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别人：有人，别人 distinguishes other people from a person or group already mentioned; here the story introduces an unspecified person who notices him.
- 写他发现的信息：写下他发现的信息，写下 means to record information or discoveries; 写 alone does not clearly express recording them.
- 哪一个植物能不能吃：哪些植物能吃，哪些不能吃，do not combine the interrogative phrase 哪一个 with the A-not-A question form 能不能. Use parallel alternatives to show which plants are edible and which are not.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 比较好 -- “神农一吃就觉得比较好”：好一些，states more clearly that his physical condition improved by a small amount after eating the medicine.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 给他吃一个灵芝：给他吃了一个灵芝，these are two completed actions in a sequence, so the final verb takes 了 to mark the completed giving-and-eating event.
- Expressiveness: After the medicine is given, add a physical detail showing the recovery, such as “他吃了以后，肚子慢慢不疼了。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where the helper sees him, add the helper’s urgent reaction to make the moment more vivid, such as “那个人着急地说：‘快吃这个！’”
- Expressiveness: When he records his discovery, specify the two plants and their different effects, such as “他写下：‘夹竹桃有毒，灵芝可以治肚子疼。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly keep 神农 as the focus throughout the scene, and the use of “一吃就” effectively shows that the medicine works quickly.
