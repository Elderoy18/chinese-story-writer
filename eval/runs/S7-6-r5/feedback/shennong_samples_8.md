## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个人叫神农。一天，神农正在路上走，一看很多人正在吃一些植物。一些人一吃植物就生病了，神农想帮别人。所以想知道哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，The expression repeats the time meaning because 古代 already names a historical period and 的时候 also means “the time of.”
- 一看很多人正在吃一些植物：看到很多人正在吃一些植物，Here the verb means that Shennong perceived the people, so use the resultative form 看到 rather than 看; delete 一 because there is no following 就-clause.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 想知道 -- “想知道哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃”：想弄清，emphasizes clearly distinguishing edible plants from inedible ones in this context.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the people become ill, add Shennong’s immediate feeling to show why he wants to help. Example: “神农很担心。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where he decides to learn about the plants, add a short thought or line of speech. Example: “我一定要弄清楚！”

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduce Shennong at the beginning, so the reader knows who the main character is.
- You also use progressive actions effectively to show what is happening on the road and what the people are doing.

## Scene 2

> 然后，神农开始尝很多植物，比方说他吃过生姜、芥兰和菠菜。一些植物吃真好，特别是黄瓜。但是，一些植物毒得很，特别是夹竹桃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一些植物吃真好：一些植物很好吃，With a plant as the subject, use the adjective predicate 好吃 to describe its taste; 吃 cannot directly be followed by 真好 in this structure.
- 一些植物毒得很：一些植物有毒，毒 is a noun and cannot be intensified with 得很; use 有毒 to describe a poisonous plant.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After mentioning the tasty plants, add one sensory detail, such as “黄瓜又脆又清凉。” This makes the contrast with the dangerous plants more vivid.
- Expressiveness: After introducing the poisonous flower, show the immediate consequence with “神农一吃夹竹桃，肚子就疼了起来。” This gives the danger a concrete effect.
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought after he tries the plants, such as “神农想：哪些植物能做药呢？” This shows his purpose and inner motivation.

### Encouragement
You clearly contrast plants that are good to eat with plants that are dangerous, and the examples of specific vegetables and flowers make the scene easy to picture.

## Scene 3

> 一个人对神农说，麻烦我们可以吃什么植物？神农说，这就要看你想不想生病了。以后，神农告诉别人一些植物能吃，一些植物不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 麻烦我们可以吃什么植物？：请问，我们可以吃什么植物？，麻烦 is used for making a request that may inconvenience someone, not for introducing an information question. 请问 politely introduces a question.
- 以后，神农告诉别人一些植物能吃，一些植物不能吃。：后来，神农告诉别人一些植物能吃，一些植物不能吃。，When a new sentence means “afterwards,” use 后来; 以后 normally refers to a time after a stated event or time.
- 告诉别人：告诉人们，别人 distinguishes other people from a previously mentioned person or group; here the general group of listeners is meant, so 人们 is appropriate.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 说 -- “一个人对神农说”：问，specifies that the person is asking a question rather than simply speaking.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the question, add a short explanation in Shen Nong’s reply to make his knowledge more vivid, for example: “有些植物有毒，吃了会生病。”
- Expressiveness: Name one safe and one dangerous plant in the reply, for example: “黄瓜可以吃，可是夹竹桃不能吃。”
- Expressiveness: Add the questioner’s reaction after hearing the warning, for example: “那个人听了以后，有点儿害怕。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show a question-and-answer exchange between the person and Shen Nong. You also express the important contrast between plants that can be eaten and plants that cannot.
