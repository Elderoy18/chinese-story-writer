## Scene 1

> 很久以前，有一个老男人叫神农。神农在古代每一天看到了别的小镇人生病还是不舒服，所以他决定开始找新的药。他下一天开始爬当地的山脉，找了各种各样的植物。神农试一试每个植物，知道有什么好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别的：其他，The word refers to all alternatives to one already identified item; no particular town has been established here, so this word fits the general meaning of other towns.
- 小镇人：小镇的人，The modifier needs to be linked to the noun with this particle: the people are from the small towns.
- 看到了：看到，This is a routine marked by 每一天, so use the zero aspect form rather than the completed-action marker.
- 还是：或者，Use this conjunction for alternatives in a statement; the original conjunction is normally used in questions or indirect questions.
- 找了：找到了，The intended meaning is that he found the plants. This verb means “look for,” while the corrected form means “find.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 爬 -- “开始爬当地的山脉”：攀登，emphasizes climbing up steep mountain slopes and makes the journey more vivid.
- 好处 -- “知道有什么好处”：药用价值，specifies the useful medical value of the plants in the context of looking for medicine.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农在古代每天看到其他小镇的人生病或者不舒服：他在古代每天看到其他小镇的人生病或者不舒服，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the character was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农试一试每个植物：他试一试每个植物，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence of the same character.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 所以：因为……，所以……，The first clause gives the cause of his decision, so use the paired cause-and-effect connector.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 神农试一试每个植物，知道有什么好处：神农试一试每个植物，就知道有什么好处，Add a connector to show that learning the benefits follows from testing the plants.
- Expressiveness: At the point where people are ill, add one concrete symptom to show why the problem matters, such as “很多人常常肚子疼”.
- Expressiveness: After the decision, add Shen Nong’s determined inner thought or speech, such as “我一定要找出能做药的植物”.
- Expressiveness: While describing the mountain journey, add movement and landscape detail, such as “他走啊走啊，爬过了很多高山”.

### Encouragement
- Your scene has a clear opening in time and introduces Shen Nong right away. You also show his motivation, his journey, and his method of learning in a logical order.

## Scene 2

> 比方说，他一次试一试有些植物的叶，吃得又新鲜又甜。煮这些叶以后，他一喝就知道他发现新的茶叶。虽然他找到了很多的新的植物，但是他的旅程有的时候特别是危险。比方说，他一吃灵芝就开始生病。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一次试一试有些植物的叶：尝了一些植物的叶，The time-measure phrase does not work before this reduplicated verb here; use a completed tasting action with a quantity object.
- 吃得又新鲜又甜：这些叶子吃起来又新鲜又甜，A complement with 得 describes how the subject performs an action, but 新鲜 describes the leaves themselves; use 吃起来 to describe their taste and texture.
- 特别是危险：特别危险，特别是 must introduce a singled-out example or clause, while this sentence needs 特别 to intensify the adjective.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 新 -- “新的茶叶”：未尝过的，specifies that this is tea he has not tasted before, rather than simply something new.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 知道他发现：知道发现，The second pronoun unnecessarily repeats the same subject within one sentence; use zero form when the referent remains clear.
- Coherence (Temporality): 发现新的茶叶：发现了新的茶叶，The discovery is a completed result that he realizes after drinking, so the verb needs 了.
- Expressiveness: After the leaves are boiled, add a sensory detail about the drink, such as “煮过以后，水里有一股淡淡的清香。”
- Expressiveness: At the dangerous part of the journey, add Shen Nong’s feeling and determination, such as “他心里有点害怕，但是还是继续找植物。”
- Expressiveness: You could make the illness more vivid by describing its immediate effect, such as “他一吃灵芝，肚子就开始疼了起来。”

### Encouragement
- You use both 虽然……但是…… and 一……就…… in appropriate sentence patterns, which helps show contrast and quick cause-and-effect events.
- The scene also connects the tea discovery to the journey’s danger, giving the events a clear narrative direction.

## Scene 3

> 一个爬山的男人正好看到神农的麻烦，然后给了他一些药。神农最终他的小镇回到，跟别的人分享他的发现。你认为神农的故事真的吗？这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 最终他的小镇回到：最终回到他的小镇，The adverbial word “最终” should come before the verb, while the destination follows “回到.”
- 你认为神农的故事真的吗？：你认为神农的故事是真的吗？，A yes-or-no question about whether a story is true needs the predicate structure “是……吗.”
- 这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处。：这就要看你相不相信中药对身体有好处了。，After “这就要看,” the following part must be a question form; “相不相信” is an A-not-A question form, and the pattern ends with “了.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 看到 -- “一个爬山的男人正好看到神农的麻烦”：发现，emphasizes that the man notices and recognizes that Shennong is in trouble rather than merely seeing him
- 药 -- “然后给了他一些药”：草药，specifies that the medicine is made from plants and fits the traditional-medicine context

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 回到他的小镇，跟别的人分享他的发现：回到他的小镇，跟别的人分享了他的发现，These are completed consecutive actions in the narrative, so only the last verb in the series takes “了.”
- Expressiveness: At “神农的麻烦,” add a concrete physical detail to show the problem: “神农肚子疼得厉害，躺在地上。”
- Expressiveness: Make the helper more vivid by describing his appearance and quick action: “一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人赶快拿出一些草药。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech after the medicine is given: “男人说：‘快吃这个，它能帮你。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduce the new man with a full noun phrase, and the actions of seeing Shennong and giving him medicine are easy to follow.
- Ending with a question is an engaging way to invite the reader to think about Shennong’s story.
