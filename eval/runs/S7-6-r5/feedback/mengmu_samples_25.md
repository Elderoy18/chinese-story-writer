## Scene 1

> 这是古代的故事。孟子小的时候，他的爸爸很早就去世了。从他的家到墓地很近，所以他每天老是从早到晚去墓地跟朋友们玩玩儿。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 从他的家到墓地很近：孟子的家离墓地很近，use 离 to describe that two places are close; 从……到…… normally marks a route or range rather than proximity.
- 从早到晚去墓地跟朋友们玩玩儿：从早到晚都在墓地跟朋友们玩儿，from-morning-to-evening duration should describe the sustained activity at the location; 玩玩儿 suggests a brief, casual activity and does not fit an all-day duration.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 玩玩儿 -- “跟朋友们玩玩儿”：嬉戏，emphasizes lively, carefree play with friends.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子小的时候：有一个小男孩叫孟子。孟子小的时候，this is the character’s first mention, so introduce his name and identity clearly before using him in the story.
- Coherence (Reference): 所以他每天：所以孟子每天，repeat the name because the previous sentence also mentions his father, so the pronoun can have two possible male referents.
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery is introduced, add a concrete detail about what Mengzi sees there, such as “孟子每天看到很多人来墓地祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: Add Mengzi’s feeling to show why he wants to play there, such as “他觉得他们的动作很好玩。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of dialogue with his friends, such as “孟子对朋友说：‘我们也来学他们吧！’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish an important background event with “去世了,” using 了 appropriately for a completed event.
- The connection between the home being close to the cemetery and Mengzi going there is easy to understand.

## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈觉得在墓地玩儿，害得，所以他们搬家。搬家对孟子有好的影响，他才五岁，可是每天不是写字就是帮妈妈的工作。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在墓地玩儿，害得：孟子在墓地玩儿会给她带来麻烦，害得 must be followed by the harmed person and a harmful result; the original leaves this causative structure unfinished.
- 帮妈妈的工作：帮妈妈做事，帮 cannot directly take the noun phrase 妈妈的工作 in this meaning; use a verb phrase after 帮 to state the action being helped with.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 影响 -- “搬家对孟子有好的影响”：好处，names the direct benefit to孟子 more clearly in this context.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 所以他们搬家：所以他们搬家了，this relocation is presented as a completed past event, so the final verb needs 了.
- Expressiveness: At the cemetery scene, add a concrete action to show what孟子 does there, such as “孟子和朋友一边笑，一边学别人祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: Show the mother’s worry with a short thought or quotation after the problem appears, such as “孟母很担心，心想：‘住在这儿会影响孟子。’”

### Encouragement
You used the contrast pattern with 才 and 可是 effectively to show that孟子 is young but already has a busy daily routine. The pattern 不是……就是…… is also a clear way to describe two regular activities.

## Scene 3

> 他很聪明的人，难怪他成为有名的人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他很聪明的人：他是一个很聪明的人，A noun phrase used as the predicate needs the copula 是; 一个 also supplies the appropriate classifier phrase before 人.
- 成为：成为了，This is a completed change of status in the narrative, so becoming a well-known person takes 了.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他：∅，Within the same sentence, the subject has already been established, so the later pronoun should be omitted and understood as a zero form.
- Expressiveness: At the end, specify what kind of well-known person he became, for example: “后来，他成为了一位有名的哲学家。”
- Expressiveness: Add a brief evaluative phrase to show the narrator’s view of his success, for example: “他成为了一位有名的哲学家，大家都很佩服他。”

### Encouragement
- You used 难怪 well to connect his intelligence with his later achievement. The sentence also gives the scene a clear concluding feeling.
