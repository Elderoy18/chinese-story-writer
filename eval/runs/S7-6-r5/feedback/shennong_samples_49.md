## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有很多人不知道什么植物能吃，什么植物不能吃。有很多人吃植物以后就吃坏肚子了，因为他们吃了毒。那个时候，有一个老人叫神农，他什么植物都觉得好吃，不怕生病，不怕毒。他希望别人也可以吃好吃的植物。可是他知道，如果他们还怕吃毒，不会吃他喜欢的植物。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，The time noun already names the historical period, so adding 的时候 is redundant.
- 吃了毒：吃了有毒的植物，毒 alone means poison; here the people become ill after eating plants, so the noun phrase should identify the plants as toxic.
- 怕吃毒：怕吃到有毒的植物，吃到 expresses the unwanted result of ending up eating something, and 有毒的植物 clearly names the dangerous plants rather than poison in general.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不知道 -- “有很多人不知道什么植物能吃，什么植物不能吃”：分不清，emphasizes that people cannot distinguish edible plants from inedible ones.
- 喜欢 -- “他喜欢的植物”：爱吃的，makes clear that these are plants he enjoys eating, matching the surrounding discussion of food.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the sentence about people becoming ill, add a physical detail such as “很多人捂着肚子，疼得直冒汗。” This makes the consequence more vivid.
- Expressiveness: After introducing 神农, add a short thought such as “神农想：我要找出能吃的植物，帮助大家。” This shows his motivation and care for others.
- Expressiveness: In the final sentence, add a worried reaction from other people, such as “他们说：‘要是吃错了怎么办？’” This makes their fear more concrete.

### Encouragement
You clearly introduce the problem of edible and dangerous plants, and the connection between eating harmful plants and stomach pain is easy to understand. You also use 神农’s wish to help others to give the scene a caring focus.

## Scene 2

> 所以，他上山下山找植物，吃了很多样子的植物。他是第一人，找到了茶。他把所有的植物写下来了。他一直都吃了，他连毒都能吃。可是他还没吃了一个叫夹竹桃的植物。他找到了那个像星星的植物，觉得很漂亮，马上就吃下了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很多样子的植物：很多种植物，样子 refers to an object’s appearance, while 种 is the measure word for different types of plants.
- 他是第一人，找到了茶：他是第一个找到茶的人，use 第一个 + verb phrase + 的人 to identify the first person who completed an action.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 找 -- “他上山下山找植物”：探寻，emphasizes exploring unknown plants in order to discover them；寻找，emphasizes a sustained search over many places.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 一直都吃了：一直都吃，habits or continuing states use the zero particle rather than 了, which marks a completed action.
- Coherence (Temporality): 还没吃了：还没吃过，after 没, do not use 了; 吃过 expresses that he has not yet had the experience of trying this plant.
- Expressiveness: At the discovery of tea, add what made it special: “他发现，茶放在水里很好喝，还可以做药。”
- Expressiveness: When describing the new plant, add a clearer visual detail: “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: Before he eats it, add his thought to show his confidence: “他想：这种植物看起来一定很好吃。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the sequence of Shennong’s exploration, from searching for plants to finding tea and then trying a new plant.
- Your use of 找到了 and 吃下了 effectively shows that these actions were completed.

## Scene 3

> 可是夹竹桃的毒很重，让他肚子很痛。他在地上躺着，觉得他快会死了。可是正好有别人找到他了，给了他一个像伞的植物，叫灵芝。他吃灵芝以后就觉得很好，不再生病了。他测量起来了，像星星的植物是毒，像伞的植物是药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 快会：快要，Use “快要” to express that an event is about to happen; “快会” does not form the standard imminent-event expression.
- 别人：一个人，“别人” distinguishes other people from a person already under discussion; here a new, unspecified individual is being introduced, so “一个人” is needed.
- 一个像伞的植物：一株像伞的植物，“株” is the appropriate measure word for an individual plant; “个” is too general in this context.
- 是毒：有毒，“毒” is a noun here, while “有毒” is the adjective phrase meaning poisonous.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很重 -- “夹竹桃的毒很重”：毒性很强，identifies specifically that the poison is powerful rather than describing physical weight.
- 痛 -- “让他肚子很痛”：疼得厉害，shows the degree of pain more vividly in this situation.
- 很好 -- “就觉得很好”：好多了，more precisely shows that he feels better after eating the medicine.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃灵芝以后：吃了灵芝以后，The eating is completed before the later feeling, so the completed action takes “了”.
- Expressiveness: At “他在地上躺着”, add a physical detail to show his suffering, such as “他疼得直冒冷汗。”
- Expressiveness: After “觉得他快要死了”, add his worried thought or speech, such as “他想：我会死吗？”
- Expressiveness: When the helper arrives, describe the person’s action more clearly, such as “那个人赶快拿起一株灵芝给他吃。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the cause of the stomach pain and the later recovery, so the events are easy to follow.
- You also use “躺着” well to show that the character’s pain continues for a period of time.
