## Scene 1

> 有一天孟子去了墓地玩儿，但是他妈妈觉得这是不合适的地方让孩子玩，所以她决定了他们会搬家找更好的地方。她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福，而且住在这儿教育不太好。因为她望子成龙，所以她会一直搬家才能找到质量好的教育。然后他们搬到了离城市更近的地方。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不合适的地方让孩子玩：不适合孩子玩的地方，use “适合” to say that a place suits a person or activity; this phrase means a place that is not suitable for children to play in.
- 住在这儿教育不太好：住在这儿，孩子受到的教育不太好，“住在这儿” needs a complete following clause; adding “孩子受到的” makes clear whose education is being discussed.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 更好 -- “找更好的地方”：更合适的地方，specifies that the new place is more suitable for the child rather than simply better in a general way
- 质量好 -- “质量好的教育”：优质的教育，expresses a reliably high standard of education more compactly and precisely

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 有一天孟子去了墓地玩儿：有一天，有一个小男孩叫孟子。他去了墓地玩儿，at the first mention, introduce the character with both a role and a name; after that close re-occurrence, use a pronoun.
- Coherence (Reference): 他们会搬家找更好的地方：搬家，找更好的地方，after “决定,” use the same subject in zero form and follow it directly with the verb phrase; do not repeat the subject or add “会.”
- Coherence (Temporality): 决定了：决定，“决定” is a mental-state verb, so it normally takes the zero particle rather than “了.”
- Coherence (Conjunction): 她会一直搬家才能找到质量好的教育：她得不断搬家，才能找到质量好的教育，“得……才能……” clearly links a necessary condition with its result; “会” does not express that necessary condition.
- Expressiveness: At the cemetery-play moment, add one concrete action showing what interested the child, such as “孟子看见有人来祭拜，就学着他们的样子。”
- Expressiveness: At the mother’s worry, add a short thought or quotation to show her feelings, such as “孟母着急地想：‘这儿会影响孩子的成长，我得搬家。’”
- Expressiveness: After the move, add one sensory detail about the new neighbourhood, such as “新家附近有很多商人，从早到晚都很热闹。”

### Encouragement
You used “望子成龙” effectively to show the mother’s motivation, and the move with “搬到了” clearly marks a completed change of place. The cause-and-effect structure with “因为……所以……” also helps the reader follow her reason for moving.

## Scene 2

> 他们住才一月就又搬家。古代的时候，一般人不喜欢卖东西的人。但是孟子老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿，害得妈妈着急得很，因为她恐怕儿子会成为跟他们很像。她告诉了孟子：他们不是朋友，而是不好的人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，The original redundantly combines two time expressions; the corrected form uses one natural time phrase.
- 孟子老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿：孟子老是跟这些卖东西的人玩儿，Two frequency adverbs should not redundantly modify the same action; keep one of them.
- 害得妈妈着急得很：让妈妈很着急，害得 requires a clearly harmful result after the affected person; being worried is better expressed with 让 in this context.
- 她恐怕儿子会成为跟他们很像：她担心儿子会变得跟他们很像，恐怕 does not take a third-person subject expressing fear; 担心 does. Also, 变得 introduces an adjective phrase such as 跟他们很像, whereas 成为 normally introduces a noun identity.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 卖东西的人 -- “一般人不喜欢卖东西的人”：商人，names this group more precisely as people whose work is selling goods；小贩，specifies small-scale street sellers if that is what the scene means.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们住才一月就又搬家。：他们才住了一个月，就又搬家了。，Use 了 for the completed period of living and for the completed move; the 才…就 structure also shows that the stay was unexpectedly short.
- Coherence (Temporality): 她告诉了孟子：她告诉孟子：，A speaking verb that introduces quoted speech takes the zero particle rather than 了.
- Expressiveness: Near the sellers, add one concrete marketplace detail, such as “街上有很多商人叫卖，来买东西的人很多。”
- Expressiveness: After the action of playing, show what Mengzi copies: “孟子学着商人收钱，还跟别人讨价还价。”
- Expressiveness: Make the mother’s warning more emotional with a speech tag: “妈妈着急地说：‘他们不是朋友，而是不好的人。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show why the mother is worried, and the direct quotation gives the scene a strong ending. You also keep the references to Mengzi, his mother, and the sellers mostly easy to follow.

## Scene 3

> 你不是当数学家，而是当哲学家，不能当卖东西的人。所以他们又搬家。在他们第三个家，孟子学了怎么祭拜。妈妈感觉这个地方适合他们，告诉了孟子：我们找到了最后的家。在这儿妈妈让儿子从早到晚学习，他难怪成为有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在他们第三个家：在他们的第三个家里，The possessive modifier needs 的 before the noun, and a home used as a location normally takes 里.
- 他难怪：难怪他，This adverb must introduce the whole clause and come before its subject.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 感觉 -- “妈妈感觉这个地方适合他们”：觉得，more specifically shows that the mother is making a judgment about whether the place suits the family.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 又搬家：又搬家了，The move has already happened, so the completed action needs 了; 又 marks a repeated action that has already occurred.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉了孟子：告诉孟子，A speaking verb that introduces quoted speech uses the zero particle, not 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为：成为了，Becoming a philosopher is presented as a completed change of state, so it needs 了.
- Expressiveness: At the part about learning, add a small action detail to show how Mengzi learns: “孟子认真地看书，遇到不懂的地方就问老师。”
- Expressiveness: Show the mother’s feeling when she believes the home is suitable: “妈妈高兴地说：‘我们终于找到了最后的家。’”
- Expressiveness: Add a detail about the peaceful study environment: “这儿很安静，孟子可以从早到晚专心学习。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the family moving again and include the mother’s speech, which helps the scene feel like part of a continuing story. The contrast structure makes the mother’s hopes for Mengzi easy to understand.
