## Scene 1

> 孟子小的时候，从早到晚都跟朋友玩。孟子和他的朋友喜欢玩在墓地里，装祭拜。孟子五岁的时候，跟妈妈搬家。他们搬家不是因为孟子玩得太多，而是因为妈妈认为他们的家离墓地太近。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 喜欢玩在墓地里：喜欢在墓地里玩，Location phrases normally come before the main verb they modify.
- 装祭拜：假装祭拜，装 does not mean “pretend to do an action” in this structure; 假装 + VP expresses pretending to perform that action.
- 跟妈妈搬家：跟妈妈搬了家，This is one completed move at a specific age, so the action verb needs 了.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 朋友 -- “孟子和他的朋友喜欢在墓地里玩”：小伙伴，specifies that these are playmates of a similar age and fits a child-character story.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子小的时候：有一个小男孩叫孟子。他小的时候，A character’s first mention should introduce both the character and who he is; afterwards, the close re-occurrence can use a pronoun.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和他的朋友喜欢：他们喜欢，The previous sentence has just mentioned孟子and his friends, so a pronoun naturally refers back to this group.
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery-play sentence, add a concrete action to show what the children copy: “他们学着大人的样子低头鞠躬。”
- Expressiveness: Add孟子的 feeling when he plays there: “孟子觉得这样很好玩，笑个不停。”
- Expressiveness: Near the final sentence, add a short thought from the mother to show her concern: “妈妈心想：‘这里会不会影响孟子长大？’”

### Encouragement
You clearly used the paired connector “不是……而是……” to distinguish the real reason for the move from an incorrect one. The time phrase “从早到晚” also effectively shows how often孟子 played.

## Scene 2

> 妈妈认为，住离墓地太近，害得孟子的心气。孟子和孟母搬到子思老师的家，跟子思老师住。孟母帮子思打扫房间和做饭。子思帮孟母教她的儿子。因为孟子现在没有朋友，他老是读书。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 住离墓地太近：住在离墓地太近的地方，A location phrase after this verb needs the structure with 在, and the place should be expressed as a complete noun phrase.
- 害得孟子的心气：会影响孟子，害得 must be followed by a person and a harmful result clause; 心气 is a noun and cannot serve as that result.
- 搬到：搬到了，This move is a completed event in the narrative, so the verb needs 了.
- 老是读书：一直读书，老是 often suggests that a repeated action is annoying or excessive; 一直 neutrally describes Mengzi’s continuous reading here.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 朋友 -- “因为孟子现在没有朋友”：玩伴，specifies friends a child plays with；同伴，emphasizes that he has no companion around him.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟母：孟子和她，The mother was mentioned in the immediately preceding sentence, so a pronoun is more natural for a close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Conjunction): ，他：，所以他，This is a cause-and-effect relationship, so adding 所以 clearly introduces the result of having no friends.
- Expressiveness: After the mother’s concern about the cemetery, add her worried feeling or thought: “孟母很担心，怕孟子会学坏。”
- Expressiveness: When Mengzi reads because he has no friends, add a small action showing his concentration: “他每天坐在桌子前，安静地看书。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech from Zisi to make the teaching relationship clearer: “子思老师说：‘孟子，你要认真学习。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the change from the cemetery environment to a new learning environment, and the final sentence gives a logical reason for Mengzi’s reading.
