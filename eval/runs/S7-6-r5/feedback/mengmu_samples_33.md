## Scene 1

> 一天，很久很久以前，孟子和他妈妈住在中国。孟子妈妈的工作是祭拜，孟子不高兴，因为他说我们老是搬家，为什么？他妈妈说，从早到晚，我一直祭拜，很多墓地得我祭拜。所以我们得去很多不同的地方。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他说我们老是搬家，为什么？：他说：“我们为什么老是搬家？”，A direct question following a speaking verb should be introduced with a colon and quotation marks; placing the question word before the statement makes the question clear.
- 他妈妈说，从早到晚，我一直祭拜，很多墓地得我祭拜。：他妈妈说：“从早到晚，我一直祭拜，我得祭拜很多墓地。”，A modal verb such as 得 follows its subject and comes before the main verb; the original order incorrectly makes the graves appear before the person who must do the action.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和他妈妈：有一个小男孩叫孟子，他和妈妈，On first mention, introduce the character with both a description and a name; after that introduction, the pronoun can refer clearly to him.
- Expressiveness: Near the question about moving, add a visible feeling: “孟子皱着眉头，觉得搬家很麻烦。”
- Expressiveness: After the mother explains her work, show how demanding it is: “妈妈每天从早到晚祭拜，累得满头大汗。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show why the family has to travel, and the final sentence gives a logical result from the mother’s explanation.
- The dialogue helps the reader understand both Mengzi’s unhappiness and his mother’s situation.

## Scene 2

> 孟子想，难怪我们常常搬家，原来我妈妈很忙。可是常常搬家害得孟子很难找到朋友。每次搬家孟子也觉得新的地方不是太热就是太冷，不是太大就是太小。在哪的地方，他都不高兴。孟子想帮助妈妈祭拜，可是他六岁，所以不能祭拜。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在哪的地方：在哪个地方，When asking or referring generally to a location, use 哪个 + noun; 哪的地方 is not a natural noun phrase.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 找到 -- “很难找到朋友”：结交，specifically means to make new friends and build a friendship in this context.
- 不高兴 -- “他都不高兴”：闷闷不乐，shows a quieter, continuing feeling of unhappiness after each move.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After “很难找到朋友,” add Mengzi’s personal feeling about leaving friends behind: “每次刚认识一个朋友，我们就又要搬家了。”
- Expressiveness: At “孟子想帮助妈妈祭拜,” show what he sees his mother doing: “他看着妈妈忙着祭拜，心里很着急。”
- Expressiveness: After “不是太大就是太小,” add one concrete detail about an uncomfortable new place: “有的地方夏天很热，太阳晒得他满头大汗。”

### Encouragement
You used the paired pattern “难怪……原来……” correctly to show Mengzi’s realization. The two “不是……就是……” patterns also clearly describe the problems he feels in each new place.

## Scene 3

> 四年后，孟子开始帮妈妈祭拜。他画画很厉害，他越来越喜欢祭拜，所以他祭拜不是想帮妈妈工作，而是他很喜欢画画。孟子成为很著名的古代画家。今天很多中国人很喜欢孟子。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他祭拜不是想帮妈妈工作，而是他很喜欢画画：他祭拜不是为了帮妈妈工作，而是因为他很喜欢画画，Use matching purpose/cause phrases on both sides of this contrast: the first side rejects the purpose, and the second gives the real reason.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 厉害 -- “他画画很厉害”：擅长画画，states more precisely that painting is Mengzi’s particular skill.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他画画很厉害，他越来越喜欢祭拜：他画画很厉害，越来越喜欢祭拜，After Mengzi has been named at the start of the sentence and no other character competes as the subject, use zero form rather than repeating the pronoun.
- Coherence (Temporality): 孟子成为很著名的古代画家：孟子成为了很著名的古代画家，This is a completed change of identity in the narrative, so the verb needs 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 所以：删除，所以 should introduce a result caused by the preceding information, but the following sentence instead explains Mengzi’s motivation with a contrast structure.
- Expressiveness: After Mengzi begins helping, add a small action showing how his painting skill appears during the ritual, such as “孟子一边祭拜，一边认真地画画。” This makes the connection between the two activities clearer.
- Expressiveness: Add Mengzi’s feeling when he can finally help his mother, such as “他觉得自己终于长大了，很开心。” This lets the reader understand his inner response.

### Encouragement
You used a clear time marker, “四年后,” to move the narrative forward. You also showed Mengzi’s development by connecting his new role with his interest in painting.
