## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个男人，他的名字是神农。他看到了有很多人不知道哪个植物好吃，也不知道什么植物对你的身体有好处。这个问题神农觉得很糟糕，所以神农去了吃每种植物。他吃了一种植物以后，就写了这个植物对你有没有好处。有的时候，神农吃了很好吃的植物。有的时候，他吃了很危险的植物。他吃完了每种植物以后，会来跟他的朋友聊到他吃的植物了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，this avoids repeating two time expressions with the same meaning.
- 去了吃：去吃了，when a verb phrase begins with 去, the completion marker follows the main action verb rather than 去.
- 写了：记下了，this context means recording information about a plant; the listed vocabulary pattern uses 写下 or 记下 for recording such findings.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 看到 -- “他看到了有很多人不知道哪个植物好吃”：发现，emphasizes that he notices and realizes this problem after observing people.
- 糟糕 -- “这个问题神农觉得很糟糕”：令人担忧，specifies that the problem makes him worried about people’s health.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 对你的身体有好处：对身体有好处，the pronoun refers to the reader, but the sentence is about people in general; use a generic form instead.
- Coherence (Reference): 所以神农：所以他，after the recent mention of the same character and with no competing character, use a pronoun rather than repeat the name.
- Coherence (Reference): 对你有没有好处：对身体有没有好处，the pronoun refers to the reader rather than to people generally; use a generic form here.
- Coherence (Temporality): 有的时候，神农吃了很好吃的植物：有的时候，神农吃很好吃的植物，this describes a repeated habit, so it takes the zero particle rather than 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 有的时候，他吃了很危险的植物：有的时候，他吃很危险的植物，this is also a repeated habit, so no 了 is needed.
- Coherence (Temporality): 聊到他吃的植物了：聊到他吃的植物，after the modal verb 会 in a habitual future-style statement, the action does not take 了 for completion.
- Expressiveness: After describing that people do not know which plants are safe, add a concrete consequence to make the problem clearer: “很多人因为吃了有毒的植物，常常生病。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where he decides to try plants, include his determination: “神农想：我一定要找出哪些植物可以吃。”
- Expressiveness: When he talks with his friends, add a short line of speech showing what he records: “这种植物对身体有好处，我要记下来。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduce the main character and explain why he begins testing plants. The repeated use of “有的时候” also effectively shows that his experiences include both safe and dangerous plants.

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一个别的人：另一个人，Here, “别的” is used to distinguish others from a person or group already under discussion; “另一个人” correctly introduces one additional questioner.
- 一个别的人：又一个人，This questioner appears after two earlier questioners, so “又一个人” naturally means one further person; “别的” does not fit this use.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很多种 -- “有很多种植物对你的身体有好处”：各种各样的，emphasizes the variety of beneficial plants rather than only their number

### Coherence & Expressiveness

- Expressiveness: When naming the sweet fruit, add one sensory detail to make the examples more vivid, for example: 又甜又多汁的西瓜。
- Expressiveness: At the final question, show Shen Nong’s careful attitude before he answers, for example: 神农想了一想，说：“有些植物对身体有坏处。”

### Encouragement

## Scene 3

> 神农说，有一天，我吃了一个黄色的花，名字是夹竹桃。我一吃夹竹桃，就觉得这种植物对我的脑有坏处。可是，一个灵芝和一个男人正好在我的旁边，我说，麻烦你把那个植物给我吃一下。我一吃这个灵芝就好了。所以，你得小心吃植物，因为有的植物对你的健康有坏处，特别是夹竹桃。因为夹竹桃有毒。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一个黄色的花：一朵黄色的花，The appropriate measure word for a flower is 朵; 个 is too general here.
- 对我的脑有坏处：对我的大脑有坏处，When referring to the organ, 大脑 is the natural full noun in this context; 脑 alone does not fit naturally after 对我的.
- 一个灵芝：一株灵芝，灵芝 is a growing plant-like organism in this scene, so 株 is an appropriate measure word rather than the general classifier 个.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 坏处 -- “有的植物对你的健康有坏处”：危害健康，states directly that the plants can damage health and is more concise in this warning.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 那个植物：灵芝，There are two plant referents here, 夹竹桃 and 灵芝, so repeating 灵芝 makes the requested plant unambiguous.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 特别是夹竹桃。因为夹竹桃有毒。：特别是夹竹桃，因为它有毒。，A 因为 clause needs to connect to the reason or result it explains; here it explains why 夹竹桃 is especially dangerous.
- Expressiveness: After the yellow flower is introduced, add a visual detail to make it memorable: “那朵花像星星一样，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: After eating the poisonous flower, describe a physical feeling before the help arrives: “我的肚子突然疼了起来，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Give the man a small action before the request to make the rescue scene livelier: “这时，一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人走了过来。”

### Encouragement
- You used the pattern 一……就…… correctly to show an immediate result, and the final warning clearly explains why readers should be careful with plants.
