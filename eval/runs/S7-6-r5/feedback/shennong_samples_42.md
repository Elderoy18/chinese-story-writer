## Scene 1

> 古代的时候有一个人叫神农。他需要当上医，一看别的人就想帮助他们。但是，他有一个问题，他不知道什么东西能做药。神农听说一个远远的地方有一种植物，什么病人吃都会觉得身体更好。神农三个月走去，找到有植物的地方，他看到一个很漂亮的植物，又香又好看。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，this phrase is redundant because 古代 already expresses a time period.
- 当上医：当上医生，医 is not normally used alone as the name of an occupation; 医生 means a doctor.
- 一看：一看到，this means “see someone,” so the verb needs the resultative complement 到 rather than only describing the act of looking.
- 别的人：别人，别人 refers to other people; 别的 is used to distinguish a different item or type.
- 一个很漂亮的植物：一株很漂亮的植物，株 is the appropriate measure word for one individual plant; 个 is too generic here.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 问题 -- “但是，他有一个问题”：难题，specifies that this is a difficult problem that needs to be solved.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他有一个问题，他不知道：他有一个问题，不知道，after the subject has already been established in the same sentence, use zero form rather than repeating the pronoun.
- Coherence (Temporality): 什么病人吃都会觉得身体更好：什么病人吃了都会觉得身体更好，eating happens before the later feeling, so use 了 to mark the completed action.
- Coherence (Temporality): 神农三个月走去，找到有植物的地方：神农走了三个月，找到了有植物的地方，了 marks the completed duration and the completed finding; 去 cannot follow 走 without a destination.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他看到一个很漂亮的植物：他看到了一个很漂亮的植物，this is a completed discovery in the narrative, so add 了 after the resultative complement.
- Expressiveness: At the moment Shen Nong sees the plant, add a more memorable visual detail, such as “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花。”
- Expressiveness: After he realizes he does not know what can become medicine, show his determination with an inner thought, such as “他想：我一定要找到能做药的植物。”
- Expressiveness: During the long journey, add a feeling and a contrast to show perseverance, such as “他又累又饿，但是没有放弃。”

### Encouragement
You clearly introduce Shen Nong by name and establish his wish to help others. The final description includes both smell and appearance, which makes the plant easy to imagine.

## Scene 2

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- Nothing to flag.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 没有病 -- “他没有病”：病好了，makes clear that he recovered from the illness rather than simply stating that he is not ill.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 神农快生病：神农很快就生病了，The illness begins as a completed change of state in this past event, so the verb needs 了; 很快就 also clearly shows that it happened soon after eating.
- Coherence (Temporality): 快给他一种红的灵芝：赶快给了他一种红的灵芝，Giving the plant is a completed action in the action sequence, so only this final verb needs 了.
- Expressiveness: After the illness begins, add a physical feeling to show how serious it is, such as “神农肚子疼得不得了，倒在地上。”
- Expressiveness: Describe the blue-clothed person’s urgent action more vividly at the point where he gives the plant, such as “他赶快拿起像伞一样的植物，给神农吃了。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought after recovery to show Shennong’s realization, such as “神农想：原来灵芝真的可以做药！”

### Encouragement

## Scene 3

> 啊，他怎么忘记了？他把灵芝放在包里。一回家就告诉别人。他找到药以后他写下怎么做药在书上。所以人们都知道，那红灵芝对身体好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他把灵芝放在包里：他把灵芝放在包里了，This completed action needs 了 after the verb phrase to show that the placement was finished.
- 一回家就告诉别人：一回家就告诉了别人，The telling is a completed event in this narrative sequence, so the final verb takes 了.
- 他找到药以后他写下怎么做药在书上：他找到药以后，在书上写下了怎么做药，The location phrase 在书上 should come before 写下, and the completed act of writing takes 了.
- 那红灵芝：那种红灵芝，A demonstrative before a noun needs a measure word; 种 is suitable for a kind of plant.
- 对身体好处：对身体有好处，The fixed expression is 对身体有好处; it means “be beneficial to the body.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 放 -- “他把灵芝放在包里”：收进，specifies that the lingzhi is put into the bag and kept there.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: At “一回家就告诉了别人,” add a short line of speech to show Shen Nong’s wish to help others: “灵芝是药，可以吃！”
- Expressiveness: After the writing action, add a purpose or result: “这样，大家就不会吃错植物了。”
- Expressiveness: You could make the writing detail more concrete by naming both plants: “他把灵芝和夹竹桃都写在书上。”

### Encouragement
You use clear event order, moving from taking the lingzhi home to telling people and recording the information. Using “一……就……” also gives the scene a quick, active feeling.
