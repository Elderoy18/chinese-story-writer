## Scene 1

> 在古代，很多医生不知道怎么用药。所以他们找到了很多植物对药。神农先看到很多病人，他们吃很多植物对药。但是我觉得他们的吃的植物，让他们病人。神农觉得这个植物可能特别是毒。所以他想找一个植物对药，他们可以吃对药。正好，他找到了一把花，他想把花放在茶里，对药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 找到了很多植物对药：找到了很多可以做药的植物，`对药` is not a valid phrase here; `做药` means “to be used to make medicine,” and the whole phrase needs to modify `植物`.
- 植物对药：用植物做的药，when the patients take medicine made from plants, `用植物做的` must modify `药`; `对药` cannot express this meaning.
- 让他们病人：让他们生病，`病人` is a noun meaning “patient,” while `生病` is the verb phrase needed after `让` to describe becoming ill.
- 特别是毒：特别有毒，`毒` cannot be used as an adjective after `特别`; `有毒` describes something that is poisonous.
- 想找一个植物对药，他们可以吃对药：想找一种可以做药、可以吃的植物，`对药` cannot be used to describe a medicinal plant, and both purposes should modify the plant being sought.
- 把花放在茶里，对药：把花放在茶里做药，`对药` is not a valid verb phrase; `做药` expresses making or using something as medicine.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农先看到很多病人：一个叫神农的男人先看到很多病人，this is the character’s first mention, so introduce the name together with a full noun phrase rather than assuming the reader already knows who he is.
- Coherence (Reference): 这个植物：其中有些植物，no single plant has been identified before this point; refer back clearly to some of the many plants already mentioned.
- Coherence (Reference): 把花：把它，after `花` was mentioned in the immediately previous sentence, a pronoun is more natural for this close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们吃很多植物：他们吃了很多植物，this describes a completed earlier action by the patients, so `吃` needs `了`.
- Expressiveness: At the mention of the patients, add a specific symptom to show why the situation is serious, such as `很多病人吃了植物以后，肚子疼得很厉害。`
- Expressiveness: When describing the poisonous plants, add a visual detail, such as `有些植物开着黄色的花，像星星一样。`
- Expressiveness: After the thought about poison, show Shen Nong’s worry with an internal question, such as `神农想：这些植物会不会有毒？`

### Encouragement
You clearly connect the doctors’ problem to Shen Nong’s action with a cause-and-effect sequence. You also introduce medicine, plants, patients, and poison-related ideas that fit the scene well.

## Scene 2

> 他不知道如果这把花对你的身体好，这就要看如果这把花毒不毒了。神农想找别的植物，但是这很麻烦。正好，他找到了别的花。他一吃花，很快就生病了。他的肚子疼死了。神农不知道他吃不吃灵芝或者夹竹桃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 如果这把花对：这把花对，After 不知道, state the uncertain proposition directly; 如果 introduces a condition and does not fit here.
- 看如果这把花毒不毒：看这把花毒不毒，The fixed structure is 这就要看……了; the part after 看 should be the question being considered, not a conditional clause with 如果.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 麻烦 -- “但是这很麻烦”：繁琐，emphasizes that finding and checking plants involves many complicated steps；艰辛，emphasizes that the work is difficult and demanding.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 你的身体：自己的身体，Because the flower is being judged for 神农 himself, use the reflexive possessive to make the owner clear.
- Coherence (Reference): 一吃花：一吃这种花，This refers back to the particular kind of flower just found; the bare noun 花 can sound like flowers in general.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农不知道他：神农不知道自己，The pronoun 他 can suggest another male person, while 自己 clearly refers back to 神农.
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃不吃灵芝或者夹竹桃：吃的是灵芝还是夹竹桃，The eating has already happened in the previous sentence, so this should identify what he ate; 吃不吃 asks whether he eats at all, while 是……还是…… presents the two possible identities.
- Expressiveness: After “正好，他找到了别的花”, add a visual detail about the flower, such as “花是黄色的，像星星一样，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: After “他的肚子疼死了”, show his physical reaction and feelings, such as “他躺在地上，难受得不得了，心想：我会不会死？”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought when he is unsure about the plant, such as “神农想：这种花到底能不能吃呢？”

### Encouragement
You used completed action clearly in “找到了别的花” and “就生病了.” The strong phrase “肚子疼死了” also gives the scene an immediate sense of pain and danger.

## Scene 3

> 别的人给了神农一个灵芝。这个植物可以让神农更好。比方说，他会觉得更健康。吃灵芝以后，他可以再走。现在他觉得更好。最后，神农找到了很多植物，他可以用对药。这些植物让神农很高兴。神农想跟很多人分享他的新药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别的人给了神农：一个人给了神农，别的人 means “other people” in contrast with a previously mentioned person or group; here a new, unspecified person is being introduced.
- 用对药：用来做药，use 用来 to introduce the purpose of the plants; 对 does not mean “to make medicine,” while 做药 means making medicine from them.

### Coherence & Expressiveness

- Coherence (Reference): 最后，神农找到了很多植物：最后，他找到了很多植物，Shen Nong was the clear referent in the preceding sentence, so a pronoun avoids an unnecessary repeated name.
- Coherence (Reference): 这些植物让神农很高兴：这些植物让他很高兴，Use a pronoun when the recently mentioned character is still clear.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农想跟很多人分享他的新药：他想跟很多人分享他的新药，The same character continues from the previous sentence, so a pronoun is more natural.
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃灵芝以后：吃了灵芝以后，The eating is completed before Shen Nong can walk again, so it needs 了.

### Encouragement
You use clear time markers such as “吃灵芝以后” and “最后” to move the scene forward. You also show Shen Nong’s recovery and his wish to help others, which gives the scene a positive direction.
