## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，孟子和孟母住了在靠墓地的家。孩子才五岁就每天在墓地祭拜了。在那个城市，孟母从早到晚都找工作，可是找不到，害得孟母没有钱。难怪她跟孩子搬家，原来买不了房。他们搬了到别一个城市找工作。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，this phrase redundantly combines two time expressions; use the shorter time expression.
- 住了在靠墓地的家：住在靠近墓地的家里，the aspect marker 了 cannot appear before the location phrase, and a continuing living situation uses zero aspect here.
- 跟孩子搬家：带着孩子搬家，跟 expresses doing an action together, while 带着 shows that the mother takes her child with her when moving.
- 搬了到：搬到了，place 了 after the directional/resultative element 到.
- 别一个城市：另一个城市，另一个 is the standard phrase for one additional or different city; 别一个 is not natural Chinese.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟母：一个叫孟子的小男孩和他的妈妈，at first mention, introduce the named character and the family relationship clearly rather than assuming the reader already knows them.
- Coherence (Reference): 孩子：他，when the character was named in the immediately previous sentence and no other boy is present, use a pronoun for close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Reference): 害得孟母没有钱：害得她没有钱，after mentioning the mother earlier in the same sentence, use a pronoun rather than repeating her name.
- Coherence (Temporality): 每天在墓地祭拜了：每天在墓地祭拜，every day describes a routine, so the verb takes zero aspect rather than completed-action 了.
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery activity, add the child’s interest and imitation: “他觉得祭拜很有趣，常常学着别人祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: At the job-search sentence, show the mother’s feeling: “孟母找不到工作，心里很着急。”
- Expressiveness: Before the move, add a short line of speech: “孟母叹了一口气，说：‘我们得去别的地方找工作了。’”

### Encouragement
You used the inference pattern 难怪……原来…… effectively to connect the mother’s move with her financial difficulty. The scene also has a clear sequence from living near the cemetery to searching for work and moving away.

## Scene 2

> 这个城市老是很吵，不是人吵架，就是都叫，害得孟子一直不能睡觉。在那儿，孟母也找不到工作。孟子和妈妈要又一个次搬了家。在第三个城市，孟母找到工作，在商店做衣服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 又一个次搬：再搬一次，With a future plan after 要, use 再 for a repeat that has not happened yet; 再 comes before the verb, and 一次 follows the verb.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 吵 -- “这个城市老是很吵”：嘈杂，describes many mixed, disturbing sounds in a busy place more specifically.
- 做 -- “在商店做衣服”：缝制，specifies that her work is sewing and making clothes.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟母也找不到工作：她也找不到工作，孟母 was mentioned in the previous sentence and no other female character competes as the referent, so a pronoun makes the connection smoother.
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬了家：搬家，The move is still a future plan after 要, not a completed action, so it should not take 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 找到工作：找到了工作，Finding the job is a completed result, so add 了 after the resultative complement 到.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 不是人吵架，就是都叫：不是有人吵架，就是有人大叫，The two alternatives in the 不是……就是…… pattern need parallel subject-verb phrases; this version clearly presents two possible sources of noise.
- Expressiveness: After the noisy-city description, add a sensory detail such as “外面的人声和吵架声从早到晚都不停。” This shows why the noise troubles the family.
- Expressiveness: At the point where he cannot sleep, add Mengzi’s feeling or speech, such as “孟子捂着耳朵说：‘妈妈，这里太吵了！’” This makes his problem more vivid.
- Expressiveness: After the job is found, show Mengmu’s reaction, such as “孟母终于松了一口气。” This gives the successful change emotional weight.

### Encouragement
You clearly show why the family needs to move again: the noise affects Mengzi’s sleep, and Mengmu still cannot find work. Your final sentence also gives the new city a positive change by showing that Mengmu finds a job.

## Scene 3

> 孟子在那个商店也找了到工作，可是不是做衣服的工作，而是喜欢的工作。孟子学习馆学了十五年，他的学习和他的旅行经验很重要，对他很多好处。他是大人的时候，他成为很有名的哲人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 学习馆学：在学习馆学习，A place used as the location of an action needs the preposition “在”; “学习” is the verb here.
- 对他很多好处：对他有很多好处，The fixed collocation is “对……有好处”; “好处” is a noun and needs “有” as its verb.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很有名 -- “他成为很有名的哲人”：享有盛名的，describes a philosopher who is widely known and respected, rather than only generally “famous”.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子学习馆学了十五年：他在学习馆学习了十五年，Use a pronoun when the same character was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other male character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Reference): 他的学习和他的旅行经验：他的学习和旅行经验，Within one coordinated phrase, the second possessive pronoun is unnecessary because both kinds of experience clearly belong to the same person; use zero form.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为：成为了，“成为哲人” describes a completed change of state in this past narrative, so it takes the completion marker 了.
- Expressiveness: After the study period, add a concrete learning detail, such as “他每天认真看书，常常读到很晚。”
- Expressiveness: Before the final achievement, add Mengzi’s feeling about his mother’s choices, such as “孟子想：‘妈妈搬家是为了让我好好学习。’”
- Expressiveness: At the ending, add other people’s reaction, such as “人们都说：‘孟子的成功离不开孟母三次搬家。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly show a long period of study with “十五年,” which helps the reader understand why Mengzi later becomes successful. You also used the contrast pattern to distinguish the kind of work from clothing work, giving the scene a clear focus.
