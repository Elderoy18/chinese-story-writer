## Scene 1

> 古代，孟子跟孟母住在墓地的旁边。可是孟母注意，住在墓地的旁边影响孟子。他常常跟朋友们祭拜玩，所以孟母决定他们会搬家。这次，他们住在城市里的商店的旁边，从早到晚很吵。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 孟母注意：孟母注意到，the resultative complement 到 shows that she became aware of the following situation.
- 常常跟朋友们祭拜玩：常常跟朋友们一起学着别人祭拜，祭拜 is the action being imitated; it cannot be followed by 玩 in this word order. Use 学着 + verb to describe copying an action.
- 决定他们会搬家：决定搬家，决定 should be followed directly by the action phrase that is decided; do not repeat the people who will do it.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很吵 -- “从早到晚很吵”：嘈杂，describes a place filled with many mixed, unpleasant sounds and is more specific than a general statement that it is noisy.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After “孟子……祭拜,” add a small action detail to show that he treats it as play, for example: “一边笑一边学着别人磕头。”
- Expressiveness: At “所以孟母决定搬家,” add Meng Mu’s worried thought or speech, for example: “孟母心想：‘住在这儿会影响孩子。’”
- Expressiveness: After “从早到晚很吵,” add a concrete sound detail, for example: “商人不停地叫卖，街上人来人往。”

### Encouragement
- You introduced the setting, Mengzi, and Meng Mu clearly in the opening sentence. You also used a cause-and-effect connection with “所以” to show why Meng Mu makes her decision.

## Scene 2

> 城市也有点儿危险。害得孟母只好一直陪孟子。孟子也没有朋友，难怪他老是看起来不高兴。住在商店的旁边不是适合孟子，而是让他觉得很伤心。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 害得孟母只好一直陪孟子：让孟母只好一直陪孟子，Use 让 because having to accompany someone is an unwanted necessity, but it does not state a clearly harmful result required by 害得.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 住在商店的旁边不是适合孟子，而是让他觉得很伤心：住在商店的旁边不适合孟子，因为这让他觉得很伤心，The second idea gives the reason why the place is unsuitable, so use 因为 for a cause relation rather than 不是……而是……, which refutes one idea and replaces it with another.
- Expressiveness: After the description of the city as dangerous, add a concrete street detail to show why it feels unsafe, for example: “街上人来人往，商人的叫卖声从早到晚都不停。”
- Expressiveness: At “孟子也没有朋友,” show his lonely feeling through a small action, for example: “孟子一个人坐在窗边，看着外面的孩子玩儿。”
- Expressiveness: Give Meng Mu a short line of speech after she realizes he is unhappy, for example: “孟母着急地说：‘这里不适合你，我们得再搬家。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly connect the noisy city setting to Meng Zi’s unhappiness, and “难怪他老是看起来不高兴” expresses the result of having no friends naturally.

## Scene 3

> 所以，孟母和孟子搬到学校的旁边住。才五岁孟子开始学哲学。在学校，孟子不是看书，就是练习写字。将来，他成为中国的一个非常有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 孟母和孟子搬到学校的旁边住：孟母和孟子搬到了学校的旁边住，The relocation is a completed event in this narrative, so the verb needs 了.
- 才五岁孟子开始学哲学：孟子才五岁就开始学哲学，Use the pattern 才 + age + 就 + verb to show that an action began at an early age.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为：成为了，After the sequence marker later is used, this describes a completed change of state, so becoming a philosopher takes 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 将来：后来，Use 后来 to place his later achievement after the school-years events in this past narrative; it expresses sequence.
- Expressiveness: After the move, add a small detail about the learning environment, such as “学校里很安静，常常能听见读书声。” This helps the reader picture why this place supports learning.
- Expressiveness: When describing his study, add a feeling or action, such as “孟子觉得读书很有意思，常常认真地写字。” This shows his interest instead of only listing activities.
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought from his mother after seeing him study, such as “孟母高兴地想：‘这儿很适合孟子学习。’” This gives the scene more emotion.

### Encouragement
- You used the pattern “不是……就是……” correctly to show two regular school activities. Your final sentence also clearly shows the important result of Mengzi’s learning.
