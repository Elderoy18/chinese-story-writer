## Scene 1

> 古代的时候人觉得祭拜非常重要，孟子很小的时候，从早到晚一直祭拜，因为他妈妈告诉他祭拜很重要。虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜。他的同学们老是对他开玩笑了。孟子妈妈看了他的同学们对孟子不好的影响，所以孟子和孟子妈妈搬家了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，this expression redundantly combines two time expressions; use the shorter time phrase.
- 人：人们，this refers to a group of people in that historical period, so the plural collective noun is needed.
- 孟子妈妈看了他的同学们对孟子不好的影响：孟子的妈妈看到他的同学们对孟子有不好的影响，the intended meaning is that she perceived the result, so use the resultative form；the noun phrase also needs 有 to state that the classmates had a negative effect.
- 孟子和孟子妈妈：孟子和他的妈妈，once the mother has been mentioned, use a pronoun rather than repeating the name inside the same noun phrase.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 重要 -- “人们觉得祭拜非常重要”：至关重要，emphasizes that people considered it crucial rather than merely important.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子很小的时候：有一个小男孩叫孟子，他很小的时候，孟子 appears for the first time here, so introduce him with a full noun phrase before using a pronoun.
- Coherence (Temporality): 开玩笑了：开玩笑，this describes a repeated habit, so use the zero particle rather than 了, which marks a completed action.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜：因为他每天只祭拜，所以他没有朋友，these two ideas express cause and effect, so use the paired cause connector rather than 虽然.
- Expressiveness: After the first sentence, add a setting detail to show where the worship happened, such as “墓地里常常有人来祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where the classmates joke with him, show Mengzi’s feeling with a short thought or reaction, such as “孟子觉得很难过，不知道自己做错了什么。”
- Expressiveness: When his mother notices the effect, add her concern through quoted speech, such as “孟子的妈妈着急地说：‘我们不能再住在这里了。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly used a cause-and-effect idea to connect Mengzi’s mother’s observation with the family’s move. You also used useful time phrases such as “从早到晚” to show how often Mengzi worshipped.

## Scene 2

> 孟子才十一岁，成为一个哲学家。孟子不是祭拜，而是看政治文章，难怪他成为了哲学家。不过，看过很多政治文章对他害得，他有很多问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 对他害得：害得他，害得 must be followed by the person affected and then the harmful result; the preposition 对 cannot come before this structure.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 问题 -- “他有很多问题”：疑问，specifies that these are things he does not yet understand after reading

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子不是祭拜：他不是祭拜，after a recent mention of the same character with no competing person, use a pronoun rather than repeating the name.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为一个哲学家：成为了一个哲学家，becoming a philosopher is presented as a completed event, so the verb needs 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 才十一岁，：才十一岁，就，after 才 plus an age, add 就 before the action to show that it happened unusually early.
- Expressiveness: At “看政治文章,” add a small action showing how carefully he reads, such as “他一边看，一边认真地想。”
- Expressiveness: At “他有很多问题,” show one specific thought or question, such as “他心想：‘为什么有的人支持这个办法？’”
- Expressiveness: Add a feeling about his early success, such as “他很高兴，也觉得自己还要继续学习。”

### Encouragement
You clearly connect Mengzi’s reading to his later achievement, and you also include a consequence of reading, which makes the scene more thoughtful.

## Scene 3

> 他一直问问题，但是不能解决那些问题。孟子长大，他开始能解决问题。他常常建议你不是学金融，而是学政治，因为如果你懂政治，你会成为高薪。后来孟子成为教授，他教学生很厉害。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不是学金融，而是学政治：不要学金融，而要学政治，After a verb of advice, use an action phrase that tells the listener what not to do and what to do instead; this expresses the intended recommendation more clearly.
- 成为高薪：拿到高薪，High salary is income, not a role that someone can become; this verb means to receive that income.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 一直 -- “他一直问问题”：不断，emphasizes that the questions keep coming one after another.
- 解决 -- “不能解决那些问题”：解答，specifically emphasizes answering intellectual or academic questions.
- 很厉害 -- “他教学生很厉害”：很有办法，specifically shows that he knows effective ways to teach students；教得很好，states his teaching ability more directly.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 你：人们，There is no addressee for this pronoun in the scene, so introduce the group receiving the advice with a full noun phrase on its first mention.
- Coherence (Reference): 他开始能解决问题：开始能解决问题，After the name has appeared in the same sentence, use zero form rather than repeating a pronoun.
- Coherence (Temporality): 孟子长大：孟子长大了，Use 了 because growing up is presented as a completed change of state before he can solve problems.
- Coherence (Temporality): 后来孟子成为教授：后来孟子成为了教授，Use 了 to show that becoming a professor is a completed event in the narrative.
- Expressiveness: After he solves a question, add his feeling to show his intellectual growth: “每解决一个问题，孟子都觉得很有成就感。”
- Expressiveness: Make his teaching style more vivid with a concrete action: “他耐心地听学生的问题，再一步一步地解释。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech when he gives advice: “孟子对学生说：‘学习要认真想一想，不要只看钱。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly show Mengzi’s progress from having unanswered questions to being able to solve them. The contrast in the first sentence also gives the scene a clear challenge for him to overcome.
