## Scene 1

> 很久以前，古代的人很不知道什么植物能吃，也什么植物不能吃。所以很多人常常吃不安全的植物，吃不安全的植物对你的身体有坏处，很麻烦的情况。但是一个人，神农，要解决这个问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的人：人们，This sentence refers to a concrete group of people in the past, so the plural noun is needed rather than the generic noun.
- 很不知道：不知道，A negative verb phrase cannot normally be modified with 很; remove it here.
- 不安全的植物：有毒的植物，The intended meaning is plants that harm people when eaten. 有毒 specifically means “poisonous,” while 不安全 is too general for this kind of plant.
- 很麻烦的情况：是一种很麻烦的情况，After the subject clause, the sentence needs a predicate; adding 是 makes the noun phrase a complete statement.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 坏处 -- “吃不安全的植物对你的身体有坏处”：危害，specifies physical harm to the body rather than a general disadvantage；伤害，directly emphasizes that the plants can hurt the body

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 你的身体：他们的身体，The sentence is about many people, so the possessive pronoun should refer back to that group rather than directly address the reader.
- Coherence (Reference): 吃不安全的植物（第二处）：这样做，The action was stated immediately before, so use a short referring expression instead of repeating the full phrase.
- Coherence (Reference): 但是一个人，神农，要解决这个问题：但是有一个叫神农的人，想要解决这个问题，On first mention, introduce the character clearly with a name and a brief identifying noun phrase.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 也什么植物不能吃：也不知道什么植物不能吃，After 也, the verb phrase needs to be present; 也 cannot directly introduce the noun phrase here.
- Expressiveness: After the harmful plants are mentioned, add a physical result to show the danger, for example: “很多人吃了有毒的植物以后，肚子疼得厉害。”
- Expressiveness: When introducing 神农, add his purpose or feeling to make him more vivid, for example: “神农看到大家常常生病，心里很着急。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish the problem at the start and use a cause-and-effect connection with 所以. You also give 神农 a clear goal, which is a strong way to begin a story scene.

## Scene 2

> 他又聪明又努力，旅行很远找到很多植物。比方说，灵芝和夹竹桃。他一吃各种各样的植物就知道能不能吃这些植物。然后，神农写他的主意。他喜欢植物的味道，特别是花。神农遇到了一个黄色的花，一吃就觉得不舒服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 旅行很远：到很远的地方旅行，To express travelling to a distant place, use a location phrase with 到; 很远 cannot directly modify 旅行 in this way.
- 特别是花：特别是花的味道，特别是 should introduce an item in the same category as the earlier phrase; here the sentence is about 味道, so 花 needs 味道 after it.
- 一个黄色的花：一朵黄色的花，The appropriate measure word for an individual flower is 朵, not the general measure word 个.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不舒服 -- “一吃就觉得不舒服”：难受，more specifically conveys physical discomfort, especially after eating something harmful.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：他，After the same character has remained the clear subject across nearby sentences, use a pronoun rather than repeating the name.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：他，The character is still the clear, nearby referent, so a pronoun creates a smoother connection between the sentences.
- Coherence (Temporality): 找到很多植物：找到了很多植物，Finding the plants is a completed event; in this series of actions, only the final verb takes 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 写：写下了，This is a completed next action after 然后. Use the resultative complement 下 before 了: 写下了.
- Expressiveness: At the description of the flower, add a visual detail to show why it caught his attention, for example: “黄色的花像星星一样，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: After he feels ill, show a physical reaction, for example: “他的肚子疼了起来，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Add his worried thought at that moment, for example: “他想：‘我会死吗？’”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the sequence of Shennong travelling, trying plants, recording what he learns, and then becoming ill. The use of the immediate-result pattern in the final sentence makes the dangerous moment easy to understand.

## Scene 3

> 发现了夹竹桃有毒。别人看到他，很快给他吃一个灵芝。神农一吃就觉得比较好。然后，他继续写他发现的信息。会告诉他的朋友哪一个植物能不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别人：一个男人，This word means people other than the speaker or a previously contrasted person; here a new helper should be introduced with a full noun phrase.
- 很快给他吃一个灵芝：很快给了他一个灵芝吃，The giving is a completed action in the narrative, so the verb needs 了; this word order clearly shows that the lingzhi is for him to eat.
- 写：写下，When recording information discovered about plants, this verb needs to express writing it down rather than simply writing.
- 哪一个植物能不能吃：哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃，A selector question with 哪一个 cannot be combined with the yes-or-no form 能不能; use parallel categories to explain which plants are edible and which are not.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 信息 -- “他发现的信息”：发现的结果，makes clear that these are the results of his plant testing rather than general information

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他发现的信息：自己发现的信息，Because the discoverer and the writer are the same person, this possessive form makes that relationship explicit after another man has appeared in the previous sentence.
- Expressiveness: At the rescue moment, describe the helper briefly: “一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人看到神农，赶快给了他一个灵芝吃。”
- Expressiveness: Show Shennong’s physical change after eating the medicine: “神农的肚子疼得厉害，吃了灵芝以后，才觉得好一点了。”
- Expressiveness: Make the recorded information more concrete: “他写下了灵芝能治肚子疼，夹竹桃有毒，不能吃。”

### Encouragement
You clearly connect Shennong’s discovery, the help he receives, and his plan to record what he learns. You also use the useful pattern 一……就…… well to show an immediate result.
