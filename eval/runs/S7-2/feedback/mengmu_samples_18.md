## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个男孩名字叫孟子。孟子和孟子的妈妈两个人住在很穷的农村。他们住的地方旁边是个墓地，墓地老是有很多人祭拜。因为人们在祭拜，他们一直在哭。孟子和孟子的妈妈住在这里才一个星期就觉得受不了了，而且害得孟子的学习。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，This expression is redundant because 古代 already names a time period, while 的时候 also means “at the time of.” [vocab list]
- 有一个男孩名字叫孟子：有一个男孩叫孟子，When 叫 introduces a person’s name, the person can directly be its object. Using 名字 here would require a separate clause such as a subject-predicate phrase. [rule: none]
- 而且害得孟子的学习：这里的环境还影响了孟子的学习，害得 must be followed by the person harmed and a harmful result clause; 孟子的学习 alone cannot be the result after 得. [teacher rule: haide_structure]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很穷 -- “住在很穷的农村”：贫困，specifies that the area has difficult economic conditions.
- 很多人 -- “墓地老是有很多人祭拜”：许多来祭拜的人，identifies exactly who the people are while keeping the meaning that there are many of them.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子的妈妈两个人：孟子和他的妈妈两个人，Use a pronoun for the close re-occurrence of 孟子 rather than repeating his name inside the same noun phrase. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子的妈妈住在这里：孟子和妈妈住在这里，Within the same noun phrase, omit the repeated possessor when the relationship is already clear and no other mother has been mentioned. [rule: none]
- Coherence (Temporality): 住在这里才一个星期：在这里才住了一个星期，The week of living there is completed by the time they reach the point of not being able to bear it, so this duration takes 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为人们在祭拜，他们一直在哭：因为人们在祭拜，所以他们一直在哭，Use 因为……所以…… to mark the cause-and-effect relation clearly. [rule: none]
- Expressiveness: At the cemetery description, add a sensory detail about what Mengzi’s family hears, such as “墓地里常常传来伤心的哭声。”
- Expressiveness: After the final sentence, show the mother’s concern through an inner thought, such as “孟子的妈妈很担心，心想：‘这里不适合孩子学习。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly introduce Mengzi at the start and establish the cemetery setting in a way that is easy to picture. Your use of “才……就……” also effectively shows that the family quickly became unable to tolerate the environment.

## Scene 2

> 孟子的妈妈觉得孟子取得良好的教育是很重要的，所以孟子的妈妈认为这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好，就打算跟孟子搬家。孟子的妈妈带孟子不是搬到城市，而是搬到了孟子老师的家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 取得良好的教育：接受良好的教育，取得 does not collocate naturally with education; 接受 describes receiving education. [rule: none]
- 孟子老师：孟子的老师，的 is needed to show that the teacher belongs to孟子 rather than referring to a person called孟子老师. [rule: none]
- 跟孟子搬家：带着孟子搬家，跟 means “together with,” but the mother is the person responsible for taking her child to move. [vocab list]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不太好 -- “这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好”：不利于儿子的教育，states more precisely that the environment can negatively affect his education

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子的妈妈认为：认为，when the same subject continues within one sentence, use zero form rather than repeating the full noun phrase. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子的妈妈带孟子：她带孟子，when the same person was named in the immediately previous sentence and no other woman competes as the referent, use a pronoun. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Conjunction): 不是搬到城市，而是：(delete)，不是……而是…… should correct a conventional or mistaken idea, not simply state the next destination in a story. [rule: bushi_ershi_construction]
- Expressiveness: After the mother decides to move, add her worried inner thought: “如果继续住在墓地旁边，孟子就不能安心学习。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech to show her purpose clearly: “我得给孟子找一个更适合学习的地方。”
- Expressiveness: Describe her feeling before the move: “孟母很着急，担心这里的环境会影响孟子的学习。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly connected the mother’s concern about education with her decision to move, which gives the scene a logical motivation.
- You also used the completed-action marker correctly in 搬到了 to show that the move happened.

## Scene 3

> 孟子跟他的老师从早到晚学习，难怪他的成绩很快就进步了很多。孟子学习的时候，孟子的妈妈不是待在家里就是编织衣服赚钱。以后孟子成为了中国有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 以后：后来，At the start of a new narrative sentence meaning “afterwards,” use this time-ordering word rather than the future-oriented expression. [vocab list]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子学习的时候：他学习的时候，This is a close re-occurrence of the same character from the previous sentence, so a pronoun makes the connection smoother. [rule: none]
- Expressiveness: At the study scene, add one small detail showing his effort, such as “孟子每天认真地跟老师读书，常常忘了休息。”
- Expressiveness: After the improvement in his results, show the mother’s feeling with an action or quotation, such as “孟母看到他的成绩，很高兴地说：‘你真的进步了！’”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the link between sustained study and better results, and the completed-action marker in the improvement phrase is used well. The final sentence also gives the scene a clear long-term outcome.
