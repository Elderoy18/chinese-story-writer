## Scene 1

> 在古代，很多医生不知道怎么用药。所以他们找到了很多植物对药。神农先看到很多病人，他们吃很多植物对药。但是我觉得他们的吃的植物，让他们病人。神农觉得这个植物可能特别是毒。所以他想找一个植物对药，他们可以吃对药。正好，他找到了一把花，他想把花放在茶里，对药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很多植物对药：很多可以做药的植物，The phrase “对药” cannot describe plants serving as medicine; use “可以做药” as a modifier of “植物”. [rule: none]
- 对药：来治病，To express the purpose of eating plants, use “来” followed by an action; “对药” is not a valid purpose phrase here. [rule: none]
- 他们的吃的植物：他们吃的植物，“他们吃的” is the verb-plus-modifier structure for plants that they eat; “他们的” incorrectly adds a possessive marker. [rule: none]
- 让他们病人：让他们生病，“病人” is a noun meaning a sick person, but after “让” the sentence needs a verb phrase; “生病” means to become ill. [rule: none]
- 可能特别是毒：可能特别有毒，“毒” cannot be directly modified by “特别”; “有毒” is the correct predicate meaning poisonous. [teacher rule: du_youdu]
- 一个植物：一种植物，Because the sentence refers to a type of plant that can be used as medicine, “种” is the suitable measure word. [rule: none]
- 对药：做药，“对药” is not a verb phrase; “做药” expresses using the plant as medicine. [rule: none]
- 吃对药：吃药，“对药” does not mean medicine in this context; “吃药” is the correct verb-object phrase. [rule: none]
- 对药：做药，“做药” expresses the intended purpose, while “对药” is not grammatical here. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：一个叫神农的人，On a character’s first mention, briefly introduce the person with a full noun phrase rather than using only the name. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Reference): 这个植物：这些植物，No single plant has been introduced immediately before this sentence; “这些植物” clearly refers back to the many plants the patients ate. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Reference): 他们可以吃药：病人可以吃药，The pronoun could refer either to the doctors or to the patients, so repeat the noun for a clear reference. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Temporality): 先看到：先看到了，“看到” is a completed action in this sequence, so it takes “了”. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: At “很多病人”, add a visible sign of illness, such as “很多病人捂着肚子，脸色很难看。” This helps the reader see why the plants are dangerous.
- Expressiveness: When Shennong finds the flowers, describe their appearance, such as “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花。” This makes the setting more vivid.
- Expressiveness: After he suspects the plants are poisonous, add his thought in quotation marks, such as “神农想：‘这种花能吃吗？’” This shows his careful thinking.

### Encouragement
You clearly set the story in the past with “在古代” and use connectors such as “所以” and “但是” to show relationships between events. You also give Shennong an active role in observing people and searching for a useful plant.

## Scene 2

> 他不知道如果这把花对你的身体好，这就要看如果这把花毒不毒了。神农想找别的植物，但是这很麻烦。正好，他找到了别的花。他一吃花，很快就生病了。他的肚子疼死了。神农不知道他吃不吃灵芝或者夹竹桃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 如果：∅，After a verb meaning “not know,” an embedded yes/no question cannot use 如果; use an A-not-A form instead. [rule: a_not_a_question]
- 好：好不好，The question after 不知道 needs the A-not-A form to ask whether the flower is good for the body. [rule: a_not_a_question]
- 如果：∅，After 看 in this sentence, the following 毒不毒 already forms an A-not-A question, so 如果 is not used. [rule: a_not_a_question]
- 神农不知道他吃不吃灵芝或者夹竹桃：神农不知道自己吃的是灵芝还是夹竹桃，The sentence asks which plant he ate, not whether he ate. Use 是……还是…… to present two noun alternatives, and 自己 clearly refers back to Shennong. [rule: a_not_a_question]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 麻烦 -- “找别的植物，但是这很麻烦”：费事，emphasizes that finding more plants requires substantial time and effort.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 你的身体：他的身体，The narration is about Shennong, so the body should be referred to with the third-person pronoun rather than an unintroduced listener. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Expressiveness: After “他的肚子疼死了,” add a physical reaction to show the severity of his illness, such as “他在地上躺着，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: At the moment he becomes ill, add his worried inner thought: “我会死吗？”
- Expressiveness: When he finds the flowers, describe their appearance to make the setting clearer, such as “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花。”

### Encouragement
- You used “一……就……” effectively to show that Shennong became ill immediately after eating the flower. The short sentence about his stomach pain is also vivid and strongly shows his discomfort.

## Scene 3

> 别的人给了神农一个灵芝。这个植物可以让神农更好。比方说，他会觉得更健康。吃灵芝以后，他可以再走。现在他觉得更好。最后，神农找到了很多植物，他可以用对药。这些植物让神农很高兴。神农想跟很多人分享他的新药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别的人给了神农一个灵芝：一个人给了神农一个灵芝，别的人 identifies people as “others” in contrast with a stated person or group; here the giver is an unknown new character, so introduce this person as 一个人. [teacher rule: bieren]
- 这个植物可以让神农更好：这个植物可以让神农身体好一些，when describing recovery, 更好 alone is too vague for a person; 身体好一些 clearly describes an improvement in health. [rule: none]
- 最后，神农找到了很多植物，他可以用对药：最后，神农找到了很多可以用来做药的植物，用来做药 expresses that the plants have the purpose of being made into medicine; 对药 does not form a correct phrase here. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 再走 -- “他可以再走”：继续赶路，shows that he is able to resume his journey, not simply walk again.
- 新药 -- “神农想跟很多人分享他的新药”：新发现的药，clarifies that the medicine is newly discovered rather than newly manufactured.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农想跟很多人分享他的新药：他想跟很多人分享他的新药，神农 was mentioned in the immediately preceding sentence and no other male character competes for the reference, so a pronoun is more natural for close re-occurrence. [rule: redundancy_delete]
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃灵芝以后：吃了灵芝以后，eating is completed before he is able to continue walking, so use 了 for the completed earlier action. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: After the first sentence, describe the helper briefly so the reader can picture the new character: “这时，一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人走过来。”
- Expressiveness: At “现在他觉得更好,” add a physical reaction to show Shen Nong’s relief: “他的肚子不那么疼了，他松了一口气。”
- Expressiveness: After finding the useful plants, add a short thought or line of speech to show his excitement: “神农高兴地说：‘我终于找到可以做药的植物了！’”

### Encouragement
You use time markers such as 吃灵芝以后, 现在, and 最后 to show a clear sequence of recovery and discovery. You also clearly show that the medicine helps Shen Nong and motivates him to help other people.
