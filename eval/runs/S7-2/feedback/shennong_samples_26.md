## Scene 1

> 很久以前，有一个老男人叫神农。神农在古代每一天看到了别的小镇人生病还是不舒服，所以他决定开始找新的药。他下一天开始爬当地的山脉，找了各种各样的植物。神农试一试每个植物，知道有什么好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别的：删除，this word distinguishes other people from a previously stated group, but no such group has been established here
- 小镇人：小镇上的人们，this refers to a concrete group of people, so use 人们; the location is expressed with 小镇上
- 看到了：看到，a daily routine marked by 每一天 uses the zero aspect form rather than 了
- 下一天：第二天，use this conventional narrative time expression for the day after the previous event
- 找了各种各样的植物：找到了各种各样的植物，找 means “look for,” while 找到 means “find”; the completed discovery needs 找到了
- 每个植物：每种植物，种 is the appropriate measure word for types of plants

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好处 -- “知道有什么好处”：药用价值，specifies that he is learning about each plant’s medicinal use

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农在古代每一天看到：他在古代每一天看到，after the character was named in the immediately preceding sentence, use a pronoun for close re-occurrence
- Coherence (Reference): 神农试一试每种植物：他试一试每种植物，after the recent mention of the same character, a pronoun is more natural than repeating the name
- Coherence (Temporality): 试一试：试了试，this is a completed attempt in a past narrative, so the verb reduplication takes 了
- Coherence (Conjunction): ，知道有什么好处：以后，知道它们有什么好处，use 以后 to show the sequence: he tries the plants first and learns their benefits afterwards
- Expressiveness: At the point where he sees sick people, add his concern and motivation: “他看见很多人生病，心里很难过。”
- Expressiveness: When he decides to search for medicine, add a short thought or line of speech: “神农想：‘我一定要找到能治病的植物。’”
- Expressiveness: Make the mountain-climbing scene more vivid with one concrete detail: “山很高，路也很难走，但是他没有放弃。”

### Encouragement
- You introduced the main character clearly at the beginning and used a logical cause-and-effect connection with 所以.
- Your use of 各种各样的植物 already gives the search scene a wider and more interesting sense of discovery.

## Scene 2

> 比方说，他一次试一试有些植物的叶，吃得又新鲜又甜。煮这些叶以后，他一喝就知道他发现新的茶叶。虽然他找到了很多的新的植物，但是他的旅程有的时候特别是危险。比方说，他一吃灵芝就开始生病。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 特别是危险：特别危险，特别是 normally introduces a particular item that follows it; before an adjective such as 危险, use 特别.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 新的 -- “很多的新的植物”：未尝过的，makes clear that these are plants he has not tasted before；陌生的，emphasizes that the plants are unfamiliar to him.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 煮这些叶以后：煮了这些叶子以后，煮 is completed before 以后, so it needs 了 to mark the finished action.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他一喝就知道他发现新的茶叶：他一喝就发现了这是一种茶叶，发现 is a completed realization in this event, so it takes 了; this wording also directly expresses what he realizes.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他一吃灵芝就开始生病：他一吃灵芝就开始生病了，this is a change of state in a particular story event, so 开始生病 takes 了.
- Expressiveness: After the tea discovery, add a sensory reaction: “他喝了一口，觉得茶水又香又甜。”
- Expressiveness: When describing the dangerous journey, add a concrete setting: “山路又高又陡，他走得很小心。”
- Expressiveness: After the illness begins, show his physical feeling: “他捂着肚子，难受得不得了。”

### Encouragement
You used both the contrast connector 虽然……但是…… and the sequence pattern 一……就……, which help the events move forward clearly. You also included concrete actions such as boiling and drinking the leaves, making the discovery scene easy to imagine.

## Scene 3

> 一个爬山的男人正好看到神农的麻烦，然后给了他一些药。神农最终他的小镇回到，跟别的人分享他的发现。你认为神农的故事真的吗？这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 神农最终他的小镇回到：神农最终回到他的小镇，The verb phrase must come before its destination: the action is returning, and the town is the place returned to.
- 你认为神农的故事真的吗？：你认为神农的故事是真的吗？，The question needs the linking verb before the adjective phrase: the story “is true.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 麻烦 -- “看到神农的麻烦”：身体不适，states more exactly that Shen Nong is feeling unwell, which fits the earlier context.
- 药 -- “给了他一些药”：草药，specifies that the medicine is herbal and fits this traditional story setting.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处。：这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处了。，“这就要看……了” is a fixed structure; its final “了” belongs to the structure rather than marking completion.
- Expressiveness: When the man helps Shen Nong, add one visible detail so the new character is easier to picture: “一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人正好看到神农的麻烦。”
- Expressiveness: After Shen Nong receives the medicine, add his relieved feeling or a short line of speech: “神农觉得好多了，松了一口气，说：‘谢谢你！’”
- Expressiveness: At the return-home moment, show why sharing the discovery matters to him: “神农高兴地回到小镇，跟别人分享自己的发现。”

### Encouragement
You clearly connected the help Shen Nong receives with his later return to the town. The final question also gives the scene an interesting, reflective ending.
