## Scene 1

> 神农和他的家人住在古代，所以不知道什么植物能吃。因为他的家人常常生病了，所以神农和他的哥哥一起去旅行找可以吃的植物。他们去很多不一样的地方，比方说高山、山谷和沙漠。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 神农和他的家人住在古代：在古代，神农和他的家人生活，古代 is a time expression, not a place following 住在; place the time phrase before the clause instead.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 找 -- “神农和他的哥哥一起去旅行找可以吃的植物”：探寻，emphasizes a longer, exploratory search across many places.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：一个叫神农的人，At the first mention, introduce the character with a name and a full noun phrase so the reader knows who he is.
- Coherence (Temporality): 常常生病了：常常生病，A habitual condition uses the zero particle; 了 is not used for a routine action.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们去很多不一样的地方：他们去了很多不一样的地方，This narrative event is presented as completed, so use 了 after 去.
- Expressiveness: After the family’s illness is mentioned, add Shennong’s feeling or motivation, such as “很担心家人的身体”, to make his decision to travel more personal.
- Expressiveness: Make the places more vivid with one descriptive phrase, such as “空气很新鲜的高山” or “又热又干的沙漠”.

### Encouragement
You clearly connect the family’s illness with Shennong’s reason for travelling by using 因为……所以……. The list of mountains, valleys, and deserts also gives the journey a good sense of variety.

### Vocabulary Suggestions

### Coherence & Expressiveness

### Encouragement

## Scene 3

> 夹竹桃是毒的，我想知道灵芝会帮他。”哥哥给神农灵芝。神农吃完了以后开始痊愈了。神农说：“谢谢哥哥，你帮得真好。灵芝我们可以吃，特别是如果我们已经吃了夹竹桃。“

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 夹竹桃是毒的：夹竹桃有毒，Use 有毒 to describe the poisonous property of a plant; 是毒的 is not the natural predicate form here.
- 开始痊愈了：开始好转了，痊愈 means becoming fully well, so it does not fit with 开始; 好转 describes the beginning of improvement.
- 夹竹桃。“：夹竹桃。”，A closing quotation mark must follow the sentence-final punctuation; the original uses an opening quotation mark instead.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 吃 -- “灵芝我们可以吃”：服用，specifies taking it as a medicinal plant rather than simply eating food.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农说：他说，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the immediately preceding sentence is already about the same character and no competing referent intervenes.
- Coherence (Temporality): 哥哥给神农灵芝：哥哥给了神农灵芝，This is a completed action before 神农 eats the medicine, so add 了 after the verb.
- Expressiveness: At the recovery moment, add one physical detail to show that the medicine is working. Example: “神农觉得肚子没有那么疼了。”
- Expressiveness: In the thank-you dialogue, add a stronger feeling of relief. Example: “哥哥，真的谢谢你！我刚才很害怕。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the event sequence: the brother gives the medicine, 神农 takes it, and then he thanks his brother.
- The dialogue “谢谢哥哥，你帮得真好” gives the scene a warm and grateful feeling.
