## Scene 1

> 古代时候，人不知道他们能吃什么植物，常常一吃就死。但是不知道为什么，没有办法。所以有一天，神农出去试一试很多种植物，告诉人什么植物是安全什么是危险。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代时候：古代的时候，The time phrase needs 的 before 时候 when the preceding time word has more than one syllable. [teacher rule: de_shihou]
- 人：人们，This refers to a concrete group of people in the story, so the plural noun is needed rather than the generic noun. [teacher rule: ren_renmen]
- 人：人们，This later occurrence also refers to the people whom 神农 teaches, so use the concrete-group noun. [teacher rule: ren_renmen]
- 什么植物是安全什么是危险：什么植物是安全的，什么植物是危险的，When an adjective follows 是 in this classification structure, it needs 的; the two parallel embedded clauses also need to be separated. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他们能吃什么植物：什么植物能吃，The subject has already appeared in the same clause, so omit the repeated pronoun; this also makes the embedded question more natural. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：一个叫神农的人，This is the character’s first mention, so introduce him with a full noun phrase rather than using only his name. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Temporality): 神农出去试一试很多种植物，告诉：神农出去试很多种植物，告诉了，Testing many kinds is not a brief trial, so do not use the reduplicated verb; in this series of actions, the final completed action takes 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Conjunction): 但是不知道为什么，没有办法：因为不知道为什么，所以没有办法，The second idea gives the reason for having no solution, not a contrast, so use the cause-and-effect connector. [rule: none]
- Expressiveness: At the moment when 神农 tests plants, add a setting detail such as “很多高山、各种各样的植物” to make his journey easier to imagine.
- Expressiveness: Add a short description of 神农’s attitude, such as “不怕危险”, to show why he is willing to test unfamiliar plants.
- Expressiveness: Add his question or thought with the phrase “能不能吃” to show that he is trying to solve the people’s problem.

### Encouragement
You clearly explain the problem that people face and connect it to 神农’s attempt to help. Your use of “常常一吃就死” gives the danger a strong, direct impact.

## Scene 2

> 发现有的植物对身体非常好，特别是茶，让肚子感觉又暖和又清楚。他告诉朋友：“你吃哪种植物，这就要看你的口味。比方说，你爱吃甜的，可能喜欢糖。”他最喜欢吃花，除了夹竹桃以外，什么都花想吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 又暖和又清楚：又暖和又舒服，清楚 describes clarity of thought or perception, not a comfortable physical feeling; 舒服 describes how the body feels. [rule: none]
- 什么都花想吃：什么花都想吃，花 belongs with 什么 to form the object phrase 什么花, and 都 comes before the verb to express “every kind.” [rule: none]
- 你吃哪种植物，这就要看你的口味：你喜欢吃哪种植物？这就要看你喜欢什么口味了，the pattern 这就要看……了 responds to an uncertain question, and the part after 看 needs a question form; 你的口味 alone is not a question form. [rule: zhe_jiu_yao_kan]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 非常好 -- “有的植物对身体非常好”：有益，states more precisely that the plants benefit health.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 发现：发现了，this discovery is a completed event in the narrative, so it needs 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: After mentioning tea, add a sensory detail about its smell or taste, such as “茶水闻起来很香，喝下去以后肚子暖暖的。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought to show the character’s discovery, such as “他想：原来茶不但好喝，还对身体有好处！”
- Expressiveness: Make the example in the quoted speech more vivid with a reaction, such as “你爱吃甜的，看到糖的时候就会很高兴。”

### Encouragement
You give a clear example with tea and connect it to a physical feeling. You also use direct speech to show the character explaining his idea to a friend.

## Scene 3

> 他发现这个口味，一百岁时候，不小心吃了毒的花。一吃就开始死。但是生病时候告诉人什么植物是药，什么植物是毒。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一百岁时候：一百岁的时候，Multi-syllable expressions before “时候” require the particle “的.” [teacher rule: de_shihou]
- 毒的花：有毒的花，The adjective meaning “poisonous” is “有毒的”; “毒的” is not the natural modifier here. [vocab list]
- 开始死：快要死了，Death is normally treated as a change of state rather than a gradual action after “开始”; “快要……了” expresses that the person was close to dying. [rule: none]
- 生病时候：生病的时候，Multi-syllable expressions before “时候” require the particle “的.” [teacher rule: de_shihou]
- 什么植物是毒：什么植物有毒，“有毒” means “to be poisonous”; “是毒” does not describe a plant’s poisonous property naturally. [vocab list]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不小心吃了 -- “不小心吃了毒的花”：误食了，specifies more exactly that he accidentally ate something unsuitable or dangerous.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 人：人们，“人” is a generic noun, while the people receiving this information are a concrete group; use “人们.” [teacher rule: ren_renmen]
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉人们：告诉了人们，This is one completed telling action in the narrative, so the verb needs “了.” [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: After the accidental eating, add a physical reaction to show the danger. Example: “他的肚子马上疼了起来，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Add the character’s worried thought after he becomes seriously ill. Example: “他想：‘我会不会死？’”
- Expressiveness: Make the information he gives people sound more like direct speech. Example: “他说：‘有毒的花不能随便吃。’”

### Encouragement
- You used the pattern “一……就……” effectively to show that the danger happened immediately after eating the flower. You also clearly contrasted plants that are medicine with plants that are poisonous.
