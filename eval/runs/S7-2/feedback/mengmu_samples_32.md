## Scene 1

> 有一天孟子去了墓地玩儿，但是他妈妈觉得这是不合适的地方让孩子玩，所以她决定了他们会搬家找更好的地方。她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福，而且住在这儿教育不太好。因为她望子成龙，所以她会一直搬家才能找到质量好的教育。然后他们搬到了离城市更近的地方。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不合适的地方让孩子玩：不适合孩子玩的地方，适合 must take the person or thing it suits as its object; this phrase means a place that is not suitable for a child to play in. [vocab list]
- 他们会搬家找更好的地方：搬家，去找更好的地方，after 决定, use the action phrase directly; do not repeat a new subject before the action. [teacher rule: jueding_vp]
- 她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福：她担心儿子的童年不会幸福，恐怕 means “I am afraid that” and cannot follow another subject in this way; 担心 expresses that she is worried about her son’s future. [teacher rule: kongpa]
- 而且住在这儿教育不太好：而且这儿的教育环境不太好，use a clear noun phrase for the quality of education in this place; 这儿的教育环境 identifies what is not good. [rule: none]
- 她会一直搬家才能找到质量好的教育：她得一直搬家，才能找到质量好的教育，才能 introduces a necessary condition, so use 得 to express “has to” rather than 会 to express a future prediction. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 更好的地方 -- “搬家，去找更好的地方”：更适合孩子成长的地方, specifies that the new place should support the child’s development；教育环境更好的地方, makes clear that the mother is looking for a place with better educational conditions

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 决定了：决定，决定 is a mental-state verb, so it normally takes the zero particle rather than 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery is mentioned, add one concrete detail about its atmosphere, such as “墓地旁边常常有人哭，四周静悄悄的。” This helps the reader understand why the mother dislikes the location.
- Expressiveness: Show the mother’s concern through an inner thought or quoted speech after she decides to move, such as “孟母心想：‘我不能让孟子在这里长大。’”
- Expressiveness: When the family reaches the new place, add a sensory detail to contrast it with the cemetery, such as “城市附近有很多商店，街上人来人往。”

### Encouragement
You clearly explain the mother’s motivation for moving, and the cause-and-effect connection with 因为……所以…… is used effectively. The final sentence also moves the story forward naturally to the family’s new home.

## Scene 2

> 他们住才一月就又搬家。古代的时候，一般人不喜欢卖东西的人。但是孟子老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿，害得妈妈着急得很，因为她恐怕儿子会成为跟他们很像。她告诉了孟子：他们不是朋友，而是不好的人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 住才一月：才住一个月，In the pattern with a short duration followed by an early next action, place 才 before the verb: 才 + verb + duration. [rule: cai_jiu_construction]
- 古代的时候：在古代，The original redundantly combines a time noun with a “time of” phrase. [vocab list]
- 老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿：老是跟这些卖东西的人玩儿，Both frequency adverbs express repeated action, so using them together is redundant; keep one. [rule: none]
- 她恐怕儿子会：她担心儿子会，恐怕 means “I am afraid that” and cannot take another subject before it in this structure; use 担心 for a character’s worry. [teacher rule: kongpa]
- 成为跟他们很像：变得跟他们很像，成为 normally introduces a new identity or role, while 变得 expresses a change into a similar state or quality. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 卖东西的人 -- “这些卖东西的人”：商人，identifies their occupation more precisely as people who do business or trade.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 妈妈：孟子的妈妈，After other people and information have intervened since the mother was last named, repeat a clear noun phrase rather than using the vague family-role noun alone. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Temporality): 住一个月就又搬家：住了一个月，就又搬家了，Both the completed period of living there and the completed move need 了 to show finished actions. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉了孟子：告诉孟子，A speaking verb that introduces quoted speech uses the zero particle rather than 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: After the mention of the sellers, add a brief sensory detail about the setting, such as “热闹的街市、此起彼伏的叫卖声,” to show why the place attracts Meng子.
- Expressiveness: At “孟子的妈妈着急得很,” add a short thought showing her concern, such as “担心儿子会学会讨价还价,” to make her worry more concrete.
- Expressiveness: The mother’s warning could include a stronger emotional tone, for example “你不能老跟他们玩儿！”, before her explanation about friendship.

### Encouragement
- You clearly show a cause-and-result relationship with 因为 and 害得, which helps the reader understand why the mother is worried.
- The final direct speech gives the scene a clear conflict and makes the mother’s response easy to follow.

## Scene 3

> 你不是当数学家，而是当哲学家，不能当卖东西的人。所以他们又搬家。在他们第三个家，孟子学了怎么祭拜。妈妈感觉这个地方适合他们，告诉了孟子：我们找到了最后的家。在这儿妈妈让儿子从早到晚学习，他难怪成为有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他们第三个家：他们的第三个家，A pronoun used as a possessive modifier before a noun needs the particle 的. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 卖东西的人 -- “不能当卖东西的人”：商人，identifies more precisely a person whose work is selling goods.
- 在这儿 -- “在这儿妈妈让儿子从早到晚学习”：在这个新家里，makes the location more specific by linking it to the family’s new home.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 在这儿妈妈让儿子从早到晚学习：在这儿她让儿子从早到晚学习，The mother was named in the previous sentence and no other female character intervenes, so a pronoun is more natural for this close re-occurrence. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉了孟子：告诉孟子，A speaking verb that introduces direct speech takes the zero particle rather than 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 所以他们又搬家：所以他们又搬家了，The move is a completed event in the narrative, so the final verb needs 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 他难怪成为有名的哲学家：难怪他成为了有名的哲学家，难怪 should introduce the conclusion clause, and becoming a philosopher is a completed change of state, so 成为 takes 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: At the third-home sentence, add one concrete detail about the learning environment, such as “这一次，他们搬到了学校旁边。” This helps the reader picture why the place supports study.
- Expressiveness: In the mother’s speech, show her feeling and explain her judgment with a short quotation, such as “妈妈高兴地说：‘这儿的环境很适合你学习。’”
- Expressiveness: Before the final result, add Mengzi’s response to studying, such as “孟子觉得看书很有意思，开始认真学习。” This makes his later success feel more connected to his own experience.

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect the family’s move, the new home, Mengzi’s study, and his later achievement. You also use direct speech to show the mother’s hopes for her son.
