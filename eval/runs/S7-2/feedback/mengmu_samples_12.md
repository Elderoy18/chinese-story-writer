## Scene 1

> 古代，孟子跟孟母住在墓地的旁边。可是孟母注意，住在墓地的旁边影响孟子。他常常跟朋友们祭拜玩，所以孟母决定他们会搬家。这次，他们住在城市里的商店的旁边，从早到晚很吵。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代：在古代，Use the preposition before a historical time period.
- 孟母注意：孟母注意到，The verb needs 到 when it means that someone has noticed a fact or situation.
- 他常常跟朋友们祭拜玩：他常常跟朋友们玩祭拜的游戏，The verb 玩 cannot directly follow 祭拜 in this structure; this revision clearly expresses that they treat worship as a game.
- 孟母决定他们会搬家：孟母决定搬家，决定 is followed directly by the action that is decided; do not repeat the people who will do it.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 吵 -- “从早到晚很吵”：嘈杂，specifies that the place is full of overlapping noise and voices.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子跟孟母住在墓地的旁边：有一个小男孩叫孟子，他和母亲住在墓地旁边，On first mention, briefly introduce the main character with who he is, rather than assuming the reader already knows him.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 可是孟母注意到：孟母注意到，可是 introduces contrast, but simply noticing a problem after living somewhere is not a contrast.
- Expressiveness: At the cemetery setting, add one concrete detail about the mourners and Mengzi’s reaction, such as: “墓地里有人伤心地哭，孟子觉得很好玩。”
- Expressiveness: When Mengmu notices the problem, show her feelings or thoughts: “孟母皱着眉头想：‘不能再住在这儿了。’”
- Expressiveness: Make the noisy shop area more vivid with sounds and movement: “商人不停地叫卖，街上人来人往。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish both settings in this scene, and the use of 所以 successfully shows why Mengmu makes her decision.

## Scene 2

> 城市也有点儿危险。害得孟母只好一直陪孟子。孟子也没有朋友，难怪他老是看起来不高兴。住在商店的旁边不是适合孟子，而是让他觉得很伤心。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- Nothing to flag.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不高兴 -- “难怪他老是看起来不高兴”：闷闷不乐，describes a continuing, visibly unhappy mood；愁眉苦脸，adds the detail that his sadness shows on his face
- 危险 -- “城市也有点儿危险”：不太安全，states more precisely that the place does not feel safe

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 城市也有点儿危险：城市有点儿危险，"也" needs an earlier comparable proposition; the earlier scene does not establish that another place is dangerous, so the additive connector is unsupported.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 不是适合孟子，而是让他觉得很伤心：不适合孟子，因为这让他觉得很伤心，the second part gives a cause for why the place is unsuitable, rather than correcting one idea with another; use a cause connector instead of the contrast pair.
- Expressiveness: After mentioning the shops, add a concrete sound detail to show why the environment is unpleasant: “从早到晚，外面人来人往，吵得孟子静不下心。”
- Expressiveness: Show Mengzi’s loneliness with a small action and inner feeling: “他看着别的孩子一起玩儿，心里觉得很孤单。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech for Mengmu’s concern: “孟母叹了口气，说：‘这儿也不适合孩子成长。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect the unsafe city environment with Mengmu’s need to stay with Mengzi, and you use “难怪” effectively to show a reason for his unhappiness.

## Scene 3

> 所以，孟母和孟子搬到学校的旁边住。才五岁孟子开始学哲学。在学校，孟子不是看书，就是练习写字。将来，他成为中国的一个非常有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 将来：后来，This is a completed event in a past narrative, so use the narrative time word rather than a future-time word.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 才五岁孟子开始学哲学：才五岁，孟子就开始学哲学，The pattern with a young age is 才 + number + 就 + verb, showing that the action began unusually early.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为中国的一个非常有名的哲学家：成为了中国的一个非常有名的哲学家，This biographical change is completed, so the verb needs 了.
- Expressiveness: At the school setting, add a detail about Mengzi’s focused learning, such as “孟子每天安静地坐在教室里看书。”
- Expressiveness: After the final sentence, add Mengmu’s feeling to show why the move mattered, such as “孟母看到孟子认真学习，心里很高兴。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech from Mengmu after the move, such as “孟母说：‘这里很适合你学习。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the important change from the noisy city environment to the school environment. The sentence “孟子不是看书，就是练习写字” uses a strong pattern to describe his daily learning activities.
