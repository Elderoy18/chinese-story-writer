## Scene 1

> 古代的时候有一个人叫神农。他需要当上医，一看别的人就想帮助他们。但是，他有一个问题，他不知道什么东西能做药。神农听说一个远远的地方有一种植物，什么病人吃都会觉得身体更好。神农三个月走去，找到有植物的地方，他看到一个很漂亮的植物，又香又好看。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，The original repeats the time meaning: the noun already refers to an historical time period, so the added time phrase is redundant. [vocab list]
- 当上医：当上医生，医 is not normally used alone as the noun for a doctor in this sentence; 医生 is the standard noun. [rule: none]
- 别的人：别人，别人 refers to all people other than the person being discussed; adding 的 is not needed here. [teacher rule: bieren]
- 一个远远的地方：一个遥远的地方，远远的 usually describes distance adverbially, while 遥远的 naturally modifies a distant place. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 帮助 -- “一看别人就想帮助他们”：医治，specifies that he wants to give medical treatment, which matches his goal of becoming a doctor.
- 好看 -- “又香又好看”：秀丽，describes the plant’s graceful natural appearance more specifically.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 神农三个月走去，找到有植物的地方：神农走了三个月，找到了有植物的地方，Put the duration after the travel verb, and use 了 to show that the long journey and the finding were completed events. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: At the journey, add a physical detail to show how difficult it is, such as “他走啊走啊，累得腿都酸了。”
- Expressiveness: When the plant appears, add a visual detail to make the setting clearer, such as “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花。”
- Expressiveness: After seeing the plant, show Shen Nong’s reaction, such as “神农觉得很惊喜，马上走过去看。”

### Encouragement
- You introduce Shen Nong clearly at the beginning and keep the events in a logical order. The details “又香又好看” already help the plant feel vivid to the reader.

## Scene 2

> 他觉得，这种一定是做药的花。原来他想错了。吃了以后，神农快生病。 这时一个穿着蓝色衣服的人帮神农，快给他一种红的灵芝。神农吃了以后，他没有病，想起来以前一个人告诉他，灵芝对身体有好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- Nothing to flag.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 帮 -- “一个穿着蓝色衣服的人帮神农”：医治，specifies that the person is treating Shen Nong’s illness；救助，emphasizes giving help in an urgent situation

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他 -- 神农，Two male characters are present in this sentence, so the recipient of the herb should be named rather than using an ambiguous pronoun. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Reference): 他没有病 -- 没有病，After “神农” has appeared earlier in the same sentence and no new subject intervenes, use zero form instead of repeating the pronoun. [rule: redundancy_delete]
- Coherence (Reference): 以前一个人告诉他 -- 以前有一个人告诉过他，A newly mentioned, unspecified person needs the introductory form “有一个人”; “过” marks the earlier experience that Shen Nong now remembers. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Temporality): 快生病 -- 很快就生病了，“很快就” shows that the illness began soon after the eating, and “了” marks the completed change into being ill. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 给 -- 给了，Giving the herb is a completed action in this event sequence, so the verb needs “了”. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: After Shen Nong becomes ill, add a physical symptom to show how serious it feels, such as “神农的肚子疼了起来，脸色也变白了。”
- Expressiveness: At the moment the blue-clothed person arrives, add urgent speech, such as “蓝衣人说：‘快吃这个灵芝！’”
- Expressiveness: In the final sentence, add Shen Nong’s realization, such as “神农想：‘原来灵芝真的可以治病。’”

### Encouragement
You used “这时” effectively to introduce the new helper at an important moment. The scene also has a clear event sequence: Shen Nong eats the flower, becomes ill, receives the herb, and remembers its benefit.

## Scene 3

> 啊，他怎么忘记了？他把灵芝放在包里。一回家就告诉别人。他找到药以后他写下怎么做药在书上。所以人们都知道，那红灵芝对身体好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他把灵芝放在包里：他把灵芝放在了包里，This is a completed action in the narrative, so the verb needs 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- 告诉别人：告诉大家，别人 distinguishes people from a specifically mentioned person; here there is no such contrast, and the intended meaning is to inform everyone. [teacher rule: bieren]
- 一回家就告诉别人：一回家就告诉了别人，The telling is completed in this past event sequence, so 告诉 needs 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- 写下怎么做药在书上：在书上写下了怎么做药，Place phrases such as 在书上 go before the verb here, and the completed act of writing needs 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- 那红灵芝：那种红灵芝，When 那 introduces a particular kind of plant, it needs the classifier 种 before the noun phrase. [teacher rule: zhe_na_mw]
- 对身体好处：对身体有好处，The fixed predicate is 对……有好处; 好处 is a noun and cannot directly follow 对身体 in this structure. [vocab list]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他找到药以后他写下：他找到药以后，写下，The second 他 refers to the same subject within the same sentence, so Chinese naturally uses zero form rather than repeating the pronoun. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Expressiveness: At “啊，他怎么忘记了？”, add a small action and stronger surprise, such as “他拍了拍头，说：‘我怎么把灵芝忘了！’”
- Expressiveness: When he tells everyone, add a short line of speech to show what he has learned, such as “他说：‘这种灵芝可以做药，对身体有好处。’”

### Encouragement
- You used “一……就……” and “所以” to connect the events in a clear order. The scene also clearly shows that Shennong shares his new knowledge with other people.

## End of story

- Before he travels, the story needs to show that 神农 decides to test different plants to learn which ones people can eat safely, and that he climbs mountains to try them.
