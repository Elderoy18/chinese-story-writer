## Scene 1

> 古代的时候人觉得祭拜非常重要，孟子很小的时候，从早到晚一直祭拜，因为他妈妈告诉他祭拜很重要。虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜。他的同学们老是对他开玩笑了。孟子妈妈看了他的同学们对孟子不好的影响，所以孟子和孟子妈妈搬家了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，this phrase redundantly combines a time period with another “time of” expression. [vocab list]
- 人：人们，this refers to a concrete group of people in that period, so the plural noun is needed. [teacher rule: ren_renmen]
- 看了：看到了，here the mother perceives an effect; 看 means “look,” while 看到 means “see/perceive.” [vocab list]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不好 -- “他的同学们对孟子不好的影响”：负面的，specifies that the influence harms Mengzi rather than merely being generally “not good.”

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子妈妈搬家了：孟子和他的妈妈搬家了，after the mother has already been mentioned, use a pronoun-based possessive phrase instead of repeating the full name. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Temporality): 老是对他开玩笑了：老是对他开玩笑，老是 describes a habitual action, so it takes the zero particle rather than completed-action 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Conjunction): 虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜：因为他每天只祭拜，所以他没有朋友，this is a cause-and-effect relationship, so use 因为……所以…… rather than mixing 虽然 with 因为. [rule: coherence_connectives]
- Expressiveness: After the opening statement about worship, add a visual detail about the people’s mood, such as “来祭拜的人都穿着黑衣服，脸上很难过。”
- Expressiveness: At the classmates’ teasing, add a short line of speech to show their attitude, such as “同学们笑着说：‘你每天只会祭拜吗？’”
- Expressiveness: When the mother notices the influence, include her private worry, such as “孟子妈妈心想：‘再这样下去，孩子会越来越不开心。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly show Mengzi’s daily habit and connect it to his mother’s decision to move. The scene also has a clear sequence from his behaviour, to his classmates’ reaction, to the family’s response.

## Scene 2

> 孟子才十一岁，成为一个哲学家。孟子不是祭拜，而是看政治文章，难怪他成为了哲学家。不过，看过很多政治文章对他害得，他有很多问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 孟子才十一岁，成为一个哲学家：孟子才十一岁，就成为了一个哲学家，The pattern with 才 introduces an unexpectedly early age and normally pairs with 就 before the result; 了 marks the completed achievement. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- 对他害得：害得他，害得 follows the action that causes harm and is followed by the person affected plus the harmful result. [teacher rule: haide_structure]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 看 -- “看政治文章”：阅读，specifies reading written articles rather than simply looking at something.
- 问题 -- “他有很多问题”：疑问，makes clear that these are intellectual questions arising from his reading, rather than practical problems.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子不是祭拜：他不是祭拜，Use a pronoun when the same character was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other character competes as the referent. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Expressiveness: After the first sentence, add a reaction from other people to show how unusual his achievement is: “大家都很惊讶。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where he has many questions, show his thoughts with a short quotation: “这个问题到底是什么意思呢？”
- Expressiveness: Add an action that shows his serious attitude toward learning: “他常常一边看文章，一边认真思考。”

### Encouragement
You clearly connect Mengzi’s reading habit with his later achievement, and you use the contrast pattern “不是……而是……” to show a change in his focus.

## Scene 3

> 他一直问问题，但是不能解决那些问题。孟子长大，他开始能解决问题。他常常建议你不是学金融，而是学政治，因为如果你懂政治，你会成为高薪。后来孟子成为教授，他教学生很厉害。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不是学金融，而是学政治：不要学金融，而是学政治，After a verb of advice, use a negative directive to tell the listener not to do something; this is not a correction of a previously stated fact. [teacher rule: bushi_ershi]
- 成为高薪：有一份高薪工作，High salary describes a job or income, so a person cannot “become” high salary. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很厉害 -- “他教学生很厉害”：很有办法，emphasizes that he knows effective ways to teach students；很擅长，states more precisely that teaching is a skill he does well

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 你：人们，This listener is introduced for the first time and has no clear identity in the third-person narrative; use a full noun phrase for a general group. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Reference): 他：删除，After the subject has been named in the same sentence, use zero form for the continuing action rather than repeating the pronoun. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为教授：成为了教授，This later event is presented as a completed change of role, so it needs 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Conjunction): 孟子长大，：孟子长大后，Use 后 to show the sequence: first he grows up, and then he can solve problems. [rule: coherence_connectives]
- Expressiveness: At the point where he finally solves problems, add a brief feeling to show his growth: “孟子想：‘我终于明白了！’”
- Expressiveness: When describing his teaching, add one concrete classroom action: “他耐心地给学生解释问题。”
- Expressiveness: You could add a short line of student speech after his lesson: “学生说：‘孟老师讲得很清楚！’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show Mengzi’s development from being unable to solve questions to becoming someone who can teach others. Your use of words such as “后来” also helps move the narrative forward.

- After the market, the story needs the move to a place near a school, where 孟子 begins to study every day.

### Overall Encouragement
