## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子。他们住在墓地的旁边，所以孟子老是祭拜。孟子不是祭拜，就是跟朋友聊天。他很喜欢他的城市，但是城市有才两个学校，都很小。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，the original repeats the time meaning because both 古代 and 的时候 refer to a time period.
- 有才两个学校：才有两所学校，才 must come before the verb 有, and 所 is the matching measure word for schools.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 小 -- “都很小”：规模都不大, specifies that the schools are small in scale rather than physically small.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子：一个叫孟子的男孩子和他的妈妈住在中国，Introduce the character’s name together with who he is at first mention, rather than naming him only in the next sentence.
- Coherence (Reference): 他很喜欢他的城市：他很喜欢自己的城市，When the city belongs or is closely connected to the same person as the subject, 自己的 makes that relationship clear.
- Expressiveness: Near the cemetery description, add a detail about the visitors’ actions to make the setting vivid, such as “不同的人来墓地祭拜，有的人在哭”.
- Expressiveness: After mentioning that孟子 often worships, add his feeling or reason, such as “孟子觉得祭拜很好玩”.
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought or line of speech from the mother after the school description, such as “妈妈想：‘这里的学校太小了。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduce the main characters and use several useful story connectors, including 所以 and 但是.
- Your paired structure “不是……就是……” is well used to show what孟子 often does.

## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈不是不喜欢他们的城市，而是想孟子有很好的教育。他们搬家到有很多学校的城市。他们从早到晚走路，孟子一直抱怨。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 想孟子有很好的教育：想让孟子受到很好的教育，When expressing a wish for another person’s situation, use the causative pattern 想让 + person + verb phrase; this clearly means that the mother wants him to receive an education.
- 搬家到：搬到了，The relocation is a completed event in the narrative, so use 了 after the verb; 搬到 is the appropriate verb for moving to a destination.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 有很多学校的城市：一个有很多学校的城市，This is a new destination city, so introduce it as a nonspecific noun phrase with 一个 on its first mention.
- Expressiveness: After the mother’s reason for moving, add her private determination, such as: 她心想：“我要让孟子受到很好的教育。”
- Expressiveness: Make the long walk more vivid through physical detail and dialogue, such as: 走了很久以后，孟子累得满头大汗，说：“我走不动了！”
- Expressiveness: Briefly show what the new city looks like, such as: 城市里有好几所学校，路上还有很多背着书包的学生。

### Encouragement
You clearly showed the mother’s motivation for moving and used time and duration expressions effectively to show that the journey was difficult for the child.

## Scene 3

> 他难怪抱怨，他想他的朋友，因为他不要离开他的城市。他的妈妈找到孟子一个老师。跟老师孟子学最重要的文学，所以他成为哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不要离开：不想离开，`不要` usually expresses a command, prohibition, or refusal; `不想` expresses that someone does not want to do something.
- 找到孟子一个老师：给孟子找了一个老师，put the beneficiary before the verb with `给`, and use `了` because the mother’s finding a teacher is a completed action.
- 跟老师孟子学：孟子跟老师学，place the subject `孟子` before the `跟老师` phrase.
- 成为哲学家：成为了哲学家，use `了` after `成为` to mark the completed change of state.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 想 -- “他想他的朋友”：想念，specifically expresses missing friends who are not nearby.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他的朋友：自己的朋友，when the possessor is the same person as the subject, `自己的` makes that relationship clear.
- Coherence (Reference): 他的城市：自己的城市，when the city belongs to the subject of the clause, `自己的` clearly identifies the possessor.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他难怪抱怨：难怪他抱怨，原来，`难怪` introduces a conclusion, and `原来` should introduce the explanation that makes the conclusion understandable.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为：也，missing his friends and not wanting to leave his city are two parallel reasons for his complaint, so `也` connects them rather than presenting the second reason as the cause of the first.
- Expressiveness: After the mother finds a teacher, add her positive feeling: `孟子的妈妈很高兴，觉得孟子终于可以好好学习了。`
- Expressiveness: Show how Mengzi studies with one concrete action: `孟子认真地听老师讲课，还常常问问题。`
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of dialogue from the teacher: `老师说：“孟子，你要认真学习。”`

### Encouragement
You clearly show Mengzi’s wish to stay near his friends and connect his later study to becoming a philosopher. Your scene also has a clear movement from the mother’s action to Mengzi’s learning.
