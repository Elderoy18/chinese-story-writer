## Scene 1

> 这是古代的故事。孟子小的时候，他的爸爸很早就去世了。从他的家到墓地很近，所以他每天老是从早到晚去墓地跟朋友们玩玩儿。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 从他的家到墓地很近：他的家离墓地很近，Use 离…很近 to express the distance between two places; 从 marks a starting point rather than a distance relationship.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子小的时候，他的爸爸很早就去世了：古代有一个小男孩叫孟子。他小的时候，他的爸爸很早就去世了，On a character’s first mention, introduce the name together with a brief identity; after that, use a pronoun for the same character.
- Expressiveness: At the cemetery sentence, add one visible detail to create the setting. Example: 墓地里有人穿着黑衣服哭。
- Expressiveness: After the playing action, add Mengzi’s thought or feeling to show why he likes going there. Example: 孟子觉得他们祭拜的样子很有趣。

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the cause-and-effect relationship with 因为…所以…, and the routine action is easy to understand. The completed-action marker in 去世了 is also used well.

## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈觉得在墓地玩儿，害得，所以他们搬家。搬家对孟子有好的影响，他才五岁，可是每天不是写字就是帮妈妈的工作。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在墓地玩儿，害得：孟子在墓地玩儿会带来很多麻烦，The embedded action needs a clear subject, and the original use of “害得” is incomplete: this structure must be followed by the person affected and a harmful result.
- 帮妈妈的工作：帮妈妈工作，After “帮”, the person being helped is followed directly by the action; “的” cannot connect “妈妈” to “工作” in this meaning.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好的 -- “搬家对孟子有好的影响”：积极的，specifies that the effect supports his development or learning in a positive way.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他的妈妈：孟子的妈妈，This is the mother’s first mention, so use the full relationship phrase with the character’s name rather than a possessive pronoun.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们搬家：他们搬家了，This move is presented as a completed story event, so add “了” after the verb.
- Expressiveness: After the mother’s concern about the cemetery, add her thought to show why she decides to move, such as “要是一直住在这儿，麻烦会越来越多。”
- Expressiveness: In the final sentence, show how Mengzi writes or helps rather than only listing the activities, such as “他认真地写字，还帮妈妈做事。”

### Encouragement
- You used “才五岁” effectively to emphasize Mengzi’s young age, and the “不是……就是……” pattern gives the final sentence a clear sense of his busy daily routine.

## Scene 3

> 他很聪明的人，难怪他成为有名的人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他很聪明的人：他是一个很聪明的人，With a noun such as 人 as the description of the subject, the sentence needs 是 and an appropriate measure word; 很聪明 correctly modifies 人 with 的.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为有名的人：成为了有名的人，This is a completed change of state in the story, so 成为 takes 了 to mark completion.
- Expressiveness: Before the final result, add a small detail about his study habits, such as “孟子在学校里认真地看书学习。” This shows why he is intelligent rather than only stating it.
- Expressiveness: Add a feeling or line of speech from his mother before the conclusion, such as “孟母高兴地说：‘这儿的环境很适合孩子。’” This makes the ending warmer and more vivid.

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect Mengzi’s intelligence with his later success by using 难怪, which creates a logical ending for the scene.
