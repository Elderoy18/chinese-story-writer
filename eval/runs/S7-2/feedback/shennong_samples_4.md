## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个男人，他的名字是神农。他看到了有很多人不知道哪个植物好吃，也不知道什么植物对你的身体有好处。这个问题神农觉得很糟糕，所以神农去了吃每种植物。他吃了一种植物以后，就写了这个植物对你有没有好处。有的时候，神农吃了很好吃的植物。有的时候，他吃了很危险的植物。他吃完了每种植物以后，会来跟他的朋友聊到他吃的植物了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，Both parts refer to time, so the original is redundant; use the shorter time expression.
- 哪个植物好吃：哪些植物能吃，The issue is whether a plant is edible, not whether it tastes good; use the modal verb form for “can be eaten.”
- 去了吃：去吃了，When an action is followed by a result or another verb, place 了 after the final verb phrase.
- 写了：写下了，写下 means to record information, while bare 写 only means to write; it fits recording the plant’s effects.
- 有的时候，神农吃了很好吃的植物：有的时候，神农吃很好吃的植物，A repeated or habitual action after 有的时候 takes the zero particle, not 了.
- 有的时候，他吃了很危险的植物：有的时候，他吃很危险的植物，A repeated or habitual action after 有的时候 takes the zero particle, not 了.
- 聊到他吃的植物：聊他吃过的植物，聊 takes the discussion topic directly; 聊到 usually marks reaching a time or topic, rather than introducing the topic in this structure.
- 植物了：植物，The modal verb 会 describes a routine or possibility, so the following action does not take 了.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 糟糕 -- “这个问题神农觉得很糟糕”：严重，shows that the problem has important harmful consequences；棘手，emphasizes that the problem is difficult to solve.
- 很危险 -- “他吃了很危险的植物”：有毒，states more exactly what makes the plants dangerous in this story.
- 好吃 -- “神农吃很好吃的植物”：可口，gives a more vivid description of food or plants with a pleasant taste.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 对你的身体：对身体，The pronoun shifts from “many people” to an unspecified reader, so it has no clear referent; use a general expression instead.
- Coherence (Reference): 所以神农去了吃每种植物：所以去吃了每种植物，Within the same sentence, 神农 has already been named, so use the zero form rather than repeating the name.
- Expressiveness: After “这个问题神农觉得很糟糕,” add his concern to show his motivation, such as “他很担心大家会生病。”
- Expressiveness: At “很危险的植物,” describe a physical reaction to make the danger vivid, such as “他的肚子突然疼了起来。”
- Expressiveness: When he talks with his friends, add a short line of speech, such as “这种植物不能吃，会让人不舒服。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show Shennong’s repeated testing and recording of plants, and the repeated time phrase helps the reader understand his routine.  
- You also use sequence expressions such as “以后” effectively to show the order of his actions.

## Scene 2

> 一个人问神农，哪个植物好吃。神农说，这就要看你的口味了。要是你喜欢吃甜的，有很多甜的水果，比方说苹果、西瓜和梨。一个别的人问神农，哪个植物对你的身体有好处。神农说，有很多种植物对你的身体有好处，比方说小白菜、黄瓜、茶和菠菜。一个别的人问神农，哪个植物对你的身体有坏处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 哪个植物（all three occurrences）：哪种植物，Use “哪种” when asking about a type or category of plant; the examples in the answers are plant types, not individual plants.
- 这就要看你的口味了：这就要看你喜欢什么口味了，The pattern “这就要看……了” needs a question form after “看,” such as a wh-question, to express what the answer depends on.
- 一个别的人（both occurrences）：另一个人，“别人/别的” distinguishes other people from the speaker or a stated person and does not naturally follow “一个” here; “另一个人” introduces one additional person.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 问 -- “一个人问神农”：向神农请教, emphasizes that the person is seeking knowledge from Shennong as an expert.
- 好吃 -- “哪种植物好吃”：可口, describes food that tastes pleasant and suitable to eat.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: When the first person asks about food, add a small reason or feeling to make the question sound more natural, such as “我想吃点儿甜的，哪种植物好吃？”
- Expressiveness: Add a short response from Shennong after naming the fruits, such as “苹果和西瓜都很甜，你可以试一试。”
- Expressiveness: For the final question, show the questioner’s concern with a brief action or expression, such as “那个人皱着眉头问：‘哪种植物对身体有坏处？’”

### Encouragement
- You used clear dialogue to organize the scene, and each question stays focused on one topic.
- Your examples of fruits, vegetables, and tea make Shennong’s answers concrete and easy to understand.

## Scene 3

> 神农说，有一天，我吃了一个黄色的花，名字是夹竹桃。我一吃夹竹桃，就觉得这种植物对我的脑有坏处。可是，一个灵芝和一个男人正好在我的旁边，我说，麻烦你把那个植物给我吃一下。我一吃这个灵芝就好了。所以，你得小心吃植物，因为有的植物对你的健康有坏处，特别是夹竹桃。因为夹竹桃有毒。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一个黄色的花：一朵黄色的花，Use the measure word “朵” for an individual flower; “个” is not the matching measure word here.
- 我的脑：我的脑子，When referring to a person’s brain in this everyday expression, “脑子” is the natural noun; “脑” is normally used in compounds or more formal anatomical language.
- 一个灵芝：一株灵芝，Use a matching measure word for a plant rather than the general measure word “个”.
- 小心吃植物：吃植物的时候得小心，The original makes “小心” describe the manner of eating. This revised order clearly means that a person should be cautious when eating plants.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好了 -- “我一吃这个灵芝就好了”：痊愈了，states more precisely that the illness completely went away.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为夹竹桃有毒。：特别是夹竹桃，因为它有毒。，A “因为” clause gives a cause and needs to connect to the statement it explains; here it can directly explain why oleander needs special caution.
- Expressiveness: After the character eats the flower, add a physical reaction to show the danger, such as “我的肚子突然疼了起来。”
- Expressiveness: Make the man more memorable with one visible detail and an urgent action, such as “一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人赶快走过来。”
- Expressiveness: Add a worried thought before the man helps, such as “我会不会死？”

### Encouragement
- You use the pattern “一……就……” effectively to show that the effects of eating each plant happen immediately.
- Your final warning gives the scene a clear takeaway and explains that some plants can harm health.
