## Scene 1

> 孟子小的时候，从早到晚都跟朋友玩。孟子和他的朋友喜欢玩在墓地里，装祭拜。孟子五岁的时候，跟妈妈搬家。他们搬家不是因为孟子玩得太多，而是因为妈妈认为他们的家离墓地太近。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 玩在墓地里：在墓地里玩，A location phrase with 在 should come before the action verb when it tells where the action happens.
- 装祭拜：假装祭拜，假装 means pretending to carry out an action; 装 does not naturally combine with 祭拜 in this context.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 玩 -- “从早到晚都跟朋友玩”：嬉戏，emphasizes lively, carefree play between children.
- 朋友 -- “从早到晚都跟朋友玩”：玩伴，specifies that these friends are companions he plays with.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子小的时候：有一个小男孩叫孟子。孟子小的时候，On a character’s first mention, introduce both the name and who the character is, rather than assuming the reader already knows the name.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和他的朋友：他和朋友，For a close re-occurrence, use a pronoun for the already mentioned main character; the possessive pronoun is unnecessary because the friends are already clear from the sentence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 跟妈妈搬家：跟妈妈搬了家，This is a completed move, so the separable verb takes 了 after the verb: 搬了家.
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery sentence, add one concrete action to show how the children copied the adults, for example: “他们笑着学大人鞠躬。”
- Expressiveness: In the final sentence, show the mother’s worry through an inner thought, for example: “妈妈很担心，觉得这儿不适合孩子长大。”

### Encouragement
You used “从早到晚都” effectively to show how often Mengzi played. The final sentence also uses the contrast pattern “不是……而是……” clearly to explain why the family moved.

## Scene 2

> 妈妈认为，住离墓地太近，害得孟子的心气。孟子和孟母搬到子思老师的家，跟子思老师住。孟母帮子思打扫房间和做饭。子思帮孟母教她的儿子。因为孟子现在没有朋友，他老是读书。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 住离墓地太近：住得离墓地太近，Use 得 after 住 to introduce the degree or distance description.
- 害得孟子的心气：害得孟子心情不好，害得 must be followed by the affected person and a harmful result clause; a possessive noun phrase alone does not state the result.
- 老是读书：一直读书，老是 has a negative, complaining tone, while this sentence neutrally describes Mengzi’s regular reading.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 帮 -- “孟母帮子思打扫房间和做饭”：替，makes it more specific that Mengmu performs these household tasks for Zisi.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟母搬到子思老师的家：他们搬到子思老师的家，Mengzi and his mother were both mentioned in the immediately preceding sentence, and no other pair competes as the referent, so a pronoun is more natural for close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬到子思老师的家：搬到了子思老师的家，The move is a completed story event, so 搬到 takes 了 after the resultative complement.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为孟子现在没有朋友，他老是读书：因为孟子现在没有朋友，所以他老是读书，Add 所以 to complete the cause-and-effect connector pair.
- Expressiveness: After the first sentence, add Mengmu’s worried reaction, such as “孟母皱着眉头，心里很担心。” This shows why she wants to change Mengzi’s environment.
- Expressiveness: At the point where Zisi teaches Mengzi, add a short line of dialogue, such as “子思说：‘孟子，你要认真读书。’” This makes the teacher’s role more vivid.

### Encouragement
You clearly introduce 子思老师 when he first appears, so the reader can understand who Mengzi and Mengmu move in with. You also use 因为 to show the reason for Mengzi’s reading, which gives the scene a clear logical direction.

## Scene 3

> 子思认为，对孩子来说，读书最重要，特别是学哲学。孟子不是练习写文章，就是看哲学书。孟子从五岁一直学哲学。他才五岁就学哲学，难怪他成为很有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 从五岁一直学哲学：从五岁开始一直学哲学，Use 从 + time + 开始 to mark the starting point of an action that continues afterward.
- 成为很有名的哲学家：成为了很有名的哲学家，This describes a completed life outcome, so add 了 after the verb.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 重要 -- “读书最重要”：首要，specifies that reading is the highest-priority thing for children in this context.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子从五岁开始一直学哲学：他从五岁开始一直学哲学，Since 孟子 was the clear subject in the immediately previous sentence and no other character intervenes, use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子才五岁就学哲学：他才五岁就学哲学，Since the same character remains the clear subject from the preceding sentence, use a pronoun rather than repeating the name.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他才五岁就学哲学，难怪他成为了很有名的哲学家：难怪他成为了很有名的哲学家，原来他才五岁就学哲学，Use 难怪 for the surprising result first, followed by 原来 to introduce its explanation.
- Expressiveness: After the sentence about reading philosophy, add Mengzi’s interest or concentration: “他觉得哲学很有意思，读得很认真。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of praise from 子思 after seeing Mengzi study: “子思高兴地说：‘孟子，你读书真认真！’”
- Expressiveness: Make the final achievement more vivid by adding how others viewed him: “后来，很多人都来听他讲哲学。”

### Encouragement
You used the “才……就……” pattern accurately to show that Mengzi started learning at a very young age. Your “不是……就是……” sentence also clearly shows his two main study activities.
