## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有很多人不知道什么植物能吃，什么植物不能吃。有很多人吃植物以后就吃坏肚子了，因为他们吃了毒。那个时候，有一个老人叫神农，他什么植物都觉得好吃，不怕生病，不怕毒。他希望别人也可以吃好吃的植物。可是他知道，如果他们还怕吃毒，不会吃他喜欢的植物。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，The time noun already expresses the historical period, so adding a second “time of” phrase is redundant.
- 吃了毒：吃了有毒的植物，The noun refers to poison itself, but this scene is about people eating plants; the corrected phrase names the plants and describes them as poisonous.
- 怕吃毒：怕吃有毒的植物，The noun alone means poison and does not identify the thing being eaten; the corrected phrase clearly refers to poisonous plants.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好吃 -- “他什么植物都觉得好吃”：可口，more specifically describes a pleasant taste in this sentence.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 有很多人吃植物以后就吃坏肚子了：他们吃植物以后就吃坏肚子了，The group was introduced in the previous sentence and no other group intervenes, so a pronoun makes this close re-occurrence smoother.
- Expressiveness: After the people become ill, add a concrete physical detail to show their discomfort, for example: “他们捂着肚子，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where Shennong likes the plants, add a short thought to show his personality, for example: “他想：这些植物看起来都很好吃！”
- Expressiveness: You could make the danger clearer through a brief line of speech from the people, for example: “他们说：‘我们不敢再随便吃植物了。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly introduce Shennong with both his age group and name, so the reader can follow who he is. You also use a cause-and-effect explanation for why people get sick, which makes the events easy to understand.

## Scene 2

> 所以，他上山下山找植物，吃了很多样子的植物。他是第一人，找到了茶。他把所有的植物写下来了。他一直都吃了，他连毒都能吃。可是他还没吃了一个叫夹竹桃的植物。他找到了那个像星星的植物，觉得很漂亮，马上就吃下了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很多样子的植物：很多种植物，样子 means an object’s appearance or form; 种 is the measure word used to count different kinds of plants.
- 第一人：第一个人，when referring to the first person to do something, use the ordinal phrase with the measure word 个.
- 一个叫夹竹桃的植物：一种叫夹竹桃的植物，种 is the appropriate measure word for a type of plant.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 上山下山 -- “他上山下山找植物”：翻山越岭，emphasizes travelling across many mountains while searching.
- 写下来 -- “他把所有的植物写下来了”：记录下来，emphasizes preserving his discoveries as a record.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 一直都吃了：一直都吃，an ongoing or habitual action with 一直都 takes the zero particle, not 了, because it does not present the action as completed.
- Coherence (Temporality): 还没吃了：还没吃过，use 没……过 to say that someone has not yet had the experience of eating something; 了 cannot follow 没 in this completed-action meaning.
- Expressiveness: At the discovery of the flower, add visual detail from the scene: “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花。”
- Expressiveness: After he sees the flower, show his thought before he eats it: “他想：这么漂亮的花，一定很好吃。”
- Expressiveness: After the final action, add a physical reaction to make the danger vivid: “他一吃，肚子就疼了起来。”

### Encouragement
- You used a clear sequence of actions: searching, finding tea, recording plants, and then discovering another plant. Your use of 找到了 and 写下来了 also shows that you can mark completed discoveries and actions well.

## Scene 3

> 可是夹竹桃的毒很重，让他肚子很痛。他在地上躺着，觉得他快会死了。可是正好有别人找到他了，给了他一个像伞的植物，叫灵芝。他吃灵芝以后就觉得很好，不再生病了。他测量起来了，像星星的植物是毒，像伞的植物是药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 快会死了：快要死了，Use the imminent pattern with “快要……了” to show that death seems about to happen.
- 有别人：有人，“别人” distinguishes other people from a stated person or group; here the helper is simply an unspecified person.
- 找到他了：找到了他，With a resultative verb, place “了” after the resultative complement: “找到 + 了 + object.”
- 测量起来了：发现，“测量” means measuring physical size and does not express learning that a plant is poisonous or medicinal; “发现” fits this realization.
- 是毒：有毒，“毒” is a noun here, while “有毒” means that a plant is poisonous.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 毒 -- “夹竹桃的毒很重”：毒性很强，names the plant’s poisonous potency more precisely in this context.
- 很好 -- “他吃灵芝以后就觉得很好”：好多了，specifically shows that his condition improved after eating the plant；舒服多了，describes his physical relief more vividly.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他：自己，After the same character is the subject of “觉得,” “他” can sound like another male person; “自己” clearly refers back to the subject.
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃灵芝以后：吃了灵芝以后，The eating is completed before the later feeling of improvement, so use “了” for the completed action.
- Expressiveness: At the moment of pain, add a physical detail to show how serious it feels, for example: “他在地上躺着，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: When the helper arrives, add one identifying detail and a short line of speech, for example: “一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人走过来，说：‘快吃这个！’”
- Expressiveness: After the discovery, let the character state what he has learned, for example: “这种像伞一样的植物可以做药。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the movement from pain, to help, to recovery. The final comparison between the poisonous plant and the medicinal plant also makes the scene easy to understand.

### Overall Encouragement
