## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男人觉得植物很有意思。他叫神农。有的植物吃了以后他的肚子疼死了，可是有的吃了以后很舒服。他找很多植物。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 already expresses a time period, so adding 的时候 is redundant.
- 他找很多植物：他找到了很多植物，找 means “to look for,” while 找到 means that he successfully found the plants; 了 shows this narrative action was completed.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一个男人觉得植物很有意思。他叫神农：一个叫神农的男人觉得植物很有意思，On a character’s first mention, introduce the name together with who the character is, rather than naming him only in the following sentence.
- Expressiveness: After the first sentence, add an inner thought to show why he is interested in plants, such as: “他想知道这些植物有什么用。”
- Expressiveness: When describing the painful result, add a stronger physical detail, such as: “他的肚子疼得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: At the searching spot, add a simple action detail, such as: “他走过高山，仔细看着路边的植物。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly contrast the two kinds of plants with 有的……可是有的……, which makes the different results easy to understand. Your use of 吃了以后 also helps show the order of events.

## Scene 2

> 凉山很大的山，这也找植物。一天，他找到黄色的花。他听说你跟黄色的花做茶很有用。比方说，你的头发疼死，这冰茶是药。他一喝茶就很不舒服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 凉山很大的山：凉山是一座很大的山，A noun used as the predicate needs the linking verb and an appropriate measure word here.
- 这也找植物：他也在那里找植物，A place should be expressed with a location phrase such as 在那里, and the action needs a clear actor.
- 跟黄色的花做茶：用黄色的花做茶，跟 means “with/together with” a person or thing, while 用 marks the material used to make something.
- 头发疼死：头疼得要命，头发 means “hair,” which cannot feel pain; use 头疼 for a headache, and use 得 to link the degree complement.
- 这冰茶：这杯冰茶，Demonstratives such as 这 need a measure word before a countable noun.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 大 -- “凉山很大的山”：高大，describes the mountain’s large, tall shape more specifically.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 黄色的花：一种黄色的花，This is the first mention of this flower, so introduce it as a new object with a measure word.
- Coherence (Reference): 你：删除，你 refers to the listener, but no listener has been introduced; removing it keeps the statement about making tea general.
- Coherence (Temporality): 找到：找到了，Finding the flower is a completed event in the story, so the completed-action marker 了 is needed.
- Expressiveness: When the flower appears, add one visual detail to make the setting easier to imagine: “那儿开着金黄色的花，像星星一样。”
- Expressiveness: After drinking the tea, show Shennong’s physical reaction and feeling: “他觉得肚子疼了起来，心里很害怕。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought to show his uncertainty: “这种茶不是很有用吗？为什么我这么难受？”

### Encouragement
- You created a clear sequence: Shennong finds a flower, hears about making tea, drinks it, and then feels unwell.
- You also used the 一……就…… pattern effectively to show that the discomfort happened immediately after drinking the tea.

## Scene 3

> 糟糕，这黄色的花是夹竹桃，特别是毒。正好在这儿，灵芝药的夹竹桃他吃完了，也写一个课文的植物。谢谢神农。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 特别是毒：特别有毒，Use 有毒 to describe a plant that contains poison; 毒 is not the correct adjectival form in this position.
- 灵芝药的夹竹桃：灵芝和夹竹桃，When naming two plants, use 和 to coordinate them; 的 would incorrectly make 灵芝药 a modifier of 夹竹桃.
- 一个课文的植物：一篇关于植物的文章，Use the measure word 篇 for an article, and place the topic before the noun as 关于植物的文章.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 也写：也写了，Writing the article is presented as a completed action, so the verb needs 了.
- Expressiveness: After the yellow flower appears, add a visual detail: “这朵花像星星一样，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: When the flower is identified as dangerous, add Shennong’s thought: “神农想：‘我会死吗？’”
- Expressiveness: At the end, make the praise more personal: “神农真了不起！”

### Encouragement
You clearly identified the yellow flower as 夹竹桃 and included its danger. Using “糟糕” also gives the scene an immediate worried feeling.

## End of story

### Content & Completeness
- At the beginning, the story needs to explain that people often became sick because they did not know which plants were safe to eat.
- Before 神农 starts looking for plants, the story needs to show that he decides to test plants so people can know which ones are safe and which ones are poisonous.
- After finding 茶, 神农 should record it as a useful plant; he should not become sick from drinking it.
- When 神农 tries 夹竹桃, he should faint from its poison.
- After 神农 faints, a person in blue should arrive, give him 灵芝, and 神农 should feel better after eating it.
- After 神农 feels better, a person in brown should come and talk with him.
- At the end, 神农 should clearly record both 灵芝 and 夹竹桃, including that 灵芝 is helpful and 夹竹桃 is poisonous.

### Overall Encouragement
- You included 神农, plants, 茶, 夹竹桃, and 灵芝, so you remembered several important parts of the story. You also showed that some plants can help people and some can be dangerous.
