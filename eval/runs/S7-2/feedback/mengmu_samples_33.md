## Scene 1

> 一天，很久很久以前，孟子和他妈妈住在中国。孟子妈妈的工作是祭拜，孟子不高兴，因为他说我们老是搬家，为什么？他妈妈说，从早到晚，我一直祭拜，很多墓地得我祭拜。所以我们得去很多不同的地方。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一天，很久很久以前：很久很久以前的一天，A broad past-time expression should modify the specific day in this order. [rule: time_expression]
- 孟子不高兴，因为他说我们老是搬家，为什么？：孟子不高兴，因为他说：“我们为什么老是搬家？”，Direct speech after a speaking verb needs a colon and quotation marks; the question word also naturally comes before the subject in this question. [rule: none]
- 很多墓地得我祭拜：我得去很多墓地祭拜，The subject comes before the modal verb, and the modal verb must come before the verb phrase it modifies. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不高兴 -- “孟子不高兴”：不满，specifies that he is dissatisfied with having to move repeatedly.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和他妈妈住在中国：有一个小男孩叫孟子，他和妈妈住在中国，On first mention, introduce the character with both a name and a brief identity before using a pronoun or family relationship. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Expressiveness: At the point where Mengzi feels unhappy, add a physical reaction to show his emotion: “孟子皱着眉头，觉得搬家太累了。”
- Expressiveness: In the mother’s explanation, add her tired feeling to make her reason more vivid: “我每天都要去很多墓地祭拜，真的很累。”
- Expressiveness: When mentioning the cemeteries, add one simple setting detail: “墓地里很安静，只有风吹树叶的声音。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show why Mengzi is unhappy and give his mother a logical reason for needing to travel. The dialogue helps the reader understand both characters’ viewpoints.

## Scene 2

> 孟子想，难怪我们常常搬家，原来我妈妈很忙。可是常常搬家害得孟子很难找到朋友。每次搬家孟子也觉得新的地方不是太热就是太冷，不是太大就是太小。在哪的地方，他都不高兴。孟子想帮助妈妈祭拜，可是他六岁，所以不能祭拜。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在哪的地方：不管在哪个地方，The location phrase needs the measure word 个 after 哪 before 地方; 不管……都…… naturally expresses that the result is the same in every place. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 新的 -- “孟子也觉得新的地方不是太热就是太冷”：陌生的，emphasizes that each place feels unfamiliar to Meng子 after moving.
- 不高兴 -- “他都不高兴”：不满意，more precisely shows that he is dissatisfied with the place’s temperature and size；闷闷不乐，shows a continuing low mood rather than a general lack of happiness.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子很难找到朋友：他很难找到朋友，孟子 was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no competing male character has appeared, so a pronoun is more natural for a close re-occurrence. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Reference): 每次搬家孟子也觉得：每次搬家他也觉得，use a pronoun because the same character was mentioned in the previous sentence. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子想帮助妈妈祭拜：他想帮助妈妈祭拜，use a pronoun for this close re-occurrence of the same character. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Expressiveness: After “不是太热就是太冷”, add a short thought to show Meng子的 reaction, such as “他心想：‘这里怎么这么冷？’”
- Expressiveness: At “想帮助妈妈祭拜”, add his mixed feeling with a simple inner thought, such as “他很想帮妈妈，可是觉得自己还太小。”

### Encouragement
You used the pattern “难怪……，原来……” accurately to connect Meng子的 discovery with his mother’s busy work. You also gave clear, contrasting details about why the new places make him unhappy.
