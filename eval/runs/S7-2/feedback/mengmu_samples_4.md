## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，孟子和孟母住了在靠墓地的家。孩子才五岁就每天在墓地祭拜了。在那个城市，孟母从早到晚都找工作，可是找不到，害得孟母没有钱。难怪她跟孩子搬家，原来买不了房。他们搬了到别一个城市找工作。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，this phrase redundantly combines a historical time period with a time-expression ending; use the shorter form for a historical setting. [vocab list]
- 跟孩子搬家：带着孩子搬家，this action means the mother takes the child with her when moving; the listed incorrect pattern uses 跟 where 带着 is needed. [vocab list]
- 别一个城市：另一个城市，别的 distinguishes other items from a stated item, while 另一个 means one additional or different city. [teacher rule: bieren]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 靠 -- “住在靠墓地的家”：靠近，more explicitly expresses that the home is near the cemetery；紧挨着，specifies that the home is directly beside the cemetery.
- 找不到 -- “可是找不到”：始终找不到，emphasizes that the unsuccessful search continued over time.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟母：有一个小男孩叫孟子，他和母亲，at a character’s first mention, introduce the character with a name and a clear identity before using shorter references. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Reference): 孩子：他，after the boy was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other male character competes, use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Reference): 那个城市：他们住的城市里，this is the first mention of the city, so a full noun phrase is needed rather than a demonstrative reference with no established referent. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Reference): 害得孟母没有钱：害得她没有钱，within the same sentence, the mother has already been named, so a pronoun avoids unnecessarily repeating the noun. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Temporality): 住了在：住在，residence is a continuing state here, so it does not take completed-action 了; also, 了 cannot be placed before the location complement. [teacher rule: le_placement]
- Coherence (Temporality): 每天在墓地祭拜了：每天在墓地祭拜，actions marked by 每天 are routines, so they use the zero particle rather than completed-action 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬了到：搬到了，place 了 after the verb and before the location result; the move is presented as completed. [teacher rule: le_placement]
- Expressiveness: At the cemetery scene, add the boy’s interest in what he sees: “孟子觉得祭拜很有趣，就学着别人祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: Show the mother’s feeling after the unsuccessful job search: “孟母很着急，担心没有钱照顾孩子。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought before the move to make her decision vivid: “孟母心想：‘我们得搬到一个能找到工作的地方。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the family’s problem and connect it to their move, which gives the scene a strong cause-and-effect direction.
- Your use of “才五岁就” effectively highlights how young the child is.

## Scene 2

> 这个城市老是很吵，不是人吵架，就是都叫，害得孟子一直不能睡觉。在那儿，孟母也找不到工作。孟子和妈妈要又一个次搬了家。在第三个城市，孟母找到工作，在商店做衣服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 又一个次搬了家：再搬一次家，After the modal verb 要, a future repeated action uses 再 and follows the pattern V. + number + measure word + object; because the move has not happened yet, completed-action 了 is not used. [teacher rule: zai_structure]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 吵 -- “这个城市老是很吵”：嘈杂，specifies that the city is full of mixed, disturbing noise.
- 不能睡觉 -- “孟子一直不能睡觉”：睡不着，more precisely shows that he tries to sleep but cannot fall asleep because of the noise.
- 做衣服 -- “在商店做衣服”：缝衣服，specifies the clothing-making action as sewing.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和妈妈：他们，Both characters were mentioned recently and no other possible pair competes as the referent, so the plural pronoun is more natural. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Temporality): 孟母找到工作：孟母找到了工作，Finding the job is a completed action before the ongoing work in the shop, so it needs completed-action 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Conjunction): 不是人吵架，就是都叫：不是有人吵架，就是有人大叫，The paired connector expresses alternative noisy events; each half needs a complete event with a clear subject. [rule: coherence_connectives]
- Expressiveness: At the description of the noisy city, add a sound detail such as “从早到晚，街上有人吵架，也有人大声叫卖。” This makes the noise easier for the reader to imagine.
- Expressiveness: At the decision to move, show Meng Mu’s worry through speech: “孟母着急地说：‘这儿太吵了，我们得再搬一次家。’”
- Expressiveness: At the shop, add a small action detail such as “她每天在商店里缝衣服。” This makes her new job more concrete.

### Encouragement
You clearly connect the city’s noise with Mengzi’s difficulty sleeping by using a cause-and-result structure. You also give the scene a clear change of place and show what Meng Mu does in the new city.

## Scene 3

> 孟子在那个商店也找了到工作，可是不是做衣服的工作，而是喜欢的工作。孟子学习馆学了十五年，他的学习和他的旅行经验很重要，对他很多好处。他是大人的时候，他成为很有名的哲人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 孟子学习馆学了十五年：孟子在学习馆学习了十五年，A location noun needs the preposition 在 before it, and 学习 is the appropriate verb for the action done there. [rule: none]
- 对他很多好处：对他有很多好处，The fixed pattern is 对……有好处; 有 is the predicate meaning “to have benefits for.” [vocab list]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): ，他成为很有名的哲人：，成为很有名的哲人，The subject has already been stated earlier in the same sentence, so the second pronoun should be omitted through zero form. [rule: none]
- Coherence (Temporality): 找了到工作：找到了工作，The resultative complement 到 must come directly after the verb, and 了 follows the completed verb-result compound. [teacher rule: le_placement]
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为很有名的哲人：成为了很有名的哲人，Becoming a philosopher is presented as a completed change of state, so use 了 after 成为. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: At the job sentence, add Mengzi’s feeling or a short line of speech to show why the job suits him, for example: 孟子高兴地说：“这份工作正是我喜欢的！”
- Expressiveness: At the fifteen-year study point, add one concrete study habit to make the long period vivid, for example: 十五年里，他每天认真读书，也记下旅行中的见闻。
- Expressiveness: At the final sentence, add an evaluative word that highlights the long effort, for example: 后来，他终于成为了很有名的哲人。

### Encouragement
You clearly show a progression from finding a suitable job to long-term study and later success. The contrast with 可是不是……而是…… also helps the reader understand that Mengzi’s job is different from the clothing work.
