## Scene 1

> 在古代，很多医生不知道怎么用药。所以他们找到了很多植物对药。神农先看到很多病人，他们吃很多植物对药。但是我觉得他们的吃的植物，让他们病人。神农觉得这个植物可能特别是毒。所以他想找一个植物对药，他们可以吃对药。正好，他找到了一把花，他想把花放在茶里，对药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 植物对药：很多可以做药的植物，The phrase “对药” is not a valid verb-object expression here; “做药” means that the plants can be made into medicine. [rule: word_choice]
- 对药：来治病，“对药” cannot describe the purpose of eating plants; “来治病” clearly expresses that the patients eat them in order to treat an illness. [rule: word_choice]
- 他们的吃的植物，让他们病人：他们吃的植物让他们生病，Use a verb phrase after “让”; “病人” is a noun meaning a sick person, while “生病” is the verb phrase for becoming ill. [rule: word_class_misuse]
- 特别是毒：特别有毒，“是毒” is not the correct predicate for a poisonous plant; “有毒” describes the plant as poisonous, and it can follow “特别”. [vocab list]
- 一个植物对药：一种可以做药的植物，“对药” is not a suitable expression, and the modifier needs to come before “植物” with “的”; “一种” is also the natural measure word for a type of plant. [rule: attributive_modifier_de]
- 吃对药：吃，“对药” cannot follow “吃”; after the plant has been described as medicine, “人们可以吃” is complete. [rule: word_choice]
- 对药：做药，“对药” is not a valid expression for making medicine; “做药” states the intended use of the flowers. [rule: word_choice]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不知道 -- “很多医生不知道怎么用药”：不了解，emphasizes that they lack knowledge about how medicine should be used.
- 想 -- “所以他想找”：打算，shows that he has formed a clearer plan to search for a plant.
- 放 -- “他想把花放在茶里”：泡在，more specifically describes soaking the flowers in tea.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农先看到很多病人：一个叫神农的人先看到了很多病人，This is the first mention of this character, so introduce him with a full noun phrase rather than using only his name. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Reference): 这个植物：有些植物，No particular single plant has been introduced before this point, so “这个植物” has no clear referent; use a noun phrase that matches the previously mentioned plants. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Reference): 他们可以吃：人们可以吃，Several groups have appeared earlier, including doctors and patients, so “他们” is ambiguous here; “人们” clearly identifies the intended group. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Reference): 他想把花放在茶里：他想把它放在茶里，“一把花” was mentioned immediately before, with no competing object, so a pronoun is more natural for this close re-occurrence. [rule: redundancy_delete]
- Coherence (Temporality): 先看到：先看到了，This is a completed event in the past narrative, so add “了” after the resultative complement “到”. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃很多植物：吃了很多植物，The patients’ eating is presented as a completed past action, so it needs “了”. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: When the sick people first appear, add a visible sign of their illness, such as: “有的病人脸色发白，捂着肚子。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where Shennong suspects danger, add his worried thought: “神农想：这种植物会不会有毒？”
- Expressiveness: When the flowers are introduced, describe their appearance more vividly: “那些花是黄色的，像星星一样，又大又漂亮。”

### Encouragement
You already use a clear cause-and-effect connection with “所以”, and you correctly use “找到了” to show that Shennong successfully found something. Your scene also has a logical focus on Shennong observing illness and searching for useful plants.

## Scene 2

> 他不知道如果这把花对你的身体好，这就要看如果这把花毒不毒了。神农想找别的植物，但是这很麻烦。正好，他找到了别的花。他一吃花，很快就生病了。他的肚子疼死了。神农不知道他吃不吃灵芝或者夹竹桃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不知道如果这把花对你的身体好：不知道这把花对他的身体有没有好处，An embedded yes/no question after “不知道” uses an A-not-A form such as “有没有,” not “如果”; the third-person narration should also refer to 神农 with “他的.” [rule: a_not_a_question]
- 要看如果：要看，An embedded question after “看” does not use “如果.” [rule: a_not_a_question]
- 毒不毒：有没有毒，“毒” is not used as an adjective in this pattern; use “有毒” and its A-not-A form “有没有毒.” [teacher rule: du_youdu]
- 他一吃花，很快就生病了：他一吃花，就很快生病了，The pattern “一……就……” needs “就” to show that the second event follows immediately. [teacher rule: yi_jiu]
- 吃不吃灵芝或者夹竹桃：该吃灵芝还是夹竹桃，When choosing between two possibilities, use “还是” rather than combining “吃不吃” with “或者.” [rule: a_not_a_question]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 找 -- “神农想找别的植物”：寻找, emphasizes an ongoing search for plants；探寻, emphasizes exploring in order to discover something unknown.
- 麻烦 -- “但是这很麻烦”：艰辛, emphasizes that the search requires effort and is not easy；繁琐, emphasizes that the work involves many troublesome steps.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 但是这很麻烦：但是找别的植物很麻烦，“这” is too vague here; repeat the action or noun phrase so the reader knows exactly what is difficult. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Reference): 神农不知道他吃不吃灵芝或者夹竹桃：神农不知道该吃灵芝还是夹竹桃，When the subject of the following action is still 神农 in the same sentence, use zero form rather than repeating “他.” [teacher rule: jueding_vp]
- Expressiveness: When the flower appears, add one visual detail so the danger contrasts with its beauty, for example: “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花。”
- Expressiveness: After the stomach pain begins, show 神农’s physical feeling more clearly, for example: “他在地上躺着，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought at the moment he becomes ill, for example: “他想：我会不会死？”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show a cause-and-effect sequence: 神农 finds a flower, eats it, and then becomes ill.
- You also used “找到了” well to show that the search had a successful result.

## Scene 3

> 别的人给了神农一个灵芝。这个植物可以让神农更好。比方说，他会觉得更健康。吃灵芝以后，他可以再走。现在他觉得更好。最后，神农找到了很多植物，他可以用对药。这些植物让神农很高兴。神农想跟很多人分享他的新药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别的人给了神农：有人给了神农，别的人 refers to people contrasted with a particular person already mentioned; here no such contrast has been established, so 有人 is appropriate. [teacher rule: bieren]
- 让神农更好：让神农的身体更好，without a noun such as 身体, 更好 can suggest that Shennong becomes a better person rather than that his health improves. [rule: word_choice]
- 很多植物，他可以用对药：很多可以用来做药的植物，use 用来做药 to express the plants’ purpose; this modifier belongs before 植物 and describes which plants Shennong found. [rule: attributive_modifier_de]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 高兴 -- “这些植物让神农很高兴”：欣慰，shows satisfied happiness after his effort has produced a useful result.
- 新药 -- “分享他的新药”：新发现的药，clarifies that the medicine was newly discovered rather than newly made.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农想跟很多人分享他的新药：他想跟很多人分享他的新药，use a pronoun when the same character was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other character competes as the referent. [rule: redundancy_delete]
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃灵芝以后：吃了灵芝以后，this is one completed action before the later action of walking again, so use 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: After the recovery, add a concrete bodily detail to show improvement, such as “神农觉得肚子不那么疼了。”
- Expressiveness: At the moment he finds the useful plants, add an internal reaction, such as “神农又惊讶又高兴。”
- Expressiveness: When he wants to share the medicine, add a short line of speech, such as “这些植物可以做药，我要告诉大家！”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the sequence from receiving the medicine to feeling better and continuing the journey. You also use useful story words such as 灵芝, 植物, and 分享 to keep the scene focused.
