## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个男人，他的名字是神农。他看到了有很多人不知道哪个植物好吃，也不知道什么植物对你的身体有好处。这个问题神农觉得很糟糕，所以神农去了吃每种植物。他吃了一种植物以后，就写了这个植物对你有没有好处。有的时候，神农吃了很好吃的植物。有的时候，他吃了很危险的植物。他吃完了每种植物以后，会来跟他的朋友聊到他吃的植物了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 already names a time period, so adding 的时候 is redundant.
- 写了这个植物对你有没有好处：写下了这个植物对身体有没有好处，写下 means to record a finding; 写 alone does not clearly express recording information about the plant.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 糟糕 -- “这个问题神农觉得很糟糕”：令人担忧，shows that the problem makes Shen Nong worried, rather than only saying it is bad.
- 好吃 -- “神农吃了很好吃的植物”：味道鲜美，describes the plant’s taste more specifically and vividly.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 对你的身体有好处：对身体有好处，this is general information for everyone, not advice addressed to the reader, so omit 你.
- Coherence (Reference): 这个问题神农觉得很糟糕，所以神农去了吃每种植物：这个问题他觉得很糟糕，所以去吃了每种植物，after Shen Nong has just been mentioned and no competing character has become the subject, use a pronoun; within the continuing sentence, use zero form rather than repeating his name.
- Coherence (Temporality): 去了吃每种植物：去吃了每种植物，put 了 after the completed action sequence, not between 去 and the following verb.
- Coherence (Temporality): 有的时候，神农吃了很好吃的植物：有的时候，神农吃很好吃的植物，a routine marked by 有的时候 uses the zero particle rather than 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 有的时候，他吃了很危险的植物：有的时候，他吃很危险的植物，another routine action should use the zero particle rather than 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 会来跟他的朋友聊到他吃的植物了：会来跟他的朋友聊到他吃的植物，modal verb 会 does not take sentence-final 了 when describing a regular or future action.
- Expressiveness: When describing why the problem matters, add a concrete result for the people, such as “很多人因为吃了有毒的植物，常常生病。”
- Expressiveness: At the moment Shen Nong decides to try the plants, add his determined thought or speech, such as “神农想：‘我一定要找出哪些植物能吃。’”
- Expressiveness: Give one plant a small sensory detail to make the testing scene more vivid, such as “这种植物闻起来很香，吃起来有一点儿甜。”

### Encouragement

## Scene 3

> 神农说，有一天，我吃了一个黄色的花，名字是夹竹桃。我一吃夹竹桃，就觉得这种植物对我的脑有坏处。可是，一个灵芝和一个男人正好在我的旁边，我说，麻烦你把那个植物给我吃一下。我一吃这个灵芝就好了。所以，你得小心吃植物，因为有的植物对你的健康有坏处，特别是夹竹桃。因为夹竹桃有毒。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一个黄色的花：一朵黄色的花，朵 is the appropriate measure word for an individual flower; 个 is too generic here.
- 一个灵芝：一株灵芝，株 is an appropriate measure word for an individual medicinal plant such as 灵芝; 个 is too generic in this context.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 吃 -- “我一吃这个灵芝就好了”：服用，specifies taking a medicinal plant as medicine rather than ordinary eating；服下，emphasizes completing the act of taking the medicine.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 夹竹桃有毒：它有毒，use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the referent has just been named and no other object competes with it.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 特别是夹竹桃。因为：特别是夹竹桃，因为，because the toxicity explains why 夹竹桃 is especially dangerous, the cause clause should be connected to the preceding statement with the cause connector.
- Expressiveness: At “一个黄色的花,” add a visual detail to make the plant memorable, for example: “那朵花像星星一样，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: At the point when the poison affects him, show a physical reaction and feeling, for example: “我的肚子突然疼了起来，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: When the man appears, add one identifying detail, for example: “这时，一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人走了过来。”

### Encouragement
