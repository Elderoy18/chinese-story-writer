## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个男孩名字叫孟子。孟子和孟子的妈妈两个人住在很穷的农村。他们住的地方旁边是个墓地，墓地老是有很多人祭拜。因为人们在祭拜，他们一直在哭。孟子和孟子的妈妈住在这里才一个星期就觉得受不了了，而且害得孟子的学习。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，This expression is redundant because 古代 already gives the time period.
- 有一个男孩名字叫孟子：有一个叫孟子的男孩，The naming phrase should function as a modifier before the noun, using 的.
- 害得孟子的学习：影响了孟子的学习，害得 must be followed by a person and a harmful result clause; it cannot take only a noun phrase as its result.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子的妈妈：孟子和他的妈妈，After the name has just been established and no competing male character is present, use a pronoun instead of repeating the name inside the same noun phrase.
- Coherence (Temporality): 住在这里才一个星期：才在这里住了一个星期，The one-week stay is completed by the time they cannot tolerate the place, so use 了 with the completed action.
- Expressiveness: At the description of the cemetery, add a visual detail about the visitors’ sadness, such as “来祭拜的人低着头，脸上满是泪水。”
- Expressiveness: At the final sentence, show Mengzi’s mother’s reaction or thoughts, such as “孟母皱着眉头，心想：‘这里会影响孟子的学习。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly introduce Mengzi and his mother and establish the cemetery setting early. The detail about people crying also helps create a sad atmosphere.

## Scene 2

> 孟子的妈妈觉得孟子取得良好的教育是很重要的，所以孟子的妈妈认为这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好，就打算跟孟子搬家。孟子的妈妈带孟子不是搬到城市，而是搬到了孟子老师的家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 孟子取得良好的教育：孟子接受良好的教育，取得 usually collocates with results or achievements, while education is something a person receives.
- 跟孟子搬家：带着孟子搬家，跟 describes doing an action together, but a mother relocating her child should use 带着 to show that she takes the child with her.
- 不是搬到城市，而是搬到了：搬到了，the “not X but Y” construction is for correcting a stated or conventional mistaken idea; no such mistaken destination has been introduced here.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 地方 -- “这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好”：环境，specifies that the concern is the surroundings and atmosphere in which the child grows up.
- 不太好 -- “对儿子的教育不太好”：不太合适，more precisely expresses that this setting is unsuitable for the child’s education.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 所以孟子的妈妈认为：所以认为，once the mother has been named at the start of the sentence, use zero form for her next action in the same sentence.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子的妈妈带孟子：她带孟子，her name appeared in the immediately preceding sentence and no other female character competes as the referent, so a pronoun is more natural.
- Expressiveness: At the mother’s decision, add her worry in a brief internal thought, such as: “她心想：‘如果一直住在这儿，会影响孟子的学习。’”
- Expressiveness: After the move, add one concrete detail about the new setting to show why it may help learning, such as: “老师的家很安静，书架上放着很多书。”

### Encouragement
You clearly connect the mother’s concern about education to her decision to move by using 所以. You also correctly use 搬到了 to show that the move in the second sentence has been completed.
