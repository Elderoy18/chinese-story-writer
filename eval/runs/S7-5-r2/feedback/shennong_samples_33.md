## Scene 1

> 古代时候，人不知道他们能吃什么植物，常常一吃就死。但是不知道为什么，没有办法。所以有一天，神农出去试一试很多种植物，告诉人什么植物是安全什么是危险。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代时候：在古代，use this time expression for a historical setting; the original combines two overlapping time expressions.
- 人：人们，use 人们 for the concrete group of people in this scene; 人 is a generic noun.
- 他们能吃什么植物：能吃什么植物，delete the redundant pronoun because the people who may eat the plants are already the subject of the sentence.
- 试一试很多种植物：尝试很多种植物，试一试 suggests a brief trial, while 尝试 naturally describes testing many different kinds of plants.
- 人：人们，use 人们 for the group being told this information.
- 什么植物是安全什么是危险：哪些植物是安全的，哪些植物是危险的，use parallel question phrases and add 的 after the adjectives when they follow 是.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：一个叫神农的人，this is the character’s first mention, so introduce him with a full noun phrase rather than using only his name.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉人们：告诉了人们，these are a series of completed narrative actions, so the last verb in the series takes 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 尝试很多种植物，告诉了人们：尝试很多种植物，然后告诉了人们，use 然后 to show the sequence: he tested the plants first and shared the results afterward.
- Expressiveness: After “常常一吃就死,” add a concrete consequence to make the danger vivid, such as “大家都很害怕，不敢随便吃野外的植物。”
- Expressiveness: At the moment Shen Nong decides to act, add his thought or speech, such as “神农想：‘我要找出能吃的植物。’”
- Expressiveness: Describe his testing action with one simple detail, such as “他看见一种植物，就摘下一点儿，小心地尝一尝。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish the problem at the beginning: people do not know which plants they can eat, and this gives Shen Nong a strong reason to act. Your use of “一吃就” effectively shows the immediate danger.

## Scene 2

> 发现有的植物对身体非常好，特别是茶，让肚子感觉又暖和又清楚。他告诉朋友：“你吃哪种植物，这就要看你的口味。比方说，你爱吃甜的，可能喜欢糖。”他最喜欢吃花，除了夹竹桃以外，什么都花想吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 让肚子感觉又暖和又清楚：让人觉得又暖和又清醒，清楚 does not describe a stomach sensation; 清醒 describes a person’s alert state, and 让 + person + 觉得 naturally introduces this feeling.
- 你吃哪种植物，这就要看你的口味。：你该吃哪种植物？这就要看你的口味怎么样了。，这就要看……了 gives a flexible answer to an uncertain question; the part after 看 needs a question form, such as 怎么样, and the final 了 belongs to this structure.
- 什么都花想吃：什么花都想吃，when 什么……都 means “every/any,” the noun belongs after 什么 and before 都.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 非常好 -- “有的植物对身体非常好”：很有益处，states more precisely that the plants benefit health while keeping the same positive strength.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the sentence about tea, add a sensory detail about preparing it, such as “把茶放在水里，茶香慢慢飘出来。”
- Expressiveness: Show the character’s reaction to the tea more vividly, such as “他喝了一口，觉得肚子暖暖的，精神也好了很多。”
- Expressiveness: Near the sentence about flowers, describe their appearance, such as “那些黄色的花像星星一样，又大又漂亮。”

### Encouragement
You clearly connect plants with their effects on the body, and you use a direct quotation to show the character explaining his ideas. The example about sweet food also helps make the dialogue easier to understand.

## Scene 3

> 他发现这个口味，一百岁时候，不小心吃了毒的花。一吃就开始死。但是生病时候告诉人什么植物是药，什么植物是毒。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一百岁时候：一百岁的时候，The time phrase needs 的 before 时候 when the preceding expression has more than one syllable.
- 毒的花：有毒的花，毒 cannot modify a noun directly here; 有毒的 means “poisonous.”
- 一吃就开始死：一吃就开始生病，死 is a completed event and does not normally follow 开始; 生病 can describe the onset of an illness.
- 人：人们，This refers to the concrete group of people whom he tells, so 人们 is needed rather than the generic noun 人.
- 是毒：有毒，Use 有毒 to describe a plant as poisonous; 是毒 does not fit this predicate structure.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不小心 -- “不小心吃了毒的花”：误食，more precisely means accidentally eating something, especially something harmful.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 这个口味：一种花的味道，This is the first mention of the flavour, so 这个 cannot point to a previously named object; introduce it with a full noun phrase instead.
- Expressiveness: After the accidental eating, add a visual detail about the flower: “那是一朵黄色的花，像星星一样。”
- Expressiveness: Show the physical effect of the poisonous flower: “他的肚子疼了起来，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Add direct speech when he explains his discovery: “这种花有毒，不能吃；这种植物是药，可以吃。”

### Encouragement
You used the pattern 一……就…… well to show an immediate result. You also clearly included the important contrast between plants that are medicine and plants that are poisonous.
