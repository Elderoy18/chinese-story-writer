## Scene 1

> 很久以前，有一个老男人叫神农。神农在古代每一天看到了别的小镇人生病还是不舒服，所以他决定开始找新的药。他下一天开始爬当地的山脉，找了各种各样的植物。神农试一试每个植物，知道有什么好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一个老男人叫神农：一个叫神农的老人，A name phrase needs to modify the noun with 的; 老人 is the usual neutral noun for an elderly man in this story.
- 别的小镇人：小镇上的人，别的 is used to distinguish other people or things from a stated one, but no comparison group appears here; the corrected phrase clearly names the town’s residents.
- 生病还是不舒服：生病或感到不舒服，还是 is normally used to present alternatives in a question; an affirmative statement listing two possibilities uses 或.
- 下一天：第二天，第二天 is the standard narrative expression for the day after the event just described.
- 找了各种各样的植物：找到了各种各样的植物，找 means “look for,” while 找到 states that he successfully found the plants.
- 每个植物：每种植物，Plants are classified by type with 种 in this context, rather than the general measure word 个.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很久以前 -- “很久以前，有一个老男人叫神农”：在遥远的古代，sets the story clearly in an ancient time and gives the opening a more story-like setting.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农在古代每一天看到了别的小镇人生病还是不舒服：他在古代每一天看到了别的小镇人生病还是不舒服，After the name has appeared in the immediately preceding sentence and no other character competes with it, use a pronoun for close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农试一试每个植物：他试一试每个植物，The same character was the subject in the preceding sentence, so a pronoun is more natural for close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 每一天看到了：每天看到，每天 describes a routine, so the verb takes the zero aspect marker rather than completed-action 了.
- Expressiveness: At the point where he sees sick people, add his concern to show his motivation more vividly: “他看见人们很难受，心里很担心。”
- Expressiveness: During the mountain journey, add a simple sense of distance and effort: “山路很长，他走啊走啊，去了很多高山。”
- Expressiveness: After he learns about the plants, show how he keeps his discoveries: “他把这些发现写在书上。”

### Encouragement
You gave the scene a clear cause-and-effect structure with 因为……所以……, showing why Shennong begins his search. The phrase 各种各样的植物 also gives the search a good sense of variety.

## Scene 2

> 比方说，他一次试一试有些植物的叶，吃得又新鲜又甜。煮这些叶以后，他一喝就知道他发现新的茶叶。虽然他找到了很多的新的植物，但是他的旅程有的时候特别是危险。比方说，他一吃灵芝就开始生病。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一次试一试有些植物的叶：尝了一些植物的叶，Verb reduplication is for a light or tentative plan/request; an action that was actually carried out should use a regular completed verb.
- 吃得又新鲜又甜：叶子又新鲜又甜，A complement with 得 describes the manner or degree of an action, but “fresh and sweet” describes the leaves themselves.
- 特别是危险：特别危险，特别是 must introduce a typical example or clause; it cannot directly modify an adjective.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 新的 -- “很多的新的植物”：陌生的，emphasizes that the plants were unfamiliar to him；未尝过的，specifies that he had not tasted these plants before.
- 吃 -- “他一吃灵芝就开始生病”：尝，suggests that he tasted it as part of testing plants, rather than eating a full amount.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他发现新的茶叶：自己发现新的茶叶，When the person who discovers the tea is the same as the subject who knows this, 自己 makes the shared referent clear.
- Coherence (Temporality): 煮这些叶以后：煮了这些叶以后，The boiling is completed before the later action, so the completed action needs 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 发现新的茶叶：发现了新的茶叶，The discovery is a completed event in this past sequence, so it needs 了.
- Expressiveness: After the tea is boiled, add a sensory detail about its smell or appearance, such as “水里飘出一股淡淡的清香。”
- Expressiveness: At the moment he becomes ill, add his physical feeling or a short thought, such as “他的肚子突然疼起来，心里很害怕。”

### Encouragement
You clearly used time order to show the testing, boiling, drinking, and discovery. The contrast with 虽然……但是…… also effectively shows that the journey had both successful discoveries and danger.

## Scene 3

> 一个爬山的男人正好看到神农的麻烦，然后给了他一些药。神农最终他的小镇回到，跟别的人分享他的发现。你认为神农的故事真的吗？这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 最终他的小镇回到：最终回到他的小镇，The destination phrase must follow the verb-resultative form, so the town comes after the action of returning.
- 跟别的人：跟别人，This context does not contrast these people with one specific previously mentioned person, so use the general word for other people.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 药 -- “给了他一些药”：草药，specifies that the medicine is made from plants and fits the later mention of traditional medicine.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 给了他一些药：给了神农一些药，Two male characters appear in the same sentence, so the pronoun can be unclear; repeat the name of the recipient.
- Coherence (Temporality): 分享他的发现：分享了他的发现，The sharing is a completed event in this past narrative; in a series of actions, the final action takes 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): ，跟别人分享：以后，跟别人分享，Use 以后 to show that sharing happened after the return.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处：这就要看你相不相信中药对身体有没有好处了，The part after this expression must be an uncertain question form, such as an A-not-A question; the final 了 belongs to the fixed expression.
- Expressiveness: At the rescue, add a physical detail to show why the man gives medicine, such as “神农躺在地上，肚子疼得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech after the medicine helps, such as “神农说：‘谢谢你，这是什么药？’”

### Encouragement
You introduced the new helper with a full noun phrase and used the completed giving action accurately. The final question also gives the scene an interesting reflective ending.
