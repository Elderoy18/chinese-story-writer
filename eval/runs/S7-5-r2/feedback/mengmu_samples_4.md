## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，孟子和孟母住了在靠墓地的家。孩子才五岁就每天在墓地祭拜了。在那个城市，孟母从早到晚都找工作，可是找不到，害得孟母没有钱。难怪她跟孩子搬家，原来买不了房。他们搬了到别一个城市找工作。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 already names a time period, so adding 的时候 is redundant; use 在古代 for this historical setting.
- 住了在：住在，住 describes the family’s setting/state here rather than a completed action, so it does not take 了.
- 跟孩子搬家：带着孩子搬家，跟 means “together with,” while a mother moving a young child to a new place should use 带着.
- 搬了到：搬到了，when 到 is the resultative/directional complement of 搬, 了 follows the complement rather than coming between the verb and 到.
- 别一个城市：另一个城市，别 cannot modify a classifier phrase to mean “another”; 另一个 correctly refers to a different city.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 找工作 -- “孟母从早到晚都找工作”：求职，emphasizes actively seeking employment rather than simply having work.
- 买不了房 -- “原来买不了房”：买不起房，states more precisely that the problem is not having enough money to afford a home.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 在那个城市：在他们住的城市里，那个 suggests that the city has already been identified, but no city has appeared yet; introduce it with a full noun phrase that connects it to the family.
- Coherence (Reference): 害得孟母没有钱：害得她没有钱，孟母 was the subject in the same sentence, so a pronoun is more natural for a close re-occurrence with no competing female character.
- Coherence (Temporality): 每天在墓地祭拜了：每天在墓地祭拜，每天 marks a routine, so use the zero particle rather than 了, which marks a completed action.
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery activity, add what the child notices or feels, such as “孟子觉得祭拜很好玩，就学着别人低头。”
- Expressiveness: At the job-search sentence, show the mother’s tired feeling with a small action, such as “孟母叹了口气，还是继续找工作。”
- Expressiveness: Before the move, add a brief line of speech to show the mother’s decision, such as “我们得找一个更好的地方住。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the family’s difficult situation through the mother’s unsuccessful job search and lack of money. You also used the early-action pattern with age effectively to show that the child was very young.

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 要又一个次搬了家：得再搬一次家，For a future repeated action, use a modal verb followed by 再, then the verb and its measure phrase; the completed-action marker 了 does not fit this future meaning.

### Coherence & Expressiveness

## Scene 3

> 孟子在那个商店也找了到工作，可是不是做衣服的工作，而是喜欢的工作。孟子学习馆学了十五年，他的学习和他的旅行经验很重要，对他很多好处。他是大人的时候，他成为很有名的哲人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 找了到：找到了，In a resultative verb compound, the completion marker follows the full compound; use the completed form for finding a job.
- 学习馆学了十五年：学习了十五年，The extra character makes the verb phrase ungrammatical here; this sentence needs the verb phrase for studying over a completed period.
- 对他很多好处：对他有很多好处，The verb 有 is required in the collocation “对某人有好处.”
- 他是大人的时候：长大以后，This expression means becoming an adult; “大人的时候” does not naturally express this time point.
- 成为很有名的哲人：成为了很有名的哲人，Use 了 to mark the completed change of becoming a philosopher.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很有名 -- “他成为很有名的哲人”：享有盛名，specifies that he is widely known and respected.
- 旅行经验 -- “他的学习和他的旅行经验很重要”：游历经验，more specifically describes experience gained by travelling to different places.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他的学习和他的旅行经验：自己的学习和旅行经验，When the learning and travel experience belong to the previously mentioned subject, 自己的 makes that possession relationship especially clear.
- Expressiveness: After the long period of study, add a concrete learning detail, such as “他每天认真看书，也常常向老师请教。”
- Expressiveness: At the point when he becomes successful, add an inner feeling or reaction, such as “孟子终于明白了，学习让他看到了更大的世界。”
- Expressiveness: Show his mother’s pride with a short line of speech, such as “孟母高兴地说：‘你多年的努力没有白费！’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show a long time span with “十五年,” and you connect Mengzi’s learning and travel experience to his later success. Your final sentence also gives the scene a strong sense of achievement.
