## Scene 1

> 有一天孟子去了墓地玩儿，但是他妈妈觉得这是不合适的地方让孩子玩，所以她决定了他们会搬家找更好的地方。她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福，而且住在这儿教育不太好。因为她望子成龙，所以她会一直搬家才能找到质量好的教育。然后他们搬到了离城市更近的地方。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 这是不合适的地方让孩子玩：这不是适合孩子玩的地方，The adjective “适合” needs to describe what the place is suitable for; “孩子玩的” correctly modifies “地方.”
- 而且住在这儿教育不太好：而且觉得这儿的教育不太好，“住在这儿” cannot directly modify “教育”; use “这儿的” to show that the education belongs to this location.
- 会一直搬家才能找到质量好的教育：要不断搬家，才能找到优质的教育环境，“要……才能……” expresses a necessary condition, and “找到” needs a findable object such as an educational environment rather than “教育” by itself.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 她决定了他们会搬家找更好的地方：她决定搬家，找一个更好的地方，After “决定,” use the action directly rather than repeating the people who will do it.
- Coherence (Temporality): 决定了：决定，“决定” is a mental-state verb, so it normally takes the zero particle rather than “了.”
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为她望子成龙，她要不断搬家：因为她望子成龙，所以她要不断搬家，Use “因为……所以……” to make the cause-and-effect relationship explicit.
- Expressiveness: At the mother’s reaction, add an internal thought to show her worry, for example: “孟母很着急，心想：‘这里不适合孩子长大。’”
- Expressiveness: When describing the cemetery, add one concrete detail that helps readers understand why the mother dislikes it, for example: “墓地旁边常常有人祭拜，气氛有点阴森。”
- Expressiveness: At the new location, add a detail about the city atmosphere, for example: “城市附近很热闹，到处都有商人卖东西。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the mother’s motivation for moving and connect it to her hope for her son’s future. You also used “搬到了” well to show that the move was completed.

## Scene 2

> 他们住才一月就又搬家。古代的时候，一般人不喜欢卖东西的人。但是孟子老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿，害得妈妈着急得很，因为她恐怕儿子会成为跟他们很像。她告诉了孟子：他们不是朋友，而是不好的人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 住才：才住，The adverb 才 should come before the verb to show that the stay lasted only a short time.
- 一月：一个月，A duration of one month needs the measure word 个.
- 古代的时候：在古代，The original repeats the idea of “time,” because 古代 is already a time expression.
- 老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿：老是跟这些卖东西的人玩儿，Using both 老是 and 常常 together redundantly marks the same habitual meaning; keep one.
- 害得妈妈着急得很：让妈妈很着急，害得 must introduce a clearly harmful consequence, while being worried is better expressed with 让 in this sentence.
- 她恐怕：她担心，恐怕 cannot take another person as its subject in this pattern; use 担心 to say that she is afraid something may happen.
- 成为跟他们很像：变得跟他们很像，成为 normally takes a noun phrase as its complement, while 变得 can be followed by an adjectival phrase such as 很像.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 告诉 -- “她告诉了孟子”：告诫，emphasizes that the mother is warning Mengzi seriously about whom he should spend time with

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 这些卖东西的人：他们，The vendors were named in the previous sentence, so a pronoun makes the close re-occurrence smoother and avoids repeating the full noun phrase.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们住才一月就又搬家：他们才住了一个月，就又搬家了，Both the stay and the move are completed past events, so they take 了; the comma also clearly separates the two sequential actions.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉了孟子：告诉孟子，A speaking verb that introduces direct speech takes the zero particle rather than 了.
- Expressiveness: At the point where Mengzi plays with the vendors, add a sensory detail about the busy street, such as: “从早到晚，街上叫卖声不断。”
- Expressiveness: Show the mother’s inner worry before her speech, such as: “孟母心想：‘孟子每天跟着他们玩儿，以后怎么学习呢？’”
- Expressiveness: Make the mother’s warning more vivid with direct speech, such as: “孟母说：‘他们不是你的好朋友，你应该多看书学习。’”

### Encouragement
- You used 但是 to create a clear contrast between ordinary people and Mengzi, and you showed the mother’s concern through both narration and dialogue.

## Scene 3

> 你不是当数学家，而是当哲学家，不能当卖东西的人。所以他们又搬家。在他们第三个家，孟子学了怎么祭拜。妈妈感觉这个地方适合他们，告诉了孟子：我们找到了最后的家。在这儿妈妈让儿子从早到晚学习，他难怪成为有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他们第三个家：他们的第三个家，A possessive modifier before a noun needs 的.
- 告诉了孟子：告诉孟子，A speaking verb that introduces quoted speech takes the zero particle rather than 了.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 地方 -- “妈妈感觉这个地方适合他们”：居住环境，specifies that she is judging the home setting rather than an unspecified place.
- 卖东西的人 -- “不能当卖东西的人”：商人，names the occupation more precisely.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 所以他们又搬家：所以他们又搬家了，A repeated relocation that has already happened uses 又, and the completed action needs 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为有名的哲学家：成为了有名的哲学家，Becoming a philosopher is presented as a completed change of state, so it needs 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 妈妈让儿子从早到晚学习，他难怪成为有名的哲学家：因为妈妈让孟子从早到晚学习，所以他成为了有名的哲学家，The two clauses express cause and effect, so use 因为……所以…… to connect them clearly.
- Expressiveness: At “妈妈感觉这个地方适合他们,” add the mother’s happiness and a short line of speech, such as 妈妈高兴地说：“这儿的环境很适合孩子。”
- Expressiveness: At “孟子学了怎么祭拜,” show how he studies, such as 孟子每天安静地看书，认真地学习。
- Expressiveness: At the final sentence, add Mengzi’s own feeling about learning, such as 孟子觉得读书很有意思，也想学到更多知识。

### Encouragement
You clearly show the important change from an unsuitable home to a place where Mengzi can study. Your use of 不是……而是…… is also appropriate for contrasting two different careers.
