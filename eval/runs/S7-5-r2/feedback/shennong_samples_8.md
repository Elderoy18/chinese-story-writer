## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个人叫神农。一天，神农正在路上走，一看很多人正在吃一些植物。一些人一吃植物就生病了，神农想帮别人。所以想知道哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，This phrase redundantly combines two time expressions: 古代 already names a time period, while 的时候 also means “at the time of.” [vocab list]
- 一看很多人正在吃一些植物：看到很多人正在吃一些植物，Here the meaning is that 神农 perceived the scene, so use 看到 rather than 看, which means “to look.” [rule: resultative_complement]
- 别人：人们，别人 distinguishes other people from a particular person or group already under discussion; here 神农 wants to help people in general. [teacher rule: bieren]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 知道 -- “想知道哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃”：弄清，emphasizes resolving the uncertainty about which plants are safe；查明，emphasizes finding out the answer through investigation.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After 神农 sees the people, add a detail showing that they eat without knowing the danger: “很多人正在随便吃各种植物。”
- Expressiveness: At “神农想帮人们,” add his concerned feeling: “神农看见他们生病了，心里很担心。”
- Expressiveness: You could add a short thought to show his purpose more vividly: “神农想：‘我一定要弄清哪些植物能吃。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduce 神农 at the start and use a logical cause-and-effect connection with 所以.
- The contrast between plants that can be eaten and plants that cannot be eaten is clear, and your use of 正在 and 一……就…… helps show the actions vividly.

## Scene 2

> 然后，神农开始尝很多植物，比方说他吃过生姜、芥兰和菠菜。一些植物吃真好，特别是黄瓜。但是，一些植物毒得很，特别是夹竹桃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 吃真好：真好吃，The adjective compound “好吃” describes the taste of food; the degree adverb “真” goes before “好吃,” not between “吃” and “好.” [rule: none]
- 毒得很：有毒，“毒” cannot take the degree complement “得很” in this use. Use “有毒” to state that a plant is poisonous. [teacher rule: du_youdu]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 尝 -- “神农开始尝很多植物”：尝试，emphasizes testing unfamiliar plants to learn about them；试吃，makes clear that he eats the plants as part of the test.
- 很多 -- “神农开始尝很多植物”：各种，highlights that the plants are of many different kinds, not merely a large number.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: When introducing the dangerous plant, add a visual detail to make the contrast stronger: “夹竹桃开着黄色的花，像星星一样，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: After describing Shennong’s testing, add his determined thought to show his purpose: “他想：我一定要找出哪些植物能吃。”

### Encouragement
You used “比方说” correctly to introduce clear examples, and “特别是” effectively highlights cucumber and oleander as examples of the two groups. The contrast between tasty plants and poisonous plants is easy to follow.

## Scene 3

> 一个人对神农说，麻烦我们可以吃什么植物？神农说，这就要看你想不想生病了。以后，神农告诉别人一些植物能吃，一些植物不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 麻烦：请问，麻烦 means “trouble” or introduces a request for someone to do something; it cannot by itself introduce an information question. 请问 is the appropriate polite question opener here. [rule: word_choice]
- 这就要看你想不想生病了：这就要看是哪种植物了，this pattern should give the condition that determines the answer to the earlier question. Whether someone wants to get sick does not answer which plants can be eaten; the phrase after 看 is correctly a question form in the revision. [rule: zhe_jiu_yao_kan]
- 以后：后来，when it begins a new sentence and means “afterwards,” use 后来 rather than 以后. [vocab list]
- 别人：那个人，别人 distinguishes other people from a person already being discussed. Here, the listener is the same person who asked the question, so use a specific reference. [teacher rule: bieren]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 一些 -- “一些植物能吃，一些植物不能吃”：有的，creates a clearer parallel contrast between the two groups of plants.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉：告诉了，this later narrative event is presented as completed, so the verb needs 了; speaking verbs take the zero particle only when they directly introduce quoted speech. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: At the question, add the person’s worried expression: “一个人皱着眉头问：‘请问，我们可以吃什么植物？’”
- Expressiveness: Let Shennong give a more vivid explanation by pointing to plants: “神农指着两种植物说：‘这种可以吃，那种有毒，不能吃。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly set up a question-and-answer exchange, and you make the important contrast between plants that can and cannot be eaten. The use of the pattern 这就要看……了 shows that you are trying more advanced response structures.
