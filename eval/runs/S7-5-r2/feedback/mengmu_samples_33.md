## Scene 1

> 一天，很久很久以前，孟子和他妈妈住在中国。孟子妈妈的工作是祭拜，孟子不高兴，因为他说我们老是搬家，为什么？他妈妈说，从早到晚，我一直祭拜，很多墓地得我祭拜。所以我们得去很多不同的地方。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一天，很久很久以前：很久很久以前的一天，A specific day in the distant past is expressed by placing the long time phrase before the noun it modifies.
- 因为他说我们老是搬家，为什么？：因为他说：“我们为什么老是搬家？”，Direct speech in writing needs a colon and quotation marks; placing the question word before the predicate makes the question’s meaning clear.
- 从早到晚，我一直祭拜，很多墓地得我祭拜：我从早到晚一直祭拜，得祭拜很多墓地，The subject should come before time adverbials, and the modal verb 得 must come before the verb phrase it modifies.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子妈妈：他妈妈，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the referent was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other person competes with it.
- Expressiveness: At the moment when Mengzi is unhappy, add a facial expression or action to show his feeling: “孟子皱着眉头，难过地问：‘我们为什么老是搬家？’”
- Expressiveness: Give the mother’s voice an emotion that matches her busy work: “妈妈叹了一口气，说：‘我从早到晚都在祭拜。’”
- Expressiveness: Add a small detail about the repeated travel to make the reason more vivid: “有时候在城外，有时候在很远的村子里。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the reason for the family’s frequent moves, and the dialogue helps the reader understand Mengzi’s feelings.
- The use of “从早到晚” and “一直” gives a strong sense that the mother’s work is tiring and continuous.

## Scene 2

> 孟子想，难怪我们常常搬家，原来我妈妈很忙。可是常常搬家害得孟子很难找到朋友。每次搬家孟子也觉得新的地方不是太热就是太冷，不是太大就是太小。在哪的地方，他都不高兴。孟子想帮助妈妈祭拜，可是他六岁，所以不能祭拜。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在哪的地方：不管在哪个地方，The original incorrectly combines the question phrase “在哪” with “的地方.” Use “哪个” before the classifier “个” and noun “地方”; “不管” expresses “wherever.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 忙 -- “我妈妈很忙”：忙个不停, emphasizes that her work keeps her continuously occupied
- 新的 -- “新的地方”：陌生的, emphasizes that each place is unfamiliar to the child after moving

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: At “每次搬家,” add a small detail about the unfamiliar homes to show why the child feels unhappy, for example: “他总是要重新认识邻居和学校。”
- Expressiveness: After “很难找到朋友,” add an internal feeling to make the problem more personal, for example: “他觉得很孤单。”
- Expressiveness: At the end, add a short line of thought showing his wish to help his mother, for example: “妈妈这么忙，我也想帮帮她。”

### Encouragement
You used “难怪……原来……” very naturally to show Mengzi’s realization. The two “不是……就是……” patterns also clearly describe how unsuitable the new places feel to him.

## Scene 3

> 四年后，孟子开始帮妈妈祭拜。他画画很厉害，他越来越喜欢祭拜，所以他祭拜不是想帮妈妈工作，而是他很喜欢画画。孟子成为很著名的古代画家。今天很多中国人很喜欢孟子。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不是想帮妈妈工作，而是他很喜欢画画：不是为了帮妈妈工作，而是因为他很喜欢画画，The two halves of this paired structure should give parallel reasons: the rejected reason uses “为了…”, and the affirmed reason uses “因为…”.
- 孟子成为很著名的古代画家：孟子成为了很著名的古代画家，Use “了” to show that becoming a painter is a completed change of state in the narrative.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很厉害 -- “他画画很厉害”：画得很出色，describes the quality of his painting more specifically and vividly.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 所以：。，“所以” should introduce a result caused by the preceding idea, but liking worship does not logically lead to the following contrast about why he worships. Separate these as two statements instead.
- Expressiveness: After the first sentence, add a small action detail to show what his worship looks like, such as “孟子一边笑，一边祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: After saying that he likes worship, add his inner feeling, such as “孟子觉得祭拜很有趣。”
- Expressiveness: After mentioning his painting skill, add a brief response from his mother, such as “妈妈说：‘你画得真好！’”

### Encouragement
- You used a clear time expression at the beginning to move the story forward, and you included both Mengzi’s interests and his later achievement.
