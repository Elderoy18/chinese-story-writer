## Scene 1

> 古代的时候人觉得祭拜非常重要，孟子很小的时候，从早到晚一直祭拜，因为他妈妈告诉他祭拜很重要。虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜。他的同学们老是对他开玩笑了。孟子妈妈看了他的同学们对孟子不好的影响，所以孟子和孟子妈妈搬家了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 already expresses a time period, so adding 的时候 redundantly repeats the time meaning. [vocab list]
- 人：人们，this sentence refers to people collectively, so 人们 is the appropriate noun. [teacher rule: ren_renmen]
- 看了：看到了，the mother is meant to perceive the classmates’ effect on Mengzi; 看 means “look,” while 看到 expresses successfully seeing or noticing something. [vocab list]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不好的 -- “他的同学们对孟子不好的影响”：对孟子的负面影响，specifies that the influence has harmful or negative consequences rather than simply being generally “not good.”

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子妈妈：孟子和妈妈，within the same sentence, do not repeat the name inside the second noun phrase when 妈妈 clearly refers to Mengzi’s mother. [rule: redundancy_delete]
- Coherence (Temporality): 老是对他开玩笑了：老是对他开玩笑，老是 describes a habitual action, so it takes the zero particle rather than 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Conjunction): 虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜：因为他每天只祭拜，所以他没有朋友，this is a cause-and-effect relationship, so use 因为……所以…… rather than 虽然. [rule: coherence_connectives]
- Expressiveness: After the description of Mengzi’s worshipping, add his feeling or reason, such as “孟子觉得祭拜很有趣，常常学大人的样子。”
- Expressiveness: At the classmates’ teasing, add a short line of dialogue, such as “你怎么每天都在祭拜呀？”
- Expressiveness: When his mother notices the effect, show her reaction, such as “妈妈皱着眉头，心里很着急。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show a cause for Mengzi’s behaviour and connect the mother’s observation to the family’s move. The completed action in 搬家了 is also used effectively to show that the move happened.

## Scene 2

> 孟子才十一岁，成为一个哲学家。孟子不是祭拜，而是看政治文章，难怪他成为了哲学家。不过，看过很多政治文章对他害得，他有很多问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 看过很多政治文章对他害得，他有很多问题：看过很多政治文章害得他有很多问题，害得 must be followed directly by the person affected and a harmful result; 对他 cannot be inserted into this structure. [teacher rule: haide_structure]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子不是祭拜，而是看政治文章：他不是祭拜，而是看政治文章，After the recent mention of 孟子 and with no competing character, use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为一个哲学家：就成为了一个哲学家，After 才十一岁, use 就 to show that the achievement happened unusually early; 了 marks the completed achievement. [rule: cai_jiu_construction]
- Expressiveness: After the first sentence, add Mengzi’s feeling about such an early achievement, for example: “孟子高兴极了。”
- Expressiveness: At the final sentence, make the “problems” more vivid with a short internal thought, for example: “孟子心想：‘这些文章太难懂了。’”

### Encouragement
- You used the contrast pattern “不是……而是……” effectively to show a change in Mengzi’s activity. You also used “难怪” to connect his reading with his later achievement.

## Scene 3

> 他一直问问题，但是不能解决那些问题。孟子长大，他开始能解决问题。他常常建议你不是学金融，而是学政治，因为如果你懂政治，你会成为高薪。后来孟子成为教授，他教学生很厉害。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 孟子长大：孟子长大以后，Use a time expression with 以后 to show that the next action happens after he grows up. [rule: time_expression]
- 不是学金融，而是学政治：不要学金融，而要学政治，After 建议, use a negative instruction and a preferred action; 不是 here states a fact rather than giving the advice not to do something. [rule: none]
- 成为高薪：拿到高薪，The word 高薪 describes pay, so it cannot be the complement of 成为; use 拿到 for receiving a salary. [rule: verb_object_collocation]
- 后来孟子成为教授：后来孟子成为了教授，This is a completed change in the narrative, so 成为 takes 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 解决 -- “他开始能解决问题”：解答，more specifically means answering intellectual questions or doubts rather than handling general problems.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 你：人们，The narrative has not introduced an addressee for 你, so 人们 gives this general advice a clear referent. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Expressiveness: After the first sentence, add Mencius’s feeling to show why the unanswered questions matter: “他感到很苦恼。”
- Expressiveness: At the advice, add a short quotation to make his ideas sound more like a teacher’s: “他常常说：‘学习政治可以帮助人们了解国家。’”
- Expressiveness: After the final sentence, show the students’ response with one concrete detail: “学生们都认真地听他讲课。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show Mencius’s development from having questions to becoming a professor. Your use of short sentences also makes the main sequence of events easy to follow.
