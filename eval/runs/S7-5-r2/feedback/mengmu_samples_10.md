## Scene 1

> 孟子小的时候，从早到晚都跟朋友玩。孟子和他的朋友喜欢玩在墓地里，装祭拜。孟子五岁的时候，跟妈妈搬家。他们搬家不是因为孟子玩得太多，而是因为妈妈认为他们的家离墓地太近。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 玩在墓地里：在墓地里玩，A place phrase normally comes before the action verb it modifies.
- 装祭拜：假装祭拜，Use 假装 before an action to mean pretending to do it; 装 does not directly fit before 祭拜 in this meaning.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和他的朋友：他和他的朋友，Since 孟子 was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other male character intervenes, use a pronoun for this close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 跟妈妈搬家：跟妈妈搬了家，This is one completed move at age five, so use 了 to mark the completed action.
- Expressiveness: At the cemetery scene, add a small action showing how the children play, such as “一边笑一边假装祭拜,” to make their behaviour easier to imagine.
- Expressiveness: Near the reason for moving, add the mother’s feeling or concern, such as “很着急，担心会影响孟子的学习,” to show why the distance from the cemetery matters to her.

### Encouragement
You clearly establish Mengzi’s childhood habit and then give a logical reason for the family’s move. The contrast structure connecting the two possible reasons for moving is used effectively.

## Scene 2

> 妈妈认为，住离墓地太近，害得孟子的心气。孟子和孟母搬到子思老师的家，跟子思老师住。孟母帮子思打扫房间和做饭。子思帮孟母教她的儿子。因为孟子现在没有朋友，他老是读书。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 住离墓地太近，害得孟子的心气：住在离墓地太近的地方，害得孟子没心思学习，A location phrase before the verb needs 在, and 害得 must be followed by the person affected and a harmful result clause; 心气 alone does not state that result.
- 老是读书：一直读书，老是 usually conveys a negative or complaining tone; 一直 neutrally describes an ongoing habit of studying.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 做饭 -- “孟母帮子思打扫房间和做饭”：准备饭菜，specifies that she prepares the meals rather than merely naming the general activity.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 跟子思老师住：跟他住，Since 子思老师 has just been named in the same sentence, use a pronoun for this close re-occurrence rather than repeating the full name.
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬到子思老师的家：搬到了子思老师的家，The move is a completed event in the narrative, so 搬到 takes 了.
- Expressiveness: After the mother’s concern, add a short inner thought to show why she worries: “住在这儿会影响孟子的学习。”
- Expressiveness: After the final sentence, add Mengzi’s feeling to make his studying more personal: “他觉得很孤单，只好一个人看书。”
- Expressiveness: When introducing the teacher’s home, add one concrete detail about the learning environment: “子思老师的家很安静，书架上有很多书。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show how Mengzi’s environment changes and how adults support his learning. The cause-and-effect structure with 因为 also gives the final sentence a clear reason for his behavior.

## Scene 3

> 子思认为，对孩子来说，读书最重要，特别是学哲学。孟子不是练习写文章，就是看哲学书。孟子从五岁一直学哲学。他才五岁就学哲学，难怪他成为很有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 从五岁一直学哲学：从五岁开始一直学哲学，A starting age needs the structure “从 + time + 开始” before describing an action that continues.
- 成为很有名的哲学家：成为了很有名的哲学家，This later achievement is presented as completed, so “成为” takes the perfective marker “了.”
