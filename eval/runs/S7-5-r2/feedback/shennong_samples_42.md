## Scene 1

> 古代的时候有一个人叫神农。他需要当上医，一看别的人就想帮助他们。但是，他有一个问题，他不知道什么东西能做药。神农听说一个远远的地方有一种植物，什么病人吃都会觉得身体更好。神农三个月走去，找到有植物的地方，他看到一个很漂亮的植物，又香又好看。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，This time phrase is redundant because the time noun already gives the setting.
- 当上医：当医生，The noun for the profession is “医生”; “当医生” means to work as a doctor.
- 一看：一看到，Here the intended meaning is that he sees someone, so the resultative complement is needed.
- 别的人：别人，Use “别人” for people other than oneself when no specific comparison group has been introduced.
- 什么病人吃都会觉得身体更好：不管什么病人，吃了这种植物都会觉得身体更好，Use “不管什么……都……” to express that the result applies to every kind of patient, and state what the patients eat.
- 一个很漂亮的植物：一种很漂亮的植物，“植物” takes the measure word “种,” not the general measure word “个.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 东西 -- “他不知道什么东西能做药”：植物，identifies the exact kind of thing being discussed and matches the later description of a plant.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 有植物的地方：有这种植物的地方，Repeat the specific noun phrase to link this place clearly to the plant Shennong heard about, rather than referring vaguely to any plant.
- Coherence (Temporality): 神农三个月走去，找到：神农走了三个月，找到了，Put the duration after the verb and use “了” for the completed walking period and the completed finding action.
- Expressiveness: When Shennong hears about the plant, add his hopeful feeling to show why he starts travelling: “神农很高兴，觉得这种植物可能能帮助病人。”
- Expressiveness: At the moment he sees the plant, add a concrete visual detail: “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought after he notices its smell and appearance: “这是什么植物？我一定要试一试。”

### Encouragement
You clearly give Shennong a helpful goal, and the final description uses both smell and appearance to make the plant easier to imagine.

## Scene 2

> 他觉得，这种一定是做药的花。原来他想错了。吃了以后，神农快生病。 这时一个穿着蓝色衣服的人帮神农，快给他一种红的灵芝。神农吃了以后，他没有病，想起来以前一个人告诉他，灵芝对身体有好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 快生病：很快生病了，Use 很快 to mean “soon/quickly” in a completed narrative event; add 了 because the illness begins after the eating action is completed.
- 快：赶快，快 before a verb commonly sounds like an instruction, while 赶快 describes the blue-clothed person acting urgently in the narration.
- 给：给了，The giving is a completed action in this past event, so the verb needs 了.
- 没有病：病好了，After someone has become ill, 病好了 specifically expresses recovery; 没有病 only states the general absence of illness.
- 告诉他：告诉过他，The telling happened before the present moment of remembering, so 过 marks it as a past experience.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他：delete 他，Since 神农 has already been named in the same sentence, use zero form rather than repeating a pronoun.
- Expressiveness: At the moment he mistakes the flower for medicine, add one visual or sensory detail to show why he believes it is useful, such as “花又香又好看，神农觉得它一定可以做药。”
- Expressiveness: When the illness starts, add a physical feeling to make the danger clearer, such as “他的肚子突然疼了起来，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Let the blue-clothed person speak while helping, such as “他着急地说：‘快吃这个灵芝！’”

### Encouragement
You clearly show an effective sequence: the character makes a mistake, becomes ill, receives help, and then remembers what he learned before. You also use “原来” and “这时” well to make the events more story-like.

## Scene 3

> 啊，他怎么忘记了？他把灵芝放在包里。一回家就告诉别人。他找到药以后他写下怎么做药在书上。所以人们都知道，那红灵芝对身体好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 那红灵芝：那种红灵芝，A demonstrative referring to a kind of plant needs a measure word before the noun phrase; 种 identifies the type of plant.
- 对身体好处：对身体有好处，The phrase needs 有 between 对身体 and 好处; 对身体有好处 means that something benefits the body.
- 写下怎么做药在书上：在书上写下怎么做药，A place adverbial introduced by 在 comes before the verb, so 在书上 should come before 写下.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 放 -- “他把灵芝放在包里”：收进，shows that the lingzhi is put inside the bag and kept there more specifically.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 他把灵芝放在包里：他把灵芝放在包里了，This is a completed placement in the narrative, so 了 marks the completed action and resulting new location.
- Coherence (Temporality): 一回家就告诉别人：一回家就告诉了别人，This is a one-time completed action after returning home, so 告诉 takes 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 写下：写下了，The writing is completed before people know the information, so 了 marks completion.
- Expressiveness: At the opening question, add a short thought to show why he is surprised: “他想：我怎么会把这么重要的事忘了？”
- Expressiveness: When he puts away the medicine, add careful action and ownership: “他小心地把红灵芝收进自己的包里。”
- Expressiveness: After writing, add a simple line explaining the discovery: “灵芝对身体有好处，可以做药。”

### Encouragement
You used a clear event sequence with “一回家就” and connected the result with “所以.” You also kept the important object, the lingzhi, consistent throughout the scene.
