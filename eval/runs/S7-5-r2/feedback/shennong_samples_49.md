## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有很多人不知道什么植物能吃，什么植物不能吃。有很多人吃植物以后就吃坏肚子了，因为他们吃了毒。那个时候，有一个老人叫神农，他什么植物都觉得好吃，不怕生病，不怕毒。他希望别人也可以吃好吃的植物。可是他知道，如果他们还怕吃毒，不会吃他喜欢的植物。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 already functions as a time expression, so adding 的时候 redundantly repeats the idea of “time.” [teacher rule: gudai_de_shihou_why]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不知道 -- “很多人不知道什么植物能吃，什么植物不能吃”：分不清，emphasizes that people could not distinguish edible plants from inedible ones.
- 喜欢 -- “他喜欢的植物”：爱吃的，specifies that these are plants he enjoys eating, which fits the surrounding idea of trying plants.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the people become ill, add a physical detail to show their discomfort, such as “肚子疼得直冒冷汗”.
- Expressiveness: When introducing Shennong’s motivation, add an inner thought or short quotation, such as “我一定要找出能吃的植物”.
- Expressiveness: Describe the plants more specifically when Shennong likes them, such as “又嫩又香的植物”, to help the reader imagine what he finds appealing.

### Encouragement
You clearly explain the central problem: people did not know which plants were safe to eat. You also introduce Shennong smoothly and show his different attitude toward plants.

## Scene 2

> 所以，他上山下山找植物，吃了很多样子的植物。他是第一人，找到了茶。他把所有的植物写下来了。他一直都吃了，他连毒都能吃。可是他还没吃了一个叫夹竹桃的植物。他找到了那个像星星的植物，觉得很漂亮，马上就吃下了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很多样子的植物：很多种植物，样子 describes an object’s appearance; 种 is the measure word for different kinds of plants. [rule: word_choice]
- 他是第一人，找到了茶：他是第一个找到茶的人，an ordinal meaning “the first person” requires the measure word 个, and the action of finding tea should modify 人 in this sentence. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 找 -- “他上山下山找植物”：寻找, emphasizes a deliberate, ongoing search；探寻, emphasizes exploring in order to discover unfamiliar plants.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 他一直都吃了：他一直都在吃，this describes an ongoing activity, so use 在 rather than 了, which marks a completed action. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 他还没吃了一个叫夹竹桃的植物：他还没吃过一种叫夹竹桃的植物，没 negates a completed action and does not combine with 了; 过 shows that he has not tried this kind of plant before. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 他找到了那个像星星的植物，觉得很漂亮，马上就吃下了：他找到那个像星星的植物，觉得很漂亮，马上就吃下了，when several actions are presented in one series, only the final action takes 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: At the final sentence, add visual details about the flower before he eats it, such as: “那儿开着黄色的花，像星星一样，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: At the moment he decides to eat it, add his thought to show his confidence: “他心想：这么漂亮的植物，一定可以吃。”
- Expressiveness: When he records the plants, specify what he writes down: “他把植物的颜色、大小和有没有毒都写下来了。”

### Encouragement
- Your scene has a clear action sequence: he travels, searches, finds tea, records plants, and then tries a new plant. You also used useful result phrases such as 写下来 and 吃下了.

## Scene 3

> 可是夹竹桃的毒很重，让他肚子很痛。他在地上躺着，觉得他快会死了。可是正好有别人找到他了，给了他一个像伞的植物，叫灵芝。他吃灵芝以后就觉得很好，不再生病了。他测量起来了，像星星的植物是毒，像伞的植物是药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 夹竹桃的毒很重：夹竹桃有毒，Here, 毒 cannot be used as a gradable description with 很重; 有毒 correctly states that the plant is poisonous. [rule: word_class_misuse]
- 别人：有人，别人 distinguishes other people from a previously mentioned person or group; no such comparison is present here. [teacher rule: bieren]
- 一个像伞的植物：一种像伞的植物，种 is the suitable measure word when referring to a type of plant; 个 is too general here. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 痛 -- “让他肚子很痛”：疼得厉害，makes the physical pain more vivid while keeping the same strong degree.
- 很好 -- “他吃灵芝以后就觉得很好”：好多了，specifically shows that he improved after being in pain；舒服多了，focuses on his physical relief and recovery.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 觉得他快会死了：觉得快会死了，The person having the thought is the same as the subject of the sentence, so use zero form rather than repeating a pronoun. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他在地上躺着：所以，他在地上躺着，His lying on the ground is a result of the stomach pain, so 所以 clearly marks the cause-and-effect relation. [rule: coherence_connectives]
- Expressiveness: At the moment of pain, add a physical reaction to show how badly he suffers, for example: “他捂着肚子，疼得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: When the helper arrives, describe him briefly to make the scene easier to picture, for example: “一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人赶快跑过来。”
- Expressiveness: Add Shennong’s worried thought while he is lying down, for example: “我会死吗？”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the important sequence of pain, rescue, and recovery. The use of “吃灵芝以后就” also makes the recovery happen in a clear order.
