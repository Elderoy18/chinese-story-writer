## Scene 1

> 这是古代的故事。孟子小的时候，他的爸爸很早就去世了。从他的家到墓地很近，所以他每天老是从早到晚去墓地跟朋友们玩玩儿。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 从他的家到墓地很近：他的家离墓地很近，Use 离 to describe that one place is close to another; 从……到…… introduces a route or range and does not fit this distance pattern here.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子小的时候：有一个小男孩叫孟子。孟子小的时候，On a character’s first mention, introduce the name together with a brief identifying noun phrase, rather than assuming the reader already knows who the named person is.
- Expressiveness: At the cemetery action, add a specific observation such as “不同的人来墓地祭拜” to make the setting easier to picture.
- Expressiveness: After the friends’ activity, add Mengzi’s thought or feeling, such as “觉得祭拜很有趣”, to show why he keeps returning.

### Encouragement
You clearly establish both the family background and the reason Mengzi often goes to the cemetery. The cause-and-effect connection is easy to follow, and the completed-action marker in the father’s death is used well.

## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈觉得在墓地玩儿，害得，所以他们搬家。搬家对孟子有好的影响，他才五岁，可是每天不是写字就是帮妈妈的工作。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在墓地玩儿，害得：孟子在墓地玩儿，害得她老是道歉，The verb requires both an affected person and a harmful result; this also supplies the subject of the action that the mother thinks about.
- 帮妈妈的工作：帮妈妈做事，The intended meaning is to assist the mother with tasks. A possessive noun phrase cannot directly follow 帮 in this meaning; use a verb phrase after the person being helped.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好的影响 -- “搬家对孟子有好的影响”：好处，states the benefit more directly and naturally in this sentence；积极的影响，specifies that the effect supports his development in a positive way

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 所以他们搬家：所以他们搬家了，The move is a completed event in the story, so the final verb needs 了.
- Expressiveness: At the cemetery scene, add a contrast between the mourners’ feelings and the child’s behaviour, for example: “来祭拜的人都很伤心，孟子却笑着学他们。”
- Expressiveness: At the decision to move, add the mother’s worried thought or speech, for example: “孟母心想：‘不能再住在这儿了。’”
- Expressiveness: When describing his daily activities, add an attitude or feeling, for example: “孟子每天认真写字，觉得很有意思。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show a reason for the family’s move and create an effective contrast between Mencius’s young age and his daily activities.

## Scene 3

> 他很聪明的人，难怪他成为有名的人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他很聪明的人：他是一个很聪明的人，When describing someone as a person with a quality, use 是 to introduce the noun phrase 一个很聪明的人; 很聪明 by itself can also be an adjective predicate.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为有名的人：成为了有名的人，Becoming well known is presented as a completed result in the narrative, so the verb needs 了.
- Expressiveness: At the ending, you could name what he became to make “有名的人” more concrete: “难怪他后来成为了有名的哲学家。”
- Expressiveness: You could add a comment from other people to give the ending a stronger story-like conclusion: “人们都说，孟子的成功离不开妈妈的帮助。”

### Encouragement
- You used 难怪 effectively to connect Mengzi’s intelligence with his later success. The sentence also gives the scene a clear concluding feeling.
