## Scene 1

> 古代，孟子跟孟母住在墓地的旁边。可是孟母注意，住在墓地的旁边影响孟子。他常常跟朋友们祭拜玩，所以孟母决定他们会搬家。这次，他们住在城市里的商店的旁边，从早到晚很吵。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 孟母注意：孟母注意到，The result complement 到 is needed here because the meaning is that she came to notice or realize something, rather than simply paid attention.
- 祭拜玩：学着别人祭拜，祭拜 means carrying out a real mourning or worship ritual; it cannot be directly followed by 玩 to mean imitating it for fun.
- 孟母决定他们会搬家：孟母决定搬家，决定 is followed directly by the verb phrase describing the decision; repeating the people who will move is unnecessary here.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很吵 -- “从早到晚很吵”：吵闹，describes a continuously noisy and bustling environment；嘈杂，specifically suggests many mixed, unpleasant sounds.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子跟孟母：有一个小男孩叫孟子，他和妈妈，On first mention, introduce the main character with a name and identifying noun phrase before using him in the narrative.
- Expressiveness: At the moment when孟母 notices the problem, add her inner worry, such as “孟母心想：‘这样下去，孟子会学坏的。’”
- Expressiveness: After describing the noisy city, add one sensory detail to show why it is noisy, such as “街上人来人往，商人不停地叫卖。”
- Expressiveness: Show孟母’s emotion before the move with a short line of speech, such as “孟母着急地说：‘我们不能再住在这儿了。’”

### Encouragement
You set up both locations clearly and used the characters’ names consistently. The use of 所以 also shows that you are connecting Mengzi’s behaviour to Mengmu’s decision.

## Scene 2

> 城市也有点儿危险。害得孟母只好一直陪孟子。孟子也没有朋友，难怪他老是看起来不高兴。住在商店的旁边不是适合孟子，而是让他觉得很伤心。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 也：delete，Here no earlier person or place has been described as dangerous, so 也 has no comparison or repeated action to refer back to.
- 孟子也没有朋友：孟子没有朋友，Here 也 incorrectly suggests that another person also has no friends, but no such person has been mentioned.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 危险 -- “城市有点儿危险”：不太安全，specifies that the problem is safety rather than a general kind of danger.
- 陪 -- “一直陪孟子”：照看，more specifically shows that Meng’s mother is watching over and protecting him.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 住在商店的旁边不是适合孟子，而是让他觉得很伤心：住在商店的旁边不但不适合孟子，还让他觉得很伤心，Both ideas are true consequences of living there, so use the paired connector expressing addition rather than 不是……而是……, which corrects one idea by replacing it with another.
- Expressiveness: After “城市有点儿危险,” add a concrete detail showing why it feels unsafe: “商店旁边人来人往，马路上的车很多。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where Mengzi feels sad, add his inner feeling or a short thought: “孟子看着热闹的商店，心里却觉得很孤单。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show Mengzi’s emotion with “很伤心” and “看起来不高兴.” The scene also gives a clear reason for Meng’s mother to stay close to him.

## Scene 3

> 所以，孟母和孟子搬到学校的旁边住。才五岁孟子开始学哲学。在学校，孟子不是看书，就是练习写字。将来，他成为中国的一个非常有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 才五岁孟子开始学哲学：孟子才五岁就开始学哲学，The pattern requires the subject before the age phrase and uses “才 + age + 就 + verb” to show that the action started at an unusually young age.
- 将来：后来，This is a completed event in a past narrative; “后来” introduces a later event, while “将来” refers to the future.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 看书 -- “孟子不是看书，就是练习写字”：读书，more specifically expresses reading books rather than simply looking at them；阅读，emphasizes the act of reading and learning from written material.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬到学校的旁边住：搬到了学校的旁边住，The move is a completed story event, so the verb needs “了” to mark completion.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为中国的一个非常有名的哲学家：成为了中国的一个非常有名的哲学家，After “后来,” becoming a philosopher is presented as a completed change of state, so use “了.”
- Expressiveness: After the school setting appears, add one concrete detail about the learning environment, such as “学校里很安静，到处都能听见读书声。”
- Expressiveness: At the end, show Mengmu’s feeling with a short line of speech, such as “孟母高兴地说：‘这儿很适合你学习！’”

### Encouragement
- You used the pattern “不是……就是……” correctly to show Mengzi’s two daily activities clearly. The school setting also gives this scene a strong, positive contrast with the earlier noisy home.
