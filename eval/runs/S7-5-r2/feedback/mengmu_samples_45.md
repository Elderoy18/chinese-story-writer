## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子。他们住在墓地的旁边，所以孟子老是祭拜。孟子不是祭拜，就是跟朋友聊天。他很喜欢他的城市，但是城市有才两个学校，都很小。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 有才两个学校：才有两个学校，When 才 expresses that the number is limited, it comes before 有: “only have two schools.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 小 -- “都很小”：规模都不大，specifies that the schools are small in scale rather than merely physically small

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子：一个叫孟子的男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国，At first mention, introduce the character with both the name and a full noun phrase, rather than naming him only with a pronoun in the next sentence.
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery sentence, add a feeling and a concrete action, such as “孟子觉得祭拜很有趣，还学着别人鞠躬。”
- Expressiveness: When describing the schools, add one visible detail, such as “两所学校的教室都很小，操场也不大。”

### Encouragement
You used the contrast connector “但是” appropriately to show that the boy likes the city despite its small schools. The “不是……就是……” pattern also gives a clear picture of his usual activities.

## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈不是不喜欢他们的城市，而是想孟子有很好的教育。他们搬家到有很多学校的城市。他们从早到晚走路，孟子一直抱怨。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 想孟子有很好的教育：想让孟子受到很好的教育，When expressing a wish for another person to receive or do something, use 想让 + person + verb phrase; 受到教育 is the natural verb phrase for receiving an education.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬家到：搬家到了，The move is a completed event in the story, so add 了 after the resultative complement 到.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 。他们搬家：，所以他们搬家，The mother’s wish for better education is the cause of the move, so use 所以 to show the cause-and-effect connection.
- Expressiveness: At the final sentence, add a physical detail to show why the journey feels difficult: “孟子走得腿都酸了。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short complaint from孟子 after “一直抱怨” to make his feeling more vivid: “孟子说：‘我们还要走多久？’”
- Expressiveness: At the first sentence, show the mother’s motivation through an inner thought: “妈妈心想：‘我想让孟子受到更好的教育。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the mother’s positive reason for moving, rather than simply saying that she dislikes the old city. The use of “从早到晚” and “一直抱怨” also effectively shows that the journey is long and tiring.

## Scene 3

> 他难怪抱怨，他想他的朋友，因为他不要离开他的城市。他的妈妈找到孟子一个老师。跟老师孟子学最重要的文学，所以他成为哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 找到孟子一个老师：给孟子找到了一个老师，The person receiving help must be introduced with 给; 找到了 also shows that the mother successfully found a teacher.
- 跟老师孟子学最重要的文学：孟子跟老师学最重要的文学，The subject should come before the phrase 跟老师 so that it is clear who is studying.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 想 -- “他想他的朋友”：想念，more specifically expresses missing friends who are not nearby.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为哲学家：成为了哲学家，This is a completed change of identity in the narrative, so 成为 takes 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他难怪抱怨：难怪他抱怨，原来，The result follows 难怪, and 原来 introduces the reason that explains it.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为：也，The two ideas are both reasons for the complaint, so 也 links them as parallel reasons rather than making one the cause of the other.
- Expressiveness: At the opening, add a short line of speech to show why he is upset, such as: “我不想离开我的朋友！”
- Expressiveness: When the mother finds a teacher, add one detail about the learning environment, such as: “老师每天教孟子读书和写字。”
- Expressiveness: Before the final sentence, show Meng子’s response to learning, such as: “孟子慢慢喜欢上了学习。”

### Encouragement
You clearly connected Meng子’s feelings about his friends and city to his complaint. You also gave the scene a clear outcome by showing that he studied with a teacher and later became a philosopher.
