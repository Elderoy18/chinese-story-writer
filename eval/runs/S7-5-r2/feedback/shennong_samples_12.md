## Scene 1

> 很久以前，古代的人很不知道什么植物能吃，也什么植物不能吃。所以很多人常常吃不安全的植物，吃不安全的植物对你的身体有坏处，很麻烦的情况。但是一个人，神农，要解决这个问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很不知道：不知道，Degree adverbs such as 很 do not normally modify the negative knowledge verb 不知道 in this meaning. [rule: none]
- 也什么植物不能吃：也不知道什么植物不能吃，Also, 也 must be followed by a verb phrase; here it should modify 不知道 rather than directly precede the noun phrase. [teacher rule: ye_vp]
- ，很麻烦的情况：，这很麻烦。，The original ending is only a noun phrase and cannot function as a complete predicate after the comma; use 这 as the subject and 麻烦 as the predicate. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不安全 -- “吃不安全的植物”：有害的，specifies that the plants harm people’s health；有毒的，more specifically identifies plants containing poison in this story
- 有坏处 -- “对你的身体有坏处”：有害，more concise and precise for a harmful effect on the body

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 你的身体：他们的身体，The sentence is about many people, so the pronoun should continue to refer to that group rather than suddenly addressing the reader. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Reference): 一个人，神农：一个叫神农的人，On first mention, introduce the character with a full noun phrase that clearly gives both the name and identity. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Conjunction): 但是一个人：于是，一个叫神农的人，The previous sentences describe a problem, and this character’s wish to solve it is a response or result, not a contrast; use a result connector. [rule: none]
- Expressiveness: After the opening sentence, make the uncertainty more concrete: “人们不知道什么植物能吃，什么植物不能吃。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where people eat harmful plants, add a clear consequence: “很多人吃了有毒的植物以后，常常生病。”
- Expressiveness: When introducing Shennong, add his purpose to make him sound determined: “有一个叫神农的老人决定找出哪些植物可以做药。”

### Encouragement
You clearly establish the main problem of the scene: people do not know which plants are safe to eat. You also introduce a character who wants to solve that problem, which gives the scene a strong direction.

## Scene 2

> 他又聪明又努力，旅行很远找到很多植物。比方说，灵芝和夹竹桃。他一吃各种各样的植物就知道能不能吃这些植物。然后，神农写他的主意。他喜欢植物的味道，特别是花。神农遇到了一个黄色的花，一吃就觉得不舒服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 旅行很远找到很多植物：去了很远的地方，找到了很多植物，Distance is expressed with a destination phrase such as “很远的地方,” and the completed narrative actions need “了.” [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- 写他的主意：把自己的主意写下来了，“写下” is a resultative form meaning to record something, and “自己的” clearly shows that the ideas belong to the subject. [rule: resultative_complement]
- 一个黄色的花：一朵黄色的花，“花” uses the measure word “朵,” not the general measure word “个.” [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不舒服 -- “一吃就觉得不舒服”：肚子疼，names the physical problem more specifically；难受得不得了，shows that the discomfort is very strong

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：他，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the same character was named in the previous sentence and no other male character competes as the referent. [rule: redundancy_delete]
- Expressiveness: After the final sentence, describe the pain and his physical reaction: “他在地上躺着，肚子疼得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Make the flower more vivid when it first appears: “那儿开着黄色的像星星一样的花，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: Add Shennong’s worried thought after he feels sick: “他想：我会死吗？”

### Encouragement
You used the pattern “一……就……” correctly to show an immediate result. You also named specific plants, which helps the reader picture Shennong’s discoveries.

## Scene 3

> 发现了夹竹桃有毒。别人看到他，很快给他吃一个灵芝。神农一吃就觉得比较好。然后，他继续写他发现的信息。会告诉他的朋友哪一个植物能不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别人：一个人，别人 means “other people” in contrast with a previously specified person or group; use 一个 to introduce an unspecified person here. [teacher rule: bieren]
- 写他发现的信息：记下他发现的信息，写 only describes producing written text, while 记下 means recording discovered information so it is kept. [vocab list]
- 哪一个植物能不能吃：哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃，哪一个 and 能不能 create two overlapping question forms; use parallel clauses to state which plants are edible and which are not. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 比较好 -- “神农一吃就觉得比较好”：好一些了，makes the physical improvement after eating the medicine clearer without suggesting a complete recovery.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 给他吃一个灵芝：给他吃了一个灵芝，This is a completed action in the narrative, so use 了 to mark completion. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: After the medicine works, add a concrete physical detail, such as “他的肚子不那么疼了，觉得好一些了。”
- Expressiveness: When introducing the helper, add one visual detail, such as “一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人看到神农。”
- Expressiveness: When Shennong tells his friends, add a short line of speech, such as “这种植物有毒，不能吃。”

### Encouragement
You used the “一……就……” pattern well to show that Shennong felt better immediately after eating the medicine. The scene also has a clear sequence, moving from discovering danger to receiving help and sharing what he learned.
