## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个男孩名字叫孟子。孟子和孟子的妈妈两个人住在很穷的农村。他们住的地方旁边是个墓地，墓地老是有很多人祭拜。因为人们在祭拜，他们一直在哭。孟子和孟子的妈妈住在这里才一个星期就觉得受不了了，而且害得孟子的学习。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，this time expression is redundant because 古代 already names a time period, while 的时候 also means “at the time of.”
- 有一个男孩名字叫孟子：有一个叫孟子的男孩，use the modifier structure “叫…的” before the noun to introduce a named character clearly.
- 老是：常常，老是 usually carries a negative, complaining tone; this sentence neutrally describes a frequent event.
- 害得孟子的学习：影响了孟子的学习，害得 must be followed by a person and a harmful result clause; it cannot directly take a noun phrase such as “孟子的学习.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子的妈妈两个人：孟子和他的妈妈两个人，when the same character is mentioned again within the same clause, use a pronoun rather than repeating the name.
- Coherence (Reference): 因为人们在祭拜，他们一直在哭：因为人们在祭拜，一直在哭，within the same sentence, use zero form for the repeated subject; 他们 could also be confused with the boy and his mother mentioned earlier.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子的妈妈住在这里：孟子和他的妈妈住在这里，after naming 孟子, use the pronoun in the possessive phrase rather than repeating his name.
- Coherence (Temporality): 住在这里才一个星期就觉得受不了了：在这里才住了一个星期就觉得受不了了，use 了 with 住 to mark the completed one-week period before the following reaction.
- Expressiveness: When introducing the cemetery, add a sensory detail to show its atmosphere, such as “墓地里传来哭声，气氛很伤心。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where the family cannot bear the place, show the boy’s or mother’s feelings through an action, such as “孟子觉得有点害怕，拉着妈妈的手。”

### Encouragement
You clearly establish the setting and explain why the cemetery is difficult for the family. The sentence with “才一个星期就觉得受不了了” effectively shows that the problem became serious very quickly.

## Scene 2

> 孟子的妈妈觉得孟子取得良好的教育是很重要的，所以孟子的妈妈认为这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好，就打算跟孟子搬家。孟子的妈妈带孟子不是搬到城市，而是搬到了孟子老师的家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 取得良好的教育：接受良好的教育，接受教育 is the natural verb–object combination; 取得 is normally used for achievements, results, or qualifications rather than education itself.
- 跟孟子搬家：带着孟子搬家，跟孟子搬家 suggests that the mother and son move together as equals; 带着孟子搬家 correctly shows that the mother takes her child with her.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 地方 -- “这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好”：环境，specifies that the concern is the child’s living and learning surroundings.
- 不太好 -- “对儿子的教育不太好”：不合适，states more precisely that this setting is unsuitable for the child’s education.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子的妈妈觉得孟子取得良好的教育是很重要的，所以孟子的妈妈认为：孟子的妈妈觉得孟子接受良好的教育很重要，所以她认为，use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence of the same character when no other person competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 孟子的妈妈带孟子不是搬到城市，而是搬到了孟子老师的家：孟子的妈妈带着孟子搬到了孟子老师的家，the paired connector 不是……而是…… should refute a stated or conventional mistaken idea; here there is no earlier expectation that they would move to the city, so a direct sequence is clearer.
- Expressiveness: After the mother decides to move, add her anxiety to show why the decision matters: “孟母很着急，担心这里会影响孟子的学习。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short inner thought before the move: “她心想：‘如果一直住在这里，孟子怎么能好好学习呢？’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect the mother’s wish for her son’s education with her decision to move, so the reason for her action is easy to understand.
- Your use of the completed action in “搬到了” effectively shows that the move has been finished.

## Scene 3

> 孟子跟他的老师从早到晚学习，难怪他的成绩很快就进步了很多。孟子学习的时候，孟子的妈妈不是待在家里就是编织衣服赚钱。以后孟子成为了中国有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 以后孟子成为了中国有名的哲学家：后来孟子成为了中国有名的哲学家，At the start of a new narrative sentence meaning “afterwards,” use “后来”; “以后” normally follows a time expression or means a future time.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 成绩 -- “他的成绩很快就进步了很多”：学业成绩，specifies that the improvement is in his school performance rather than another kind of result.
- 有名 -- “中国有名的哲学家”：闻名全国的，makes the scope of his fame more specific: he is known throughout the country.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子学习的时候，孟子的妈妈：孟子学习的时候，他的妈妈，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the referent is clear and no other person competes with it.
- Expressiveness: At the first sentence, add a small detail about how hard he studies, such as “孟子每天认真地跟老师读书，常常读到天黑。”
- Expressiveness: After the improvement in grades, add his mother’s feeling to make the result more vivid, such as “孟母看到孟子进步了，心里很高兴。”
- Expressiveness: At the final sentence, add a short line of speech from his mother, such as “孟母高兴地说：‘这儿的环境真适合学习！’”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the connection between steady study and improved grades, and “从早到晚” gives the first sentence a strong sense of effort. The final sentence also uses “成为了” correctly to describe a completed achievement.
