## Scene 1

> 很久以前，古代的人很不知道什么植物能吃，也什么植物不能吃。所以很多人常常吃不安全的植物，吃不安全的植物对你的身体有坏处，很麻烦的情况。但是一个人，神农，要解决这个问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的人很不知道：古代的人们不知道，Use 人们 for a specific group of people, and 很 cannot modify the negative mental-state verb 不知道.
- 也什么植物不能吃：也不知道什么植物不能吃，Also must modify a verb phrase, so the predicate 不知道 needs to be repeated after 也.
- 很麻烦的情况：这是很麻烦的情况，A noun phrase such as 很麻烦的情况 cannot stand alone as the final predicate; add 这 and 是 to make a complete clause.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不安全 -- “吃不安全的植物”：危险的，specifies that the plants may cause harm；有毒的，more exactly identifies the danger in this plant-eating context.
- 有坏处 -- “对你的身体有坏处”：会伤害，states the harmful physical effect more directly and vividly.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 但是一个人，神农，要解决这个问题：但是有一个叫神农的人要解决这个问题，Follow the first-mention rule: introduce a new character with a full noun phrase giving both the name and who the person is.
- Expressiveness: After the sentence about unsafe plants, add a concrete consequence such as “很多人吃了以后，常常生病。” This makes the danger more vivid.
- Expressiveness: Show Shen Nong’s determination with an internal thought or short quotation, such as “神农想：我一定要找出能吃的植物。” This gives the character a clearer motivation.
- Expressiveness: Add one sensory detail about the plants he will investigate, such as “山上的植物有红的，也有黄的。” This can make the setting easier to imagine.

### Encouragement
- You clearly set up the main problem—people do not know which plants are edible—and connect it to harmful consequences with 所以.
- You also introduce Shen Nong’s goal of solving the problem, which gives this scene a strong direction.

## Scene 2

> 他又聪明又努力，旅行很远找到很多植物。比方说，灵芝和夹竹桃。他一吃各种各样的植物就知道能不能吃这些植物。然后，神农写他的主意。他喜欢植物的味道，特别是花。神农遇到了一个黄色的花，一吃就觉得不舒服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 旅行很远：到很远的地方旅行，Use a destination phrase with 到 + place; 旅行 cannot directly be modified by 很远 to mean that someone travelled a long distance.
- 写：写下，Use 写下 when the intended meaning is to record an idea or observation.
- 一个黄色的花：一朵黄色的花，朵 is the appropriate measure word for a flower; 个 is too generic here.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 味道 -- “他喜欢植物的味道，特别是花”：气味，specifies that he likes the smell of plants, which fits the mention of flowers；香味，adds the detail that the flower scent is pleasant.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 找到很多植物：找到了很多植物，Use 了 for the completed narrative action of finding the plants.
- Expressiveness: When the yellow flower appears, add a visual detail to make the setting clearer: “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: After the uncomfortable feeling, show a more specific physical reaction or thought: “他肚子疼了起来，心想：我会死吗？”
- Expressiveness: When recording his ideas, state what he learned about the plants: “他写下了灵芝和夹竹桃的用处。”

### Encouragement
- You use “又聪明又努力” effectively to describe the character’s strengths, and “一…就…” clearly shows an immediate result. The scene also introduces the two important plants in a clear way.

## Scene 3

> 发现了夹竹桃有毒。别人看到他，很快给他吃一个灵芝。神农一吃就觉得比较好。然后，他继续写他发现的信息。会告诉他的朋友哪一个植物能不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 比较好：好多了，比较 requires a stated comparison, while the intended meaning is that his condition improved after taking the medicine.
- 写他发现的信息：记下他发现的信息，记下 means to record information so it can be kept; bare 写 is not natural for recording discovered information in this context.
- 哪一个植物能不能吃：哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃，do not combine a question word with an A-not-A question when stating general information; this version clearly contrasts edible and inedible plants.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 别人看到他：一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人看到他，别人 distinguishes other people from the speaker and cannot introduce one specific new helper; at first mention, use a full noun phrase.
- Coherence (Temporality): 很快给他吃一个灵芝：很快给他吃了一个灵芝，use 了 after the completed action of giving him the medicine to eat.
- Expressiveness: After the medicine is given, add a physical detail about the recovery, such as “神农的肚子慢慢不疼了”.
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech when the helper gives the medicine, such as “快吃这个，也许能帮你！”.
- Expressiveness: Show Shen Nong’s feeling after he improves, such as “神农松了一口气，觉得自己好多了”.

### Encouragement
- You used the pattern “一……就……” effectively to show that Shen Nong felt better immediately after eating the medicine.
- The scene also has a clear order of events: discovering the poison, receiving medicine, recovering, and recording the discovery.
