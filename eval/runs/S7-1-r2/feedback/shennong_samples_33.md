## Scene 1

> 古代时候，人不知道他们能吃什么植物，常常一吃就死。但是不知道为什么，没有办法。所以有一天，神农出去试一试很多种植物，告诉人什么植物是安全什么是危险。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代时候：在古代，古代 is already a time expression, so adding 时候 is redundant; use 在 to introduce the historical time setting.
- 人：人们，use 人们 for a concrete group of people; 人 is a generic noun and does not refer naturally to the people in this event.
- 告诉人：告诉人们，use 人们 when referring to the group receiving Shennong’s information.
- 什么植物是安全什么是危险：什么植物是安全的，什么是危险的，an adjective used after 是 to describe a thing normally takes 的 in this pattern.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：有一个叫神农的人，follow the first-mention rule: introduce a new character with a full noun phrase before using only the name in the story.
- Coherence (Reference): 他们能吃：能吃，use the zero form for the already established group in this general statement; the pronoun is unnecessary after 人们.
- Coherence (Temporality): 试一试很多种植物：试了很多种植物，use 了 for the completed past action of trying many kinds of plants; the last verb in this action sequence takes 了.
- Expressiveness: After the deaths are mentioned, add a short detail about people’s fear or confusion, such as “大家都很害怕，不知道该吃什么。”
- Expressiveness: When introducing Shennong’s action, show his purpose or determination with an internal thought, such as “神农想：我一定要找到安全的植物。”
- Expressiveness: Add one concrete plant detail to make his testing action easier to imagine, such as “他看见一朵黄色的花，就摘下来尝了一点。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish the problem first and then show Shennong taking action to solve it. The pattern “一吃就死” is also an effective way to show an immediate result.

## Scene 2

> 发现有的植物对身体非常好，特别是茶，让肚子感觉又暖和又清楚。他告诉朋友：“你吃哪种植物，这就要看你的口味。比方说，你爱吃甜的，可能喜欢糖。”他最喜欢吃花，除了夹竹桃以外，什么都花想吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 清楚：舒服，清楚 describes whether information or a view is clear; it does not describe a physical sensation in the stomach. 舒服 describes bodily comfort.
- 你吃哪种植物，这就要看你的口味。：你喜欢吃哪种植物，要看你的口味。，这就要看……了 requires an uncertain question after 看, but 你的口味 is only a noun phrase. The revised wording directly states that food preference depends on taste.
- 什么都花想吃：什么花都想吃，For the “every kind of” meaning, the noun must follow 什么, and 都 comes after the full phrase: 什么花都…….

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好 -- “有的植物对身体非常好”：有益，specifies that the plants benefit health rather than being generally “good.”
- 爱吃 -- “你爱吃甜的”：偏爱甜食，more precisely expresses a particular preference and names sweet foods as a category.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 发现有的植物：发现了有的植物，Use 了 after 发现 because this is a completed discovery in the sequence of past events.
- Expressiveness: After the tea discovery, add a concrete bodily detail, such as “喝了茶以后，他觉得肚子里暖暖的，很舒服。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where he excludes the dangerous flower, add his cautious thought: “他心想：夹竹桃不能吃，我以后要小心。”
- Expressiveness: You could show that he values the discovery by adding “他把茶对身体有好处这件事记在书上。”

### Encouragement
- You used a direct quotation to show the character explaining his idea, which makes the scene more lively.
- The contrast created by “除了夹竹桃以外” clearly shows that the character knows one flower is different from the others.

## Scene 3

> 他发现这个口味，一百岁时候，不小心吃了毒的花。一吃就开始死。但是生病时候告诉人什么植物是药，什么植物是毒。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- “一百岁时候”： “一百岁的时候”，A multi-syllable time expression before “时候” needs “的”.
- “毒的花”： “有毒的花”，Use “有毒的 + noun” to describe a poisonous plant; “毒的植物” is not the natural form.
- “开始死”： “快要死了”，“死” is a change-of-state verb and does not naturally take “开始” in this meaning; “快要……了” expresses that death is imminent.
- “生病时候”： “生病的时候”，The verb phrase before “时候” needs “的”.
- “人”： “人们”，Here the meaning is people in general, so the generic group noun should be “人们”.
- “什么植物是毒”： “什么植物有毒”，Use “有毒” to say that a plant is poisonous; “是毒” does not fit this description.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不小心 -- “不小心吃了毒的花”：误食了，specifically means accidentally eating something that should not be eaten and is especially fitting for a poisonous flower.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): “这个”： “一种”，This is a first mention, so “这个” has no clear earlier referent; introduce it with an indefinite noun phrase instead.
- Coherence (Temporality): “发现”： “发现了”，The discovery is a completed event in this past narrative, so it needs the completion marker “了”.
- Coherence (Temporality): “告诉”： “告诉了”，This is a completed action in the narrative, so use “了” after the verb.
- Expressiveness: Before the accidental eating, describe the flower so the danger feels more vivid: “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花。”
- Expressiveness: After the poisoning, add a physical reaction to show the severity: “他的肚子疼了起来，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Add Shen Nong’s worried thought after he becomes very ill: “他想：我会死吗？”

### Encouragement
- You used the pattern “一……就……” to show that the consequence happened very quickly. You also clearly tried to distinguish plants that are medicine from plants that are poisonous.
