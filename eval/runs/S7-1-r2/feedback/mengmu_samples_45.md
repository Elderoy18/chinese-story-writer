## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子。他们住在墓地的旁边，所以孟子老是祭拜。孟子不是祭拜，就是跟朋友聊天。他很喜欢他的城市，但是城市有才两个学校，都很小。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 already expresses a time period, so adding 的时候 is redundant.
- 有才两个学校：才有两所学校，才 must come before the verb 有, and 所 is the appropriate measure word for schools.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很小 -- “都很小”：规模很小，specifies that the schools are small in overall scale, such as their campus or number of students.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子：一个叫孟子的男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国，follow the first-mention rule by introducing the character’s name together with the noun phrase.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子不是祭拜：他不是祭拜，after a recent mention of 孟子 with no competing male character, use a pronoun for close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Reference): 他的城市：自己的城市，when the possessor is the same person as the subject, 自己的 makes that relationship clear.
- Coherence (Reference): 但是城市：但是它，use a pronoun for the close re-occurrence of the city mentioned earlier in the same sentence.
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery is introduced, add a visual detail about the visitors, such as “每天都有来祭拜的人在墓地哭。”
- Expressiveness: At “孟子老是祭拜,” show his reason or feeling: “孟子觉得祭拜很好玩。”
- Expressiveness: After “跟朋友聊天,” add a brief line from his mother to show her concern: “孟母说：‘不要再学别人祭拜了。’”

### Encouragement
- You used “不是……就是……” correctly to show the two things Mengzi often does.
- The final sentence creates a clear contrast between how much he likes the city and its limited, small schools.

## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈不是不喜欢他们的城市，而是想孟子有很好的教育。他们搬家到有很多学校的城市。他们从早到晚走路，孟子一直抱怨。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 想孟子有很好的教育：想让孟子受到很好的教育，use 想让 + person + verb phrase to express wanting someone to receive or do something.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很好的 -- “孟子有很好的教育”：优质的，specifies that the education is high in quality.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们搬家到有很多学校的城市：他们搬到了一个有很多学校的城市，use 了 after 搬到 to mark the completed move; add 一个 to introduce the new destination as an indefinite place.
- Expressiveness: After the move, add a concrete detail about the new city’s atmosphere, such as “街上有很多商人卖各种各样的东西，从早到晚都很热闹。”
- Expressiveness: Show Mengzi’s feeling through an action and quoted speech at “孟子一直抱怨,” such as “孟子皱着眉头说：‘我走得脚都疼了！’”

### Encouragement
You clearly contrast the mother’s reason for moving with her feelings about the city. The final sentence also gives Mengzi a believable reaction, which helps the scene feel more lively.

## Scene 3

> 他难怪抱怨，他想他的朋友，因为他不要离开他的城市。他的妈妈找到孟子一个老师。跟老师孟子学最重要的文学，所以他成为哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他难怪抱怨：难怪他抱怨，难怪 is an adverb meaning “no wonder,” so it normally comes before the subject and the action it explains.
- 不要离开：不想离开，不要 is usually used for a prohibition or refusal, while 不想 expresses that someone does not want to do something.
- 跟老师孟子学：孟子跟老师学，put the subject before the prepositional phrase so that it is clear who is studying.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 想 -- “他想自己的朋友”：想念，more precisely expresses missing friends who are far away.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他的朋友：自己的朋友，use 自己的 when the possessor is the same person as the subject; this clearly shows that the friends are Mengzi’s own friends.
- Coherence (Reference): 他的城市：自己的城市，use 自己的 for the subject’s own possession, making it clear that this is the city Mengzi does not want to leave.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他的妈妈找到孟子一个老师：他的妈妈给孟子找到了一个老师，this is a completed search, so 了 follows the resultative complement 到; 给孟子 also places the beneficiary before the verb.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为哲学家：成为了哲学家，use 了 to mark that becoming a philosopher is a completed later result.
- Expressiveness: At the point where Mengzi misses his friends, add an inner thought to show his sadness: “我不想离开这里，我会想念朋友。”
- Expressiveness: When his mother finds a teacher, add a short line of speech showing her hope for him: “你要认真学习，将来会有出息的。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly give Mengzi a reason for complaining by connecting it to his friends and his city.
- Your final sentence also shows a clear long-term result from Mengzi’s learning.
