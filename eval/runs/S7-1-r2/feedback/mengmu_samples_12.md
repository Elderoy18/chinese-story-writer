## Scene 1

> 古代，孟子跟孟母住在墓地的旁边。可是孟母注意，住在墓地的旁边影响孟子。他常常跟朋友们祭拜玩，所以孟母决定他们会搬家。这次，他们住在城市里的商店的旁边，从早到晚很吵。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 孟母注意，住在墓地的旁边影响孟子：孟母注意到住在墓地的旁边会影响孟子，use the result complement 到 with 注意 to show that she noticed or discovered something; the following clause is the content she noticed.
- 跟朋友们祭拜玩：跟朋友们一起学着别人祭拜，祭拜 is a ritual action, so it cannot be followed by 玩 to mean “play at doing it”; 学着别人祭拜 clearly expresses copying the ritual.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很吵 -- “从早到晚很吵”：嘈杂，specifies that the place is full of mixed, disturbing noise.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子跟孟母：有一个小男孩叫孟子，他和妈妈，at first mention, introduce the character with a name and role; after introducing 孟子, use 他 rather than repeating the name.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟母决定他们会搬家：孟母决定搬家，after 决定, use a verb phrase directly; the moving group is already clear, so repeating it as 他们 is unnecessary.
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery affects the child, add Mengmu’s worried feeling: “孟母很担心孟子会学坏。”
- Expressiveness: Make the city scene more vivid by showing where the noise comes from: “很多商人一直在叫卖东西。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought from Mengmu before the decision: “孟母心想：‘这里不适合孟子。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the change from the quiet cemetery setting to a noisy city setting, and the use of 所以 gives Mengmu’s decision a clear reason.

## Scene 2

> 城市也有点儿危险。害得孟母只好一直陪孟子。孟子也没有朋友，难怪他老是看起来不高兴。住在商店的旁边不是适合孟子，而是让他觉得很伤心。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- Nothing to flag.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 危险 -- “城市也有点儿危险”：不太安全，specifies that the problem is the city’s safety rather than an immediate danger.
- 陪 -- “一直陪孟子”：照看，more clearly shows that孟母 is supervising and protecting孟子 because she is worried.
- 伤心 -- “让他觉得很伤心”：很孤单，more precisely describes the feeling of having no friends.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): “住在商店的旁边不是适合孟子，而是让他觉得很伤心”：“住在商店的旁边不适合孟子，因为这让他觉得很伤心”，the original uses the paired connector “不是……而是……” even though the second clause gives the cause of the first evaluation, rather than correcting or contrasting it. “因为” expresses the cause relation.
- Expressiveness: After “城市也有点儿危险”, add one concrete detail about what makes the area unsafe, such as “街上人很多，马车也跑得很快。”
- Expressiveness: At “孟子也没有朋友”, show孟子’s feeling through an action, such as “他常常一个人坐在窗边，看着外面的孩子玩儿。”
- Expressiveness: After孟母 has to stay with him, add her inner thought or speech, such as “孟母心想：‘这儿真的不适合孟子长大。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect the city environment to孟子’s feelings and孟母’s concern. The phrase “难怪他老是看起来不高兴” gives a natural explanation for his unhappiness.

## Scene 3

> 所以，孟母和孟子搬到学校的旁边住。才五岁孟子开始学哲学。在学校，孟子不是看书，就是练习写字。将来，他成为中国的一个非常有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 搬到：搬到了，A completed move in a past narrative takes 了 after the resultative complement 到: V + 到 + 了.
- 才五岁孟子开始学哲学：孟子才五岁就开始学哲学，Use the paired pattern 才 + age/number + 就 + verb to show that an action began unusually early.
- 成为：成为了，Becoming a philosopher is a completed change of state in this narrative, so the verb takes 了.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 写字 -- “孟子不是看书，就是练习写字”：练字，more specifically means practising handwriting or Chinese characters.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 将来：后来，Use 后来 to mark a later event in a completed past narrative; 将来 refers to the future from the present viewpoint.
- Expressiveness: After the move, add a sensory detail about the school environment, such as “学校里很安静，学生们都在认真读书。”
- Expressiveness: Show Meng Mu’s positive feeling about this new place with a short thought or quotation, such as “孟母很高兴，心想：‘这里很适合孟子学习。’”
- Expressiveness: Add Meng Zi’s feeling while studying to make his change clearer, such as “孟子觉得看书很有意思。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the positive change after the family moves to the school area. The pattern “不是……就是……” is used well to describe Meng Zi’s daily study activities.
