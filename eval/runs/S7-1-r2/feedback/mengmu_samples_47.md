## Scene 1

> 这个男孩子叫孟子，孟子是古代的人。孟子的妈妈觉得住在哪儿对童年很重要，所以他们常常搬了家。她望子成龙，所以孟子的妈妈安排他的生活。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 对童年：对孩子的成长，童年 names a period of life; here the intended meaning is the child’s development, which can be influenced by where the family lives.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 古代的人 -- “孟子是古代的人”：古人，a concise noun for a person who lived in ancient times.
- 生活 -- “安排他的生活”：日常生活，specifies that the mother organizes his everyday routine.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 这个男孩子叫孟子：有一个男孩子叫孟子，This is the character’s first mention, so use an indefinite noun phrase rather than a demonstrative phrase that assumes the reader already knows him.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子是古代的人：他是古代的人，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when no other male character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Reference): 她望子成龙，所以孟子的妈妈安排他的生活：她望子成龙，所以安排他的生活，Use zero form for the same subject within the same sentence rather than repeating the full noun phrase.
- Coherence (Temporality): 常常搬了家：常常搬家，The zero particle is required for a habitual action; 了 marks a completed event, while 常常 describes a routine.
- Expressiveness: After the moving sentence, add a concrete setting detail such as “他们搬到了一个墓地旁边。” This gives the move a clearer scene.
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery detail, show what influences the child with “孟子每天看见人们来祭拜，觉得很好玩。” This adds his perspective and helps explain the mother’s concern.
- Expressiveness: At the mother’s decision, add an inner thought or quoted speech such as “她心想：‘住的地方会影响孩子的成长。’” This makes her motivation more vivid.

### Encouragement
- You use a clear cause-and-effect link to connect the mother’s belief about the home environment with the family’s moves.
- The idiom 望子成龙 is well chosen and clearly shows the mother’s hopes for her son.

## Scene 2

> 因为他们常常搬了家，中国人说，孟母三迁。孟子没有爸爸，所以，他的妈妈更重要。从这儿到那儿，妈妈教孟子很多技能。先她让孟子跟朋友玩儿，然后教他怎么买又便宜又好质量的东西。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 先她让孟子跟朋友玩儿：她先让孟子跟朋友玩儿，time-order adverbs such as 先 normally come after the subject and before the verb.
- 又便宜又好质量的东西：又便宜又质量好的东西，质量 is a noun, so 好 should follow it as the predicate 质量好; this also makes the two descriptions parallel.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 技能 -- “妈妈教孟子很多技能”：生活技能，specifies that these are practical skills used in everyday life；生活本领，emphasizes abilities that help him manage daily life independently.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 常常搬了家：常常搬家，常常 describes a routine action, so the zero particle is required rather than completed-action 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为他们常常搬了家，中国人说，孟母三迁：因为他们常常搬家，所以中国人说：“孟母三迁。”，因为……所以…… is the paired connector for a cause-and-effect relationship.
- Expressiveness: After the mother teaches him to buy things, add a short line of advice to show her care: “妈妈耐心地说：‘买东西要看质量，也要看价格。’”
- Expressiveness: Add Mengzi’s response after the lesson to show his feelings: “孟子点点头，觉得妈妈教得很有用。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the mother’s important role in Mengzi’s life, and your use of 先……然后…… gives the teaching actions a clear sequence.

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- Nothing to flag.

### Vocabulary Suggestions

### Encouragement
