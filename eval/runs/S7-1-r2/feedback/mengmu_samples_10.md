## Scene 1

> 孟子小的时候，从早到晚都跟朋友玩。孟子和他的朋友喜欢玩在墓地里，装祭拜。孟子五岁的时候，跟妈妈搬家。他们搬家不是因为孟子玩得太多，而是因为妈妈认为他们的家离墓地太近。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 玩在墓地里，装祭拜：在墓地里玩，假装祭拜，Location phrases normally come before the action verb, and “装” cannot directly introduce the action being imitated; “假装” is used before a verb phrase to mean pretending to do it.
- 跟妈妈：妈妈带着他，“跟” presents Mengzi as accompanying his mother, but the intended meaning is that his mother moved him with her; “带着” expresses that she took him along.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 朋友 -- “孟子和他的朋友”：玩伴，specifies that these are friends he regularly plays with and fits the following action.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子小的时候：有一个小男孩叫孟子。他小的时候，The first-mention rule requires introducing a new character with a full noun phrase before using a name or pronoun in the narrative.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和他的朋友：他和朋友，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the referent has just been named and no other character competes with it.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子五岁的时候：他五岁的时候，Use a pronoun for this close re-occurrence of Mengzi.
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬家：搬了家，This is a completed past action, so the main action verb needs the perfective marker “了”; “带着” already marks the accompanying state.
- Expressiveness: At the cemetery scene, add a visible action to show how the children imitate the ritual: “孟子和朋友一边笑，一边假装祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: At the final sentence, show the mother’s worry through an inner thought or quotation: “妈妈心想：‘这儿离墓地太近了。’”

### Encouragement
- You used “从早到晚” effectively to show that Mengzi spent a great deal of time playing.
- Your “不是……而是……” sentence clearly contrasts an incorrect reason for moving with the real reason.

## Scene 2

> 妈妈认为，住离墓地太近，害得孟子的心气。孟子和孟母搬到子思老师的家，跟子思老师住。孟母帮子思打扫房间和做饭。子思帮孟母教她的儿子。因为孟子现在没有朋友，他老是读书。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 住离墓地太近：住在离墓地太近的地方，A location phrase after 住 needs 在, and the phrase needs 地方 to complete the location.
- 害得孟子的心气：会害得孟子没有心气，害得 must be followed by the affected person and a harmful result clause; 孟子的心气 is only a noun phrase and does not state the result.
- 老是读书：一直读书，老是 has a negative, complaining tone, while 一直 neutrally describes continuing to read.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 朋友 -- “因为孟子现在没有朋友”：玩伴，specifies that he lacks companions to play with, which fits the context of a young child.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 妈妈认为：她认为，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence: 妈妈 was named in the immediately preceding scene and has no competing female referent.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟母搬到子思老师的家：他们搬到子思老师的家，Use a pronoun for the close re-occurrence of the two previously mentioned characters.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟母帮子思打扫房间和做饭：她帮子思打扫房间和做饭，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when only 孟母 can be the female subject.
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬到子思老师的家：搬到了子思老师的家，The move is completed before the later actions of living, cleaning, and teaching, so use 了 after the resultative complement 到.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为孟子现在没有朋友，他：因为孟子现在没有朋友，所以他，Use 因为……所以…… to mark the cause-and-effect relation.
- Expressiveness: At the opening, add the mother’s worry to make her decision feel more personal, for example: “孟母很担心：‘住在这儿会影响孟子的学习。’”
- Expressiveness: In the final sentence, show how 孟子 feels about having no companions, for example: “孟子觉得很孤单，只好安静地坐在桌前读书。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect the lack of friends with 孟子’s reading habit, so the reader can understand his situation.
- You also introduce 子思老师 clearly before describing the actions in his home.

## Scene 3

> 子思认为，对孩子来说，读书最重要，特别是学哲学。孟子不是练习写文章，就是看哲学书。孟子从五岁一直学哲学。他才五岁就学哲学，难怪他成为很有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 从五岁一直学哲学：从五岁开始一直学哲学，When stating the starting point of a continuing action, use 从 + time + 开始.
- 才五岁就学哲学：才五岁就开始学哲学，The 才 + number + 就 + verb pattern needs 开始 here to show that he began studying at an unusually young age.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 重要 -- “读书最重要”：关键，emphasizes that reading plays a decisive role for a child.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子从五岁开始一直学哲学：他从五岁开始一直学哲学，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the referent is clear from the previous sentence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为很有名的哲学家：成为了很有名的哲学家，Use 了 with 成为 to mark the completed change of becoming a philosopher.
- Expressiveness: After the first sentence, add a short line of speech from the teacher to show his teaching: 子思说：“读书能让你明白很多道理。”
- Expressiveness: In the sentence about philosophy books, add Mengzi’s feeling to make his learning more vivid: 孟子觉得哲学书很有意思。

### Encouragement
- You used the 不是……就是…… pattern correctly to show Mengzi’s study routine.
- The connection between starting philosophy early and later becoming a famous philosopher is clear and logical.
