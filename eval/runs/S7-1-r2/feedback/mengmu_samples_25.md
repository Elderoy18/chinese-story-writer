## Scene 1

> 这是古代的故事。孟子小的时候，他的爸爸很早就去世了。从他的家到墓地很近，所以他每天老是从早到晚去墓地跟朋友们玩玩儿。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 从他的家到墓地很近：他的家离墓地很近，Use “离 + place + 很近” to express the distance between two locations. “从” marks a starting point and cannot by itself express that two places are close.
- 从早到晚去墓地跟朋友们玩玩儿：从早到晚都在墓地跟朋友们玩儿，“从早到晚” should modify an ongoing activity, so “在墓地玩儿” fits better than the arrival action “去墓地”. Also, verb reduplication in “玩玩儿” suggests a brief or casual action, which conflicts with an all-day duration.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子小的时候：有一个小男孩叫孟子。孟子小时候，The first-mention rule asks for a new main character to be introduced with both a name and an identifying noun phrase, rather than appearing only as a name.
- Expressiveness: At the mention of the cemetery, add a visual detail to create the setting, such as “墓地里常有人低着头祭拜，四周很安静。”
- Expressiveness: Show why the child likes going there by adding his feeling or action, such as “孟子觉得祭拜很好玩，笑着学大人的样子。”

### Encouragement
You used “所以” appropriately to connect the nearby cemetery with Mengzi’s daily visits. You also correctly used “去世了” to show that his father’s death was a completed past event.

## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈觉得在墓地玩儿，害得，所以他们搬家。搬家对孟子有好的影响，他才五岁，可是每天不是写字就是帮妈妈的工作。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在墓地玩儿，害得：孟子在墓地玩儿会带来不好的影响，The original leaves the verb without a person affected and a harmful result. This verb pattern must be followed by someone and the negative result, so a different result expression is clearer here.
- 帮妈妈的工作：帮妈妈工作，The particle turns “mother’s work” into a noun phrase, but the intended meaning is to help Mother do her work. Use the verb phrase without the particle.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好的 -- “搬家对孟子有好的影响”：积极的，identifies the effect specifically as beneficial and constructive；正面的，clearly describes the impact as favorable.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他的妈妈：孟子的妈妈，This is the mother’s first mention in the story, so use the character’s name plus her relationship to introduce her unambiguously.
- Coherence (Temporality): 所以他们搬家：所以他们搬家了，Use 了 for the completed moving action in this past event.
- Expressiveness: At the mother’s concern in the first sentence, add her feeling to show why the move matters: “孟子的妈妈很担心。”
- Expressiveness: You could add a short thought or line of speech before the move: “她心想：‘不能让孟子每天在墓地玩儿。’”
- Expressiveness: When describing the new routine, add a concrete detail about learning: “孟子每天坐在桌子前，一笔一画地写字。”

### Encouragement
- You used 才……可是…… to create a clear contrast between Mengzi’s young age and his daily routine.
- The 不是……就是…… pattern is a good choice for showing repeated everyday activities.

## Scene 3

> 他很聪明的人，难怪他成为有名的人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很聪明的人：是一个很聪明的人，To describe someone as a “smart person,” use the copula plus a measure word before the noun; an adjective predicate cannot be followed directly by a noun in this structure.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为：成为了，Use 了 after the verb to mark the completed change of becoming a well-known person in this past narrative.
- Expressiveness: After the first clause, add one concrete habit that shows his intelligence, such as “他很爱看书，也常常认真思考。” This gives the reader evidence rather than only stating the quality.
- Expressiveness: At the end, add a specific sign that he became well known, such as “后来，很多人都来听他讲道理。” This makes “有名” more vivid and concrete.

### Encouragement
- You chose a clear final result for the character and used “难怪” to connect his intelligence with his later success.
