## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个人叫神农。一天，神农正在路上走，一看很多人正在吃一些植物。一些人一吃植物就生病了，神农想帮别人。所以想知道哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 already expresses a time period, so adding 时候 is redundant.
- 一看很多人正在吃一些植物：看到很多人正在吃一些植物，看到 means “see”; 看 means “look,” so it does not fit when the narrator means that 神农 noticed this scene.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 想知道 -- “所以想知道哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃”：想弄清楚，emphasizes resolving the uncertainty about edible plants；想辨别，emphasizes telling edible and inedible plants apart.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 所以想知道：所以他想知道，after 神农 was named in the immediately preceding sentence, use the pronoun 他 to maintain a clear close re-occurrence reference.
- Expressiveness: At “一些人一吃植物就生病了,” add a concrete symptom to show the harm: “一些人一吃植物就肚子疼得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: At “神农想帮别人,” show 神农’s thoughts with a short quotation: “神农心想：我要找出能吃的植物，帮大家少生病。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduce 神农 and use the contrast “哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃” to show his goal. The sequence from people becoming ill to 神农 wanting to help is also easy to follow.

## Scene 2

> 然后，神农开始尝很多植物，比方说他吃过生姜、芥兰和菠菜。一些植物吃真好，特别是黄瓜。但是，一些植物毒得很，特别是夹竹桃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一些植物吃真好：一些植物很好吃，To describe that food tastes good, use the evaluative complement pattern with 好吃; 吃真好 does not naturally express the taste of the plants.
- 毒得很：有毒，毒 is not used with degree complements such as 得很; use 有毒 to describe poisonous plants.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After listing the plants, add a sensory detail to show how they differ, for example: “生姜有点辣，黄瓜吃起来很清爽。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where Shennong begins tasting plants, add his thought to show his purpose and uncertainty, for example: “神农想：这些植物到底能不能吃呢？”

### Encouragement
You use “然后” effectively to continue from the earlier scene, and the specific examples of plants make Shennong’s testing feel concrete. The contrast between plants that taste good and plants that are dangerous is also clear.

## Scene 3

> 一个人对神农说，麻烦我们可以吃什么植物？神农说，这就要看你想不想生病了。以后，神农告诉别人一些植物能吃，一些植物不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 麻烦：请问，麻烦 usually introduces a request for someone to do something, while 请问 is the polite expression used to introduce a question.
- 以后，神农告诉别人一些植物能吃，一些植物不能吃：后来，神农告诉人们，一些植物能吃，一些植物不能吃，后来 means “afterwards” when it begins a new narrative sentence; 别人 requires a contrast with the speaker or a particular person, so 人们 is the appropriate general group here.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: At the question, add the person’s feeling to make the encounter livelier: “一个人担心地问：‘请问，我们可以吃什么植物？’”
- Expressiveness: After the explanation, add one concrete example of a dangerous plant: “神农说：‘像星星一样的花有毒，不能吃。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly contrast the two important ideas, “能吃” and “不能吃,” which makes the information easy to understand.
- You also used the A-not-A form “想不想” correctly after “这就要看……了.”
