## Scene 1

> 很久以前，有一个老男人叫神农。神农在古代每一天看到了别的小镇人生病还是不舒服，所以他决定开始找新的药。他下一天开始爬当地的山脉，找了各种各样的植物。神农试一试每个植物，知道有什么好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别的小镇人：小镇上的人，Use a location phrase with 上的 to refer to residents of a town; 别 means “other” and does not identify people who live in this town.
- 还是：或，Use 或 or 或者 to link alternatives in a declarative statement; 还是 is normally used for alternatives in a question.
- 下一天：第二天，Use 第二天 for “the next day” when telling past events; 下一天 is not the usual narrative time expression.
- 找了各种各样的植物：找到了各种各样的植物，找 means “to look for,” while 找到 means “to find.” Here the plants were found.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好处 -- “知道有什么好处”：药用价值，specifies the useful medicinal function of each plant in this context；作用，gives a broader but still clear description of what each plant can do.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农在古代每一天看到了：他在古代每一天看到了，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence: 神农 was introduced in the immediately preceding sentence, and no other character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农试一试每个植物：他试一试每个植物，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence: the preceding sentence already identifies him as 他, so repeating the name is unnecessary.
- Coherence (Temporality): 每一天看到了：每天看到，Use the zero particle for a habitual action. 每天 describes a routine, so 了 should not be used.
- Expressiveness: After the sick townspeople are mentioned, add Shen Nong’s motivation with a thought: “神农想：我要找到能治病的植物。”
- Expressiveness: When he climbs the mountains, add a simple setting detail: “高山上长着很多他不认识的植物。”
- Expressiveness: Show how carefully he learns from the plants: “他把每种植物的作用记下来。”

### Encouragement
You clearly introduced the main character with both a time setting and a name. You also used 所以 to show why Shen Nong decides to search for medicine, which makes the events easy to follow.

## Scene 2

> 比方说，他一次试一试有些植物的叶，吃得又新鲜又甜。煮这些叶以后，他一喝就知道他发现新的茶叶。虽然他找到了很多的新的植物，但是他的旅程有的时候特别是危险。比方说，他一吃灵芝就开始生病。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一次试一试有些植物的叶：有一次，他尝了一些植物的叶子，Use 有一次 to introduce one particular occasion. An action that was actually carried out should not use verb reduplication; use a completed verb with 了 instead.
- 吃得又新鲜又甜：吃起来又新鲜又甜，A 得-complement describes the manner or degree of the action, but these adjectives describe the leaves’ taste. Use 吃起来 to describe how the food tastes.
- 特别是危险：特别危险，特别是 introduces a specific item or category after “especially”; it cannot directly intensify an adjective. Use 特别 before an adjective.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 新的 -- “新的茶叶”：陌生的，specifies that this is a kind of tea leaf that he did not know before, rather than simply something newly made or obtained.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 知道他发现新的茶叶：知道自己发现新的茶叶，Use 自己 when the discoverer is the same person as the subject of 知道; this avoids repeating a pronoun whose reference could be read as another person.
- Coherence (Temporality): 煮这些叶以后：煮了这些叶以后，The boiling happens before the later drinking, so use 了 to mark the completed action in this sequence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 发现新的茶叶：发现了新的茶叶，Finding the tea leaves is a completed discovery here, so 发现 takes 了.
- Expressiveness: After the boiling, add a sensory detail about the drink, such as “水里慢慢散发出清香。”
- Expressiveness: At the moment of illness, show the physical feeling instead of only naming the illness, such as “他捂着肚子，疼得躺在地上。”
- Expressiveness: Add an anxious thought after he becomes ill, such as “我会不会死？”

### Encouragement
- You use clear example markers to move from the general journey to specific plants, and the contrast with 虽然……但是…… is a good attempt to show that exploration can be rewarding but risky.

## Scene 3

> 一个爬山的男人正好看到神农的麻烦，然后给了他一些药。神农最终他的小镇回到，跟别的人分享他的发现。你认为神农的故事真的吗？这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别的人：别人，Use 别人 for unspecified other people; 别的人 is used when distinguishing other people from a particular person or group already under discussion.
- 这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处。：这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处了。The sentence pattern 这就要看……了 normally takes sentence-final 了; this 了 belongs to the fixed pattern rather than marking a completed action.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 正好看到：正好看到了，The seeing is a completed narrative event, so 了 follows the resultative complement in 看到了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 神农最终他的小镇回到：神农最终回到了自己的小镇，A completed return takes 了 after the resultative complement in 回到了; the destination follows 回到, and 自己的 clearly refers back to the subject.
- Expressiveness: At the rescue moment, add a physical detail about Shen Nong’s condition, such as “神农躺在地上，肚子疼得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Make the helpful man more vivid with one identifying detail, such as “一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人正好看到了神农。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of dialogue when the medicine is given, such as “快吃这个，这个可以做药！”

### Encouragement
- You create a clear sequence from help, to returning home, to sharing discoveries. The final question also gives the reader a direct way to think about the story.

## End of story

### Content & Completeness
- At the beginning, explain that people did not know which plants were safe to eat, so they sometimes ate harmful plants and became sick.
- When 神农 begins climbing the mountains, make clear that he wants to learn which plants are safe to eat and which are not, not only to find new medicine.
- After discovering tea, add that 神农 writes down a record of it.
- After finding tea, the dangerous plant should be “夹竹桃”: 神农 eats it and faints, rather than becoming sick after eating “灵芝”.
- When 神农 faints, describe the person in blue giving him “灵芝”, and show that 神农 feels better after eating it.
- After 神农 recovers, add the visit and conversation with the person in brown.
- At the end of these meetings, show 神农 recording both “灵芝” and “夹竹桃”, including what happened when he tried them.

### Overall Encouragement
- You gave 神农 a clear mountain journey and included his discovery of tea, danger, and his return to share what he learned. Your story already has the feeling of an important adventure.
