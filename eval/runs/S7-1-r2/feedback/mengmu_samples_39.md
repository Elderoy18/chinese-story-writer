## Scene 1

> 古代的时候人觉得祭拜非常重要，孟子很小的时候，从早到晚一直祭拜，因为他妈妈告诉他祭拜很重要。虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜。他的同学们老是对他开玩笑了。孟子妈妈看了他的同学们对孟子不好的影响，所以孟子和孟子妈妈搬家了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候人：在古代，人们，古代 already expresses a time period, so adding 的时候 is redundant; 人 is a generic noun here, while 人们 correctly refers to people in general.
- 看了他的同学们对孟子不好的影响：看到了他的同学们对孟子的不良影响，看到 means “see/notice” and is needed here because the mother notices an effect, rather than simply looking at it.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 一直 -- “从早到晚一直祭拜”：不停地，emphasizes that the worshipping continued without stopping throughout the day.
- 不好的 -- “同学们对孟子不好的影响”：负面的，more precisely identifies the influence as harmful or unfavorable.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子很小的时候：有一个叫孟子的小男孩，孟子 is appearing for the first time, so use a full introduction with his name and identity rather than introducing only the name in a time phrase.
- Coherence (Reference): 所以孟子和孟子妈妈搬家了：所以她带着孟子搬家了，after the mother was named in the immediately previous clause, use the pronoun 她 rather than repeating both names; 带着孟子 also clearly shows that the mother took him with her.
- Coherence (Temporality): 老是对他开玩笑了：老是对他开玩笑，老是 describes a repeated habit, so it takes the zero aspect form rather than completed-action 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜：因为他每天只祭拜，所以没有朋友，this is a cause-and-effect relationship and needs 因为……所以……; 虽然 requires a contrasting main clause.
- Expressiveness: After the description of his daily worshipping, add an inner feeling to show why it matters to him: “他觉得祭拜很有趣。”
- Expressiveness: When describing the classmates, add a short line of speech to make their teasing more vivid: “他们笑着说：‘你每天只会祭拜！’”
- Expressiveness: Show the mother’s concern before the move with a thought or quotation: “孟子的妈妈很担心：‘这样会影响孩子。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the cause of Mengzi’s daily behavior by explaining that his mother told him worshipping was important.
- The phrase 从早到晚 gives the reader a strong sense of how often and how seriously he worshipped.

## Scene 2

> 孟子才十一岁，成为一个哲学家。孟子不是祭拜，而是看政治文章，难怪他成为了哲学家。不过，看过很多政治文章对他害得，他有很多问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 才十一岁，：才十一岁，就，The pattern with 才 + age/number needs 就 before the result clause to show that the achievement happened unusually early.
- 不过，看过很多政治文章对他害得，他有很多问题。：不过，看过很多政治文章害得他有很多问题。，The causative structure 害得 places the affected person directly after it and then gives the harmful result; 对他 cannot be inserted in this structure.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子不是祭拜：他不是祭拜，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the same character was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no competing referent is present.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为一个哲学家：成为了一个哲学家，Use 了 after a completed change-of-state action; becoming a philosopher is presented as finished.
- Expressiveness: After the reading activity, add an internal reaction to show why he has questions: “他越看越糊涂，心想：‘这些文章到底是什么意思？’”
- Expressiveness: Add a small detail about his unusual young age before the first sentence: “十一岁的孟子常常安静地读书，还会认真思考文章里的道理。”

### Encouragement
- You use the paired contrast structure effectively to show the difference between worshipping and reading. The sentence with “难怪” also gives the reader a clear connection between his activity and his achievement.

## Scene 3

> 他一直问问题，但是不能解决那些问题。孟子长大，他开始能解决问题。他常常建议你不是学金融，而是学政治，因为如果你懂政治，你会成为高薪。后来孟子成为教授，他教学生很厉害。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 建议你不是学金融，而是学政治：建议人们不要学金融，而要学政治，Advice about a preferred action uses the pattern “不要……，而要……”; “不是……而是……” is for correcting or rejecting a stated idea.
- 成为高薪：获得高薪，“成为” takes an identity, role, or status as its complement, while “高薪” is pay; “获得” correctly expresses receiving high pay.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 问问题 -- “他一直问问题”：提出问题, emphasizes raising questions for discussion or investigation.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子长大，他开始能解决问题：他长大了，开始能解决问题，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence, and use zero form for the same subject in the following clause rather than repeating “他”.
- Coherence (Reference): 后来孟子成为教授，他教学生很厉害：后来，他成为了教授，很会教学生，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence, and use zero form when the same subject continues within the sentence.
- Coherence (Reference): 建议你：建议人们，The second-person pronoun has no introduced referent in this third-person narrative; use a full generic noun phrase when referring to people generally.
- Coherence (Temporality): 长大：长大了，Use “了” after a change-of-state verb to show that growing up has been completed before the next event begins.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为教授：成为了教授，Use “了” to mark the completed change into the role of professor.
- Expressiveness: After his difficulty solving problems, add an internal feeling, such as “他觉得很着急，可是没有放弃”.
- Expressiveness: Make his teaching ability more concrete with a classroom detail, such as “他总是耐心地回答学生的问题”.
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of advice to show his personality, such as “他对学生说：‘学习政治，要先学会思考。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly show Mengzi’s development from someone who cannot solve problems to a capable professor. The repeated focus on questions and learning gives this scene a clear theme.
