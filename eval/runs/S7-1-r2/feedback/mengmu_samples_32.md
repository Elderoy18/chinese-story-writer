## Scene 1

> 有一天孟子去了墓地玩儿，但是他妈妈觉得这是不合适的地方让孩子玩，所以她决定了他们会搬家找更好的地方。她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福，而且住在这儿教育不太好。因为她望子成龙，所以她会一直搬家才能找到质量好的教育。然后他们搬到了离城市更近的地方。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 这是不合适的地方让孩子玩：这是一个不适合孩子玩儿的地方，Use the pattern “适合 + person + verb phrase” to say that a place is suitable for someone to do something; the verb phrase modifies “地方” with “的”.
- 她决定了他们会搬家找更好的地方：她决定带着孟子搬到一个更好的地方，Mental-state verbs such as “决定” normally take the zero particle rather than “了”, and “决定” should be followed directly by the action phrase it decides on.
- 她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福：她担心儿子的童年不会幸福，“恐怕” means “I am afraid that” and cannot take another subject after it in this pattern; use “担心” when the mother is afraid about her son’s future.
- 住在这儿教育不太好：这儿的教育不太好，Use “place + 的 + noun” to show that the education belongs to or is available in that place.
- 她会一直搬家才能找到质量好的教育：她得一直搬家，才能找到质量好的教育，“得……才能……” expresses a necessary condition; “会” cannot introduce the condition before “才能” in this structure.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 更好的 -- “找更好的地方”：更适合孩子成长的，specifies that the new place is better for the child’s development；教育条件更好的，specifies that the improvement concerns education.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 有一天孟子去了墓地玩儿：有一天，一个叫孟子的小男孩去了墓地玩儿，On a first mention, introduce a character with a full noun phrase that gives both the name and who the character is.
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery is mentioned, add a concrete action showing what interests the child, such as “孟子觉得祭拜很有趣，跟着别人学祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: At the mother’s decision, add her worried inner thought or quoted speech, such as “孟母心想：‘如果一直住在这儿，孩子可能会学坏。’”
- Expressiveness: When the family arrives nearer the city, describe the new setting with a sensory detail, such as “新家附近从早到晚都很热闹。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect the mother’s concern, her wish for her son’s success, and the family’s move with logical cause-and-effect links.
- You also used completed-action forms well in “去了” and “搬到了” to show the sequence of events.

## Scene 2

> 他们住才一月就又搬家。古代的时候，一般人不喜欢卖东西的人。但是孟子老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿，害得妈妈着急得很，因为她恐怕儿子会成为跟他们很像。她告诉了孟子：他们不是朋友，而是不好的人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 already expresses a time period, so adding 的时候 is redundant.
- 老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿：老是跟这些卖东西的人玩儿，two habitual-frequency adverbs should not be stacked here; 老是 already expresses that the action happens repeatedly.
- 她恐怕儿子会成为跟他们很像：她担心儿子会变得跟他们很像，恐怕 cannot take 她 as the person who is afraid; use 担心 for her concern. Also, 成为 takes a noun identity, while 变得 is used before an adjective phrase such as 跟他们很像.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们住才一月就又搬家：他们才住了一个月就又搬家了，use 才 before the duration phrase, and use 了 for the two completed actions in this past sequence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 她告诉了孟子：她告诉孟子：, use the zero particle with a saying verb that introduces direct speech.
- Expressiveness: After the sentence about sellers, add a small market detail to make the setting more vivid, such as “街上有很多商人叫卖，热闹极了。”
- Expressiveness: Show what Mengzi learns from the sellers instead of only saying that he plays with them, such as “孟子还学着他们收钱、讨价还价。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought or spoken line for his mother after she becomes worried, such as “孟母心想：‘这样下去可不行。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the contrast between Mengzi enjoying time with the sellers and his mother becoming worried. The use of 害得妈妈着急得很 effectively connects his behaviour to her reaction.

## Scene 3

> 你不是当数学家，而是当哲学家，不能当卖东西的人。所以他们又搬家。在他们第三个家，孟子学了怎么祭拜。妈妈感觉这个地方适合他们，告诉了孟子：我们找到了最后的家。在这儿妈妈让儿子从早到晚学习，他难怪成为有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 你不是当数学家，而是当哲学家，不能当卖东西的人：你不要当数学家或卖东西的人，而要当哲学家，Use 不要……而要…… to give advice about a future choice. 不是……而是…… corrects or contrasts an existing fact, so it does not fit this instruction.
- 在他们第三个家：在他们第三个家里，A location phrase with an ordinal home needs 里 to show that the action happens inside that home.
- 告诉了孟子：告诉孟子，A speaking verb that introduces direct speech takes the zero particle, not 了.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 又搬家：又搬家了，Use 了 for the completed moving event.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为：成为了，Use 了 because becoming a philosopher is a completed change of state.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 在这儿妈妈让儿子从早到晚学习，他难怪：因为在这儿妈妈让儿子从早到晚学习，所以他，Use 因为……所以…… to make the cause-and-effect relationship between studying and later success explicit.
- Expressiveness: When describing the third home, add one environmental detail to show why it supports learning, such as “新家旁边有一所学校，环境很安静。”
- Expressiveness: Add the mother’s feeling when she sees her son studying, such as “妈妈看着孟子认真学习，心里很高兴。”
- Expressiveness: Give the mother a short line of speech that shows her relief, such as “妈妈说：‘这儿的环境很适合你学习。’”

### Encouragement
- You use clear time detail in “从早到晚学习,” which helps the reader understand how hard Mengzi studies.
- You also include the mother’s speech, which makes the scene feel more like a story rather than only a list of events.
