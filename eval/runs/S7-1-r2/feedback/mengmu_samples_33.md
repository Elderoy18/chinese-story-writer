## Scene 1

> 一天，很久很久以前，孟子和他妈妈住在中国。孟子妈妈的工作是祭拜，孟子不高兴，因为他说我们老是搬家，为什么？他妈妈说，从早到晚，我一直祭拜，很多墓地得我祭拜。所以我们得去很多不同的地方。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一天，很久很久以前：很久很久以前的一天，A time phrase such as “very long ago” should modify “one day” with 的 when both are used together.
- 孟子不高兴，因为他说我们老是搬家，为什么？：孟子不高兴，因为他说：“我们为什么老是搬家？”，Direct speech should be introduced with a colon and quotation marks; the question word naturally comes before the reason or action being questioned.
- 很多墓地得我祭拜：我得祭拜很多墓地，The modal verb 得 needs the person who must do the action as its subject; “many cemeteries” cannot be the subject that “must worship.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不高兴 -- “孟子不高兴”：不满，more precisely shows that he is dissatisfied with moving so often.
- 工作 -- “孟子妈妈的工作是祭拜”：职责，emphasizes that worshipping is the responsibility she has to carry out.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和他妈妈住在中国：有一个小男孩叫孟子，他和妈妈住在中国，On first mention, introduce a character with a brief noun phrase that tells the reader who he is; after that introduction, a pronoun is natural.
- Expressiveness: At the first sentence, add a small detail about the graveyard setting to make the place easier to imagine, such as “他们的家在墓地旁边，周围安安静静的。”
- Expressiveness: After Mengzi’s complaint, show his feeling through an action or tone, such as “孟子皱着眉头，小声问妈妈。”
- Expressiveness: In the mother’s reply, add her own emotion about the difficult routine, such as “妈妈叹了口气，说：‘我每天都很忙。’”

### Encouragement
- You used a clear cause-and-effect connection with “因为” and “所以,” and the dialogue helps show why the family moves often.

## Scene 2

> 孟子想，难怪我们常常搬家，原来我妈妈很忙。可是常常搬家害得孟子很难找到朋友。每次搬家孟子也觉得新的地方不是太热就是太冷，不是太大就是太小。在哪的地方，他都不高兴。孟子想帮助妈妈祭拜，可是他六岁，所以不能祭拜。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- Nothing to flag.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 找到朋友 -- “常常搬家害得孟子很难找到朋友”：交到朋友，emphasizes forming friendships rather than simply locating people.
- 新的 -- “孟子也觉得新的地方不是太热就是太冷”：陌生的，emphasizes that each place feels unfamiliar to him after a move.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 在哪的地方，他都不高兴：不管在哪个地方，他都不高兴，use the paired connector 不管……都…… to express the “wherever” relationship; before a noun, 哪 needs the classifier 个.
- Expressiveness: After the difficulty making friends, add an inner thought to show his loneliness: “我什么时候才能有自己的朋友呢？”
- Expressiveness: At the description of the new places, add one concrete detail about what feels uncomfortable: “有的地方风很大，吹得他脸都红了。”
- Expressiveness: When he wants to help, add a short line of dialogue to show his intention: “妈妈，我也想帮你祭拜。”

### Encouragement
- You used 难怪……原来…… effectively to show that he understands why the family moves so often.
- The paired patterns 不是……就是…… clearly describe several reasons why he feels unhappy in the new places.

## Scene 3

> 四年后，孟子开始帮妈妈祭拜。他画画很厉害，他越来越喜欢祭拜，所以他祭拜不是想帮妈妈工作，而是他很喜欢画画。孟子成为很著名的古代画家。今天很多中国人很喜欢孟子。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 孟子成为很著名的古代画家：孟子成为了很著名的古代画家，Use 了 after 成为 to mark the completed change of state.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 画画很厉害 -- “他画画很厉害”：画得很出色, specifies that the quality of his drawing is especially good.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 而是他很喜欢画画：而是很喜欢画画，Use zero form for the same subject within one sentence when no other person competes as the referent.
- Expressiveness: At the point where he begins helping, show his positive feeling with a short detail, such as “孟子觉得能帮妈妈，心里很高兴。”
- Expressiveness: Add one concrete action to make the worship scene easier to picture, such as “他认真地准备祭拜用的东西。”
- Expressiveness: You could add a brief line from his mother after he helps, such as “孟母说：‘你长大了，能帮我了！’”

### Encouragement
You use “四年后” clearly to move the story forward in time. The final sentence also gives the reader a clear sense that孟子 remains well known today.
