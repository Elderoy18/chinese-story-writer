## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，孟子和孟母住了在靠墓地的家。孩子才五岁就每天在墓地祭拜了。在那个城市，孟母从早到晚都找工作，可是找不到，害得孟母没有钱。难怪她跟孩子搬家，原来买不了房。他们搬了到别一个城市找工作。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 already functions as a time expression, so adding 的时候 redundantly repeats the idea of “time.”
- 住了在靠墓地的家：住在靠近墓地的家里，住 describes a continuing state here and takes no 了; use 在 + place, and 家里 clearly marks the location.
- 跟孩子搬家：带着孩子搬家，跟 means “with” or “together with,” while 带着 shows that the mother takes her child with her when moving.
- 搬了到：搬到了，了 follows the verb plus its resultative complement: 搬到 + 了.
- 别一个城市：另一个城市，别的 distinguishes other things from a stated one and normally needs 的; 另一个 means one additional or different city.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 买不了 -- “原来买不了房”：买不起，specifies that the house cannot be bought because of insufficient money.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟母：有一个小男孩叫孟子，他和妈妈，on first mention, introduce the character with a name and a brief identifying noun phrase rather than assuming the reader already knows who he is.
- Coherence (Reference): 孩子：他，use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the previously mentioned character is clear and no other male character competes.
- Coherence (Reference): 害得孟母没有钱：害得她没有钱，after mentioning the mother earlier in the same sentence, use a pronoun for this close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 每天在墓地祭拜了：每天在墓地祭拜，daily actions are routines, so they take the zero particle rather than completed-action 了.
- Expressiveness: At the cemetery scene, add what the child finds interesting and what he copies. Example: “孟子觉得祭拜很有趣，就笑着学别人祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: Show the mother’s worry before the move through an internal thought or quoted speech. Example: “孟母心想：‘如果一直住在这儿，麻烦会越来越多。’”
- Expressiveness: When the family reaches the new city, add one sensory detail about the environment. Example: “城市里从早到晚都很热闹。”

### Encouragement
The scene has a clear sequence of living near the cemetery, facing financial difficulty, and moving away. You also used the age pattern well to show that the child was very young when the events began.

## Scene 2

> 这个城市老是很吵，不是人吵架，就是都叫，害得孟子一直不能睡觉。在那儿，孟母也找不到工作。孟子和妈妈要又一个次搬了家。在第三个城市，孟母找到工作，在商店做衣服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 人：人们，Use 人们 for a concrete group of people; 人 is a generic noun and does not naturally refer to the people making noise in this city.
- 要又一个次搬了家：要再搬一次家，With a modal verb expressing a future plan, use 要 + 再 + verb + number + measure word + object. 再 marks a repetition that has not happened yet, and completed-action 了 is not used after 要.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 吵 -- “这个城市老是很吵”：嘈杂，specifies that the city is full of mixed, overlapping noise.
- 叫 -- “不是人吵架，就是都叫”：喊叫，makes clear that the people are loudly calling out rather than simply making a sound.
- 做 -- “在商店做衣服”：缝制，more precisely describes making clothes by sewing.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 孟母找到工作：孟母找到了工作，Use 了 after 找到 to mark that finding the job was a completed action in the narrative.
- Expressiveness: After “这个城市老是很吵”, add a concrete sound detail, such as “从早到晚，街上都是吵闹声。”
- Expressiveness: Show Mengzi’s feeling after “一直不能睡觉”, such as “孟子又累又烦，晚上常常躺在床上叹气。”
- Expressiveness: Add Mengmu’s inner thought before the move, such as “孟母心想：‘这儿太吵了，孩子不能好好休息。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show why this home is unsuitable for Mengzi by connecting the city’s noise to his inability to sleep. You also move the scene forward effectively by showing that Mengmu finds a job in the next city.

## Scene 3

> 孟子在那个商店也找了到工作，可是不是做衣服的工作，而是喜欢的工作。孟子学习馆学了十五年，他的学习和他的旅行经验很重要，对他很多好处。他是大人的时候，他成为很有名的哲人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 学习馆学：在学校学习，学习馆 is not a conventional word for a school in this context; use 学校 as the place noun and 在 to introduce the location.
- 对他很多好处：对他有很多好处，when something benefits someone, the structure is 对 + person + 有 + 好处.
- 是大人的时候：长大以后，长大以后 expresses the transition from childhood to adulthood; 是大人的时候 only describes the state of being an adult and does not fit this change-of-state context.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很重要 -- “他的学习和他的旅行经验很重要”：宝贵，emphasizes that these experiences have lasting value to him.
- 很有名的 -- “他成为很有名的哲人”：享有盛名的，specifies that he is widely known and respected.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子：他，use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when no other male character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Reference): 他的旅行经验：旅行经验，within the same noun phrase, use zero form rather than repeating the same possessor pronoun.
- Coherence (Reference): 他成为：成为，after the subject has already appeared in the same sentence, use zero form rather than repeating the pronoun.
- Coherence (Temporality): 找了到：找到了，place 了 after the resultative complement: 找到 + 了 marks that the finding was completed.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为：成为了，use 了 after 成为 to mark the completed change of state.
- Expressiveness: After the job is found, add Mengzi’s emotion or inner thought: “他高兴地想：‘我终于找到自己喜欢的工作了！’”
- Expressiveness: When mentioning the fifteen years of study and travel, give one concrete detail: “他读了很多书，也去过很多地方。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show a progression from finding work to gaining knowledge and eventually becoming a respected philosopher.
- Your use of the contrast pattern 不是……而是…… effectively distinguishes the clothing job from the work Mengzi prefers.
