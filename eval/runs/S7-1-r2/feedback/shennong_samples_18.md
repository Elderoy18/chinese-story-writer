## Scene 1

> 神农和他的家人住在古代，所以不知道什么植物能吃。因为他的家人常常生病了，所以神农和他的哥哥一起去旅行找可以吃的植物。他们去很多不一样的地方，比方说高山、山谷和沙漠。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 住在古代：在古代生活，古代 is a time expression rather than a physical location; 住在 normally takes a place, while 在古代 can introduce the time of the action.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 找 -- “找可以吃的植物”：寻找，emphasizes the ongoing process of searching for plants.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：一个叫神农的人，follow the first-mention rule by introducing a new character with a full noun phrase before later using the name or pronouns.
- Coherence (Temporality): 常常生病了：常常生病，use the zero particle for a habitual action; 常常 shows that the illness happens repeatedly, not as one completed event.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们去很多不一样的地方：他们去了很多不一样的地方，use 了 for the completed narrative action of visiting many places.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 所以不知道：，不知道，this is a time/background relationship rather than a direct cause-and-effect relationship, so the cause connector 所以 is not needed.
- Expressiveness: After the family’s illness, add Shennong’s feeling to show his motivation: “神农看着生病的家人，心里很担心。”
- Expressiveness: In the travel sentence, add one concrete difficulty from the journey: “他们走过很高的山，也走过又热又干的沙漠。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech before the journey: “神农对哥哥说：‘我们一定要找到可以吃的植物。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show why Shennong begins searching, and the list of places—“高山、山谷和沙漠”—makes the journey feel broad and adventurous.

## Scene 2

> 他们在很漂亮的河找夹竹桃。神农问他的哥哥：“我应该吃这种植物吗？”哥哥说：“这就要看你想要安全还是接近知道了。“神农一吃夹竹桃就生病了。哥哥说：“太麻烦了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在很漂亮的河找夹竹桃：在很漂亮的河边找夹竹桃，After 在, use a location expression. 河边 specifies the place beside the river; 河 alone does not fit this location structure.
- 这就要看你想要安全还是接近知道了。“：这就要看你是想安全，还是想知道这种植物能不能吃了。”，接近 cannot be used with 知道 to mean “find out.” The pattern 这就要看……了 must be followed by a question form; 是……还是…… provides two clear alternatives here. The quotation also needs the closing mark ” rather than another opening mark “.
- 哥哥说：“太麻烦了。：哥哥说：“太麻烦了。”，A direct quotation needs a closing quotation mark after its final sentence punctuation.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 麻烦 -- “太麻烦了”：棘手，emphasizes that the sudden illness is difficult to deal with.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the illness, add a physical reaction to show how serious it feels: “神农捂着肚子，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: At the river-setting sentence, add one concrete visual detail: “河水清清的，河边开着黄色的花。”
- Expressiveness: Make the brother’s reaction more vivid with an anxious line of speech: “哥哥着急地说：‘快躺下休息！’”

### Encouragement
You used a clear question-and-answer exchange between the two characters. The 一……就…… pattern also effectively shows that the illness happened immediately after eating the plant.
