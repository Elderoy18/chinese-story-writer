## Scene 1

> 古代的时候有一个人叫神农。他需要当上医，一看别的人就想帮助他们。但是，他有一个问题，他不知道什么东西能做药。神农听说一个远远的地方有一种植物，什么病人吃都会觉得身体更好。神农三个月走去，找到有植物的地方，他看到一个很漂亮的植物，又香又好看。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，this time expression is redundant because the time noun already identifies a historical period; adding 的时候 repeats the time meaning.
- 需要当上医：想当医生，the sentence expresses his goal, so 想 is needed; 当上 emphasizes successfully attaining a position, and 医 alone is not the usual noun for this profession.
- 一看别的人：一看到人们，看到 is required when the meaning is “see/encounter,” rather than “look at”; 别的人 means people distinct from a specifically mentioned person or group, while 人们 refers generally to people.
- 一个远远的地方：一个很远的地方，远远 is normally an adverb and does not naturally modify 地方 here; 很远 describes the distance of the place.
- 什么病人吃都会觉得身体更好：不管什么病人，吃了都会觉得身体好多了，use the paired pattern 不管……都…… to mean “regardless of which patient”; 了 marks eating as completed before the resulting improvement.
- 三个月走去：走了三个月，an action duration follows the verb, and 了 marks the completed period of walking; 走去 also needs a stated destination when used as a directional expression.
- 一个很漂亮的植物：一株很漂亮的植物，株 is the appropriate measure word for an individual plant.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 东西 -- “他不知道什么东西能做药”：植物，specifies that he is trying to identify plants that can be used as medicine.
- 帮助 -- “一看到人们就想帮助他们”：医治，specifies helping people by treating their illnesses, which fits his wish to become a doctor.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他有一个问题，他不知道什么东西能做药：他有一个问题：不知道什么东西能做药，apply the zero-form rule for the same referent within one sentence; after the first clause establishes him as the subject, the repeated pronoun is unnecessary.
- Coherence (Reference): 找到有植物的地方：到了有那种植物的地方，use 那种植物 to repeat the previously introduced plant clearly; 有植物 is too general and does not clearly link back to the plant he heard about.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他看到一个很漂亮的植物：他看到了一株很漂亮的植物，use 了 with 看到 because this is a completed discovery in the sequence of events.
- Expressiveness: After the long journey, add a physical feeling to show its difficulty: “走了三个月以后，他又累又饿。”
- Expressiveness: Make the plant more vivid by describing its flowers: “那种植物开着黄色的、像星星一样的花。”
- Expressiveness: Add his thought when he finds the plant: “神农心想：这也许能帮助很多病人。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduce Shennong and establish his wish to help people, which gives the scene a strong purpose.
- The details about the distant place and the fragrant, beautiful plant make the journey feel more interesting and visual.

- Coherence (Temporality): 帮神农，快给他一种红的灵芝：帮神农，赶快给了他一种红的灵芝，In this series of actions, the final verb takes 了 to show that the giving was completed.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他没有病：他没有病了，Use 了 after a negative state when it has changed: he was ill before, but is no longer ill now.
- Expressiveness: When the flower makes Shennong ill, add a physical detail to show how serious it is: “他的肚子疼了起来，疼得直冒冷汗。”

## Scene 3

> 啊，他怎么忘记了？他把灵芝放在包里。一回家就告诉别人。他找到药以后他写下怎么做药在书上。所以人们都知道，那红灵芝对身体好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别人：人们，别人 distinguishes people from a previously mentioned person or group; here the intended meaning is people in general.
- 那红灵芝：那种红灵芝，this demonstrative needs a measure word before the noun; 种 identifies a type of mushroom.
- 对身体好处：对身体有好处，this expression requires the verb 有 before 好处.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他找到药以后他写下：他找到药以后，写下，use zero form for the same subject when it continues within the same sentence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他把灵芝放在包里：他把灵芝放在了包里，use 了 for the completed action of putting the mushroom into the bag.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉：告诉了，use 了 because telling the people is a completed narrative action.
- Coherence (Temporality): 写下怎么做药在书上：在书上写下了怎么做药，put the location before the verb phrase, and use 了 after the resultative verb 写下 to show that the writing was completed.
- Expressiveness: At the moment he puts the mushroom away, add a small action and feeling, such as “他小心地把灵芝放进包里，心里很高兴。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech when he tells people the news, such as “他说：“我找到一种对身体有好处的灵芝了！””
- Expressiveness: Show what he records in the book with one concrete detail, such as “他在书上写下灵芝的颜色和它能治病的作用。”

### Encouragement
- You used the sequence pattern “一……就……” effectively to show that the action happened immediately after he returned home.
- You also connected the ending to the earlier discovery by explaining that people learned about the mushroom’s benefit.
