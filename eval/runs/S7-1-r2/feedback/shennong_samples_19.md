## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男人觉得植物很有意思。他叫神农。有的植物吃了以后他的肚子疼死了，可是有的吃了以后很舒服。他找很多植物。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，Both expressions refer to a historical time, so combining them is redundant; use the shorter time expression.
- 他找很多植物：他找到了很多植物，找 means “to look for,” while 找到 means “to find successfully.” The resultative complement 到 and the completed-action marker 了 show that the plants were found.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一个男人觉得植物很有意思。他叫神农：一个叫神农的男人觉得植物很有意思，Use the character’s name together with a full noun phrase at first mention, rather than introducing him first as an unnamed person and naming him only in the next sentence.
- Expressiveness: After the first sentence, add a thought to show why the plants interest him: “神农想：这些植物到底有什么用呢？”
- Expressiveness: When describing the painful result, add a physical action to make the feeling vivid: “他捂着肚子，难受得不得了。”

### Encouragement
You clearly used the contrast pattern with “可是” to show that different plants have different effects. The repeated “吃了以后” also helps the reader understand the cause of each result.

## Scene 2

> 凉山很大的山，这也找植物。一天，他找到黄色的花。他听说你跟黄色的花做茶很有用。比方说，你的头发疼死，这冰茶是药。他一喝茶就很不舒服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 凉山很大的山：凉山是一座很大的山，A noun phrase used as the predicate needs the linking verb 是 here, and 座 is the appropriate measure word for a mountain.
- 这也找植物：在这里找植物，A location before an action needs 在, and 也 cannot refer to a place; 这里 names the location.
- 跟黄色的花做茶：用黄色的花做茶，跟 normally introduces a companion, while 用 introduces the material used to make something.
- 头发疼死：头疼得要命，头发 does not normally take 疼 to describe physical pain; use 头疼, and use 得 to introduce the degree complement.
- 这冰茶：这杯茶，This demonstrative phrase needs the pattern 这 + measure word + noun.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很有用 -- “用黄色的花做茶很有用”：对身体有好处，specifies that the tea’s usefulness is a health benefit in this medicinal context.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 你跟黄色的花做茶很有用：用黄色的花做茶很有用，The pronoun 你 has no introduced referent in the narrative; use an impersonal construction for a general statement.
- Coherence (Temporality): 找到黄色的花：找到了一朵黄色的花，Use 了 after 找到 to show that the finding was completed; 一朵 also introduces this flower as a new object.
- Expressiveness: When the flower first appears, add a visual detail: “黄色的花像星星一样，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: After the tea makes him uncomfortable, show his physical feeling: “他捂着肚子，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought to show his worry: “他想：‘我会不会生病？’”

### Encouragement
- You used 一天 to move the story forward in time, and the 一……就…… pattern clearly connects drinking the tea with feeling uncomfortable.
- You also tried to give a concrete example after explaining that the tea is useful, which helps develop the scene.

## Scene 3

> 糟糕，这黄色的花是夹竹桃，特别是毒。正好在这儿，灵芝药的夹竹桃他吃完了，也写一个课文的植物。谢谢神农。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 特别是毒：特别有毒，毒 is not used as an adjective after an adverb such as 特别; use 有毒 to describe a poisonous plant.
- 灵芝药的夹竹桃：灵芝是药，夹竹桃有毒，的 makes the words before it modify the following noun, but here 灵芝 and 夹竹桃 need separate subject-predicate clauses.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 也写：也写了，use 了 after 写 to mark the completed action of writing.
