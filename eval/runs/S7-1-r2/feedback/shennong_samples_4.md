## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个男人，他的名字是神农。他看到了有很多人不知道哪个植物好吃，也不知道什么植物对你的身体有好处。这个问题神农觉得很糟糕，所以神农去了吃每种植物。他吃了一种植物以后，就写了这个植物对你有没有好处。有的时候，神农吃了很好吃的植物。有的时候，他吃了很危险的植物。他吃完了每种植物以后，会来跟他的朋友聊到他吃的植物了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 already expresses a time period, so adding 的时候 is redundant.
- 哪个植物：哪种植物，when asking about a type or kind of plant, use the classifier 种 rather than the individual-item classifier 个.
- 写了：写下了，写下 means to record information in writing; it fits recording whether a plant is beneficial.
- 有的时候，神农吃了很好吃的植物：有的时候，神农吃很好吃的植物，use the zero particle for a repeated or habitual action; 了 marks one completed event.
- 有的时候，他吃了很危险的植物：有的时候，他吃很危险的植物，use the zero particle for a repeated or habitual action; 了 is not used here.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 糟糕 -- “这个问题神农觉得很糟糕”：严重，specifies that the problem has serious consequences；令人担忧，shows that the problem makes people feel worried.
- 看到了 -- “他看到了有很多人不知道”：注意到，emphasizes that he observed the situation and became aware of it.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 所以神农去了吃每种植物：所以去吃了每种植物，when the same subject continues in the same sentence, use zero form rather than repeating the name.
- Coherence (Temporality): 去了吃每种植物：去吃了每种植物，place 了 after the verb phrase; with a directional verb plus another verb, use 去 + verb + 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 会来跟他的朋友聊到他吃的植物了：会来跟他的朋友聊他吃过的植物，after the modal 会, use the zero particle rather than 了; 吃过 marks that the plants were tried before the conversation.
- Expressiveness: After the sentence about the problem, add Shennong’s determination or concern: “神农想：我一定要找出什么植物可以吃。”
- Expressiveness: When describing the dangerous plants, add a physical reaction to make the event vivid: “他吃了以后，肚子疼了起来。”
- Expressiveness: Expand the record with one concrete detail: “他写下了植物的颜色、味道和对身体的用处。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduce Shennong at the beginning and keep the scene focused on his wish to help people understand plants.
- You also use time expressions and the sequence pattern with 以后 well to show the order of his actions.

> 神农说，有一天，我吃了一个黄色的花，名字是夹竹桃。我一吃夹竹桃，就觉得这种植物对我的脑有坏处。可是，一个灵芝和一个男人正好在我的旁边，我说，麻烦你把那个植物给我吃一下。我一吃这个灵芝就好了。所以，你得小心吃植物，因为有的植物对你的健康有坏处，特别是夹竹桃。因为夹竹桃有毒。

- 坏处 -- “有的植物对你的健康有坏处”：有害，states more directly that the plants harm health.

### Coherence & Expressiveness

- Coherence (Conjunction): 。因为：，因为，“因为” introduces the cause of the warning and should connect to the preceding clause rather than stand as a separate sentence.
- Expressiveness: After eating the poisonous flower, add a physical feeling to show the danger more vividly: “我的肚子疼了起来，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: At the moment the helper appears, describe him and mark the interruption: “这时，一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人走过来。”

### Encouragement
