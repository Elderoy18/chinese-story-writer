## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有很多人不知道什么植物能吃，什么植物不能吃。有很多人吃植物以后就吃坏肚子了，因为他们吃了毒。那个时候，有一个老人叫神农，他什么植物都觉得好吃，不怕生病，不怕毒。他希望别人也可以吃好吃的植物。可是他知道，如果他们还怕吃毒，不会吃他喜欢的植物。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，this is redundant because 古代 already names a time period, while 的时候 also means “the time when.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 毒 -- “因为他们吃了毒”：有毒的植物，specifies that the harmful substance came from the plants mentioned in the previous sentence.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: At the description of people becoming ill, add a physical detail to show the result: “有的人肚子疼得直不起腰。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where 神农 is introduced, add his determined attitude: “神农心想：我一定要找出能吃的植物。”
- Expressiveness: Before the final conditional sentence, add a short thought to make his wish for others clearer: “他想让大家吃得健康，不再生病。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduce the problem that people cannot tell which plants are safe, and you create a useful contrast between 神农 and the other people.

## Scene 2

> 所以，他上山下山找植物，吃了很多样子的植物。他是第一人，找到了茶。他把所有的植物写下来了。他一直都吃了，他连毒都能吃。可是他还没吃了一个叫夹竹桃的植物。他找到了那个像星星的植物，觉得很漂亮，马上就吃下了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很多样子的植物：很多种植物，样子 refers to an object’s appearance, while 种 is the measure word for different types or kinds of plants.
- 他是第一人，找到了茶：他是第一个找到茶的人，when describing the first person to complete an action, use 第一个 + verb phrase + 的 + 人. Bare 第一人 usually means a leading or number-one figure and does not specify the action.
- 他一直都吃了：他一直都吃，routine or ongoing actions with 一直 use the zero aspect particle, so 了 should not be added.
- 还没吃了一个叫夹竹桃的植物：还没吃过一种叫夹竹桃的植物，negative past experience uses 没 + verb + 过, not 没 + verb + 了; use 种, rather than 个, for a type of plant.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 上山下山 -- “他上山下山找植物”：翻山越岭，vividly shows travelling across many mountains while searching.
- 写下来 -- “他把所有的植物写下来了”：记下来，emphasizes recording his discoveries so that the information is kept.
- 吃下 -- “马上就吃下了”：吞下，more specifically describes swallowing the plant immediately.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: When he finds the tea, add a sensory detail such as “闻起来清香的茶叶” to make the discovery easier to imagine.
- Expressiveness: Before he eats the flower-like plant, show his curiosity with a detail such as “好奇地看着那些花”.
- Expressiveness: Right after he eats it, foreshadow danger through a physical reaction such as “肚子突然疼起来”.

### Encouragement
- You used a clear action sequence: searching in the mountains, finding tea, recording plants, and then trying a new plant.
- The comparison “像星星的植物” gives the scene a visual image and makes the final plant stand out.

## Scene 3

> 可是夹竹桃的毒很重，让他肚子很痛。他在地上躺着，觉得他快会死了。可是正好有别人找到他了，给了他一个像伞的植物，叫灵芝。他吃灵芝以后就觉得很好，不再生病了。他测量起来了，像星星的植物是毒，像伞的植物是药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 快会：快要，For an imminent event, use 快要 before the verb; 快 does not naturally modify the modal 会 in this meaning.
- 别人：一个男人，别人 distinguishes other people from a person already under discussion, but no such contrast exists here; a newly appearing character needs a full noun phrase.
- 像伞的：像伞一样的，Use 像……一样的 to describe something that resembles another object.
- 测量起来了：明白了，测量 means determining physical dimensions such as size or length, but this context is about understanding which plants are harmful or medicinal; 明白了 expresses a completed realization.
- 像星星的：像星星一样的，Use 像……一样的 to make the comparison grammatical.
- 是毒：有毒，毒 is a noun, so a plant’s harmful property should be expressed with 有毒.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很痛 -- “让他肚子很痛”：疼得厉害，shows more vividly how severe the stomach pain is.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 觉得他：觉得自己，Use 自己 when the person who has the thought and the person who may die are the same character; this makes the reference clear.
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃灵芝以后：吃了灵芝以后，Use 了 for the completed action that happened before the later feeling of recovery.
- Expressiveness: After the stomach pain, add a stronger physical reaction or feeling: “他难受得不得了，脸都白了。”
- Expressiveness: At the rescue, describe the new man’s action more vividly: “一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人赶快拿起灵芝，给神农吃。”
- Expressiveness: Turn the final classification into a short line of speech: “神农说：‘像星星一样的花有毒，像伞一样的植物是药。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the cause-and-effect sequence from eating the plant to stomach pain and then receiving help.  
- You also use the contrast between the star-shaped and umbrella-shaped plants to organize the ending clearly.
