## Scene 1

> 古代的时候有一个人叫神农。他需要当上医，一看别的人就想帮助他们。但是，他有一个问题，他不知道什么东西能做药。神农听说一个远远的地方有一种植物，什么病人吃都会觉得身体更好。神农三个月走去，找到有植物的地方，他看到一个很漂亮的植物，又香又好看。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 当上医：当上医生，In this context, 医 is not normally used alone as the name of the profession; 医生 is the ordinary noun for a doctor.
- 看别的人：看到别人，看到 means “see,” while 看 means “look”; use 别人 for people in general when there is no specific contrasting person.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 什么东西能做药：什么植物能做药，东西 is too vague here; repeat the relevant noun 植物 to make clear what Shennong is trying to identify.
- Coherence (Reference): 有植物的地方：有这种植物的地方，Use 这种植物 to refer back clearly to the plant introduced in the previous sentence, rather than to plants in general.
- Coherence (Temporality): 找到：找到了，Add 了 because finding the place is a completed action in this past narrative.
- Expressiveness: When Shennong sees the plant, add a visual detail to make the setting clearer, for example: 那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花。
- Expressiveness: Add Shennong’s hopeful thought after he finds the plant, for example: 他高兴地想：“这种植物可能可以做药。”
- Expressiveness: Describe his reaction to the scent and appearance, for example: 他觉得这种植物又香又美，一定很特别。

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduce Shennong and explain his wish to help other people, which gives the scene a strong purpose.
- The description 又香又好看 is a good sensory detail and helps the reader imagine the plant.

## Scene 2

> 他觉得，这种一定是做药的花。原来他想错了。吃了以后，神农快生病。 这时一个穿着蓝色衣服的人帮神农，快给他一种红的灵芝。神农吃了以后，他没有病，想起来以前一个人告诉他，灵芝对身体有好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 快：很快，When describing that an action happens quickly, use the adverbial phrase “很快”; bare “快” here normally suggests that something is about to happen and needs a form such as “快要……了”.
- 快给他：赶快给他，“赶快” naturally modifies an action that the helper performs immediately in this past event.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农吃了以后，他没有病：神农吃了以后，没有病，Use zero form when the same character has already been named in the same sentence and no other possible referent intervenes.
- Coherence (Temporality): 这时一个穿着蓝色衣服的人帮神农，赶快给他一种红的灵芝：这时一个穿着蓝色衣服的人帮神农，赶快给了他一种红的灵芝，Use “了” after “给” because giving the medicine is a completed action in the action sequence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 想起来以前一个人告诉他：想起了以前有一个人告诉过他，Use “了” after the resultative complement “起来” to show the completed change of state of remembering; use “过” for the earlier experience of someone having told him this information.
- Expressiveness: When the illness begins, add a concrete physical reaction: “他的肚子疼了起来，疼得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Describe the helper’s urgent movement to make the rescue vivid: “他赶快拿出灵芝，递给神农。”
- Expressiveness: Add Shennong’s relieved feeling after the medicine works: “神农觉得身体好多了，心里很感谢他。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the order of events with “吃了以后” and “这时,” which helps the scene move forward. You also introduce the new helper with a useful visual detail, “穿着蓝色衣服的人.”

## Scene 3

> 啊，他怎么忘记了？他把灵芝放在包里。一回家就告诉别人。他找到药以后他写下怎么做药在书上。所以人们都知道，那红灵芝对身体好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别人：人们，`别人` means people other than a specified person or group; use `人们` for people in general.
- 写下怎么做药在书上：在书上写下怎么做药，The location phrase with `在` should come before the verb `写下`.
- 知道，那红灵芝：知道那个红灵芝，The object of `知道` should follow directly without this comma, and `那` needs a measure word before a noun.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 放 -- “他把灵芝放在包里”：收进，emphasizes putting the herb away safely inside the bag.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他找到药以后他写下怎么做药在书上：他找到药以后，写下怎么做药在书上，For a re-occurring subject in the same sentence, use zero form rather than repeating `他`.
- Coherence (Temporality): 放在：放在了，Use `了` after a completed action in this narrative.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉：告诉了，Use `了` because telling the people is a completed event, not quoted speech being introduced.
- Coherence (Temporality): 写下：写下了，Use `了` for the final completed action in this sequence.
- Expressiveness: After `啊，他怎么忘记了？`, add a small action that shows his sudden realization, such as `他拍了拍自己的头`.
- Expressiveness: At the point where he writes in the book, add the exact information he records, such as `灵芝可以做药，对身体有好处`.
- Expressiveness: Show his satisfaction after sharing his discovery with people, such as `他高兴地笑了`.

### Encouragement
You used `一……就……` effectively to show that he acted immediately after returning home. The final `所以` also gives the scene a clear result: people learn about the herb’s benefit.
