## Scene 1

> 很久以前，古代的人很不知道什么植物能吃，也什么植物不能吃。所以很多人常常吃不安全的植物，吃不安全的植物对你的身体有坏处，很麻烦的情况。但是一个人，神农，要解决这个问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很不知道什么植物能吃，也什么植物不能吃：不知道什么植物能吃，什么植物不能吃，The degree adverb 很 cannot modify the negative mental-state verb 不知道, and 也 must be followed by a verb phrase rather than directly introducing the noun clause 什么植物不能吃.
- 吃不安全的植物对你的身体有坏处，很麻烦的情况：吃不安全的植物对身体有坏处，是一件很麻烦的事，The original ends with a noun phrase and lacks the predicate needed to complete the sentence; use 是一件……的事 to describe this whole situation.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 有坏处 -- “吃不安全的植物对你的身体有坏处”：有害，more concise and precise for something that damages health.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 但是一个人，神农，要解决这个问题：但是，有一个叫神农的人要解决这个问题，This is Shennong’s first mention, so introduce him with a full noun phrase that gives both his name and who he is.
- Expressiveness: After the harmful plants are mentioned, add a concrete physical result, such as “很多人吃了以后肚子疼，还会生病。”
- Expressiveness: At the introduction of Shennong, add his determined thought or speech, such as “神农想：‘我一定要找出能吃的植物。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish the main problem first—people do not know which plants are edible—and then introduce Shennong as someone who wants to solve it. The cause-and-effect link with 所以 also helps the scene move forward.

## Scene 2

> 他又聪明又努力，旅行很远找到很多植物。比方说，灵芝和夹竹桃。他一吃各种各样的植物就知道能不能吃这些植物。然后，神农写他的主意。他喜欢植物的味道，特别是花。神农遇到了一个黄色的花，一吃就觉得不舒服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 旅行很远：到很远的地方旅行，use a location phrase after 到 to show where the travel goes; 旅行 cannot directly take 很远 as its object or complement here.
- 写他的主意：把自己的想法写下来，主意 usually means a plan or solution, while 想法 means the thoughts he wants to record; 写下 is needed for recording thoughts.
- 遇到了一个黄色的花：看到了一朵黄色的花，看到 is the appropriate verb for visually noticing a flower, and 朵 is the matching measure word for flowers.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 努力 -- “他又聪明又努力”：勤奋，emphasizes that he works hard consistently rather than making only a single effort.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农写他的主意：他把自己的想法写下来，after the recent references to the same character and with no competing character, use the pronoun rather than repeating the name.
- Coherence (Temporality): 找到很多植物：找到了很多植物，this is a completed series of actions, so only the final verb in the series takes 了.
- Expressiveness: At the flower, add a visual detail to make it memorable, such as “那朵花像星星一样，又大又漂亮”.
- Expressiveness: After he feels ill, show a physical reaction and feeling, such as “他肚子疼了起来，难受得不得了”.
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought at the moment he becomes sick, such as “他想：我会死吗？”.

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect Shennong’s travel, plant testing, note-taking, and illness in a logical order. You also use the useful pattern “一……就……” correctly to show that one action happens immediately after another.

## Scene 3

> 发现了夹竹桃有毒。别人看到他，很快给他吃一个灵芝。神农一吃就觉得比较好。然后，他继续写他发现的信息。会告诉他的朋友哪一个植物能不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别人：一个男人，别人 distinguishes other people from a person already being contrasted; it cannot introduce one new rescuer in this scene.
- 写他发现的信息：写下他发现的信息，写 information down requires the result complement 下 to show that the information is recorded.
- 哪一个植物能不能吃：哪一种植物能吃，哪一种植物不能吃，哪一个 asks the reader to choose one item, while 能不能 asks a yes-or-no question. Use the paired structure to explain which types are edible and which are not.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 比较好 -- “神农一吃就觉得比较好”：好多了，makes the improvement after taking the medicine more concrete and natural.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 很快给他吃一个灵芝：很快给他吃了一个灵芝，了 marks the completed action of giving him the medicine; in this series, the final action takes 了.
- Expressiveness: After the medicine works, add a physical detail about recovery, such as “神农的肚子慢慢不疼了，他松了一口气。”
- Expressiveness: Describe the new man briefly when he appears, such as “一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人看到他。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of thanks after the recovery, such as “神农说：‘谢谢你救了我！’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the important sequence of finding the poison, receiving medicine, feeling better, and recording the discovery. You also use the pattern “一……就……” correctly to show an immediate result.
