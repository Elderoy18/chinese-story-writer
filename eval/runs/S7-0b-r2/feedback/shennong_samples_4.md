## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个男人，他的名字是神农。他看到了有很多人不知道哪个植物好吃，也不知道什么植物对你的身体有好处。这个问题神农觉得很糟糕，所以神农去了吃每种植物。他吃了一种植物以后，就写了这个植物对你有没有好处。有的时候，神农吃了很好吃的植物。有的时候，他吃了很危险的植物。他吃完了每种植物以后，会来跟他的朋友聊到他吃的植物了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 already names a time period, so adding 的时候 is redundant.
- 对你的身体有好处：对身体有好处，this is a general statement about people’s health, so 你 should not shift the narration directly to the reader.
- 写了这个植物对你有没有好处：写下了这个植物对身体有没有好处，写下 means to record information; the completed recording action needs 了, and 对身体 avoids the inappropriate shift to 你.
- 聊到他吃的植物：聊一聊他吃过的植物，聊到 usually means “happen to mention”; 聊一聊 expresses discussing a topic, and 吃过的 marks that the plants were previously eaten.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 糟糕 -- “这个问题神农觉得很糟糕”：严重，specifies that the problem has serious consequences for people’s health.
- 危险 -- “他吃了很危险的植物”：有毒的，identifies the particular danger in this story: plants that can poison someone.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 这个问题神农觉得很糟糕：这个问题他觉得很糟糕，use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the previously mentioned character is still the only possible referent.
- Coherence (Reference): 所以神农去了吃每种植物：所以他去吃了每种植物，use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence; 神农 was named in the immediately preceding sentence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 去了吃每种植物：去吃了每种植物，when 去 is followed by an action, 了 follows the action verb to mark the completed series: 去吃了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 会来跟他的朋友聊到他吃的植物了：会来跟他的朋友聊一聊他吃过的植物，modal 会 takes the zero particle; the final 了 is not needed for a habitual or expected action.
- Expressiveness: After the dangerous plant is eaten, add a physical reaction to show the risk: “他马上觉得肚子疼了起来。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where he writes down his discovery, add a brief thought: “他想：这样别人就不会吃错植物了。”
- Expressiveness: When he speaks with his friends, add a short line of dialogue: “这种植物不能吃，吃了会生病。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduce 神农 before using pronouns, and you organize his actions in a logical order with time phrases such as 以后 and 有的时候.
- Your contrast between beneficial plants and dangerous plants gives the scene a clear purpose.

## Scene 3

> 神农说，有一天，我吃了一个黄色的花，名字是夹竹桃。我一吃夹竹桃，就觉得这种植物对我的脑有坏处。可是，一个灵芝和一个男人正好在我的旁边，我说，麻烦你把那个植物给我吃一下。我一吃这个灵芝就好了。所以，你得小心吃植物，因为有的植物对你的健康有坏处，特别是夹竹桃。因为夹竹桃有毒。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一个黄色的花：一朵黄色的花，Flowers take the classifier 朵 rather than the generic classifier 个.
- 对我的脑有坏处：对我的大脑有坏处，脑 is usually a bound form in this meaning; use the noun 大脑 or 脑子 when referring to the body part.
- 一个灵芝：一株灵芝，The classifier 株 is used for a growing plant or fungus such as 灵芝.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 有坏处 -- “有的植物对你的健康有坏处”：有害，states more directly that the plants can cause harm to health.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为夹竹桃有毒。：特别是夹竹桃，因为它有毒。，A 因为 clause gives a cause and needs to connect to a main clause; here it explains why the warning about 夹竹桃 is especially important.
- Expressiveness: When the effect begins, add a physical feeling to make the danger vivid: “我肚子疼了起来，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Describe the plant before the character eats it: “那儿开着黄色的像星星一样的花，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: After getting better, add a short line of speech that shows gratitude and curiosity: “谢谢你！这是什么植物？”

### Encouragement
- You used the pattern “一……就……” well to show an immediate result, and the direct speech makes the scene easier to follow. Your final warning also clearly communicates that some plants can be dangerous.
