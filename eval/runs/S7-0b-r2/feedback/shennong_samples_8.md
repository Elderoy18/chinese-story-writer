## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个人叫神农。一天，神农正在路上走，一看很多人正在吃一些植物。一些人一吃植物就生病了，神农想帮别人。所以想知道哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，the time noun is already sufficient; adding the extra time phrase is redundant.
- 看：看到，use the resultative form when the meaning is “see” rather than “look at.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 想知道 -- “所以想知道哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃”：弄清，emphasizes finding a clear answer after investigating the plants.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一天，神农：一天，他，after a character has just been introduced and no other character competes as the referent, use a pronoun for the close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 一看到很多人正在吃一些植物。：一看到很多人正在吃一些植物，就……，the pattern for an immediate sequence is “一…就…”, so the second event needs the connector.
- Expressiveness: After seeing the sick people, add Shen Nong’s feeling, such as “神农觉得很担心。”
- Expressiveness: At the moment he sees people eating plants, add a short thought or question, such as “什么植物能吃，什么植物有毒呢？”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the cause-and-effect problem: people eat plants and then become ill. You also use “正在” well to show the ongoing actions in the scene.

## Scene 2

> 然后，神农开始尝很多植物，比方说他吃过生姜、芥兰和菠菜。一些植物吃真好，特别是黄瓜。但是，一些植物毒得很，特别是夹竹桃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一些植物吃真好：一些植物很好吃，The adjective phrase should describe the plants directly; “很好吃” means that a food tastes good, while “吃真好” does not form a natural predicate here.
- 毒得很：有毒，“毒” is a noun in this use and cannot take the degree complement “得很”; use “有毒” to describe poisonous plants.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 吃过 -- “他吃过生姜、芥兰和菠菜”：品尝过，emphasizes that Shennong tasted the plants deliberately as part of testing them.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After “神农开始尝很多植物,” add his purpose or thought to show his motivation, for example: “他想：我一定要找到能吃的植物。”
- Expressiveness: When introducing the poisonous plant, add a visual detail to make the contrast memorable, for example: “夹竹桃开着黄色的、像星星一样的花。”
- Expressiveness: Add Shennong’s reaction after learning that the plant is dangerous, for example: “神农觉得很危险，以后要更小心地尝植物。”

### Encouragement
- You use “然后” effectively to continue from the earlier scene, and the contrast between plants that taste good and plants that are dangerous gives this scene a clear direction.

## Scene 3

> 一个人对神农说，麻烦我们可以吃什么植物？神农说，这就要看你想不想生病了。以后，神农告诉别人一些植物能吃，一些植物不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 麻烦我们可以吃什么植物？：请问，我们可以吃什么植物？，麻烦 is a noun/adjective or a verb meaning “to trouble someone”; it cannot introduce a question. Use 请问 to politely begin a question.
- 这就要看你想不想生病了：这就要看是什么植物了，this pattern must answer the uncertain question by using a question form after 看. Whether someone wants to be ill does not determine which plants are edible.
- 以后：后来，when it begins a new sentence to mean “afterwards,” use 后来. 以后 normally means “after a particular time/event” and needs that time or event before it.
- 别人：人们，别人 distinguishes other people from a stated person or group, while 人们 refers to people in general. Here 神农 is sharing knowledge with the general public.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 一些 -- “一些植物能吃”：有些，more clearly marks one group of plants as only a subset, especially when contrasted with another group that cannot be eaten.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉人们：告诉了人们，use 了 after 告诉 because this is a completed narrative action: 神农 finished telling people this information.
- Expressiveness: After the question, add the speaker’s worried feeling to show why the answer matters: 他着急地问：“我们可以吃什么植物？”
- Expressiveness: At 神农’s reply, add a small action or example to make his explanation clearer: 神农指着两种植物说：“这种能吃，那种不能吃。”

### Encouragement
- You used a clear question-and-answer exchange, which makes the scene easy to follow. You also clearly contrasted plants that can be eaten with plants that cannot.
