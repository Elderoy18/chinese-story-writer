## Scene 1

> 这个男孩子叫孟子，孟子是古代的人。孟子的妈妈觉得住在哪儿对童年很重要，所以他们常常搬了家。她望子成龙，所以孟子的妈妈安排他的生活。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 哪儿：那儿，In this declarative clause, the place is already being discussed, so use the demonstrative place word rather than the question word.
- 对童年很重要：对孩子的成长很重要，This context needs the person or development that the living environment affects; the original word refers to a period of life rather than the child’s development.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 安排 -- “她安排他的生活”：规划，emphasizes that the mother is deliberately making a plan for her son’s life and future.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 这个男孩子叫孟子：有一个男孩子叫孟子，On first mention, introduce a new character with an indefinite noun phrase rather than using a demonstrative form that suggests the reader already knows him.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子是古代的人：是古代的人，Use zero form for the same character in the same sentence instead of repeating the name.
- Coherence (Reference): 所以孟子的妈妈安排他的生活：所以她安排他的生活，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when no other female character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Temporality): 常常搬了家：常常搬家，Use the zero particle for a habitual action; the adverb shows that this happened repeatedly, rather than marking one completed move.
- Expressiveness: After the mother’s thought about the environment, add an inner thought to show her concern: 孟子的妈妈心想：“住的地方会影响孩子的成长。”
- Expressiveness: Make the mother’s planning more concrete with one daily-life detail: 她认真地安排孟子每天学习和休息的时间。

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduce the main character and explain the mother’s motivation for moving. The cause-and-effect connection with “所以” also helps the reader understand why she makes plans for her son.

## Scene 2

> 因为他们常常搬了家，中国人说，孟母三迁。孟子没有爸爸，所以，他的妈妈更重要。从这儿到那儿，妈妈教孟子很多技能。先她让孟子跟朋友玩儿，然后教他怎么买又便宜又好质量的东西。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 先她：她先，The time-order adverb should follow the subject when it modifies her action.
- 好质量：质量好，In Chinese, the adjective follows the noun in this predicate phrase; use the pattern “质量 + adjective.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 技能 -- “妈妈教孟子很多技能”：生活技能，specifies that these are practical skills for everyday life；生活本领，adds a sense of useful abilities learned while growing up

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 妈妈教孟子很多技能：她教孟子很多技能，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence: the mother was named in the immediately preceding sentence, and no other female character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Temporality): 常常搬了家：常常搬家，Use the zero particle for a habitual action; “常常” describes a routine, not one completed move.
- Coherence (Temporality): 教孟子很多技能：教了孟子很多技能，Use 了 for the completed teaching action in this past narrative.
- Coherence (Temporality): 然后教他：然后教了他，In a series of completed actions, only the last verb takes 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为他们常常搬家，中国人说：因为他们常常搬家，所以中国人说，Use the paired connector “因为……所以……” to mark the cause-and-effect relation.
- Expressiveness: After the final sentence, add a short line of the mother’s teaching, such as “买东西的时候，要看价格，也要看质量。” This makes her teaching more concrete.
- Expressiveness: Describe Mengzi’s response after learning a skill, such as “孟子认真地点点头，觉得妈妈教得很有用。” This adds his feelings and reaction.

### Encouragement
- You used clear time-order words to show the teaching sequence, and the example about buying affordable, good-quality things is a concrete and believable detail.
