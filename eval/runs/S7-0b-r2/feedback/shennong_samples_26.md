## Scene 1

> 很久以前，有一个老男人叫神农。神农在古代每一天看到了别的小镇人生病还是不舒服，所以他决定开始找新的药。他下一天开始爬当地的山脉，找了各种各样的植物。神农试一试每个植物，知道有什么好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别的小镇人：小镇上的人，别的 is used to distinguish others from a previously mentioned person or group; here the intended meaning is the people living in the town. A location phrase with 的 is needed before 人.
- 生病还是不舒服：生病或觉得不舒服，还是 normally introduces alternatives in a question; use 或 or 或者 for alternatives in this statement.
- 下一天：第二天，第二天 is the usual time expression for the following day in a narrative.
- 爬当地的山脉：爬当地的山，山脉 refers to an extended mountain range, while 爬 is used for climbing an individual mountain.
- 找了各种各样的植物：找到了各种各样的植物，找 means “look for,” while 找到 means “find”; the completed finding action needs 找到.
- 每个植物：每种植物，种 is the appropriate measure word for types of plants.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农在古代每一天看到了：他在古代每一天看到了，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence: the character’s name was introduced in the immediately preceding sentence, and no other character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农试一试每种植物：他试一试每种植物，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence because the same character remains the clear subject.
- Coherence (Temporality): 每一天看到了：每一天看到，Use the zero particle for a routine or habitual action. The completed-action marker 了 does not fit with 每一天 here.
- Expressiveness: After the description of the townspeople’s illness, add a concrete symptom to show why the problem concerns him, for example: “很多人吃了有毒的植物，肚子疼得厉害。”
- Expressiveness: Show the character’s motivation with an inner thought before he begins searching, for example: “神农很担心，心想：‘我一定要找到能治病的植物。’”
- Expressiveness: Add a small detail about the mountains to make the journey more vivid, for example: “山路又高又陡，但是他还是一直往前走。”

### Encouragement
- You introduce the main character clearly at the beginning and give him a strong reason to act. Your scene also has a clear sequence from seeing a problem to beginning the search.

## Scene 2

> 比方说，他一次试一试有些植物的叶，吃得又新鲜又甜。煮这些叶以后，他一喝就知道他发现新的茶叶。虽然他找到了很多的新的植物，但是他的旅程有的时候特别是危险。比方说，他一吃灵芝就开始生病。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一次试一试有些植物的叶：有一次，他尝了一些植物的叶子，Verb reduplication is for a light attempt or suggestion, not for an action that was actually carried out; use a completed verb here.

- 吃得又新鲜又甜：吃起来又新鲜又甜，Use a result or impression expression with the leaves as the thing being described; this says that the leaves taste fresh and sweet.

- 特别是危险：特别危险，The adverb modifies the adjective directly; the copula is not used between them.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 新的 -- “他找到了很多的新的植物”：陌生的，makes clear that the plants were unfamiliar to him rather than newly grown；未尝过的，specifies that he had not tasted these plants before.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 煮这些叶以后：煮了这些叶以后，Use 了 for the completed action that happens before the later action of drinking.

- Coherence (Temporality): 就开始生病：就开始生病了，Use 了 to mark the completed change of state: he became ill after eating it.

- Coherence (Conjunction): 虽然他找到了很多的新的植物，但是他的旅程有的时候特别是危险：虽然他找到了很多的新的植物，但是他的旅程有的时候特别危险，The paired connector needs 但是 to mark the contrast between success in finding plants and danger during the journey.

- Expressiveness: After the leaves are boiled, add a sensory detail about their smell or appearance, such as “锅里慢慢飘出一股淡淡的香味。”

- Expressiveness: At the moment of illness, show his physical feeling and emotion, such as “他捂着肚子，难受极了，心里也很害怕。”

### Encouragement
- You use concrete taste words to describe the leaves, which helps the reader imagine the tea. You also use the 一……就…… pattern to show a quick result clearly.

## Scene 3

> 一个爬山的男人正好看到神农的麻烦，然后给了他一些药。神农最终他的小镇回到，跟别的人分享他的发现。你认为神农的故事真的吗？这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 最终他的小镇回到：最终回到了他的小镇，With a directional verb, the verb plus directional complement comes before the destination: “回到 + place”. Add 了 because the return is completed.
- 跟别的人分享他的发现：跟小镇的人们分享他的发现，别的人 means “other people” in contrast with a stated person or group. Here, name the group receiving the discoveries instead.
- 你认为神农的故事真的吗？：你认为神农的故事是真的吗？，Use 是 before 真的 when asking whether a story is true.
- 这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处。：这就要看你是否相信中药对身体有好处了。，This fixed “这就要看……” structure normally ends with 了; this 了 is part of the sentence pattern, not a completion marker.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 药 -- “给了他一些药”：草药，specifies that the medicine comes from plants and suits this traditional-medicine story.
- 发现 -- “分享他的发现”：研究发现，makes clear that these are results from his exploration and testing.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他的小镇：自己的小镇，When the possessor is the same person as the subject, use 自己的 to make the reference clearly point back to the subject rather than the mountain-climbing man.
- Expressiveness: After the man gives the medicine, add Shennong’s physical change, such as “神农吃了药以后，觉得肚子好多了。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech when Shennong shares his discovery, such as “他说：‘这种植物可以做药。’”
- Expressiveness: Describe the helper briefly to make him easier to imagine, such as “那个男人穿着蓝色的衣服，赶快拿出一些草药。”

### Encouragement
- You introduced the new helper clearly with “一个爬山的男人,” rather than using an unclear pronoun. You also ended the scene with a question that invites the reader to think about the story.
