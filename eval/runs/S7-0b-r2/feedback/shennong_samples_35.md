## Scene 1

> 在古代，很多医生不知道怎么用药。所以他们找到了很多植物对药。神农先看到很多病人，他们吃很多植物对药。但是我觉得他们的吃的植物，让他们病人。神农觉得这个植物可能特别是毒。所以他想找一个植物对药，他们可以吃对药。正好，他找到了一把花，他想把花放在茶里，对药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 对药：可以做药的，“对药” cannot describe a plant’s function here; “可以做药的植物” means plants that can be used as medicine.
- 他们的吃的植物：他们吃的植物，A relative clause before a noun uses the verb phrase directly: “他们吃的” modifies “植物”; “的” cannot be placed between the subject and verb.
- 让他们病人：让他们生病，After “让 + person,” Chinese needs a verb phrase showing the result; “病人” is a noun meaning “patient,” while “生病” means “become ill.”
- 特别是毒：有毒，“毒” is a noun and cannot be modified by “特别” as an adjective; “有毒” is the correct predicate for “be poisonous.”
- 找一个植物对药：找一个可以做药的植物，“可以做药的” must be placed before “植物” as its modifying phrase.
- 吃对药：吃，“对药” cannot follow “吃”; deleting it leaves the correct verb phrase “可以吃.”
- 对药：做药，“做药” means “use [something] as medicine”; “对药” does not express this meaning.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：一个叫神农的男人，This is the character’s first mention, so use a full noun phrase to introduce him clearly before later using pronouns.
- Coherence (Reference): 他们可以吃：人们可以吃，The pronoun is ambiguous because both doctors and patients have appeared; repeat a clear noun for a farther reference.
- Coherence (Temporality): 神农先看到很多病人：神农先看到了很多病人，This is a completed event in the narrative, so the verb needs the completion marker “了.”
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们吃很多植物：他们吃了很多植物，This refers to completed eating that led to illness, so use “了” after the verb.
- Expressiveness: After “一把花,” add a visual detail to make the discovery more vivid, such as “黄色的、像星星一样的花”.
- Expressiveness: At “神农觉得这个植物可能有毒,” add his worried thought, such as “这些植物会不会有毒？”.
- Expressiveness: When he sees the patients, add a detail about their condition, such as “他们捂着肚子，脸色很不好”.

### Encouragement
- You establish the historical setting clearly with “在古代,” and you use “所以” to show how Shennong’s observation leads to his next action. You also try to show his thinking and purpose, which helps make the scene feel like a story rather than only a list of events.

## Scene 2

> 他不知道如果这把花对你的身体好，这就要看如果这把花毒不毒了。神农想找别的植物，但是这很麻烦。正好，他找到了别的花。他一吃花，很快就生病了。他的肚子疼死了。神农不知道他吃不吃灵芝或者夹竹桃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不知道如果这把花对你的身体好：不知道这把花对你的身体好不好，An embedded yes/no question after 不知道 uses an A-not-A form such as 好不好, not 如果.
- 要看如果这把花毒不毒：要看这把花有没有毒，After 这就要看……了, the following part must be a question form; 有没有毒 is the appropriate A-not-A question.
- 吃不吃灵芝或者夹竹桃：吃的是灵芝还是夹竹桃，When choosing between two possibilities, use 是……还是……; 吃不吃 does not correctly connect with 或者 in this meaning.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 找 -- “神农想找别的植物”：探寻，emphasizes searching and exploring plants whose nature is still unknown.
- 麻烦 -- “但是这很麻烦”：艰辛，emphasizes that the search is difficult and requires effort, rather than merely involving many small problems.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 你的身体：自己的身体，Use 自己的 when the possessor is the same person as the main subject; here the body belongs to 神农, not to the reader.
- Coherence (Reference): 他一吃花：他一吃那些花，Use a specific re-occurrence form for the flowers just mentioned. 花 by itself sounds like flowers in general, while 那些花 clearly refers back to the flowers he found.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农不知道他：神农不知道自己，Use 自己 to make clear that the person who ate the plants is 神农, the subject of the sentence.
- Expressiveness: When the flowers are found, add a visual detail to make the setting easier to imagine, for example: “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花。”
- Expressiveness: After the stomach pain, show the physical suffering with an action, for example: “他在地上躺着，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Add 神农’s worried thought after he becomes ill, for example: “他想：我会死吗？”

### Encouragement
You use completed-action forms well in “找到了” and “生病了,” and “肚子疼死了” is a vivid, natural way to show strong pain. The scene also has a clear cause-and-result sequence: eating the flowers leads quickly to illness.

## Scene 3

> 别的人给了神农一个灵芝。这个植物可以让神农更好。比方说，他会觉得更健康。吃灵芝以后，他可以再走。现在他觉得更好。最后，神农找到了很多植物，他可以用对药。这些植物让神农很高兴。神农想跟很多人分享他的新药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别的人：一个男人，别的人 means people other than a person already identified in the conversation; this new giver should be introduced as a new individual.
- 用对药：用来做药，use 用来 + verb phrase to express purpose; 对药 is not a verb phrase and cannot follow 用 in this meaning.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 走 -- “他可以再走”：继续赶路，makes clear that he is able to resume his journey after recovering.
- 高兴 -- “这些植物让神农很高兴”：欣慰，expresses the satisfied, relieved feeling of finding useful plants.
- 新药 -- “分享他的新药”：新发现的药，clarifies that these are medicines he has newly discovered.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农想跟很多人分享他的新药：他想跟很多人分享他的新药，apply the pronoun rule: after 神农 was named in the immediately previous sentence and no other person competes as the referent, use 他 instead of repeating the name.
- Expressiveness: When the man gives the medicine, add a brief action and a line of speech: “一个男人赶快拿出一株灵芝，说：‘快吃这个！’”
- Expressiveness: After taking the medicine, describe Shennong’s physical change more vividly: “过了一会儿，神农的肚子不那么疼了，也有力气站起来了。”
- Expressiveness: When he finds useful plants, add his inner feeling: “神农看着这些植物，心里又高兴又欣慰。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the sequence from receiving the medicine, feeling better, walking again, and continuing to find plants. Your use of “吃灵芝以后” also gives the events a clear time order.
