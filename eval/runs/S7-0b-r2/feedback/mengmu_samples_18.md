## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个男孩名字叫孟子。孟子和孟子的妈妈两个人住在很穷的农村。他们住的地方旁边是个墓地，墓地老是有很多人祭拜。因为人们在祭拜，他们一直在哭。孟子和孟子的妈妈住在这里才一个星期就觉得受不了了，而且害得孟子的学习。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，the time noun already expresses the setting, so adding 的时候 is redundant.
- 有一个男孩名字叫孟子：有一个叫孟子的男孩，use the attributive pattern with 的 to place the name before the noun it describes.
- 害得孟子的学习：还影响了孟子的学习，害得 must be followed by a person and a harmful result clause; 影响 can directly take 学习 as its object.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很多人 -- “墓地老是有很多人祭拜”：前来祭拜的人，identifies these people more precisely by their purpose and movement toward the cemetery.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子的妈妈两个人：孟子和他的妈妈两个人，use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence; repeating the name in the same noun phrase is unnecessary.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子的妈妈住在这里：孟子和他的妈妈住在这里，use a pronoun for the mother because she was named recently and no other mother competes as the referent.
- Expressiveness: At the cemetery description, add a concrete sound or action to make the setting vivid, for example: “有人低着头，小声地哭。”
- Expressiveness: After the crying, show Mengzi’s reaction or curiosity, for example: “孟子觉得很奇怪，想知道他们为什么哭。”
- Expressiveness: Before the final sentence, add the mother’s feeling to show why the place troubles the family, for example: “孟子的妈妈听着哭声，心里很不舒服。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish the setting, the family, and the nearby cemetery. You also use ongoing-action forms well in “在祭拜” and “一直在哭” to show what is happening in the scene.

## Scene 2

> 孟子的妈妈觉得孟子取得良好的教育是很重要的，所以孟子的妈妈认为这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好，就打算跟孟子搬家。孟子的妈妈带孟子不是搬到城市，而是搬到了孟子老师的家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 取得良好的教育：接受良好的教育，取得 usually collocates with an achieved result, while education is something a child receives or accepts.
- 跟孟子搬家：带着孟子搬家，跟 means “together with,” but the mother is moving her child, so the verb phrase needs to show that she takes him with her.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 地方 -- “这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好”：环境，specifies that the concern is the overall surroundings and their effect on education.
- 不太好 -- “对儿子的教育不太好”：不太合适，more precisely expresses that this setting does not suit the child’s education.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 所以孟子的妈妈认为：所以认为，after the mother has already been named as the subject in the same sentence, use zero form rather than repeating the noun phrase.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 带孟子不是搬到城市，而是搬到了孟子老师的家：带孟子搬到了孟子老师的家，the paired connector is used to correct or refute an established expectation; here no earlier information creates an expectation that they would move to the city.
- Expressiveness: At the mother’s decision, add her worried inner thought to show why education matters: 孟母很着急，心想：“住在这儿会影响孟子的学习。”
- Expressiveness: After the move, add one concrete detail about the new setting, such as 孟子老师的家很安静，书架上放着很多书。

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the mother’s reason for moving by linking her concern about education to her decision. You also use a complete moving action with 搬到了 effectively in the second sentence.
