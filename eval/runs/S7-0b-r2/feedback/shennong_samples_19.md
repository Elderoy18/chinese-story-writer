## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男人觉得植物很有意思。他叫神农。有的植物吃了以后他的肚子疼死了，可是有的吃了以后很舒服。他找很多植物。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他找很多植物：他找到了很多植物，找 means “look for,” while 找到 means “find.” Because the finding is presented as a completed narrative action, it also needs 了.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一个男人觉得植物很有意思。他叫神农。：一个叫神农的男人觉得植物很有意思。，On first mention, introduce a character with both the name and a clear noun phrase, rather than naming him only in the following sentence.
- Expressiveness: After the stomach pain, add a physical detail to show how severe it feels, for example: 神农疼得弯下了腰。
- Expressiveness: At the point where he finds plants interesting, add an inner thought or a short question, for example: 他想：“哪些植物可以吃呢？”
- Expressiveness: Describe what makes the helpful plants pleasant, for example: 吃了以后，他觉得肚子暖暖的，很舒服。

### Encouragement
- You used the contrast between different kinds of plants clearly with 有的……可是有的……, which helps the reader understand Shennong’s different experiences. Your use of 吃了以后 also shows that you can express the order of actions.

## Scene 2

> 凉山很大的山，这也找植物。一天，他找到黄色的花。他听说你跟黄色的花做茶很有用。比方说，你的头发疼死，这冰茶是药。他一喝茶就很不舒服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 凉山很大的山：凉山是一座很大的山，A noun-predicate sentence identifying what something is needs 是, and mountains take the measure word 座.
- 你跟黄色的花：用黄色的花，Use 用 to introduce the material used to make something; 你 is a pronoun, and 跟 does not introduce a material in this structure.
- 头发：头，Hair itself cannot ache; 头疼 expresses having a headache.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很大 -- “凉山是一座很大的山”：高大，describes the mountain’s large, tall physical appearance more specifically.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 这也找植物：他也在这儿找植物，After introducing the mountain, use the pronoun 他 to return to the already established character; otherwise, the omitted subject may seem to refer to the mountain. Add 在这儿 to make the location clear.
- Coherence (Temporality): 疼死：疼死了，Use 了 after the resultative expression to show the new state of pain after the event.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他一喝茶就很不舒服：他一喝茶就很不舒服了，Use 了 to mark the change of state: he became uncomfortable after drinking the tea.
- Expressiveness: When the yellow flowers first appear, add one visual detail: “黄色的花又大又漂亮，像星星一样。”
- Expressiveness: Show his physical reaction after drinking the tea: “他捂着肚子，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Add his worried thought at the moment he feels ill: “他想：‘我会不会生病？’”

### Encouragement
- You use the useful pattern “一……就……” correctly to connect drinking the tea with feeling uncomfortable. You also introduce the yellow flowers before the character drinks the tea, which makes the event order easy to follow.

## Scene 3

> 糟糕，这黄色的花是夹竹桃，特别是毒。正好在这儿，灵芝药的夹竹桃他吃完了，也写一个课文的植物。谢谢神农。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 特别是毒：特别有毒，When describing how toxic something is, use the adjective phrase “有毒”; “毒” alone does not fit after the degree adverb “特别”.
- 灵芝药的夹竹桃：正好在这儿有灵芝这种药，他把灵芝吃完了，“灵芝”“药”“夹竹桃” cannot be linked with “的” because they do not form one modifier–noun phrase. State that “灵芝” is a medicine, then give “灵芝” as the object of the eating action.
- 一个课文的植物：这两种植物，“课文” means a lesson text and cannot describe the plants being recorded. Use a noun phrase referring to the two plants already mentioned.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 也写：也把这两种植物写到了书上，This is a completed action, so it needs “了”. In the “写到” structure, “了” follows the resultative/directional complement “到”.
- Expressiveness: After the first sentence, describe the flower’s appearance before revealing its danger: “黄色的花像星星一样，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: Show Shennong’s physical reaction after he eats the flower: “他一吃夹竹桃，肚子就疼了起来。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought to show his fear at the dangerous moment: “神农想：‘我会不会生病？’”

### Encouragement
- You named both key plants, “夹竹桃” and “灵芝”, which shows that you are following the important events in this scene. Your opening “糟糕” also creates an immediate sense that the flower is dangerous.

## End of story

- Then, before the ending, a person in brown needs to come and have a conversation with 神农.

### Overall Encouragement
