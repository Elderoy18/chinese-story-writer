## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，孟子和孟母住了在靠墓地的家。孩子才五岁就每天在墓地祭拜了。在那个城市，孟母从早到晚都找工作，可是找不到，害得孟母没有钱。难怪她跟孩子搬家，原来买不了房。他们搬了到别一个城市找工作。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，this expression is redundant because 古代 already expresses a time period, while 的时候 also means “the time when.”
- 跟孩子搬家：带着孩子搬家，跟 means “with” as a companion, but a mother moving her child to a new home should use 带着 to show that she takes the child with her.
- 别一个城市：另一个城市，另一个 means “another one”; 别一个 is not the standard form for “another city.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 在那个城市：在他们住的地方，this is the first mention of a city, so 那个城市 has no clearly introduced referent; use a noun phrase that refers back to the place already mentioned.
- Coherence (Reference): 害得孟母没有钱：害得她没有钱，use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when no other female character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Temporality): 住了在：住在，住 describes a continuing state here, so it takes the zero aspect particle; also 在 must come before the place phrase.
- Coherence (Temporality): 每天在墓地祭拜了：每天在墓地祭拜，daily actions are habitual routines, so they take no 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬了到：搬到了，when a completed movement uses 到 as a directional complement, 了 follows 到: 搬到了.
- Expressiveness: At the cemetery scene, add what the child does while copying the mourners, such as “他一边笑，一边学别人祭拜,” to make the action easier to picture.
- Expressiveness: After the job-search difficulty, show the mother’s worry through an inner thought, such as “孟母心想：‘这样下去怎么办呢？’”
- Expressiveness: When they move, add a small feeling or reaction from the child, such as “孟子觉得很奇怪，不知道为什么又要搬家。”

### Encouragement
- You used the pattern “才五岁就” well to show that the child was very young. You also connected the job-search problem and the lack of money with “害得,” which helps show a cause and result.

## Scene 2

> 这个城市老是很吵，不是人吵架，就是都叫，害得孟子一直不能睡觉。在那儿，孟母也找不到工作。孟子和妈妈要又一个次搬了家。在第三个城市，孟母找到工作，在商店做衣服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 人：人们，Use 人们 for the concrete group of people in this city; 人 is a generic noun and does not naturally refer to this specific group.
- 要又一个次搬了家：要再搬一次家，With a modal verb for a future repeated action, use 要再搬一次家: 再 marks a repeat that has not happened yet, the number phrase is 一次, and 了 is not used after 要 for an uncompleted action.
- 找到工作：找到了工作，Use 了 after 找到 to mark the completed action of finding a job.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 吵 -- “这个城市老是很吵”：吵闹，describes a place with frequent loud activity and noise more specifically.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 吵架，就是都叫：在吵架，就是都在叫，Use 在 for the ongoing actions that are causing the city’s noise.
- Expressiveness: After the first sentence, add Mengzi’s physical reaction and speech, such as “孟子捂着耳朵说：“太吵了，我睡不着！””
- Expressiveness: Before the decision to move, show Mengmu’s worry with an inner thought, such as “孟母叹了口气，心想：“这里太吵了，孟子不能好好休息。””

### Encouragement
- You clearly explain why the family needs to move again: the noise affects Mengzi’s sleep, and Mengmu also cannot find work. The final sentence gives a hopeful change by showing that Mengmu finds a job.

## Scene 3

> 孟子在那个商店也找了到工作，可是不是做衣服的工作，而是喜欢的工作。孟子学习馆学了十五年，他的学习和他的旅行经验很重要，对他很多好处。他是大人的时候，他成为很有名的哲人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 找了到：找到了，when “find” is the meaning, use the result complement 到 after 找; the completion marker 了 comes after the result complement.
- 孟子学习馆学了十五年：孟子在学习馆学了十五年，use 在 before a place noun to show where the action happened.
- 对他很多好处：对他有很多好处，use 对……有好处; 有 is the verb that expresses that something brings benefits.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 旅行经验 -- “他的学习和他的旅行经验很重要”：游历经验，more specifically describes experience gained from travelling to different places.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他的学习和他的旅行经验：他的学习和旅行经验，apply the zero-form rule within one noun phrase: once the possessor has been stated, do not repeat the pronoun before the second item.
- Coherence (Reference): 他是大人的时候，他成为很有名的哲人：他是大人的时候，成为很有名的哲人，apply the zero-form rule when the subject of the following clause is the same person.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为很有名的哲人：成为了很有名的哲人，use 了 after 成为 to mark the completed change of becoming a philosopher.
- Expressiveness: After the fifteen years of study, add one concrete learning detail to show what he studied, for example: “他每天读很多书，也常常思考书里的道理。”
- Expressiveness: At the point when he becomes well known, add an inner thought or short comment to make the achievement feel meaningful, for example: “他终于明白，学习和旅行都让他看到了更大的世界。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show a long passage of time with “十五年” and connect study and travel experience to the character’s later success. The contrast between the two kinds of work also helps the reader understand that he chose work he liked.
