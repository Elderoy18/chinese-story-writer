## Scene 1

> 古代，孟子跟孟母住在墓地的旁边。可是孟母注意，住在墓地的旁边影响孟子。他常常跟朋友们祭拜玩，所以孟母决定他们会搬家。这次，他们住在城市里的商店的旁边，从早到晚很吵。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 注意：注意到，The result complement 到 is needed here because the mother has noticed or realized a fact; 注意 alone means “pay attention.”
- 住在墓地的旁边影响孟子：住在墓地的旁边会影响孟子，A condition or general result needs 会 before the consequence.
- 祭拜玩：玩祭拜的游戏，The verb 玩 must come before the activity or game being played; 祭拜玩 does not have a natural verb-object order.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 吵 -- “从早到晚很吵”：嘈杂，describes a place filled with overlapping, disturbing sounds and keeps the negative meaning.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子跟孟母住在墓地的旁边：有一个小男孩叫孟子，他和孟母住在墓地的旁边，The first-mention rule asks for a brief introduction of a new main character rather than assuming the reader already knows who he is.
- Coherence (Reference): 决定他们会搬家：决定搬家，After 决定, use the zero form and a verb phrase; repeating the people who will act is unnecessary.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 可是孟母注意到：孟母注意到，The connector 可是 expresses contrast, but the mother’s realization follows naturally from living beside the cemetery and does not contrast with it.
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery setting, add a concrete sound or sight to show why the place affects the child, such as “他每天听到有人哭，也看到人们祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: Show the mother’s worry with an inner thought or short quotation after she notices the problem, such as “孟母想：‘这儿不适合孟子长大。’”
- Expressiveness: Make the city-shop area more vivid by describing its sounds, such as “商人一直叫卖，马车声也很大。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish the two locations in this scene and use “从早到晚” effectively to show that the noise lasts all day. The decision to move also follows logically from the mother’s concern.

## Scene 2

> 城市也有点儿危险。害得孟母只好一直陪孟子。孟子也没有朋友，难怪他老是看起来不高兴。住在商店的旁边不是适合孟子，而是让他觉得很伤心。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- Nothing to flag.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不高兴 -- “难怪他老是看起来不高兴”：闷闷不乐，shows that his unhappiness is quiet and lasting rather than a brief bad mood.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): “住在商店的旁边不是适合孟子，而是让他觉得很伤心”：“住在商店的旁边不适合孟子，因为这让他觉得很伤心”，the second idea gives the cause of why the place is unsuitable, so use the cause connector rather than the refuting pair “不是……而是……”.
- Expressiveness: After “城市也有点儿危险”, add one concrete detail to show what is dangerous. For example: “街上人来人往，孟母总担心孟子会走丢。”
- Expressiveness: At “害得孟母只好一直陪孟子”, add the mother’s worried thought or speech. For example: “孟母心想：‘这里太乱了，我不能让孟子一个人出去。’”
- Expressiveness: After “孟子也没有朋友”, show his lonely action. For example: “他常常一个人坐在窗边，看着别的孩子一起玩。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect the noisy city environment with both Mengzi’s loneliness and his mother’s concern. The use of “难怪” also gives a natural explanation for his unhappy mood.

## Scene 3

> 所以，孟母和孟子搬到学校的旁边住。才五岁孟子开始学哲学。在学校，孟子不是看书，就是练习写字。将来，他成为中国的一个非常有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 成为中国的一个非常有名的哲学家：成为了中国的一个非常有名的哲学家，Use “成为了” for the completed change of becoming a philosopher.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子（in “才五岁孟子开始学哲学”）：他，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the referent is clear from the previous sentence.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子（in “在学校，孟子不是看书”）：他，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence of the same character.
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬到学校的旁边住：搬到了学校的旁边住，Use “了” after “到” to mark the completed move.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 才五岁孟子开始学哲学：才五岁，他就开始学哲学，The “才 + age + 就 + verb” pattern shows that an action began unusually early.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 将来：后来，“后来” orders a later completed event in a past narrative; “将来” refers to the future.
- Expressiveness: After the move, add Mengzi’s positive feeling about the school environment, such as: “孟子觉得学校旁边很安静，也很适合学习。”
- Expressiveness: Add a brief line of speech when he begins learning, such as: “孟子高兴地说：‘我想每天都看书！’”
- Expressiveness: Make the study scene more vivid with a small detail, such as: “他认真地坐在桌子前，一笔一画地练习写字。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the change from the noisy city to a school environment, and “不是……就是……” is used well to describe Mengzi’s daily learning activities.
