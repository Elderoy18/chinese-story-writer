## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有很多人不知道什么植物能吃，什么植物不能吃。有很多人吃植物以后就吃坏肚子了，因为他们吃了毒。那个时候，有一个老人叫神农，他什么植物都觉得好吃，不怕生病，不怕毒。他希望别人也可以吃好吃的植物。可是他知道，如果他们还怕吃毒，不会吃他喜欢的植物。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，this time expression is redundant because 古代 already names a time period; use 在古代 to introduce the historical setting.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好吃 -- “他什么植物都觉得好吃”：可口，specifies that the plants taste pleasant；美味，gives a more vivid sense that he finds the plants delicious.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃植物以后就吃坏肚子了：吃了植物以后就吃坏肚子了，use 了 in the action before 以后 because eating is completed before the stomachache happens.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 如果他们还怕吃毒，不会吃他喜欢的植物：如果他们还怕吃毒，就不会吃他喜欢的植物，add 就 in the result clause to complete the conditional connector 如果……就…….
- Expressiveness: After “吃坏肚子了,” add a physical detail to show the illness, such as “他们肚子疼得不得了，只能躺在床上。”
- Expressiveness: At “有一个老人叫神农,” add one detail about his purpose or feeling, such as “神农很想找出什么植物能吃。”
- Expressiveness: After “不怕毒,” add a short thought or quoted line that shows his courage, such as “他想：‘我要试一试，帮助大家。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduce the problem of people becoming ill after eating plants, and you then introduce 神农 as someone who wants others to eat good plants. Your use of “什么植物能吃，什么植物不能吃” is clear and effective.

## Scene 2

> 所以，他上山下山找植物，吃了很多样子的植物。他是第一人，找到了茶。他把所有的植物写下来了。他一直都吃了，他连毒都能吃。可是他还没吃了一个叫夹竹桃的植物。他找到了那个像星星的植物，觉得很漂亮，马上就吃下了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很多样子的植物：很多种植物，"样子" refers to appearance or shape, while "种" is used to count different kinds of plants.
- 他是第一人，找到了茶：他是第一个找到茶的人，To say that someone was the first to do an action, use an ordinal with a measure word and a relative clause.
- 一个叫夹竹桃的植物：一种叫夹竹桃的植物，Use "种" as the measure word when introducing a type of plant.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 找 -- “他上山下山找植物”：探寻，emphasizes searching and exploring over a long journey.
- 写下来 -- “他把所有的植物写下来了”：记下来，emphasizes recording his discoveries so the information is kept.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 他一直都吃了：他一直都吃，An ongoing or habitual action with "一直" uses the zero particle, not completed-action "了".
- Coherence (Temporality): 还没吃了：还没吃，After "没", do not use completed-action "了"; the action has not happened yet.
- Expressiveness: When he finds the flower, add a visual detail to show why it attracts him, such as “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花。”
- Expressiveness: Before he eats it, add his thought or a short line of speech to show his confidence, such as “这朵花这么漂亮，一定很好吃！”
- Expressiveness: When he finds tea, add a small discovery detail, such as “他把茶叶放在水里，闻到一股清香。”

### Encouragement
You create a clear sequence of searching, finding, recording, and trying plants. The details about tea and the star-like plant make this scene easy to picture.

## Scene 3

> 可是夹竹桃的毒很重，让他肚子很痛。他在地上躺着，觉得他快会死了。可是正好有别人找到他了，给了他一个像伞的植物，叫灵芝。他吃灵芝以后就觉得很好，不再生病了。他测量起来了，像星星的植物是毒，像伞的植物是药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别人：一个人，别人 distinguishes another person from the speaker or a known group; here the helper is being introduced for the first time, so an indefinite noun phrase is needed.
- 快会死了：快要死了，快要 expresses that a change is about to happen; 会 does not fit naturally with this imminent change-of-state expression.
- 是毒：有毒，毒 is a noun, while 有毒 is the adjective meaning “poisonous” and can describe the plant.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很好 -- “他吃灵芝以后就觉得很好”：好多了，shows specifically that his physical condition improved after being unwell.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 觉得他：觉得，by the zero-form rule, do not repeat a pronoun for the same character within the same sentence when the referent remains clear.
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃灵芝以后：吃了灵芝以后，use 了 for the completed action that happens before the later feeling of recovery.
- Expressiveness: At the moment of pain, add a stronger physical detail, such as “他疼得在地上打滚”.
- Expressiveness: After the fear of dying, add a short inner thought or quoted question, such as “他想：我会不会死？”
- Expressiveness: When the helper arrives, describe one visible detail, such as “一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人跑过来”.

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the cause of the stomach pain and the later recovery after eating the medicine.
- The comparisons with stars and an umbrella make the two plants easy for the reader to picture.
