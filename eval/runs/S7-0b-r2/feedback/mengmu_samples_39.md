## Scene 1

> 古代的时候人觉得祭拜非常重要，孟子很小的时候，从早到晚一直祭拜，因为他妈妈告诉他祭拜很重要。虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜。他的同学们老是对他开玩笑了。孟子妈妈看了他的同学们对孟子不好的影响，所以孟子和孟子妈妈搬家了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，this time expression is redundant because 古代 already refers to a time period.
- 人：人们，use 人们 for people as a general group; 人 is a generic singular noun and does not naturally refer to a concrete group here.
- 看了：看到了，看到 means “to see/notice” a result, while 看 means “to look at”; the mother notices the influence rather than looks at it.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不好 -- “他的同学们对孟子不好的影响”：不良，this is more precise for a harmful influence on someone.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子很小的时候，从早到晚一直祭拜：有一个叫孟子的小男孩，他小时候每天从早到晚都祭拜，follow the first-mention rule by introducing the character with a name and a brief description before using the name alone.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子妈妈搬家了：孟子和妈妈搬家了，after the mother has just been mentioned, use the shorter noun phrase rather than repeating the full possessive name.
- Coherence (Temporality): 老是对他开玩笑了：老是对他开玩笑，老是 describes a repeated habit, so it takes the zero particle rather than completed-action 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜：因为他每天只祭拜，所以没有朋友，use 因为……所以…… to show the cause-and-effect relationship; 虽然 needs a contrasting result clause with 但是 or 可是.
- Expressiveness: After the opening, add a visual detail about the people at the cemetery, such as “有的人穿着黑衣服，有的人在墓碑前哭”.
- Expressiveness: Show Mengzi’s feeling about the ritual to make his repeated action more understandable, such as “孟子觉得大家的动作很有趣”.
- Expressiveness: Add the mother’s reaction when she notices the classmates’ influence, such as “孟母很担心，心想：‘这儿不适合孟子。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show a cause for Mengzi’s behaviour and a consequence that leads to the family’s move. Your use of “从早到晚一直” also gives the reader a strong sense that his worshipping is frequent.

## Scene 2

> 孟子才十一岁，成为一个哲学家。孟子不是祭拜，而是看政治文章，难怪他成为了哲学家。不过，看过很多政治文章对他害得，他有很多问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 对他害得，他有很多问题：害得他有很多问题，害得 must be followed by the person affected and then the harmful result; it cannot be combined with 对他.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 问题 -- “他有很多问题”：疑问，makes clear that these are questions he wants to understand rather than general problems.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子不是祭拜：他不是祭拜，use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the same character was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other possible referent intervenes.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为一个哲学家：成为了一个哲学家，use 了 after a completed change of state: becoming a philosopher is presented as finished.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 才十一岁，成为了一个哲学家：才十一岁，就成为了一个哲学家，才……就…… links the unusually early age with the completed result.
- Expressiveness: After the first sentence, add a brief detail showing his interest in learning, such as “他每天都认真地看文章，常常想很久。”
- Expressiveness: At “他有很多问题,” add one specific thought to make the questions vivid, such as “他想：‘为什么有的人同意这个办法，有的人不同意呢？’”
- Expressiveness: You could show his reaction to becoming a philosopher with a feeling, such as “他很高兴，也觉得自己要继续学习更多东西。”

### Encouragement
- You used 不是……而是…… effectively to contrast two activities, and 难怪 clearly connects his reading with his later achievement.

## Scene 3

> 他一直问问题，但是不能解决那些问题。孟子长大，他开始能解决问题。他常常建议你不是学金融，而是学政治，因为如果你懂政治，你会成为高薪。后来孟子成为教授，他教学生很厉害。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不是学金融，而是学政治：不要学金融，而要学政治，After a verb of advice, use a negative directive and a positive directive to state what the listener should and should not do. The pattern with these two connectors is for correcting or contrasting a proposition, not for giving this kind of advice.
- 成为高薪：拿到高薪，"高薪" means a high salary, so it is something a person receives rather than a position or identity that someone can become.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 问 -- “他一直问问题”：提出，emphasizes actively raising questions for discussion or thought.
- 厉害 -- “他教学生很厉害”：很有办法，adds the specific idea that he has effective ways to teach students.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他开始能解决问题：开始能解决问题，Use zero form after the name has already appeared in the same sentence; the subject remains孟子.
- Coherence (Reference): 你：人们，A bare second-person pronoun has no introduced referent in this narration. Use a full noun phrase when referring generally to the people he advises.
- Coherence (Reference): 他教学生很厉害：教学生很厉害，Use zero form when the same subject,孟子, continues in the next clause of the same sentence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 孟子长大：孟子长大了，Use了for the completed change of state before he begins being able to solve problems.
- Coherence (Temporality): 孟子成为教授：孟子成为了教授，Use了because becoming a professor is presented as a completed later event.
- Expressiveness: After he can solve problems, add his feeling or thought to show his growth: “孟子很高兴，觉得自己终于明白了很多事情。”
- Expressiveness: When describing his teaching, add one concrete classroom detail: “学生都认真地听他讲课，还常常问他问题。”

### Encouragement
- You use time words such as “后来” clearly to move the narrative forward, and the contrast between asking questions and later solving them gives the scene a clear sense of development.
