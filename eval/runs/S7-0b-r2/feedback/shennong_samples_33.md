## Scene 1

> 古代时候，人不知道他们能吃什么植物，常常一吃就死。但是不知道为什么，没有办法。所以有一天，神农出去试一试很多种植物，告诉人什么植物是安全什么是危险。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代时候：在古代，古代 already names a historical time period, so adding 时候 is redundant; use the time phrase 在古代.
- 人不知道他们能吃什么植物：人们不知道什么植物能吃，use 人们 for a concrete group of people; the embedded question asks which plants are edible, so 什么植物能吃 is the natural form.
- 神农出去试一试很多种植物，告诉人什么植物是安全什么是危险：神农出去尝试了很多种植物，告诉了人们什么植物是安全的，什么植物是危险的，these are completed narrative actions, so use 了; 人们 is needed for the group being told, and predicate adjectives after 是 take 的 here.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很多种 -- “尝试了很多种植物”：各种各样的，emphasizes the variety of plant types he tested.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：一个叫神农的人，this is the character’s first mention, so introduce him with a full noun phrase rather than assuming the reader already knows who he is.
- Expressiveness: After the deaths, add the people’s feelings to show why the problem matters: “人们又害怕又着急。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where Shennong begins testing plants, add his determination or inner thought: “他想：我一定要找出能吃的植物。”
- Expressiveness: Describe the setting of his search with one simple detail: “他走到山上，看见了许多不同颜色的植物。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly set up the central problem first and then introduced Shennong’s action as a response to it. Your use of the pattern 一……就…… effectively shows the dangerous result of eating the wrong plants.

## Scene 2

> 发现有的植物对身体非常好，特别是茶，让肚子感觉又暖和又清楚。他告诉朋友：“你吃哪种植物，这就要看你的口味。比方说，你爱吃甜的，可能喜欢糖。”他最喜欢吃花，除了夹竹桃以外，什么都花想吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 让肚子感觉又暖和又清楚：让他觉得肚子又暖和又舒服，清楚 describes information or a person’s mind, not a physical feeling in the stomach; 舒服 correctly describes a pleasant bodily feeling.
- 什么都花想吃：什么花都想吃，when 什么 modifies a noun, it comes before that noun; the pattern is 什么 + noun + 都 + verb.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好 -- “有的植物对身体非常好”：有益，states more precisely that the plants benefit health；对身体很有益，keeps the strong meaning while making the health benefit more specific.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 这就要看你的口味：这就要看你的口味了，the fixed expression 这就要看……了 takes final 了; this 了 is part of the expression rather than a completion marker.
- Expressiveness: At the tea discovery, add a small action and bodily reaction: “他把茶放在水里，喝了一口，觉得肚子暖暖的，很舒服。”
- Expressiveness: Add Shennong’s thought to show why the discovery matters: “他想：‘原来茶不但好喝，还对身体有好处。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly connect the plants to their effects on the body, and the direct speech gives the scene a lively storytelling voice. The final sentence also uses the exception pattern naturally to show which flower he does not want to eat.

## Scene 3

> 他发现这个口味，一百岁时候，不小心吃了毒的花。一吃就开始死。但是生病时候告诉人什么植物是药，什么植物是毒。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一百岁时候：一百岁的时候，Multi-syllable time expressions before “时候” need the particle “的”.
- 毒的花：有毒的花，Use “有毒的 + noun” to describe a poisonous plant; “毒的植物/花” is not the standard adjective form.
- 什么植物是毒：什么植物有毒，Use “有毒” to state that a plant is poisonous; “是毒” is not natural in this meaning.
- 人：人们，Use “人们” for a concrete group of people in the story; “人” is a generic noun.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不小心 -- “不小心吃了毒的花”：误食，more precisely describes accidentally eating something that should not be eaten.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉：告诉了，This is a completed narrative action, so the verb needs “了” to mark completion.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 但是：［delete］，“但是” introduces contrast, but the following event is a time relationship instead. The time phrase “生病的时候” already connects the events.
- Expressiveness: Before the eating action, add a visual detail about the flower, such as “黄色的像星星一样的花”.
- Expressiveness: After “一吃就开始死”, add a physical feeling to show the danger, such as “肚子疼了起来”.
- Expressiveness: Add a short frightened thought at that moment, such as “会死吗”.

### Encouragement
- You used the “一……就……” pattern effectively to show that the dangerous result happened immediately after eating the flower. You also clearly included the important distinction between plants that are medicine and plants that are poisonous.
