## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子。他们住在墓地的旁边，所以孟子老是祭拜。孟子不是祭拜，就是跟朋友聊天。他很喜欢他的城市，但是城市有才两个学校，都很小。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 already functions as a time expression, so adding 的时候 is redundant.
- 城市有才两个学校：城市才有两所学校，才 must come before the verb it modifies; use the measure word 所 for schools.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 小 -- “都很小”：规模很小，specifies that the schools are small in scale rather than only physically small.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子：一个叫孟子的男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国，follow the first-mention rule by introducing the character’s name together with the full noun phrase.
- Coherence (Reference): 他的城市：自己住的城市，when the possessor is the same person as the subject, 自己 makes that relationship clear; adding 住的 also specifies which city is meant.
- Expressiveness: After “他们住在墓地的旁边,” add a concrete detail about the place, such as “每天都有很多人来墓地祭拜，还有人伤心地哭。”
- Expressiveness: At “孟子老是祭拜,” show his feelings or imitation of others: “孟子觉得祭拜很好玩，就学着别人的样子祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: Give the mother a brief reaction after his repeated actions: “妈妈看到以后很着急，觉得这样会影响孟子。”

### Encouragement
- You introduce the main characters and use a clear contrast with 但是 to show that the city has both positive and negative points. Your use of the pattern 不是……就是…… also makes Mengzi’s repeated activities easy to understand.

## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈不是不喜欢他们的城市，而是想孟子有很好的教育。他们搬家到有很多学校的城市。他们从早到晚走路，孟子一直抱怨。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 想孟子有很好的教育：想让孟子受到很好的教育，想 needs a verb phrase as its object; use 让 + person + verb to express wanting someone to receive something.
- 搬家：搬，use 搬到 + place for changing residence; 搬家 cannot be followed directly by 到 + destination in this structure.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬到有很多学校的城市：搬到了有很多学校的城市，use 了 after 搬到 to mark the completed move.
- Coherence (Temporality): 从早到晚走路：从早到晚都在走路，use 都在 for an action that continues throughout the period from morning to evening.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他们搬到了有很多学校的城市。他们从早到晚都在走路：他们搬家时，从早到晚都在走路，孟子一直抱怨，use a temporal connector to show that the walking and complaining happened during the move.
- Expressiveness: After the complaint, add a short line of quoted speech to show Mengzi’s feeling, such as “孟子说：‘妈妈，我的脚很疼，我们什么时候才能到？’”
- Expressiveness: Add one physical detail during the walk to make the scene more vivid, such as “孟子走得满头大汗，腿也越来越酸。”

### Encouragement
- You used the contrast pattern “不是……而是……” effectively to explain the mother’s reason for moving.
- “从早到晚” and “一直抱怨” clearly show that the journey feels long and difficult for Mengzi.

## Scene 3

> 他难怪抱怨，他想他的朋友，因为他不要离开他的城市。他的妈妈找到孟子一个老师。跟老师孟子学最重要的文学，所以他成为哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不要离开：不想离开，Use 不想 to express that a person does not want to do something; 不要 is normally used for a refusal, instruction, or prohibition.
- 找到孟子：给孟子找到，The person who benefits from the action should be introduced with 给 before the verb: 给 + recipient + verb + object.
- 跟老师孟子学：孟子跟老师学，The subject must come before the prepositional phrase 跟老师; 跟老师 describes whom 孟子 studies with.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他的朋友：自己的朋友，Use 自己的 when the possessor is the same person as the subject; this makes clear that the friends belong to him.
- Coherence (Reference): 他的城市：自己的城市，Use 自己的 when the subject does not want to leave his own city.
- Coherence (Reference): 所以他成为哲学家：所以成为哲学家，Use zero form for the same subject in the following clause of one sentence; after 孟子 has been named, repeating 他 is unnecessary.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他的妈妈找到孟子一个老师：他的妈妈给孟子找到了一个老师，The completed action needs 了 after the resultative verb 找到.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为哲学家：成为了哲学家，Use 了 for the completed change of state: he eventually became a philosopher.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他难怪抱怨，他想他的朋友，因为：难怪他抱怨，原来，Use 难怪……，原来…… to show that the following information explains why the complaint is understandable.
- Expressiveness: At the point where he studies with the teacher, add a study detail: “孟子每天认真地看书，还问老师很多问题。”
- Expressiveness: You could show the mother’s feeling after finding a teacher: “他的妈妈很高兴，说：‘你要好好学习。’”
- Expressiveness: When describing his success, add an evaluative detail: “后来，他成为了一个很有名的哲学家。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect Mengzi’s wish to stay in his city with his complaining, and you give his story a clear later achievement.
