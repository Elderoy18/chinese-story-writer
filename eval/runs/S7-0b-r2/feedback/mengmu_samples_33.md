## Scene 1

> 一天，很久很久以前，孟子和他妈妈住在中国。孟子妈妈的工作是祭拜，孟子不高兴，因为他说我们老是搬家，为什么？他妈妈说，从早到晚，我一直祭拜，很多墓地得我祭拜。所以我们得去很多不同的地方。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一天，很久很久以前：很久很久以前的一天，A complete time expression should place the broad time modifier before the specific day.
- 他说我们老是搬家，为什么？：他说：“我们老是搬家，为什么？”，Direct speech, especially a spoken question, should be introduced with a colon and enclosed in quotation marks.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不高兴 -- “孟子不高兴”：烦恼, specifies that the repeated moves are troubling him；闷闷不乐, shows a quietly unhappy mood rather than only stating that he is unhappy.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和他妈妈：有一个小男孩叫孟子，他和妈妈，This is the first mention of the main character, so use a name plus a brief introduction before using the pronoun-like family reference.
- Expressiveness: At the point where Mengzi feels unhappy, add a small action showing his mood, such as “孟子皱着眉头，低着头”.
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of dialogue from the mother to show her feelings about the difficult routine, such as “妈妈叹了口气，说：‘我也很累，可是这些地方都得去。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly explain the reason the family has to travel, using the mother’s speech to connect the events.
- The phrase “从早到晚” gives the mother’s routine a clear sense of duration.

## Scene 2

> 孟子想，难怪我们常常搬家，原来我妈妈很忙。可是常常搬家害得孟子很难找到朋友。每次搬家孟子也觉得新的地方不是太热就是太冷，不是太大就是太小。在哪的地方，他都不高兴。孟子想帮助妈妈祭拜，可是他六岁，所以不能祭拜。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在哪的地方：在那些地方，哪 is an interrogative word; when referring back to the places already mentioned, use 那些.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 常常 -- “难怪我们常常搬家”：频繁，more precisely emphasizes that the moves happen many times.
- 新的 -- “孟子也觉得新的地方不是太热就是太冷”：刚搬去的，specifies that these are places the family has only just moved to.
- 不高兴 -- “他都不高兴”：闷闷不乐，shows a quieter, lasting unhappy mood.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After “很难找到朋友,” add a brief inner feeling to show why this is difficult for him, for example: “孟子觉得很孤单。”
- Expressiveness: At “不是太热就是太冷,” add one concrete physical detail, for example: “夏天热得满头大汗。”
- Expressiveness: Let Mengzi speak when he wants to help, for example: “妈妈，我也想帮你祭拜。”

### Encouragement
You clearly connect Mengzi’s family’s frequent moves with his difficulty making friends. You also use the paired pattern “不是……就是……” effectively to describe the uncomfortable places.
