## Scene 1

> 有一天孟子去了墓地玩儿，但是他妈妈觉得这是不合适的地方让孩子玩，所以她决定了他们会搬家找更好的地方。她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福，而且住在这儿教育不太好。因为她望子成龙，所以她会一直搬家才能找到质量好的教育。然后他们搬到了离城市更近的地方。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 这是不合适的地方让孩子玩：这是一个不适合孩子玩的地方，适合 takes the person or group as its object, and the action of playing modifies the place with 的.
- 她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福：她担心儿子的童年不会幸福，恐怕 expresses the speaker’s uncertainty or concern and cannot describe a third person’s feeling in this structure; 担心 means that she is worried.
- 住在这儿教育不太好：而且这儿的教育不太好，when a place modifies the noun 教育, use 的 to show that it is the education in that place.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 更好的 -- “找更好的地方”：更适合孩子成长的，explains exactly why the new place would be better for the child.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 有一天孟子去了墓地玩儿：有一天，一个小男孩叫孟子，他去了墓地玩儿，at first mention, introduce a character clearly with both an identifying noun phrase and the name.
- Coherence (Temporality): 所以她决定了他们会搬家找更好的地方：所以她决定搬家，找更好的地方，决定 is a mental-state verb and follows the zero-particle rule, so it does not take 了; it can directly take the action phrase that follows.
- Expressiveness: After the mother sees where he is playing, add her immediate reaction: “孟母看见孟子在墓地玩儿，心里很着急。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of thought after her concern about his childhood: “她心想：‘我不能让孩子一直在这儿玩。’”
- Expressiveness: Describe the new area with one concrete detail after the final sentence: “那儿每天都有很多商人卖东西，街上很热闹。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the mother’s reason for moving by using a cause-and-effect connection with 因为 and 所以.
- You also use completed-action markers well in 去了 and 搬到了, which helps the events move forward clearly.

## Scene 2

> 他们住才一月就又搬家。古代的时候，一般人不喜欢卖东西的人。但是孟子老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿，害得妈妈着急得很，因为她恐怕儿子会成为跟他们很像。她告诉了孟子：他们不是朋友，而是不好的人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 住才一月：才住了一个月，In a duration expression, the time phrase follows the verb, and a completed period of living takes 了.
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 already expresses a time period, so adding 的时候 is redundant.
- 老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿：老是跟这些卖东西的人玩儿，老是 and 常常 are both frequency adverbs; use only one of them here.
- 她恐怕：她担心，恐怕 means “I am afraid that” and cannot describe another person’s fear with 她 as its subject; 担心 expresses that she is worried.
- 成为跟他们很像：变得跟他们很像，成为 is followed by a noun identifying a role or identity, while 变得 is used before an adjective phrase describing a change of qualities.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 卖东西的人 -- “这些卖东西的人”：商人，names their occupation more precisely as people who do business or sell goods.
- 着急得很 -- “害得妈妈着急得很”：焦虑不安，shows that the mother is not only worried but also feels unsettled and uneasy.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 妈妈：孟子的妈妈，Repeat the name/noun when the mother has not been mentioned recently and other people have appeared, so the reader can identify whose mother is meant.
- Coherence (Temporality): 又搬家：又搬家了，The final action is completed, so the last verb in this sequence takes 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉了孟子：告诉孟子，A saying verb that introduces quoted speech uses the zero particle rather than 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 一般人不喜欢卖东西的人。但是孟子老是跟这些卖东西的人玩儿，Add 但是 to mark contrast: Mengzi’s behaviour goes against what the previous sentence says about other people.
- Expressiveness: After the mention of the sellers, add a small scene detail to show why the place attracts Mengzi, such as “街上从早到晚都很热闹，商人卖各种各样的东西。”
- Expressiveness: Add the mother’s inner thought before her speech to show her worry, such as “孟子的妈妈心想：‘要是他一直跟他们玩儿，以后可能会学坏。’”
- Expressiveness: Make the mother’s warning more vivid with a short reason, such as “她告诉孟子：‘他们不是你的朋友，你不能老是跟他们一起玩儿。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the cause of the mother’s worry with 害得 and 因为, and the final quoted speech gives the scene a strong ending.
