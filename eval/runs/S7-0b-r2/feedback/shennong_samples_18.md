## Scene 1

> 神农和他的家人住在古代，所以不知道什么植物能吃。因为他的家人常常生病了，所以神农和他的哥哥一起去旅行找可以吃的植物。他们去很多不一样的地方，比方说高山、山谷和沙漠。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 住在古代：生活在古代，古代 is a time period rather than a physical place, so a verb such as 生活 is needed to describe living in that historical time.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 找 -- “去旅行找可以吃的植物”：寻找，emphasizes the ongoing process of looking for plants；探寻，adds the sense of exploring unknown plants.
- 不一样 -- “很多不一样的地方”：各不相同，makes it clearer that the places differ from one another in their features.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农和他的家人：一个叫神农的人和他的家人，on first mention, introduce a character with a name and a brief identifying noun phrase rather than assuming the reader already knows the name.
- Coherence (Temporality): 常常生病了：常常生病，use the zero particle for a habitual action; 常常 shows that becoming ill happens repeatedly, not as one completed event.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们去很多不一样的地方：他们去了很多不一样的地方，use 了 for the completed narrative action of visiting many places.
- Expressiveness: After the family’s illnesses, add Shennong’s feeling or thought to show his motivation: “神农很担心家人，心想：我一定要找到能治病的植物。”
- Expressiveness: Make the journey’s places more vivid with one sensory detail: “高山又高又冷，山谷里开着很多花。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short description of the brothers’ effort during the trip: “他们走了很远的路，脚都累了。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect the family’s repeated illnesses with Shennong’s decision to look for edible plants. Your list of places also gives the journey a broad and adventurous feeling.

## Scene 2

> 他们在很漂亮的河找夹竹桃。神农问他的哥哥：“我应该吃这种植物吗？”哥哥说：“这就要看你想要安全还是接近知道了。“神农一吃夹竹桃就生病了。哥哥说：“太麻烦了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在很漂亮的河找：在一条很漂亮的河边找，After 在, the location needs a complete place phrase; 河边 names the place beside the river, and 一条 is the matching measure word for 河.
- 哥哥说：“这就要看你想要安全还是接近知道了。“：哥哥说：“这就要看你是想要安全，还是想知道答案了。”，接近 means “move close to” and cannot directly modify 知道. The 这就要看……了 structure requires a question or choice after 看, such as A还是B, and its fixed 了 comes at the end.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 找 -- “他们在很漂亮的河找夹竹桃”：寻找, emphasizes an ongoing search for the plant in this place.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: When the river and flowers first appear, add one visual detail to make the setting easier to imagine, for example: “河边开着黄色的花，像星星一样。”
- Expressiveness: After the illness, show a physical feeling rather than only stating the event, for example: “神农的肚子疼了起来，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: After the brother says “太麻烦了,” add his worried reaction or a question, for example: “哥哥着急地说：‘你觉得怎么样？’”

### Encouragement
- You used direct speech to show the brothers discussing a decision, and you correctly used the 一……就…… pattern to connect eating the plant with becoming ill.

## Scene 3

> 夹竹桃是毒的，我想知道灵芝会帮他。”哥哥给神农灵芝。神农吃完了以后开始痊愈了。神农说：“谢谢哥哥，你帮得真好。灵芝我们可以吃，特别是如果我们已经吃了夹竹桃。“

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 夹竹桃是毒的：夹竹桃有毒，Use 有毒 to describe that a plant contains poison; 毒 is a noun and does not normally follow 是 in this description.
- 灵芝会帮他：灵芝会不会帮他，After 想知道, use a question form such as 会不会 when the speaker is wondering whether something will happen.
- 特别是如果我们已经吃了夹竹桃。“：特别是如果我们已经吃了夹竹桃。”，The final quotation mark should be a closing quotation mark.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好 -- “你帮得真好”：周到，describes the brother’s help as considerate and well planned in this situation.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他：神农，Use the name again when a pronoun could refer either to the brother or to Shennong.
- Coherence (Temporality): 哥哥给神农灵芝：哥哥给了神农灵芝，Use 了 after 给 because this is a completed action in the sequence.
- Expressiveness: After the recovery, add a physical feeling to show the change: “他的肚子慢慢不疼了，觉得好多了。”
- Expressiveness: Make the medicine more vivid when the brother gives it: “哥哥拿起一株像伞一样的灵芝，给神农吃。”
- Expressiveness: Add an emotion to Shennong’s thanks: “神农高兴地说：‘谢谢哥哥！’”

### Encouragement
- You used 吃完了以后 well to show the order of events. Your dialogue also clearly shows Shennong’s gratitude to his brother.
