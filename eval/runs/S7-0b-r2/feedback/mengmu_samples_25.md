## Scene 1

> 这是古代的故事。孟子小的时候，他的爸爸很早就去世了。从他的家到墓地很近，所以他每天老是从早到晚去墓地跟朋友们玩玩儿。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 从他的家到墓地很近：他的家离墓地很近，Use 离 to state that one place is close to another; 从…到… normally introduces a route or distance range.
- 每天老是从早到晚去墓地：每天从早到晚一直去墓地，老是 describes repeatedly occurring actions, while 一直 fits one continuous action lasting from morning until evening.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 所以他每天：所以孟子每天，Repeat the name after another character, his father, has been mentioned, because the pronoun could otherwise refer to either person.
- Expressiveness: At the point where he goes to the cemetery, add a concrete detail about what he sees there, such as “孟子常常看到有人来墓地祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: Add Mengzi’s reaction to make his reason for going there more vivid, such as “他觉得这些仪式很有趣。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish Mengzi’s early family situation and use a logical cause-and-effect link to explain why he often goes to the cemetery. The time phrase “从早到晚” also gives the scene a strong sense of routine.

## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈觉得在墓地玩儿，害得，所以他们搬家。搬家对孟子有好的影响，他才五岁，可是每天不是写字就是帮妈妈的工作。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 害得：害得她常常道歉，The pattern with 害得 must be followed by the person affected and a harmful result; it cannot stand alone.
- 帮妈妈的工作：帮妈妈做事，After 帮妈妈, use a verb phrase to state the action the child helps with; 妈妈的工作 is only a noun phrase.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好的 -- “搬家对孟子有好的影响”：积极的，This specifies that the influence supports his development rather than merely being generally good.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 所以他们搬家：所以他们搬家了，Use 了 for the completed action of moving house.
- Expressiveness: After the graveyard setting, add a concrete detail about what Mengzi sees: “他每天看到有人在墓地哭，还学着他们祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: Show the mother’s concern with an inner thought or quoted speech: “妈妈心想：‘再住在这儿，他会学坏的。’”

### Encouragement
- You used 所以 to show a cause-and-effect relationship, and the phrase 才五岁 gives the reader a clear sense of Mengzi’s young age.

## Scene 3

> 他很聪明的人，难怪他成为有名的人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他很聪明的人：他是一个很聪明的人，A noun phrase used as the predicate needs the copula 是; 一个 is needed before the countable noun 人.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为有名的人：成为了有名的人，Becoming someone is a completed change of state in this concluding narrative, so the verb takes 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他是一个很聪明的人，难怪他成为有名的人：难怪他成为有名的人，原来他是一个很聪明的人，The connector pattern 难怪……，原来…… presents the surprising result first and then gives its reason.
- Expressiveness: After the final result, add a detail about how others responded, such as 人们都很尊敬他.
- Expressiveness: Make the conclusion more specific by naming his achievement, such as 他后来成为了有名的哲学家.

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect the character’s intelligence with his later success, and 难怪 is a good word for showing that connection.
