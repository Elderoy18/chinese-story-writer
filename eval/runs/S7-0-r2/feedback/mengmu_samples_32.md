## Scene 1

> 有一天孟子去了墓地玩儿，但是他妈妈觉得这是不合适的地方让孩子玩，所以她决定了他们会搬家找更好的地方。她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福，而且住在这儿教育不太好。因为她望子成龙，所以她会一直搬家才能找到质量好的教育。然后他们搬到了离城市更近的地方。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 这是不合适的地方让孩子玩：这是不适合孩子玩儿的地方，use the pattern “适合 + person + verb phrase”; the verb phrase must modify “地方” with “的”.
- 他们会搬家找更好的地方：搬家，找更好的地方，after “决定”, use a verb phrase directly; the subject does not need to be repeated because it is already clear from “她决定”.
- 她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福：她担心儿子的童年不会幸福，“恐怕” means “I am afraid that” and cannot follow another subject in this pattern; “担心” expresses that she is worried about her son’s future.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 有一天孟子去了墓地玩儿：有一天，有一个小男孩叫孟子，他去了墓地玩儿，at first mention, introduce a character with a name and a brief identifying noun phrase rather than using only the name.
- Coherence (Temporality): 她决定了搬家，找更好的地方：她决定搬家，找更好的地方，“决定” is a mental-state verb, so it normally takes the zero particle rather than completed-action “了”.
- Expressiveness: After the visit to the cemetery, add what makes the place interesting to the child, for example: “孟子觉得别人祭拜很有趣，也跟着学。”
- Expressiveness: Show the mother’s worry with an internal thought after she notices his behaviour, for example: “孟母很着急，心想：‘住在这儿会影响孩子。’”
- Expressiveness: Add a brief detail about the new area to make the move more vivid, for example: “那儿每天都有商人卖东西，热闹极了。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly used cause-and-effect linking with “因为……所以……”, and you also moved the events forward naturally with “然后”.
- The reason for the mother’s decision is easy to understand, especially through the idea of wanting good education for her child.

## Scene 2

> 他们住才一月就又搬家。古代的时候，一般人不喜欢卖东西的人。但是孟子老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿，害得妈妈着急得很，因为她恐怕儿子会成为跟他们很像。她告诉了孟子：他们不是朋友，而是不好的人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，this expression is redundant because 古代 already names a time period, while 的时候 also means “the time when.”
- 老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿：老是跟这些卖东西的人玩儿，do not use two frequency adverbs together when they give the same meaning; 老是 already expresses that the action happens repeatedly.
- 因为她恐怕儿子会成为跟他们很像：因为她担心儿子会变得跟他们很像，恐怕 cannot follow another subject such as 她 to mean that she is afraid; use 担心 here. Also, 成为 needs a noun or identity as its complement, while 变得 can be followed by an adjective phrase such as 跟他们很像.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 卖东西的人 -- “一般人不喜欢卖东西的人”：做生意的人，describes people whose occupation is selling or trading, rather than people who may simply be selling something once.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们住才一月就又搬家：他们才住了一个月，就又搬家了，use the 才 + verb + 了 + duration， 就 + verb + 了 pattern to show that they moved again after only a short time; both moves are completed actions.
- Coherence (Temporality): 她告诉了孟子：她告诉孟子，use the zero particle with a saying verb that introduces quoted speech; 告诉 does not take 了 in this use.
- Expressiveness: After the sentence about playing with the sellers, add a concrete action to show what Mengzi copies, such as: “孟子学着商人收钱、讨价还价，玩得很开心。”
- Expressiveness: Show the mother’s worried thoughts before her speech, such as: “孟母心想：‘住在这儿会影响孩子的学习。’”
- Expressiveness: Add one detail about the busy surroundings to make the setting clearer, such as: “从早到晚，街上都很热闹。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the mother’s concern through both her feelings and her direct speech. The use of 因为 also helps connect Mengzi’s behaviour with why his mother becomes worried.

## Scene 3

> 你不是当数学家，而是当哲学家，不能当卖东西的人。所以他们又搬家。在他们第三个家，孟子学了怎么祭拜。妈妈感觉这个地方适合他们，告诉了孟子：我们找到了最后的家。在这儿妈妈让儿子从早到晚学习，他难怪成为有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在他们第三个家：在他们的第三个家里，The possessive marker 的 is needed after the pronoun, and 里 makes the home a complete location phrase after 在.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 卖东西的人 -- “不能当卖东西的人”：商人，names the occupation more precisely than a general description of someone who sells things.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 又搬家：又搬家了，Use 了 for the completed move in this narrative sequence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉了孟子：告诉孟子，A saying verb that introduces quoted speech takes the zero particle, not 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为：成为了，Use 了 to show that becoming a philosopher was a completed change of state.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他难怪：难怪他，Place 难怪 before the clause it introduces; it expresses the inference that his long hours of study explain his later success.
- Expressiveness: After “在他们的第三个家里”, add one detail about the learning environment, such as “学校旁边很安静，常常能听见学生读书的声音。”
- Expressiveness: At “妈妈感觉这个地方适合他们”, show the mother’s happiness with an inner thought or speech line, such as “妈妈高兴地想：‘这儿终于适合孟子学习了。’”
- Expressiveness: After “从早到晚学习”, add a feeling or action that shows effort, such as “孟子虽然有点累，可是每天都认真地看书。”

### Encouragement
- You used the contrast pattern “不是……而是……” correctly to show the mother’s hopes for Mengzi.
- Your scene also has a clear sequence: the family moves, finds a suitable home, and Mengzi studies hard.
