## Scene 1

> 古代，孟子跟孟母住在墓地的旁边。可是孟母注意，住在墓地的旁边影响孟子。他常常跟朋友们祭拜玩，所以孟母决定他们会搬家。这次，他们住在城市里的商店的旁边，从早到晚很吵。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 注意：注意到，The result complement 到 shows that she has noticed a fact; 注意 by itself means “pay attention” and does not fit this completed discovery.
- 跟朋友们祭拜玩：跟朋友们一起学别人祭拜，祭拜 is a ritual action and cannot be followed by 玩 to mean “do it for fun”; this revision clearly expresses that he imitates other people’s ritual behaviour.
- 决定他们会搬家：决定带着孟子搬家，决定 takes a verb phrase directly; it should not be followed by a future clause with a repeated subject.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 吵 -- “从早到晚很吵”：嘈杂，describes a place with many mixed, confusing sounds；喧闹，emphasizes the lively and noisy atmosphere around the shops.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子跟孟母住在墓地的旁边：有一个小男孩叫孟子，他和妈妈住在墓地的旁边，This is the first mention of the characters, so introduce the boy clearly with his name and identity before using family-role references.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 可是孟母注意：孟母注意到，The connector 可是 expresses contrast, but noticing the harmful effect naturally follows from living near the cemetery rather than contrasting with it.
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery is introduced, add a sensory detail about the people there, such as “孟子每天都看见有人在墓地哭。”
- Expressiveness: Show why Mengzi finds the ritual attractive by adding his feeling, such as “他觉得这样很好玩。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where his mother decides to move, add her inner thought or speech, such as “孟母心想：‘不能再住在这儿了。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish the setting, the problem near the cemetery, and the move to a new place. The use of “从早到晚” also gives the final location a strong sense of its daily atmosphere.

## Scene 2

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- Nothing to flag.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 危险 -- “城市也有点儿危险”：不太安全，states more specifically that the place does not feel safe while keeping the mild meaning.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子 -- 他，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the same character was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other possible referent competes.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 住在商店的旁边不是适合孟子，而是让他觉得很伤心 -- 住在商店的旁边不适合孟子，因为这让他觉得很伤心，The two ideas have a cause relationship: living there is unsuitable because it makes him sad. The paired connector 不是……而是…… is for correcting or contrasting two alternatives, not for giving a reason.
- Expressiveness: After “城市也有点儿危险”, add a concrete detail about the busy commercial area, such as “从早到晚，街上又吵又挤。”
- Expressiveness: After “孟子也没有朋友”, show his feeling through an action, such as “他常常一个人坐在窗边发呆。”
- Expressiveness: At the end, add Meng Mu’s worried thought or speech, such as “孟母心想：‘这儿真的不适合他。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect the new environment with Meng Zi’s feelings and Meng Mu’s response. Your use of “难怪” effectively introduces the reason for his unhappiness.

## Scene 3

> 所以，孟母和孟子搬到学校的旁边住。才五岁孟子开始学哲学。在学校，孟子不是看书，就是练习写字。将来，他成为中国的一个非常有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 将来：后来，This story is narrated as a completed past event, so this time word should sequence a later event in the past rather than refer to the future from the present.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬到学校的旁边住：搬到了学校的旁边住，The move is a completed action, so the verb-complement sequence needs the completion marker 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 才五岁孟子开始学哲学：孟子才五岁就开始学哲学，The paired pattern uses 才 with an age or small amount and 就 before the action to show that it happened especially early.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他成为中国的一个非常有名的哲学家：他成为了中国的一个非常有名的哲学家，Becoming a philosopher is presented as a completed change of state, so 成为 takes 了.
- Expressiveness: After the move, add the mother’s relief about the school environment, for example: 孟母很高兴，觉得这里很适合孟子学习。
- Expressiveness: Make Mengzi’s studying more vivid by adding a small action, for example: 他每天认真地看书，还常常向老师问问题。

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the change from a noisy city environment to a school setting, and the either-or sentence pattern makes Mengzi’s daily study routine easy to understand.
