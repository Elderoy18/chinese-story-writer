## Scene 1

> 神农和他的家人住在古代，所以不知道什么植物能吃。因为他的家人常常生病了，所以神农和他的哥哥一起去旅行找可以吃的植物。他们去很多不一样的地方，比方说高山、山谷和沙漠。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 住在古代：生活在古代，住 requires a location as its complement, while an era is expressed with 生活在 plus the time period.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不一样 -- “很多不一样的地方”：各不相同的，emphasizes that the places differ clearly from one another；不同类型的，makes the kinds of places more specific before the examples of mountains, valleys, and deserts.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：一个叫神农的人，this is the character’s first mention, so introduce the name with a short noun phrase that identifies him.
- Coherence (Temporality): 常常生病了：常常生病，habits and repeated states use the zero particle; 了 is not used with a routine marked by 常常.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们去很多不一样的地方：他们去了很多不一样的地方，this describes completed travel events in the story, so the verb needs completed-action 了.
- Expressiveness: Before the journey, add Shen Nong’s purpose or determination, for example: 神农说：“我要找出能吃的植物，帮大家少生病。”
- Expressiveness: Make the journey more vivid by showing how they move through the listed places, for example: 他们翻过高山，穿过山谷，走进沙漠。

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect the family’s illness to Shen Nong’s decision to travel by using 因为……所以…….
- Your list of 高山、山谷和沙漠 gives the journey a good sense of variety.

## Scene 2

> 他们在很漂亮的河找夹竹桃。神农问他的哥哥：“我应该吃这种植物吗？”哥哥说：“这就要看你想要安全还是接近知道了。“神农一吃夹竹桃就生病了。哥哥说：“太麻烦了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在很漂亮的河找夹竹桃：在很漂亮的河边找夹竹桃，when an action happens beside a river, the location noun should be 河边; 河 alone does not name the place where they are searching.
- 这就要看你想要安全还是接近知道了：这就要看你想不想安全地知道答案了，the pattern 这就要看……了 must be followed by a question form, such as 想不想. 接近 cannot take 知道 as its object; 安全地知道答案 expresses “know the answer safely.”
- 太麻烦了。：太麻烦了。”，a direct quotation opened with “ must end with ”.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 找 -- “他们在很漂亮的河找夹竹桃”：寻找，emphasizes an ongoing search or exploration for the plant.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: At the river scene, add a visual detail about the flower, such as “河边开着黄色的花，像星星一样，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: After the illness, show Shennong’s physical feeling more clearly: “他的肚子疼了起来，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Make the brother’s reaction more specific with quoted speech: “糟糕！这种花可能有毒！”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the cause and result with “神农一吃夹竹桃就生病了,” which makes the event easy to understand. You also use dialogue to show what the two brothers are thinking.

## Scene 3

> 夹竹桃是毒的，我想知道灵芝会帮他。”哥哥给神农灵芝。神农吃完了以后开始痊愈了。神农说：“谢谢哥哥，你帮得真好。灵芝我们可以吃，特别是如果我们已经吃了夹竹桃。“

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 夹竹桃是毒的：夹竹桃有毒，毒 is a noun and cannot be used as an adjective in this pattern; 有毒 describes that a plant contains poison.

### Vocabulary Suggestions

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 哥哥给神农灵芝：哥哥给了神农灵芝，Use 了 after 给 because this is a completed action in the narrative sequence.
- Expressiveness: After 神农吃完了以后开始痊愈了, add a physical detail to show his recovery, such as 神农的肚子慢慢不疼了，脸色也好了一些。
- Expressiveness: When the brother gives the medicine, describe the plant briefly, such as 哥哥拿出了一株像小伞一样的灵芝。
- Expressiveness: Make Shen Nong’s gratitude more emotional with a short line of speech, such as 神农高兴地说：“我好多了，谢谢你！”

### Encouragement
- You used 吃完了以后 clearly to show the order of events, and your dialogue makes the characters’ actions easy to follow.
