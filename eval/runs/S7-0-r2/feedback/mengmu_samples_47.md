### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 安排 -- “孟子的妈妈安排他的生活”：规划，emphasizes that she carefully makes plans for his daily life and future.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 这个男孩子叫孟子，孟子是古代的人：在古代，有一个男孩子叫孟子，a first mention should introduce the character with a full noun phrase rather than using 这个 without an earlier referent.
- Coherence (Reference): 她望子成龙，所以孟子的妈妈安排他的生活：她望子成龙，所以安排他的生活，when the same person continues as the subject within one sentence, use zero form rather than repeating the name.
- Coherence (Temporality): 常常搬了家：常常搬家，routines or repeated habits use the zero particle, not completed-action 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 孟子的妈妈觉得住在哪儿对童年很重要，所以：因为孟子的妈妈觉得住在哪儿对童年很重要，所以，use 因为……所以…… to mark the cause-and-effect relation clearly.
- Expressiveness: After the mother’s concern about where to live, add her worry about Mengzi’s development: “她担心孟子会学坏。”
- Expressiveness: At the end, add a short inner thought to show the mother’s motivation: “她心想：‘我一定要让孟子有一个好的学习环境。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the mother’s motivation with the fitting idiom “望子成龙,” and the relationship between Mengzi and his mother is easy to understand.

## Scene 2

> 因为他们常常搬了家，中国人说，孟母三迁。孟子没有爸爸，所以，他的妈妈更重要。从这儿到那儿，妈妈教孟子很多技能。先她让孟子跟朋友玩儿，然后教他怎么买又便宜又好质量的东西。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 先她让：她先让，The time-order adverb “先” normally follows the subject and comes before the verb.
- 又便宜又好质量的东西：又便宜又质量好的东西，“质量” is a noun, so it cannot directly follow “好” as an adjective phrase; use “质量好” or “质量好的” to describe the goods.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 重要 -- “他的妈妈更重要”：更关键，emphasizes that the mother has a central role in Mengzi’s upbringing.
- 技能 -- “妈妈教孟子很多技能”：本领，fits practical abilities learned from an adult；生活本领，more specifically highlights useful everyday abilities such as choosing goods.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 妈妈教孟子很多技能：她教孟子很多技能，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence: the mother was named in the immediately preceding sentence, and no competing female character has appeared.
- Coherence (Temporality): 常常搬了家：常常搬家，Use the zero particle for a habitual action; “常常” describes a routine, so it does not take completed-action “了”.
- Coherence (Conjunction): ，中国人说：，所以中国人说，Use the paired connector “因为……所以……” to make the cause-and-effect relationship explicit.
- Expressiveness: After the shopping lesson, add a short line of speech to show how the mother teaches: “妈妈说：‘买东西要看质量，也要问价钱。’”
- Expressiveness: Add Mengzi’s reaction after learning the skill, such as: “孟子觉得很新鲜，也想自己试一试。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect the family’s moving with the saying about Mengzi’s mother, and you also try to show events in order with “先” and “然后”.
- The detail about choosing inexpensive, good-quality goods makes the mother’s teaching feel practical and specific.
