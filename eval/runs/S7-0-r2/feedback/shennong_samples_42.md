## Scene 1

> 古代的时候有一个人叫神农。他需要当上医，一看别的人就想帮助他们。但是，他有一个问题，他不知道什么东西能做药。神农听说一个远远的地方有一种植物，什么病人吃都会觉得身体更好。神农三个月走去，找到有植物的地方，他看到一个很漂亮的植物，又香又好看。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，English explanation: “古代” is already a time expression, so adding “的时候” is redundant.
- 当上医：当上医生，English explanation: “医” is not normally used alone as the noun for this occupation; “医生” is the standard noun.
- 一个很漂亮的植物：一株很漂亮的植物，English explanation: “株” is the matching measure word for an individual plant; “个” is too generic here.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他有一个问题，他不知道什么东西能做药：他有一个问题，不知道什么东西能做药，English explanation: use zero form for the same subject when it re-occurs within the same sentence and no other character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Temporality): 找到有植物的地方：找到了有植物的地方，English explanation: use 了 after the resultative verb phrase to mark the completed action of finding the place.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他看到一个很漂亮的植物：他看到了一个很漂亮的植物，English explanation: use 了 after the resultative complement 到 to mark this completed event in the narrative.
- Expressiveness: After hearing about the plant, add Shennong’s hopeful purpose: “神农心想：要是找到这种植物，就能帮助很多人了！”
- Expressiveness: At the moment he sees the plant, add one sensory detail: “花散发着淡淡的香味。”
- Expressiveness: Make the plant’s appearance more memorable with a simple comparison: “黄色的花像星星一样，又大又漂亮。”

### Encouragement
You introduced Shennong clearly at the beginning and kept the events in an easy-to-follow order. The description “又香又好看” already gives the plant two appealing details.

## Scene 2

> 他觉得，这种一定是做药的花。原来他想错了。吃了以后，神农快生病。 这时一个穿着蓝色衣服的人帮神农，快给他一种红的灵芝。神农吃了以后，他没有病，想起来以前一个人告诉他，灵芝对身体有好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 这种一定是做药的花：这种花一定能做药，Use the noun after the demonstrative phrase: “这种花”. “能做药” clearly states that the flower can be used as medicine.
- 以前一个人：以前有一个人，Use “有” to introduce the existence of a person who had told him this information.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农吃了以后，他没有病：神农吃了以后，没有病，Use zero form after “神农” has already been named in the same sentence; the repeated pronoun is unnecessary.
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃了以后，神农快生病：吃了以后，神农就生病了，The illness is a completed result after eating, so use “了”; “就” clearly marks the immediate result.
- Coherence (Temporality): 快给他一种红的灵芝：赶快给了他一种红的灵芝，In this series of actions, the final completed action takes “了”. “赶快” is the appropriate adverb for doing something quickly.
- Coherence (Temporality): 想起来以前有一个人告诉他：想起了以前有一个人告诉他，Use “了” after “想起” to show that the act of remembering was completed.
- Expressiveness: When Shennong becomes ill, add a physical feeling to show how serious it is: “神农肚子疼得不得了，躺在地上。”
- Expressiveness: At the moment the blue-clothed person helps him, add a short line of speech: “他说：‘快吃这个，它能帮你！’”
- Expressiveness: After eating the medicine, show Shennong’s relief: “过了一会儿，神农觉得肚子好多了，终于放心了。”

### Encouragement
- You used time links such as “原来”, “这时”, and “吃了以后” to move the events forward clearly. You also introduced a helper with a concrete detail, “穿着蓝色衣服的人”, which helps the reader picture the scene.

## Scene 3

> 啊，他怎么忘记了？他把灵芝放在包里。一回家就告诉别人。他找到药以后他写下怎么做药在书上。所以人们都知道，那红灵芝对身体好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他把灵芝放在包里：他把灵芝放在了包里，A completed action in a past narrative needs 了; with a location complement, 了 follows the complement.
- 别人：人们，别人 means “other people” in contrast with a particular person or group; here the sentence means people in general.
- 写下怎么做药在书上：在书上写下怎么做药，The location phrase should come before the verb when it describes where the writing happens.
- 那红灵芝：那种红灵芝，A demonstrative before a noun normally needs a measure word; 种 is appropriate for a type of plant.
- 对身体好处：对身体有好处，The fixed structure is 对……有好处; it means “to be beneficial to.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 放 -- “他把灵芝放在包里”：收进，emphasizes putting the plant safely inside the bag for carrying.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他找到药以后他写下：他找到药以后，写下，Use zero form for the same subject in the following clause when no competing character has appeared.
- Coherence (Temporality): 找到药以后：找到了药以后，The action before 以后 is completed, so it needs 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 写下怎么做药：写下了怎么做药，The final action in this completed sequence needs 了; with the resultative complement 下, 了 follows 下.
- Expressiveness: After putting the plant in the bag, add the character’s relieved feeling, such as: 他松了一口气，心想：“幸好我把灵芝带回来了。”
- Expressiveness: When he tells people, add a short line of speech to make the moment vivid: 他说：“这种红灵芝对身体有好处，可以做药。”
- Expressiveness: Make the writing more specific by naming what he records: 他在书上写下灵芝的样子和它的作用。

### Encouragement
- You used the sequence pattern 一……就…… well to show that the character told people immediately after returning home. You also clearly connected the plant to its benefit for health.
