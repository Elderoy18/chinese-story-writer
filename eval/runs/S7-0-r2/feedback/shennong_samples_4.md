## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个男人，他的名字是神农。他看到了有很多人不知道哪个植物好吃，也不知道什么植物对你的身体有好处。这个问题神农觉得很糟糕，所以神农去了吃每种植物。他吃了一种植物以后，就写了这个植物对你有没有好处。有的时候，神农吃了很好吃的植物。有的时候，他吃了很危险的植物。他吃完了每种植物以后，会来跟他的朋友聊到他吃的植物了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 already names a time period, so adding 的时候 is redundant.
- 去了吃：去吃了，when 去 is followed by another action, the completion marker follows the main action: 去 + V. + 了.
- 写了这个植物对你有没有好处：写下了这种植物对身体有没有好处，写下 is used for recording information or findings; 对身体 is the natural general expression here, while 你 would address the reader directly.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 糟糕 -- “这个问题神农觉得很糟糕”：严重，states more precisely that the problem has serious consequences for people’s health.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农觉得很糟糕，所以神农去吃了每种植物：神农觉得很糟糕，所以他去吃了每种植物，follow the pronoun rule: after the name was used in the immediately preceding clause and no other male character competes, use 他 rather than repeating the name.
- Coherence (Reference): 有的时候，神农吃很好吃的植物：有的时候，他吃很好吃的植物，follow the pronoun rule: the recently mentioned character should be referred to with a pronoun when the reference is clear.
- Coherence (Temporality): 有的时候，神农吃了很好吃的植物：有的时候，神农吃很好吃的植物，follow the zero-particle rule: 有的时候 describes a repeated habit, so 吃 does not take 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 有的时候，他吃了很危险的植物：有的时候，他吃很危险的植物，follow the zero-particle rule: a repeated habitual action does not take 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 会来跟他的朋友聊到他吃的植物了：会来跟他的朋友聊到他吃的植物，follow the zero-particle rule: 会 is a modal verb, and this repeated future-like action should not end with 了.
- Expressiveness: After describing the problem, add a concrete effect on the people, such as “很多人吃错了植物，常常生病。”
- Expressiveness: When Shennong decides to test the plants, add his inner thought, such as “神农想：‘我一定要找出哪些植物能吃。’”
- Expressiveness: At the writing spot, add details about what he records, such as “他把植物的颜色、味道和作用一一记下。”

### Encouragement
You clearly establish the problem that motivates Shennong’s actions, and you use repeated-time phrases to show that he tries different plants again and again. The sequence of eating, recording, and talking with friends gives the scene a clear progression.

## Scene 2

> 一个人问神农，哪个植物好吃。神农说，这就要看你的口味了。要是你喜欢吃甜的，有很多甜的水果，比方说苹果、西瓜和梨。一个别的人问神农，哪个植物对你的身体有好处。神农说，有很多种植物对你的身体有好处，比方说小白菜、黄瓜、茶和菠菜。一个别的人问神农，哪个植物对你的身体有坏处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 这就要看你的口味了：这就要看你喜欢什么口味了，In the pattern “这就要看……了”, the part after “看” should be a question form; “喜欢什么口味” makes the condition being considered explicit.
- 一个别的人：另一个人，Use “另一个人” when introducing the next individual in a sequence. “别的人” contrasts one person with other people and is not natural in this “one more person” introduction.
- 一个别的人：另一个人，Use “另一个人” when introducing another new individual in the sequence.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 问 -- “一个人问神农”：请教，emphasizes that the person is seeking knowledge from someone who understands plants.
- 坏处 -- “哪个植物对你的身体有坏处”：有害，forms the more specific phrase “对身体有害” and directly describes harm to the body.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the first question, add a small reaction from the knowledgeable character, such as “神农想了想，说：‘这要看你喜欢什么口味。’”
- Expressiveness: Make the food examples more vivid by adding a simple sensory detail, such as “苹果又甜又脆，西瓜凉凉的，很甜。”
- Expressiveness: At the final question, add the character’s concern to show why the question matters, such as “那个人有点担心，问：‘哪个植物对身体有坏处？’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly organized this scene as a series of questions and answers, which makes the information easy to follow.
- Your examples of fruit, vegetables, and tea are varied and fit the topic of plants and health well.

## Scene 3

> 神农说，有一天，我吃了一个黄色的花，名字是夹竹桃。我一吃夹竹桃，就觉得这种植物对我的脑有坏处。可是，一个灵芝和一个男人正好在我的旁边，我说，麻烦你把那个植物给我吃一下。我一吃这个灵芝就好了。所以，你得小心吃植物，因为有的植物对你的健康有坏处，特别是夹竹桃。因为夹竹桃有毒。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一个黄色的花：一朵黄色的花，Use the measure word “朵” for an individual flower; “个” is too general for this noun.
- 对我的脑有坏处：对我的大脑有坏处，“大脑” is the standard noun in this meaning; “脑” alone does not fit naturally after “对我的”.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 有坏处 -- “有的植物对你的健康有坏处”：有害，more concise and precise for something that can damage health.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 那个植物：灵芝，Use the specific noun because “那个植物” could refer either to the earlier flower or to the nearby lingzhi; this is an ambiguous-reference problem.
- Coherence (Reference): 夹竹桃有毒：它有毒，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the same plant was named in the immediately preceding phrase.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 特别是夹竹桃。因为：特别是夹竹桃，因为，The connector “因为” introduces the cause of why this plant needs special caution, so it should join directly to the previous statement rather than stand as a separate fragment.
- Expressiveness: After the moment of eating the flower, add a physical feeling to show the danger: “我肚子疼了起来，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Describe the flower before the narrator eats it: “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: Give the helpful man a small identifying detail: “这时，一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人走过来。”

### Encouragement
You used the useful pattern “一……就……” correctly to show an immediate result, and your final warning clearly explains that plants can affect health differently.
