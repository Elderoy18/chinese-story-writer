## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个人叫神农。一天，神农正在路上走，一看很多人正在吃一些植物。一些人一吃植物就生病了，神农想帮别人。所以想知道哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 already refers to a time period, so adding 的时候 is redundant.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

- Coherence (Conjunction): 一看很多人正在吃一些植物。一些人一吃植物就生病了：一看到很多人正在吃一些植物，就发现一些人一吃植物就生病了，the pattern 一V就… needs 就 to link the first event to its immediate result in a sequence.
- Expressiveness: After the illness is mentioned, add one visible symptom to make the danger more concrete, such as “有的人肚子疼得直不起腰来”.
- Expressiveness: At the point where he decides to investigate, add Shen Nong’s thought or speech, such as “神农想：我一定要弄清楚哪些植物能吃。”

### Encouragement
- You introduce 神农 clearly on his first appearance and use 正在 well to show an action in progress. The sentence 一些人一吃植物就生病了 also clearly shows the quick result of eating the plants.

## Scene 2

> 然后，神农开始尝很多植物，比方说他吃过生姜、芥兰和菠菜。一些植物吃真好，特别是黄瓜。但是，一些植物毒得很，特别是夹竹桃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一些植物吃真好：一些植物吃起来真好，Use the complement `起来` after an action verb to describe the quality experienced when doing that action; `真` then modifies the adjective `好`.
- 一些植物毒得很：一些植物有毒，`毒` cannot be intensified with `得很`; use `有毒` to say that a plant is poisonous.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 尝 -- “神农开始尝很多植物”：尝试，emphasizes that he is testing the plants to learn whether they are safe or useful.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the positive example, add a sensory detail such as `黄瓜吃起来又脆又清凉。` This makes the good taste more vivid.
- Expressiveness: At the poisonous-plant moment, show the physical result with `神农一吃夹竹桃，肚子就疼了起来。` This gives the danger a clear effect.
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought after he becomes ill, such as `神农想：“这种花为什么会让我肚子疼？”` This shows his curiosity and purpose.

### Encouragement
- You use `然后` to continue the earlier scene naturally, and `但是` clearly contrasts safe plants with dangerous ones. The examples of familiar plants also make the scene easy to picture.

## Scene 3

> 一个人对神农说，麻烦我们可以吃什么植物？神农说，这就要看你想不想生病了。以后，神农告诉别人一些植物能吃，一些植物不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 麻烦我们可以吃什么植物？：请问，我们可以吃什么植物？，麻烦 cannot by itself introduce a polite question. It is used for “trouble” or in a request structure such as 麻烦 + person + verb phrase; 请问 is the appropriate question-opening expression here.
- 这就要看你想不想生病了：能不能吃，这就要看是什么植物了，the 这就要看……了 pattern should give the factor that determines an uncertain result. Whether someone wants to become ill does not determine whether a plant is edible; the type of plant is the relevant factor.
- 以后：后来，when it begins a new sentence to mean “afterwards,” use 后来 rather than 以后.
- 别人：人们，别人 means “other people” in contrast with a particular person or group. Here 神农 is speaking generally to people, so 人们 is the fitting noun.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 说 -- “一个人对神农说”：问道，specifically shows that the person is asking a question rather than making a general statement.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 神农告诉人们：神农告诉了人们，this is a completed narrative action, so the verb takes 了 to mark completion.
- Expressiveness: At the person’s question, add a physical sign of worry: “一个人捂着肚子，着急地问：‘请问，我们可以吃什么植物？’”
- Expressiveness: After the general explanation about edible plants, add one concrete example: “神农说：‘像灵芝一样的植物是药，可以吃。’”
- Expressiveness: Add a warning to make the contrast more vivid: “有毒的植物不能吃，吃了可能会生病。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the contrast between plants that can be eaten and plants that cannot, which gives the reader an important piece of information. You also used a question-and-answer exchange to make the scene feel like a story rather than only a list of facts.
