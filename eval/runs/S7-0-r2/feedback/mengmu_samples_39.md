## Scene 1

> 古代的时候人觉得祭拜非常重要，孟子很小的时候，从早到晚一直祭拜，因为他妈妈告诉他祭拜很重要。虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜。他的同学们老是对他开玩笑了。孟子妈妈看了他的同学们对孟子不好的影响，所以孟子和孟子妈妈搬家了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候人：在古代，人们，古代 already expresses a time period, so 的时候 is redundant; 人们 is needed for people in general rather than the generic noun 人.
- 看了：看到了，看 means “look,” while 看到 means “see/perceive”; 了 follows the resultative form to show that the observation was completed.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不好 -- “他的同学们对孟子不好的影响”：不良的，specifies that the influence is harmful or negative rather than simply “not good.”

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子很小的时候：有一个小男孩叫孟子。他很小的时候，first mention should introduce the character with a name and a brief noun phrase before using the name alone.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子妈妈：孟子和他的妈妈，when the same person is mentioned again within one clause, use a pronoun rather than repeating the name.
- Coherence (Temporality): 同学们老是对他开玩笑了：同学们老是对他开玩笑，老是 describes a habitual action, so the zero particle is required rather than 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜：因为他每天只祭拜，所以他没有朋友，this is a cause-and-effect relationship, so it needs 因为……所以…… rather than 虽然……因为…….
- Expressiveness: At the worshipping sentence, add a visible action to show what he does: “他学着大人鞠躬，还一本正经地祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: When his mother notices the negative influence, add her feeling or thoughts: “孟子的妈妈很着急，心想：‘这样下去会影响孩子。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show a cause-and-effect chain: the child worships often, his classmates react, and his mother decides to move. You also used useful time phrases such as “从早到晚” and “每天” to show repeated actions.

## Scene 2

> 孟子才十一岁，成为一个哲学家。孟子不是祭拜，而是看政治文章，难怪他成为了哲学家。不过，看过很多政治文章对他害得，他有很多问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 看过很多政治文章对他害得：不过，他看过很多政治文章，还是有很多问题，害得 must be followed by the person affected and a harmful result; it cannot be used with 对他 in this structure.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 问题 -- “他有很多问题”：疑问，specifies that these are unanswered intellectual questions rather than general problems；困惑，emphasizes that the ideas in the articles made him feel puzzled.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子不是祭拜：他不是祭拜，use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the same character was named in the immediately preceding sentence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 孟子才十一岁，成为一个哲学家：孟子才十一岁，就成为了一个哲学家，才…就… marks that the result happened unusually early, and 了 marks the completed change of becoming a philosopher.
- Expressiveness: At the point where he reads, add a small detail about his focused action, such as “他每天坐在桌子前，认真地看政治文章。”
- Expressiveness: When he has questions, show his thoughts through a short quotation, such as “他想：‘为什么有些人不同意这个想法呢？’”
- Expressiveness: Add his feeling about reading and learning, such as “孟子觉得这些文章很有意思，越看越想知道更多。”

### Encouragement
- You used the contrast pattern “不是……而是……” correctly to show that Mengzi chose reading over worshipping. You also connected his reading to his later achievement with “难怪,” which gives the scene a clear idea.

## Scene 3

> 他一直问问题，但是不能解决那些问题。孟子长大，他开始能解决问题。他常常建议你不是学金融，而是学政治，因为如果你懂政治，你会成为高薪。后来孟子成为教授，他教学生很厉害。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 建议你不是学金融，而是学政治：建议学生不要学金融，而要学政治，After “建议,” use a person plus an action; for advice not to do one thing but to do another, use “不要……而要……” rather than “不是……而是……”.
- 会成为高薪：会拿到高薪，“高薪” is a noun meaning a high salary, so it cannot follow “成为”; use “拿到” to express receiving a high salary.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 厉害 -- “他教学生很厉害”：很有一套，emphasizes that he has effective teaching methods and real skill with students.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 孟子长大：孟子长大了，Use “了” after a completed change of state: growing up has been completed before he begins solving problems.
- Coherence (Temporality): 后来孟子成为教授：后来孟子成为了教授，Use “了” for the completed action of becoming a professor.
- Expressiveness: After “孟子长大了,” add his confidence or relief when he can solve problems, for example: “他觉得很有信心。”
- Expressiveness: At “他教学生很厉害,” add one concrete teaching action, for example: “他总是耐心地回答学生的问题。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech when he advises students, for example: “学习政治能帮助你们了解社会。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show Mengzi’s development from someone who cannot solve problems to a professor who teaches students. You also use time words such as “后来” to move the narrative forward.
