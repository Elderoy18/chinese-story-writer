- 住离墓地太近，害得孟子的心气：住在离墓地太近的地方，会影响孟子的心情，A location after 住 needs 在 and a complete place phrase. Also, 害得 must be followed by a person and a harmful result clause; it cannot take the noun phrase 孟子的心气 as its result.
- 老是读书：一直读书，老是 usually carries a negative, complaining tone. For a neutral routine of studying, 一直 is the fitting word.

### Coherence & Expressiveness

- Coherence (Reference): 孟母帮子思：她帮他，Use pronouns for close re-occurrence when the people have just been named and the referents are clear.
- Coherence (Reference): 子思帮孟母教她的儿子：他帮她教儿子，Use pronouns for recently mentioned characters, then use zero form for the repeated possessor within the same clause.
- Coherence (Temporality): 跟子思老师住：跟子思老师住了，This is a completed move-and-settle sequence. In a series of actions, only the last verb takes 了.
