## Scene 1

> 古代时候，人不知道他们能吃什么植物，常常一吃就死。但是不知道为什么，没有办法。所以有一天，神农出去试一试很多种植物，告诉人什么植物是安全什么是危险。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 人不知道他们能吃什么植物：人们不知道什么植物能吃，English uses “人们” for a concrete group of people; “人” is a generic noun. The pronoun is unnecessary because the people who can eat the plants are already understood.
- 告诉人：告诉人们，Use “人们” rather than generic “人” when referring to the group of people in the story.
- 什么植物是安全什么是危险：什么植物是安全的，什么植物是危险的，When an adjective such as “安全” or “危险” follows “是,” it needs “的”; the two parallel clauses also need punctuation.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：一个叫神农的男人，This is the character’s first mention, so introduce him with a full noun phrase rather than using only his name.
- Coherence (Temporality): 出去试一试很多种植物：出去试了很多种植物，Use “了” for the completed action of testing many kinds of plants; “试一试” usually describes a brief or tentative try.
- Expressiveness: After the deaths, add a physical detail to show the danger, such as “很多人吃了有毒的植物，肚子疼得厉害。”
- Expressiveness: When introducing the problem, add the people’s worried reaction, such as “人们很害怕，不知道该吃什么。”
- Expressiveness: Give the new character a purpose or thought before he acts, such as “神农想：我一定要找出安全的植物。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish the central problem before introducing the character who will act. Your use of “一吃就” effectively shows an immediate, serious result.

## Scene 2

> 发现有的植物对身体非常好，特别是茶，让肚子感觉又暖和又清楚。他告诉朋友：“你吃哪种植物，这就要看你的口味。比方说，你爱吃甜的，可能喜欢糖。”他最喜欢吃花，除了夹竹桃以外，什么都花想吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 清楚：舒服，清楚 means “clear” and cannot describe a warm physical feeling in the stomach; 舒服 describes bodily comfort.
- 你吃哪种植物，这就要看你的口味：你喜欢吃哪种植物，要看你的口味，the fixed pattern 这就要看……了 requires a question-form expression after 看; an ordinary noun phrase such as 你的口味 should follow 要看 instead.
- 什么都花想吃：什么花都想吃，什么 directly modifies the noun 花, and 都 follows the complete phrase 什么花.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好 -- “有的植物对身体非常好”：有益，forms 对身体非常有益 and states more precisely that the plants benefit the body；对健康非常有帮助，emphasizes a positive effect on health.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: When mentioning tea, add a sensory detail to show why it is special, such as “茶又香又暖，喝了以后肚子很舒服”.
- Expressiveness: After the discovery, add Shennong’s reaction or a short thought, such as “他高兴地想：原来有些植物真的可以帮助人！”
- Expressiveness: The dialogue could include the friend’s response to make the exchange livelier, such as “朋友问：那夹竹桃能吃吗？”

### Encouragement
- You used a clear example with 茶 and tried to explain its effect on the body.
- Your dialogue also shows that you are beginning to use a character’s speech to present ideas in the story.

## Scene 3

> 他发现这个口味，一百岁时候，不小心吃了毒的花。一吃就开始死。但是生病时候告诉人什么植物是药，什么植物是毒。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一百岁时候：一百岁的时候，The structure with a multi-syllable time expression before requires 的 before 时候.
- 毒的花：有毒的花，Use 有毒的 to describe a poisonous plant; 毒 cannot directly modify a noun in this way.
- 开始死：快要死了，Death is a change of state, so 开始 does not naturally describe it as an ongoing action; this correction expresses that he was close to dying.
- 生病时候：生病的时候，The structure with a verb phrase before requires 的 before 时候.
- 人：人们，Use 人们 for a concrete group of people in the story; 人 is a generic noun.
- 是毒：有毒，Use 有毒 to say that a plant is poisonous; 是毒 does not fit this predicate meaning.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 这个口味：自己喜欢吃花，The demonstrative phrase has no clear antecedent in the nearby context. Use a clear phrase that refers to the earlier information about his preference.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉：告诉了，Use 了 for this completed narrative action; this use of 告诉 does not introduce direct speech.
- Expressiveness: After the poisonous flower is mentioned, add a visual detail such as 黄色的、像星星一样的花 to make the danger more vivid.
- Expressiveness: After the immediate consequence, describe his physical feeling with 肚子疼了起来，难受得不得了.
- Expressiveness: At the moment he becomes very ill, add an inner thought or quoted question, such as 他想：“我会死吗？”

### Encouragement
- You used the immediate-result pattern with 一……就……, which clearly shows that the dangerous effect happened very quickly.
- You also tried to explain what he learned about medicinal and poisonous plants, keeping the scene focused on his discovery.
