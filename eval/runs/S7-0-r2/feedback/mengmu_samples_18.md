## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个男孩名字叫孟子。孟子和孟子的妈妈两个人住在很穷的农村。他们住的地方旁边是个墓地，墓地老是有很多人祭拜。因为人们在祭拜，他们一直在哭。孟子和孟子的妈妈住在这里才一个星期就觉得受不了了，而且害得孟子的学习。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 already expresses a time period, so adding 的时候 is redundant.
- 有一个男孩名字叫孟子：有一个男孩叫孟子，when 名字叫 introduces a person’s name, it needs a pause after the noun or a possessive structure; here the simpler pattern is noun + 叫 + name.
- 孟子和孟子的妈妈两个人：孟子和他的妈妈两个人，after mentioning 孟子, use 他的 to show that the mother is Mengzi’s mother without repeating the name.
- 住在很穷的农村：住在一个贫穷的农村地区，住在 needs a clear place phrase, and 农村地区 is a more natural place noun in this context.
- 老是：常常，老是 usually carries a negative or complaining tone; the repeated visits to the cemetery are described neutrally here.
- 害得孟子的学习：影响了孟子的学习，害得 must be followed by the person affected and a harmful result clause; 影响了孟子的学习 directly expresses harm to his studies.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 住在这里才一个星期：在这里才住了一个星期，use 了 after 住 to mark that this stay of one week has been completed before the characters reach the point of feeling unable to endure it.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为人们在祭拜，他们一直在哭：因为人们在祭拜，所以他们一直在哭，because this sentence presents crying as the result of the worship, use the paired cause-and-effect connector 因为……所以…….
- Expressiveness: After “他们一直在哭,” add a sound detail to make the cemetery feel more vivid: “哭声听起来很伤心。”
- Expressiveness: At the final sentence, add Mengzi’s mother’s worried thought: “孟子的妈妈心想：‘住在这里会影响孩子学习。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduce Mengzi, his mother, and their home near the cemetery, so the setting is easy to understand. You also use ongoing-action forms such as “在祭拜” and “一直在哭” well to show what is happening at the cemetery.

## Scene 2

> 孟子的妈妈觉得孟子取得良好的教育是很重要的，所以孟子的妈妈认为这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好，就打算跟孟子搬家。孟子的妈妈带孟子不是搬到城市，而是搬到了孟子老师的家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 取得良好的教育：接受良好的教育，取得 normally collocates with achievements or results, while 接受 is the appropriate verb for receiving an education.
- 跟孟子搬家：带着孟子搬家，跟孟子搬家 presents the mother and child as simply moving together; 带着孟子搬家 clearly shows that the mother is taking her child with her.
- 不是搬到城市，而是搬到了孟子老师的家：搬到了孟子老师的家，the construction 不是……而是…… is used to correct a conventional or previously stated belief; here no such contrast has been established, so the destination can be stated directly.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 地方 -- “这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好”：环境，specifies that the concern is the child’s surrounding living and learning setting.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子的妈妈认为：她认为，after the mother has just been named and no other possible referent appears, use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子的妈妈带孟子：她带孟子，at the start of the next sentence, the mother is still the clear, recently mentioned subject, so a pronoun avoids unnecessary repetition of the name.
- Expressiveness: After the mother decides to move, add her feeling to show why education matters so much to her, for example: “孟母很着急。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of inner speech after her judgment of the place, for example: “这个环境不适合孩子学习。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect the mother’s educational goal with her decision to move, which gives the scene a logical direction.
- You also use a specific destination, helping the reader understand where the characters go next.

## Scene 3

> 孟子跟他的老师从早到晚学习，难怪他的成绩很快就进步了很多。孟子学习的时候，孟子的妈妈不是待在家里就是编织衣服赚钱。以后孟子成为了中国有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 以后孟子成为了中国有名的哲学家：后来孟子成为了中国有名的哲学家，Sentence-initial “以后” does not mean “afterwards” in a narrative; use “后来” to introduce a later event.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 成绩 -- “他的成绩很快就进步了很多”：学习成绩，specifies that the improvement is in school performance rather than another kind of result.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子学习的时候，孟子的妈妈不是待在家里就是编织衣服赚钱：他学习的时候，他的妈妈不是待在家里就是编织衣服赚钱，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence: “孟子” was the subject of the preceding sentence, and no other male character competes as the referent.
- Expressiveness: After “从早到晚学习”, add a small detail about the long study day, such as “他常常读书读到天黑”。
- Expressiveness: At “孟子的妈妈不是待在家里就是编织衣服赚钱”, add her feeling about her son’s learning, such as “她看到孟子认真学习，心里很高兴”。
- Expressiveness: Before the final sentence, add a short thought or comment that links his childhood effort to his future, such as “孟子的妈妈相信，只要他继续努力，一定会有出息”。

### Encouragement
You used “从早到晚” well to show that Mengzi studied consistently. The sentence with “难怪” also clearly connects his hard work with his improved school performance.
