## Scene 1

> 很久以前，古代的人很不知道什么植物能吃，也什么植物不能吃。所以很多人常常吃不安全的植物，吃不安全的植物对你的身体有坏处，很麻烦的情况。但是一个人，神农，要解决这个问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很不知道：根本不知道，The degree adverb 很 does not naturally modify the negative verb phrase 不知道; 根本 emphasizes that they had no knowledge at all.
- 不安全的植物：有毒的植物，In this context, the plants make people ill because they contain poison; 有毒的植物 names this specific danger, while 不安全 describes something generally unsafe.
- 很麻烦的情况：是很麻烦的事，A noun phrase such as 很麻烦的情况 cannot serve as the predicate here by itself; add 是 and use 事 to describe the whole situation.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 有坏处 -- “对你的身体有坏处”：会伤害身体，states the physical harm more directly and specifically in this context.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 你的身体：他们的身体，Use a pronoun that refers back to the recently mentioned group, 很多人, rather than suddenly addressing the reader.
- Coherence (Reference): 但是一个人，神农：但是，有一个叫神农的人，Follow the first-mention rule by introducing the new character with a full noun phrase that clearly gives his name.
- Expressiveness: After “很多人常常吃有毒的植物,” add the result to make the danger vivid, for example: “他们常常肚子疼，还会生病。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where 神农 appears, add his reason or inner thought, for example: “神农想：‘我一定要找到能吃的植物。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish the problem at the beginning: people do not know which plants are safe, and this causes harm. Your use of 所以 also shows that you are connecting cause and result in the story.

## Scene 2

> 他又聪明又努力，旅行很远找到很多植物。比方说，灵芝和夹竹桃。他一吃各种各样的植物就知道能不能吃这些植物。然后，神农写他的主意。他喜欢植物的味道，特别是花。神农遇到了一个黄色的花，一吃就觉得不舒服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 旅行很远：到很远的地方旅行，use a place phrase after 到; 很远 describes the place or distance, not the verb 旅行 directly.
- 写他的主意：把自己的想法写下来，use 写下/写下来 when recording ideas; 自己的 clearly shows that the ideas belong to the subject.
- 遇到了：看到了，遇到 is normally used for encountering a person, event, or difficulty; 看到了 is appropriate for noticing a flower.
- 一个黄色的花：一朵黄色的花，朵 is the matching measure word for flowers.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 然后，神农写他的主意：然后，他把自己的想法写下来，after 神农 has just been referred to as 他 and no other character has appeared, use the pronoun rule rather than repeating the name.
- Coherence (Temporality): 找到很多植物：找到了很多植物，use 了 on the last verb of this completed series of actions to show that finding the plants was completed.
- Expressiveness: After the flower appears, add a visual detail to make it memorable: “那朵花又黄又大，像天上的星星。”
- Expressiveness: At the moment he feels ill, add a physical feeling or thought: “他的肚子突然疼了起来，心里很害怕。”
- Expressiveness: You could add a short thought after he tastes the flower: “这种花这么漂亮，应该可以吃吧？”

### Encouragement

- Naming specific plants such as 灵芝 and 夹竹桃 makes the scene more concrete.

## Scene 3

> 发现了夹竹桃有毒。别人看到他，很快给他吃一个灵芝。神农一吃就觉得比较好。然后，他继续写他发现的信息。会告诉他的朋友哪一个植物能不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别人：有人，别人 means “other people” and requires a contrast with a stated person or group; use 有人 for an unspecified person who sees him.
- 写他发现的信息：记下他发现的信息，写 does not naturally mean recording discovered information in this context; 记下 emphasizes writing information down so it is kept.
- 哪一个植物能不能吃：哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃，an information report should state the two categories clearly; combining 哪一个 with 能不能 creates an unnatural indirect question here.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好 -- “神农一吃就觉得比较好”：舒服一些，specifies that his physical discomfort became less severe.
- 很快 -- “有人看到他，很快给他吃一个灵芝”：立刻，emphasizes that the person acted immediately when seeing him.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 给他吃一个灵芝：给他吃了一个灵芝，use 了 after the final verb in this completed action sequence to show that the person gave him the medicine.
- Expressiveness: After he eats the medicine, add a physical feeling to show his recovery, such as “神农的肚子慢慢不疼了。”
- Expressiveness: Describe the helpful stranger briefly at the moment he appears, such as “这时，一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人走过来。”
- Expressiveness: Turn the final information into direct speech to make the scene livelier, such as “神农对朋友说：‘灵芝可以吃，夹竹桃有毒，不能吃。’”

### Encouragement
- You used the pattern “一吃就” well to show that the medicine worked immediately. Your scene also has a clear sequence: someone helps Shen Nong, he feels better, and then he records what he learned.
