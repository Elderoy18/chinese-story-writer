## Scene 1

> 在古代，很多医生不知道怎么用药。所以他们找到了很多植物对药。神农先看到很多病人，他们吃很多植物对药。但是我觉得他们的吃的植物，让他们病人。神农觉得这个植物可能特别是毒。所以他想找一个植物对药，他们可以吃对药。正好，他找到了一把花，他想把花放在茶里，对药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很多植物对药：很多对身体有好处的植物，the preposition “对” needs a target such as “身体” and cannot be placed after “植物” to mean that plants are medicine or beneficial.
- 他们的吃的植物：他们吃的植物，when a verb phrase modifies a noun, the verb comes before “的”; “他们吃的” describes the plants.
- 让他们病人：害得他们生病了，“病人” is a noun meaning a sick person, not a verb. Use “害得 + person + harmful result” to show that the plants caused the patients to become ill.
- 特别是毒：特别有毒，“毒” cannot be modified by “特别” as an adjective in this meaning. “有毒” means poisonous.
- 一个植物：一种植物，“植物” takes the matching measure word “种” when referring to one type of plant.
- 对药，他们可以吃对药：能做药、可以吃，“对” cannot mean “serve as medicine.” Use “做药” for a plant that can be used as medicine; the revised phrase also makes the two qualities of the plant parallel.
- 对药：做药，“做药” means to use something as medicine, while “对药” is not a correct phrase here.

- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农先看到很多病人：一个叫神农的男人先看到了很多病人，first mention rule: introduce a new character with a name and a brief noun phrase rather than assuming the reader already knows who he is.
- Coherence (Reference): 这个植物：有些植物，reference rule: “这个” needs one specific, previously identified plant. The earlier sentence mentions many plants, so “有些植物” matches that plural reference.
- Coherence (Temporality): 先看到：先看到了，了 marks the completed action of seeing in this past narrative event.
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃很多植物药：吃了很多植物药，了 marks the completed action of taking the herbal medicines before the later result of becoming ill.
- Expressiveness: When the flowers appear, add one visual detail to make the setting easier to imagine, for example: “那儿开着黄色的花，像星星一样，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: After 神农 realizes that some plants may be poisonous, add his worried thought, for example: “他想：这些植物真的能做药吗？”
- Expressiveness: At the final sentence, explain why he wants to put the flowers in tea, for example: “他想试一试这种花能不能做药。”

### Encouragement
- You use a clear sequence of events, moving from the doctors and patients to 神农’s plan to find a useful plant.
- You also try to show cause and effect with “所以” and describe 神农’s thinking, which are both useful storytelling skills.

## Scene 2

> 他不知道如果这把花对你的身体好，这就要看如果这把花毒不毒了。神农想找别的植物，但是这很麻烦。正好，他找到了别的花。他一吃花，很快就生病了。他的肚子疼死了。神农不知道他吃不吃灵芝或者夹竹桃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他不知道如果这把花对你的身体好：他不知道这把花对他的身体好不好，An embedded yes/no question after “不知道” uses an A-not-A form, not “如果”; the possessor should also match 神农 rather than shifting to “你”.
- 这就要看如果这把花毒不毒了：这就要看这把花毒不毒了，In the pattern “这就要看……了”, the part after “看” must be a question form; “毒不毒” already provides that question, so “如果” is not used.
- 他吃不吃灵芝或者夹竹桃：自己吃的是灵芝还是夹竹桃，When choosing between two possibilities, use “是……还是……” rather than combining an A-not-A question with “或者”.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很麻烦 -- “但是这很麻烦”：很不容易，states more specifically that finding other plants requires considerable effort；费劲，emphasizes the effort involved in the search.
- 很快 -- “很快就生病了”：没过多久，shows that only a short time passed before he became ill.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 但是这很麻烦：但是找别的植物很麻烦，The reference word “这” is too vague here; repeat the action or noun phrase when it is the topic being evaluated.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他一吃花，很快就生病了：他一吃花，就很快生病了，The “一……就……” pattern needs “就” to link the first action to its immediate result.
- Expressiveness: At “正好，他找到了别的花”, add a visual detail that makes the flower memorable, such as “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花。”
- Expressiveness: After “他的肚子疼死了”, show his physical reaction more vividly, such as “他躺在地上，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Add an internal thought after he becomes ill to show fear, such as “他想：我会死吗？”

### Encouragement
- You correctly used “找到了” to show that the search was completed, and the strong phrase “肚子疼死了” clearly conveys how badly 神农 feels.
- You also introduced the specific plant names “灵芝” and “夹竹桃”, which helps make the scene more concrete.

## Scene 3

> 别的人给了神农一个灵芝。这个植物可以让神农更好。比方说，他会觉得更健康。吃灵芝以后，他可以再走。现在他觉得更好。最后，神农找到了很多植物，他可以用对药。这些植物让神农很高兴。神农想跟很多人分享他的新药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别的人给了：有一个人给了，别的人 is used to contrast other people with a person already under discussion; this helper is newly introduced, so use 一个 to introduce an unspecified person.
- 用对药：用来做药，use 用来 + verb phrase to state a purpose; 对药 does not mean “make medicine.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 再走 -- “吃灵芝以后，他可以再走”：继续赶路，makes clear that he can continue his journey after recovering.
- 新药 -- “神农想跟很多人分享他的新药”：新发现的药，specifies that the medicine is newly discovered through his search.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他可以用对药：可以用来做药，after 神农 has appeared earlier in the same sentence, use zero form rather than repeating a pronoun.
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃灵芝以后：吃了灵芝以后，use 了 for the completed action that happens before the later action of continuing the journey.
- Expressiveness: After “现在他觉得更好,” add a physical detail to show his recovery, such as “他的肚子不那么疼了。”
- Expressiveness: When the helper appears, add one simple identifying detail, such as “一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人给了神农一个灵芝。”
- Expressiveness: At “分享他的新药,” add a short line of speech that shows what Shen Nong wants to tell people: “这种植物叫灵芝，可以做药。”

### Encouragement
- You use time words such as “吃灵芝以后,” “现在,” and “最后” to show the order of events clearly. You also show Shen Nong’s improving condition and his wish to help others.
