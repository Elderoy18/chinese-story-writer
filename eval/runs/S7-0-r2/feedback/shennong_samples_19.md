## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男人觉得植物很有意思。他叫神农。有的植物吃了以后他的肚子疼死了，可是有的吃了以后很舒服。他找很多植物。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，The two time expressions are redundant: 古代 already names a historical time period, so it should not be followed by 的时候.
- 他找很多植物：他找到了很多植物，找 means “to look for,” while 找到 means “to find successfully.” The completed discovery in this narrative also needs 了.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: At the point where his stomach hurts, add a physical reaction to show how severe it is, such as “他在地上躺着，难受得不得了”.
- Expressiveness: After “肚子疼死了,” add a short thought or question to reveal his fear, such as “我会死吗？”.

### Encouragement

## Scene 2

> 凉山很大的山，这也找植物。一天，他找到黄色的花。他听说你跟黄色的花做茶很有用。比方说，你的头发疼死，这冰茶是药。他一喝茶就很不舒服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 凉山很大的山，这也找植物：凉山是一座很大的山，神农也在这里找植物，A sentence identifying what something is needs 是, and a mountain takes the measure word 座; use 在这里 for a location where an action happens.
- 你跟黄色的花做茶：用黄色的花做的茶，Use 用 to introduce the material used to make something; 跟 does not mark the material in this phrase, and 你 incorrectly introduces an unexplained listener.
- 你的头发疼死：头疼得要死，Use 头 for a headache, not 头发; a degree complement such as 要死 requires 得 after the verb.
- 这冰茶：这杯茶，A demonstrative plus a countable noun needs a measure word: 这 + 杯 + 茶.

- Coherence (Temporality): 找到：找到了，Use 了 after the resultative verb 找到 to show that the finding was completed in this past event.

- Expressiveness: After drinking the tea, show his physical reaction more clearly, for example: “他一喝茶，肚子就疼了起来。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought to show his worry at that moment, for example: “他想：我是不是生病了？”

### Encouragement

## Scene 3

> 糟糕，这黄色的花是夹竹桃，特别是毒。正好在这儿，灵芝药的夹竹桃他吃完了，也写一个课文的植物。谢谢神农。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 这黄色的花：这朵黄色的花，The demonstrative 这 needs a measure word before a countable noun such as 花.
- 特别是毒：特别有毒，Use 有毒 to describe a poisonous plant; 毒 alone is a noun and cannot serve as the predicate here.
- 灵芝药的夹竹桃：灵芝这种药，灵芝 and 夹竹桃 are different plants, so 灵芝 cannot modify 夹竹桃 with 的 in this phrase.
- 一个课文的植物：这种植物的用处，课文 is a written text and does not describe a plant with 的; when recording a plant, state the information being recorded, such as its use.

### Vocabulary Suggestions

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 也写：也写下了，The action of recording is completed, so the final verb in this series needs 了; 写下 also specifically means to record information.
- Expressiveness: After identifying the flower as poisonous, add the physical result to make the danger vivid: “他一吃，肚子就疼了起来。”

### Encouragement
