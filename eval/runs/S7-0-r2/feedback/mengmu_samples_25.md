## Scene 1

> 这是古代的故事。孟子小的时候，他的爸爸很早就去世了。从他的家到墓地很近，所以他每天老是从早到晚去墓地跟朋友们玩玩儿。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 从他的家到墓地很近：他的家离墓地很近，Use 离 to state that one place is close to another; 从……到…… normally introduces a route or distance rather than directly modifying 近.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 小 -- “孟子小的时候”：年幼，more precisely describes being at a very young age.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the visit to the cemetery, add what Mengzi notices there, such as “他每天都看到有人来墓地祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: Add Mengzi’s reaction to make his motivation clearer, such as “孟子觉得很有趣，就学着他们祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: Add a small action detail to make the scene livelier, such as “他一边笑，一边跟朋友们做祭拜的动作。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish Mengzi’s childhood situation and give a reason why he often goes to the cemetery. The routine expression also makes the scene feel like part of his daily life.

## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈觉得在墓地玩儿，害得，所以他们搬家。搬家对孟子有好的影响，他才五岁，可是每天不是写字就是帮妈妈的工作。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 害得：会带来麻烦，害得 must be followed by the person affected and a harmful result; it cannot stand alone before a cause-and-effect clause.
- 帮妈妈的工作：帮妈妈做事，帮 needs a person plus a verb phrase here; 妈妈的工作 is a noun phrase, so it cannot directly complete this meaning.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好的 -- “搬家对孟子有好的影响”：积极的，more specifically describes an influence that supports his development.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们搬家：他们搬家了，use 了 for the completed action of moving house.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他才五岁，可是每天不是写字就是帮妈妈的工作：他才五岁，就每天不是写字就是帮妈妈做事，use 才……就…… to show that he began doing these things at an unexpectedly young age; 可是 does not express this relationship.
- Expressiveness: After the mother’s thought, add her worry to show why the move matters: “孟子的妈妈很担心他的学习。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of quoted speech when she decides to move: “我们不能再住在这儿了。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the mother’s decision and connect moving house with its effect on Mengzi. You also used the pattern “不是……就是……” well to describe his daily activities.
