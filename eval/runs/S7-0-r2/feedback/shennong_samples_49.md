## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有很多人不知道什么植物能吃，什么植物不能吃。有很多人吃植物以后就吃坏肚子了，因为他们吃了毒。那个时候，有一个老人叫神农，他什么植物都觉得好吃，不怕生病，不怕毒。他希望别人也可以吃好吃的植物。可是他知道，如果他们还怕吃毒，不会吃他喜欢的植物。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 is already a time expression, so adding 的时候 is redundant.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 毒 -- “因为他们吃了毒”：有毒的植物，makes clear that the harmful thing they ate was a plant, matching the surrounding context.
- 好吃 -- “也可以吃好吃的植物”：味道鲜美，describes the plants’ pleasant taste more specifically.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After “有一个老人叫神农,” add a small detail about his actions to make him more vivid, such as “他走过很多山，常常观察路边的植物。”
- Expressiveness: When describing people becoming ill, add a physical detail to show the consequence, such as “他们肚子疼得很厉害，只能躺在床上休息。”
- Expressiveness: After “他希望别人也可以吃好吃的植物,” add Shennong’s thought or speech, such as “他说：‘我要找出哪些植物能吃，哪些不能吃。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly introduce Shennong and keep the references to him and to other people easy to follow. You also correctly use completed-action forms in “吃坏肚子了” and “吃了毒” to describe what happened.

## Scene 2

> 所以，他上山下山找植物，吃了很多样子的植物。他是第一人，找到了茶。他把所有的植物写下来了。他一直都吃了，他连毒都能吃。可是他还没吃了一个叫夹竹桃的植物。他找到了那个像星星的植物，觉得很漂亮，马上就吃下了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他是第一人：他是第一个人，Ordinal expressions use 第一个人 rather than 第一人 in this meaning.
- 连毒都能吃：连有毒的植物都能吃，毒 is a poison/substance; when referring to plants that are poisonous, use 有毒的植物.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 他一直都吃了：他一直都吃，Use the zero particle with an ongoing or habitual action; 了 marks a completed action and does not fit 一直 here.
- Coherence (Temporality): 还没吃了一个叫夹竹桃的植物：还没吃过一种叫夹竹桃的植物，For a negative past experience, use 没 + verb + 过, not 没 + verb + 了; 一种 is the suitable measure word for a kind of plant.
- Expressiveness: After finding the flower, add a visual detail such as “黄色的花像小星星一样，又大又漂亮。” This helps the reader picture why it catches his attention.
- Expressiveness: Add Shennong’s immediate thought before he eats it, for example: “这种花这么漂亮，应该很好吃吧！” This shows his confidence and makes the action more lively.
- Expressiveness: Describe the action with a feeling word, such as “他好奇地摘下一朵花，马上吃了下去。” This explains his motivation rather than only listing the event.

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the sequence of Shennong’s exploration: he travels, finds tea, records plants, and then discovers a new flower. The use of “找到了” and “吃下了” also effectively shows completed discoveries and actions.

## Scene 3

> 可是夹竹桃的毒很重，让他肚子很痛。他在地上躺着，觉得他快会死了。可是正好有别人找到他了，给了他一个像伞的植物，叫灵芝。他吃灵芝以后就觉得很好，不再生病了。他测量起来了，像星星的植物是毒，像伞的植物是药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 快会死了：快要死了，use the imminent marker before a verb when something is about to happen; the modal verb does not express this immediate danger naturally here.
- 别人：一个人，this word means “other people” in contrast with a stated person, so it does not introduce an unspecified new helper.
- 像伞的植物：像伞一样的植物，comparisons of resemblance normally use the pattern with the comparison marker.
- 像星星的植物：像星星一样的植物，use the comparison marker to show that the flowers resemble stars.
- 是毒：有毒，use this adjective form to describe a plant as poisonous; the original noun form does not fit after the subject here.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很痛 -- “让他肚子很痛”：疼得厉害，makes the physical pain more vivid and specific.
- 很好 -- “就觉得很好”：好多了，shows that his condition has improved compared with his earlier stomach pain.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他（在“觉得他快会死了”中）：自己，when the person who is thinking and the person who may die are the same, use the reflexive form to make the reference clear.
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃灵芝以后：吃了灵芝以后，use 了 for the completed action of eating before the later event introduced by 以后.
- Expressiveness: After the stomach pain, add a stronger bodily feeling, such as “他在地上躺着，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: At the moment he fears death, add his inner thought or a short quotation, such as “他想：‘我会死吗？’”
- Expressiveness: When the helper arrives, add one visual detail, such as “这时，一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人赶快跑过来。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the cause and result of eating the poisonous plant, and you also include a helpful new character at an exciting moment.
- Your use of actions such as “躺着” and “吃灵芝以后” gives the scene a clear sequence.
