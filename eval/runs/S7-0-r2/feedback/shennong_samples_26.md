## Scene 1

> 很久以前，有一个老男人叫神农。神农在古代每一天看到了别的小镇人生病还是不舒服，所以他决定开始找新的药。他下一天开始爬当地的山脉，找了各种各样的植物。神农试一试每个植物，知道有什么好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别的小镇人：小镇里的人，This noun phrase needs the location marker 里 before 人; 别的 is used to contrast with a previously specified town, but no such town has been introduced here.
- 生病还是不舒服：生病或者身体不舒服，In a statement listing two possible conditions, use 或者 rather than the question-choice connector 还是.
- 下一天：第二天，Use 第二天 to mean “the following day” in a narrative.
- 爬当地的山脉：爬附近的山，爬 normally takes an individual mountain or hills as its object; 山脉 is a mountain range and would more naturally go with 翻越.
- 找了各种各样的植物：找到了各种各样的植物，找 means “look for,” while 找到 means “find.” The sentence says that the plants were found, so it needs 找到.
- 每个植物：每种植物，Use 种, not the general measure word 个, when counting different types of plants.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不舒服 -- “生病还是不舒服”：身体不适，This specifies physical discomfort more clearly in a scene about illness.
- 好处 -- “知道有什么好处”：药用价值，This identifies the benefits more exactly as the plants’ medicinal uses.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农在古代每一天：他在古代每一天，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence: 神农 was named in the immediately preceding sentence, and no other male character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农试一试每个植物：他试一试每个植物，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence: the previous sentence already continues with 神农 as the only character.
- Coherence (Temporality): 每一天看到了：每天看到，Use the zero particle for a routine or habitual action; 每天 describes something that happens regularly, not one completed act.
- Coherence (Temporality): 试一试：试了试，Use 了 for the completed testing action in this past narrative; 知道 is a mental-state verb and does not need 了.
- Expressiveness: When describing the sick people, add one visible detail to make their condition clearer, such as: “有的人捂着肚子，脸色很难看。”
- Expressiveness: After the decision, add Shennong’s purpose or inner thought, such as: “神农想：‘我一定要找到能治病的植物。’”

### Encouragement
- You introduced the main character clearly at the beginning with both his age and name.
- You also used a clear cause-and-effect connection with 所以 to show why Shennong decided to search for medicine.

## Scene 2

> 比方说，他一次试一试有些植物的叶，吃得又新鲜又甜。煮这些叶以后，他一喝就知道他发现新的茶叶。虽然他找到了很多的新的植物，但是他的旅程有的时候特别是危险。比方说，他一吃灵芝就开始生病。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他一次试一试有些植物的叶：有一次，他尝了一些植物的叶子，This is an action that was carried out in the narrative, so verb reduplication is not appropriate; use the completed action form with 尝了. The noun 叶子 is also the usual form for individual leaves.
- 吃得又新鲜又甜：觉得这些叶子又新鲜又甜，A 得-complement describes the degree or result of the action before it. Here, 新鲜 and 甜 describe the leaves, so use 觉得 to introduce the character’s evaluation.
- 特别是危险：特别危险，特别是 introduces a specific example or item; it cannot directly intensify an adjective. Use 特别 directly before 危险.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 新的 -- “他找到了很多的新的植物”：未尝过的，specifies that these are plants he has not tasted before and fits his exploration of plants.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 煮这些叶以后：煮了这些叶以后，Use 了 for the completed action that happens before the later action marked by 以后.
- Coherence (Temporality): 发现新的茶叶：发现了新的茶叶，Use 了 because this discovery is presented as a completed event after he drinks the tea.
- Expressiveness: At the tea-discovery moment, add a sensory detail about the drink. For example: “他发现，茶水很好喝，还能做药。”
- Expressiveness: When he becomes ill, add a physical feeling to show the effect more vividly. For example: “他的肚子疼了起来，难受得不得了。”

### Encouragement
- You use useful story structures such as “虽然……但是……” and “一……就……,” which help show contrast and quick cause-and-effect events. The tea discovery also gives your scene a clear focus.

## Scene 3

> 一个爬山的男人正好看到神农的麻烦，然后给了他一些药。神农最终他的小镇回到，跟别的人分享他的发现。你认为神农的故事真的吗？这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别的人：人们，别的人 distinguishes people from a particular person already under discussion; here the meaning is people in general, so 人们 fits.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 药 -- “给了他一些药”：解毒药，specifies that the medicine counters the harmful effect of the plant.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农的麻烦：正躺在地上、难受得不得了的神农，麻烦 is too vague to show what the man saw; use a specific noun phrase to identify Shen Nong’s condition.
- Coherence (Reference): 他的发现：自己的发现，when the possessor is the same person as the subject, 自己的 makes that relationship explicit.
- Coherence (Temporality): 神农最终他的小镇回到：神农最终回到了自己的小镇，回到 is a completed return here, so 了 follows the resultative complement 到; the destination also follows 回到.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 神农的故事真的吗？：你认为神农的故事是真的吗？，after 认为, use a complete clause; 是……的吗 forms a yes-or-no question about whether the story is true.
- Expressiveness: At the moment the man finds Shen Nong, add a visual detail such as 穿着蓝色衣服的男人 to make the new character easier to imagine.
- Expressiveness: Show Shen Nong’s physical feeling before the medicine, for example 肚子疼得厉害 or 难受得不得了.
- Expressiveness: Add a short reaction after Shen Nong returns, such as 高兴地跟人们分享自己的发现, to show his feeling about helping others.

### Encouragement
- You used 然后 effectively to show the order of the man seeing Shen Nong and giving him medicine.
- Your final question directly involves the reader and gives the scene an engaging ending.
