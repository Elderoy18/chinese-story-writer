## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，孟子和孟母住了在靠墓地的家。孩子才五岁就每天在墓地祭拜了。在那个城市，孟母从早到晚都找工作，可是找不到，害得孟母没有钱。难怪她跟孩子搬家，原来买不了房。他们搬了到别一个城市找工作。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，this expression redundantly combines two time expressions; use the shorter time phrase.
- 跟孩子搬家：带着孩子搬家，when a mother moves together with her child, use this phrase to show that she takes the child with her.
- 别一个城市：另一个城市，use this form for one additional or different city; the original phrase is not a standard noun phrase.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 找不到 -- “可是找不到”：始终找不到，emphasizes that the job search remains unsuccessful over a period of time.
- 买不了房 -- “原来买不了房”：无力买房，more precisely shows that the reason is a lack of financial ability.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孩子才五岁：他才五岁，after a close mention of 孟子, use a pronoun for the same character.
- Coherence (Reference): 在那个城市：在那里，the city has not been introduced before, so the original noun phrase has an unclear referent; use a place reference to the previously mentioned home area.
- Coherence (Reference): 害得孟母没有钱：害得她没有钱，after the recent mention of 孟母, use a pronoun rather than repeating the same name.
- Coherence (Temporality): 住了在：住在，use the zero particle for the continuing state of living somewhere; 了 is not used here, and the location follows the verb with 在.
- Coherence (Temporality): 每天在墓地祭拜了：每天在墓地祭拜，use the zero particle for a daily routine; 了 would incorrectly present the routine as a completed action.
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬了到：搬到了，place 了 after the resultative element in a completed movement: the pattern is verb plus resultative plus 了.
- Expressiveness: At the cemetery scene, add what 孟子 sees to make the setting clearer, such as: “他每天看到很多人来墓地祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: Show why the child performs this action with a feeling or thought, such as: “他觉得很有趣，就学着别人祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: At the job-search sentence, add 孟母’s emotion or inner thought, such as: “孟母很着急，心想：‘这样下去可不行。’”

### Encouragement
- You used the pattern “才五岁就” well to show that the child was very young when he began the action.
- Your scene also includes clear time and place details, such as “从早到晚” and “在墓地,” which help the reader follow the events.

## Scene 2

> 这个城市老是很吵，不是人吵架，就是都叫，害得孟子一直不能睡觉。在那儿，孟母也找不到工作。孟子和妈妈要又一个次搬了家。在第三个城市，孟母找到工作，在商店做衣服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不是人吵架，就是都叫：人们不是在吵架，就是在大叫，Use 人们 for a concrete group of people; placing the shared subject before the paired structure makes both alternatives clear.
- 又一个次搬：再搬一次，For a repeat that has not happened yet, use 再; the correct order is 再 + verb + number + measure word.
- 找到工作：找到了工作，找到 means “find,” and this is a completed event, so it needs the perfective marker 了.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 吵 -- “这个城市老是很吵”：嘈杂，describes a place filled with many mixed, unpleasant sounds and fits a noisy city scene.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬了家：搬家，After the modal verb 要, the future action takes the zero particle; 了 would wrongly present the move as already completed.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 孟母也找不到工作。孟子和妈妈要再搬一次家。：孟母也找不到工作，所以孟子和妈妈要再搬一次家。，Use 所以 to show the cause-and-effect relation: not finding work leads to the decision to move again.
- Expressiveness: After “这个城市老是很吵,” add a sound detail, such as “街上从早到晚都是吵闹声。”
- Expressiveness: At “孟子一直不能睡觉,” add his feeling or a short thought, such as “孟子很累，心想：‘这里太吵了！’”
- Expressiveness: At “孟母找到工作,” show her reaction, such as “孟母终于松了一口气。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show how the noisy environment affects Mengzi by using the effective result structure “害得孟子一直不能睡觉.”
- You also use place phrases well to move the story from one city to the next.

## Scene 3

> 孟子在那个商店也找了到工作，可是不是做衣服的工作，而是喜欢的工作。孟子学习馆学了十五年，他的学习和他的旅行经验很重要，对他很多好处。他是大人的时候，他成为很有名的哲人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 找了到：找到了，A result complement follows the verb before the completion marker: use “找到了” for a completed action of finding a job.
- 孟子学习馆学了十五年：孟子在学校学习了十五年，Use “在” before a place where an action happens. “学习馆” is not a natural place word in this sentence; “学校” fits the meaning of studying for many years.
- 对他很多好处：对他有很多好处，The verb “有” is required in this pattern: something “has many benefits” for someone.
- 是大人的时候：长大以后，“大人” is a noun, so it cannot directly follow “是” to express growing up. Use “长大以后” for the time after someone becomes an adult.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很重要 -- “他的学习和他的旅行经验很重要”：意义很大，makes the importance more specific by showing that these experiences matter greatly in his life.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他的学习和他的旅行经验：他的学习和旅行经验，Use zero form for the repeated possessor within the same noun phrase when nothing competes with the referent.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为很有名的哲人：成为了很有名的哲人，Use “了” after “成为” to mark the completed change of state.
- Expressiveness: After the long study period, add Mengzi’s feelings about learning, such as “孟子觉得学习很有意思，也很想知道更多事情。”
- Expressiveness: Make his achievement more vivid with a short reaction from other people, such as “后来，很多人都来听孟子讲学问。”
- Expressiveness: Add a brief line of speech when he becomes successful, such as “孟母高兴地说：‘你终于实现自己的梦想了！’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly connected Mengzi’s work, long period of study, travel experience, and later achievement. The sentence about his study and travel experience shows a good attempt to explain why he became successful.
