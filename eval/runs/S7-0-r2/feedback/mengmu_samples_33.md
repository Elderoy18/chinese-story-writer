## Scene 1

> 一天，很久很久以前，孟子和他妈妈住在中国。孟子妈妈的工作是祭拜，孟子不高兴，因为他说我们老是搬家，为什么？他妈妈说，从早到晚，我一直祭拜，很多墓地得我祭拜。所以我们得去很多不同的地方。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一天，很久很久以前：很久很久以前的一天，A broad past-time expression should come before the specific day; this combined time phrase is natural in narrative opening.
- 孟子不高兴，因为他说我们老是搬家，为什么？：孟子不高兴，问妈妈：“我们为什么老是搬家？”，A question should be introduced by a speaking or asking verb and quotation marks. Here, 因为 cannot introduce the content of Mengzi’s question.
- 很多墓地得我祭拜：我得去祭拜很多墓地，With the modal verb 得, the person who must do the action is the subject before 得, followed by the verb phrase.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和他妈妈：孟子和他的妈妈，On first mention, introduce the relationship with a full noun phrase rather than the pronoun-based phrase.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子妈妈的工作：她的工作，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the referent has just been introduced and no other female character competes with it.
- Expressiveness: When Mengzi asks his question, add a visible sign of his unhappiness, such as “孟子皱着眉头问妈妈”.
- Expressiveness: Show the mother’s tiredness before her explanation, such as “妈妈叹了口气，说”.
- Expressiveness: Add one small detail about the repeated travel, such as “他们常常带着行李，从一个墓地走到另一个墓地”.

### Encouragement
- You clearly show both Mengzi’s feeling and his mother’s reason for moving, so the dialogue has a meaningful cause-and-effect relationship.
- The phrase “从早到晚” gives a strong sense that the mother’s work takes a long time.

## Scene 2

> 孟子想，难怪我们常常搬家，原来我妈妈很忙。可是常常搬家害得孟子很难找到朋友。每次搬家孟子也觉得新的地方不是太热就是太冷，不是太大就是太小。在哪的地方，他都不高兴。孟子想帮助妈妈祭拜，可是他六岁，所以不能祭拜。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在哪的地方，他都不高兴：不管在哪个地方，他都不高兴，哪 must be followed by a measure word before a noun, as in 哪个地方; 不管……都…… expresses that the result is the same in every place.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 找到 -- “害得孟子很难找到朋友”：交到，emphasizes successfully forming friendships rather than simply locating people.
- 新的 -- “新的地方不是太热就是太冷”：陌生的，emphasizes that each place feels unfamiliar to Mengzi.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子很难找到朋友：他很难找到朋友，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the previous sentence has already named the character and no male character competes with the reference.
- Coherence (Reference): 每次搬家孟子也觉得：每次搬家他也觉得，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence rather than repeating the name.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子想帮助妈妈祭拜：他想帮助妈妈祭拜，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence after the preceding sentence has continued to refer to Mengzi.
- Expressiveness: After the difficulty making friends, add a small detail showing his loneliness: “他看着别的孩子一起玩，心里很孤单。”
- Expressiveness: At the end, add a short line of dialogue to show his wish to copy his mother: “妈妈，我也想学祭拜。”

### Encouragement
You used the pattern “难怪……，原来……” well to show Mengzi’s new understanding. The paired structure “不是……就是……” also clearly describes why he dislikes the new places.

- Expressiveness: You could include a short thought or line of speech from his mother, such as “妈妈说：‘你画得真好！’”

### Encouragement
