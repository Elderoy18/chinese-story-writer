## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子。他们住在墓地的旁边，所以孟子老是祭拜。孟子不是祭拜，就是跟朋友聊天。他很喜欢他的城市，但是城市有才两个学校，都很小。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，the phrase is redundant because 古代 already expresses a time period, while 的时候 also means “at the time of.”
- 城市有才两个学校：城市里才有两所学校，才 must come before 有 to mean “only”; also, 所 is the matching measure word for schools.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 小 -- “都很小”：规模很小，specifies that the schools are small in size or capacity.
- 聊天 -- “跟朋友聊天”：闲聊，emphasizes casual, unstructured conversation with friends.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子：一个叫孟子的男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国，follow the first-mention rule by giving the boy’s name when he is first introduced, rather than introducing him first with a general noun and naming him only in the next sentence.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子不是祭拜：他不是祭拜，follow the pronoun rule: after 孟子 was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other male character competes as a referent, use a pronoun.
- Coherence (Reference): 他很喜欢他的城市：他很喜欢自己的城市，when the possessor is the same person as the subject, 自己的 makes that relationship clear.
- Expressiveness: At the cemetery sentence, add what Mengzi notices and how he feels about it, such as: “孟子觉得祭拜很有趣。”
- Expressiveness: Make his imitation more vivid by showing an action, such as: “他一边笑，一边学着别人祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: At the end, show why the small number of schools matters to his mother, such as: “妈妈觉得这儿不适合孟子学习。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduced the main setting and the relationship between Mengzi and his mother.
- Your use of the paired pattern “不是……就是……” gives the scene a clear rhythm and shows what Mengzi often does.

## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈不是不喜欢他们的城市，而是想孟子有很好的教育。他们搬家到有很多学校的城市。他们从早到晚走路，孟子一直抱怨。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 想孟子有很好的教育：想让孟子接受很好的教育，想 must be followed by a verb phrase; use 让 + person + verb phrase to express wanting someone to receive something.

### Vocabulary Suggestions

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬家到：搬到，搬家 is an intransitive verb, so use 搬到 + place to show the completed change of location.
- Coherence (Temporality): 城市：城市了，add 了 after the resultative phrase 搬到 to mark that the move was completed.
- Coherence (Temporality): 走路：走了一整天的路，use 了 for the completed action of walking over a bounded period of time.
- Coherence (Conjunction): ，孟子一直抱怨：所以孟子一直抱怨，use 所以 to introduce the effect: walking all day caused Mengzi to complain.
- Expressiveness: At the point where Mengzi complains, add a physical sign of tiredness, such as “孟子走得腿都酸了”.
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of dialogue to show his feelings more directly, such as “孟子皱着眉头说：‘妈妈，我们还要走多久？’”
- Expressiveness: When they arrive in the new city, add one sensory detail about its atmosphere, such as “城市里从早到晚都很热闹”.

### Encouragement

## Scene 3

> 他难怪抱怨，他想他的朋友，因为他不要离开他的城市。他的妈妈找到孟子一个老师。跟老师孟子学最重要的文学，所以他成为哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他不要离开：他不想离开，In a statement about a character’s wish, use 不想 to mean “does not want to”; 不要 usually expresses a prohibition or instruction.
- 找到孟子一个老师：给孟子找了一个老师，The person who benefits from finding something is introduced with 给; this completed action also needs 了 after 找.
- 跟老师孟子学最重要的文学：孟子跟老师学最重要的文学，Place the subject 孟子 before the prepositional phrase 跟老师.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 想 -- “他想自己的朋友”：想念，specifically expresses missing friends who are no longer nearby.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他的朋友：自己的朋友，Use 自己的 when the possessor is the same person as the subject; it makes clear that the friends belong to 孟子.
- Coherence (Reference): 他的城市：自己的城市，Use 自己的 when the city is the one connected to the subject of the sentence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为哲学家：成为了哲学家，Use 了 after 成为 to mark the completed change of state.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他难怪抱怨，他想：难怪他抱怨，原来他想，Use the paired connector 难怪……，原来…… to present the result first and then reveal its explanation; 难怪 should introduce the result clause.
- Expressiveness: After the complaint, add a small action that shows his unhappiness, such as “孟子低着头，走得很慢。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of inner thought after he misses his friends, such as “他心想：‘我真不想离开我的朋友。’”
- Expressiveness: Show the mother’s purpose when she finds the teacher, such as “妈妈希望孟子能学到更多知识。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect Mengzi’s complaint with his reluctance to leave, and you also show that his mother is trying to help his education.
