## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子。他们住在墓地的旁边，所以孟子老是祭拜。孟子不是祭拜，就是跟朋友聊天。他很喜欢他的城市，但是城市有才两个学校，都很小。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，this time expression is redundant because 古代 already names a time period.
- 有才两个学校：才有两所学校，才 should come before 有, and 所 is the matching measure word for schools.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 小 -- “都很小”：规模都很小，specifies that the schools are small in overall scale.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子：一个叫孟子的男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国，first mention should introduce the character by name rather than using a pronoun to name him only in the next sentence.
- Coherence (Reference): 他的城市：自己住的城市，when the possessor is the same person as the subject, 自己 makes the relationship clear and avoids suggesting that the city belongs to him.
- Coherence (Reference): 但是城市：但是它，use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the city was just mentioned and no other place competes as the referent.
- Expressiveness: After “孟子老是祭拜,” add a brief action detail to show what he copies, such as “学着别人低头行礼”.
- Expressiveness: In the final sentence, add Mengzi’s feeling about the small schools, such as “他觉得这儿不太适合读书”.
- Expressiveness: You could add a short line of thought after “都很小,” such as “这儿的学校太少了。”

### Encouragement
You clearly introduce Mengzi’s home near the cemetery and show his everyday activities with both “祭拜” and “跟朋友聊天.” The contrast between what he likes about the city and its limited schools also gives the scene a clear direction.

## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈不是不喜欢他们的城市，而是想孟子有很好的教育。他们搬家到有很多学校的城市。他们从早到晚走路，孟子一直抱怨。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 想孟子有很好的教育：想让孟子受到很好的教育，想 cannot directly take a person plus a full predicate in this pattern; use 让 to introduce the person affected by the wish. 受到教育 means “receive an education.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很好 -- “孟子受到很好的教育”：良好，more precise for describing the quality of education；优质，emphasizes that the educational resources and teaching are of high quality.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们搬家到有很多学校的城市：他们搬到了有很多学校的城市，搬到 is the verb-plus-place structure, and 了 marks the completed move.
- Expressiveness: After “孟子一直抱怨,” add a short line of quoted speech to show his tiredness, such as: “孟子说：‘妈妈，我们还要走多久？我累了。’”
- Expressiveness: Describe the mother’s reaction after the complaint to show her feelings and purpose, such as: “妈妈虽然也很累，可是她想让孟子受到良好的教育。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the mother’s motivation for moving and connect it to Mengzi’s education. The contrast structure makes the reason for her decision easy to understand.

## Scene 3

> 他难怪抱怨，他想他的朋友，因为他不要离开他的城市。他的妈妈找到孟子一个老师。跟老师孟子学最重要的文学，所以他成为哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 找到孟子一个老师：给孟子找了一个老师，Use 给 to introduce the person who receives the benefit of the action; the completed action also needs 了.
- 跟老师孟子学最重要的文学：孟子跟老师学最重要的文学，Place the subject before the phrase showing who the learner studies with.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 文学 -- “学最重要的文学”：文学作品, specifies that he is studying literary texts rather than the academic field in general；经典文学作品, identifies works that are widely regarded as important.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他的朋友：自己的朋友，Use 自己的 when the possessor is the same person as the subject, making it clear that the friends are his own.
- Coherence (Reference): 他的城市：自己的城市，Use 自己的 to show clearly that the city is the one he does not want to leave.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为哲学家：成为了哲学家，Use 了 after 成为 to mark the completed change of state.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他难怪抱怨，他想他的朋友，因为他不要离开他的城市：他抱怨，因为他想念自己的朋友，也不想离开自己的城市，难怪 needs a result followed or supported by an explanation, often with 原来; here 因为 introduces the reasons for his complaint, and 也 links the two parallel reasons.
- Expressiveness: At the point where he complains, add his feeling of reluctance: “孟子很不舍得离开自己的朋友。”
- Expressiveness: You could add a short line of quoted speech to show his complaint: “孟子说：‘我不想离开这座城市！’”
- Expressiveness: When his mother finds a teacher, add her purpose or hope: “妈妈希望孟子能好好学习。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect the boy’s complaint with his attachment to his friends and city. You also give the scene a strong long-term outcome by showing that he later becomes a philosopher.
