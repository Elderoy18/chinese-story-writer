## Scene 1

> 古代的时候有一个人叫神农。他需要当上医，一看别的人就想帮助他们。但是，他有一个问题，他不知道什么东西能做药。神农听说一个远远的地方有一种植物，什么病人吃都会觉得身体更好。神农三个月走去，找到有植物的地方，他看到一个很漂亮的植物，又香又好看。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 already expresses a time period, so adding 的时候 is redundant.
- 当上医：当上医生，医 is not the noun for a doctor; 医生 is the correct occupation noun.
- 一看别的人：一看到病人，看到 means “to see,” while 看 means “to look.” Also, 别人 requires a contrast with a person or group already mentioned; 病人 fits the medical context precisely.
- 一个远远的地方：在一个很远的地方，an existential sentence needs 在 to introduce the location, and 很远 naturally modifies a place.
- 什么病人：病人，什么病人 asks “which patient”; the sentence means patients in general, so it should use the generic noun without 什么.
- 他看到一个很漂亮的植物：他看到了一棵很漂亮的植物，看到 is needed for “see,” and 棵 is the matching measure word for a plant.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 问题 -- “但是，他有一个问题”：困难，emphasizes that this is an obstacle he needs to overcome；难题，emphasizes that the problem is difficult to solve.
- 好看 -- “又香又好看”：美丽，gives a more story-like description of the plant’s appearance；引人注目，emphasizes that its appearance catches Shen Nong’s attention.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 有植物的地方：有这种植物的地方，use 这种植物 to refer back clearly to the one kind of plant mentioned in the previous sentence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 神农三个月走去，找到有植物的地方：神农走了三个月，才找到了有这种植物的地方，put the duration after the verb with 了, and use 了 on 找到 because the search is completed; 才 also shows that the search took a long time.
- Coherence (Temporality): 病人吃都会觉得身体更好：病人吃了都会觉得身体好多了，use 了 after 吃 for the completed action before the result, and 好多了 marks the change to a better physical condition.
- Expressiveness: When Shen Nong hears about the plant, add his motivation or inner thought: 神农想：“如果这种植物真的能治病，我一定要去找它。”
- Expressiveness: At the description of the plant, add a concrete visual detail to make it vivid: 那儿开着黄色的花，像星星一样，又大又漂亮。
- Expressiveness: When he arrives after the long walk, add a feeling: 神农虽然很累，但是看到这种植物，心里很高兴。

### Encouragement
- You introduced Shen Nong clearly at the beginning and gave him a meaningful goal: learning which things can be made into medicine.
- The plant description, especially “又香又好看,” already helps the scene feel more vivid.

## Scene 2

> 他觉得，这种一定是做药的花。原来他想错了。吃了以后，神农快生病。 这时一个穿着蓝色衣服的人帮神农，快给他一种红的灵芝。神农吃了以后，他没有病，想起来以前一个人告诉他，灵芝对身体有好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 这种一定是做药的花：这种花一定是用来做药的，Add the noun after the demonstrative-classifier phrase so that the flower is clearly identified; this word order also makes the purpose of the flower natural.
- 快生病：就生病了，In a completed past event, use the completed form with 了. Here, 快 before a verb usually suggests “about to,” not that the illness happened quickly.
- 快给：赶快给了，Use 赶快 for “quickly” in a declarative narrative sentence, and add 了 because the giving action was completed.
- 以前一个人告诉他：以前有一个人告诉过他，Use 有 to introduce a first-mentioned indefinite person. The particle 过 marks the earlier experience that he now remembers.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好处 -- “灵芝对身体有好处”：疗效，specifies the beneficial medical effect of the mushroom more clearly in this context.
- 没有病 -- “他没有病”：病好了，shows more precisely that his illness recovered after he ate the mushroom.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 给他：给神农，Repeat the name because the sentence contains both the blue-clothed man and 神农; the pronoun could refer to either man.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农吃了以后，他没有病：神农吃了以后，没有病，Use zero form for the continuing subject within the same sentence rather than repeating a pronoun.
- Coherence (Temporality): 想起来：想起了，Use 了 after the resultative verb because this is a completed remembering event; the placement is verb + resultative + 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 帮神农，：为了帮神农，Use 为了 to connect the helper’s purpose with his following action of giving the mushroom.
- Expressiveness: After the illness begins, add a physical feeling to show how serious it is, such as “神农肚子疼得很厉害，脸都白了。”
- Expressiveness: When the blue-clothed man appears, describe his urgent action more vividly, such as “他赶快从地上拿起一朵像伞一样的灵芝。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought when 神农 remembers the earlier advice, such as “神农想：原来灵芝真的可以救人！”

### Encouragement
- You used the sequence “吃了以后” to organize the events clearly, and “原来他想错了” creates a good change from expectation to discovery.

## Scene 3

> 啊，他怎么忘记了？他把灵芝放在包里。一回家就告诉别人。他找到药以后他写下怎么做药在书上。所以人们都知道，那红灵芝对身体好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 那红灵芝：那种红灵芝，A demonstrative before a noun needs a matching measure word; use 种 for a kind of plant.
- 对身体好处：对身体有好处，The predicate structure is 对……有好处; 好处 is a noun and needs 有 before it.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他找到药以后他写下怎么做药在书上：他找到药以后写下怎么做药在书上，Use zero form for the same character when the subject continues within the same sentence; the second 他 is unnecessary.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他把灵芝放在包里：他把灵芝放进了包里，Use 了 after a completed action in past narration; 进 also shows that the item went into the bag.
- Coherence (Temporality): 一回家就告诉别人：一回家就告诉了大家，Use 了 for the completed act of telling. 别人 distinguishes other people from a specific person, while 大家 fits telling the general public.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他找到药以后他写下怎么做药在书上：他找到药以后，在书上写下了怎么做药，Use 了 after 写下 because recording this information is a completed action. The location phrase 在书上 should come before the verb.
- Expressiveness: After putting the plant away, add a small feeling detail to show his relief, for example: 他松了一口气，觉得自己终于找到了一种有用的药。
- Expressiveness: When he tells people, add a short line of speech to make the moment more lively, for example: “这种红灵芝能帮助生病的人！”
- Expressiveness: At the writing point, name one specific use of the plant, for example: 他在书上写下：红灵芝对身体有好处。

### Encouragement
- You use the sequence pattern 一……就…… effectively to show what happens as soon as he gets home. You also connect the final result to the earlier actions with 所以, which helps the scene move forward clearly.
