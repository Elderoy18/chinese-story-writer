## Scene 1

> 一天，很久很久以前，孟子和他妈妈住在中国。孟子妈妈的工作是祭拜，孟子不高兴，因为他说我们老是搬家，为什么？他妈妈说，从早到晚，我一直祭拜，很多墓地得我祭拜。所以我们得去很多不同的地方。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一天，很久很久以前：很久很久以前的一天，Chinese places the broad time expression before “一天” when both describe when the story begins.
- 因为他说我们老是搬家，为什么？：问：“我们为什么老是搬家？”，a question should use a questioning verb such as “问” and direct speech; “因为” cannot introduce the content of the question here.
- 很多墓地得我祭拜：很多墓地都需要我去祭拜，“得” cannot mean “need” before a person in this structure; “需要” correctly expresses that the cemeteries require her to perform the action.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 工作 -- “孟子妈妈的工作是祭拜”：职责，specifies the duty she is responsible for；任务，emphasizes a particular assigned responsibility.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After “孟子不高兴,” add a small action to show his feeling, such as “孟子皱着眉头，低着头不说话。”
- Expressiveness: Make the mother’s explanation sound more personal with a feeling, such as “妈妈叹了口气，说：‘我也觉得很累，可是很多墓地都需要我去祭拜。’”
- Expressiveness: Add one concrete detail about the moving, such as “他们的东西不多，只有几件衣服和一个小箱子。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show both Mengzi’s question and his mother’s reason, so the conversation has a logical beginning and response. You also used “从早到晚” effectively to show that the mother’s work takes a long time.

## Scene 2

> 孟子想，难怪我们常常搬家，原来我妈妈很忙。可是常常搬家害得孟子很难找到朋友。每次搬家孟子也觉得新的地方不是太热就是太冷，不是太大就是太小。在哪的地方，他都不高兴。孟子想帮助妈妈祭拜，可是他六岁，所以不能祭拜。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 哪的地方：哪个地方，when an interrogative word modifies a noun, use the classifier phrase “哪个 + noun”; “哪的地方” is not a valid noun phrase.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 找到 -- “很难找到朋友”：交到，specifically means successfully forming friendships with other children.
- 新的 -- “新的地方”：陌生的，makes clear that each place feels unfamiliar to Mengzi after moving.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 每次搬家孟子也觉得：每次搬家，他也觉得，use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the previously mentioned character is still clear.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子想帮助妈妈祭拜：他想帮助妈妈祭拜，use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence because the previous sentence already clearly refers to Mengzi.
- Expressiveness: After “很难找到朋友,” add Mengzi’s feeling of loneliness, such as “他觉得很孤单。”
- Expressiveness: Make the weather more vivid after “太热就是太冷,” such as “有的地方热得他满头大汗。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short inner thought at the end to show his wish to help, such as “我什么时候才能帮妈妈呢？”

### Encouragement
You used “难怪……原来……” well to show Mengzi’s realization about why the family moves often. You also clearly connected the frequent moves to his difficulty making friends.
