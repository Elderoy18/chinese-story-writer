## Scene 1

> 古代的时候人觉得祭拜非常重要，孟子很小的时候，从早到晚一直祭拜，因为他妈妈告诉他祭拜很重要。虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜。他的同学们老是对他开玩笑了。孟子妈妈看了他的同学们对孟子不好的影响，所以孟子和孟子妈妈搬家了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，this expression is redundant because 古代 already refers to a time period.
- 人：人们，use 人们 for a concrete group of people who share this belief.
- 孟子妈妈看了：孟子的妈妈看到了，use 的 to link a name with a family relationship. 看 means “look at,” while 看到 means “see/notice”; the completed resultative verb takes 了 after 到.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不好 -- “他的同学们对孟子不好的影响”：负面的，specifies that the influence harms him rather than merely being generally “not good.”

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子很小的时候：有一个小男孩叫孟子；他很小的时候，first mention should introduce the character with a noun phrase as well as the name; after that, use a pronoun for the same character.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子妈妈：孟子和他的妈妈，孟子 has just been mentioned, so the pronoun rule calls for 他的妈妈 rather than repeating the name.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他的同学们老是对他开玩笑了：他的同学们老是对他开玩笑，老是 describes a habitual action, so it uses the zero particle rather than completed-action 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜：因为他每天只祭拜，所以没有朋友，this is a cause-and-effect relation, so use the paired connector 因为……所以…… rather than 虽然.
- Expressiveness: After the description of his worshipping, add his feeling and one visible action, for example: “孟子觉得祭拜很有趣，学着大人的样子鞠躬。”
- Expressiveness: At the classmates’ teasing, add a short line of speech to make the scene more lively, for example: “一个同学笑着说：‘你怎么每天都在祭拜？’”
- Expressiveness: When his mother notices the problem, add her worried thought, for example: “孟子的妈妈很着急，心想：‘这样会不会影响孩子？’”

### Encouragement
You clearly show a cause for the family’s move, and your use of 从早到晚一直 makes Mengzi’s daily behaviour easy to picture.

## Scene 2

> 孟子才十一岁，成为一个哲学家。孟子不是祭拜，而是看政治文章，难怪他成为了哲学家。不过，看过很多政治文章对他害得，他有很多问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 对他害得：害得他有很多问题，害得 must be followed by the person affected and then the harmful result; 对 cannot be placed before 害得 in this structure.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 问题 -- “他有很多问题”：疑问，specifies that he has questions he does not yet understand after reading.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子不是祭拜：他不是祭拜，use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the same character was named in the immediately preceding sentence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 孟子才十一岁，成为一个哲学家：孟子才十一岁，就成为了一个哲学家，才 with an age is paired with 就 to show an unexpectedly early result, and 了 marks the completed change of status.
- Expressiveness: After the reading activity, add a concrete learning action, such as “他一边看，一边把不懂的问题记下来。”
- Expressiveness: Show Mengzi’s interest with an inner thought or a short quotation, such as “孟子心想：‘这些文章真有意思，我还想知道得更多。’”

### Encouragement
- You used the contrast pattern “不是……而是……” correctly to show that Mengzi changed what he did. You also tried to connect reading with his becoming a philosopher, which gives the scene a clear main idea.

## Scene 3

> 他一直问问题，但是不能解决那些问题。孟子长大，他开始能解决问题。他常常建议你不是学金融，而是学政治，因为如果你懂政治，你会成为高薪。后来孟子成为教授，他教学生很厉害。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 成为高薪：得到高薪，the verb “become” must be followed by a person, role, or identity; “high salary” is payment, so use a verb meaning “receive/earn.”
- 教学生很厉害：很擅长教学生，an adjective such as “very skilled” cannot directly follow a verb-object phrase in this structure; this version clearly says that he is skilled at teaching students.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 问 -- “他一直问问题”：提出，emphasizes raising questions for discussion or thought.
- 解决 -- “他开始能解决问题”：解答，more precise when the “problems” are questions that need answers.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子：他，use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the previous sentence already identifies the same character and no other character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子：他，use a pronoun because the same character was mentioned in the immediately preceding sentence.
- Coherence (Reference): 他教学生很厉害：教学生很厉害，within the same sentence, use zero form rather than repeating the subject pronoun after it has already been stated.
- Coherence (Temporality): 长大：长大了，use 了 for a completed change of state: he has grown up.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为教授：成为了教授，use 了 because becoming a professor is a completed change of role at this point in the narrative.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 如果你懂政治，你会：如果你懂政治，你就会，use the conditional pair “if…then…” to connect the condition and its result.
- Expressiveness: After the first sentence, add a brief feeling to show why the unanswered questions matter, such as “他觉得很着急：‘这些问题太难了！’”
- Expressiveness: When he becomes a professor, add one detail about his teaching or his students’ response, such as “学生们都认真地听他讲课。”

### Encouragement
- You use a clear contrast between studying finance and studying politics, and you show Mengzi’s development from asking questions to teaching students.
