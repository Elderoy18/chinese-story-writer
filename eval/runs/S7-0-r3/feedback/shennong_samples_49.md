## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有很多人不知道什么植物能吃，什么植物不能吃。有很多人吃植物以后就吃坏肚子了，因为他们吃了毒。那个时候，有一个老人叫神农，他什么植物都觉得好吃，不怕生病，不怕毒。他希望别人也可以吃好吃的植物。可是他知道，如果他们还怕吃毒，不会吃他喜欢的植物。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 already names a time period, so adding 的时候 is redundant.
- 吃植物以后：吃了植物以后，use 了 for the completed eating action that happens before the later result.
- 吃了毒：吃了有毒的植物，毒 by itself is a noun meaning “poison”; use 有毒的植物 to describe poisonous plants.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 如果他们还怕吃毒，不会吃他喜欢的植物：如果他们还怕吃毒，就不会吃他喜欢的植物，an if-condition needs 就 in the result clause to show the conditional relation clearly.
- Expressiveness: After “吃坏肚子了”, add a physical detail to show how ill the people feel, such as “肚子疼得不得了”.
- Expressiveness: At “他什么植物都觉得好吃”, add Shennong’s confident thought or speech, such as “他说：‘我不怕，我想试一试。’”
- Expressiveness: Describe Shennong’s wish more personally after “他希望别人也可以吃好吃的植物”, such as “他希望大家都能吃得健康，不会生病。”

### Encouragement
You clearly introduce the problem that people cannot tell which plants are safe, and you introduce Shennong with his brave personality. The contrast word “可是” also begins to show his different idea from other people.

## Scene 2

> 所以，他上山下山找植物，吃了很多样子的植物。他是第一人，找到了茶。他把所有的植物写下来了。他一直都吃了，他连毒都能吃。可是他还没吃了一个叫夹竹桃的植物。他找到了那个像星星的植物，觉得很漂亮，马上就吃下了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很多样子的植物：很多种植物，样子 refers to appearance, while 种 is the measure word used to count different types of plants.
- 他是第一人：他是第一个找到茶的人，use 第一个 + verb phrase + 的人 to say that someone was the first person to do something.
- 一个叫夹竹桃的植物：一种叫夹竹桃的植物，植物 types take the measure word 种 rather than the general measure word 个.
- 像星星的植物：像星星一样的植物，comparisons of appearance normally use 像……一样 to show that the plant looks like a star.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 漂亮 -- “觉得很漂亮”：艳丽，adds the idea of bright, striking colour, which fits the yellow flower；引人注目，emphasizes that the flower catches his attention.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 他一直都吃了：他一直都在吃植物，an action continuing over a period uses 在 rather than completed-action 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 还没吃了：还没吃过，没 negates a completed or experienced action, so it cannot be followed by 了; 过 marks that he has not had this experience before.
- Expressiveness: After the flower is introduced, add a visual detail to make the setting clearer: “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: Add Shennong’s immediate thought before he eats it to show his curiosity: “他心想：这朵花这么漂亮，应该可以吃吧。”

### Encouragement
- You used a clear sequence of actions, moving from searching for plants to finding tea, writing notes, and then seeing another plant.
- Your use of completed-action forms in “找到了茶”, “写下来了”, and “吃下了” helps the events feel active and easy to follow.

## Scene 3

> 可是夹竹桃的毒很重，让他肚子很痛。他在地上躺着，觉得他快会死了。可是正好有别人找到他了，给了他一个像伞的植物，叫灵芝。他吃灵芝以后就觉得很好，不再生病了。他测量起来了，像星星的植物是毒，像伞的植物是药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 快会死了：快要死了，快要 marks that a change is imminent; 会 is normally used for ability, learned skill, or a general future prediction, so it does not fit this immediate danger.
- 别人：一个人，别人 means “other people” in contrast with a specified person or group; here a new helper is being introduced, so use an indefinite noun phrase.
- 测量起来了：发现，测量 means measuring size, length, or quantity and normally needs a measurable object; 发现 can introduce what he learns about the two plants.
- 是毒：有毒，有毒 is the adjective meaning “poisonous”; 毒 by itself is a noun meaning “poison.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很好 -- “他吃灵芝以后就觉得很好”：好多了，shows specifically that his condition improved after eating the medicine.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他：自己，when the person who is dying is the same person as the one who feels this, use 自己 to make the self-reference clear.
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃灵芝以后：吃了灵芝以后，use 了 because the eating is completed before the later feeling begins.
- Expressiveness: After the stomach pain, add a stronger physical feeling to show his suffering: “他在地上躺着，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: At the moment of danger, add an internal question to show his fear: “他想：‘我会死吗？’”
- Expressiveness: When the helper appears, add one visual detail so the new character is easier to imagine: “这时，一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人走过来。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the change from pain to recovery, and the contrast between the poisonous star-shaped plant and the medicinal umbrella-shaped plant is easy to understand.
