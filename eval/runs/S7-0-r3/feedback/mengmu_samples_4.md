## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，孟子和孟母住了在靠墓地的家。孩子才五岁就每天在墓地祭拜了。在那个城市，孟母从早到晚都找工作，可是找不到，害得孟母没有钱。难怪她跟孩子搬家，原来买不了房。他们搬了到别一个城市找工作。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，the phrase is redundant because 古代 already expresses a time period, while 的时候 also means “at the time of.”
- 靠墓地的家：靠近墓地的家里，use 靠近 to describe proximity, and use 家里 as the location where someone lives.
- 跟孩子搬家：带着孟子搬家，跟孟子搬家 means that both people move together as equal participants; 带着孟子搬家 correctly shows that the mother takes her child with her.
- 别一个城市：另一个城市，use 另一个 for “another one”; 别的 is normally used before a noun to mean “other/different.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 买不了 -- “原来买不了房”：负担不起房子，more precisely expresses that she cannot afford a house because of limited money.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟母：有一个小男孩叫孟子，他和妈妈，at first mention, briefly introduce the main character with his identity before using the names or family role.
- Coherence (Reference): 孩子才五岁：他才五岁，use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the referent is clear and no other male character competes with it.
- Coherence (Reference): 在那个城市：在一个城市里，this is the first mention of a city, so use a full noun phrase rather than the demonstrative phrase 那个城市, whose referent has not been introduced.
- Coherence (Temporality): 住了在：住在，住 is a continuing state here, so it takes the zero particle rather than 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 每天在墓地祭拜了：每天在墓地祭拜，every day marks a routine, so use the zero particle rather than completed-action 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 她跟孩子搬家：她带着孟子搬家了，this move has already happened, so completed-action 了 is needed at the end of the verb phrase.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们搬了到：他们搬到了，when a verb is followed by the resultative/directional complement 到, place 了 after the complement: 搬到了.
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery-worship sentence, add what the child sees and does to make the setting vivid: “他每天看到很多人来墓地祭拜，也跟着学他们的样子。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where the mother cannot find work, add her feeling or inner thought: “孟母很着急，心想：‘再这样下去，我们怎么生活呢？’”
- Expressiveness: You could describe the new place with one sensory detail: “那个城市从早到晚都很热闹，街上有很多商人卖东西。”

### Encouragement

- The use of 难怪……，原来…… also creates a useful link between the family’s problem and their decision to move.

## Scene 2

> 这个城市老是很吵，不是人吵架，就是都叫，害得孟子一直不能睡觉。在那儿，孟母也找不到工作。孟子和妈妈要又一个次搬了家。在第三个城市，孟母找到工作，在商店做衣服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不是人吵架，就是都叫：不是有人在吵架，就是有人在大叫，Chinese uses 人 as a generic noun rather than for a concrete group in a scene; 有人 introduces the people making each kind of noise clearly.
- 要又一个次搬了家：要再搬一次家，With a future modal verb such as 要, use 要 + 再 + verb + number + measure word + object. 再 marks a move that has not happened yet, and completed-action 了 is not used after a future plan.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 吵 -- “这个城市老是很吵”：嘈杂，specifies that the city has many mixed, disturbing sounds rather than simply being loud.
- 做 -- “在商店做衣服”：缝制，specifies that the work involves sewing clothes.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 孟母找到工作：孟母找到了工作，Use 了 with 找到 to mark the completed result of successfully finding a job.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 在第三个城市：后来，在第三个城市，Later events need a sequence connector because the family first plans another move and then the narration shifts to the third city.
- Expressiveness: After the noisy-city description, add one or two specific sounds, such as “汽车声和叫卖声从早到晚都不停。”
- Expressiveness: Show Mengzi’s tired feeling after he cannot sleep, such as “孟子困得睁不开眼睛，白天也没有精神。”
- Expressiveness: At the point when the family plans to move, add Mengmu’s thoughts or speech, such as “孟母着急地说：‘不行，我们得再搬一次家。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show how the noisy environment affects Mengzi by using the cause-and-result structure “害得孟子一直不能睡觉.”
- The final sentence gives Mengmu a new job and provides a clear, concrete detail about her work.

## Scene 3
