### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 already names a time period, so adding 的时候 is redundant.
- 所以神农去了吃每种植物：所以他去吃了每种植物，put 了 after the verb phrase 去吃 to mark the completed action; 去了吃 has incorrect 了 placement.
- 就写了这个植物对你有没有好处：就写下这种植物对身体有没有好处，use 写下 when recording information about a plant; 对身体 keeps the reference general instead of suddenly addressing the reader as 你.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 糟糕 -- “这个问题神农觉得很糟糕”：严重，specifies that the problem has serious consequences rather than only being generally bad.
- 看到了 -- “他看到了有很多人不知道哪个植物好吃”：发现，emphasizes that he came to realize this problem through observation.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 所以神农去了吃每种植物：所以他去吃了每种植物，use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence: 神农 was named in the immediately previous sentence, and no competing male character has appeared.
- Coherence (Temporality): 有的时候，神农吃了很好吃的植物：有的时候，神农吃很好吃的植物，use the zero particle for a routine or repeated action introduced by 有的时候.
- Coherence (Temporality): 有的时候，他吃了很危险的植物：有的时候，他吃很危险的植物，use the zero particle for a routine or repeated action introduced by 有的时候.
- Coherence (Temporality): 会来跟他的朋友聊到他吃的植物了：会来跟他的朋友聊聊他吃过的植物，use the zero particle after the modal verb 会; the final 了 does not mark a completed action here.
- Expressiveness: After “这个问题神农觉得很糟糕,” add his concern to show why he acts: “他想：我要找出哪些植物能吃。”
- Expressiveness: At “很危险的植物,” add a physical reaction to make the danger vivid: “他吃了以后，肚子疼得很厉害。”
