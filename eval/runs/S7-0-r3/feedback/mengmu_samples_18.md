## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个男孩名字叫孟子。孟子和孟子的妈妈两个人住在很穷的农村。他们住的地方旁边是个墓地，墓地老是有很多人祭拜。因为人们在祭拜，他们一直在哭。孟子和孟子的妈妈住在这里才一个星期就觉得受不了了，而且害得孟子的学习。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 already functions as a time expression, so adding 的时候 is redundant.
- 有一个男孩名字叫孟子：有一个叫孟子的男孩，an attributive phrase describing the boy must come before the noun and use 的.
- 墓地老是有很多人祭拜：墓地常常有很多人祭拜，老是 has a negative or complaining tone; 常常 neutrally describes a frequent event.
- 而且害得孟子的学习：而且还影响了孟子的学习，害得 must be followed by a person and a harmful result clause; 影响了 can directly take 学习 as its object.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很穷 -- “住在很穷的农村”：贫困，specifically describes an area with limited money and resources.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子的妈妈两个人：孟子和他的妈妈两个人，after 孟子 has just been named and no other male character competes with him, use the pronoun 他 for the repeated possessor.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子的妈妈住在这里：孟子和他的妈妈住在这里，repeat the name 孟子 after other people have been discussed, but use the pronoun 他 for his mother’s possessor.
- Coherence (Temporality): 住在这里才一个星期：才住了一个星期，use 了 after 住 to show that this one-week period of living there has been completed.
- Expressiveness: At the cemetery description, add a sound detail to make the setting more vivid: “墓地里不时传来低低的哭声。”
- Expressiveness: At the final sentence, show the mother’s concern through an internal thought: “孟子的妈妈皱着眉头，心想：‘住在这里会影响孟子的学习。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish the setting and explain why the cemetery environment is difficult for the family. The use of 因为 to connect the mourning and crying also helps the reader understand the scene.

## Scene 2

> 孟子的妈妈觉得孟子取得良好的教育是很重要的，所以孟子的妈妈认为这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好，就打算跟孟子搬家。孟子的妈妈带孟子不是搬到城市，而是搬到了孟子老师的家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 取得良好的教育：接受良好的教育，Use “接受” for receiving an education; “取得” is normally used for achievements or results.
- 跟孟子搬家：带着孟子搬家，Use “带着” when a mother moves together with and takes her child to a new home; “跟” does not express this relationship as precisely.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 这样的地方 -- “这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好”：这样的环境，makes clear that the surroundings and atmosphere may affect the child’s education.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子的妈妈认为：她认为，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the mother was named earlier in the same sentence and no other possible referent competes.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子的妈妈带孟子：她带孟子，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence across the sentence boundary because the mother remains the clear subject.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 不是搬到城市，而是搬到了孟子老师的家：搬到了孟子的老师家里，The “不是……而是……” connector should refute a conventional or previously mistaken idea; no such contrast has been established here, so a simple statement is clearer.
- Expressiveness: After the mother decides to move, add her worried inner thought: “孟母心想：‘这儿会影响孟子的学习。’”
- Expressiveness: Show the mother’s feeling before the move with a short detail such as “孟母很着急，不知道该搬到哪里。”

### Encouragement

## Scene 3
