## Scene 1

> 在古代，很多医生不知道怎么用药。所以他们找到了很多植物对药。神农先看到很多病人，他们吃很多植物对药。但是我觉得他们的吃的植物，让他们病人。神农觉得这个植物可能特别是毒。所以他想找一个植物对药，他们可以吃对药。正好，他找到了一把花，他想把花放在茶里，对药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很多植物对药：很多可以做药的植物，"对药" is not a valid phrase for describing a plant’s use; "可以做药" means that the plants can be used as medicine.
- 吃很多植物对药：吃了很多可以做药的植物，use "可以做药的植物" to modify the plants, and add 了 because this describes a completed past action.
- 让他们病人：让他们生病了，"病人" is a noun meaning a sick person; after 让, use the verb phrase "生病" to express becoming ill.
- 特别是毒：有毒，"毒" cannot be modified by "特别是" in this way; "有毒" is the adjective phrase meaning poisonous.
- 一个植物对药：一种可以做药的植物，use "一种" for a type of plant, and use "可以做药" rather than "对药".
- 吃对药：吃这种植物，"对药" cannot follow 吃; refer back to the medicinal plant with "这种植物".
- 对药：看看能不能做药，"对药" is not a verb phrase and cannot state the purpose of putting the flower into tea; this revision expresses testing whether it can be used as medicine.

- Coherence (Reference): 这个植物：这些植物，after several plants have been mentioned, the singular reference is unclear; repeat the relevant noun with "这些" to identify the group being discussed.
- Coherence (Reference): 他们可以吃这种植物：人们可以吃这种植物，"他们" could refer to the doctors or the patients mentioned earlier; repeat a clear noun when the pronoun’s referent is ambiguous.
- Expressiveness: When the patients become ill, add a physical detail to show the result, such as: "他们吃了以后，肚子很疼。"
- Expressiveness: At the point where the flower is found, describe its appearance to make the discovery vivid, such as: "那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花。"
- Expressiveness: Add Shennong’s thought before he tests the flower, such as: "神农想：这种花能不能做药呢？"

## Scene 2

> 他不知道如果这把花对你的身体好，这就要看如果这把花毒不毒了。神农想找别的植物，但是这很麻烦。正好，他找到了别的花。他一吃花，很快就生病了。他的肚子疼死了。神农不知道他吃不吃灵芝或者夹竹桃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不知道如果这把花对你的身体好：不知道这把花对他的身体好不好，An embedded yes/no question after “不知道” uses an A-not-A form, not “如果”; the body pronoun should also stay in the third-person narrator’s perspective.
- 看如果这把花毒不毒：看这把花有没有毒，In the pattern “这就要看……了”, the part after “看” must be a question form; “如果” cannot introduce an embedded A-not-A question here.
- 吃不吃灵芝或者夹竹桃：吃的是灵芝还是夹竹桃，When choosing between two specific possibilities in an embedded question, use “是……还是……”, not an A-not-A question together with “或者”.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很快 -- “他一吃花，很快就生病了”：不一会儿，specifies that only a short time passed before he became ill.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他一吃花：他一吃这种花，Use a demonstrative plus measure word and noun for the particular flower just mentioned, rather than the generic noun “花”.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农不知道他吃的是灵芝还是夹竹桃：神农不知道自己吃的是灵芝还是夹竹桃，Use “自己” when the person who ate is the same person as the main subject; this makes the co-reference clear.
- Expressiveness: At the point where he finds the flower, add one visual detail to make it memorable, such as “那种花又黄又大，像星星一样漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: After the stomach pain, show Shennong’s fear or discomfort, such as “他躺在地上，难受得不得了，心想：我会不会死？”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech after he becomes ill, such as “神农说：‘我刚才吃的到底是什么花？’”

### Encouragement
- You used the sequence pattern “一……就……” effectively to show that the illness happened immediately after eating the flower. The scene also clearly moves from searching for a plant to becoming sick, so the main event sequence is easy to follow.

## Scene 3

> 别的人给了神农一个灵芝。这个植物可以让神农更好。比方说，他会觉得更健康。吃灵芝以后，他可以再走。现在他觉得更好。最后，神农找到了很多植物，他可以用对药。这些植物让神农很高兴。神农想跟很多人分享他的新药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 更好：身体好一些，When describing recovery or health, “让某人更好” is too vague and does not naturally describe a person’s physical condition; “身体好一些” clearly means that his health has improved.
- 更好：好多了，After recovering from illness, “好多了” is the natural expression for feeling much better; “更好” needs a clear comparison and is not enough by itself here.
- 很多植物，他可以用对药：很多可以用来做药的植物，“用对药” is not a valid verb phrase for saying that plants can serve as medicine. Use “用来做药” to express purpose or function.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 高兴 -- “这些植物让神农很高兴”：欣慰，adds the sense that he feels satisfied because his hard work has helped him find useful plants.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 这个植物：它，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence: the plant has just been introduced as “一个灵芝”, so “它” refers to it clearly and avoids repeating a full noun phrase.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农想跟很多人分享他的新药：他想跟很多人分享他的新药，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence: “神农” was named in the immediately preceding sentence, and no other male character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃灵芝以后：吃了灵芝以后，Use 了 for the completed action that happens before the later event; he eats the herb first, and then he is able to continue walking.
- Expressiveness: After “他觉得好多了”, add a physical detail showing his recovery, such as “他的肚子不疼了，也有力气站起来了。”
- Expressiveness: At “这些植物让神农很高兴”, add his inner thought, such as “他想：我终于找到能帮助病人的药了！”
- Expressiveness: When he wants to share the medicine, add a short line of speech, such as “神农说：‘这些植物可以救人，我要告诉大家！’”

### Encouragement
- You used time order clearly with “吃灵芝以后” and “最后”, which helps the reader follow the events.
- You also showed Shen Nong’s improvement and his wish to help other people, giving this scene a positive ending.
