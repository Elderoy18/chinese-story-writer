## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男人觉得植物很有意思。他叫神农。有的植物吃了以后他的肚子疼死了，可是有的吃了以后很舒服。他找很多植物。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，this expression is redundant because 古代 already names a time period, while 的时候 also means “the time when.”
- 他找很多植物：他找到了很多植物，找 means “to look for,” while 找到 means “to find successfully.” The result complement 到 and 了 show that the finding was completed.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 有意思 -- “植物很有意思”：奇特，emphasizes that the plants have unusual qualities；有趣，emphasizes that learning about the plants attracts Shennong’s attention.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一个男人觉得植物很有意思。他叫神农：一个叫神农的男人觉得植物很有意思，at first mention, introduce the character with both the name and a noun phrase, rather than naming him only in the next sentence.
- Expressiveness: After “植物很有意思,” add a concrete feature that Shennong notices, such as “有的叶子又大又绿，有的花很香,” to show why he is interested.
- Expressiveness: At the stomachache moment, add Shennong’s feeling or thought, such as “神农想：为什么这种植物会让我肚子疼？”, to make his reaction more vivid.
- Expressiveness: You could make the physical contrast clearer with “有的植物吃了以后，他的肚子疼得要命；有的植物吃了以后，他觉得身体很舒服。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly contrast two kinds of plants with “有的……可是有的……,” and you show their different effects on Shennong’s body. This gives the scene a clear and interesting starting point.

## Scene 2

> 凉山很大的山，这也找植物。一天，他找到黄色的花。他听说你跟黄色的花做茶很有用。比方说，你的头发疼死，这冰茶是药。他一喝茶就很不舒服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 凉山很大的山：凉山是一座很大的山，A noun sentence describing what a place is needs the copula “是”; “一座” is the matching measure word for a mountain.
- 你跟黄色的花做茶很有用：用黄色的花做的茶很有用，“用” introduces the material used to make something, while “跟” does not; the verb phrase needs “的” before “茶” to describe the tea.
- 头发疼死：头疼死了，“头发” means hair, but a headache is expressed with “头疼”; “了” follows the result complement “死” to show the strong painful state has occurred.
- 这冰茶：这杯冰茶，A demonstrative such as “这” needs a measure word before a countable noun.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 有用 -- “用黄色的花做的茶很有用”：能治病，specifies that the tea is useful because it can treat illness in this context.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 这也找植物：神农也在那里找植物，Use the character’s name again after the description of the new place, because “这” cannot clearly refer to the person who is doing the searching; “那里” clearly refers back to the mountain.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他找到黄色的花：他找到了黄色的花，The completed finding action needs “了” after the resultative complement “到”.
- Expressiveness: When the yellow flowers appear, add a visual detail to make the setting clearer, for example: “那儿开着黄色的花，像星星一样，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: After drinking the tea, show the character’s physical feeling more vividly, for example: “他一喝茶，肚子就疼了起来。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought when he feels ill, for example: “他想：我会不会生病了？”

### Encouragement
- You used a clear colour detail with “黄色的花,” and your “一喝……就……” pattern successfully shows that the uncomfortable feeling happened immediately after drinking the tea.

## Scene 3

> 糟糕，这黄色的花是夹竹桃，特别是毒。正好在这儿，灵芝药的夹竹桃他吃完了，也写一个课文的植物。谢谢神农。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 这黄色的花：这朵黄色的花，A demonstrative before a countable noun needs a matching measure word; use 朵 for a flower.
- 特别是毒：特别有毒，Use 有毒 to describe a plant that contains poison. 毒 is a noun here and cannot follow 特别是 in this meaning.
- 灵芝药的夹竹桃：灵芝和夹竹桃，The original combines nouns with 的, which creates an incorrect modifier relationship. Use 和 to coordinate the two plant names.
- 也写一个课文的植物：也把这些植物写进一本书里，课文 is a lesson text, not a book for recording plant information. Use 一本书, and use 写进 to show that the information is recorded in the book.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 写进：写进了，Use 了 after the resultative complement 进 to show that the recording action was completed: 写进了.
- Expressiveness: After “糟糕”, add Shen Nong’s physical reaction to make the danger more vivid, for example: “神农的肚子疼了起来，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: When mentioning the two plants, add a short line of speech that clearly contrasts their uses, for example: “神农说：‘灵芝是药，夹竹桃有毒，不能吃。’”
- Expressiveness: At the writing spot, mention what he records, for example: “他写下了灵芝可以做药，夹竹桃不能吃。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly identified the important plants, 夹竹桃 and 灵芝, and you used “糟糕” to show that the narrator recognizes danger.
