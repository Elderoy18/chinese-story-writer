## Scene 1

> 很久以前，古代的人很不知道什么植物能吃，也什么植物不能吃。所以很多人常常吃不安全的植物，吃不安全的植物对你的身体有坏处，很麻烦的情况。但是一个人，神农，要解决这个问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很不知道：都不知道，Degree adverbs such as 很 do not normally modify the negative knowledge verb 不知道 here; 都 correctly indicates that the people generally lacked this knowledge.
- 吃不安全的植物对你的身体有坏处，很麻烦的情况：吃这些植物会对人们的身体有坏处，是一种很麻烦的情况，The original ends with a noun phrase and lacks a predicate. Add 会 to show a likely harmful result and 是 to make the final phrase a complete sentence.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不安全 -- “很多人常常吃不安全的植物”：有毒的，identifies the specific danger in this story: plants containing poison；有害的，states more precisely that the plants harm the body.
- 有坏处 -- “对人们的身体有坏处”：有伤害，more specifically describes physical harm to the body；造成伤害，emphasizes that eating the plants produces the harmful result.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 也什么植物不能吃：什么植物不能吃，The connector 也 cannot directly introduce this noun phrase; use parallel clauses to name the two kinds of plants clearly.
- Coherence (Reference): 对你的身体：对人们的身体，The narration is about ancient people in general, so 你的 incorrectly shifts the reference to the reader. Repeat 人们 for the general group.
- Coherence (Reference): 一个人，神农：一个叫神农的人，On first mention, introduce the character with a full noun phrase that gives both his name and who he is.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 但是一个人，神农，要解决这个问题：于是，一个叫神农的人要解决这个问题，The relationship is cause and effect: because people did not know which plants were safe, this person wanted to solve the problem. Use a sequence/result connector rather than the contrast connector 但是.
- Expressiveness: After “不安全的植物,” add one concrete result of eating them, such as “有的人吃了以后肚子很疼。”
- Expressiveness: When introducing Shennong, show his purpose or feeling with an inner thought, such as “神农想：我一定要找出能吃的植物。”
- Expressiveness: Add a simple detail about his action to make his decision vivid, such as “他带着一个小袋子，准备去山上找植物。”

### Encouragement
You clearly establish the central problem—people did not know which plants were edible—and introduce Shennong as someone who wants to help. Your use of “所以” also shows that you are thinking about how one event leads to another.

## Scene 2

> 他又聪明又努力，旅行很远找到很多植物。比方说，灵芝和夹竹桃。他一吃各种各样的植物就知道能不能吃这些植物。然后，神农写他的主意。他喜欢植物的味道，特别是花。神农遇到了一个黄色的花，一吃就觉得不舒服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 旅行很远：到很远的地方旅行，A travel destination should be expressed with a place phrase; “very far” cannot directly follow this verb in this way.
- 特别是花：特别是花的味道，The item after this phrase should be the specific kind of “taste” being liked.
- 遇到了一个黄色的花：看到了一朵黄色的花，This verb is normally used for encountering people or events; use a perception verb for a flower. The matching measure word for a flower is “朵,” not the general measure word “个.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 努力 -- “他又聪明又努力”：勤奋，emphasizes steady and persistent hard work.
- 不舒服 -- “一吃就觉得不舒服”：难受，more vividly conveys physical discomfort.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 然后，神农：然后，他，Use a pronoun when the same character was mentioned recently and no other character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农遇到了：他遇到了，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence of the same character.
- Coherence (Temporality): 找到很多植物：找到了很多植物，Use “了” for the completed action of finding the plants.
- Coherence (Temporality): 然后，神农写他的主意：然后，他写了他的主意，Use “了” because this is a completed event in a sequence introduced by “然后.”
- Expressiveness: When describing the flower, add a visual comparison: “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花。”
- Expressiveness: After the character feels unwell, describe a physical reaction: “他的肚子疼了起来，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Add an internal question to show fear: “他想：‘我会死吗？’”

### Encouragement
- You used the conditional pattern “一……就……” effectively to show an immediate result. You also included specific plant names, which makes the scene easier to picture.

## Scene 3

> 发现了夹竹桃有毒。别人看到他，很快给他吃一个灵芝。神农一吃就觉得比较好。然后，他继续写他发现的信息。会告诉他的朋友哪一个植物能不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 会告诉他的朋友哪一个植物能不能吃：他会告诉他的朋友哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃，an embedded question cannot use both a selection question with “哪一个” and a yes-or-no form with “能不能” in this way. Use parallel clauses to state which plants are edible and which are not.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 比较好 -- “神农一吃就觉得比较好”：好一些，more naturally describes a small improvement in his physical condition.
- 信息 -- “他继续写他发现的信息”：发现的内容，more specifically refers to the things he learned about the plants.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 别人看到他：一个人看到他，“别人” distinguishes other people from a stated person or group, but no such contrast is present here. This is a first mention, so introduce the new character with a full noun phrase.
- Coherence (Temporality): 给他吃一个灵芝：给他吃了一个灵芝，this is the final completed action in a series, so it needs the completion marker “了”.
- Coherence (Temporality): 然后，他继续写他发现的信息：然后，他继续写了他发现的信息，this is a completed narrative action, so “了” marks its completion.
- Expressiveness: After the poison is discovered, add a physical detail about Shen Nong’s recovery, such as “神农的肚子慢慢不疼了。”
- Expressiveness: Show Shen Nong’s feeling after receiving the medicine, such as “神农很感谢那个人。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech when he tells his friends, such as “夹竹桃有毒，不能吃！”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the important sequence of discovering the poison, taking the medicine, recovering, and sharing the new knowledge. You also use “然后” well to move the scene forward.
