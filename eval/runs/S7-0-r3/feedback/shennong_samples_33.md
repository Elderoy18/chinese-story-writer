## Scene 1

> 古代时候，人不知道他们能吃什么植物，常常一吃就死。但是不知道为什么，没有办法。所以有一天，神农出去试一试很多种植物，告诉人什么植物是安全什么是危险。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代时候，人不知道他们能吃什么植物：在古代，人们不知道什么植物能吃，Use “在古代” for a time setting; “人们” refers to a concrete group of people, while “人” is generic. In an embedded question about ability, use “什么植物能吃”; the pronoun “他们” is unnecessary here.
- 什么植物是安全什么是危险：什么植物是安全的，什么植物是危险的，Use “的” after the adjective in this “是……的” pattern, and repeat “什么植物” to make the two categories parallel and clear.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：一个叫神农的男人，This is the character’s first mention, so introduce him with a full noun phrase rather than using only his name.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉人什么植物是安全什么是危险：告诉了人们什么植物是安全的，什么植物是危险的，Use “了” for the completed final action in this series: he went out, tried the plants, and then informed people.
- Expressiveness: At “常常一吃就死,” add a concrete consequence to make the danger more vivid, such as “很多人因为吃了有毒的植物，常常生病。”
- Expressiveness: After introducing the man, add his purpose or inner thought, such as “他想：我一定要找出能吃的植物。”
- Expressiveness: Add one small action detail to the experiment, such as “他看到一种植物，就摘下来尝一尝。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish the problem at the beginning and connect it to the character’s reason for acting with “所以.”
- The contrast between safe and dangerous plants gives the scene a clear focus.

- 什么都花想吃：什么花都想吃，when a question word is followed by 都 to mean “every/any,” the noun must stay with the question word: 什么花都.

### Vocabulary Suggestions

### Coherence & Expressiveness

## Scene 3

> 他发现这个口味，一百岁时候，不小心吃了毒的花。一吃就开始死。但是生病时候告诉人什么植物是药，什么植物是毒。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 毒的花：有毒的花，Use 有毒的 before a noun to describe a poisonous plant; 毒 alone does not work as this attributive adjective.
- 告诉人：告诉了人们，This is a completed informing action in the narrative, so it needs 了; 人 is generic, while 人们 refers to the group of people he informed.
- 什么植物是毒：什么植物有毒，Use 有毒 to state that a plant is poisonous; 是毒 is not the natural predicate here.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不小心 -- “不小心吃了有毒的花”：误食了，specifically means accidentally ate something, especially something harmful or unsuitable.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 这个口味：这个味道，The reference rule requires 这 to point to a clear earlier noun; 口味 usually means a person’s food preference, while 味道 refers to the taste of something.
- Coherence (Temporality): 一百岁时候：一百岁的时候，The time-expression pattern needs 的 before 时候 when the preceding phrase is not a monosyllabic time word.
- Coherence (Temporality): 生病时候：生病的时候，The time-expression pattern needs 的 in a verb phrase followed by 时候.
- Expressiveness: After the accidental eating, add a physical reaction to show the danger, for example: “他的肚子疼了起来，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: When he informs people, add a short line of speech, for example: “这种花有毒，不能吃！”
- Expressiveness: Describe the flower briefly before he eats it, for example: “那朵黄花又大又漂亮，像星星一样。”

### Encouragement
