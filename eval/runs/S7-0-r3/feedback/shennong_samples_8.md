## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个人叫神农。一天，神农正在路上走，一看很多人正在吃一些植物。一些人一吃植物就生病了，神农想帮别人。所以想知道哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 already names a time period, so adding 的时候 is redundant.
- 别人：人们，别人 distinguishes other people from the speaker or a specified person; here 神农 wants to help the general population.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 想知道 -- “所以想知道哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃”：弄清，emphasizes finding a clear answer through investigation.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 一看很多人正在吃一些植物：一看到很多人正在吃一些植物，就……，the 一……就…… pattern needs 就 to link the first event to the immediate result; 看 should be 看到 here because 神农 sees the people.
- Expressiveness: After 神农 sees the sick people, add his concern: “神农看见他们生病了，心里很着急。”
- Expressiveness: At the end, add a short inner question to show his purpose: “神农想：‘这些植物到底能不能吃呢？’”

### Encouragement
- You introduce 神农 clearly at the beginning and use 一……就…… well to show that eating certain plants quickly leads to illness.

## Scene 2

> 然后，神农开始尝很多植物，比方说他吃过生姜、芥兰和菠菜。一些植物吃真好，特别是黄瓜。但是，一些植物毒得很，特别是夹竹桃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一些植物吃真好：一些植物吃起来真好，Use the complement pattern “verb + 起来” to describe the general quality or feeling of doing an action, such as how these plants taste when eaten.
- 一些植物毒得很：一些植物有毒，The adjective “毒” cannot be intensified with “得很”; use “有毒” to say that a plant is poisonous.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 尝 -- “神农开始尝很多植物”：尝试，emphasizes that he is testing plants to learn whether they are safe or useful.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the list of plants, add a short detail about Shen Nong’s careful testing, such as “他每次只吃一点儿，看看身体有没有不舒服。”
- Expressiveness: At the poisonous-plant sentence, add his physical reaction to make the danger vivid, such as “他吃了夹竹桃以后，马上觉得肚子疼。”
- Expressiveness: Add a brief thought after he finds the dangerous plant, such as “神农想：这种植物不能让别人吃。”

### Encouragement
You use “然后” well to continue from the earlier scene, and the contrast between safe plants and poisonous plants is clear. The examples of specific plants also make the scene more concrete.

## Scene 3

> 一个人对神农说，麻烦我们可以吃什么植物？神农说，这就要看你想不想生病了。以后，神农告诉别人一些植物能吃，一些植物不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 麻烦：请问，For a polite question, use “请问”. “麻烦” needs a listener and a requested action, such as “麻烦你……”, so it cannot introduce this question by itself.
- 以后：后来，“以后” does not begin a new sentence to mean “afterwards”; use “后来” to introduce the next event in the story.
- 别人：人们，“别人” means other people in contrast with a particular person or group. Here the meaning is people in general, so “人们” is needed.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 神农告诉人们一些植物能吃：神农告诉了人们一些植物能吃，The action of telling happens as a completed event in the narrative, so use the completion marker “了” after “告诉”.
- Expressiveness: At the person’s question, add a worried feeling to show why he or she asks. Example: “请问，我们可以吃什么植物？我怕吃了会生病。”
- Expressiveness: In Shennong’s reply, add one concrete example of a safe and unsafe plant. Example: “像灵芝一样的植物可以吃，可是有毒的夹竹桃不能吃。”

### Encouragement
- You used the A-not-A pattern “想不想” correctly after “看”, and your scene clearly continues the topic of learning which plants can be eaten.
