## Scene 2

> 因为他们常常搬了家，中国人说，孟母三迁。孟子没有爸爸，所以，他的妈妈更重要。从这儿到那儿，妈妈教孟子很多技能。先她让孟子跟朋友玩儿，然后教他怎么买又便宜又好质量的东西。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 先她让孟子跟朋友玩儿：她先让孟子跟朋友玩儿，In an ordinary statement, the time-order adverbial comes after the subject: subject + 先 + verb.
- 好质量的东西：质量好的东西，Chinese normally uses the pattern noun + adjective + 的 before the head noun; the quality is good, so the phrase should be “质量好的”.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很多 -- “妈妈教孟子很多技能”：各种，emphasizes that the skills are of different types, which fits the following examples of playing and buying things.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 妈妈教孟子很多技能：她教孟子很多技能，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence: the mother was mentioned in the immediately preceding sentence, and no other female character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Temporality): 常常搬了家：常常搬家，Use the zero particle for a habitual action; “常常” describes something that happened repeatedly, not one completed move.
- Coherence (Temporality): 然后教他怎么买又便宜又好质量的东西：然后教了他怎么买又便宜又质量好的东西，In a series of completed actions, only the last verb takes 了; here it marks that the teaching action was completed after the earlier action.
- Expressiveness: After the buying lesson, add one market detail to make the setting vivid, such as “市场里很热闹，商人不停地叫卖。”
- Expressiveness: Add Mengzi’s reaction while learning this skill, such as “孟子觉得讨价还价很有意思，认真地听妈妈说。”

### Encouragement
- You use clear time-order words such as “先” and “然后,” which helps the reader follow the mother’s teaching steps. You also give a specific example of what Mengzi learns, rather than only saying that he learns skills.
