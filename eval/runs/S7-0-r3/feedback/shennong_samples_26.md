## Scene 1

> 很久以前，有一个老男人叫神农。神农在古代每一天看到了别的小镇人生病还是不舒服，所以他决定开始找新的药。他下一天开始爬当地的山脉，找了各种各样的植物。神农试一试每个植物，知道有什么好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别的小镇人：小镇上的人们，别的 needs a contrast with an already identified person or group; for the concrete group who live in the town, use the location phrase with 人们.
- 生病还是不舒服：生病或感到不舒服，还是 normally links alternatives in a question or an uncertainty structure; this declarative sentence needs 或, and 感到 makes the physical feeling explicit.
- 找了各种各样的植物：找到了各种各样的植物，找 means “look for,” while 找到 means “find”; the sentence describes plants that he successfully found.
- 每个植物：每种植物，种 is the appropriate measure word for types of plants.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 试一试 -- “神农试一试每个植物”：尝一尝，specifically means tasting something, which more precisely describes how he learns whether a plant is useful.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农在古代每一天看到了别的小镇人生病还是不舒服：他在古代每一天看到了别的小镇人生病还是不舒服，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence: 神农 was introduced in the immediately preceding sentence and no other male character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农试一试每个植物：他试一试每个植物，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence: the previous sentence already names only 神农 as the actor.
- Coherence (Temporality): 每一天看到了：每一天看到，A routine marked by 每一天 uses the zero particle; 了 marks a completed single action, not a habitual action.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 所以：因为……所以……，Use the paired cause-and-effect connector to show that seeing people become ill is the reason for his decision.
- Expressiveness: When he sees ill people, add his concern or determination: “他看见大家常常生病，心里很担心。”
- Expressiveness: At the mountain-climbing moment, add a simple setting detail: “山上有高大的树，也有很多他从来没见过的植物。”
- Expressiveness: Add a brief thought before he tests the plants: “他想：‘我一定要找到能治病的植物。’”

### Encouragement
- You introduced 神农 clearly at the beginning and kept the scene focused on his purpose and actions. Your use of words such as 各种各样 and 山脉 also gives the exploration a strong sense of scale.

## Scene 2

> 比方说，他一次试一试有些植物的叶，吃得又新鲜又甜。煮这些叶以后，他一喝就知道他发现新的茶叶。虽然他找到了很多的新的植物，但是他的旅程有的时候特别是危险。比方说，他一吃灵芝就开始生病。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一次试一试有些植物的叶：尝了有些植物的叶子，Verb reduplication such as “试一试” is for a light or tentative plan, not an action that has already happened in narration; use a completed verb here. “叶子” is also the usual noun form for leaves.
- 吃得又新鲜又甜：这些叶子又新鲜又甜，A phrase with “得” describes the manner or result of the verb action, but “新鲜、甜” describe the leaves themselves, not how he eats.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 新的 -- “很多的新的植物”：陌生的，emphasizes that these are plants he did not know before；未尝过的，makes clear that he has not tasted these plants before.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 煮这些叶以后：煮了这些叶以后，Use “了” before “以后” because cooking the leaves is a completed action before the next event.
- Coherence (Temporality): 知道他发现新的茶叶：发现了新的茶叶，“发现” directly expresses realizing something through experience here, and “了” marks the completed discovery after he drank it.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 虽然他找到了很多的新的植物，但是他的旅程有的时候特别是危险：虽然他找到了很多新的植物，但是他的旅程有的时候特别危险，Use “虽然……但是……” for contrast: finding many plants is positive, while the journey is dangerous. “特别危险” is the correct adverb-adjective structure; “特别是” introduces a specific example instead.
- Expressiveness: After the tea is discovered, add a sensory detail about its taste or smell, such as “茶水闻起来很清香，喝起来甜甜的。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where he becomes ill, show his physical feeling more clearly, such as “他的肚子突然疼了起来，疼得直冒冷汗。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought to show his fear during the illness, such as “他心想：这种植物到底能不能吃？”

### Encouragement
- You use clear time order with actions such as cooking, drinking, and discovering, so the scene is easy to follow.
- You also tried to use the contrast pattern “虽然……但是……”, which is a useful structure for making a story more interesting.

## Scene 3

> 一个爬山的男人正好看到神农的麻烦，然后给了他一些药。神农最终他的小镇回到，跟别的人分享他的发现。你认为神农的故事真的吗？这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 神农的麻烦：神农遇到了麻烦，an abstract problem is not normally the direct object of “see” here; use a clause to say that Shen Nong encountered trouble.
- 最终他的小镇回到：最终回到了他的小镇，place the destination after the directional complement “回到”, and place completed-action “了” after the complement.
- 跟别的人分享：跟小镇的人们分享，“别的人” distinguishes other people from a previously specified person or group; “人们” clearly refers to the people in the town.
- 你认为神农的故事真的吗？：你认为神农的故事是真的吗？After “认为”, the embedded yes-or-no question needs “是” before “真的吗”.
- 看你相信中药对身体有好处：看你相不相信中药对身体有好处，after “这就要看”, use the yes-or-no form to express that the answer depends on whether the reader believes it.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 药 -- “给了他一些药”：解毒药，specifies that the medicine is meant to counter the harmful effects of the plant.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他的发现：自己的发现，when the possessor is the same person as the subject, “自己的” makes that relationship clear.
- Expressiveness: At the moment the man finds Shen Nong, add a physical detail to show the trouble: “神农躺在地上，肚子疼得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: After the medicine is given, add Shen Nong’s feeling to make the recovery more vivid: “神农吃了药以后，觉得好多了。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech when Shen Nong shares his discovery: “这种植物有毒，不能吃。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the helper’s action with “正好” and “然后”, and “给了” correctly marks that the action was completed. Your final question also directly invites the reader to think about the story.
