## Scene 1

> 有一天孟子去了墓地玩儿，但是他妈妈觉得这是不合适的地方让孩子玩，所以她决定了他们会搬家找更好的地方。她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福，而且住在这儿教育不太好。因为她望子成龙，所以她会一直搬家才能找到质量好的教育。然后他们搬到了离城市更近的地方。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 这是不合适的地方让孩子玩：这是一个不适合孩子玩的地方，Use the pattern “适合 + person + verb phrase”; “不合适的地方让孩子玩” does not form a natural modifier for “地方.”
- 她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福：她担心儿子的童年不会幸福，恐怕 means “I am afraid that” and cannot take another subject as the person who is afraid; 担心 correctly expresses the mother’s worry.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 更好 -- “找更好的地方”：条件更好的地方，specifies that the new place has better living or learning conditions；更适合孩子成长的地方，makes clear why the place is better for the child.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 有一天孟子去了墓地玩儿：有一天，有一个小男孩叫孟子，他去了墓地玩儿，This is a first mention, so introduce the character with a full noun phrase before using later references.
- Coherence (Temporality): 她决定了他们会搬家找更好的地方：她决定搬家，找更好的地方，决定 is a mental-state verb and takes the zero particle, not 了; after 决定, use a verb phrase directly without repeating the subject.
- Expressiveness: After the visit to the cemetery, add a concrete detail about what the child does there, such as “孟子学着别人祭拜，还觉得很好玩。”
- Expressiveness: At the mother’s decision, add her inner thought or quoted speech, such as “孟母心想：‘住在这儿会影响孟子的成长。’”
- Expressiveness: Describe the new area more vividly after the final sentence, such as “那里每天都有商人卖东西，街上很热闹。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the mother’s reason for moving by using a cause-and-effect connection, and the final sentence moves the scene forward naturally.
- You also use completed-action forms well in “去了” and “搬到了,” which makes the events easy to follow.

## Scene 2

> 他们住才一月就又搬家。古代的时候，一般人不喜欢卖东西的人。但是孟子老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿，害得妈妈着急得很，因为她恐怕儿子会成为跟他们很像。她告诉了孟子：他们不是朋友，而是不好的人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他们住才一月就又搬家：他们才住了一个月，就又搬家了，In the 才…就… pattern, 才 comes before the duration; a completed period of living takes 了, and 就 introduces the following result.
- 孟子老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿：孟子老是跟这些卖东西的人玩儿，Do not use two frequency adverbs with the same habitual meaning together; both 老是 and 常常 modify the verb as “often.”
- 她恐怕儿子会：她担心儿子会，恐怕 cannot mean that another subject is afraid; use 担心 when the subject 她 is worried about a future event.
- 成为跟他们很像：变得跟他们很像，成为 needs a noun phrase after it, while 跟他们很像 is an adjective phrase describing a change of state.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 卖东西的人 -- “这些卖东西的人”：商人，names their role more precisely as people who do business and sell goods.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 她告诉了孟子：她告诉孟子：A saying verb that introduces direct speech takes the zero particle, not 了.
- Expressiveness: After the description of the sellers, add a detail showing the busy market atmosphere, such as “街上有人大声叫卖，也有人拿着东西走来走去。”
- Expressiveness: Show the mother’s private reaction before she speaks, such as “妈妈看着孟子，心里越来越担心。”
- Expressiveness: Make the mother’s warning more vivid with a short line of speech, such as “你不能每天只想着玩儿，要好好学习。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show why the mother is worried, and you use the structure 害得妈妈着急得很 effectively to connect Mengzi’s behaviour with her reaction.

## Scene 3

> 你不是当数学家，而是当哲学家，不能当卖东西的人。所以他们又搬家。在他们第三个家，孟子学了怎么祭拜。妈妈感觉这个地方适合他们，告诉了孟子：我们找到了最后的家。在这儿妈妈让儿子从早到晚学习，他难怪成为有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在他们第三个家：在他们的第三个家里，A possessive modifier needs 的 here, and a location phrase with a home naturally uses 里.
- 告诉了孟子：告诉孟子，A speaking verb that introduces direct speech normally takes the zero particle rather than 了.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 卖东西的人 -- “不能当卖东西的人”：商人，names the occupation more precisely.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 所以他们又搬家：所以他们又搬家了，Use 了 for the completed move before the next events in the new home.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为有名的哲学家：成为了有名的哲学家，Use 了 to mark the completed change of becoming a philosopher.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他难怪成为了有名的哲学家：难怪他成为了有名的哲学家，难怪 introduces the inferred result, so it comes before the clause that explains the result.
- Expressiveness: At the description of the third home, add a detail about the learning environment: “新家就在学校旁边，四周很安静。”
- Expressiveness: After the mother decides the place is suitable, show her relief or happiness: “妈妈终于放心了，脸上露出了笑容。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of dialogue when the mother asks her son to study: “妈妈说：‘你要认真读书，将来做一个有学问的人。’”

### Encouragement
- You used the contrast pattern with 不是……而是…… clearly, and you connected the family’s move to the mother’s decision with 所以.
- Your scene also shows the important change from moving homes to studying, which gives the events a clear direction.
