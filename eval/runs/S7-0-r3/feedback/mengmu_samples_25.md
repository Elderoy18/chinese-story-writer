## Scene 1

> 这是古代的故事。孟子小的时候，他的爸爸很早就去世了。从他的家到墓地很近，所以他每天老是从早到晚去墓地跟朋友们玩玩儿。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 玩玩儿：玩，the reduplicated form suggests playing only briefly or casually, which conflicts with the long duration expressed by “从早到晚”.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 去世 -- “他的爸爸很早就去世了”：过世，a gentler and more respectful way to describe someone’s death in this family-story context

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他的家：孟子的家，repeat the name after another male character, “爸爸”, has appeared; this makes the possessor of the home unambiguous.
- Coherence (Reference): 他每天：孟子每天，repeat the name when the immediately preceding sentence also mentions his father, so the pronoun cannot be read as referring to the father.
- Expressiveness: After “去墓地”, add a small visual detail about the people there, such as “他每天看到来祭拜的人在墓地哭”.
- Expressiveness: Add Mengzi’s feeling or reason for copying what he sees, such as “孟子觉得祭拜很有趣，也想学他们”.

### Encouragement
- You introduced Mengzi and his father clearly, and “很早就去世了” effectively gives important background in a natural way.
- “从早到晚” is a strong time expression that helps the reader picture how often Mengzi went to the cemetery.

## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈觉得在墓地玩儿，害得，所以他们搬家。搬家对孟子有好的影响，他才五岁，可是每天不是写字就是帮妈妈的工作。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在墓地玩儿，害得：孟子在墓地玩儿会害得来祭拜的人不高兴，The structure “害得” must be followed by the person affected and a harmful result.
- 可是：就，Use the pattern “才 + age/number + 就 + verb phrase” to show that someone did something at an unusually early age.
- 帮妈妈的工作：帮妈妈做事，After “帮 + person,” use a verb phrase to state the help given; “妈妈的工作” is a noun phrase and cannot directly complete this structure.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好的 -- “搬家对孟子有好的影响”：积极的，specifies that the influence is beneficial rather than merely “good.”

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他的妈妈：孟子的妈妈，This is the mother’s first mention in the story, so introduce her with a full noun phrase rather than a pronoun-based phrase.
- Expressiveness: After the visitors are affected, add a visible reaction to make the problem clearer, such as “来祭拜的人皱着眉头，看起来很生气。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where the mother decides to move, add her inner thought or speech, such as “孟子的妈妈心想：‘不能再住在这儿了。’”

### Encouragement
- You used “所以” to show the result of the mother’s concern, and you also used the “不是……就是……” pattern well to describe Mengzi’s daily activities.

## Scene 3

> 他很聪明的人，难怪他成为有名的人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他很聪明的人：他很聪明，An adjective such as “聪明” can serve directly as the predicate; “人” cannot be added after it without a structure such as “是……的人”.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 难怪他成为有名的人：难怪成为有名的人，Use zero form when the same character remains the subject in the same sentence, rather than repeating the pronoun.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为有名的人：成为了有名的人，Use 了 after “成为” to mark the completed result in this past narrative.
- Expressiveness: After “他很聪明”, add a small detail that shows his intelligence, such as “他常常认真思考问题。”
- Expressiveness: You could show his mother’s pride with a short thought or quotation, such as “孟母高兴地想：‘孩子终于长大了！’”

### Encouragement
- You used “难怪” effectively to connect his intelligence with his later achievement. The sentence also gives the scene a clear concluding feeling.
