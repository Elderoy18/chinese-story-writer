## Scene 1

> 孟子小的时候，从早到晚都跟朋友玩。孟子和他的朋友喜欢玩在墓地里，装祭拜。孟子五岁的时候，跟妈妈搬家。他们搬家不是因为孟子玩得太多，而是因为妈妈认为他们的家离墓地太近。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 玩在墓地里：在墓地里玩，A location phrase with 在 should come before the main verb 玩.
- 装祭拜：假装祭拜，装 cannot directly introduce an action in this meaning; 假装 means “pretend to” and can be followed by an action.
- 跟妈妈搬家：孟子的妈妈带着他搬家，跟妈妈搬家 means that Mengzi moved together with his mother, but the intended meaning is that his mother took him when she moved; use 带着孟子/他搬家.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和他的朋友：他和他的朋友，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence: Mengzi was named in the immediately preceding sentence, and no competing male character makes 他 unclear.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子五岁的时候：他五岁的时候，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence of the same character.
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬家：搬了家，Use 了 for the completed move in this past narrative event.
- Expressiveness: After the friends’ activity in the cemetery, add a concrete action to show what “pretending to worship” looks like, for example: “他们笑着学别人鞠躬。”
- Expressiveness: At the final sentence, add the mother’s feeling to make the reason for moving more vivid, for example: “妈妈很担心，觉得这里会影响孟子。”
- Expressiveness: You could include a short thought from the mother after she notices the cemetery is too close, for example: “妈妈心想：‘我们不能住在这儿了。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish Mengzi’s childhood habit and then give a reason for the family’s move. The contrast structure “不是……而是……” is used well to distinguish the real reason from an incorrect one.

## Scene 2

> 妈妈认为，住离墓地太近，害得孟子的心气。孟子和孟母搬到子思老师的家，跟子思老师住。孟母帮子思打扫房间和做饭。子思帮孟母教她的儿子。因为孟子现在没有朋友，他老是读书。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 住离墓地太近，害得孟子的心气：住在离墓地太近的地方，会影响孟子的学习，住 requires 在 before a place phrase, and 害得 must be followed by a person plus a harmful result clause; it cannot end with the possessive noun phrase 孟子的心气.
- 老是：一直，老是 usually carries a negative or complaining tone; 一直 neutrally describes a continuing habit.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为孟子现在没有朋友，他一直读书：因为孟子现在没有朋友，所以他一直读书，because this is a cause-and-effect relationship, 因为 should be paired with 所以.
- Expressiveness: At the mother’s opinion, add a small action and feeling to show her worry: “孟母皱着眉头，担心孟子会学坏。”
- Expressiveness: After the teacher begins helping, add Mengzi’s reaction to make the change of environment clearer: “孟子觉得子思老师很有学问，开始认真听他讲课。”
- Expressiveness: At the final sentence, add a brief thought from Mengzi: “孟子心想：‘虽然没有朋友，可是读书也很有意思。’”

### Encouragement

## Scene 3

> 子思认为，对孩子来说，读书最重要，特别是学哲学。孟子不是练习写文章，就是看哲学书。孟子从五岁一直学哲学。他才五岁就学哲学，难怪他成为很有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 成为很有名的哲学家：成为了很有名的哲学家，Use “成为了” for the completed change of becoming a philosopher; “成为” here describes a finished result in the past.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 有名 -- “他成为了很有名的哲学家”：享有盛名，emphasizes that he was widely known and respected for his reputation.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子从五岁一直学哲学：他从五岁一直学哲学，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence: “孟子” was the subject of the immediately preceding sentence, and no competing male character appears there.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 从五岁一直学哲学：从五岁开始一直学哲学，Use “从 + time + 开始” to mark the starting point of a continuing action.
- Expressiveness: After “孟子不是练习写文章，就是看哲学书”, add a detail about his focused behaviour, such as “他常常安静地坐在桌前，一看就是很久。”
- Expressiveness: At the final sentence, add a brief thought or reaction from the teacher, such as “子思看着他，心想：‘这个孩子以后一定会很有学问。’”

### Encouragement
- You used the “不是……就是……” pattern accurately to show that Mengzi spent his time on two kinds of study. The “才五岁就……” sentence also clearly emphasizes how early he began learning philosophy.
