## Scene 1

> 古代，孟子跟孟母住在墓地的旁边。可是孟母注意，住在墓地的旁边影响孟子。他常常跟朋友们祭拜玩，所以孟母决定他们会搬家。这次，他们住在城市里的商店的旁边，从早到晚很吵。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 孟母注意：孟母注意到，The verb needs the result complement 到 when it means “notice” a fact or situation.
- 他常常跟朋友们祭拜玩：他常常跟朋友们学着别人祭拜，祭拜 is the action of paying respects and cannot be followed by 玩 to mean “imitate for fun”; 学着别人祭拜 clearly expresses copying what other people do.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很吵 -- “从早到晚很吵”：嘈杂，describes a place filled with many mixed, loud sounds；喧闹，adds the sense of constant noisy activity.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 古代，孟子跟孟母住在墓地的旁边：在古代，有一个小男孩叫孟子，他和妈妈住在墓地的旁边，first mention should briefly introduce a character with a name and identifying information before using the character in the story.
- Coherence (Reference): 他们会搬家：带着孟子搬家，after 决定, use a verb phrase directly; the mother’s decision can naturally include 带着孟子 without repeating the pronoun reference.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他常常跟朋友们祭拜玩，所以孟母决定他们会搬家：因为他常常跟朋友们学着别人祭拜，所以孟母决定带着孟子搬家，use 因为……所以…… to mark the cause-and-effect relation between Mengzi’s behaviour and his mother’s decision.
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery description, add a concrete detail about what Mengzi sees, such as “他每天看到有人穿着黑衣服，哭着来墓地祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: Show Mengmu’s worry through an inner thought before her decision, such as “孟母心想：‘要是一直住在这儿，孟子会学坏的。’”
- Expressiveness: Make the noisy new neighbourhood more vivid with a sound detail, such as “商人一直大声叫卖，马车也不停地走来走去。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the contrast between the quiet cemetery setting and the noisy city setting. You also use a cause-and-effect idea to explain why Mengmu wants to move.

## Scene 3

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 才五岁孟子开始学哲学：孟子才五岁就开始学哲学，The pattern for an unexpectedly early action is 才 + age + 就 + verb phrase.

### Vocabulary Suggestions

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟母和孟子：他们，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the two characters have already been named and no competing pair is present.
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬到学校的旁边住：搬到了学校的旁边住，Use 了 after 搬到 to mark the completed move in this past narrative.
- Coherence (Temporality): 将来：后来，Use 后来 to introduce a later completed event in a past narrative; 将来 refers to the future.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为中国的一个非常有名的哲学家：成为了中国的一个非常有名的哲学家，Use 了 with 成为 to show that becoming a philosopher was completed.

- Expressiveness: Add Meng Mu’s feeling and a short line of speech after she sees Meng Zi studying, such as “孟母很高兴，说：‘这儿的环境很适合孩子。’”
