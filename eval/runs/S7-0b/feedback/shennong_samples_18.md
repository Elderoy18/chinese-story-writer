## Scene 1

> 神农和他的家人住在古代，所以不知道什么植物能吃。因为他的家人常常生病了，所以神农和他的哥哥一起去旅行找可以吃的植物。他们去很多不一样的地方，比方说高山、山谷和沙漠。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 住在古代：在古代，神农和他的家人住在一起，古代 is a time expression, not a place where someone can live; use 在古代 to set the time.
- 常常生病了：常常生病，常常 marks a habitual action or state, so it takes the zero aspect particle rather than completed-action 了.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 找 -- “去旅行找可以吃的植物”：寻找，emphasizes actively looking for the plants；探寻，emphasizes exploring in search of an answer.
- 常常 -- “他的家人常常生病”：反复，more specifically shows that the illness happened again and again.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：一个叫神农的男人，this is the character’s first mention, so introduce him with a fuller noun phrase before using his name in the story.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们去很多不一样的地方：他们去了很多不一样的地方，use 了 after 去 to mark the completed narrative action of visiting many places.
- Expressiveness: After the family’s illnesses, add Shen Nong’s concern: 神农很担心家人的身体，决定去找能吃的植物。
- Expressiveness: When they travel, add one concrete landscape detail: 高山又高又陡，山谷里开着很多花。
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of dialogue before the journey: 神农对哥哥说：“我们一起去找对身体有好处的植物吧！”

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect the family’s illness with Shen Nong’s reason for searching for edible plants. You also use a varied list of places—high mountains, valleys, and deserts—to make the journey feel broad and adventurous.
