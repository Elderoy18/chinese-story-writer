## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈觉得在墓地玩儿，害得，所以他们搬家。搬家对孟子有好的影响，他才五岁，可是每天不是写字就是帮妈妈的工作。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 觉得在墓地玩儿，害得：觉得孟子在墓地玩儿会惹麻烦，The embedded action needs a clear subject, and the structure “害得” must be followed by the person affected and a harmful result.
- 帮妈妈的工作：帮妈妈工作，“帮” can take a person plus a verb phrase; “妈妈的工作” is a noun phrase and cannot directly follow “帮” here.
- 他才五岁，可是每天：他才五岁，就每天，Use the pattern “才 + number + 就 + verb phrase” to show that an action or habit began unusually early.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好 -- “有好的影响”：积极的影响, specifies that the influence is positive rather than merely “good”；有益的影响, emphasizes that the influence benefits Mengzi’s growth.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 所以他们搬家：所以他们搬家了，Use “了” after a completed action; the move happened as the result of the mother’s decision.
- Expressiveness: After the mother’s concern about the cemetery, add a concrete example of what Mengzi does there, such as “孟子一边笑一边学别人祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: At the decision to move, add the mother’s inner thought or speech, such as “这样会打扰别人，我得带孟子搬家。”
- Expressiveness: When describing Mengzi’s daily routine, show his attitude with a detail such as “孟子每天认真地写字，也帮妈妈工作。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show a cause-and-effect idea: the mother worries about the cemetery, so the family moves. You also use “不是……就是……” well to describe Mengzi’s daily routine.
