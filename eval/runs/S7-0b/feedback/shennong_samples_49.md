## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有很多人不知道什么植物能吃，什么植物不能吃。有很多人吃植物以后就吃坏肚子了，因为他们吃了毒。那个时候，有一个老人叫神农，他什么植物都觉得好吃，不怕生病，不怕毒。他希望别人也可以吃好吃的植物。可是他知道，如果他们还怕吃毒，不会吃他喜欢的植物。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 already expresses a time period, so adding 的时候 is redundant.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好吃 -- “他什么植物都觉得好吃”：味道好，makes it clearer that he is judging the plants by their taste；可口，describes food or plants that taste pleasant.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 如果他们还怕吃毒，不会吃他喜欢的植物：如果他们还怕吃毒，就不会吃他喜欢的植物，use the paired conditional connector 如果……就…… to show the result of the condition.
- Expressiveness: After “吃坏肚子了,” add a physical detail to show the illness more vividly, such as “肚子疼得厉害”.
- Expressiveness: At “有一个老人叫神农,” add Shen Nong’s purpose or inner thought, such as “他想：我要找出什么植物能吃”.
- Expressiveness: When describing Shen Nong’s bravery, add a short evaluative detail, such as “别人都不敢吃，他却愿意试一试”.

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduce Shen Nong with his name and identity before using “他,” which makes the character easy to follow.
- You also use “因为” to explain why people became ill, giving the events a clear cause-and-effect connection.

## Scene 2

> 所以，他上山下山找植物，吃了很多样子的植物。他是第一人，找到了茶。他把所有的植物写下来了。他一直都吃了，他连毒都能吃。可是他还没吃了一个叫夹竹桃的植物。他找到了那个像星星的植物，觉得很漂亮，马上就吃下了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很多样子的植物：很多种植物，Use the measure word “种” to count different kinds of plants; “样子” refers to appearance or shape, not plant types.
- 他是第一人，找到了茶：他是第一个找到茶的人，An ordinal such as “第一个” needs to modify the noun “人”, and the action of finding tea should form a relative clause before that noun.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 找 -- “他上山下山找植物”：寻找，emphasizes an ongoing search for plants over many places；探寻，emphasizes exploring in order to discover unknown plants.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 他一直都吃了：他一直都吃，“一直都” describes an ongoing or habitual action, so it takes the zero aspect particle rather than completed-action “了”.
- Coherence (Temporality): 还没吃了一个叫夹竹桃的植物：还没吃过一个叫夹竹桃的植物，Use “没” with “过” to say that someone has not had the experience of doing something; completed-action “了” cannot follow “没”.
- Expressiveness: When he notices the flower, add visual detail about its colour and shape: “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: Before he eats it, add his thought to show why he acts so quickly: “他想：‘这种花这么漂亮，应该很好吃。’”

### Encouragement
- You use a clear sequence of actions, from searching in the mountains to finding and trying a new plant. Your use of “把所有的植物写下来了” also shows that the character records his discoveries carefully.

## Scene 3

> 可是夹竹桃的毒很重，让他肚子很痛。他在地上躺着，觉得他快会死了。可是正好有别人找到他了，给了他一个像伞的植物，叫灵芝。他吃灵芝以后就觉得很好，不再生病了。他测量起来了，像星星的植物是毒，像伞的植物是药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别人：一个人，别人 distinguishes other people from a stated person or group; here a new helper is being introduced, so an indefinite noun phrase is needed.
- 他测量起来了：他发现，测量 means physically measuring size, length, or quantity; it cannot introduce the conclusion that one plant is poisonous and another is medicine. 发现 correctly expresses learning this information.
- 是毒：有毒，毒 is a noun, while 有毒 means “poisonous” and can describe the plant.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很重 -- “夹竹桃的毒很重”：毒性很强，states more precisely that the plant has strong poisonous properties.
- 很痛 -- “让他肚子很痛”：疼得厉害，gives a more vivid description of the intensity of his pain.
- 很好 -- “就觉得很好”：好受多了，more specifically describes feeling much better after being ill.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: At “他在地上躺着”, add a physical detail to show how unwell he is, such as “他在地上躺着，脸色很难看，疼得直冒汗。”
- Expressiveness: After “觉得他快会死了”, add his worried thought in quoted speech, such as “他想：‘我会不会死在这里？’”
- Expressiveness: When the helper arrives, describe the person’s action or words to make the rescue more vivid, such as “那个人赶快拿起灵芝，说：‘快吃这个！’”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the cause-and-effect sequence: the poisonous plant hurts him, another person helps him, and then he feels better. You also use useful action words such as “躺着” and “给了”, which help the scene move forward.
