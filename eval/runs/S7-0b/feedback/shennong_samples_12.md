## Scene 1

> 很久以前，古代的人很不知道什么植物能吃，也什么植物不能吃。所以很多人常常吃不安全的植物，吃不安全的植物对你的身体有坏处，很麻烦的情况。但是一个人，神农，要解决这个问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很不知道：不知道，The degree adverb 很 cannot modify the negated mental-state verb phrase 不知道.
- 也什么植物不能吃：什么植物不能吃，Also must modify a verb phrase; it cannot be placed directly before the nominal question phrase 什么植物不能吃.
- 很麻烦的情况：这是一种很麻烦的情况，The original is only a noun phrase and cannot stand as a complete clause here; adding 这是一种 creates a complete statement.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不安全 -- “很多人常常吃不安全的植物”：有毒，specifies that the plants are unsafe because they contain poison and fits the harmful effect on the body.
- 有坏处 -- “吃不安全的植物对你的身体有坏处”：有害，states more directly that the plants cause harm to the body.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 你的身体：他们的身体，Use a pronoun that refers back to the already mentioned 很多人; shifting to 你 changes the narrative viewpoint without introducing the reader as a character.
- Coherence (Reference): 一个⼈，神农：一个叫神农的人，On first mention, introduce a character with a full noun phrase containing the name, rather than placing the name after an unclear apposition.
- Expressiveness: After the harmful plants are mentioned, add a concrete physical result to make the danger vivid: “很多人吃了以后肚子疼，还会生病。”
- Expressiveness: When introducing Shennong, add his determined thought or a short line of inner speech: “神农想：我一定要找出能做药的植物。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish the central problem: people do not know which plants are safe to eat. You also use the cause-and-effect idea with 所以 to connect people’s lack of knowledge to their dangerous actions.

## Scene 2

> 他又聪明又努力，旅行很远找到很多植物。比方说，灵芝和夹竹桃。他一吃各种各样的植物就知道能不能吃这些植物。然后，神农写他的主意。他喜欢植物的味道，特别是花。神农遇到了一个黄色的花，一吃就觉得不舒服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 旅行很远找到：旅行到很远的地方，找到了，A destination phrase is needed after the travel verb, and in this completed series of actions only the final verb takes 了.
- 写：写下，Use 写下 when the meaning is to record an idea in writing; 写 alone does not naturally mean “record” here.
- 特别是花：特别是花的味道，The phrase after 特别是 should match the thing being liked: the character likes a flavour, not the flowers themselves.
- 遇到了一个黄色的花：看到了一朵黄色的花，看到 is the natural verb for noticing a flower, and 朵 is the matching measure word for flowers.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农写：他写，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the same character was named in the recent sentence and no other character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Reference): 他的主意：自己的主意，When the possessor is the same person as the subject, use 自己的 to make that relationship clear.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农遇到了：他遇到了，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence; the previous sentence already clearly refers to 神农.
- Expressiveness: After the journey, add one concrete travel detail, such as “他走啊走啊，去了很多高山。” This makes the search feel longer and more difficult.
- Expressiveness: When the yellow flower appears, describe its appearance, such as “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花，又大又漂亮。” This helps the reader picture why he notices it.
- Expressiveness: After he feels ill, show his physical feeling or worried thought, such as “他肚子疼了起来，心想：我会死吗？” This makes the danger more vivid.

### Encouragement
You clearly show that 神农 is active and determined, travelling and trying many kinds of plants. You also used the pattern 一……就…… correctly to show that one action quickly leads to another.

## Scene 3

> 发现了夹竹桃有毒。别人看到他，很快给他吃一个灵芝。神农一吃就觉得比较好。然后，他继续写他发现的信息。会告诉他的朋友哪一个植物能不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别人：一个男人，“别人” means other people in contrast with a previously mentioned person or group; a new, concrete character should be introduced with a full noun phrase.
- 很快给他吃一个灵芝：很快给他吃了一个灵芝，This is a completed event. In a series of actions, only the final verb takes 了, so it follows 吃.
- 写他发现的信息：记下他发现的信息，When recording discovered information, 记下 means to write it down and keep a record; 写 does not fit this meaning as precisely here.
- 哪一个植物能不能吃：哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃，哪一个 asks the listener to choose one specific plant, while 能不能 asks a yes-or-no question. Use parallel clauses to state which plants are edible and which are not.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the medicine is given, add a physical detail to show the recovery, such as “他的肚子慢慢不疼了。”
- Expressiveness: At “一个男人”, describe the new character briefly so the scene is easier to imagine, such as “一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人看到他。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought or line of speech after the medicine works, such as “神农说：‘谢谢你，我好多了！’”

### Encouragement
- You used the pattern “一……就……” effectively to show that the medicine worked immediately. You also kept the scene focused on the important actions: finding the poison, receiving help, and recording the discovery.
