## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个男孩名字叫孟子。孟子和孟子的妈妈两个人住在很穷的农村。他们住的地方旁边是个墓地，墓地老是有很多人祭拜。因为人们在祭拜，他们一直在哭。孟子和孟子的妈妈住在这里才一个星期就觉得受不了了，而且害得孟子的学习。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 already expresses a time period, so adding 的时候 is redundant.
- 害得孟子的学习：还害得孟子没法学习，害得 must be followed by the person affected and a harmful result clause, not by a noun phrase.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子的妈妈两个人：孟子和他的妈妈两个人，after the name has just introduced the boy, use the pronoun 他 for the closely repeated possessor.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子的妈妈住在这里：孟子和他的妈妈住在这里，repeat the name 孟子 after other people have been discussed, but use 他 for the closely connected reference to his mother.
- Coherence (Temporality): 住在这里才一个星期就觉得受不了了：住在这里才住了一个星期就觉得受不了了，住 is a completed duration up to the moment when they begin to feel unable to bear it, so it needs 了.
- Expressiveness: At the cemetery description, add a sound detail to show why the place feels disturbing, for example: 来祭拜的人一直大声哭。
- Expressiveness: At the final sentence, show the mother’s inner worry with a short thought, for example: 孟母心想：“住在这儿会影响孟子的学习。”
- Expressiveness: You could show what Mengzi notices at the cemetery, for example: 孟子觉得人们祭拜的样子很奇怪。

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduce Mengzi, his mother, their home, and the nearby cemetery. The use of 因为 also helps connect the worshippers’ actions with their crying.

## Scene 2

> 孟子的妈妈觉得孟子取得良好的教育是很重要的，所以孟子的妈妈认为这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好，就打算跟孟子搬家。孟子的妈妈带孟子不是搬到城市，而是搬到了孟子老师的家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 取得良好的教育：能受到良好的教育，取得 is used for achievements or results gained through effort, while 受到教育 means “receive an education.”
- 跟孟子搬家：带着孟子搬家，跟孟子搬家 suggests that the mother moves together with him as an equal participant; 带着孟子搬家 correctly shows that she takes her child with her.
- 带孟子不是搬到城市，而是搬到了：没有带孟子搬到城市，而是搬到了，use 没有……而是…… to contrast the actual destination with the destination that did not happen; the original places 不是 after the action in an unnatural way.
- 孟子老师：孟子的老师，孟子老师 can mean a teacher called “Mengzi”; 的 is needed to show that the teacher belongs to or teaches Mengzi.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 地方 -- “这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好”：环境，specifies that it is the surrounding atmosphere and conditions that may affect the child’s education.
- 不太好 -- “对儿子的教育不太好”：不太合适，more precisely says that the place is unsuitable for the child’s education.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 所以孟子的妈妈认为：所以她认为，after the mother has just been named and no other female character competes as the referent, use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子的妈妈带孟子：她带孟子，at the beginning of the next sentence, the mother is still the clear referent, so a pronoun avoids unnecessarily repeating her full noun phrase.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 孟子的妈妈带孟子不是搬到城市，而是搬到了孟子老师的家：后来，孟子的妈妈没有带孟子搬到城市，而是搬到了孟子的老师的家，后来 marks the sequence from making the plan to carrying it out.
- Expressiveness: After the mother decides that the place is unsuitable, add her worried feeling or thought, such as: 孟母很着急，心想：“住在这儿会影响孟子的学习。”
- Expressiveness: You could add a short line of speech when she decides to move, such as: 她说：“我们得找一个更适合学习的地方。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the mother’s reason for moving and connect her concern about education to her decision. The contrast between the two possible destinations also makes the action easy to follow.
