## Scene 1

> 很久以前，有一个老男人叫神农。神农在古代每一天看到了别的小镇人生病还是不舒服，所以他决定开始找新的药。他下一天开始爬当地的山脉，找了各种各样的植物。神农试一试每个植物，知道有什么好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别的小镇人：小镇上的人们，别的 is used to distinguish other people or things from one already mentioned; here the sentence means the people in the town. Use 人们 for a concrete group of people.
- 生病还是不舒服：生病或者不舒服，还是 introduces an alternative in a question or an uncertain choice; use 或者 to join two possibilities in this statement.
- 下一天：第二天，第二天 is the usual narrative time expression for the day after the previous event.
- 每个植物：每种植物，种 is the matching measure word for types of plants.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 每一天看到了：每天看到，every day describes a routine, so use the zero aspect particle rather than completed-action 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 神农试一试每种植物，知道它们有什么好处：神农试了试每种植物，知道它们有什么好处，trying the plants is a completed action in this narrative moment, so it needs 了; 知道 is a mental-state verb and takes the zero particle.
- Expressiveness: At the point where the townspeople are ill, add a concrete symptom to show why the problem worries him: “有的人肚子疼得站不起来。”
- Expressiveness: After he decides to search, add his determined inner thought: “神农想：我一定要找到能治病的植物。”
- Expressiveness: When he climbs the mountains, add a simple landscape detail: “山上长着很多从来没见过的植物。”

### Encouragement
- You introduce 神农 clearly in the first sentence, so the reader immediately knows who the scene is about. You also use a clear cause-and-effect connection with 所以 to show why he begins his search.

## Scene 2

> 比方说，他一次试一试有些植物的叶，吃得又新鲜又甜。煮这些叶以后，他一喝就知道他发现新的茶叶。虽然他找到了很多的新的植物，但是他的旅程有的时候特别是危险。比方说，他一吃灵芝就开始生病。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一次试一试有些植物的叶：试吃了一些植物的叶子，An action that was actually carried out should not use verb reduplication. Use a completed verb such as “试吃了”; “一次” also cannot come before the verb in this structure.
- 吃得又新鲜又甜：觉得又新鲜又甜，The pattern “吃得 + adjective” describes the manner of eating, but “新鲜” and “甜” describe the leaves. Use a perception verb to describe their taste.
- 特别是危险：特别危险，“特别” is an adverb and directly modifies the adjective “危险”; do not insert “是” between them.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 新的 -- “找到了很多的新的植物”：陌生的，emphasizes that these were plants he did not know before; 未尝过的，more specifically shows that he had not tasted these plants before.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他一喝就知道他发现：他一喝就知道发现，Use zero form for the same subject within one sentence; the second “他” is unnecessarily repeated.
- Coherence (Temporality): 煮这些叶以后：煮了这些叶以后，Use “了” before “以后” to show that boiling was completed before the next action.
- Coherence (Temporality): 发现新的茶叶：发现了新的茶叶，Use “了” because the discovery is presented as a completed action.
- Expressiveness: After the tea is boiled, add a sensory detail about its appearance or smell, such as “水变成了淡黄色，还有一股清香”.
- Expressiveness: At the point where he becomes ill, add a physical feeling to show the danger, such as “他的肚子疼得不得了，只能躺在地上”.
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought after he becomes sick to show his worry, such as “他想：这种植物不能吃吗？”.

### Encouragement
You used concrete actions such as “煮” and “喝” to show how the tea was discovered. You also used both “虽然……但是……” and “一……就……” to connect events clearly.

## Scene 3

> 一个爬山的男人正好看到神农的麻烦，然后给了他一些药。神农最终他的小镇回到，跟别的人分享他的发现。你认为神农的故事真的吗？这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他的小镇回到：回到他的小镇，A location object must follow the verb-complement form “回到”; it cannot be placed before “回到”.
- 别的人：别人，“别人” refers to other people as a group; “别的” is normally used before a noun to distinguish a different item or person.
- 神农的故事真的吗：神农的故事是真的吗，An adjectival predicate in this question needs the linking verb “是”.
- 你相信中药对身体有好处：你相不相信中药对身体有好处，The alternative-question pattern “相不相信” expresses “whether you believe”; “看” here introduces something that depends on this choice.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 药 -- “给了他一些药”：草药，specifies that the medicine is made from plants and fits the mountain and traditional-medicine setting.
- 发现 -- “跟别人分享他的发现”：研究成果，emphasizes the useful results of his experiments with plants rather than a single discovery.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 回到：回到了，Use “了” after “回到” because returning to the town is a completed action in the narrative.
- Coherence (Temporality): 这就要看你相不相信中药对身体有好处：这就要看你相不相信中药对身体有好处了，The final “了” belongs to the fixed concluding structure “这就要看……了”; it signals that the answer now depends on that condition.
- Expressiveness: After “一个爬山的男人”, add one visible detail to make the helper easier to imagine, such as “一个穿着蓝色衣服、正在爬山的男人”.
- Expressiveness: At the moment he gives the medicine, add urgency or quoted speech, such as “他赶快给了神农一些药，说：‘快吃这个！’”
- Expressiveness: When Shennong returns, add his feeling about sharing what he learned, such as “神农很高兴，终于能跟别人分享自己的发现了。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect the helper’s action, Shennong’s return, and the final question about traditional medicine. The ending question is also a thoughtful way to invite the reader to consider the story’s message.
