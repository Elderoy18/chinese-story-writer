## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个男人，他的名字是神农。他看到了有很多人不知道哪个植物好吃，也不知道什么植物对你的身体有好处。这个问题神农觉得很糟糕，所以神农去了吃每种植物。他吃了一种植物以后，就写了这个植物对你有没有好处。有的时候，神农吃了很好吃的植物。有的时候，他吃了很危险的植物。他吃完了每种植物以后，会来跟他的朋友聊到他吃的植物了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，The phrase is redundant because 古代 already names a time period, while 的时候 also means “at the time of.”
- 写了这个植物对你有没有好处：写下了这种植物对身体有没有好处，写下 means to record information; it fits writing down what the plant does. Also, the narration is about people in general, so 你 should not suddenly address the reader.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很糟糕 -- “这个问题神农觉得很糟糕”：令人担忧，specifies that the problem causes worry and concern.
- 危险 -- “他吃了很危险的植物”：有害，specifies that the plants can harm someone who eats them.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 所以神农去了吃每种植物：所以去吃了每种植物，Use zero form for a re-occurring character in the same sentence: 神农 was already named in the preceding clause.
- Coherence (Temporality): 去了吃：去吃了，With 去 + action, place 了 after the main action to show completion: 去吃了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 有的时候，神农吃了很好吃的植物：有的时候，神农吃很好吃的植物，Use the zero particle for a habitual action introduced by 有的时候; 了 is not used for routines.
- Coherence (Temporality): 有的时候，他吃了很危险的植物：有的时候，他吃很危险的植物，Use the zero particle for this repeated, habitual action.
- Coherence (Temporality): 会来跟他的朋友聊到他吃的植物了：会来跟他的朋友聊到他吃的植物，Use the zero particle after 会; a modal verb does not take sentence-final 了 for a routine or future action.
- Expressiveness: After 神农 sees the problem, add his worry or determination, such as “他想：这样下去，大家会生病的！”
- Expressiveness: When describing a beneficial plant, add one visible or sensory detail, such as “这种植物的叶子绿绿的，闻起来很香。”
- Expressiveness: When he eats a harmful plant, show his reaction, such as “他马上觉得肚子疼，脸色也变白了。”

### Encouragement
You clearly introduce 神农 and explain why he begins testing plants. You also use time phrases such as 以后 and 有的时候 to organize the events in a logical order.

## Scene 2

> 一个人问神农，哪个植物好吃。神农说，这就要看你的口味了。要是你喜欢吃甜的，有很多甜的水果，比方说苹果、西瓜和梨。一个别的人问神农，哪个植物对你的身体有好处。神农说，有很多种植物对你的身体有好处，比方说小白菜、黄瓜、茶和菠菜。一个别的人问神农，哪个植物对你的身体有坏处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 这就要看你的口味了：这就要看你喜欢吃什么了，In the pattern “这就要看……了,” the part after “看” should express an open question or choice; “你喜欢吃什么” gives the needed uncertain condition.
- 一个别的人：另一个人，“别的人” contrasts this person with other people, while “另一个人” correctly introduces another individual in the sequence of questioners.
- 一个别的人：另一个人，“另一个人” is the natural expression for a further individual asking a question.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 坏处 -- “哪个植物对你的身体有坏处”：有害，states more precisely that the plant causes harm to the body.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 要是你喜欢吃甜的，有很多甜的水果：要是你喜欢吃甜的，可以吃很多甜的水果，A conditional connector needs a result clause; “可以吃” gives the consequence of the condition introduced by “要是.”
- Expressiveness: After the final question, add a concrete answer that contrasts a safe plant with a harmful one: “灵芝是药，可以吃；夹竹桃有毒，不能吃。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short reaction from the questioner after the explanation to make the dialogue livelier: “那个人说：‘原来不同的植物不一样！’”

### Encouragement
- You use clear question-and-answer dialogue, and your examples of fruits and vegetables make the information easy to understand.
- You also use “比方说” well to introduce specific examples.

## Scene 3

> 神农说，有一天，我吃了一个黄色的花，名字是夹竹桃。我一吃夹竹桃，就觉得这种植物对我的脑有坏处。可是，一个灵芝和一个男人正好在我的旁边，我说，麻烦你把那个植物给我吃一下。我一吃这个灵芝就好了。所以，你得小心吃植物，因为有的植物对你的健康有坏处，特别是夹竹桃。因为夹竹桃有毒。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一个黄色的花：一朵黄色的花，花 takes the matching measure word 朵 rather than the general measure word 个.
- 对我的脑有坏处：对我的大脑有坏处，脑 is usually used in compounds; 大脑 is the natural noun for the body part in this context.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 旁边 -- “正好在我的旁边”：身边，more specifically describes a person or thing close to someone.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 那个植物：灵芝，repeat the noun when the demonstrative reference is unclear: both 夹竹桃 and 灵芝 are plants in the recent context, so 那个植物 could refer to either one.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 特别是夹竹桃。因为夹竹桃有毒。：特别是夹竹桃，因为它有毒。，because introduces a cause and needs to connect to the effect in the same sentence; here it explains why readers should be careful.
- Expressiveness: After “就觉得这种植物对我的大脑有坏处,” add a physical feeling to show Shen Nong’s reaction, such as “我的头很疼，觉得很难受。”
- Expressiveness: Describe the helpful man when he appears, such as “这时，一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人走过来。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech after Shen Nong feels better, such as “谢谢你！你给我吃的是什么？”

### Encouragement
- You clearly use cause and effect when warning readers about poisonous plants, and the sentence with “我一吃夹竹桃，就……” creates a strong quick-action sequence.
