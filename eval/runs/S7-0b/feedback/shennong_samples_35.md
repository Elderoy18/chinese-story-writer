## Scene 1

> 在古代，很多医生不知道怎么用药。所以他们找到了很多植物对药。神农先看到很多病人，他们吃很多植物对药。但是我觉得他们的吃的植物，让他们病人。神农觉得这个植物可能特别是毒。所以他想找一个植物对药，他们可以吃对药。正好，他找到了一把花，他想把花放在茶里，对药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很多植物对药：很多药用植物，药用植物 means plants that can be used as medicine; 对药 does not express this meaning.
- 很多植物对药：很多药用植物，药用植物 is the correct noun phrase for plants used medicinally.
- 他们的吃的植物：他们吃的植物，when a verb phrase modifies a noun, the possessor comes before the verb: the plants that they ate.
- 让他们病人：让他们生病了，病人 is a noun meaning a sick person; after 让, use the verb 生病, and add 了 for the resulting change of state.
- 一个植物对药：一种药用植物，use 种 for a type of plant, and use 药用植物 rather than 对药.
- 对药：做药，做药 means “make/use as medicine”; 对药 is not a verb phrase with this meaning.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 看到 -- “神农先看到很多病人”：发现，emphasizes that he notices the patients and their condition through observation.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农先看到很多病人：一个叫神农的男人先看到了很多病人，first mention rule: introduce a new character with both a name and a brief identifying noun phrase.
- Coherence (Reference): 他们可以吃对药：病人可以吃这种药用植物，repeat the name/noun rule: after several sentences about 神农 and plants, 他们 could refer to either the doctors or the patients, so repeat 病人.
- Coherence (Reference): 这个植物：这些植物，reference rule: no single plant has been identified immediately before this sentence, so 这个植物 has no clear referent; use the plural noun phrase for the previously mentioned plants.
- Coherence (Temporality): 先看到：先看到了，了 marks the completed action of seeing the patients in this past narrative.
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃很多药用植物：吃了很多药用植物，了 marks the completed eating action that led to the patients’ illness.
- Expressiveness: After the patients are mentioned, add one concrete sign of illness, such as “他们肚子疼得很厉害，脸色也很不好。”
- Expressiveness: At “神农觉得这些植物可能特别有毒,” add his worried thought, such as “神农想：这些植物到底能不能吃呢？”
- Expressiveness: When he finds the flowers, describe their appearance to make the discovery vivid, such as “那些花是黄色的，像小星星一样，又大又漂亮。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show Shennong’s reasoning: he sees sick patients, thinks about the cause, and wants to find useful plants. You also use narrative connectors such as “所以” and “正好” to move the scene forward.

## Scene 2

> 他不知道如果这把花对你的身体好，这就要看如果这把花毒不毒了。神农想找别的植物，但是这很麻烦。正好，他找到了别的花。他一吃花，很快就生病了。他的肚子疼死了。神农不知道他吃不吃灵芝或者夹竹桃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 如果这把花对你的身体好：这把花对自己的身体好不好，An embedded yes/no question after 不知道 uses an A-not-A form, not 如果; because the flower concerns 神农 himself, use 自己的身体 to show that the possessor is the same person as the subject.
- 如果这把花毒不毒：这把花有没有毒，After 看, an embedded yes/no question uses A-not-A, such as 有没有毒; 如果 is not used for “whether” questions.
- 吃不吃灵芝或者夹竹桃：该吃灵芝还是夹竹桃，When choosing between two alternatives in an embedded question, use 还是 rather than 或者; 该 shows that the question is about what he should eat.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 麻烦 -- “找别的植物，但是这很麻烦”：繁琐, emphasizes that the work involves many troublesome steps；艰辛, emphasizes that the search is difficult and tiring.
- 很快 -- “他一吃花，很快就生病了”：没过多久, emphasizes the short time between eating the flower and becoming ill.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他一吃花：他一吃这种花，For a close re-occurrence of the flower just mentioned, use a demonstrative noun phrase to identify that particular flower; bare 花 sounds like flowers in general.
- Coherence (Reference): 但是这很麻烦：但是找别的植物很麻烦，The pronoun 这 has a vague referent here; repeat the action as a noun-like phrase so the reader knows exactly what is difficult.
- Expressiveness: After “他一吃这种花”, add a visual detail about the flower, such as “这种花又黄又大，像天上的星星一样。”
- Expressiveness: After “他的肚子疼死了”, show his physical feeling more clearly, such as “他躺在地上，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Add his worried thought at this painful moment, such as “他想：‘我会不会死？’”

### Encouragement
- You used “这就要看……了” to give an answer that depends on a condition, and you clearly showed the quick change from eating the flower to becoming sick.

## Scene 3

> 别的人给了神农一个灵芝。这个植物可以让神农更好。比方说，他会觉得更健康。吃灵芝以后，他可以再走。现在他觉得更好。最后，神农找到了很多植物，他可以用对药。这些植物让神农很高兴。神农想跟很多人分享他的新药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别的人给了神农一个灵芝：别人给了神农一个灵芝，Use 别人 for unspecified other people; 别的 is normally used to distinguish other people or things from a stated one.
- 这个植物可以让神农更好：这个植物可以让神农感觉好一些，A person does not normally become 更好 in this sense; use 感觉好一些 or refer specifically to the person’s body or condition.
- 神农找到了很多植物，他可以用对药：神农找到了很多可以用来做药的植物，Use 用来 + verb to state a plant’s purpose or function. 对药 is not a valid expression here.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 新药 -- “神农想跟很多人分享他的新药”：新发现的药，makes clear that the medicine is new because Shennong has only just discovered it.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 这个植物可以让神农感觉好一些：它可以让神农感觉好一些，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the plant was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no competing object is present.
- Coherence (Reference): 这些植物让神农很高兴：它们让神农很高兴，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence of the plants named in the previous sentence.
- Expressiveness: After the medicine helps him, add a physical detail showing his recovery, such as “他觉得肚子不那么疼了，慢慢站了起来。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where he wants to share the medicine, add his purpose or a short line of speech, such as “他说：‘我要把这些药告诉大家。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the sequence from receiving the medicine, feeling healthier, and continuing the journey. You also use useful story words such as 灵芝, 植物, and 分享.
