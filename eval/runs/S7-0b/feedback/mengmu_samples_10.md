## Scene 1

> 孟子小的时候，从早到晚都跟朋友玩。孟子和他的朋友喜欢玩在墓地里，装祭拜。孟子五岁的时候，跟妈妈搬家。他们搬家不是因为孟子玩得太多，而是因为妈妈认为他们的家离墓地太近。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 喜欢玩在墓地里：喜欢在墓地里玩，A location phrase such as “在墓地里” normally comes before the main verb “玩”.
- 装祭拜：假装祭拜，“装” does not directly introduce an action verb in this meaning; use “假装 + verb phrase” to mean pretending to do an action.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 玩得太多 -- “不是因为孟子玩得太多”：贪玩，describes a child who likes playing excessively and makes the reason more compact and precise.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子小的时候：有一个小男孩叫孟子。孟子小的时候，On first mention, introduce the character with a brief noun phrase that identifies who he is, rather than only giving the name.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和他的朋友喜欢玩在墓地里：他和朋友喜欢玩在墓地里，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the named character from the previous sentence has no competing singular referent.
- Coherence (Temporality): 孟子五岁的时候，跟妈妈搬家：孟子五岁的时候，跟妈妈搬了家，Use 了 for the completed moving event in this past narrative.
- Expressiveness: After the pretending action in the second sentence, add Mengzi’s feeling to show why he does it: “他觉得祭拜很有趣。”
- Expressiveness: At the end, add a short line of speech from his mother to make her concern vivid: “妈妈叹了口气，说：‘这儿离墓地太近了。’”

### Encouragement
- You use clear time phrases such as “小的时候” and “五岁的时候” to place the events in order. The “不是……而是……” sentence also clearly distinguishes the real reason for the move from the mistaken one.

## Scene 2

> 妈妈认为，住离墓地太近，害得孟子的心气。孟子和孟母搬到子思老师的家，跟子思老师住。孟母帮子思打扫房间和做饭。子思帮孟母教她的儿子。因为孟子现在没有朋友，他老是读书。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 住离墓地太近，害得孟子的心气：住得离墓地太近，会害得孟子没有心思学习，use 得 after 住 to introduce the degree phrase; also, 害得 must be followed by the person affected and a harmful result clause, not only a noun such as 心气.
- 老是读书：一直读书，老是 usually carries a complaining or negative connotation; 一直 neutrally describes continuing to read and study.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 做饭 -- “孟母帮子思打扫房间和做饭”：准备饭菜，specifies that she prepares the meals rather than only performing the act of cooking.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟母：他们，use a pronoun for close re-occurrence when the two people were named in the immediately preceding context and no competing referents are present.
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬到：搬到了，use 了 after 搬到 because the move to the teacher’s home is presented as a completed event.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为孟子现在没有朋友，他一直读书：因为孟子现在没有朋友，所以他一直读书，add 所以 to complete the cause-and-effect connector pair.
- Expressiveness: After the final sentence, add a feeling to show how the mother views the change: “孟母看到孟子认真读书，心里很高兴。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of quoted speech after the move to show the teacher’s support: “子思老师说：‘孟子，你可以在这里安心学习。’”
- Expressiveness: Describe the new study environment more vividly before the reading sentence: “子思老师的家很安静，书架上放着很多书。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the helpful relationship between the teacher and Mengzi’s mother, and you give a logical reason for why Mengzi begins reading more.
