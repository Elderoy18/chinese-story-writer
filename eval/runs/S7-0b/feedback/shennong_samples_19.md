## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男人觉得植物很有意思。他叫神农。有的植物吃了以后他的肚子疼死了，可是有的吃了以后很舒服。他找很多植物。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 already expresses a time period, so adding 的时候 is redundant.

### Vocabulary Suggestions

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一个男人觉得植物很有意思。他叫神农：一个叫神农的男人觉得植物很有意思，follow the first-mention rule: introduce a new character with his name and a full noun phrase rather than naming him only in the next sentence.
- Expressiveness: After “肚子疼死了,” add a physical reaction to show how severe the pain is, such as “他在地上躺着，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: After “很舒服,” add Shennong’s thought to show his discovery, such as “神农想：这种植物也许能帮助生病的人。”

## Scene 2

> 凉山很大的山，这也找植物。一天，他找到黄色的花。他听说你跟黄色的花做茶很有用。比方说，你的头发疼死，这冰茶是药。他一喝茶就很不舒服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 凉山很大的山：凉山是一座很大的山，A noun predicate needs the linking verb “是” here, and a mountain takes the measure word “座”.
- 跟黄色的花做茶：用黄色的花做茶，“用” introduces the material used to make tea; “跟” does not express this material relationship.
- 头发疼死：头疼死了，“头发” means hair and cannot normally be the body part that aches; “头疼” means to have a headache, and “了” completes the intensified state.
- 这冰茶：这杯冰茶，A demonstrative before a countable noun needs a measure word: “这” + measure word + noun.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很不舒服 -- “他一喝茶就很不舒服”：难受得很，more vividly describes physical discomfort in this situation.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 这也找植物：他也在那里找植物，Use a pronoun for the previously introduced character; “这” refers to an object or place and cannot be the person who searches for plants.
- Coherence (Reference): 你：omit it in “他听说用黄色的花做茶很有用”，The narrator has not introduced an addressee, so this pronoun has no clear referent; a general statement about making tea does not need a subject.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他一喝茶就很不舒服：他一喝茶就很不舒服了，Use “了” with a new resulting state: the discomfort begins after he drinks the tea.
- Expressiveness: After “他一喝茶就很不舒服了”, add a body reaction to show how serious it is, such as “他的肚子疼了起来，脸色也变白了。”
- Expressiveness: Add Shen Nong’s worried thought after he becomes ill, such as “他想：这种花不会有毒吧？”
- Expressiveness: Describe the yellow flowers before he makes tea, such as “那些花又黄又大，像小星星一样开在地上。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly move the scene forward from finding a yellow flower to making tea and feeling ill. You also used the useful sequence pattern “一……就……” to show that the discomfort happened immediately.

## Scene 3

> 糟糕，这黄色的花是夹竹桃，特别是毒。正好在这儿，灵芝药的夹竹桃他吃完了，也写一个课文的植物。谢谢神农。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 特别是毒：特别有毒，Use 有毒 to describe the poisonous property of a plant; 毒 is a noun and cannot directly follow 特别 in this predicate.
- 灵芝药的夹竹桃：灵芝是药，夹竹桃有毒，The original string incorrectly puts two plant names into one noun phrase without showing their different relationships. Use separate predicates to state that one is medicine and the other is poisonous.
- 写一个课文的植物：把这些植物写进一本书，A plant cannot directly be the object of 写 when the meaning is to record information about it. Use 写进 to show that the information is recorded in a book; 一本 is the correct measure-word phrase for a book.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 正好在这儿：这时，Use 这时 to introduce a new event at this point in the story; 正好在这儿 only gives a location and does not connect the events clearly.
- Coherence (Temporality): 写进一本书：写进了一本书，The writing is a completed action in this past narrative, so the verb phrase needs the perfective marker 了.
- Expressiveness: When the flower is identified, add a visual detail: 黄色的花像星星一样，又大又漂亮。
- Expressiveness: Show Shennong’s physical reaction after eating the poisonous flower: 神农的肚子突然疼了起来，难受得躺在地上。
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought or line of speech after he learns about the two plants: 神农说：“灵芝是药，夹竹桃有毒，不能吃。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly identified the important plants, including 夹竹桃 and 灵芝, and you also showed that Shennong records what he learns. The opening 糟糕 creates an immediate sense that something dangerous has happened.
