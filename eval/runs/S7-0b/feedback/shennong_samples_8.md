## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个人叫神农。一天，神农正在路上走，一看很多人正在吃一些植物。一些人一吃植物就生病了，神农想帮别人。所以想知道哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，both parts refer to time, so using them together is redundant.
- 别人：人们，use this for the concrete group of people in the scene; 别人 usually distinguishes other people from the speaker or a particular person.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 神农正在路上走，一看很多人正在吃一些植物。一些人一吃植物就生病了：神农正在路上走，一看很多人正在吃一些植物，就发现一些人一吃植物就生病了，the connector pattern 一……就…… links the observation to its immediate result.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 神农想帮人们。所以想知道哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃：神农想帮人们，所以想知道哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃，use 所以 to connect the cause and its result within one sentence.
- Expressiveness: After the people become ill, add one physical detail to show the illness: “有的人捂着肚子，脸色发白。”
- Expressiveness: Add Shennong’s inner question after he decides to learn about the plants: “神农心想：‘到底哪些植物能吃呢？’”
- Expressiveness: Describe the plants beside the road more vividly before the people eat them: “路边长着各种各样的植物，有绿叶的，也有开花的。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduce the main character and use the contrast between plants that can and cannot be eaten well. Your use of ongoing actions in “正在路上走” and “正在吃” also helps the scene feel active.

## Scene 2

> 然后，神农开始尝很多植物，比方说他吃过生姜、芥兰和菠菜。一些植物吃真好，特别是黄瓜。但是，一些植物毒得很，特别是夹竹桃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一些植物吃真好：一些植物吃起来真好，Use the complement pattern verb + 起来 + adjective to describe how something tastes when eaten.
- 毒得很：有毒，毒 is a noun in this use and cannot take degree complements such as 得很; 有毒的 means “poisonous.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很多 -- “神农开始尝很多植物”：各种各样的，emphasizes that he tried many different kinds of plants rather than only a large number.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After listing the edible plants, add a short reaction to show Shennong’s discovery, for example: “他觉得黄瓜又脆又清凉。”
- Expressiveness: When introducing the dangerous plant, add a visible detail that foreshadows its danger, for example: “夹竹桃开着黄色的花，像星星一样漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: Add Shennong’s cautious thought after he learns that some plants are dangerous, for example: “神农心想：我得小心一点儿。”

### Encouragement
- You use clear contrast between plants that are good to eat and plants that are dangerous. The concrete examples of vegetables make the scene easy to understand.

## Scene 3

> 一个人对神农说，麻烦我们可以吃什么植物？神农说，这就要看你想不想生病了。以后，神农告诉别人一些植物能吃，一些植物不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 麻烦我们可以吃什么植物：请问，我们可以吃什么植物？，麻烦 means “trouble” or “to inconvenience someone” and cannot introduce a question politely by itself; 请问 is used to begin a polite question.
- 这就要看你想不想生病了：这就要看植物有没有毒了，this fixed pattern should give the condition that determines the answer to the previous question. Whether a plant is edible depends on whether it is poisonous, not on whether the listener wants to become ill.
- 以后：后来，when it starts a new sentence and means “afterwards,” use 后来; 以后 usually means “after a specified time or event.”
- 别人：人们，别人 distinguishes other people from a particular person or group already being discussed; here the meaning is people in general, so 人们 is needed.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉：告诉了，this is a completed narrative action, so it needs 了. The zero particle is used when a speaking verb directly introduces quoted speech, but this sentence reports a completed act of telling.
- Expressiveness: After the question, make the speaker sound curious or worried with a brief detail: “他担心地问：‘请问，我们可以吃什么植物？’”
- Expressiveness: Make Shen Nong’s answer more informative by giving one clear example: “神农说：‘像灵芝一样的植物是药，可以吃；有毒的植物不能吃。’”

### Encouragement
- You used a question-and-answer exchange, which makes this scene feel like a real conversation. You also clearly contrasted edible plants with plants that cannot be eaten.
