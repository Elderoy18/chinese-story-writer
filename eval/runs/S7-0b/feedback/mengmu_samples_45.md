## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子。他们住在墓地的旁边，所以孟子老是祭拜。孟子不是祭拜，就是跟朋友聊天。他很喜欢他的城市，但是城市有才两个学校，都很小。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 already expresses a time period, so adding 的时候 is redundant.
- 城市有才两个学校：城市才有两个学校，才 must come before the verb 有 to mean “there are only two schools.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 小 -- “都很小”：规模很小，specifies that the schools are small in their overall scale rather than using a general description.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子：一个叫孟子的男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国，by the first-mention rule, introduce a new main character with his name and a full noun phrase rather than naming him only in the following sentence.
- Coherence (Reference): 他很喜欢他的城市：他很喜欢自己的城市，when the subject and the possessor are the same person, 自己的 makes that relationship clear.
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery is introduced, add a sensory detail about what Mengzi sees or hears there, such as “他常常听到有人在墓地里哭。”
- Expressiveness: Show Mengzi’s reason or feeling about worshipping, such as “孟子觉得祭拜很好玩。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought from his mother after she notices his behaviour, such as “孟母心想：‘这儿不太适合孩子。’”

### Encouragement
- You use both a cause-and-effect connector, “所以”, and a contrast connector, “但是”, to connect ideas clearly. The “不是……就是……” pattern also gives a lively picture of Mengzi’s daily activities.

## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈不是不喜欢他们的城市，而是想孟子有很好的教育。他们搬家到有很多学校的城市。他们从早到晚走路，孟子一直抱怨。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 想孟子有很好的教育：想让孟子接受很好的教育，Education is something a person receives rather than possesses with 有; when one person wants another person to do something, use 想让 + person + verb phrase.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们从早到晚走路：他们从早到晚走了一天的路，This is one completed journey in the narrative, so the completed action needs 了; adding 一天的路 also makes the duration and action sound natural together.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 不是不喜欢他们的城市，而是想孟子有很好的教育：不是因为不喜欢他们的城市，而是因为想让孟子接受很好的教育，Use the paired connector to contrast two reasons: the move was not caused by one reason but by another.
- Expressiveness: At the point where孟子抱怨, add a short line of speech to show his tiredness, such as “妈妈，我们还要走多久？我的脚好疼！”
- Expressiveness: When they arrive in the new city, add one concrete detail that shows why it feels different, such as “街上有很多商人卖各种各样的东西，到处都很热闹。”
- Expressiveness: After the mother’s reason for moving, add her inner thought, such as “她心想：孟子在这儿一定能好好学习。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the mother’s goal for moving and孟子’s unhappy reaction during the journey. The contrast structure and the time phrase from morning to evening help the scene move forward clearly.

## Scene 3

> 他难怪抱怨，他想他的朋友，因为他不要离开他的城市。他的妈妈找到孟子一个老师。跟老师孟子学最重要的文学，所以他成为哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 想他的朋友：想自己的朋友，When the person missing the friends is also the subject, use 自己的 to make the possessive relationship clear.
- 不要离开：不想离开，Use 不想 to express that someone does not want to do something; 不要 is normally used for a prohibition, instruction, or refusal.
- 他的妈妈找到孟子一个老师：他的妈妈给孟子找到了一个老师，Place the beneficiary after 给 before the verb, and use 了 after the resultative verb 找到 to show that the search was completed.
- 跟老师孟子学最重要的文学：孟子跟老师学最重要的文学，The subject 孟子 should come before the phrase 跟老师.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 最重要的文学 -- “孟子跟老师学最重要的文学”：经典文学，specifies that he studied recognised, important literary works rather than vaguely “the most important” literature.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他难怪抱怨，：难怪他抱怨，原来，Use 难怪 before the result and 原来 to introduce the newly understood reason.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为哲学家：成为了哲学家，Use 了 after 成为 to mark the completed change of becoming a philosopher.
- Expressiveness: At the point where he complains, add a small physical detail to show his unhappiness, such as “孟子叹了口气，一直低着头走路。”
- Expressiveness: You could add a short line of speech after his reason for complaining, such as “他说：‘我不想离开自己的朋友。’”
- Expressiveness: When his mother finds a teacher, describe her feeling or purpose, such as “妈妈希望孟子能好好学习，心里放心了一点。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show why the boy is unhappy and then move the scene forward to his education and later achievement. Your use of a teacher and studying literature gives the scene a clear direction.
