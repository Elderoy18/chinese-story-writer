## Scene 1

> 古代时候，人不知道他们能吃什么植物，常常一吃就死。但是不知道为什么，没有办法。所以有一天，神农出去试一试很多种植物，告诉人什么植物是安全什么是危险。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代时候：在古代，古代 is already a time expression, so adding 时候 is redundant; unlike the monosyllabic expression 古时候, it does not take 时候.
- 什么植物是安全什么是危险：哪些植物安全，哪些植物危险，coordinated embedded questions need two parallel clauses, so both parts should identify which plants are being described.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 危险 -- “什么植物是安全什么是危险”：有毒的，specifies that the danger comes from poison, which is more exact when the plants are being eaten.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 人不知道他们能吃什么植物：人们不知道什么植物能吃，use 人们 for a concrete group of people; after the group has been named, omit the repeated pronoun in the same clause because it has the same referent.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农出去试一试很多种植物：有一个叫神农的人出去试一试很多种植物，this is the character’s first mention, so introduce him with a full noun phrase rather than only a name.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉人：告诉了人们，this is the final action in a completed series of events, so the last verb takes 了; 人们 names the concrete audience.
- Expressiveness: After the deaths are mentioned, add the people’s reaction to make the problem feel more immediate: “人们又害怕又着急。”
- Expressiveness: After introducing Shennong, add his purpose or inner thought: “他想：我要找出能吃的植物。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly set up the central problem—people do not know which plants they can eat—and then introduce a character who tries to solve it. The contrast between safe and dangerous plants is a strong idea for this story.

## Scene 2

> 发现有的植物对身体非常好，特别是茶，让肚子感觉又暖和又清楚。他告诉朋友：“你吃哪种植物，这就要看你的口味。比方说，你爱吃甜的，可能喜欢糖。”他最喜欢吃花，除了夹竹桃以外，什么都花想吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 肚子感觉又暖和又清楚：人感觉又暖和又舒服，清楚 describes whether information or thoughts are clear; it cannot describe a bodily feeling from the stomach. 舒服 appropriately describes a pleasant physical sensation.
- 什么都花想吃：什么花都想吃，when 什么花 means “every kind of flower,” the noun 花 must come directly after 什么, followed by 都 and the verb.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好 -- “有的植物对身体非常好”：对身体很有益，specifically expresses that the plants benefit health；对身体很有好处，clearly states the positive effect on the body.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 告诉朋友：告诉他的一个朋友，朋友 is a new character here, so the first-mention rule requires a clear full noun phrase rather than a bare noun.
- Coherence (Temporality): 发现有的植物：发现了有的植物，了 marks the completed discovery in this narrative sequence.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 你吃哪种植物，这就要看你的口味：你想吃哪种植物？这就要看你的口味了，this use of 这就要看……了 needs an uncertain question before it, and the phrase after 看 should answer what the choice depends on.
- Expressiveness: After mentioning tea, add a concrete taste or physical detail, such as “茶水喝起来有一点苦，可是很香。”
- Expressiveness: Show the character’s reaction to the discovery after the first sentence, such as “他高兴地想：原来植物还能帮助人！”

### Encouragement
- You used a clear example with tea and then gave a simple example about sweet food, which helps the reader follow the character’s idea.
- The contrast with 夹竹桃 shows that you remember an important dangerous plant from the earlier scene.

## Scene 3

> 他发现这个口味，一百岁时候，不小心吃了毒的花。一吃就开始死。但是生病时候告诉人什么植物是药，什么植物是毒。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一百岁时候：一百岁的时候，A multi-syllable age expression before 时候 needs the particle 的.
- 毒的花：有毒的花，Use 有毒的 to describe a poisonous plant; 毒 alone is a noun and cannot directly modify 花 here.
- 一吃就开始死：一吃就生病了，死 describes an endpoint rather than an action that normally begins gradually; 生病了 naturally expresses the change of state that follows eating the flower.
- 告诉人：告诉人们，Use 人们 for a concrete group of people; 人 is a generic noun and does not refer naturally to the people being told.
- 什么植物是毒：什么植物有毒，Use 有毒 to describe a plant’s poisonous property; 是毒 does not fit this predicate pattern.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不小心 -- “不小心吃了毒的花”：误食了，specifically means accidentally eating something and is especially useful when the food or plant may be harmful.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉人们：告诉了人们，This is a completed past action in the narrative, so the verb takes 了; speaking verbs use the zero particle only when they introduce direct quoted speech.
- Expressiveness: After the flower is mentioned, add one visual detail: “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花。”
- Expressiveness: Make the illness more vivid after “一吃就生病了”: “他的肚子马上疼了起来，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Add the character’s worried thought at the moment he becomes ill: “他想：‘我会不会死？’”

### Encouragement
- You used the pattern “一……就……” well to show that the reaction happened immediately after eating the flower.
- You also clearly contrast plants that can be medicine with plants that are poisonous, which fits the central idea of this scene.
