## Scene 1

> 有一天孟子去了墓地玩儿，但是他妈妈觉得这是不合适的地方让孩子玩，所以她决定了他们会搬家找更好的地方。她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福，而且住在这儿教育不太好。因为她望子成龙，所以她会一直搬家才能找到质量好的教育。然后他们搬到了离城市更近的地方。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 这是不合适的地方让孩子玩：这是不适合孩子玩的地方，use “适合 + person + verb phrase” to say that a place suits someone’s activity; the original word order does not form this pattern naturally.
- 她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福：她担心儿子的童年不会幸福，when the mother is the subject who feels worried, use “担心”; “恐怕” means “I am afraid that” and cannot take another subject in this structure.
- 住在这儿教育不太好：这儿的教育不太好，“教育” needs a clear possessor or location phrase; “这儿的” shows that the education in this place is being evaluated.
- 她会一直搬家才能找到质量好的教育：她得一直搬家才能找到质量好的教育，use “得……才能……” to express that repeated moving is necessary in order to achieve the result.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 更好的 -- “找更好的地方”：更适合孩子成长的，specifies that the place is better because it supports the child’s development.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 有一天孟子去了墓地玩儿：有一天，有一个小男孩叫孟子，他去了墓地玩儿，at first mention, introduce the character with a noun phrase explaining who he is before using a pronoun.
- Coherence (Reference): 他妈妈：孟子的妈妈，at the mother’s first mention, use a full noun phrase rather than a possessive pronoun, so the new character is introduced clearly.
- Coherence (Temporality): 决定了：决定，“决定” is a mental-state verb and takes the zero particle rather than completed-action “了”.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们会搬家找更好的地方：搬到一个更好的地方，after “决定”, use a verb phrase directly; the same subject does not need to be repeated.
- Expressiveness: After the visit to the cemetery, add a visual detail to make the setting clearer, such as “墓地里有人安静地祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: Show the mother’s worry through an inner thought or quoted speech after she sees Mengzi playing there, such as “她心想：‘孩子每天在这儿玩，会学到什么呢？’”

### Encouragement
- You used the cause-and-effect pair “因为……所以……” well to explain the mother’s reason for moving. You also correctly used “搬到了” to show that the move was completed.

## Scene 2

> 他们住才一月就又搬家。古代的时候，一般人不喜欢卖东西的人。但是孟子老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿，害得妈妈着急得很，因为她恐怕儿子会成为跟他们很像。她告诉了孟子：他们不是朋友，而是不好的人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 成为跟他们很像：变得跟他们很像，成为 needs a noun phrase that identifies what someone becomes, while 跟他们很像 is an adjective phrase describing a state; use 变得 before an adjective phrase.
- 她恐怕：她担心，恐怕 means “I am afraid that” and cannot use another person as its experiencer in this structure; 担心 correctly expresses that she is worried about her son.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 卖东西的人 -- “这些卖东西的人”：商人，more precisely identifies people whose work is selling goods.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他们不是朋友：他们不是你的朋友，repeat the relationship with 你的 because without it, this means that the sellers are not friends with each other, rather than that they are not Meng子’s friends.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们住才一月就又搬家：他们才住了一个月，就又搬家了，use 了 after 住 to show the completed period of living there; the sequence pattern is 才 + duration + 就 + action.
- Coherence (Temporality): 她告诉了孟子：她告诉孟子，use the zero particle with a speaking verb that introduces quoted speech.
- Expressiveness: After the description of the sellers, add a market detail to show why the place affects Meng子, such as “从早到晚都很热闹。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where Meng母 becomes worried, add her inner thought, such as “她心想：‘他不能每天只知道玩儿。’”
- Expressiveness: Show what Meng子 copies from the sellers, such as “他还学着他们收钱、喊别人来买东西。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the cause of Meng母’s worry with 害得 and 因为, and the final quotation gives her an active voice in the scene.

## Scene 3

> 你不是当数学家，而是当哲学家，不能当卖东西的人。所以他们又搬家。在他们第三个家，孟子学了怎么祭拜。妈妈感觉这个地方适合他们，告诉了孟子：我们找到了最后的家。在这儿妈妈让儿子从早到晚学习，他难怪成为有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 告诉了孟子：告诉孟子，Speaking verbs that introduce quoted speech use the zero aspect particle; do not add 了 here.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 卖东西的人 -- “不能当卖东西的人”：商人，names the occupation more precisely than the general description.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 所以他们又搬家：所以他们又搬家了，Use 了 for the completed moving event in this past narrative.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他难怪成为有名的哲学家：他难怪成为了有名的哲学家，Use 了 with 成为 to mark the completed change of state.
- Expressiveness: At “在他们第三个家,” add a concrete detail about the new environment, such as “他们的新家在学校旁边，周围很安静。”
- Expressiveness: At “妈妈感觉这个地方适合他们,” show the mother’s relief with a thought or quoted speech, such as “妈妈高兴地说：‘这儿终于适合你好好学习了。’”
