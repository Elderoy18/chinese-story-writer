## Scene 1

> 古代的时候人觉得祭拜非常重要，孟子很小的时候，从早到晚一直祭拜，因为他妈妈告诉他祭拜很重要。虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜。他的同学们老是对他开玩笑了。孟子妈妈看了他的同学们对孟子不好的影响，所以孟子和孟子妈妈搬家了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，the phrase is redundant because 古代 already expresses a time period, while 的时候 also means “the time when.”
- 人觉得：人们觉得，use 人们 for people in general; 人 alone is a generic noun and does not naturally refer to a concrete group of people.
- 看了他的同学们对孟子不好的影响：看到了他的同学们对孟子不好的影响，看 means “look,” while 看到 means “see/notice”; use 看到了 for a completed act of noticing.
- 他同学们老是对他开玩笑了：他同学们老是对他开玩笑，老是 describes a habitual action, so it takes the zero aspect form rather than 了.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子很小的时候：有一个叫孟子的小男孩，他很小的时候，孟子 is appearing for the first time, so introduce him with his name and who he is rather than using only the name.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜：他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜，because introduces a cause, while 虽然 needs a contrasting second clause with 但是 or 可是; this sentence only needs the cause connector.
- Expressiveness: After the classmates’ teasing, add a short line of quoted speech to show what they say, such as 同学们笑着说：“你怎么每天都在祭拜？”
- Expressiveness: When the mother notices the influence, add her feeling to make her decision more understandable, such as 孟子的妈妈很担心。
- Expressiveness: Add Mengzi’s own reason for enjoying the activity before the classmates tease him, such as 孟子觉得祭拜很有趣。

### Encouragement
- You clearly show a cause-and-effect chain: Mengzi worships often, his classmates react, and his mother decides to move. You also use useful time expressions such as 从早到晚 to show how often the action happens.

## Scene 2

> 孟子才十一岁，成为一个哲学家。孟子不是祭拜，而是看政治文章，难怪他成为了哲学家。不过，看过很多政治文章对他害得，他有很多问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不是祭拜，而是看政治文章：不是在祭拜，而是在看政治文章，when contrasting two actions with the pattern “不是……而是……,” use “在” before each ongoing action.

- 对他害得：害得他，after “害得,” the person affected comes directly after “得”; “对” cannot be used in this structure.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子不是祭拜：他不是在祭拜，after a recent mention of the same character and with no competing character, use a pronoun rather than repeating the name.

- Coherence (Temporality): 孟子才十一岁，成为一个哲学家：孟子才十一岁，就成为了一个哲学家，use “才……就……” to show that the achievement happened at an unexpectedly young age, and use “了” after “成为” for a completed change of state.

- Expressiveness: At the reading moment, add a small action showing that he is thinking carefully, for example: “他一边看，一边皱着眉头想问题。”
- Expressiveness: Make his questions more concrete at the final sentence, for example: “他想：‘为什么有的人很穷，有的人很富？’”

### Encouragement
- You used “不是……而是……” to create a clear contrast between two activities, and you also tried to explain a consequence with “害得,” which is a useful storytelling structure.

## Scene 3

> 他一直问问题，但是不能解决那些问题。孟子长大，他开始能解决问题。他常常建议你不是学金融，而是学政治，因为如果你懂政治，你会成为高薪。后来孟子成为教授，他教学生很厉害。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 会成为高薪：会得到高薪，high salary is something a person receives or earns; a person cannot become a salary.
- 他教学生很厉害：他教学生教得很厉害，when a verb is evaluated with an adjective, use the degree-complement pattern verb + 得 + adjective. When the object comes before the complement, repeat the verb.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 问问题 -- “他一直问问题”：提出疑问，emphasizes that he raises questions for others to consider.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 孟子长大：孟子长大了，use 了 after a completed change of state: he has grown up.
- Coherence (Temporality): 后来孟子成为教授：后来孟子成为了教授，use 了 to mark the completed change into a professor.
- Expressiveness: After the advice about politics, add a short quotation to show Mengzi’s voice, such as: “学政治可以帮助大家了解国家。”
- Expressiveness: At “孟子长大了”, add an evaluative detail about his ability, such as: “孟子长大了，懂得的道理越来越多。”
- Expressiveness: Show how the students feel about his teaching after “他教学生教得很厉害”, such as: “学生们都很喜欢上他的课。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show Mengzi’s development from someone who cannot answer questions to someone who becomes a professor. Your use of “后来” also gives the final event a clear place in the time sequence.
