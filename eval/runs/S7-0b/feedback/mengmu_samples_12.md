## Scene 1

> 古代，孟子跟孟母住在墓地的旁边。可是孟母注意，住在墓地的旁边影响孟子。他常常跟朋友们祭拜玩，所以孟母决定他们会搬家。这次，他们住在城市里的商店的旁边，从早到晚很吵。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代：在古代，A time noun used as a narrative time adverbial normally takes 在.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 吵 -- “从早到晚很吵”：嘈杂，describes a place filled with mixed, unpleasant noise；喧闹，adds the sense of a busy and noisy commercial area.

- Coherence (Reference): 孟母决定他们会搬家：孟母决定搬家，After 决定, use a zero form when the person deciding is also the person carrying out the action; repeating a new subject is unnecessary.
- Coherence (Temporality): 孟母注意：孟母注意到了，注意到 uses 到 as a resultative complement for noticing something, and 了 marks this noticing as a completed discovery.
- Coherence (Temporality): 祭拜玩：祭拜着玩，Use 着 when the first action continues as the accompanying way in which someone does another action.
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery is introduced, add one concrete sound or sight to show why the place affects the child, such as “孟子常常听到有人在墓地里哭。”
- Expressiveness: At the mother’s decision, add her worried thought or speech, such as “孟母心想：‘这儿不适合孟子长大。’”
- Expressiveness: At the new home, show the noise with an example rather than only stating it, such as “商人一直在门口大声叫卖。”

### Encouragement
- You introduced the setting, the family members, and the move in a clear order. You also used “从早到晚” effectively to show that the new neighbourhood is noisy throughout the day.

## Scene 2

### Encouragement
