## Scene 1

> 一天，很久很久以前，孟子和他妈妈住在中国。孟子妈妈的工作是祭拜，孟子不高兴，因为他说我们老是搬家，为什么？他妈妈说，从早到晚，我一直祭拜，很多墓地得我祭拜。所以我们得去很多不同的地方。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一天，很久很久以前：很久很久以前的一天，A time expression used as a modifier should come before the noun it modifies.
- 他说我们老是搬家，为什么？：他问：“我们老是搬家，为什么？”，Use 问, rather than 说, to introduce a direct question; direct speech is normally marked with a colon and quotation marks.
- 很多墓地得我祭拜：有很多墓地需要我祭拜，得 is a modal verb meaning “must” and should precede the action performed by its subject. 需要 correctly expresses that the graves require the mother’s worship.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不高兴 -- “孟子不高兴”：不耐烦，shows that he is tired of moving repeatedly；委屈，shows that he feels unfairly treated by the repeated moves.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和他妈妈：有一个小男孩叫孟子，他和妈妈，By the first-mention rule, introduce a new main character with a full noun phrase that says who he is before using pronouns for him and his family.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为：，The connector 因为 introduces a cause, but the following question does not explain the cause of his unhappiness. Use punctuation to introduce his question instead.
- Expressiveness: After “孟子不高兴,” add a visible reaction to show his feeling, such as “孟子皱着眉头，低着头不说话。”
- Expressiveness: At the mother’s reply, add an action or feeling before her explanation, such as “妈妈叹了口气，说：‘我也不想一直搬家。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show both Mengzi’s confusion and his mother’s reason for moving, so the dialogue has a logical purpose.
- Your use of “从早到晚” and “所以” helps the reader understand the mother’s busy routine and the result.

## Scene 2

> 孟子想，难怪我们常常搬家，原来我妈妈很忙。可是常常搬家害得孟子很难找到朋友。每次搬家孟子也觉得新的地方不是太热就是太冷，不是太大就是太小。在哪的地方，他都不高兴。孟子想帮助妈妈祭拜，可是他六岁，所以不能祭拜。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在哪的地方：在那些地方，哪 is an interrogative word for asking about a place; to refer back to the places just described, use the demonstrative phrase 那些地方.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 常常 -- “我们常常搬家”：频繁地，emphasizes that the moves happen repeatedly and close together.
- 新的 -- “新的地方”：陌生的，emphasizes that each place is unfamiliar to Mengzi after moving.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After “新的地方”, add a small concrete detail about what feels uncomfortable, such as “房子又小又冷”.
- Expressiveness: After “他都不高兴”, show Mengzi’s inner feeling more specifically, such as “他觉得很孤单”.
- Expressiveness: Before the final sentence, add a short line of thought or speech, such as “妈妈，我什么时候才能有朋友？”.

### Encouragement
You used the pattern “难怪……，原来……” very naturally to show Mengzi’s new understanding. You also clearly connected frequent moving with his difficulty making friends and feeling unhappy.

## Scene 3

> 四年后，孟子开始帮妈妈祭拜。他画画很厉害，他越来越喜欢祭拜，所以他祭拜不是想帮妈妈工作，而是他很喜欢画画。孟子成为很著名的古代画家。今天很多中国人很喜欢孟子。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他祭拜不是想帮妈妈工作，而是他很喜欢画画：他祭拜，不是为了帮妈妈工作，而是因为他很喜欢画画，The paired structure “不是……而是……” should contrast parallel reasons or purposes. Use “为了” for the purpose being rejected and “因为” for the actual reason.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 厉害 -- “他画画很厉害”：画得很出色，describes the quality of his paintings more specifically than a general statement of ability.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 孟子成为很著名的古代画家：孟子成为了很著名的古代画家，Use “了” after “成为” because becoming something is a completed change of state in this narrative.
- Expressiveness: After “他画画很厉害,” add a small concrete detail about what he paints, such as “他常常认真地画下墓地旁的花草。”
- Expressiveness: After the sentence about his interest in painting, add an inner thought to show his feeling, such as “他觉得画画让他特别快乐。”

### Encouragement
- You used clear time framing with “四年后” and “今天,” which helps the reader see the change from Mencius’s childhood to his later reputation. You also clearly showed that he has a strong interest in painting.
