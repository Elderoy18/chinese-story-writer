## Scene 1

> 古代的时候有一个人叫神农。他需要当上医，一看别的人就想帮助他们。但是，他有一个问题，他不知道什么东西能做药。神农听说一个远远的地方有一种植物，什么病人吃都会觉得身体更好。神农三个月走去，找到有植物的地方，他看到一个很漂亮的植物，又香又好看。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，this phrase redundantly combines a historical time period with a second time expression.
- 当上医：当上医生，医生 is the usual modern noun for the occupation; 上医 is a specialised classical term for a highly skilled doctor, not a general word for “doctor.”
- 别的人：病人，别人 refers to people other than a previously specified person or group; here the people needing help should be identified as patients.
- 神农三个月走去：神农走了三个月，an action lasting a completed period takes 了 after the verb, and 走去 does not fit here without a destination.
- 找到有植物的地方：找到了有植物的地方，找到 needs 了 here because the finding is a completed event.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农听说一个远远的地方有一种植物：他听说一个远远的地方有一种植物，use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when no competing named character has intervened.
- Expressiveness: When he hears about the plant, add his hopeful reaction and an action: “神农听了很高兴，马上出发了。”
- Expressiveness: At the beautiful plant, add a more memorable visual detail: “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花。”
- Expressiveness: Add his inner thought before he approaches it: “神农想：这种植物也许能做药。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduce the main character and give him a helpful motivation. The final description, “又香又好看,” already uses two sensory details to make the plant more vivid.

## Scene 2

> 他觉得，这种一定是做药的花。原来他想错了。吃了以后，神农快生病。 这时一个穿着蓝色衣服的人帮神农，快给他一种红的灵芝。神农吃了以后，他没有病，想起来以前一个人告诉他，灵芝对身体有好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 快：很快，Placed directly before a verb, “快” usually means “be about to”; “很快” means that the event happened soon after the previous action.
- 一种红的灵芝：一朵红的灵芝，The measure word “种” counts types or varieties, while “朵” counts one individual mushroom-like plant.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 觉得 -- “他觉得，这种一定是做药的花”：认定，emphasizes that he confidently believed the flower could be used as medicine.
- 好处 -- “灵芝对身体有好处”：疗效，specifies the beneficial effect as a medical or healing effect.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 给他：给了神农，The pronoun is ambiguous because both the blue-clothed person and 神农 are male characters in the same sentence; repeat the name to make the recipient clear.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农吃了以后，他没有病：神农吃了以后，病好了，Use zero form when the same character continues across closely connected clauses; this also gives a natural recovery result.
- Coherence (Temporality): 生病：生病了，Use “了” for the completed change of state after he ate the flower.
- Coherence (Temporality): 快给：赶快给了，Use “了” for the completed action of giving in this past event.
- Expressiveness: At “神农快生病了,” add a physical feeling to show how serious the illness is, such as “肚子疼了起来，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: When the helper arrives, describe the helpful action more vividly, such as “一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人赶快跑过来，拿起一朵像伞一样的灵芝。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought when 神农 remembers the earlier advice, such as “他想：原来灵芝真的可以治病！”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the important cause-and-effect sequence: 神农 eats the flower, becomes ill, receives 灵芝, and then recovers.
- The detail “穿着蓝色衣服的人” is a strong introduction because it helps the reader picture the new character.

## Scene 3

> 啊，他怎么忘记了？他把灵芝放在包里。一回家就告诉别人。他找到药以后他写下怎么做药在书上。所以人们都知道，那红灵芝对身体好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别人：大家，Use this when he is sharing the information with people generally; 别人 normally distinguishes other people from a person already mentioned.
- 写下怎么做药在书上：在书上写下怎么做药，The location phrase should come before the verb when it tells where the writing is recorded.
- 对身体好处：对身体有好处，The fixed pattern is 对……有好处; 有 expresses that something brings a benefit.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 那红灵芝：那种红灵芝，The reference rule requires a measure word after 那 before a noun; 种 is suitable for a type of medicinal plant.
- Coherence (Temporality): 把灵芝放在包里：把灵芝放在包里了，Use 了 after the completed action of putting the herb into the bag.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉：告诉了，Use 了 because telling everyone is a completed narrative action here.
- Coherence (Temporality): 写下：写下了，Use 了 because recording how to make medicine is completed before people know the information.
- Expressiveness: After the opening question, add an internal realization to show why he remembers, for example: “原来有人告诉过我，灵芝对身体有好处！”
- Expressiveness: When he tells everyone, add a short line of speech to make the moment more vivid, for example: “这种红灵芝可以做药！”

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect the herb to its benefit for people’s health, and the ending shows the result of sharing the discovery. Your opening question also gives the character a lively moment of remembering.
