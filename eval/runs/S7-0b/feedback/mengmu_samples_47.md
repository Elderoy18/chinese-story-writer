### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 常常 -- “所以他们常常搬了家”：一再，emphasizes that the moves happened repeatedly rather than as a regular routine.
- 安排 -- “孟子的妈妈安排他的生活”：规划，emphasizes that the mother deliberately organized his life and future.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 这个男孩子叫孟子：有一个男孩子叫孟子，on first mention, introduce a new character with an indefinite full noun phrase rather than a demonstrative phrase.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子是古代的人：他是古代的人，after the name has just appeared and no other male character competes, use a pronoun for the close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Reference): 她望子成龙，所以孟子的妈妈安排他的生活：她望子成龙，所以安排他的生活，when the same subject continues within one sentence, use zero form rather than repeating the noun phrase.
- Coherence (Temporality): 常常搬了家：常常搬家，use the zero particle for a habitual action; 了 marks a completed individual event and does not fit with 常常 here.
- Expressiveness: After “她望子成龙,” add the mother’s worried inner thought to show why she keeps moving: “孟母心想：‘住在哪儿会影响孩子的成长，我得给他找一个好地方。’”
- Expressiveness: At “他们常常搬家,” add Mengzi’s feeling about the repeated moves: “孟子觉得很奇怪，不知道为什么又要搬家。”
- Expressiveness: Make the mother’s planning more concrete with one small detail: “她每天都提醒孟子看书，还给他安排学习的时间。”

## Scene 2

> 因为他们常常搬了家，中国人说，孟母三迁。孟子没有爸爸，所以，他的妈妈更重要。从这儿到那儿，妈妈教孟子很多技能。先她让孟子跟朋友玩儿，然后教他怎么买又便宜又好质量的东西。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 先她让孟子跟朋友玩儿：她先让孟子跟朋友玩儿，A sequence adverb such as “先” normally follows the subject and comes before the verb phrase.
- 又便宜又好质量：又便宜又质量好，“质量好” is an adjective-predicate phrase; “好” must follow the noun it describes here.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 从这儿到那儿 -- “从这儿到那儿，妈妈教孟子很多技能”：搬来搬去，specifies that the family moved repeatedly between different places.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他的妈妈更重要：她更重要，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the referent, “孟母,” was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other female character competes with it.
- Coherence (Reference): 妈妈教孟子很多技能：她教孟子很多技能，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence of the same character rather than repeating the noun.
- Coherence (Temporality): 常常搬了家：常常搬家，Use the zero particle for a habitual action; “常常” describes a routine, so it does not take completed-action “了.”
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为他们常常搬家，中国人说，孟母三迁：因为他们常常搬家，所以中国人说：“孟母三迁。”，Use the paired connector “因为……所以……” to make the cause-and-effect relationship explicit.
- Expressiveness: After the statement about the father, add the mother’s feeling or determination, such as “她想：我一定要好好照顾孟子。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where the mother teaches shopping, add a short line of dialogue to show the lesson, such as “妈妈说：‘买东西要看质量，也要看价钱。’”
- Expressiveness: Make the moving feel more vivid by showing the mother’s effort, such as “她搬来搬去，觉得很累，可是没有放弃。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show that the mother cares for孟子 and teaches him useful everyday skills. The use of “先……然后……” also gives the final sentence a clear sequence.
