## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，孟子和孟母住了在靠墓地的家。孩子才五岁就每天在墓地祭拜了。在那个城市，孟母从早到晚都找工作，可是找不到，害得孟母没有钱。难怪她跟孩子搬家，原来买不了房。他们搬了到别一个城市找工作。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，the time word 古代 already names a period, so adding 的时候 is redundant.
- 跟孩子搬家：带着孩子搬家，跟 means “together with,” while a mother moving her child to a new home is expressed with 带着.
- 别一个城市：另一个城市，另一个 is the standard expression for “another” one; 别的 is used to distinguish other things from the one being discussed.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 买不了 -- “原来买不了房”：无力购买房子，makes clear that the obstacle is a lack of money, which matches the previous sentence.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟母：一个叫孟子的小男孩和他的妈妈，first mention should introduce the character with both a name and who he is.
- Coherence (Temporality): 住了在：住在，a continuing living state takes the zero particle rather than 了, and 了 cannot be placed before the locative complement.
- Coherence (Reference): 孩子：他，use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the referent was named in the previous sentence and no other person competes with it.
- Coherence (Temporality): 每天在墓地祭拜了：每天在墓地祭拜，daily routines take the zero particle rather than completed-action 了.
- Coherence (Reference): 在那个城市：在他们住的城市里，this is the first mention of the city, so use a full noun phrase rather than the demonstrative phrase 那个城市.
- Coherence (Reference): 害得孟母没有钱：害得自己没有钱，when the person affected is the same as the subject, use 自己 to show that relationship clearly instead of repeating the name.
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬了到：搬到了，when 到 is a directional complement, completed-action 了 comes after it: 搬到了.
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery setting, add a detail about what the child sees and feels: “孟子每天看到很多人来墓地祭拜，觉得很好玩。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where the mother decides to move, add her inner thought or speech: “孟母心想：‘住在这儿会影响孩子，我得带着他搬家。’”

### Encouragement
- You used the pattern 才五岁就 well to show that the child was very young when he began an action. Your use of 从早到晚 also clearly shows how hard the mother tried to find work.

## Scene 2

> 这个城市老是很吵，不是人吵架，就是都叫，害得孟子一直不能睡觉。在那儿，孟母也找不到工作。孟子和妈妈要又一个次搬了家。在第三个城市，孟母找到工作，在商店做衣服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不是人吵架，就是都叫：不是人们在吵架，就是人们都在叫，use 人们 for a concrete group of people; 人 is a generic noun and does not naturally refer to the people making the noise in this scene.
- 要又一个次搬了家：要再搬一次家，use 要 + 再 + verb + number + measure word + object for a future repeated action; 又 and 了 describe a repeat that has already happened, and the correct measure-word phrase is 一次.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 吵 -- “这个城市老是很吵”：嘈杂，specifies that many different loud sounds are mixed together in the city.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 孟母找到工作：孟母找到了工作，use 了 after 找到 to mark the completed action of successfully finding a job.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 在那儿，孟母也找不到工作。孟子和妈妈要又一个次搬了家。：因为孟母在那儿也找不到工作，所以他们要再搬一次家，use 因为……所以…… to show that not finding work is the cause of the next move.
- Expressiveness: After “害得孟子一直不能睡觉,” add a physical reaction to show his tiredness, such as “他每天早上都觉得很累。”
- Expressiveness: Add one concrete city sound after “很吵,” such as “晚上，街上还有人在大声说话和叫喊。”
- Expressiveness: At “孟母找到工作,” add Mengmu’s feeling, such as “孟母终于放心了一点。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the problem in the city and its effect on Mengzi, especially with the cause-and-result phrase “害得孟子一直不能睡觉.”
- Your scene also moves smoothly through different places and gives Mengmu a new job in the final sentence.

## Scene 3

> 孟子在那个商店也找了到工作，可是不是做衣服的工作，而是喜欢的工作。孟子学习馆学了十五年，他的学习和他的旅行经验很重要，对他很多好处。他是大人的时候，他成为很有名的哲人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 孟子学习馆学了十五年：孟子在学校学习了十五年，学习馆 is not a standard noun for a place of study here; use the location noun 学校 after 在, followed by the verb 学习.
- 对他很多好处：对他有很多好处，when 好处 is the benefit someone receives, use 有 to express that something “has benefits” for that person.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 旅行 -- “他的学习和他的旅行经验很重要”：游历，more specifically suggests experience gained through travelling to different places.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他是大人的时候，他成为了很有名的哲人：他是大人的时候，成为了很有名的哲人，after the first 他 establishes the subject in this sentence, use the zero form rather than repeating the pronoun.
- Coherence (Temporality): 找了到工作：找到了工作，when 了 marks a completed result, it follows the result complement: 找到 + 了, not 找 + 了 + 到.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为很有名的哲人：成为了很有名的哲人，use 了 after 成为 to mark the completed change of state in this past narrative event.
- Expressiveness: After the fifteen years of study, add a concrete learning detail, such as: “他每天都认真读书，也常常思考问题。”
- Expressiveness: At the final sentence, show Mengzi’s feeling about his achievement, such as: “他觉得多年的努力没有白费。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short comment from other people to make his fame vivid, such as: “人们都说：‘他是一位很有智慧的哲人。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show a long passage of time with “学了十五年” and connect Mengzi’s education and travel experience to his later success. The final achievement is also stated clearly and gives the scene a strong ending.
