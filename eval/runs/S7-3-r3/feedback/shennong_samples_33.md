## Scene 1

> 古代时候，人不知道他们能吃什么植物，常常一吃就死。但是不知道为什么，没有办法。所以有一天，神农出去试一试很多种植物，告诉人什么植物是安全什么是危险。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代时候：在古代，use this time expression because the era itself already expresses time; adding 时候 is redundant. [teacher rule: gudai_de_shihou_why]
- 人不知道他们能吃什么植物：人们不知道什么植物能吃，use 人们 for a concrete group of people; the repeated pronoun is unnecessary in this general statement. [teacher rule: ren_renmen]
- 告诉人什么植物是安全什么是危险：告诉人们什么植物是安全的，什么植物是危险的，use 人们 for the group being told; after 是, the adjectives need 的, and the two parallel clauses need clear separation. [teacher rule: ren_renmen] [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：一个叫神农的人，This is the character’s first mention, so introduce him with both his name and a brief noun phrase rather than assuming the reader already knows who he is. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Expressiveness: At the danger in the first sentence, add a specific cause to make the problem clearer: “很多人因为吃了有毒的植物，常常生病。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where the character begins his task, add his determination or inner thought: “神农想：我一定要找出能吃的植物。”
- Expressiveness: When he goes out to test plants, add a small journey detail: “他走啊走啊，去了很多高山。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish the danger people face and give the main character a purposeful reason to act. Your use of the result pattern with 一吃就 also makes the danger feel immediate.

## Scene 2

> 发现有的植物对身体非常好，特别是茶，让肚子感觉又暖和又清楚。他告诉朋友：“你吃哪种植物，这就要看你的口味。比方说，你爱吃甜的，可能喜欢糖。”他最喜欢吃花，除了夹竹桃以外，什么都花想吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 发现：发现了，This discovery is a completed event in the narrative, so the verb needs 了 to mark completion. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- 让肚子感觉又暖和又清楚：让人感觉又暖和又清醒，清楚 describes whether information or an idea is understandable; 清醒 describes a person’s alert physical or mental state and can naturally pair with 暖和 here. [rule: none]
- 你吃哪种植物，这就要看你的口味：你想吃哪种植物？这就要看你的口味了，这就要看……了 should respond to an uncertain question rather than follow a statement; the final 了 belongs to this fixed structure. [rule: zhe_jiu_yao_kan]
- 什么都花想吃：什么花都想吃，With an indefinite “every kind” phrase, use 什么 + noun + 都 + verb; 花 must follow 什么. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好 -- “有的植物对身体非常好”：很有好处，specifies that the plants benefit health; 非常有益，states the health benefit more directly and precisely.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the discovery of tea, add a sensory detail such as “茶水冒着热气，喝起来有一点苦。” This helps the reader imagine the tea.
- Expressiveness: You could show the character’s reaction to the discovery with a thought or a short line of speech, such as “原来茶不但好喝，还对身体有好处！”
- Expressiveness: When mentioning the flowers, add one visual detail such as “那些花有黄色的花瓣，像小星星一样。” This makes the final sentence more vivid.

### Encouragement
You used a clear example marker, “比方说,” to explain the idea about taste. The phrase “除了夹竹桃以外” also shows that you are learning useful sentence patterns for adding exceptions.

## Scene 3

> 他发现这个口味，一百岁时候，不小心吃了毒的花。一吃就开始死。但是生病时候告诉人什么植物是药，什么植物是毒。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一百岁时候：一百岁的时候，A multi-syllable time expression before 时候 needs 的. [teacher rule: de_shihou]
- 毒的花：有毒的花，毒 is a noun; use 有毒的 before a noun to describe a poisonous plant or flower. [vocab list]
- 开始死：快要死了，死 is a change-of-state event, so it does not naturally follow 开始; 快要 expresses that death is about to happen. [rule: none]
- 生病时候：生病的时候，The verb phrase before 时候 needs 的. [teacher rule: de_shihou]
- 什么植物是毒：什么植物有毒，有毒 describes a plant that contains poison; 是毒 would identify the whole plant as “poison” rather than describe its property. [vocab list]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不小心 -- “不小心吃了毒的花”：误食了，specifically means accidentally eating something unsuitable or harmful, which fits the poisonous-flower situation.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 这个口味：一种味道，This is the first mention of this taste, so 这个 has no clear earlier referent; introduce the object with a full noun phrase instead. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Reference): 告诉人：告诉人们，This refers to a concrete group of people, so 人们 is needed rather than the generic noun 人. [teacher rule: ren_renmen]
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉人们：告诉了人们，This is a completed storytelling action, so add 了 after the verb. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: At the point where he eats the flower, add a visual detail about it, such as: “那朵花又黄又大，像星星一样。”
- Expressiveness: After the harmful effect begins, show a physical feeling rather than only saying that he may die, such as: “他的肚子突然疼了起来，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Add an inner thought to show his fear, such as: “他想：我会不会死？”

### Encouragement
You used the pattern “一……就……” effectively to show that eating the flower quickly caused a serious result. The word “不小心” also clearly shows that the character did not mean to eat it.
