## Scene 1

> 很久以前，古代的人很不知道什么植物能吃，也什么植物不能吃。所以很多人常常吃不安全的植物，吃不安全的植物对你的身体有坏处，很麻烦的情况。但是一个人，神农，要解决这个问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的人：人们，Use 人们 for a concrete group of people in general, rather than 人.
- 很不知道：不知道，A degree adverb such as 很 does not modify the negated mental-state verb 不知道.
- 也什么植物不能：什么植物不能，也 must be followed by a verb phrase, not directly by the noun phrase 什么植物.
- 很麻烦的情况：是一件很麻烦的事，The original is only a noun phrase after the comma; adding 是 creates a complete predicate, and 一件 is the appropriate measure-word structure for an event.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 坏处 -- “对你的身体有坏处”：有害，states more directly that the plants damage health.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 吃不安全的植物对你的身体：这对人们的身体，This repeats the action immediately and then unexpectedly addresses the reader with 你的; use 这 to refer back to the preceding action and keep 人们 as the general group being discussed.
- Coherence (Reference): 但是一个人，神农：但是有一个叫神农的人，This is Shen Nong’s first mention, so introduce him clearly with his name and a full noun phrase.
- Expressiveness: After the harmful plants are mentioned, add a concrete result to show why they are dangerous, such as “很多人吃了以后肚子疼，还会生病。”
- Expressiveness: At the introduction of Shen Nong, add his purpose or determination, such as “神农想：我一定要找出能吃的植物。”
- Expressiveness: Add one small descriptive detail about his exploration, such as “他走进高山，认真地看各种各样的植物。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish the central problem of not knowing which plants are edible, and you introduce Shen Nong as someone who wants to solve it. The use of 所以 also gives the scene a clear cause-and-effect direction.

## Scene 2

> 他又聪明又努力，旅行很远找到很多植物。比方说，灵芝和夹竹桃。他一吃各种各样的植物就知道能不能吃这些植物。然后，神农写他的主意。他喜欢植物的味道，特别是花。神农遇到了一个黄色的花，一吃就觉得不舒服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 旅行很远找到很多植物：去了很远的地方，找到了很多植物，Use a destination phrase after 去; both going and finding are completed actions in this past narrative, so they take 了.
- 写：写下了，写下 means to record an idea in writing, and 了 marks this completed action.
- 一个黄色的花：一朵黄色的花，朵 is the matching measure word for a flower; 遇到 is usually used for people, events, or problems, while 看到 fits noticing a flower.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很多 -- “找到了很多植物”：许多种，specifies that he found many different kinds of plants；各类，highlights the range of plant types he found.
- 不舒服 -- “一吃就觉得不舒服”：肚子不舒服，identifies the discomfort as physical and located in his stomach；身体不适，states more clearly that he feels physically unwell.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 然后，神农：然后，他，Use a pronoun when the same character was named recently and no other character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农遇到了：他遇到了，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence of the same character.
- Coherence (Reference): 他的主意：自己的主意，When the possessor is the same person as the subject, 自己的 makes that relationship clear.
- Expressiveness: At the point where he sees the flower, add visual detail to make the setting vivid, for example: “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花。”
- Expressiveness: After he feels ill, show a specific physical reaction, for example: “他的肚子疼了起来，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought to show his fear at that moment, for example: “他想：‘我会死吗？’”

### Encouragement
- You used the patterns “又……又……” and “一……就……” effectively to describe Shennong’s qualities and quick reactions.
- Your scene clearly introduces the important plants and shows that tasting one of them has a harmful result.

## Scene 3

> 发现了夹竹桃有毒。别人看到他，很快给他吃一个灵芝。神农一吃就觉得比较好。然后，他继续写他发现的信息。会告诉他的朋友哪一个植物能不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别人：一个人，The word “别人” distinguishes other people from a stated person or group; it cannot introduce an unspecified helper here.
- 比较好：好多了，In this recovery context, “好多了” expresses that his condition improved from before; “比较好” normally compares one option or state with another stated or understood comparison.
- 继续写他发现的信息：继续记下他发现的信息，“写信息” can mean composing a message, while “记下信息” means recording the information he discovered.
- 哪一个植物能不能吃：哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃，The original combines a question about one specific plant with a can-or-cannot-eat question. This correction clearly states the two groups of plants he can explain.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很快 -- “很快给他吃一个灵芝”：赶快，adds the sense that the helper acts urgently because Shen Nong needs help right away；立刻，emphasizes that the action happens without delay

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 给他吃一个灵芝：给他吃了一个灵芝，This is one completed rescue action, so the verb phrase needs completed-action “了.”
- Expressiveness: At the moment Shen Nong becomes ill, add a physical detail to show how bad he feels: “他躺在地上，肚子疼得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: When the helper arrives, describe him and the medicinal plant more vividly: “这时，一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人走过来，给神农吃了一个像伞一样的灵芝。”
- Expressiveness: Add Shen Nong’s worried thought before the helper appears: “神农想：我会不会死？”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the cause-and-effect sequence: Shen Nong learns that the flower is poisonous, eats lingzhi, feels better, and records what he learned. You also used the pattern “一……就……” effectively to connect the medicine with his improvement.
