## Scene 1

> 古代的时候人觉得祭拜非常重要，孟子很小的时候，从早到晚一直祭拜，因为他妈妈告诉他祭拜很重要。虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜。他的同学们老是对他开玩笑了。孟子妈妈看了他的同学们对孟子不好的影响，所以孟子和孟子妈妈搬家了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，this phrase redundantly combines a historical time period with a second “time of” expression.
- 人：人们，use 人们 for a concrete group of people who share this belief.
- 看了：看到了，here the mother has visually noticed the classmates’ negative influence; 看 means “look,” while 看到 means “see/notice.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不好的影响 -- “他的同学们对孟子不好的影响”：负面影响，specifies that the effect is harmful in a clear, concise way；不良影响，also precisely describes an undesirable effect.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子很小的时候：有一个叫孟子的小男孩很小的时候，at a character’s first mention, introduce the name together with a full noun phrase that identifies who the character is.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子妈妈：孟子和妈妈，within the same clause, do not repeat the name inside a possessive phrase when the relationship is already clear; use the shorter reference form.
- Coherence (Temporality): 老是对他开玩笑了：老是对他开玩笑，老是 describes a habitual action, so it takes the zero particle rather than completed-action 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜：因为他每天只祭拜，所以他没有朋友，this is a cause-and-effect relationship, so use the paired connector 因为……所以…… rather than mixing 虽然 with 因为.
- Expressiveness: After the description of worshipping all day, add an action detail to show what he copies: “他学着大人的样子，双手合十，还不停地鞠躬。”
- Expressiveness: Before the move, show the mother’s feelings and thoughts: “孟子妈妈很着急，觉得这样下去会影响他的学习。”
- Expressiveness: You could make the classmates’ teasing more vivid with a short line of dialogue: “同学笑着说：‘你怎么每天都在祭拜？’”

### Encouragement
You clearly show a cause for the move in the final sentence, which gives the scene a logical direction. You also use time expressions such as “从早到晚” effectively to show how often the action happens.

## Scene 2

> 孟子才十一岁，成为一个哲学家。孟子不是祭拜，而是看政治文章，难怪他成为了哲学家。不过，看过很多政治文章对他害得，他有很多问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 才十一岁，：才十一岁，就，When 才 introduces an unexpectedly early age before an achieved result, it should pair with 就 before the verb.
- 看过很多政治文章对他害得，他有很多问题：看过很多政治文章害得他有很多问题，害得 must be followed by the person affected and then a harmful result; 对他 cannot fill that position.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子不是祭拜：他不是祭拜，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the same character was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为一个哲学家：成为了一个哲学家，Becoming a philosopher is presented as a completed change of state, so the verb needs 了.
- Expressiveness: At the point where he reads the articles, add a physical detail to show his concentration, such as “孟子皱着眉头，一边看文章，一边想。”
- Expressiveness: After mentioning his questions, add one short inner thought or question to make them concrete, such as “他想：‘为什么人们要这样做？’”

### Encouragement
- You used the contrast pattern “不是……而是……” clearly to show the difference between worshipping and reading. The phrase “才十一岁” also creates an interesting sense that his achievement happened very early.

## Scene 3

> 他一直问问题，但是不能解决那些问题。孟子长大，他开始能解决问题。他常常建议你不是学金融，而是学政治，因为如果你懂政治，你会成为高薪。后来孟子成为教授，他教学生很厉害。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 建议你不是学金融，而是学政治：建议你不要学金融，而是学政治，After 建议 + person, a negative recommendation uses 不要 + VP; 不是……而是…… cannot directly replace the negative action in this structure.
- 成为高薪：成为高薪人士，High salary is a type of pay, not a role or identity that someone can become; 高薪人士 refers to a person who earns a high salary.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 问 -- “他一直问问题”：提出问题，emphasizes raising questions for discussion or thought；不断追问，emphasizes repeatedly asking for an answer to unresolved questions
- 很厉害 -- “他教学生很厉害”：很会教学生，states more precisely that he is skilled at teaching students；教学很有办法，adds that he has effective teaching methods

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子长大：他长大，Use a pronoun when the same character was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other possible referent has appeared.
- Coherence (Temporality): 长大：长大了，Growing up is a completed change of state here, so it needs 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为教授：成为了教授，Becoming a professor is a completed change of state in this past sequence, so it needs 了.
- Expressiveness: At the point where he cannot solve the questions, add an internal feeling to show why this matters: “他觉得很着急，决定认真学习。”
- Expressiveness: When he teaches students, add one concrete teaching action: “他耐心地给学生解释难懂的政治问题。”
- Expressiveness: A short line of speech could make his role as a professor more vivid: “孟子对学生说：‘遇到问题，要先认真思考。’”

### Encouragement
You use time words such as 后来 to guide the reader through the scene. The final sentence also clearly shows Mengzi’s strength as a teacher.
