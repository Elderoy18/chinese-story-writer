## Scene 1

> 古代的时候有一个人叫神农。他需要当上医，一看别的人就想帮助他们。但是，他有一个问题，他不知道什么东西能做药。神农听说一个远远的地方有一种植物，什么病人吃都会觉得身体更好。神农三个月走去，找到有植物的地方，他看到一个很漂亮的植物，又香又好看。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，The original repeats the time meaning because 古代 already names a time period. [vocab list]
- 当上医：当上医生，医生 is the ordinary occupation noun after 当上; 医 alone does not fit naturally in this position. [rule: none]
- 一看别的人：一看到别人，看到 means successfully seeing someone, while 看 is simply looking; 别人 refers to other people, whereas 别的 is used to distinguish another item from the one being discussed. [vocab list]
- 神农听说一个远远的地方：神农听说在一个很远的地方，The location needs 在, and 很远的地方 is the natural way to describe a faraway place. [rule: none]
- 一个很漂亮的植物：一株很漂亮的植物，株 is the matching measure word for an individual plant; 个 is too general here. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 香 -- “又香又好看”：芬芳，specifies that the plant has a pleasant, noticeable floral fragrance.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 什么东西能做药：什么植物能做药，东西 is too vague in this context; naming 植物 makes clear what kind of thing the narrator means. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Temporality): 神农三个月走去：神农走了三个月，A completed journey lasting a period uses the pattern verb + 了 + duration. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 找到：找到了，Finding the place is a completed result in the narrative, so the final verb in this action sequence needs 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 看到：看到了，Seeing the plant is a completed event; with a resultative verb, 了 follows the resultative complement. [teacher rule: le_placement]
- Expressiveness: At the description of the plant, add a visual detail that makes it more memorable, such as “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花。”
- Expressiveness: After seeing the plant, add Shen Nong’s reaction to show his interest, such as “神农觉得这种植物很特别。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought before he approaches it, such as “他想：这种植物会不会能做药呢？”

### Encouragement
You clearly introduce Shen Nong and keep the scene focused on his wish to help others. The final description uses both smell and appearance, which already helps the plant feel more vivid.

## Scene 2

> 他觉得，这种一定是做药的花。原来他想错了。吃了以后，神农快生病。 这时一个穿着蓝色衣服的人帮神农，快给他一种红的灵芝。神农吃了以后，他没有病，想起来以前一个人告诉他，灵芝对身体有好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- “这种一定是做药的花”：“这种花一定可以做药”，Use a modal verb before the predicate to state that the plant can be used as medicine; the original noun phrase is not a natural predicate here. [rule: none]
- “快”：“赶快”，In narration, this adverb means acting promptly; the original form is normally used to urge someone to act. [rule: none]
- “告诉他”：“告诉过他”，The telling happened before the moment of remembering, so the experiential particle marks that earlier event. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): “他”：“∅”，The subject has already been named in the same sentence, so use zero form rather than repeat a pronoun. [rule: redundancy_delete]
- Coherence (Temporality): “神农快生病”：“神农很快就生病了”，This illness is a completed change of state after the eating, so it needs 了; the revised timing phrase shows that it happened soon afterward. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): “给他”：“给了他”，Giving the medicine is a completed action in the event sequence, so it needs 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): “想起来”：“想起了”，Remembering is presented as a completed event in the sequence, so 了 follows the resultative verb. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: At the moment of illness, add a physical feeling to show how serious it is: “神农肚子疼得不得了，只能躺在地上。”
- Expressiveness: When the helper arrives, add urgency and a short line of speech: “一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人跑过来，说：‘快吃这个！’”
- Expressiveness: After recovering, show relief and gratitude through an action or speech: “神农松了一口气，说：‘谢谢你！这是什么？’”

### Encouragement
You clearly show an important reversal when the character realizes that his earlier belief was wrong. You also use a time-transition effectively to introduce the helper at a dramatic moment.

## Scene 3

> 啊，他怎么忘记了？他把灵芝放在包里。一回家就告诉别人。他找到药以后他写下怎么做药在书上。所以人们都知道，那红灵芝对身体好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 写下怎么做药在书上：在书上写下怎么做药，In a no-把 construction, the locative phrase showing where the writing happens comes before the verb; placing it after the object is not natural. [rule: none]
- 那红灵芝：那种红灵芝，A demonstrative before a noun needs an appropriate measure word; 种 identifies a type of plant. [teacher rule: zhe_na_mw]
- 对身体好处：对身体有好处，The expression requires 有 between 对身体 and 好处. [vocab list]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他找到药以后他写下怎么做药：他找到药以后写下怎么做药，For the same character in the same sentence, use zero form rather than repeating the pronoun. [rule: none]
- Coherence (Temporality): 他把灵芝放在包里：他把灵芝放在了包里，This is a completed placement action in the narrative, so it needs 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 一回家就告诉别人：一回家就告诉了别人，This tells a completed action after returning home, so 告诉 takes 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 写下怎么做药：写下了怎么做药，The writing is presented as completed, so 了 follows the result complement 下. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: After the first question, add a short thought to show why he suddenly remembers, such as: “啊，原来灵芝对身体有好处！”
- Expressiveness: When he writes in the book, add a feeling or purpose, such as: “他认真地把灵芝的好处写在书上，希望能帮助更多人。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the sequence from returning home to sharing and recording the discovery. Your use of a cause-and-effect link also helps show how the information becomes known to people.
