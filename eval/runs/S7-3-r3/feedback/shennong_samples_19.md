## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男人觉得植物很有意思。他叫神农。有的植物吃了以后他的肚子疼死了，可是有的吃了以后很舒服。他找很多植物。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 already expresses a time period, so adding 的时候 is redundant.
- 他找很多植物：他找到了很多植物，找 means “to look for,” while 找到 means “to successfully find”; this completed event needs 了.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一个男人觉得植物很有意思。他叫神农：一个叫神农的男人觉得植物很有意思，On first mention, introduce the character’s name together with a full noun phrase, rather than naming him only in the following sentence.
- Expressiveness: After the character becomes interested in plants, add his question or curiosity: “什么植物可以吃呢？” This shows his reason for looking for plants.
- Expressiveness: When describing the painful result, add a physical detail: “他疼得在地上打滚。” This makes the pain easier for the reader to imagine.
- Expressiveness: Add one visible detail about a plant he finds, such as: “有一种植物开着黄色的花，像星星一样。” This makes the scene more vivid.

### Encouragement
- You clearly show two different results from eating different plants, and the contrast with 可是 helps the reader understand the danger.

## Scene 2

> 凉山很大的山，这也找植物。一天，他找到黄色的花。他听说你跟黄色的花做茶很有用。比方说，你的头发疼死，这冰茶是药。他一喝茶就很不舒服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 凉山很大的山：凉山是一座很大的山，A noun cannot directly modify another noun as the main predicate here; use 是 and the measure word 座 to describe what kind of place it is.
- 这也找植物：他也在这儿找植物，The action needs a person as its subject, and a location for an action is expressed with 在 plus a location word such as 这儿.
- 找到黄色的花：找到了一朵黄色的花，This is a completed finding event, so add 了 after the resultative verb; 一朵 also introduces the newly found flower clearly.
- 跟黄色的花做茶：用黄色的花做茶，跟 links people or things doing an action together, while 用 marks the material used to make something.
- 头发疼死：头疼得要死，头发 is hair and cannot be the body part that feels pain; use 头疼 for a headache, with 得要死 as a degree complement.
- 这冰茶：这杯冰茶，A demonstrative before a countable noun needs a measure word; 杯 is the suitable measure word for a drink.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很大 -- “凉山是一座很大的山”：高大，specifies that the mountain is notably tall as well as large；又高又大，adds both height and size in a vivid but simple way.
- 很有用 -- “用黄色的花做茶很有用”：可以做药，states the useful medicinal purpose more precisely；能治头疼，specifies the exact benefit if the tea is meant to help with a headache.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 你：人们，The pronoun has no introduced referent in this narrative. Use a full noun for a newly mentioned general group.
- Expressiveness: After the flower appears, add a visual detail: “黄色的花又大又漂亮，像星星一样。”
- Expressiveness: When the character becomes uncomfortable, show a physical reaction and thought: “他捂着肚子，难受得不得了，心想：‘我会不会生病了？’”
- Expressiveness: You could make the information about the tea sound more story-like with reported speech: “神农听别人说：‘用这种黄色的花做茶，可以治头疼。’”

### Encouragement
- You used the pattern 一……就…… correctly to show that the discomfort happened immediately after drinking the tea.
- You also tried to give an example with 比方说, which is a useful way to explain why something is helpful.

## Scene 3

> 糟糕，这黄色的花是夹竹桃，特别是毒。正好在这儿，灵芝药的夹竹桃他吃完了，也写一个课文的植物。谢谢神农。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 这黄色的花：这朵黄色的花，A demonstrative such as 这 needs a matching measure word before a countable noun; 朵 is the measure word for flowers.
- 特别是毒：特别有毒，毒 cannot be used as an adjectival predicate after a degree expression; 有毒 means “poisonous” and can follow 特别.
- 正好在这儿，灵芝：这儿正好有灵芝，The original places a location phrase before a noun but has no predicate to state that the plant is present; use 有 to introduce something existing in a place.
- 灵芝药的夹竹桃：灵芝是药，夹竹桃有毒，的 must link a modifier to a noun; it cannot join two separate statements about what lingzhi and oleander are.
- 也写一个课文的植物：也把这些植物写进了一本书里，When the meaning is to record plants, 写 cannot directly take 植物 as its object; use a recording structure such as 写进……里. The original noun phrase also does not correctly express a book about plants.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the opening exclamation, add Shennong’s physical reaction to show why the discovery matters: 神农的肚子疼了起来，难受得不得了。
- Expressiveness: At the point where lingzhi appears, describe its shape so the reader can picture it: 旁边有一种像伞一样的植物，叫灵芝。
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought after Shennong learns about the poisonous flower: 神农想：“这种黄色的花不能吃。”

### Encouragement
You used an effective emotional opening with 糟糕, which immediately signals danger. You also included both 夹竹桃 and 灵芝, showing that you are connecting the plants to Shennong’s discoveries.
