## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有很多人不知道什么植物能吃，什么植物不能吃。有很多人吃植物以后就吃坏肚子了，因为他们吃了毒。那个时候，有一个老人叫神农，他什么植物都觉得好吃，不怕生病，不怕毒。他希望别人也可以吃好吃的植物。可是他知道，如果他们还怕吃毒，不会吃他喜欢的植物。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，The original phrase redundantly combines two time expressions; use the shorter time expression for the historical setting.
- 吃了毒：吃了有毒的植物，毒 refers to poison itself, but the object here is a plant; use 有毒的 to describe a poisonous plant.
- 怕吃毒：怕吃有毒的植物，毒 alone refers to poison, while 有毒的植物 clearly names the poisonous plants that people are afraid to eat.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the sentence about people becoming ill, add a physical detail to show their discomfort, such as “有的人捂着肚子，疼得直冒汗。”
- Expressiveness: After introducing 神农, add his purpose or inner thought to make his character more vivid, such as “他想：我要找出哪些植物能吃。”
- Expressiveness: In the final sentence, add a short line of thought to show why he hopes others will try the plants, such as “他想让大家都不用再吃坏肚子。”

### Encouragement
You clearly introduce the problem of people not knowing which plants are safe, and you introduce 神农 with a full noun phrase before using a pronoun. The contrast between his hope and people’s fear also gives the scene a clear direction.

## Scene 2

> 所以，他上山下山找植物，吃了很多样子的植物。他是第一人，找到了茶。他把所有的植物写下来了。他一直都吃了，他连毒都能吃。可是他还没吃了一个叫夹竹桃的植物。他找到了那个像星星的植物，觉得很漂亮，马上就吃下了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很多样子的植物：很多种植物，Use 种 to count different types of plants; 样子 refers to appearance or shape, not categories.
- 他是第一人，找到了茶：他是第一个找到茶的人，An ordinal such as 第一个 must modify 人, and the action that identifies the person needs a relative clause with 的.
- 一个叫夹竹桃的植物：一种叫夹竹桃的植物，Use 种 when referring to one type or species of plant; 个 does not fit this meaning.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 找 -- “他上山下山找植物”：搜寻, emphasizes looking carefully over a wide area, which fits going up and down mountains；探寻, emphasizes exploring in order to discover unfamiliar plants.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 一直都吃了：一直都吃，The phrase 一直 describes an ongoing action, so it takes the zero particle rather than completed-action 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 还没吃了：还没吃过，After 还没, 了 cannot mark a completed action because completion is negated; 过 shows that this is an experience he has not had.
- Expressiveness: Before the plant is eaten, add a visual detail about its colour and shape: “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花。”
- Expressiveness: After the eating action, show the immediate physical result: “他一吃，肚子就疼了起来。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short worried thought after the pain to show his feelings: “他想：我会不会死？”

### Encouragement
You clearly showed the sequence of searching, finding tea, recording plants, and then trying a new plant. The phrase “上山下山” is especially vivid because it shows how much effort the character makes.

## Scene 3

> 可是夹竹桃的毒很重，让他肚子很痛。他在地上躺着，觉得他快会死了。可是正好有别人找到他了，给了他一个像伞的植物，叫灵芝。他吃灵芝以后就觉得很好，不再生病了。他测量起来了，像星星的植物是毒，像伞的植物是药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 快会：快要，Use the imminence pattern with this verb to express that death seemed about to happen; 会 does not naturally express this immediate situation.
- 别人：人，别人 distinguishes other people from the speaker or another stated person; for an unspecified rescuer, 有人 is the appropriate expression.
- 是毒：有毒，毒 is a noun here, so the plant should be described as having poison with 有毒.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 痛 -- “让他肚子很痛”：疼得厉害, makes the intensity of his stomach pain more vivid；剧痛, gives a concise, stronger description of severe pain.
- 很好 -- “他吃灵芝以后就觉得很好”：好多了, specifically shows that his condition improved after being ill；舒服多了, emphasizes his physical relief after taking the medicine.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 觉得他快要死了：觉得快要死了，Use zero form when the person who feels something and the person about to die are the same character in this close context.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他测量起来了，：他测量这些植物后，发现，Use ……以后 to show that the discovery came after the measuring action, and use 发现 to connect the action with its result.
- Expressiveness: After the stomach pain, add a physical detail to show how serious it is, such as “他的脸色变得很白，疼得直冒汗。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where he thinks he may die, add an inner thought or quoted question, such as “他心想：我会不会死在这里？”
- Expressiveness: Describe the rescuer briefly to make the entrance clearer, such as “这时，一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人快步走过来。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the cause of Shennong’s pain and the sequence in which he receives and eats the medicine. The contrast between the poisonous plant and the medicinal plant is also easy for the reader to understand.
