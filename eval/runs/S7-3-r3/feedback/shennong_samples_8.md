## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个人叫神农。一天，神农正在路上走，一看很多人正在吃一些植物。一些人一吃植物就生病了，神农想帮别人。所以想知道哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，The time word already expresses a historical period, so adding 的时候 is redundant. [vocab list]
- 看：看到，看 means “look at,” while 看到 means “see”; here Shennong sees the people eating plants. [vocab list]
- 帮别人：帮人们，别人 distinguishes other people from a particular person already being discussed; this sentence means the general group of people who are becoming ill. [teacher rule: bieren]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 想知道 -- “所以想知道哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃”：弄清，emphasizes finding a clear answer after investigation.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 一看到很多人正在吃一些植物。一些人一吃植物就生病了：一看到很多人正在吃一些植物，就发现一些人一吃植物就生病了，A clause beginning with 一V needs 就 before the immediate result; 就 links the observation to what Shennong discovers. [teacher rule: yi_jiu]
- Expressiveness: After the people become ill, add a visible symptom to make the danger concrete: “一些人捂着肚子，脸色很难看。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where Shennong decides to investigate, add his inner voice: “神农想：我一定要弄清楚哪些植物能吃！”
- Expressiveness: When Shennong is walking, add one detail about the setting: “路边长着各种各样的植物。”

### Encouragement
You introduce Shennong clearly and give the scene a logical purpose: he sees illness and wants to find out which plants are safe. You also use progressive actions effectively to show what people are doing in the moment.

## Scene 2

> 然后，神农开始尝很多植物，比方说他吃过生姜、芥兰和菠菜。一些植物吃真好，特别是黄瓜。但是，一些植物毒得很，特别是夹竹桃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一些植物吃真好：一些植物很好吃，Use the adjective phrase with the plant as its subject; this states that the plants taste good. [rule: none]
- 一些植物毒得很：一些植物有毒，The word 毒 cannot take a degree complement such as 得很; use 有毒 to describe a poisonous plant. [teacher rule: du_youdu]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 吃过 -- “比方说他吃过生姜、芥兰和菠菜”：尝过，emphasizes tasting plants as part of careful testing and matches the scene’s exploration.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the list of plants, add a small reaction to show that the tasting was successful, for example: “他觉得生姜有一点辣，可是很好吃。”
- Expressiveness: At the dangerous plant, add Shennong’s cautious thought to make the contrast more vivid, for example: “神农想：这种花这么漂亮，能吃吗？”
- Expressiveness: Describe the oleander briefly before naming it, for example: “夹竹桃开着黄色的花，看起来像小星星。”

### Encouragement
- You used clear examples of specific plants, which makes the scene easy to imagine.
- The contrast between plants that are good to eat and poisonous plants gives this short scene a strong, logical structure.

## Scene 3

> 一个人对神农说，麻烦我们可以吃什么植物？神农说，这就要看你想不想生病了。以后，神农告诉别人一些植物能吃，一些植物不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 麻烦我们可以吃什么植物：请问，我们可以吃什么植物，麻烦 needs a person and a requested action; it cannot directly introduce a question. [rule: none]
- 以后：后来，when a new sentence means “afterwards” in a narrative, use 后来; 以后 normally refers to a stated future time or event. [vocab list]
- 告诉：告诉了，this is a completed reporting action in the narrative, so it needs 了; the zero particle applies to a speaking verb that introduces direct quoted speech. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- 别人：人们，别人 distinguishes other people from the speaker or a specified group, while this sentence refers generally to the people Shennong taught. [teacher rule: bieren]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 说 -- “一个人对神农说”：问，identifies the utterance more precisely as a question; 询问，emphasizes that the person is seeking information.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：他，Shennong was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no competing male character has been introduced, so use a pronoun for this close re-occurrence. [rule: redundancy_delete]
- Expressiveness: At the opening question, add the person’s feeling to show why the question matters, such as “一个人着急地问：‘请问，我们可以吃什么植物？’”
- Expressiveness: When Shennong explains, add a short, clear contrast in his speech, such as “他认真地说：‘有些植物能吃，有些植物有毒，不能吃。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly included a question and an answer, which makes this short scene feel like a real conversation. You also successfully used the A-not-A pattern in “想不想,” and you clearly contrasted edible and non-edible plants.
