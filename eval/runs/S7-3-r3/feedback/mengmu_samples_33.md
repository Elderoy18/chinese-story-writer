## Scene 1

> 一天，很久很久以前，孟子和他妈妈住在中国。孟子妈妈的工作是祭拜，孟子不高兴，因为他说我们老是搬家，为什么？他妈妈说，从早到晚，我一直祭拜，很多墓地得我祭拜。所以我们得去很多不同的地方。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一天，很久很久以前：很久很久以前的一天，When a time phrase modifies a specific day, it should come before that day as one complete time expression. [rule: time_expression]
- 因为他说我们老是搬家，为什么？：因为他们老是搬家。他问妈妈：“我们老是搬家，为什么？”，A question should be introduced as direct speech with quotation marks, or with a question verb such as 问; saying the question directly after 说 creates an incomplete reported-speech structure. [rule: none]
- 很多墓地得我祭拜：我得祭拜很多墓地，When 得 means “must,” the person who has the obligation is the subject, and 得 comes before the action verb. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 一直 -- “从早到晚，我一直祭拜”：不停地，emphasizes that the worship continues without breaks throughout the day.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和他妈妈住在中国：有一个小男孩叫孟子，他和妈妈住在中国，At first mention, introduce the character with both a name and a brief identifying noun phrase; then use a pronoun for the close re-occurrence. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Expressiveness: At the point where Mengzi is unhappy, add a small physical reaction to show his feeling: “孟子皱着眉头，觉得很烦恼。”
- Expressiveness: Make Mengzi’s question sound more personal through quoted speech: “妈妈，我们为什么又要搬家？”
- Expressiveness: After the mother explains her work, add a detail about her tiredness: “她每天走来走去，累得满头大汗。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show both Mengzi’s unhappiness and his mother’s reason for moving, so the conversation has a logical purpose.
- The phrase “从早到晚” is a strong detail that helps the reader understand how busy the mother is.

## Scene 2

> 孟子想，难怪我们常常搬家，原来我妈妈很忙。可是常常搬家害得孟子很难找到朋友。每次搬家孟子也觉得新的地方不是太热就是太冷，不是太大就是太小。在哪的地方，他都不高兴。孟子想帮助妈妈祭拜，可是他六岁，所以不能祭拜。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在哪的地方：在哪个地方，The interrogative determiner 哪 needs the classifier 个 before the noun 地方. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 新的地方 -- “孟子也觉得新的地方不是太热就是太冷”：新住处，specifies that the new place is where the family lives；新家，makes the moving-home context more immediate and personal.
- 不高兴 -- “他都不高兴”：闷闷不乐，shows that he feels quietly unhappy over time；不开心，gives a more natural everyday expression of his feeling.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 每次搬家孟子也觉得：每次搬家他也觉得，孟子 was the subject of the immediately previous sentence, so the close re-occurrence rule calls for a pronoun. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子想帮助妈妈祭拜：他想帮助妈妈祭拜，The previous sentence already identifies 孟子 as 他, so a pronoun is more natural for this close re-occurrence. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Expressiveness: After “很难找到朋友,” add a small feeling detail to show why this is difficult, such as “他觉得很孤单，也很想念以前的朋友。”
- Expressiveness: At “不是太热就是太冷,” add a concrete sensory detail, such as “夏天太阳晒得他满头大汗，冬天冷得他直发抖。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought after “不能祭拜” to show his disappointment, such as “孟子心想：‘我什么时候才能帮妈妈呢？’”

### Encouragement
You used the pattern “难怪……，原来……” very well to connect Mengzi’s realization with the reason for the family’s frequent moves. You also clearly expressed several different difficulties he feels about moving.

### Vocabulary Suggestions

- Coherence (Reference): 今天很多中国人很喜欢孟子：今天很多中国人很喜欢他，The character was named in the immediately preceding sentence and there is no competing referent, so a pronoun is more natural here. [rule: none]

### Encouragement
