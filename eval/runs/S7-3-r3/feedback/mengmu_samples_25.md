## Scene 1

> 这是古代的故事。孟子小的时候，他的爸爸很早就去世了。从他的家到墓地很近，所以他每天老是从早到晚去墓地跟朋友们玩玩儿。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 从他的家到墓地很近：他的家离墓地很近，Use 离 to describe the distance between two places; 从……到…… normally needs a distance expression such as a length or 不远. [vocab list]
- 玩玩儿：玩，Verb reduplication here suggests doing something briefly or casually, which conflicts with an activity lasting from morning to evening. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 这是古代的故事。孟子小的时候，他的爸爸很早就去世了。：在古代，有一个小男孩叫孟子。他小时候，爸爸很早就去世了。Introduce the main character clearly as a boy named Mengzi at his first mention before using pronouns or family references. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Expressiveness: After the mention of the cemetery, add a concrete visual detail such as “每天都有不同的人来墓地祭拜。” This helps the reader picture why the place catches the child’s attention.
- Expressiveness: Add Mengzi’s thought or feeling before he plays there, for example “孟子觉得祭拜很有趣。” This gives his repeated visits a clearer personal motivation.

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish the setting, Mengzi’s family situation, and his daily habit in only three sentences. The cause-and-effect link is also easy to follow.

## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈觉得在墓地玩儿，害得，所以他们搬家。搬家对孟子有好的影响，他才五岁，可是每天不是写字就是帮妈妈的工作。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 觉得在墓地玩儿，害得：觉得孟子在墓地玩儿会害得他学坏，害得 must be followed by the affected person and a harmful result; the corrected version states who is harmed and what the result is. [teacher rule: haide_structure]
- 帮妈妈的工作：帮妈妈做事，帮 needs a person plus an action phrase here; the original phrase treats the action as a noun phrase and does not clearly express what Mengzi does to help. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好的影响 -- “搬家对孟子有好的影响”：积极的影响，specifies that the influence supports Mengzi’s development in a positive direction；益处，more directly names the benefit of moving in this sentence.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他的妈妈：孟子的妈妈，when Mengzi’s mother appears for the first time, use a full noun phrase to introduce her clearly rather than a possessive pronoun. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Temporality): 所以他们搬家：所以他们搬家了，the move is a completed event, so the final verb needs 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他才五岁，可是每天不是写字就是帮妈妈做事：他才五岁，就每天不是写字就是帮妈妈做事，才五岁 introduces an unexpectedly early age, so it pairs with 就 rather than a contrast connector. [rule: cai_jiu_construction]
- Expressiveness: After the mother’s concern, add a short thought to show her worry, such as “如果一直住在这儿，孟子会学坏的。”
- Expressiveness: At the writing detail, add a small action detail to make the scene easier to picture, such as “孟子每天一笔一画地写字。”

### Encouragement
- You used the pattern “不是……就是……” well to show Mengzi’s daily routine. The detail “才五岁” also creates a clear impression of his young age.

## Scene 3

> 他很聪明的人，难怪他成为有名的人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他很聪明的人：他是一个很聪明的人，The phrase needs a predicate: add 是一个 before the noun phrase, while 很聪明 modifies 人. [rule: adjective_predicate]
- 成为：成为了，This describes a completed change in the past, so the verb takes 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 难怪他成为有名的人：难怪成为有名的人，The subject has already appeared in the first clause of the same sentence, so use zero form rather than repeating the pronoun. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Expressiveness: At the final result, name his achievement more specifically with a phrase such as “成为了一位有名的哲学家”.
- Expressiveness: Show the connection to his mother’s actions with an evaluative phrase such as “多亏孟母三次搬家”.

### Encouragement
- You used 难怪 effectively to connect his intelligence with his later success. The final sentence also gives the scene a clear concluding feeling.
