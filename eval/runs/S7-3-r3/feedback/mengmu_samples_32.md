## Scene 1

> 有一天孟子去了墓地玩儿，但是他妈妈觉得这是不合适的地方让孩子玩，所以她决定了他们会搬家找更好的地方。她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福，而且住在这儿教育不太好。因为她望子成龙，所以她会一直搬家才能找到质量好的教育。然后他们搬到了离城市更近的地方。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不合适的地方让孩子玩：不适合孩子玩的地方，To describe a place as suitable or unsuitable for an activity, use the pattern “适合 + person + verb + 的 + noun”; “让” would mean that the place causes or permits the child to play. [rule: none]
- 决定了：决定，“决定” is a mental-state verb, so it normally takes the zero particle rather than “了”. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- 他们会搬家找更好的地方：搬家，找更好的地方，“决定” should be followed directly by the action that is decided; do not repeat the subject or add “会” here. [teacher rule: jueding_vp]
- 她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福：她担心儿子的童年不会幸福，“恐怕” means “I am afraid that” and cannot describe another person’s fear after “她”; “担心” expresses her worry correctly. [teacher rule: kongpa]
- 住在这儿教育不太好：这儿的教育不太好，“教育” needs a clear location-based modifier here, because the sentence means that the education in this place is not good. [rule: none]
- 质量好的教育：高质量的教育，“高质量的” is the usual modifier for education of good quality; “质量好” is more natural as a predicate, such as “教育质量很好”. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 更好的 -- “找更好的地方”：更适合孩子成长的，specifies that the new place is better because it supports the child’s development.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为她望子成龙，所以她会一直搬家才能找到质量好的教育。，“因为” introduces a cause, so add “所以” before the result clause to show the cause-and-effect relationship clearly. [rule: none]
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery setting, add one concrete detail about what Meng子 sees or copies, such as “孟子常常看见人们来祭拜，还学着他们的样子鞠躬。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where his mother worries, add her inner thought or quoted speech, such as “孟母心想：‘如果一直住在这儿，孟子可能会学坏。’”
- Expressiveness: When they move nearer the city, describe the new surroundings, such as “城市里从早到晚很热闹，商人不停地叫卖。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the mother’s reason for moving and use “因为” to connect her hope for her son with her actions. The sequence from the cemetery to the new place is also easy to follow.

## Scene 2

> 他们住才一月就又搬家。古代的时候，一般人不喜欢卖东西的人。但是孟子老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿，害得妈妈着急得很，因为她恐怕儿子会成为跟他们很像。她告诉了孟子：他们不是朋友，而是不好的人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 住才一月：才住了一个月，The duration phrase should follow the verb, and this past completed stay needs the measure word and completed-action marker. [rule: cai_jiu_construction]
- 古代的时候：在古代，This expression redundantly combines two time expressions; use the simpler historical-time phrase. [vocab list]
- 老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿：老是跟这些卖东西的人玩儿，Using two frequency adverbs for the same habitual action is redundant; keep one. [rule: none]
- 她恐怕：她担心，This subject is afraid about her son’s future, so use the verb meaning “to worry”; 恐怕 cannot follow a subject in this structure. [teacher rule: kongpa]
- 会成为跟他们很像：会变得跟他们很像，The verb meaning “become” needs a noun phrase here, while the phrase describes a change into a similar state; use the change-of-state verb instead. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 卖东西的人 -- “这些卖东西的人”：商贩，names people whose work is selling goods more precisely.
- 不好 -- “不好的人”：品行不好，specifies that the criticism is about the people’s moral character.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 又搬家：又搬家了，The move is a completed event in the past, so the final action takes 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉了孟子：告诉孟子，A speaking verb that introduces quoted speech normally uses the zero particle rather than 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: After the mention of the sellers, add one sensory detail about the marketplace, such as “市场从早到晚都很热闹，到处都是叫卖声。”
- Expressiveness: Show what Mengzi copies from the sellers to make his behaviour clearer, such as “孟子还学着他们收钱、讨价还价。”
- Expressiveness: Give the mother a short inner thought before her warning, such as “孟母心想：‘如果他天天这样玩儿，以后还会认真学习吗？’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the mother’s worry and connect it to Mengzi’s behaviour with a cause-and-effect relationship.
- Ending the scene with the mother’s direct speech is effective because it lets the reader hear her concern directly.

## Scene 3

> 你不是当数学家，而是当哲学家，不能当卖东西的人。所以他们又搬家。在他们第三个家，孟子学了怎么祭拜。妈妈感觉这个地方适合他们，告诉了孟子：我们找到了最后的家。在这儿妈妈让儿子从早到晚学习，他难怪成为有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections

- 告诉了孟子：告诉孟子，A speaking verb that introduces quoted speech takes the zero particle, not 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- 成为有名的哲学家：成为了有名的哲学家，Becoming a philosopher is a completed change of state in the narrative, so use 了 after the verb. [rule: aspect_le_completed]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 卖东西的人 -- “不能当卖东西的人”：商人，identifies the people more precisely as people whose work is buying and selling.
- 地方 -- “妈妈感觉这个地方适合他们”：环境，emphasizes the home’s surroundings and conditions for the child’s learning.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After “妈妈感觉这个地方适合他们,” add a concrete detail about the new surroundings, such as “这儿很安静，学校也在旁边。”
- Expressiveness: Give the mother a feeling or a short line of speech at “我们找到了最后的家,” such as “妈妈高兴地说：‘这儿终于适合你学习了！’”
- Expressiveness: At the ending, show why the long study time helped Mengzi, such as “孟子每天认真看书，也常常思考老师的话。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the important final change: the mother finds a place that supports Mengzi’s learning. You also use the contrast structure “不是……而是……” correctly to show what future she wants for him.
