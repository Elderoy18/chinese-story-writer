## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个男人，他的名字是神农。他看到了有很多人不知道哪个植物好吃，也不知道什么植物对你的身体有好处。这个问题神农觉得很糟糕，所以神农去了吃每种植物。他吃了一种植物以后，就写了这个植物对你有没有好处。有的时候，神农吃了很好吃的植物。有的时候，他吃了很危险的植物。他吃完了每种植物以后，会来跟他的朋友聊到他吃的植物了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，Both parts refer to time, so using them together is redundant. [teacher rule: gudai_de_shihou_why]
- 去了吃：去吃了，With a verb following 去, the completion marker comes after that verb: 去 + V. + 了. [teacher rule: le_placement]
- 写了：记下了，Here the action is recording information about each plant; this needs the recording verb rather than the general verb for writing. [vocab list]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 糟糕 -- “这个问题神农觉得很糟糕”：严重，specifies that the problem has serious consequences；令人担忧，shows more clearly why the problem worries Shennong.
- 危险 -- “他吃了很危险的植物”：有害的，specifies that these plants can harm someone’s body.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 所以神农：所以他，Shennong was named in the immediately previous sentence and no other male character intervenes, so a pronoun is more natural for a close re-occurrence. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Temporality): 有的时候，神农吃了很好吃的植物：有的时候，神农吃很好吃的植物，This describes something that happens sometimes, so a routine uses the zero particle rather than completed-action 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 有的时候，他吃了很危险的植物：有的时候，他吃很危险的植物，This is also a repeated habit, so the verb takes no 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃完了每种植物以后：吃完每种植物以后，This clause sets a repeated condition before the later action, so it uses the zero particle. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 聊到他吃的植物了：聊到他吃的植物，The modal verb 会 describes a habitual or future action, so the following verb phrase does not take 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: When describing the harmful plants, add a physical reaction to show the danger: “他吃了以后，肚子疼了起来。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where he records his discoveries, add one concrete detail about what he writes: “他记下了植物的颜色、味道和用处。”
- Expressiveness: When he talks with his friends, add a short line of speech: “这种植物对身体有好处，可以做药。”

### Encouragement
You clearly introduce Shennong at the beginning, and you show both the helpful and dangerous sides of the plants. The repeated time phrases also help the reader understand that he tests many different plants.

## Scene 2

> 一个人问神农，哪个植物好吃。神农说，这就要看你的口味了。要是你喜欢吃甜的，有很多甜的水果，比方说苹果、西瓜和梨。一个别的人问神农，哪个植物对你的身体有好处。神农说，有很多种植物对你的身体有好处，比方说小白菜、黄瓜、茶和菠菜。一个别的人问神农，哪个植物对你的身体有坏处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 哪个植物好吃：哪种植物好吃，Use 种 when asking which variety of plant is being discussed; 个 does not match the intended “type of plant” meaning. [rule: none]
- 一个别的人：另一个人，别人 means “other people” as a group distinguished from the speaker or a stated person; for one additional individual, use 另一个人. [teacher rule: bieren]
- 哪个植物对你的身体有好处：哪种植物对你的身体有好处，Use 种 when asking which variety of plant is being discussed. [rule: none]
- 一个别的人：另一个人，别人 means “other people” as a group distinguished from the speaker or a stated person; for one additional individual, use 另一个人. [teacher rule: bieren]
- 哪个植物对你的身体有坏处：哪种植物对你的身体有坏处，Use 种 when asking which variety of plant is being discussed. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 坏处 -- “哪个植物对你的身体有坏处”：有害，states more directly that the plant can harm the body.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 这就要看你的口味了：这就要看你喜欢吃什么了，The pattern 这就要看……了 requires a question form after 看; 你喜欢吃什么 makes the answer depend on the listener’s preference. [rule: zhe_jiu_yao_kan]
- Expressiveness: In the answer about fruit, add a brief taste detail, such as “苹果又甜又脆，西瓜很清爽。”
- Expressiveness: Show that 神农 is thinking carefully before answering, such as “神农想了想，说：‘这就要看你喜欢吃什么了。’”
- Expressiveness: Add a short response from the questioner after learning about healthy plants, such as “那个人高兴地说：‘原来茶也对身体有好处！’”

### Encouragement
You used clear question-and-answer dialogue, and the examples of fruits and vegetables make 神农’s explanations easy to understand. The phrase 对你的身体有好处 is used accurately and naturally.

## Scene 3

> 神农说，有一天，我吃了一个黄色的花，名字是夹竹桃。我一吃夹竹桃，就觉得这种植物对我的脑有坏处。可是，一个灵芝和一个男人正好在我的旁边，我说，麻烦你把那个植物给我吃一下。我一吃这个灵芝就好了。所以，你得小心吃植物，因为有的植物对你的健康有坏处，特别是夹竹桃。因为夹竹桃有毒。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一个黄色的花：一朵黄色的花，Flowers use the matching measure word 朵 rather than the general measure word 个. [rule: none]
- 一个灵芝：一株灵芝，A medicinal fungus growing as a plant-like specimen is more naturally counted with 株 than the general measure word 个. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好了 -- “我一吃这个灵芝就好了”：痊愈了, specifies that the illness completely recovered；恢复正常了, makes clear that the speaker’s health returned to its normal state
- 旁边 -- “一个灵芝和一个男人正好在我的旁边”：身边, specifies that they were immediately at the speaker’s side；附近, indicates that they were near the speaker without necessarily being directly beside him

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 那个植物：灵芝，Repeat the plant’s name because both 夹竹桃 and 灵芝 have appeared, so 那个植物 could refer to either plant. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Conjunction): 特别是夹竹桃。因为夹竹桃有毒。：特别是有毒的夹竹桃。，The second sentence begins with 因为 but has no following result clause; combining the reason into the noun phrase makes the cause-and-effect connection complete and avoids repeating the same connector. [rule: none]
- Expressiveness: After the harmful plant is eaten, add a physical reaction to show the danger more vividly, such as “我肚子疼了起来，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: At the moment the man appears, describe his appearance so the reader can picture him, such as “这时，一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人走过来。”
- Expressiveness: Add an internal question when the speaker feels ill to show fear, such as “我会死吗？”

### Encouragement
You used the pattern 一……就…… effectively to show that the reaction happened immediately. The scene also has a clear change from a personal experience to a general warning about dangerous plants.
