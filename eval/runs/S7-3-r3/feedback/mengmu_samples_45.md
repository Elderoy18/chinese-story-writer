## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子。他们住在墓地的旁边，所以孟子老是祭拜。孟子不是祭拜，就是跟朋友聊天。他很喜欢他的城市，但是城市有才两个学校，都很小。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，The time noun and the time phrase duplicate the same meaning; this form is redundant. [teacher rule: gudai_de_shihou_why]
- 城市有才两个学校：城市只有两所学校，Use 只有 before the quantity to mean “only,” and use 所 as the measure word for schools. [teacher rule: zhiyou_caiyou]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 小 -- “都很小”：规模都很小，specifies that the schools are small in overall scale, such as their size or number of students.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子：一个叫孟子的男孩子和他的妈妈住在中国，Introduce the main character by name in the first mention instead of naming him only after a pronoun has already been used. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Reference): 他很喜欢他的城市：他很喜欢自己的城市，When the city belongs to or is associated with the sentence subject, 自己的 makes that relationship clear. [teacher rule: ziji_possessor]
- Expressiveness: At the cemetery sentence, add a reason for the boy’s interest, such as “孟子觉得祭拜很有趣，笑着跟朋友学。”
- Expressiveness: You could show the mother’s reaction after his behavior with a thought or quotation, such as “孟母很着急，心想：‘住在这儿真麻烦。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduce the setting and keep Mengzi as the focus of the scene.
- The contrast with “但是” helps show that he likes the city despite its small number of schools.

## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈不是不喜欢他们的城市，而是想孟子有很好的教育。他们搬家到有很多学校的城市。他们从早到晚走路，孟子一直抱怨。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 想孟子有很好的教育：想让孟子受到很好的教育，Use 想让 + person + verb phrase to express what someone hopes another person will experience or do; 受到教育 is the natural verb phrase here. [rule: none]
- 搬家到有很多学校的城市：搬到了有很多学校的城市，Use 搬到 + place for moving to a destination; the completed move takes 了. [rule: preposition_dao_place]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很好的 -- “想让孟子受到很好的教育”：优质的，specifies that the education is high in quality rather than simply “good.”

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他们从早到晚走路，孟子一直抱怨：因为他们从早到晚走路，所以孟子一直抱怨，Use 因为……所以…… to make the cause-and-effect relationship between the long walk and the complaint explicit. [rule: coherence_connectives]
- Expressiveness: After the walking sentence, add a physical detail to show why the complaint happens, such as “孟子累得满头大汗，脚也很疼。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech after the complaint to show Mengzi’s feelings, such as “孟子说：‘妈妈，我们什么时候才能到？’”
- Expressiveness: At the beginning, show the mother’s hope through an inner thought, such as “孟母心想：‘我希望孟子能受到更好的教育。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the mother’s reason for moving and Mengzi’s reaction to the journey. The contrast structure also helps the reader understand that the move is connected to education.

## Scene 3

> 他难怪抱怨，他想他的朋友，因为他不要离开他的城市。他的妈妈找到孟子一个老师。跟老师孟子学最重要的文学，所以他成为哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不要：不想，The negative form 不要 is used for prohibition or refusal; 不想 expresses that he does not want to leave. [rule: none]
- 找到孟子一个老师：给孟子找一个老师，Put the beneficiary after 给 before the verb; 找到 cannot take both a person and a thing as consecutive objects in this pattern. [rule: none]
- 跟老师孟子学：孟子跟老师学，The subject should come before the prepositional phrase 跟老师. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 想 -- “他想他的朋友”：想念，makes clear that he misses his friends rather than merely thinks about them.
- 最重要的文学 -- “孟子学最重要的文学”：经典文学，specifies that he studies recognised important literary works.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他的朋友：自己的朋友，Use 自己的 when the possessor is the same person as the subject, so the friends are clearly Mengzi’s own friends. [teacher rule: ziji_possessor]
- Coherence (Reference): 他的城市：自己的城市，Use 自己的 to show clearly that the city belongs to the subject of the clause. [teacher rule: ziji_possessor]
- Coherence (Temporality): 找一个老师：找了一个老师，Finding the teacher is a completed event, so the verb needs 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为哲学家：成为了哲学家，This is a completed change of state in the narrative, so use 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他难怪抱怨，他想：难怪他抱怨，原来他想，Use 难怪……，原来…… to introduce an understandable result and then explain its reason. [teacher rule: nanguai_yuanlai]
- Expressiveness: After the learning sentence, add a detail about Mengzi’s actions, such as “认真地听老师讲课”, to show how he learns.
- Expressiveness: Add Mengzi’s changing feeling after he meets the teacher, such as “觉得读书很有意思”, to make his development more vivid.
- Expressiveness: Add his mother’s reaction after she finds the teacher, such as “妈妈很高兴”, to show why this change matters to her.

### Encouragement
You clearly show Mengzi’s reason for complaining and connect his learning to his later becoming a philosopher. Your use of words such as 抱怨 and 哲学家 suits this story well.
