## Scene 1

> 很久以前，有一个老男人叫神农。神农在古代每一天看到了别的小镇人生病还是不舒服，所以他决定开始找新的药。他下一天开始爬当地的山脉，找了各种各样的植物。神农试一试每个植物，知道有什么好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别的小镇人：小镇里的人们，别的 is used to distinguish other people or places from one already mentioned; no such comparison has been established here. [teacher rule: bieren]
- 生病还是不舒服：生病或感到不舒服，还是 is used for alternatives in a question; a declarative list of possibilities uses 或 or 或者. [rule: none]
- 下一天：第二天，第二天 is the usual narrative time expression for the day after the previous event. [rule: time_expression]
- 爬当地的山脉：爬当地的山，爬 normally takes a specific mountain as its object; a mountain range is more naturally crossed rather than climbed. [rule: none]
- 找了各种各样的植物：找到了各种各样的植物，找 means “look for,” while 找到 means “find”; the sentence describes plants that he successfully found. [vocab list]
- 每个植物：每一种植物，when referring to each type of plant for testing, 种 is the appropriate measure word. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农试一试每个植物：他试一试每一种植物，use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when only one character has recently been mentioned. [rule: redundancy_delete]
- Coherence (Temporality): 每一天看到了：每天看到，an action that happens every day is a routine, so it takes the zero particle rather than completed-action 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: When describing the sick townspeople, add one visible symptom to make the problem more vivid, such as “有的人捂着肚子，脸色很难看。”
- Expressiveness: After the decision to search for medicine, add Shen Nong’s inner thought or a short quotation, such as “我一定要找出能治病的植物！”
- Expressiveness: When he tests plants, show how carefully he learns from them, such as “他把植物的样子和作用记下来。”

### Encouragement
- You introduced Shen Nong clearly at the beginning, so the reader immediately knows who the main character is.
- You also gave his search a clear reason and used “各种各样的植物” effectively to show that his task is broad and difficult.

## Scene 2

> 比方说，他一次试一试有些植物的叶，吃得又新鲜又甜。煮这些叶以后，他一喝就知道他发现新的茶叶。虽然他找到了很多的新的植物，但是他的旅程有的时候特别是危险。比方说，他一吃灵芝就开始生病。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一次试一试有些植物的叶：有一次，他尝了一些植物的叶，The reduplicated verb is for a light or tentative plan, not for an action that was actually carried out; use a completed verb here. [rule: verb_reduplication]
- 吃得又新鲜又甜：这些叶吃起来又新鲜又甜，The complement with 得 describes how an action is performed, while the leaves’ taste should be described with 吃起来. [rule: none]
- 特别是危险：特别危险，特别 directly modifies an adjective; 是 cannot appear between them. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 新的 -- “很多的新的植物”：未尝过的，specifies that these were plants he had not tasted before and fits the exploration context.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 知道他发现了新的茶叶：知道发现了新的茶叶，After the same person has already been named in this sentence, omit the repeated pronoun when the referent remains clear. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Temporality): 煮这些叶以后：煮了这些叶以后，This describes a completed action before the next event, so use 了 to mark completion. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 发现新的茶叶：发现了新的茶叶，Finding the tea leaves is a completed discovery, so the verb needs 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 一吃灵芝就开始生病：一吃灵芝就开始生病了，The beginning of an illness is a change of state in this narrated event, so use 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: At the point where he tastes the leaves, add a sensory detail about the tea water, such as “煮出来的水又香又清爽。”
- Expressiveness: After he discovers the tea, add his reaction or thought, such as “神农高兴地想：这可能是一种可以做药的植物！”

### Encouragement
- You used clear sequence language with 以后 and 一……就……, which helps the reader follow the actions. Your use of 虽然……但是…… also shows that you are trying more advanced sentence connections.

## Scene 3

> 一个爬山的男人正好看到神农的麻烦，然后给了他一些药。神农最终他的小镇回到，跟别的人分享他的发现。你认为神农的故事真的吗？这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他的小镇回到：回到他的小镇，The directional verb must come before its destination; the town cannot appear before the action of returning. [rule: none]
- 神农的故事真的吗：神农的故事是真的吗，A noun phrase needs 是 before the adjective-like predicate 真 in this yes-or-no question. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 给了 -- “然后给了他一些药”：递给了, emphasizes physically handing the medicine directly to him
- 麻烦 -- “神农的麻烦”：困境, describes a difficult situation more specifically than the general word 麻烦

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 回到：回到了，Returning is a completed action here, so 了 follows the completed verb-resultative phrase. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处。：这就要看你相不相信中药对身体有好处了，This fixed structure ends with 了; the yes-or-no form makes clear that the answer depends on whether the reader believes this claim. [teacher rule: zhe_jiu_yao_kan_le]
- Coherence (Conjunction): 跟别的人分享：跟别人分享，别人 refers to people other than the speaker or subject; 别的人 instead means different people and does not fit this meaning as well. [teacher rule: bieren]
- Expressiveness: When the man helps, add a visible action to make the moment more vivid, such as “他赶快从包里拿出一些药，递给神农。”
- Expressiveness: After receiving the medicine, add Shennong’s feeling or speech, such as “神农觉得好多了，说：‘谢谢你救了我！’”
- Expressiveness: When Shennong returns, show the townspeople’s response, such as “小镇的人们听了以后，都觉得神农很了不起。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly sequence the rescue with 然后, and your final question invites the reader to think about the story’s message.
