## Scene 1

> 古代，孟子跟孟母住在墓地的旁边。可是孟母注意，住在墓地的旁边影响孟子。他常常跟朋友们祭拜玩，所以孟母决定他们会搬家。这次，他们住在城市里的商店的旁边，从早到晚很吵。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 孟母注意，住在墓地的旁边影响孟子：孟母注意到住在墓地旁边会影响孟子，use “注意到” when someone notices or realizes a fact; “注意” alone means to pay attention to something.
- 祭拜玩：模仿别人祭拜，“祭拜” is an act of worship and cannot be directly followed by “玩” to mean playing; “模仿别人祭拜” clearly expresses that the children copy the worshippers.
- 孟母决定他们会搬家：孟母决定搬家，“决定” is followed directly by the action phrase, so the repeated reference and “会” are unnecessary.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很吵 -- “从早到晚很吵”：嘈杂，specifies that many mixed sounds make the place unpleasantly noisy；喧闹，describes a busy place full of noise and activity.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the children’s activity, add a visible detail to show why it worries the mother: “孟子一边笑，一边学着别人祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where the mother notices the problem, add her thought or speech: “孟母心想：‘这里不适合孟子长大。’”
- Expressiveness: Describe the new location with a concrete sound detail: “商店门口的人一直叫卖，马车也来来往往。”

### Encouragement
You clearly establish both locations in this scene, and “所以” effectively connects Mengzi’s behavior with his mother’s decision. The time phrase “从早到晚” also gives the final setting a stronger sense of daily atmosphere.

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections

### Vocabulary Suggestions

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子也没有朋友：他也没有朋友，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the referent was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other male character competes.
- Coherence (Reference): 适合孟子：适合他，Use a pronoun because the previous sentence has already established that the discussion is about Mengzi.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 住在商店的旁边不是适合孟子，而是让他觉得很伤心：住在商店的旁边不但不适合他，而且让他觉得很伤心，The two ideas strengthen each other rather than refuting one idea and replacing it with another, so use a paired additive connector instead of “不是……而是……”.
- Expressiveness: After the danger is mentioned, add one concrete detail showing why Mengmu worries. For example: “街上车很多，孟子不敢一个人出去。”
- Expressiveness: Show Mengzi’s loneliness through an action or thought after “没有朋友”. For example: “孟子看着来来往往的商人，心里很孤单。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of dialogue from Mengmu to show her concern. For example: “孟母说：‘这儿人太多了，我得陪着你。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly connect the noisy city environment with Mengzi’s feelings, and “难怪他老是看起来不高兴” gives the reader a clear emotional result.

## Scene 3

> 所以，孟母和孟子搬到学校的旁边住。才五岁孟子开始学哲学。在学校，孟子不是看书，就是练习写字。将来，他成为中国的一个非常有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 才五岁孟子开始学哲学：孟子才五岁就开始学哲学，The subject should come before the age phrase, and the pattern requires 才 + number + 就 + verb to show that he began unusually early.
- 将来：后来，This is a past narrative sequence, so 后来 marks a later event in the story; 将来 refers to the future.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 有名 -- “中国的一个非常有名的哲学家”：享有盛名，expresses that he is widely known and respected; 闻名，emphasizes that his name became well known.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为：成为了，His becoming a philosopher is presented as a completed later outcome, so the verb needs 了.
- Expressiveness: At “在学校，孟子不是看书，就是练习写字,” add a small detail showing his concentration, such as “孟子每天坐在教室里，认真地看书写字。”
- Expressiveness: After he begins learning, add his mother’s happy reaction through a short thought or quotation, such as “孟母高兴地说：‘这里很适合孟子学习。’”
- Expressiveness: At the final achievement, add an evaluative detail about his reputation, such as “后来，很多人都来向孟子请教问题。”

### Encouragement
You used the “不是……就是……” pattern effectively to show Mengzi’s regular school activities. The scene also has a clear transition from moving near the school to his later success.
