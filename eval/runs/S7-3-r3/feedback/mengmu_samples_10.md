## Scene 1

> 孟子小的时候，从早到晚都跟朋友玩。孟子和他的朋友喜欢玩在墓地里，装祭拜。孟子五岁的时候，跟妈妈搬家。他们搬家不是因为孟子玩得太多，而是因为妈妈认为他们的家离墓地太近。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 玩在墓地里：在墓地里玩，The location phrase must come before the action verb in this verb phrase.
- 装祭拜：装作祭拜，装作 means “pretend to do something”; it must be followed by the action being imitated.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和他的朋友：他和朋友，孟子 was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other possible main character has intervened, so a pronoun is more natural for a close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 跟妈妈搬家：跟妈妈搬了家，This is one completed move at age five, so the completed action needs 了.
- Expressiveness: At the part about pretending to worship, add one visible action to show what the children do. For example: “他们学着大人的样子，低着头，还不停地鞠躬。”
- Expressiveness: At the reason for moving, add the mother’s worried thought or speech. For example: “妈妈很担心，说：‘住在墓地旁边，孩子会学到不好的东西。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish the time period with “从早到晚” and “五岁的时候,” which helps the reader follow the events. Your use of “不是……而是……” also clearly explains the real reason for the move.

## Scene 2

> 妈妈认为，住离墓地太近，害得孟子的心气。孟子和孟母搬到子思老师的家，跟子思老师住。孟母帮子思打扫房间和做饭。子思帮孟母教她的儿子。因为孟子现在没有朋友，他老是读书。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 住离墓地太近：住在离墓地太近的地方，A location phrase needs 在 after the verb, and the phrase needs a place noun to complete the meaning.
- 害得孟子的心气：害得孟子心情不好，害得 must be followed by the affected person and a harmful result; the original ends with a noun phrase rather than stating the result.
- 搬到子思老师的家：搬到了子思老师的家，The move is a completed action, so 搬到 needs the completion marker 了.
- 老是读书：一直读书，老是 has a complaining or negative connotation, while this sentence simply describes ongoing study.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 做饭 -- “孟母帮子思打扫房间和做饭”：准备饭菜，specifies that she prepares meals for him in this household context；下厨，adds the concrete image of cooking in the kitchen.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为孟子现在没有朋友，他一直读书：因为孟子现在没有朋友，所以他一直读书，Because introduces a cause, and 所以 clearly introduces its result.
- Expressiveness: After the mother’s opinion, add her worry to show her feelings, for example: “孟母很担心孟子会学坏。”
- Expressiveness: After the final sentence, show Mengzi’s inner state instead of only reporting the action, for example: “孟子觉得很孤单，只好一个人安静地读书。”
- Expressiveness: You could add a short line of speech from the teacher to make the new setting feel more alive, for example: “子思说：‘孟子，我们一起读书吧。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly name the main characters and show how they help one another in the new home. The final sentence also gives a clear reason for Mengzi’s change in daily activities.
