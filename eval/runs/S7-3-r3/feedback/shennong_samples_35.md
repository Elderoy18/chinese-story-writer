## Scene 1

> 在古代，很多医生不知道怎么用药。所以他们找到了很多植物对药。神农先看到很多病人，他们吃很多植物对药。但是我觉得他们的吃的植物，让他们病人。神农觉得这个植物可能特别是毒。所以他想找一个植物对药，他们可以吃对药。正好，他找到了一把花，他想把花放在茶里，对药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很多植物对药：很多可以做药的植物，Plants cannot be described with “对药”; “可以做药的植物” means plants that can be used to make medicine.
- 他们吃很多植物对药：他们吃很多用植物做的药，“用植物做的药” is the correct noun phrase for medicine made from plants; “对药” does not express this meaning.
- 他们的吃的植物：他们吃的植物，A verb phrase modifying a noun places the subject before the verb: the plants that they ate are “他们吃的植物.”
- 让他们病人：让他们生病，After “让,” use a verb phrase showing the result; “病人” is a noun meaning a sick person, while “生病” is the needed verb.
- 可能特别是毒：可能有毒，“特别是” introduces an example or a specially selected item and cannot modify “毒”; use “有毒” to describe a poisonous plant.
- 一个植物对药，他们可以吃对药：一种可以做药、病人可以吃的植物，“种” is the suitable measure word for a type of plant, and “可以做药” correctly expresses that the plant can be used as medicine.
- 对药：做药，“对药” is not a verb phrase for using something as medicine; “做药” expresses that purpose.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农先看到很多病人：一个叫神农的人先看到很多病人，On a character’s first mention, introduce the name together with a full noun phrase so the reader knows who the character is.
- Expressiveness: At the point where the sick people appear, add one physical symptom to show their suffering, such as “有的病人捂着肚子，疼得直冒汗。”
- Expressiveness: When the flowers appear, describe their colour and shape so that the discovery feels more vivid, such as “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花。”
- Expressiveness: After finding the flowers, add Shen Nong’s thought or question, such as “这种花能不能做药呢？”

### Encouragement
You clearly establish the ancient setting and introduce a problem involving medicine and sick people. You also give Shen Nong a clear goal: finding plants that people can safely use as medicine.

## Scene 2

> 他不知道如果这把花对你的身体好，这就要看如果这把花毒不毒了。神农想找别的植物，但是这很麻烦。正好，他找到了别的花。他一吃花，很快就生病了。他的肚子疼死了。神农不知道他吃不吃灵芝或者夹竹桃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不知道如果这把花对你的身体好：不知道这把花对身体好不好，An embedded question after “不知道” needs an A-not-A form such as “好不好”; “如果” cannot introduce “whether” in this structure.
- 看如果这把花毒不毒：看这把花毒不毒，The part after “看” is an embedded question, so “如果” should be removed; “毒不毒” already provides the A-not-A question form.
- 吃不吃灵芝或者夹竹桃：吃的是灵芝还是夹竹桃，This is a choice between two plants, so use “是……还是……” rather than the yes/no form “吃不吃” plus “或者”.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 找 -- “神农想找别的植物”：寻找，emphasizes deliberately looking for plants; 探寻, emphasizes exploring in order to discover something unknown.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他：∅，The subject is already “神农” in the same sentence, so omit the repeated pronoun when the same person performs the embedded action.
- Expressiveness: After “正好，他找到了别的花”, add a visual detail about the flowers: “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花。”
- Expressiveness: After “他的肚子疼死了”, show the physical result: “他在地上躺着，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: At the moment he becomes ill, add his worried thought: “他想：‘我会死吗？’”

### Encouragement
You clearly show a cause-and-effect sequence: Shennong eats the flower and then becomes ill. The completed-action marking in “找到了” and the change-of-state marking in “生病了” also fit the events well.

## Scene 3

> 别的人给了神农一个灵芝。这个植物可以让神农更好。比方说，他会觉得更健康。吃灵芝以后，他可以再走。现在他觉得更好。最后，神农找到了很多植物，他可以用对药。这些植物让神农很高兴。神农想跟很多人分享他的新药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别的人：别人，Use this word for “other people” when no specific contrast with another group has been established.
- 很多植物，他可以用对药：很多可以用来做药的植物，The phrase “对药” is not a valid verb phrase here. Use “用来 + verb” to show the purpose of the plants, and use “做药” to mean “make medicine.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 更好 -- “这个植物可以让神农更好”：更健康，states specifically that the improvement is in Shennong’s health；好转，emphasizes that his condition is improving.
- 再走 -- “吃灵芝以后，他可以再走”：继续赶路，makes clear that he can resume his journey；继续寻找植物，states more precisely what he will continue doing.
- 高兴 -- “这些植物让神农很高兴”：感到欣慰，shows satisfaction and relief after finding useful plants；非常兴奋，shows stronger excitement about the discovery.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 这些植物让神农很高兴：这些植物让他很高兴，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when Shennong was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other person competes as the referent.
- Expressiveness: When Lingzhi is first mentioned, add one visual detail to make the plant memorable, such as “灵芝像一把小伞，长在花的旁边。”
- Expressiveness: After Shennong recovers, add his immediate feeling or a short thought, such as “神农摸着肚子想：‘我好多了！’”
- Expressiveness: At the end, add a line of speech to show why he wants to share the medicine, such as “神农高兴地说：‘我要告诉大家哪些植物可以做药！’”

### Encouragement
You use a clear sequence of recovery, continued travel, and discovery. The completed-action forms in “给了” and “找到了” also help the events move forward clearly.
