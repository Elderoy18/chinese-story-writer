## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，孟子和孟母住了在靠墓地的家。孩子才五岁就每天在墓地祭拜了。在那个城市，孟母从早到晚都找工作，可是找不到，害得孟母没有钱。难怪她跟孩子搬家，原来买不了房。他们搬了到别一个城市找工作。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，Both parts express time, so using them together is redundant. [vocab list]
- 别一个：另一个，This refers to one different city; 别的 distinguishes other things or people from a previously mentioned one, while 另一个 is needed for one additional/different city. [teacher rule: bieren]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 找不到 -- “可是找不到”：一直没找到，emphasizes that the search continued but did not succeed during that time.
- 买不了 -- “原来买不了房”：买不起，more precisely shows that the problem is insufficient money.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟母：有一个小男孩叫孟子，他和妈妈，This is the first mention of both characters, so introduce the boy with his name and identity, and identify the other character as his mother. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Reference): 孩子：他，After the recent mention of Mengzi, use a pronoun for this close re-occurrence because no other male character competes as the referent. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Reference): 在那个城市：在他们住的城市，The city has not been introduced before, so the demonstrative reference is unclear; use a full noun phrase to identify it. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Reference): 害得孟母没有钱：害得她没有钱，The mother was named earlier in the same sentence, so a pronoun is natural for this close re-occurrence. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Temporality): 住了在靠墓地的家：住在靠墓地的家里，This describes a living situation rather than a completed action, so it takes no 了; the location follows 住在. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 每天在墓地祭拜了：每天在墓地祭拜，A routine marked by 每天 uses the zero particle, not completion marker 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬了到：搬到了，Put 了 after the directional/resultative element 到. [teacher rule: le_placement]
- Expressiveness: At the cemetery scene, add what Mengzi sees and how he reacts: “孟子看到很多人来祭拜，觉得很好玩。”
- Expressiveness: Show the mother’s worry after the failed job search with an inner thought: “孟母心想：‘这样下去，我们怎么生活呢？’”
- Expressiveness: Add one sensory detail about the new city to make the move vivid: “新城市的街上人来人往，非常热闹。”

### Encouragement
- You used the early-age pattern correctly to show that the child began the action very young, and the scene has a clear movement from the cemetery home to a new city.
- The explanatory paired pattern near the end effectively gives the mother’s reason for moving.

## Scene 2

> 这个城市老是很吵，不是人吵架，就是都叫，害得孟子一直不能睡觉。在那儿，孟母也找不到工作。孟子和妈妈要又一个次搬了家。在第三个城市，孟母找到工作，在商店做衣服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 要又一个次搬了家：要再搬一次家，With a future action after a modal verb, use the pattern modal verb + 再 + verb + number + measure word + object. “再” is for a repeat that has not happened yet, and future actions after “要” do not take 了. [teacher rule: zai_structure]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 吵 -- “这个城市老是很吵”：嘈杂，describes a place filled with many mixed, disturbing sounds rather than simply being loud.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 人：人们，This refers to the concrete people making noise in the city, so use 人们 rather than the generic noun 人. [teacher rule: ren_renmen]
- Coherence (Temporality): 孟母找到工作：孟母找到了工作，Finding the job is a completed event in the narrative, so use 了 after the resultative complement 到. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: After “这个城市老是很吵,” add one or two specific sounds to show why it is noisy, for example: “街上有人大声吵架，还有人一直叫卖。”
- Expressiveness: At “害得孟子一直不能睡觉,” add Mengzi’s feeling or a small action, for example: “孟子躺在床上，捂着耳朵，觉得很难受。”
- Expressiveness: After Mengmu cannot find work, add a brief thought or line of speech to show her worry, for example: “孟母心想：‘这里太吵了，我们得再找一个安静的地方。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly connect the noisy environment to Mengzi’s inability to sleep by using 害得, which shows a strong cause-and-effect relationship. You also move the story forward effectively by showing Mengmu’s job search and the family’s next move.

## Scene 3

> 孟子在那个商店也找了到工作，可是不是做衣服的工作，而是喜欢的工作。孟子学习馆学了十五年，他的学习和他的旅行经验很重要，对他很多好处。他是大人的时候，他成为很有名的哲人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 孟子在那个商店也找了到工作：孟子在那个商店也找到了工作，The perfective marker must come after the resultative complement: the resultative verb is 找到, so 了 follows 到. [teacher rule: le_placement]
- 孟子学习馆学了十五年：孟子在学校学习了十五年，学校 is the ordinary noun for the place where someone studies, and a place before an action needs 在. [rule: none]
- 对他很多好处：对他有很多好处，The phrase 对他 introduces the person affected; 有 is needed as the verb stating that the experience brings benefits. [rule: none]
- 他是大人的时候：他长大以后，Use 长大以后 to express the later time after someone becomes an adult. [rule: time_expression]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很有名 -- “他成为很有名的哲人”：广为人知，specifies that many people know this philosopher；享有盛名，expresses a well-established positive reputation.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 他成为很有名的哲人：他成为了很有名的哲人，This is a completed achievement in the narrative, so becoming takes 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: At the point about fifteen years of study and travel, add one concrete detail about what he did or learned, for example: “十五年里，他一边学习，一边到各地旅行，认识了很多人。”
- Expressiveness: After his achievement, add his feeling toward his mother to make the ending warmer, for example: “孟子终于成为了很有名的哲人，心里十分感激妈妈。”
- Expressiveness: You could add a short line of speech from his mother to show her pride, for example: “孟母高兴地说：‘这次搬家太对了！’”

### Encouragement
You used the contrast pattern 不是……而是…… effectively to distinguish the kind of job Mengzi found. The final sentence also gives the scene a clear sense of achievement by showing that he became a well-known philosopher.
