## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个男孩名字叫孟子。孟子和孟子的妈妈两个人住在很穷的农村。他们住的地方旁边是个墓地，墓地老是有很多人祭拜。因为人们在祭拜，他们一直在哭。孟子和孟子的妈妈住在这里才一个星期就觉得受不了了，而且害得孟子的学习。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 already expresses a time period, so adding 的时候 is redundant. [vocab list]
- 老是：常常，老是 has a complaining or negative connotation; this sentence neutrally describes a frequent event at the cemetery. [rule: laoshi_negative_connotation]
- 而且害得孟子的学习：这里的环境还影响了孟子的学习，害得 must be followed by a person and a harmful result clause; it cannot directly take 孟子的学习 as its object. [teacher rule: haide_structure]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 住的地方 -- “他们住的地方旁边是个墓地”：家，more specifically identifies the place as their home and makes the sentence more concise.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子的妈妈两个人：孟子和他的妈妈两个人，after 孟子 has been named, use the pronoun 他 before 妈妈 to avoid unnecessarily repeating the name in the same noun phrase. [rule: none]
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子的妈妈：孟子和他的妈妈，use a pronoun for the close re-occurrence of the same character rather than repeating the name. [rule: none]
- Coherence (Temporality): 住在这里才一个星期：在这里才住了一个星期，住 describes the completed duration before they could no longer tolerate the place, so it needs 了; 觉得 is a mental-state verb and correctly takes no aspect particle. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: When describing the people at the cemetery, add a visual or sound detail to make the setting more vivid, for example: “来祭拜的人低着头，伤心地哭着。”
- Expressiveness: At the final sentence, show the mother’s reaction or thought, for example: “孟子的妈妈皱着眉头，心想：‘这里太吵了。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly establish the setting and introduce both Mengzi and his mother early in the scene. Your use of ongoing actions in “在祭拜” and “一直在哭” also helps the reader picture what is happening at the cemetery.

## Scene 2

> 孟子的妈妈觉得孟子取得良好的教育是很重要的，所以孟子的妈妈认为这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好，就打算跟孟子搬家。孟子的妈妈带孟子不是搬到城市，而是搬到了孟子老师的家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 取得良好的教育：接受良好的教育，取得 usually means achieving or obtaining a concrete result, while education is normally something a person receives or accepts. [rule: none]
- 跟孟子搬家：带着孟子搬家，跟孟子搬家 suggests that the mother and Mengzi moved together as equal participants; 带着孟子搬家 makes clear that she took her child with her. [vocab list]
- 不是搬到城市，而是搬到了孟子老师的家：搬到了孟子的老师的家，而没有搬到城市，the “不是……而是……” pattern is for correcting an established or conventional mistaken idea; here a direct contrast between the two destinations is more natural. [rule: bushi_ershi_construction]
- 孟子老师：孟子的老师，without 的, this can be read as a title meaning “Teacher Mengzi”; 的 marks that the teacher belongs to Mengzi. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不太好 -- “对儿子的教育不太好”：不利于，specifies that the place negatively affects the child’s education；会影响，makes the harmful effect on education more concrete.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子的妈妈认为：认为，when the same subject continues within one sentence, use zero form rather than repeating the full noun phrase. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子的妈妈带孟子：她带孟子，after the mother was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no competing female character appears, use a pronoun for close re-occurrence. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Conjunction): 觉得孟子取得良好的教育是很重要的，所以：因为觉得孟子接受良好的教育很重要，所以，these ideas have a cause-and-effect relationship, so use the paired connector to introduce the reason clearly. [rule: coherence_connectives]
- Expressiveness: At the mother’s decision, add a brief facial expression or feeling, such as “孟母皱着眉头，心里很着急。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought or line of speech to show why she wants to move, such as “她心想：‘这里会影响孟子的学习。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly explain the mother’s reason for moving, and you use education-related vocabulary such as “良好的教育” and “儿子的教育” effectively.

## Scene 3

> 孟子跟他的老师从早到晚学习，难怪他的成绩很快就进步了很多。孟子学习的时候，孟子的妈妈不是待在家里就是编织衣服赚钱。以后孟子成为了中国有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 以后：后来，Sentence-initial 以后 does not mean “afterwards” in a narrative; 后来 introduces a later event. [vocab list]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 成绩 -- “他的成绩很快就进步了很多”：学习成绩，specifies that the improvement is in school performance rather than achievements in general
- 进步 -- “他的成绩很快就进步了很多”：提高，more specifically describes grades becoming better

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the first sentence, add the teacher’s reaction to make Mengzi’s progress feel more vivid. Example: 老师高兴地说：“孟子，你进步得真快！”
- Expressiveness: At the sentence about Mengzi’s mother, add her feeling while she works. Example: 妈妈虽然很辛苦，但是看到孟子认真学习，她很开心。
- Expressiveness: Before the final sentence, add a short thought that shows the mother’s hope for Mengzi. Example: 妈妈心想：“孟子以后一定会有出息。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show Mengzi’s hard work with the specific time phrase 从早到晚. You also use the paired structure 不是……就是…… effectively to describe his mother’s routine.
