## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男人觉得植物很有意思。他叫神农。有的植物吃了以后他的肚子疼死了，可是有的吃了以后很舒服。他找很多植物。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 already names a time period, so adding 的时候 is redundant.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 有意思 -- “植物很有意思”：很奇妙，highlights that the plants have unusual and intriguing qualities；很吸引人，emphasizes that they strongly draw Shennong’s attention.
- 舒服 -- “吃了以后很舒服”：身体很舒服，makes clear that this is a physical feeling；浑身舒畅，describes a comfortable feeling throughout the body.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the stomach pain, add a short description of Shennong’s physical reaction, such as “他马上捂着肚子，疼得满头大汗。”
- Expressiveness: Show his curiosity through an internal thought after he notices different effects from the plants, such as “神农想：为什么有的植物会让我肚子疼呢？”
- Expressiveness: Add a concrete detail about the plants he searches for, such as “他在山上寻找各种各样的花和草。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show a contrast between plants that cause pain and plants that make Shennong feel well. The repeated structure in “有的……可是有的……” makes the comparison easy to understand.

## Scene 2

> 凉山很大的山，这也找植物。一天，他找到黄色的花。他听说你跟黄色的花做茶很有用。比方说，你的头发疼死，这冰茶是药。他一喝茶就很不舒服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 凉山很大的山：凉山是一座很大的山，A named mountain needs a predicate, and 座 is the appropriate measure word for a mountain.
- 这也：在这里，Also, 也 cannot function as a location word; 在这里 gives the place where the action happens.
- 你跟黄色的花做茶：用黄色的花做的茶，Use 用 to introduce the material used to make something, and use 做的 to modify 茶.
- 你的头发疼死：你的头疼得要命，头发 refers to hair, while 头疼 means having a headache; 得要命 expresses a very strong degree of pain.
- 这冰茶：这杯茶，A demonstrative such as 这 needs a measure word before a countable noun; 杯 is suitable for tea.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 黄色 -- “一天，他找到黄色的花”：金黄色，specifies a warmer, brighter shade and makes the flower easier to imagine.
- 有用 -- “用黄色的花做的茶很有用”：对身体有好处，specifies that the tea benefits health; 有疗效，specifies that it has a medicinal effect.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 找到：找到了，The finding is a completed event in this past narrative, so add 了 after the resultative complement 到.
- Expressiveness: At the mountain description, add one visual detail, such as “山上开着很多金黄色的花。”
- Expressiveness: After drinking the tea, show his physical reaction, such as “他捂着肚子，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought to show his worry, such as “他想：‘我是不是生病了？’”

### Encouragement
- You used a clear event sequence, moving from finding flowers to hearing about tea and then drinking it.
- The sentence with 一……就…… effectively shows that the discomfort happened immediately after he drank the tea.

## Scene 3

> 糟糕，这黄色的花是夹竹桃，特别是毒。正好在这儿，灵芝药的夹竹桃他吃完了，也写一个课文的植物。谢谢神农。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 这黄色的花：这朵黄色的花，A demonstrative such as 这 needs a suitable measure word before a concrete countable noun such as 花.
- 特别是毒：特别有毒，毒 is a noun here; use 有毒 to describe a plant’s poisonous property.
- 正好在这儿，灵芝药的夹竹桃他吃完了：正好在这儿有灵芝这种药，神农吃完了灵芝，药的 cannot connect 灵芝 and 夹竹桃 into one noun phrase. Use 有 to introduce the plant at this place, and use 灵芝这种药 to identify it clearly.
- 写一个课文的植物：记下了这两种植物，Use 记下 to mean recording plants or discoveries; 写 cannot directly take 植物 in this meaning, and 一个课文的植物 does not form a meaningful noun phrase.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the discovery of the flower, add a brief reaction to make the danger feel immediate: “神农吓了一跳，马上明白这朵花不能吃。”
- Expressiveness: When introducing the helpful plant, describe its appearance so the reader can picture it: “灵芝像一把小伞，长在夹竹桃旁边。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech after the discovery to show what Shennong learns: “神农说：‘夹竹桃有毒，灵芝可以做药。’”

### Encouragement
You used important story vocabulary such as 夹竹桃 and 灵芝, and you tried to connect Shennong’s eating and recording actions in one scene. The opening “糟糕” also gives the moment an appropriately worried feeling.
