## Scene 1

> 古代的时候人觉得祭拜非常重要，孟子很小的时候，从早到晚一直祭拜，因为他妈妈告诉他祭拜很重要。虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜。他的同学们老是对他开玩笑了。孟子妈妈看了他的同学们对孟子不好的影响，所以孟子和孟子妈妈搬家了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，this expression redundantly combines two time expressions; use the shorter time phrase.
- 人：人们，this refers to a concrete group of people in that period, so use the plural noun.
- 孟子妈妈：孟子的妈妈，when a proper name modifies a kinship noun, add 的 to form the attributive phrase.
- 看了：看到了，看 means “look,” while 看到 means “see”; the completed observation also takes 了.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 非常重要 -- “人们觉得祭拜非常重要”：意义重大，emphasizes that the practice has substantial significance in people’s lives.
- 不好 -- “同学们对孟子不好的影响”：不良，forms the precise and common phrase for a negative influence.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子妈妈搬家了：孟子和他的妈妈搬家了，his mother was mentioned in the immediately preceding sentence, so a pronoun is more natural for this close re-occurrence; 的 also marks the possessive relationship.
- Coherence (Temporality): 老是对他开玩笑了：老是对他开玩笑，老是 describes a repeated routine, so use the zero particle rather than completed-action 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜：因为他每天只祭拜，所以没有朋友，this is a cause-and-effect relation, which needs the paired connector because...therefore..., rather than an unfinished although clause.
- Expressiveness: After the description of his worship, add his inner feeling, for example: “孟子觉得祭拜很有趣。”
- Expressiveness: At the classmates’ reaction, show their teasing through dialogue, for example: “同学们笑着说：‘你每天只会祭拜吗？’”
- Expressiveness: When his mother notices the influence, add her worry and thought, for example: “妈妈很着急，心想：‘这样会影响孟子。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly show a cause for Mengzi’s behavior by explaining what his mother told him. You also connect the mother’s observation to the family’s move, giving the scene a clear sequence of events.

## Scene 2

> 孟子才十一岁，成为一个哲学家。孟子不是祭拜，而是看政治文章，难怪他成为了哲学家。不过，看过很多政治文章对他害得，他有很多问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 孟子才十一岁，成为一个哲学家：孟子才十一岁，就成为了一个哲学家，The pattern with 才 normally uses 就 to show that the change happened unexpectedly early, and 了 marks the completed change of becoming a philosopher.
- 看过很多政治文章对他害得，他有很多问题：看过很多政治文章害得他有很多问题，害得 must be followed by the person affected and then the harmful result; 对他 cannot fill that position.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 看 -- “看过很多政治文章”：读过，specifies reading written articles rather than generally looking at something.
- 问题 -- “他有很多问题”：疑问，makes clear that these are questions or uncertainties in his mind.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子不是祭拜：他不是祭拜，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence because the previous sentence has already named 孟子 and no other male character has appeared.
- Expressiveness: At the final sentence, show one specific question that reading creates for him: 他一边看一边想：“为什么有的人很有钱，有的人却很穷？”
- Expressiveness: After he becomes a philosopher, add his feeling to make the achievement more personal: 孟子觉得很高兴，因为他终于找到了自己喜欢研究的事情。

### Encouragement
You clearly show a contrast between worshipping and reading, and you also try to explain how reading leads Mengzi to think more deeply. The use of 不过 effectively introduces a downside after his success.

## Scene 3

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不是学金融，而是学政治：不要学金融，而要学政治，After 建议, use a directive form to recommend avoiding one action and choosing another; 不是……而是…… is for correcting or contrasting statements, not for giving this kind of advice.
