## Scene 1

> 一天，很久很久以前，孟子和他妈妈住在中国。孟子妈妈的工作是祭拜，孟子不高兴，因为他说我们老是搬家，为什么？他妈妈说，从早到晚，我一直祭拜，很多墓地得我祭拜。所以我们得去很多不同的地方。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一天，很久很久以前：很久很久以前的一天，The time phrase needs to modify “一天” with “的”; this order clearly means one day in the distant past.
- 他妈妈说，从早到晚，我一直祭拜，很多墓地得我祭拜。：他妈妈说：“从早到晚，我得去很多墓地祭拜。”，Direct speech needs a colon and quotation marks. Also, the person who has an obligation must be the subject of the modal verb “得”: “我得 + verb phrase,” not “很多墓地得我祭拜.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不高兴 -- “孟子不高兴”：不满, specifies that he is dissatisfied with the repeated moves.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和他妈妈：有一个小男孩叫孟子，他和妈妈，This is the first mention of the main character, so introduce him clearly with his name and identity before using a pronoun for his mother.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子妈妈的工作：她的工作，For a close re-occurrence, use a pronoun when the referent is clear; “她” clearly refers to Mengzi’s mother here.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为他说我们老是搬家，为什么？：说：“我们老是搬家，为什么？”，“因为” should introduce a cause, but this question expresses Mengzi’s complaint rather than a cause. Use direct speech to show what he says.
- Expressiveness: After Mengzi’s question, add a small physical detail to show his feeling, such as “孟子皱着眉头，觉得很不明白。”
- Expressiveness: Make the mother’s explanation more vivid by showing her tiredness, such as “妈妈叹了一口气，说：‘从早到晚，我得去很多墓地祭拜。’”

### Encouragement
- You set the scene clearly with a distant-past time setting and introduced the family’s repeated-moving problem right away.
- The dialogue helps the reader understand why Mengzi is unhappy and why his mother needs to travel.

## Scene 2

> 孟子想，难怪我们常常搬家，原来我妈妈很忙。可是常常搬家害得孟子很难找到朋友。每次搬家孟子也觉得新的地方不是太热就是太冷，不是太大就是太小。在哪的地方，他都不高兴。孟子想帮助妈妈祭拜，可是他六岁，所以不能祭拜。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- Nothing to flag.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很忙 -- “原来我妈妈很忙”：工作繁忙，specifies that her busyness comes from her work and fits this narration naturally.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 每次搬家孟子也觉得：每次搬家，他也觉得，use a pronoun when the same character was named in the immediately previous sentence and no competing male character has appeared.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子想帮助妈妈祭拜：他想帮助妈妈祭拜，use a pronoun for the close re-occurrence of the same character.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 在哪的地方，他都不高兴：不管在哪个地方，他都不高兴，use the paired structure to connect an unrestricted place with the result marked by 都.
- Expressiveness: After the difficulty finding friends, add Mengzi’s lonely feeling or a short thought, such as “我什么时候才能有一个一直在一起的朋友呢？”
- Expressiveness: At the description of the new places, add one concrete detail that shows why he dislikes them, such as “有的地方风很大，晚上冷得睡不着。”
- Expressiveness: Before the final sentence, add a brief line of dialogue to show his wish to help, such as “妈妈，我也想跟你一起祭拜。”

### Encouragement
You used the pattern “难怪……，原来……” very naturally to show Mengzi’s realization. The paired descriptions “不是太热就是太冷” and “不是太大就是太小” clearly show why he feels unhappy in each new place.

## Scene 3

> 四年后，孟子开始帮妈妈祭拜。他画画很厉害，他越来越喜欢祭拜，所以他祭拜不是想帮妈妈工作，而是他很喜欢画画。孟子成为很著名的古代画家。今天很多中国人很喜欢孟子。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不是想帮妈妈工作，而是他很喜欢画画：不是为了帮妈妈工作，而是因为喜欢画画，The two halves of this contrast should give parallel reasons for the action; use “不是为了……，而是因为……” here, and omit the repeated subject in the second half.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 厉害 -- “他画画很厉害”：出色，emphasizes that the quality of his drawings is especially good；擅长画画，states more precisely that drawing is a skill he does well

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为很著名的古代画家：成为了很著名的古代画家，This is a completed change of state in the narrative, so use 了 after the verb.
- Expressiveness: After “孟子开始帮妈妈祭拜,” add a small action showing what he does during the ceremony, such as “他一边祭拜，一边学着别人画画。”
- Expressiveness: After “他越来越喜欢祭拜,” add his feeling to make the reason more vivid, such as “他觉得祭拜很有趣，常常开心地笑起来。”
- Expressiveness: You could add a short line of speech from his mother after he starts helping, such as “妈妈说：‘祭拜的时候要认真一点。’”

### Encouragement
- You use the time phrase “四年后” clearly to move the story forward, and “越来越喜欢” effectively shows a gradual change in Mengzi’s feelings.
