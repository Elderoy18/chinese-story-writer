## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个男孩名字叫孟子。孟子和孟子的妈妈两个人住在很穷的农村。他们住的地方旁边是个墓地，墓地老是有很多人祭拜。因为人们在祭拜，他们一直在哭。孟子和孟子的妈妈住在这里才一个星期就觉得受不了了，而且害得孟子的学习。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，This expression redundantly combines a time period with a phrase meaning “at the time of”; use the shorter time expression.
- 害得孟子的学习：这里的环境还会影响他的学习，The pattern with 害得 must be followed by a person and a harmful result clause; 影响 can directly take 学习 as its object.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 穷 -- “很穷的农村”：贫困，specifies that the rural area has difficult economic conditions

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子的妈妈两个人：孟子和他的妈妈两个人，Under the close re-occurrence pronoun rule, use a pronoun for the already mentioned character rather than repeating the name inside the same noun phrase.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子的妈妈：孟子和他的妈妈，Under the close re-occurrence pronoun rule, the possessive should use a pronoun because the reader already knows whose mother is meant.
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery is introduced, add one sensory detail about its atmosphere, such as “墓地里很安静，只有人们的哭声。”
- Expressiveness: Show how the setting affects the boy’s feelings, such as “孟子听见哭声，心里有点害怕。”
- Expressiveness: At the end, add a short thought or line of speech from the mother, such as “孟子的妈妈想：‘这里会影响他的学习。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly establish the setting and introduce the main character and his mother early in the scene. Your use of details such as the cemetery and people crying also gives the place a serious mood.

## Scene 2

> 孟子的妈妈觉得孟子取得良好的教育是很重要的，所以孟子的妈妈认为这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好，就打算跟孟子搬家。孟子的妈妈带孟子不是搬到城市，而是搬到了孟子老师的家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 取得良好的教育：接受良好的教育，取得 is used for achievements or results, while education is something a child receives or accepts.
- 跟孟子搬家：带着孟子搬家，this phrase should show that the mother takes her child with her when moving.
- 不是搬到城市，而是搬到了：搬到了，this situation does not correct a previously stated or conventional assumption, so the contrast construction is unnecessary.
- 孟子老师的家：孟子的老师的家，use 的 after 孟子 to show that the teacher belongs to or teaches 孟子.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 地方 -- “这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好”：环境，specifies the surrounding living and learning conditions that affect the child’s education.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 所以孟子的妈妈认为：所以她认为，after the mother has just been named and no other female character competes with her, use a pronoun for close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子的妈妈带孟子：她带孟子，her name appeared in the immediately preceding sentence, so a pronoun makes the connection smoother.
- Expressiveness: At the mother’s decision to move, add an emotional detail to show her concern, such as “孟母皱着眉头，心里很着急”.
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of inner speech before the move to make her reason more vivid, such as “这里会影响孟子的学习，我得搬家”.

### Encouragement
You clearly show the mother’s reason for moving and use a cause-and-effect relationship to connect her concern about education with her decision. The scene also keeps the focus on the mother and Mengzi’s education.

## Scene 3

> 孟子跟他的老师从早到晚学习，难怪他的成绩很快就进步了很多。孟子学习的时候，孟子的妈妈不是待在家里就是编织衣服赚钱。以后孟子成为了中国有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 以后孟子成为了中国有名的哲学家：后来孟子成为了中国有名的哲学家，At the beginning of a sentence, “以后” does not mean “afterwards” in a past narrative; use “后来” to introduce a later event.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 进步 -- “他的成绩很快就进步了很多”：提高了很多，specifies more directly that his marks rose by a large amount
- 学习 -- “孟子跟他的老师从早到晚学习”：刻苦学习，emphasizes the sustained effort suggested by studying from morning to evening

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子学习的时候，孟子的妈妈不是待在家里就是编织衣服赚钱：孟子学习的时候，他妈妈不是待在家里就是编织衣服赚钱，The second mention is close to “孟子” and no other male character competes as the possessor, so use a pronoun rather than repeat the name.
- Expressiveness: After “从早到晚学习,” add one concrete study activity to show what he does, such as “他认真地读书、写字，还常常问老师问题。”
- Expressiveness: At the mother’s work sentence, add a small detail that shows her effort, such as “她坐在桌子旁，手里不停地织着衣服。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of encouragement from the mother after his progress, such as “孟母高兴地说：‘你学得越来越好了！’”

### Encouragement
You clearly show that Mengzi studies diligently and that his achievement improves as a result. The final sentence also gives the scene a clear sense of time moving forward.
