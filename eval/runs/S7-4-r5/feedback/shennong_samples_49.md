## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有很多人不知道什么植物能吃，什么植物不能吃。有很多人吃植物以后就吃坏肚子了，因为他们吃了毒。那个时候，有一个老人叫神农，他什么植物都觉得好吃，不怕生病，不怕毒。他希望别人也可以吃好吃的植物。可是他知道，如果他们还怕吃毒，不会吃他喜欢的植物。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，the time word and the phrase meaning “at the time of” are redundant together.
- 吃了毒：吃了有毒的植物，毒 names poison itself; here the sentence needs to identify the poisonous plants that people ate.
- 怕吃毒：怕吃到有毒的植物，吃 describes the action, while 吃到 shows that someone ends up consuming the plant; 有毒的植物 clearly names the danger.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 如果他们还怕吃毒，不会吃他喜欢的植物：如果他们还怕吃到有毒的植物，就不会吃他喜欢的植物，A conditional relation is clearer with the paired connector 如果……就……, showing the result of their fear.
- Expressiveness: After the people become ill, add a concrete physical detail such as “他们肚子疼得直冒汗。” This helps the reader feel how serious the illness is.
- Expressiveness: When introducing Shennong, add his determination with an inner thought, such as “神农想：我一定要找出能吃的植物。” This makes his reason for acting more vivid.
- Expressiveness: At the final contrast, add a short line of speech from Shennong, such as “别害怕，我会先试一试。” This can show his bravery directly.

### Encouragement
You clearly introduce Shennong after describing the problem people face, so the scene has an easy-to-follow structure. The use of “可是” also successfully begins to show the difference between Shennong’s hope and the other people’s fear.

## Scene 2

> 所以，他上山下山找植物，吃了很多样子的植物。他是第一人，找到了茶。他把所有的植物写下来了。他一直都吃了，他连毒都能吃。可是他还没吃了一个叫夹竹桃的植物。他找到了那个像星星的植物，觉得很漂亮，马上就吃下了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很多样子的植物：很多种植物，Use 种 to count different varieties of plants; 样子 refers to an object’s appearance and does not naturally classify plants here.
- 他是第一人，找到了茶：他是第一个找到茶的人，An ordinal description of a person needs the classifier and a modifying clause to state what he was first to do.
- 一个叫夹竹桃的植物：一种叫夹竹桃的植物，Use 种 as the measure word for a type of plant.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 找 -- “他上山下山找植物”：寻找，emphasizes an ongoing search through the mountains; 探寻，highlights exploration to discover unknown plants.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 他一直都吃了：他一直都吃，The action is ongoing or habitual because of 一直, so it takes the zero particle rather than completed-action 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 还没吃了：还没吃，A negated completed action with 没 does not take 了.
- Expressiveness: When he sees the plant, add visual detail to show why it attracts him: “那儿开着黄色的像星星一样的花，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: Add Shennong’s thought before he eats it to show his confidence: “他想：这么漂亮的植物，一定很好吃！”

### Encouragement
You clearly show Shennong’s active exploration through the mountains and his discovery of tea. The final action also creates a strong sense that he acts quickly when he finds the beautiful plant.

## Scene 3

> 可是夹竹桃的毒很重，让他肚子很痛。他在地上躺着，觉得他快会死了。可是正好有别人找到他了，给了他一个像伞的植物，叫灵芝。他吃灵芝以后就觉得很好，不再生病了。他测量起来了，像星星的植物是毒，像伞的植物是药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 快会：快要，Use 快要 to show that an event is about to happen; 快会 is not the usual form for an imminent event.
- 别人：一个人，别人 distinguishes other people from a speaker or a stated person; an unknown rescuer should be introduced as 一个人.
- 是毒：有毒，毒 is a noun here, while 有毒 describes that the plant is poisonous.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 毒很重 -- “夹竹桃的毒很重”：毒性很强，states more precisely that the plant has strong toxicity.
- 很好 -- “他吃灵芝以后就觉得很好”：好多了，shows that his condition has improved compared with the earlier pain；舒服多了，specifically describes physical relief after feeling ill.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 找到他了：找到了他，Finding him is a completed action, so 了 follows the verb before its object.
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃灵芝以后：吃了灵芝以后，This refers to one completed act of eating, so add 了 to mark completion before 以后.
- Expressiveness: At the moment of pain, add a stronger physical reaction to show how serious it feels, for example: “他的肚子疼了起来，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: When the rescuer appears, add one visual detail to make the new character memorable, for example: “这时，一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人走过来。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought while he is lying on the ground to show fear, for example: “他想：‘我会死吗？’”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the sequence from pain to help to recovery, and your use of “给了他一个……” makes the giving action easy to understand. You also correctly use “叫灵芝” to name the new plant.
