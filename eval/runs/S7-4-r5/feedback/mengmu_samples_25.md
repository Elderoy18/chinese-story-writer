## Scene 1

> 这是古代的故事。孟子小的时候，他的爸爸很早就去世了。从他的家到墓地很近，所以他每天老是从早到晚去墓地跟朋友们玩玩儿。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 从他的家到墓地很近：他的家离墓地很近，Use 离 to express that one place is close to another; 从 marks a starting point for movement rather than a distance relationship.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很早 -- “他的爸爸很早就去世了”：在孟子年幼时，specifies that the father died during Mencius’s childhood and makes the timing clearer.
- 朋友们 -- “去墓地跟朋友们玩玩儿”：小伙伴们，more specifically describes friends of a young child.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子小的时候，他的爸爸很早就去世了：有一个小男孩叫孟子。孟子小的时候，他的爸爸很早就去世了，On a character’s first mention, introduce the character with a full noun phrase and name before using later references.
- Expressiveness: At the cemetery scene, add what Mencius notices to create a clearer setting, for example: “墓地里常常有人哭，也有人来祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: Add Mencius’s feeling and imitation of the adults, for example: “孟子觉得祭拜很有趣，就学着大人的样子祭拜。”

### Encouragement
You clearly establish the time period and explain why the cemetery is easy for Mencius to visit. The sentence about his father’s death also gives the scene an important emotional background.

## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈觉得在墓地玩儿，害得，所以他们搬家。搬家对孟子有好的影响，他才五岁，可是每天不是写字就是帮妈妈的工作。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 觉得在墓地玩儿：觉得孟子在墓地玩儿，The embedded action needs its own subject; without it, the sentence incorrectly suggests that the mother is playing at the cemetery.
- 害得：害得她得老是道歉，This structure requires the person affected and a harmful result after it.
- 帮妈妈的工作：帮妈妈干活，After 帮妈妈, use a verb phrase to state what help he gives; 干活 means doing work or chores.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好的 -- “搬家对孟子有好的影响”：积极的，specifies that the influence is beneficial rather than simply “good.”

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 所以他们搬家：所以他们搬家了，The move is presented as a completed result of the earlier problem, so use 了 for completion.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他才五岁，可是每天不是写字就是帮妈妈的工作：他才五岁，就每天不是写字就是帮妈妈干活，Use 才五岁就 to show that he began doing these things at an unusually young age; 可是 is not needed for this early-action relationship.
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery activity, add the mother’s feeling to make the reason for moving more vivid, for example: 孟母很担心，也觉得很烦恼。
- Expressiveness: You could add a short thought from the mother before the move, for example: 孟母心想：“如果一直住在这儿，麻烦会越来越多。”

### Encouragement
- You used the pattern 不是……就是…… effectively to show the child’s daily routine. The cause-and-result idea with 所以 also gives the scene a clear direction.

## Scene 3

> 他很聪明的人，难怪他成为有名的人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他很聪明的人：他是一个很聪明的人，When an adjective modifies the noun 人, it needs a noun phrase such as 一个很聪明的人, with 是 linking the subject to that noun phrase.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为有名的人：成为了有名的人，This is a completed achievement in the story, so the verb needs 了 to mark completion.
- Expressiveness: At the final achievement, add a more specific identity rather than only saying 有名的人. Example: 成为了有名的哲学家。
- Expressiveness: Add a short reaction from other people to make his success feel more vivid. Example: 人们都很尊敬他。

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect his intelligence with his later success by using 难怪. This is a concise ending that shows the character’s achievement.
