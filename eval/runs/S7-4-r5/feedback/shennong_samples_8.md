## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个人叫神农。一天，神农正在路上走，一看很多人正在吃一些植物。一些人一吃植物就生病了，神农想帮别人。所以想知道哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，the time phrase is redundant because 古代 already names a time period.
- 一看很多人正在吃一些植物：看到很多人正在吃一些植物，the intended meaning is that 神农 perceived the people; 看 means “look,” while 看到 marks the result of seeing.
- 别人：人们，别人 means other people contrasted with a stated person or group, while this sentence refers to people generally.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 想知道 -- “所以想知道哪些植物能吃”：想弄清楚，emphasizes finding a clear answer after observing the problem.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一天，神农：一天，他，use a pronoun when the character was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other character competes as the referent.
- Expressiveness: At “很多人正在吃一些植物,” add a visible detail showing that the plants are being eaten carelessly, for example: “很多人不知道这些植物有没有毒，就随手吃了起来。”
- Expressiveness: After “一些人一吃植物就生病了,” show their symptoms to make the danger clearer, for example: “一些人捂着肚子，脸色很难看。”
- Expressiveness: After 神农 decides to investigate, add his thought, for example: “神农心想：我一定要弄清楚哪些植物能吃。”

### Encouragement
You clearly set the scene and introduced 神农 before using his name. You also used the pattern “一吃……就……” correctly to show that eating the plants quickly caused illness.

## Scene 2

> 然后，神农开始尝很多植物，比方说他吃过生姜、芥兰和菠菜。一些植物吃真好，特别是黄瓜。但是，一些植物毒得很，特别是夹竹桃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一些植物吃真好：一些植物真好吃，The flavor description should use the adjective compound “好吃”; “真” modifies “好吃,” not the verb “吃.”
- 一些植物毒得很：一些植物有毒，“毒” cannot be intensified with “得很”; use “有毒” to state that a plant is poisonous.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 吃过 -- “比方说他吃过生姜、芥兰和菠菜”：尝过, emphasizes trying these plants as part of an experiment rather than simply eating them.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：他，The character was named in the immediately preceding context and no other possible subject competes here, so a pronoun makes the continued action flow more naturally.
- Expressiveness: After the first sentence, show the effort involved in the search with a short action detail, such as “他走过很多高山，寻找各种植物。”
- Expressiveness: At “特别是黄瓜,” add a sensory detail to show why it tastes good, such as “黄瓜又脆又清凉。”
- Expressiveness: At “特别是夹竹桃,” show the danger through Shen Nong’s reaction, such as “他一吃夹竹桃，肚子就疼了起来。”

### Encouragement
You clearly contrast plants that are pleasant to eat with plants that are dangerous, and your specific examples make the scene easy to understand.

## Scene 3

> 一个人对神农说，麻烦我们可以吃什么植物？神农说，这就要看你想不想生病了。以后，神农告诉别人一些植物能吃，一些植物不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 麻烦我们可以吃什么植物：请问，我们可以吃什么植物，麻烦 cannot directly introduce a whole question in this way; 请问 is the appropriate polite question opener.
- 这就要看你想不想生病了：这就要看是什么植物了，this pattern should identify the uncertain factor that determines the answer; whether someone wants illness does not determine which plants are edible.
- 以后：后来，when a new sentence means “afterwards” in a narrative, use 后来 rather than 以后.
- 别人：人们，别人 distinguishes other people from a particular speaker or group, but this narration needs the concrete group of people receiving Shennong’s information.

- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
