## Scene 1

> 神农和他的家人住在古代，所以不知道什么植物能吃。因为他的家人常常生病了，所以神农和他的哥哥一起去旅行找可以吃的植物。他们去很多不一样的地方，比方说高山、山谷和沙漠。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 住在古代：生活在古代，古代 is a historical time period rather than a physical place of residence; use 生活在古代 to place the characters in that era.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 找 -- “去旅行找可以吃的植物”：寻找, emphasizes a continuing, purposeful search for edible plants.
- 不一样 -- “很多不一样的地方”：各不相同, makes clear that the places differed from one another.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：一个叫神农的人，On a character’s first mention, introduce the person with both a name and a noun such as 人, rather than assuming the reader already knows the name.
- Coherence (Temporality): 常常生病了：常常生病，A habitual action takes the zero particle; 了 does not mark a routine action.
- Coherence (Temporality): 去很多不一样的地方：去了很多不一样的地方，The visits to many places are completed narrative actions, so use 了.
- Expressiveness: After the family’s illnesses, add Shennong’s feeling to show his motivation more clearly: “神农很担心家人的身体。”
- Expressiveness: When describing the journey, add one action detail to make the travel more vivid: “他们翻过高山，穿过山谷。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech before the journey: “神农说：‘我要找到可以吃的植物。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly connect the family’s illnesses with Shennong’s decision to search for edible plants. You also give several specific destinations, which makes the journey feel broader and more adventurous.
