## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子。他们住在墓地的旁边，所以孟子老是祭拜。孟子不是祭拜，就是跟朋友聊天。他很喜欢他的城市，但是城市有才两个学校，都很小。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，this expression is redundant because 古代 already names a time period.
- 有才两个学校：才有两所学校，when 才 expresses a small number, it comes before 有; 所 is the appropriate measure word for schools.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 小 -- “都很小”：规模很小，specifies that each school is small in overall size or capacity.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他的城市：自己的城市，when the possessor is the same person as the subject, 自己的 makes that relationship clear.
- Coherence (Reference): 但是城市：但是，the city was mentioned immediately before in the same sentence, so a zero form is more natural than repeating the noun.
- Expressiveness: After “他们住在墓地的旁边”, add one concrete detail about the setting, such as “每天都有不同的人来墓地祭拜”.
- Expressiveness: At “都很小”, describe what makes the schools seem small, such as “两所学校的校舍都不大”.

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduce the boy, his mother, and their living place. You also use both 所以 and 不是……就是…… to connect ideas and describe Mengzi’s routine.

## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈不是不喜欢他们的城市，而是想孟子有很好的教育。他们搬家到有很多学校的城市。他们从早到晚走路，孟子一直抱怨。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 想孟子有很好的教育：想让孟子接受很好的教育，For the intended meaning of wanting something for another person, use 想让 + person + verb phrase; 想 + clause more often expresses what someone thinks is true.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们搬家到有很多学校的城市：他们搬到了有很多学校的城市，Use 搬到 + destination rather than 搬家到 + destination; because the move is presented as a completed event, add 了 after 到.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们从早到晚走路：他们从早到晚走了一天的路，This is one completed journey in the narrative, so the action needs 了; adding 一天的路 also makes the duration and activity more natural.
- Expressiveness: At the walking scene, add a physical detail to show why the complaint continues, such as “孟子走得腿都酸了”.
- Expressiveness: You could include a short line of speech after the complaint, such as “孟子说：‘我们什么时候才到？’”
- Expressiveness: At the mother’s motivation, add her inner thought, such as “妈妈心想：‘我要让孟子接受很好的教育。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly explain the mother’s reason for moving with a useful contrast structure, and the detail that Mengzi complains during the long walk helps show his feelings.

## Scene 3

> 他难怪抱怨，他想他的朋友，因为他不要离开他的城市。他的妈妈找到孟子一个老师。跟老师孟子学最重要的文学，所以他成为哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 跟老师孟子学：孟子跟老师学，The subject should come before the phrase beginning with 跟 so that it is clear that Mencius is learning from the teacher.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 想 -- “他想他的朋友”：想念，specifically expresses missing friends who are no longer nearby；挂念，emphasizes caring about and thinking of friends who are far away

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 找到孟子一个老师：给孟子找到了一个老师，孟子 is the beneficiary and needs 给; the finding has been completed, so add 了 after the resultative complement 到.
- Coherence (Temporality): 学最重要的文学：学了最重要的文学，Learning is presented as a completed action that leads to the later result, so it needs 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为哲学家：成为了哲学家，Becoming a philosopher is a completed change of state, so use 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他难怪抱怨，他想他的朋友：难怪他抱怨，原来他想朋友，Use 难怪……，原来…… to introduce an understandable result and then reveal its reason.
- Expressiveness: At the complaint, add Mencius’s feeling about leaving his friends: “孟子很难过，说：‘我不想离开我的朋友！’”
- Expressiveness: When the mother finds a teacher, describe the teacher with one simple detail: “这个老师很耐心，也很喜欢读书。”
- Expressiveness: Show Mencius’s response to studying: “孟子觉得文学很有意思，每天都认真学习。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect Mencius’s complaint to his friends and his reluctance to leave the city.
- You also give the scene a clear next step by introducing a teacher and showing how Mencius begins learning.
