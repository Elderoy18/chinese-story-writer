## Scene 1

> 很久以前，有一个老男人叫神农。神农在古代每一天看到了别的小镇人生病还是不舒服，所以他决定开始找新的药。他下一天开始爬当地的山脉，找了各种各样的植物。神农试一试每个植物，知道有什么好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 老男人：老人，The original term can sound impolite or carry an unwanted negative tone; this word is the neutral term for an elderly man.
- 别的小镇人：小镇上的人，The word distinguishes other ones from a stated one, but no contrasting town has been established; this location phrase clearly identifies the people.
- 还是：或，Use this conjunction for alternatives in a statement; the original conjunction is normally used for alternatives in a question.
- 下一天：第二天，Use this time expression to mean “the next day” in a narrative.
- 找了各种各样的植物：找到了各种各样的植物，The original verb means “look for”; the corrected form means that he successfully found the plants.
- 试一试每个植物：尝一尝每种植物，The verb precisely describes tasting plants to test them, and the measure word is used for kinds of plants rather than individual objects.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 药 -- “开始找新的药”：草药，specifies that the medicine is made from plants, matching the mountain-and-plant setting.
- 好处 -- “知道有什么好处”：药用价值，specifies that he learns how a plant can be used medicinally.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农试一试每个植物：他试一试每个植物，Use a pronoun when the same character was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Temporality): 每一天看到了：每天看到，This is a habitual action, so use the zero particle rather than completed-action 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 神农在古代每一天看到了别的小镇人生病还是不舒服，所以他决定开始找新的药：因为神农在古代每一天看到了别的小镇人生病还是不舒服，所以他决定开始找新的药，Use the paired connector to show that seeing ill people is the cause of his decision.
- Expressiveness: At the point where he sees ill people, add his concern and a simple thought: “神农很担心，心想：我要找到能治病的植物。”
- Expressiveness: When he climbs the mountains, add a visual detail about the setting: “他走过一座又一座高山，看见了很多不同颜色的植物。”
- Expressiveness: After he tastes a plant, show a specific discovery: “他尝了一片叶子，发现这种植物可以让肚子舒服一点。”

### Encouragement
You gave the character a clear name at the beginning and used time expressions to move the scene forward. The phrase “各种各样的植物” is also a strong, specific description for this story.

## Scene 2

> 比方说，他一次试一试有些植物的叶，吃得又新鲜又甜。煮这些叶以后，他一喝就知道他发现新的茶叶。虽然他找到了很多的新的植物，但是他的旅程有的时候特别是危险。比方说，他一吃灵芝就开始生病。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 试一试：尝了，Verb reduplication is for a light or tentative action, not for an action that has actually happened; use a completed verb here.
- 吃得又新鲜又甜：吃起来又新鲜又甜，Use 起来 when describing how food tastes; 新鲜 and 甜 describe the leaves’ taste, not the manner of eating.
- 知道他发现：发现，知道 means “know a fact,” while 发现 means “make a discovery”; the intended meaning is that he discovered tea.
- 特别是危险：特别危险，特别 directly modifies an adjective such as 危险; 是 is not used between them.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 新的 -- “很多的新的植物”：未尝过的，makes clear that these were plants he had not tasted before.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 煮这些叶以后：煮了这些叶以后，The boiling must be marked as completed before the later action introduced by 以后.
- Coherence (Temporality): 发现新的茶叶：发现了新的茶叶，This discovery is a completed event in the past narrative, so 发现 takes 了.
- Expressiveness: After the tea is prepared, add a sensory detail about its smell or taste, such as “茶水闻起来清香扑鼻。”
- Expressiveness: At the dangerous part of the journey, describe the setting more specifically, such as “山路又陡又滑，他走得很小心。”
- Expressiveness: When he becomes ill, add his worried thought or speech, such as “他捂着肚子想：‘我会不会死？’”

### Encouragement
- You use the paired contrast connector 虽然……但是…… well to show that finding plants was successful but the journey was still dangerous. Your scene also has clear cause-and-effect sequencing through 一……就…… .

## Scene 3

> 一个爬山的男人正好看到神农的麻烦，然后给了他一些药。神农最终他的小镇回到，跟别的人分享他的发现。你认为神农的故事真的吗？这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 神农最终他的小镇回到：神农最终回到了他的小镇，The destination must follow the resultative verb, and the completed return takes 了 after 到.
- 跟别的人分享他的发现：跟别人分享了他的发现，The noun 别人 is the usual form for “other people” here, and the final action in this completed sequence needs 了.
- 你认为神农的故事真的吗？：你认为神农的故事是真的吗？，A noun phrase followed by 真的 requires 是 to form a yes-or-no question about whether it is true.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 麻烦 -- “看到神农的麻烦”：困境，makes the difficulty more specific as a hard situation that Shen Nong is facing
- 小镇 -- “回到了他的小镇”：家乡，identifies the town more precisely as the place Shen Nong comes from

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处。：这就要看你相不相信中药对身体有好处了。，This fixed response structure ends with 了, and 看 naturally introduces whether the listener believes the statement.
- Expressiveness: At “一个爬山的男人,” add one visual detail so the new character is easier to picture, for example: “一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人赶快走过来。”
- Expressiveness: After the medicine is given, show Shen Nong’s physical change or feeling, for example: “神农吃了药以后，觉得舒服多了。”
- Expressiveness: At the return to the town, add Shen Nong’s purpose in a short thought or quotation, for example: “神农说：‘我希望大家以后不会随便吃植物。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly move the scene from Shen Nong receiving help to returning and sharing what he learned. The direct question at the end is also a good way to involve the reader.
