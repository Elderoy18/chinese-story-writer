## Scene 1

> 有一天孟子去了墓地玩儿，但是他妈妈觉得这是不合适的地方让孩子玩，所以她决定了他们会搬家找更好的地方。她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福，而且住在这儿教育不太好。因为她望子成龙，所以她会一直搬家才能找到质量好的教育。然后他们搬到了离城市更近的地方。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 决定了：决定，Mental-state verbs such as “决定” use the zero particle when stating a decision, not “了”.
- 他们会搬家找更好的地方：搬家，找更好的地方，After “决定”, use the verb phrase directly; do not repeat a new subject before the action.
- 她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福：她担心儿子的童年不会幸福，“恐怕” means “I am afraid that” and cannot take “她” as its subject; use “担心” for her fear.
- 住在这儿教育不太好：这儿的教育不太好，Use a location phrase with “的” to show that the education belongs to or is available in that place.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 地方 -- “搬家找更好的地方”：更合适的地方，specifies that the new place is more suitable for the child, not simply better in a general way
- 质量好的教育 -- “才能找到质量好的教育”：高质量的教育，uses the common phrase for education with a high standard of quality；优质的教育，emphasizes that the education is good in quality

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为她望子成龙，所以她会一直搬家才能找到质量好的教育：因为她望子成龙，所以她会一直搬家才能找到质量好的教育，Use “因为……所以……” to mark the cause-and-effect relationship between her hopes for her son and her decision to keep moving.
- Expressiveness: After the mother thinks the cemetery is unsuitable, add her visible feeling or a short line of speech, such as: 孟母皱着眉头说：“墓地不是孩子玩儿的地方。”
- Expressiveness: When describing the move closer to the city, add one concrete city detail, such as: 城市里有很多商人卖东西，街上很热闹。
- Expressiveness: Show the mother’s worry through an inner thought before the decision, such as: 她心想：“我不能让孟子一直住在这儿。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the mother’s motivation with “望子成龙,” and the events move in a logical order from the cemetery to the new home.
- Your use of completed-action markers in “去了” and “搬到了” effectively shows that those actions have finished.

## Scene 2

> 他们住才一月就又搬家。古代的时候，一般人不喜欢卖东西的人。但是孟子老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿，害得妈妈着急得很，因为她恐怕儿子会成为跟他们很像。她告诉了孟子：他们不是朋友，而是不好的人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 住才一月：才住一个月，In the pattern with a short duration followed by an early result, put 才 before the verb; use 一个月 for a duration of one month.
- 古代的时候：在古代，The original repeats the time meaning because 古代 already refers to a period of time.
- 老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿：常常跟这些卖东西的人玩儿，Using both 老是 and 常常 gives the same frequency meaning twice and is unnatural here.
- 她恐怕：她担心，恐怕 means “I am afraid that” and cannot describe another person’s fear in this structure; 担心 expresses that she fears something may happen.
- 成为跟他们很像：变得跟他们很像，成为 normally takes a noun phrase describing an identity or role, while 变得 is used for changing into a state or quality.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 卖东西的人 -- “这些卖东西的人”：商人，identifies them more precisely as people whose occupation is selling goods.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 妈妈：她，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the mother was mentioned in the earlier scene and no other female character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Temporality): 住：住了，This stay is completed before the later move, so the completed action needs 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬家：搬家了，The move is a completed narrative event, so add 了 after the verb.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉了：告诉，A speaking verb that introduces quoted speech normally uses the zero particle rather than 了.
- Expressiveness: After the description of the sellers, add a sound detail to make the place feel lively, such as “叫卖声不断”.
- Expressiveness: At the mother’s worry, add her private thought to show why she is anxious, such as “如果一直住在这儿，孩子会越来越像他们。”
- Expressiveness: Make the mother’s warning more emotional with a short quotation, such as “他们不是你的朋友，你不能总跟着他们玩儿。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the connection between Mengzi’s behaviour and his mother’s worry, especially with the causative structure “害得”.
- You also use direct speech to give the mother a clear voice in the scene.

## Scene 3

> 你不是当数学家，而是当哲学家，不能当卖东西的人。所以他们又搬家。在他们第三个家，孟子学了怎么祭拜。妈妈感觉这个地方适合他们，告诉了孟子：我们找到了最后的家。在这儿妈妈让儿子从早到晚学习，他难怪成为有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他难怪：难怪他，The conclusion marker should introduce the clause that follows it, so it comes before the subject.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 卖东西的人 -- “不能当卖东西的人”：商人，identifies more precisely a person whose occupation is selling goods.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉了孟子：告诉孟子，A speaking verb that introduces quoted speech takes the zero particle, not 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为有名的哲学家：成为了有名的哲学家，Use 了 because becoming a philosopher is presented as a completed outcome in the past.
- Expressiveness: After the mother decides that the place is suitable, add her relief or happiness, such as “松了一口气” or “很高兴”.
- Expressiveness: Make the new home’s learning environment more vivid with a detail such as “环境很安静” or “附近有学校”.
- Expressiveness: Add Mengzi’s feeling about studying, such as “觉得学习很有意思”, to show why he studies from morning to evening.

### Encouragement
You used the contrast pattern with “不是……而是……” clearly to show the mother’s hope for Mengzi. The time phrase “从早到晚” also effectively shows that his studying became a regular routine.
