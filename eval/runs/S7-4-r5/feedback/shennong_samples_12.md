## Scene 1

> 很久以前，古代的人很不知道什么植物能吃，也什么植物不能吃。所以很多人常常吃不安全的植物，吃不安全的植物对你的身体有坏处，很麻烦的情况。但是一个人，神农，要解决这个问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很不知道：不知道，The negative knowledge verb does not take the degree adverb 很; use the zero form here.
- 也什么植物不能吃：也不知道什么植物不能吃，Also must modify a verb phrase, so repeat 不知道 before the second embedded question.
- 很麻烦的情况：是一种很麻烦的情况，This noun phrase needs 是 to function as the predicate, and 一种 introduces the type of situation.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不安全 -- “很多人常常吃不安全的植物”：有毒, specifies that the plants harm people because they contain poison
- 有坏处 -- “吃不安全的植物对你的身体有坏处”：会伤害身体, states the physical harm more directly and clearly

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一个人，神农：一个叫神农的人，At a character’s first mention, introduce the name together with a clear noun phrase identifying the person.
- Expressiveness: After the sentence about harmful plants, add one concrete physical result, such as “很多人吃了有毒的植物以后，常常生病。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where 神农 appears, add his determined thought or speech, such as “神农想：‘我要找出哪些植物能吃。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly establish the main problem—people do not know which plants are safe—and you introduce 神农 as someone who wants to solve it. The cause-and-result idea in the second sentence gives the scene a clear direction.

## Scene 2

> 他又聪明又努力，旅行很远找到很多植物。比方说，灵芝和夹竹桃。他一吃各种各样的植物就知道能不能吃这些植物。然后，神农写他的主意。他喜欢植物的味道，特别是花。神农遇到了一个黄色的花，一吃就觉得不舒服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 旅行很远：去很远的地方旅行，A destination needs to be expressed with a place phrase; “很远” alone cannot directly modify “旅行” in this structure.
- 写他的主意：记下自己的主意，“记下” means to record an idea so it is kept; “自己的” clearly shows that the ideas belong to the subject.
- 遇到了一个黄色的花：看到了一朵黄色的花，“遇到” is normally used for meeting people or events, while “看到” fits noticing a flower; the matching measure word for a flower is “朵,” not “个.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很多 -- “找到很多植物”：多种，specifies that he found several different kinds of plants；许多种，also highlights the variety of plant types.
- 不舒服 -- “一吃就觉得不舒服”：肚子疼，identifies the discomfort more clearly as stomach pain.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 然后，神农写他的主意：然后，他记下自己的主意，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when no other character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农遇到了一个黄色的花：他看到了一朵黄色的花，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence because the previous sentence already refers to the same character.
- Coherence (Temporality): 找到很多植物：找到了很多植物，“找到” is a completed discovery in this event sequence, so it needs the completion marker “了.”
- Expressiveness: At the yellow-flower moment, add a visual detail to make the place easier to imagine: “那儿开着又大又漂亮的黄色花，像星星一样。”
- Expressiveness: After the character feels ill, add an inner thought to show his fear: “他想：我会死吗？”
- Expressiveness: Add a physical action to show the discomfort rather than only naming it: “他捂着肚子，躺在地上。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show that the character is hardworking and curious, and the sequence from trying plants to feeling ill gives this scene a strong sense of action.

## Scene 3

> 发现了夹竹桃有毒。别人看到他，很快给他吃一个灵芝。神农一吃就觉得比较好。然后，他继续写他发现的信息。会告诉他的朋友哪一个植物能不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别人：一个男人，This is a new, unspecified character. Use an indefinite noun phrase rather than a word that distinguishes “other people” from a stated person or group.
- 给他吃一个灵芝：给他吃了一个灵芝，The giving/feeding action is completed in this event, so the verb needs 了 before its object.
- 写他发现的信息：写下他发现的信息，When recording discovered information, 写下 means to write it down and keep a record; 写 alone does not clearly express that recording meaning here.
- 哪一个植物能不能吃：哪一种植物能吃，哪一种不能吃，哪一种 already asks which type of plant, so it should not be combined with the yes-or-no form 能不能. Use parallel clauses to state which plants are edible and which are not.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 比较好 -- “神农一吃就觉得比较好”：舒服些，specifies that his physical discomfort became less severe in this situation

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the medicine works, add a concrete physical detail, such as “神农吃了灵芝以后，肚子不那么疼了。”
- Expressiveness: Give the helper one identifying detail and a short line of speech, such as “一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人说：‘快吃这个！’”
- Expressiveness: Add Shen Nong’s relieved feeling after he improves, such as “神农终于放心了一点。”

### Encouragement
You keep the events in a clear order: discovering the danger, receiving medicine, feeling better, and recording the discovery. You also correctly use the pattern 一……就…… to show that Shen Nong felt better immediately after eating the medicine.
