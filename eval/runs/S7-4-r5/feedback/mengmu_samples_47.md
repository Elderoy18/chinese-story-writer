## Scene 1

> 这个男孩子叫孟子，孟子是古代的人。孟子的妈妈觉得住在哪儿对童年很重要，所以他们常常搬了家。她望子成龙，所以孟子的妈妈安排他的生活。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 常常搬了家：常常搬家，A habitual action takes the zero aspect marker; 了 is used for a completed event.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 童年 -- “住在哪儿对童年很重要”：成长，refers more precisely to the child’s whole process of growing up；成长环境，specifies that it is the environment in which the child grows up that matters.
- 安排 -- “孟子的妈妈安排他的生活”：规划，emphasizes that the mother thoughtfully organizes his life over time.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 这个男孩子叫孟子：一个男孩子叫孟子，A character’s first mention should use an unambiguous full noun phrase; 这个 makes the boy sound already known.
- Coherence (Reference): 她望子成龙，所以孟子的妈妈安排他的生活：她望子成龙，所以安排他的生活，After the mother is named by the pronoun in the same sentence, use zero form rather than repeating her full name.
- Expressiveness: At “她望子成龙,” add the mother’s inner thought to show her concern, for example: 孟母心想：“我一定要让孟子在一个好地方长大。”
- Expressiveness: Add a feeling after “安排他的生活” to show why she takes charge, for example: 她很着急，怕孟子学到不好的习惯。

### Encouragement
You used the idiom “望子成龙” very well to show the mother’s hopes for Mengzi. The cause-and-effect connection with “所以” also clearly links her belief about where to live with the family’s moves.

## Scene 2

> 因为他们常常搬了家，中国人说，孟母三迁。孟子没有爸爸，所以，他的妈妈更重要。从这儿到那儿，妈妈教孟子很多技能。先她让孟子跟朋友玩儿，然后教他怎么买又便宜又好质量的东西。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 常常搬了家：常常搬家，A habitual action takes the zero aspect marker rather than completed-action 了.
- 先她让孟子跟朋友玩儿：她先让孟子跟朋友玩儿，The sequence adverb 先 comes after the subject and before the verb phrase.
- 又便宜又好质量的东西：又便宜又质量好的东西，The phrase for “good quality” is 质量好; the full adjective phrase can then modify 东西.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 技能 -- “妈妈教孟子很多技能”：生活技能，specifies that these are practical skills for daily life；生活本领，emphasizes useful abilities that a child learns from a parent.
- 买 -- “教他怎么买又便宜又好质量的东西”：选购，emphasizes choosing goods by comparing price and quality；挑选，highlights carefully selecting the best item.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 妈妈教孟子很多技能：她教孟子很多技能，This is a close re-occurrence of the mother, with no competing female character, so a pronoun is more natural.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 中国人说：中国人所以说，The first clause gives the cause, so the result clause should use 所以 to complete the 因为……所以…… connection.
- Expressiveness: At the teaching moment, add a short line of advice from the mother, such as “买东西要先看价格，再看质量。”
- Expressiveness: When describing the moves, add a concrete action detail, such as “妈妈带着孟子收拾行李，搬到新的地方。”
- Expressiveness: Add Mengzi’s feeling when he learns to choose goods, such as “孟子觉得很有意思，认真地跟妈妈学。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the mother’s influence on Mengzi through teaching and everyday activities. The sequence from playing with friends to learning how to choose goods also gives the scene a clear order.
