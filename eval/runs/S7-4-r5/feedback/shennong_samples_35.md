## Scene 1

> 在古代，很多医生不知道怎么用药。所以他们找到了很多植物对药。神农先看到很多病人，他们吃很多植物对药。但是我觉得他们的吃的植物，让他们病人。神农觉得这个植物可能特别是毒。所以他想找一个植物对药，他们可以吃对药。正好，他找到了一把花，他想把花放在茶里，对药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很多植物对药：很多能做药的植物，The modifier needs to describe what the plants can do. “能做药的植物” means plants that can be used as medicine; “对药” is not a valid expression here.
- 植物对药：能做药的植物，Use “能做药的植物” to describe plants used as medicine; “对药” cannot follow a noun as its modifier.
- 他们的吃的植物：他们吃的植物，The first “的” incorrectly makes the pronoun possessive. In a relative clause, the pronoun should directly be the subject of the action before “的”.
- 让他们病人：让他们生病，After “让”, the result must be a verb phrase. “病人” is a noun meaning a sick person, while “生病” is the verb needed here.
- 特别是毒：特别有毒，“有毒” is the correct adjective phrase meaning poisonous. “是毒” cannot describe a plant in this way.
- 一个植物对药，他们可以吃对药：一种可以做药、他们可以吃的植物，“种” is the suitable measure word for a type of plant. Both descriptions must come before the noun, and “对药” is not a valid expression for “can be used as medicine”.
- 对药：做药，“做药” means to make or use something as medicine; “对药” does not fit as a verb phrase in this sentence.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农先看到：一个叫神农的男人先看到，This is the character’s first mention, so introduce him with both his name and a short identifying noun phrase.
- Coherence (Reference): 一把花，他想把花：一把花，他想把它，When an object was named very recently and no other object competes with it, use a pronoun rather than repeating the noun.
- Coherence (Reference): 这个植物：这些植物，No single plant has been identified before this point; the earlier reference is to many plants, so the singular demonstrative noun phrase is unclear.
- Coherence (Temporality): 神农先看到很多病人：神农先看到了很多病人，This is a completed event in the narrative, so use “了” after the resultative verb.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们吃很多能做药的植物：他们吃了很多能做药的植物，Their eating is presented as a completed earlier action connected to their illness, so it needs “了”.
- Expressiveness: After the patients are mentioned, add one physical symptom to show why they need help: “有的人吃了以后，肚子疼得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: When the flowers appear, describe their appearance before the character picks them: “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: After the character decides to search, add an internal question to show his purpose: “神农想：哪一种植物可以做药呢？”

### Encouragement
The opening gives the scene a clear historical setting and quickly establishes a problem for the doctors and patients. You also use a logical cause-and-effect idea when the character begins searching for plants that people can safely eat.

## Scene 2

> 他不知道如果这把花对你的身体好，这就要看如果这把花毒不毒了。神农想找别的植物，但是这很麻烦。正好，他找到了别的花。他一吃花，很快就生病了。他的肚子疼死了。神农不知道他吃不吃灵芝或者夹竹桃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 如果这把花对你的身体好：这把花对你的身体好不好，After 不知道, an embedded yes/no question needs the A-not-A form instead of 如果 plus a statement.
- 看如果这把花毒不毒：看这把花有没有毒，The part after 这就要看 must be a question form; 有没有毒 is the appropriate A-not-A question, without 如果.
- 他吃不吃灵芝或者夹竹桃：他吃的是灵芝还是夹竹桃，When choosing between two possibilities after 不知道, use 是……还是…… rather than 吃不吃……或者…… .

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 麻烦 -- “但是这很麻烦”：艰辛，emphasizes that searching for many plants requires difficult effort；繁琐，emphasizes that the work has many complicated steps.
- 生病 -- “很快就生病了”：发病，more specifically describes the moment when an illness begins.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 你的身体：自己的身体，The flower is being tested by 神农, so the body should refer back to the subject rather than to an unintroduced listener.
- Coherence (Reference): 花：其中的一朵花，The earlier phrase 别的花 introduces multiple flowers; this wording clearly identifies that he ate one flower from that group.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 很快：就很快，The pattern 一……就…… needs 就 to connect the first action with its immediate result.
- Expressiveness: At “找到了别的花,” add a visual detail about the flowers, such as “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: After “他的肚子疼死了,” show 神农’s physical state and thoughts: “他躺在地上，难受得不得了，心想：我会死吗？”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech when he is unsure about the plants: “神农问：‘这两种植物，哪一种可以吃？’”

### Encouragement
- You used the useful pattern 这就要看……了 to show that the answer depends on a condition. You also made the illness happen quickly after eating the flower, which gives the scene a clear sense of cause and effect.

## Scene 3

> 别的人给了神农一个灵芝。这个植物可以让神农更好。比方说，他会觉得更健康。吃灵芝以后，他可以再走。现在他觉得更好。最后，神农找到了很多植物，他可以用对药。这些植物让神农很高兴。神农想跟很多人分享他的新药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别的人给了神农一个灵芝：一个人给了神农一个灵芝，别的人 means people other than a previously identified person or group; no such contrast has been established here, so introduce this new person with 一个 人 instead.
- 他可以用对药：可以用来做药，植物 can be used as medicine with 用来做药; 对药 is not a valid phrase for this meaning.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 更好 -- “这个植物可以让神农更好”：感觉好一些，specifies that his physical condition has improved.
- 再走 -- “吃灵芝以后，他可以再走”：继续走，makes it clearer that he resumes his journey after recovering.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 这个植物：它，灵芝 was named in the immediately preceding sentence, so use a pronoun for this close re-occurrence rather than repeating a noun phrase.
- Coherence (Reference): 这些植物让神农很高兴：这些植物让他很高兴，神农 was mentioned recently and there is no competing male character, so a pronoun is more natural.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农想跟很多人分享他的新药：他想跟很多人分享他的新药，continue with a pronoun when the same character remains the clear subject in the next sentence.
- Expressiveness: After “吃灵芝以后,” add a physical detail showing his recovery, such as “他的肚子不疼了，觉得好多了。”
- Expressiveness: Make the person who gives the medicine more vivid by adding one simple detail, such as “一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人给了神农一个灵芝。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech when Shen Nong understands the plant’s use, such as “神农高兴地说：‘这种植物可以做药！’”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the sequence of recovery by using “吃灵芝以后” and “最后.” You also connect Shen Nong’s discovery of plants with his wish to help other people, which gives the scene a clear purpose.
