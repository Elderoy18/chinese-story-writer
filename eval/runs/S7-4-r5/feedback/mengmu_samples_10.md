## Scene 1

> 孟子小的时候，从早到晚都跟朋友玩。孟子和他的朋友喜欢玩在墓地里，装祭拜。孟子五岁的时候，跟妈妈搬家。他们搬家不是因为孟子玩得太多，而是因为妈妈认为他们的家离墓地太近。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 跟妈妈搬家：孟母带着他搬家，The subject who moves together with someone uses 跟; when the mother takes her child to move, use 带着.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 朋友 -- “从早到晚都跟朋友玩”：玩伴，specifies that these friends are companions who play with him.
- 玩得太多 -- “不是因为孟子玩得太多”：太贪玩，describes more precisely that he was excessively fond of playing.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和他的朋友：他和朋友，Use a pronoun when the character was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no competing referent has appeared.
- Expressiveness: At “在墓地里”, add one visual detail about the setting, such as “墓地里有很多石碑，周围安安静静的”.
- Expressiveness: After “装祭拜”, add a small action to show what the children did, such as “他们学着大人的样子，低头鞠躬”.
- Expressiveness: In the final sentence, show the mother’s concern with an internal thought, such as “妈妈觉得这里的环境不适合孩子长大”.

### Encouragement
- You clearly established the main character, his daily habit, and the reason for the family’s move. Your use of “不是……而是……” effectively contrasts the two possible reasons for moving.

## Scene 2

> 妈妈认为，住离墓地太近，害得孟子的心气。孟子和孟母搬到子思老师的家，跟子思老师住。孟母帮子思打扫房间和做饭。子思帮孟母教她的儿子。因为孟子现在没有朋友，他老是读书。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 住离墓地太近：住在离墓地太近的地方，A location phrase after this verb needs the preposition and a complete place expression.
- 害得孟子的心气：害得孟子心情不好，This pattern needs the affected person directly after the causative verb, followed by a harmful result.
- 搬到：搬到了，The move is a completed past action, so the verb needs the completion marker.
- 老是读书：一直读书，This is a neutral result of having no friends, while this word carries a complaining or negative connotation.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 搬到 -- “孟子和孟母搬到了子思老师的家”：搬进，specifies that they moved into the teacher’s home, rather than simply moving to its location

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为孟子现在没有朋友，他一直读书：因为孟子现在没有朋友，所以他一直读书，Use the paired connector to show that having no friends is the cause and studying is the result.
- Expressiveness: After the mother’s concern, add a small physical detail to show her worry, such as “孟母皱着眉头，心里很担心。”
- Expressiveness: When the teacher helps with the child’s learning, add a short line of speech, such as “子思老师说：‘孟子，你要认真读书。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the change in the family’s living situation and include how different people help one another. The cause for the child’s increased reading is also easy to understand.
