## Scene 1

> 古代，孟子跟孟母住在墓地的旁边。可是孟母注意，住在墓地的旁边影响孟子。他常常跟朋友们祭拜玩，所以孟母决定他们会搬家。这次，他们住在城市里的商店的旁边，从早到晚很吵。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 孟母注意：孟母注意到，The result complement indicates that she perceived the following fact.
- 祭拜玩：玩祭拜的游戏，To express “play a game about worshipping,” use 玩 followed by a noun phrase; 祭拜 cannot directly take 玩 after it in this meaning.
- 所以孟母决定他们会搬家：所以孟母决定搬家，After 决定, use the verb phrase directly; do not repeat the shared subject as a new clause.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 吵 -- “从早到晚很吵”：嘈杂，specifies that many mixed sounds make the place unpleasantly noisy；吵闹，suggests continual lively noise from people and shops

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子跟孟母：有一个小男孩叫孟子，他和妈妈，On first mention, briefly introduce the character and his relationship rather than assuming the reader already knows who they are.
- Expressiveness: At “他常常跟朋友们玩祭拜的游戏,” add a small action detail to show how the children play, for example: “他们一边笑，一边学大人祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: After “孟母注意到,” add her worried thought or speech, for example: “孟母心想：‘住在这儿会影响孟子的学习。’”
- Expressiveness: At the end, describe the sounds near the shops more concretely, for example: “商人一直叫卖，马车走过的声音也很大。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish the first home, the reason for moving, and the noisier new environment. Your use of “所以” also shows the cause-and-effect connection between Mengzi’s behaviour and Mengmu’s decision.

## Scene 2

- Nothing to flag.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 危险 -- “城市也有点儿危险”：不太安全，states more specifically that the city is not a safe place to live.

### Coherence & Expressiveness

- Expressiveness: After “城市也有点儿危险”, add a concrete detail about the surroundings, such as “从早到晚，街上又吵又乱。” This helps the reader picture why the place feels unsafe.
- Expressiveness: After “孟子也没有朋友”, show his loneliness through an action or thought, such as “他看着别人一起玩儿，心里很孤单。”
- Expressiveness: At the end, add a short thought from the mother, such as “孟母想：‘这儿不适合孟子，我得再找一个安静的地方。’”

### Encouragement

## Scene 3

> 所以，孟母和孟子搬到学校的旁边住。才五岁孟子开始学哲学。在学校，孟子不是看书，就是练习写字。将来，他成为中国的一个非常有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 才五岁孟子开始学哲学：孟子才五岁就开始学哲学，The pattern for an unexpectedly early action is subject + 才 + age/number + 就 + verb.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 有名 -- “中国的一个非常有名的哲学家”：享有盛名，shows that many people widely recognize and admire his reputation.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟母和孟子搬到学校的旁边住：他们搬到学校的旁边住，Both characters were established in the earlier scenes, so the collective pronoun is natural here and avoids repeating both names.
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬到学校的旁边住：搬到了学校的旁边住，The move is a completed narrative event, so the verb needs 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为中国的一个非常有名的哲学家：成为了中国的一个非常有名的哲学家，Becoming a philosopher is presented as a completed change of state, so 成为 takes 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 将来：后来，Later events in a past-tense story sequence use 后来 rather than the future-time expression 将来.
- Expressiveness: At the school setting, add a sensory detail about the learning environment, such as “学校里每天都传来朗朗的读书声。”
- Expressiveness: After the study routine, show Meng Mu’s feeling with a short thought, such as “孟母看到孟子认真学习，心里很高兴。”
- Expressiveness: Add a small detail showing Meng Zi’s attitude toward learning, such as “孟子觉得哲学很有意思，常常问老师很多问题。”

### Encouragement
You used the contrast pattern “不是……就是……” correctly to show Meng Zi’s daily study routine. The scene also has a clear progression from moving near the school to Meng Zi’s later achievement.
