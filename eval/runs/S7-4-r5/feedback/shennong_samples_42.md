## Scene 1

> 古代的时候有一个人叫神农。他需要当上医，一看别的人就想帮助他们。但是，他有一个问题，他不知道什么东西能做药。神农听说一个远远的地方有一种植物，什么病人吃都会觉得身体更好。神农三个月走去，找到有植物的地方，他看到一个很漂亮的植物，又香又好看。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，This expression is redundant because 古代 already refers to a time period.
- 医：医生，医 is not normally used alone for the occupation in this context; 医生 means “doctor.”
- 看：看到，看到 is needed when the meaning is successfully seeing someone, rather than simply directing one’s eyes toward them.
- 别的人：别人，别人 refers to other people in general; 别的 is used to distinguish something from a specific one already under discussion.
- 一个很漂亮的植物：一株很漂亮的植物，株 is an appropriate measure word for an individual plant; 个 is too generic here.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 东西 -- “他不知道什么东西能做药”：植物，identifies more precisely what he is trying to find, matching the later mention of a plant.
- 香 -- “又香又好看”：芬芳，specifies that the plant has a pleasant, flower-like scent.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农三个月走去：他三个月走去，Use a pronoun because 神农 was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Temporality): 三个月走去，找到有植物的地方：走了三个月，找到了有植物的地方，Both actions are completed: 了 follows 走 before its duration phrase, and 找到了 marks the completed result of finding the place.
- Expressiveness: At the description of the plant, add a visual detail to make it easier to imagine: “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花。”
- Expressiveness: After the doctor’s goal is introduced, add Shennong’s thought to show his determination: “他想：我要找到能治病的植物。”
- Expressiveness: During the long journey, add a simple detail about his effort: “他走啊走啊，翻过了很多高山。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduce Shennong’s goal of helping other people, which gives this scene a strong direction.
- The description “又香又好看” is a good start because it uses both smell and appearance to describe the plant.

## Scene 2

> 他觉得，这种一定是做药的花。原来他想错了。吃了以后，神农快生病。 这时一个穿着蓝色衣服的人帮神农，快给他一种红的灵芝。神农吃了以后，他没有病，想起来以前一个人告诉他，灵芝对身体有好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 快生病：很快就生病了，In this completed past event, “很快” describes how rapidly the illness began, and “了” marks that becoming ill as completed.
- 快给他：赶快给了他，In a past narrative, “赶快” expresses that the helper acted without delay, and “给了” marks the completed act of giving.
- 以前一个人告诉他：以前有一个人告诉过他，“有” is needed to introduce the existence of this person, and “过” marks the earlier experience that he now remembers.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好处 -- “灵芝对身体有好处”：疗效，specifically names the beneficial medical effect of a medicine in this context.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 他没有病：他的病好了，This is a change from being ill to recovering, so “好了” marks the completed change of state.
- Expressiveness: At the moment he becomes ill, add a physical reaction to show his discomfort, such as “神农的肚子突然疼了起来。”
- Expressiveness: When the blue-clothed person helps, add a hurried action to make the rescue more vivid, such as “他赶快拿出灵芝，给神农吃了。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought when Shen Nong remembers the earlier advice, such as “神农想：原来灵芝真的能治病！”

### Encouragement
You clearly show an effective sequence: Shen Nong makes a mistaken guess, becomes ill, receives help, and remembers useful knowledge about the herb. The blue clothing detail also helps the new helper stand out clearly.

## Scene 3

> 啊，他怎么忘记了？他把灵芝放在包里。一回家就告诉别人。他找到药以后他写下怎么做药在书上。所以人们都知道，那红灵芝对身体好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别人：大家，This scene does not set up a particular person from whom these people are being distinguished; use a word that clearly names the group being told.
- 写下怎么做药在书上：把做药的方法写在书上了，In a 把 construction, place the thing being written after 把, then use the verb and its location; the completed writing action also needs 了.
- 那红灵芝：那种红灵芝，A demonstrative before a noun needs an appropriate classifier; 种 identifies a type of plant.
- 对身体好处：对身体有好处，The fixed expression is 对……有好处; 有 is required before 好处.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 放 -- “他把灵芝放在包里”：放进，specifies the movement of putting the lingzhi into the bag；收进，adds the idea of putting it away carefully for keeping.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他找到药以后他写下：他找到药以后，写下，After the first 他 has already identified the subject in this sentence, use zero form for the same subject in the following clause.
- Coherence (Temporality): 放在包里：放在包里了，This is a completed action in the past narrative, so it needs 了 to mark completion.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉：告诉了，The telling is a completed event in the narrative, not a verb introducing quoted speech, so it needs 了.
- Expressiveness: At the moment he puts away the lingzhi, add careful action to show that it is valuable: “他小心地把灵芝放进包里了。”
- Expressiveness: When he tells everyone, add a short line of speech to make the discovery vivid: “他说：‘这种红灵芝对身体有好处！’”

### Encouragement
You used useful story connectors such as 一……就……, 以后, and 所以 to show the order and result of events. You also clearly showed that the character shares his new knowledge with other people.
