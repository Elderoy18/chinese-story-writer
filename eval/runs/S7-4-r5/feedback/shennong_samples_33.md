## Scene 1

> 古代时候，人不知道他们能吃什么植物，常常一吃就死。但是不知道为什么，没有办法。所以有一天，神农出去试一试很多种植物，告诉人什么植物是安全什么是危险。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代时候：在古代，古代 already identifies a time period, so adding 时候 is redundant.
- 人：人们，this refers to a concrete group of people, so use 人们 rather than the generic noun 人.
- 人：人们，this is again a concrete group receiving the information, so 人们 is needed.
- 什么植物是安全什么是危险：什么植物是安全的，什么植物是危险的，when an adjective follows 是 to describe an omitted noun category, use 的; repeating 什么植物 also makes the two categories parallel.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 试一试 -- “神农出去试一试很多种植物”：尝一尝，specifies that he samples the plants by eating them to learn whether they are edible.
- 常常 -- “常常一吃就死”：往往，emphasizes that this result frequently happened whenever people ate a plant.
- 没有办法 -- “不知道为什么，没有办法”：束手无策，expresses more vividly that people were helpless and could not find a solution.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农出去试一试很多种植物：一个叫神农的人出去试一试很多种植物，this is the character’s first mention, so introduce him with a full noun phrase rather than only a name.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉：告诉了，telling people is presented as the completed final action in this series, so it takes 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 但是不知道为什么，没有办法：不知道为什么，也没有办法，但是 marks contrast, but these two ideas are additional related problems rather than contrasting ideas; 也 links the second problem.
- Expressiveness: Before the journey, add Shennong’s determination or a short thought, such as “神农想：‘我一定要找出能吃的植物。’” This shows his motivation.
- Expressiveness: After he goes out, add a simple journey detail, such as “他走啊走啊，去了很多高山。” This makes the action easier to picture.
- Expressiveness: After he learns about the plants, add a recording detail, such as “他把发现写在书上。” This shows how he shared the knowledge with others.

### Encouragement
You clearly present the danger people faced and connect it to Shennong’s action with 所以. The pattern 一……就…… is also used effectively to show a quick result.

## Scene 2

> 发现有的植物对身体非常好，特别是茶，让肚子感觉又暖和又清楚。他告诉朋友：“你吃哪种植物，这就要看你的口味。比方说，你爱吃甜的，可能喜欢糖。”他最喜欢吃花，除了夹竹桃以外，什么都花想吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 让肚子感觉又暖和又清楚：让人觉得肚子又暖和又舒服，A person, rather than the stomach itself, has the feeling; also, 清楚 describes clear understanding or perception, while 舒服 describes a pleasant bodily sensation.
- 你吃哪种植物，这就要看你的口味。：你吃哪种植物？这就要看你的口味了。The fixed response pattern 这就要看……了 needs the final 了; the first part is the uncertain question being answered.
- 什么都花想吃：什么花都想吃，The question-word noun phrase must stay together as 什么花, followed by 都 to mean “every kind of flower.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好 -- “有的植物对身体非常好”：有益，specifies that the plants provide a health benefit rather than being generally good.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the tea discovery, add a sensory result such as “喝了以后，身体暖暖的，很舒服。” This makes the health benefit more concrete.
- Expressiveness: When introducing his preference for flowers, add a brief thought such as “他想：除了夹竹桃，别的花应该都能吃吧。” This shows his reasoning and makes the narration more personal.

### Encouragement
You clearly connect tea with a positive effect on the body, and you use direct speech to show the character explaining his ideas. The phrase “除了夹竹桃以外” also shows that you can express an exception clearly.

## Scene 3

> 他发现这个口味，一百岁时候，不小心吃了毒的花。一吃就开始死。但是生病时候告诉人什么植物是药，什么植物是毒。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一百岁时候：一百岁的时候，A multi-syllable age expression before 时候 needs the linking particle 的.
- 毒的花：有毒的花，Use 有毒的 to describe a poisonous plant; 毒 cannot directly modify 花 in this form.
- 开始死：开始死了，The particle 了 marks the completed change of state: he began to die after eating the flower.
- 人：人们，This refers to a concrete group whom he tells, so use 人们 rather than the generic noun 人.
- 告诉：告诉了，This is a completed narrative action, so add 了 after the verb.
- 什么植物是毒：什么植物有毒，Use 有毒 to say that a plant is poisonous.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不小心 -- “不小心吃了毒的花”：误食，states more precisely that he accidentally consumed the flower rather than simply acting carelessly.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 这个口味：自己的口味，The demonstrative 这个 needs a clear previously named referent, but no particular taste has been identified; 自己的 clearly shows that the taste preference is his.
- Expressiveness: After he eats the poisonous flower, add a physical reaction such as “肚子疼得不得了，躺在地上”, so the reader can feel how seriously ill he becomes.
- Expressiveness: At the moment he starts dying, add an inner thought such as “我会不会死？”, which shows his fear directly.
- Expressiveness: When he explains the plants to others, add a short warning such as “这种花有毒，不能吃！”, making his message clearer and more dramatic.

### Encouragement
- You used the useful pattern “一……就……” to show that the dangerous result happened immediately after he ate the flower.
- You also included both medicinal and poisonous plants, which helps show what the character learns from his experience.
