## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，孟子和孟母住了在靠墓地的家。孩子才五岁就每天在墓地祭拜了。在那个城市，孟母从早到晚都找工作，可是找不到，害得孟母没有钱。难怪她跟孩子搬家，原来买不了房。他们搬了到别一个城市找工作。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，This expression is redundant because the time noun already identifies the historical period.
- 靠墓地的家：靠近墓地的家里，The verb phrase needs a clear location: this wording specifies that the home is near the cemetery and that they live inside it.
- 跟孩子搬家：带着孩子搬家，This action means the mother takes the child with her when moving; the original wording suggests that they move together as equal participants.
- 别一个城市：另一个城市，This refers to one additional or different city; this use requires the word meaning “another,” rather than the form used to distinguish other people or things.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 买不了 -- “原来买不了房”：买不起，specifies that the problem is lacking enough money to afford a home.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟母：一个叫孟子的小男孩和他的妈妈，At first mention, introduce the child with a name and identity so the reader does not need prior knowledge of the characters.
- Coherence (Reference): 害得孟母没有钱：害得自己没有钱，The affected person is the same as the subject earlier in the sentence, so the reflexive form avoids unnecessarily repeating the name in the same sentence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 住了在：住在，Living in a place is a state here, not a completed action, so it does not take 了; the location follows 在.
- Coherence (Temporality): 每天在墓地祭拜了：每天在墓地祭拜，A daily routine uses the zero aspect form rather than 了, which marks a completed action.
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬了到：搬到了，For a completed move, 了 follows the resultative/directional part 到, not the main verb 搬.
- Expressiveness: At the cemetery sentence, add a brief reason for the child’s action or feeling, such as: “孟子觉得祭拜很有趣，就跟着别人祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: When the mother cannot find work, show her emotion with a small action: “孟母叹了口气，觉得很着急。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech before the move to show the mother’s decision: “孟母说：‘我们得去别的地方找工作。’”

### Encouragement
- You used the pattern “才五岁就” correctly to show that the child began an activity at a young age.
- Your scene also gives a clear cause for the family’s move by connecting the mother’s unsuccessful job search with their lack of money.

## Scene 2

> 这个城市老是很吵，不是人吵架，就是都叫，害得孟子一直不能睡觉。在那儿，孟母也找不到工作。孟子和妈妈要又一个次搬了家。在第三个城市，孟母找到工作，在商店做衣服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 人：人们，This refers to the concrete group of people making noise in the city, so use the plural noun rather than the generic noun.
- 要又一个次搬了家：要再搬一次家，With a modal verb for a future repeated action, use the pattern modal verb + 再 + verb + number + measure word + object; completed 了 is not used here.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 吵 -- “这个城市老是很吵”：嘈杂，specifies that the place is full of unpleasant, mixed noise; 喧闹，emphasizes the lively but noisy sounds of many people.
- 做 -- “在商店做衣服”：缝制，specifies making clothes by sewing; 制作，emphasizes producing clothes rather than doing general work.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 孟母找到工作：孟母找到了工作，Finding the job is a completed action, so the verb needs 了 after the result complement.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 在那儿，孟母也找不到工作。孟子和妈妈要：因为孟母在那儿也找不到工作，所以她要，Use 因为……所以…… to show that not being able to find work is the cause of moving again.
- Expressiveness: At the description of the noisy city, add specific sounds to show why it is unpleasant: “街上有人大声吵架，还有人不停地叫喊。”
- Expressiveness: After the sleeping problem, add Mengzi’s physical feeling: “孟子又困又烦，躺在床上翻来覆去。”
- Expressiveness: When the mother finds work, add her happy reaction or speech: “孟母高兴地说：‘我终于找到工作了！’”

### Encouragement
You clearly show how the noisy environment affects the child by using a cause-and-result structure. The move to the third city also leads naturally into the mother finding a job.

## Scene 3

> 孟子在那个商店也找了到工作，可是不是做衣服的工作，而是喜欢的工作。孟子学习馆学了十五年，他的学习和他的旅行经验很重要，对他很多好处。他是大人的时候，他成为很有名的哲人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 找了到：找到了，了 must follow the resultative complement 到, not come between the verb and its resultative complement.
- 孟子学习馆学了十五年：孟子在学习馆学习了十五年，A place where an action happens needs 在 before it, and 学习 is the action being described.
- 对他很多好处：对他有很多好处，The verb 有 is required in this pattern to say that something brings benefits to someone.
- 他是大人的时候：他长大以后，Use 长大以后 to introduce the later stage after someone has grown up.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 做衣服 -- “不是做衣服的工作”：缝制衣服，specifies that the work involves sewing garments rather than referring generally to making clothes
- 很有名 -- “成为很有名的哲人”：广为人知，makes clear that many people know him

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他成为很有名的哲人：成为很有名的哲人，After the same person has already been named in the time clause, use zero form rather than repeating the pronoun in the same sentence.
- Coherence (Reference): 他的学习和他的旅行经验：自己的学习和旅行经验，Use 自己的 to show clearly that the learning and travel experience belong to Mengzi, the person being discussed.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为很有名的哲人：成为了很有名的哲人，Becoming a philosopher is a completed change of state in this retrospective narrative, so it takes 了.
- Expressiveness: After the first sentence, add Mengzi’s feeling about finding work, such as “孟子很高兴，心想：‘我终于找到自己喜欢的工作了！’”
- Expressiveness: When describing his later success, add a small detail about how others respond to him, such as “很多人都来听他讲道理。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the contrast between making clothes and the work Mengzi prefers, using the pattern “不是……而是……”.
- You also give Mengzi a long period of learning and travel, which helps show why he later becomes well known.
