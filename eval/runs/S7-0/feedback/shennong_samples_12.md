## Scene 1

> 很久以前，古代的人很不知道什么植物能吃，也什么植物不能吃。所以很多人常常吃不安全的植物，吃不安全的植物对你的身体有坏处，很麻烦的情况。但是一个人，神农，要解决这个问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很不知道：不知道，Degree adverbs such as “很” do not normally modify the negative knowledge verb “不知道.”
- 也什么植物不能吃：也不知道什么植物不能吃，The adverb “也” must modify a verb phrase; it cannot be followed directly by a noun clause.
- ，很麻烦的情况：，是一件很麻烦的事，A noun phrase such as “很麻烦的情况” cannot stand alone as the predicate here; add “是” and use “事” for the problematic matter.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不安全的 -- “很多人常常吃不安全的植物”：危险的，specifies that the plants may cause danger；有毒的，more precisely identifies plants containing poison.
- 有坏处 -- “对你的身体有坏处”：有害，states more directly that the plants harm the body.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 但是一个人，神农：有一个叫神农的人，This is the first mention of the character, so introduce him clearly with a full noun phrase rather than placing his name after the vague noun “一个人.”
- Coherence (Reference): 对你的身体：对身体，The sentence makes a general statement about many people, so “你的” shifts unexpectedly to the reader; use a general reference instead.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 但是一个人，神农，要解决这个问题：有一个叫神农的人要解决这个问题，“但是” marks contrast, but the arrival of someone who wants to solve the problem does not contrast with the previous sentence. Use no contrast connector here.
- Expressiveness: After the sentence about harmful plants, add a concrete result, such as “很多人吃了以后常常生病。” This shows why the situation is serious.
- Expressiveness: When introducing the character, state his goal more specifically: “他决定找出哪些植物能吃，哪些植物有毒。” This makes his purpose clearer.

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish the main problem—people do not know which plants are edible—and connect it to the harm caused by unsafe plants. You also introduce a character who wants to solve that problem, which gives the scene a clear direction.

## Scene 2

> 他又聪明又努力，旅行很远找到很多植物。比方说，灵芝和夹竹桃。他一吃各种各样的植物就知道能不能吃这些植物。然后，神农写他的主意。他喜欢植物的味道，特别是花。神农遇到了一个黄色的花，一吃就觉得不舒服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 旅行很远：到很远的地方旅行，use a destination phrase with 到……地方 to say where someone travels; 旅行 cannot directly take 很远 in this way.
- 写他的主意：写下自己的想法，写下 means to record thoughts in writing, and use 自己的 when the writer and the possessor of the thoughts are the same person.
- 遇到了一个黄色的花：看到了一朵黄色的花，遇到 is normally used for unexpectedly encountering people or events; use 看到 for noticing a flower, and 朵 is the matching measure word for 花.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 然后，神农：然后，他，use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the same character was mentioned in the previous sentence and no competing character has appeared.
- Coherence (Temporality): 写：写下了，this is a completed narrative action, so use 了 after the resultative complement 下: 写下了.
- Expressiveness: After the journey, add a small detail about the difficult route, such as “他走啊走啊，去了很多高山。”
- Expressiveness: When describing the yellow flower, make its appearance more vivid, such as “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: Show Shen Nong’s physical feeling after eating it, such as “他的肚子疼了起来，难受得不得了。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show Shen Nong’s qualities with “又聪明又努力,” and your use of “一……就……” creates a strong cause-and-result link between tasting plants and learning about them.

## Scene 3

> 发现了夹竹桃有毒。别人看到他，很快给他吃一个灵芝。神农一吃就觉得比较好。然后，他继续写他发现的信息。会告诉他的朋友哪一个植物能不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 写他发现的信息：写下他发现的信息，When recording information that has been learned, 写下 is needed to show that it is written down and kept; 写 alone is too general in this context.
- 哪一个植物能不能吃：哪些植物能吃，哪些不能吃，哪一个 asks which single plant it is, while 能不能 asks whether it can be eaten; use parallel phrases to state both groups clearly.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 比较好 -- “神农一吃就觉得比较好”：好多了，shows a clearer improvement from feeling unwell to feeling much better.
- 信息 -- “他发现的信息”：他的发现，refers more specifically to the knowledge he learned about the plants.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 别人看到他：一个男人看到他，别人 means people other than a previously relevant person and does not introduce a new character clearly. Use a full noun phrase for this character’s first mention.
- Coherence (Temporality): 给他吃一个灵芝：给他吃了一个灵芝，Use 了 after the completed action of giving him the medicine to eat.
- Expressiveness: When the man appears, add one visible detail to make the rescue easier to picture, such as “一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人走过来”.
- Expressiveness: After taking the medicine, add Shennong’s physical feeling, such as “他的肚子慢慢不疼了”.
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought or question after he recovers, such as “这是什么植物呢？”.

### Encouragement
- You used the pattern “一……就……” well to show that the medicine worked immediately. Your scene also follows a clear sequence by moving from discovery, to help, to recording what was learned.
