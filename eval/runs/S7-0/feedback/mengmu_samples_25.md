## Scene 1

> 这是古代的故事。孟子小的时候，他的爸爸很早就去世了。从他的家到墓地很近，所以他每天老是从早到晚去墓地跟朋友们玩玩儿。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 从他的家到墓地很近：他的家离墓地很近，Use 离 to express the distance between two places. 从 marks a starting point and is normally used with a movement action.
- 每天老是从早到晚去墓地跟朋友们玩玩儿：每天从早到晚都在墓地跟朋友们玩儿，老是 describes repeated actions and often carries a mildly negative tone; 从早到晚都 more precisely expresses an activity lasting throughout the day.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the mention of the cemetery, add a visual detail about the visitors: “他常常看见有人低着头来墓地祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: Show Mengzi’s reaction to what he sees at the cemetery: “孟子觉得很好奇，觉得祭拜很好玩儿。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of dialogue from his friends when they play there: “朋友说：‘我们也来学他们祭拜吧！’”

### Encouragement

## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈觉得在墓地玩儿，害得，所以他们搬家。搬家对孟子有好的影响，他才五岁，可是每天不是写字就是帮妈妈的工作。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在墓地玩儿，害得：孟子在墓地玩儿很麻烦，The verb “觉得” needs a complete clause with a predicate. Also, “害得” requires a cause, an affected person, and a harmful result, so it cannot end the clause by itself.
- 帮妈妈的工作：帮妈妈干活，After “帮妈妈”, use a verb phrase to say what help is given. “妈妈的工作” is a noun phrase, so it cannot directly follow “帮” in this meaning.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好的 -- “搬家对孟子有好的影响”：积极的，specifies that the effect supports Mengzi’s development；正面的，emphasizes that the effect is beneficial rather than harmful.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们搬家：他们搬家了，Use “了” for the completed action of moving house.
- Expressiveness: At the cemetery sentence, show why the situation is troublesome with a concrete action and reaction: “孟子一边笑一边学别人祭拜，来祭拜的人很生气。”
- Expressiveness: Add the mother’s thoughts before the move to show her worry: “孟母心想：‘住在这儿太麻烦了。’”
- Expressiveness: Near “他才五岁”, add the mother’s feeling when she sees Mengzi studying: “孟母看见孟子认真写字，心里很高兴。”

### Encouragement
- You used “才五岁” well to emphasize that Mengzi is very young. You also used the pattern “不是……就是……” effectively to describe his daily activities.
