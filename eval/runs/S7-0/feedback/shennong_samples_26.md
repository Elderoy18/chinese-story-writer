## Scene 1

> 很久以前，有一个老男人叫神农。神农在古代每一天看到了别的小镇人生病还是不舒服，所以他决定开始找新的药。他下一天开始爬当地的山脉，找了各种各样的植物。神农试一试每个植物，知道有什么好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 老男人：老人，老男人 can sound negative or refer broadly to an adult male; 老人 is the appropriate noun for an elderly person in this story.
- 别的小镇人：别的小镇的人，when referring to people from other small towns, the location phrase needs 的 before 人.
- 生病还是不舒服：生病或者觉得不舒服，还是 introduces alternatives in a question or an indirect question; use 或者 for alternatives in this statement.
- 下一天：第二天，第二天 is the usual time expression for “the next day” in a past narrative.
- 找了各种各样的植物：找到了各种各样的植物，找 means “look for,” while 找到 means “find”; the sentence describes plants that Shennong found.
- 每个植物：每种植物，种 is the measure word for types of plants; 个 does not fit here.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好处 -- “知道有什么好处”：功效，specifies the useful effects of a plant or medicine in this context；药用价值，specifies a plant’s value for making medicine.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农在古代每一天：他在古代每天，after a character has just been introduced and no other character competes as the referent, use a pronoun rather than repeating the name.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农试一试：他试一试，use a pronoun for this close re-occurrence of the same character.
- Coherence (Temporality): 每一天看到了：每天看到，everyday actions are routines, so they take the zero particle rather than completed-action 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 试一试：试了试，this is a completed trial in the narrative, so the reduplicated verb needs 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 试了试每种植物，知道：试了试每种植物，才知道，才 links the trial to the result and shows that knowledge came only after testing the plants.
- Expressiveness: After the mountain-climbing sentence, add a sense of effort and distance, such as “他走啊走啊，去了很多高山。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where he tests plants, add Shennong’s inner question to show his purpose, such as “他想：这种植物能做药吗？”
- Expressiveness: Add a small visual detail about the plants he finds, such as “有的叶子又圆又绿，有的花颜色很鲜艳。”

### Encouragement
You clearly introduce Shennong at the beginning and keep the scene focused on his effort to find medicine. You also use a useful cause-and-effect connection with 所以 to show why he makes his decision.

## Scene 2

> 比方说，他一次试一试有些植物的叶，吃得又新鲜又甜。煮这些叶以后，他一喝就知道他发现新的茶叶。虽然他找到了很多的新的植物，但是他的旅程有的时候特别是危险。比方说，他一吃灵芝就开始生病。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他一次试一试有些植物的叶：他尝了有些植物的叶，Verb reduplication such as “试一试” is for a light attempt or suggestion, not for an action that was actually carried out; “尝了” marks the completed tasting.
- 吃得又新鲜又甜：吃起来又新鲜又甜，“吃得” must be followed by a result or degree describing the action of eating; “吃起来” introduces the taste of the leaves.
- 特别是危险：特别危险，“特别是” introduces a specific item or example, while “特别” directly modifies an adjective such as “危险”.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 知道 -- “他一喝就知道自己发现了新的茶叶”：意识到，emphasizes the moment when he suddenly realizes what he has discovered.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他一喝就知道他发现了新的茶叶：他一喝就知道自己发现了新的茶叶，Use “自己” when the person who discovered the tea leaves is the same person as the subject of “知道”; this makes the reference unambiguous.
- Coherence (Temporality): 煮这些叶以后：煮了这些叶以后，Use “了” for the completed action that happens before the later action introduced by “以后”.
- Coherence (Temporality): 发现新的茶叶：发现了新的茶叶，Use “了” after “发现” because the discovery is completed at the moment he realizes it.
- Expressiveness: After the leaves are cooked, add a sensory detail about the drink, such as “叶子煮好以后，散发出淡淡的香味。”
- Expressiveness: At the moment he becomes ill, add his physical feeling to show why the journey is dangerous, such as “他觉得肚子突然疼起来，难受得不得了。”

### Encouragement
- You used “虽然……但是……” to connect the successful discoveries with the danger of the journey, and you also used “一……就……” to show a quick sequence of actions.

## Scene 3

> 一个爬山的男人正好看到神农的麻烦，然后给了他一些药。神农最终他的小镇回到，跟别的人分享他的发现。你认为神农的故事真的吗？这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别的人：小镇的人们，别人 distinguishes other people from a stated person or group; here the sentence simply means the people in the town.
- 你认为神农的故事真的吗：你认为神农的故事是真的吗，after 认为, the following clause needs 是 to link the subject to the predicate 真.
- 这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处：这就要看你相不相信中药对身体有好处了，this pattern needs an unresolved alternative such as 相不相信, and 了 completes the fixed expression.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 麻烦 -- “神农的麻烦”：困境，identifies his difficult situation more specifically than the general word 麻烦.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 神农最终他的小镇回到：神农最终回到了自己的小镇，回到 is a resultative verb, so the destination follows it, and the completed return needs 了 after 到; 自己的 also makes clear that the town belongs to 神农.
- Expressiveness: When the man appears, add one visual detail to make him easier to imagine, for example: “一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人正好走过来。”
- Expressiveness: Show Shen Nong’s physical feeling before the medicine is given, for example: “神农躺在地上，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech when the man offers the medicine, for example: “快吃这个药吧！”

### Encouragement
- The opening clearly introduces the new man with a full noun phrase before using pronouns. The closing question also gives the scene an engaging, reader-facing ending.
