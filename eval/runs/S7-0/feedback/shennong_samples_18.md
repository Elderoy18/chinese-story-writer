### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 神农和他的家人住在古代：在古代，神农和他的家人住在一起，古代 is a time expression, not a location after 住在; place the time expression at the beginning, while 住在 needs a location or 住在一起.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 常常生病了：常常生病，remove 了 because 常常 marks a habitual state, which uses the zero particle.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们去很多不一样的地方：他们去了很多不一样的地方，add 了 to mark the completed action in this past narrative.
- Expressiveness: After the family’s illness, add Shen Nong’s feeling to show his motivation: 神农看见家人常常生病，心里很难过。
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech before the journey: 神农说：“我要找出能吃的植物，帮家人治病。”
- Expressiveness: Describe one feature of the places they visit, such as: 高山上很冷，山谷里开着很多花。

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect the family’s illnesses to Shen Nong’s decision to search for edible plants, and your use of 因为……所以…… makes that cause-and-effect relationship easy to follow.

## Scene 3

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 夹竹桃是毒的：夹竹桃是有毒的，Use the adjective phrase with “有毒”; “毒” alone is a noun and does not describe the plant naturally here.
- 我想知道灵芝会帮他：神农想知道灵芝会不会帮神农，The first-person pronoun is not established in this third-person narration, and “他” could refer to either Shen Nong or his brother. The question form “会不会” also matches an uncertain thought.
- 哥哥给神农灵芝：哥哥给了神农灵芝，Add “了” after a completed giving action.
- 开始痊愈了：开始好转了，“痊愈” means to recover completely, so it cannot naturally follow “开始”; “好转” means that the condition begins to improve.
- 我想知道灵芝会帮他。”：我想知道灵芝会帮他。，“” cannot be an ending quotation mark without a matching opening quotation mark.
- 特别是如果我们已经吃了夹竹桃。“：特别是如果我们已经吃了夹竹桃。”，A quotation needs the closing mark “”.

### Vocabulary Suggestions

- 吃 -- “灵芝我们可以吃”：服用，specifies taking it as a medicinal plant.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 我：神农，Use the character’s name when the narration has not introduced a first-person narrator; this keeps the reference clear and maintains the third-person viewpoint.
- Coherence (Reference): 他：神农，Repeat the name because the previous scene also mentions the brother, so the pronoun has two possible referents.

- Expressiveness: After the recovery, add a physical feeling to show the change, such as “他的肚子慢慢不疼了，觉得好多了。”
- Expressiveness: Make Shen Nong’s thanks more emotional with a short line of speech, such as “谢谢哥哥！我刚才真的很害怕。”
- Expressiveness: Add one small detail about the medicinal plant before it is eaten, such as “灵芝像一把小伞，长在夹竹桃旁边。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the remedy sequence: the brother gives the medicine, Shen Nong eats it, and his condition improves. You also chose the precise recovery word “痊愈,” which shows strong vocabulary knowledge.
