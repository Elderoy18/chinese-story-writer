## Scene 1

> 古代的时候有一个人叫神农。他需要当上医，一看别的人就想帮助他们。但是，他有一个问题，他不知道什么东西能做药。神农听说一个远远的地方有一种植物，什么病人吃都会觉得身体更好。神农三个月走去，找到有植物的地方，他看到一个很漂亮的植物，又香又好看。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 当上医：当医生，Use the standard occupation noun after 当; 医生 means “doctor,” while 上医 is not the ordinary job title intended here.
- 一个远远的地方：一个很远的地方，Use 很远的 to describe the distance of a place in this noun phrase.
- 神农三个月走去：神农走了三个月，A duration follows the verb it describes. Also, 走去 normally needs a stated destination, so it does not fit here without one.
- 一个很漂亮的植物：一株很漂亮的植物，株 is the appropriate measure word for an individual plant.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 漂亮 -- “一个很漂亮的植物”：艳丽，specifically describes a plant with striking, beautiful colours and appearance.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农三个月走去，找到有植物的地方，他看到一个很漂亮的植物：神农走了三个月，找到有植物的地方，看到一株很漂亮的植物，Use zero form after a name has already appeared in the same sentence; repeating 他 is unnecessary.
- Coherence (Temporality): 找到有植物的地方：找到了有植物的地方，Use 了 for the completed action of reaching and finding the place.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 神农走了三个月，找到有植物的地方：神农走了三个月，才找到有植物的地方，才 connects the long duration with the later result.
- Expressiveness: At the plant description, add its colour and shape to make the setting vivid: “那儿开着黄色的花，像星星一样。”
- Expressiveness: After seeing the beautiful plant, add Shennong’s immediate feeling or thought: “神农觉得很奇怪，心想：这种植物会不会能做药呢？”
- Expressiveness: When introducing his wish to help others, add a short line of inner speech: “他想：我要学会治病，帮助生病的人。”

### Encouragement
- You introduce Shennong clearly at the beginning and give him a helpful motivation. The description “又香又好看” also gives the plant two sensory qualities rather than only naming it.

## Scene 2

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 以前一个人告诉他：以前有一个人告诉他，an indefinite person being introduced in this past event needs 有 before the noun phrase.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 没有病 -- “他没有病”：病好了, makes it clear that he recovered from the illness rather than simply stating that he does not have an illness.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 给他：给神农，repeat the name because the blue-clothed person and 神农 are both male characters in the same sentence, so the pronoun can be unclear.
- Coherence (Temporality): 快生病：很快就生病了，use 很快就 for an event that happens soon, and add 了 because becoming ill is a completed change of state.
- Coherence (Temporality): 快给：赶快给了，use 赶快 as the adverb for acting quickly, and use 了 because giving the medicine is a completed action.
- Expressiveness: When 神农 becomes ill, add a physical symptom to show the danger more vividly, such as “神农的肚子疼了起来，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: At the helper’s arrival, add a short line of speech to make the rescue feel immediate, such as “他赶快说：‘快吃这个！’”
- Expressiveness: After recovery, show 神农’s discovery through an inner thought, such as “神农想：‘原来灵芝对身体有好处！’”

### Encouragement

- The description “一个穿着蓝色衣服的人” introduces the helper clearly and gives the reader a visual detail.

## Scene 3

> 啊，他怎么忘记了？他把灵芝放在包里。一回家就告诉别人。他找到药以后他写下怎么做药在书上。所以人们都知道，那红灵芝对身体好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他找到药以后他写下怎么做药在书上：他找到药以后，把做药的方法写在书上了，After the verb phrase “写下,” the content should be a noun phrase such as “做药的方法.” Use the disposal structure with “把” to place that content before the verb, and place “在书上” before the verb as the location.
- 别人：大家，“别人” distinguishes other people from a person or group already mentioned; here he is sharing the information generally, so “大家” is the appropriate listener.
- 对身体好处：对身体有好处，“有好处” is the required expression meaning “to be beneficial to the body.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他找到药以后他写下：他找到药以后，写下，After the same subject has appeared in the first part of a sentence, use zero form rather than repeating the pronoun.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他把灵芝放在包里：他把灵芝放在包里了，Use “了” for this completed action in the narrative.
- Coherence (Temporality): 一回家就告诉大家：一回家就告诉了大家，Use “了” after the completed telling action.
- Coherence (Reference): 那红灵芝：那种红灵芝，Use “那 + measure word + noun” when referring to a specific kind of object.
- Expressiveness: After he remembers the use of the plant, add his relieved or excited thought: “他高兴地想：原来灵芝可以救人！”
- Expressiveness: Make the action of putting the plant away more careful: “他小心地把灵芝放进包里。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech when he tells people: “灵芝是药，对身体有好处！”
