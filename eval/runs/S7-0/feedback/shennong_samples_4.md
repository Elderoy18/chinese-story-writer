## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个男人，他的名字是神农。他看到了有很多人不知道哪个植物好吃，也不知道什么植物对你的身体有好处。这个问题神农觉得很糟糕，所以神农去了吃每种植物。他吃了一种植物以后，就写了这个植物对你有没有好处。有的时候，神农吃了很好吃的植物。有的时候，他吃了很危险的植物。他吃完了每种植物以后，会来跟他的朋友聊到他吃的植物了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 already names a time period, so adding 的时候 is redundant.
- 写了这个植物：写下了这种植物，when recording information about a plant, 写下 means to write the information down; 写 alone does not clearly express recording it.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 糟糕 -- “这个问题神农觉得很糟糕”：严重，specifies that the problem has serious consequences.
- 很好吃 -- “神农吃了很好吃的植物”：味道鲜美，describes the plant’s pleasant taste more vividly and precisely.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 所以神农去了吃每种植物：所以他去了吃每种植物，repeat the name with a pronoun when the same character was mentioned in the immediately preceding sentence and no other character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Reference): 有的时候，神农吃了很好吃的植物：有的时候，他吃了很好吃的植物，use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence of the same character.
- Coherence (Reference): 对你的身体有好处：对身体有好处，your shifts the reference from the people in the sentence to the reader; keep the reference general or use the people’s own bodies.
- Coherence (Temporality): 去了吃每种植物：去吃了每种植物，use the placement 去 + verb + 了 for a completed action; 了 cannot go between 去 and the main verb.
- Coherence (Temporality): 有的时候，神农吃了很好吃的植物：有的时候，神农吃很好吃的植物，use the zero particle for a habitual action introduced by 有的时候.
- Coherence (Temporality): 有的时候，他吃了很危险的植物：有的时候，他吃很危险的植物，use the zero particle for a repeated habitual action.
- Coherence (Temporality): 会来跟他的朋友聊到他吃的植物了：会来跟他的朋友聊到他吃的植物，use the zero particle after the modal verb 会; sentence-final 了 does not fit this future or habitual action.
- Expressiveness: After the sentence about people not knowing plants, add a concrete harmful result: “很多人吃了有毒的植物，常常生病。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where the problem worries him, show Shennong’s determination with an inner thought: “神农想：我一定要找出可以吃的植物。”
- Expressiveness: When contrasting helpful and dangerous plants, add a physical reaction to make the danger vivid: “他吃了有毒的植物，肚子疼了起来。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduce Shennong at the start and keep the scene focused on his effort to learn about plants.
- You also use useful time expressions such as “以后” and “有的时候” to organize the events.

## Scene 2

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一个别的人：另一个人，别的人 refers to people other than the person being spoken to; for one additional individual after the first questioner, use 另一个人.
- 一个别的人：另一个人，Use 另一个人 again because this is another single, newly introduced questioner.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 要是你喜欢吃甜的，有很多甜的水果：要是你喜欢吃甜的，就有很多甜的水果，Use the paired connector 要是……就…… to show the condition and its result.
- Expressiveness: After the first question, add a small action to make the conversation feel more alive, for example: 神农想了想，说：“这就要看你喜欢什么口味了。”
- Expressiveness: When listing the fruit, add one sensory detail, for example: 苹果又红又脆，西瓜清甜多汁。
- Expressiveness: Give the later questioners a brief feeling or purpose, for example: 另一个人担心地问：“哪个植物对身体有坏处？”

### Encouragement

## Scene 3

> 神农说，有一天，我吃了一个黄色的花，名字是夹竹桃。我一吃夹竹桃，就觉得这种植物对我的脑有坏处。可是，一个灵芝和一个男人正好在我的旁边，我说，麻烦你把那个植物给我吃一下。我一吃这个灵芝就好了。所以，你得小心吃植物，因为有的植物对你的健康有坏处，特别是夹竹桃。因为夹竹桃有毒。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一个黄色的花，名字是夹竹桃：一朵叫夹竹桃的黄色花，Flowers take the matching measure word 朵 rather than 个, and 叫 naturally introduces the name of a plant or flower.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 有坏处 -- “这种植物对我的脑有坏处”：有害，states more directly that the plant harms the brain or health.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为夹竹桃有毒。：夹竹桃有毒。，Because needs a following result clause; the previous sentence has already given the reason with 因为, so this extra cause-only sentence is incomplete and repetitive.
- Expressiveness: After “我一吃夹竹桃”, add a physical reaction to show the effect: “我的肚子马上疼了起来。”
- Expressiveness: When the man appears, add one visual detail to make him easier to imagine: “一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人正好在我旁边。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short worried thought before asking for help: “我会不会死呢？”

### Encouragement
- You use the 一……就…… pattern clearly to show quick results, and your final warning connects the experience to a useful lesson about being careful with plants.
