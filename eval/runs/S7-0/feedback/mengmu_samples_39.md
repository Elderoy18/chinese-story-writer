## Scene 1

> 古代的时候人觉得祭拜非常重要，孟子很小的时候，从早到晚一直祭拜，因为他妈妈告诉他祭拜很重要。虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜。他的同学们老是对他开玩笑了。孟子妈妈看了他的同学们对孟子不好的影响，所以孟子和孟子妈妈搬家了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，the time expression is redundant because 古代 already refers to a time period.
- 人觉得：人们觉得，use 人们 for a concrete group of people in general; 人 by itself is too generic here.
- 看了：看到了，看 means “look at,” while 看到 means “see/notice.” The resultative complement 到 shows that she noticed the completed effect.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不好 -- “他的同学们对孟子不好的影响”：不良，specifically describes a harmful or negative influence in this context；负面，emphasizes that the influence affected him in an undesirable direction.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子很小的时候：一个叫孟子的小男孩很小的时候，follow the first-mention rule: introduce a new character with a full noun phrase before later using pronouns or the name alone.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子妈妈：孟子和妈妈，within the same clause, do not repeat the same name inside a close possessive reference; use the shorter repeated reference.
- Coherence (Temporality): 老是对他开玩笑了：老是对他开玩笑，老是 describes a habitual action, so it takes the zero particle rather than completed-action 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜：因为他每天只祭拜，所以没有朋友，use 因为……所以…… to show the cause-and-effect relationship.
- Expressiveness: After the worship routine, add Mengzi’s feeling to show why he keeps doing it, for example: “孟子觉得祭拜很有趣。”
- Expressiveness: Make the classmates’ teasing more visible through an action, for example: “同学们笑着模仿他祭拜的样子。”
- Expressiveness: Show the mother’s concern before the move, for example: “孟子妈妈很担心，心想：‘再这样下去，会影响孩子。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show a cause for Mengzi’s behavior and connect the mother’s discovery to the family’s move.
- You also used useful story-time phrases such as “从早到晚” and “一直” to describe a repeated routine.

## Scene 2

> 孟子才十一岁，成为一个哲学家。孟子不是祭拜，而是看政治文章，难怪他成为了哲学家。不过，看过很多政治文章对他害得，他有很多问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 孟子才十一岁，成为一个哲学家：孟子才十一岁，就成为了一个哲学家，use the paired pattern 才……就…… to show that the result happened at an unexpectedly young age; 成为 is a completed change of state here, so it takes 了.
- 看过很多政治文章对他害得：他看过很多政治文章，the phrase 对他害得 is not grammatical here. 害得 must introduce a harmful result after it, in the pattern 害得 + person + result; it cannot be used simply to connect the reading to having questions.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 问题 -- “他有很多问题”：疑问，makes clear that these are intellectual questions he wants to understand, rather than practical difficulties.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子不是祭拜：他不是祭拜，use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence: the previous sentence has already named 孟子, and no competing male character appears.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 孟子不是祭拜，而是看政治文章，难怪他成为了哲学家：难怪他成为了哲学家，原来他不祭拜，而是看政治文章，with 难怪, state the conclusion first and use 原来 to introduce the reason.
- Expressiveness: At the point where he reads, add a small detail about his concentration, such as “他每天认真地看政治文章，还把不懂的地方记下来。”
- Expressiveness: Show his thinking with a short line of speech, such as “孟子说：‘为什么国家要有这么多规矩呢？’”

### Encouragement
- You used the contrast pattern 不是……而是…… to show how Mengzi’s interests changed, and you connected his reading to his later achievement as a philosopher.

## Scene 3

> 他一直问问题，但是不能解决那些问题。孟子长大，他开始能解决问题。他常常建议你不是学金融，而是学政治，因为如果你懂政治，你会成为高薪。后来孟子成为教授，他教学生很厉害。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不是学金融，而是学政治：不要学金融，而要学政治，After a verb of recommendation, use a negative command such as “不要” and a contrasting positive command such as “而要”; “不是……而是……” does not fit this recommendation structure here.
- 成为高薪：得到一份高薪的工作，“高薪” describes a salary or a job with a high salary; it cannot be the role or identity that follows “成为”.
- 教学生很厉害：很会教学生，“厉害” after this verb phrase can suggest teaching very strictly or intensely. Use “很会” to express that he is skilled at teaching.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 问 -- “他一直问问题”：提出，emphasizes formulating and raising questions for discussion；不断提出疑问，makes the repeated questioning more vivid and specific.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子长大：他长大，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence: the previous sentence has already established that “他” refers to Meng子.
- Coherence (Temporality): 长大：长大了，Use “了” after a change-of-state verb to show that growing up has been completed before the next ability begins.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 孟子长大，他开始能解决问题：孟子长大以后，他开始能解决问题，Use “以后” to show the sequence: his ability to solve problems begins after he grows up.
- Coherence (Reference): 你：人们，“你” has not been introduced as a listener and suddenly shifts the narrative to the reader. Use the full noun “人们” for the general group receiving his advice.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 如果你懂政治，你会得到一份高薪的工作：如果你懂政治，就会得到一份高薪的工作，Use “如果……就……” to connect a condition with its result.
- Coherence (Reference): 后来孟子成为教授：后来他成为教授，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence because the preceding sentence is still about Meng子.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为教授：成为了教授，Use “了” to mark the completed change into the role of professor.
- Coherence (Reference): 他教学生：教学生，Use zero form for the same subject in the second clause of the same sentence rather than repeating “他”.
- Expressiveness: At the teaching moment, add a classroom detail, such as “学生们都认真地听他讲课”, to show why he is a strong teacher.
- Expressiveness: Add Meng子的 feeling after he can solve problems, such as “他觉得很有成就感”, to show his inner change.
- Expressiveness: You could include a short line of advice, such as “他说：‘学习要多想，多问。’”, to make his role as a professor more lively.

### Encouragement
You use clear time words such as “后来” to move the narrative forward. You also show Meng子的 development from asking questions to solving them, which gives this scene a clear focus.
