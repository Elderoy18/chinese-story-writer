## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子。他们住在墓地的旁边，所以孟子老是祭拜。孟子不是祭拜，就是跟朋友聊天。他很喜欢他的城市，但是城市有才两个学校，都很小。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 already refers to a historical time period, so adding 的时候 is redundant.
- 城市有才两个学校：城市里只有两所学校，use 只有 rather than 才有 to mean “there are only”; schools also take the measure word 所.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 小 -- “都很小”：规模很小，specifies that the schools are small in scale rather than merely physically small.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子不是祭拜，就是跟朋友聊天：他不是祭拜，就是跟朋友聊天，after 孟子 was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other male character competes as the referent, use a pronoun for close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Reference): 他的城市：自己的城市，when the possessor is the same person as the subject, use 自己的 to make the relationship clear.
- Expressiveness: Add a cemetery detail after “墓地的旁边” to create the setting more vividly, such as “墓地里常常有人哭，也有人烧纸。”
- Expressiveness: Show why Mengzi imitates the rituals by adding his thought or feeling near “孟子老是祭拜,” such as “孟子觉得这些动作很好玩。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of dialogue from the mother after Mengzi chats or worships, such as “妈妈说：‘你应该多读书，不要老是在这儿玩。’”

### Encouragement
- You introduced Mengzi and his mother clearly at the beginning, and your use of “不是……就是……” gives the description of Mengzi’s daily activities a natural rhythm.

## Scene 3

> 他难怪抱怨，他想他的朋友，因为他不要离开他的城市。他的妈妈找到孟子一个老师。跟老师孟子学最重要的文学，所以他成为哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他难怪抱怨：难怪他抱怨，难怪 introduces a conclusion based on a reason, so it comes before the subject and verb clause.
- 不要离开：不想离开，when the subject does not want to do something, use 不想 + verb phrase; 不要 is usually an instruction, prohibition, or command.
- 找到孟子一个老师：给孟子找了一个老师，use 给 + person + 找 + object to say that someone finds or arranges something for a person; 了 marks the completed action.
- 跟老师孟子学最重要的文学：孟子跟老师学最重要的文学，place the subject before the phrase beginning with 跟老师.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 想 -- “他想他的朋友”：想念，specifically expresses missing friends who are not nearby.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他的朋友：自己的朋友，when the possessor is the same person as the subject, use 自己的 to make that relationship clear.
- Coherence (Reference): 他的城市：自己的城市，use 自己的 because the city belongs to the same person who does not want to leave it.
- Coherence (Temporality): 所以他成为哲学家：所以他成为了哲学家，becoming something is a completed change of state in this narrative, so use 了 after the verb.
- Expressiveness: After the mother finds the teacher, add her feeling to show why this is important: “孟子的妈妈很高兴，觉得孟子终于可以好好学习了。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech from the teacher after the learning begins: “老师说：‘你要认真看书，多思考。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly show Mengzi’s reason for complaining by connecting it to his friends and his city. You also give the scene a strong forward direction by showing that his mother finds him a teacher.
