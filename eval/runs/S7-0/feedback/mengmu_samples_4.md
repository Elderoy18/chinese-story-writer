## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，孟子和孟母住了在靠墓地的家。孩子才五岁就每天在墓地祭拜了。在那个城市，孟母从早到晚都找工作，可是找不到，害得孟母没有钱。难怪她跟孩子搬家，原来买不了房。他们搬了到别一个城市找工作。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，this phrase is redundant because 古代 already expresses a time period, while 的时候 also means “the time when.”
- 住了在靠墓地的家：住在靠近墓地的家里，use 住在 + place for a living location; a continuing state does not take 了 here.
- 跟孩子搬家：带着孩子搬家，跟 means “together with” as equal participants, while 带着 shows that the mother takes her young child with her when moving.
- 搬了到别一个城市：搬到了另一个城市，put 了 after the directional/result complement in 搬到了; use 另一个, not 别一个, to mean “another one.”

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孩子才五岁：他才五岁，after 孟子 was named in the immediately previous sentence, use a pronoun for this close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Reference): 害得孟母没有钱：害得她没有钱，within the same sentence, the character has already been named, so use a pronoun for the close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 每天在墓地祭拜了：每天在墓地祭拜，remove 了 because 每天 describes a routine action, which takes the zero aspect particle.
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery sentence, add what Mengzi sees and feels, such as “孟子看到很多人哭着来祭拜，觉得很好玩。”
- Expressiveness: After the job-search sentence, show Mengmu’s emotion with a small action: “孟母叹了口气，心里很着急。”

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections

## Scene 3

> 孟子在那个商店也找了到工作，可是不是做衣服的工作，而是喜欢的工作。孟子学习馆学了十五年，他的学习和他的旅行经验很重要，对他很多好处。他是大人的时候，他成为很有名的哲人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 找了到：找到了，The result complement must follow the verb before the completion marker: use 找到 to mean “find,” and place 了 after it.
- 学习馆学了十五年：学习了十五年，馆 is a noun element referring to a building or venue and cannot be inserted after the verb 学习 in this sentence.
- 对他很多好处：对他有很多好处，The pattern is 对 + person + 有 + benefit; 有 is required to state that something brings benefits to someone.
- 他是大人的时候：他长大以后，Use 长大以后 to express the time after someone became an adult; 是大人 is not the natural expression in this time clause.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他的学习和他的旅行经验：自己的学习和旅行经验，When the possessor is the same person as the continuing subject, use 自己的 to make the relationship clear; repeating 他的 is unnecessary here.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他成为很有名的哲人：他成为了很有名的哲人，Use 了 after 成为 to mark the completed life achievement.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 可是不是做衣服的工作，而是喜欢的工作：不是做衣服的工作，而是喜欢的工作，Remove 可是 because this is not a contrast between two events; 不是……而是…… already connects the correction or contrast between the two kinds of work.

- Expressiveness: When he becomes well known, add an inner feeling such as “感到很自豪,” to make his achievement more personal.
