### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一天，很久很久以前：很久很久以前的一天，To describe “one day” in the distant past, the time phrase should modify the noun phrase; the original separates two time expressions unnaturally.
- 孟子不高兴，因为他说我们老是搬家，为什么？：孟子不高兴，问妈妈：“我们为什么老是搬家？”，A direct question uses the question word 为什么 before the verb phrase. Use 问 to introduce a question rather than 因为他说.
- 很多墓地得我祭拜：我得祭拜很多墓地，The modal verb 得 must come before the verb phrase it modifies. Here it expresses that the mother has to perform the worship action.

- Expressiveness: Before the mother explains her work, add a small action showing her mood, such as “妈妈叹了口气，说：‘从早到晚，我一直祭拜。’” This makes the dialogue more vivid.
- Expressiveness: At “很多不同的地方,” add one concrete detail, such as “有的墓地在山边，有的墓地在城外。” This gives the travel a clearer picture.

## Scene 2

> 孟子想，难怪我们常常搬家，原来我妈妈很忙。可是常常搬家害得孟子很难找到朋友。每次搬家孟子也觉得新的地方不是太热就是太冷，不是太大就是太小。在哪的地方，他都不高兴。孟子想帮助妈妈祭拜，可是他六岁，所以不能祭拜。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在哪的地方，他都不高兴：无论在哪个地方，他都不高兴，哪 cannot modify a noun with 的 in this pattern; use 哪个地方, and 无论……都…… expresses “no matter where.”

- 忙 -- “原来我妈妈很忙”：工作繁忙，specifies that her work keeps her occupied；忙得不可开交，vividly emphasizes that she has so much work to do.
- 不高兴 -- “他都不高兴”：不满意，more specifically shows that he is dissatisfied with each new place；觉得不自在，shows that he does not feel comfortable in the unfamiliar environment.

### Coherence & Expressiveness

- Expressiveness: At “他都不高兴,” add Mengzi’s inner feeling, such as “他觉得每个新地方都很陌生，也很孤单。”
- Expressiveness: After “不能祭拜,” add a short thought or line of speech, such as “孟子叹了口气，说：‘我什么时候才能帮妈妈呢？’”

### Encouragement
