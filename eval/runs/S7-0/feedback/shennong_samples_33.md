## Scene 1

> 古代时候，人不知道他们能吃什么植物，常常一吃就死。但是不知道为什么，没有办法。所以有一天，神农出去试一试很多种植物，告诉人什么植物是安全什么是危险。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代时候：在古代，a time noun such as “古代” should not be followed directly by “时候”; “在古代” is the natural time-setting structure.
- 人：人们，“人” is a generic noun and does not refer naturally to the concrete group in this scene; use “人们” for people in general.
- 他们：自己，when the possessor is the same group as the sentence subject, “自己” clearly refers back to that subject.
- 试一试很多种植物：尝了很多种植物，“尝” means to taste or eat a small amount in order to find out about food or plants, while “试” is too general here; use “了” because the tasting is a completed action in the narrative.
- 告诉人：告诉人们，“人” is too generic for the people receiving the information; “人们” refers to the group clearly.
- 什么植物是安全什么是危险：什么植物是安全的，什么植物是危险的，coordinated clauses need a comma, and the adjective phrases “安全的” and “危险的” modify the omitted noun “植物”.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 安全 -- “什么植物是安全的”：无毒的，specifies that the plants are safe because they contain no poison；可以吃的，states exactly that people are able to eat the plants.
- 危险 -- “什么植物是危险的”：有毒的，identifies poison as the specific danger in this plant-testing context；不能吃的，states the practical result for people directly.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：一个叫神农的男人，this is a first mention, so introduce the character with a full noun phrase rather than using only the name.
- Expressiveness: After the opening sentence, add a concrete result of the danger, such as “很多人吃了有毒的植物以后，肚子疼得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: When introducing the character, add a motivation or thought, such as “一个叫神农的男人想：我一定要找出能吃的植物。”
- Expressiveness: Make the plant-testing action more vivid with a setting detail, such as “他走到高山上，看见了各种各样的植物。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish the central problem first and then connect it to the character’s action with “所以”, which gives the scene a logical direction.
- Your use of “一吃就死” effectively shows the immediate danger that motivates the character.

## Scene 2

> 发现有的植物对身体非常好，特别是茶，让肚子感觉又暖和又清楚。他告诉朋友：“你吃哪种植物，这就要看你的口味。比方说，你爱吃甜的，可能喜欢糖。”他最喜欢吃花，除了夹竹桃以外，什么都花想吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 清楚：舒服，清楚 describes whether information or thinking is clear; it cannot describe a physical feeling in the stomach. Use 舒服 for a comfortable bodily sensation.
- 这就要看你的口味：这就要看你的口味怎么样了，this pattern requires an uncertain question before it and a question form after 看. The final 了 belongs to the fixed structure.
- 什么都花想吃：什么花都想吃，什么 must modify the noun 花, and 都 follows the noun phrase in the “every kind of…” pattern.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好 -- “有的植物对身体非常好”：有益，more specifically means beneficial to health in this context；有好处，clearly states that the plant brings a health benefit.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 朋友：一个朋友，this is the first mention of this character, so use a full noun phrase to introduce the friend clearly.
- Expressiveness: After mentioning tea, add a concrete effect to show why it is beneficial, such as “神农喝了茶，觉得身体暖暖的，很有精神。”
- Expressiveness: At the explanation about food preferences, add a short reaction from the friend, such as “朋友点点头，说：‘原来每个人喜欢吃的不一样。’”
- Expressiveness: When describing the flowers, add a visual detail and the character’s interest, such as “那些花颜色鲜艳，神农看着很想尝一尝。”

### Encouragement
- You used a clear quoted-speech section to show what the character says, and the contrast with 夹竹桃 shows that you remember it is dangerous.

## Scene 3

> 他发现这个口味，一百岁时候，不小心吃了毒的花。一吃就开始死。但是生病时候告诉人什么植物是药，什么植物是毒。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一百岁时候：一百岁的时候，A multi-syllable time expression before “时候” needs the structural particle “的”.
- 毒的花：有毒的花，“毒” is a noun; use “有毒的” as the adjective phrase meaning “poisonous”.
- 人：人们，“人” is a generic noun and does not naturally refer to a concrete group of people in this context.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不小心 -- “不小心吃了有毒的花”：误食，specifically describes accidentally eating something that should not be eaten.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 这个口味：一种花，This is the first mention of the object connected to the poisonous flower, so use a clear full noun phrase rather than an ungrounded “this taste”.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉人们什么植物是药，什么植物有毒：告诉了人们什么植物是药，什么植物有毒，This is a completed narrative action, so “了” marks completion; the zero particle is mainly used when a saying verb introduces direct quoted speech.
- Expressiveness: After the accidental eating, add a physical reaction to show the danger, such as “他突然肚子疼了起来，脸色也变白了。”
- Expressiveness: Add his worried thought after “一吃就开始死”, such as “他心想：我会不会死？”
- Expressiveness: Describe the flower before he eats it, such as “那朵花又黄又大，像一颗星星。”

### Encouragement
- You used the “一……就……” pattern correctly to show that the consequence happened immediately after eating. You also clearly tried to contrast plants that can be medicine with plants that are poisonous.
