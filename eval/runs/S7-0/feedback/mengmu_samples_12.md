## Scene 1

> 古代，孟子跟孟母住在墓地的旁边。可是孟母注意，住在墓地的旁边影响孟子。他常常跟朋友们祭拜玩，所以孟母决定他们会搬家。这次，他们住在城市里的商店的旁边，从早到晚很吵。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 注意：注意到，the result complement 到 is needed here because the mother has become aware of the following fact; 注意 by itself usually means “pay attention” or “be careful.”
- 跟朋友们祭拜玩：跟朋友们一起学别人祭拜，祭拜 and 玩 cannot be placed together as consecutive verbs in this way. 学着/学别人 + verb expresses copying an action for fun.
- 决定他们会搬家：决定带着孟子搬家，决定 should be followed directly by the action the subject decides to do. 孟母 is deciding her own action, so 她 does not need to repeat 他们, and 会 is not used for a decided plan.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 常常 -- “他常常跟朋友们一起学别人祭拜”：老是，emphasizes that the behaviour happens repeatedly and is troublesome in this context.
- 吵 -- “从早到晚很吵”：嘈杂，describes a place as noisily busy, with many mixed sounds.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 可是：后来，the two ideas do not form a contrast: living near the cemetery leads to the mother noticing its effect. 后来 gives the sequence relation.
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery setting, add a sensory detail about the visitors, such as “每天都有人来墓地哭。”
- Expressiveness: Show Mengzi’s feelings or playful behaviour more clearly after he copies the ritual, such as “孟子觉得很好玩，还笑着学他们祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: Add Mengmu’s worried thought before her decision, such as “孟母心想：‘住在这儿会影响孩子。’”

### Encouragement
- You introduced both 孟子 and 孟母 clearly at the start, and the pronoun 他 clearly refers to 孟子 in the next sentence.
- Your scene also has a clear setting change from the cemetery to the city, and “从早到晚很吵” gives the new place a strong atmosphere.

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections

### Vocabulary Suggestions

### Coherence & Expressiveness

- Coherence (Temporality): 成为：成为了，Use 了 after the verb to show that becoming a philosopher was completed later in the narrative.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 将来：后来，Use 后来 to connect a later event in a past narrative; 将来 refers to the future.
