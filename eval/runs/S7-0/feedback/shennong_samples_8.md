## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个人叫神农。一天，神农正在路上走，一看很多人正在吃一些植物。一些人一吃植物就生病了，神农想帮别人。所以想知道哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 already refers to a time period, so adding 的时候 is redundant.
- 一看很多人正在吃一些植物：看到很多人正在吃一些植物，看到 means “to see,” while 看 means “to look”; here Shennong sees the people eating.
- 神农想帮别人：神农想帮人们，别人 distinguishes people from the speaker or another person, but this sentence refers to the group of people already mentioned.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the people become ill, add a clearer cause, such as “很多人因为吃了有毒的植物，常常生病。” This explains why they get sick and makes the danger more vivid.
- Expressiveness: At the point where Shennong decides to help, add his inner thought or quoted speech, such as “神农想：我要找出哪些植物可以吃。” This shows his determination.

### Encouragement
You clearly introduce Shennong before continuing with his actions, and you use 正在 well to show actions that are happening at that moment. The pattern 一……就…… is also used correctly to show that people become ill immediately after eating plants.

## Scene 2

> 然后，神农开始尝很多植物，比方说他吃过生姜、芥兰和菠菜。一些植物吃真好，特别是黄瓜。但是，一些植物毒得很，特别是夹竹桃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一些植物吃真好：一些植物很好吃，Use the adjective phrase with the plant as the subject to describe its taste; “吃真好” is not a natural predicate here.
- 一些植物毒得很：一些植物有毒，The word “毒” cannot be modified by “得很” to describe a plant. Use “有毒” for plants that contain poison.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农开始尝很多植物：他开始尝很多植物，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence: the previous scene has just named 神农, and no other male character competes as the referent.
- Expressiveness: After listing the edible plants, add a sensory detail to make the tasting more vivid, such as: “生姜辣辣的，黄瓜吃起来很清爽。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where he tastes the dangerous plant, add his reaction or inner thought, such as: “神农吃了一口，心想：这种花的味道真奇怪。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly contrast plants that are good to eat with plants that are dangerous, and your examples make the scene easy to follow.

## Scene 3

> 一个人对神农说，麻烦我们可以吃什么植物？神农说，这就要看你想不想生病了。以后，神农告诉别人一些植物能吃，一些植物不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 麻烦我们可以吃什么植物？：请问，我们可以吃什么植物？，麻烦 cannot introduce a question by itself; use 请问 as a polite question opener.
- 这就要看你想不想生病了：这就要看是什么植物了，this structure should answer the uncertain question by putting a wh-question or an A-not-A question after 看. Whether a plant can be eaten depends on what plant it is, not on whether the listener wants to become ill.
- 以后，神农告诉别人一些植物能吃：后来，神农告诉人们一些植物能吃，use 后来 to begin a sentence meaning “afterwards.” 别人 distinguishes other people from a stated person, while 人们 refers to people in general here.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 说 -- “一个人对神农说”：问，specifically shows that the person is asking a question rather than making a general statement.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: At the person’s question, add a worried feeling or a short reason for asking, such as “请问，什么植物能吃？我不想生病。”
- Expressiveness: When 神农 explains, add a small action and a specific contrast, such as “神农指着植物说：‘这种能吃，那种有毒，不能吃。’”

### Encouragement
- You used a clear question-and-answer exchange, and you correctly contrasted plants that can be eaten with plants that cannot.
