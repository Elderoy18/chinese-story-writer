## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男人觉得植物很有意思。他叫神农。有的植物吃了以后他的肚子疼死了，可是有的吃了以后很舒服。他找很多植物。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，the phrase repeats the time meaning: 古代 already names a period, so 的时候 is redundant.
- 他找很多植物：他找到了很多植物，找 means “to look for,” while 找到 means “to find”; 到 shows that the search reached a result, and 了 marks the completed narrative action.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一个男人觉得植物很有意思。他叫神农。：一个叫神农的男人觉得植物很有意思。，On a character’s first mention, introduce the name together with the noun phrase, so the reader knows immediately who the new character is.
- Expressiveness: After the first sentence, add Shennong’s question to show his curiosity: “哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃呢？”
- Expressiveness: Make the painful reaction more vivid at the point where he eats some plants: “有的植物吃了以后，他肚子疼得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Before the final sentence, add his purpose for searching: “神农决定找出哪些植物能吃，哪些植物有毒。”

### Encouragement
- You created a clear contrast with “有的……可是有的……”, which helps the reader understand that different plants have different effects. The phrase “吃了以后” also clearly connects eating the plants with the results.

## Scene 2

> 凉山很大的山，这也找植物。一天，他找到黄色的花。他听说你跟黄色的花做茶很有用。比方说，你的头发疼死，这冰茶是药。他一喝茶就很不舒服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 凉山很大的山：凉山是一座很大的山，A sentence that identifies what something is needs the copula `是`; `一座` is the matching measure word for a mountain.
- 这也找植物：他也在这儿找植物，Use `在 + place + verb` to show where an action happens; `这儿` means “here.”
- 找到黄色的花：找到了一朵黄色的花，Use `了` after the completed finding action, and use the measure word `朵` for one flower.
- 跟黄色的花做茶：用黄色的花做茶，`用` introduces the material used to make something; `跟` means “with” and cannot show that the flowers are the ingredient.
- 头发疼死：头疼得很厉害，`头发` means hair, which cannot feel pain; a headache is `头疼`. Use `得 + degree complement` to describe how painful it is.
- 这冰茶：这杯冰茶，A demonstrative such as `这` needs a measure word before a countable noun; `杯` is the appropriate measure word for a drink.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 你：他，The narration is about `他`, so the second-person pronoun `你` has no clear referent. Repeat the established character with `他`.
- Coherence (Temporality): 很不舒服：很不舒服了，Use `了` after the adjective to mark the change of state: he became uncomfortable after drinking the tea.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他一喝茶就很不舒服：可是，他一喝茶就很不舒服，`可是` marks contrast: he had heard that the tea was useful, but it made him feel ill.
- Expressiveness: When the yellow flower appears, add a visual detail, such as “黄色的花像星星一样，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: After he drinks the tea, describe the physical feeling more vividly, such as “他的肚子疼了起来，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Add his worried thought at that moment, such as “他心想：我是不是吃错东西了？”

### Encouragement
- You used `一天` to move the story to a new event, and your `一喝茶就……` structure clearly shows that one action quickly led to another.

## Scene 3

> 糟糕，这黄色的花是夹竹桃，特别是毒。正好在这儿，灵芝药的夹竹桃他吃完了，也写一个课文的植物。谢谢神农。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 这黄色的花：这朵黄色的花，A demonstrative noun phrase needs a measure word between 这 and the noun.
- 特别是毒：特别有毒，毒 is a noun; use 有毒 as the adjective for “poisonous,” which can be modified by 特别.
- 灵芝药的夹竹桃：灵芝和夹竹桃，Use 和 to coordinate two plant names. 的 introduces an attributive modifier and cannot connect two items in a list.
- 一个课文的植物：一篇关于这些植物的课文，课文 takes the measure word 篇, and the topic should be introduced with 关于 before 课文.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 也写一个课文的植物：也写了一篇关于这些植物的课文，The writing is presented as a completed action in the past, so the 了 perfective marker is needed.
- Expressiveness: After the warning about the flower, add a visual detail to make it easier to imagine, for example: “黄色的花像星星一样，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where he eats the plant, add his physical reaction or thought, for example: “他刚吃下去，肚子就疼了起来。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech after the discovery, for example: “神农说：‘这种花有毒，不能吃！’”

### Encouragement
