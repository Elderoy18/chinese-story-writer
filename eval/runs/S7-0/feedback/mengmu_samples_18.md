## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个男孩名字叫孟子。孟子和孟子的妈妈两个人住在很穷的农村。他们住的地方旁边是个墓地，墓地老是有很多人祭拜。因为人们在祭拜，他们一直在哭。孟子和孟子的妈妈住在这里才一个星期就觉得受不了了，而且害得孟子的学习。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 already expresses a time period, so adding 的时候 is redundant.
- 而且害得孟子的学习：这里的环境还影响了孟子的学习，害得 must be followed by a person and a harmful result clause; it cannot directly take a possessive noun phrase such as 孟子的学习.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子的妈妈：孟子和他的妈妈，when the same person is mentioned again within the same noun phrase, use a pronoun rather than repeating the name.
- Coherence (Temporality): 住在这里才一个星期：才住了一个星期，use 了 after 住 to mark the completed duration before the later reaction.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为人们在祭拜，他们一直在哭：因为人们在祭拜，所以他们一直在哭，use the paired connector 因为……所以…… to show the intended cause-and-effect relation.
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery is introduced, add a concrete sound detail to make the setting vivid, such as “墓地里常常传来哭声”.
- Expressiveness: Show how the boy reacts to the scene, such as “孟子觉得很害怕，晚上也睡不好”.
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought or line of speech from the mother near the final sentence, such as “孟子的妈妈想：‘这里太影响孩子学习了。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduce the cemetery setting and connect the mourners’ actions to why the family feels uncomfortable. Your use of ongoing actions in “在祭拜” and “一直在哭” helps the scene feel active.

## Scene 2

> 孟子的妈妈觉得孟子取得良好的教育是很重要的，所以孟子的妈妈认为这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好，就打算跟孟子搬家。孟子的妈妈带孟子不是搬到城市，而是搬到了孟子老师的家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 取得良好的教育：接受良好的教育，The verb 取得 does not normally collocate with 教育; 接受 means to receive education.
- 跟孟子搬家：带着孟子搬家，When a mother moves together with her child, 带着 expresses that she takes the child with her; 跟孟子搬家 suggests that she and 孟子 are equal participants in the move.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 地方 -- “这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好”：环境，This identifies the place more precisely as the child’s learning and living setting.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子的妈妈认为：她认为，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence: the mother was already the subject earlier in the same sentence, and 她 is clear here.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子的妈妈带孟子：她带孟子，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence: the mother was the clear subject in the preceding sentence.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 不是搬到城市，而是搬到了：搬到了，The 不是……而是…… connector should refute a stated or conventional mistaken idea before giving the correction. Here there is no earlier expectation that the move would be to the city, so a direct statement of the destination is clearer.
- Expressiveness: After the mother decides to move, add an inner thought to show her concern, such as: “她想：‘我一定要给孟子找一个更适合学习的地方。’”
- Expressiveness: At the new destination, add one concrete detail about the learning environment, such as: “老师家里有很多书，周围也很安静。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the mother’s reason for moving, using a logical connection between the child’s education and her decision. You also correctly use 搬到了 to show that the move was completed.

## Scene 3

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 成绩很快就进步了很多：成绩很快就提高了很多，use “提高” for an improvement in grades; “进步” normally describes a person’s learning or ability improving.
- 以后孟子成为了中国有名的哲学家：后来孟子成为了中国有名的哲学家，“以后” cannot begin a narrative sentence to mean “afterwards”; use “后来” for a later event in a past story.
