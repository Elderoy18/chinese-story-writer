## Scene 1

> 有一天孟子去了墓地玩儿，但是他妈妈觉得这是不合适的地方让孩子玩，所以她决定了他们会搬家找更好的地方。她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福，而且住在这儿教育不太好。因为她望子成龙，所以她会一直搬家才能找到质量好的教育。然后他们搬到了离城市更近的地方。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 这是不合适的地方让孩子玩：这不是适合孩子玩儿的地方，Use 适合 + person/activity to say that a place suits someone or an activity; this structure clearly states that the place is unsuitable for the child’s play.
- 他们会搬家找更好的地方：搬到更好的地方，After 决定, use a verb phrase directly. 搬到 means “move to” and must be followed by the destination.
- 她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福：她担心儿子的童年不会幸福，恐怕 means “I am afraid that” and cannot describe another person’s fear with 她 as its subject; 担心 correctly expresses the mother’s worry.
- 住在这儿教育不太好：这儿的教育不太好，Use 的 to link the place to the education system located there.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 质量好的教育 -- “才能找到质量好的教育”：优质的教育，this is a more concise and natural phrase for education of high quality.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 有一天孟子去了墓地玩儿：有一天，有一个小男孩叫孟子，他去了墓地玩儿，On first mention, introduce a character with a name and a brief noun phrase so the reader knows who the character is.
- Coherence (Temporality): 决定了：决定，决定 is a mental-state verb and takes the zero particle; do not add 了 when stating a decision.
- Expressiveness: At the cemetery, add one concrete detail about what the child sees, such as “每天有人来墓地祭拜，还有人哭。”
- Expressiveness: When the mother worries, add her inner thought or quoted speech, such as “如果一直住在这儿，孟子可能学到不好的东西。”
- Expressiveness: Show the mother’s emotion before the move with a phrase such as “孟母很着急，觉得不能再住下去了。”

### Encouragement
- You use cause-and-effect connectors well in “因为……所以……”, and the completed action in “搬到了” is used correctly. Your scene also clearly shows the mother’s concern for her child.

## Scene 2

> 他们住才一月就又搬家。古代的时候，一般人不喜欢卖东西的人。但是孟子老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿，害得妈妈着急得很，因为她恐怕儿子会成为跟他们很像。她告诉了孟子：他们不是朋友，而是不好的人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他们住才一月就又搬家：他们才住了一个月，就又搬家了，Use the pattern 才 + duration + verb + 了，…就 + completed action + 了; the duration expression is 一个月.
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 already expresses a time period, so adding 的时候 is redundant.
- 因为她恐怕儿子会成为跟他们很像：因为她担心儿子会变得跟他们很像，恐怕 cannot take another subject before it in this meaning; use 担心 for a mother being worried. Also, 成为 takes a noun phrase, while 变得 is used before an adjective phrase such as 跟他们很像.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 卖东西的人 -- “这些卖东西的人”：商人，identifies their occupation more specifically as people who conduct business.
- 不好的人 -- “而是不好的人”：品行不好的人，specifies that the problem is their character or behaviour.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 她告诉了孟子：他们不是朋友，而是不好的人：她告诉孟子：他们不是朋友，而是不好的人，Use the zero particle with a speaking verb that introduces quoted speech; the focus is on the words that follow, not on completion of the telling.
- Expressiveness: At the point where the merchants appear, add one concrete detail about the busy surroundings, such as “街上有很多人在买东西，叫卖声很大。”
- Expressiveness: Show what Mengzi does while playing with the merchants, such as “孟子学着他们收钱，还跟别人讨价还价。”
- Expressiveness: Give the mother a short inner thought before she speaks, such as “孟母心想：‘他不能每天都学这些。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly connect Mengzi’s behaviour with his mother’s worry by using the result pattern “害得……”. The final quoted speech also makes the mother’s reaction easy for the reader to understand.

## Scene 3

> 你不是当数学家，而是当哲学家，不能当卖东西的人。所以他们又搬家。在他们第三个家，孟子学了怎么祭拜。妈妈感觉这个地方适合他们，告诉了孟子：我们找到了最后的家。在这儿妈妈让儿子从早到晚学习，他难怪成为有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在他们第三个家：在他们的第三个家里，A possessive modifier needs the particle 的, and 家里 makes the location clearer after 在.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 卖东西的人 -- “不能当卖东西的人”：商人，names the occupation more precisely.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们又搬家：他们又搬家了，Use 了 after 搬家 to show that this narrative action was completed.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉了孟子：告诉孟子，A speaking verb that introduces direct speech takes the zero particle rather than 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为有名的哲学家：成为了有名的哲学家，Use 了 to mark the completed change of state.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他难怪成为有名的哲学家：所以他后来成为有名的哲学家，Use 所以 to connect the cause—his mother made him study all day—with the result; 后来 places his achievement later in the narrative.
- Expressiveness: At the point where the mother judges the new home, add her pleased inner thought or speech, such as “妈妈高兴地说：‘这儿的环境很适合孩子。’”
- Expressiveness: When describing his study, add a small visual detail to show his effort, such as “孟子每天坐在桌前，认真地看书。”

### Encouragement
You used the contrast pattern “不是……而是……” effectively to show what the mother wants for her son. The time phrase “从早到晚” also gives the study routine a clear sense of effort.
