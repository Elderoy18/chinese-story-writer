## Scene 2

> 所以，他上山下山找植物，吃了很多样子的植物。他是第一人，找到了茶。他把所有的植物写下来了。他一直都吃了，他连毒都能吃。可是他还没吃了一个叫夹竹桃的植物。他找到了那个像星星的植物，觉得很漂亮，马上就吃下了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很多样子的植物：很多种植物，Use the measure word “种” to count different types of plants; “样子” describes appearance rather than kinds.
- 他是第一人，找到了茶：他是第一个找到茶的人，An ordinal before a person normally needs the measure word “个,” and this structure clearly states what he was first to do.
- 像星星的植物：像星星一样的植物，Use “像……一样” to make a comparison of shape or appearance.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 一直都吃了：一直都吃植物，With a continuing or habitual action marked by “一直,” use the zero aspect form rather than “了.”
- Coherence (Temporality): 还没吃了：还没吃，After “没,” do not use the completion marker “了”; “没吃” already means that the action has not happened.
- Expressiveness: When he finds tea, add its usefulness to make the discovery more meaningful, such as “他发现茶不但好喝，而且对身体有好处。”
- Expressiveness: At the moment he sees the flower, describe its colour and shape more vividly, such as “那儿开着黄色的花，像星星一样，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: Add a brief thought before he eats the flower to show his character, such as “他想：这种植物这么漂亮，一定很好吃。”

### Encouragement
- You used a clear sequence of actions, from searching for plants to finding and eating one. You also used “可是” effectively to show that the new plant was different from the ones he had tried before.

## Scene 3

> 可是夹竹桃的毒很重，让他肚子很痛。他在地上躺着，觉得他快会死了。可是正好有别人找到他了，给了他一个像伞的植物，叫灵芝。他吃灵芝以后就觉得很好，不再生病了。他测量起来了，像星星的植物是毒，像伞的植物是药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 像星星的植物是毒，像伞的植物是药：像星星一样的植物有毒，像伞一样的植物是药，Use the comparison pattern 像……一样的 to describe the plants’ shapes. For a poisonous plant, use 有毒; 是毒 is not a natural predicate.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很好 -- “他吃灵芝以后就觉得很好”：好多了，emphasizes that his condition improved after eating the medicine.
- 毒很重 -- “夹竹桃的毒很重”：毒性很强，states more precisely that the plant’s poisonous effect is strong.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 觉得他快会死了：觉得快会死了，Follow the zero-form rule: when the subject remains the same within the sentence, do not repeat the pronoun.
- Coherence (Reference): 有别人找到他了：有一个人找到他了，别人 distinguishes other people from the speaker, so it does not fit this narrator’s introduction of a new character. Use a full noun phrase for a first mention.
- Expressiveness: After “让他肚子很痛,” add a stronger physical reaction, such as “他难受得不得了，脸色也变白了。”
- Expressiveness: Add Shennong’s worried thought while he is lying down, such as “他想：我会不会死在这里？”
- Expressiveness: When the helper appears, describe one clear detail, such as “这时，一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人走了过来。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the cause-and-effect sequence: eating the poisonous plant leads to stomach pain, and eating the medicine helps him feel better. You also use “躺着” well to show Shennong’s ongoing condition.
