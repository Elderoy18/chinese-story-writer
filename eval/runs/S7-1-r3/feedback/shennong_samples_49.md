## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有很多人不知道什么植物能吃，什么植物不能吃。有很多人吃植物以后就吃坏肚子了，因为他们吃了毒。那个时候，有一个老人叫神农，他什么植物都觉得好吃，不怕生病，不怕毒。他希望别人也可以吃好吃的植物。可是他知道，如果他们还怕吃毒，不会吃他喜欢的植物。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 already expresses a time period, so adding 的时候 is redundant.
- 毒：有毒的植物，毒 alone refers to poison or toxicity; here the people are eating plants, so the phrase should specify that the plants are toxic.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好吃 -- “他什么植物都觉得好吃”：味道不错，gives a more specific evaluation of the plants’ taste without making it overly strong；可口，describes food or edible plants as pleasant to eat.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃植物以后：吃了植物以后，use 了 for the completed action that happens before the later result of getting an upset stomach.
- Expressiveness: After the people become ill, add a physical detail to show how uncomfortable they are: “有的人肚子疼得直不起腰。”
- Expressiveness: When introducing the plants, add a contrast between their appearance and danger: “有些植物看起来很漂亮，却会让人吃坏肚子。”
- Expressiveness: After 神农 says he is not afraid, add a short thought or quotation that shows his determination: “神农心想：‘我先试一试。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduce the main problem—people do not know which plants are safe—and then introduce 神农 and his different attitude toward plants.
- The contrast with 可是 at the end effectively shows why other people may still hesitate to eat the plants.

## Scene 2

> 所以，他上山下山找植物，吃了很多样子的植物。他是第一人，找到了茶。他把所有的植物写下来了。他一直都吃了，他连毒都能吃。可是他还没吃了一个叫夹竹桃的植物。他找到了那个像星星的植物，觉得很漂亮，马上就吃下了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很多样子的植物：很多种植物，样子 describes an object’s appearance; use 种 to count different kinds of plants.
- 他是第一人，找到了茶：他是第一个找到茶的人，use the pattern 第一个 + verb phrase + 的 + 人 to identify the first person who completed an action.
- 一个叫夹竹桃的植物：一种叫夹竹桃的植物，use 种 for a named type or species of plant rather than the general measure word 个.
- 像星星的植物：像星星一样的植物，comparisons of this kind normally use 像……一样 to state what the plant resembles.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 找 -- “他上山下山找植物”：搜寻，emphasizes carefully looking over a wide area, which fits travelling up and down mountains.
- 漂亮 -- “觉得很漂亮”：艳丽，suggests a striking, vividly beautiful appearance and suits the flower-like plant in this scene.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 他一直都吃了：他一直都在吃，an action described with 一直 is ongoing or durative, so use 在 rather than completed-action 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 还没吃了：还没吃过，negative completed actions use 没 rather than 没……了; 过 marks that he has not had this experience before.
- Expressiveness: When he sees the plant, add visual detail to make the setting clearer: “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: After he eats it, add a short thought to show his confidence: “他心想：这么漂亮的植物，一定可以吃。”
- Expressiveness: Make his notes more concrete by naming what he records: “他把植物的大小、颜色和有没有毒都写下来。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the sequence of Shennong’s actions, from searching in the mountains to finding tea and then seeing the new plant.
- Your use of action words such as 上山下山 and 吃下了 helps the scene move quickly.

## Scene 3

> 可是夹竹桃的毒很重，让他肚子很痛。他在地上躺着，觉得他快会死了。可是正好有别人找到他了，给了他一个像伞的植物，叫灵芝。他吃灵芝以后就觉得很好，不再生病了。他测量起来了，像星星的植物是毒，像伞的植物是药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 快会死了：快要死了，Use the pattern 快要 + verb phrase to express that an event will happen very soon.
- 别人：一个人，别人 means “other people” in contrast with the speaker or a named group; introduce this new helper as 一个人人.
- 一个像伞的植物：一种像伞一样的植物，Use 种 for a type of plant, and use 像……一样的 to make the comparison complete.
- 是毒：有毒，Use 有毒 to describe a poisonous plant; 毒 is a noun and cannot directly follow 是 in this meaning.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 毒很重 -- “夹竹桃的毒很重”：毒性很强，specifies that the plant’s poisonous effect is strong.
- 很痛 -- “让他肚子很痛”：疼得厉害，uses a degree complement to make the physical pain more vivid.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他 -- 自己，Use 自己 when the person who is thinking and the person who may die are the same; this makes the self-reference clear.
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃灵芝以后：吃了灵芝以后，Use 了 because eating the medicine is a completed action before the following result.
- Expressiveness: At the moment of pain, add a stronger bodily detail, such as “肚子疼得不得了”.
- Expressiveness: Add the character’s frightened thought after “快要死了”, such as “我会死吗？”.
- Expressiveness: Describe the helper more clearly when he first appears, such as “一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人正好走过来”.

### Encouragement
You use 着 effectively in 躺着 to show an ongoing state. The scene also has a clear contrast between Shennong’s pain and the helper’s arrival.
