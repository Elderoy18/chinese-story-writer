## Scene 1

> 神农和他的家人住在古代，所以不知道什么植物能吃。因为他的家人常常生病了，所以神农和他的哥哥一起去旅行找可以吃的植物。他们去很多不一样的地方，比方说高山、山谷和沙漠。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 住在古代：生活在古代，住 is used for residing in a physical place, while 生活在 correctly locates someone’s life in a historical era.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 找 -- “一起去旅行找可以吃的植物”：寻找，emphasizes a deliberate search for edible plants；探寻，highlights exploration of plants whose properties are still unknown.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：一个叫神农的人，under the first-mention rule, introduce a new character with both the name and a clear noun phrase identifying the character.
- Coherence (Temporality): 常常生病了：常常生病，use the zero particle for a habitual situation; 常常 shows that the illness happens repeatedly, so 了 is not used.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们去很多不一样的地方：他们去了很多不一样的地方，use 了 for the completed action of visiting several places in the past.
- Expressiveness: After explaining that the family is often ill, add Shennong’s feeling to show his motivation: “神农看见家人常常生病，心里很着急。”
- Expressiveness: At the moment they set out, add a short line of speech that shows their purpose: “神农说：‘我们要找出可以吃的植物。’”
- Expressiveness: When listing the places, add one sensory detail to make the journey vivid: “在沙漠里，风吹得他们满脸都是沙。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect the family’s repeated illness with Shennong’s decision to search for plants. The three concrete landforms also make the journey feel broad and adventurous.

## Scene 2

> 他们在很漂亮的河找夹竹桃。神农问他的哥哥：“我应该吃这种植物吗？”哥哥说：“这就要看你想要安全还是接近知道了。“神农一吃夹竹桃就生病了。哥哥说：“太麻烦了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在很漂亮的河找夹竹桃：在一条很漂亮的河边找夹竹桃，A location after 在 should be a complete place phrase; 河边 means the area beside the river, where the characters can search.
- 这就要看你想要安全还是接近知道了：这就要看你想要安全，还是想知道这种植物能不能吃了，The pattern 这就要看……了 must be followed by a question or an A-or-B choice. 接近知道 is not a natural verb phrase, and 知道 as a mental-state verb does not take 了 here.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 麻烦 -- “太麻烦了”：糟糕，more precisely expresses the brother’s negative reaction to Shennong suddenly becoming ill.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After “神农一吃夹竹桃就生病了,” add a physical symptom to show how ill he feels, such as “他的肚子马上疼了起来。”
- Expressiveness: Make the brother’s reaction more vivid with an action or feeling before his speech, such as “哥哥很着急，说：‘太糟糕了！’”

### Encouragement
- You used the pattern “一……就……” well to show that Shennong became ill immediately after eating the plant. You also included dialogue, which helps the scene feel active.

## Scene 3

> 夹竹桃是毒的，我想知道灵芝会帮他。”哥哥给神农灵芝。神农吃完了以后开始痊愈了。神农说：“谢谢哥哥，你帮得真好。灵芝我们可以吃，特别是如果我们已经吃了夹竹桃。“

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 夹竹桃是毒的：夹竹桃有毒，毒 is a noun; use 有毒 to describe a plant as poisonous.
- 夹竹桃。“：夹竹桃。”，A quotation must end with the closing quotation mark ”, not the opening mark “.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 灵芝会帮他：灵芝会不会帮神农，Both the older brother and Shennong are recent male characters, so 他 has an unclear referent; repeat the name. 会不会 also matches the uncertainty introduced by 想知道.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农说：他说，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence: the previous sentence already names Shennong, and he is the clear speaker.
- Coherence (Temporality): 哥哥给神农灵芝：哥哥给了神农灵芝，Use 了 for the completed action of giving the medicine.
- Coherence (Temporality): 开始痊愈了：开始好起来了，痊愈 describes a fully completed recovery, so it does not naturally follow 开始. 好起来了 expresses that the improvement has begun, with 了 marking the change of state.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 灵芝我们可以吃，特别是如果我们已经吃了夹竹桃：如果我们已经吃了夹竹桃，灵芝就可以吃，特别是 does not introduce a condition here. Use 如果……就…… for the conditional relationship.
- Expressiveness: After the recovery sentence, add a physical detail to show the change: 神农慢慢坐起来，觉得肚子不那么疼了。
- Expressiveness: Make the thanks more emotional by showing why Shennong is grateful: 神农说：“哥哥，多亏你给我灵芝，我现在好多了！”

### Encouragement
- You use direct speech effectively to let the characters explain the medicine, and the recovery event follows naturally after the brother gives Shennong the lingzhi.
