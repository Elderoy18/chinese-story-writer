## Scene 1

> 孟子小的时候，从早到晚都跟朋友玩。孟子和他的朋友喜欢玩在墓地里，装祭拜。孟子五岁的时候，跟妈妈搬家。他们搬家不是因为孟子玩得太多，而是因为妈妈认为他们的家离墓地太近。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 喜欢玩在墓地里：喜欢在墓地里玩，A location phrase such as “在墓地里” normally comes before the main verb “玩”.
- 装祭拜：假装祭拜，“装” cannot directly take an action verb in this meaning; “假装 + verb phrase” means to pretend to perform that action.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 玩 -- “从早到晚都跟朋友玩”：嬉戏，specifies carefree, childlike play and makes the action more vivid.
- 家 -- “他们的家离墓地太近”：住处，specifies the place where the family lives, which fits a sentence about moving house.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子小的时候：有一个小男孩叫孟子。孟子小的时候，The first mention rule requires introducing a new main character with a full noun phrase that explains who he is.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和他的朋友：他和朋友，Under the close re-occurrence pronoun rule, use “他” after “孟子” has just been named and no other male character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Temporality): 跟妈妈搬家：跟妈妈搬了家，Use “了” for the completed, one-time moving action in this past narrative event.
- Expressiveness: After the children’s play at the cemetery, add a concrete action to show what “祭拜” looks like, such as “他和朋友学着大人的样子，低着头祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: Add Mengzi’s feeling about the game at “从早到晚都跟朋友玩,” such as “孟子觉得这样很好玩，常常笑个不停。”
- Expressiveness: At the mother’s reason for moving, add a short thought or speech line, such as “妈妈心想：‘住在这儿，孩子会一直只想玩。’”

### Encouragement
- You used “从早到晚” effectively to show how often Mengzi played, and your “不是……而是……” structure clearly explains the real reason for the move.

## Scene 2

> 妈妈认为，住离墓地太近，害得孟子的心气。孟子和孟母搬到子思老师的家，跟子思老师住。孟母帮子思打扫房间和做饭。子思帮孟母教她的儿子。因为孟子现在没有朋友，他老是读书。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 住离墓地太近：住得离墓地太近，Use 得 after a verb when the following phrase describes the degree or condition of the action.
- 害得孟子的心气：害得孟子没有心思学习，The pattern 害得 needs an affected person followed by a harmful result clause; a bare noun phrase does not complete this structure.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 教 -- “子思帮孟母教她的儿子”：辅导，specifies that the teacher is helping the child with his learning or schoolwork.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬到：搬到了，Use 了 after the resultative complement in 搬到 to show that the move was completed.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为孟子现在没有朋友，：因为孟子现在没有朋友，所以，Use 因为……所以…… to connect the cause and its result clearly.
- Expressiveness: At the mother’s concern about the cemetery, add her worried expression or inner thought, such as “孟母皱着眉头，心里很担心。”
- Expressiveness: When the teacher helps Mengzi, add a short line of speech to show his guidance, such as “孟子，你要认真读书。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show how the setting changes and how different adults help Mengzi. You also use a cause-and-effect idea to explain why he reads more.

## Scene 3

> 子思认为，对孩子来说，读书最重要，特别是学哲学。孟子不是练习写文章，就是看哲学书。孟子从五岁一直学哲学。他才五岁就学哲学，难怪他成为很有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 成为很有名的哲学家：成为了很有名的哲学家，Use 了 after 成为 to mark the completed change of state: he eventually became a well-known philosopher.
- 才五岁就学哲学：才五岁就开始学哲学，In the 才 + age + 就 pattern, add 开始 to show that learning philosophy began at that early age.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子从五岁一直学哲学：他从五岁开始一直在学哲学，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence: the previous sentence has already named Mengzi, and no competing male character appears.
- Coherence (Temporality): 从五岁一直学哲学：从五岁开始一直在学哲学，Use 从 + time + 开始 for the starting point, and use 一直在 + verb to show an ongoing activity over a long period.
- Expressiveness: At the study routine in the second sentence, add a concrete detail about how he studies, such as “孟子每天坐在桌子前，认真地读书。”
- Expressiveness: After the description of philosophy books, add Mengzi’s feeling to explain his interest, such as “他觉得哲学很有意思，也很想知道更多问题的答案。”
- Expressiveness: You could add a short comment from Zisi after seeing Mengzi study, such as “子思高兴地说：‘孟子，你学得很认真。’”

### Encouragement
- You used the 不是……就是…… structure well to show Mengzi’s two main study activities.
- The final sentence clearly connects his early start in philosophy with his later success.
