## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个人叫神农。一天，神农正在路上走，一看很多人正在吃一些植物。一些人一吃植物就生病了，神农想帮别人。所以想知道哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 already names a time period, so adding 的时候 is redundant.
- 一看：看到，看 means “look,” while 看到 means “see”; here 神农 notices the people eating plants.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 知道 -- “想知道哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃”：弄清楚，emphasizes finding a clear answer to the uncertain question；辨别，emphasizes telling edible and inedible plants apart.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 神农想帮别人。所以想知道哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃：神农想帮别人，所以想知道哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃，the connector 所以 expresses a cause-and-effect relation and should link the cause and result smoothly; the subject can stay omitted because it is still 神农.
- Expressiveness: At “正在路上走,” add a little setting detail to make the journey easier to picture: “山路两边长满了各种各样的植物。”
- Expressiveness: After “生病了,” show what being ill looks like: “他们有的捂着肚子，有的脸色发白。”
- Expressiveness: After 神农 decides to help, add his inner thought: “神农心想：我一定要弄清楚哪些植物能吃。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduce 神农 at the start and keep the focus on his discovery of the problem.
- Your use of “一吃……就……” effectively shows that eating the plants quickly led to illness.

## Scene 2

> 然后，神农开始尝很多植物，比方说他吃过生姜、芥兰和菠菜。一些植物吃真好，特别是黄瓜。但是，一些植物毒得很，特别是夹竹桃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一些植物吃真好：一些植物很好吃，use the predicate adjective phrase “很好吃” to describe that a plant tastes good; “吃真好” does not form a natural predicate here.
- 毒得很：有毒，“毒” cannot take the degree complement “得很” in this use; “有毒” correctly describes a poisonous plant.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 吃过 -- “比方说他吃过生姜、芥兰和菠菜”：尝过，emphasizes sampling these plants as part of an experiment rather than simply eating them.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After “特别是黄瓜”, add a sensory detail about its taste, such as “黄瓜吃起来又脆又清甜。”
- Expressiveness: After mentioning the poisonous plant, show Shennong’s physical reaction, such as “他吃了夹竹桃以后，肚子疼了起来。”
- Expressiveness: Add Shennong’s immediate thought after the reaction, such as “神农想：‘这种花怎么会让我肚子疼呢？’”

### Encouragement
You use “然后” effectively to continue from the earlier scene, and “但是” clearly introduces the contrast between edible and poisonous plants. The examples of specific plants also make the scene easier to picture.

## Scene 3

> 一个人对神农说，麻烦我们可以吃什么植物？神农说，这就要看你想不想生病了。以后，神农告诉别人一些植物能吃，一些植物不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 麻烦我们可以吃什么植物？：请问，我们可以吃什么植物？，“麻烦” means “trouble” and cannot by itself introduce a question; “请问” is the polite question opener.
- 以后，神农告诉别人一些植物能吃，一些植物不能吃。：后来，神农告诉别人一些植物能吃，一些植物不能吃。，“以后” needs a stated event or time before it; when beginning a sentence to mean “afterwards,” use “后来.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 说 -- “一个人对神农说”：问，identifies the speech specifically as a question rather than a general statement.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: When the person asks the question, add a small detail about concern to make the dialogue feel more natural: “请问，什么植物能吃？我不想再生病了。”
- Expressiveness: At 神农’s reply, add a manner of speaking and a clearer warning: “神农耐心地说：‘有毒的植物不能吃。’”
- Expressiveness: Before the final sentence, add 神农’s purpose or feeling: “神农想：‘我一定要告诉大家，不能乱吃植物。’”

### Encouragement
You used “想不想” correctly as an A-not-A question after “这就要看……了.” You also clearly contrasted plants that can be eaten with plants that cannot.
