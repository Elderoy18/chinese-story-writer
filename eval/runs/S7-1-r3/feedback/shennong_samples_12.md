## Scene 1

> 很久以前，古代的人很不知道什么植物能吃，也什么植物不能吃。所以很多人常常吃不安全的植物，吃不安全的植物对你的身体有坏处，很麻烦的情况。但是一个人，神农，要解决这个问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很不知道：都不知道，The degree adverb 很 does not naturally modify 不知道 in this general statement; 都 shows that all the people lacked this knowledge.
- 也什么植物不能吃：什么植物不能吃，The adverb 也 must modify a verb phrase and cannot directly introduce an embedded question clause; the two parallel questions need no 也.
- 很麻烦的情况：是一件很麻烦的事，A bare noun phrase cannot finish this statement; use 是 with the measure word 一件 to make it a complete predicate.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不安全 -- “常常吃不安全的植物”：有毒的，identifies the specific danger in this story: plants containing poison that people should not eat.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 你的身体：他们的身体，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence: the previous sentence introduces 很多人, so 他们 clearly refers back to that group instead of shifting to the reader with 你.
- Coherence (Reference): 但是一个人，神农，要解决这个问题：但是，有一个叫神农的人要解决这个问题，Follow the first-mention rule by introducing the new character with a complete noun phrase that gives both his name and identity.
- Expressiveness: After “对他们的身体有坏处,” add a concrete physical result, such as “很多人吃了以后肚子疼，常常生病,” to show what the harm looks like.
- Expressiveness: Show 神农’s determination with a short thought or speech line after his introduction, such as “神农想：我一定要找出能吃的植物！”.

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish the central problem before introducing 神农, so the reader can understand why he wants to help.
- Your use of “什么植物能吃” shows that you are building an important contrast for this story.

## Scene 2

> 他又聪明又努力，旅行很远找到很多植物。比方说，灵芝和夹竹桃。他一吃各种各样的植物就知道能不能吃这些植物。然后，神农写他的主意。他喜欢植物的味道，特别是花。神农遇到了一个黄色的花，一吃就觉得不舒服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 旅行很远：去了很远的地方，use a place phrase with a movement verb to express travelling to a distant location; 旅行 does not naturally take 很远 directly in this sentence.
- 一个黄色的花：一朵黄色的花，朵 is the matching measure word for an individual flower.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不舒服 -- “一吃就觉得不舒服”：肚子疼，specifies the kind of discomfort as stomach pain, which makes the physical reaction clearer.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 然后，神农写他的主意：然后，他写他的主意，use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the referent is still clearly 神农 and no other character competes.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农遇到了一个黄色的花：他遇到了一朵黄色的花，use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence after the preceding sentence already identifies 神农.
- Coherence (Temporality): 找到很多植物：找到了很多植物，use 了 for the completed action of finding plants in this past narrative.
- Coherence (Temporality): 写：写下了，use 了 after the resultative complement 下: 写下了 marks the recording action as completed after 然后.
- Expressiveness: Before the flower appears, add a visual detail to make the setting easier to picture: “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: After the discomfort, add Shen Nong’s worried thought to show his feelings: “他想：‘我会死吗？’”

### Encouragement
- You use the pattern “又……又……” well to describe Shen Nong’s qualities, and “一……就……” clearly shows the quick result after he eats a plant.

## Scene 3

> 发现了夹竹桃有毒。别人看到他，很快给他吃一个灵芝。神农一吃就觉得比较好。然后，他继续写他发现的信息。会告诉他的朋友哪一个植物能不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 写他发现的信息：写下他发现的信息，写 means composing text, while 写下 is used for recording information or discoveries.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 比较好 -- “神农一吃就觉得比较好”：舒服一些，more specifically describes physical relief after feeling unwell.
- 信息 -- “他发现的信息”：发现的内容，more precisely refers to the findings he records.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 别人：一个人，别人 distinguishes other people from a speaker or a previously identified group; for a newly appearing single helper, use an indefinite full noun phrase on first mention.
- Coherence (Temporality): 发现了夹竹桃有毒：发现夹竹桃有毒，发现 is a mental-state verb here, so it takes the zero particle rather than 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 给他吃一个灵芝：给他吃了一个灵芝，了 marks the completed action of giving him the medicine; in this action sequence, the final completed verb takes 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 会告诉他的朋友哪一个植物能不能吃：也会告诉他的朋友哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃，use 也 to connect this additional action to writing down the information; paired clauses clearly show the contrast between plants that can and cannot be eaten.
- Expressiveness: After the medicine works, add a bodily detail to show his recovery, such as “他的肚子不那么疼了。”
- Expressiveness: At the moment he receives the medicine, add a short question to show his curiosity, such as “神农问：‘你给我吃的是什么？’”
- Expressiveness: Describe the medicine briefly when it first appears, such as “灵芝像一把小伞。”

### Encouragement
You clearly keep the events moving forward with “然后,” and you correctly use “夹竹桃有毒” to state the danger of the plant. The recovery after eating the medicine is also easy for the reader to understand.
