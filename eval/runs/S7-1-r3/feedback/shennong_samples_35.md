## Scene 1

> 在古代，很多医生不知道怎么用药。所以他们找到了很多植物对药。神农先看到很多病人，他们吃很多植物对药。但是我觉得他们的吃的植物，让他们病人。神农觉得这个植物可能特别是毒。所以他想找一个植物对药，他们可以吃对药。正好，他找到了一把花，他想把花放在茶里，对药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很多植物对药：很多可以用来做药的植物，The phrase with 对 does not mean “used as medicine”; use 可以用来做药 to describe plants that can serve as medicine.
- 植物对药：用来治病的植物，Use a relative phrase before 植物 to explain its purpose; 对药 is not a natural way to say that plants are used to treat illness.
- 他们的吃的植物，让他们病人：他们吃的植物让他们生病了，A verb phrase modifying a noun uses the pattern “subject + verb + 的 + noun,” so use 他们吃的植物. Also, 病人 is a noun meaning “patient”; after 让, use a verb phrase such as 生病了.
- 特别是毒：有毒，毒 is a noun and cannot be intensified with 特别 in this meaning. Use 有毒 to describe a poisonous plant.
- 一个植物对药：一种可以用来做药的植物，对药 does not mean “to be used as medicine.” Use 做药, and 一种 is the suitable measure word when referring to a type of plant.
- 吃对药：吃，The phrase 对药 cannot follow 吃 as its object or complement. After identifying a plant that can be eaten, simply use 可以吃.
- 把花放在茶里，对药：把花放在茶里，做成药，对药 is not a verb phrase meaning “make medicine.” Use 做成药 to express making the flowers into medicine.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 这个植物：这些植物，The previous sentence refers to many plants, so repeat the noun with a plural reference. 这个植物 introduces one specific plant without a clear antecedent.
- Coherence (Reference): 他们可以吃：人们可以吃，The pronoun 他们 could refer to the doctors or the patients. Repeat the general noun 人们 when the intended meaning is people in general.
- Coherence (Reference): 他找到了一把花，他想把花：他找到了一把花，想把花，Use zero form for the same subject in the next clause of the same sentence; repeating 他 is unnecessary.
- Coherence (Temporality): 神农先看到很多病人：神农先看到了很多病人，The seeing event is completed before the following events, so use 了 after the resultative verb 看到.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 所以他们找到了很多植物对药：后来，他们找到了很多植物对药，The first sentence states that the doctors did not know how to use medicine, but this does not directly cause the completed finding event. Use 后来 to show sequence instead of 所以 for cause and effect.
- Expressiveness: When describing the patients, add a physical symptom to show their suffering: “很多病人吃了有毒的植物，肚子疼得直冒汗。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where Shen Nong decides to search, add his inner thought or quoted speech: “神农想：我一定要找出能治病的植物。”
- Expressiveness: When the flowers appear, add a visual detail so the setting is easier to imagine: “那些花是黄色的，像星星一样，又大又漂亮。”

### Encouragement
You set the scene clearly with a time setting and introduce the doctors before using pronouns for them. You also give Shen Nong a clear goal and move the scene forward from sick patients to his search for a useful plant.

## Scene 2

> 他不知道如果这把花对你的身体好，这就要看如果这把花毒不毒了。神农想找别的植物，但是这很麻烦。正好，他找到了别的花。他一吃花，很快就生病了。他的肚子疼死了。神农不知道他吃不吃灵芝或者夹竹桃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不知道如果这把花对你的身体好：不知道这朵花对身体好不好，an embedded yes/no question after 不知道 uses an A-not-A form such as 好不好, not 如果; 朵 is the matching measure word for an individual flower.
- 看如果这把花毒不毒：看这朵花有没有毒，after 看, the embedded question should directly use an A-not-A form such as 有没有毒, without 如果; 朵 is the appropriate measure word here.
- 神农不知道他吃不吃灵芝或者夹竹桃：神农不知道他吃的是灵芝还是夹竹桃，when choosing between two possible identities, use 是……还是……; 吃不吃 asks whether the action happened, rather than which plant he ate.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 找 -- “神农想找别的植物”：寻找，emphasizes an ongoing search for plants rather than a single quick action.
- 麻烦 -- “但是这很麻烦”：艰难，specifies that finding more plants is difficult and requires effort.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: At “正好，他找到了别的花,” add a visual detail about the flower: “那朵花又黄又大，像星星一样。”
- Expressiveness: At “他的肚子疼死了,” add a short line of speech to show his immediate pain: “神农捂着肚子说：‘哎哟，疼死我了！’”
- Expressiveness: Before the final sentence, show his uncertainty with an inner thought: “神农心想：‘我吃的到底是什么？’”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the event sequence: Shennong finds a flower, eats it, and then becomes ill. The sentence “他的肚子疼死了” is vivid and effectively communicates how strongly he feels the pain.

## Scene 3

> 别的人给了神农一个灵芝。这个植物可以让神农更好。比方说，他会觉得更健康。吃灵芝以后，他可以再走。现在他觉得更好。最后，神农找到了很多植物，他可以用对药。这些植物让神农很高兴。神农想跟很多人分享他的新药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 神农找到了很多植物，他可以用对药：神农找到了很多可以用来做药的植物，the phrase “用对药” is not a valid verb phrase. Use “用来做药” to show that the plants can be used for making medicine.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 更好 -- “这个植物可以让神农更好”：感觉好一些，specifies that his physical condition has improved after taking the medicine.
- 高兴 -- “这些植物让神农很高兴”：欣喜，shows more clearly that he feels joyful about finding useful plants.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 别的人：一个人，this is the first mention of the helper, so use an indefinite full noun phrase. “别的人” presupposes a contrast with another already identified person.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农想跟很多人分享他的新药：他想跟很多人分享他的新药，use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when no other character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃灵芝以后：吃了灵芝以后，use 了 for the completed action that happens before the later action.
- Expressiveness: After the medicine helps him, add a physical sign of relief, such as “肚子不那么疼了”.
- Expressiveness: At the point where he feels better, add an inner thought or short quotation, such as “太好了，我能继续走了”.
- Expressiveness: When he finds the plants, add one visible detail about them, such as “有的叶子圆圆的，有的花很香”.

### Encouragement
- You use a clear time sequence with “吃灵芝以后”, “现在”, and “最后”, which helps the reader follow the events.
- You also show Shen Nong’s recovery and his wish to share his discovery with others.
