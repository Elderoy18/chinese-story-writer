## Scene 1

> 古代时候，人不知道他们能吃什么植物，常常一吃就死。但是不知道为什么，没有办法。所以有一天，神农出去试一试很多种植物，告诉人什么植物是安全什么是危险。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代时候：古代的时候，The structure ……的时候 requires 的 after a multi-syllable time word such as 古代.
- 人不知道他们能吃什么植物：人们不知道什么植物能吃，Use 人们 for a concrete group of people; 人 is a generic noun. The pronoun 他们 is also unnecessary here because the sentence can directly state which plants people can eat.
- 试一试很多种植物：试了很多种植物，Verb reduplication in 试一试 expresses a brief or light attempt, so it does not fit trying many kinds of plants; use the completed action form 试了 with 很多种植物.
- 告诉人什么植物是安全什么是危险：告诉人们什么植物是安全的，什么植物是危险的，Use 人们 for the group being told, and make the two embedded clauses parallel. After 是, an adjective used to classify something takes 的 here.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 没有办法 -- “但是不知道为什么，没有办法”：想不出办法，specifies that people cannot think of a solution to the problem.
- 很多种 -- “试了很多种植物”：各种各样的，emphasizes that the plants are of many different kinds.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：一个叫神农的男人，This is the character’s first mention, so introduce him with a full noun phrase rather than using only his name.
- Expressiveness: After the people’s problem, add a short line showing Shennong’s purpose: “神农想：‘我要找出能吃的植物。’”
- Expressiveness: At “神农出去试了很多种植物,” add a setting detail to make his journey more vivid: “他走啊走啊，去了很多高山。”
- Expressiveness: Describe how the dangerous plants affect people rather than only saying they die: “很多人吃了有毒的植物，常常生病。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish the main problem first and then introduce Shennong’s action with 所以, which gives the scene a logical direction.
- Your use of 一吃就 correctly shows an immediate result and makes the danger feel strong.

## Scene 2

> 发现有的植物对身体非常好，特别是茶，让肚子感觉又暖和又清楚。他告诉朋友：“你吃哪种植物，这就要看你的口味。比方说，你爱吃甜的，可能喜欢糖。”他最喜欢吃花，除了夹竹桃以外，什么都花想吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 又暖和又清楚：又暖和又舒服，清楚 does not describe a stomach sensation; 舒服 describes physical comfort and fits the verb 感觉.
- 什么都花想吃：什么花都想吃，when 什么 means “every kind of,” the noun follows 什么 and 都 comes before the verb.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好 -- “有的植物对身体非常好”：有益，states more precisely that the plants benefit health；有好处，clearly describes a positive effect on the body.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 发现有的植物：发现了有的植物，use 了 after 发现 because this is a completed discovery in the sequence of past events.
- Expressiveness: After mentioning tea, add a sensory detail to show its effect: “他喝了一口茶，觉得茶水有一点苦，可是肚子暖和多了。”
- Expressiveness: Add Shennong’s reaction when he learns about different tastes: “他说：‘原来每种植物的味道都不一样！’”

### Encouragement
You used the quoted speech clearly, and the example introduced with 比方说 helps show how personal taste can differ. The structure 除了……以外 is also used well to show an exception.

## Scene 3

> 他发现这个口味，一百岁时候，不小心吃了毒的花。一吃就开始死。但是生病时候告诉人什么植物是药，什么植物是毒。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一百岁时候：一百岁的时候，The structure with a multi-syllable time expression before 时候 requires 的.
- 毒的花：有毒的花，Use 有毒的 to describe a poisonous plant; 毒 cannot directly modify a noun in this way.
- 开始死：快要死了，死 describes an endpoint rather than an action that normally unfolds with 开始; this revision expresses that death is imminent.
- 人：人们，Use 人们 for a concrete group of people in this context.
- 什么植物是毒：什么植物有毒，Use 有毒 to say that a plant is poisonous; 是毒 does not fit here.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不小心吃了 -- “不小心吃了毒的花”：误食了，specifically means accidentally eating something, especially something unsuitable or harmful.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 这个口味：一种口味，This is a first mention, so an indefinite noun phrase is needed; 这个 requires a specific, already identifiable referent.
- Coherence (Temporality): 生病的时候告诉人们：生病的时候告诉了人们，This is one completed telling event in the narrative, so the main verb takes 了; the zero particle rule for a speaking verb applies when it introduces direct quoted speech.
- Expressiveness: At the moment of eating the flower, add a visual detail to make the danger less sudden: “那儿开着黄色的花，像星星一样，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: After the illness begins, add a bodily feeling and an inner thought: “他的肚子疼了起来，心想：‘我会死吗？’”

### Encouragement
- You use the pattern 一……就…… effectively to show an immediate result after eating the flower. You also clearly contrast plants that are medicine with plants that are poisonous.
