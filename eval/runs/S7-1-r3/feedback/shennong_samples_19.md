## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男人觉得植物很有意思。他叫神农。有的植物吃了以后他的肚子疼死了，可是有的吃了以后很舒服。他找很多植物。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，the noun already expresses a time period, so adding 的时候 is redundant.
- 他找很多植物：他找到了很多植物，找 means “to look for,” while 找到 means “to successfully find.” Because this is a completed past event, 了 follows the resultative complement 到.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 舒服 -- “有的吃了以后很舒服”：身体很舒坦，specifies that he feels physically comfortable after eating the plant.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一个男人觉得植物很有意思。他叫神农：一个叫神农的男人觉得植物很有意思，by the first-mention rule, introduce a new character with both his name and role in one clear noun phrase.
- Expressiveness: At the point where he becomes interested in plants, add a thought to show his curiosity, for example: “这些植物到底有什么用呢？”
- Expressiveness: After the stomachache, add a physical detail to make the reaction vivid, for example: “他疼得弯下了腰，脸都白了。”
- Expressiveness: You could make the contrast between the plants more concrete with one brief description, for example: “有的植物味道清香，吃了以后身体很舒坦。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show that different plants can have very different effects, and the contrast connector makes the two outcomes easy to understand.
- Your use of completed actions in the eating clauses helps the events move forward clearly.

## Scene 2

> 凉山很大的山，这也找植物。一天，他找到黄色的花。他听说你跟黄色的花做茶很有用。比方说，你的头发疼死，这冰茶是药。他一喝茶就很不舒服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 凉山很大的山：凉山是一座很大的山，A noun used as the predicate needs the copula 是 here, and a mountain takes the measure word 座.
- 这也找植物：他也在这里找植物，This needs a person as the subject, and 这里 is the location form; 这 alone cannot mean “here” before the verb.
- 跟黄色的花做茶：用黄色的花泡茶，跟 means “with/together with,” while 用 introduces the material used to make or brew the tea.
- 你的头发疼死：头疼得厉害的时候，头发 means “hair,” not “head.” Use 头疼 for a headache, and use 得 before an adjective complement such as 厉害.
- 这冰茶：这杯茶，A demonstrative before a countable drink needs a measure word: 这 + 杯 + noun.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很有用 -- “他听说你跟黄色的花做茶很有用”：有药用价值，specifies that the usefulness is medicinal rather than general.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 你：(delete)，The second-person pronoun has no established addressee in this third-person story. Use zero form for the general action instead.
- Coherence (Reference): 黄色的花：一朵黄色的花，On first mention, introduce this new object as an indefinite noun phrase with a measure word.
- Coherence (Temporality): 找到黄色的花：找到了一朵黄色的花，Use 了 after 找到 because this is a completed discovery in the sequence of events.
- Expressiveness: When the flower appears, add a visual detail to make the setting easier to imagine: “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: After the tea makes him uncomfortable, show his physical feeling and thought: “他捂着肚子，难受得不得了，心想：‘我会死吗？’”

### Encouragement
- You give the scene a clear setting with a named mountain and move from finding a flower to its effect on the character. The immediate-result pattern in the final sentence is also a good way to show what happens after he drinks the tea.

## Scene 3

> 糟糕，这黄色的花是夹竹桃，特别是毒。正好在这儿，灵芝药的夹竹桃他吃完了，也写一个课文的植物。谢谢神农。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 灵芝药的夹竹桃：灵芝是药，夹竹桃有毒，two different plants cannot be joined with 的 in this way; use separate clauses to state that 灵芝 is medicine and 夹竹桃 is poisonous.
- 写植物：记下植物，写 does not mean to record information about plants in this context; 记下 means to make a record of what was learned.
- 一个课文：一本书，课文 is a passage used for study, while a book takes the measure word 本.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 糟糕 -- “糟糕，这黄色的花是夹竹桃”：不妙，suggests that the discovery is dangerous or worrying；不好了，adds a more immediate feeling of alarm.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 也写一个课文的植物：也在一本书里记下了这些植物，this is another completed action after 吃完了, so the final action needs 了; use the completed form 记下了.
- Expressiveness: After identifying the poisonous flower, add a physical reaction to show the danger: “他一吃，肚子就疼了起来。”
- Expressiveness: When recording the plants, add his determination despite feeling ill: “他虽然很难受，还是记下了夹竹桃有毒。”
- Expressiveness: At the end, add a brief evaluative sentence to make the gratitude more vivid: “神农真了不起！”

### Encouragement
- You clearly identify 夹竹桃 as the dangerous plant, which helps the reader understand the key discovery in this scene.
- Starting with 糟糕 creates an immediate worried mood and makes the moment feel dramatic.

## End of story

### Overall Encouragement
