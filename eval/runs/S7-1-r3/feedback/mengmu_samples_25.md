## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈觉得在墓地玩儿，害得，所以他们搬家。搬家对孟子有好的影响，他才五岁，可是每天不是写字就是帮妈妈的工作。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 觉得在墓地玩儿，害得：觉得孟子在墓地玩儿，害得自己得老是道歉，害得 must be followed by the person affected and a harmful result clause; adding 孟子 also makes clear who is playing.
- 帮妈妈的工作：帮妈妈做事，帮 must be followed by a person and an action. 工作 is a noun here, so it cannot directly follow 帮 in this meaning.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好的 -- “搬家对孟子有好的影响”：积极的，specifies that the influence supports his development in a constructive way；有益的，states more precisely that the influence is beneficial to him.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 所以他们搬家：所以他们搬家了，use 了 for the completed move that happens as the result of the problem.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他才五岁，可是每天不是写字就是帮妈妈做事：他才五岁，就每天不是写字就是帮妈妈做事，use 才……就…… to show that he began doing these things at an unusually young age; 可是 does not express this early-action relationship.
- Expressiveness: After the mother notices the problem at the cemetery, add her worried thought to show her reason for moving: “如果一直住在这儿，麻烦会越来越多。”
- Expressiveness: At the part about his daily activities, add one concrete action to make his helpfulness visible: “他每天认真写字，还帮妈妈拿东西。”

### Encouragement
- You created a clear cause-and-result movement with the cemetery problem and the family’s move. You also used the pattern 不是……就是…… to show Mengzi’s daily activities.

### Encouragement
