## Scene 1

> 古代的时候有一个人叫神农。他需要当上医，一看别的人就想帮助他们。但是，他有一个问题，他不知道什么东西能做药。神农听说一个远远的地方有一种植物，什么病人吃都会觉得身体更好。神农三个月走去，找到有植物的地方，他看到一个很漂亮的植物，又香又好看。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 already names a time period, so adding 的时候 is redundant.
- 当上医：当上医生，医 is not a complete noun for a person’s profession here; 医生 means “doctor.”
- 一看别的人：一看到别人，看到 means “see,” while 看 means “look”; 别人 is the natural term for other people, whereas 别的人 distinguishes other people from a specific previously mentioned person.
- 远远的地方：很远的地方，远远 is usually an adverbial expression; a noun phrase needs an adjective such as 很远的 or 遥远的.
- 一个很漂亮的植物：一株很漂亮的植物，株 is the matching measure word for one individual plant.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 问题 -- “但是，他有一个问题”：疑问，specifies that he has a question he does not know the answer to.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他有一个问题，他不知道什么东西能做药：他有一个问题，不知道什么东西能做药，apply the zero-form rule: after the same subject has already appeared in this sentence, omit the repeated pronoun.
- Coherence (Reference): 有植物的地方：有这种植物的地方，repeat the specific noun with 这种 because the place contains the particular plant Shennong heard about, not just any plants.
- Coherence (Temporality): 神农三个月走去，找到：神农走了三个月，找到了，place the duration after the verb, and use 了 for the completed journey and completed result of finding the place.
- Expressiveness: At “又香又好看,” add a concrete visual detail about the plant: “黄色的花像星星一样，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: After Shennong sees the plant, add his thought to show his hope: “神农心想：这可能就是能做药的植物。”
- Expressiveness: Add a feeling during the long journey to make the effort more vivid: “他走了三个月，觉得又累又渴，但是没有放弃。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduce 神农 at the beginning and keep him as the focus of the scene.
- The details “又香又好看” already help the reader imagine the plant Shennong finds.

## Scene 2

> 他觉得，这种一定是做药的花。原来他想错了。吃了以后，神农快生病。 这时一个穿着蓝色衣服的人帮神农，快给他一种红的灵芝。神农吃了以后，他没有病，想起来以前一个人告诉他，灵芝对身体有好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 这种一定是做药的花：这种花一定能做药，Place the noun after the demonstrative and measure word, and use 能做药 to express that the flower can be used as medicine.
- 吃了以后，神农快生病：吃了以后，神农很快就生病了，Use 很快 to describe that the change happened quickly, and add 了 because becoming ill is a completed change of state.
- 快给他一种红的灵芝：赶快给了他一种红色的灵芝，赶快 is the adverb meaning “quickly”; add 了 after 给 because the giving action is completed, and 红色的 is the usual colour modifier before a noun.
- 想起来以前一个人告诉他：想起了以前有一个人告诉过他，Use 有一个人 to introduce an unspecified person, and use 想起了 for a completed act of remembering; 告诉过 shows that the person had told him this before.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: At “这种花一定能做药,” add a visual detail that explains why the flower seems safe, such as “花是黄色的，像星星一样，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: After he eats the flower, show his physical feeling rather than only saying that he becomes ill: “他的肚子疼了起来，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: When the blue-clothed person helps, add a short line of speech to make the moment more lively: “快吃这个，也许能帮你！”

### Encouragement

## Scene 3

> 啊，他怎么忘记了？他把灵芝放在包里。一回家就告诉别人。他找到药以后他写下怎么做药在书上。所以人们都知道，那红灵芝对身体好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 那红灵芝：那个红灵芝，A demonstrative such as “那” normally needs a measure word before a concrete noun.
- 别人：人们，“别人” distinguishes other people from a person or group already being discussed; use “人们” for people in general.
- 写下怎么做药在书上：在书上写下怎么做药，The location phrase “在书上” should come before the verb it modifies.
- 对身体好处：对身体有好处，“对身体有好处” is the required structure for saying that something benefits the body.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他找到药以后他写下：他找到药以后写下，Use zero form for the same character re-occurring within one sentence when no competing character has appeared.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他把灵芝放在包里：他把灵芝放进了包里，Use “了” after the resultative complement “进” to show that this placement action was completed.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉：告诉了，Use “了” for the completed action of telling people after returning home.
- Coherence (Temporality): 写下：写下了，Use “了” after the resultative complement “下” to show that the writing was completed.
- Expressiveness: After putting the medicine away, add his relief or sudden realization: “他拍了拍自己的包，心想：‘幸好灵芝还在！’”
- Expressiveness: When he tells people, add a short line of speech to make the moment livelier: “神农说：‘这种红灵芝对身体有好处！’”
- Expressiveness: At the end, show the people’s response: “人们听了很高兴，都记住了这种药。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect the actions in sequence, especially with “一回家就” and “找到药以后.”  
- You also show the result of the character’s discovery by explaining that people learn about the medicine’s benefit.
