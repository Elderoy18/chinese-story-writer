## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个男孩名字叫孟子。孟子和孟子的妈妈两个人住在很穷的农村。他们住的地方旁边是个墓地，墓地老是有很多人祭拜。因为人们在祭拜，他们一直在哭。孟子和孟子的妈妈住在这里才一个星期就觉得受不了了，而且害得孟子的学习。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 is already a time expression, so adding 的时候 is redundant.
- 有一个男孩名字叫孟子：有一个叫孟子的男孩，use an attributive phrase with 的 to describe the boy’s name.
- 而且害得孟子的学习：而且这里的哭声还害得孟子不能学习，害得 must be followed by the person harmed and a harmful result clause; it cannot directly take a noun such as 学习.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 旁边 -- “他们住的地方旁边是个墓地”：紧挨着，emphasizes that the home and cemetery are directly next to each other；附近，indicates that the cemetery is close by without necessarily being immediately adjacent.
- 哭 -- “他们一直在哭”：不停地哭，emphasizes that the crying continues without stopping.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子的妈妈两个人：孟子和他的妈妈两个人，use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence: after naming 孟子, repeat the name only once and use 他的 for his mother.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子的妈妈住在这里：孟子和他的妈妈住在这里，use a pronoun for the repeated possessor within the same noun phrase.
- Coherence (Temporality): 住在这里才一个星期：才在这里住了一个星期，use 了 after a completed duration of an action; they have already lived there for one week at the point when they cannot endure it.
- Expressiveness: After introducing the cemetery, add a sensory detail about the mourners’ noise, such as “来祭拜的人哭得很大声。”
- Expressiveness: Show the mother’s worry before the final sentence, such as “孟子的妈妈很担心孟子不能好好学习。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech to make the reason for leaving more vivid, such as “这儿太吵了，孟子没法学习。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduce the setting, the cemetery, and the problem that the family faces. You also use 因为 and ongoing-action language such as 在 and 一直 to connect the events clearly.

## Scene 2

> 孟子的妈妈觉得孟子取得良好的教育是很重要的，所以孟子的妈妈认为这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好，就打算跟孟子搬家。孟子的妈妈带孟子不是搬到城市，而是搬到了孟子老师的家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 取得良好的教育：接受良好的教育，取得 usually collocates with achievements or results, while a person receives education through 接受.
- 跟孟子搬家：带着孟子搬家，跟孟子搬家 means moving together with Mencius as equal participants; 带着孟子搬家 accurately expresses that the mother takes her child with her.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 地方 -- “这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好”：环境，more specifically refers to the surroundings that affect a child’s education.
- 不太好 -- “对儿子的教育不太好”：不利，states more precisely that the place has a negative effect on the education.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子的妈妈认为：认为，under the zero-form rule, when the same subject continues within one sentence and no other person competes as the referent, omit the repeated noun phrase.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 带孟子不是搬到城市，而是搬到了孟子老师的家：带孟子搬到了孟子的老师家，the paired connector 不是……而是…… is for correcting or refuting an existing belief; here a direct completed-action statement gives the sequence more naturally.
- Expressiveness: At the point where the mother judges the location, add her concern to show her inner thoughts, for example: “孟母心想：‘住在这儿会影响孟子的学习。’”
- Expressiveness: After the moving decision, add a short line of dialogue to make her determination clear, for example: “我们得找一个更适合学习的地方。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect the mother’s concern about education to her decision to move, and you use the cause-and-effect connector correctly.

## Scene 3

> 孟子跟他的老师从早到晚学习，难怪他的成绩很快就进步了很多。孟子学习的时候，孟子的妈妈不是待在家里就是编织衣服赚钱。以后孟子成为了中国有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 以后：后来，A sentence-initial time word meaning “afterwards” should be 后来. 以后 normally follows a stated time or event.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the first sentence, add the teacher’s reaction or a short quotation to show Mencius’s effort: “老师看着孟子，笑着说：‘你真用功！’”
- Expressiveness: In the sentence about his mother, add a concrete visual detail about her work: “孟母坐在灯下，一针一线地织衣服。”
- Expressiveness: At the ending, add people’s response to make Mencius’s achievement feel more meaningful: “后来，孟子成为了中国有名的哲学家，大家都很尊敬他。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show Mencius studying consistently from morning to night and connect this effort to his improving results. The paired structure for his mother’s activities also makes the sentence varied and easy to follow.
