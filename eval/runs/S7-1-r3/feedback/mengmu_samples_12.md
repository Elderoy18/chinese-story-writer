## Scene 1

> 古代，孟子跟孟母住在墓地的旁边。可是孟母注意，住在墓地的旁边影响孟子。他常常跟朋友们祭拜玩，所以孟母决定他们会搬家。这次，他们住在城市里的商店的旁边，从早到晚很吵。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 孟母注意：孟母注意到，when the meaning is “realized/noticed that,” the verb needs the result complement 到 before the following clause.
- 常常跟朋友们祭拜玩：常常跟朋友们一起学着别人祭拜，祭拜 is a solemn action and cannot be followed by 玩 to mean “play at worship”; 学着别人祭拜 clearly expresses imitation.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 吵 -- “从早到晚很吵”：嘈杂，emphasizes the mixed, unpleasant sounds around the shops；喧闹，describes a place that is noisily busy with people and activity.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟母决定他们会搬家：孟母决定搬家，after 决定, use the zero form before the action phrase; repeating 他们 is unnecessary because the person making the decision is already clear.
- Expressiveness: After the first sentence, add a concrete detail about the people at the cemetery, such as “孟子每天都看到很多人来墓地哭。”
- Expressiveness: At “从早到晚很吵,” describe a sound to make the setting vivid, such as “商人一直大声叫卖，街上人来人往。”
- Expressiveness: Add Mengmu’s worry after she notices the influence, such as “孟母心想：‘这儿不是孩子该住的地方。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly establish both settings in this scene: the quiet cemetery and the noisy area near the shops. You also show a logical reason for Mengmu’s decision to move.

## Scene 2

> 城市也有点儿危险。害得孟母只好一直陪孟子。孟子也没有朋友，难怪他老是看起来不高兴。住在商店的旁边不是适合孟子，而是让他觉得很伤心。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 害得孟母只好一直陪孟子：所以孟母只好一直陪孟子，害得 must introduce a clearly harmful result affecting someone; needing to accompany a child is not itself a harmful result, so a cause-and-effect connector fits here.
- 不是适合孟子：不适合孟子，适合 is a verb that takes its object directly, so the negative 不 goes immediately before 适合.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不高兴 -- “难怪他老是看起来不高兴”：闷闷不乐，describes a visibly low and gloomy mood, which fits something that others can notice.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子也没有朋友：他也没有朋友，follow the pronoun rule: Mengzi was named in the immediately preceding sentence, and 他 clearly refers back to him.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 而是让他觉得很伤心：会让他觉得很伤心，the second clause is a consequence of the location being unsuitable, not an alternative that corrects the first clause; 不是……而是…… requires two contrasting alternatives.
- Expressiveness: After “城市也有点儿危险,” add one concrete detail about what makes the area feel unsafe, such as “晚上路上很黑，常常听到有人大声吵架。”
- Expressiveness: Show Mengzi’s loneliness through an action after “孟子也没有朋友,” such as “他常常一个人站在窗边，看着别的孩子一起玩儿。”
- Expressiveness: Add Mengmu’s worried inner thought after the final sentence, such as “孟母心想：‘这里真的不适合孩子长大。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect the city environment to Mengzi’s feelings, especially by explaining that he has no friends and looks unhappy.
- Your use of “难怪” effectively shows the reason for Mengzi’s mood.

## Scene 3

> 所以，孟母和孟子搬到学校的旁边住。才五岁孟子开始学哲学。在学校，孟子不是看书，就是练习写字。将来，他成为中国的一个非常有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 才五岁孟子开始学哲学：孟子才五岁就开始学哲学，The pattern for an early action is “才 + age + 就 + verb”; place the subject before the age phrase.
- 将来，他成为中国的一个非常有名的哲学家：后来，他成为了中国的一个非常有名的哲学家，In a completed past narrative, use “后来” rather than future-looking “将来”; “成为” describes a completed change of status here, so it takes “了”.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 在学校，孟子不是看书，就是练习写字：在学校，他不是看书，就是练习写字，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence: “孟子” was the clear subject in the immediately preceding sentence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 孟母和孟子搬到学校的旁边住：孟母和孟子搬到了学校的旁边住，Use “了” after the resultative complement “到” to show that the move was completed.
- Expressiveness: After the move, add one sensory detail about the learning environment, such as “学校旁边很安静，常常能听见学生读书的声音。”
- Expressiveness: Show Mengzi’s feeling about studying with a short internal thought or action, such as “孟子觉得看书很有意思，每天都认真地练习写字。”

### Encouragement
You used the contrast pattern “不是……就是……” correctly to show Mengzi’s daily learning activities. The scene also has a clear progression from moving near the school to Mengzi studying and later becoming a philosopher.
