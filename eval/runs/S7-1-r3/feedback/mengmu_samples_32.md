## Scene 1

> 有一天孟子去了墓地玩儿，但是他妈妈觉得这是不合适的地方让孩子玩，所以她决定了他们会搬家找更好的地方。她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福，而且住在这儿教育不太好。因为她望子成龙，所以她会一直搬家才能找到质量好的教育。然后他们搬到了离城市更近的地方。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 这是不合适的地方让孩子玩：这不是适合孩子玩儿的地方，Use the verb pattern “适合 + person + activity” to describe whether a place suits an activity; the place is modified by the full phrase ending in 的.
- 他们会搬家找更好的地方：搬到更好的地方，After 决定, use a verb phrase whose subject is the person making the decision; do not repeat a separate subject such as 他们会. 到 is needed before the destination.
- 她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福：她担心儿子的童年不会幸福，恐怕 means “I am afraid that” and cannot take 她 as its subject in this pattern. 担心 correctly expresses that she is worried about her son’s future.
- 住在这儿教育不太好：这儿的教育不太好，Use 的 to show that the education belongs to or is available in this place; the verb phrase 住在这儿 cannot directly modify 教育 in this sentence.
- 她会一直搬家才能找到质量好的教育：她得不断搬家，才能找到高质量的教育，才能 introduces a necessary condition, so 得 expresses necessity more accurately than 会, which only predicts a future action. 高质量的教育 is the usual collocation for education of good quality.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 更好的地方 -- “搬到更好的地方”：教育条件更好的地方，specifies that the new place is better because it offers better educational conditions for the child.
- 一直 -- “她会一直搬家”：不断，emphasizes repeated moves without stopping, rather than one continuous action.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 有一天孟子去了墓地玩儿：有一天，一个叫孟子的小男孩去了墓地玩儿，On first mention, introduce a character with both the name and a brief noun phrase identifying who the character is.
- Coherence (Temporality): 决定了：决定，决定 is a mental-state verb, so it normally takes the zero particle rather than 了.
- Expressiveness: After the visit to the cemetery, add a concrete detail about what Mengzi does there, such as “孟子觉得祭拜很有趣，学着别人祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: Show the mother’s worry through an internal thought or quoted speech after she sees him playing: “孟母心想：‘住在这儿会影响孟子的学习。’”
- Expressiveness: Make the new location more vivid by adding a city sound or sight: “新家附近有很多商人，从早到晚都很热闹。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the mother’s concern for her son and connect her concern to the decision to move.  
- The completed action in “他们搬到了离城市更近的地方” is expressed clearly with 搬到了.

## Scene 2

> 他们住才一月就又搬家。古代的时候，一般人不喜欢卖东西的人。但是孟子老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿，害得妈妈着急得很，因为她恐怕儿子会成为跟他们很像。她告诉了孟子：他们不是朋友，而是不好的人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 is already a time expression, so adding 的时候 is redundant.
- 老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿：老是跟这些卖东西的人玩儿，Two frequency adverbs should not both modify the same action; keep either 老是 or 常常.
- 她恐怕儿子会成为跟他们很像：她担心儿子会变得跟他们很像，恐怕 means “I am afraid that” and cannot describe her fear in this structure; use 担心. Also, 成为 needs a noun phrase as its complement, while 变得 can be followed by an adjective phrase such as 跟他们很像.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 卖东西的人 -- “这些卖东西的人”：商人，identifies the people more directly by their occupation and avoids repeating a long action description.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们住才一月就又搬家：他们才住了一个月，就又搬家了，In the 才 + duration + 就 construction, 才 comes before the verb. Use 了 after 住 and 搬家 for completed narrative actions, and use 个 in the duration phrase.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉了孟子：告诉孟子，A saying verb that introduces direct speech takes the zero particle, so do not add 了.
- Expressiveness: At “这些卖东西的人,” add one concrete image of the busy market, such as “每天都有很多商人卖各种各样的东西，街上热闹极了。”
- Expressiveness: After the mother becomes worried, show her private thought, such as “妈妈心想：‘如果一直住在这儿，孩子会不会只学着卖东西？’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the mother’s worry and give a reason for it, which helps the reader understand her reaction.
- The direct speech at the end is a good way to make the scene feel more active.

## Scene 3

> 你不是当数学家，而是当哲学家，不能当卖东西的人。所以他们又搬家。在他们第三个家，孟子学了怎么祭拜。妈妈感觉这个地方适合他们，告诉了孟子：我们找到了最后的家。在这儿妈妈让儿子从早到晚学习，他难怪成为有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在他们第三个家：在他们的第三个家里，Use 的 after the possessive pronoun and add 里 to show that the home is the location.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 卖东西的人 -- “不能当卖东西的人”：商人，names the occupation more precisely than the general description of someone who sells things.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 所以他们又搬家：所以他们又搬家了，Use 了 because the move is presented as a completed event in the narrative.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉了孟子：告诉孟子，A speaking verb that introduces direct quotation takes the zero particle, not 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为：成为了，Use 了 to mark the completed change of state into a philosopher.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他难怪成为有名的哲学家：难怪他成为了有名的哲学家，Place 难怪 before the whole clause it introduces; it marks a conclusion based on the earlier reason.
- Expressiveness: At the mother’s judgment of the new home, add her happiness and a short quotation, such as “孟母很高兴，说：‘这儿的环境很适合孩子。’”
- Expressiveness: At the study routine, add a detail about the setting or Mengzi’s effort, such as “学校旁边很安静，孟子每天认真读书。”
- Expressiveness: Before the final result, add a time marker to make the long development feel clearer, such as “后来，孟子成为了有名的哲学家。”

### Encouragement
You use direct speech to show the mother’s wishes, which helps the scene feel more personal. You also connect the decision and the move with 所以, giving the events a clear cause-and-effect order.
