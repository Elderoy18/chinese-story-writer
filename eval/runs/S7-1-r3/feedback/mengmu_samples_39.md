## Scene 1

> 古代的时候人觉得祭拜非常重要，孟子很小的时候，从早到晚一直祭拜，因为他妈妈告诉他祭拜很重要。虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜。他的同学们老是对他开玩笑了。孟子妈妈看了他的同学们对孟子不好的影响，所以孟子和孟子妈妈搬家了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 看了：看到了，When the verb means “to see or notice,” use the resultative form “看到,” not “看,” which means “to look at.” The completion marker follows the resultative complement: “看到了.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子很小的时候：有一个小男孩叫孟子。他很小的时候，This is the first mention of the character, so introduce him with a name and a full noun phrase before using a pronoun.
- Coherence (Temporality): 老是对他开玩笑了：老是对他开玩笑，Use the zero particle for a habitual action. “老是” describes something that happens repeatedly, so it does not take “了.”
- Coherence (Conjunction): 虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜：因为他每天只祭拜，所以他没有朋友，Use “因为……所以……” for cause and effect. “虽然” needs a contrasting second clause with “但是” or “可是,” so it does not fit here.
- Coherence (Reference): 所以孟子和孟子妈妈搬家了：所以她带着孟子搬家了，Repeat the name only when needed for clarity. Since the mother was just mentioned and no other female character competes, use “她”; “带着孟子” also makes the mother’s role in the move clear.
- Expressiveness: After the description of worshipping, add Mencius’s feeling to show why he keeps doing it: “孟子觉得祭拜很有趣，跟着别人又笑又拜。”
- Expressiveness: At the classmates’ reaction, include a short line of speech: “同学笑着说：‘你怎么每天都祭拜？’”
- Expressiveness: Before the move, show the mother’s worry through an inner thought: “孟子妈妈很担心，心想：‘不能让他再这样下去了。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show a cause-and-effect chain: Mencius worships often, his classmates react, and his mother decides to move. You also used useful story vocabulary such as “祭拜,” “影响,” and “搬家.”

## Scene 2

> 孟子才十一岁，成为一个哲学家。孟子不是祭拜，而是看政治文章，难怪他成为了哲学家。不过，看过很多政治文章对他害得，他有很多问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 才十一岁，成为：才十一岁，就成为，The pattern for an unexpectedly early result is 才 + age/number + 就 + verb.
- 看过很多政治文章对他害得：看了很多政治文章，害得他，害得 introduces a harmful result: the cause comes before 害得, followed by the affected person and the result. 对 cannot be placed before 害得 here.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 问题 -- “他有很多问题”：疑问，specifies that these are questions he wants answered after reading.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子不是祭拜：他不是祭拜，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the same character was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为一个哲学家：成为了一个哲学家，Use 了 after 成为 to mark the completed change of state.
- Expressiveness: After the first sentence, add a reaction that shows how surprising his young age is, such as “大家都很惊讶，因为他才十一岁。”
- Expressiveness: At “看政治文章,” add a small detail about his reading or thinking, such as “他一边看，一边认真地想里面的问题。”
- Expressiveness: Give him a short inner question after the articles create questions, such as “孟子心想：‘为什么国家要这样做呢？’”

### Encouragement
You clearly show a contrast between two activities with 不是……而是……, and the final sentence tries to explain how reading leads to the character’s questions.

## Scene 3

> 他一直问问题，但是不能解决那些问题。孟子长大，他开始能解决问题。他常常建议你不是学金融，而是学政治，因为如果你懂政治，你会成为高薪。后来孟子成为教授，他教学生很厉害。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他常常建议你不是学金融，而是学政治：他常常建议你不要学金融，而是学政治，After 建议, negative advice needs 不要 before the verb. The pattern 不是……而是…… alone does not form a natural instruction here.
- 成为高薪：找到一份高薪工作，成为 needs a noun for an identity or role, while 高薪 describes a salary. A person can find or have a high-paying job.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 问 -- “他一直问问题”：提出，emphasizes formulating and raising questions rather than simply asking them.
- 厉害 -- “他教学生很厉害”：很有办法，emphasizes that he has effective ways to teach students；教得很好，states more precisely that his teaching performance is good.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 孟子长大：孟子长大了，Use 了 for a completed change of state before the next stage of the story begins.
- Coherence (Temporality): 后来孟子成为教授：后来孟子成为了教授，Use 了 to mark that becoming a professor is a completed later event.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为如果你懂政治，你会：因为他认为，如果你懂政治，就能，The causal connector 因为 should introduce the reason for the advice, while the conditional pair 如果……就…… clearly connects the condition and its result.
- Expressiveness: At the point where he cannot solve the questions, add a visible sign of his thoughts or feelings, such as “他常常皱着眉头，想了很久。”
- Expressiveness: When he gives advice, add a short line of dialogue to show his voice, such as “孟子对学生说：‘不要只看钱，要多学政治。’”
- Expressiveness: In the final sentence, show the students’ response to make his teaching ability concrete, such as “学生们都认真地听，还不停地点头。”

### Encouragement
You create a clear progression from asking difficult questions to becoming a professor. You also use contrast and conditional ideas to explain why Mengzi gives his advice.

## End of story
