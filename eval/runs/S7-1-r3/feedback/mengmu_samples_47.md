## Scene 1

> 这个男孩子叫孟子，孟子是古代的人。孟子的妈妈觉得住在哪儿对童年很重要，所以他们常常搬了家。她望子成龙，所以孟子的妈妈安排他的生活。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- Nothing to flag.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 常常 -- “所以他们常常搬了家”：频繁地，emphasizes that the moves happened at a notably high frequency.
- 安排 -- “孟子的妈妈安排他的生活”：规划，emphasizes that the mother carefully planned his life and future.
- 生活 -- “安排他的生活”：日常生活，makes clear that she was managing his everyday routines.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): “这个男孩子叫孟子”：“有一个男孩子叫孟子”，on first mention, use an indefinite full noun phrase; “这个” normally points to a person already established in the story.
- Coherence (Reference): “孟子是古代的人”：“他是古代的人”，after the name has just been mentioned and no other character competes, use a pronoun for close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Reference): “她望子成龙，所以孟子的妈妈安排他的生活”：“她望子成龙，所以安排他的生活”，when the same subject continues within one sentence, use zero form rather than repeating the noun phrase.
- Coherence (Temporality): “他们常常搬了家”：“他们常常搬家”，“常常” describes a routine, so use the zero particle rather than completed-action “了”.
- Expressiveness: At the mother’s thought about moving, add her worry and an inner thought: “孟子的妈妈很担心，心想：‘住的地方会影响孟子的成长。’”
- Expressiveness: Make the repeated moves more concrete with a brief detail: “他们搬了很多次家，孟子觉得很累。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line showing the mother’s goal for her son: “她说：‘我希望孟子以后能好好学习。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly introduced the main characters and showed the mother’s strong hopes for Mengzi through “望子成龙.” You also used “所以” to connect her belief about a child’s environment with the family’s moves.

## Scene 2

> 因为他们常常搬了家，中国人说，孟母三迁。孟子没有爸爸，所以，他的妈妈更重要。从这儿到那儿，妈妈教孟子很多技能。先她让孟子跟朋友玩儿，然后教他怎么买又便宜又好质量的东西。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 常常搬了家：常常搬家，A habitual action takes the zero aspect form; do not use 了 with 常常.
- 中国人说，孟母三迁：中国人说：“孟母三迁。”，A saying verb that introduces direct speech should be followed by a colon and quotation marks.
- 先她让孟子跟朋友玩儿：她先让孟子跟朋友玩儿，Place 先 after the subject or at the beginning of the clause, not directly before the subject.
- 又便宜又好质量的东西：又便宜又质量好的东西，In this coordinated description, 质量 needs the adjective phrase 质量好; 好质量 is not a natural modifier order here.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很多技能 -- “妈妈教孟子很多技能”：生活技能，specifies that these are practical abilities for daily life, fitting the following example about buying things.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 从这儿到那儿，妈妈教孟子很多技能：从这儿到那儿，她教孟子很多技能，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence: the mother was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other female character competes as the referent.
- Expressiveness: At the point where the mother teaches him, add one action detail about the children’s play, such as “孟子和朋友在院子里跑来跑去。”
- Expressiveness: After the shopping lesson, add Mengzi’s reaction or speech, such as “孟子高兴地说：‘这个真便宜！’”
- Expressiveness: After explaining the mother’s importance, show her sense of responsibility, such as “她觉得自己要更努力地照顾孟子。”

### Encouragement
- You used both a cause-and-effect connection and a clear sequence with 先 and 然后. The specific example about buying inexpensive, good-quality things also makes the mother’s teaching more concrete.

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- Nothing to flag.

### Vocabulary Suggestions

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After “她让孟子学写作,” add a brief detail about his study habits, such as “孟子每天认真地写文章，也读很多书。”
