## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，孟子和孟母住了在靠墓地的家。孩子才五岁就每天在墓地祭拜了。在那个城市，孟母从早到晚都找工作，可是找不到，害得孟母没有钱。难怪她跟孩子搬家，原来买不了房。他们搬了到别一个城市找工作。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，The original redundantly combines a historical-time noun with a time-frame phrase; use the shorter time expression.
- 跟孩子搬家：带着孩子搬家，Use this when the mother takes her child with her during the move; the original wording suggests that the child is an equal companion in carrying out the move.
- 别一个城市：另一个城市，Use this expression for one different/additional city; the original word is used to distinguish other people or things from a particular one.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟母：有一个小男孩叫孟子，他和妈妈，First mention should briefly introduce the character by name and identity before later references.
- Coherence (Reference): 孩子：他，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the previous sentence has clearly named only one child.
- Coherence (Reference): 在那个城市：在孟子住的城市里，A first mention of this city needs a full noun phrase because the reader has not yet been told which city it is.
- Coherence (Reference): 害得孟母没有钱：害得她没有钱，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence of the same character within the sentence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 住了在靠墓地的家：住在靠近墓地的家里，A continuing residence is a state, so it takes the zero particle rather than 了; use 在 before the location phrase.
- Coherence (Temporality): 每天在墓地祭拜了：每天在墓地祭拜，A routine marked by 每天 takes the zero particle, not completed-action 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬了到：搬到了，With a resultative complement, place 了 after the complement: verb plus resultative plus 了.
- Expressiveness: At the cemetery scene, add the child’s feeling and an imitation detail, for example: “他觉得祭拜很有趣，常常学着别人祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: When the mother cannot find work, show her worry through an action or thought, for example: “孟母叹了口气，心想：‘这样下去，日子怎么过呢？’”
- Expressiveness: After the move, describe the new city’s atmosphere, for example: “新家附近从早到晚都很热闹，商人卖着各种东西。”

### Encouragement
You used the pattern “才五岁就” correctly to show that the child was very young. You also connected the mother’s job-searching difficulty to the family’s move, which gives the scene a clear cause-and-effect direction.

## Scene 2

> 这个城市老是很吵，不是人吵架，就是都叫，害得孟子一直不能睡觉。在那儿，孟母也找不到工作。孟子和妈妈要又一个次搬了家。在第三个城市，孟母找到工作，在商店做衣服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不是人吵架，就是都叫：不是人们在吵架，就是人们在大叫，Use 人们 for a concrete group of people; the second clause also needs an explicit subject to match the parallel structure.
- 要又一个次搬了家：要再搬一次家，With a modal verb for an action that has not happened yet, use 要 + 再 + verb + number + measure word + object. 再 expresses a future repetition, and completed-action 了 is not used here.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 吵 -- “这个城市老是很吵”：嘈杂，specifies that many mixed sounds make the city unpleasantly noisy.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 孟母找到工作：孟母找到了工作，Use 了 after 找到 to mark the completed action of successfully finding a job.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 在那儿，孟母也找不到工作。孟子和妈妈：在那儿，孟母也找不到工作，所以孟子和妈妈，Use 所以 to show the cause-and-effect link between not finding work and needing to move.
- Expressiveness: After the description of the noisy city, add a concrete sound detail, such as “街上不时传来吵架声和喊叫声。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where the mother finds work, show her feeling with an action or thought, such as “孟母松了一口气，心想：‘这次终于找到工作了。’”
- Expressiveness: Make the shop setting more vivid with one visual detail, such as “商店里挂着很多五颜六色的衣服。”

### Encouragement

- The move to the third city is easy to follow, and the new job gives the scene a hopeful change.

## Scene 3

> 孟子在那个商店也找了到工作，可是不是做衣服的工作，而是喜欢的工作。孟子学习馆学了十五年，他的学习和他的旅行经验很重要，对他很多好处。他是大人的时候，他成为很有名的哲人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 找了到：找到了，A resultative complement is needed here: 找 means “look for,” while 找到 means “find.” The completion marker follows the resultative complement: 找到了.
- 孟子学习馆学了十五年：孟子在学习馆学习了十五年，A place where an action happens needs 在 before it, and 学习馆 must be followed by the verb 学习.
- 对他很多好处：对他有很多好处，The pattern is 对……有好处; 有 is required to state that something brings benefits.
- 他是大人的时候：他长大以后，Use 长大以后 to express the time after a child becomes an adult; 是大人 describes a state and does not naturally mark this life-stage change.
- 成为很有名：成为了很有名，Use 了 after 成为 to show that becoming a philosopher was a completed change of state.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 做 -- “不是做衣服的工作”：缝制，specifies that the work involves sewing clothes rather than using the general verb 做.
- 有名 -- “成为了很有名的哲人”：享有盛名，expresses that he has an established and widely recognized reputation.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他的学习和他的旅行经验：自己的学习和旅行经验，Use 自己的 when the possessor is the same person as the recent subject, so the connection to 孟子 is especially clear.
- Coherence (Reference): 他是大人的时候，他成为：他是大人的时候，成为，Within the same sentence, use zero form rather than repeating the same subject pronoun.
- Expressiveness: After the job sentence, add a feeling to show why this work matters to him, such as “孟子终于找到自己喜欢的工作，心里很高兴。”
- Expressiveness: At the ending, show how other people viewed his achievement with a short comment, such as “人们都很佩服他，说：‘孟子真是个有学问的人！’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly used the contrast pattern “不是……而是……” to distinguish two kinds of work.
- You also included a long period of study and travel experience, which gives a strong basis for the character’s later success.
