## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子。他们住在墓地的旁边，所以孟子老是祭拜。孟子不是祭拜，就是跟朋友聊天。他很喜欢他的城市，但是城市有才两个学校，都很小。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 already expresses a time period, so adding 的时候 is redundant.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 小 -- “都很小”：规模都很小，specifies that the schools are small in overall size.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子：有一个叫孟子的男孩子和他的妈妈住在中国，follow the first-mention rule by introducing the main character with his name and identity together, rather than naming him only in the next sentence.
- Coherence (Reference): 他的城市：自己的城市，when the possessor is the same person as the subject, use 自己的 to make that relationship clear.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 但是城市有才两个学校：但是城市里才有两个学校，place 才 before 有 to show that the number of schools is unexpectedly small; 但是 expresses the contrast between liking the city and its limited schools.
- Expressiveness: At “孟子老是祭拜,” show that he is copying what he sees and add his mood: “孟子一边笑一边学别人祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: After “都很小,” add Mengzi’s reaction to make the setting feel more personal: “孟子看着学校，觉得有一点失望。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech after the description of the schools: “这儿的学校怎么这么小？”

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduce the setting, Mengzi, and his mother before moving to his daily activities.
- The pattern “不是……就是……” is used effectively to show what Mengzi often does.

## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈不是不喜欢他们的城市，而是想孟子有很好的教育。他们搬家到有很多学校的城市。他们从早到晚走路，孟子一直抱怨。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 想孟子有很好的教育：想让孟子受到很好的教育，想 needs a verb phrase after it; use 让 + person + verb phrase to express wanting someone to receive an education.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 一直 -- “孟子一直抱怨”：不停地，emphasizes that his complaints continue without a break during the walk.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们搬家到有很多学校的城市：他们搬到了有很多学校的城市，this is a completed relocation in the narrative, so use 了 after the resultative complement 到: 搬到了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他们从早到晚走路，孟子一直抱怨：因为他们从早到晚走路，所以孟子一直抱怨，use 因为……所以…… to make the cause-and-effect relation clear.
- Expressiveness: After the long walk, add a physical detail to show why the child complains: “孟子走得腿都酸了，满头大汗。”
- Expressiveness: Give Mengzi a short line of speech to make his complaint vivid: “孟子说：‘妈妈，我们还要走多久？’”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the mother’s reason for moving, rather than simply stating that the family changes cities. The time phrase “从早到晚” also gives the journey a strong sense of length.

## Scene 3

> 他难怪抱怨，他想他的朋友，因为他不要离开他的城市。他的妈妈找到孟子一个老师。跟老师孟子学最重要的文学，所以他成为哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不要：不想，Use 不想 for a character’s own wish or unwillingness; 不要 usually expresses a prohibition or an instruction not to do something.
- 找到孟子一个老师：给孟子找到了一个老师，Put the beneficiary introduced by 给 before the verb, and use 了 after the resultative verb 找到 to mark the completed successful action.
- 跟老师孟子学：孟子跟老师学，The subject should come before the phrase beginning with 跟老师.
- 成为哲学家：成为了哲学家，Use 了 to mark the completed change of state: he eventually became a philosopher.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他的朋友：自己的朋友，Use 自己的 when the possessor is the same person as the subject; this makes clear that the friends belong to him.
- Coherence (Reference): 他的城市：自己的城市，Use 自己的 when the city belongs to the subject, so the reference is clear.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他难怪抱怨，他想他的朋友，因为他不要离开他的城市：因为他想自己的朋友，也不想离开自己的城市，所以他一直抱怨，Use 因为……所以…… to show the cause-and-effect relationship clearly. 难怪 introduces a conclusion after a reason is already known, so it does not fit this sentence.
- Expressiveness: At the point where he complains, add a physical sign of his unhappiness, such as “孟子低着头，走得很慢。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech to show why he misses his old life, such as “孟子说：‘我想跟我的朋友一起玩。’”
- Expressiveness: Show the mother’s response after finding the teacher, such as “他的妈妈觉得很放心，希望他能认真学习。”

### Encouragement
You clearly keep the focus on Mengzi’s feelings about leaving his city. You also connect his learning with his later achievement, which gives the scene a clear direction.
