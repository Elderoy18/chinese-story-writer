## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个男人，他的名字是神农。他看到了有很多人不知道哪个植物好吃，也不知道什么植物对你的身体有好处。这个问题神农觉得很糟糕，所以神农去了吃每种植物。他吃了一种植物以后，就写了这个植物对你有没有好处。有的时候，神农吃了很好吃的植物。有的时候，他吃了很危险的植物。他吃完了每种植物以后，会来跟他的朋友聊到他吃的植物了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 already names a time period, so adding 的时候 is redundant.
- 去了吃：去吃了，when a verb is followed by another verb phrase, the completion marker follows the final verb: 去吃了.
- 写了：记下了，when recording information about a plant, use 记下 rather than bare 写; 下 shows that the information has been recorded, and 了 follows the resultative complement.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 看到 -- “他看到了有很多人不知道哪个植物好吃”：发现，emphasizes that Shennong realizes a fact rather than merely seeing something.
- 很糟糕 -- “这个问题神农觉得很糟糕”：很严重，makes the harmful consequences of people’s lack of knowledge more specific.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 所以神农：所以，use zero form when the same subject, 神农, continues within the same sentence.
- Coherence (Reference): 有的时候，神农：有的时候，他，use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when no other character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Temporality): 有的时候，神农吃了很好吃的植物：有的时候，他吃很好吃的植物，use the zero particle for a habitual action introduced by 有的时候.
- Coherence (Temporality): 有的时候，他吃了很危险的植物：有的时候，他吃很危险的植物，use the zero particle for a habitual action introduced by 有的时候.
- Coherence (Temporality): 会来跟他的朋友聊到他吃的植物了：会来跟他的朋友聊到他吃的植物，use the zero particle after the modal verb 会; sentence-final 了 does not mark a completed action here.
- Expressiveness: When Shennong notices the problem, add a concrete consequence for the people: “很多人因为吃了有毒的植物，常常生病。”
- Expressiveness: After the dangerous plant, describe his physical reaction to make the risk vivid: “他一吃那种植物，肚子就疼了起来。”
- Expressiveness: Add Shennong’s determination after he sees the problem: “神农想：我一定要找出哪些植物能吃，哪些不能吃。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduce 神农 at the beginning and keep the scene focused on his effort to learn about plants.
- You also use time phrases such as 吃了一种植物以后 to show the order of events.

## Scene 2

> 一个人问神农，哪个植物好吃。神农说，这就要看你的口味了。要是你喜欢吃甜的，有很多甜的水果，比方说苹果、西瓜和梨。一个别的人问神农，哪个植物对你的身体有好处。神农说，有很多种植物对你的身体有好处，比方说小白菜、黄瓜、茶和菠菜。一个别的人问神农，哪个植物对你的身体有坏处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 哪个植物：哪种植物，Use 种 when asking which category or type of plant fits the description; 个 points to one individual plant.
- 这就要看你的口味了：这就要看你喜欢吃什么了，In the pattern 这就要看……了, the part after 看 should be a question form, such as a wh-question.
- 一个别的人：另一个人，别人 means “other people” and does not modify a singular noun this way; use 另一个人 for one additional person.
- 哪个植物：哪种植物，Use 种 when the question asks about a type of plant rather than one individual plant.
- 一个别的人：另一个人，别人 cannot be used as an adjective before 人 here; 另一个人 clearly introduces another questioner.
- 哪个植物：哪种植物，Use 种 for a category question about plants and their effects.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很多 -- “有很多甜的水果”：多种，specifies that there are several varieties of sweet fruit rather than simply a large quantity.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 要是你喜欢吃甜的，有很多甜的水果：要是你喜欢吃甜的，可以吃很多甜的水果，The connector 要是 introduces a condition, so it needs a result clause explaining what the person can do in that condition.
- Expressiveness: At the first question, show the questioner’s interest with a small action or feeling: “一个人好奇地问：‘哪种植物好吃？’”
- Expressiveness: When listing the healthy plants, add one visual detail to make the examples easier to imagine: “翠绿的小白菜和黄瓜对身体有好处。”
- Expressiveness: Before the final question, show concern about harmful plants: “另一个人皱着眉头问：‘哪种植物对身体有坏处？’”

### Encouragement
- You used a clear question-and-answer structure, and the examples of fruits, vegetables, and tea make each answer concrete.
- Your repeated focus on plants and their effects on the body keeps this scene easy to follow.

## Scene 3

> 神农说，有一天，我吃了一个黄色的花，名字是夹竹桃。我一吃夹竹桃，就觉得这种植物对我的脑有坏处。可是，一个灵芝和一个男人正好在我的旁边，我说，麻烦你把那个植物给我吃一下。我一吃这个灵芝就好了。所以，你得小心吃植物，因为有的植物对你的健康有坏处，特别是夹竹桃。因为夹竹桃有毒。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一个黄色的花：一朵黄色的花，Use the measure word 朵 for an individual flower rather than the general classifier 个.
- 一个灵芝：一株灵芝，When counting an individual medicinal plant such as 灵芝, 株 is the appropriate measure word rather than the general classifier 个.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 有坏处 -- “这种植物对我的脑有坏处”：有害，states more directly that the plant causes harm rather than giving a general “disadvantage.”

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为夹竹桃有毒。：它有毒，所以不能吃。A clause beginning with 因为 needs a result clause; this revision uses 所以 to form a complete cause-and-effect statement.
- Expressiveness: After the flower is introduced, add a visual detail to make it memorable: “那种花又黄又大，像星星一样。”
- Expressiveness: After eating the flower, show the physical feeling and worry: “我的肚子疼了起来，难受得不得了。我想：我会不会死？”
- Expressiveness: When the man appears, add one identifying detail and an action: “一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人走过来，把灵芝给我吃了。”

### Encouragement
You use the 一……就…… pattern effectively to show that the result happens immediately after eating the plant. Your final warning about being careful with plants also makes the main message clear.
