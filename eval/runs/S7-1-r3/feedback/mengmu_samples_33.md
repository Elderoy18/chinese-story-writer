## Scene 1

> 一天，很久很久以前，孟子和他妈妈住在中国。孟子妈妈的工作是祭拜，孟子不高兴，因为他说我们老是搬家，为什么？他妈妈说，从早到晚，我一直祭拜，很多墓地得我祭拜。所以我们得去很多不同的地方。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一天，很久很久以前：很久很久以前的一天，When a distant-past phrase modifies a particular day, place it before the noun with 的.
- 很多墓地得我祭拜：我得去很多墓地祭拜，The modal verb 得 must come before the verb phrase performed by the person who has the obligation; a cemetery cannot be the subject that “must worship.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不高兴 -- “孟子不高兴”：烦恼，shows that repeated moving is making him troubled and worried, not simply unhappy.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子妈妈的工作：他妈妈的工作，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence: 孟子 was named in the immediately preceding sentence, and no other possible mother has been introduced.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 孟子不高兴，因为他说我们老是搬家，为什么？：孟子不高兴，因为他们老是搬家。他问妈妈：“为什么？”，The connector 因为 should introduce the cause of his unhappiness; “they keep moving” is the cause, while the question should be presented separately as his speech.
- Expressiveness: After “孟子不高兴,” add a small physical detail to show his feeling: “孟子皱着眉头，觉得很烦恼。”
- Expressiveness: Make his question sound more like a child speaking directly: “妈妈，我们为什么老是搬家呀？”
- Expressiveness: Add a brief scene detail after “从早到晚” to make the cemetery setting more vivid: “墓地里常常有人低着头，安静地祭拜。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly explain why the family has to travel to many places, and the time phrase “从早到晚” gives the mother’s work a strong sense of duration.

## Scene 2

> 孟子想，难怪我们常常搬家，原来我妈妈很忙。可是常常搬家害得孟子很难找到朋友。每次搬家孟子也觉得新的地方不是太热就是太冷，不是太大就是太小。在哪的地方，他都不高兴。孟子想帮助妈妈祭拜，可是他六岁，所以不能祭拜。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- Nothing to flag.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 找到 -- “很难找到朋友”：结交，emphasizes forming new friendships rather than simply locating people.
- 新的 -- “新的地方”：陌生的，highlights that each place feels unfamiliar to Mengzi after a move.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 在哪的地方，他都不高兴：不管在哪儿，他都不高兴，use the paired connector pattern “不管……都……” to show that the feeling applies in every location; “在哪的地方” is not a correct location phrase.
- Expressiveness: After the difficulty of making friends, add Mengzi’s feeling of having to leave them: “他刚认识一个朋友，就又要搬家了。”
- Expressiveness: Make the uncomfortable places more vivid with a physical detail: “有的地方风很大，吹得他直发抖。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of dialogue when Mengzi wants to help: “妈妈，我也想帮你祭拜。”

### Encouragement
- You used the paired pattern “难怪……原来……” effectively to show Mengzi’s realization about why the family moves so often.
- Your contrasts “不是太热就是太冷” and “不是太大就是太小” clearly show why Mengzi feels unhappy in the new places.

## Scene 3

> 四年后，孟子开始帮妈妈祭拜。他画画很厉害，他越来越喜欢祭拜，所以他祭拜不是想帮妈妈工作，而是他很喜欢画画。孟子成为很著名的古代画家。今天很多中国人很喜欢孟子。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- Nothing to flag.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 画画很厉害 -- “他画画很厉害”：很擅长画画，states more exactly that he has strong ability in painting；绘画技术很高，specifies that his artistic technique is strong.
- 今天 -- “今天很多中国人很喜欢孟子”：至今，shows that this feeling has continued from the past up to the present.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): “孟子成为很著名的古代画家”：“孟子成为了很著名的古代画家”，use 了 after 成为 to mark this as a completed change of state in the narrative.
- Coherence (Conjunction): “所以他祭拜不是想帮妈妈工作，而是他很喜欢画画”：“他祭拜不是为了帮妈妈工作，而是因为喜欢画画”，所以 should introduce a cause-and-effect relation, but the preceding statements do not cause the motive stated here. The revision removes the unsupported connector and makes the contrasting motives explicit.
- Expressiveness: After “孟子开始帮妈妈祭拜,” add one concrete action to show how he helps, such as “他学着妈妈的样子，认真地准备祭拜的东西。”
- Expressiveness: Add Mengzi’s feeling when he begins helping, such as “孟子觉得自己长大了，心里很高兴。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of dialogue from his mother, such as “妈妈笑着说：‘你现在真的能帮我了。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show a time jump with “四年后” and describe how Mengzi’s interests develop. Your use of “不是……而是……” also shows that you are trying to explain his motivation, not only list events.
