## Scene 1

> 很久以前，古代的人很不知道什么植物能吃，也什么植物不能吃。所以很多人常常吃不安全的植物，吃不安全的植物对你的身体有坏处，很麻烦的情况。但是一个人，神农，要解决这个问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很不知道：不知道，A negative mental-state verb such as 不知道 cannot be modified by 很 in this way; use 不知道 to state a lack of knowledge.
- 也什么植物不能吃：什么植物不能吃，Also cannot directly be followed by a noun phrase here; the two parallel question phrases can be placed side by side without it.
- 吃不安全的植物对你的身体有坏处，很麻烦的情况：吃不安全的植物对你的身体有坏处，这是一种很麻烦的情况，The final noun phrase needs a subject and predicate; adding this structure makes it a complete sentence.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不安全 -- “吃不安全的植物”：有害的，specifies more directly that the plants harm people’s health in this context.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一个，神农：有一个叫神农的人，On a character’s first mention, introduce the person clearly with a full noun phrase rather than placing the name after an unclear noun phrase.
- Expressiveness: When explaining the danger, add a physical result to make it more vivid: “很多人吃了有毒的植物以后，肚子疼得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where Shennong appears, add his inner thought to show his purpose: “神农想：我要找出哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃。”
- Expressiveness: You could contrast helpful and harmful plants with a concrete detail: “有些植物可以做药，有些植物有毒，不能吃。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish the problem at the beginning and use 所以 to connect people’s lack of knowledge with the danger they face. Shennong’s goal to solve the problem also gives this scene a clear direction.

## Scene 2

> 他又聪明又努力，旅行很远找到很多植物。比方说，灵芝和夹竹桃。他一吃各种各样的植物就知道能不能吃这些植物。然后，神农写他的主意。他喜欢植物的味道，特别是花。神农遇到了一个黄色的花，一吃就觉得不舒服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 旅行很远找到很多植物：走了很远，找到了很多植物，Use a distance verb phrase such as “走了很远” rather than “旅行很远”; both actions are completed events in the narrative, so they take 了.
- 写他的主意：写下自己的主意，“写下” means to record an idea in writing; “写主意” does not naturally express recording one’s ideas, and “自己的” clearly shows that the ideas belong to the subject.
- 一个黄色的花：一朵黄色的花，花 requires the matching measure word “朵,” not the general measure word “个.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不舒服 -- “一吃就觉得不舒服”：肚子疼，states the physical symptom more specifically in this situation；难受得不得了，shows that the discomfort is especially strong

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 能不能吃这些植物：能不能吃，After mentioning “各种各样的植物” in the same sentence, omit the repeated noun because the object is already clear.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 神农遇到了一个黄色的花：有一天，神农遇到了一朵黄色的花，Use “有一天” to introduce the new specific event after the general description of his preferences; it marks sequence in the story.
- Expressiveness: After the opening description, show his effort with a concrete action, such as “他走啊走啊，去了很多高山。”
- Expressiveness: When he sees the flower, add visual detail, such as “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: Before he eats it, add a short thought or question, such as “这是什么花呢？能吃吗？”

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect the plants to Shennong’s testing work, especially with the effective pattern “一吃……就……”.
- The ending creates a strong change in the scene: he feels unwell immediately after eating the yellow flower.

## Scene 3

> 发现了夹竹桃有毒。别人看到他，很快给他吃一个灵芝。神农一吃就觉得比较好。然后，他继续写他发现的信息。会告诉他的朋友哪一个植物能不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别人：一个人，The narrator is not contrasting this person with themself or another stated person, so this word does not fit here; use an indefinite noun phrase for the newly appearing helper.
- 比较好：好多了，比较好 requires a stated comparison, while this scene describes Shen Nong’s condition improving after eating the medicine.
- 写：写下，Use this verb when recording discovered information so that it is kept in writing.
- 哪一个植物能不能吃：哪一种植物能吃，哪一种植物不能吃，Do not combine a selection question with an A-not-A question in the same noun phrase; state the two categories clearly.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 给：给了，The helper’s action of giving the medicine is completed, so it needs 了.
- Expressiveness: At the point when the medicine works, add a physical detail about Shen Nong’s recovery: “神农的肚子不那么疼了，慢慢坐了起来。”
- Expressiveness: When the helper gives the medicine, add a short line of speech to show urgency: “快吃这个，也许能帮你！”
- Expressiveness: When Shen Nong records his discovery, add his thought or decision: “神农想：我一定要记下夹竹桃和灵芝的作用。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the medicine helping Shen Nong and keep the actions in a logical sequence. The scene also uses a helpful action to move the story forward.
