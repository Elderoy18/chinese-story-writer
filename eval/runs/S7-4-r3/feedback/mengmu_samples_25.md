## Scene 1

> 这是古代的故事。孟子小的时候，他的爸爸很早就去世了。从他的家到墓地很近，所以他每天老是从早到晚去墓地跟朋友们玩玩儿。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 从他的家到墓地很近：他家离墓地很近，Use 离 to state that two places are close; 从……到…… normally introduces a route or distance measurement.
- 玩玩儿：玩，The reduplicated verb suggests a brief, casual action, which conflicts with the long duration expressed by 从早到晚.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子小的时候，他的爸爸很早就去世了：有一个小男孩叫孟子。他小的时候，他的爸爸很早就去世了，On a character’s first mention, introduce the character with a name and a full noun phrase before using a pronoun.
- Expressiveness: At the cemetery scene, add a small setting detail to show what Mengzi sees there. Example: “墓地里常常有人来祭拜，还有人伤心地哭。”
- Expressiveness: Show Mengzi’s feeling about copying the visitors’ actions. Example: “孟子觉得祭拜很好玩，就跟朋友一起学别人祭拜。”

### Encouragement
You clearly establish the early family background and give a logical reason why Mengzi often goes to the cemetery. The time phrase 从早到晚 also creates a strong sense of how often he is there.

## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈觉得在墓地玩儿，害得，所以他们搬家。搬家对孟子有好的影响，他才五岁，可是每天不是写字就是帮妈妈的工作。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 害得，所以：会带来麻烦，所以，害得 must be followed by the person affected and a harmful result; it cannot stand alone before a connector.
- 他们搬家：他们搬家了，The move is presented as a completed event, so the verb needs 了.
- 帮妈妈的工作：帮妈妈干活，After 帮妈妈, use an action such as 干活 to say what he helps her do; 妈妈的工作 is a noun phrase and cannot directly serve as the action after 帮.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好的 -- “搬家对孟子有好的影响”：积极的，specifies that the influence benefits his development；有益的，emphasizes that the move brings him a real benefit.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: At the mother’s decision to move, add her worried feeling, such as “很担心孟子会学坏”, to show why she sees the cemetery environment as harmful.
- Expressiveness: When describing what Mengzi does at the cemetery, add a concrete action, such as “学着别人祭拜”, so the reader can picture his play more clearly.
- Expressiveness: After the move, add a brief evaluative detail about its effect, such as “开始认真学习”, to make the positive influence more specific.

### Encouragement
- You used a clear contrast with 才五岁 and 可是, which effectively highlights Mengzi’s young age and his daily activities.
- You also connected the move to its influence on Mengzi, showing an understanding of cause and result in the story.

## Scene 3

> 他很聪明的人，难怪他成为有名的人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很聪明的人：很聪明，an adjective can serve directly as the predicate after the subject; adding 人 makes the predicate incomplete.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他：孟子，because the previous scene also mentions his mother, repeat the character’s name at this new scene boundary so that the subject is immediately clear.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为有名的人：成为了有名的人，this describes a completed life outcome in the narrative, so the verb needs 了.
- Expressiveness: After the first clause, add a concrete learning habit to show why he is intelligent, such as “他喜欢看书，也常常问老师问题。”
- Expressiveness: Make the final achievement more specific with a model detail, such as “后来，孟子成为了一位有名的哲学家。”

### Encouragement
- You used 难怪 effectively to connect Mengzi’s intelligence with his later success. The sentence also gives the scene a clear concluding feeling.
