## Scene 1

> 古代的时候有一个人叫神农。他需要当上医，一看别的人就想帮助他们。但是，他有一个问题，他不知道什么东西能做药。神农听说一个远远的地方有一种植物，什么病人吃都会觉得身体更好。神农三个月走去，找到有植物的地方，他看到一个很漂亮的植物，又香又好看。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，This expression redundantly combines a time period with a phrase meaning “the time of”; use the shorter time expression.
- 当上医：当医生，医 is not normally used alone as the occupation noun in modern Chinese; 医生 means “doctor.”
- 一看：一看到，The meaning is “see someone and then want to help,” so the perception has a result and needs the resultative complement 到.
- 别的人：别人，Use 别人 for other people in general; 别的 is used to distinguish a different one or different kind from a previously mentioned one.
- 一个很漂亮的植物：一株很漂亮的植物，株 is the appropriate measure word for an individual plant; 个 is too generic here.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 香 -- “又香又好看”：芬芳，specifies that the plant has a pleasant floral fragrance.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农听说：他听说，The name was used recently and no other singular character competes with it, so use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农三个月走去：他三个月走去，The same character remains the subject from the previous sentence, so a pronoun is more natural than repeating the name.
- Coherence (Temporality): 三个月走去：走了三个月，A duration phrase follows the action verb, and 了 marks the three-month journey as completed.
- Coherence (Temporality): 找到有植物的地方：找到了有植物的地方，This is the completed final action in the narrative sequence, so it needs 了.
- Expressiveness: At the plant-description sentence, add a color and shape to make the image clearer: “开着黄色的、像星星一样的花”.
- Expressiveness: After hearing about the plant, show Shennong’s motivation or feeling: “神农很期待，决定去找这种植物。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought when he sees the plant to show his curiosity: “这会不会就是能做药的植物？”

### Encouragement
- You clearly explain Shennong’s wish to help sick people and give the plant sensory details through its smell and appearance. The scene also has a clear movement from hearing about the plant to finding it.

## Scene 2

> 他觉得，这种一定是做药的花。原来他想错了。吃了以后，神农快生病。 这时一个穿着蓝色衣服的人帮神农，快给他一种红的灵芝。神农吃了以后，他没有病，想起来以前一个人告诉他，灵芝对身体有好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- Nothing to flag.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好处 -- “灵芝对身体有好处”：能帮助身体恢复，specifies that the benefit is helping Shen Nong recover after becoming ill.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农吃了以后，他没有病：神农吃了以后，没有病，after naming the same character in the first clause, use zero form rather than repeating a pronoun in the same sentence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 神农快生病：神农很快就生病了，use 很快 to show that the illness began rapidly, and use 了 because becoming ill is a completed change of state.
- Coherence (Temporality): 快给他一种红的灵芝：赶快给了他一种红的灵芝，the giving is a completed action; in this action series, the final verb takes 了.
- Expressiveness: When Shen Nong becomes ill, add a physical feeling to show the effect of the flower: “肚子就疼了起来，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Make the blue-clothed person’s help more vivid with a short action and line of speech: “他赶快拿起灵芝，说：‘快吃这个！’”
- Expressiveness: After eating the medicine, show Shen Nong’s relief: “神农觉得肚子舒服多了，松了一口气。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the important sequence of eating a flower, becoming ill, and receiving help. You also use “这时” effectively to introduce the helper at the moment of danger.

## Scene 3

> 啊，他怎么忘记了？他把灵芝放在包里。一回家就告诉别人。他找到药以后他写下怎么做药在书上。所以人们都知道，那红灵芝对身体好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他把灵芝放在包里：他把灵芝放在包里了，This is a completed placement action in the past narrative, so 了 marks its completion.
- 一回家就告诉别人：一回家就告诉了别人，The telling is a completed action after returning home, so it needs 了.
- 写下怎么做药在书上：把做药的方法写在书上了，Use 把 to introduce the thing being written, and place the resulting location after the verb; 做药的方法 is a noun phrase that can be written down.
- 那红灵芝：那种红灵芝，A demonstrative such as 那 needs a measure word before a noun.
- 对身体好处：对身体有好处，The expression for being beneficial to the body is 对身体有好处; 好处 needs 有 in this predicate pattern.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 放 -- “他把灵芝放在包里”：装进，specifies that the lingzhi is put inside the bag rather than merely placed somewhere.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 以后他写：以后，写，The same character has already been named in the first part of this sentence, so use zero form rather than repeating 他 in the main clause.
- Expressiveness: After putting the lingzhi away, add a small action and feeling, such as “他小心地把灵芝装进包里，心里很高兴。”
- Expressiveness: When he tells people, add a short line of speech, such as “灵芝对身体有好处，可以做药！”
- Expressiveness: At the point where he writes, make the record more concrete, such as “他把灵芝和夹竹桃的用处都写在书上了。”

### Encouragement
You used a clear immediate-action pattern to show that he tells others as soon as he returns home. The final sentence also creates a logical cause-and-effect link between writing down the information and people learning it.
