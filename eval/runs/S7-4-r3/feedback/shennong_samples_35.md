## Scene 1

> 在古代，很多医生不知道怎么用药。所以他们找到了很多植物对药。神农先看到很多病人，他们吃很多植物对药。但是我觉得他们的吃的植物，让他们病人。神农觉得这个植物可能特别是毒。所以他想找一个植物对药，他们可以吃对药。正好，他找到了一把花，他想把花放在茶里，对药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很多植物对药：很多可以做药的植物，the phrase “对药” cannot describe a plant’s purpose; “可以做药” correctly means that the plants can be used as medicine.
- 吃很多植物对药：吃了很多用来做药的植物，“用来做药的” is the correct modifier for plants used as medicine, and “了” marks the completed eating in this past event.
- 他们的吃的植物，让他们病人：他们吃的植物让他们生病了，an attributive clause uses the subject directly before “吃”, not the possessive marker in “他们的”; after “让”, a verb phrase is needed, while “病人” is a noun meaning “patient.”
- 可能特别是毒：可能有毒，“是毒” is not the natural predicate for saying that a plant is poisonous; “有毒” means “poisonous.”
- 一个植物对药：一种可以做药的植物，“一种” is appropriate for one kind of plant, and “可以做药的” correctly describes its medicinal use.
- 吃对药：吃，“对药” cannot follow “吃”; deleting it leaves the correct verb phrase meaning that people can eat the plant.
- 把花放在茶里，对药：用花泡茶做药，“对药” does not mean “make medicine”; “做药” expresses using something as medicine.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 这个植物：这些植物，no single plant has been introduced immediately before this sentence, so the singular demonstrative has no clear referent; the earlier reference is to multiple plants.
- Coherence (Reference): 他们可以吃：人们可以吃，“他们” could refer to the doctors, the patients, or another group; repeat a clear noun when several groups have appeared.
- Expressiveness: When Shen Nong sees the patients, add a visible sign of their illness, such as “病人捂着肚子，脸色很差。”
- Expressiveness: After he suspects the plants, add his worried thought: “这些植物会不会有毒？”
- Expressiveness: When he finds the flowers, describe their appearance to make the discovery vivid: “那些黄色的花像星星一样，又大又漂亮。”

### Encouragement
You used “在古代” naturally to establish the story’s time, and you correctly used “找到了” to show that the search had a result. You also tried to connect causes and actions with “所以,” which is a useful storytelling habit.

## Scene 2

> 他不知道如果这把花对你的身体好，这就要看如果这把花毒不毒了。神农想找别的植物，但是这很麻烦。正好，他找到了别的花。他一吃花，很快就生病了。他的肚子疼死了。神农不知道他吃不吃灵芝或者夹竹桃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 如果：（删去），After 不知道, an embedded yes/no question does not use 如果; use an A-not-A form instead.
- 好：好不好，The embedded question after 不知道 needs the A-not-A form 好不好.
- 如果：（删去），After 看 in this structure, use the existing A-not-A question 毒不毒 rather than 如果.
- 吃不吃灵芝或者夹竹桃：吃的是灵芝还是夹竹桃，When choosing between two possibilities, use 是……还是…… rather than combining an A-not-A question with 或者.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 找 -- “神农想找别的植物”：寻找, emphasizes the ongoing process of looking for plants.
- 麻烦 -- “但是这很麻烦”：艰辛, makes clear that the task requires considerable effort and is difficult.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 你的身体：自己的身体，Because the flower’s effect concerns the same person as the subject, use 自己的 to make the possessor clear.
- Coherence (Reference): 他： （删去），When the subject is already named in the same sentence, Chinese can use zero form rather than repeating a pronoun: 神农不知道吃的是灵芝还是夹竹桃.
- Expressiveness: After the flower is found, add one visual detail to make it memorable, such as “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花。”
- Expressiveness: After the stomachache, show Shennong’s physical feeling, such as “他躺在地上，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Add a brief worried thought after he becomes ill, such as “他想：我会死吗？”

### Encouragement
You used completed-action markers well in “找到了” and “生病了.” The pattern “一吃……就……” also clearly shows the quick result of eating the flower.

## Scene 3

> 别的人给了神农一个灵芝。这个植物可以让神农更好。比方说，他会觉得更健康。吃灵芝以后，他可以再走。现在他觉得更好。最后，神农找到了很多植物，他可以用对药。这些植物让神农很高兴。神农想跟很多人分享他的新药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别的人：有人，别的人 refers to all other people in contrast with a person already mentioned; here no such contrast has been established, so an indefinite person should be introduced with 有人.
- 他可以用对药：这些植物可以用来做药，using 用来做药 shows that the plants have a medicinal use; 用 needs a clear object, and 对药 is not a valid expression for “make medicine.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 更好 -- “现在他觉得更好”：好多了，makes the degree of recovery clearer in this context.
- 高兴 -- “这些植物让神农很高兴”：欣慰，specifies that he feels pleased and relieved after finding useful plants.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃灵芝以后：吃了灵芝以后，this is a completed action before the following event, so 了 marks its completion.
- Expressiveness: After “吃了灵芝以后,” add a physical detail to show his recovery, such as “他的肚子不那么疼了。”
- Expressiveness: After finding the plants, describe their usefulness more specifically, such as “他找到了很多对身体有好处的植物。”
- Expressiveness: At “想跟很多人分享,” add a short thought or line of speech, such as “神农想：我要告诉大家哪些植物可以做药。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the change from being ill to feeling better, and you use time-order words such as “最后” to move the scene forward.
