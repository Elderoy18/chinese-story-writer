## Scene 1

> 很久以前，有一个老男人叫神农。神农在古代每一天看到了别的小镇人生病还是不舒服，所以他决定开始找新的药。他下一天开始爬当地的山脉，找了各种各样的植物。神农试一试每个植物，知道有什么好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别的小镇人：别的小镇的人，The original phrase does not clearly form a noun phrase for residents; adding 的 correctly links the town to its people.
- 生病还是不舒服：生病或者感到不舒服，In a declarative statement listing alternatives, use 或者 rather than 还是; 还是 is normally used for alternatives in a question.
- 下一天：第二天，Use 第二天 to mean “the following day” in a narrative; 下一天 is not the usual expression for this time sequence.
- 找了各种各样的植物：找到了各种各样的植物，找 means “look for,” while 找到 means “find.” Here the sentence reports that the plants were found.
- 每个植物：每种植物，Use 种 for each type of plant when discussing plants’ different effects or benefits.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很久以前 -- “很久以前，有一个老男人叫神农”：在遥远的过去，gives the opening a more story-like sense of historical distance；在很久很久以前，emphasizes that the events happened in a distant past.
- 好处 -- “知道有什么好处”：药用价值,specifies that the benefit being learned is a plant’s medicinal use；作用,makes clear that he is learning what each plant can do.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农在古代每一天看到了：他在古代每一天看到了，Use a pronoun when the named character was mentioned in the immediately preceding sentence and no other character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农试一试每个植物：他试一试每个植物，Use a pronoun for this close re-occurrence of the same character.
- Coherence (Temporality): 每一天看到了：每一天看到，A routine or habitual action takes the zero particle, not 了; 了 marks a completed event.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 试一试每个植物，知道：试一试每个植物以后，知道，Add 以后 to show the sequence: he learns the benefits after trying the plants.
- Expressiveness: After the sick people are mentioned, add a concrete cause to make the danger clearer, such as “很多人吃了有毒的植物以后，常常生病。”
- Expressiveness: When describing the mountains, add a small visual detail to make the journey more vivid, such as “山上长着许多陌生的花和草。”
- Expressiveness: Add Shennong’s purpose as a brief thought after he sees sick people, such as “他想：我一定要找到能治病的植物。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduce Shennong at the beginning, so the reader immediately knows who the scene is about.
- You also use a cause-and-effect connection with 因为……所以……, which helps explain why Shennong begins his search.

## Scene 2

> 比方说，他一次试一试有些植物的叶，吃得又新鲜又甜。煮这些叶以后，他一喝就知道他发现新的茶叶。虽然他找到了很多的新的植物，但是他的旅程有的时候特别是危险。比方说，他一吃灵芝就开始生病。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一次试一试有些植物的叶：尝了一些植物的叶，Verb reduplication is for a light or tentative plan, not for an action that was actually carried out; use a completed verb here.
- 吃得又新鲜又甜：这些叶子吃起来又新鲜又甜，A complement with 得 describes how the subject performs an action, but 新鲜 and 甜 describe the leaves’ taste; use 吃起来 to describe how food tastes.
- 特别是危险：特别危险，特别是 introduces a particular item or situation, while this sentence needs 特别 to modify the adjective 危险 directly.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 新的 -- “很多的新的植物”：陌生的，specifies that the plants were unfamiliar to him rather than simply new.
- 喝 -- “他一喝就知道”：品尝，emphasizes that he drinks the tea carefully in order to notice its taste or effect.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他发现新的茶叶：自己发现新的茶叶，The second pronoun is in the embedded clause and should be reflexive because it refers back to the same person, not to another possible character.
- Coherence (Temporality): 发现新的茶叶：发现了新的茶叶，The discovery is a completed event, so 了 follows the verb to mark completion.
- Expressiveness: After the tea is brewed, add a sensory detail about its smell or appearance, such as “水里飘出一阵清香.”
- Expressiveness: At the moment he drinks it, add Shen Nong’s reaction or thought, such as “他喝了一口，觉得又香又暖和.”
- Expressiveness: When introducing the danger in the journey, add a brief feeling to make the risk more immediate, such as “他有一点害怕，可是还是继续往前走.”

### Encouragement
You use the pattern “一……就……” effectively to show an immediate result. You also try to connect the positive discovery and the danger of the journey with 虽然……但是……, which gives the scene a clear contrast.

## Scene 3

> 一个爬山的男人正好看到神农的麻烦，然后给了他一些药。神农最终他的小镇回到，跟别的人分享他的发现。你认为神农的故事真的吗？这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 神农的故事真的吗：神农的故事是真的吗，The linking verb is needed before the predicate adjective phrase to form a natural yes-or-no question.
- 这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处：这就要看你是否相信中药对身体有好处了，This fixed expression ends with 了; adding 是否 also makes the condition being considered explicit.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 麻烦 -- “看到神农的麻烦”：困境, specifies that Shen Nong is in a difficult situation rather than referring to a general inconvenience

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 神农最终他的小镇回到：神农最终回到了自己的小镇，The return is a completed action, so 回到 takes 了; the destination follows 回到, and 自己的 clearly shows that the town belongs to the subject.
- Expressiveness: At the moment the man helps Shen Nong, add a visual detail and urgency: “一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人赶快走过来。”
- Expressiveness: You could make the medicine more vivid and connected to the setting: “他拿起一棵像伞一样的灵芝，递给神农吃。”
- Expressiveness: At the ending, add your evaluation of Shen Nong: “我觉得神农非常了不起，因为他不怕危险，帮了很多人。”

### Encouragement
You used a clear action sequence with 然后 and gave the helper a meaningful action. Your ending also tries to involve the reader with a question, which is an engaging storytelling choice.
