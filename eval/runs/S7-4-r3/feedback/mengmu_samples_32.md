## Scene 1

> 有一天孟子去了墓地玩儿，但是他妈妈觉得这是不合适的地方让孩子玩，所以她决定了他们会搬家找更好的地方。她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福，而且住在这儿教育不太好。因为她望子成龙，所以她会一直搬家才能找到质量好的教育。然后他们搬到了离城市更近的地方。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 这是不合适的地方让孩子玩：这不是一个适合孩子玩的地方，Use the pattern with “适合” to show that the place suits the activity; the action must modify the noun with “的”. [vocab list]
- 决定了他们会搬家找更好的地方：决定搬家，找更好的地方，Mental-state verbs such as “决定” normally take the zero particle, not “了”; after “决定”, attach the verb phrase directly instead of repeating a subject and using a future clause. [rule: aspect_le_completed] [teacher rule: jueding_vp]
- 住在这儿教育不太好：这儿的教育不太好，Use “的” to show that the education belongs to or is available in this place; “住在这儿” cannot directly modify “教育” in this meaning. [rule: none]
- 她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福：她担心儿子的童年不会幸福，“恐怕” means “I am afraid that” and cannot describe another subject’s worry in this structure; use “担心” for her fear about her son. [teacher rule: kongpa]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 更好的 -- “找更好的地方”：更适合孩子成长的，identifies what makes the new place better: it supports the child’s development.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子：一个叫孟子的小男孩，This is the character’s first mention in the submitted scene, so introduce him with both his name and a brief description before using pronouns. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery is mentioned, add a concrete detail showing what the child does there, such as “孟子常常学着来祭拜的人鞠躬。”
- Expressiveness: At the mother’s worry, add an internal thought or quoted speech, such as “妈妈心想：‘这儿不适合孩子长大。’”
- Expressiveness: When the family reaches the place near the city, describe its atmosphere, such as “街上有很多商人，从早到晚都很热闹。”

### Encouragement
You used clear cause-and-effect links to show why the mother wants to move. The final sentence also gives the scene a clear next step by showing that the family arrives in a new place.

- 她恐怕：她担心，this subject is afraid about a possible result, so use the verb shown; 恐怕 does not take a third-person subject in this structure. [teacher rule: kongpa]
- 会成为跟他们很像：会变得跟他们很像，成为 is used for becoming an identity or role expressed by a noun phrase, while 变得 introduces a changed state or quality. [rule: none]
