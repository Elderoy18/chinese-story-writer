## Scene 1

> 古代的时候人觉得祭拜非常重要，孟子很小的时候，从早到晚一直祭拜，因为他妈妈告诉他祭拜很重要。虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜。他的同学们老是对他开玩笑了。孟子妈妈看了他的同学们对孟子不好的影响，所以孟子和孟子妈妈搬家了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，This expression redundantly combines two time expressions; use the shorter time phrase. [teacher rule: gudai_de_shihou_why]
- 人：人们，This refers to a concrete group of people in general, so the plural collective noun is needed. [teacher rule: ren_renmen]
- 看了他的同学们对孟子不好的影响：看到了他的同学们对孟子不好的影响，Here the mother “sees” the influence, so use the resultative verb meaning “see/notice,” not the verb meaning “look at.” [vocab list]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很小 -- “孟子很小的时候”：年幼，specifies that he was in his early childhood stage.
- 不好 -- “对孟子不好的影响”：不良，forms the precise collocation for a harmful influence.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子很小的时候，从早到晚一直祭拜：有一个叫孟子的小男孩；他小时候从早到晚一直祭拜，At first mention, introduce the character with both a name and a full noun phrase rather than using only the name. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Reference): 他的同学们：孟子的同学们，The pronoun could refer either to the mother in the same sentence or to Mengzi; repeat the name to make the possessor clear. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Temporality): 老是对他开玩笑了：老是对他开玩笑，A habitual action with 老是 takes the zero particle, not completed-action 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Conjunction): 虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜。：因为他每天只祭拜，所以他没有朋友。This is a cause-and-effect relationship, so use the paired connector for cause and result; 虽然 requires a contrasting second clause. [rule: coherence_connectives]
- Expressiveness: After the opening statement about worship, add a visual detail about the people at the cemetery, such as “每天都有不同的人低着头来祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: When describing Mengzi’s worship, show his attitude through an action, such as “孟子一边笑，一边学着大人祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: At the moment his mother notices the influence, add her worried thought or speech, such as “孟子妈妈心想：‘这样下去会影响孟子。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly show a cause for the family’s move, and the final sentence gives the scene a definite outcome. You also used useful story vocabulary such as “祭拜” and “影响.”

## Scene 2

> 孟子才十一岁，成为一个哲学家。孟子不是祭拜，而是看政治文章，难怪他成为了哲学家。不过，看过很多政治文章对他害得，他有很多问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 看过很多政治文章对他害得：他看了很多政治文章，害得自己，In a causative structure with 害得, the cause comes before 害得 and the affected person comes after it; use 自己 because the reader and the affected person are the same person. [teacher rule: haide_structure]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 问题 -- “他有很多问题”：疑问, specifies that these are unanswered intellectual questions rather than general problems

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子不是祭拜：他不是祭拜，Use a pronoun when the same character was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other character competes as the referent. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为：成为了，Becoming a philosopher is presented as a completed change of state, so it needs 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Conjunction): 才十一岁，：才十一岁，就，The pattern 才…就… shows that something happened surprisingly early. [rule: none]
- Expressiveness: After “看政治文章,” add one specific topic that interests or confuses him, such as “他常常思考国家应该怎么管理。”
- Expressiveness: Add Mengzi’s inner question after “他有很多问题,” such as “他心想：‘为什么有些政策对百姓不好呢？’”

### Encouragement
You used the contrast pattern “不是……而是……” effectively to show a change in Mengzi’s interests. The final sentence also gives him an intellectual challenge, which is a strong idea for a future philosopher.

## Scene 3

> 他一直问问题，但是不能解决那些问题。孟子长大，他开始能解决问题。他常常建议你不是学金融，而是学政治，因为如果你懂政治，你会成为高薪。后来孟子成为教授，他教学生很厉害。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不是学金融，而是学政治：不要学金融，而要学政治，After a verb of advice, the first action needs the negative imperative form to show what the students should not do; this contrast recommends one choice instead of another. [teacher rule: bushi_ershi]
- 成为高薪：获得高薪，The word 高薪 means a high salary, so a person cannot “become” it; 获得 correctly means to receive it. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 问 -- “他一直问问题”：提出，emphasizes forming and raising questions for discussion；追问，emphasizes continuing to ask for an answer.
- 解决 -- “他开始能解决问题”：解答，more specifically means answering questions, which fits the earlier mention of asking questions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子：他，The name was mentioned in the immediately preceding sentence and no other character competes with it, so a pronoun is more natural for this close re-occurrence. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Reference): 你：学生，The second-person pronoun suddenly addresses the reader, but the people receiving the advice have not been identified; use a full noun for the intended group. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Reference): 后来孟子：后来，他，The same character is still the clear subject from the previous sentence, so a pronoun avoids unnecessarily repeating the name. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Temporality): 长大：长大了，This growing-up event is completed before the next ability begins, so it needs 了 to mark the completed change of state. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为教授：成为了教授，Becoming a professor is a completed change in the later narrative event, so use 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: After the first sentence, add an internal feeling to show why the unanswered questions matter: “他觉得很着急，因为没有人能回答他。”
- Expressiveness: At the advice scene, add a short line of speech to make the teaching role more vivid: “他对学生说：‘学政治能帮助你们了解社会。’”
- Expressiveness: At the final sentence, add a classroom detail showing his skill: “学生们都认真地听他讲课，还常常向他提问。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show a change from asking questions without answers to becoming able to solve them. Your use of time words such as “后来” also helps move the scene forward.
