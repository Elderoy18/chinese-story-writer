## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子。他们住在墓地的旁边，所以孟子老是祭拜。孟子不是祭拜，就是跟朋友聊天。他很喜欢他的城市，但是城市有才两个学校，都很小。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，This expression redundantly combines two time expressions; use the shorter time phrase here.
- 城市有才两个学校：城市里才有两所学校，Place the adverb 才 before 有, and use 所 as the measure word for schools.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 聊天 -- “跟朋友聊天”：闲聊，specifies relaxed, casual conversation between friends.
- 很小 -- “都很小”：规模都很小，makes clear that the schools are small in scale rather than merely physically small.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子：一个叫孟子的男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国，On first mention, introduce the character with both his name and role instead of giving the name only in the following sentence.
- Coherence (Reference): 他很喜欢他的城市：他很喜欢自己的城市，When the subject and the possessor are the same person, use 自己的 to make that relationship clear.
- Expressiveness: After “孟子老是祭拜”, add his reason or feeling, such as “孟子觉得祭拜很好玩。”
- Expressiveness: At “跟朋友聊天”, add a small action to show how they interact, such as “他一边笑一边跟朋友聊天。”
- Expressiveness: After “都很小”, add one concrete detail about the schools, such as “那两所学校的教室很少。”

### Encouragement
You used clear short sentences and introduced the relationship between 孟子 and his mother early. The contrast with 但是 also effectively shows why he may not be fully satisfied with the city.

## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈不是不喜欢他们的城市，而是想孟子有很好的教育。他们搬家到有很多学校的城市。他们从早到晚走路，孟子一直抱怨。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 想孟子有很好的教育：想让孟子接受良好的教育，The verb 想 needs 让 before a different person carries out the following action, and 接受教育 is the natural verb-object combination.
- 他们搬家到有很多学校的城市：他们搬到了有很多学校的城市，Use 搬到 + destination for moving to a place, and add 了 to show that the move was completed.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After “孟子一直抱怨,” add a short line of speech to show what he says, for example: “孟子说：‘我们什么时候才能到啊？我的脚很疼。’”
- Expressiveness: Add one physical detail during the walk to make the hardship clearer, for example: “太阳很大，他们走得满头大汗。”
- Expressiveness: Show the mother’s feeling or thought at the point of moving, for example: “妈妈虽然很累，但是希望孟子以后能好好学习。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the mother’s reason for moving, and the contrast structure in the first sentence helps the reader understand her intention. You also use “一直抱怨” effectively to show that the journey is difficult for Mengzi.

## Scene 3

> 他难怪抱怨，他想他的朋友，因为他不要离开他的城市。他的妈妈找到孟子一个老师。跟老师孟子学最重要的文学，所以他成为哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不要离开：不想离开，Use 不想 to say that a character does not want to do something; 不要 is normally used for a prohibition or instruction not to do it.
- 跟老师孟子学：孟子跟老师学，The subject should come before the phrase beginning with 跟老师; this makes clear that Mengzi is the one learning.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 想 -- “他想他的朋友”：想念，specifically means missing friends who are not nearby and makes the feeling clearer in this context.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他的朋友：自己的朋友，When the subject and the possessor are the same person, use 自己的 to make that relationship clear.
- Coherence (Reference): 他的城市：自己的城市，Use 自己的 because the city belongs to the same character who does not want to leave it.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他的妈妈找到孟子一个老师：他的妈妈给孟子找到了一个老师，The completed action of finding a teacher needs 了; use 给 before 孟子 to mark him as the person receiving the teacher.
- Coherence (Temporality): 孟子跟老师学最重要的文学：孟子跟老师学了最重要的文学，Learning is presented as a completed past action here, so 学 takes 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为哲学家：成为了哲学家，Becoming a philosopher is a completed change of state in this narrative, so add 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他难怪抱怨，他想：难怪他抱怨，原来他想，Use 难怪……，原来…… to introduce the reason that explains why someone complains.
- Expressiveness: After the complaint, add a physical sign of Mengzi’s feeling, such as: “孟子低着头，看起来很难过。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech to show why he misses his friends: “他说：‘我很想我的朋友。’”
- Expressiveness: Make the teacher’s help more vivid with one detail, such as: “老师每天教孟子读书和写字。”

### Encouragement
You clearly connected Mengzi’s feelings with his earlier move away from the city. You also showed an important later step in his life by introducing a teacher and his learning.
