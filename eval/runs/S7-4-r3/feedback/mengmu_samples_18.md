## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个男孩名字叫孟子。孟子和孟子的妈妈两个人住在很穷的农村。他们住的地方旁边是个墓地，墓地老是有很多人祭拜。因为人们在祭拜，他们一直在哭。孟子和孟子的妈妈住在这里才一个星期就觉得受不了了，而且害得孟子的学习。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 already expresses a time period, so adding 的时候 is redundant. [vocab list]
- 害得孟子的学习：这里的环境害得孟子不能好好学习，害得 must be followed by the affected person and a harmful result clause; 孟子的学习 is only a noun phrase and does not state the harmful result. [teacher rule: haide_structure]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 旁边 -- “他们住的地方旁边是个墓地”：紧挨着，makes the cemetery’s very close distance more vivid and helps explain why the setting affects the family.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子的妈妈：孟子和他的妈妈，after 孟子 has just been named, use the pronoun in the possessive phrase rather than repeating his name. [rule: none]
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子的妈妈：孟子和他的妈妈，within this close re-occurrence, the pronoun makes it clear that the mother is孟子’s mother without unnecessarily repeating the name. [rule: none]
- Coherence (Temporality): 住在这里才一个星期：才在这里住了一个星期，this completed one-week stay leads to the later change of state, so the action takes 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为人们在祭拜，他们一直在哭：因为人们在祭拜，所以他们一直在哭，use 因为……所以…… to connect a cause with its result. [rule: none]
- Expressiveness: At the cemetery description, add a sound detail such as “墓地里常常传来伤心的哭声” to make the setting feel more immediate.
- Expressiveness: When the family can no longer bear the environment, show孟子’s feeling with a sentence such as “孟子听到哭声，觉得很害怕。”
- Expressiveness: Add孟子’s mother’s internal thought at the end, for example: “孟子的妈妈心想：‘这里太吵了，会影响孟子的学习。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly introduce孟子, his mother, their home, and the nearby cemetery, so the reader can easily understand the setting. The use of “才一个星期就” also effectively shows that the family quickly found the environment difficult.

## Scene 2

> 孟子的妈妈觉得孟子取得良好的教育是很重要的，所以孟子的妈妈认为这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好，就打算跟孟子搬家。孟子的妈妈带孟子不是搬到城市，而是搬到了孟子老师的家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 取得良好的教育：接受良好的教育，Education is something a person receives; 取得 is used for obtaining achievements, results, or qualifications rather than education itself. [rule: none]
- 跟孟子搬家：带着孟子搬家，This wording means that the mother takes her child with her when moving; 跟孟子搬家 does not express that relationship as clearly. [vocab list]
- 带孟子不是搬到城市，而是搬到了孟子老师的家：带孟子搬到了孟子老师的家，The “不是……而是……” pattern is for correcting a conventional or previously stated idea; there is no such idea to refute here. [rule: bushi_ershi_construction]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 地方 -- “这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好”：环境，specifies that the concern is the surrounding living and learning setting.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 所以孟子的妈妈认为：所以她认为，After the mother has just been named and no other female character intervenes, use a pronoun rather than repeat her full noun phrase. [rule: none]
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子的妈妈带孟子：她带孟子，The mother was named in the immediately preceding sentence, so a pronoun makes the connection smoother. [rule: none]
- Expressiveness: After the mother decides to move, add her worried feeling: “孟子的妈妈很着急。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short inner thought to show her reason for moving: “她心想：‘我得找一个更适合孟子学习的地方。’”
- Expressiveness: Describe the new destination more specifically, such as its atmosphere: “那儿很安静，孟子可以安心学习。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the mother’s motivation: she values her son’s education and acts because the environment is unsuitable. The cause-and-effect connection with “所以” also makes the first sentence easy to follow.

## Scene 3

> 孟子跟他的老师从早到晚学习，难怪他的成绩很快就进步了很多。孟子学习的时候，孟子的妈妈不是待在家里就是编织衣服赚钱。以后孟子成为了中国有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 以后孟子成为了中国有名的哲学家：后来孟子成为了中国有名的哲学家，At the beginning of a new sentence, this word cannot mean “afterwards”; use this alternative to move the narrative forward in time. [vocab list]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 成绩 -- “他的成绩很快就进步了很多”：学业成绩，specifies that these are his school results；成绩提高了很多，states more exactly that the grades themselves rose.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: At the learning scene, add a small detail showing how he studies, such as “孟子每天都认真地看书学习。” This makes his later academic improvement feel more vivid.
- Expressiveness: When describing his mother, add her feeling about supporting him, such as “孟母虽然很累，但是看到孟子进步，她很高兴。” This gives the mother an inner response and links her work to her son’s progress.

### Encouragement
You used “从早到晚” effectively to show sustained effort, and “难怪” clearly connects Mencius’s hard work with his improvement.
