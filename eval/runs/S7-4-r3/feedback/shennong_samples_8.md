## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个人叫神农。一天，神农正在路上走，一看很多人正在吃一些植物。一些人一吃植物就生病了，神农想帮别人。所以想知道哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，This phrase redundantly combines two time expressions; use the shorter time expression for a historical setting. [vocab list]
- 一看很多人：一看到很多人，Here the action means that Shennong saw the people, not merely looked in their direction, so the resultative complement is needed. [rule: resultative_complement]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 想知道 -- “所以想知道哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃”：弄清楚，emphasizes finding a clear answer about which plants are safe to eat.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 一看很多人正在吃一些植物。一些人一吃植物就生病了：一看到很多人正在吃一些植物，就发现一些人一吃植物就生病了，The first event needs the matching connector to show what Shennong noticed immediately after seeing the people eat plants. [teacher rule: yi_jiu]
- Expressiveness: After the people become ill, add Shennong’s concern, such as “神农很担心他们。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where he decides to investigate, add an internal thought or quoted speech, such as “神农心想：‘我一定要弄清楚哪些植物能吃。’”

### Encouragement
You introduce the main character clearly by name and use a strong cause-and-effect idea: people become ill, so he wants to learn which plants are safe. The repeated conditional pattern also makes the danger easy to understand.

## Scene 2

> 然后，神农开始尝很多植物，比方说他吃过生姜、芥兰和菠菜。一些植物吃真好，特别是黄瓜。但是，一些植物毒得很，特别是夹竹桃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一些植物吃真好：一些植物很好吃，To describe that a food tastes good, use the predicate shown in the correction; the original word order does not form a natural predicate. [rule: none]
- 毒得很：有毒，The word for poison cannot be modified with a degree complement here; use the adjective form shown in the correction. [teacher rule: du_youdu]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 尝 -- “神农开始尝很多植物”：尝试，emphasizes that he is deliberately testing the plants；试吃，highlights eating a small amount to check whether a plant is safe.
- 吃过 -- “他吃过生姜、芥兰和菠菜”：尝过，more precisely connects his eating to the careful testing of plants.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the edible-plant example, add a sensory detail to show why it is pleasant to eat, such as “黄瓜又脆又清凉。”
- Expressiveness: After the poisonous-plant sentence, show Shen Nong’s immediate reaction, such as “神农一吃夹竹桃，肚子就疼起来。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought at that moment to reveal his worry, such as “神农想：这种植物不能随便吃！”

### Encouragement
- You used a clear sequence marker to continue from the earlier scene, and the pronoun after Shen Nong’s name has a clear referent.
- The concrete examples of edible plants and the contrast with the poisonous plant make this short scene easy to follow.

## Scene 3

> 一个人对神农说，麻烦我们可以吃什么植物？神农说，这就要看你想不想生病了。以后，神农告诉别人一些植物能吃，一些植物不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 麻烦我们可以吃什么植物：请问，我们可以吃什么植物？，请问 is used to introduce a polite question; 麻烦 cannot introduce a direct question in this way. [rule: none]
- 以后：后来，When a new sentence begins with “afterwards” in a narrative, use 后来 rather than 以后. [vocab list]
- 别人：人们，别人 distinguishes other people from a stated speaker or group; here 神农 is telling the general public, so 人们 is appropriate. [teacher rule: bieren]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 告诉 -- “神农告诉别人一些植物能吃”：说明，emphasizes that Shen Nong explains which plants are edible and which are not.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 神农告诉人们一些植物能吃，一些植物不能吃：神农告诉了人们一些植物能吃，一些植物不能吃，This is a completed event in the past narrative, so the main action needs 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: After Shen Nong’s answer, add the other person’s worried reaction, for example: “那个人听了，很担心自己会生病。”
- Expressiveness: Make Shen Nong’s explanation more helpful with a short line of dialogue, for example: “能吃的植物对身体有好处，有毒的植物不能吃。”

### Encouragement
- You used dialogue clearly to show the interaction between the person and Shen Nong. You also correctly used the A-not-A form in “想不想生病,” which fits the structure after “这就要看……了.”
