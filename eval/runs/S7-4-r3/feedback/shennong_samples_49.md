## Scene 2

> 所以，他上山下山找植物，吃了很多样子的植物。他是第一人，找到了茶。他把所有的植物写下来了。他一直都吃了，他连毒都能吃。可是他还没吃了一个叫夹竹桃的植物。他找到了那个像星星的植物，觉得很漂亮，马上就吃下了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很多样子的植物：很多种植物，样子 describes an object’s appearance, while 种 identifies different kinds of plants. [rule: none]
- 他是第一人，找到了茶：他是第一个找到茶的人，when an ordinal identifies a person by an action, use 第一个 + verb phrase + 的 + 人. [rule: none]
- 一个叫夹竹桃的植物：一种叫夹竹桃的植物，种 is the appropriate measure word when referring to one type of plant; 一个 would refer to an individual object. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 上山下山 -- “他上山下山找植物”：翻山越岭，shows more vividly that he travelled across many mountains while searching.
- 毒 -- “他连毒都能吃”：有毒的植物，specifies that he ate poisonous plants rather than poison as a substance.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 他一直都吃了：他一直都吃，an ongoing habit uses the zero particle; 了 would present the action as completed. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 还没吃了：还没吃过，when saying that someone has not had an experience up to now, use 没 + verb + 过; 了 does not follow the verb here. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: At the mountain-searching spot, add a short travel detail to make the effort more vivid: “他走啊走啊，去了很多高山。”
- Expressiveness: When the flower appears, describe its colour and shape before the character eats it: “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: Before the eating action, add the character’s thought to show why he acts so quickly: “他想：这种花这么漂亮，应该没有毒。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the character’s brave, repeated testing of plants, and the sequence from finding the flower to eating it is easy to follow.
- Your use of completed actions when he found the tea and ate the flower helps the scene move forward.

## Scene 3

> 可是夹竹桃的毒很重，让他肚子很痛。他在地上躺着，觉得他快会死了。可是正好有别人找到他了，给了他一个像伞的植物，叫灵芝。他吃灵芝以后就觉得很好，不再生病了。他测量起来了，像星星的植物是毒，像伞的植物是药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别人：一个人，别人 distinguishes other people from a stated speaker or group, but no such contrast is present here; an unknown person should be introduced as 一个人. [teacher rule: bieren]
- 不再生病了：病好了，生病 means “to become ill,” so 不再生病 describes not falling ill again in the future. For recovery from the illness already described, use 病好了. [rule: word_class_misuse]
- 测量起来了：看了看，发现，测量 means measuring size or length; it does not fit the action of identifying which plants are poisonous or medicinal. 看了看，发现 introduces an observation and its result. [rule: none]
- 是毒：有毒，毒 is a noun here; 有毒 means “poisonous” and correctly describes the plant’s property. [vocab list]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 重 -- “夹竹桃的毒很重”：毒性很强，more precisely describes how powerful the poison is.
- 痛 -- “让他肚子很痛”：疼得厉害，gives a more vivid sense of the intensity of his stomach pain.
- 很好 -- “他吃灵芝以后就觉得很好”：好些了，specifically shows that his condition improved after taking the medicine；好多了，emphasizes a clear improvement from his earlier pain.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他 -- “觉得他快会死了”：omit 他，The second pronoun refers to the same person as the subject, so Chinese naturally leaves it unspoken here: 觉得快要死了. [rule: none]
- Coherence (Temporality): 找到他了：找到了他，找到 has a resultative complement, so the completed-action marker follows the whole verb-result compound: 找到了. [teacher rule: le_placement]
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃灵芝以后：吃了灵芝以后，Eating the medicine is completed before the later improvement, so this action needs 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: At “他在地上躺着,” add a physical detail to show how badly he feels, such as “他疼得满头大汗，连站起来的力气都没有。”
- Expressiveness: At the moment when the unknown person arrives, add a short line of speech to make the rescue more lively, such as “那个人说：‘快吃这个！它能治肚子疼。’”
- Expressiveness: After he recovers, add Shen Nong’s surprised feeling, such as “神农又惊讶又高兴，觉得这种像伞的植物真特别。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the important change from Shen Nong’s severe pain to feeling better after eating the medicine. You also use useful story details, including the star-shaped and umbrella-shaped plants.
