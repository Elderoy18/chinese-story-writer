## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个男人，他的名字是神农。他看到了有很多人不知道哪个植物好吃，也不知道什么植物对你的身体有好处。这个问题神农觉得很糟糕，所以神农去了吃每种植物。他吃了一种植物以后，就写了这个植物对你有没有好处。有的时候，神农吃了很好吃的植物。有的时候，他吃了很危险的植物。他吃完了每种植物以后，会来跟他的朋友聊到他吃的植物了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，The original repeats the time meaning because the phrase already names an era. [vocab list]
- 去了吃：去吃了，The aspect particle follows the main verb phrase rather than appearing between the directional verb and the action verb. [teacher rule: le_placement]
- 写了：写下了，This action means recording information about a plant, so the resultative form expresses that the information was written down. [vocab list]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很糟糕 -- “这个问题神农觉得很糟糕”：很严重，states more precisely that the problem has serious consequences；令人担忧，emphasizes that the problem makes people feel concerned.
- 危险 -- “很危险的植物”：有毒，specifies that the danger comes from poison in the plants.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 你的身体：人的身体，The sentence begins with many people as the general group, so the later second-person reference shifts the viewpoint unnecessarily; use a general noun to keep the reference consistent. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Reference): 所以神农：所以他，The character was named in the immediately preceding clause and no competing character appears, so a pronoun is the natural close re-occurrence form. [rule: redundancy_delete]
- Coherence (Reference): 对你有没有好处：对人的身体有没有好处，The general second-person reference does not match the narration about the character’s observations; a general noun makes the referent clear. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Temporality): 有的时候，神农吃了很好吃的植物：有的时候，神农吃很好吃的植物，This describes a repeated habit, so the verb takes the zero particle rather than completed-action 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 有的时候，他吃了很危险的植物：有的时候，他吃很危险的植物，This is also a routine action introduced by a habitual time phrase, so no 了 is needed. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 会来跟他的朋友聊到他吃的植物了：会来跟他的朋友聊到他吃的植物，The modal verb does not take completed-action 了; use the zero particle for an action presented as something that will happen. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: At the first mention of people not knowing which plants are safe, add a consequence to make the problem vivid: “很多人吃了有毒的植物，常常生病。”
- Expressiveness: After the dangerous-plant sentence, show the character’s physical reaction: “他的肚子突然疼了起来，疼得直冒冷汗。”
- Expressiveness: When he records his discoveries, add a brief thought to show his purpose: “神农想：我要记下这些植物，帮助更多人。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduce the central problem and show that the main character takes action to solve it.
- You also use time phrases to organize repeated events, which gives the scene a clear pattern.

## Scene 2

> 一个人问神农，哪个植物好吃。神农说，这就要看你的口味了。要是你喜欢吃甜的，有很多甜的水果，比方说苹果、西瓜和梨。一个别的人问神农，哪个植物对你的身体有好处。神农说，有很多种植物对你的身体有好处，比方说小白菜、黄瓜、茶和菠菜。一个别的人问神农，哪个植物对你的身体有坏处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一个别的人：另一个人，Use “另一个人” to introduce one additional individual. “别的” distinguishes other people or things from a stated one and does not naturally form this countable phrase. [teacher rule: bieren]
- 一个别的人：另一个人，Use “另一个人” for the next individual questioner; “一个别的人” is not the natural way to refer to one more person. [teacher rule: bieren]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 坏处 -- “哪个植物对你的身体有坏处”：有害，states more specifically that the plant harms the body；会伤害身体，makes the harmful physical effect more direct.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the fruit examples, add one sensory detail about flavour, such as “苹果又甜又脆，西瓜很清凉。”
- Expressiveness: In the sentence about beneficial plants, give one concrete use, such as “茶不但好喝，还可以做药。”
- Expressiveness: After the final question, show the possible result of harmful plants, such as “有些植物有毒，吃了会肚子疼。”

### Encouragement
You used clear question-and-answer exchanges, which makes this scene easy to follow. Your examples of fruits, vegetables, and tea also make the answers specific and understandable.

## Scene 3

> 神农说，有一天，我吃了一个黄色的花，名字是夹竹桃。我一吃夹竹桃，就觉得这种植物对我的脑有坏处。可是，一个灵芝和一个男人正好在我的旁边，我说，麻烦你把那个植物给我吃一下。我一吃这个灵芝就好了。所以，你得小心吃植物，因为有的植物对你的健康有坏处，特别是夹竹桃。因为夹竹桃有毒。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一个黄色的花：一朵黄色的花，Flowers normally take the measure word 朵 rather than the general measure word 个. [rule: none]
- 我的脑：我的大脑，脑 is usually used inside compounds; 大脑 is the natural noun for the body part in this context. [rule: none]
- 一个灵芝：一株灵芝，A growing medicinal fungus or plant can be counted with 株; 个 is not the fitting measure word here. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 那个植物：灵芝，There are two plants in the scene, so 那个植物 could refer either to the earlier flower or to the nearby medicinal fungus. Repeat the specific noun to make the intended referent clear. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为夹竹桃有毒。：夹竹桃有毒。，因为 introduces a cause and needs a following result clause; here the result has already been stated, so this sentence should state the reason independently. [rule: none]
- Expressiveness: After eating the flower, add a physical reaction to show the danger more vividly, such as: “我的肚子疼了起来，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Describe the helpful man when he appears, such as: “一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人正好走过来。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought when the speaker feels ill, such as: “我心想：我会不会死？”

### Encouragement
- You used the pattern “一……就……” correctly to show an immediate result, and your warning about harmful plants gives the scene a clear lesson.
