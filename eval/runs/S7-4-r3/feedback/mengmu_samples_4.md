## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，孟子和孟母住了在靠墓地的家。孩子才五岁就每天在墓地祭拜了。在那个城市，孟母从早到晚都找工作，可是找不到，害得孟母没有钱。难怪她跟孩子搬家，原来买不了房。他们搬了到别一个城市找工作。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，This expression redundantly combines a historical time period with a “time of” phrase.
- 跟孩子搬家：带着孩子搬家，The pattern means taking the child along when moving; 跟孩子搬家 does not express this relationship as precisely.
- 别一个城市：另一个城市，别的 distinguishes other people or things from a stated one, while 另一个 means one additional/different city.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟母：有一个小男孩叫孟子，他和妈妈，This is the first mention of the main character, so introduce him with his name and identity before using later references.
- Coherence (Reference): 孩子：他，孟子 was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other male character competes with him, so a pronoun is more natural for this close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Reference): 在那个城市：在他们住的城市里，那个城市 suggests that the city has already been identified, but it has not been mentioned; this fuller phrase makes the place clear.
- Coherence (Temporality): 住了在靠墓地的家：住在墓地旁边的家里，了 cannot come before the locative complement; moreover, 住 describes an ongoing living state here, so it does not take 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 每天在墓地祭拜了：每天在墓地祭拜，A routine marked by 每天 uses the zero particle rather than 了, because the sentence does not describe one completed act.
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬了到别一个城市：搬到了另一个城市，Put 了 after the resultative/directional complement 到: 搬到了 marks that the move was completed.
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery sentence, add a small action showing what the child does there, such as “他学着大人的样子，双手合十，嘴里还念念有词。” This makes the routine easier to picture.
- Expressiveness: At the point where the mother cannot find work, add her feeling: “孟母又累又着急，不知道该怎么办。” This gives the problem an emotional effect.
- Expressiveness: Before the move, add a short thought or line of speech from the mother, such as “孟母心想：‘这儿不能再住下去了。’” This makes the decision feel motivated.

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish the main difficulties in this scene: the cemetery setting, the mother’s job search, and the family’s move.
- You also used a strong cause-and-result idea with 难怪……原来……, which helps the reader understand the mother’s situation.

## Scene 2

> 这个城市老是很吵，不是人吵架，就是都叫，害得孟子一直不能睡觉。在那儿，孟母也找不到工作。孟子和妈妈要又一个次搬了家。在第三个城市，孟母找到工作，在商店做衣服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 要又一个次搬了家：要再搬一次家，With a modal verb for a future repeated action, use the pattern modal verb + 再 + verb + number + measure word + object. The move has not happened yet, so do not use 又 or 了; “一次” is the correct number-measure-word phrase.

- 吵 -- “这个城市老是很吵”：嘈杂，describes a place filled with mixed, unpleasant noise and fits the reason Mengzi cannot sleep.
- 不能睡觉 -- “孟子一直不能睡觉”：难以入睡，more specifically describes having trouble falling asleep because of the noise.
- 做衣服 -- “在商店做衣服”：缝制衣服，specifies that her work involves sewing and making clothing.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 人：人们，This refers to a concrete group making noise in the city, so use 人们 rather than the generic noun 人.
- Coherence (Temporality): 找到工作：找到了工作，Finding the job is a completed action before the description of her work in the shop, so it needs 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 在第三个城市：后来，他们搬到了第三个城市，The previous sentence states a plan to move; later introduces the next event in sequence and makes the completed move clear.
- Expressiveness: After “这个城市老是很吵,” add one concrete sound source, such as “商人从早到晚大声叫卖”.
- Expressiveness: Show Mengzi’s feeling after “一直不能睡觉,” for example: “孟子又累又烦，躺在床上翻来覆去”.
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought or line of speech for Mengmu before the move, such as “孟母心想：‘这儿太吵了，孩子没法好好休息。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly connect the noisy environment to Mengzi’s sleeping problem with “害得,” which gives the sentence a strong cause-and-effect relationship. You also use the known characters’ names consistently, so the reader can follow who is affected and who finds work.

## Scene 3

> 孟子在那个商店也找了到工作，可是不是做衣服的工作，而是喜欢的工作。孟子学习馆学了十五年，他的学习和他的旅行经验很重要，对他很多好处。他是大人的时候，他成为很有名的哲人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 找了到：找到了，The completion marker must follow the resultative complement: the order is verb + resultative complement + 了.
- 孟子学习馆学了十五年：孟子在学校学习了十五年，A place before an action needs 在, and 学习馆 is not the usual word for the school where someone studies.
- 对他很多好处：对他有很多好处，The fixed predicate is 对……有好处; 有 is required before 好处.
- 成为很有名的哲人：成为了很有名的哲人，This is a completed change of status, so 成为 takes 了.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 做 -- “不是做衣服的工作”：缝制，specifies that the work involves sewing garments；制作，emphasizes making garments rather than doing an unspecified kind of work
- 有名 -- “成为很有名的哲人”：享有盛名的，emphasizes that his reputation is widely recognized

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他的学习和他的旅行经验：他的学习和旅行经验，After the first possessive pronoun establishes the owner for both coordinated nouns, use zero form rather than repeating the pronoun in the same phrase.
- Expressiveness: At the moment he finds a job he likes, add his reaction or quoted speech, such as: “孟子高兴地说：‘这正是我想做的工作！’”
- Expressiveness: In the fifteen-year study period, add one concrete learning habit to show his effort, such as: “十五年里，他每天认真读书，还常常向老师请教。”
- Expressiveness: Before his later achievement, add a brief reflection on what he learned, such as: “他觉得，学习和旅行让他懂得了很多道理。”

### Encouragement
You used a clear contrast with “不是……而是……”, which helps the reader understand that the two jobs are different. The long time span of fifteen years also gives the final achievement a strong sense of development.
