### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 旅行 -- “神农和他的哥哥一起去旅行找可以吃的植物”：考察，identifies the trip more specifically as an investigation connected with finding plants.
- 不一样 -- “他们去很多不一样的地方”：地形各异，specifies that the places differ in their natural landscapes, matching the following examples of mountains, valleys, and deserts.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：一个叫神农的人，This is the character’s first mention, so introduce him with a name and a full noun phrase rather than using only the name.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农和他的哥哥：他和他的哥哥，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the referent has just been established and no other character competes as the main referent.
- Coherence (Temporality): 常常生病了：常常生病，A routine or repeated condition takes the zero particle; 了 marks a completed event and does not fit with 常常 here.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们去很多不一样的地方：他们去了很多不一样的地方，The visits to many places are completed actions in this past narrative, so the verb needs 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 神农和他的家人住在古代，所以不知道什么植物能吃：在古代，神农和他的家人住在一起，不知道什么植物能吃，Ancient times provide the time setting rather than a direct cause, so 所以 should not link these two ideas as cause and effect.
- Expressiveness: After the family’s illness, add Shen Nong’s feeling to show his reason for acting: “神农看见家人又生病了，心里很着急。”
- Expressiveness: When describing the journey, add sensory details about the landscapes: “高山又高又冷，沙漠里又干又热。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of dialogue before the trip to make the goal more vivid: “神农对哥哥说：‘我们去找能吃的植物吧！’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect the family’s repeated illness to Shen Nong’s wish to find edible plants, and your list of mountains, valleys, and deserts gives the journey a broad sense of adventure.
