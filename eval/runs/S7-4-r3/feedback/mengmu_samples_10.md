## Scene 1

> 孟子小的时候，从早到晚都跟朋友玩。孟子和他的朋友喜欢玩在墓地里，装祭拜。孟子五岁的时候，跟妈妈搬家。他们搬家不是因为孟子玩得太多，而是因为妈妈认为他们的家离墓地太近。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 玩在墓地里：在墓地里玩，A location phrase normally comes before the main action verb.
- 装祭拜：装作祭拜，装作 must be followed by an action phrase to express pretending to do that action.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子小的时候：有一个小男孩叫孟子。他小的时候，The first mention should introduce the character with a full noun phrase and name; later in the same scene, use a pronoun for the already introduced character.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和他的朋友：他和朋友，Use a pronoun when the same character was mentioned in the immediately preceding sentence and no competing referent is present.
- Coherence (Temporality): 跟妈妈搬家：跟妈妈搬了家，This is one completed move at age five, so the separable verb needs 了 after the verb.
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery-play sentence, add Mengzi’s feeling to explain why the activity attracts him, for example: “孟子觉得祭拜很有趣。”
- Expressiveness: At the end, add the mother’s worry to make the reason for moving more vivid, for example: “妈妈很担心孟子会学坏。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly state the time, place, and reason for the move, and the contrast structure with 不是……而是…… effectively explains the family’s decision.

## Scene 2

> 妈妈认为，住离墓地太近，害得孟子的心气。孟子和孟母搬到子思老师的家，跟子思老师住。孟母帮子思打扫房间和做饭。子思帮孟母教她的儿子。因为孟子现在没有朋友，他老是读书。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 住离墓地太近：住得离墓地太近，A verb followed by a degree/complement phrase uses 得 before the complement.
- 害得孟子的心气：害得孟子心情不好，害得 must be followed by the affected person and a harmful result; 心情不好 gives the required result and fits this meaning.
- 老是读书：一直读书，老是 has a negative, complaining tone, while reading here is presented as a neutral activity.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 做饭 -- “孟母帮子思打扫房间和做饭”：准备饭菜，emphasizes preparing the meal and makes the household work more concrete

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬到子思老师的家：搬到了子思老师的家，This move is a completed past action, so 搬到 takes 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为孟子现在没有朋友，他一直读书：因为孟子现在没有朋友，所以他一直读书，Because the lack of friends is presented as the cause of his reading, use the paired cause-and-effect connector 因为……所以…….
- Expressiveness: After the move, add one quiet detail about the new home, such as `子思老师的家很安静，书架上摆满了书。`
- Expressiveness: Show Mengzi’s feeling about having no friends, such as `孟子觉得有一点孤单，可是他慢慢喜欢上了读书。`
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of dialogue when Zisi teaches him, such as `子思老师说：“读书的时候要认真思考。”`

### Encouragement
- You clearly show how each person helps in the new home: Mengmu does household work, while Zisi teaches Mengzi.
- The cause in the final sentence is easy to understand, and it gives a clear reason for Mengzi’s new habit.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
