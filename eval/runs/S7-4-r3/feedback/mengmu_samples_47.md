## Scene 1

> 这个男孩子叫孟子，孟子是古代的人。孟子的妈妈觉得住在哪儿对童年很重要，所以他们常常搬了家。她望子成龙，所以孟子的妈妈安排他的生活。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 常常搬了家：常常搬家，A habitual action uses the zero particle; 了 marks a completed event.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 这个男孩子叫孟子，孟子是古代的人。：在古代，有一个男孩子叫孟子。，On first mention, introduce the character with an indefinite full noun phrase rather than using a demonstrative phrase that assumes the reader already knows him.
- Coherence (Reference): 她望子成龙，所以孟子的妈妈安排他的生活。：她望子成龙，所以安排他的生活。，When the same character continues as the subject in the next clause, use zero form instead of repeating the noun phrase.
- Expressiveness: At the point where the mother thinks about where to live, add her concern in a short inner thought, such as: 孟母心想：“住的地方会影响孟子的学习。”
- Expressiveness: After mentioning the frequent moves, add one concrete detail about an environment that affects Mengzi, such as: 孟子每天看到很多人来墓地祭拜。
- Expressiveness: Show the mother’s feeling before she arranges Mengzi’s life, such as: 孟母很着急，想让孟子认真学习。

### Encouragement
- You used the precise idiom “望子成龙” well to show the mother’s hopes for Mengzi.
- The cause-and-effect connector “所以” clearly links the mother’s belief with the family’s actions.

## Scene 2

> 因为他们常常搬了家，中国人说，孟母三迁。孟子没有爸爸，所以，他的妈妈更重要。从这儿到那儿，妈妈教孟子很多技能。先她让孟子跟朋友玩儿，然后教他怎么买又便宜又好质量的东西。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 先她：她先，A temporal adverb such as “先” normally follows the subject and comes before the verb.
- 好质量的东西：高质量的东西，“好” can describe quality in a predicate such as “质量好”, but the standard attributive phrase before a noun is “高质量的”.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 技能 -- “妈妈教孟子很多技能”：生活本领，specifies that these are useful everyday abilities；本领，emphasizes abilities that a child learns to do independently.
- 买 -- “教他怎么买又便宜又好质量的东西”：选购，specifies choosing items carefully while buying them, especially by considering price and quality.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 妈妈教孟子很多技能：她教孟子很多技能，The mother was mentioned in the immediately previous sentence and no other female character competes as the referent, so use a pronoun for this close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 常常搬了家：常常搬家，“常常” describes a habitual action, so it takes the zero particle rather than completed-action “了”.
- Expressiveness: At the buying-skills spot, add a short line of dialogue to show what Mengzi practises: “这个太贵了，能便宜一点儿吗？”
- Expressiveness: After the mother teaches him, add her purpose or feeling: “妈妈希望孟子学会照顾自己。”
- Expressiveness: Add a small reaction from Mengzi after learning the skill: “孟子觉得很有意思，也想自己试一试。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly organize the scene around the mother teaching Mengzi practical skills, and the sequence with “先” and “然后” helps the reader follow the events.
- The detail about choosing inexpensive, good-quality items is concrete and makes the lesson feel useful.
