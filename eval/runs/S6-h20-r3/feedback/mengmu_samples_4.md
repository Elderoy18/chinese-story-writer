## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，孟子和孟母住了在靠墓地的家。孩子才五岁就每天在墓地祭拜了。在那个城市，孟母从早到晚都找工作，可是找不到，害得孟母没有钱。难怪她跟孩子搬家，原来买不了房。他们搬了到别一个城市找工作。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，this time expression is not idiomatic in narrative Chinese; use “在古代” or “古代”.
- 住了在靠墓地的家：住在靠近墓地的家里，location phrases follow “住在”, and the state verb “住” does not take “了” here.
- 她跟孩子搬家：她带着孩子搬了家，“跟孩子搬家” is not the appropriate expression for moving together with one’s child; “带着孩子搬家” means taking the child along. Use “了” because the move is completed.
- 搬了到别一个城市：搬到了另一个城市，when “搬” is followed by the directional complement “到”, “了” goes after “到”; “另一个” is the correct phrase for another city.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 买不了房 -- “原来买不了房”：无力买房，emphasizes that financial circumstances make buying a home impossible in this context.
- 没有钱 -- “害得孟母没有钱”：手头拮据，describes having insufficient money available and gives the situation more detail.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟母：有一个小男孩叫孟子，他和孟母，first mention should introduce the character with a name and a brief identity before later references.
- Coherence (Reference): 孩子才五岁：他才五岁，use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when “孟子” was named in the previous sentence and no other male character competes.
- Coherence (Reference): 在那个城市：在一个城市里，this is the first mention of the city, so it should be introduced with an indefinite full noun phrase rather than “那个”.
- Coherence (Reference): 害得孟母没有钱：害得她没有钱，within the same sentence, use a pronoun rather than repeating the same character’s name.
- Coherence (Temporality): 每天在墓地祭拜了：每天在墓地祭拜，routine actions take the zero particle, so “了” should not be used.
- Coherence (Temporality): 从早到晚都找工作：从早到晚都在找工作，“在” marks the ongoing dynamic action that lasts from morning to evening.
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery action, add what the child notices and feels, such as “他看到很多人哭，觉得祭拜很有趣。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where the mother cannot find work, show her worry through an inner thought, such as “孟母很着急，心想：‘这样下去怎么办？’”
- Expressiveness: You could make the move more vivid with a short action detail, such as “孟母收拾好东西，带着孟子离开了墓地旁的家。”

### Encouragement
- You used time and place details such as “才五岁”, “从早到晚”, and “在墓地”, which help the reader understand when and where the events happen.
- The cause-and-effect idea in “找不到，害得……” clearly shows why the mother has financial difficulties.

## Scene 2

> 这个城市老是很吵，不是人吵架，就是都叫，害得孟子一直不能睡觉。在那儿，孟母也找不到工作。孟子和妈妈要又一个次搬了家。在第三个城市，孟母找到工作，在商店做衣服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 人吵架，就是都叫：人们吵架，就是人们大叫，Use 人们 for a concrete group of people; 都 only quantifies an already established plural subject and cannot replace the subject in the second part of this parallel structure.
- 要又一个次搬了家：要再搬一次家，Use 再 for a move that is planned but has not happened yet; the measure-word phrase is 一次, not 一个次; after the modal verb 要, use the zero aspect form rather than 了 for a future action.
- 找到工作：找到了工作，Add 了 because finding the job is a completed action.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 吵 -- “这个城市老是很吵”：嘈杂，specifies that the city has unpleasant mixed noise that makes resting difficult.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟母找到工作：她找到了工作，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the same person was mentioned in the previous sentence and no other female character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 在那儿，孟母也找不到工作。孟子和妈妈要：在那儿，孟母也找不到工作，所以孟子和妈妈要，Add 所以 to show the cause-and-effect link: the noisy city and the lack of work lead to the family’s decision to move.
- Expressiveness: After the description of noise, add one concrete sound to help the reader imagine it, such as “晚上还有人在街上大声喊叫。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where Mengzi cannot sleep, add his feeling or a short line of speech, such as “孟子很累，说：‘妈妈，我睡不着。’”
- Expressiveness: When Mengmu cannot find work, show her worry with a brief action or thought, such as “孟母叹了一口气，心里很着急。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show two difficulties in the city—the noise and the lack of work—and you use 害得 effectively to connect the noise with Mengzi’s inability to sleep.

## Scene 3

> 孟子在那个商店也找了到工作，可是不是做衣服的工作，而是喜欢的工作。孟子学习馆学了十五年，他的学习和他的旅行经验很重要，对他很多好处。他是大人的时候，他成为很有名的哲人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 学习馆学了十五年：在学习馆学了十五年，A place used before an action verb needs the locative marker 在.
- 对他很多好处：对他有很多好处，The pattern is 对 + person + 有 + 好处; 有 is required to state that something brings benefits.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 有名 -- “他成为很有名的哲人”：广为人知，specifies that many people know him and makes his reputation more concrete.

### Coherence & Expressiveness

- Coherence (Temporality): 找了到工作：找到了工作，With a result complement, 到 comes directly after 找, and the completed-action marker 了 follows the complement.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他成为很有名的哲人：他成为了很有名的哲人，Use 了 for a completed change of state: becoming a philosopher is presented as a finished event in the narrative.
- Expressiveness: After the long period of study, add a feeling to show why learning mattered to him, for example: “孟子觉得读书很有意思，每天都想学更多东西。”
- Expressiveness: At the final achievement, add a brief reaction that makes the success feel more vivid, for example: “孟子很高兴，因为他终于实现了自己的梦想。”
- Expressiveness: You could add one concrete detail about his studies, for example: “他每天看很多书，也常常跟老师讨论问题。”

### Encouragement
