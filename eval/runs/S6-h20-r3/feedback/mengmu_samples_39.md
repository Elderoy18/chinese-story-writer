## Scene 1

> 古代的时候人觉得祭拜非常重要，孟子很小的时候，从早到晚一直祭拜，因为他妈妈告诉他祭拜很重要。虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜。他的同学们老是对他开玩笑了。孟子妈妈看了他的同学们对孟子不好的影响，所以孟子和孟子妈妈搬家了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候人：在古代，人们，“古代的时候” should be replaced by “在古代” for this time expression; “人” is generic and should be “人们” when referring to a group of people.
- 看了：看到了，“看” means to look at something, while “看到” means to see or notice something; this sentence means that she noticed the influence.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很小 -- “孟子很小的时候”：年幼，specifies that he was in early childhood and fits naturally with “的时候”.
- 不好的 -- “同学们对孟子不好的影响”：不良的，more specifically describes a harmful or negative influence.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子很小的时候：有一个小男孩叫孟子。孟子很小的时候，first mention rule: introduce a new character with a name and a brief description before using the name in the narrative.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子妈妈搬家了：孟子和他妈妈搬家了，pronoun rule: after the mother has just been mentioned and no other mother competes as a referent, use a pronoun rather than repeat the full noun phrase.
- Coherence (Temporality): 同学们老是对他开玩笑了：同学们老是对他开玩笑，zero-particle rule: “老是” describes a repeated habitual action, so it does not take completed-action “了”.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜：因为他每天只祭拜，所以他没有朋友，cause relation: use “因为……所以……” to show that his constant worshipping caused him to have no friends; “虽然” requires a contrasting result clause.
- Expressiveness: After “从早到晚一直祭拜”, add a small action detail to show what he did: “他学着大人的样子，低头祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: After the classmates’ teasing, add Mengzi’s feeling: “孟子觉得很难过，不知道自己做错了什么。”
- Expressiveness: At the moment his mother notices the problem, add her inner thought or speech: “孟母心想：‘这里不适合孩子长大。’”

### Encouragement
- You used clear cause-and-effect ideas, especially in explaining why Mengzi worshipped and why the family moved. You also used “从早到晚一直” well to show how long the action lasted.

## Scene 2

> 孟子才十一岁，成为一个哲学家。孟子不是祭拜，而是看政治文章，难怪他成为了哲学家。不过，看过很多政治文章对他害得，他有很多问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 才十一岁，成为：才十一岁，就成为，The pattern for an early achievement is 才 + age/number + 就 + verb.
- 对他害得：害得他，害得 must introduce the person affected before the result: subject + 害得 + person + result.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子不是祭拜：他不是祭拜，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the same character was named in the previous sentence and no other character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为一个哲学家：成为了一个哲学家，Use 了 for the completed change of state of becoming a philosopher.
- Expressiveness: At the point where he reads, add a detail about his focused learning: “他每天安静地看书学习。”
- Expressiveness: After “他有很多问题,” show his thoughts or feelings about the difficult questions: “他觉得这些问题很难，可是很想找到答案。”
- Expressiveness: You could add a short line from his mother to show her reaction: “孟母高兴地说：‘你现在越来越喜欢学习了！’”

### Encouragement
- You used the contrast pattern “不是……而是……” correctly to show that Mengzi changed what he did. The phrase “难怪” also clearly connects his reading with his becoming a philosopher.

## Scene 3

> 他一直问问题，但是不能解决那些问题。孟子长大，他开始能解决问题。他常常建议你不是学金融，而是学政治，因为如果你懂政治，你会成为高薪。后来孟子成为教授，他教学生很厉害。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 成为高薪：成为高薪人士，"高薪" means a high salary, so a person cannot become it; "高薪人士" means a person with a high salary.
- 建议你不是学金融，而是学政治：建议你不要学金融，而要学政治，after "建议", use "不要……而要……" to recommend one action instead of another; "不是……而是……" states a factual contrast.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 厉害 -- “他教学生很厉害”：很有办法，emphasizes that he knows effective ways to teach students；教得很好，states more precisely that his teaching is effective.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子长大：他长大了，use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the same character was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Reference): 你：学生，this second-person pronoun has no introduced addressee and abruptly addresses the reader; use a full noun phrase to identify who receives his advice.
- Coherence (Temporality): 孟子长大：他长大了，use "了" after "长大" to mark the completed change of state.
- Coherence (Temporality): 后来孟子成为教授：后来孟子成为了教授，use "了" to mark the completed change of becoming a professor.
- Expressiveness: At “他一直问问题”, add an inner feeling to show why he keeps asking. Example: “可是很多问题他都想不明白，心里很着急。”
- Expressiveness: At “他教学生很厉害”, add a concrete teaching action. Example: “学生问问题的时候，他总是耐心地一步一步解释。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of advice after his recommendation to make his character more vivid. Example: “他说：‘学政治能帮助你们了解社会。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly show a change in Mengzi’s life: he cannot solve problems when young, but later becomes capable and teaches others. You also used “后来” effectively to move the narrative forward in time.

## End of story

- After leaving the graveyard, the story needs the part where they move near a market and 孟子 copies people buying and selling every day.
- After the market, the story needs the part where 孟母 moves them near a school, and then 孟子 begins to study every day.
- The ending should connect these moves and his studying to the result that 孟子 grows up to become a famous philosopher, rather than adding a separate story about political articles, finance, and becoming a professor.

### Overall Encouragement
