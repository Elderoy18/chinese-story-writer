## Scene 1

> 很久以前，有一个老男人叫神农。神农在古代每一天看到了别的小镇人生病还是不舒服，所以他决定开始找新的药。他下一天开始爬当地的山脉，找了各种各样的植物。神农试一试每个植物，知道有什么好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别的小镇人：小镇的人们，Here, “别的” means people or things other than a previously mentioned one, but no comparison town has been established; “人们” is needed for a concrete group of residents.
- 生病还是不舒服：生病或者感到不舒服，In this affirmative statement, “或者” links two alternatives. “还是” is normally used in a question or in an expression of uncertainty.
- 下一天：第二天，“第二天” is the natural narrative expression for the next day.
- 爬当地的山脉：爬当地的山，“山脉” is a connected geographical range and is not normally the direct object of “爬”; a person climbs individual mountains.
- 找了各种各样的植物：找到了各种各样的植物，“找” means to look for, while “找到” means to find. The sentence refers to plants that he found before testing them.
- 试一试：尝一尝，“试” means to test or try something in general, while “尝” specifically means to taste or eat a plant.
- 每个植物：每种植物，“种” is the appropriate measure word for different kinds of plants.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好处 -- “知道有什么好处”：药用价值，specifies that he is learning about each plant’s possible medicinal use.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农试一试每个植物：他尝一尝每种植物，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence: “神农” was the subject of the immediately preceding sentence, and no competing male character has appeared.
- Coherence (Temporality): 每一天看到了：每天看到，Use the zero particle for a habitual action. “每天” describes a routine, so completed-action “了” is not used.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 神农在古代每天看到小镇的人们生病或者感到不舒服，所以：因为神农在古代每天看到小镇的人们生病或者感到不舒服，所以，Use the paired connector “因为……所以……” to make the cause-and-effect relation explicit.
- Expressiveness: When he sees sick residents, add his concern and determination, such as: “他很难过，心想：我一定要找到能治病的植物。”
- Expressiveness: While he climbs, add one concrete detail about the difficult journey, such as: “山路又陡又难走，可是他没有放弃。”
- Expressiveness: When he tastes plants, add a simple reaction to show careful observation, such as: “他尝了一口，认真地记下这种植物的味道。”

### Encouragement
- You introduced the main character clearly at the beginning and used a logical reason for his decision to search for medicine.
- “各种各样的植物” is a strong, specific phrase that makes his exploration sound broad and purposeful.

## Scene 2

> 比方说，他一次试一试有些植物的叶，吃得又新鲜又甜。煮这些叶以后，他一喝就知道他发现新的茶叶。虽然他找到了很多的新的植物，但是他的旅程有的时候特别是危险。比方说，他一吃灵芝就开始生病。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- “他一次试一试有些植物的叶”：“他尝了有些植物的叶子”，verb reduplication in “试一试” is for a light, tentative try, not for an action that was actually carried out; “尝了” expresses that he tasted the leaves.
- “吃得又新鲜又甜”：“这些叶子吃起来又新鲜又甜”，“新鲜、甜” describe the leaves’ taste, not the manner of eating; use “吃起来” to describe how food tastes.
- “知道他发现”：“知道自己发现”，“自己” is needed when the subject knows something about himself; “他” would normally refer to another male person.
- “有的时候特别是危险”：“有的时候特别危险”，“特别是” must introduce a specific item, time, or situation; it cannot directly modify an adjective such as “危险”.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 新的 -- “很多的新的植物”：陌生的，emphasizes that these are plants he did not know before, which fits his exploration；未尝过的，more specifically shows that he has not tasted these plants before.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): “煮这些叶以后”：“煮了这些叶以后”，use 了 for the completed action that happens before the later action introduced by “以后”.
- Coherence (Temporality): “发现新的茶叶”：“发现了新的茶叶”，use 了 for a completed discovery in the “一……就……” sequence.
- Expressiveness: After he drinks the boiled leaves, add his surprise or pleasure, such as “他觉得又香又暖，心里很惊喜。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech when he discovers the tea, such as “原来这种叶子可以泡水喝！”
- Expressiveness: At “旅程……危险”, give one concrete detail of the danger, such as “有的时候，山路很滑，他差一点儿摔倒。”

### Encouragement
- You use “一……就……” well to show an immediate result, and “虽然……但是……” clearly introduces a contrast between his discoveries and the danger of the journey.

## Scene 3

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- “看到神农的麻烦”： “看到神农遇到了麻烦”，Use “看到 + clause” to describe seeing an event or situation; “遇到了麻烦” describes the trouble that Shennong is experiencing.
- “神农最终他的小镇回到”： “神农最终回到了他的小镇”，The verb “回到” must come before its destination, and “了” marks the completed return.
- “你认为神农的故事真的吗？”： “你认为神农的故事是真的吗？”，“真的” needs “是” before it when it is the predicate in this yes-or-no question.
