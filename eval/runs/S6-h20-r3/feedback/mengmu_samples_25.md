## Scene 1

> 这是古代的故事。孟子小的时候，他的爸爸很早就去世了。从他的家到墓地很近，所以他每天老是从早到晚去墓地跟朋友们玩玩儿。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 从早到晚去墓地跟朋友们玩玩儿：从早到晚在墓地跟朋友们玩，the reduplicated verb “玩玩儿” usually suggests playing briefly or casually, which conflicts with an activity lasting from morning to evening. The location phrase should also describe where the long activity happens.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 这是古代的故事。孟子小的时候：在古代，有一个小男孩叫孟子。他小的时候，first mention should introduce a character with a full noun phrase, such as a name plus who the person is, before using a pronoun.
- Expressiveness: After the father’s death, add a simple detail about the child’s feelings, such as “孟子很想爸爸，觉得很难过。”
- Expressiveness: At the cemetery, describe what Mengzi notices to make the setting clearer, such as “他常常看到有人来墓地祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: Add Mengzi’s reaction to the cemetery activity, such as “他觉得很好奇，也想学一学。”

### Encouragement

## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈觉得在墓地玩儿，害得，所以他们搬家。搬家对孟子有好的影响，他才五岁，可是每天不是写字就是帮妈妈的工作。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在墓地玩儿，害得：孟子在墓地玩儿会带来不好的影响，English explanation: “害得” is a causative verb and must be followed by the affected person and an adverse result; it cannot end the clause. This revision also states clearly who is playing there.
- 帮妈妈的工作：帮妈妈做事，English explanation: after “帮 + person,” use an action phrase to say what help is given. “妈妈的工作” is a noun phrase, while “做事” supplies the needed action.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好的 -- “搬家对孟子有好的影响”：积极的，more specifically describes the effect as beneficial and constructive in this context.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他的妈妈：孟子的妈妈，First mention rule: the mother has not appeared in the earlier scene, so introduce her clearly through her relationship to the already-known character.
- Coherence (Temporality): 所以他们搬家：所以他们搬家了，The action of moving is a completed event in the narrative, so it needs the completion marker “了.”
- Expressiveness: At the cemetery-playing spot, add a concrete action to show what the children do, such as “孟子跟朋友学着别人祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: When the mother decides to move, add her worry in quoted speech, such as “孟子的妈妈心想：‘再住下去，麻烦会越来越多。’”
- Expressiveness: After describing the new routine, add the mother’s feeling, such as “孟子的妈妈看到孟子每天学习，心里很高兴。”

### Encouragement
- You used “所以” to show a cause-and-effect relationship, and you also tried the useful pattern “不是……就是……”. These are strong storytelling structures to keep practising.
