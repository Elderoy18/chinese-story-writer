## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个人叫神农。一天，神农正在路上走，一看很多人正在吃一些植物。一些人一吃植物就生病了，神农想帮别人。所以想知道哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，This is the standard expression for setting a story in an ancient time period; the original phrase is not used naturally here.
- 一看：一看到，When the verb means “to see/noticesomething,” use “看到”; “看” means “to look at.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 想知道 -- “所以想知道哪些植物能吃”：想弄清楚，emphasizes wanting to find a clear answer after investigating.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 。一些人一吃植物就生病了：，就发现一些人一吃植物就生病了，The pattern “一……就……” needs “就” to introduce the result that follows the event of seeing.
- Expressiveness: After seeing people become ill, add Shennong’s feeling and thought: 神农很担心，心想：“我得弄清楚哪些植物能吃。”
- Expressiveness: Add one visible sign of illness to make the scene more vivid: 有的人肚子疼得站不起来。

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduce Shennong and establish his goal in this scene. The paired structure “哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃” is accurate and makes his question easy to understand.

## Scene 2

> 然后，神农开始尝很多植物，比方说他吃过生姜、芥兰和菠菜。一些植物吃真好，特别是黄瓜。但是，一些植物毒得很，特别是夹竹桃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一些植物吃真好：一些植物很好吃，Use the adjective form after the subject to describe that the plants taste good; `吃真好` is not a natural verb-complement structure here.
- 一些植物毒得很：一些植物有毒，`毒` does not take degree complements such as `得很`; use `有毒` to describe poisonous plants.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很多 -- “神农开始尝很多植物”：各种各样的，specifies that he tried a wide range of different kinds of plants.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After “特别是黄瓜”, add a sensory detail such as “黄瓜又脆又清爽”, so the reader can understand why it is good to eat.
- Expressiveness: Before mentioning `夹竹桃`, describe its appearance with “夹竹桃开着像星星一样的黄花，又大又漂亮”, creating a stronger contrast between its beautiful look and its danger.
- Expressiveness: After “特别是夹竹桃”, add a feeling or physical reaction such as “神农吃了以后，肚子疼了起来”, to make the danger more vivid.

### Encouragement
- You clearly contrast plants that are good to eat with dangerous plants, and the examples `黄瓜` and `夹竹桃` make the scene easy to follow.

## Scene 3

> 一个人对神农说，麻烦我们可以吃什么植物？神农说，这就要看你想不想生病了。以后，神农告诉别人一些植物能吃，一些植物不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 麻烦我们可以吃什么植物？：请问，我们可以吃什么植物？，麻烦 cannot be used alone before a question to mean “please ask”; 请问 is the polite question-opening expression.
- 这就要看你想不想生病了：这就要看是哪种植物了，this pattern needs the part after 看 to be a question form relevant to the earlier question; 哪种植物 identifies the condition that determines whether a plant can be eaten.
- 以后：后来，when a new sentence means “afterwards” in a narrative, use 后来 rather than 以后.
- 别人：人们，别人 means “other people” in contrast with a particular person or group; this sentence refers generally to the public, so 人们 fits.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 神农告诉人们：神农告诉了人们，this is a completed event in the sequence after 后来, so the completed action takes 了; it is not a speaking verb directly introducing quoted speech.
- Expressiveness: At the question, add the person's worry to show why the question matters: 一个人着急地问：“我们吃什么植物才不会生病？”
- Expressiveness: When explaining the plants, add a concrete contrast from the story: 神农告诉人们：“像伞一样的植物是灵芝，可以吃；像星星一样的花有毒，不能吃。”
- Expressiveness: Add a feeling or manner to show Shennong’s helpful character: 神农耐心地告诉人们哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃。

### Encouragement
- You used a question-and-answer exchange effectively, which makes the scene feel like a real conversation.
- You also connected this scene to the earlier plant-testing events by explaining that some plants can be eaten and others cannot.
