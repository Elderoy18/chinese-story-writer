## Scene 1

> 古代的时候有一个人叫神农。他需要当上医，一看别的人就想帮助他们。但是，他有一个问题，他不知道什么东西能做药。神农听说一个远远的地方有一种植物，什么病人吃都会觉得身体更好。神农三个月走去，找到有植物的地方，他看到一个很漂亮的植物，又香又好看。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 当上医：当上医生，医 is usually a bound morpheme in this meaning; the full noun for a person who practises medicine is 医生.
- 一看别的人：一看到别的人，when 看 means “see/notices,” use 看到 rather than 看; this also correctly forms the conditional pattern 一……就…….
- 什么病人吃都会觉得身体更好：病人一吃这种植物，就会觉得身体好多了，什么病人 asks “what kind of patients” and cannot mean all patients here; use the conditional pattern 一……就…… to express what happens whenever a patient eats the plant.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 东西 -- “他不知道什么东西能做药”：植物，identifies the exact kind of thing he is trying to learn about.
- 帮助 -- “一看到别的人就想帮助他们”：救治，specifies the medical help that a doctor gives.
- 有 -- “找到有植物的地方”：长着，shows that the plants are growing in that place.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 但是，他有一个问题，他不知道什么东西能做药：但是，他有一个问题：不知道什么东西能做药，after the subject has already appeared in the same sentence, use zero form rather than repeating the pronoun.
- Coherence (Temporality): 神农三个月走去，找到有植物的地方：神农走了三个月，才找到了有植物的地方，completed narrative actions need 了, and a duration such as 三个月 follows the verb it measures; 才 also connects the long journey with the later result.
- Expressiveness: When the plant first appears, add a visual comparison: “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花。”
- Expressiveness: After hearing about the plant, show Shen Nong’s motivation or hope: “神农想：如果这种植物真的能治病，我一定要去看看。”
- Expressiveness: Add a feeling after the long journey to make the action more vivid: “他虽然很累，但是看见植物以后很高兴。”

### Encouragement
- You introduce Shen Nong clearly and give him a meaningful goal. The details “又香又好看” also begin to use more than one sense to describe the plant.

## Scene 2

> 他觉得，这种一定是做药的花。原来他想错了。吃了以后，神农快生病。 这时一个穿着蓝色衣服的人帮神农，快给他一种红的灵芝。神农吃了以后，他没有病，想起来以前一个人告诉他，灵芝对身体有好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 快生病：很快就生病了，Use 很快 to mean “soon/quickly” in this past event, and use 了 because becoming ill is a completed change of state.
- 帮神农，快给他一种红的灵芝：帮神农，赶快给了他一种红色的灵芝，赶快 is the appropriate adverb for an urgent action in narration, and 给 takes 了 here because the giving action was completed.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 做药 -- “这种一定是做药的花”：能入药，more precisely means that a plant can be used as medicine.
- 没有病 -- “神农吃了以后，他没有病”：病好了，clearly expresses recovery from the illness rather than simply the state of not being ill.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农吃了以后，他没有病：神农吃了以后，没有病，Use zero form after 神农 within the same sentence; repeating 他 is unnecessary when the referent has not changed.
- Coherence (Temporality): 想起来以前一个人告诉他：想起了以前有一个人告诉过他，想起 is a completed recollection here, so it needs 了; use 有一个人 to introduce the indefinite person, and 过 marks that the telling happened in the past.
- Expressiveness: At the moment he becomes ill, add a physical feeling to show the danger: “他的肚子疼了起来，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Make the helper’s urgency more vivid with a short line of speech: “快吃这个，也许能帮你！”
- Expressiveness: Describe the medicinal plant with one clear visual detail when it appears: “那种灵芝像一把小伞。”

### Encouragement
- You used time expressions to move the scene forward clearly, especially with the eating event and the helper’s arrival.
- You also introduced the new character with a useful visual detail, which helps the reader picture the scene.

## Scene 3

> 啊，他怎么忘记了？他把灵芝放在包里。一回家就告诉别人。他找到药以后他写下怎么做药在书上。所以人们都知道，那红灵芝对身体好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别人：人们，别人 distinguishes other people from a person or group already being discussed; here the sentence means people in general.
- 写下怎么做药在书上：在书上写下做药的方法，place phrases normally come before the verb, and adding 的方法 turns “how to make medicine” into a noun phrase that can be written down.
- 对身体好处：对身体有好处，this expression requires 有 between 对身体 and 好处.
- 那红灵芝：那株红灵芝，this/that + noun needs a measure word when referring to one specific plant.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他找到药以后他写下：他找到药以后，写下，when the same subject continues within one sentence, use zero form rather than repeating the pronoun.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他把灵芝放在包里：他把灵芝放进了包里，use 了 for this completed action in the past narrative.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉人们：告诉了人们，use 了 because telling the people is a completed narrative action.
- Coherence (Temporality): 写下：写下了，use 了 because recording the method is completed before the result that people know about it.
- Expressiveness: After the opening question, add Shen Nong’s realization to show his thoughts: 他想：“原来灵芝可以救人！”
- Expressiveness: At the point where he records his discovery, add specific information about what he writes: 他把灵芝的样子和用处都写在书上。
- Expressiveness: When he tells the people, add a short line of speech: 他对人们说：“灵芝对身体有好处。”

### Encouragement
- You use a clear sequence from returning home, to sharing the discovery, to writing it down. The final cause-and-effect idea also gives the scene a clear ending.
