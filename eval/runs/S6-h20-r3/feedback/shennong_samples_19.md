## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男人觉得植物很有意思。他叫神农。有的植物吃了以后他的肚子疼死了，可是有的吃了以后很舒服。他找很多植物。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，this is the standard time expression for a story set in an ancient period.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一个男人觉得植物很有意思。他叫神农：一个叫神农的男人觉得植物很有意思，follow the first-mention rule by introducing the character’s name together with a full noun phrase when he first appears.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他找很多植物：他找了很多植物，use 了 for the completed action in this narrative event.
- Expressiveness: After “肚子疼死了,” add a physical reaction to show how severe the pain is, such as “他疼得在地上打滚。”
- Expressiveness: At “很舒服,” add a feeling or thought that contrasts with the painful plant, such as “神农觉得这种植物对身体有好处。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the contrast between plants that cause pain and plants that make Shennong feel well. The phrase “肚子疼死了” is vivid and strongly communicates his pain.

## Scene 2

> 凉山很大的山，这也找植物。一天，他找到黄色的花。他听说你跟黄色的花做茶很有用。比方说，你的头发疼死，这冰茶是药。他一喝茶就很不舒服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 凉山很大的山：凉山是一座很大的山，A named mountain needs the linking verb and a measure word in this noun-description structure.
- 这：他，This is the wrong character: the sentence needs the previously introduced character as the person who looks for plants.
- 他找到：他找到了，Use 找到 rather than 找 when the meaning is “find,” and add 了 for the completed discovery.
- 跟黄色的花做茶：用黄色的花做的茶，Use 用 to introduce the material used to make something; the modifier needs 的 before 茶.
- 你的头发疼死：头疼得要死，头发 is “hair” and cannot express a headache; use 头疼, and use 得 before a degree complement such as 要死.
- 这冰茶：这杯茶，A demonstrative plus a noun needs a measure word: 这 + MW + noun.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 黄色 -- “黄色的花”：金黄色，gives the flower a more vivid, specific shade of yellow.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 你：delete 你，The pronoun rule requires an established addressee for a second-person pronoun; no listener has been introduced, so use a zero form here.
- Expressiveness: When the yellow flowers first appear, add visual detail: “黄色的花又大又漂亮，像星星一样。”
- Expressiveness: Show his physical reaction after drinking the tea: “他一喝茶，就捂着肚子，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Add an internal question to show his surprise: “他想：‘这杯茶不是很有用吗？我为什么这么难受？’”

### Encouragement
- You introduced a new place and moved the scene forward with a clear sequence: finding flowers, making tea, and then feeling unwell. The use of “一天” also gives the event a helpful time setting.

## Scene 3

> 糟糕，这黄色的花是夹竹桃，特别是毒。正好在这儿，灵芝药的夹竹桃他吃完了，也写一个课文的植物。谢谢神农。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 这黄色的花：这朵黄色的花，A demonstrative such as 这 needs a measure word before a countable noun; 朵 is the measure word for flowers.
- 特别是毒：特别有毒，Use 有毒 to describe a poisonous plant; 是毒 does not form a natural predicate here.
- 正好在这儿，灵芝药的：正好在这儿有灵芝，灵芝是药。The original has no complete predicate, and 灵芝药的 cannot grammatically modify 夹竹桃; use 有 to introduce the plant and 是药 to state its function.
- 也写一个课文的植物：也把这些植物写下来了，Use 写下 to mean “record,” rather than plain 写 with 植物 as its object. The 把 construction places the recorded plants before the verb, and 了 marks the completed recording.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the dangerous discovery, add a physical reaction to show the effect on the character, such as “神农的肚子疼了起来，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Describe the flower more vividly at the point where it appears, such as “黄色的花像星星一样，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought or spoken reaction after identifying the plant, such as “神农想：‘原来夹竹桃有毒，不能吃！’”

### Encouragement
- You used the vivid opening exclamation “糟糕” and correctly named the important plant “夹竹桃.” You also tried to connect two actions with “也,” which is a useful way to build a narrative.
