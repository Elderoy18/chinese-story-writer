### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很糟糕 -- “这个问题神农觉得很糟糕”：严重，states more precisely that the problem has serious consequences；令人担忧，emphasizes that the problem causes concern.
- 很好吃 -- “很好吃的植物”：味道鲜美，describes the plants’ pleasant taste more vividly and specifically.

- Coherence (Temporality): 会来跟他的朋友聊到他吃的植物了：会来跟他的朋友聊到他吃的植物，Use the zero particle after the modal verb 会; 了 cannot follow a modal expression in this use.
- Expressiveness: When the dangerous plant appears, add a visible detail so the reader can picture it, such as “那种植物开着黄色的、像星星一样的花。”

## Scene 2

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 这就要看你的口味了：这就要看你喜欢什么口味了，The pattern requires a question form after 看; using a wh-word makes the answer depend on the listener’s preference.
- 一个别的人：另一个人，别人 refers to people other than a specified person or group; to introduce the next individual questioner, use 另一个人.
- 一个别的人：另一个人，Use 另一个人 again for the next individual questioner; 一个别的人 is not a natural noun phrase.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很多 -- “有很多甜的水果”：多种，emphasizes that there are several different kinds of sweet fruit, matching the list that follows.
- 坏处 -- “哪个植物对你的身体有坏处”：有害，states more directly that the plant is harmful to the body.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: At the first question, add a small action or feeling to show why the person is asking, for example: “一个人摸着肚子问：‘哪个植物好吃？’”
- Expressiveness: Give the final questioner a worried tone to make the health topic more vivid, for example: “另一个人担心地问：‘哪个植物对身体有坏处？’”

### Encouragement

## Scene 3

- 一个黄色的花：一朵黄色的花，朵 is the measure word used for flowers.
- 对我的脑有坏处：对我的大脑有害，脑 is usually replaced by the full noun 大脑 when referring to the organ, and 对……有害 is the natural collocation for something harmful to health.
- 一个灵芝：一株灵芝，株 is an appropriate measure word for a medicinal plant such as 灵芝.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 旁边 -- “正好在我的旁边”：身边, emphasizes that the man and the plant are close to the narrator.

- Expressiveness: After the narrator eats 夹竹桃, add a physical feeling to show the danger more vividly, for example: “我突然觉得肚子疼得不得了，只能躺在地上。”
- Expressiveness: When the man appears, describe him briefly so the reader can picture the rescue, for example: “这时，一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人走了过来。”
- Expressiveness: At the warning about 夹竹桃, add a stronger specific warning, for example: “特别是新鲜的夹竹桃，吃了可能会死。”

### Encouragement
