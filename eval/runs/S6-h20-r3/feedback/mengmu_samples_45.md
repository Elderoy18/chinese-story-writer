## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子。他们住在墓地的旁边，所以孟子老是祭拜。孟子不是祭拜，就是跟朋友聊天。他很喜欢他的城市，但是城市有才两个学校，都很小。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，this time expression is the natural form for a historical setting; “古代的时候” is not used here.
- 城市有才两个学校：这个城市里才有两所学校，place nouns take “里” here, “才” comes before “有” to emphasize the small number, and schools use the measure word “所”.
- 老是祭拜：常常祭拜，老是 usually carries a negative or complaining tone; use 常常 for a neutral repeated action.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 小 -- “都很小”：规模都很小，specifies that the schools are small in scale rather than physically small.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子：有一个叫孟子的男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国，follow the first-mention rule by introducing the character with his name and identity together.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子不是祭拜，就是跟朋友聊天：他不是祭拜，就是跟朋友聊天，follow the pronoun rule: after the immediately preceding sentence has clearly identified him, use a pronoun rather than repeating his name.
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery setting, add what he observes and feels: “他每天看到不同的人来墓地祭拜，觉得祭拜很有趣。”
- Expressiveness: Add a small action detail to show how he imitates others: “他一边笑，一边学着别人祭拜。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduce the main character, his mother, and the cemetery setting. Your use of “不是……就是……” also creates a lively description of Mengzi’s repeated activities.

## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈不是不喜欢他们的城市，而是想孟子有很好的教育。他们搬家到有很多学校的城市。他们从早到晚走路，孟子一直抱怨。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 想孟子有很好的教育：想让孟子接受良好的教育，When the mother wants another person to receive something, use 想让 + person + verb phrase. Education normally collocates with 接受 rather than 有.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们搬家到有很多学校的城市：他们搬到了有很多学校的城市，Use 了 with 搬到 to show that the move was completed before the following events.
- Expressiveness: After the move, add a concrete detail about the new city’s atmosphere, such as “城市里有很多商人卖东西，从早到晚都很热闹。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where Mengzi complains, show what he says or feels, such as “孟子累得很，说：‘妈妈，我们什么时候才能到？’”
- Expressiveness: Add the mother’s inner thought to explain her goal more vividly, such as “妈妈心想：‘我希望孟子能在好学校学习。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the mother’s motivation for moving, and the contrast structure “不是……而是……” helps the reader understand her intention.

## Scene 3

> 他难怪抱怨，他想他的朋友，因为他不要离开他的城市。他的妈妈找到孟子一个老师。跟老师孟子学最重要的文学，所以他成为哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他难怪抱怨：他一直抱怨，难怪 means “no wonder” and introduces a conclusion based on a reason already given; it cannot mean that someone keeps doing an action.
- 他不要离开：他不想离开，不要 is usually used for a prohibition, request, or refusal, while 不想 expresses that the subject does not want to do something.
- 找到孟子一个老师：给孟子找了一个老师，找 cannot take both a person and a thing as direct objects in this pattern; 给 introduces the person who benefits from the action, and 了 marks the completed search.
- 跟老师孟子学：孟子跟老师学，when 孟子 is the subject, it must come before the phrase 跟老师.
- 成为哲学家：成为了哲学家，了 is needed after 成为 to mark the completed change of state.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 想 -- “他想他的朋友”：想念，makes clear that he misses his friends rather than merely thinks about them.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the first sentence, add a feeling to show why the move is difficult for him, such as “他觉得很孤单。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where he complains, add a short line of speech, such as “他说：‘我不想离开我的朋友！’”
- Expressiveness: When his mother finds a teacher, describe her intention or emotion, such as “妈妈希望他能好好学习。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show Mengzi’s motivation for complaining by using 因为 and mentioning his friends and his old city.
- You also connect his later learning with his becoming a philosopher, which gives the scene a clear direction.
