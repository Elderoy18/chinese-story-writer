## Scene 2

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 先她让孟子跟朋友玩儿：她先让孟子跟朋友玩儿，A time-order adverb such as “先” normally comes after the subject and before the verb.
- 又便宜又好质量的东西：又便宜又质量好的东西，Use “质量好” because the noun “质量” is modified by the adjective “好”; “好质量” is not a natural noun phrase in Chinese.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 技能 -- “妈妈教孟子很多技能”：生活技能，specifies that these are practical skills for everyday life, which fits the following example about buying things.
- 从这儿到那儿 -- “从这儿到那儿，妈妈教孟子很多技能”：搬来搬去，makes the repeated moving between homes more concrete and vivid.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 妈妈教孟子很多技能：她教孟子很多技能，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence: the mother was already mentioned in the immediately preceding sentence, and no other female character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Temporality): 常常搬了家：常常搬家，Use the zero particle for a habitual action; “常常” describes a routine, so it does not take completed-action “了”.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为他们常常搬家，中国人说，孟母三迁：因为他们常常搬家，所以中国人说：“孟母三迁。”，Use “因为……所以……” to mark the cause-and-effect relation between the repeated moves and the saying.
- Expressiveness: After “孟子没有爸爸”, add a brief feeling to show why his mother matters: “孟子觉得妈妈是他最重要的人。”
- Expressiveness: At “妈妈教孟子很多技能”, add a concrete action showing the lesson: “妈妈带着他去市场，教他怎么挑便宜又质量好的东西。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech after the shopping lesson: “妈妈说：‘买东西不能只看价格，也要看质量。’”

### Encouragement
- You used clear time-order words, “先” and “然后”, to organize the mother’s teaching activities.
- The example about buying affordable, good-quality things gives the scene a practical and believable detail.
