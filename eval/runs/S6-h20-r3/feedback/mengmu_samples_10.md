## Scene 2

> 妈妈认为，住离墓地太近，害得孟子的心气。孟子和孟母搬到子思老师的家，跟子思老师住。孟母帮子思打扫房间和做饭。子思帮孟母教她的儿子。因为孟子现在没有朋友，他老是读书。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 住离墓地太近：住在离墓地太近的地方，住 must be followed by 在 + a place expression when stating where someone lives.
- 害得孟子的心气：害得孟子心气不顺，害得 + person requires a result predicate after the person; 心气 is a noun and cannot stand alone as that result.
- 老是读书：一直读书，老是 has a negative, complaining tone; 一直 neutrally describes continuing to read and study.

### Vocabulary Suggestions

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 妈妈认为：她认为，use a pronoun for a recently mentioned character when no other possible referent has intervened.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟母：他们，use a pronoun for two characters who were both mentioned in the immediately preceding sentence and have no competing referents.
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬到子思老师的家：搬到了子思老师的家，use 了 with 搬到 because the move to the new home is a completed action.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为孟子现在没有朋友，他一直读书：因为孟子现在没有朋友，所以他一直读书，use 因为……所以…… to mark the cause-and-effect relationship.

- Expressiveness: In the final sentence, add Mengzi’s inner feeling to make his reading more meaningful, such as: 孟子觉得有点孤单，只好一个人看书。
