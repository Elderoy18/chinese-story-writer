## Scene 1

> 神农和他的家人住在古代，所以不知道什么植物能吃。因为他的家人常常生病了，所以神农和他的哥哥一起去旅行找可以吃的植物。他们去很多不一样的地方，比方说高山、山谷和沙漠。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 住在古代：生活在古代，古代 is a time period rather than a place; use 生活在 to describe living during an era, while 住在 normally takes a location.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 找 -- “去旅行找可以吃的植物”：探寻，emphasizes travelling and exploring in order to discover the plants.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：一个叫神农的男人，at first mention, introduce a character with a name plus a brief identity rather than using only the name.
- Coherence (Temporality): 常常生病了：常常生病，use the zero particle for a habitual action; 了 does not follow a routine marked by 常常.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们去很多不一样的地方：他们去了很多不一样的地方，use 了 for the completed action of visiting these places.
- Expressiveness: After the family’s illness, add a concrete symptom to show why the problem worried them: “他们吃了一些植物以后，常常肚子疼。”
- Expressiveness: When describing the journey, add one sensory detail about the landscapes: “高山上很冷，山谷里开着许多花。”
- Expressiveness: Add a brief thought from 神农 to show his motivation: “神农想：我一定要找到能吃的植物。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly explain the cause of 神农’s journey with 因为……所以……, and your examples of 高山、山谷和沙漠 make the journey feel wide-ranging.

## Scene 2

> 他们在很漂亮的河找夹竹桃。神农问他的哥哥：“我应该吃这种植物吗？”哥哥说：“这就要看你想要安全还是接近知道了。“神农一吃夹竹桃就生病了。哥哥说：“太麻烦了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- “这就要看你想要安全还是接近知道了”： “这就要看这种植物能不能吃了”，the structure “这就要看……了” must be followed by a question form such as “能不能吃”; “接近知道” is not a valid phrase because “接近” cannot modify the mental-state verb “知道” in this way.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 麻烦 -- “太麻烦了”：太严重了，states more precisely that Shen Nong’s illness is serious；太令人担心了，emphasizes the brother’s worry about his condition.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: At “很漂亮的河”, add a visual detail about the plants, such as “河边开着黄色的、像星星一样的花”.
- Expressiveness: After “神农一吃夹竹桃就生病了”, show his physical feeling more clearly, such as “他的肚子疼了起来，难受得不得了”.
- Expressiveness: After the brother says “太麻烦了”, add a short worried reaction or line of speech, such as “哥哥很着急，说：‘你怎么样？’”

### Encouragement
You used direct speech to show Shen Nong asking his brother for advice, which makes the scene feel active. The pattern “一……就……” is also used well to show that he became ill immediately after eating the plant.

## Scene 3

> 夹竹桃是毒的，我想知道灵芝会帮他。”哥哥给神农灵芝。神农吃完了以后开始痊愈了。神农说：“谢谢哥哥，你帮得真好。灵芝我们可以吃，特别是如果我们已经吃了夹竹桃。“

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 夹竹桃是毒的：夹竹桃有毒，Use “有毒” to describe that a plant contains poison; “毒” is a noun and does not fit after “是” in this meaning.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 帮 -- “我想知道灵芝会帮他”：给他解毒，states more precisely that the herb can counteract the poison.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 哥哥给神农灵芝：哥哥给了神农灵芝，Use “了” for this completed giving action.
- Expressiveness: After “开始痊愈了,” add a physical detail showing the recovery, such as “他的肚子慢慢不疼了，人也有精神了。”
- Expressiveness: Make the brother’s help more vivid by adding urgency before he gives the herb, such as “哥哥看见神农很难受，赶快给他灵芝吃。”

### Encouragement
- The recovery sequence is easy to follow through the use of “以后.”
- You also use the degree complement naturally in “帮得真好,” which makes Shennong’s gratitude sound warm.
