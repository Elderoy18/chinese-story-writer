## Scene 1

> 在古代，很多医生不知道怎么用药。所以他们找到了很多植物对药。神农先看到很多病人，他们吃很多植物对药。但是我觉得他们的吃的植物，让他们病人。神农觉得这个植物可能特别是毒。所以他想找一个植物对药，他们可以吃对药。正好，他找到了一把花，他想把花放在茶里，对药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很多植物对药：很多可以用来做药的植物，The phrase for serving as medicine is “做药” or “用来做药”; “对药” does not express this meaning.
- 植物对药：用来做药的植物，A plant that is used as medicine needs a verb phrase before the noun; “对药” is not a suitable verb phrase here.
- 他们的吃的植物：他们吃的植物，When a subject and verb modify a noun, use one modifier marker after the verb; the extra possessive marker after the subject is not needed.
- 病人：生病，The original word is a noun meaning “patient,” but after the causative verb, the sentence needs a predicate verb meaning “become ill.”
- 特别是毒：特别有毒，Use “有毒” to describe a poisonous plant. “毒” alone is a noun and cannot follow an adverb such as “特别” in this pattern.
- 一个植物对药，他们可以吃对药：一棵可以做药、可以吃的植物，Use the measure word “棵” for one individual plant, and use “可以做药” to state that it can be used as medicine.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：一个叫神农的人，On first mention, introduce a new character with the name and a brief noun phrase rather than using only the name.
- Coherence (Reference): 这个植物：这些植物，The referent is unclear because several plants were mentioned earlier; repeat a clear noun phrase that matches those plants.
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃很多：吃了很多，The patients’ eating happened before they became ill, so use “了” to mark the completed action.
- Expressiveness: After the patients become ill, add a physical symptom to show the result more vividly, for example: “他们肚子疼得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: When the flower appears, add one visual detail, for example: “花是黄色的，像星星一样。”
- Expressiveness: Add Shennong’s thought when he sees the sick people, for example: “神农想：这些植物为什么会让人生病？”

### Encouragement
- You clearly set the story in the past and introduced the problem of sick people and unknown plants. You also used connectors such as “所以” and “但是” to show relationships between ideas.

## Scene 2

> 他不知道如果这把花对你的身体好，这就要看如果这把花毒不毒了。神农想找别的植物，但是这很麻烦。正好，他找到了别的花。他一吃花，很快就生病了。他的肚子疼死了。神农不知道他吃不吃灵芝或者夹竹桃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 如果这把花对你的身体好：这把花对他的身体好不好，An embedded yes/no question after a verb such as “不知道” uses an A-not-A form rather than “如果”; the possessive pronoun must also refer to the character being discussed.
- 如果这把花毒不毒：这把花有没有毒，After “看,” an embedded yes/no question needs an A-not-A form. Use “有没有毒,” because “毒” is a noun here; “有毒” describes a poisonous plant.
- 他吃不吃灵芝或者夹竹桃：自己吃的是灵芝还是夹竹桃，For an embedded choice between two possibilities, use “是……还是……,” not “吃不吃……或者…….” “自己” correctly refers back to the subject “神农.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 找 -- “神农想找别的植物”：寻找，emphasizes an ongoing search for plants；探寻，highlights searching for answers through exploration.
- 麻烦 -- “这很麻烦”：费劲，specifies that the task takes much effort；不容易，emphasizes that the task is difficult to complete.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他一吃花：他一吃这种花，The noun “花” is unclear because both “这把花” and “别的花” have appeared. Repeat a specific noun phrase when nearby possible referents compete.
- Expressiveness: After “正好，他找到了别的花,” add a visual detail about the flower, such as “那儿开着黄色的像星星一样的花。”
- Expressiveness: After “他的肚子疼死了,” show his physical state more vividly, such as “他在地上躺着，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought at the moment he becomes ill, such as “他想：‘我会死吗？’”

### Encouragement
- You used completed-action markers well in “找到了” and “生病了,” which makes the event sequence easy to follow. The strong phrase “疼死了” also clearly communicates Shen Nong’s pain.

## Scene 3

> 别的人给了神农一个灵芝。这个植物可以让神农更好。比方说，他会觉得更健康。吃灵芝以后，他可以再走。现在他觉得更好。最后，神农找到了很多植物，他可以用对药。这些植物让神农很高兴。神农想跟很多人分享他的新药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别的人：一个人，别的人 means “other people” and normally distinguishes them from a person or group already under discussion; an unspecified individual should be introduced as 一个人人.
- 更好：好起来，after 让, 好起来 expresses the change of state that the medicine causes: recovery or improvement.
- 他可以用对药：这些植物能用来做药，use 用来做药 to say that plants can serve as medicine; 用 cannot be followed directly by 对药.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 走 -- “他可以再走”：继续赶路，specifies that he resumes his journey rather than simply walking again.
- 高兴 -- “这些植物让神农很高兴”：欣慰，more precisely shows that he feels pleased and reassured by his useful discoveries.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农想跟很多人分享他的新药：他想跟很多人分享他的新药，apply the pronoun rule: after the same character was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no competing character appears, use 他.
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃灵芝以后：吃了灵芝以后，use 了 for the completed action that happens before the later action introduced by 以后.
- Expressiveness: After the medicine helps him, add a physical feeling to show the recovery, for example: “他的肚子不那么疼了，慢慢站了起来。”
- Expressiveness: When he feels happy about the plants, add his thought or speech, for example: “神农想：‘这些植物真的可以帮助病人！’”
- Expressiveness: Describe the medicinal plant at its first appearance, for example: “灵芝像一把小伞，长在花的旁边。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the result of the medicine and then move the scene forward to Shen Nong’s next discovery. You also use time words such as 吃灵芝以后 and 最后 to organize the events.
