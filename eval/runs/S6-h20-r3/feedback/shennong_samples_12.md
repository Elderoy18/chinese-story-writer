## Scene 1

> 很久以前，古代的人很不知道什么植物能吃，也什么植物不能吃。所以很多人常常吃不安全的植物，吃不安全的植物对你的身体有坏处，很麻烦的情况。但是一个人，神农，要解决这个问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很不知道什么植物能吃，也什么植物不能吃：都不知道什么植物能吃，也不知道什么植物不能吃，Very cannot naturally modify the mental-state verb 不知道 in this meaning; use 都 to show that the people lacked both kinds of knowledge, and repeat 不知道 before the second embedded question.
- 很麻烦的情况：是一种很麻烦的情况，This noun phrase is not grammatically linked to the preceding statement; add 是 to make it the predicate describing the situation.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 有坏处 -- “对他们的身体有坏处”：有害，states more directly that the plants damage people’s health.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 你的身体：他们的身体，Use a pronoun that refers back to the previously mentioned group 很多人; 你的 unexpectedly addresses the reader instead.
- Coherence (Reference): 一个 人，神农：有一个叫神农的人，For a character’s first mention, introduce the character with an unambiguous noun phrase that gives both the name and who the person is.
- Expressiveness: After the harmful plants are mentioned, add a concrete health effect to show why the situation matters, for example: “很多人吃了以后肚子疼，还常常生病。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where Shennong appears, add his determination through a short thought or line of speech, for example: “神农想：‘我一定要找出能吃的植物！’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish the main problem—people do not know which plants are edible—and connect it to harm to their health. Introducing Shennong with the goal of solving the problem gives the scene a clear direction.

## Scene 2

> 他又聪明又努力，旅行很远找到很多植物。比方说，灵芝和夹竹桃。他一吃各种各样的植物就知道能不能吃这些植物。然后，神农写他的主意。他喜欢植物的味道，特别是花。神农遇到了一个黄色的花，一吃就觉得不舒服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 旅行很远找到很多植物：走了很远，找到了很多植物，Use “走了很远” to express travelling a long distance; both actions are completed here, so they take 了.
- 写他的主意：把自己的想法写了下来，“写” alone does not naturally mean recording an idea; “写下来” means to put an idea into writing, and 了 marks the completed action.
- 一个黄色的花：一朵黄色的花，Flowers use the measure word “朵,” not the general measure word “个.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: At the point where the yellow flower appears, add a visual detail: “那朵花又大又漂亮，像星星一样。”
- Expressiveness: After the character feels unwell, make the physical feeling more specific: “他的肚子疼了起来，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Add an internal thought after he becomes ill: “他想：‘我会不会生病？’”

### Encouragement
You clearly introduced the two important plants, “灵芝” and “夹竹桃,” and you used the pattern “一……就……” well to show an immediate result. The scene also gives Shennong a strong character through “又聪明又努力.”

## Scene 3

> 发现了夹竹桃有毒。别人看到他，很快给他吃一个灵芝。神农一吃就觉得比较好。然后，他继续写他发现的信息。会告诉他的朋友哪一个植物能不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 给他吃一个灵芝：给他吃了一个灵芝，Use 了 after the main verb 给 to mark the completed action of giving the medicinal plant.
- 继续写他发现的信息：继续记下他发现的信息，For recording information or discoveries, 记下 is the appropriate verb because it means to write something down and preserve it.
- 哪一个植物能不能吃：哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃，哪一个 asks for the identity of one item, while 能不能 asks a yes-or-no question; use parallel clauses to distinguish plants that can be eaten from those that cannot.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很快 -- “别人看到他，很快给他吃一个灵芝”：立刻，means without any delay and makes the helper’s response more immediate.
- 信息 -- “他发现的信息”：自己的发现，more specifically refers to the results he learned from trying the plants.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 别人看到他：一个人看到他，Use an indefinite full noun phrase for a character’s first mention. 别人 means people other than the speaker or listener, so it does not naturally introduce one new helper in this narration.
- Expressiveness: At the point where the medicine helps, add a physical feeling to show the change: “神农的肚子不那么疼了，觉得好多了。”
- Expressiveness: When the helper first appears, add one visible detail: “一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人看到神农躺在地上。”
- Expressiveness: After recording the information, add a short thought or line of speech: “神农想：以后大家就知道什么植物不能吃了。”

### Encouragement
- You used the pattern “一……就……” well to show that the medicine had an immediate effect. You also kept the events in a clear order with “然后”.
