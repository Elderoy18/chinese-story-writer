## Scene 1

> 古代，孟子跟孟母住在墓地的旁边。可是孟母注意，住在墓地的旁边影响孟子。他常常跟朋友们祭拜玩，所以孟母决定他们会搬家。这次，他们住在城市里的商店的旁边，从早到晚很吵。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 孟母注意：孟母注意到了，住在墓地旁边会影响孟子，the verb needs the result complement 到 when it means “notice/realize” something; 了 marks this realization as completed in the narrative.
- 祭拜玩：玩祭拜的游戏，祭拜 is an action of paying respects and cannot directly be used before 玩 in this structure; adding the noun 游戏 makes clear that the children are playing an imitation game.
- 孟母决定他们会搬家：孟母决定带着孟子搬家，决定 takes a verb phrase as its object; “decide that they will move” is not expressed with 决定 + subject + 会 in this context.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 常常 -- “他常常跟朋友们玩祭拜的游戏”：老是，adds the mother’s disapproving feeling that he does this repeatedly.
- 吵 -- “从早到晚很吵”：嘈杂，more specifically describes a place filled with many confusing, overlapping sounds.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: At the point where Mengzi plays with his friends, add a detail about the real visitors’ feelings, such as “来祭拜的人看见了，都很生气。”
- Expressiveness: After Mengmu notices the problem, add her worried thought or speech, such as “孟母心想：‘不能让孟子一直这样玩下去。’”
- Expressiveness: Describe the new neighbourhood’s noise more concretely after “从早到晚很吵,” such as “商人一直叫卖，路上的人来来往往。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the cause-and-effect sequence with “所以,” and the scene moves naturally from the cemetery to the new neighbourhood.
- Your use of “从早到晚” gives the city setting a strong sense of time and atmosphere.

## Scene 2

> 城市也有点儿危险。害得孟母只好一直陪孟子。孟子也没有朋友，难怪他老是看起来不高兴。住在商店的旁边不是适合孟子，而是让他觉得很伤心。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 住在商店的旁边不是适合孟子，而是让他觉得很伤心：住在商店旁边不适合孟子，因为这让他觉得很伤心，适合 is a verb and can directly take its object, so it does not need 是 before it; 因为 correctly introduces the reason that living there is unsuitable.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 陪 -- “害得孟母只好一直陪孟子”：照看，emphasizes that Meng Mu stays with him to keep him safe in a dangerous place.
- 不高兴 -- “他老是看起来不高兴”：闷闷不乐，describes a continuing, visibly unhappy mood more vividly.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 城市也有点儿危险。害得孟母只好一直陪孟子：城市也有点儿危险，害得孟母只好一直陪孟子，害得 links a cause directly to its harmful result, so these clauses should be connected rather than separated.
- Expressiveness: After “城市也有点儿危险,” add one concrete detail about the busy streets, such as “从早到晚，街上人来人往，商人不停地叫卖。”
- Expressiveness: At “孟子也没有朋友,” show his feeling through an action, such as “他一个人站在门口，看着别人一起玩儿，心里很难过。”
- Expressiveness: After the final sentence, add Meng Mu’s inner thought or speech, such as “孟母很着急，心想：‘住在这儿对孟子不好。’”

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 才五岁孟子开始学哲学：孟子才五岁就开始学哲学，Use the paired pattern 才 + age/number + 就 + verb to show that an action began at an early age.

### Vocabulary Suggestions

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 在学校，孟子不是看书，就是练习写字：在学校，他不是看书，就是练习写字，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the same character was named in the immediately previous sentence and no other character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Temporality): 孟母和孟子搬到学校的旁边住：孟母和孟子搬到了学校的旁边住，Add 了 to mark the completed moving action in this past narrative.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他成为中国的一个非常有名的哲学家：他成为了中国的一个非常有名的哲学家，Add 了 because becoming a philosopher is a completed change of state in the narrative.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 将来：后来，Use 后来 to sequence a later event in a past story; 将来 refers to the future from the present.
- Expressiveness: After the school setting, add Mengzi’s interest in learning, such as “觉得看书很有意思”.
