## Scene 1

> 有一天孟子去了墓地玩儿，但是他妈妈觉得这是不合适的地方让孩子玩，所以她决定了他们会搬家找更好的地方。她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福，而且住在这儿教育不太好。因为她望子成龙，所以她会一直搬家才能找到质量好的教育。然后他们搬到了离城市更近的地方。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不合适的地方让孩子玩：不适合孩子玩儿的地方，Use 适合 when saying that a place suits a person or an activity; this structure directly describes a place that is not suitable for the child to play in.
- 她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福：她担心儿子的童年不会幸福，恐怕 means “I am afraid that” and cannot take another subject such as 她 in this pattern; 担心 expresses that she is worried.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 更好的地方 -- “搬家找更好的地方”：更适合孩子成长的地方，This makes “better” more specific by showing that the new place should support the child’s development.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 决定了：决定，决定 is a mental-state verb, so it normally takes the zero particle rather than 了.
- Expressiveness: After the visit to the cemetery, add a concrete detail showing what makes the place unsuitable, such as “孟子看见很多人来墓地哭，就觉得很好玩。”
- Expressiveness: At the mother’s decision, add her inner thought or quoted speech, such as “她心想：‘我不能让孟子在这儿长大。’”
- Expressiveness: When they arrive near the city, describe the new environment briefly, such as “那儿每天都有很多商人，街上很热闹。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the mother’s concern for her son and use cause-and-effect connectors to explain why she wants to move. The final sentence also gives the scene a clear next step in the family’s move.

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections

- 老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿：老是跟这些卖东西的人玩儿，two frequency adverbs should not both modify the same action in this way.
- 她恐怕儿子会成为跟他们很像：她担心儿子会变得跟他们很像，恐怕 cannot follow another subject to mean that person is afraid; use 担心 for the mother’s worry. Also, 成为 needs a noun phrase after it, while 变得 can be followed by an adjective phrase such as 跟他们很像.

- Coherence (Temporality): 她告诉了孟子：她告诉孟子，use the zero particle with a speaking verb that introduces quoted speech.
- Expressiveness: After the mention of the sellers, add a concrete action to show what Mengzi copies: “孟子学着他们大声叫卖，还跟别人讨价还价。”
- Expressiveness: At the mother’s worry, add an inner thought or a line of speech: “孟母心想：‘孩子天天这样玩儿，会不会不想学习了？’”

## Scene 3

> 你不是当数学家，而是当哲学家，不能当卖东西的人。所以他们又搬家。在他们第三个家，孟子学了怎么祭拜。妈妈感觉这个地方适合他们，告诉了孟子：我们找到了最后的家。在这儿妈妈让儿子从早到晚学习，他难怪成为有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 告诉了孟子：我们找到了最后的家：告诉孟子：我们找到了最后的家，Speaking verbs that introduce direct speech take the zero particle; do not use 了 after 告诉 here.
- 成为有名的哲学家：成为了有名的哲学家，Use 了 with 成为 to mark the completed change of state: he eventually became a philosopher.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
