## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有很多人不知道什么植物能吃，什么植物不能吃。有很多人吃植物以后就吃坏肚子了，因为他们吃了毒。那个时候，有一个老人叫神农，他什么植物都觉得好吃，不怕生病，不怕毒。他希望别人也可以吃好吃的植物。可是他知道，如果他们还怕吃毒，不会吃他喜欢的植物。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，Use 在古代 for a broad historical time period; 古代的时候 is not the natural narrative time expression here.
- 毒：有毒的植物，毒 means poison itself, but the people are described as eating plants; 有毒的植物 specifically means plants that contain poison.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不知道 -- “有很多人不知道什么植物能吃，什么植物不能吃”：分不清，emphasizes that people cannot distinguish edible plants from poisonous ones.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 如果他们还怕吃毒，不会吃他喜欢的植物：如果他们还怕吃到有毒的植物，就不会吃他喜欢的植物，Use 如果……就…… to connect a condition with its result.
- Expressiveness: After introducing Shennong, add his determined feeling: “神农一点儿也不害怕，心想：‘我要找出能吃的植物！’”
- Expressiveness: Describe the plants more vividly after the first sentence: “有的植物开着黄色的花，看起来很漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech where Shennong explains his hope: “他对人们说：‘我希望大家都能吃到好吃的植物。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly explain the problem people faced and introduce Shennong’s wish to help them. Your use of “可是” also creates a meaningful contrast between his hope and people’s fear.

## Scene 2

> 所以，他上山下山找植物，吃了很多样子的植物。他是第一人，找到了茶。他把所有的植物写下来了。他一直都吃了，他连毒都能吃。可是他还没吃了一个叫夹竹桃的植物。他找到了那个像星星的植物，觉得很漂亮，马上就吃下了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很多样子的植物：很多种植物，样子 describes an appearance or shape, while 种 is the measure word used for different kinds of plants.
- 第一人：第一个人，an ordinal before a noun needs the measure word 个 here.
- 还没吃了一个叫夹竹桃的植物：还没吃过一种叫夹竹桃的植物，没 cannot be followed by the completed-action marker 了; 没……过 expresses that the experience has not happened. Use 种 for a plant species.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 找 -- “上山下山找植物”：寻找, emphasizes deliberately looking for plants during the journey；探寻, emphasizes exploring unknown plants.
- 上山下山 -- “他上山下山找植物”：翻山越岭, makes the long journey across mountains more vivid.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他连毒都能吃：连毒都能吃，under the zero-form rule, do not repeat the subject pronoun within the same sentence when the subject has not changed.
- Coherence (Temporality): 一直都吃了：一直都在吃，use 在 for an ongoing dynamic action; 了 marks a completed action and does not fit with 一直.
- Expressiveness: When he finds tea, add a concrete detail about what he learns from it, such as：他把茶泡在水里，喝了一口，觉得很香。
- Expressiveness: When he sees the new plant, describe its appearance more vividly before he eats it, such as：黄色的花又大又漂亮，像星星一样。
- Expressiveness: Add his thought just before the dangerous choice to show why he eats it, such as：他想：这么漂亮，应该可以吃吧！

### Encouragement
You use clear action verbs such as 找到了 and 吃下了, which help the events move forward. The phrase 上山下山 also gives the reader a strong sense of Shennong’s journey.

## Scene 3

> 可是夹竹桃的毒很重，让他肚子很痛。他在地上躺着，觉得他快会死了。可是正好有别人找到他了，给了他一个像伞的植物，叫灵芝。他吃灵芝以后就觉得很好，不再生病了。他测量起来了，像星星的植物是毒，像伞的植物是药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他快会死了：他快要死了，Use “快要” for an imminent change of state; “会” is unnecessary here.
- 测量起来了：想起来了，“测量” means to measure physical dimensions and normally needs an object; “想起来” means to recall the information that follows.
- 像星星的植物是毒：像星星一样的植物有毒，Use “像……一样” for this comparison. “有毒” means “poisonous”; “是毒” does not describe a plant naturally.
- 像伞的植物：像伞一样的植物，Use “像……一样” when saying that something resembles another thing.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很痛 -- “让他肚子很痛”：疼得厉害，shows more vividly how severe the stomach pain is.
- 很好 -- “他吃灵芝以后就觉得很好”：好多了，more precisely shows that he has improved after eating the medicine.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他在地上躺着，觉得他快会死了：他在地上躺着，觉得快要死了，Use zero form for the same character when the subject continues within the same sentence and no other character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃灵芝以后：吃了灵芝以后，Use “了” because eating the medicine is completed before the later result of feeling better.
- Expressiveness: At the moment of pain, add a physical reaction to show how badly he feels, such as “他疼得不得了，满头大汗。”
- Expressiveness: Add an inner thought after he lies down to show his fear, such as “他想：我会不会死？”
- Expressiveness: When the helper arrives, add one concrete detail about him, such as “这时，一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人走过来。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the sequence of poison, pain, help, and recovery. The use of “躺着” and “以后” also helps the reader follow the scene.
