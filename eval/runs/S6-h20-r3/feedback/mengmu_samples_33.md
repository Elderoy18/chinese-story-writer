## Scene 1

> 一天，很久很久以前，孟子和他妈妈住在中国。孟子妈妈的工作是祭拜，孟子不高兴，因为他说我们老是搬家，为什么？他妈妈说，从早到晚，我一直祭拜，很多墓地得我祭拜。所以我们得去很多不同的地方。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一天，很久很久以前：很久很久以前的一天，when a narrative time expression includes both “long ago” and “one day,” the larger time setting naturally comes before the specific day.
- 因为他说我们老是搬家，为什么？：，问：“我们老是搬家，为什么？”，a question should use a question verb such as “问” and be marked as direct speech; “因为” cannot directly introduce an unexplained question.
- 很多墓地得我祭拜：我得去很多墓地祭拜，modal “得” comes before the verb phrase it requires; the subject should come before “得”.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不同的地方 -- “所以我们得去很多不同的地方”：各个地方，emphasizes that the places are numerous and spread across different locations.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和他妈妈：有一个小男孩叫孟子，他和妈妈，by the first-mention rule, introduce the main character with a name and a brief identity before using a pronoun or kinship term.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子妈妈的工作：他妈妈的工作，by the pronoun rule, use a pronoun for a character mentioned in the immediately preceding sentence when no competing referent is present.
- Expressiveness: After “孟子不高兴,” add a small action to show his feeling: “孟子皱着眉头，觉得很奇怪。”
- Expressiveness: Make the mother’s explanation sound more like a conversation: “妈妈叹了一口气，说：‘因为我要去很多墓地祭拜。’”
- Expressiveness: Add a detail about the frequent moves before the question: “他们的行李常常还没整理好，就又要搬家了。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the reason for the family’s frequent moves, and the dialogue helps the reader understand both Mengzi’s confusion and his mother’s situation.

## Scene 2

> 孟子想，难怪我们常常搬家，原来我妈妈很忙。可是常常搬家害得孟子很难找到朋友。每次搬家孟子也觉得新的地方不是太热就是太冷，不是太大就是太小。在哪的地方，他都不高兴。孟子想帮助妈妈祭拜，可是他六岁，所以不能祭拜。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他六岁：他才六岁，Use 才 with an age here to show that six is a young age and to make the following inability a clear result.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 找到朋友 -- “常常搬家害得孟子很难找到朋友”：交到朋友，specifies forming a friendship rather than simply locating friends.
- 新的 -- “新的地方不是太热就是太冷”：陌生的，emphasizes that each place is unfamiliar to him after moving.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子很难找到朋友：他很难找到朋友，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the referent is still clear.
- Coherence (Reference): 每次搬家孟子也觉得：每次搬家，他也觉得，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence of the same character.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子想帮助妈妈祭拜：他想帮助妈妈祭拜，Use a pronoun because the previous sentence already clearly refers to him.
- Coherence (Reference): 在哪的地方：在那些地方，Use a clear demonstrative noun phrase to refer back to the different new places; 哪 cannot be used with 的地方 in this meaning.
- Expressiveness: After the difficulty with friends, add a feeling to show why moving is upsetting: “他很想念以前的朋友。”
- Expressiveness: At the description of the places, add one physical detail to make the temperatures vivid: “有的地方冷得让他直发抖。”
- Expressiveness: Turn his wish to help into a short inner thought: “我还太小，不能帮妈妈。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show Mengzi’s changing thoughts: he understands that his mother is busy, but he also explains how moving affects him. The paired patterns with “不是……就是……” describe his dissatisfaction effectively.

## Scene 3

> 四年后，孟子开始帮妈妈祭拜。他画画很厉害，他越来越喜欢祭拜，所以他祭拜不是想帮妈妈工作，而是他很喜欢画画。孟子成为很著名的古代画家。今天很多中国人很喜欢孟子。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 祭拜不是想帮妈妈工作，而是他很喜欢画画：祭拜不是为了帮妈妈的忙，而是因为喜欢画画，Use “为了” to introduce the purpose of an action, and use “因为” to introduce the reason in the second half of the contrast.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很厉害 -- “他画画很厉害”：很出色, describes his painting skill more specifically；很有天分, emphasizes that he has a natural talent for painting.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他画画很厉害，他越来越喜欢祭拜：他画画很厉害，越来越喜欢祭拜，Use zero form for a re-occurring subject within the same sentence when no other character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Reference): 所以他祭拜：所以祭拜，Use zero form again because the subject has already been established as 孟子 in the same sentence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 孟子成为很著名的古代画家：孟子成为了很著名的古代画家，Use 了 for the completed change of state of becoming a painter.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 所以：。，The ideas before and after this connector do not show a clear cause-and-effect relation, so a sequence break is clearer than using 所以.
- Expressiveness: After “孟子开始帮妈妈祭拜,” add a small action showing how he helps, such as “他学着妈妈的样子，认真地鞠躬。”
- Expressiveness: When mentioning his painting, add a concrete detail, such as “祭拜以后，他把看到的花和树画在纸上。”
- Expressiveness: Add an inner thought after he begins to enjoy the activity, such as “孟子心想：‘原来祭拜的时候也能看到这么多美丽的东西。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the passage of time with “四年后” and “今天,” which helps the reader follow the narrative. You also try to explain孟子的 interests and future achievement, giving this scene a clear focus.
