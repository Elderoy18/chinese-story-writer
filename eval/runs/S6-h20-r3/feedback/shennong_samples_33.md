## Scene 1

> 古代时候，人不知道他们能吃什么植物，常常一吃就死。但是不知道为什么，没有办法。所以有一天，神农出去试一试很多种植物，告诉人什么植物是安全什么是危险。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代时候：古代的时候，The time expression ending in “时候” needs “的” after a multisyllabic time phrase.
- 人：人们，Use “人们” for the concrete group of people in this situation; “人” is a generic noun.
- 他们：delete “他们”，The pronoun is unnecessary after “人们” and may sound as if it refers to a different group; the modal verb can directly follow the subject.
- 试一试很多种植物：试了很多种植物，The reduplicated form “试一试” describes a brief or tentative attempt and does not fit trying many types; use “了” for the completed narrative action.
- 人：人们，Use “人们” as the group receiving the information; “告诉人” is not natural for referring to people in general.
- 是安全什么是危险：是安全的，什么是危险的，When an adjective follows “是” in this pattern, “的” completes the adjectival description.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：一个叫神农的男人，This is the character’s first mention, so introduce him with a name plus a brief noun phrase rather than using only the name.
- Expressiveness: At the opening, add a concrete consequence of eating the wrong plants, such as “很多人因为吃了有毒的植物，常常生病。”
- Expressiveness: When introducing the character, add his purpose or determination, such as “他决定找出哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃。”
- Expressiveness: Add a small action detail to make the experiment more vivid, such as “他看到一种植物，就摘下来尝一尝。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the problem people faced and use “一……就……” effectively to express a quick result. The final sentence also gives the character a meaningful goal.

## Scene 2

> 发现有的植物对身体非常好，特别是茶，让肚子感觉又暖和又清楚。他告诉朋友：“你吃哪种植物，这就要看你的口味。比方说，你爱吃甜的，可能喜欢糖。”他最喜欢吃花，除了夹竹桃以外，什么都花想吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 让肚子感觉又暖和又清楚：让人觉得又暖和又清醒，清楚 describes understanding or thoughts, not a physical feeling in the stomach; 清醒 describes feeling mentally alert.
- 你吃哪种植物，这就要看你的口味：你喜欢吃哪种植物？这就要看你喜欢什么口味了，this use of 这就要看……了 needs an uncertain question before it, and the part after 看 should be a question form such as 什么口味; add 了 to complete this fixed response.
- 什么都花想吃：什么花都想吃，什么 should directly modify the noun 花 in the pattern 什么 + noun + 都 + verb.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 发现 -- “发现有的植物对身体非常好”：了解到，emphasizes gaining knowledge about the plants through trying or observing them；辨认出，emphasizes identifying which plants have benefits.
- 好 -- “对身体非常好”：很有益，states more precisely that the plants benefit health；很有好处，clearly describes a positive effect on the body.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After mentioning tea, add a sensory detail about its taste or smell, for example: “茶水闻起来很香，喝起来也很舒服。”
- Expressiveness: When describing the flowers he wants to eat, add a visual detail and his reaction, for example: “那些花有红的，也有黄的，看起来很漂亮，他很想试一试。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought to show his curiosity about plants, for example: “他想：这些花会不会也能做药呢？”

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect the discovery of helpful plants with tea, and you also try to use an example with 比方说. Your dialogue gives the scene a lively storytelling feeling.

## Scene 3

> 他发现这个口味，一百岁时候，不小心吃了毒的花。一吃就开始死。但是生病时候告诉人什么植物是药，什么植物是毒。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一百岁时候：一百岁的时候，A multi-syllable phrase before “时候” needs the particle “的”.
- 毒的花：有毒的花，“毒” is a noun here; use “有毒的” to describe a poisonous plant or flower.
- 生病时候：生病的时候，The two-syllable verb phrase “生病” needs “的” before “时候”.
- 人：人们，Use “人们” for the concrete group of people whom he tells.
- 什么植物是毒：什么植物有毒，“有毒” is the adjective phrase meaning “poisonous”; “是毒” does not describe a plant naturally.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不小心 -- “不小心吃了毒的花”：误食了，specifically means accidentally eating something unsuitable or harmful, so it fits eating a poisonous flower more precisely.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 这个口味：一种口味，Use an indefinite noun phrase for a first-mentioned specific taste; “这个” needs a clearly identifiable earlier referent.
- Coherence (Temporality): 一吃就开始死：一吃就开始死了，Use “了” to mark the change of state in this completed narrative event.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉：告诉了，Use “了” because telling the people is a completed action in this particular event, rather than a habitual action or quoted-speech introduction.
- Expressiveness: After eating the flower, add a physical reaction to show how serious it feels: “他肚子疼了起来，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where he tells people about the plants, add direct speech to make his warning clearer: “这种花有毒，不能吃。”

### Encouragement
- You used the “一……就……” pattern to show an immediate result, which is a useful structure for telling a fast-moving event. You also clearly tried to distinguish plants that are medicine from plants that are poisonous.
