## Scene 1

> 有一天孟子去了墓地玩儿，但是他妈妈觉得这是不合适的地方让孩子玩，所以她决定了他们会搬家找更好的地方。她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福，而且住在这儿教育不太好。因为她望子成龙，所以她会一直搬家才能找到质量好的教育。然后他们搬到了离城市更近的地方。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 这是不合适的地方让孩子玩：这不是一个适合孩子玩的地方，Use the structure “适合 + person + verb” to describe what a place is suitable for; “让孩子玩” cannot be attached after “地方” in this way.
- 他们会搬家：搬家，After “决定,” use a verb phrase directly; do not repeat the subject with “他们会.”
- 她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福：她担心儿子的童年不会幸福，Use “担心” when the subject is afraid that something may happen; “恐怕” cannot take another subject after it in this structure.
- 住在这儿教育不太好：这儿的教育不太好，Use a location-based noun phrase to say that the education in a place is not good; “住在这儿” cannot directly modify “教育” here.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 更好的 -- “找更好的地方”：更适合孩子成长的，specifies that the new place would better support the child’s growth and development.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 决定了：决定，Mental-state verbs such as “决定” normally use the zero particle, not “了,” because the sentence describes a decision rather than a completed physical action.
- Expressiveness: At the point where the mother sees the cemetery, add a small detail about her reaction: “孟母皱着眉头，看着安静的墓地。”
- Expressiveness: After the mother decides to move, add her inner thought or quoted speech: “孟母心想：‘这里不适合孟子长大。’”
- Expressiveness: When describing the new place, add one concrete detail that shows why it is nearer to the city: “新家附近有很多商店，街上很热闹。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the mother’s reason for moving and use the cause-and-effect pattern “因为……所以……” effectively. The scene also has a clear sequence, moving from the cemetery to a new place closer to the city.

## Scene 2

> 他们住才一月就又搬家。古代的时候，一般人不喜欢卖东西的人。但是孟子老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿，害得妈妈着急得很，因为她恐怕儿子会成为跟他们很像。她告诉了孟子：他们不是朋友，而是不好的人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 住才一月：才住一个月，In a pattern with a short duration followed by an early next action, place 才 before the verb; 一个月 is the usual duration phrase here.
- 古代的时候：在古代，This expression redundantly combines two time expressions; use one time expression instead.
- 老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿：老是跟这些卖东西的人玩儿，老是 and 常常 are both frequency adverbs, so using both for the same action is redundant and unnatural.
- 她恐怕儿子会：她担心儿子会，恐怕 means “I am afraid that” and cannot follow a different subject in this structure; 担心 expresses that she is worried about her son.
- 成为跟他们很像：变得跟他们很像，成为 introduces a new identity or role, while 变得 is used for a change into an adjective-like state such as being similar to someone.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 卖东西的人 -- “这些卖东西的人”：商贩，identifies them more specifically as people who sell goods for a living.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们住才一月就又搬家。：他们才住了一个月，就又搬家了。Both the stay and the move are completed events in the narrative, so use 了 to mark completion.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉了孟子：告诉孟子：A speaking verb that introduces quoted speech takes the zero particle, not 了.
- Expressiveness: After “卖东西的人,” add a small sound or sight detail to show the busy environment, such as “街上从早到晚都很热闹。”
- Expressiveness: At “妈妈着急得很,” add the mother’s private thought to show why she worries, such as “她心想：‘再这样下去，孟子会学坏的。’”
- Expressiveness: Make the mother’s warning more vivid with direct speech after “她告诉孟子,” such as “‘他们不是你的朋友，你不能老是跟着他们玩儿。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the mother’s concern and connect it to Mengzi’s behaviour, which gives this scene a strong emotional reason for the move.
- You also use a direct warning at the end, which is a good way to make the scene feel more like a story.

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- Nothing to flag.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 卖东西的人 -- “不能当卖东西的人”：商人，names the occupation more precisely as someone who trades or sells goods.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 妈妈让儿子从早到晚学习：她让他从早到晚学习，Both people were named in the immediately preceding sentence, so close re-occurrence normally uses pronouns.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉了孟子：我们找到了最后的家：告诉孟子：我们找到了最后的家，A speaking verb that introduces quoted speech takes the zero particle rather than 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为有名的哲学家：成为了有名的哲学家，The becoming is presented as a completed result in the past, so it needs 了.
- Expressiveness: At the description of the third home, add a small environmental detail that explains why it suits studying, such as “第三个家很安静，窗外还能听见学生读书的声音。”
- Expressiveness: In the mother’s speech, show her happiness about finding a suitable home: “妈妈高兴地说：‘我们终于找到了最后的家。’”
- Expressiveness: After the study routine, add Mengzi’s inner response to make his change clearer: “孟子觉得学习很有意思，每天都认真地看书。”

### Encouragement
You used a clear cause-and-effect connection with “所以”, and the quoted speech effectively shows the mother’s goal. The phrase “从早到晚学习” also gives the reader a clear sense of Mengzi’s new routine.
