## Scene 1

> 古代的时候有一个人叫神农。他需要当上医，一看别的人就想帮助他们。但是，他有一个问题，他不知道什么东西能做药。神农听说一个远远的地方有一种植物，什么病人吃都会觉得身体更好。神农三个月走去，找到有植物的地方，他看到一个很漂亮的植物，又香又好看。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，The original repeats the idea of “time”: 古代 already names a historical time period, so 的时候 is redundant here.
- 当上医：当上医生，医 is not normally used alone as the name of a profession; 医生 means a doctor.
- 看：看到，Here the intended meaning is “see a person,” not “look at a person,” so the resultative complement 到 is needed.
- 别的人：别人，别人 refers naturally to other people in general; 别的 is used to distinguish another type or one alternative from a stated one.
- 一个远远的地方：一个很远的地方，远远的 is not the usual modifier in this noun phrase; 很远的 directly describes a faraway place.
- 一个很漂亮的植物：一株很漂亮的植物，株 is the appropriate measure word for an individual plant.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 东西 -- “他不知道什么东西能做药”：植物，identifies the specific kind of thing being investigated in this scene.
- 问题 -- “但是，他有一个问题”：难题，shows that this is a difficult problem to solve rather than an ordinary question.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他有一个问题，他不知道：他有一个问题，不知道，After the subject has appeared in the same sentence and no other person competes as the subject, use zero form rather than repeat the pronoun.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农听说：他听说，The character was named in the preceding sentence and no competing character has appeared, so a pronoun is more natural for this close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农三个月走去：他三个月走去，The same character was just mentioned, so use a pronoun rather than repeat the name.
- Coherence (Reference): 他看到：看到，Within the same sentence, the subject is already clear, so use zero form for the later action.
- Coherence (Temporality): 三个月走去：走了三个月，For a completed journey lasting a stated period, place the duration after the verb and use 了 to mark the completed action.
- Coherence (Temporality): 看到：看到了，This is the final completed action in the sequence, so add 了 after the resultative complement 到.
- Expressiveness: At the moment he finds the plant, add a visual comparison: “黄色的花像星星一样，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: Add Shen Nong’s reaction after noticing the plant: “神农觉得很惊喜，想试一试这种植物。”
- Expressiveness: Make the distant journey feel harder with one brief detail: “他走啊走啊，脚都很累了。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduce Shen Nong and explain his wish to help other people, which gives the scene a strong purpose.
- The description of the plant as fragrant and attractive adds a sensory detail that makes the setting easier to imagine.

## Scene 2

> 他觉得，这种一定是做药的花。原来他想错了。吃了以后，神农快生病。 这时一个穿着蓝色衣服的人帮神农，快给他一种红的灵芝。神农吃了以后，他没有病，想起来以前一个人告诉他，灵芝对身体有好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 以前一个人告诉他：以前有一个人告诉过他，An indefinite person in this earlier event needs 有 to introduce them, and 过 marks the earlier completed experience of being told.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 觉得 -- “他觉得，这种一定是做药的花”：以为，more specifically shows that this was his earlier belief and fits the later discovery that he was wrong.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他：神农，Two male characters are present in this sentence, so the pronoun can be unclear; repeat the name of the recipient.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农吃了以后，他没有病：神农吃了以后，没有病，When the same subject continues within one sentence, use zero form rather than repeating a pronoun.
- Coherence (Temporality): 快给：赶快给了，This giving action is finished, so it needs 了; 赶快 naturally describes doing the action quickly in this situation.
- Expressiveness: After the illness begins, add a bodily feeling to show how serious it is: “神农肚子疼了起来，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Give the blue-clothed person a short urgent line of speech when offering the medicine: “快吃这个！”
- Expressiveness: Show Shennong’s realization with an internal thought after he remembers the benefit of the herb: “原来灵芝能治病！”

### Encouragement
- You used “原来” effectively to show that Shennong discovers his first judgment was mistaken.
- The scene also has a clear sequence: he eats the flower, becomes ill, receives help, and remembers what he learned before.

## Scene 3

> 啊，他怎么忘记了？他把灵芝放在包里。一回家就告诉别人。他找到药以后他写下怎么做药在书上。所以人们都知道，那红灵芝对身体好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 写下怎么做药在书上：在书上写下怎么做药，The location phrase should come before the verb it modifies; placing it after the object makes the word order unnatural.
- 对身体好处：对身体有好处，This expression needs 有: it means that something benefits the body.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 那红灵芝：那种红灵芝，A demonstrative such as 那 needs a measure word before a noun; 种 is the suitable measure word for a type of plant.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他把灵芝放在包里：他把灵芝放在包里了，This is a completed placement action in the narrative, so it needs 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 一回家就告诉别人：一回家就告诉了别人，The telling is a completed action after returning home, so the final verb takes 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 写下：写下了，The writing is presented as a finished narrative action, so add 了 after the verb-resultative phrase.
- Expressiveness: After remembering the medicinal plant, add a feeling to show the character’s relief, for example: 他松了一口气，心想：“幸好我没有忘记灵芝。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where he writes in the book, add a short note showing exactly what he records, for example: 他在书上写：“灵芝对身体有好处，可以做药。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show a logical sequence: the character remembers the plant, returns home, tells others, and records what he knows.
- Your use of 所以 effectively connects the written information with people learning about the medicinal plant.
