## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子。他们住在墓地的旁边，所以孟子老是祭拜。孟子不是祭拜，就是跟朋友聊天。他很喜欢他的城市，但是城市有才两个学校，都很小。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，Ancient era is already a time expression, so adding 的时候 is redundant.
- 城市有才：城市里才有，才 should come before the verb it limits; 城市里 also makes the location of the schools explicit.
- 两个学校：两所学校，所 is the matching measure word for schools.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 小 -- “都很小”：规模都很小，specifies that the schools are small in institutional size rather than physically small.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子。：一个叫孟子的男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。，Introduce the character by name when he first appears, rather than naming him only in the following sentence.
- Coherence (Reference): 他很喜欢他的城市：他很喜欢自己的城市，When the subject and possessor are the same person, 自己的 makes the relationship clear and avoids a possible reading that the city belongs to another male person.
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery is introduced, add a concrete detail about what Mengzi sees, such as “孟子每天看到很多人来墓地祭拜。” This helps the setting feel more vivid.
- Expressiveness: At “孟子老是祭拜,” add Mengzi’s feeling or reason, such as “他觉得祭拜很好玩。” This explains his repeated action.
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought from the mother after Mengzi copies the rituals, such as “妈妈觉得很不好意思。” This gives another character an emotional response.

### Encouragement
- You used the paired pattern “不是……就是……” correctly to show Mengzi’s repeated activities.
- You also connected ideas with “所以” and “但是,” showing that you are beginning to link events and contrasting details in your narrative.

## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈不是不喜欢他们的城市，而是想孟子有很好的教育。他们搬家到有很多学校的城市。他们从早到晚走路，孟子一直抱怨。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 想孟子有很好的教育：想让孟子接受良好的教育，Use 让 + person + verb phrase to express that the mother wants Mencius to receive an education; 想孟子有… is not a natural verb structure.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很多 -- “有很多学校”：多所，uses the specific measure word for schools and makes the number of schools more exact in this context

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬家到：搬到了，The relocation is a completed event, so use 了 after the resultative complement 到.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们从早到晚走路：他们从早到晚走了一天的路，This is a finished journey lasting from morning to evening, so the completed action needs 了.
- Expressiveness: At the walking scene, add a physical sign of tiredness, such as “走了很久以后，孟子的腿很酸，走得越来越慢。”
- Expressiveness: Show Mengzi’s complaint through dialogue, such as “孟子皱着眉头说：‘妈妈，我们还要走多久？’”
- Expressiveness: At the mother’s motivation, add her private hope, such as “妈妈心想：‘我希望孟子能在学校好好学习。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the mother’s reason for moving with the contrast structure, and “一直抱怨” effectively shows Mengzi’s unhappiness during the journey.

## Scene 3

> 他难怪抱怨，他想他的朋友，因为他不要离开他的城市。他的妈妈找到孟子一个老师。跟老师孟子学最重要的文学，所以他成为哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 找到孟子一个老师：给孟子找一个老师，Use 给 to introduce the person who benefits from the action; the teacher is the object of 找.
- 跟老师孟子学：孟子跟老师学，The subject should come before the prepositional phrase beginning with 跟.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 想 -- “他想他的朋友”：想念，specifically expresses that he misses his friends and feels emotionally attached to them.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他的朋友：自己的朋友，Because the subject is thinking about his own friends, 自己的 makes the possessor clear.
- Coherence (Reference): 他的城市：自己的城市，Because the subject does not want to leave his own city, 自己的 clearly links the city to him.
- Coherence (Temporality): 找一个老师：找了一个老师，Finding the teacher is a completed action in this narrative, so the verb needs 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为哲学家：成为了哲学家，Becoming a philosopher is presented as a completed change of state, so use 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他难怪抱怨，他想：难怪他抱怨，原来他想，Use 难怪……，原来…… to introduce the reason that explains his complaint.
- Expressiveness: After the complaint, add a short line of speech to show Mengzi’s feelings, such as: “我不想离开自己的城市，也不想离开朋友。”
- Expressiveness: When he studies with the teacher, add a detail about his attitude, such as: “孟子很认真地跟老师学习，慢慢喜欢上了读书。”
- Expressiveness: Make the ending more evaluative by describing his achievement, such as: “后来，孟子成为了一位有名的哲学家。”

### Encouragement
You clearly give Mengzi a personal reason for complaining, and you move the scene forward from finding a teacher to his later achievement.
