## Scene 1

> 古代，孟子跟孟母住在墓地的旁边。可是孟母注意，住在墓地的旁边影响孟子。他常常跟朋友们祭拜玩，所以孟母决定他们会搬家。这次，他们住在城市里的商店的旁边，从早到晚很吵。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代：在古代，Use the preposition with this historical time expression when it introduces the sentence.
- 注意：注意到，The resultative complement 到 shows that the noticing has been achieved; 注意 alone does not naturally introduce this completed realization here.
- 他常常跟朋友们祭拜玩：他常常跟朋友们一起学着别人祭拜，祭拜 is an action verb and cannot naturally be followed by 玩 in this structure; 学着 describes copying an observed action.
- 决定他们会搬家：决定搬家，决定 is followed directly by the action that is decided; do not repeat the subject or add 会 after it.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 吵 -- “从早到晚很吵”：嘈杂，specifies that many mixed sounds from the shops make the place noisy；喧闹，emphasizes the lively, loud activity of people and businesses

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子跟孟母：有一个小男孩叫孟子，他和妈妈，At the first mention, introduce the main character with a name and brief identity; then use a pronoun for the closely mentioned character.
- Expressiveness: At the point where the noisy shopping area is described, add a sensory detail about the street, such as “商人不停地吆喝，街上人来人往。”
- Expressiveness: When the mother realizes the environment affects her child, add her feeling or thought, such as “孟母很担心，心想：‘这里不适合孟子。’”
- Expressiveness: Before the decision to move, add a short line of speech to show her reaction, such as “孟母皱着眉头说：‘我们搬家吧。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly show a cause-and-effect link with “所以,” and “从早到晚很吵” gives the new setting a clear atmosphere.

## Scene 2

> 城市也有点儿危险。害得孟母只好一直陪孟子。孟子也没有朋友，难怪他老是看起来不高兴。住在商店的旁边不是适合孟子，而是让他觉得很伤心。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不是适合孟子，而是让他觉得很伤心：不适合孟子，只会让他觉得很伤心，Use the negative predicate 不适合 for a situation that is unsuitable. 不是……而是…… should correct or replace one claim with another, but “making him sad” does not directly replace “being suitable for him.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 危险 -- “城市也有点儿危险”：不太安全，specifies that the danger concerns safety while keeping the mild meaning of 有点儿危险
- 伤心 -- “让他觉得很伤心”：感到孤单，more specifically expresses the sadness of having no friends in this situation

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 孟子也没有朋友，难怪他老是看起来不高兴：难怪孟子老是看起来不高兴，原来他没有朋友，Use 难怪 for the conclusion first and 原来 for the explanation that reveals its cause.
- Expressiveness: After “孟子也没有朋友,” add a small action that shows his loneliness, for example: “他常常一个人坐在窗边，看着外面的商店。”
- Expressiveness: At the final sentence, add Mengmu’s worried thought or speech, for example: “孟母心想：‘这里真的不适合孟子。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect the unsafe environment with Mengmu staying beside Mengzi, and you also show Mengzi’s feelings through his lack of friends and unhappiness.

## Scene 3

> 所以，孟母和孟子搬到学校的旁边住。才五岁孟子开始学哲学。在学校，孟子不是看书，就是练习写字。将来，他成为中国的一个非常有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 才五岁孟子开始学哲学：孟子才五岁，就开始学哲学，The construction requires `就` after `才 + age` to show that the action began unusually early.

### Vocabulary Suggestions

### Coherence & Expressiveness

- Coherence (Temporality): 成为：成为了，This is the completed later outcome of the story, so the verb needs `了`.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 将来：后来，`后来` is the sequence marker for a later event in a past narrative; `将来` refers to the future.
- Expressiveness: After the move, add one sensory detail about the new setting, such as `学校旁边很安静，常常能听到孩子们读书的声音。`
