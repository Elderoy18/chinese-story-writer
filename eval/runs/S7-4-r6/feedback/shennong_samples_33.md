## Scene 1

> 古代时候，人不知道他们能吃什么植物，常常一吃就死。但是不知道为什么，没有办法。所以有一天，神农出去试一试很多种植物，告诉人什么植物是安全什么是危险。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代时候：古代的时候，A multi-syllable time noun needs 的 before 时候; only fixed expressions such as 古时候 omit it.
- 人：人们，This refers to a concrete group of people, so use 人们 rather than the generic noun 人.
- 人：人们，This refers to the people whom Shennong informs, so use 人们 rather than the generic noun 人.
- 什么植物是安全什么是危险：什么植物是安全的，什么植物是危险的，Two coordinated indirect questions need clear parallel clauses, and 安全的／危险的 describe the kind of plant after 是.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 没有办法 -- “不知道为什么，没有办法”：束手无策，emphasizes that the people cannot find any solution to the danger；毫无办法，makes the lack of a solution more definite.
- 试一试 -- “神农出去试一试很多种植物”：尝试，more specifically presents his action as testing different plants to learn about them；亲自尝试，emphasizes that he tests the plants himself.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他们能吃什么植物：能吃什么植物，Use zero form when the embedded clause has the same referent as the main-clause subject; repeating the pronoun is unnecessary here.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：一个叫神农的人，On first mention, briefly introduce a character with a full noun phrase rather than only assuming the reader knows who the name refers to.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉人们：告诉了人们，This is the final completed action in the sequence, so it takes 了; earlier actions in a series can remain without it.
- Expressiveness: After the people’s danger is introduced, add a short feeling detail to show their helplessness, such as “大家都很害怕，不知道该怎么办。”
- Expressiveness: Before Shennong tests the plants, add his purpose or inner thought, such as “神农想：我一定要找出能吃的植物。”
- Expressiveness: Add one concrete description of his journey to make the action more vivid, such as “他走过高山，寻找各种各样的植物。”

### Encouragement
You clearly establish the problem at the beginning and give Shennong a meaningful reason to act. The sequence from people’s danger to Shennong’s decision is easy to follow.

## Scene 2

> 发现有的植物对身体非常好，特别是茶，让肚子感觉又暖和又清楚。他告诉朋友：“你吃哪种植物，这就要看你的口味。比方说，你爱吃甜的，可能喜欢糖。”他最喜欢吃花，除了夹竹桃以外，什么都花想吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 又暖和又清楚：又暖和又舒服，清楚 describes mental understanding or clear information, not a bodily sensation; 舒服 describes physical comfort.
- 这就要看你的口味：要看你的口味，the pattern 这就要看……了 requires a question form after 看, but 你的口味 is a noun phrase rather than a question.
- 什么都花想吃：什么花都想吃，when an interrogative phrase means “every kind,” the noun belongs with 什么 before 都, followed by the verb.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好 -- “有的植物对身体非常好”：有益, specifies that the plants benefit health rather than being generally “good.”

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 朋友：一个朋友，this character appears for the first time, so use a full noun phrase to introduce the friend clearly.
- Expressiveness: At the tea discovery, add a sensory detail about its smell or warmth, such as “茶水又香又暖，喝了以后身体很舒服。”
- Expressiveness: When the character explains his idea to the friend, add a short line showing his confidence or curiosity, such as “神农说：‘我要继续试一试别的植物。’”

### Encouragement
You used direct speech effectively to show the character sharing his ideas. The exception structure with 夹竹桃 is also clear and helps show that the character distinguishes dangerous plants from other flowers.

## Scene 3

> 他发现这个口味，一百岁时候，不小心吃了毒的花。一吃就开始死。但是生病时候告诉人什么植物是药，什么植物是毒。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一百岁时候：一百岁的时候，A multi-syllable phrase before 时候 needs 的.
- 毒的花：有毒的花，Before a noun, use 有毒的 to describe a poisonous plant or flower; 毒的 is not the appropriate form here.
- 开始死：快要死了，Death is normally treated as an endpoint rather than an action that can simply begin; this revision expresses that he was close to dying.
- 人：人们，This refers to a concrete group of people whom he informed, so 人们 is needed rather than the generic noun 人.
- 是毒：有毒，毒 is a noun; use 有毒 to describe that a plant is poisonous.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不小心 -- “不小心吃了毒的花”：误食，specifically describes accidentally eating something that should not be eaten.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 这个口味：自己喜欢的口味，The reader does not know which specific flavour 这个 refers to; name the preference more clearly.
- Coherence (Temporality): 发现：发现了，This is a completed discovery before the later event, so it needs 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉人们：告诉了人们，In this scene, informing the group is a completed past action, so add 了; the zero particle is used for a saying verb that introduces direct quotation, which is not the case here.
- Expressiveness: After the poisonous flower is eaten, add a physical reaction such as “他肚子疼了起来，难受得不得了。” This makes the danger more vivid.
- Expressiveness: At the moment he thinks he may die, add an inner thought such as “他想：我会死吗？” This shows his fear directly.
- Expressiveness: When he informs others, add a short warning such as “这种花有毒，不能吃。” This makes his message clearer and more story-like.

### Encouragement
You used the pattern “一……就……” effectively to show that the danger happened immediately after eating the flower. You also clearly contrasted plants that are medicine with plants that are poisonous.
