## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，孟子和孟母住了在靠墓地的家。孩子才五岁就每天在墓地祭拜了。在那个城市，孟母从早到晚都找工作，可是找不到，害得孟母没有钱。难怪她跟孩子搬家，原来买不了房。他们搬了到别一个城市找工作。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，This phrase redundantly combines a historical time period with a “time of” expression; use the shorter time expression.
- 住了在靠墓地的家：住在靠墓地的家里，A continuing living state uses no 了, and 住 takes 在 before its location phrase.
- 跟孩子搬家：带着孩子搬家，Use 带着 to show that the mother takes her child with her when moving; 跟 means “with” and does not express this action clearly here.
- 搬了到：搬到了，Put 了 after the resultative/directional part of the verb phrase, not between 搬 and 到.
- 别一个城市：另一个城市，Use 另一个 for one additional or different city; 别 is used in forms such as 别人 or 别的 to distinguish others from the one being discussed.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 买不了 -- “原来买不了房”：无力买房，makes clear that the inability to buy a home is financial; 负担不起房子，specifies that the cost is beyond what the family can afford
- 靠 -- “靠墓地的家”：紧靠，emphasizes that the home is immediately next to the cemetery

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孩子才五岁：他才五岁，孟子 was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other child competes as the referent, so a pronoun is natural for a close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Reference): 在那个城市：在他们住的城市，The city has not previously been introduced, so 那个 does not have a clear antecedent; use a full noun phrase to identify it.
- Coherence (Reference): 害得孟母没有钱：害得她没有钱，孟母 has already been named at the start of this sentence and no competing female character appears, so use a pronoun for this close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 每天在墓地祭拜了：每天在墓地祭拜，A routine marked by 每天 uses the zero particle, not completed-action 了.
- Expressiveness: At the cemetery scene, add what Mengzi finds interesting about the activity, such as: “孟子觉得祭拜很有趣，一边笑一边学别人祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: After the unsuccessful job search, show Mengmu’s emotion with a small action or thought, such as: “孟母叹了一口气，心里很着急。”
- Expressiveness: When the family moves, add a short line of speech to make Mengmu’s decision vivid, such as: “孟母说：‘我们得找一个更好的地方住。’”

### Encouragement
- You used the pattern 才五岁就 correctly to show that Mengzi was very young.
- Your use of 可是 to contrast searching for work with not finding it, and 难怪……原来…… to connect the reason for moving, gives the scene a clear sequence of ideas.

## Scene 2

> 这个城市老是很吵，不是人吵架，就是都叫，害得孟子一直不能睡觉。在那儿，孟母也找不到工作。孟子和妈妈要又一个次搬了家。在第三个城市，孟母找到工作，在商店做衣服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 人：人们，This refers to a concrete group making noise in the city, so use the plural noun rather than the generic noun.
- 又一个次：再……一次，For a move that has not happened yet, use 再; after a modal verb, place 再 before the verb and place 一次 after it.
- 孟母找到工作：孟母找到了工作，Finding the job is a completed event in this narration, so add 了 after the result complement.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很吵 -- “这个城市老是很吵”：嘈杂，specifies that the city is full of confusing, unpleasant noise.
- 做 -- “在商店做衣服”：缝制，specifies that her work is sewing and making clothes.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬了家：搬家，After 要, the move is planned rather than completed, so use the zero particle rather than 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 孟子和妈妈要又一个次搬了家：所以，孟子和妈妈要再搬一次家，The mother’s inability to find work is a cause for moving again, so use 所以 to mark the result.
- Expressiveness: After describing the city as noisy, add one concrete sound, such as “有人大声吵架，有人不停地叫卖。”
- Expressiveness: Show how the noise affects the child with a detail such as “孟子捂着耳朵，还是睡不着。”
- Expressiveness: Add the mother’s worried thoughts before the move, such as “孟母心想：‘这儿太吵了，我得再搬一次家。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly show two important problems in the second city: the constant noise and the mother’s difficulty finding work. The cause-and-result idea in the sentence about the child being unable to sleep is especially clear.

## Scene 3

> 孟子在那个商店也找了到工作，可是不是做衣服的工作，而是喜欢的工作。孟子学习馆学了十五年，他的学习和他的旅行经验很重要，对他很多好处。他是大人的时候，他成为很有名的哲人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 找了到：找到了，The resultative complement 到 must come directly after the verb, and 了 follows the completed resultative form.
- 学习馆学了十五年：学习了十五年，学习 is the verb here and can take the duration directly; 馆 is a noun and cannot be placed between the verb and its duration without a location structure.
- 对他很多好处：对他有很多好处，The verb 有 is required to state that something brings benefits to someone.
- 他是大人的时候：他长大以后，Use 长大以后 to show that becoming an adult happened before the later event of becoming a philosopher.
- 成为：成为了，This is a completed change of state in the narrative, so 成为 takes 了.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 做 -- “不是做衣服的工作”：缝制，specifies that the work involves sewing clothes rather than making them in a general sense
- 很有名 -- “成为很有名的哲人”：享有盛名，expresses being widely known and respected in a more vivid way

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他的学习和他的旅行经验：他的学习和旅行经验，When the same possessor applies to two nouns in one phrase, use zero form for the second reference instead of repeating the pronoun.
- Coherence (Reference): 他长大以后，他成为了：他长大以后，成为了，The subject has already been named in the time clause, so zero form is natural in the main clause.
- Expressiveness: After the long period of study, add a detail showing Mengzi’s effort or attitude. Example: “他每天认真读书，常常读到很晚。”
- Expressiveness: Add an inner thought or a short line of speech to show why he values learning and travel. Example: “孟子心想：‘我要多学习，多看看外面的世界。’”
- Expressiveness: Describe the result of his fame more concretely. Example: “很多人从很远的地方来听他讲学。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect Mengzi’s work, long-term learning, travel experience, and later success as a philosopher. Your use of contrast in “不是……而是……” also helps show that his job was different from the clothing work.
