## Scene 1

> 很久以前，有一个老男人叫神农。神农在古代每一天看到了别的小镇人生病还是不舒服，所以他决定开始找新的药。他下一天开始爬当地的山脉，找了各种各样的植物。神农试一试每个植物，知道有什么好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别的小镇人：别的小镇的人们，A concrete group of people should use 人们, and 的 links the town phrase to the people.
- 下一天：第二天，Use 第二天 to mean the day following the previously described day in a narrative.
- 找了各种各样的植物：找到了各种各样的植物，找 means “look for,” while 找到 means “find”; the sentence describes plants that Shen Nong found.
- 每个植物：每种植物，种 is the appropriate measure word for types of plants; 个 is too generic here.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好处 -- “知道有什么好处”：药用价值，specifies that Shen Nong is learning how each plant may be useful as medicine.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 每一天看到了：每天看到，A routine action uses the zero particle, so 了 should not follow 看到 here.
- Expressiveness: After the villagers’ illness is mentioned, add a short thought to show Shen Nong’s motivation. Example: “神农想：我要找到能治病的植物。”
- Expressiveness: When Shen Nong climbs the mountains, add one visual detail about the setting or plants. Example: “山上长着许多他不认识的植物。”
- Expressiveness: After he tests a plant, add one concrete observation that he records. Example: “他记下了植物的颜色和药用价值。”

### Encouragement
- You introduced Shen Nong clearly at the beginning and used time order to move the scene forward. You also included his careful search and testing, which makes his purpose easy to understand.

## Scene 2

> 比方说，他一次试一试有些植物的叶，吃得又新鲜又甜。煮这些叶以后，他一喝就知道他发现新的茶叶。虽然他找到了很多的新的植物，但是他的旅程有的时候特别是危险。比方说，他一吃灵芝就开始生病。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一次试一试有些植物的叶：尝了一些植物的叶子，Verb reduplication is for a light or tentative plan, not for an action already carried out in a narrative; use a normal completed verb here.
- 吃得又新鲜又甜：吃起来又新鲜又甜，吃起来 introduces an evaluation of how food tastes; 吃得 cannot be used to describe the leaves’ flavour in this structure.
- 煮这些叶以后：煮了这些叶以后，The boiling is completed before the later drinking action, so it needs 了.
- 发现新的茶叶：发现了新的茶叶，Finding the tea leaves is a completed action, so add 了 after the verb.
- 特别是危险：特别危险，特别 is an adverb that directly modifies the adjective 危险; 特别是 is used to introduce a specially mentioned person, thing, or situation.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 新 -- “他发现了新的茶叶”：陌生的，shows that these tea leaves were unfamiliar to him before this discovery；未尝过的，makes clear that he had not tasted this kind before.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After “吃起来又新鲜又甜,” add a sensory detail about the drink, such as “水里还有一股淡淡的清香。”
- Expressiveness: At the moment of discovery, add Shennong’s reaction or thought, such as “他高兴地想：这种叶子可以做很好喝的饮料！”
- Expressiveness: After “开始生病,” show the physical feeling more clearly, such as “他捂着肚子，难受得不得了。”

### Encouragement
You used both “虽然……但是……” and “一……就……” effectively to connect ideas. The scene also gives clear examples of Shennong testing plants and discovering tea.

## Scene 3

> 一个爬山的男人正好看到神农的麻烦，然后给了他一些药。神农最终他的小镇回到，跟别的人分享他的发现。你认为神农的故事真的吗？这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 神农最终他的小镇回到：神农最终回到了他的小镇，The destination of 回到 follows the verb, and 了 marks the completed return.
- 跟别的人分享他的发现：跟别人分享他的发现，别的人 is used to distinguish people from someone already mentioned; here there is no such contrast, so 别人 is the natural form.
- 这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处：这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处了，This fixed structure ends with 了; this 了 belongs to the structure rather than marking a completed action.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 麻烦 -- “看到神农的麻烦”：困境，specifies that Shen Nong is in a difficult and dangerous situation rather than having a general problem.
- 药 -- “给了他一些药”：草药，specifies that the medicine is made from plants, which fits a mountain-exploration story.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: At the rescue, describe the man and the medicine more vividly: “一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人走过来，给神农吃了一种像伞一样的草药。”
- Expressiveness: After the medicine, add Shen Nong’s physical feeling to show the change: “神农吃了药以后，肚子不那么疼了，觉得好多了。”
- Expressiveness: When he shares his discovery, add a short line of speech: “神农说：‘这种植物可以做药，但是有些植物有毒，不能吃。’”

### Encouragement
You created a clear rescue action with 正好 and 然后, which helps the events move forward. Your final question also gives the scene an interesting reflective ending.
