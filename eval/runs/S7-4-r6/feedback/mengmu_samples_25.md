## Scene 1

> 这是古代的故事。孟子小的时候，他的爸爸很早就去世了。从他的家到墓地很近，所以他每天老是从早到晚去墓地跟朋友们玩玩儿。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 从早到晚去墓地跟朋友们玩玩儿：从早到晚都在墓地跟朋友们玩儿，A trip expressed by 去墓地 is a bounded action, so it does not naturally last from morning to evening; use 在墓地 for the sustained activity. The reduplication in 玩玩儿 suggests a brief or casual try, which conflicts with the long duration.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很早 -- “他的爸爸很早就去世了”：在孟子年幼时，specifies that his father died when Mengzi was still a young child.
- 朋友们 -- “去墓地跟朋友们玩儿”：玩伴，specifies that these friends are the children he plays with.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: At the mention of the cemetery, add a small visual detail to make the setting clearer: “墓地里常常有人哭，也有人带着花来祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: After the playing action, add Mengzi’s feeling or behaviour: “孟子觉得他们祭拜的样子很有意思，就跟着学起来。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of dialogue between the children: “朋友说：‘我们来学他们祭拜吧！’”

### Encouragement
- You introduced the setting and Mengzi clearly before using pronouns, and the cause-and-effect connector “所以” makes the reason for his visits easy to follow.

## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈觉得在墓地玩儿，害得，所以他们搬家。搬家对孟子有好的影响，他才五岁，可是每天不是写字就是帮妈妈的工作。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在墓地玩儿，害得：孟子在墓地玩儿会带来不好的影响，The structure 害得 must be followed by the affected person and a harmful result; the original leaves both parts unfinished, so this version states the mother’s concern clearly.
- 帮妈妈的工作：帮妈妈工作，After 帮 + person, use a verb phrase directly; 的 cannot link the person to the action in this structure.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好的 -- “搬家对孟子有好的影响”：积极的，specifies that the influence helps Mengzi develop in a constructive direction.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们搬家：他们搬家了，This is one completed move caused by the mother’s concern, so add 了 to mark the completed action.
- Expressiveness: After the mention of the cemetery, add one concrete detail about what Mengzi notices there, such as “墓地旁边常常有人哭，孟子觉得很好玩。”
- Expressiveness: Show the mother’s worry before the move with an action or a short quotation, such as “孟母皱着眉头说：‘不能让孟子每天在这儿玩。’”

### Encouragement
You used “才五岁” effectively to show Mengzi’s young age, and the pattern “不是……就是……” clearly shows what he does every day.

## Scene 3

> 他很聪明的人，难怪他成为有名的人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他很聪明的人：他是一个很聪明的人，The noun phrase needs the copula 是 to serve as the predicate; 一个 also makes the reference to one person natural.
- 成为：成为了，This describes a completed outcome in the narrative, so add 了 after the verb.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 难怪他成为了有名的人：难怪成为了有名的人，Use zero form for the same subject in the second part of the same sentence; the reader can still understand that the subject is 孟子.
- Expressiveness: Before the conclusion, show one specific action that demonstrates his intelligence, such as: “孟子每天认真看书，还常常问老师问题。”
- Expressiveness: You could add his mother’s proud reaction to make the ending warmer: “孟母看到他这么认真，心里很高兴。”

### Encouragement
- You used 难怪 well to connect his intelligence with his later success. The sentence also gives the scene a clear concluding idea.
