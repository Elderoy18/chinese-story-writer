### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 常常 -- “所以他们常常搬了家”：频繁，emphasizes that the moves happened repeatedly and often.
- 安排 -- “孟子的妈妈安排他的生活”：规划，emphasizes making a deliberate plan for his life.

- Coherence (Reference): 孟子是古代的人：他是古代的人，After the name has just appeared and no other male character competes, use a pronoun for the close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Reference): 她望子成龙，所以孟子的妈妈安排他的生活：她望子成龙，所以安排他的生活，The same subject continues across the connected clauses, so use zero form rather than repeating the full noun phrase.
- Coherence (Temporality): 常常搬了家：常常搬家，A habitual action uses the zero particle; 了 marks a completed action, not a routine.
- Expressiveness: After the mother’s reason for moving, add an inner thought to show her concern, such as: 孟子的妈妈心想：“我要给他找一个更好的地方。”

## Scene 2

> 因为他们常常搬了家，中国人说，孟母三迁。孟子没有爸爸，所以，他的妈妈更重要。从这儿到那儿，妈妈教孟子很多技能。先她让孟子跟朋友玩儿，然后教他怎么买又便宜又好质量的东西。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 搬了家：搬家，A habitual action marked by 常常 takes the zero aspect form, not 了.
- 先她让：她先让，The sequence adverb 先 should follow the subject and come before the verb phrase.
- 又便宜又好质量的东西：又便宜、质量又好的东西，质量 is a noun, so it needs the adjective 好 to describe it; the two coordinated descriptions are “cheap” and “of good quality.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很多 -- “妈妈教孟子很多技能”：各种，emphasizes that the skills are different kinds, matching the examples of playing and buying things.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 妈妈教孟子很多技能：她教孟子很多技能，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the mother was named in the preceding sentence and no female character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为他们常常搬了家，中国人说：因为他们常常搬家，所以中国人说，Use 因为……所以…… to clearly link the cause of the frequent moves with the saying that follows.
- Expressiveness: After “妈妈教孟子很多技能,” add a brief detail about how she teaches: “买东西的时候，她会让孟子先看价钱。”
- Expressiveness: After the final sentence, add a short line of dialogue to show the lesson more vividly: “妈妈说：‘买东西要看价钱，也要看质量。’”

### Encouragement
- You give clear examples of the mother’s teaching, especially playing with friends and learning how to buy things. The use of 先 and 然后 also shows that you are organizing the actions in order.
