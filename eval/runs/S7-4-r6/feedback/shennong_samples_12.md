## Scene 1

> 很久以前，古代的人很不知道什么植物能吃，也什么植物不能吃。所以很多人常常吃不安全的植物，吃不安全的植物对你的身体有坏处，很麻烦的情况。但是一个人，神农，要解决这个问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很不知道：不知道，The degree adverb 很 does not normally modify the negative knowledge verb 不知道 in this context.
- 很麻烦的情况：是一种很麻烦的情况，After the preceding clause, this noun phrase needs 是 to form a complete predicate that identifies the situation.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不安全 -- “很多人常常吃不安全的植物”：有害的，specifies that the plants can harm people’s bodies；有毒的，more specifically identifies plants containing poison in this story context
- 有坏处 -- “对你的身体有坏处”：有害，states the physical harm more directly and concisely

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 你的身体：他们的身体，The pronoun should refer back to the many people who eat the plants, rather than addressing the reader directly.
- Coherence (Reference): 但是一个人，神农，要解决这个问题：但是有一个叫神农的人要解决这个问题，On a character’s first mention, introduce the character with a clear full noun phrase including the name.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 也什么植物不能吃：什么植物不能吃，Also cannot directly be followed by a noun phrase or clause; here the two embedded questions can simply be placed side by side.
- Expressiveness: At the opening, add the consequence of people’s uncertainty to make the danger more vivid: “很多人吃了有毒的植物，常常生病。”
- Expressiveness: After introducing Shennong, show his determination through an internal thought: “神农想：我一定要找出能吃的植物！”
- Expressiveness: Add a feeling to show why the situation matters to people: “他们不知道该吃什么，心里很担心。”

### Encouragement
You clearly establish the main problem—people cannot tell which plants are edible—and introduce Shennong’s goal of solving it. Your use of connectors to move from the problem to the character’s response gives the scene a clear direction.

## Scene 2

> 他又聪明又努力，旅行很远找到很多植物。比方说，灵芝和夹竹桃。他一吃各种各样的植物就知道能不能吃这些植物。然后，神农写他的主意。他喜欢植物的味道，特别是花。神农遇到了一个黄色的花，一吃就觉得不舒服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 旅行很远：去了很远的地方，Use a place phrase to express that he travelled to a distant location; “旅行” cannot directly take “很远” in this form.
- 遇到了一个黄色的花：看到了一朵黄色的花，Use the matching measure word “朵” for a flower. “看到” is also more suitable for noticing a flower than “遇到.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农写他的主意：他写他的主意，Use a pronoun when the same character was mentioned in the immediately preceding sentence and no other character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农遇到了一个黄色的花：他遇到了一个黄色的花，Use a pronoun for the same recently mentioned character.
- Coherence (Temporality): 找到很多植物：找到了很多植物，This is a completed result of the journey, so use 了 after the verb.
- Coherence (Temporality): 写他的主意：写下了自己的主意，This is a completed action after “然后,” so use 了. “写下” expresses recording something, and “自己的” clearly shows that the ideas belong to the subject.
- Expressiveness: When he sees the flower, add a visual detail to make it memorable, such as “那朵花又黄又大，像一颗星星。”
- Expressiveness: After he feels ill, show his physical feeling or thoughts, such as “他的肚子疼了起来，心里很害怕。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought at that moment, such as “他想：这种花为什么让我这么难受？”

### Encouragement
- You used “又……又……” well to describe Shennong’s qualities, and “一……就……” clearly shows that the discomfort came immediately after he ate the plant.

## Scene 3

> 发现了夹竹桃有毒。别人看到他，很快给他吃一个灵芝。神农一吃就觉得比较好。然后，他继续写他发现的信息。会告诉他的朋友哪一个植物能不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别人：一个男人，`别人` distinguishes other people from a person already being contrasted; it cannot introduce a new rescuer here.
- 给他吃一个灵芝：给了他一个灵芝，The giving action is completed, so `给` needs `了` before its indirect and direct objects.
- 写他发现的信息：写下他发现的信息，For recording discovered information, `写下` means to write it down and keep a record of it; plain `写` does not express that intended meaning here.
- 哪一个植物能不能吃：哪一种植物能吃，哪一种不能吃，`哪一个` asks which item, while `能不能` forms an A-not-A question. Use parallel clauses to state which kinds are edible and which are not.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 比较好 -- “神农一吃就觉得比较好”：好些了，more clearly shows that his condition has improved compared with before.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: When the rescuer appears, add one visible detail so the new character is easier to imagine: “一个穿蓝色衣服的男人看到神农，赶快给了他一个灵芝。”
- Expressiveness: After the medicine works, describe Shen Nong’s physical feeling: “神农觉得好些了，肚子也不那么疼了。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short question after he recovers to make the scene more lively: “神农问：‘这是什么植物？’”

### Encouragement
You used `一……就……` effectively to show that Shen Nong felt better immediately after eating the medicine. The events also follow a clear order: discovery, help, recovery, and recording the information.
