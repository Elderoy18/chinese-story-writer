## Scene 1

> 古代的时候人觉得祭拜非常重要，孟子很小的时候，从早到晚一直祭拜，因为他妈妈告诉他祭拜很重要。虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜。他的同学们老是对他开玩笑了。孟子妈妈看了他的同学们对孟子不好的影响，所以孟子和孟子妈妈搬家了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，The time noun is redundant with the “……的时候” pattern; use the shorter time expression.
- 人：人们，This refers to a group of people in ancient times, so use the plural/group noun rather than the generic noun.
- 看了他的同学们对孟子不好的影响：看到了他的同学们对孟子不好的影响，Here the mother perceives the effect; “看到了” means “saw/noticed,” while “看” means intentionally looking.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不好的 -- “他的同学们对孟子不好的影响”：负面的，This specifies that the influence harms him rather than merely being generally “not good.”

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子妈妈搬家了：孟子和妈妈搬家了，The mother was just mentioned, so use the close re-occurrence form instead of repeating Mengzi’s name inside the same noun phrase.
- Coherence (Temporality): 老是对他开玩笑了：老是对他开玩笑，This describes a repeated habit, so it takes the zero particle rather than completed-action “了”.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜。：因为他每天只祭拜，所以没有朋友。These ideas express cause and effect, so use the paired cause-and-effect connector rather than an unfinished contrast connector.
- Expressiveness: After the classmates’ teasing, add Mengzi’s feeling, for example: “孟子觉得很难过。”
- Expressiveness: Make the teasing more concrete with a short line of speech, for example: “同学们笑着说：‘你又在祭拜！’”
- Expressiveness: At the point where his mother notices the influence, add her concern, for example: “妈妈很担心孟子。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show a cause for Mengzi’s actions and then connect it to the family’s move.
- You also use time phrases such as “从早到晚” effectively to show how often Mengzi performs the action.

## Scene 2

> 孟子才十一岁，成为一个哲学家。孟子不是祭拜，而是看政治文章，难怪他成为了哲学家。不过，看过很多政治文章对他害得，他有很多问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 才十一岁，成为一个哲学家：才十一岁，就成为了一个哲学家，With 才 + an age, 就 introduces the unexpectedly early result; becoming a philosopher is a completed change, so it takes 了.
- 看过很多政治文章对他害得：他看了很多政治文章，害得他，害得 cannot be used after 对. The structure is a cause followed by 害得 + person + harmful result.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 问题 -- “他有很多问题”：疑问，specifies that these are unanswered intellectual questions arising from his reading.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子不是祭拜：他不是祭拜，Use a pronoun when the same character was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other character competes as the referent.
- Expressiveness: At the point where he reads, add a small action detail to show concentration, such as “他每天坐在桌前认真看书。”
- Expressiveness: After “他有很多问题,” add one specific thought or question, such as “他想：国家为什么会有这么多规矩？”
- Expressiveness: Add a feeling to show his curiosity, such as “孟子觉得很好奇，想知道更多事情。”

### Encouragement
- You used the contrast structure “不是……而是……” effectively to show that Mengzi chose reading over ritual activities.
- Your scene also clearly connects his reading of political writing with his development into a philosopher.

## Scene 3

> 他一直问问题，但是不能解决那些问题。孟子长大，他开始能解决问题。他常常建议你不是学金融，而是学政治，因为如果你懂政治，你会成为高薪。后来孟子成为教授，他教学生很厉害。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 孟子长大：孟子长大了，Growing up is a completed change of state in this past narrative, so it takes 了.
- 不是学金融，而是学政治：不要学金融，而要学政治，After 建议, use a negative recommendation with 不要 rather than 不是……而是……, which refutes one statement and replaces it with another.
- 成为高薪：拿到高薪，High pay is something a person receives rather than something they become.
- 成为教授：成为了教授，Becoming a professor is a completed change in the narrative, so the verb takes 了.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 一直 -- “他一直问问题”：不断，shows that the questions came one after another；提出，makes clear that he was raising questions for discussion.
- 厉害 -- “他教学生很厉害”：很会教学生，states his teaching ability more directly；很有办法，shows that he has effective ways to teach students.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他：∅，After naming 孟子 in the same sentence, omit the repeated subject in the following clause: 孟子长大了，开始能解决问题.
- Coherence (Reference): 你：学生，The second-person pronoun has no identifiable addressee in this third-person story; introduce the people receiving his advice with a full noun.
- Coherence (Reference): 他：∅，After 孟子 has been named at the start of the sentence, omit the repeated subject in the next clause: 后来孟子成为了教授，教学生很厉害.
- Expressiveness: After he cannot solve the questions, add his feeling and a small action: “他皱着眉头，觉得很着急。”
- Expressiveness: Turn his advice into direct speech to show his personality: “他对学生说：‘不要只学金融，也要了解政治。’”
- Expressiveness: Show how the students respond to his teaching: “学生们听得很认真，还常常举手提问。”

### Encouragement
You used frequency words such as “一直” and “常常” well to show repeated actions. The transition word “后来” also helps the reader follow the time change.
