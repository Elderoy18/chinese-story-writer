## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男人觉得植物很有意思。他叫神农。有的植物吃了以后他的肚子疼死了，可是有的吃了以后很舒服。他找很多植物。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，This phrase is redundant because 古代 already expresses a time period; ……的时候 adds another “time of” meaning.
- 他找很多植物：他找到了很多植物，找 means “to look for,” while 找到 means “to find”; 到 shows that the search reached a result, and 了 marks the completed action.

### Vocabulary Suggestions

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一个男人觉得植物很有意思。他叫神农：一个叫神农的男人觉得植物很有意思，Introduce a character by name and description at the first mention, rather than naming him only in the following sentence.
- Expressiveness: After “植物很有意思,” add Shen Nong’s curious thought, such as “神农想：这些植物到底能不能吃呢？”
- Expressiveness: Make the painful reaction more vivid with a physical detail, such as “他捂着肚子，疼得直冒汗。”
- Expressiveness: When he finds plants, add a small visual detail, such as “他看见山上长着各种颜色的植物。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly contrast the different effects of plants with “有的……可是有的……,” which makes Shen Nong’s discovery easy to understand. Your use of “吃了以后” also shows the order between eating a plant and feeling its effect.

## Scene 2

> 凉山很大的山，这也找植物。一天，他找到黄色的花。他听说你跟黄色的花做茶很有用。比方说，你的头发疼死，这冰茶是药。他一喝茶就很不舒服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 凉山很大的山，这也找植物：凉山是一座很大的山，他在这儿找植物，Use 是 and the measure word 座 to describe what 凉山 is; 这 cannot be the subject that performs 找, so use the established character and 这儿 for the location.
- 找到黄色的花：找到了黄色的花，The finding is a completed event, so 找到 needs 了.
- 你跟黄色的花做茶：用黄色的花做的茶，Use 用 to introduce the material used to make tea. 跟 means “with” another person or thing and does not mark the material of a product; 做的 also turns the action into a modifier of 茶.
- 你的头发疼死：头疼得要死的时候，头发 means “hair,” while 头疼 means “to have a headache.” A degree complement uses 得 before 要死, and 的时候 makes the headache the condition for the following statement.
- 这冰茶：这杯冰茶，A demonstrative such as 这 needs a measure word before a countable noun; 杯 is the appropriate measure word for a serving of tea.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很有用 -- “用黄色的花做的茶很有用”：有疗效，specifies that the tea has a healing effect in this medicine-related context；能治病，states more directly what kind of usefulness the tea has.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他听说用黄色的花做的茶很有用。他一喝茶就很不舒服：他听说用黄色的花做的茶很有用，可是他一喝茶就很不舒服，Add 可是 because feeling unwell after drinking the tea contrasts with the expected helpful result.
- Expressiveness: At the first appearance of the flower, add one visual detail to make it memorable, for example: “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花。”
- Expressiveness: After the tea causes discomfort, show the physical feeling more clearly, for example: “他的肚子疼了起来，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Add the character’s worried thought at that moment, for example: “他想：我会不会生病了？”

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect the yellow flower, tea, and the character’s physical reaction, so the events are easy to follow. You also used the useful pattern 一……就…… correctly to show that the discomfort happened immediately after drinking the tea.

## Scene 3

> 糟糕，这黄色的花是夹竹桃，特别是毒。正好在这儿，灵芝药的夹竹桃他吃完了，也写一个课文的植物。谢谢神农。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 这黄色的花：这朵黄色的花，A demonstrative before a countable noun needs a matching measure word.
- 特别是毒：特别有毒，毒 cannot function as the predicate adjective here; 有毒 means “toxic” and can be modified by 特别.
- 灵芝药的夹竹桃：灵芝是药，夹竹桃，The original incorrectly joins three nouns with 的. 灵芝 needs 是药 to state that it is medicine, while 夹竹桃 begins a separate phrase.
- 写一个课文的植物：记下这两种植物，写 directly with plants does not express recording information about them; 记下 means to record them, and the revised phrase clearly identifies the two plants.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 也写一个课文的植物：也记下了这两种植物，This is another completed action after 吃完了, so the final verb needs 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 正好在这儿：这时，Use 这时 to introduce a new event that interrupts or follows the previous action; 正好在这儿 only gives a location and does not connect the events clearly.
- Expressiveness: After identifying the flower, add a visual detail to show why it looked tempting: “黄色的小花像星星一样，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: When the character realizes the danger, add a physical reaction or thought: “神农的肚子突然疼了起来，心想：糟糕，我吃错植物了！”
- Expressiveness: At the end, add a short line of speech to make the lesson clear: “神农说：‘夹竹桃有毒，不能吃；灵芝可以做药。’”
