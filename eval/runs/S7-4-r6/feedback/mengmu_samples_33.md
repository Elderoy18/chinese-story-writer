## Scene 1

> 一天，很久很久以前，孟子和他妈妈住在中国。孟子妈妈的工作是祭拜，孟子不高兴，因为他说我们老是搬家，为什么？他妈妈说，从早到晚，我一直祭拜，很多墓地得我祭拜。所以我们得去很多不同的地方。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 孟子不高兴，因为他说我们老是搬家，为什么？：孟子不高兴，问：“我们为什么老是搬家？”，Use a quoted question after a speaking verb; the question word belongs inside the question rather than being left after a declarative clause.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不高兴 -- “孟子不高兴”：不满, emphasizes that he is dissatisfied with the repeated moves.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一天，很久很久以前，孟子和他妈妈住在中国。：很久很久以前的一天，有一个小男孩叫孟子，他和妈妈住在中国。，On first mention, introduce the character with both a name and a brief identity, so the reader knows who he is.
- Expressiveness: After “孟子不高兴,” add a small physical reaction to show his feeling, such as “孟子皱着眉头，低声问妈妈。”
- Expressiveness: Make the mother’s work feel more demanding with a brief detail, such as “妈妈叹了口气，说：‘从早到晚，我要去很多墓地祭拜。’”

### Encouragement
You used dialogue to show why the family needs to move, which makes the scene easy to follow. The phrase “从早到晚” also clearly shows how busy the mother is.

## Scene 2

> 孟子想，难怪我们常常搬家，原来我妈妈很忙。可是常常搬家害得孟子很难找到朋友。每次搬家孟子也觉得新的地方不是太热就是太冷，不是太大就是太小。在哪的地方，他都不高兴。孟子想帮助妈妈祭拜，可是他六岁，所以不能祭拜。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在哪的地方：在哪里，The locative question word 哪 cannot be followed by 的地方 in this structure; use 哪里 to ask about or refer to an unspecified location.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 常常 -- “难怪我们常常搬家”：频繁，specifies that the moves happen many times within a relatively short period.
- 不高兴 -- “他都不高兴”：闷闷不乐，shows a quieter, lasting kind of unhappiness after arriving in each new place.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 每次搬家孟子也觉得：每次搬家他也觉得，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the preceding sentence has already named the same character and no competing person is present.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子想帮助妈妈祭拜：他想帮助妈妈祭拜，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence of the same character; the preceding sentence already makes the referent clear.
- Expressiveness: After the difficulty finding friends, add Mengzi’s personal feeling, such as “他觉得很孤单，不知道该跟谁一起玩。”
- Expressiveness: At the final sentence, add a short thought or line of speech to show his wish to help, such as “他心想：‘我什么时候才能帮妈妈呢？’”

### Encouragement
- You used the paired pattern “难怪……原来……” effectively to show Mengzi’s realization about why the family moves often.
- You also connected his age to his inability to help with a clear cause-and-effect relationship.

## Scene 3

> 四年后，孟子开始帮妈妈祭拜。他画画很厉害，他越来越喜欢祭拜，所以他祭拜不是想帮妈妈工作，而是他很喜欢画画。孟子成为很著名的古代画家。今天很多中国人很喜欢孟子。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- Nothing to flag.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 厉害 -- “他画画很厉害”：很擅长画画，identifies more precisely that his strength is drawing ability.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他越来越喜欢祭拜：越来越喜欢祭拜，The subject is the same as in the preceding clause, so use zero form rather than repeating the pronoun.
- Coherence (Reference): 所以他祭拜：所以祭拜，This continues the same subject within the sentence, so the repeated pronoun should be omitted.
- Coherence (Reference): 而是他很喜欢画画：而是很喜欢画画，The contrasting clause still has the same subject, so zero form is more natural than another pronoun.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子成为了很著名的古代画家：他成为了很著名的古代画家，The character was named recently and no other person competes as the referent, so use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Reference): 很多中国人很喜欢孟子：很多中国人很喜欢他，The character remains the clear, recently mentioned referent, so a pronoun avoids unnecessarily repeating the name.
- Coherence (Temporality): 孟子成为很著名的古代画家：孟子成为了很著名的古代画家，This describes a completed change of state in the past, so the verb needs 了.
- Expressiveness: After “孟子开始帮妈妈祭拜”, add a concrete action detail, such as “他一边祭拜，一边学妈妈的动作。”
- Expressiveness: At “他越来越喜欢祭拜”, show his personal reaction, such as “孟子觉得祭拜很有趣。”

### Encouragement
You clearly mark the time jump with “四年后,” and “越来越” effectively shows that the character’s interest is growing over time.
