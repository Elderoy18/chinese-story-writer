## Scene 1

> 神农和他的家人住在古代，所以不知道什么植物能吃。因为他的家人常常生病了，所以神农和他的哥哥一起去旅行找可以吃的植物。他们去很多不一样的地方，比方说高山、山谷和沙漠。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 住在古代：在古代住在一起，An era is a time expression, so it should be introduced with 在古代 rather than used as the location after 住在.
- 常常生病了：常常生病，A habitual action or state with 常常 uses the zero particle, not completed-action 了.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 找 -- “找可以吃的植物”：寻找, emphasizes an ongoing search for edible plants；探寻, emphasizes exploring in order to discover which plants are safe.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：一个叫神农的人，This is the character’s first mention, so introduce him with his name and a full noun phrase rather than using only the name.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们去很多不一样的地方：他们去了很多不一样的地方，This is a completed narrative action, so the verb needs 了.
- Expressiveness: After the family’s illness is mentioned, add a concrete symptom to show why the problem is serious, for example: “他们常常吃了有毒的植物，肚子很疼。”
- Expressiveness: When describing the journey, add one sensory detail about the difficult places, for example: “高山又高又冷，沙漠里很热。”
- Expressiveness: Add Shennong’s purpose or feeling before the trip, for example: “神农很担心家人，决定去找可以吃的植物。”

### Encouragement
You clearly connect the family’s illness with Shennong’s decision to travel, creating a logical reason for the journey. You also use 能 correctly in “什么植物能吃,” which clearly expresses whether plants are edible.

## Scene 3

> 夹竹桃是毒的，我想知道灵芝会帮他。”哥哥给神农灵芝。神农吃完了以后开始痊愈了。神农说：“谢谢哥哥，你帮得真好。灵芝我们可以吃，特别是如果我们已经吃了夹竹桃。“

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 开始痊愈了：开始好转了，痊愈 describes a full recovery, so it does not fit well with 开始; 好转 describes the condition beginning to improve.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 吃完了 -- “神农吃完了以后”：服下灵芝，identifies the action more precisely as taking a medicinal plant and makes the object clear.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农吃完了以后开始痊愈了：他吃完了以后开始好转了，use a pronoun for a recently mentioned character when no other referent competes; 神农 was named in the immediately preceding sentence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 哥哥给神农灵芝：哥哥给了神农灵芝，this is a completed action in the narrative, so it needs 了.
- Expressiveness: After the recovery begins, add a physical detail to show the change, such as “他的肚子慢慢不疼了。”
- Expressiveness: The brother could show concern while giving the medicine, such as “哥哥着急地说：‘快吃灵芝！’”
- Expressiveness: Make the thanks more personal by adding Shennong’s feeling, such as “神农松了一口气，说：‘谢谢哥哥！’”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the cause-and-effect sequence: Shennong eats the medicine and then begins to recover. The line of thanks also gives the scene a warm, personal ending.
