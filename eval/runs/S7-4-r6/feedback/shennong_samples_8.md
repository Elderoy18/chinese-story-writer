## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个人叫神农。一天，神农正在路上走，一看很多人正在吃一些植物。一些人一吃植物就生病了，神农想帮别人。所以想知道哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，This time expression is redundant because 古代 already names a time period; add 在 instead.
- 一看：看到，Here the verb means that Shennong perceived the people, so it needs the resultative complement 到.
- 别人：人们，别人 means “other people” in contrast with a stated person or group; this sentence refers generally to the people who were becoming sick.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 想知道 -- “所以想知道哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃”：想弄清楚，emphasizes wanting to find a clear and reliable answer through investigation.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the people become ill, add a visible symptom to make the danger more concrete: “有的人捂着肚子，脸色很难看。”
- Expressiveness: After Shennong decides to help, add his worried thought: “神农很担心，心想：‘我一定要弄清楚哪些植物能吃。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly establish the problem that motivates Shennong’s actions. You also use the immediate-result pattern effectively to show that eating certain plants causes people to become ill.

## Scene 2

> 然后，神农开始尝很多植物，比方说他吃过生姜、芥兰和菠菜。一些植物吃真好，特别是黄瓜。但是，一些植物毒得很，特别是夹竹桃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一些植物吃真好：一些植物吃起来真好，When a food or plant is evaluated by taste, use the complement 起来 after the verb to describe how it tastes.
- 毒得很：有毒，毒 cannot take a degree complement such as 得很; use 有毒 to describe a poisonous plant.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 吃过 -- “他吃过生姜、芥兰和菠菜”：试吃过，makes clear that he ate these plants in order to test them.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the examples of edible plants, add a sensory detail, such as “生姜有一点辣，黄瓜又脆又爽口。” This helps the reader imagine why these plants are good to eat.
- Expressiveness: After mentioning the poisonous plant, add a concrete result, such as “神农吃了夹竹桃以后，肚子疼了起来。” This can make the danger feel more immediate.

### Encouragement
You used “然后” to move the story forward clearly, and the contrast between plants that taste good and plants that are poisonous is easy to understand. The specific examples of plants also make the scene more vivid.

## Scene 3

> 一个人对神农说，麻烦我们可以吃什么植物？神农说，这就要看你想不想生病了。以后，神农告诉别人一些植物能吃，一些植物不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 麻烦我们可以吃什么植物？：请问，我们可以吃什么植物？，麻烦 means “trouble” or can introduce a request to someone, but it cannot introduce this question by itself; 请问 is the appropriate polite question opener.
- 这就要看你想不想生病了：这就要看是哪种植物了，this pattern should identify the uncertain condition that determines the answer; here, the type of plant determines whether it can be eaten. The part after 看 is correctly a question form.
- 以后：后来，when this word starts a new sentence to mean “afterwards,” use 后来 rather than 以后.
- 别人：人们，别人 distinguishes other people from a relevant person, while this sentence means people in general who receive Shennong’s information.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉：告诉了，this is a completed event in the past narrative, so the verb needs 了 to mark completion.
- Expressiveness: At the final explanation, add one concrete example of an edible plant: “像伞一样的植物是灵芝，是药，可以吃。” This makes Shennong’s knowledge more specific.
- Expressiveness: Show Shennong’s helpful attitude before his explanation: “神农耐心地说：‘有些植物可以吃，有些植物有毒。’” This adds character feeling and dialogue.

### Encouragement
- You clearly used the contrast pattern “一些植物能吃，一些植物不能吃” to organize the information. You also used an A-not-A question form, “想不想,” which is a useful structure to know.
