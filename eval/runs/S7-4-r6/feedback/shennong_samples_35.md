## Scene 1

> 在古代，很多医生不知道怎么用药。所以他们找到了很多植物对药。神农先看到很多病人，他们吃很多植物对药。但是我觉得他们的吃的植物，让他们病人。神农觉得这个植物可能特别是毒。所以他想找一个植物对药，他们可以吃对药。正好，他找到了一把花，他想把花放在茶里，对药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很多植物对药：很多能做药的植物，`对药` cannot describe a plant’s use; `能做药的` clearly means that the plants can be made into medicine.
- 很多植物对药：很多植物当药，`当药` means “use as medicine,” while `对药` does not express this meaning.
- 他们的吃的植物：他们吃的植物，The possessor is unnecessary because the embedded verb already identifies who eats the plants.
- 让他们病人：让他们生病，`病人` is a noun meaning “patient,” so it cannot be the result after `让`; use the verb `生病` for becoming ill.
- 特别是毒：特别有毒，A plant can be `有毒`; `是毒` does not work after an adverb such as `特别`.
- 一个植物对药：一种能做药的植物，`对药` cannot modify `植物`; `能做药的` correctly describes a plant that can be used to make medicine.
- 吃对药：吃它，`对药` cannot follow `吃`; the pronoun refers to the medicinal plant already mentioned.
- 对药：做成药，`做成药` correctly expresses the intended result of making the flower into medicine.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 想 -- “他想把花放在茶里”：打算，emphasizes that he has formed a plan to try this method.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：一个叫神农的人，This is the character’s first mention, so introduce him with a name and a brief noun phrase.
- Coherence (Reference): 这个植物：这些植物，No single plant has been identified, while several plants were mentioned just before; the plural noun gives the reader a clear referent.
- Coherence (Reference): 他们可以吃它：病人可以吃它，Both doctors and patients have appeared earlier, so the pronoun is ambiguous after intervening information; repeat `病人` to identify the intended group.
- Coherence (Temporality): 神农先看到很多病人：神农先看到了很多病人，This is a completed event in the past narrative, so `看到` needs `了`.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们吃很多植物当药：他们吃了很多植物当药，The patients’ eating is presented as a completed earlier action, so use `了` after the verb.
- Expressiveness: When Shennong sees the patients, add a physical sign of illness, such as `有的病人捂着肚子，疼得直冒汗。`
- Expressiveness: Describe the flowers more vividly when he finds them, such as `那些花是黄色的，像一颗颗小星星。`
- Expressiveness: Add Shennong’s worried thought before he experiments, such as `神农想：“这种花会不会有毒？”`

### Encouragement
You clearly try to connect events with cause-and-effect words such as `所以`, and you introduce Shennong as someone who wants to help the sick people. You also used `找到了` correctly to show that an action was completed.

## Scene 2

> 他不知道如果这把花对你的身体好，这就要看如果这把花毒不毒了。神农想找别的植物，但是这很麻烦。正好，他找到了别的花。他一吃花，很快就生病了。他的肚子疼死了。神农不知道他吃不吃灵芝或者夹竹桃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不知道如果这把花对你的身体好：不知道这把花对不对身体好，An embedded yes/no question after 不知道 needs an A-not-A form; 对不对身体好 asks whether the flower is beneficial to the body.
- 这就要看如果这把花毒不毒了：这就要看这把花有没有毒了，After 这就要看, use a question form such as 有没有毒, not 如果 plus a clause.
- 不知道他吃不吃灵芝或者夹竹桃：不知道吃的是灵芝还是夹竹桃，When choosing between two specific possibilities, use 是……还是……; 吃不吃 does not express the choice between these two plants.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 麻烦 -- “但是这很麻烦”：繁琐，emphasizes that finding and checking many plants involves many repeated steps；艰辛，emphasizes that the task is difficult and tiring.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 花：这种花，This is the specific flower just found in the previous sentence, so a demonstrative noun phrase makes the reference clear rather than using the generic noun 花.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：他，The character was mentioned in the immediately previous sentence and no other character competes as the referent, so use a pronoun for close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 一吃花，很快就：一吃花，就很快，The pattern 一……就…… needs 就 to connect the immediate action of eating with the result of becoming ill.
- Expressiveness: After finding the flower, add one visual detail to show why it attracts him. Example: “黄色的花像星星一样，又大又漂亮”.
- Expressiveness: After the stomach pain, add Shennong’s worried thought to show his fear. Example: “我会死吗？”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the cause-and-result sequence of eating the flower and then becoming ill, and you correctly use completed-action forms such as 找到了 and 生病了.

## Scene 3

> 别的人给了神农一个灵芝。这个植物可以让神农更好。比方说，他会觉得更健康。吃灵芝以后，他可以再走。现在他觉得更好。最后，神农找到了很多植物，他可以用对药。这些植物让神农很高兴。神农想跟很多人分享他的新药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别的人：有人，别的人 distinguishes other people from a previously specified person or group; here the giver has not been introduced, so an indefinite person is needed.
- 他可以用对药：可以用来做药，plants can be used as material to make medicine; 用来 must be followed by the purpose verb phrase, and 对药 is not a valid phrase here.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 高兴 -- “这些植物让神农很高兴”：感到欣慰，shows satisfaction after finding useful plants；感到兴奋，shows lively happiness at the discovery
- 新药 -- “神农想跟很多人分享他的新药”：新发现的药，clarifies that the medicine is new because Shennong has just discovered it

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 这个植物：它，after 灵芝 was named in the immediately previous sentence, use a pronoun for this close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃灵芝以后：吃了灵芝以后，this eating action is completed before Shennong can walk again, so it needs 了.
- Expressiveness: At the point where Shennong feels better, add a concrete physical change, such as “肚子不那么疼了”, to show his recovery.
- Expressiveness: When Shennong finds the plants, add one visible detail about them, such as “像伞一样的植物”, to make the discovery easier to imagine.
- Expressiveness: Before he shares the medicine, add a short thought or line of speech, such as “我要告诉大家”, to show his wish to help others.

### Encouragement
You clearly show the sequence from receiving the medicine to recovering and continuing the search. You also use completed actions such as 给了 and 找到了 well.
