## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个男孩名字叫孟子。孟子和孟子的妈妈两个人住在很穷的农村。他们住的地方旁边是个墓地，墓地老是有很多人祭拜。因为人们在祭拜，他们一直在哭。孟子和孟子的妈妈住在这里才一个星期就觉得受不了了，而且害得孟子的学习。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，this expression is redundant because both parts refer to time; use the shorter time expression.
- 有一个男孩名字叫孟子：有一个男孩叫孟子，use the verb phrase for naming a person; the original structure incorrectly treats the name as the subject of the naming verb.
- 而且害得孟子的学习：而且这里的环境影响了孟子的学习，害得 must be followed by a person and a harmful result; it cannot take a possessive noun phrase alone.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子的妈妈两个人：孟子和他的妈妈两个人，after naming the same character once in a clause, use a pronoun for the close re-occurrence instead of repeating the name.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子的妈妈住在这里：孟子和他的妈妈住在这里，use a pronoun for the mother after the first mention rather than repeating the name in the same clause.
- Coherence (Temporality): 住在这里才一个星期就觉得受不了了：才住了一个星期，就觉得受不了了，use 了 after the completed duration of living before the resulting feeling.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为人们在祭拜，他们一直在哭：因为人们在祭拜，所以他们一直在哭，add 所以 to complete the cause-and-effect connector pair.
- Expressiveness: After the description of the cemetery, add one visual detail about the mourners, such as “来祭拜的人低着头，脸上很伤心。”
- Expressiveness: Show how the sound affects the boy’s feelings after the crying is mentioned, such as “哭声让孟子觉得很奇怪，也有一点害怕。”
- Expressiveness: At the final sentence, give the mother a short line of speech to show her concern, such as “孟子的妈妈叹了口气，说：‘这里太吵了，孟子没法好好学习。’”

### Encouragement
You establish the setting clearly with the home, the nearby cemetery, and the mourners. You also use ongoing-action marking effectively to describe people who are crying during the ritual.

## Scene 2

> 孟子的妈妈觉得孟子取得良好的教育是很重要的，所以孟子的妈妈认为这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好，就打算跟孟子搬家。孟子的妈妈带孟子不是搬到城市，而是搬到了孟子老师的家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 取得良好的教育：接受良好的教育，The verb does not naturally collocate with education here; this version expresses receiving an education.
- 跟孟子搬家：带着孟子搬家，This action means that the mother takes her son with her when moving; the original wording incorrectly suggests that they move as equal partners.
- 不是搬到城市，而是搬到了孟子老师的家：搬到了孟子老师的家，This paired structure is for correcting or refuting an established, usually expected claim. No earlier claim says that they will move to the city.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 地方 -- “这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好”：环境，more precisely refers to the surroundings and conditions that affect a child’s education.
- 不太好 -- “对儿子的教育不太好”：不太有利，more specifically shows that the place does not support the child’s education.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子的妈妈认为：她认为，Use a pronoun when the same character was named very recently and no competing person makes the reference unclear.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子的妈妈带孟子：她带孟子，The mother is the close re-occurrence from the preceding sentence, so a pronoun avoids unnecessarily repeating her name.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 。孟子的妈妈带孟子搬到了孟子老师的家。：，于是她带孟子搬到了孟子老师的家。，“于是” marks the sequence and result: after deciding to move, the mother carries out the move.
- Expressiveness: At the mother’s decision, add an inner thought to show her concern, such as: “这样的环境会影响孟子的学习，我得带他搬家。”
- Expressiveness: Describe the new place with one concrete detail, such as: “孟子老师的家很安静，书架上放着很多书。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the mother’s reason for moving and use a cause-and-effect connection effectively.
- You also show that the move is completed, which helps the events progress clearly.
