## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有很多人不知道什么植物能吃，什么植物不能吃。有很多人吃植物以后就吃坏肚子了，因为他们吃了毒。那个时候，有一个老人叫神农，他什么植物都觉得好吃，不怕生病，不怕毒。他希望别人也可以吃好吃的植物。可是他知道，如果他们还怕吃毒，不会吃他喜欢的植物。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，this expression redundantly combines two time expressions; use the shorter time phrase.
- 吃了毒：吃了有毒的植物，毒 is the noun “poison,” while 有毒的植物 specifically means plants that are poisonous.
- 他什么植物都觉得好吃：他觉得什么植物都好吃，觉得 should introduce the whole evaluative clause; the speaker’s judgment comes after 觉得.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好吃 -- “他希望别人也可以吃好吃的植物”：可口，specifies that the plants taste pleasant and are suitable to eat；美味，adds a stronger sense of enjoyable flavour.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃植物以后：吃了植物以后，the eating is completed before the later result of getting an upset stomach, so use 了 for the completed action.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 如果他们还怕吃毒，不会吃他喜欢的植物：如果他们还怕吃毒，就不会吃他喜欢的植物，an if-condition normally uses 就 to introduce its result.
- Expressiveness: After the upset-stomach event, add a physical detail to show the discomfort, such as “他们肚子疼得直冒冷汗。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where the old man likes every plant, add his thought or speech to show his personality, such as “神农心想：这么好的植物，大家都应该能吃。”
- Expressiveness: Describe the danger the people feel before the final sentence, such as “他们站在植物前面，不知道该不该吃。”

### Encouragement
You clearly establish the problem that people cannot tell which plants are safe, and you use a cause-and-effect sentence to explain why they get sick. The introduction of the old man is also clear, and the pronouns remain easy to follow.

## Scene 2

> 所以，他上山下山找植物，吃了很多样子的植物。他是第一人，找到了茶。他把所有的植物写下来了。他一直都吃了，他连毒都能吃。可是他还没吃了一个叫夹竹桃的植物。他找到了那个像星星的植物，觉得很漂亮，马上就吃下了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很多样子的植物：很多种植物，样子 describes an object’s appearance, while 种 is the measure word used for different types of plants.
- 他是第一人，找到了茶：他是第一个找到茶的人，第一人 means a foremost person; use 第一个 + verb phrase + 的人 to say who did an action first.
- 吃了：在吃，an action continuing with 一直 needs the progressive form 在 + verb, not 了, which marks completion.
- 吃了：吃过，没 cannot be followed by completed-action 了 here; 吃过 expresses that he has not yet had the experience of eating it.
- 一个叫夹竹桃的植物：一种叫夹竹桃的植物，when referring to a kind of plant, use the measure word 种 rather than the general classifier 个.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他连毒都能吃：连毒都能吃，after 他 has already been used as the subject earlier in the same sentence, use zero form instead of repeating the pronoun.
- Expressiveness: At the discovery of tea, add a feeling or a short thought to show why the finding matters, such as: 他找到茶以后，心里很高兴。
- Expressiveness: When describing the plant that looks like stars, add a concrete colour and size detail, such as: 那儿开着黄色的像星星一样的花，又大又漂亮。
- Expressiveness: Before he eats the plant, add his thought to make his decision clearer, such as: 他心想：“这朵花这么漂亮，应该很好吃。”

### Encouragement
You used clear action verbs in the mountain-searching sentence, and 上山下山 gives the scene a sense of movement. Your use of the 把 structure in 把所有的植物写下来了 also clearly shows what Shennong recorded.

## Scene 3

> 可是夹竹桃的毒很重，让他肚子很痛。他在地上躺着，觉得他快会死了。可是正好有别人找到他了，给了他一个像伞的植物，叫灵芝。他吃灵芝以后就觉得很好，不再生病了。他测量起来了，像星星的植物是毒，像伞的植物是药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 快会死了：快要死了，Use 快要 to express that an event is about to happen; 快会 is not the natural pattern for this meaning.
- 是毒：有毒，毒 is a noun here; 有毒 means that the plant contains poison.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 毒 -- “夹竹桃的毒很重”：毒性很强，states more precisely that the plant’s poisonous quality is strong.
- 痛 -- “让他肚子很痛”：疼得厉害，uses a result-complement pattern to show clearly how severe the pain feels.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他：自己，Because the person who is thinking and the person who may die are the same, use 自己 to make the self-reference clear.
- Coherence (Reference): 他：神农，Repeat the name after the new person has appeared, because 他 could otherwise refer either to 神农 or to the person who found him.
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃灵芝以后：吃了灵芝以后，The eating is a completed action before he feels better, so add 了 to mark completion.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他测量起来了：后来，他测量起来了，Add 后来 to show that this is the next event after he recovers.
- Expressiveness: At “让他肚子很痛,” add a physical reaction to show the severity of the pain: “他疼得在地上打滚。”
- Expressiveness: At “觉得自己快要死了,” add an internal thought or a short question: “他想：我会不会死？”
- Expressiveness: When the helper arrives, add one visible detail and urgent action: “这时，一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人跑过来，赶快给他灵芝。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show a strong cause-and-effect sequence: the poisonous plant causes pain, and the medicinal plant helps him recover. You also use 以后 and 不再生病了 effectively to show a change in his condition.
