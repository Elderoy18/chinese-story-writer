## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个男孩名字叫孟子。孟子和孟子的妈妈两个人住在很穷的农村。他们住的地方旁边是个墓地，墓地老是有很多人祭拜。因为人们在祭拜，他们一直在哭。孟子和孟子的妈妈住在这里才一个星期就觉得受不了了，而且害得孟子的学习。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 already refers to a time period, so adding 的时候 is redundant.
- 有一个男孩名字叫孟子：有一个叫孟子的男孩，use the attributive pattern with 叫 to introduce the character’s name and identity clearly.
- 墓地老是有很多人祭拜：很多人老是在墓地里祭拜，put the people before the frequency adverbial and use a location phrase before the action.
- 害得孟子的学习：害得孟子不能好好学习，害得 must be followed by the affected person and a harmful result clause, not only a noun phrase.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子的妈妈两个人：孟子和他的妈妈两个人，after naming 孟子 once in the same phrase, use the possessive pronoun to avoid unnecessarily repeating his name.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子的妈妈住在这里：孟子和他的妈妈住在这里，repeat 孟子 after the intervening description, but use the pronoun in 妈妈’s possessive phrase rather than repeating the name.
- Coherence (Temporality): 住在这里才一个星期就觉得受不了了：才在这里住了一个星期，就觉得受不了了，use 了 after 住 to show that the one-week period had passed by the time they reached this feeling.
- Expressiveness: At the cemetery sentence, add a sound detail to make the setting more vivid: “墓地里常常传来伤心的哭声。”
- Expressiveness: At the final sentence, show the mother’s worry through an inner thought: “孟母心想：‘这里太吵了，会影响孟子学习。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish the family’s living situation and the nearby cemetery, so the reader can understand the setting quickly.
- The use of 因为 and the ongoing actions with 在 and 一直 helps show why the crying is happening.

## Scene 2

> 孟子的妈妈觉得孟子取得良好的教育是很重要的，所以孟子的妈妈认为这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好，就打算跟孟子搬家。孟子的妈妈带孟子不是搬到城市，而是搬到了孟子老师的家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 取得良好的教育：接受良好的教育，The verb “取得” is used for achievements or results that someone obtains; “接受” correctly describes receiving education.
- 跟孟子搬家：带着孟子搬家，“跟孟子搬家” suggests that the mother and Mencius move together as equal participants; “带着孟子搬家” shows that the mother takes her child with her.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 地方 -- “这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好”：环境，specifies that it is the surrounding living and learning setting that is unsuitable for the child’s education
- 不太好 -- “对儿子的教育不太好”：不合适，states more precisely that the place does not suit the child’s educational needs

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子的妈妈觉得孟子取得良好的教育是很重要的，所以孟子的妈妈认为：孟子的妈妈觉得孟子接受良好的教育很重要，所以认为，After the mother has been named and no other possible subject intervenes, use zero form rather than repeating her full noun phrase in the same sentence.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 孟子的妈妈带孟子不是搬到城市，而是搬到了孟子老师的家：孟子的妈妈没有带孟子搬到城市，而是带他搬到了孟子老师的家，The paired contrast needs two parallel actions: she did not take him to the city, but took him to the teacher’s home.
- Expressiveness: At the point where the mother decides to move, add her worry to show why education matters: “孟母很着急，担心这样的环境会影响孟子的学习。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of inner speech after her decision: “她心想：‘我一定要给孟子找一个更好的地方。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly connect the mother’s belief about education to her decision to move, so the cause-and-effect relationship is easy to follow. You also use the contrast structure to show that the destination is not the city.
