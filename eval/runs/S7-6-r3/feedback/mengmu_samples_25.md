## Scene 1

> 这是古代的故事。孟子小的时候，他的爸爸很早就去世了。从他的家到墓地很近，所以他每天老是从早到晚去墓地跟朋友们玩玩儿。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 从他的家到墓地很近：他的家离墓地很近，Use 离 to state that one place is close to another; 从…到… describes a route or distance range rather than this location relationship.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子小的时候，他的爸爸很早就去世了：孟子是一个小男孩。他小的时候，他的爸爸很早就去世了，On a character’s first mention, introduce the name together with a brief identifying noun phrase; then use a pronoun when the character is mentioned again closely.
- Expressiveness: After the mention of the cemetery, add an observed detail such as “他常常看见人们来墓地祭拜” to make the setting more concrete.
- Expressiveness: At the point where he goes there with friends, add his feeling, such as “孟子觉得祭拜很有趣”, to show why he is interested in the place.

### Encouragement
You used a clear cause-and-effect connection between the home’s location and the boy’s daily activity. The time expressions also make his routine easy to understand.

## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈觉得在墓地玩儿，害得，所以他们搬家。搬家对孟子有好的影响，他才五岁，可是每天不是写字就是帮妈妈的工作。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 害得：会有不好的影响，害得 must be followed by the person harmed and a harmful result; it cannot stand alone.
- 帮妈妈的工作：帮妈妈做事，帮妈妈的工作 does not form a suitable verb phrase here; use 帮 + person + action to describe helping someone with tasks.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好的 -- “搬家对孟子有好的影响”：积极的，gives a more specific description of a beneficial effect.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们搬家：他们搬家了，this move is presented as a completed event, so add 了 after the verb.
- Expressiveness: After the mother’s concern, add a short thought to show why she decides to move, such as “她觉得孟子在这儿学不到好东西。”
- Expressiveness: Make the contrast in the second sentence more vivid by adding the mother’s reaction, such as “妈妈看见了，觉得很高兴。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the mother’s concern for孟子 and use the contrast pattern “才……可是……” to highlight that he is young but already learning and helping.

## Scene 3

> 他很聪明的人，难怪他成为有名的人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他很聪明的人：他是一个很聪明的人，A noun phrase ending in 人 needs the copula 是 when it is the predicate, and 一个 naturally introduces one individual in this description.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 聪明 -- “一个很聪明的人”：有智慧，emphasizes deep understanding and good judgment rather than general cleverness.
- 人 -- “成为有名的人”：哲学家，identifies the particular kind of well-known person he became in this story.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为有名的人：成为了有名的人，Becoming a well-known person is a completed change of state in this past narrative, so it takes 了.
- Expressiveness: After “很聪明”, add a concrete sign of his ability, such as “他喜欢读书，也常常思考问题”, to show why he later became well known.
- Expressiveness: You could add his mother’s proud reaction after the final result, such as “孟母高兴地说：‘这几次搬家太值得了！’”, to give the ending more feeling.

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect Mengzi’s intelligence with his later success by using “难怪”, which creates a natural cause-and-result ending.
