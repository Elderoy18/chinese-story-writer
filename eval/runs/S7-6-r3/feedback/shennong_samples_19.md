## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男人觉得植物很有意思。他叫神农。有的植物吃了以后他的肚子疼死了，可是有的吃了以后很舒服。他找很多植物。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，The original phrase is redundant because 古代 already expresses a time period, while 的时候 also means “the time of.”
- 他找很多植物：他找到了很多植物，找 describes searching, while 找到 states that he successfully found the plants; 了 marks this narrative action as completed.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一个男人觉得植物很有意思。他叫神农：一个叫神农的男人觉得植物很有意思，Use the character’s name within the first full noun phrase so the reader meets him clearly on first mention.
- Expressiveness: After the stomach pain, add a physical detail to make the consequence vivid, such as `肚子疼得直冒冷汗`.
- Expressiveness: At the point where he finds plants interesting, add his question or thought to show curiosity, such as `神农想：到底什么植物能吃呢？`

### Encouragement
You clearly show the contrast between plants that cause pain and plants that make Shennong feel well by using `可是`. The cause-and-result pattern with `吃了以后` also helps the reader understand his experiences.

## Scene 2

> 凉山很大的山，这也找植物。一天，他找到黄色的花。他听说你跟黄色的花做茶很有用。比方说，你的头发疼死，这冰茶是药。他一喝茶就很不舒服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 凉山很大的山：凉山是一座很大的山，A noun sentence identifying what a place is needs the copula and a suitable measure word before the noun.
- 这也找植物：他也在这里找植物，The demonstrative cannot refer to the character who performs the action; use the established character pronoun and a location phrase.
- 你跟黄色的花做茶：用黄色的花做的茶，Use 用 to introduce the material used to make something, and add 的 so the whole verb phrase modifies 茶.
- 头发：头，头发 means “hair,” while a painful head is expressed with 头疼.
- 这冰茶：这杯冰茶，A demonstrative before a countable noun needs a measure word.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很大 -- “凉山是一座很大的山”：高大，describes the mountain’s large, imposing shape more specifically；雄伟，emphasizes that the mountain looks grand and impressive.
- 不舒服 -- “他一喝茶就很不舒服”：难受，more directly describes physical discomfort；肚子难受，specifies that the discomfort is in his stomach.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 疼死：疼死了，Use 了 to show the resulting painful state has occurred.
- Expressiveness: When the yellow flower appears, add a visual detail: “黄色的花又大又漂亮，像星星一样。”
- Expressiveness: After the tea causes discomfort, add a physical reaction: “他的肚子疼了起来，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought to show the character’s worry: “他想：‘这种茶不是很有用吗？为什么我这么难受？’”

### Encouragement
You used a clear time marker, “一天,” to begin a new event, and “他一喝茶就……” effectively shows an immediate result. The scene also introduces the yellow flower and tea in a way that connects naturally to the plant-testing story.

## Scene 3

> 糟糕，这黄色的花是夹竹桃，特别是毒。正好在这儿，灵芝药的夹竹桃他吃完了，也写一个课文的植物。谢谢神农。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 这黄色的花：这朵黄色的花，A demonstrative such as “这” needs a measure word before a noun; “朵” is the suitable measure word for a flower.
- 特别是毒：有毒，“特别是” introduces a typical example and cannot mean “very” before a description of toxicity. Use “有毒” to state that the plant is poisonous.
- 正好在这儿，灵芝药的夹竹桃他吃完了：正好在这儿有灵芝这种药。夹竹桃他吃完了，“灵芝药的夹竹桃” does not form a meaningful noun phrase: “的” cannot connect these two different plants in this way. Separate the information about the medicine from the information about the oleander.
- 一个：一篇，“课文” is counted with “篇,” not “个.”
- 课文的植物：关于植物的课文，The intended relationship is that the text is about plants, so use “关于” rather than making the plants appear to belong to the text.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 写：写了，Writing the text is presented as a completed action in this past event sequence, so the verb needs “了.”
- Expressiveness: After the discovery of the poisonous flower, add a physical reaction to show the danger, such as “他一吃夹竹桃，肚子就疼了起来。”
- Expressiveness: Describe the flower before naming it to make the discovery more vivid, such as “黄色的花像星星一样，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought after he feels ill, such as “神农想：‘我会死吗？’”

### Encouragement
You used “糟糕” effectively to show that the discovery is dangerous. You also included both “夹竹桃” and “灵芝,” which gives this scene important story vocabulary.
