## Scene 1

> 很久以前，有一个老男人叫神农。神农在古代每一天看到了别的小镇人生病还是不舒服，所以他决定开始找新的药。他下一天开始爬当地的山脉，找了各种各样的植物。神农试一试每个植物，知道有什么好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别的小镇人：人们，别的 distinguishes other people or places from one already mentioned, but no such town has been established here; 人们 naturally refers to a concrete group of people.
- 看到了：看到，this is a routine marked by 每一天, so it takes the zero particle rather than 了.
- 生病还是不舒服：生病或者不舒服，还是 is normally used for a choice or an alternative question; 或者 links two possible conditions in this statement.
- 下一天：第二天，第二天 is the usual expression for the day after the previously described day.
- 每个植物：每种植物，种 is the appropriate measure word when referring to each type of plant; 个 is too generic here.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 老男人 -- “有一个老男人叫神农”：老人，specifies that he is elderly and sounds more suitable for a respected story character
- 不舒服 -- “人生病还是不舒服”：感到不适，specifies that the people are experiencing physical discomfort

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After 神农决定找药, add his determined thought: “他想：我要找出能治病的植物！”
- Expressiveness: When he climbs the mountains, add a visual detail about the setting: “山又高又陡，路边长着许多陌生的植物。”
- Expressiveness: When he tests plants, add a specific discovery, such as: “他发现，有些植物能让人生病，有些植物却对身体有好处。”

### Encouragement
- You introduced 神农 clearly at the beginning, so the reader immediately knows who the main character is.
- You also used time words and a cause-and-effect connector, including 很久以前, 下一天, and 所以, to move the scene forward.

## Scene 2

> 比方说，他一次试一试有些植物的叶，吃得又新鲜又甜。煮这些叶以后，他一喝就知道他发现新的茶叶。虽然他找到了很多的新的植物，但是他的旅程有的时候特别是危险。比方说，他一吃灵芝就开始生病。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 吃得又新鲜又甜：这些叶子吃起来又新鲜又甜，The complement after 得 describes the manner or degree of the action, but 新鲜 and 甜 describe the leaves’ qualities. Use 吃起来 to describe how food tastes.
- 特别是危险：特别危险，特别是 must introduce a typical example or clause; it cannot be used simply before an adjective to mean “very.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 新的 -- “找到了很多的新的植物”：未尝过的，specifies that these are plants he has not tasted before；陌生的，emphasizes that the plants are unfamiliar to him

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the tea is boiled, add a sensory detail about its smell or colour, such as “叶子煮开以后，散发出淡淡的香味。”
- Expressiveness: After he realizes what he has found, add his reaction or thoughts, such as “神农高兴地想：‘这种叶子真好喝！’”
- Expressiveness: When he becomes ill, show how the danger feels to him, such as “他觉得肚子越来越疼，心里很害怕。”

### Encouragement
You used both 比方说 and the 虽然……但是…… contrast pattern appropriately to connect your ideas. The sequence with 以后 and 一……就…… also makes the events easy to follow.

## Scene 3

> 一个爬山的男人正好看到神农的麻烦，然后给了他一些药。神农最终他的小镇回到，跟别的人分享他的发现。你认为神农的故事真的吗？这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 神农最终他的小镇回到：神农最终回到小镇，The directional verb compound must come before its destination; the location follows 回到.
- 别的人：别人，别的人 is used when distinguishing other people from a specifically mentioned person or group; here the general meaning is simply “other people.”
- 你认为神农的故事真的吗：你认为神农的故事是真的吗，A noun phrase followed by an adjective in this yes-or-no question needs 是 before the adjective predicate.
- 这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处：这就要看你相不相信中药对身体有好处了，The part after 这就要看 must be a question form; 相不相信 provides the required A-not-A form.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农的麻烦：神农很难受，The noun 麻烦 is too vague for the reader to picture what the man sees; naming Shennong’s condition makes the reference specific.
- Coherence (Reference): 他的小镇，……他的发现：自己的小镇，……自己的发现，Because both possessions belong to the sentence subject, 自己的 clearly marks Shennong as the possessor, especially after another man was mentioned.
- Expressiveness: At the first sentence, add a visible detail about the helper: “一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人正好走过来。”
- Expressiveness: Show Shennong’s physical feeling before the man helps him: “神农肚子很疼，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: When Shennong shares his discovery, add a short line of speech: “这种像伞一样的植物可以做药。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduce the new man with a full noun phrase, and the action order from seeing Shennong to giving him medicine is easy to follow. Your ending also tries to involve the reader with a question, which is a nice storytelling choice.
