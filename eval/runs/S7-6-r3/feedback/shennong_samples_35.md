## Scene 1

> 在古代，很多医生不知道怎么用药。所以他们找到了很多植物对药。神农先看到很多病人，他们吃很多植物对药。但是我觉得他们的吃的植物，让他们病人。神农觉得这个植物可能特别是毒。所以他想找一个植物对药，他们可以吃对药。正好，他找到了一把花，他想把花放在茶里，对药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很多植物对药：很多可以做药的植物，"对药" does not mean “to serve as medicine.” Use "可以做药的" before the noun to describe plants that can be used as medicine.
- 对药：做的药，"对药" is not a suitable phrase here; "植物做的药" means medicine made from plants.
- 让他们病人：害得他们生病了，"病人" is a noun meaning a sick person, not a verb. For a harmful result, use "害得 + person + harmful-result clause."
- 特别是毒：特别有毒，"是毒" identifies something as poison, while "有毒" describes the poisonous property of a plant; "特别" can modify "有毒."
- 一个植物对药：一种可以做药的植物，"一种" is appropriate when referring to a type of plant, and the phrase describing its medicinal use must come before "植物" with "的."
- 吃对药：吃，"对药" cannot follow "吃" in this meaning; the plant being discussed is already understood as the thing people can eat.
- 对药：用来做药，"对药" does not express purpose. "用来做药" clearly shows that the flower is intended for making medicine.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 这个植物：这些植物，The earlier sentence refers to many plants, so the later reference should use a plural noun phrase rather than an unexplained singular noun.
- Coherence (Reference): 他们：大家，After doctors, patients, and 神农 have all appeared, "他们" can refer to more than one group; "大家" makes the intended general group clear.
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃：吃了，The patients’ eating is presented as a completed past action that led to illness, so it needs "了."
- Expressiveness: At the point where 神农 sees the patients, add one physical symptom to show their suffering. Example: “有的人肚子疼，有的人躺在地上。”
- Expressiveness: After 神农 decides to search for useful plants, add his thought or speech to show his purpose. Example: “神农想：‘我要找到能做药的植物。’”
- Expressiveness: When the flower appears, describe one visible feature before mentioning its use. Example: “这把花又黄又香。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly set the story in the past with “在古代,” and you introduced a problem that motivates 神农 to act.
- You also used “所以” to show cause and effect, which helps the scene move forward.

## Scene 2

> 他不知道如果这把花对你的身体好，这就要看如果这把花毒不毒了。神农想找别的植物，但是这很麻烦。正好，他找到了别的花。他一吃花，很快就生病了。他的肚子疼死了。神农不知道他吃不吃灵芝或者夹竹桃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不知道如果这把花对你的身体好：不知道这把花对他的身体好不好，an indirect yes-or-no question after 不知道 uses an alternative form such as 好不好, not 如果; 你的 also addresses the reader instead of referring to 神农.
- 这就要看如果这把花毒不毒了：这就要看这把花毒不毒了，this structure introduces the point that must be checked, so it does not take 如果.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 找 -- “神农想找别的植物”：寻找, emphasizes an ongoing search for plants；探寻, emphasizes exploring in order to discover something unknown.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 花：这种花，two different flowers have been mentioned, so the bare noun is unclear here; repeat a specific noun phrase to identify the flower he eats.
- Coherence (Reference): 不知道他吃不吃：不知道该不该吃，because 神农 is already the subject of this sentence, omit the repeated pronoun and use zero form for the same referent.
- Coherence (Conjunction): ，很快就：，就很快，an 一V structure needs 就 to introduce the immediate result.
- Expressiveness: After “正好，他找到了别的花”, describe the flower’s appearance so the reader can picture it: “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花。”
- Expressiveness: After “他的肚子疼死了”, add an action showing how badly he feels: “他在地上躺着，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought at the moment he becomes ill to show his fear: “他想：我会死吗？”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the cause-and-effect sequence: 神农 finds a flower, eats it, and then becomes sick. The vivid phrase 疼死了 also communicates his strong physical pain well.

## Scene 3

> 别的人给了神农一个灵芝。这个植物可以让神农更好。比方说，他会觉得更健康。吃灵芝以后，他可以再走。现在他觉得更好。最后，神农找到了很多植物，他可以用对药。这些植物让神农很高兴。神农想跟很多人分享他的新药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别的人给了神农：有人给了神农，别的人 means people other than a specific person already under discussion; here the sentence introduces an unspecified helper, so 有人 is needed.
- 他可以用对药：可以做药的植物，plants can be used as medicine with 做药; 用对药 is not a grammatical verb-object phrase.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 更好 -- “这个植物可以让神农更好”：身体更健康，specifies that the improvement is in his health；感觉好一些，describes his immediate physical relief after eating the medicine
- 新药 -- “神农想跟很多人分享他的新药”：新发现的药，explains precisely that the medicine is new because he has just discovered it

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农 -- 他，Shennong was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other person competes as the referent, so a pronoun is more natural for a close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Reference): 这些植物让神农很高兴 -- 这些植物让他很高兴，after the previous sentence has established Shennong as the subject, use a pronoun for this close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农想跟很多人分享他的新药 -- 他想跟很多人分享他的新药，there is no competing character after the previous sentence, so the close re-occurrence should use a pronoun.
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃灵芝以后 -- 吃了灵芝以后，eating the medicine is completed before the later action of walking again, so the completed action takes 了.
- Expressiveness: After “现在他觉得更好”, add a physical detail to show his recovery, such as “他的肚子不那么疼了，也能慢慢站起来了。”
- Expressiveness: At “这些植物让神农很高兴”, add his feeling and reason, such as “神农非常欣慰，因为他终于找到了能帮助病人的植物。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech when he plans to share the medicine, such as “他说：‘我要告诉大家，什么植物可以做药。’”

### Encouragement
You used clear time ordering with “吃灵芝以后” and “最后,” which helps the reader follow the events. You also showed Shennong’s positive change from feeling ill to feeling healthier.
