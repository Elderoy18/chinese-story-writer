## Scene 1

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，The time noun already refers to an ancient period, so adding the extra time phrase is redundant.
- 去了吃：去吃了，With a verb following the movement verb, the completion marker goes after the final action: the pattern is “去 + verb + 了.”
- 写了：记下了，Use this verb when recording information learned about a plant; the original verb does not clearly express recording the finding.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很糟糕 -- “这个问题神农觉得很糟糕”：令人担忧，specifies that the problem makes people feel worried rather than merely saying it is bad.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 对你的身体有好处：对身体有好处，This is a general statement in narration, so the second-person pronoun should not unexpectedly address the reader; use the zero possessive form.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：他，The character was named recently and no other person competes as the referent, so use a pronoun on this close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：(delete)，Within the same sentence, the subject has already been established, so use the zero form instead of repeating the name.
- Coherence (Reference): 对你有没有好处：对身体有没有好处，This is again a general narration statement, not direct speech to the reader, so the possessive pronoun should be omitted.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：他，The previous sentence already identifies the same character, with no competing person, so a pronoun is the natural close re-occurrence form.
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃了很好吃的植物：吃很好吃的植物，The phrase beginning with “有的时候” describes a routine, so use the zero particle rather than completed-action “了.”
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃了很危险的植物：吃很危险的植物，This is also a repeated routine introduced by “有的时候,” so it takes the zero particle.
- Coherence (Temporality): 聊到他吃的植物了：聊到他吃的植物，After the modal verb “会,” the action is not presented as completed, so do not add final “了.”
- Expressiveness: After the dangerous plant is eaten, add a physical reaction to make the danger vivid: “他肚子疼得不得了，只能躺在地上。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where he records his finding, add a short thought or quotation: “神农想：‘这种植物不能吃。’”
- Expressiveness: When he talks with his friends, show their response: “朋友们听了以后，都很感谢神农。”

### Encouragement

## Scene 3

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一个黄色的花：一朵黄色的花，The measure word for an individual flower is 朵, not 个.
- 脑：大脑，脑 is not normally used alone in this meaning; 大脑 is the natural noun for the brain.
- 一个灵芝：一株灵芝，株 is the suitable measure word for an individual medicinal fungus or plant.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好了 -- “我一吃这个灵芝就好了”：恢复过来了，specifies that the speaker recovered from the harmful effects of the plant.
- 旁边 -- “一个灵芝和一个男人正好在我的旁边”：身边，more naturally describes a person and an object close to the narrator.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 特别是夹竹桃。因为夹竹桃有毒。：特别是夹竹桃，因为夹竹桃有毒。，Because introduces the cause for the warning, so it should be joined to the preceding statement rather than stand as a separate incomplete cause clause.
