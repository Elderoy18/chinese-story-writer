## Scene 1

> 神农和他的家人住在古代，所以不知道什么植物能吃。因为他的家人常常生病了，所以神农和他的哥哥一起去旅行找可以吃的植物。他们去很多不一样的地方，比方说高山、山谷和沙漠。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 住在古代：生活在古代，住在 normally focuses on living in a particular place, while 古代 is an historical period; 生活在 can naturally describe living in an era.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 去旅行 -- “一起去旅行找可以吃的植物”：外出，makes the purpose clearer: they leave home in order to search for plants.
- 不一样 -- “很多不一样的地方”：各不相同，emphasizes that the destinations differ from one another.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：一个叫神农的男人，This is the character’s first mention, so introduce him with both a name and a brief noun phrase rather than using the name alone.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他的家人常常生病了：他的家人常常生病，常常 describes a habitual situation, so it takes the zero particle rather than completed-action 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们去很多不一样的地方：他们去了很多不一样的地方，The visits to many places are completed narrative actions, so 去 needs 了.
- Expressiveness: After mentioning the family’s illnesses, add one concrete symptom to show why the search matters, for example: “他的家人常常肚子疼，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: When describing the journey, add a small visual detail about one destination, for example: “高山又高又冷，山谷里开着许多漂亮的花。”
- Expressiveness: You could show 神农’s motivation with a thought, for example: “神农想：我一定要找到可以治病的植物。”

### Encouragement
You clearly connect the family’s frequent illness with 神农’s reason for searching for useful plants. Your list of different destinations also makes the journey feel broad and adventurous.
