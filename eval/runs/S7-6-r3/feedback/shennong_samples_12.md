## Scene 1

> 很久以前，古代的人很不知道什么植物能吃，也什么植物不能吃。所以很多人常常吃不安全的植物，吃不安全的植物对你的身体有坏处，很麻烦的情况。但是一个人，神农，要解决这个问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很不知道：不知道，A negative mental-state verb should not be modified by 很; use the plain negative form here.
- 很麻烦的情况：是一件很严重的事，This position needs a predicate with 是, and 麻烦 means troublesome or inconvenient rather than describing the seriousness of harm to health.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不安全 -- “吃不安全的植物”：有毒，specifies that the plants contain poison and directly explains why eating them harms people.
- 有坏处 -- “对你的身体有坏处”：有害，states more directly that the plants harm the body.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 但是一个人，神农，要解决这个问题：但是有一个叫神农的人要解决这个问题，On a character’s first mention, introduce the person clearly with a name and a full noun phrase.
- Expressiveness: After the sentence about harmful plants, add a concrete result such as “很多人吃了有毒的植物，常常生病。” This makes the danger easier for the reader to picture.
- Expressiveness: At the introduction of Shennong, add his purpose or thought, such as “他想：我要找出哪些植物能吃。” This gives the character a clear motivation.

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish the main problem of the scene: people do not know which plants are safe to eat. You also create a useful contrast by introducing Shennong as someone who wants to solve that problem.

## Scene 2

> 他又聪明又努力，旅行很远找到很多植物。比方说，灵芝和夹竹桃。他一吃各种各样的植物就知道能不能吃这些植物。然后，神农写他的主意。他喜欢植物的味道，特别是花。神农遇到了一个黄色的花，一吃就觉得不舒服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 旅行很远：去了很远的地方，Use a destination phrase with 去 to express travelling to a distant place; 旅行 cannot directly be modified by 很远 in this structure.
- 找到很多植物：找到了很多植物，This is a completed discovery in the narrative, so 找到 takes 了.
- 写他的主意：写下了自己的主意，写 describes the act of writing, while 写下 means recording an idea so that it is kept; 了 marks the completed recording.
- 一个黄色的花：一朵黄色的花，朵 is the matching measure word for a flower; 个 is too generic here.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 然后，神农：然后，他，The previous sentence already names the same character as 他, with no competing person, so use a pronoun for this close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农遇到了：他遇到了，The immediately preceding sentence is also about the same character, so a pronoun avoids unnecessarily repeating the name.
- Expressiveness: At the description of the flower, add visual details to make it memorable: “那儿开着黄色的像星星一样的花，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: After the character feels ill, show the physical reaction and feeling: “他在地上躺着，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short inner thought after the illness to show fear: “他心想：‘我会死吗？’”

### Encouragement
- You used “一……就……” well to show that the reaction happened immediately after eating the plant. Your examples of different plants also make the scene more specific.

## Scene 3

> 发现了夹竹桃有毒。别人看到他，很快给他吃一个灵芝。神农一吃就觉得比较好。然后，他继续写他发现的信息。会告诉他的朋友哪一个植物能不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别人：一个人，别人 means people other than a previously identified person or group; here a new helper should be introduced as an indefinite person.
- 给他吃一个灵芝：给他吃了一个灵芝，this is a completed action in the narrative, so the verb needs 了.
- 写他发现的信息：写下他发现的信息，写下 means to record information so it is kept; plain 写 does not clearly express that recording meaning here.
- 哪一个植物能不能吃：哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃，哪一个 asks which single plant, while 能不能 asks a yes-or-no question; use parallel clauses to distinguish edible plants from inedible ones.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 比较好 -- “神农一吃就觉得比较好”：好一些了，shows that his condition has improved by a small amount；舒服一些了，specifies that his physical discomfort has eased.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the helper gives the medicine, add a short action and line of speech to show urgency, such as “一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人赶快拿起灵芝，说：‘快吃吧！’”
- Expressiveness: At the recovery moment, describe the physical change more vividly, such as “他的肚子不那么疼了，心里也放心了一些。”
- Expressiveness: When he records his discoveries, name the two plants and their different effects, such as “他把夹竹桃有毒、灵芝可以做药都记下来。”

### Encouragement
You clearly connected the medicine, Shennong’s recovery, his record of the discovery, and his plan to share the knowledge. The sequence word also helps the events move forward naturally.
