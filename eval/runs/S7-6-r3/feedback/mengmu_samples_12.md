## Scene 1

> 古代，孟子跟孟母住在墓地的旁边。可是孟母注意，住在墓地的旁边影响孟子。他常常跟朋友们祭拜玩，所以孟母决定他们会搬家。这次，他们住在城市里的商店的旁边，从早到晚很吵。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 孟母注意：孟母注意到，The complement 到 shows that she has noticed or recognized something; 注意 alone does not naturally introduce this statement here.
- 他常常跟朋友们祭拜玩：他常常跟朋友们一起玩祭拜的游戏，祭拜 is an act of worship and cannot directly combine with 玩 in this way; the corrected phrase clearly means that the children treat worship as a game.
- 孟母决定他们会搬家：孟母决定带着孟子搬家，决定 should be followed by a verb phrase, without repeating a separate subject and future clause.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 吵 -- “从早到晚很吵”：嘈杂，emphasizes the mixed, unpleasant sounds around the shops；喧闹，describes a loud and busy commercial environment.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子跟孟母住在墓地的旁边：有一个小男孩叫孟子，他和妈妈住在墓地旁边，At first mention, briefly introduce the character with both his name and who he is before using a shorter reference.
- Expressiveness: At the point where Mengmu notices the problem, add her worried thought to show her feelings: “孟母心想：‘住在这儿会影响孟子。’”
- Expressiveness: When describing the shops, add a sound detail to make the new setting more vivid: “街上人来人往，叫卖声不停。”
- Expressiveness: Show why Mengzi finds the worship interesting: “他觉得祭拜很有趣，笑着学大人鞠躬。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the cause-and-effect link between Mengzi’s actions and Mengmu’s decision by using 所以. The change from the quiet cemetery setting to the noisy city shops also gives this scene a clear contrast.

## Scene 2

> 城市也有点儿危险。害得孟母只好一直陪孟子。孟子也没有朋友，难怪他老是看起来不高兴。住在商店的旁边不是适合孟子，而是让他觉得很伤心。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- Nothing to flag.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 陪 -- “害得孟母只好一直陪孟子”：照看，more precisely shows that she watches over him because the area is unsafe.
- 不高兴 -- “他老是看起来不高兴”：闷闷不乐，shows a continuing, visibly unhappy mood.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 不是适合孟子，而是让他觉得很伤心：不适合孟子，还让他觉得很伤心，use direct negation with 不适合; the two results are additive, so 还 is more suitable than the corrective contrast pair 不是……而是……
- Expressiveness: After the opening sentence, add one concrete detail explaining the danger, such as “街上人来人往，车也很多。”
- Expressiveness: At the sentence about the mother accompanying him, show her worry with an internal thought or short speech, such as “孟母很担心：‘这儿对孩子不安全。’”
- Expressiveness: Describe how the boy’s sadness appears, such as “孟子常常低着头，一个人站在商店门口。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show how the new environment affects both Mengzi and his mother. The cause-and-result idea using 害得 is especially effective here.

## Scene 3

> 所以，孟母和孟子搬到学校的旁边住。才五岁孟子开始学哲学。在学校，孟子不是看书，就是练习写字。将来，他成为中国的一个非常有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 才五岁孟子开始学哲学：孟子才五岁就开始学哲学，This construction uses 才 + age/number + 就 + verb to show that an action began at an early age.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬到学校的旁边住：搬到了学校的旁边住，The move is a completed action in the story, so 搬到 takes 了 to mark completion.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为：成为了，Becoming a philosopher is a completed change of state in this past narrative, so 成为 takes 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 将来：后来，Later is the sequence connector for the next completed event in a past story; 将来 points to the future from the present.
- Expressiveness: After the school is introduced, add one sensory detail about its calm learning environment, such as: 学校里很安静，常常能听见学生读书的声音。
- Expressiveness: When describing his study, show his curiosity with a short thought or question, such as: 孟子常常想：“人为什么要学习呢？”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the important change from the noisy city to the school environment, and your use of 孟母 and 孟子 keeps the characters easy to follow.
- The pattern 不是……就是…… works well to show that 孟子 spends his time seriously studying and practising writing.
