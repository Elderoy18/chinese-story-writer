## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，孟子和孟母住了在靠墓地的家。孩子才五岁就每天在墓地祭拜了。在那个城市，孟母从早到晚都找工作，可是找不到，害得孟母没有钱。难怪她跟孩子搬家，原来买不了房。他们搬了到别一个城市找工作。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，this time expression is redundant because both parts mean “in ancient times.”
- 跟孩子搬家：带着孩子搬家，this means the mother takes the child with her when moving; 跟 suggests that they move together as equal participants.
- 别一个城市：另一个城市，别的 distinguishes other items from a stated one, while 另一个 means “another one.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 买不了 -- “原来买不了房”：无力买房，specifies that the obstacle is a lack of financial ability, which fits the preceding mention of having no money.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟母：有一个小男孩叫孟子，他和孟母，at a character’s first mention, introduce the named character with a short identifying noun phrase.
- Coherence (Reference): 在那个城市：在一个城市里，this is the first mention of a city, so “that city” has no established referent; introduce it as a new place.
- Coherence (Temporality): 住了在：住在，living in a place is a continuing state here, not a completed action, so it takes the zero particle rather than 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 祭拜了：祭拜，每天 marks a routine action, so use the zero particle rather than 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬了到：搬到了，put 了 after the directional/resultative complement 到 in a completed move.
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery activity, add what the child copies to make the action more vivid: “孟子觉得祭拜很有趣，就学别人祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where the mother cannot find work, add her feeling: “孟母很着急，不知道该怎么办。”
- Expressiveness: Before the move, add a short thought or quotation from the mother: “孟母心想：‘不能一直住在这儿，我得带着孟子搬家。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the family’s difficulty through the sequence of living near the cemetery, searching for work, and moving.
- You also used the paired pattern “难怪……，原来……” effectively to connect the mother’s move with its reason.

## Scene 2

> 这个城市老是很吵，不是人吵架，就是都叫，害得孟子一直不能睡觉。在那儿，孟母也找不到工作。孟子和妈妈要又一个次搬了家。在第三个城市，孟母找到工作，在商店做衣服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 又：再，Because the move has not happened yet and follows the modal verb 要, use 再 for a future repeat.
- 一个次：一次，次 is already the measure word for a number of moves, so it does not take 个.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很吵 -- “这个城市老是很吵”：嘈杂，specifies that the city is filled with confusing, unpleasant noise；吵闹，describes a lively but disruptive level of noise.
- 做 -- “在商店做衣服”：缝制，specifies that her work is sewing garments；制作，emphasizes making clothes as her job.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 人：人们，This refers to a concrete group in the city, so use 人们 rather than the generic noun 人.
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬了家：搬家，After the modal verb 要, use the zero particle rather than 了 because the move is planned, not completed.
- Coherence (Temporality): 孟母找到工作：孟母找到了工作，Finding the job is a completed action, and 了 comes after the resultative complement 到.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 。孟子和妈妈要：，所以孟子和妈妈要，The inability to sleep and the failure to find work are causes of the planned move, so 所以 clearly marks the cause-and-effect relationship.
- Expressiveness: After “这个城市老是很吵”, add a concrete sound detail, such as “从早到晚都有很多人在街上喊叫”.
- Expressiveness: Show Mengzi’s feeling after “一直不能睡觉”, such as “孟子又累又烦，晚上常常睡不着”.
- Expressiveness: Add Mengmu’s worried thought before the next move, such as “孟母心想：‘这儿不适合孩子生活。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show how the noisy environment affects Mengzi by using 害得, which gives the first sentence a strong connection between the problem and its result.
- You also move the setting forward clearly with 在第三个城市 and give Mengmu a specific new job.

## Scene 3

> 孟子在那个商店也找了到工作，可是不是做衣服的工作，而是喜欢的工作。孟子学习馆学了十五年，他的学习和他的旅行经验很重要，对他很多好处。他是大人的时候，他成为很有名的哲人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 孟子学习馆学了十五年：孟子在学习馆学了十五年，A place where an action happens needs the locative structure with 在; the original sentence lacks 在 before the place phrase.
- 对他很多好处：对他有很多好处，The verb 有 is required in this expression: something can have benefits for someone.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 旅行 -- “他的旅行经验”：游历，emphasizes experience gained from visiting different places and fits a life-story narrative.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他的学习和他的旅行经验：他的学习和旅行经验，Use zero form for the repeated possessor within the same coordinated noun phrase; both learning and travel experience belong to the same person.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为：成为了，This becoming is a completed life event after the long period of study, so it takes 了.
- Expressiveness: Near the long-study sentence, add one concrete detail about his effort, such as “他每天认真读书，常常思考很多问题。”
- Expressiveness: At the final sentence, add a feeling or short thought to show his ambition, such as “孟子心想：‘我要帮助更多人。’”
- Expressiveness: You could make his reputation more vivid by showing other people’s response, such as “很多人都来听他讲道理。”

### Encouragement
- You use a clear contrast structure to distinguish the kind of work Mengzi found from clothing work.
- The scene also gives Mengzi a clear long-term path from studying to becoming a respected thinker.
