## Scene 1

> 这个男孩子叫孟子，孟子是古代的人。孟子的妈妈觉得住在哪儿对童年很重要，所以他们常常搬了家。她望子成龙，所以孟子的妈妈安排他的生活。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- Nothing to flag.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 安排 -- “安排他的生活”：规划，emphasizes making a considered, organized plan for his daily life.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子是古代的人：是古代的人，because the name has already appeared in the same sentence, use zero form rather than repeating it.
- Coherence (Reference): 所以孟子的妈妈安排他的生活：所以安排他的生活，because the same subject continues within the sentence, zero form is more natural than repeating the full noun phrase.
- Coherence (Temporality): 常常搬了家：常常搬家，a habitual action with 常常 uses the zero particle; 了 marks a completed action rather than a routine.
- Expressiveness: After the mother’s concern about where they live, add a feeling such as “孟子的妈妈很着急”.
- Expressiveness: Make the mother’s planning more concrete with one daily-life detail, such as “她每天让孟子读书、写字”.
- Expressiveness: Add a short inner thought after “望子成龙”, such as “她想：‘我一定要让孟子好好学习。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly introduce the main characters and explain the mother’s motivation. The use of “望子成龙” is especially fitting and shows a good understanding of a meaningful Chinese idiom.

## Scene 2

> 因为他们常常搬了家，中国人说，孟母三迁。孟子没有爸爸，所以，他的妈妈更重要。从这儿到那儿，妈妈教孟子很多技能。先她让孟子跟朋友玩儿，然后教他怎么买又便宜又好质量的东西。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 先她让孟子跟朋友玩儿：她先让孟子跟朋友玩儿，The subject comes before the time-order adverbial; place the subject before 先.
- 又便宜又好质量的东西：又便宜又质量好的东西，质量 is a noun, so it needs the adjective 好 after it in the phrase 质量好.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 技能 -- “妈妈教孟子很多技能”：本领，sounds more concrete for practical abilities that a child learns, such as playing with others and shopping wisely

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 常常搬了家：常常搬家，常常 describes a habitual action, so use the zero particle rather than completed-action 了.
- Expressiveness: After the mother’s lessons, add Mengzi’s reaction to show his feelings: “孟子觉得这些本领很有意思。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where the mother teaches shopping, add a short line of speech: “妈妈说：‘买东西要又便宜又质量好。’”
- Expressiveness: Add one concrete detail about the shopping lesson: “妈妈带孟子去市场，看不同商人卖的东西。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show that Mengzi’s mother actively teaches him useful things, and the sequence with 先 and 然后 makes the two lessons easy to follow.
