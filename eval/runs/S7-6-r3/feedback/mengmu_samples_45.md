## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子。他们住在墓地的旁边，所以孟子老是祭拜。孟子不是祭拜，就是跟朋友聊天。他很喜欢他的城市，但是城市有才两个学校，都很小。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，The time word and the following time phrase repeat the same meaning; use the shorter time expression.
- 城市有才两个学校：城市里才有两个学校，In an existential sentence, the limiting adverb must come before the verb, so use the order “才有”; “城里” gives the location naturally.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 小 -- “都很小”：规模不大，This specifies that the schools are small in scale rather than simply physically small.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子：有一个叫孟子的男孩子和他的妈妈住在中国，On first mention, introduce the main character with both his name and a full noun phrase instead of naming him only after a pronoun.
- Coherence (Reference): 他的城市：自己的城市，When the subject and the possessor are the same person, this form makes that relationship clear and avoids suggesting that the city belongs to another male person.
- Expressiveness: Add a visual detail about the cemetery before the character’s action, such as “孟子每天看见很多人来墓地祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: Add the boy’s thought to explain his interest, such as “他觉得祭拜很有趣。”
- Expressiveness: Add the mother’s feeling about the small schools, such as “孟子的妈妈觉得这里不适合孩子学习。”

### Encouragement
You used short sentences that make the scene easy to follow. The pattern “不是……就是……” also gives a clear picture of the boy’s usual activities.

## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈不是不喜欢他们的城市，而是想孟子有很好的教育。他们搬家到有很多学校的城市。他们从早到晚走路，孟子一直抱怨。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他的妈妈不是不喜欢他们的城市，而是想孟子有很好的教育：他的妈妈不是因为不喜欢他们的城市，而是因为想让孟子受到很好的教育，The paired structure should contrast two reasons, so both parts need to show the reason. Use “想让 + person + verb phrase” for the mother’s intended outcome, and “受到教育” is the natural collocation.
- 他们搬家到有很多学校的城市：他们搬到有很多学校的城市，Use “搬到 + place” to state the destination of a move.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们搬到有很多学校的城市：他们搬到了有很多学校的城市，This move is a completed event in the narrative, so use 了 after the verb-result sequence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们从早到晚走路：他们从早到晚走了一整天路，Walking from morning to evening is a completed action, so use 了; “一整天” also makes the duration more exact.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他们从早到晚走了一整天路，孟子一直抱怨：他们从早到晚走了一整天路，所以孟子一直抱怨，Use 所以 to show the cause-and-effect relation: the long walk causes the complaint.
- Expressiveness: At the point where Mengzi complains, add a physical detail to show why the walk is difficult, such as “孟子走得脚都疼了。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech after the complaint to show Mengzi’s feelings, such as “妈妈，我们还要走多久？”
- Expressiveness: When introducing the new city, add one sensory detail about its atmosphere, such as “街上很热闹，到处都是商人的叫卖声。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the mother’s goal for Mengzi and use “从早到晚” and “一直抱怨” effectively to show a long, tiring journey and Mengzi’s reaction.

## Scene 3

> 他难怪抱怨，他想他的朋友，因为他不要离开他的城市。他的妈妈找到孟子一个老师。跟老师孟子学最重要的文学，所以他成为哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 找到孟子一个老师：给孟子找到了一个老师，Use 给 to mark 孟子 as the person who receives the benefit of the action, and use 了 because the mother has completed finding a teacher.
- 跟老师孟子学：孟子跟老师学，The learner, 孟子, needs to come before the phrase 跟老师; the original order can incorrectly sound as if the teacher is named 孟子.
- 成为哲学家：成为了哲学家，Use 了 for the completed change of state: he later became a philosopher.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 城市 -- “离开他的城市”：原来住的城市，identifies more precisely that this is the city where he lived before moving.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他的朋友：自己的朋友，When the subject and the possessor are the same person, 自己的 makes the relationship clear.
- Coherence (Reference): 他的城市：自己的城市，When 孟子 is both the subject and the owner of the city he does not want to leave, 自己的 gives a clearer reference.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他难怪抱怨，他想他的朋友，因为他不要离开他的城市：难怪他抱怨，原来他想他的朋友，也不想离开他的城市，Use the paired pattern 难怪……，原来…… to present the complaint first and then reveal its explanation.
- Expressiveness: After the complaint, add Mengzi’s feeling in a short thought or quotation, such as: 孟子难过地说：“我真想我的朋友。”
- Expressiveness: When he studies with the teacher, add a small detail about his learning, such as: 他认真地听老师讲课，还问了很多问题。
- Expressiveness: Before the final result, show his mother’s reaction, such as: 孟子的妈妈看到他喜欢学习，心里很高兴。

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the sequence from moving away, to studying with a teacher, to becoming a philosopher. You also try to explain Mengzi’s complaint with a reason, which helps the reader understand his feelings.
