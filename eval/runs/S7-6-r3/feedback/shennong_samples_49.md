## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有很多人不知道什么植物能吃，什么植物不能吃。有很多人吃植物以后就吃坏肚子了，因为他们吃了毒。那个时候，有一个老人叫神农，他什么植物都觉得好吃，不怕生病，不怕毒。他希望别人也可以吃好吃的植物。可是他知道，如果他们还怕吃毒，不会吃他喜欢的植物。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，The original repeats two time expressions: 古代 already names a time period, so adding 的时候 is redundant.
- 吃了毒：吃了有毒的植物，毒 names poison itself; in this scene, the people are eating plants that contain poison, so the full noun phrase is needed.
- 怕吃毒：怕吃到有毒的植物，吃到 marks the result of encountering and eating a poisonous plant, rather than deliberately taking poison.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不怕 -- “不怕生病，不怕毒”：毫不害怕，emphasizes that Shen Nong has no fear at all in this dangerous situation.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the people become ill, add a physical detail to show their discomfort, such as “很多人肚子疼得直不起腰。”
- Expressiveness: When Shen Nong appears, add a short thought or quotation to show his purpose, such as “我要找出能吃的植物！”
- Expressiveness: At “他希望别人也可以吃好吃的植物,” add a feeling word to show that he cares about other people, such as “他很担心人们再吃错植物。”

### Encouragement
You clearly explain the problem people faced before introducing Shen Nong, so the scene has a clear purpose. You also use pronouns consistently after introducing him, which makes the writing easy to follow.

## Scene 2

> 所以，他上山下山找植物，吃了很多样子的植物。他是第一人，找到了茶。他把所有的植物写下来了。他一直都吃了，他连毒都能吃。可是他还没吃了一个叫夹竹桃的植物。他找到了那个像星星的植物，觉得很漂亮，马上就吃下了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很多样子的植物：很多种植物，样子 describes an object’s appearance, while 种 is the measure word used for different kinds of plants.
- 第一人：第一个人，an ordinal expression for the first individual needs the measure word 个 here.
- 毒：有毒的植物，毒 is a noun meaning “poison”; the intended food item is a plant that is poisonous.
- 一个叫夹竹桃的植物：一种叫夹竹桃的植物，种 is the suitable measure word when referring to one type of plant.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 上山下山 -- “他上山下山找植物”：翻山越岭，creates a clearer image of travelling across many mountains during the search.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 一直都吃了：一直都在吃，an action continuing over time needs 在 for the progressive state; 了 marks completion and does not fit with 一直 here.
- Coherence (Temporality): 还没吃了：还没吃过，after 没, the action is not completed, so it cannot take 了; 过 expresses that he has not had this experience before.
- Expressiveness: After the plant is found, add a visual detail to make its appearance memorable. Example: “那儿开着黄色的花，像星星一样，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: Before he eats it, add his thought to show why he acts so quickly. Example: “他想：这种植物真漂亮，一定很好吃！”

### Encouragement
You used several useful action verbs to show Shennong actively searching and trying plants. The ending also creates a clear moment of suspense when he quickly eats the beautiful plant.

## Scene 3

> 可是夹竹桃的毒很重，让他肚子很痛。他在地上躺着，觉得他快会死了。可是正好有别人找到他了，给了他一个像伞的植物，叫灵芝。他吃灵芝以后就觉得很好，不再生病了。他测量起来了，像星星的植物是毒，像伞的植物是药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一个像伞的植物：一个像伞一样的植物，A comparison used before a noun normally needs the pattern with 一样的 to show that the plant resembles an umbrella.
- 像星星的植物：像星星一样的植物，The comparison needs 一样的 before the noun to describe the plant’s star-like shape.
- 是毒：有毒，毒 is a noun here; use 有毒 to describe that a plant contains poison.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很重 -- “夹竹桃的毒很重”：毒性很强，This states more precisely that the plant’s poisonous effect is strong.
- 很痛 -- “让他肚子很痛”：疼得厉害，This shows the degree of pain more vividly.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 觉得他快会死了：觉得快会死了，When the same character remains the subject in the same sentence and no other person competes as the referent, use zero form rather than repeating the pronoun.
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃灵芝以后：吃了灵芝以后，The eating action is completed before the later feeling, so the verb before 以后 needs 了.
- Expressiveness: At “他在地上躺着,” add a physical detail of his suffering, such as “疼得在地上打滚，难受得不得了,” to help the reader feel how serious the pain is.
- Expressiveness: At “正好有人找到他了,” describe the helper briefly, such as “一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人走过来,” so the rescue scene is easier to picture.

### Encouragement
The scene has a clear sequence: the poison causes pain, someone helps, and Shen Nong learns the difference between the two plants. You also use action words such as “躺着” and “给了” effectively to show what happens.
