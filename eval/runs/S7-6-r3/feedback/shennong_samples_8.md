## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个人叫神农。一天，神农正在路上走，一看很多人正在吃一些植物。一些人一吃植物就生病了，神农想帮别人。所以想知道哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，This expression redundantly combines a time period with a “time of” structure; use the shorter time expression.
- 一看：一看到，The verb needs a resultative complement because the sentence means that Shennong successfully saw the people, not that he merely looked.
- 别人：人们，The word refers to “other people” in contrast with a particular person already under discussion; here Shennong wants to help people in general.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 想知道 -- “所以想知道哪些植物能吃”：想弄清，emphasizes wanting to reach a clear answer after investigating the plants.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 神农想帮别人。所以想知道：神农想帮人们，所以想知道，Use the connector to join the motivation and its result: wanting to help people is the cause of wanting to learn which plants are safe.
- Expressiveness: After the people become ill, add one visible detail to show the problem rather than only naming it, such as: “有的人捂着肚子，脸色很难看。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where Shennong decides to investigate, add his inner thought to show his concern and determination, such as: “神农心想：我一定要弄清楚哪些植物能吃。”
- Expressiveness: You could add a brief description of the setting while he is walking, such as: “路边长着各种各样的花草。”

### Encouragement
- You introduced Shennong clearly at the beginning and used the ongoing-action marker well to show what was happening on the road.
- The sentence with the paired sequence pattern clearly shows that eating plants led to illness.

## Scene 2

> 然后，神农开始尝很多植物，比方说他吃过生姜、芥兰和菠菜。一些植物吃真好，特别是黄瓜。但是，一些植物毒得很，特别是夹竹桃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一些植物吃真好：一些植物吃起来真好，The complement “起来” is needed after the verb to describe the quality experienced when eating the plants.
- 毒得很：有毒，“毒” cannot take the degree complement “得很”; use “有毒” to describe a poisonous plant.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很多 -- “神农开始尝很多植物”：各种各样的，emphasizes that the plants were of many different types rather than only a large number.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: At “特别是黄瓜,” add a sensory detail about its taste, such as “黄瓜吃起来清凉又脆。” This lets the reader understand why it is a good example.
- Expressiveness: At “特别是夹竹桃,” describe the flower’s appearance before revealing its danger, such as “夹竹桃开着黄色的花，像星星一样漂亮。” This creates a stronger contrast with its poisonous nature.
- Expressiveness: Add Shennong’s thought when he sees the attractive plant, such as “神农想：这种花这么漂亮，应该可以吃吧？” This shows his uncertainty and makes the scene more lively.

### Encouragement
You used “比方说” correctly to introduce clear examples, and “特别是” correctly to single out cucumber and oleander from the larger groups of plants. The contrast between plants that taste good and plants that are poisonous is also easy to follow.

## Scene 3

> 一个人对神农说，麻烦我们可以吃什么植物？神农说，这就要看你想不想生病了。以后，神农告诉别人一些植物能吃，一些植物不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 麻烦：请问，麻烦 cannot introduce a polite question in this position; 请问 is used to politely begin a question.
- 以后，神农告诉别人一些植物能吃，一些植物不能吃：后来，神农告诉人们一些植物能吃，一些植物不能吃，sentence-initial 以后 does not mean “afterwards”; use 后来. Also, 别人 distinguishes other people from a stated person or group, while 人们 refers to people generally.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: At the reply, add a brief reason for the warning, such as “有的植物有毒，吃了会生病。”, to make the information more vivid and clear.
- Expressiveness: In the final sentence, give one concrete example of each kind of plant, such as “灵芝是药，可以吃；夹竹桃有毒，不能吃。”, so the reader can remember the contrast.

### Encouragement
You used the pattern “这就要看……了” accurately. The scene also clearly presents the important contrast between plants that can be eaten and plants that cannot.
