## Scene 1

> 古代的时候人觉得祭拜非常重要，孟子很小的时候，从早到晚一直祭拜，因为他妈妈告诉他祭拜很重要。虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜。他的同学们老是对他开玩笑了。孟子妈妈看了他的同学们对孟子不好的影响，所以孟子和孟子妈妈搬家了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，The original expression redundantly combines two time expressions; the corrected form introduces the historical setting directly.
- 人：人们，This refers to a concrete group of people in ancient times, so the plural collective noun is needed.
- 孟子妈妈：孟子的妈妈，A named person used as a possessor normally takes the possessive particle before a family-role noun.
- 看了：看到了，Here the mother perceives the harmful influence; the resultative form expresses that she successfully saw/noticed it, rather than merely looked at it.
- 孟子妈妈：孟子的妈妈，A named person used as a possessor normally takes the possessive particle before a family-role noun.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不好的 -- “他的同学们对孟子不好的影响”：负面的，This specifically describes the influence as harmful or adverse rather than using a broad evaluation.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子很小的时候：有一个小男孩叫孟子。他很小的时候，On his first mention, introduce the character with both his name and role before using later references to him.
- Coherence (Temporality): 同学们老是对他开玩笑了：同学们老是对他开玩笑，A habitual action marked by 老是 uses the zero particle, not 了, because it is not one completed event.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜：因为他每天只祭拜，所以没有朋友，The intended relationship is cause and effect, so use 因为……所以…… rather than mixing 虽然 with 因为.
- Expressiveness: After the description of the daily worship, add the boy’s personal feeling, such as “孟子觉得祭拜很有趣。”
- Expressiveness: When describing the classmates’ reaction, show how they tease him with an action, such as “他们一边笑，一边学他祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: Add the mother’s worried thought when she notices the influence, such as “孟子的妈妈很担心：‘这里不适合孟子长大。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly establish the ancient setting and explain why the boy worships so often. You also include a consequence of his behaviour, which helps the scene move forward.

## Scene 2

> 孟子才十一岁，成为一个哲学家。孟子不是祭拜，而是看政治文章，难怪他成为了哲学家。不过，看过很多政治文章对他害得，他有很多问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 孟子才十一岁，成为：孟子才十一岁，就成为，The pattern for an unexpectedly early action is “才 + age/number + 就 + verb.”
- 对他害得：害得他，The structure is “害得 + person + harmful result”; do not put 对 before the affected person.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 问题 -- “他有很多问题”：疑问，specifies that these are questions or uncertainties in his mind, rather than practical problems.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子不是祭拜：他不是祭拜，Use a pronoun when the same character was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other person competes as the referent.
- Expressiveness: After “看政治文章,” add an inner reaction to show why the reading matters: “他一边看一边想：‘为什么国家会有这么多问题？’”
- Expressiveness: After “他有很多问题,” add a detail about his learning attitude: “他把不懂的问题都写下来，准备以后问老师。”

### Encouragement
- You used the contrast pattern “不是……而是……” clearly to show a change in Mengzi’s interests.
- You also connected his reading to his later achievement with “难怪,” which gives the scene a clear idea flow.

## Scene 3

> 他一直问问题，但是不能解决那些问题。孟子长大，他开始能解决问题。他常常建议你不是学金融，而是学政治，因为如果你懂政治，你会成为高薪。后来孟子成为教授，他教学生很厉害。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不是学金融，而是学政治：不要学金融，而要学政治，After “建议”, use a verb phrase that states the recommended action; “不要……，而要……” expresses advising someone not to do one action but to do another.
- 成为高薪：拿到高薪，High salary is remuneration, not a role that someone can become; “拿到高薪” means earning a high salary.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 问问题 -- “他一直问问题”：提出问题，emphasizes formulating and raising questions for discussion or thought.
- 解决 -- “不能解决那些问题”：解答，more precisely means giving answers to questions.
- 很厉害 -- “他教学生很厉害”：很擅长教学生，states specifically that teaching students is an ability he does well.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 你：人们，“你” has not been introduced as a character or audience member, so its referent is unclear; use a full noun phrase for this new, general group.
- Coherence (Temporality): 孟子长大：孟子长大了，“长大” describes a completed change of state here, so it needs 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为教授：成为了教授，Becoming a professor is a completed event in the later part of the narrative, so use 了.
- Expressiveness: After the questions in the first sentence, add an internal thought to show why politics interests him: “孟子觉得政治可以帮助人们解决很多问题。”
- Expressiveness: Turn his advice into quoted speech so that his character has a clearer voice: “他说：‘不要只学金融，也要学政治。’”
- Expressiveness: At the end, add one concrete detail about his teaching: “学生们都喜欢听他讲政治。”

### Encouragement
- You create a clear progression from Mengzi having unanswered questions to growing up and becoming a professor. The use of “后来” also helps the reader follow the later stage of his life.
