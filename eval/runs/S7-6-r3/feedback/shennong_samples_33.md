## Scene 1

> 古代时候，人不知道他们能吃什么植物，常常一吃就死。但是不知道为什么，没有办法。所以有一天，神农出去试一试很多种植物，告诉人什么植物是安全什么是危险。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代时候：在古代，古代 is already a time expression, so adding 时候 is redundant.
- 人：人们，this refers to a concrete group of people in the story, so use 人们 rather than the generic noun 人.
- 什么植物是安全什么是危险：什么植物是安全的，什么植物是危险的，each coordinated clause needs its own complete predicate; an adjective following 是 takes 的 here.
- 告诉人：告诉人们，this refers to the group of people in the story, so 人们 is needed.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 没有办法 -- “但是不知道为什么，没有办法”：束手无策，more vividly shows that the people cannot find any solution in this situation；毫无办法，emphasizes that there is no solution at all.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他们：删除，人们 has already been mentioned in the same clause, so use zero form rather than repeating the referent with 他们.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉：告诉了，this is the final action in a completed series of events, so the last verb takes 了.
- Expressiveness: After “常常一吃就死”, add a concrete consequence such as “很多人因为吃了有毒的植物，常常生病” to show why the problem is serious.
- Expressiveness: At “神农出去试一试很多种植物”, add Shennong’s purpose or thought, such as “神农想：我要找出哪些植物能吃”, to show his motivation.

### Encouragement
You clearly establish the central problem before introducing Shennong, and “常常一吃就死” creates a strong sense of danger. Your use of “所以” also gives Shennong’s action a clear reason.

## Scene 2

> 发现有的植物对身体非常好，特别是茶，让肚子感觉又暖和又清楚。他告诉朋友：“你吃哪种植物，这就要看你的口味。比方说，你爱吃甜的，可能喜欢糖。”他最喜欢吃花，除了夹竹桃以外，什么都花想吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 让肚子感觉又暖和又清楚：让人觉得肚子又暖和又舒服，清楚 describes clarity of thought, sight, or information; it cannot describe a stomach’s physical sensation.
- 什么都花想吃：什么花都想吃，when expressing “every kind of flower,” the noun must follow the question word before 都.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 非常好 -- “有的植物对身体非常好”：非常有好处，states the health benefit more precisely；特别有益，emphasizes that the plants are beneficial to health.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 这就要看你的口味：这就要看你的口味了，了 is part of the fixed expression and does not mark a completed action here.
- Expressiveness: After mentioning tea, add a concrete action to show its effect, such as “他把茶叶放在热水里，喝了以后觉得全身暖和。”
- Expressiveness: Give the speaker a feeling or expression before the dialogue, such as “他高兴地告诉朋友：‘茶喝起来暖暖的！’”

### Encouragement
You use direct speech well to give the scene a character’s voice. The phrase “除了夹竹桃以外” also correctly sets up an exception.

## Scene 3

> 他发现这个口味，一百岁时候，不小心吃了毒的花。一吃就开始死。但是生病时候告诉人什么植物是药，什么植物是毒。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一百岁时候：一百岁的时候，The structure with a multi-syllable time expression requires 的 before 时候.
- 毒的花：有毒的花，Use 有毒的 to describe a flower that contains poison; 毒的 is not the correct adjective form here.
- 生病时候：生病的时候，The structure with a verb phrase before 时候 requires 的.
- 告诉人：告诉人们，This refers to a concrete group whom he tells, so use 人们 rather than the generic noun 人.
- 什么植物是毒：什么植物有毒，毒 is a noun; use 有毒 to state that a plant is poisonous.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不小心 -- “一百岁时候，不小心吃了毒的花”：误食，specifically means accidentally eating something and fits this dangerous-food situation.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After “不小心吃了有毒的花,” add a physical reaction to show how serious the poisoning is, for example: “他一吃，肚子就疼了起来。”
- Expressiveness: Add his worried thought after he becomes ill, for example: “他想：我会死吗？”
- Expressiveness: Describe the flower briefly before he eats it, for example: “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the important sequence of eating a poisonous flower, becoming ill, and then sharing knowledge about plants. Your use of “一……就……” effectively shows that the result happened very quickly.
