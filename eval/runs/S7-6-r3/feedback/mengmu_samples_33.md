## Scene 1

> 一天，很久很久以前，孟子和他妈妈住在中国。孟子妈妈的工作是祭拜，孟子不高兴，因为他说我们老是搬家，为什么？他妈妈说，从早到晚，我一直祭拜，很多墓地得我祭拜。所以我们得去很多不同的地方。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一天，很久很久以前：很久很久以前的一天，The time expression should place the broad time before the specific “day.”
- 他妈妈说，从早到晚，我一直祭拜，很多墓地得我祭拜。所以我们得去很多不同的地方。：他妈妈说：“从早到晚，我一直祭拜。我得去很多不同的墓地祭拜，所以我们得去很多不同的地方。”，Direct speech needs a colon and quotation marks. In the corrected sentence, the subject comes before the modal verb, and the modal verb comes before the verb phrase and its destination.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不高兴 -- “孟子不高兴”：委屈，shows that he feels unfairly affected by the repeated moves；烦恼，shows that the repeated moves are troubling him.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和他妈妈：有一个小男孩叫孟子，他和妈妈，On first mention, introduce the character with a name and a brief identity, rather than assuming the reader already knows who he is.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子妈妈的工作：他妈妈的工作，Since孟子was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other possible mother has appeared, use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为他说我们老是搬家，为什么？：问：“我们为什么老是搬家？”，The connector因为should introduce an actual cause. Here, the character is asking a question rather than giving the cause of his unhappiness.
- Expressiveness: After孟子不高兴, add a small action to show his feeling rather than only naming it: “孟子皱着眉头，低着头问。”
- Expressiveness: During the mother’s explanation, add a detail showing that her work is tiring: “妈妈叹了口气，说：‘从早到晚，我一直祭拜。’”
- Expressiveness: You could make the many locations easier to imagine with one concrete cemetery detail: “有的墓地在山上，有的墓地离家很远。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show why the family has to move so often, and the phrase “从早到晚” effectively communicates how long the mother works. The dialogue also helps the reader hear both孟子’s question and his mother’s explanation.

## Scene 2

> 孟子想，难怪我们常常搬家，原来我妈妈很忙。可是常常搬家害得孟子很难找到朋友。每次搬家孟子也觉得新的地方不是太热就是太冷，不是太大就是太小。在哪的地方，他都不高兴。孟子想帮助妈妈祭拜，可是他六岁，所以不能祭拜。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在哪的地方，他都不高兴。：“不管在哪个地方，他都不高兴。”，哪 must be followed by the suitable measure word 个 before 地方, and 不管……都…… expresses that the feeling applies in every location.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 找到 -- “常常搬家害得孟子很难找到朋友”：交到，emphasizes successfully forming friendships rather than physically finding people；结识，emphasizes meeting and becoming acquainted with new friends
- 不高兴 -- “他都不高兴”：闷闷不乐，shows a continuing low and gloomy mood more vividly；不开心，gives a more natural everyday description of his unhappy feeling

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After “孟子想帮助妈妈祭拜”, add his reason for wanting to join, such as “他觉得祭拜很好玩，也想跟妈妈一起做。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of dialogue to show Mengzi’s wish directly, such as “妈妈，我也想祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: At “很难交到朋友”, add a small feeling detail, such as “每次刚认识一个朋友，就又要离开了，他觉得很难过。”

### Encouragement
You used the challenging pattern “难怪……，原来……” well to show Mengzi’s realization. You also used both pairs of “不是……就是……” correctly to describe the uncomfortable new places.

## Scene 3

> 四年后，孟子开始帮妈妈祭拜。他画画很厉害，他越来越喜欢祭拜，所以他祭拜不是想帮妈妈工作，而是他很喜欢画画。孟子成为很著名的古代画家。今天很多中国人很喜欢孟子。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 孟子成为很著名的古代画家：孟子成为了很著名的古代画家，This describes a completed change in Mengzi’s life, so the verb needs 了 to mark completion.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很厉害 -- “他画画很厉害”：绘画技艺高超, specifies that his high ability is in painting and gives the description a more vivid tone.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他画画很厉害，他越来越喜欢祭拜：他画画很厉害，越来越喜欢祭拜，The subject has already appeared in the same sentence, so use zero form rather than repeating the pronoun.
- Coherence (Reference): 所以他祭拜不是想帮妈妈工作：所以祭拜不是想帮妈妈工作，The subject remains Mengzi throughout this connected sentence, so zero form makes the reference smoother.
- Coherence (Reference): 而是他很喜欢画画：而是很喜欢画画，The same subject continues after the paired connector, so the repeated pronoun can be omitted.
- Expressiveness: After “孟子开始帮妈妈祭拜,” add one concrete action to show how he helps, such as: “他认真地摆好祭拜的东西，还画下墓地旁边的花。”
- Expressiveness: At “他越来越喜欢祭拜,” add Mengzi’s thought or speech to show his feelings, such as: “孟子心想：‘祭拜的时候，我可以看到很多不一样的东西。’”
- Expressiveness: After the final sentence, add a short evaluative detail about his reputation, such as: “人们常常欣赏他的画，说他的画很有意思。”

### Encouragement
You used a clear time transition with “四年后,” and you showed Mengzi’s developing interest through “越来越喜欢.” The scene also gives the reader a clear idea of his talent for painting.
