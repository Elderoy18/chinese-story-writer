## Scene 1

> 古代的时候有一个人叫神农。他需要当上医，一看别的人就想帮助他们。但是，他有一个问题，他不知道什么东西能做药。神农听说一个远远的地方有一种植物，什么病人吃都会觉得身体更好。神农三个月走去，找到有植物的地方，他看到一个很漂亮的植物，又香又好看。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，This phrase redundantly combines a historical time noun with another “time of” expression.
- 医：医生，The original word refers to medicine or medical treatment; the noun for a person whose job is treating patients is the corrected word.
- 一看：一看到，The sentence means that he sees someone, rather than merely looks; this meaning needs the resultative complement.
- 别的人：别人，Use the corrected word for people other than oneself or the person being discussed; the original form is not appropriate here.
- 一个很漂亮的植物：一种很漂亮的植物，Plants use the measure word shown in the correction, not the general measure word used for people or many objects.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 东西 -- “他不知道什么东西能做药”：植物，specifies that he is looking for a particular kind of natural thing that can become medicine.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他有一个问题，他不知道：他有一个问题，不知道，After the subject has appeared earlier in the same sentence, use zero form rather than repeating the pronoun.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农听说：他听说，The name was mentioned in the immediately preceding sentence and there is no competing character, so a pronoun is more natural for this close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 神农三个月走去，找到有植物的地方：神农走了三个月，找到了有植物的地方，Put the duration after the action verb; both the journey and the successful finding are completed actions, so they take 了.
- Expressiveness: When the plant first appears, add its colour and shape to make the setting easier to imagine: “开着黄色的、像星星一样的花。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where he has walked for a long time, add his determined feeling: “他虽然很累，可是还是继续往前走。”
- Expressiveness: After he sees the attractive plant, add a short thought to show his curiosity: “这是什么植物呢？”

### Encouragement
- You introduce the main character clearly and keep the scene focused on his wish to help others.
- The description “又香又好看” gives the plant pleasant sensory detail and makes the ending more vivid.

## Scene 2

> 他觉得，这种一定是做药的花。原来他想错了。吃了以后，神农快生病。 这时一个穿着蓝色衣服的人帮神农，快给他一种红的灵芝。神农吃了以后，他没有病，想起来以前一个人告诉他，灵芝对身体有好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- Nothing to flag.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 想错了 -- “原来他想错了”：判断错了, specifies that his judgment about the flower was mistaken；看走眼了, gives a more vivid sense that he misjudged it from its appearance

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他：∅，After naming the character at the beginning of the sentence, use a zero subject for the following closely connected actions rather than repeating a pronoun.
- Coherence (Temporality): 快生病：很快就生病了，快 alone usually expresses that something is about to happen; 很快 describes a quick onset, and 了 marks that the illness began as a completed past event.
- Coherence (Temporality): 快给他：赶快给了他，The giving is a completed narrative action, so use 给了; 赶快 describes that the person acted in a hurry.
- Coherence (Temporality): 没有病：病好了，This is a change from being ill to recovering, so 好了 marks the completed change of state.
- Expressiveness: After the illness begins, add a physical reaction to show how serious it feels: “神农肚子疼得不得了，躺在地上。”
- Expressiveness: When the blue-clothed person helps, add a short line of speech to make the rescue more lively: “快吃这个，也许能帮你！”
- Expressiveness: Add one visual detail about the flower before the character eats it: “那种花又黄又大，像星星一样漂亮。”

### Encouragement
You used “原来” effectively to show that the character’s first judgment was mistaken. You also introduced the new helper clearly with a full description, “一个穿着蓝色衣服的人,” which helps the reader picture the scene.

## Scene 3

> 啊，他怎么忘记了？他把灵芝放在包里。一回家就告诉别人。他找到药以后他写下怎么做药在书上。所以人们都知道，那红灵芝对身体好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 写下怎么做药在书上：在书上写下怎么做药，The location phrase should come before the verb it modifies: the writing happens in the book.
- 那红灵芝：那个红灵芝，A demonstrative such as 那 needs a measure word before the noun.
- 对身体好处：对身体有好处，The phrase needs 有 to express that something has benefits for the body.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他找到药以后他写下怎么做药：他找到药以后，写下怎么做药，After the same subject has appeared in the first clause, use zero form rather than repeating 他 in the same sentence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他把灵芝放在包里：他把灵芝放在包里了，This is a completed action in the narrative, so add 了 after the verb phrase.
- Coherence (Temporality): 一回家就告诉别人：一回家就告诉了别人，Telling others is a completed narrative action here, so 告诉 takes 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 写下：写下了，Writing down the information is completed, so add 了 after the resultative complement 下.
- Expressiveness: After “啊，他怎么忘记了？”, add an inner thought to show his surprise: “他想：我怎么把灵芝忘了？”
- Expressiveness: When he puts away the herb, add a careful action detail: “他小心地把红灵芝放进包里了。”
- Expressiveness: After returning home, add a short line of speech: “灵芝对身体有好处，可以做药！”

### Encouragement
You clearly connected the red 灵芝 to its helpful effect on the body, and your use of “一回家就” gives the events a quick, active pace.
