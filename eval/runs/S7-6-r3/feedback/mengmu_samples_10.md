## Scene 1

> 孟子小的时候，从早到晚都跟朋友玩。孟子和他的朋友喜欢玩在墓地里，装祭拜。孟子五岁的时候，跟妈妈搬家。他们搬家不是因为孟子玩得太多，而是因为妈妈认为他们的家离墓地太近。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 喜欢玩在墓地里：喜欢在墓地里玩，A location phrase normally comes before the main action verb in this pattern: “like + place + action.”
- 装祭拜：假装祭拜，The verb “pretend” needs a form such as this before an action; the original word cannot directly introduce the action in this meaning.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和他的朋友：他和朋友，Use a pronoun for a recently mentioned character when no other singular male character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子五岁的时候：他五岁的时候，Use a pronoun for the close re-occurrence of the same character.
- Coherence (Temporality): 跟妈妈搬家：跟妈妈搬了家，This is a completed move in the past, so the final action takes 了 to mark completion.
- Expressiveness: At the cemetery-play moment, show how the children act so the scene feels more vivid: “他们一边笑，一边学别人祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: At the final sentence, add the mother’s feeling or thought to make her reason more personal: “妈妈很担心，觉得这里会影响孟子。”

### Encouragement
The scene has a clear time order, moving from childhood play to the family’s decision to move. The final sentence also uses a strong contrast structure to explain the reason for the move.

## Scene 2

> 妈妈认为，住离墓地太近，害得孟子的心气。孟子和孟母搬到子思老师的家，跟子思老师住。孟母帮子思打扫房间和做饭。子思帮孟母教她的儿子。因为孟子现在没有朋友，他老是读书。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 住离墓地太近：住在离墓地太近的地方，The verb for living somewhere needs the location structure with 在, and the place phrase needs a noun such as 地方.
- 害得孟子的心气：害得孟子心情不好，The structure 害得 needs a person followed by a harmful result clause; the original ends with only a noun phrase and does not state the result.
- 搬到：搬到了，This move is presented as a completed event, so the verb needs 了.
- 老是读书：一直读书，老是 can imply an unwanted or annoying repeated action; 一直 describes continuous reading more neutrally in this context.

### Vocabulary Suggestions

### Coherence & Expressiveness

- Coherence (Reference): 跟子思老师住：跟他住，After the teacher has just been named, use a pronoun for the close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Reference): 帮子思打扫：帮他打扫，Use a pronoun when the same character was named in the immediately preceding sentence.
- Coherence (Reference): 子思帮孟母：他帮她，Both characters were mentioned in the preceding sentence, so pronouns make the connection smoother and avoid unnecessarily repeating their names.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为孟子现在没有朋友，他老是读书：因为孟子现在没有朋友，所以他一直读书，Because the lack of friends causes the reading, use the paired cause-and-effect connector.

- Expressiveness: After the mother helps with chores, add one concrete detail, such as “孟母每天早早起来做饭，还把房间打扫得很干净。” This makes her work easier for the reader to imagine.
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of encouragement from the mother after Mengzi begins reading, such as “孟母高兴地说：‘你好好读书吧！’” This gives the scene a warmer emotional voice.

### Encouragement

## Scene 3

> 子思认为，对孩子来说，读书最重要，特别是学哲学。孟子不是练习写文章，就是看哲学书。孟子从五岁一直学哲学。他才五岁就学哲学，难怪他成为很有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 从五岁一直学哲学：从五岁开始一直学哲学，A starting age needs the pattern “从 + time + 开始” before the continuing action.

### Vocabulary Suggestions

### Coherence & Expressiveness

- Expressiveness: After the sentence about studying philosophy, add a small detail about his study habits, such as “他每天认真地读书，还常常问子思老师问题。”
- Expressiveness: At the final sentence, add his mother’s feeling to make the achievement more personal, such as “孟母看到孟子这么努力，心里很高兴。”

### Encouragement
