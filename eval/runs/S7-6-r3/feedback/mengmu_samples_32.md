## Scene 1

> 有一天孟子去了墓地玩儿，但是他妈妈觉得这是不合适的地方让孩子玩，所以她决定了他们会搬家找更好的地方。她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福，而且住在这儿教育不太好。因为她望子成龙，所以她会一直搬家才能找到质量好的教育。然后他们搬到了离城市更近的地方。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 她决定他们会搬家找更好的地方：她决定搬家，找更好的地方，after 决定, use the verb phrase that she decides to do; do not repeat the people who will carry out the action.
- 她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福：她担心儿子的童年不会幸福，恐怕 means “I am afraid that” and cannot take another subject such as 她 before it; 担心 expresses that she is worried.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好 -- “找更好的地方”：更适合孩子成长的，explains exactly why the new place would be better for the child.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子去了墓地玩儿：有一个小男孩叫孟子，他去了墓地玩儿，at the first mention, introduce the character with a full noun phrase before using a pronoun later.
- Coherence (Temporality): 决定了：决定，决定 is a mental-state verb, so it uses the zero particle rather than 了.
- Expressiveness: At the point where the mother judges the cemetery unsuitable, add her visible reaction to show her concern, for example: “孟母皱着眉头，心里很着急。”
- Expressiveness: After the mother worries about the child’s future, add a short line of thought or speech, for example: “她心想：‘住在这儿会影响孟子的学习，我得再搬一次家。’”
- Expressiveness: After the family moves closer to the city, add one concrete detail about the new setting, for example: “街上有很多商人卖东西，到处都很热闹。”

### Encouragement
You used clear cause-and-effect connectors, especially 因为……所以……, to explain the mother’s motivation. You also used 搬到了 correctly to show that the move was completed.

## Scene 2

> 他们住才一月就又搬家。古代的时候，一般人不喜欢卖东西的人。但是孟子老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿，害得妈妈着急得很，因为她恐怕儿子会成为跟他们很像。她告诉了孟子：他们不是朋友，而是不好的人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 住才一月：才住了一个月，Place the duration after the verb, and use 了 because the month of living there ended before the move.
- 搬家：搬家了，The move is a completed event, so the final action needs 了.
- 古代的时候：在古代，The original repeats the time meaning: 古代 already refers to a historical time, while 的时候 also means “at the time of.”
- 老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿：常常跟这些卖东西的人玩儿，老是 and 常常 both mark a frequent habit, so using both together is redundant here.
- 害得妈妈着急得很：让妈妈很着急，害得 must introduce a clearly harmful result affecting someone; ordinary worry does not meet that harmful-result requirement.
- 她恐怕儿子会成为跟他们很像：她担心儿子会变得跟他们很像，恐怕 cannot take 她 as its subject; use 担心 for her fear. Also, 变得 is needed before an adjective phrase such as 跟他们很像.
- 成为跟他们很像：变得跟他们很像，成为 introduces an identity or role, while 变得 introduces a change into a described state.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不好的人 -- “他们不是朋友，而是不好的人”：品行不好的人，specifies that the problem is these people’s conduct, rather than giving only a general negative judgment

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉了孟子：告诉孟子，A speaking verb that introduces quoted speech normally takes the zero particle, not 了.
- Expressiveness: After the mention of the sellers, add a market detail to make the setting more vivid, such as “街上从早到晚都很热闹。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where孟子 plays with the sellers, show what he copies, such as “孟子学着他们收钱，还学他们讨价还价。”
- Expressiveness: The mother’s direct speech could show her concern more strongly, such as “孟子，你不能每天只想着玩儿，应该好好学习。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show that this is another move by using 又, and you give the mother a direct line of speech, which makes the scene feel more immediate.

## Scene 3

> 你不是当数学家，而是当哲学家，不能当卖东西的人。所以他们又搬家。在他们第三个家，孟子学了怎么祭拜。妈妈感觉这个地方适合他们，告诉了孟子：我们找到了最后的家。在这儿妈妈让儿子从早到晚学习，他难怪成为有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在他们第三个家：在他们的第三个家里，A possessive phrase and an ordinal modifier need 的 before the noun, and 里 clearly marks the home as the location.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 地方 -- “妈妈感觉这个地方适合他们”：环境，identifies the surrounding conditions that make the home suitable rather than referring to an unspecified place

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 所以他们又搬家：所以他们又搬家了，The move is a completed event, so 又搬家 needs 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉了孟子：告诉孟子，A saying verb that introduces quoted speech takes the zero particle rather than 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为：成为了，Becoming a philosopher is presented as a completed life achievement, so the verb needs 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他难怪：难怪他，难怪 introduces the conclusion of an inference and should come before the subject of that conclusion.
- Expressiveness: At the point where the mother decides that the home is suitable, add her feeling and a short line of speech, such as: 妈妈高兴地说：“这儿的环境很适合你。”
- Expressiveness: When describing the new place, add one concrete detail that explains why studying is easier there, such as: 学校旁边很安静，孟子每天认真看书。

### Encouragement
You use a clear cause-and-effect transition for the family’s move. The mother’s direct speech also helps show her hope for her son’s future.
