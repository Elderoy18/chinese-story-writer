## Scene 1

> 很久以前，古代的人很不知道什么植物能吃，也什么植物不能吃。所以很多人常常吃不安全的植物，吃不安全的植物对你的身体有坏处，很麻烦的情况。但是一个人，神农，要解决这个问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很不知道：不知道，The adverb 很 cannot modify the mental-state verb 不知道 in this way.
- 也什么植物不能吃：什么植物不能吃，When contrasting two embedded categories, use parallel clauses; 也 cannot directly introduce this noun clause.
- 很麻烦的情况：是一件很麻烦的事，The original is only a noun phrase and cannot finish the sentence after the statement about harm; add a predicate with 是.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不安全 -- “吃不安全的植物”：有毒的，specifies that the plants are dangerous because they contain poison, which fits the health effect described here
- 有坏处 -- “对你的身体有坏处”：有害，states more directly that something harms the body

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 你的身体：他们的身体，The previous sentence names 很多人, so the pronoun should refer back to that group rather than unexpectedly address the reader.
- Coherence (Reference): 一个人，神农：有一个人叫神农，他，A character’s first mention should introduce the person clearly with a full noun phrase and name.
- Expressiveness: After “吃不安全的植物”, add a concrete result such as “有的人吃了以后肚子疼，有的人还生病了。”
- Expressiveness: At the moment 神农 appears, show his determination with a thought or quoted speech, such as “神农想：‘我一定要找出能吃的植物。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish the central problem: people do not know which plants are edible, and this gives 神农 a meaningful reason to act. You also use the cause-and-effect connector 所以 effectively to link the lack of knowledge to people’s actions.

## Scene 2

> 他又聪明又努力，旅行很远找到很多植物。比方说，灵芝和夹竹桃。他一吃各种各样的植物就知道能不能吃这些植物。然后，神农写他的主意。他喜欢植物的味道，特别是花。神农遇到了一个黄色的花，一吃就觉得不舒服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 旅行很远：到很远的地方旅行，A destination should be expressed with 到 + place; 很远 describes the place rather than directly modifying 旅行 in this sentence.
- 他的主意：自己的想法，Because the ideas belong to the subject 神农, use 自己的 to make the possessor clear; 想法 is the natural noun for ideas that someone puts into writing.
- 一个黄色的花：一朵黄色的花，朵 is the matching measure word for flowers, not 个.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 努力 -- “他又聪明又努力”：勤奋，emphasizes steady hard work and persistence.
- 各种各样的 -- “他一吃各种各样的植物”：不同种类的，makes clear that the plants belong to different types.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 然后，神农：然后，他，Use a pronoun when the same character was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other person competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Temporality): 找到：找到了，This is a completed discovery in the past narrative, so the final action in the series takes 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 写：写下了，This writing happens as a completed next event after 然后, so use 写下了; 写下 also specifically means recording something in writing.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：他，Use a pronoun for the same character because the previous sentence already clearly refers to him.
- Expressiveness: At the point where he sees the flower, add a visual detail about its shape and appearance: “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: After he eats the flower, show a specific physical feeling instead of only saying 不舒服: “他一吃，肚子就疼了起来，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Add a brief worried thought after the pain to show his fear: “我会死吗？”

### Encouragement
You clearly show that 神农 is curious and hardworking, and the sentence with 一……就…… effectively shows how he tests plants and learns from them. You also used examples such as 灵芝 and 夹竹桃 to make the scene more concrete.

## Scene 3

> 发现了夹竹桃有毒。别人看到他，很快给他吃一个灵芝。神农一吃就觉得比较好。然后，他继续写他发现的信息。会告诉他的朋友哪一个植物能不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别人：一个人，别人 means “other people” in contrast with the speaker or a stated group; here a new, unspecified person is being introduced.
- 看到他：看到了他，this is a completed action in the past, so the verb needs 了.
- 给他吃一个灵芝：给了他一个灵芝吃，the giving is completed, so 给 needs 了; this wording also makes clear that the lingzhi was for him to eat.
- 写他发现的信息：记下他发现的信息，in this context, the character is recording discoveries, so 记下 emphasizes writing information down for later reference.
- 哪一个植物能不能吃：哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃，哪一个 asks for a choice, while 能不能 is a yes-or-no question; use parallel phrases to explain which plants are edible and which are not.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 比较好 -- “神农一吃就觉得比较好”：舒服一些，specifies that his physical discomfort became less severe；好一些，gives a clearer comparison with how he felt before eating the medicine.
- 很快 -- “很快给他吃一个灵芝”：马上，emphasizes that the helper acted immediately after seeing him.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the helper appears, add one visual detail to make the new character easier to imagine, such as “一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人跑过来。”
- Expressiveness: At “神农一吃就觉得比较好,” show the change in his body, such as “他的肚子慢慢不疼了。”
- Expressiveness: When he first feels ill, add an internal thought to show his fear, such as “神农想：我会不会死？”

### Encouragement
- You clearly showed the important sequence: discovering the danger, receiving medicine, feeling better, and recording the information.
- Your use of “一吃就” effectively shows that the medicine worked immediately.
