## Scene 1

> 古代，孟子跟孟母住在墓地的旁边。可是孟母注意，住在墓地的旁边影响孟子。他常常跟朋友们祭拜玩，所以孟母决定他们会搬家。这次，他们住在城市里的商店的旁边，从早到晚很吵。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代：在古代，A historical time expression should use the preposition before the time phrase.
- 孟母注意：孟母注意到，When noticing a fact expressed by a following clause, use the result complement 到 to show that the fact was noticed.
- 跟朋友们祭拜玩：跟朋友们一起学着祭拜，The verb 祭拜 cannot be directly followed by 玩 to mean “play at doing something”; 学着 + verb clearly expresses imitating an action.
- 决定他们会搬家：决定搬家，决定 should be followed directly by a verb phrase; the subject of the moving action is understood from the context.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 朋友们 -- “他常常跟朋友们祭拜玩”：小伙伴们，specifies that these are the child’s peers and fits the story about a young boy.
- 吵 -- “从早到晚很吵”：嘈杂，describes the many mixed sounds of a busy commercial area more vividly.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery setting, add one sensory detail about the mourners to make the place feel real, for example: “孟子每天都看见有人穿着黑衣服，在墓地里哭。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where Mengzi imitates the rituals, add his playful feeling or action, for example: “他觉得很好玩，一边笑一边学着大人祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: Before the decision to move, add Mengmu’s inner thought or quoted speech, for example: “孟母想：‘这里会影响孩子，我不能再住在这儿了。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly establish the first home, Mengzi’s habit, Mengmu’s response, and the new environment in a logical order. The time phrases and routine words also help the reader understand that these events happen repeatedly.

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 有点儿危险 -- “城市也有点儿危险”：不太安全，describes the city more specifically in terms of safety while keeping the mild meaning.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子：他，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the preceding sentence has already made the referent clear.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 不是……而是：不……，因为，The sentence gives a reason for the place being unsuitable, rather than correcting one statement with another; use a cause connector instead of the paired contrast structure.
- Expressiveness: After “城市也有点儿危险,” add one concrete detail about the busy environment, such as “街上人来人往，车子和叫卖声让人觉得很不安全。”
- Expressiveness: At “他老是看起来不高兴,” show the boy’s feelings through an action, such as “他常常一个人坐在窗边，看着外面的商店。”
- Expressiveness: Add the mother’s inner thought after the final sentence to make her concern vivid, such as “孟母心想：‘这儿真的不适合孩子成长。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect the unsafe setting with the mother’s concern and the boy’s loneliness. The use of “难怪” also helps show a logical relationship between having no friends and feeling unhappy.

## Scene 3

> 所以，孟母和孟子搬到学校的旁边住。才五岁孟子开始学哲学。在学校，孟子不是看书，就是练习写字。将来，他成为中国的一个非常有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 才五岁孟子开始学哲学：孟子才五岁就开始学哲学，Use the paired pattern with a small age or amount: 才 + number + 就 + verb.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 在学校，孟子不是看书，就是练习写字：在学校，他不是看书，就是练习写字，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the previous sentence has already named the same character and no other character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬到学校的旁边住：搬到了学校的旁边住，The move is a completed event in the narrative, so the verb needs 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 将来：后来，Use 后来 to sequence a later event in a past narrative; 将来 refers to the future.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为：成为了，Becoming a philosopher is presented as a completed change of state, so use 了.
- Expressiveness: At the school scene, add a small detail showing how seriously he studies, such as: “孟子每天认真地看书，安静地练习写字。”
- Expressiveness: After the move, add Meng Mu’s positive feeling about the new environment, such as: “孟母很高兴，觉得学校旁边很适合孟子学习。”

### Encouragement
You used the contrast pattern “不是……就是……” correctly to describe Mengzi’s school routine. The scene also clearly shows his development from a young child into a respected philosopher.
