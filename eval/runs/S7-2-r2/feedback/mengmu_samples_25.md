## Scene 1

> 这是古代的故事。孟子小的时候，他的爸爸很早就去世了。从他的家到墓地很近，所以他每天老是从早到晚去墓地跟朋友们玩玩儿。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- Nothing to flag.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很早 -- “他的爸爸很早就去世了”：在孟子年幼时，specifies that his father died when Mengzi was still a young child.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子小的时候：有一个小男孩叫孟子。他小的时候，On a character’s first mention, introduce the character with a full noun phrase that gives both the name and role; after that, a pronoun is natural. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Expressiveness: At the cemetery scene, add a detail about the visitors to make the setting clearer, for example: “孟子每天看到很多人来墓地祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: Add Mengzi’s reaction to explain why he keeps going there, for example: “他觉得他们祭拜的样子很有趣。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish Mengzi’s early family situation and connect the nearby cemetery to his daily actions with “所以.” The time phrases “每天” and “从早到晚” also make his routine easy to imagine.

## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈觉得在墓地玩儿，害得，所以他们搬家。搬家对孟子有好的影响，他才五岁，可是每天不是写字就是帮妈妈的工作。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 害得：会带来麻烦，害得 must be followed by the person affected and a harmful result; here the sentence instead needs a verb phrase stating the possible problem. [teacher rule: haide_structure]
- 帮妈妈的工作：帮妈妈工作，after 帮 + person, use a verb to state the help given. The 的 incorrectly makes 妈妈 modify 工作 and leaves no action after 帮. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好的 -- “搬家对孟子有好的影响”：积极的，specifies that the influence is beneficial rather than merely “good”；有益的，emphasizes that the move helps孟子.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 在墓地玩儿：孟子在墓地玩儿，because the current subject is his mother, the omitted player could be understood as her; repeat孟子’s name to identify who is playing. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们搬家：他们搬家了，this move is a completed event in the story, so the final verb needs 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: At the point where his mother decides to move, add her feeling or thought: “孟母很担心，心想：‘这里不是孩子该玩的地方。’”
- Expressiveness: Make孟子’s learning action more visual after the move: “他认真地一笔一画地写字。”

### Encouragement
You used a clear cause-and-effect connector with 所以, and the contrast in 才五岁，可是…… helps show that孟子 is very young but already doing grown-up activities.

## Scene 3

> 他很聪明的人，难怪他成为有名的人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他很聪明的人：他是一个很聪明的人，A noun predicate needs 是, and the adjective phrase 很聪明 must take 的 before the noun 人. [rule: attributive_modifier_de]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为：成为了，Becoming a well-known person is a completed past change, so use 了 after the verb. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他很聪明的人，难怪他成为有名的人：难怪他成为了有名的人，原来他很聪明，Use 难怪……，原来…… to present the surprising result first and then reveal its reason. [teacher rule: nanguai_yuanlai]
- Expressiveness: At the ending, specify his achievement rather than only calling him a famous person, for example: “孟子后来成为了有名的哲学家。”
- Expressiveness: Add a closing comment from other people to make the conclusion sound more story-like, for example: “人们都说，孟子的成功离不开妈妈的三次搬家。”

### Encouragement
- You chose 很聪明 and 难怪 well to show the connection between Mengzi’s ability and his later success.

## End of story

### Overall Encouragement
