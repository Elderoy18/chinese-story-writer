## Scene 1

> 古代的时候有一个人叫神农。他需要当上医，一看别的人就想帮助他们。但是，他有一个问题，他不知道什么东西能做药。神农听说一个远远的地方有一种植物，什么病人吃都会觉得身体更好。神农三个月走去，找到有植物的地方，他看到一个很漂亮的植物，又香又好看。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，Both parts mean “in ancient times,” so using them together is redundant.
- 当上医：当医生，医 is not normally used alone as the occupation after 当; 医生 is the standard noun for a doctor.
- 一看别的人：一看到别人，看到 means “see,” while 看 means “look.” Also, 别人 is appropriate when referring to other people generally; 别的人 requires a specific comparison group.
- 一个远远的地方：一个很远的地方，远远地 is normally an adverb describing an action from afar, while 很远的 describes the distance of a place.
- 什么病人吃都会觉得身体更好：不管什么病人，吃了这种植物都会觉得身体更好，什么病人 needs 不管 to mean “any kind of patient,” and 了 marks the completed eating before the resulting feeling.
- 神农三个月走去：神农走了三个月，Duration follows the verb, and 了 marks the completed period of walking; 走去 does not work here to express “walked for three months.”
- 有植物的地方：有这种植物的地方，The known plant has just been introduced, so 这种植物 identifies the specific plant rather than referring vaguely to any plant.
- 一个很漂亮的植物：一株很漂亮的植物，株 is the matching measure word for an individual plant.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 帮助 -- “一看到别人就想帮助他们”：医治，specifies that he wants to treat sick people, fitting his goal of becoming a doctor；救治，emphasizes providing medical help to people who are ill.
- 很漂亮 -- “一个很漂亮的植物”：艳丽，gives a more vivid visual impression of a striking flower or plant；引人注目，shows that its appearance immediately catches Shennong’s attention.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他有一个问题，他不知道：他有一个问题：不知道，After the subject has been named in the same sentence, use zero form rather than repeating the pronoun.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 神农听说一个远远的地方有一种植物……。神农三个月走去：神农听说一个很远的地方有一种植物……，于是走了三个月，The connector 于是 shows the cause-and-effect link: hearing about the plant leads him to travel there.
- Expressiveness: At the point where Shennong sees the plant, add a concrete visual detail: “那种植物开着黄色的花，像星星一样。”
- Expressiveness: After he hears about the plant, add his determined thought: “神农想：我一定要去看看这种植物。”
- Expressiveness: When describing the plant’s scent, make the reaction more personal: “香味飘过来，神农觉得很奇怪。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduce Shennong and explain both his wish to help people and the problem he needs to solve.
- The final description uses sensory detail with “又香又好看,” which makes the plant feel interesting and important.

## Scene 2

> 他觉得，这种一定是做药的花。原来他想错了。吃了以后，神农快生病。 这时一个穿着蓝色衣服的人帮神农，快给他一种红的灵芝。神农吃了以后，他没有病，想起来以前一个人告诉他，灵芝对身体有好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- Nothing to flag.

### Vocabulary Suggestions

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 快生病：很快生病了，Here the illness happens after the completed eating action, so the completed change of state needs 了; 很快 means “soon/quickly,” while 快生病了 usually means “is about to become ill.”
- Coherence (Temporality): 快给：赶快给了，赶快 describes the blue-clothed person hurrying to act, and the completed giving action needs 了.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农吃了以后，他没有病：神农吃了以后，没有病，Use zero form after the same character has already been named in the same sentence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 想起来以前一个人告诉他：想起了以前有一个人告诉过他，The remembering is a completed action in this event sequence, so it needs 了; 告诉过 marks that the telling happened earlier.
- Expressiveness: When the illness begins, add a physical reaction to show how serious it feels, such as: “神农的肚子突然疼了起来，疼得站不稳。”
- Expressiveness: At the blue-clothed person’s arrival, describe the person’s urgent movement, such as: “他赶快跑过来，从地上拿起灵芝。”
- Expressiveness: Add Shennong’s relieved inner feeling after taking the herb, such as: “神农觉得肚子舒服多了，心里很惊讶。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show a logical sequence: Shennong makes a mistaken judgment, becomes ill, receives help, and then remembers what he learned about lingzhi. The use of “原来” effectively shows that he realizes his earlier thought was wrong.

## Scene 3

> 啊，他怎么忘记了？他把灵芝放在包里。一回家就告诉别人。他找到药以后他写下怎么做药在书上。所以人们都知道，那红灵芝对身体好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别人：人们，Here the people receiving the information are the general public, not “other people” contrasted with a particular person; use 人们.
- 写下怎么做药在书上：把怎么做药的方法写在了书上，A 把-construction places the thing being written before the verb, and the location follows the verb; 方法 makes the content being recorded a clear noun phrase.
- 那红灵芝：那种红灵芝，A demonstrative such as 那 needs a measure word before the noun; 种 fits when referring to a type of plant.
- 对身体好处：对身体有好处，The phrase requires 有 between the body-benefit phrase and 好处.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 药 -- “他找到药以后”：药材，identifies it more exactly as a natural material used as medicine rather than finished medicine

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 放在包里：放在了包里，This is a completed action in the narrative, so use 了 after the verb and before the location phrase.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉：告诉了，Returning home and informing people is a completed narrative action, so it needs 了.
- Expressiveness: After he remembers the plant’s value, add his relieved thought: “啊，我想起来了！灵芝对身体有好处。”
- Expressiveness: When he records the discovery, add a concrete detail about what he writes: “他把灵芝的样子和用处写在书上。”
- Expressiveness: Show his careful action with the plant: “他小心地把灵芝放进包里。”

### Encouragement
- You connect the events clearly with the sequence patterns 一……就…… and ……以后, and the final 所以 gives the scene a clear result.
- You also keep the focus on the same important object, 灵芝, throughout the scene.
