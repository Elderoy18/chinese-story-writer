## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子。他们住在墓地的旁边，所以孟子老是祭拜。孟子不是祭拜，就是跟朋友聊天。他很喜欢他的城市，但是城市有才两个学校，都很小。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，Both expressions refer to a time period, so using them together is redundant.
- 有才两个学校：才有两个学校，才 must come before 有 to show that the number is small or limited.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 聊天 -- “跟朋友聊天”：闲聊，specifies relaxed, casual conversation between friends.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子：一个叫孟子的男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国，Introduce the character’s name together with who he is at his first mention.
- Coherence (Reference): 他的城市：自己的城市，When the subject and the possessor are the same person, this form makes the relationship clear.
- Coherence (Reference): 但是城市：但是那里，After the city has just been mentioned, use a pronoun for the close re-occurrence rather than repeating the noun.
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery is introduced, add a concrete detail about what Mengzi sees there, such as “孟子每天看见很多人来墓地祭拜，有的人还在哭。”
- Expressiveness: Explain Mengzi’s feeling about worshipping to make his action more understandable, such as “孟子觉得祭拜很有趣。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech from a visitor at the cemetery, such as “一个人说：‘不要在这里玩！’”

### Encouragement
You used short, clear sentences, and the pattern “不是……就是……” effectively shows Mengzi’s repeated activities. The contrast about the city and its schools also gives the scene an interesting direction.

## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈不是不喜欢他们的城市，而是想孟子有很好的教育。他们搬家到有很多学校的城市。他们从早到晚走路，孟子一直抱怨。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 想孟子有很好的教育：想让孟子受到很好的教育，The verb 想 needs a following verb phrase or a clause; use 让 to show that the mother wants this result for Mengzi, and 受到教育 is the natural verb-object combination.
- 搬家：搬，Before 到 + destination, use 搬到 + place rather than 搬家到 + place.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 走路 -- “他们从早到晚走路”：赶路，emphasizes walking as part of a journey toward the new city.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 到有很多学校的城市：到了有很多学校的城市，The move is presented as a completed event in the narrative, so 到 takes 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他们从早到晚走路，孟子一直抱怨：因为他们从早到晚走路，所以孟子一直抱怨，Use 因为……所以…… to show that the long walk caused Mengzi’s complaints.
- Expressiveness: At the point where Mengzi complains, add a short line of speech to show his frustration, such as: “妈妈，我们还要走多久？”
- Expressiveness: After the long walk, add one physical detail to make the journey vivid, such as: “孟子走得脚都酸了。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly explain the mother’s motivation with a contrast structure, and “从早到晚” effectively shows that the journey felt long and tiring.

## Scene 3

> 他难怪抱怨，他想他的朋友，因为他不要离开他的城市。他的妈妈找到孟子一个老师。跟老师孟子学最重要的文学，所以他成为哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不要离开：不想离开，Use 不想 to express that a character does not want to do something; 不要 is more often used for a prohibition, command, or refusal.
- 找到孟子一个老师：给孟子找到了一个老师，Put the recipient after 给 and before the verb. The completed result uses 了 after the resultative complement 到.
- 跟老师孟子学：孟子跟老师学，Place the subject before the phrase that states whom he learns from.
- 成为哲学家：成为了哲学家，This is a completed change of identity in the past narrative, so add 了 after the verb.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他的朋友：自己的朋友，Because the friends belong to the same person as the subject, 自己的 makes that relationship clear.
- Coherence (Reference): 他的城市：自己的城市，Because the city belongs to the same person as the subject, 自己的 clearly refers back to him.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他难怪抱怨：难怪他抱怨，Put 难怪 before the clause it explains, and use 原来 to introduce the reason that follows.
- Expressiveness: At the opening, add a physical sign of Mengzi’s unhappiness, such as: “孟子皱着眉头，一直叹气。”
- Expressiveness: You could show his feelings through quoted speech after he thinks of his friends, such as: “他说：‘我不想离开我的朋友。’”
- Expressiveness: When his mother finds a teacher, add her hope for him, such as: “妈妈希望孟子能好好学习，将来成为一个聪明的人。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect Mengzi’s unhappiness with leaving his friends and city, which gives his complaint a meaningful reason.
- You also show an important change from finding a teacher to Mengzi eventually becoming a philosopher.
