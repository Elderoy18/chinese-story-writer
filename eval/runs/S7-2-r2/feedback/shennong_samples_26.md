## Scene 1

> 很久以前，有一个老男人叫神农。神农在古代每一天看到了别的小镇人生病还是不舒服，所以他决定开始找新的药。他下一天开始爬当地的山脉，找了各种各样的植物。神农试一试每个植物，知道有什么好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他下一天开始：第二天，他开始，Use the standard narrative time expression for the day after the previous event; the time phrase should come before the subject and action. [rule: time_expression]
- 爬当地的山脉：爬当地的山，The verb phrase for climbing mountains is used with individual mountains; a mountain range is more naturally crossed or traversed rather than climbed in this context. [rule: none]
- 找了各种各样的植物：找到了各种各样的植物，The verb means “look for”; when he successfully finds the plants, use the result complement. [vocab list]
- 每个植物：每种植物，Use the matching measure word for types of plants. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不舒服 -- “别的小镇人生病还是不舒服”：身体不适，specifies that the discomfort is physical and fits the illness context.
- 好处 -- “知道有什么好处”：作用，more precisely describes what effect each plant has.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农试一试每个植物：他试一试每个植物，Use a pronoun because the same character was mentioned in the immediately preceding sentence and no other character competes as the referent. [rule: none]
- Coherence (Temporality): 每一天看到了：每天看到，A routine action takes the zero particle rather than completed-action 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Conjunction): 生病还是不舒服：生病或者感到不舒服，Use the declarative alternative connector here; 还是 is normally used for a question or an indirect question. [rule: none]
- Expressiveness: When describing the sick people, add a concrete physical detail, such as “有的人肚子疼，有的人全身没有力气”.
- Expressiveness: After his decision, show his sense of responsibility with an inner thought, such as “神农想：我一定要找到能帮大家的药。”
- Expressiveness: While he is climbing, add a simple setting detail, such as “山上空气很新鲜，路却又长又难走。”

### Encouragement
You clearly introduce 神农 at the beginning, and the cause-and-effect link with 所以 makes his decision easy to understand. You also use varied plant-related vocabulary, such as 山脉 and 各种各样的植物, to show the scope of his search.

## Scene 2

> 比方说，他一次试一试有些植物的叶，吃得又新鲜又甜。煮这些叶以后，他一喝就知道他发现新的茶叶。虽然他找到了很多的新的植物，但是他的旅程有的时候特别是危险。比方说，他一吃灵芝就开始生病。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 试一试：尝了，The reduplicated form is for a light, tentative plan or request; this sentence describes an action that was carried out, so use a completed verb. [rule: verb_reduplication]
- 吃得又新鲜又甜：这些叶子又新鲜又甜，The pattern with 得 describes the manner or degree of eating, but the adjectives describe the leaves’ quality and taste, so they should be predicates for the leaves. [rule: none]
- 特别是危险：特别危险，Here 特别 is a degree adverb modifying the adjective 危险; 特别是 introduces an item that is especially being discussed. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 新的 -- “很多的新的植物”：陌生的，emphasizes that these were plants he did not know before；未尝过的，makes clear that he had not tasted these plants before.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 煮这些叶以后：煮了这些叶以后，The boiling is completed before the later action of drinking, so it needs 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 发现新的茶叶：发现了新的茶叶，The discovery happens as a completed result after he drinks the leaves, so use 了 after the verb. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: After the tea is boiled, add a sensory detail about its smell or appearance, such as “水里飘出一股清香。”
- Expressiveness: At the moment he becomes ill, show his physical feeling and thought, such as “他捂着肚子，心想：我会不会吃错了？”

### Encouragement
You used clear time and contrast connections, especially with “煮这些叶以后” and “虽然……但是……”. Your scene also shows Shennong experimenting with plants, which gives the events a logical sequence.

## Scene 3

> 一个爬山的男人正好看到神农的麻烦，然后给了他一些药。神农最终他的小镇回到，跟别的人分享他的发现。你认为神农的故事真的吗？这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 小镇回到：回到小镇，The destination should follow the verb phrase “回到”; the original reverses this order. [rule: none]
- 别的人：小镇上的人们，“别人” distinguishes other people from a speaker or a stated person; here, the people in the town should be identified specifically. [teacher rule: bieren]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 麻烦 -- “看到神农的麻烦”：困境，specifies that Shen Nong is in a difficult situation rather than referring generally to trouble.
- 药 -- “给了他一些药”：灵芝，names the particular medicinal plant and makes the detail more specific in this story.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他的小镇：自己的小镇，When the possessor is the same person as the subject, “自己的” clearly shows that the town belongs to Shen Nong. [teacher rule: ziji_possessor]
- Coherence (Reference): 他的发现：自己的发现，Use “自己的” to make clear that the discoveries are Shen Nong’s own discoveries. [teacher rule: ziji_possessor]
- Coherence (Temporality): 分享他的发现：分享了自己的发现，This is the final completed action in the action sequence, so it needs “了.” [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处：这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处了，The final “了” is required in the fixed structure “这就要看……了”; it does not mark completion here. [teacher rule: zhe_jiu_yao_kan_le]
- Expressiveness: At the rescue moment, add a physical detail that shows Shen Nong’s condition, such as “神农疼得满头大汗，躺在地上动不了。”
- Expressiveness: After the man gives the medicine, add a short line of speech to make the help feel immediate: “快吃这个，也许能帮你！”
- Expressiveness: When Shen Nong returns, add his feeling about sharing what he learned: “神农很高兴，因为他终于能帮助小镇上的人们了。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the rescue, Shen Nong’s return, and the lesson of the story in a short scene. The final question also invites the reader to think about the story’s message.

## End of story

### Content & Completeness
- At the beginning, explain that people became sick because they did not know which plants were safe to eat; this gives a clearer reason for 神农’s journey.
- When 神农 decides to climb the mountains, show that he wants to learn which plants people can eat safely and which ones are dangerous, not only to find new medicine.
- After discovering 茶, add that 神农 writes it down in his record.
- The dangerous plant should be 夹竹桃: 神农 eats it and faints. In your retelling, he becomes sick after eating 灵芝 instead.
- After 神农 faints, include the person in blue who gives him 灵芝, and show that 神农 feels better after eating it.
- Before the ending, add the person in brown who comes and talks with 神农.
- After he recovers, show 神农 recording both 灵芝 and 夹竹桃.

### Overall Encouragement
- You gave 神农 a clear journey in the mountains and included his discovery of 茶. You also showed that his plant-testing journey could be dangerous, which is an important part of the story.
