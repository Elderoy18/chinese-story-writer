## Scene 1

> 古代的时候人觉得祭拜非常重要，孟子很小的时候，从早到晚一直祭拜，因为他妈妈告诉他祭拜很重要。虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜。他的同学们老是对他开玩笑了。孟子妈妈看了他的同学们对孟子不好的影响，所以孟子和孟子妈妈搬家了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- `古代的时候`：`在古代`，This phrase is redundant because `古代` already expresses a time period. [vocab list]
- `人觉得`：`人们觉得`，Use `人们` for a concrete collective group of people rather than the generic noun `人`. [teacher rule: ren_renmen]
- `看了他的同学们对孟子不好的影响`：`看到了他的同学们对孟子不好的影响`，`看` means “look,” while `看到` means “see” or “notice”; here the mother notices an effect. [vocab list]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- `重要` -- `祭拜非常重要`：`意义重大`，emphasizes that the ritual has substantial significance in that society；`十分重要`，keeps the same strength while varying the degree expression.
- `不好` -- `他的同学们对孟子不好的影响`：`负面`，specifies that the influence is harmful; use it in `他的同学们对孟子的负面影响`.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): `，孟子很小的时候`：`。有一个叫孟子的小男孩。孟子很小的时候`，On the first mention, introduce the character as a child with his name before continuing the narration. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Reference): `孟子和孟子妈妈`：`他们`，Both people were named in the immediately preceding clause, so a pronoun naturally refers back to them without repeating their names. [rule: none]
- Coherence (Temporality): `老是对他开玩笑了`：`老是对他开玩笑`，`老是` describes a repeated habit, so the verb takes the zero particle rather than completed-action `了`. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Conjunction): `虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜`：`他没有朋友，因为他每天只祭拜`，`虽然` needs a contrasting result clause, but this sentence gives a cause; use `因为` to show the cause-and-effect relation. [rule: coherence_connectives]
- Expressiveness: After `从早到晚一直祭拜`, add a small action detail to show what the ritual looks like, such as `他学着大人的样子，低着头祭拜。`
- Expressiveness: When the classmates tease him, show Mengzi’s feeling, such as `孟子觉得很难过，不知道自己做错了什么。`
- Expressiveness: At the moment his mother notices the influence, add her inner thought or speech, such as `孟子妈妈很担心，心想：“不能让他再这样下去了。”`

### Encouragement
- You clearly show that ritual is important to Mengzi and connect his mother’s decision to move with what she notices at school. The phrase `从早到晚一直祭拜` also gives the reader a strong sense of his daily routine.

## Scene 2

> 孟子才十一岁，成为一个哲学家。孟子不是祭拜，而是看政治文章，难怪他成为了哲学家。不过，看过很多政治文章对他害得，他有很多问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 对他害得，他有很多问题：害得他有很多问题，害得 introduces the person affected directly, followed by the harmful result; 对 cannot be placed before it. [teacher rule: haide_structure]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子不是祭拜：他不是祭拜，use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the name was just given and no other character competes as the referent. [rule: none]
- Coherence (Temporality): 孟子才十一岁，成为一个哲学家：孟子才十一岁，就成为了一个哲学家，this pattern needs 就 after 才, and 了 marks that becoming a philosopher was completed. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: After the opening, add a small action that shows his serious interest in reading, such as “他每天认真地阅读政治文章。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where he has many questions, show one specific thought in quoted speech, such as “这些文章说得一样吗？”

### Encouragement
You used a clear contrast between two activities with 不是……而是……, and the age detail makes the opening direct and easy to understand.

## Scene 3

> 他一直问问题，但是不能解决那些问题。孟子长大，他开始能解决问题。他常常建议你不是学金融，而是学政治，因为如果你懂政治，你会成为高薪。后来孟子成为教授，他教学生很厉害。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不是学金融，而是学政治：不要学金融，而要学政治，After 建议, use 不要……而要…… to state the action he advises against and the action he recommends; 不是……而是…… is for refuting or correcting a proposition. [teacher rule: bushi_ershi]
- 成为高薪：拿到高薪， 高薪 refers to high pay and cannot directly follow 成为; 拿到高薪 expresses earning a high salary. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 厉害 -- “他教学生很厉害”：很擅长教学生，states more precisely that he has strong teaching ability；很有教学方法，adds that his teaching is effective because he knows how to teach students well

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子长大：他长大，孟子 was named in the immediately previous sentence and no other character competes as the referent, so a pronoun is more natural for close re-occurrence. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子成为教授：他成为教授，The same character was just referred to as 他, so repeating the name is unnecessary in this close context. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Reference): 你：人们，The pronoun has no introduced referent in this narrative; use a full generic noun when referring to people in general. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Temporality): 长大：长大了，Growing up is a completed change of state before he becomes able to solve problems, so it needs 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为教授：成为了教授，Becoming a professor is presented as a completed later event, so add 了 after the verb. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: At the point where he cannot solve the questions, add an inner feeling to show his effort, such as “他越想越着急。”
- Expressiveness: When he gives advice, add a short line of speech, such as “他对人们说：‘我觉得学政治很重要。’”
- Expressiveness: After he becomes a professor, show the students’ response, such as “学生们都认真地听他讲课。”

### Encouragement
- You use time words such as 后来 to move the scene forward clearly, and you show Mengzi’s development from having questions to teaching students.

## End of story

### Content & Completeness
- At the beginning, the story needs to say that 孟子 and 孟母 lived near a graveyard, and that 孟子 copied the people there by offering sacrifices every day.
- After 孟母 sees him copying sacrifices, the reason for moving should be that she worries this behavior will have a bad influence on him, not that his classmates are unkind to him.
- After leaving the graveyard, the story needs the move near a market, where 孟子 copies people buying and selling things every day.
- The story also needs the next move from the market to a place near a school, where 孟子 begins to study every day before he later becomes a famous philosopher.

### Overall Encouragement
- You included 孟子’s later interest in politics and showed that he grows up to become a teacher and philosopher. Your ending gives him a successful future, which is a good conclusion.
