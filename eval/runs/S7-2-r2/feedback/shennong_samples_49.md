## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有很多人不知道什么植物能吃，什么植物不能吃。有很多人吃植物以后就吃坏肚子了，因为他们吃了毒。那个时候，有一个老人叫神农，他什么植物都觉得好吃，不怕生病，不怕毒。他希望别人也可以吃好吃的植物。可是他知道，如果他们还怕吃毒，不会吃他喜欢的植物。

- 古代的时候：在古代，The original expression redundantly combines two time expressions; use the shorter time phrase. [vocab list]
- 吃了毒：吃了有毒的植物，毒 refers to poison itself, while this scene describes people eating plants that are poisonous. [vocab list]

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After “吃坏肚子了,” add a visible symptom to show how serious the illness is, such as “有的人肚子疼得直冒冷汗。”
- Expressiveness: After introducing 神农, add his determined thought to show his personality, such as “神农想：我一定要找出能吃的植物。”

## Scene 2

> 所以，他上山下山找植物，吃了很多样子的植物。他是第一人，找到了茶。他把所有的植物写下来了。他一直都吃了，他连毒都能吃。可是他还没吃了一个叫夹竹桃的植物。他找到了那个像星星的植物，觉得很漂亮，马上就吃下了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很多样子的植物：很多种植物，样子 describes appearance or shape, while 种 is the classifier used for different categories of plants. [rule: none]
- 他是第一人，找到了茶：他是第一个找到茶的人，第一人 usually means a leading or exceptional person; use 第一个……的人 to say who first completed an action. [rule: none]
- 把所有的植物写下来了：把所有植物的信息写下来了，写下来 records words or information, so the object should be information about the plants rather than the plants themselves. [rule: none]
- 一个叫夹竹桃的植物：一种叫夹竹桃的植物，种 is the appropriate classifier when referring to a named type of plant. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 一直都吃了：一直都吃，an action described as continuing or habitual with 一直都 uses the zero particle, not completed-action 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 还没吃了：还没吃，after 没, do not use completed-action 了; the negative form already shows that the action has not been completed. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: At the moment he sees the plant, add a more concrete visual detail: 那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花，又大又漂亮。
- Expressiveness: Before he eats it, add a brief thought to show his curiosity and impulsiveness: 他心想：“这种花真漂亮，我尝一尝吧！”

### Encouragement
- You make the action sequence easy to follow: he searches for plants, finds tea, records what he learns, and then encounters a new plant.
- Your use of pronouns is clear because 神农 was introduced in the earlier scene, and the completed actions with 找到了 and 吃下了 fit the narrative well.

## Scene 3

> 可是夹竹桃的毒很重，让他肚子很痛。他在地上躺着，觉得他快会死了。可是正好有别人找到他了，给了他一个像伞的植物，叫灵芝。他吃灵芝以后就觉得很好，不再生病了。他测量起来了，像星星的植物是毒，像伞的植物是药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 快会死了：快要死了，For an imminent event, use the pattern with 要; 会 expresses future possibility and does not fit naturally after 快 here. [rule: none]
- 别人：一个人，别人 distinguishes other people from a stated person or group, but no such contrast has been established here; this is a newly introduced helper. [teacher rule: bieren]
- 是毒：有毒，毒 is a noun, while 有毒 describes that a plant contains poison. [vocab list]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 毒 -- “夹竹桃的毒很重”：毒性很强，states the strength of the plant’s toxicity more precisely in this context.
- 很好 -- “他吃灵芝以后就觉得很好”：好多了，shows that his condition improved after eating the medicine.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他：自己，When the person who feels something and the person who may die are the same, use 自己 to make the self-reference clear. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃灵芝以后：吃了灵芝以后，The eating is a completed action before the later feeling of recovery, so it needs 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: At the moment he is lying down, add a physical reaction and an inner thought: “他捂着肚子，在地上躺着，心想：‘我会不会死？’”
- Expressiveness: Make the helper more vivid by adding appearance and urgency: “这时，一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人赶快走过来。”
- Expressiveness: Turn the final plant information into dialogue: “他说：‘像星星一样的植物有毒，不能吃；像伞一样的植物是药，可以吃。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the change from pain to recovery after the medicine, which gives this scene a strong sequence of events. You also use the durative form well when describing him lying on the ground.
