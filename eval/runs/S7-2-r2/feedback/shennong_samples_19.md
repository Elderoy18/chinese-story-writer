## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男人觉得植物很有意思。他叫神农。有的植物吃了以后他的肚子疼死了，可是有的吃了以后很舒服。他找很多植物。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，The original repeats the meaning of “time”: 古代 already names a time period, so adding 的时候 is redundant.
- 他找很多植物：他找到了很多植物，找 means “to look for,” while 找到 means “to find”; the result complement 到 shows that he successfully found the plants.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 有意思 -- “一个男人觉得植物很有意思”：对植物很好奇，more specifically shows that the plants make him want to learn more about them.
- 很舒服 -- “可是有的吃了以后很舒服”：身体很轻松，more precisely describes physical comfort after eating the plants.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一个男人觉得植物很有意思。他叫神农。：一个叫神农的男人觉得植物很有意思。，Introduce the character’s name and role together at first mention, so the reader knows immediately who the man is.
- Expressiveness: After “植物很有意思,” add a short thought to show his curiosity, such as：这些植物到底能不能吃呢？
- Expressiveness: After the stomachache, add a physical reaction to make the pain vivid, such as：他疼得躺在地上，满头大汗。
- Expressiveness: At “很舒服,” add a comparison showing the change in his body, such as：吃了以后，他觉得肚子不疼了，身体轻松多了。

### Encouragement
- You clearly show two different results from eating different plants, and the contrast with 可是 helps the reader understand the danger and discovery in this scene.

## Scene 2

> 凉山很大的山，这也找植物。一天，他找到黄色的花。他听说你跟黄色的花做茶很有用。比方说，你的头发疼死，这冰茶是药。他一喝茶就很不舒服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 凉山很大的山：凉山是一座很大的山，A noun phrase needs a verb such as 是 to state what something is, and 山 takes the measure word 座 in this meaning.
- 你跟黄色的花做茶很有用：用黄色的花做的茶很有用，Use 用 to show the material used for making something; 的 connects the action of making tea to the noun 茶.
- 你的头发疼死：头疼得要死，头发 means “hair” and cannot feel pain; use 头疼 for a headache and 得要死 as a degree complement.
- 这冰茶：这杯冰茶，A demonstrative must be followed by a measure word before a countable noun.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 黄色 -- “黄色的花”：金黄色，adds a brighter, richer shade of yellow and makes the flowers easier to picture.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: When the yellow flowers first appear, add one visual detail: “黄色的花像星星一样，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: After he feels ill, show his reaction with a thought or line of speech: “他的肚子疼了起来，心想：‘我会不会生病了？’”
- Expressiveness: Add a physical detail to make “很不舒服” more vivid: “他躺在地上，难受得不得了。”

### Encouragement
- You used the pattern “一……就……” correctly in “他一喝茶就很不舒服,” which clearly shows an immediate result.
- Your scene also introduces the yellow flowers before describing what happens after the tea is drunk, so the events are easy to follow.

## Scene 3

> 糟糕，这黄色的花是夹竹桃，特别是毒。正好在这儿，灵芝药的夹竹桃他吃完了，也写一个课文的植物。谢谢神农。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 这黄色的花：这朵黄色的花，A demonstrative before a countable noun needs a matching measure word.
- 特别是毒：特别有毒，Use 有毒 to describe that a plant is poisonous; 毒 alone cannot function as the adjective after 特别.
- 灵芝药的夹竹桃他吃完了：他吃完了灵芝这种药，The original string wrongly links two different plants with 的 and leaves the object relationship unclear; this version clearly states that he finished taking the medicine.
- 一个课文的植物：这些植物的用处，When recording information about plants, the useful information to write down is their uses; 课文 means a lesson text and does not fit this meaning.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 也写：也写下了，The writing is presented as a completed action, so it needs 了 after the resultative verb phrase.
- Expressiveness: After identifying the dangerous flower, add a visual detail to make it easier for the reader to imagine it, for example: “这朵花像星星一样，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: After he takes the medicine, show his physical feeling and worry before he improves, for example: “他捂着肚子，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Before the closing gratitude, add a short thought or line of speech to show his relief, for example: “神农说：‘谢谢你，这种药救了我！’”

### Encouragement
You clearly identify the dangerous flower at the opening, and the immediate alarm creates tension. The closing expression of gratitude also shows that the character appreciates the help he received.

- After 神农 recovers, add the person wearing brown who comes and talks with him.
- At the end, show 神农 recording both 灵芝 and 夹竹桃, so readers know he remembers which plant helps people and which plant is poisonous.

### Overall Encouragement
- You included 神农, the mountain, 茶, 夹竹桃, and 灵芝, so you have several important parts of the story. Your retelling also has a clear idea that some plants can help and some can hurt.
