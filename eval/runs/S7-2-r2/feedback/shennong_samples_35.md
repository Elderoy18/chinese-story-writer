## Scene 1

> 在古代，很多医生不知道怎么用药。所以他们找到了很多植物对药。神农先看到很多病人，他们吃很多植物对药。但是我觉得他们的吃的植物，让他们病人。神农觉得这个植物可能特别是毒。所以他想找一个植物对药，他们可以吃对药。正好，他找到了一把花，他想把花放在茶里，对药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很多植物对药：很多可以做药的植物，The phrase “对药” cannot describe what the plants are used for; “可以做药” clearly means that the plants can be used as medicine.
- 他们的吃的植物：他们吃的植物，A relative clause before a noun uses the verb phrase directly; the extra possessive marker after “他们” does not fit this structure.
- 特别是毒：特别有毒，“有毒” is the adjectival expression for “toxic”; bare “毒” cannot be used this way after “是” or modified by “特别.”
- 想找一个植物对药，他们可以吃对药：想找一种可以做药、人们可以吃的植物，“对药” cannot follow either “找” or “吃.” The two correct properties of the plant are that it can be medicine and that people can eat it.
- 把花放在茶里，对药：把花放在茶里做药，“做药” means to use something as medicine; “对药” does not form a verb phrase here.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农先看到很多病人：一个叫神农的人先看到很多病人，This is the character’s first mention, so introduce him with a full noun phrase rather than using only his name.
- Coherence (Temporality): 神农先看到很多病人：神农先看到了很多病人，This is a completed event in the narrative, so “看到” takes 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们吃很多植物：他们吃了很多植物，The eating happened before the plants made the patients ill, so it needs 了 to mark the completed action.
- Coherence (Temporality): 让他们病人：让他们生病了，After “让,” use a verb phrase to show the result; “生病了” marks the resulting change of state.
- Expressiveness: When introducing the sick people, add one concrete symptom to show their condition, such as: “很多人肚子疼得厉害。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where Shen Nong suspects the plant, add his worried thought, such as: “神农想：这种植物会不会有毒？”
- Expressiveness: Describe the flowers he finds with one visual detail, such as: “他看到黄色的花，像星星一样，又大又漂亮。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly set the story in the past and introduced the problem of people becoming ill after eating plants. You also used completed-action forms correctly in “找到了” and “找到了一把花,” which helps move the narrative forward.

## Scene 2

> 他不知道如果这把花对你的身体好，这就要看如果这把花毒不毒了。神农想找别的植物，但是这很麻烦。正好，他找到了别的花。他一吃花，很快就生病了。他的肚子疼死了。神农不知道他吃不吃灵芝或者夹竹桃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 如果这把花对……好：这把花对……好不好，An embedded yes/no question after 不知道 needs an A-not-A form, so use 好不好 rather than 如果.
- 要看如果这把花毒不毒：要看这把花毒不毒，In the pattern 这就要看……了, the part after 看 is directly a question form such as 毒不毒; do not add 如果.
- 吃不吃灵芝或者夹竹桃：吃的是灵芝还是夹竹桃，When the uncertainty is a choice between two things, use 是……还是…… rather than an A-not-A question followed by 或者.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 麻烦 -- “找别的植物，但是这很麻烦”：费劲，emphasizes that finding the plants requires a lot of effort；困难，emphasizes the obstacles in the task.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 你的身体：他的身体，The narration is about Shennong, not directly addressing the reader, so use a third-person possessive reference.
- Coherence (Reference): 他一吃花：他一吃这种花，Repeat the specific noun phrase to show that this is the flower he has just found; bare 花 sounds like flowers in general.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农不知道他吃的是灵芝还是夹竹桃：神农不知道吃的是灵芝还是夹竹桃，Within the same sentence, the subject is already 神农, so use zero form instead of repeating a pronoun for the same person.
- Expressiveness: After “他一吃花”, describe the flower so the reader can picture what attracted him, for example: “黄色的花像星星一样，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: After “他的肚子疼死了”, add his physical reaction and feeling, for example: “他躺在地上，难受得不得了，心想：‘我会死吗？’”

### Encouragement
- You used 找到了 correctly to show that the search was completed, and 一……就…… clearly connects eating the flower with becoming ill. Your sentence “他的肚子疼死了” is also vivid and strongly shows Shennong’s pain.

## Scene 3

> 别的人给了神农一个灵芝。这个植物可以让神农更好。比方说，他会觉得更健康。吃灵芝以后，他可以再走。现在他觉得更好。最后，神农找到了很多植物，他可以用对药。这些植物让神农很高兴。神农想跟很多人分享他的新药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别的人：别人，Use “别人” for other people in general when no specific group is being contrasted; “别的人” normally contrasts with an already mentioned group of people.
- 一个灵芝：一株灵芝，“株” is the suitable measure word for a whole medicinal fungus or plant; “个” is too general here.
- 很多植物，他可以用对药：很多可以用来做药的植物，“用对药” means using medicine correctly and cannot describe plants that can serve as medicine; “用来做药” states the plants’ purpose.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 更好 -- “这个植物可以让神农更好”：好一些，more specifically describes an improvement in his health；恢复得更好，highlights that he is recovering after being ill
- 走 -- “他可以再走”：继续赶路，makes clear that he is able to resume his journey
- 新药 -- “他的新药”：新发现的药材，more precisely refers to the medicinal plants Shennong has discovered

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 这个植物可以让神农更好：它可以让神农更好，Use a pronoun for an object mentioned in the immediately preceding sentence when no other possible referent competes with it.
- Coherence (Reference): 最后，神农找到了很多植物：最后，他找到了很多植物，Use a pronoun when the same character has just been mentioned and no other character has intervened.
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃灵芝以后：吃了灵芝以后，The eating is a completed action before the later ability to continue walking, so it needs completed-action “了.”
- Expressiveness: After “他会觉得更健康,” add a physical detail showing his recovery, for example: “神农的肚子慢慢不疼了，脸色也好多了。”
- Expressiveness: After “神农很高兴,” add his feeling or thought, for example: “他心想：我终于找到可以帮助病人的植物了！”
- Expressiveness: At the sharing point, add a short line of speech, for example: “神农对大家说：‘这些植物可以做药，可是有些植物不能吃。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the recovery sequence with “吃灵芝以后” and “现在,” which helps the reader follow the events. You also use “找到了” well to show that Shennong’s search has reached a result.
