## Scene 1

> 古代时候，人不知道他们能吃什么植物，常常一吃就死。但是不知道为什么，没有办法。所以有一天，神农出去试一试很多种植物，告诉人什么植物是安全什么是危险。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代时候：在古代，古代 already names a time period, so use the time preposition rather than adding 时候 after it.
- 人：人们，this refers to a concrete group of people in the story, so use 人们 rather than the generic noun 人.
- 试一试很多种植物：尝一尝各种植物，尝 is the verb for tasting plants or food; 试一试 is less suitable here because it usually means trying an action, method, or object out.
- 什么植物是安全什么是危险：什么植物是安全的，什么植物是危险的，when 安全 and 危险 describe a noun after 是, use 的, and repeat the noun phrase to make the two alternatives parallel.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很多种 -- “试一试很多种植物”：各种各样的，emphasizes that the plants differ in many forms and types；不同种类的，clearly highlights the different categories of plants.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：一个叫神农的人，at a character’s first mention, briefly introduce the name together with a full noun phrase so the reader knows who is entering the story.
- Coherence (Reference): 他们能吃什么植物：什么植物能吃，after 人们 has already been named and no other character competes as the referent, omit the repeated pronoun and state the general question directly.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉人：告诉了人们，this is a completed action in a past narrative event, so add 了 to mark completion.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 但是不知道为什么，没有办法：他们不知道为什么，也没有办法，these two ideas add information rather than contrast with each other, so use 也 instead of 但是.
- Expressiveness: After the deaths in the first sentence, add a physical consequence to make the danger more vivid, such as “有的人肚子疼得厉害，有的人甚至死了。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where the new character begins his work, add his determination through inner thought or speech, such as “神农想：‘我一定要找出能吃的植物。’”
- Expressiveness: Describe the setting where he searches for plants, such as “他走到高山上，看见了很多从来没见过的植物。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish the central problem: people do not know which plants are edible, and this gives the character a meaningful reason to act.
- Your use of “一吃就死” effectively shows the immediate danger in a short, powerful way.

## Scene 2

> 发现有的植物对身体非常好，特别是茶，让肚子感觉又暖和又清楚。他告诉朋友：“你吃哪种植物，这就要看你的口味。比方说，你爱吃甜的，可能喜欢糖。”他最喜欢吃花，除了夹竹桃以外，什么都花想吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 清楚：舒服，清楚 describes understanding or visual clarity, not a physical feeling in the stomach; 舒服 describes feeling physically comfortable.
- 什么都花想吃：什么花都想吃，when 什么 modifies the noun 花, the noun must come directly after 什么, and 都 follows the noun phrase.
- 这就要看你的口味：这就要看你的口味了，this fixed expression ends with 了; here it signals that the answer depends on the listener’s taste.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 非常好 -- “有的植物对身体非常好”：有益, specifies that the plants bring a health benefit.
- 吃 -- “他最喜欢吃花”：品尝, emphasizes trying the taste of different flowers.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After mentioning tea, add a simple action detail: “他把茶放在水里，茶水闻起来很香。”
- Expressiveness: At “他最喜欢吃花,” add his feeling or reason: “他觉得有些花甜甜的，味道很特别。”
- Expressiveness: You could add a short thought before he avoids oleander: “可是夹竹桃有毒，我不能吃。”

### Encouragement
You clearly connect plants to their effects on the body, and the quotation gives the scene a character’s voice. The phrase “除了夹竹桃以外” also shows that you can express an exception clearly.

## Scene 3

> 他发现这个口味，一百岁时候，不小心吃了毒的花。一吃就开始死。但是生病时候告诉人什么植物是药，什么植物是毒。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一百岁时候：一百岁的时候，The multi-syllable time expression needs 的 before 时候.
- 毒的花：有毒的花，毒 is a noun; use 有毒的 before a noun to describe something poisonous.
- 生病时候：生病的时候，The multi-syllable verb phrase needs 的 before 时候.
- 人：人们，This refers to a concrete group of people whom he tells, so 人们 is needed rather than the generic noun 人.
- 什么植物是毒：什么植物有毒，Use 有毒 to say that a plant is poisonous; 是毒 is not the correct predicate here.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 吃 -- “不小心吃了有毒的花”：误食了，states more precisely that he accidentally ate something dangerous.
- 告诉 -- “告诉了人们什么植物是药”：向人们说明了，emphasizes that he explained the information clearly to the group.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 这个口味：一种花的味道，This is the first mention of the taste, so a full noun phrase introduces it clearly instead of using 这个 without an established referent.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他发现：他发现了，This is a completed discovery in the narrative, so the verb needs 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉：告诉了，This telling is a completed action in the past narrative, so it needs 了; it is not a speech verb directly introducing quoted speech.
- Expressiveness: After “一吃就开始死”, add a physical feeling to show the danger, such as “他的肚子疼得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where he explains the plants, add a short line of speech, such as “这种花有毒，不能吃！”

### Encouragement
- You used the pattern “一……就……” effectively to show that the dangerous result happened immediately after eating the flower.
- You also clearly tried to contrast poisonous plants with medicinal plants, which is an important idea for this scene.
