## Scene 1

> 孟子小的时候，从早到晚都跟朋友玩。孟子和他的朋友喜欢玩在墓地里，装祭拜。孟子五岁的时候，跟妈妈搬家。他们搬家不是因为孟子玩得太多，而是因为妈妈认为他们的家离墓地太近。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 喜欢玩在墓地里：喜欢在墓地里玩，A location phrase should come before the action verb it modifies. [rule: none]
- 装祭拜：装作祭拜，装作 is used before a verb phrase to express pretending to do an action. [rule: none]
- 跟妈妈搬家：妈妈带着他搬家了，The mother is the person who takes the child when the family moves, so 带着 expresses the correct relationship; this single move is completed, so it also needs 了. [vocab list]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和他的朋友：他和朋友，Use a pronoun for a recently mentioned character when no other possible referent has appeared. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子五岁的时候：他五岁的时候，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence of the same character. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Expressiveness: At the description of the children’s game, add how they imitate the people at the cemetery, for example: “他们学着大人的样子，低着头祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: Add Mengzi’s feeling to show why he enjoys the game, for example: “孟子觉得这样很好玩，一边笑一边祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: After the mother notices the problem, add her worried thought, for example: “妈妈心想：‘住在这儿会影响孩子。’”

### Encouragement

## Scene 2

> 妈妈认为，住离墓地太近，害得孟子的心气。孟子和孟母搬到子思老师的家，跟子思老师住。孟母帮子思打扫房间和做饭。子思帮孟母教她的儿子。因为孟子现在没有朋友，他老是读书。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 住离墓地太近：住得离墓地太近，use 得 between the verb and its degree complement to show how close the home is to the cemetery. [rule: none]
- 害得孟子的心气：害得孟子心情不好，害得 must be followed by the affected person and a harmful result clause; the noun phrase “孟子的心气” does not state a result. [teacher rule: haide_structure]
- 老是读书：一直读书，老是 has a negative, complaining tone, while regular reading here is a neutral habit. [rule: laoshi_negative_connotation]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 做饭 -- “孟母帮子思打扫房间和做饭”：准备饭菜，specifies preparing the meal and fits naturally with helping in a home；下厨，adds the detail that she personally cooks the food.
- 没有朋友 -- “因为孟子现在没有朋友”：没有玩伴，more specifically shows that he has no companions to play with.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟母搬到子思老师的家：他们搬到子思老师的家，after both characters have just been mentioned and no other pair competes as the referent, use a pronoun for their close re-occurrence. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬到子思老师的家：搬到了子思老师的家，the move is a completed story event, so the verb needs 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为孟子现在没有朋友，他：因为孟子现在没有朋友，所以他，use 因为……所以…… to make the cause-and-effect relationship explicit. [rule: coherence_connectives]
- Expressiveness: After the first sentence, add Mengzi’s feeling about leaving the cemetery, such as “孟子觉得很舍不得朋友，也有一点难过。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of dialogue when Zisi agrees to help, such as “子思老师说：‘别担心，我会教孟子读书。’”
- Expressiveness: Describe the new study setting when Mengzi reads, such as “房间里很安静，桌子上放着很多书。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show how the adults help each other and how Mengzi’s new situation leads him to read more. The scene has a clear cause-and-effect idea and introduces the new teacher naturally.

## Scene 3

> 子思认为，对孩子来说，读书最重要，特别是学哲学。孟子不是练习写文章，就是看哲学书。孟子从五岁一直学哲学。他才五岁就学哲学，难怪他成为很有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 从五岁一直学哲学：从五岁开始一直学哲学，Use 开始 after 从 + time to mark the starting point of the continuing action. [teacher rule: cong_kaishi]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 看 -- “就是看哲学书”：研读，emphasizes reading philosophical books carefully and thoughtfully.

### Coherence & Expressiveness

### Encouragement
- You used both the 不是……就是…… pattern and the 才……就…… pattern effectively to show Mengzi’s study habits and his early start.
