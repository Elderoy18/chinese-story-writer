## Scene 1

> 有一天孟子去了墓地玩儿，但是他妈妈觉得这是不合适的地方让孩子玩，所以她决定了他们会搬家找更好的地方。她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福，而且住在这儿教育不太好。因为她望子成龙，所以她会一直搬家才能找到质量好的教育。然后他们搬到了离城市更近的地方。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不合适的地方让孩子玩：一个不适合孩子玩儿的地方，适合 needs to introduce who or what is suited; this structure says that the place is suitable or unsuitable for a child to play in.
- 他们会搬家找更好的地方：搬家，找更好的地方，after 决定, use the action phrase directly; do not repeat a new subject before the action.
- 她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福：她担心儿子的童年不会幸福，恐怕 means “I am afraid that” and cannot follow another subject in this way; 担心 expresses that she is worried about her son’s future.
- 她会一直搬家才能找到：她得一直搬家，才能找到，得 expresses the necessary condition before 才能; 会 only predicts a future action and does not express this requirement.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不太好 -- “住在这儿教育不太好”：不理想, shows more precisely that the education does not meet the mother’s hopes or needs.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子：一个叫孟子的小男孩，at a character’s first mention, briefly introduce the character with a full noun phrase rather than using only the name.
- Coherence (Temporality): 决定了：决定，决定 is a mental-state verb, so it normally uses the zero particle rather than 了.
- Expressiveness: At the cemetery, add a detail about what Mengzi notices, such as “墓地里有人在哭，孟子觉得很好奇。”
- Expressiveness: When the mother decides to move, add her thoughts or speech, such as “孟母心想：‘这儿不适合孩子，我得带他搬家。’”
- Expressiveness: After the move, describe the new area with one sensory detail, such as “城市附近很热闹，到处都有商人的叫卖声。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the mother’s reason for moving, and your use of 因为……所以…… effectively connects her hopes for her son with her actions.
- You also use completed actions such as 去了 and 搬到了 well to move the scene forward.

## Scene 2

> 他们住才一月就又搬家。古代的时候，一般人不喜欢卖东西的人。但是孟子老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿，害得妈妈着急得很，因为她恐怕儿子会成为跟他们很像。她告诉了孟子：他们不是朋友，而是不好的人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 already names a time period, so adding 的时候 is redundant.
- 老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿：老是跟这些卖东西的人玩儿，老是 and 常常 are both frequency adverbs, so using both together is redundant.
- 她恐怕：她担心，恐怕 means “I am afraid that” and cannot take another subject such as 她 in this structure.
- 会成为跟他们很像：会变得跟他们很像，成为 introduces a new identity or role, while 变得 is used for changing into an adjective state such as 很像.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不好 -- “而是不好的人”：品行不好的人，specifies that the people have poor behaviour or character rather than being “bad” in an unspecified way.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们住才一月就又搬家：他们才住了一个月，就又搬家了，use the pattern 才 + duration + 就 + verb; the completed period of living and the later move need 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉了孟子：告诉孟子，an introducing speaking verb takes the zero particle rather than 了.
- Expressiveness: At the mention of the sellers, add a sensory detail about the busy street, such as “街上到处都是叫卖声，热闹极了。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where the mother becomes worried, add her private thought to show her concern, such as “孟母心想：‘这样下去，孟子会不会也只想做买卖？’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the mother’s worry and give her a line of dialogue, which helps the scene feel more like a story. The contrast between ordinary people and Mengzi’s behaviour is also easy to understand.

## Scene 3

> 你不是当数学家，而是当哲学家，不能当卖东西的人。所以他们又搬家。在他们第三个家，孟子学了怎么祭拜。妈妈感觉这个地方适合他们，告诉了孟子：我们找到了最后的家。在这儿妈妈让儿子从早到晚学习，他难怪成为有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 又搬家：又搬家了，The move is a completed event in the narrative, so the verb needs 了.
- 在他们第三个家：在他们第三个家里，A home needs the location word 里 after this numbered noun phrase.
- 告诉了孟子：告诉孟子，A speaking verb that introduces quoted speech takes the zero particle rather than 了.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 在这儿妈妈让儿子从早到晚学习，他难怪成为有名的哲学家。：难怪孟子后来成为了有名的哲学家，原来妈妈在这儿让他从早到晚学习。Use the conclusion–explanation pattern 难怪……，原来……; it also makes the causal link between studying and his later achievement clear.
- Expressiveness: After the third home is introduced, add one setting detail showing why it is good for study, for example: “新家在学校旁边，环境很安静。”
- Expressiveness: Show the mother’s happiness through an action or a short line of speech before her announcement, for example: “妈妈高兴地说：‘我们找到了最后的家。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the family’s move to a new stage with “所以他们又搬家,” and you use dialogue to show the mother’s hopes for her son.
