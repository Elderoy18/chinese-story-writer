## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，孟子和孟母住了在靠墓地的家。孩子才五岁就每天在墓地祭拜了。在那个城市，孟母从早到晚都找工作，可是找不到，害得孟母没有钱。难怪她跟孩子搬家，原来买不了房。他们搬了到别一个城市找工作。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，this expression is redundant because both parts refer to time. [vocab list]
- 靠墓地的家：墓地附近的家里，this needs a clear location phrase after the residence verb; this wording means a home located near the cemetery. [rule: location_zai_place]
- 她跟孩子搬家：她带着孩子搬家，use the verb meaning “take someone along” when a mother moves with her child; the original wording treats them as equal companions. [vocab list]
- 别一个城市：另一个城市，the word for “another one” before a measure word is needed here. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 买不了 -- “原来买不了房”：买不起，specifies that the home cannot be bought because there is not enough money

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟母：有一个小男孩叫孟子，他和孟母，at first mention, introduce the main character with a name and a brief identity before using the family title. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Reference): 孩子：他，after the character was named in the immediately previous sentence, use a pronoun for this close re-occurrence. [rule: none]
- Coherence (Reference): 在那个城市：在他们住的城市里，this city has not previously been introduced, so the definite reference is unclear; identify it through the family’s already-mentioned home. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Reference): 害得孟母没有钱：害得她没有钱，within the same sentence, the mother has already been named, so a pronoun avoids unnecessary repetition. [rule: none]
- Coherence (Temporality): 住了在：住在，living in a place is a continuing state here, so it takes the zero particle rather than completed-action 了; the location follows 住 with 在. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 祭拜了：祭拜，this is a daily routine, so use the zero particle rather than 了, which marks a completed action. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬了到：搬到了，this completed move needs 了 after the resultative element 到, not between the verb and it. [teacher rule: le_placement]
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery sentence, add a small visual detail about the visitors, such as “孟子每天看到很多人拿着鲜花来祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: At the end of the cemetery sentence, show the child’s interest with an internal thought, such as “他觉得这些动作很有意思。”
- Expressiveness: Before the move, add Mengmu’s concern through a short thought or quotation, such as “孟母心想：‘我们得找一个更好的地方住。’”

### Encouragement
- You used the pattern 才……就…… effectively to show that Mengzi was very young. You also chose a clear cause-and-result expression with 害得 and used 难怪……原来…… in a fitting way.

## Scene 2

> 这个城市老是很吵，不是人吵架，就是都叫，害得孟子一直不能睡觉。在那儿，孟母也找不到工作。孟子和妈妈要又一个次搬了家。在第三个城市，孟母找到工作，在商店做衣服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 人：人们，This refers to a concrete group of people in the city, so use 人们 rather than the generic noun 人. [teacher rule: ren_renmen]
- 要又一个次搬了家：要再搬一次家，For a move that is planned but has not happened yet, use modal verb + 再 + verb + number + measure word + object. 又 and final 了 describe a repeat that has already happened, and the numeral phrase is 一次 rather than 一个次. [teacher rule: zai_structure]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很吵 -- “这个城市老是很吵”：嘈杂，specifies that many different sounds overlap in the city；喧闹，suggests a loudly busy atmosphere with people and activity.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 孟母找到工作：孟母找到了工作，Finding the job is a completed action in this past sequence, so use 了 after the resultative complement 到. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: After the noisy-city description, add a physical reaction from Mengzi, such as “孟子捂着耳朵说：‘太吵了，我睡不着！’”
- Expressiveness: At the decision to move, show Mengmu’s worry with an inner thought, such as “孟母着急地想：‘我们得再搬一次家。’”
- Expressiveness: In the final sentence, add one small detail about her job, such as “她每天在商店里缝衣服，很认真地工作。”

### Encouragement

## Scene 3

> 孟子在那个商店也找了到工作，可是不是做衣服的工作，而是喜欢的工作。孟子学习馆学了十五年，他的学习和他的旅行经验很重要，对他很多好处。他是大人的时候，他成为很有名的哲人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 找了到：找到了，The resultative complement must follow the verb, and the completion marker comes after the verb-plus-complement sequence. [teacher rule: le_placement]
- 孟子学习馆学了十五年：孟子在学馆学习了十五年，A place needs the preposition 在 before it, and 学习 is the action verb; 学馆 is the place where he studies. [rule: none]
- 对他很多好处：对他有很多好处，The phrase 对他 introduces the person affected, while 有 is needed as the predicate to state that something brings benefits. [vocab list]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很有名 -- “他成为很有名的哲人”：享有盛名的，shows that many people know and admire him, rather than simply saying he is famous

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 他成为很有名的哲人：他成为了很有名的哲人，Becoming a philosopher is presented as a completed past achievement, so use 了 after the verb. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: After “孟子在学馆学习了十五年,” add a concrete study detail, such as “他每天认真地看书，遇到不懂的地方就问老师。”
- Expressiveness: At the final achievement, add effort and a feeling of completion: “经过十五年的努力，他终于成为了很有名的哲人。”
- Expressiveness: You could show Meng母’s happiness through speech after his success: “孟母高兴地说：‘这儿的环境很适合孩子！’”

### Encouragement

- Your use of “不是……而是……” effectively contrasts the type of work he finds.
