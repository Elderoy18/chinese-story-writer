## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个男孩名字叫孟子。孟子和孟子的妈妈两个人住在很穷的农村。他们住的地方旁边是个墓地，墓地老是有很多人祭拜。因为人们在祭拜，他们一直在哭。孟子和孟子的妈妈住在这里才一个星期就觉得受不了了，而且害得孟子的学习。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，The original repeats the time meaning: the first word already names a historical period, while the second phrase also means “at the time of.” [vocab list]
- 有一个男孩名字叫孟子：有一个叫孟子的男孩，A name can modify the noun before it with 的; this structure introduces the boy and his name naturally. [rule: attributive_modifier_de]
- 住在这里才一个星期：才住了一个星期，The week of living there is a completed duration before the later reaction, so the action needs 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- 而且害得孟子的学习：而且这儿的环境还害得孟子不能学习，害得 must be followed by the affected person and a harmful result clause; a possessive noun alone cannot complete this structure. [teacher rule: haide_structure]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很穷 -- “住在很穷的农村”：一个非常贫困的农村地区，specifies that the area has serious economic hardship and gives the location noun a clearer form.
- 受不了 -- “就觉得受不了了”：无法忍受，states more precisely that the situation has become impossible for the characters to bear.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子的妈妈两个人：孟子和妈妈两个人，Once the sentence has named the boy, repeating his name inside the same noun phrase is unnecessary; use the close re-occurrence form for his mother. [rule: none]
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子的妈妈：孟子和妈妈，The second use of the boy’s name in the same noun phrase is redundant; the family relationship is already clear. [rule: none]
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为人们在祭拜，他们一直在哭：因为人们在祭拜，所以他们一直在哭，Use the full cause-and-effect connector to show that the mourning activity causes the crying. [rule: none]
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery is introduced, add one sensory detail to make its atmosphere clearer, such as “墓地里不时传来哭声，气氛很沉重。”
- Expressiveness: At the final sentence, show the mother’s worry through an inner thought or short quotation, such as “孟子的妈妈很担心：‘住在这里会影响孩子学习。’”
- Expressiveness: You could show the boy’s reaction to the mourners, such as “孟子听见哭声，觉得很害怕。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduce the setting, the cemetery, and the people’s mourning, so the reader can understand why the family feels uncomfortable.
- You also used ongoing-action forms effectively when describing the people worshipping and crying.

## Scene 2

> 孟子的妈妈觉得孟子取得良好的教育是很重要的，所以孟子的妈妈认为这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好，就打算跟孟子搬家。孟子的妈妈带孟子不是搬到城市，而是搬到了孟子老师的家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 取得良好的教育：接受良好的教育，The verb is not the usual collocation for receiving schooling; this phrase means to receive a good education. [rule: none]
- 跟孟子搬家：带着孟子搬家，This wording means moving together with the child; the original wording does not express that the mother takes her child with her. [vocab list]
- 不是搬到城市，而是搬到了：搬到了，This paired structure is for correcting a conventional or previously stated mistaken idea, but no such idea is being refuted here. [rule: bushi_ershi_construction]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 地方 -- “这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好”：环境，specifies that it is the surrounding living and learning conditions that affect the child.
- 不太好 -- “对儿子的教育不太好”：不利于，states more precisely that the place has a negative effect on the child’s education.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子的妈妈认为：她认为，The mother was named earlier in the same sentence, and no other female character competes as the referent, so use a pronoun for this close re-occurrence. [rule: none]
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子的妈妈带孟子：她带孟子，The mother remains the clear subject from the preceding sentence, so a pronoun is more natural than repeating her full name. [rule: none]
- Expressiveness: At the mother’s decision, add her worry about her son’s future, such as: “她很担心孟子的将来。”
- Expressiveness: You could show her reason through a short thought or line of speech, such as: “如果一直住在这儿，会影响孟子的学习。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the mother’s reason for wanting to move, and the cause-and-effect connection is easy to follow. You also use the completed-action marker well in the final moving action.
