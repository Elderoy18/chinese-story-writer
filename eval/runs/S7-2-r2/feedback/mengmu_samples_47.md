## Scene 1

> 这个男孩子叫孟子，孟子是古代的人。孟子的妈妈觉得住在哪儿对童年很重要，所以他们常常搬了家。她望子成龙，所以孟子的妈妈安排他的生活。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 常常搬了家：常常搬家，A habitual action takes the zero aspect form, so it should not use 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 安排 -- “孟子的妈妈安排他的生活”：规划，emphasizes that the mother is making careful, long-term plans for his life

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 这个男孩子叫孟子：有一个叫孟子的男孩子，This is the boy’s first mention, so introduce him with a full, non-specific noun phrase rather than using 这个. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子是古代的人：他是古代的人，After the name has just appeared and no other male character competes as the referent, use a pronoun. [rule: none]
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子的妈妈安排他的生活：安排他的生活，The mother is already the subject in the first clause of this sentence, so use the zero form rather than repeating her full noun phrase. [rule: none]
- Expressiveness: At the mother’s thought about where to live, add a feeling of concern, such as “孟母很担心孟子会学坏。”
- Expressiveness: After the mother’s wish for her son, add a short line of inner speech, such as “我要给孟子找一个更好的地方。”
- Expressiveness: Add one concrete detail about the child’s life that the mother wants to manage, such as “她安排孟子每天看书学习。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the mother’s motivation with a cause-and-effect connector, and the idiom 望子成龙 effectively expresses her hopes for her son.

## Scene 2

> 因为他们常常搬了家，中国人说，孟母三迁。孟子没有爸爸，所以，他的妈妈更重要。从这儿到那儿，妈妈教孟子很多技能。先她让孟子跟朋友玩儿，然后教他怎么买又便宜又好质量的东西。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 常常搬了家：常常搬家，A habitual action takes the zero aspect form; do not use 了 for a routine. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- 先她：她先，The adverb 先 should come after the subject and before the verb it modifies. [rule: none]
- 好质量的东西：质量好的东西，To describe an item’s quality, use the noun phrase 质量 followed by the adjective 好; 好质量 is not the natural modifier order. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很多技能 -- “妈妈教孟子很多技能”：各种生活技能，specifies that these are practical abilities for daily life.
- 东西 -- “怎么买又便宜又质量好的东西”：商品，more precisely refers to items that people buy and sell.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 妈妈教孟子：她教孟子，The mother was named in the immediately previous sentence and no other female character competes as the referent, so use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Conjunction): ，中国人说：，所以中国人说，Use 所以 after 因为 to clearly complete the cause-and-effect connection: the repeated moves are the reason for the saying. [rule: coherence_connectives]
- Expressiveness: At the buying lesson, add a short line of speech to show what the mother teaches: “妈妈说：‘买东西要看质量，也要看价钱。’”
- Expressiveness: After the lesson, add Mengzi’s feeling to make the scene more lively: “孟子学会了，觉得很有意思。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the mother’s role in Mengzi’s life, and the sequence with 先 and 然后 helps the reader follow the teaching activities.

## Scene 3

> 最后，她让孟子学写作。他不是写作就是看书，难怪他很聪明。现在在中国，孟子很有名。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- Nothing to flag.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 有名 -- “孟子很有名”：广为人知，emphasizes that many people across China know him；家喻户晓，emphasizes that he is known by almost every household.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After “他不是写作就是看书”, add a detail about his study habits, such as “他每天认真地读很多书，也常常写下自己的想法。”
- Expressiveness: At “难怪他很聪明”, add the mother’s feeling or speech, such as “妈妈看到他这么爱学习，心里很高兴。”
- Expressiveness: At the final sentence, add a specific achievement to show why he is famous, such as “后来，他成为了一位著名的哲学家。”

### Encouragement
- You used “最后” effectively to show the final step in the mother’s teaching. The sentence “他不是写作就是看书” clearly shows Mengzi’s regular study routine.
