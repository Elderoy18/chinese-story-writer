## Scene 1

> 有一天孟子去了墓地玩儿，但是他妈妈觉得这是不合适的地方让孩子玩，所以她决定了他们会搬家找更好的地方。她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福，而且住在这儿教育不太好。因为她望子成龙，所以她会一直搬家才能找到质量好的教育。然后他们搬到了离城市更近的地方。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不合适的地方让孩子玩：不适合孩子玩儿的地方，Use 适合 to state that a place suits a person or activity; the corrected phrase clearly makes the whole phrase modify 地方.
- 住在这儿教育不太好：这儿的教育环境不太好，The location should modify the noun phrase with 的, and 教育环境 clearly names what is not good.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 玩儿 -- “孟子去了墓地玩儿”：嬉戏，adds the idea of carefree, playful activity.
- 更好的 -- “找更好的地方”：更适合孩子成长的，explains specifically why the new place would be better for the child.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他们会搬家找更好的地方：搬家，找一个更好的地方，After 决定, use the verb phrase directly rather than repeating a new subject and future clause.
- Coherence (Temporality): 决定了：决定，决定 is a mental-state verb, so it takes the zero particle rather than 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 会一直搬家才能找到：得一直搬家，才能找到，才能 introduces a necessary condition; use 得 before the action to express that moving repeatedly is necessary.
- Expressiveness: After the visit to the cemetery, add one concrete detail about what Mengzi does there, such as “孟子觉得祭拜很好玩，也学着别人祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: Show the mother’s concern through a short thought or quotation after she decides to move, such as “孟母心想：‘住在这儿会影响孟子的学习。’”
- Expressiveness: When they arrive near the city, describe the new setting with a sensory detail, such as “城市里每天都有很多商人卖东西，很热闹。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the mother’s reason for moving and use a logical cause-and-effect structure with 因为……所以…… . You also used completed-action markers accurately in “去了” and “搬到了”.

## Scene 2

> 他们住才一月就又搬家。古代的时候，一般人不喜欢卖东西的人。但是孟子老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿，害得妈妈着急得很，因为她恐怕儿子会成为跟他们很像。她告诉了孟子：他们不是朋友，而是不好的人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，The original repeats the idea of “time”: the noun already names a historical period, so the added time phrase is redundant.
- 老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿：常常跟这些卖东西的人玩儿，Two frequency adverbs should not modify the same verb when they give the same meaning.
- 害得妈妈着急得很：让妈妈很着急，This result is emotional concern rather than a clearly harmful result, so use a neutral causative verb instead of the harmful-result structure.
- 恐怕：担心，This word cannot follow another subject to mean that person is afraid; use the verb meaning “to worry” here.
- 成为跟他们很像：变得跟他们很像，The first verb introduces a new identity or role, while the second verb correctly expresses a change into a similar state.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 卖东西的人 -- “这些卖东西的人”：商贩，identifies them more specifically as people who sell goods in a marketplace or street-business setting

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他们不是朋友：他们不是你的朋友，The original can mean that the sellers are not friends with one another. Adding the listener’s possessor makes clear that they are not Mengzi’s friends.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们住才一月就又搬家：他们才住了一个月，就又搬家了，Completed actions need 了, and the paired sequence places 才 before the first action and 就 before the next one.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉了：告诉，A speaking verb that introduces direct speech takes the zero particle rather than 了.
- Expressiveness: At the point where the sellers are introduced, add one marketplace sound or sight to make the setting vivid: “街上到处都是叫卖声。”
- Expressiveness: Show Mengzi’s interest with a small action before his mother becomes worried: “孟子学着商人收钱，还觉得很好玩儿。”
- Expressiveness: Give the mother a brief inner thought before her warning: “孟母心想：这样下去可不行。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show why the mother becomes worried, and the cause introduced with “因为” helps the reader follow her feelings.
- The direct speech at the end is a good choice because it lets the mother’s opinion appear in her own voice.

## Scene 3

> 你不是当数学家，而是当哲学家，不能当卖东西的人。所以他们又搬家。在他们第三个家，孟子学了怎么祭拜。妈妈感觉这个地方适合他们，告诉了孟子：我们找到了最后的家。在这儿妈妈让儿子从早到晚学习，他难怪成为有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在他们第三个家：在他们的第三个家里，A possessive modifier needs 的 before the noun, and 里 marks that the action happens inside the home.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 地方 -- “这个地方适合他们”：这里的环境，specifies the surroundings that make the location suitable；这里的居住环境，focuses more precisely on conditions for living there

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 所以他们又搬家：所以他们又搬家了，This move is a completed event in the narrative, so use 了; 又 shows that the move happened again.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉了孟子：告诉孟子，A speaking verb that introduces quoted speech takes the zero particle rather than 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为有名的哲学家：成为了有名的哲学家，Becoming a philosopher is presented as a completed later achievement, so use 了.
- Expressiveness: After “孟子学了怎么祭拜,” add one action that shows how he learns. Example: “孟子跟着大人，认真地学怎么祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: Show the mother’s feeling when she believes the home is suitable. Example: “妈妈高兴地说：‘我们终于找到一个合适的家了！’”
- Expressiveness: At the final sentence, add a small detail about Mencius’s effort before his achievement. Example: “孟子每天认真学习，常常读书到很晚。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the family’s repeated moves with 又, and the mother’s direct speech helps the reader understand her hopes for Mencius. The final achievement also gives this scene a clear ending.
