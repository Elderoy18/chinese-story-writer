## Scene 1

> 很久以前，有一个老男人叫神农。神农在古代每一天看到了别的小镇人生病还是不舒服，所以他决定开始找新的药。他下一天开始爬当地的山脉，找了各种各样的植物。神农试一试每个植物，知道有什么好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别的小镇人：小镇里的人，The word for “other” needs a stated contrast, and people belonging to a town must be expressed with a location phrase before the noun for people.
- 生病还是不舒服：生病或者感到不舒服，In a declarative statement listing two possible conditions, use the connector for alternatives rather than the connector typically used for a question or an uncertain choice.
- 他下一天开始：第二天，他开始，This narrative time expression should use the standard phrase for “the next day,” and the time phrase should be separated from the main clause.
- 找了各种各样的植物：找到了各种各样的植物，The verb means “look for”; when the plants were found, it needs the resultative complement showing success.
- 每个植物：每种植物，Plants are counted by type with this measure word when referring to each kind of plant.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农在古代：他在古代，After the character has been introduced in the immediately preceding sentence and no other person is competing as the referent, use a pronoun.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农试一试：他试一试，The same character was the clear subject of the preceding sentence, so a pronoun is more natural for a close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 每一天看到了：每一天看到，This is a habitual action, so it takes the zero particle rather than completed-action 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 试一试：试了试，This happens as a completed event in the past narrative, so the reduplicated verb needs 了 to mark completion.
- Expressiveness: After the mountain sentence, add one visual detail about the setting, such as “高山上长着各种各样的植物。” This helps the reader picture where the search happens.
- Expressiveness: At the point where he tests plants, add his purpose or inner question, such as “他想：哪一种植物可以治病呢？” This makes his decision feel more personal.

### Encouragement
You clearly introduce the main character at the beginning, so the reader immediately knows who the scene is about. You also use a cause-and-effect connection to explain why he begins his search, which gives the scene a clear direction.

## Scene 2

> 比方说，他一次试一试有些植物的叶，吃得又新鲜又甜。煮这些叶以后，他一喝就知道他发现新的茶叶。虽然他找到了很多的新的植物，但是他的旅程有的时候特别是危险。比方说，他一吃灵芝就开始生病。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他一次试一试有些植物的叶，吃得又新鲜又甜：他尝一尝有些植物的叶子；这些叶子又新鲜又甜，The reduplicated verb already expresses a brief attempt, so it should not be preceded by 一次 here. Use 尝 for tasting food, and make the leaves—not the act of eating—the subject of the adjectives 新鲜 and 甜.
- 特别是危险：特别危险，特别是 introduces a specific example or clause; it cannot directly modify an adjective such as 危险.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 有的时候 -- “他的旅程有的时候特别危险”：偶尔，states more precisely that the danger happened only on some occasions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 知道他发现：知道发现，The second pronoun refers to the same character as the subject, with no competing person in the sentence, so use zero form rather than repeating the pronoun.
- Coherence (Temporality): 煮这些叶以后：煮了这些叶以后，The boiling must be completed before the later action of drinking, so use 了 for the completed action.
- Coherence (Temporality): 发现新的茶叶：发现了新的茶叶，The discovery is presented as a completed result after he drinks the tea, so add 了.
- Expressiveness: At the tea-drinking moment, add a sensory detail about its smell or taste, such as “茶水又香又清甜。”
- Expressiveness: Add Shennong’s thought when he realizes what he has found, such as “原来这种叶子可以泡水喝！”
- Expressiveness: At the illness moment, describe one physical feeling, such as “他的肚子疼了起来，难受得不得了。”

### Encouragement
You used the connector pair 虽然……但是…… correctly to show that finding plants was useful but the journey was still difficult. The scene also has a clear action sequence from tasting leaves, to boiling and drinking them, to facing danger.

## Scene 3

> 一个爬山的男人正好看到神农的麻烦，然后给了他一些药。神农最终他的小镇回到，跟别的人分享他的发现。你认为神农的故事真的吗？这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他的小镇回到：回到他的小镇，The destination must follow the verb in the pattern “回到 + place.”
- 别的人：别人，Use this for unspecified other people; “别的” distinguishes a different category from one already mentioned.
- 你认为神农的故事真的吗？：你认为神农的故事是真的吗？，To ask whether the story is true with “真的吗,” use the copular predicate “是真的.”
- 这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处。：这就要看你相不相信中药对身体有好处了。, This structure requires an A-not-A or other question form after “看,” and its final “了” belongs to the fixed structure.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 麻烦 -- “神农的麻烦”：困境，more specifically describes the difficult situation that Shennong is in.
- 药 -- “给了他一些药”：解毒药，specifies that the medicine is intended to counter poisoning.
- 发现 -- “分享他的发现”：研究发现，makes clear that these are findings from his plant research.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 给了他一些药：给了神农一些药，Two male characters appear in the same sentence, so “他” could refer to either one; repeat the name for clarity.
- Coherence (Temporality): 回到：回到了，Returning to the town is a completed narrative event, so it needs “了.”
- Expressiveness: After the helper appears, add a visual detail about him and the medicine, such as “一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人拿着像伞一样的灵芝。”
- Expressiveness: After the medicine is given, show Shennong’s physical feeling and gratitude, such as “神农觉得肚子好多了，感激地说：‘谢谢你！’”
- Expressiveness: When Shennong shares his findings, add a short explanation of the two plants, such as “灵芝是药，可以吃；夹竹桃有毒，不能吃。”

### Encouragement
You created a clear sequence: someone helps Shennong, and then Shennong returns to share what he learned. Ending with a question also gives the scene an interesting reflective tone.
