## Scene 1

> 古代时候，人不知道他们能吃什么植物，常常一吃就死。但是不知道为什么，没有办法。所以有一天，神农出去试一试很多种植物，告诉人什么植物是安全什么是危险。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代时候：在古代，A time expression should not combine the time noun with 时候 here; 在古代 is the natural form.
- 人：人们，This refers to a concrete group of people, so use 人们 rather than the generic noun 人.
- 试一试：尝试，尝试 means to test or try many kinds of plants; 试一试 more often describes briefly trying one particular thing.
- 人：人们，This is again a concrete group receiving the information, so 人们 is needed.
- 什么植物是安全什么是危险：哪些植物安全，哪些植物危险，The two parts should be parallel and each should clearly state which plants are being identified.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他们：删除，The subject 人们 has already appeared in the same sentence, so use zero form instead of repeating it with a pronoun.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：一个叫神农的老人，This is the character’s first mention, so introduce him with a name and a brief noun phrase rather than using only the name.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉：告诉了，The testing and telling are completed narrative actions; in a series of actions, the last verb takes 了.
- Expressiveness: After “常常一吃就死,” add the people’s emotional reaction to make the danger more vivid, for example: “大家都很害怕。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where the character begins his work, add a short thought or line of speech showing his purpose, for example: “神农想：‘我要找出哪些植物能吃。’”
- Expressiveness: When describing his search, add a simple journey detail to show effort, for example: “他走啊走啊，去了很多高山。”

### Encouragement
- You created a clear problem at the beginning: people do not know which plants they can eat, and this gives a strong reason for the next action.
- Your use of “一吃就死” effectively shows an immediate, serious result.

## Scene 2

> 发现有的植物对身体非常好，特别是茶，让肚子感觉又暖和又清楚。他告诉朋友：“你吃哪种植物，这就要看你的口味。比方说，你爱吃甜的，可能喜欢糖。”他最喜欢吃花，除了夹竹桃以外，什么都花想吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 让肚子感觉又暖和又清楚：让人觉得肚子暖和，头脑清醒，清楚 describes a person’s mind or understanding, not their stomach; use separate objects for the physical warmth and mental clarity.
- 这就要看你的口味：这就要看你的口味怎么样了，the part after 这就要看 must be a question form; adding 怎么样 makes it ask about the taste preference and completes the fixed structure.
- 什么都花想吃：什么花都想吃，when 什么花 is the object meaning “every kind of flower,” it must stay together before 都.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 非常好 -- “有的植物对身体非常好”：特别有益，states more precisely that the plants benefit health.
- 爱吃甜的 -- “你爱吃甜的”：偏爱甜食，makes the preference and the type of food more specific.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the discovery of tea, add its taste or effect to make the finding vivid, for example: “茶水喝起来香香的，肚子也暖和了。”
- Expressiveness: In the quoted speech, add a short reaction from the friend to make the dialogue feel more natural, for example: “朋友说：‘原来每个人喜欢的东西不一样！’”
- Expressiveness: At “他最喜欢吃花,” add a thought showing his curiosity, for example: “他心想：‘这些花看起来这么漂亮，会不会也很好吃？’”

### Encouragement
- You used direct speech to show what the character thinks about food preferences, which makes the scene more lively. The exception phrase “除了夹竹桃以外” also clearly shows that the character knows one flower should be avoided.

## Scene 3

> 他发现这个口味，一百岁时候，不小心吃了毒的花。一吃就开始死。但是生病时候告诉人什么植物是药，什么植物是毒。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一百岁时候：一百岁的时候，A multi-syllable time expression before 时候 requires 的.
- 毒的花：有毒的花，Use 有毒的 to describe a poisonous plant or flower; 毒的 does not express this meaning naturally.
- 生病时候：生病的时候，A multi-syllable verb phrase before 时候 requires 的.
- 是毒：有毒，是毒 identifies something as the noun 毒, while 有毒 describes the property of being poisonous.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不小心吃了 -- “不小心吃了毒的花”：误食了，specifically means accidentally eating something that should not be eaten.
- 告诉 -- “告诉人什么植物是药”：向人们说明，emphasizes clearly explaining the difference between medicinal and poisonous plants.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 告诉人：告诉人们，Use 人们 for the concrete group of people whom he is informing.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他发现这个口味：他发现了这个口味，This is a completed discovery in a past narrative, so it needs 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 一吃就开始死：一吃就开始死了，The beginning of dying is a change of state in this particular event, so add 了 at the end.
- Expressiveness: After the poisonous-flower event, add a visual detail about the flower, such as: 黄色的花像星星一样，又大又漂亮。
- Expressiveness: Show his physical pain and fear instead of only saying that he starts dying: 他躺在地上，肚子疼得不得了，心想：“我会死吗？”
- Expressiveness: Make his warning to others more vivid with direct speech: “像星星一样的花有毒，不能吃；像伞一样的植物是药，可以吃。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the dangerous result of eating the flower and then connect it to the useful knowledge he shares with others. The sentence pattern 一……就…… is also a good choice for showing an immediate result.
