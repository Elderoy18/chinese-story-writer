## Scene 1

> 古代的时候有一个人叫神农。他需要当上医，一看别的人就想帮助他们。但是，他有一个问题，他不知道什么东西能做药。神农听说一个远远的地方有一种植物，什么病人吃都会觉得身体更好。神农三个月走去，找到有植物的地方，他看到一个很漂亮的植物，又香又好看。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，Both expressions refer to the same time period, so using them together is redundant.
- 当上医：当医生，医生 is the usual occupation noun here; 当上 indicates successfully attaining a role and does not fit the intended meaning of needing to work as a doctor.
- 别的人：别人，别人 refers to other people as a group; 别的 is used to distinguish a different item or type.
- 一看：一看到，The sentence means that he sees someone and then wants to help, so the resultative complement 到 is needed.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 远远的 -- “一个远远的地方”：遥远的，more clearly conveys that the place is a great distance away and creates a stronger sense of a difficult journey.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 什么病人吃都会觉得身体更好：什么病人吃了都会觉得身体更好，The eating happens before the patient feels better, so 了 marks the completed action.
- Coherence (Temporality): 神农三个月走去，找到有植物的地方：神农走了三个月，找到了有植物的地方，Both the long walk and the finding are completed events in the narrative, so they need 了.
- Expressiveness: At “一个很漂亮的植物”, add one visual detail to show why it is beautiful, such as “开着黄色的像星星一样的花”.
- Expressiveness: After “又香又好看”, show Shennong’s reaction with an internal thought, such as “神农心想：这种植物也许能做药。”
- Expressiveness: At the end of the scene, add a small action to build suspense before he tries the plant, such as “他小心地摘下一片叶子，放进嘴里。”

### Encouragement
- You introduce Shennong clearly at the beginning and keep the focus on his wish to help others.
- Your description “又香又好看” already gives the plant two sensory details, which makes the scene more vivid.

## Scene 2

> 他觉得，这种一定是做药的花。原来他想错了。吃了以后，神农快生病。 这时一个穿着蓝色衣服的人帮神农，快给他一种红的灵芝。神农吃了以后，他没有病，想起来以前一个人告诉他，灵芝对身体有好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 快：很快，Before a declarative verb phrase, “很快” means that the action happened rapidly; “快” alone is normally used to urge someone to act.
- 生病：生病了，The illness begins as a completed change of state in this past narrative, so the verb needs 了.
- 快：很快，Before the completed action of giving, “很快” correctly means that the person gave it rapidly; “快” alone is usually an imperative-like prompt.
- 给：给了，Giving the medicine is a completed action, so 给 needs 了.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 想错 -- “原来他想错了”：判断错了，specifies that his assessment of the flower was wrong；弄错了，emphasizes that he made a mistaken conclusion.
- 没有病 -- “神农吃了以后，他没有病”：病好了，more precisely shows that he recovered after becoming ill.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农吃了以后，他没有病：神农吃了以后，没有病，When the same character has already been named earlier in the same sentence, use zero form rather than repeating a pronoun.
- Expressiveness: After “原来他想错了,” add a physical reaction to show the danger, such as “他一吃这种花，肚子马上疼了起来。”
- Expressiveness: At “这时一个穿着蓝色衣服的人,” describe the helpful plant so the reader can picture it, such as “他拿起一棵像小伞一样的植物。”
- Expressiveness: After eating the medicine, add Shennong’s feeling or a short thought, such as “神农觉得舒服多了，心想：这种植物真的能做药！”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the important sequence of Shennong making a mistaken guess, becoming ill, receiving help, and remembering what he knew about lingzhi.
- The description “一个穿着蓝色衣服的人” is a helpful, concrete detail that makes the new character easy to picture.

## Scene 3

> 啊，他怎么忘记了？他把灵芝放在包里。一回家就告诉别人。他找到药以后他写下怎么做药在书上。所以人们都知道，那红灵芝对身体好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别人：人们，别人 means people other than a particular person already being contrasted; here the sentence refers generally to the public, so 人们 fits.
- 写下怎么做药在书上：在书上写下了怎么做药的方法，place words such as 在书上 come before the writing verb, and the content of writing needs the noun 方法 after the question phrase; 了 marks the completed writing action.
- 那红灵芝：那种红灵芝，this demonstrative phrase needs a measure word before the noun.
- 对身体好处：对身体有好处，有好处 is the correct phrase for something that benefits the body.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他找到药以后他写下：他找到药以后，写下，when the same character continues as the subject within one sentence, use zero form instead of repeating the pronoun.
- Coherence (Temporality): 放在包里：放在包里了，putting the object into the bag is a completed action in this past narrative, so it needs 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉：告诉了，telling the people is presented as a completed action; this is not a speaking verb introducing direct quotation, so it takes 了.
- Expressiveness: After the opening question, add a short inner thought to show why he suddenly remembers the medicine, such as “原来灵芝可以救人，我怎么忘了？”
- Expressiveness: When he writes in the book, add one concrete detail about what he records, such as “他写下灵芝像一把小伞，对身体有好处。”
- Expressiveness: Add a feeling after people learn about the medicine, such as “人们听了以后，都很感谢神农。”

### Encouragement
- You used an effective opening question to show the character’s sudden realization. The sequence from bringing the medicine home to sharing its benefit is also easy to follow.
