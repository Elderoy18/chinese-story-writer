## Scene 1

> 古代的时候人觉得祭拜非常重要，孟子很小的时候，从早到晚一直祭拜，因为他妈妈告诉他祭拜很重要。虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜。他的同学们老是对他开玩笑了。孟子妈妈看了他的同学们对孟子不好的影响，所以孟子和孟子妈妈搬家了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，this phrase is redundant because both parts refer to time.
- 人：人们，this refers to a concrete group of people in ancient times, so the plural noun is needed.
- 看了：看到了，here the mother perceives the result or effect, so use the resultative form meaning “see/noticed,” rather than the process verb meaning “look.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不好的影响 -- “他的同学们对孟子不好的影响”：对孟子的负面影响，identifies the effect more specifically as harmful or negative；对孟子的不良影响，uses a precise expression for an undesirable effect.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子很小的时候：有一个小男孩叫孟子。他很小的时候，Mencius is first introduced in this scene, so introduce him with both his identity and name before using the name alone.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他的同学们老是对他开玩笑了：他的同学们老是对他开玩笑，this is a repeated habit signaled by “老是,” so it takes the zero particle rather than completed-action “了.”
- Coherence (Conjunction): 虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜：因为他每天只祭拜，所以没有朋友，this is a cause-and-effect relation, which needs the connector pair “因为……所以……”; “虽然” introduces contrast instead.
- Expressiveness: After the description of worship, add Mencius’s feeling to show why he repeated the action, for example: “他觉得祭拜很有趣。”
- Expressiveness: Make the classmates’ jokes more vivid with a short line of speech, for example: “同学们笑着说：‘你怎么每天都祭拜？’”
- Expressiveness: Show the mother’s concern before the move, for example: “孟子妈妈很担心，心想：‘这样下去会影响孟子。’”

### Encouragement

## Scene 2

> 孟子才十一岁，成为一个哲学家。孟子不是祭拜，而是看政治文章，难怪他成为了哲学家。不过，看过很多政治文章对他害得，他有很多问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 才十一岁，成为：才十一岁，就成为，Use the paired structure with a young age: 才 + age + 就 + verb.
- 看过很多政治文章对他害得，他有很多问题：看过很多政治文章，害得他有很多问题，害得 must be followed by the person affected and then the harmful result; 对他 cannot come before 害得 in this structure.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 问题 -- “他有很多问题”：疑问，specifies that these are questions or uncertainties created by his reading rather than general problems.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子不是祭拜：他不是祭拜，Use a pronoun when the same character was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other person competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为一个哲学家：成为了一个哲学家，This is a completed change of state in the narrative, so the verb needs 了.
- Expressiveness: After “看政治文章,” add a small detail about his study setting, such as “他每天坐在安静的房间里认真看书。”
- Expressiveness: Show Mengzi’s thoughts after reading, such as “孟子心想：‘为什么人们要这样管理国家呢？’”
- Expressiveness: Add Mengzi’s mother’s reaction to his learning, such as “孟母看到他认真学习，心里很高兴。”

### Encouragement

## Scene 3

> 他一直问问题，但是不能解决那些问题。孟子长大，他开始能解决问题。他常常建议你不是学金融，而是学政治，因为如果你懂政治，你会成为高薪。后来孟子成为教授，他教学生很厉害。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不是学金融，而是学政治：不要学金融，而要学政治，A recommendation between two choices normally uses a negative command and a positive alternative; the original contrast pattern is for correcting a mistaken claim rather than giving advice.
- 成为高薪：拿到高薪，This word names pay, not a profession or identity that someone can become; the correction expresses receiving high pay.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 问 -- “他一直问问题”：提出，emphasizes that he raises questions for discussion or study；追问，emphasizes asking further questions after an earlier answer.
- 解决 -- “开始能解决问题”：解答，more specifically describes answering questions or working out their answers.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他开始能解决问题：孟子长大了，开始能解决问题，After naming the character in the same sentence, use zero form rather than repeating a pronoun when no other character competes as the subject.
- Coherence (Reference): 建议你：建议大家，No listener has been introduced, so the second-person pronoun unexpectedly addresses the reader; name the intended general audience instead.
- Coherence (Reference): 他教学生很厉害：教学生很厉害，After naming the character in the same sentence, omit the repeated pronoun because the subject remains clear.
- Coherence (Temporality): 孟子长大：孟子长大了，Growing up is a completed change of state before the next action begins, so it needs 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 孟子成为教授：孟子成为了教授，This is a completed change of status in the past, so use 了 after the verb.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 如果你懂政治，你会拿到高薪：如果懂政治，就会拿到高薪，A conditional relation needs the result marker 就 in the second clause; the omitted subject can refer back to the general audience.
- Expressiveness: After he can answer questions, add a short thought to show his growth, such as “孟子觉得很自信，因为他终于能回答很多问题了。”
- Expressiveness: Turn his advice into direct speech so that his voice feels more lively, such as “孟子对大家说：‘学政治吧，这样能了解国家。’”
- Expressiveness: At the end, show the students’ response to make his teaching ability concrete, such as “学生们都认真听他讲课，也常常问他问题。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show Mengzi’s development from having many questions to becoming a professor. The scene also uses several useful time and frequency words to organize his progress.
