## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个男孩名字叫孟子。孟子和孟子的妈妈两个人住在很穷的农村。他们住的地方旁边是个墓地，墓地老是有很多人祭拜。因为人们在祭拜，他们一直在哭。孟子和孟子的妈妈住在这里才一个星期就觉得受不了了，而且害得孟子的学习。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，The original expression redundantly combines a historical time period with a phrase meaning “at the time of.”
- 害得孟子的学习：而且人们的哭声害得孟子不能专心学习，The structure requires a person after 害得 and then a harmful result clause; a study activity cannot directly fill the person position.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很穷 -- “住在很穷的农村”：十分贫困，specifies that the area lacks economic resources while keeping the strong degree of poverty.
- 很多人 -- “墓地老是有很多人祭拜”：许多人前来祭拜，adds that these people come to the cemetery specifically to worship.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子的妈妈住在这里：孟子和妈妈住在这里，The mother has already been introduced, so the second use of the name is unnecessary; use the shorter family reference for a close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 住在这里才一个星期：才在这里住了一个星期，The stay has lasted for a completed period before they reach the feeling of not being able to bear it, so the duration verb needs 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为人们在祭拜，他们一直在哭：因为人们在祭拜，所以他们一直在哭，A cause-and-effect relationship introduced by 因为 should use 所以 before the result clause.
- Expressiveness: After describing the visitors, add one visible detail about their grief, such as “有人低着头，眼泪不停地流下来。”
- Expressiveness: When the boy sees the worshippers, add his reaction to make the scene more personal, such as “孟子觉得祭拜很有趣，常常学着他们的样子。”
- Expressiveness: Give the mother a short thought or line of speech before the final sentence, such as “孟子的妈妈叹了口气，说：‘这里不适合孩子成长。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish the setting and introduce the main characters right away. The use of the short stay before they feel unable to continue also creates a strong sense of how difficult the environment is.

## Scene 2

> 孟子的妈妈觉得孟子取得良好的教育是很重要的，所以孟子的妈妈认为这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好，就打算跟孟子搬家。孟子的妈妈带孟子不是搬到城市，而是搬到了孟子老师的家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 取得良好的教育：接受良好的教育，教育 is normally something a child receives; 取得 is used for achievements or results rather than for education.
- 跟孟子搬家：带着孟子搬家，跟 describes doing an action together, while 带着 shows that the mother takes her child along when she moves.
- 孟子老师的家：孟子的老师的家，的 is needed to show that the teacher belongs to or teaches 孟子.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不太好 -- “这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好”：不利，states more precisely that the place has a negative effect on his education

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 所以孟子的妈妈认为：所以她认为，Use a pronoun when the same character was named earlier in the same sentence and no other possible referent intervenes.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子的妈妈带孟子：她带孟子，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the mother is still the only possible referent.
- Expressiveness: At the point where the mother decides to move, add her inner thought to show her concern: “孟母心想：‘这里不适合孟子学习。’”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech after the move to show the mother’s intention: “她说：‘我想给你找一个更好的地方。’”

### Encouragement
- You used both a cause-and-effect connector and a contrast connector effectively, so the mother’s reasoning is easy to follow. The completed-action form in 搬到了 is also used well.

## Scene 3

> 孟子跟他的老师从早到晚学习，难怪他的成绩很快就进步了很多。孟子学习的时候，孟子的妈妈不是待在家里就是编织衣服赚钱。以后孟子成为了中国有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 以后：后来，At the beginning of a sentence meaning “afterwards” in a narrative, use this time-sequencing word rather than the time expression used after a stated time point.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 进步 -- “他的成绩很快就进步了很多”：提高，emphasizes that his measurable academic results became higher.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the academic improvement, add a brief feeling or action to show Mengzi’s effort, for example: “他看到自己的成绩进步了，心里很高兴。”
- Expressiveness: Make the mother’s work more vivid with one concrete detail, for example: “孟子的妈妈坐在窗边，一边编织衣服，一边想着怎么照顾儿子。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of dialogue when the mother sees his progress, for example: “孟子的妈妈高兴地说：‘你要继续认真学习！’”

### Encouragement
The first sentence clearly connects Mengzi’s long hours of study with his improved grades. You also used the “不是……就是……” structure accurately to describe the mother’s routine.
