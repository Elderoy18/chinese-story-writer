## Scene 1

> 这个男孩子叫孟子，孟子是古代的人。孟子的妈妈觉得住在哪儿对童年很重要，所以他们常常搬了家。她望子成龙，所以孟子的妈妈安排他的生活。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 常常搬了家：常常搬家，A habitual action takes the zero aspect particle; do not use 了 for a routine.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 生活 -- “孟子的妈妈安排他的生活”：日常生活，specifies that she organizes his everyday routines and activities.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 这个男孩子叫孟子：有一个男孩子叫孟子，On first mention, use an indefinite full noun phrase rather than 这个, which normally points to a previously identified person.
- Coherence (Reference): 所以孟子的妈妈安排他的生活：所以安排他的生活，After the mother has just been named with 她, repeat neither her name nor a pronoun when she remains the subject in the same connected sentence.
- Expressiveness: After “她望子成龙”, add the mother’s inner thought to show her concern, for example: “她想：‘我一定要给孟子找一个好环境。’”
- Expressiveness: Add one concrete detail after “安排他的生活” to show what she does, for example: “她每天陪孟子读书，也不让他跟坏孩子玩。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduce the relationship between 孟子 and his mother, and you use 因为/所以-style cause-and-effect thinking to explain why the family moves.

## Scene 2

> 因为他们常常搬了家，中国人说，孟母三迁。孟子没有爸爸，所以，他的妈妈更重要。从这儿到那儿，妈妈教孟子很多技能。先她让孟子跟朋友玩儿，然后教他怎么买又便宜又好质量的东西。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 先她：她先，The subject must come before the time-order adverbial.
- 好质量的东西：质量好的东西，To describe the noun, the phrase must use the pattern “quality + adjective”; “好” cannot directly modify “质量” in this position.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 技能 -- “妈妈教孟子很多技能”：生活技能，specifies that these are practical abilities for everyday life.
- 从这儿到那儿 -- “从这儿到那儿，妈妈教孟子很多技能”：在一次次搬家中，makes the repeated moving process more specific and connects it more directly to the teaching.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 妈妈：她，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the mother was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other female character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Temporality): 常常搬了家：常常搬家，A habitual action takes the zero particle rather than completed-action 了.
- Expressiveness: At the teaching moment, add the mother’s patient manner: “她耐心地教孟子怎么买东西。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech to make the shopping lesson concrete: “妈妈说：‘买东西要先看质量，再问价钱。’”
- Expressiveness: Near the buying example, add a little marketplace activity: “商店里很热闹，商人都在讨价还价。”

### Encouragement
You clearly connect the mother’s actions to Mencius’s learning, and the example about choosing inexpensive, good-quality items makes the scene concrete. Your use of a cause-and-effect connector and a sequence connector also helps the events move forward.
