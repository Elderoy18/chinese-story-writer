### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一天，很久很久以前：很久很久以前的一天，Place the broad time period before the specific day so the time expression is naturally ordered.

### Vocabulary Suggestions

- Coherence (Conjunction): 孟子不高兴，因为他说我们老是搬家，为什么？：因为他们老是搬家，所以孟子不高兴，问：“为什么？”，Use 因为……所以…… to connect the cause of the frequent moves with Mengzi’s unhappiness; 问 clearly introduces the question.
- Expressiveness: After Mengzi becomes unhappy, add a small physical reaction to show his feeling: “孟子皱着眉头，觉得很烦。”
- Expressiveness: When his mother explains her work, add a detail about her tiredness: “妈妈叹了一口气，说她每天都很累。”

### Encouragement

## Scene 2

> 孟子想，难怪我们常常搬家，原来我妈妈很忙。可是常常搬家害得孟子很难找到朋友。每次搬家孟子也觉得新的地方不是太热就是太冷，不是太大就是太小。在哪的地方，他都不高兴。孟子想帮助妈妈祭拜，可是他六岁，所以不能祭拜。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在哪的地方，他都不高兴：不管在哪儿，他都不高兴，The interrogative place phrase should be used with a connector such as “不管” to express “regardless of where”; “在哪的地方” cannot form a natural place expression.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 找到 -- “很难找到朋友”：交到朋友，more exactly describes forming friendships rather than simply locating people.
- 不高兴 -- “他都不高兴”：闷闷不乐，shows a quieter, continuing kind of unhappiness that fits his repeated moves.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子很难找到朋友：他很难找到朋友，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the previously named character is still clear; no other male character competes as the referent here.
- Coherence (Reference): 每次搬家孟子也觉得：每次搬家他也觉得，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence of the same character rather than repeating the name.
- Expressiveness: After the sentence about not finding friends, add a small detail showing his loneliness, such as “他看着陌生的街道，心里很孤单。”
- Expressiveness: At the final sentence, add an inner thought to show why he wants to help, such as “他心想：‘妈妈每天都这么忙，我也想帮帮她。’”

### Encouragement
You used the pattern “难怪……，原来……” very effectively to show Mengzi’s realization. The sentence about moving making it difficult to make friends also clearly explains his feelings and situation.

## Scene 3
