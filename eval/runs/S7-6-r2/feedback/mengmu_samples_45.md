## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子。他们住在墓地的旁边，所以孟子老是祭拜。孟子不是祭拜，就是跟朋友聊天。他很喜欢他的城市，但是城市有才两个学校，都很小。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，this phrase redundantly combines a time period with a “time of” expression; use the concise time expression instead.
- 有才两个学校：只有两个学校，use “只有” to express that the city has only two schools; “有才” does not form this limited-quantity pattern.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 小 -- “都很小”：规模都很小，specifies that the schools are small in overall scale.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子：一个叫孟子的男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国，at first mention, introduce the character with both a noun and his name rather than naming him only in the following sentence.
- Coherence (Reference): 他的城市：自己的城市，when the city belongs to or is associated with the subject, use “自己” to make the possessor clearly refer back to him.
- Coherence (Reference): 但是城市：但是它，because “城市” was just mentioned, use a pronoun for this close re-occurrence rather than repeating the noun.
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery sentence, add what Mengzi sees there, such as “孟子每天都看见有人来墓地祭拜。” This makes his interest in worshipping more vivid.
- Expressiveness: Add Mengzi’s thought or feeling before he copies the worshippers, such as “他觉得祭拜很有趣，就学着别人祭拜。” This shows his motivation rather than only listing his actions.
- Expressiveness: You could show the mother’s concern with a short inner thought, such as “孟母心想：‘如果一直住在这儿，麻烦会越来越多。’” This adds emotion to the scene.

### Encouragement
- You used “所以” to connect the location and Mengzi’s action, and “但是” to introduce a limitation in the city. The pattern “不是……就是……” is also used naturally to describe his two usual activities.

## Scene 2

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- Nothing to flag.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬家到有很多学校的城市：搬到了有很多学校的城市，This move is presented as a completed story event, so the verb needs 了; use 搬到 + place, with 了 after 到.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他们搬到了有很多学校的城市：所以，他们搬到了有很多学校的城市，The move is the result of the mother wanting better education for Mengzi, so 所以 clearly marks the cause-and-effect relation.
- Expressiveness: After the walking sentence, add Mengzi’s physical feeling to show why he complains, such as “孟子走得腿很酸，累得满头大汗。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short complaint in quoted speech after “孟子一直抱怨”, such as “孟子皱着眉头说：‘妈妈，我们还要走多久？’”
- Expressiveness: Show the mother’s reason through an inner thought, such as “孟母心想：‘为了让孟子受到更好的教育，再辛苦也值得。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the mother’s motivation and Mengzi’s different reaction to the move. The contrast between her concern for education and his complaining makes the scene easy to understand.

## Scene 3

> 他难怪抱怨，他想他的朋友，因为他不要离开他的城市。他的妈妈找到孟子一个老师。跟老师孟子学最重要的文学，所以他成为哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 跟老师孟子学：孟子跟老师学，The learner who performs the action should be placed before the phrase introduced by 跟.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 想 -- “他想他的朋友”：想念, makes clear that he misses his friends rather than is merely thinking about them

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他的朋友：自己的朋友，Because the friends belong to the subject of the clause, 自己的 makes the possessor clearly refer back to him.
- Coherence (Reference): 他的城市：自己的城市，Because the city belongs to the subject of the clause, 自己的 makes the possessive relationship clear.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他的妈妈找到孟子一个老师：他的妈妈给孟子找到了一个老师，Finding a teacher is a completed action here, so the verb needs 了; 给 marks Mengzi as the person who benefits from the action.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为哲学家：成为了哲学家，Becoming a philosopher is presented as a completed change of state, so 成为 takes 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他难怪抱怨，：难怪他抱怨，原来，Use the paired pattern 难怪……，原来…… to present the complaint first and then reveal its reason.
- Expressiveness: After the complaint, add a small physical action or feeling to show Mengzi’s sadness, for example: “孟子叹了口气，觉得很孤单。”
- Expressiveness: Let Mengzi speak directly about his friends, for example: “他说：‘我真想念我的朋友。’”
- Expressiveness: Add one detail showing how his mother helps him meet the teacher, for example: “孟母带孟子去见老师，老师对他笑了笑。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show that Mengzi’s feelings about leaving his city connect to his later education. The final sentence also gives the scene a clear outcome by showing that he eventually became a philosopher.
