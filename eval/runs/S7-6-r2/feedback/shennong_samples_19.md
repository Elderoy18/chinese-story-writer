## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男人觉得植物很有意思。他叫神农。有的植物吃了以后他的肚子疼死了，可是有的吃了以后很舒服。他找很多植物。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，The original expression redundantly combines a historical time period with a “time of” phrase; use the concise historical-time expression instead.
- 他找很多植物：他找到了很多植物，Here the narration describes plants that he successfully found and then ate. 找 means “look for,” while 找到 means “find”; the resultative complement 到 shows that the search reached its result, and 了 marks the completed event.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After “植物很有意思,” add one visible detail to show why the plants interest him, such as “有的叶子又绿又亮，有的花像小星星一样。”
- Expressiveness: After the painful result, add Shennong’s immediate feeling or thought, such as “他捂着肚子想：这种植物不能吃。”
- Expressiveness: Make the comfortable result more vivid by showing a physical change, such as “吃了以后，他觉得身体暖暖的，肚子也不疼了。”

### Encouragement
You clearly introduced Shennong and used a strong contrast between plants that cause pain and plants that make him feel well. The cause-and-result pattern with “吃了以后” also helps the reader understand his experiences.

## Scene 2

> 凉山很大的山，这也找植物。一天，他找到黄色的花。他听说你跟黄色的花做茶很有用。比方说，你的头发疼死，这冰茶是药。他一喝茶就很不舒服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 凉山很大的山：凉山是一座很大的山，A noun phrase needs a verb such as 是 to state what the mountain is; 座 is the matching measure word for a mountain.
- 这也找植物：他也在这儿找植物，The person doing the action must be a character, and a location before the action needs 在 + place.
- 你跟黄色的花做茶很有用：用黄色的花做的茶很有用，跟 means “with/together with” and cannot introduce the material used to make tea; 用 introduces the material, and 的 turns the verb phrase into a modifier for 茶.
- 你的头发疼死：你头疼得要死，头发 means “hair,” which cannot feel pain; 头疼 describes a headache, and 得要死 is a degree complement.
- 这冰茶：这杯冰茶，This/that + measure word + noun is required when referring to one drink.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很大 -- “凉山是一座很大的山”：高大，specifies that the mountain is both tall and large in scale.
- 有用 -- “用黄色的花做的茶很有用”：有药用价值，states more precisely that the tea is useful because it may have a medicinal effect.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 他找到黄色的花：他找到了黄色的花，This is a completed, one-time discovery, so 了 follows the resultative complement 到.
- Expressiveness: At the point where the flower is found, add a visual detail to make the setting clearer: “黄色的花又大又漂亮，像星星一样。”
- Expressiveness: When he feels ill after drinking the tea, name a specific physical feeling: “他的肚子疼了起来，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought to show his surprise: “他想：这不是有药用价值的茶吗？为什么我这么难受？”

### Encouragement
- You clearly connected the yellow flower, tea, and the character’s physical reaction, so the scene already has a strong cause-and-effect direction.
- You also used the useful pattern “一……就……” correctly in “他一喝茶就很不舒服.”

## Scene 3

> 糟糕，这黄色的花是夹竹桃，特别是毒。正好在这儿，灵芝药的夹竹桃他吃完了，也写一个课文的植物。谢谢神农。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 特别是毒：特别有毒，特别是 must introduce a typical example noun or clause; it cannot modify 毒 as an adjective.
- 正好在这儿，灵芝药的夹竹桃他吃完了：这儿正好有灵芝；灵芝是药。夹竹桃他吃完了，The original places unrelated noun phrases together and does not form a clear predicate. The revision states that lingzhi is present, identifies it as medicine, and then keeps the completed action separate.
- 一个课文的植物：一篇关于植物的文章，课文 means a textbook passage, not a piece of writing created by the character; 一篇……文章 correctly names an article about plants.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 黄色 -- “这黄色的花”：金黄色，identifies a brighter, more specific shade of yellow and makes the flower easier to picture.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 也写：也写了，The writing is presented as a completed action after eating, so it needs 了.
- Expressiveness: After the first sentence, add a visual detail about the flower: “黄色的花像星星一样，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: When the character feels ill, add an inner thought: “神农肚子疼得不得了，心想：我会死吗？”
- Expressiveness: When introducing lingzhi, describe its shape: “灵芝长得像一把小伞。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly identified the important plants, 夹竹桃 and 灵芝, and you used 糟糕 effectively to show that the moment is dangerous.
