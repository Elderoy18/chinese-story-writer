## Scene 1

> 孟子小的时候，从早到晚都跟朋友玩。孟子和他的朋友喜欢玩在墓地里，装祭拜。孟子五岁的时候，跟妈妈搬家。他们搬家不是因为孟子玩得太多，而是因为妈妈认为他们的家离墓地太近。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 喜欢玩在墓地里：喜欢在墓地里玩，The location phrase should come before the action verb it modifies.
- 装祭拜：假装祭拜，装 does not naturally take an action such as 祭拜 as its object here; 假装 means to pretend to perform an action.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 玩 -- “从早到晚都跟朋友玩”：嬉戏，specifies cheerful, childlike play with friends.
- 玩得太多 -- “不是因为孟子玩得太多”：玩得过头，more precisely shows that the playing exceeded an appropriate limit.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子小的时候：有一个小男孩叫孟子。孟子小的时候，On first mention, introduce the character with both a full noun phrase and a name, rather than assuming the reader already knows who 孟子 is.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和他的朋友：他和朋友，孟子 was named in the immediately preceding sentence, so a pronoun is more natural for a close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 跟妈妈搬家：跟妈妈搬了家，This is one completed move at age five, so the last verb in the action phrase takes 了.
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery sentence, add a concrete detail about what the children copy, such as: “他和朋友一边笑，一边学着大人低头祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: At the final sentence, show the mother’s feeling with a brief thought or quotation, such as: “妈妈皱着眉头想：‘这儿会影响孩子。’”
- Expressiveness: Describe the cemetery’s atmosphere before the children play there, such as: “墓地里常常有人安静地祭拜，也有人伤心地哭。”

### Encouragement
You clearly explain the reason for the move with the effective contrast structure “不是……而是……”. You also use “从早到晚都” naturally to show that Mengzi played with his friends for a long time each day.

## Scene 2

> 妈妈认为，住离墓地太近，害得孟子的心气。孟子和孟母搬到子思老师的家，跟子思老师住。孟母帮子思打扫房间和做饭。子思帮孟母教她的儿子。因为孟子现在没有朋友，他老是读书。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 住离墓地太近，害得孟子的心气：住在离墓地太近的地方，会影响孟子的学习，“住在” needs a location after it, and “害得” must be followed by a person plus a harmful result; “孟子的心气” is not a complete harmful result.
- 搬到子思老师的家：搬到了子思老师的家，the move is presented as a completed narrative action, so the verb needs 了.
- 老是读书：一直看书学习，“老是” often suggests an annoying or repeated action, while “一直” describes sustained studying in this context.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 朋友 -- “孟子现在没有朋友”：玩伴，specifies that he has no companions to play with, which connects naturally to the earlier context of playing with friends.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 跟子思老师住：跟他住，after “子思老师” has just been introduced in the same sentence and no other male person competes as the referent, use a pronoun rather than repeating the name.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为孟子现在没有朋友，他一直看书学习：因为孟子现在没有朋友，所以他一直看书学习，use the paired connector to make the cause-and-effect relationship explicit.
- Expressiveness: After the final sentence, add Mengzi’s feeling about losing his playmates, such as: “孟子觉得有一点孤单。”
- Expressiveness: When describing the new home, add one simple setting detail to show why studying is easier there, such as: “子思老师的家很安静。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech from Mengmu after she sees him studying, such as: “孟母说：‘你要认真读书。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly show how the adults support Mengzi: Mengmu helps at home, and Zisi teaches him. You also use a clear cause for Mengzi’s change from playing with friends to studying.
