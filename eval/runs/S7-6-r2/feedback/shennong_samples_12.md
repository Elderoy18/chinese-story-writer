## Scene 1

> 很久以前，古代的人很不知道什么植物能吃，也什么植物不能吃。所以很多人常常吃不安全的植物，吃不安全的植物对你的身体有坏处，很麻烦的情况。但是一个人，神农，要解决这个问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很不知道：不知道，The adverb 很 does not naturally modify the negative mental-state verb phrase 不知道; use the verb phrase without 很.
- 也什么植物不能吃：什么植物不能吃，Also cannot directly introduce a noun phrase or clause here; it must be followed by a verb phrase.
- 很麻烦的情况：是很麻烦的情况，A noun phrase cannot serve as the predicate after the comma by itself here; add 是 to link the situation to its description.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不安全 -- “不安全的植物”：有毒的，identifies the specific danger as poison；有害的，states directly that the plants can harm the body without necessarily saying they are poisonous

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 但是一个人，神农，要解决这个问题。：但是，一个叫神农的人要解决这个问题。，On first mention, introduce the character with a full noun phrase that includes the name, rather than placing the name after a vague noun.
- Coherence (Reference): 对你的身体有坏处：对人们的身体有坏处，The pronoun 你 has no clear referent in this third-person story; repeat the noun for the affected group.
- Expressiveness: After the sentence about unsafe plants, add a concrete physical result, such as “很多人吃了以后常常肚子疼。” This makes the danger easier for the reader to imagine.
- Expressiveness: At the point where 神农 wants to help, add his thought: “神农想：我要找出哪些植物能吃。” This gives the character a clear purpose and voice.

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish the central problem: people do not know which plants are safe to eat. You also introduce 神农 as someone who wants to solve that problem, which gives the scene a strong direction.

## Scene 2

> 他又聪明又努力，旅行很远找到很多植物。比方说，灵芝和夹竹桃。他一吃各种各样的植物就知道能不能吃这些植物。然后，神农写他的主意。他喜欢植物的味道，特别是花。神农遇到了一个黄色的花，一吃就觉得不舒服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 旅行很远：到很远的地方旅行，The verb phrase needs a destination expression; use a place after 到 rather than placing a distance directly after 旅行.
- 一个黄色的花：一朵黄色的花，朵 is the matching measure word for a flower.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不舒服 -- “一吃就觉得不舒服”：肚子疼，specifies the physical symptom after eating the flower；难受，emphasizes the character’s uncomfortable feeling more directly.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农写他的主意：他写他的主意，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the previous sentence already clearly refers to the same character and no other character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农遇到了一个黄色的花：他遇到了一朵黄色的花，Use a pronoun for this close re-occurrence because the preceding sentences still clearly refer to the same character.
- Coherence (Temporality): 找到很多植物：找到了很多植物，Finding the plants is a completed action in this past narrative, so it needs 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 然后，神农写他的主意：然后，他写下了自己的主意，This is the next completed event in the sequence, so use the completed form 写下了; 下 shows that the idea was successfully recorded.
- Expressiveness: After the flower appears, add a visual detail to make it memorable, such as “那朵花又大又漂亮，像星星一样。”
- Expressiveness: At the moment of illness, describe a specific physical reaction, such as “他的肚子疼了起来，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought to show the character’s fear, such as “他想：我会死吗？”

### Encouragement
- You used the immediate-result pattern effectively to show that eating the plants helps the character learn about them. Your scene also gives a clear sequence from travelling, to finding plants, to becoming ill.

## Scene 3

> 发现了夹竹桃有毒。别人看到他，很快给他吃一个灵芝。神农一吃就觉得比较好。然后，他继续写他发现的信息。会告诉他的朋友哪一个植物能不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别人：一个人，别人 distinguishes other people from an already mentioned person or group; this new helper should be introduced as an indefinite person.
- 给他吃一个灵芝：给了他一个灵芝，the giving is a completed action in the story, so the verb needs 了.
- 写他发现的信息：记下他发现的信息，记下 means to record newly discovered information so it can be kept, while 写 is too general for this recording action.
- 哪一个植物能不能吃：哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃，哪一个 selects one item, but the sentence needs to distinguish the two groups of plants: those that can be eaten and those that cannot.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 比较好 -- “神农一吃就觉得比较好”：好一些了，more clearly shows that his condition improved after he ate the medicine.
- 很快 -- “别人看到他，很快给他吃一个灵芝”：立刻，emphasizes that the helper acted without delay.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: When the helper appears, add one visual detail so the reader can picture this new person: “一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人看到神农。”
- Expressiveness: At the moment before the medicine helps, show Shennong’s physical discomfort: “神农捂着肚子，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: After he feels better, add a short question to make the scene more lively: “神农问：‘这是什么植物？’”

### Encouragement
You use the pattern 一……就…… effectively to show that the medicine works immediately. The pronouns after 神农 are also mostly clear, and 然后 gives the actions a clear order.
