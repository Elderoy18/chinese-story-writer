## Scene 1

> 这是古代的故事。孟子小的时候，他的爸爸很早就去世了。从他的家到墓地很近，所以他每天老是从早到晚去墓地跟朋友们玩玩儿。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 从他的家到墓地很近：他的家离墓地很近，Static proximity is expressed with 离 + place + 很近; 从……到…… describes a route or distance instead.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 小 -- “孟子小的时候”：年幼，more specifically shows that Mengzi was still a child.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子小的时候，他的爸爸很早就去世了：有一个小男孩叫孟子；他小的时候，爸爸很早就去世了，On first mention, introduce the character with a full noun phrase and name; after that, 他 can refer clearly to Mengzi.
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery is mentioned, add what Mengzi sees there to make the setting more concrete, for example: “他每天看到很多人来墓地祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: At the end of the scene, add Mengzi’s reaction or action to show why he enjoys going there, for example: “孟子觉得很有趣，也学着别人祭拜。”

### Encouragement
You clearly establish the time, Mengzi’s family situation, and the reason he often goes to the cemetery. The routine expression with “每天” and “从早到晚” gives the scene a strong sense of his daily life.

## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈觉得在墓地玩儿，害得，所以他们搬家。搬家对孟子有好的影响，他才五岁，可是每天不是写字就是帮妈妈的工作。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 害得：害得她老是道歉，The verb requires the person affected plus a harmful result; it cannot stand alone here.
- 帮妈妈的工作：帮妈妈工作，The verb phrase should use the person after the verb and the action as its complement; the original noun phrase does not fit the verb’s required structure.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好 -- “有好的影响”：积极的，shows more specifically that the move supports his development；有益的，emphasizes that it brings him benefit.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 所以他们搬家：所以他们搬家了，This move is presented as a completed event, so the final verb in the action sequence needs 了.
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery activity, add a concrete detail such as “孟子常常学着别人祭拜，一边笑一边玩儿。” This helps the reader picture what he does there.
- Expressiveness: Show the mother’s worry with an internal thought or quoted speech, for example: “再住下去，麻烦会越来越多。” This makes her reason for moving more vivid.

### Encouragement
- You used the contrast pattern “他才五岁，可是……” effectively to show that Mengzi is very young but already has responsibilities. Your scene also clearly connects the move to its effect on him.

## Scene 3

> 他很聪明的人，难怪他成为有名的人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他很聪明的人：他很聪明，An adjective such as “smart” can directly function as the predicate; adding “的人” without a linking structure makes the original predicate incomplete.
- 成为：成为了，Use 了 after 成为 to show that the change into a famous person was completed.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 难怪他成为了有名的人：难怪成为了有名的人，The subject has already been named in the same sentence, so use zero form rather than repeating the pronoun.
- Expressiveness: After the first clause, add a concrete sign of intelligence, such as “他喜欢看书，也常常认真思考”.
- Expressiveness: Make the final achievement more vivid by adding other people’s reaction, such as “后来，很多人都来向他请教问题”.

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect the character’s intelligence with his later success, and “难怪” creates a useful cause-and-result relationship.
