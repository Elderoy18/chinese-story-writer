## Scene 1

> 神农和他的家人住在古代，所以不知道什么植物能吃。因为他的家人常常生病了，所以神农和他的哥哥一起去旅行找可以吃的植物。他们去很多不一样的地方，比方说高山、山谷和沙漠。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 住在古代：在古代生活，An era is a time expression, not a place where someone can live; use the time phrase before the action.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 找 -- “一起去旅行找可以吃的植物”：寻找, emphasizes a deliberate search for plants；探寻, highlights exploring in order to discover unknown plants.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：一位叫神农的老人，At this character’s first mention, introduce him with a full noun phrase so the reader knows who he is.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们去很多不一样的地方：他们去了很多不一样的地方，This describes completed travel during the journey, so use 了 after the verb.
- Expressiveness: After the list of places, add one visual detail about the difficult journey, such as “高山又高又冷，沙漠又热又干。”
- Expressiveness: Add a brief feeling for the two brothers while they search, such as “神农很担心家人的身体，可是他没有放弃。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of dialogue before the journey, such as “神农对哥哥说：‘我们一定要找到能吃的植物。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly used cause-and-effect connectors to show why the journey begins. The specific list of mountains, valleys, and deserts also makes the journey feel broad and adventurous.

## Scene 2

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在很漂亮的河找：在很漂亮的河边找，A river itself is not normally used as the location for this action; add the localizer to show that the search happens beside the river.
- 接近知道：想知道答案，The verb 接近 means “approach” in distance or degree and cannot directly modify the mental-state verb 知道; this revision also makes the two alternatives parallel.
- 了。“：了。”，Direct speech needs a closing quotation mark after the final punctuation mark.
- 太麻烦了。：太麻烦了。”，The final direct-speech quotation needs its closing quotation mark.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the river setting, add a visual detail about the flower: “河边开着黄色的、像星星一样的花。”
- Expressiveness: When the illness begins, describe Shen Nong’s physical feeling more vividly: “他肚子疼了起来，在地上躺着，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: At the brother’s final line, show his worry through dialogue: “哥哥着急地说：‘你怎么样？’”

### Encouragement

- The dialogue clearly shows Shen Nong asking for advice and his brother giving an answer.

## Scene 3

> 夹竹桃是毒的，我想知道灵芝会帮他。”哥哥给神农灵芝。神农吃完了以后开始痊愈了。神农说：“谢谢哥哥，你帮得真好。灵芝我们可以吃，特别是如果我们已经吃了夹竹桃。“

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 夹竹桃是毒的：夹竹桃是有毒的，Use 有毒 to describe the poisonous property of a plant; 毒 alone is a noun and does not fit this description here.
- 夹竹桃。“：夹竹桃。”，The quotation must end with a closing quotation mark, not an opening quotation mark.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 我想知道灵芝会帮他：我想知道灵芝会帮神农，Both the brother and 神农 are present in the nearby context, so 他 does not clearly identify the person whom 灵芝 may help; repeat the name for clarity.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农说：他说，神农 was the subject of the immediately preceding sentence, and no new competing character appears there, so a pronoun is more natural for this close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 哥哥给神农灵芝：哥哥给了神农灵芝，The giving is a completed event, so 给 needs 了.
- Expressiveness: After the recovery, add a concrete change in his body to show that the medicine worked, such as “神农的肚子慢慢不疼了。”
- Expressiveness: Add a worried thought before he eats 灵芝 to make the danger clearer, such as “神农心想：我会不会死？”
- Expressiveness: Make the thanks more emotional by adding his feeling, such as “神农高兴地说：‘谢谢哥哥！我现在好多了。’”

### Encouragement
- You used a clear time sequence with 吃完了以后, and 痊愈 is a precise word for recovering from illness.
- The dialogue between 神农 and his brother helps show the characters’ actions and their relationship.
