## Scene 1

> 古代，孟子跟孟母住在墓地的旁边。可是孟母注意，住在墓地的旁边影响孟子。他常常跟朋友们祭拜玩，所以孟母决定他们会搬家。这次，他们住在城市里的商店的旁边，从早到晚很吵。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 注意：注意到，The resultative complement 到 shows that Meng’s mother became aware of the following fact.
- 跟朋友们祭拜玩：跟朋友们玩祭拜的游戏，玩 needs an object here; the revised phrase makes the activity a game.
- 所以孟母决定他们会搬家：所以孟母决定搬家，决定 takes a verb phrase directly, so the repeated reference and future clause are unnecessary.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 吵 -- “从早到晚很吵”：嘈杂，more precisely describes a place filled with many mixed, unpleasant sounds.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: At the cemetery setting, add one concrete sight or sound to make the place vivid: “墓地里常常有人哭，也有人烧纸。”
- Expressiveness: After the children’s game, add Meng’s mother’s feeling: “孟母看见了，觉得很着急。”
- Expressiveness: At the shops, show why the area is noisy with specific actions: “商人一直叫卖，客人讨价还价。”

### Encouragement
You clearly establish the two main characters and use location phrases effectively. The cause-and-effect connection leading to the decision to move also makes the scene easy to follow.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 陪 -- “害得孟母只好一直陪孟子”：照看，emphasizes that Meng’s mother is watching over and protecting him because she is worried about his safety.
- 不高兴 -- “他老是看起来不高兴”：闷闷不乐，describes a continuing, visibly unhappy mood more vividly.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 孟子也没有朋友，难怪他老是看起来不高兴：难怪孟子老是看起来不高兴，原来他没有朋友，the pattern places the surprising result after 难怪 and introduces its reason with 原来.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 住在商店的旁边不是适合孟子，而是让他觉得很伤心：住在商店的旁边不适合孟子，会让他觉得很伤心，the two ideas show a negative effect rather than correcting a conventional belief with “不是……而是……”.
- Expressiveness: After “城市也有点儿危险”, add one concrete detail about the surroundings, such as “街上人来人往，马车跑得很快”, to show why the place feels unsafe.
- Expressiveness: At “害得孟母只好一直陪孟子”, add Meng’s mother’s worried thought, such as “孟母心想：‘我不能让孟子一个人出去。’”
- Expressiveness: After “孟子也没有朋友”, show his feeling through an action, such as “他一个人坐在门口，看着别的孩子一起玩儿。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect the busy city environment to Meng Zi’s loneliness and Meng’s mother’s worry. Your use of “只好” effectively shows that she has little choice.

## Scene 3

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 才五岁孟子开始学哲学：孟子才五岁就开始学哲学，The pattern for an action that starts at an unusually early age is “才 + age + 就 + verb.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬到学校的旁边住：搬到了学校的旁边住，The move is a completed event, so the verb needs 了 to mark completion.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为：成为了，This is the completed result of Mengzi’s development, so 了 marks the change of state.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 将来：后来，In a historical narration, this event happens after the school scene, so 后来 is the sequence connector rather than a future-time prediction.
- Expressiveness: At the school scene, add a small detail about Mengzi’s focused study, such as “孟子坐在窗边，认真地看书。”
- Expressiveness: You could show Mengmu’s happiness through a short line of speech after she sees him studying: “孟母高兴地说：‘这儿真适合你学习！’”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the important change from the noisy city to a school environment. The paired structure “不是……，就是……” is used well to describe Mengzi’s new daily routine.
