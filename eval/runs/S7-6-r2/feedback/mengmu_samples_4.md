## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，孟子和孟母住了在靠墓地的家。孩子才五岁就每天在墓地祭拜了。在那个城市，孟母从早到晚都找工作，可是找不到，害得孟母没有钱。难怪她跟孩子搬家，原来买不了房。他们搬了到别一个城市找工作。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，The original redundantly combines two time expressions: the historical-period noun already expresses the time meaning.
- 跟孩子搬家：带着孩子搬家，This action means that the mother takes her child along when moving; the original phrase suggests that they move together as equal participants.
- 别一个城市：另一个城市，Use this phrase to mean one different city; the original combination is not the standard way to express “another one.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 住了在：住在，This is a continuing living situation, so it takes the zero particle rather than completed-action 了; the locative marker must directly follow the verb.
- Coherence (Temporality): 每天在墓地祭拜了：每天在墓地祭拜，A daily routine takes the zero particle because it is habitual rather than a completed single action.
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬了到：搬到了，The resultative complement comes before 了: the move is completed only after reaching the new place.
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery activity, add what the child finds interesting and does, such as: `孟子觉得祭拜很有趣，跟着朋友学别人祭拜。`
- Expressiveness: At the point where the mother cannot find work, show her emotion: `孟母很着急，不知道该怎么办。`
- Expressiveness: Before the move, add the mother’s inner thought or speech: `孟母心想：“再住下去，麻烦会越来越多。”`

### Encouragement
You used `才五岁就` effectively to emphasize how young the child is. You also used the `难怪……，原来……` pattern well to connect the move with its reason.

## Scene 2

> 这个城市老是很吵，不是人吵架，就是都叫，害得孟子一直不能睡觉。在那儿，孟母也找不到工作。孟子和妈妈要又一个次搬了家。在第三个城市，孟母找到工作，在商店做衣服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不是人吵架，就是都叫：不是有人吵架，就是有人大叫，The two alternatives need parallel subject–verb clauses. “有人” introduces an unspecified person in each noisy event, while “都” cannot stand alone here as the subject of the second clause.
- 要又一个次搬：要再搬一次，For a move that has not happened yet, use “再,” not “又,” and the pattern is modal verb + 再 + verb + numeral + measure word.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 吵 -- “这个城市老是很吵”：嘈杂，specifies that the city is full of mixed, disturbing sounds；吵闹，emphasizes lively but unpleasant noise from people.
- 做 -- “在商店做衣服”：缝制，specifies making clothes by sewing；制作，emphasizes producing the clothes as part of her work.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬了家：搬家，After the modal verb “要,” the future action takes the zero particle, not “了.”
- Coherence (Temporality): 孟母找到工作：孟母找到了工作，Finding the job is a completed event, so the verb needs “了.”
- Coherence (Conjunction): 在那儿，孟母也找不到工作。孟子和妈妈要再搬一次家：在那儿，孟母也找不到工作，所以孟子和妈妈要再搬一次家，“所以” clearly links the mother’s inability to find work with the decision to move again as cause and effect.
- Expressiveness: After the description of the noisy city, add one concrete sound detail, such as “晚上还有人在街上大叫。”
- Expressiveness: Show Mengzi’s feeling about being unable to sleep, such as “孟子很累，也很烦。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of inner thought for Mengmu before the move, such as “孟母心想：‘这儿不适合孩子住。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show two important problems in the city—noise and difficulty finding work—and “害得孟子一直不能睡觉” effectively explains the effect on Mengzi.

## Scene 3

> 孟子在那个商店也找了到工作，可是不是做衣服的工作，而是喜欢的工作。孟子学习馆学了十五年，他的学习和他的旅行经验很重要，对他很多好处。他是大人的时候，他成为很有名的哲人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 学习馆学了十五年：在学校学习了十五年，The location should be introduced with a location phrase, and “学校” is the usual word for the place where formal learning happens.
- 对他很多好处：对他有很多好处，This expression requires the verb “有”: something “has benefits for” someone.
- 是大人的时候：长大以后，“是大人” is not a natural time expression here; “长大以后” clearly marks the time after he grew up.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他成为很有名的哲人：成为很有名的哲人，After the first pronoun already refers to Mengzi and no other male character intervenes, use zero form for the same subject in the next clause.
- Coherence (Temporality): 找了到：找到了，With a resultative complement, the completion marker follows the complement: “找到” becomes “找到了.”
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为：成为了，Becoming a famous philosopher is presented as a completed change of state in the past, so it needs “了.”
- Expressiveness: After the long period of study, add a concrete learning detail, such as: “他每天安静地看书，也向老师请教问题。”
