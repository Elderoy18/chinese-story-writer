## Scene 3

> 可是夹竹桃的毒很重，让他肚子很痛。他在地上躺着，觉得他快会死了。可是正好有别人找到他了，给了他一个像伞的植物，叫灵芝。他吃灵芝以后就觉得很好，不再生病了。他测量起来了，像星星的植物是毒，像伞的植物是药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 快会死了：快要死了，The future auxiliary does not combine naturally with this form of imminent timing; use the imminent marker before the verb to show that death feels very near.
- 别人：一个人，This word distinguishes other people from a person already being contrasted; here the rescuer is being introduced for the first time, so use an indefinite noun phrase.
- 是毒：有毒，Use this predicate to describe a plant as poisonous; the original form identifies the plant itself as poison rather than describing its property.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很痛 -- “让他肚子很痛”：疼得厉害，gives a more vivid description of how severe the stomach pain is.
- 很好 -- “就觉得很好”：好多了，shows clearly that his condition has improved after eating the medicine.
- 一个 -- “给了他一个像伞的植物”：一株，uses a more precise measure word for an individual plant.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃灵芝以后：吃了灵芝以后，The eating is a completed action before the later feeling, so this time phrase needs 了.
- Expressiveness: At the moment of pain, add a physical reaction to make the danger vivid, for example: “他疼得直冒冷汗。”
- Expressiveness: When the rescuer appears, add one visible detail to introduce this new person, for example: “这时，一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人走了过来。”
- Expressiveness: Add Shennong’s worried thought after he lies down, for example: “他想：‘我会不会死？’”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the cause of Shennong’s pain and the sequence from illness to recovery. You also use the time phrase with “以后” effectively to connect the medicine to its result.
