## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个人叫神农。一天，神农正在路上走，一看很多人正在吃一些植物。一些人一吃植物就生病了，神农想帮别人。所以想知道哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，This phrase redundantly combines a historical time period with a second “time of” expression; use the shorter time expression.
- 一看很多人：看到很多人，Here the intended meaning is “see many people,” not “look at many people,” so the resultative complement is needed.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 想知道 -- “所以想知道哪些植物能吃”：弄清楚，emphasizes finding a clear answer about which plants are safe to eat.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After people become ill, add visible symptoms to make the danger concrete: “有的人捂着肚子，脸色发白。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where Shen Nong decides to help, add his feeling and inner voice: “神农心里很着急，想：‘我一定要找出能吃的植物。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the cause-and-effect sequence between eating plants and becoming ill. The ongoing-action forms also help the reader picture Shen Nong walking and noticing the people.

## Scene 2

> 然后，神农开始尝很多植物，比方说他吃过生姜、芥兰和菠菜。一些植物吃真好，特别是黄瓜。但是，一些植物毒得很，特别是夹竹桃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一些植物吃真好：一些植物很好吃，Use the adjective predicate 好吃 to describe a plant’s taste; 吃 cannot be followed directly by 真好 with this meaning.
- 毒得很：有毒，毒 cannot take the degree complement 得很; use 有毒 to describe a poisonous plant.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 吃过 -- “比方说他吃过生姜、芥兰和菠菜”：尝过, emphasizes sampling the plants as part of an experiment or investigation.
- 很多 -- “神农开始尝很多植物”：各种各样的, makes clear that he tested many different kinds of plants.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After listing the plants, add one sensory detail to show their different tastes, such as “生姜有一点儿辣，黄瓜吃起来很清爽。”
- Expressiveness: After the poisonous-plant sentence, add Shennong’s purpose or thought, such as “神农想：我要弄清楚哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃。”

### Encouragement
You used a clear contrast between plants that taste good and plants that are poisonous. Your specific examples of vegetables and plants also make the scene easy to picture.

## Scene 3

> 一个人对神农说，麻烦我们可以吃什么植物？神农说，这就要看你想不想生病了。以后，神农告诉别人一些植物能吃，一些植物不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 麻烦我们可以吃什么植物：请问，我们可以吃什么植物，麻烦 must introduce a request that someone performs, such as asking someone to explain something; it cannot directly introduce this question.
- 以后：后来，when a new past event begins “afterwards,” use 后来; 以后 normally follows a stated time or event.
- 别人：人们，别人 distinguishes other people from a previously mentioned person, while this sentence means people in general.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 说 -- “一个人对神农说”：问（改为“一个人问神农”），identifies the speech precisely as a question rather than a general statement.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After 神农’s reply, add a short reason to make his knowledge clearer, such as “有些植物有毒，吃了会生病。”
- Expressiveness: Add the speaker’s worried feeling before the question, such as “那个人很担心，问神农：‘请问，我们可以吃什么植物？’”

### Encouragement
The question-and-answer dialogue is easy to follow. You also used 这就要看……了 naturally in 神农’s reply.
