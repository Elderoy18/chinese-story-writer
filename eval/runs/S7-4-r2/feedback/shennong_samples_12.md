## Scene 1

> 很久以前，古代的人很不知道什么植物能吃，也什么植物不能吃。所以很多人常常吃不安全的植物，吃不安全的植物对你的身体有坏处，很麻烦的情况。但是一个人，神农，要解决这个问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很不知道：不太知道，Very is not normally used directly before the negative form of a mental-state verb such as 知道; 不太知道 expresses limited knowledge naturally.
- 也什么植物不能吃：什么植物不能吃，Also must be followed by a verb phrase; here it cannot directly introduce the noun phrase 什么植物.
- 很麻烦的情况：这种情况很麻烦，A noun phrase with an adjective modifier cannot stand alone as the final predicate; this version makes 情况 the subject and 很麻烦 its predicate.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不安全 -- “不安全的植物”：有毒的，specifies that the plants are unsafe because they contain poison.
- 有坏处 -- “对你的身体有坏处”：会伤害身体，states the physical harm more directly and vividly in this context.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一个 人，神农：一个叫神农的人，On a character’s first mention, use a clear noun phrase that both identifies and names the character.
- Coherence (Reference): 你的身体：人们的身体，The narration is about many people, not directly addressing the reader; repeat the appropriate noun phrase to keep the reference clear.
- Expressiveness: After the mention of harmful plants, add a concrete physical result, such as “很多人吃了以后，肚子疼得厉害。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where 神农 appears, add his purpose as an internal thought: “神农想：我要找出能做药的植物。”
- Expressiveness: You could make the ancient setting more visual before introducing 神农, for example: “高山上长着各种各样的植物。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish the main problem—people do not know which plants are safe—and introduce 神农’s goal to solve it.
- Your use of 所以 successfully shows a cause-and-effect connection between people’s lack of knowledge and the danger they face.

## Scene 2

> 他又聪明又努力，旅行很远找到很多植物。比方说，灵芝和夹竹桃。他一吃各种各样的植物就知道能不能吃这些植物。然后，神农写他的主意。他喜欢植物的味道，特别是花。神农遇到了一个黄色的花，一吃就觉得不舒服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 旅行很远：去很远的地方旅行，A travel destination needs a place phrase such as 去很远的地方; 很远 alone cannot serve as the destination after 旅行.
- 写：写下，To mean recording an idea so that it is kept, use 写下 rather than the plain action verb 写.
- 一个黄色的花：一朵黄色的花，朵 is the matching measure word for flowers; 个 is too general here.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很多 -- “找到很多植物”：许多种，specifies that he found many different kinds of plants rather than only a large number of individual plants.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农写他的主意：他写他的主意，Use a pronoun when the same character was mentioned in the immediately previous sentence and no other character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农遇到了一个黄色的花：他遇到了一朵黄色的花，Use a pronoun for the same continuing character when the previous sentence already refers clearly to him.
- Coherence (Temporality): 找到很多植物：找到了很多植物，Finding the plants is a completed action in this past narrative, so it needs 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 神农写他的主意：他写下了他的主意，Writing down the idea is presented as a completed narrative action, so 写下 takes 了.
- Expressiveness: After the plants are introduced, add one visual detail to make them easier to imagine: “灵芝像一把小伞，夹竹桃开着黄色的花。”
- Expressiveness: At the moment he feels ill, add a physical feeling or a thought: “他肚子疼了起来，心想：‘我会不会生病？’”

### Encouragement
You used “一……就……” correctly to show that tasting a plant quickly leads to knowing whether it can be eaten. Giving examples with “比方说” also helps make the plants in the scene more concrete.

## Scene 3

> 发现了夹竹桃有毒。别人看到他，很快给他吃一个灵芝。神农一吃就觉得比较好。然后，他继续写他发现的信息。会告诉他的朋友哪一个植物能不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别人：一个男人，别人 distinguishes other people from an already established speaker or group; no such group has been established here, so introduce the new helper with a noun phrase.
- 给他吃一个灵芝：给了他一个灵芝吃，this is a completed rescue action, so the verb 给 needs 了.
- 写他发现的信息：写下他发现的信息，写 means to write or compose, while 写下 means to record information so it is kept.
- 哪一个植物能不能吃：哪一种植物能吃，哪一种植物不能吃，哪一个 asks which single item, while 能不能 asks a yes-or-no question; use parallel clauses to explain which plants are edible and which are not.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 发现 -- “发现了夹竹桃有毒”：了解到，emphasizes learning the fact after trying the plant；查明，emphasizes determining clearly that the plant is poisonous.
- 比较好 -- “神农一吃就觉得比较好”：好一点了，shows a small improvement in his condition after eating the medicine.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: At “神农一吃就觉得比较好,” add a physical detail to show his relief, for example: “肚子没有那么疼了。”
- Expressiveness: At the helper’s first appearance, add one visible detail to make him memorable, for example: “一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人看到他。”
- Expressiveness: After the discovery of the poison, add Shennong’s thought to show his realization, for example: “原来夹竹桃有毒，不能吃！”

### Encouragement
You clearly showed the important sequence of discovery, help, recovery, and recording information. Your use of “一……就……” effectively shows that Shennong felt better immediately after eating the medicine.
