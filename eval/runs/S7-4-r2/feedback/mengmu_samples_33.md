## Scene 1

> 一天，很久很久以前，孟子和他妈妈住在中国。孟子妈妈的工作是祭拜，孟子不高兴，因为他说我们老是搬家，为什么？他妈妈说，从早到晚，我一直祭拜，很多墓地得我祭拜。所以我们得去很多不同的地方。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一天，很久很久以前：很久很久以前的一天，The two time expressions should be combined so that the longer time period modifies the specific day.
- 因为他说我们老是搬家，为什么？：因为他说：“我们老是搬家，为什么？”，A direct question introduced by 说 should be marked as quoted speech with a colon and quotation marks.
- 从早到晚，我一直祭拜，很多墓地得我祭拜：从早到晚，我得去很多墓地祭拜，A 墓地 is the place where the worship happens, rather than the object being worshipped; the person with the obligation should be the subject before 得.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 地方 -- “所以我们得去很多不同的地方”：各地，more specifically conveys going to many separate locations.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子妈妈的工作：他妈妈的工作，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence: the mother was just mentioned in the preceding sentence, and no other mother competes as the referent.
- Expressiveness: At the point where Mengzi complains, add a small physical detail to show his feeling, such as “孟子皱着眉头，觉得很委屈。”
- Expressiveness: When the mother explains her work, add a concrete detail about her long day, such as “天还没亮，妈妈就出门了，天黑才回来。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show a cause for Mengzi’s unhappiness and give his mother a response, which makes the exchange easy to follow. You also use “从早到晚” effectively to show how demanding her work is.

## Scene 2

> 孟子想，难怪我们常常搬家，原来我妈妈很忙。可是常常搬家害得孟子很难找到朋友。每次搬家孟子也觉得新的地方不是太热就是太冷，不是太大就是太小。在哪的地方，他都不高兴。孟子想帮助妈妈祭拜，可是他六岁，所以不能祭拜。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在哪的地方：在哪个地方，哪 cannot directly modify 地方 in this construction; use the measure word 个 between it and the noun.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 常常 -- “可是常常搬家害得孟子很难找到朋友”：频繁，specifies that the moves happen many times and close together.
- 新的 -- “孟子也觉得新的地方不是太热就是太冷”：陌生的，emphasizes that each place is unfamiliar to him after moving.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子 -- “可是常常搬家害得孟子很难找到朋友”：他，Mengzi was the clear focus of the preceding sentence, so a pronoun makes the close re-occurrence smoother.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子 -- “每次搬家孟子也觉得新的地方不是太热就是太冷”：他，use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when no other possible referent has intervened.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子 -- “孟子想帮助妈妈祭拜”：他，after the previous sentence already refers clearly to Mengzi as 他, a pronoun is more natural for the close re-occurrence.
- Expressiveness: After the description of the temperatures, add a physical reaction to show why he dislikes the places, such as “太热的时候，他满头大汗；太冷的时候，他冷得发抖。”
- Expressiveness: At the end, add a short line of dialogue to show Mengzi’s wish to help, such as “妈妈，我也想跟你一起祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: Add Mengzi’s feeling about being unable to make friends after moving, such as “他觉得很孤单，因为刚认识朋友就要搬走。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show Mengzi’s thoughts and explain why frequent moving is difficult for him. The paired alternatives for the different places create a strong, easy-to-follow contrast.

## Scene 3

> 四年后，孟子开始帮妈妈祭拜。他画画很厉害，他越来越喜欢祭拜，所以他祭拜不是想帮妈妈工作，而是他很喜欢画画。孟子成为很著名的古代画家。今天很多中国人很喜欢孟子。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- Nothing to flag.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很厉害 -- “他画画很厉害”：擅长画画，states more precisely that drawing is Mengzi’s particular skill；画得很好，describes the quality of the pictures more directly.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他画画很厉害，他越来越喜欢祭拜，所以他祭拜：他画画很厉害，越来越喜欢祭拜，所以祭拜，after the subject has been named at the start of the sentence, use zero form for the same subject in the following clauses.
- Coherence (Temporality): 孟子成为很著名的古代画家：孟子成为了很著名的古代画家，this is a completed change of identity in the narrative, so the verb needs 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 不是想帮妈妈工作，而是他很喜欢画画：不是为了帮妈妈工作，而是因为喜欢画画，the paired connector needs a parallel contrast between two motives; 为了 introduces the rejected purpose and 因为 introduces the actual reason.
- Expressiveness: After “他画画很厉害,” add one concrete detail about his drawings, such as “他画的画又整齐又好看。”
- Expressiveness: At “越来越喜欢祭拜,” show Mengzi’s feelings while he takes part, for example: “孟子一边祭拜，一边笑着画画。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought from Mengzi to explain his interest, such as “孟子心想：‘祭拜的时候，我可以画很多东西。’”

### Encouragement
You used “四年后” clearly to show a time jump, and “越来越喜欢” effectively shows that Mengzi’s feelings are changing over time. You also gave him a specific ability, which makes the scene more vivid.
