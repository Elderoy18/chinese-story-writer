## Scene 1

> 很久以前，有一个老男人叫神农。神农在古代每一天看到了别的小镇人生病还是不舒服，所以他决定开始找新的药。他下一天开始爬当地的山脉，找了各种各样的植物。神农试一试每个植物，知道有什么好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别的小镇人：小镇里的人们，The original phrase lacks the possessive marker 的, and 别的 needs an already established town to contrast with; here, the people are being introduced as the people in the town.
- 每个植物：每种植物，Use 种 for each kind of plant; 个 is too general for classifying plant types.
- 找了各种各样的植物：找到了各种各样的植物，找 means “look for,” while 找到 means “find”; the sentence describes plants that he found.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好处 -- “知道有什么好处”：药用价值，specifies the useful medicinal function of each plant in this medicine-searching context

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农试一试每个植物：他试一试每个植物，Use a pronoun when the same character was named recently and no other possible referent has appeared.
- Coherence (Temporality): 每一天看到了：每天看到，A routine action takes the zero particle rather than completed-action 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 试一试：试了试，This is a completed experiment in the past narrative, so the action can take 了.
- Expressiveness: After the mountain-climbing sentence, add a simple visual detail about the setting, such as “山上长着很多绿色的植物。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where he tests the plants, add Shen Nong’s thought to show his purpose: “这些植物能不能做药呢？”

### Encouragement
You introduced Shen Nong clearly at the start, so the reader immediately knows who the main character is. You also used 所以 well to show that seeing sick people led to his decision to search for medicine.

## Scene 2

> 比方说，他一次试一试有些植物的叶，吃得又新鲜又甜。煮这些叶以后，他一喝就知道他发现新的茶叶。虽然他找到了很多的新的植物，但是他的旅程有的时候特别是危险。比方说，他一吃灵芝就开始生病。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他一次试一试有些植物的叶：有一次，他尝了一些植物的叶子，For a past event meaning “on one occasion,” use `有一次`; an action that was actually carried out should not use the tentative `V一V` form, and `尝` is the verb for tasting food.
- 吃得又新鲜又甜：这些叶子又新鲜又甜，`得` introduces a complement describing how someone performs an action, but `新鲜` and `甜` describe the leaves themselves.
- 特别是危险：特别危险，`特别是` introduces a specific example or category, while `特别` directly modifies an adjective such as `危险`.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 新的 -- “找到了很多的新的植物”：陌生的，specifies that these were plants he did not know before；未尝过的，makes clear that he had not tasted these plants before.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 煮这些叶以后：煮了这些叶子以后，The boiling must be completed before the action introduced by `以后`, so it needs completed-action `了`.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他发现新的茶叶：他发现了新的茶叶，The discovery is a completed event in this past sequence, so use `发现了`.
- Coherence (Temporality): 开始生病：开始生病了，This marks the completed change into a sick state after he eats the plant.
- Expressiveness: After the tea is boiled, add a smell or taste detail, such as: `一股清香从锅里飘出来。`
- Expressiveness: At the point where he discovers tea, add his reaction or thought, such as: `神农高兴地想：“这种叶子真特别！”`
- Expressiveness: When the journey becomes dangerous, describe the setting more concretely, such as: `山路又陡又滑，他走得很小心。`

### Encouragement
You used the paired connector `虽然……但是……` correctly to show contrast. You also included concrete actions such as boiling, drinking, and tasting, which helps the scene move forward clearly.

## Scene 3

> 一个爬山的男人正好看到神农的麻烦，然后给了他一些药。神农最终他的小镇回到，跟别的人分享他的发现。你认为神农的故事真的吗？这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 神农的麻烦：神农遇到了麻烦，Use the verb phrase for an event: “遇到麻烦” means that someone encounters a difficulty; the possessive noun phrase is not natural here.
- 跟别的人：跟别人，Without a specific contrast group, “别人” is the natural word for people other than the speaker or subject; “别的人” unnecessarily distinguishes a particular group of people.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 药 -- “给了他一些药”：草药，specifies that the medicine comes from plants and fits the mountain setting.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 最终他的小镇回到：最终回到了他的小镇，Place the destination after “回到,” and add “了” because returning is a completed action before the following sharing action.
- Coherence (Temporality): 这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处：这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处了，This fixed structure ends with “了”; here it is part of the structure rather than a marker of completed action.
- Expressiveness: When the man helps, add one visual detail to make him easier to imagine, such as “一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人正好看到神农遇到了麻烦。”
- Expressiveness: Name the medicine and describe its shape to make the discovery vivid: “他拿起一棵像伞一样的灵芝，给神农吃了。”
- Expressiveness: At the sharing moment, add a short line of speech that explains the discovery: “神农说：‘灵芝可以做药，夹竹桃有毒，不能吃。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly connected the help scene to the ending, showing that Shennong shares what he has learned with others. Your use of “然后” also gives the events a clear sequence.
