### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，The original repeats the time meaning because both parts refer to a time period.
- 去了吃每种植物：去吃了每种植物，With a verb following 去, the completion marker comes after that action, not between 去 and the action.
- 写了：写下了，Use 写下 when recording information so that it is kept; the completion marker follows the resultative verb.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 看到了 -- “他看到了有很多人不知道哪个植物好吃”：发现，emphasizes that Shennong came to realize this problem through observation.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 所以神农：所以，Shennong was already named earlier in the same sentence, with no competing character, so use zero form rather than repeating his name.
- Coherence (Temporality): 有的时候，神农吃了很好吃的植物：有的时候，神农吃很好吃的植物，A habitual action introduced by a routine-time expression uses the zero particle, not 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 有的时候，他吃了很危险的植物：有的时候，他吃很危险的植物，A habitual action introduced by a routine-time expression uses the zero particle, not 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 会来跟他的朋友聊到他吃的植物了：会来跟他的朋友聊到他吃的植物，After the modal verb 会, the predicted action takes the zero particle; the final 了 is not needed.
- Expressiveness: When describing the problem that Shennong notices, add a concrete consequence for the people, such as “很多人吃了有毒的植物，常常生病。”
- Expressiveness: After Shennong decides to try the plants, add his thought to show his determination, such as “神农想：‘我一定要找出哪些植物可以吃。’”
- Expressiveness: Make the dangerous plants more vivid with a small descriptive detail, such as “有的花又大又漂亮，可是吃了以后肚子会疼。”
