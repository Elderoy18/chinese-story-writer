## Scene 1

> 古代的时候人觉得祭拜非常重要，孟子很小的时候，从早到晚一直祭拜，因为他妈妈告诉他祭拜很重要。虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜。他的同学们老是对他开玩笑了。孟子妈妈看了他的同学们对孟子不好的影响，所以孟子和孟子妈妈搬家了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，This expression redundantly combines a historical time period with a “time of” phrase; use the shorter time expression. [vocab list]
- 人：人们，This refers to a group of people in ancient times, so the plural human-group noun is needed. [teacher rule: ren_renmen]
- 看了：看到了，Here the mother perceives the influence; 看 means “look,” while 看到 means “see/perceive.” [vocab list]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子很小的时候：有一个叫孟子的小男孩。他很小的时候，A character’s first appearance should introduce both the name and who the character is, rather than giving only a name. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Temporality): 老是对他开玩笑了：老是对他开玩笑，A habitual action uses the zero particle, not 了, because it describes something that happens repeatedly rather than one completed event. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Conjunction): 虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜。：因为他每天只祭拜，所以他没有朋友。The relationship is cause and effect, so use the paired connector to show that the daily behaviour causes the result. [rule: coherence_connectives]
- Expressiveness: At the classmates’ teasing, add a short line of speech to show what they say: “同学们笑着说：‘你怎么每天都祭拜？’”
- Expressiveness: After the mother sees the harmful influence, add her worried thought before the move: “孟子妈妈很着急，心想：‘这里不适合孟子。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show a repeated daily routine with the time phrase from morning to evening, and you give a reason for why the family decides to move.

## Scene 2

> 孟子才十一岁，成为一个哲学家。孟子不是祭拜，而是看政治文章，难怪他成为了哲学家。不过，看过很多政治文章对他害得，他有很多问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 对他害得，他有很多问题：害得他有很多问题，The pattern is “害得 + person + harmful result”; the affected person follows “得” directly, so “对” cannot be placed before it. [teacher rule: haide_structure]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子不是祭拜：他不是祭拜，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the character was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other person competes as the referent. [rule: none]
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为一个哲学家：成为了一个哲学家，This is a completed change of state in the narrative, so use 了 after the verb. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: After the reading activity, add a physical sign of confusion, such as “他读着读着，皱起了眉头。” This shows his reaction instead of only stating that he has questions.
- Expressiveness: At the point where he has questions, add a short thought or line of speech, such as “这些文章为什么这样说呢？” This makes his curiosity more immediate.

### Encouragement
You used the contrast pattern effectively to show that Mengzi had changed from one activity to another. The final sentence also gives him an interesting intellectual reaction to what he reads.

## Scene 3

> 他一直问问题，但是不能解决那些问题。孟子长大，他开始能解决问题。他常常建议你不是学金融，而是学政治，因为如果你懂政治，你会成为高薪。后来孟子成为教授，他教学生很厉害。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不是学金融，而是学政治：不要学金融，而要学政治，This is advice about what to choose, not a correction of a mistaken statement; use a negative recommendation with `不要……而要……`. [teacher rule: bushi_ershi]
- 成为高薪：成为高薪人士，`高薪` describes a salary and cannot stand alone as the complement of `成为`; `高薪人士` means a person who earns a high salary. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 问 -- “他一直问问题”：提出，emphasizes raising questions for discussion or investigation.
- 解决 -- “不能解决那些问题”：解答，more specifically means answering questions or puzzles.
- 厉害 -- “他教学生很厉害”：很有办法，more specifically shows that he knows effective ways to teach students.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 如果你懂政治，你会：如果人们懂政治，他们会，The first `你` has no identified addressee in this third-person narrative. Introduce the generic group with `人们`, then use `他们` for the close re-occurrence. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Temporality): 孟子长大：孟子长大了，`长大` marks a completed change of state before the next action begins, so it needs `了`. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 后来孟子成为教授：后来孟子成为了教授，Becoming a professor is a completed change in the story sequence, so use `成为了`. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: After the unanswered questions, add an inner feeling such as `感到很着急`, showing why he wants to learn more.
- Expressiveness: At the point where he gives advice, add a short reason in his own voice, such as `“学政治能帮助人们了解社会。”`
- Expressiveness: When describing his teaching, add a student reaction such as `学生们都认真地听他讲课`, making his teaching ability more concrete.

### Encouragement
You create a clear progression from having difficult questions to becoming a professor. You also use contrast and cause-effect ideas to explain his advice about studying politics.
