## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男人觉得植物很有意思。他叫神农。有的植物吃了以后他的肚子疼死了，可是有的吃了以后很舒服。他找很多植物。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，The original expression redundantly combines a time period with a “time of” structure. [vocab list]
- 他找很多植物：他找到了很多植物，The verb 找 means “to look for,” while 找到 means “to find”; the completed discovery also needs 了. [vocab list]

### Vocabulary Suggestions

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一个男人觉得植物很有意思。他叫神农。：一个叫神农的男人觉得植物很有意思。，A character’s first mention should introduce both the name and a full noun phrase, so the reader knows who the character is immediately. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Expressiveness: After the stomachache, add a physical reaction to show how severe it feels: “他捂着肚子，疼得直冒汗。”
- Expressiveness: After noticing the different effects of plants, add an inner question that shows his curiosity: “他想：‘哪些植物能吃呢？’”

### Encouragement

## Scene 2

> 凉山很大的山，这也找植物。一天，他找到黄色的花。他听说你跟黄色的花做茶很有用。比方说，你的头发疼死，这冰茶是药。他一喝茶就很不舒服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 凉山很大的山：凉山是一座很大的山，A noun predicate identifying a place needs the copula 是, and mountains use the measure word 座. [rule: none]
- 你跟黄色的花做茶很有用：用黄色的花做的茶很有用，Use 用 to show the material used to make tea; the modifier 用黄色的花做的 needs 的 before the noun 茶. [rule: none]
- 头发疼死：头疼死了，头发 means “hair,” which cannot be the body part that hurts here; 头疼 expresses a headache, and 了 marks the painful state as having occurred. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- 这冰茶：这杯冰茶，A demonstrative before a countable noun needs a measure word; 杯 is the appropriate measure word for a drink. [teacher rule: zhe_na_mw]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很大 -- “凉山很大的山”：高大，specifies that the mountain is large in height and scale；雄伟，adds a grand, impressive appearance suitable for a mountain.
- 黄色 -- “黄色的花”：金黄色，gives the flowers a brighter and more specific shade of yellow.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 这也找植物：他也在这里找植物，After the sentence introduces 凉山, the zero subject could incorrectly seem to be the mountain; repeat the established character with 他 and use 在这里 for the location. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Temporality): 找到黄色的花：找到了黄色的花，This is a completed discovery on that day, so the completed action takes 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: When the yellow flowers appear, add one visual comparison to make the setting easier to imagine: “那儿开着金黄色的花，像星星一样。”
- Expressiveness: After drinking the tea, show the character’s physical reaction and thought: “他捂着肚子，心想：‘我会不会生病？’”

### Encouragement
You used the 一……就…… pattern well to show that the discomfort came immediately after drinking tea. The detail about the yellow flowers also gives the scene a clear visual focus.

## Scene 3

> 糟糕，这黄色的花是夹竹桃，特别是毒。正好在这儿，灵芝药的夹竹桃他吃完了，也写一个课文的植物。谢谢神农。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 这黄色的花：这朵黄色的花，A demonstrative needs the pattern 这/那 + measure word + noun; 朵 is the suitable measure word for a flower. [teacher rule: zhe_na_mw]
- 特别是毒：特别有毒，毒 is a noun here; use 有毒 to describe a plant that contains poison. [vocab list]
- 灵芝药的夹竹桃：灵芝和夹竹桃，药的 is a possessive modifier and cannot connect these two plant names; use 和 to coordinate them. [rule: none]
- 一个课文的植物：一篇关于这些植物的文章，The original phrase means “plants belonging to one lesson.” To express an article whose topic is the plants, use 一篇 for an article and put 关于这些植物 before 文章. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 写：写了，This narration describes a completed action after eating the plants, so 写 needs 了 to mark completion. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Conjunction): 正好在这儿：这时，正好在这儿 only gives a location and does not clearly link to the next event; 这时 introduces the next event in the sequence. [rule: none]
- Expressiveness: After identifying the dangerous flower, add Shennong’s immediate reaction, such as: “神农说：‘糟糕，夹竹桃有毒！’”
- Expressiveness: When mentioning the medicinal plant, describe its appearance to make the scene easier to imagine, such as: “灵芝像一把小伞。”
- Expressiveness: Add a physical feeling after eating the poisonous plant, such as: “他吃了夹竹桃以后，肚子疼了起来。”

### Encouragement
