### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代：在古代，A time expression used as the setting normally takes the preposition shown here.
- 孟母注意：孟母注意到，The verb needs the result complement to express that she noticed the following situation.
- 跟朋友们祭拜玩：跟朋友们一起学着别人祭拜，The original word order does not form a natural expression: this action is imitating a formal ritual, not using the ritual directly as something to play.
- 孟母决定他们会搬家：孟母决定带着孟子搬家，A decision is followed directly by the action phrase being decided on; the subject does not need to be repeated as a future clause.

### Vocabulary Suggestions

- Expressiveness: After the cemetery setting, add what made the ritual interesting to the child, such as: “孟子觉得祭拜很好玩。”
- Expressiveness: In the final sentence, add a sensory detail about the noisy area, such as: “商人一直大声叫卖，马路上人来人往。”
- Expressiveness: Add the mother’s worried thought before the decision, such as: “孟母心想：‘这里太吵了，会影响孟子学习。’”

### Encouragement
