## Scene 1

> 在古代，很多医生不知道怎么用药。所以他们找到了很多植物对药。神农先看到很多病人，他们吃很多植物对药。但是我觉得他们的吃的植物，让他们病人。神农觉得这个植物可能特别是毒。所以他想找一个植物对药，他们可以吃对药。正好，他找到了一把花，他想把花放在茶里，对药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很多植物对药：很多能做药的植物，The phrase “对药” cannot describe plants that can be used as medicine; “能做药的” correctly modifies “植物”. [rule: none]
- 他们的吃的植物：他们吃的植物，In a relative clause, the subject comes directly before the verb; “他们吃的植物” means the plants that they ate. [rule: none]
- 让他们病人：让他们生病，“病人” is a noun meaning an ill person, not a result that can follow “让”; use the verb phrase “生病”. [rule: word_class_misuse]
- 特别是毒：特别有毒，“是毒” does not describe a plant’s poisonous property; “有毒” means poisonous and can follow “特别”. [vocab list]
- 一个植物对药，他们可以吃对药：一种能做药、可以吃的植物，Use “能做药” for a plant that can be made into medicine, and use “可以吃的” as a modifier for edible plants. [rule: none]
- 对药：用来做药，“对药” is not a valid phrase after this action; “用来做药” states the purpose of using the flower. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：一个叫神农的医生，“神农” is introduced for the first time here, so give the character a name and role rather than assuming the reader already knows him. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Reference): 这个植物：这些植物，No single plant has been identified in the previous sentence, so “这个植物” has an unclear referent; the plural noun refers clearly to the plants already mentioned. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Reference): 他找到了一把花，他想：他找到了一把花，想，When the same character performs the next action in the same sentence, use zero form rather than repeating the pronoun. [rule: redundancy_delete]
- Coherence (Temporality): 看到很多病人：看到了很多病人，This is a completed event in the narrative, so “看到” needs the perfective marker “了”. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: After the patients are mentioned, add one physical detail to show their illness: “他们常常肚子疼，脸色很不好。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where the character decides to search, add an inner thought: “神农想：我一定要找到能治病的植物。”
- Expressiveness: When the flower is found, describe its appearance more vividly: “那儿开着黄色的花，像星星一样漂亮。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish a problem at the beginning and show the character trying to solve it. The scene also uses cause-and-effect ideas to connect illness, plants, and the search for medicine.

## Scene 2

> 他不知道如果这把花对你的身体好，这就要看如果这把花毒不毒了。神农想找别的植物，但是这很麻烦。正好，他找到了别的花。他一吃花，很快就生病了。他的肚子疼死了。神农不知道他吃不吃灵芝或者夹竹桃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 如果：delete it，An embedded yes/no question after “不知道” cannot use “如果”; it needs an A-not-A form instead. [rule: a_not_a_question]
- 好：好不好，The embedded question needs the A-not-A form to ask whether the flower benefits the body. [rule: a_not_a_question]
- 看如果：看，After “看,” the clause already uses the A-not-A form “毒不毒,” so “如果” is not used. [rule: a_not_a_question]
- 他吃不吃灵芝或者夹竹桃：他吃的是灵芝还是夹竹桃，This asks which of two plants he ate, so use “是……还是……” rather than “吃不吃……或者……”. [rule: a_not_a_question]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 麻烦 -- “但是这很麻烦”：艰辛，emphasizes that finding and testing plants takes difficult effort；不容易，states more specifically that the task is difficult to accomplish.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 你的身体：他的身体，There is no listener addressed as “你”; the flower is being tested by 神农, so this pronoun should refer back to him. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Reference): 他一吃花：他一吃那种花，The earlier sentence introduces a particular different kind of flower; “那种花” refers back to that plant instead of making “花” sound like flowers in general. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Expressiveness: At “正好，他找到了别的花,” add one visual detail about the plant, such as “那种花是黄色的，像星星一样，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: After “他的肚子疼死了,” show his physical reaction, such as “他躺在地上，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought after he becomes ill, such as “他想：我会死吗？”

### Encouragement
- You used “这就要看……了” in a suitable situation where the answer depends on whether the flower is poisonous.  
- The event sequence is easy to follow: 神农 finds a flower, eats it, and then becomes sick.

## Scene 3

> 别的人给了神农一个灵芝。这个植物可以让神农更好。比方说，他会觉得更健康。吃灵芝以后，他可以再走。现在他觉得更好。最后，神农找到了很多植物，他可以用对药。这些植物让神农很高兴。神农想跟很多人分享他的新药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别的人给了神农一个灵芝：有人给了神农一个灵芝，别的人 means “other people” in contrast with a previously mentioned person or group; no such contrast is established here, so 有人 introduces an unspecified helper naturally. [teacher rule: bieren]
- 很多植物，他可以用对药：很多可以用来做药的植物，use 用来做药 to express a plant’s purpose; 用对药 is not a valid verb-complement structure. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 高兴 -- “这些植物让神农很高兴”：欣慰，shows that he feels pleased and relieved because his difficult work has produced results.
- 分享 -- “神农想跟很多人分享他的新药”：分给，more specifically describes giving portions of medicine to many people.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农想跟很多人分享他的新药：他想跟很多人分享他的新药，神农 was the subject in the immediately preceding sentence and no other male character intervenes, so a pronoun is more natural for a close re-occurrence. [rule: redundancy_delete]
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃灵芝以后：吃了灵芝以后，eating happens before the later action of walking again, so use 了 to mark the completed action before 以后. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: After “现在他觉得更好,” add a physical sign of recovery, such as “他的肚子不再疼了，慢慢站了起来。”
- Expressiveness: At “这些植物让神农很高兴,” show his feeling through a short thought or line of speech, such as “神农高兴地说：‘我终于找到可以做药的植物了！’”
- Expressiveness: When he finds the plants, add one observable detail to make the discovery vivid, such as “他仔细看了看叶子的颜色，又闻了闻花的味道。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the recovery sequence: receiving the medicine, feeling healthier, and walking again. You also give Shen Nong a positive goal at the end by having him want to help many people.
