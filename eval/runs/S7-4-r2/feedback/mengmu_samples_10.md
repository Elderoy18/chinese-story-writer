## Scene 1

> 孟子小的时候，从早到晚都跟朋友玩。孟子和他的朋友喜欢玩在墓地里，装祭拜。孟子五岁的时候，跟妈妈搬家。他们搬家不是因为孟子玩得太多，而是因为妈妈认为他们的家离墓地太近。

- 喜欢玩在墓地里：喜欢在墓地里玩，A location phrase should come before the verb it modifies; here the playing happens in the cemetery. [rule: none]
- 装祭拜：装作祭拜，装作 means “pretend to do something” and must be followed by the action being pretended. [rule: none]

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 跟妈妈搬家：跟妈妈搬家了，This is one completed move at a specific time in the past, so it needs 了 to mark completion. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: At the cemetery setting, add one sensory detail, such as “墓地里传来的哭声”, to make the place feel more real.
- Expressiveness: After the children’s action, add Mengzi’s thought or feeling, such as “觉得祭拜很有趣”, to show why he joins the game.
- Expressiveness: When explaining the mother’s decision, add her emotion, such as “孟母很担心孟子”, to make her reason more personal.

### Encouragement

- 住离墓地太近，害得孟子的心气：住得离墓地太近，害得孟子没了心气，degree complements use 得 after the verb, and 害得 must be followed by the affected person plus a harmful result rather than only a possessive noun phrase. [teacher rule: haide_structure]
- 老是读书：一直读书，老是 often conveys complaint or criticism, while reading is a neutral activity here. [vocab list]
