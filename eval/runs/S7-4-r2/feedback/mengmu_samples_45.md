## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子。他们住在墓地的旁边，所以孟子老是祭拜。孟子不是祭拜，就是跟朋友聊天。他很喜欢他的城市，但是城市有才两个学校，都很小。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，this expression is redundant because both parts express time; use the shorter time expression instead. [vocab list]
- 城市有才两个学校：城市里才有两所学校，place 才 before the verb it modifies, and use 所 as the measure word for schools. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 小 -- “都很小”：规模都不大，specifies that the schools are small in scale rather than merely physically small.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子：一个叫孟子的男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国，Introduce the main character’s name in the first mention rather than naming him only after a pronoun. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子不是祭拜：他不是祭拜，Use a pronoun when the same character was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no competing male character has appeared. [rule: none]
- Coherence (Reference): 他的城市：自己的城市，Use the reflexive possessor to make clear that the city belongs to the subject of the sentence. [teacher rule: ziji_possessor]
- Expressiveness: At the burial-ground sentence, add a concrete detail about what people are doing, such as “孟子看到有人在墓地前哭”.
- Expressiveness: Show why the activity interests him through an internal thought, such as “他觉得这样很好玩”.
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech from the mother to show her concern, such as “孟母说：‘你不能老是这样。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduce the setting and use a cause-and-effect connector to link the home near the burial ground with Mengzi’s behavior. The paired structure in the next sentence also gives the scene a clear rhythm.

## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈不是不喜欢他们的城市，而是想孟子有很好的教育。他们搬家到有很多学校的城市。他们从早到晚走路，孟子一直抱怨。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 想孟子有很好的教育：想让孟子受到很好的教育，想 needs a verb phrase; 让 introduces the person and the result that the mother wants for him. [rule: none]
- 搬家到有很多学校的城市：搬到有很多学校的城市，use 搬到 + place for moving to a destination; 搬家到 does not fit this destination pattern. [rule: preposition_dao_place]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们搬家到有很多学校的城市：他们搬到了有很多学校的城市，this move is a completed story event, so 搬到 takes 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: At the walking scene, add a physical detail to show that the journey feels long: “他们走了很久，孟子累得满头大汗。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of dialogue after the complaint to reveal Mengzi’s feelings: “孟子皱着眉头说：‘妈妈，我们还要走多久？’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the mother’s reason for moving, and the contrast structure helps the reader understand that she cares about Mengzi’s education. The final sentence also gives Mengzi a believable reaction to the long walk.

## Scene 3

> 他难怪抱怨，他想他的朋友，因为他不要离开他的城市。他的妈妈找到孟子一个老师。跟老师孟子学最重要的文学，所以他成为哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他难怪抱怨，他想：难怪他一直抱怨，原来他想，Use the paired pattern with the result first and the explanation after it: 难怪……，原来…… . [teacher rule: nanguai_yuanlai]
- 找到孟子一个老师：给孟子找到了一个老师，Put the recipient before the verb with 给; because 找到 is a verb-resultative compound, 了 follows the resultative complement to show that the search was completed. [teacher rule: le_placement]
- 跟老师孟子学最重要的文学：孟子跟老师学最重要的文学，The subject should come before the coverb phrase 跟老师. [rule: none]
- 成为哲学家：成为了哲学家，This change of state is presented as completed in the narrative, so it needs 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 老师 -- “他的妈妈给孟子找到了一个老师”：文学老师，specifies that the teacher teaches the subject mentioned in the next sentence；教文学的老师，makes the teacher’s role especially clear.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他的朋友：自己的朋友，When the subject and the possessor are the same person, use 自己的 to make the relationship clear. [teacher rule: ziji_possessor]
- Coherence (Reference): 他的城市：自己的城市，When the subject is referring to his own possession or place, 自己的 avoids possible ambiguity. [teacher rule: ziji_possessor]
- Expressiveness: At the complaint, show Mengzi’s emotion with an action or a short quotation, such as “孟子低着头说：‘我想跟朋友一起玩。’”
- Expressiveness: When he studies with the teacher, add a concrete learning detail, such as “孟子认真地听老师讲课，还问了很多问题。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect Mengzi’s unhappiness with missing his friends and leaving his city. You also show an important change in his life by moving from complaining to studying with a teacher.
