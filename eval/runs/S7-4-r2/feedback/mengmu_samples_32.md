## Scene 1

> 有一天孟子去了墓地玩儿，但是他妈妈觉得这是不合适的地方让孩子玩，所以她决定了他们会搬家找更好的地方。她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福，而且住在这儿教育不太好。因为她望子成龙，所以她会一直搬家才能找到质量好的教育。然后他们搬到了离城市更近的地方。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 决定了他们会搬家找：决定搬家，找，A decision should take the action phrase directly; a mental-state verb such as 决定 does not take 了, and the repeated subject is unnecessary.
- 她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福：她担心儿子的童年不会幸福，恐怕 means “I am afraid that” and cannot take another subject before it; use 担心 when the mother is afraid about her son’s future.
- 住在这儿教育不太好：这儿的教育不太好，The place needs to be marked as the possessor of 教育 with 的; otherwise 住在这儿 cannot naturally serve as the subject of 教育不太好.
- 会一直搬家才能：得一直搬家，才能，才能 expresses a required condition, so the earlier clause should use 得 or 要 rather than the future marker 会.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 地方 -- “找更好的地方”：更适合孩子成长的地方，specifies what makes the new place better for the mother’s purpose.
- 质量好的 -- “找到质量好的教育”：高质量的，states the quality of the education more naturally；优质的，gives a concise and precise description of good educational quality.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After “孟子去了墓地玩儿,” add one concrete action to show what he does there, such as “他跟着来祭拜的人学着磕头。”
- Expressiveness: At the mother’s decision, add her inner thought or quoted speech, such as “孟母心想：‘这儿不适合孩子成长，我得带他搬家。’”
- Expressiveness: After the move closer to the city, add a sensory detail about the new area, such as “街上有很多商人，叫卖声从早到晚没有停过。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the mother’s reason for moving and use the cause-and-effect connection effectively. The completed actions in “去了” and “搬到了” also make the sequence of events easy to follow.

## Scene 2

> 他们住才一月就又搬家。古代的时候，一般人不喜欢卖东西的人。但是孟子老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿，害得妈妈着急得很，因为她恐怕儿子会成为跟他们很像。她告诉了孟子：他们不是朋友，而是不好的人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，this expression redundantly combines two time expressions; use the shorter time phrase.
- 老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿：常常跟这些卖东西的人玩儿，use only one frequency adverb here because both words express a habitual action.
- 她恐怕儿子会：她担心儿子会，恐怕 means “I am afraid that” and cannot take another subject before it; use 担心 to express the mother’s fear.
- 成为跟他们很像：变得跟他们很像，成为 normally introduces a new identity or role expressed by a noun, while 变得 introduces a change into an adjective-like state.
- 他们不是朋友，而是不好的人：他们不是你的朋友，是不好的人，朋友 needs the possessor to show that they are not Mengzi’s friends, and 不是……而是…… is not needed unless the speaker is explicitly correcting a mistaken or conventional belief.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 卖东西的人 -- “这些卖东西的人”：商人，names their occupation more precisely and avoids repeating a long descriptive phrase.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们住才一月就又搬家。：他们才住了一个月，就又搬家了。，in the 才……就…… pattern, 才 comes before the duration and 就 introduces the following action; 了 marks the completed period of living there and the completed move.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉了孟子：告诉孟子：，a speaking verb that introduces quoted speech uses the zero particle rather than 了.
- Expressiveness: After the sellers are introduced, add one market detail to make the setting visible, such as “街上有很多人卖衣服、卖菜，还不停地叫卖。”
- Expressiveness: Show what Mengzi does with the sellers rather than only saying that he plays with them, such as “孟子学着商人收钱，还跟客人讨价还价。”
- Expressiveness: At the mother’s speech, add her expression or tone to reveal her worry, such as “妈妈皱着眉头说：‘你不能老是学他们。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the mother’s worry and give her a line of dialogue, which makes the scene feel more active. The contrast between ordinary people and Mengzi also creates a clear reason for the mother to react.
