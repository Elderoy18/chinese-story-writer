## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，孟子和孟母住了在靠墓地的家。孩子才五岁就每天在墓地祭拜了。在那个城市，孟母从早到晚都找工作，可是找不到，害得孟母没有钱。难怪她跟孩子搬家，原来买不了房。他们搬了到别一个城市找工作。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，this expression redundantly combines a historical time noun with a time-of construction.
- 住了在靠墓地的家：住在靠近墓地的家里，a location phrase follows the verb directly; the aspect marker cannot appear before the preposition.
- 祭拜了：祭拜，this is a daily routine, so it takes the zero aspect particle rather than 了.
- 跟孩子搬家：带着孟子搬家，跟 means “together with,” while 带着 expresses that the mother takes her child along when moving.
- 搬了到：搬到了，了 follows the verb plus its directional/resultative element, as in 搬到 + 了.
- 别一个城市：另一个城市，别的 distinguishes other items from a previously specified one, while 另一个 means one additional or different city.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 没有钱 -- “害得孟母没有钱”：手头拮据，specifies that she is short of money and under financial pressure in this situation；经济困难，states more clearly that the problem is financial difficulty.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟母住：有一个小男孩叫孟子，他和妈妈住，孟子 is appearing for the first time, so introduce him clearly before using family labels; after that, 他 can refer back to him.
- Coherence (Reference): 孩子才五岁：他才五岁，孟子 was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other child competes as the referent, so use a pronoun.
- Coherence (Reference): 害得孟母没有钱：害得她没有钱，孟母 is already the subject earlier in the same sentence, so a pronoun avoids unnecessarily repeating the name.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 。他们搬了到别一个城市找工作。：，所以他们搬到了另一个城市找工作。The move is the result of not finding work and lacking money, so use 所以 to show the cause-and-effect relation.
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery sentence, add one concrete image of what Mengzi sees, such as: “他看见有人哭着祭拜。” This makes the setting easier to imagine.
- Expressiveness: Show Mengzi’s feeling about copying the visitors’ actions, such as: “孟子觉得很好玩，也学着他们祭拜。” This gives the character a reason for his behavior.
- Expressiveness: Add Mengmu’s worried thought before the move, such as: “孟母心想：‘我们不能一直住在这儿。’” This can make her decision feel more personal.

### Encouragement
- You used a clear time progression, moving from the family’s home near the cemetery to their decision to move away.
- Your use of “才五岁就” effectively shows that Mengzi was very young when these events happened.

## Scene 2

> 这个城市老是很吵，不是人吵架，就是都叫，害得孟子一直不能睡觉。在那儿，孟母也找不到工作。孟子和妈妈要又一个次搬了家。在第三个城市，孟母找到工作，在商店做衣服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 人吵架，就是都叫：人们吵架，就是人们都叫，A concrete group of people should be called 人们, not 人; the second parallel clause also needs its group subject for 都 to refer to.
- 孟子和妈妈要又一个次搬了家：孟子和妈妈要再搬一次家，For a future repeated action after a modal verb, use 要 + 再 + verb + number + measure word + object. The action is only planned, so do not use 了.
- 找到工作：找到了工作，The finding is a completed action in this past event, so add 了 after the resultative verb 找到.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很吵 -- “这个城市老是很吵”：嘈杂，specifies that many mixed sounds fill the city；吵闹，describes the noisy commotion caused by arguing and shouting.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 在第三个城市：搬到第三个城市以后，Use 以后 to show the sequence: first the family moved, and afterwards Meng’s mother found work.
- Expressiveness: After the description of the noise, add a sensory detail about Mengzi trying to sleep, such as “孟子躺在床上，外面还是有人大声叫。”
- Expressiveness: Add Meng’s mother’s worried thought before the move, such as “孟母心想：‘这儿太吵了，我们得再搬家。’”
- Expressiveness: Make the new job more concrete by adding Meng’s mother’s feeling, such as “孟母终于找到了工作，心里很高兴。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show how the noisy city affects Mengzi by using 害得 to connect the cause and result. You also move the story forward with a new city and Meng’s mother finding work.

## Scene 3

> 孟子在那个商店也找了到工作，可是不是做衣服的工作，而是喜欢的工作。孟子学习馆学了十五年，他的学习和他的旅行经验很重要，对他很多好处。他是大人的时候，他成为很有名的哲人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 学习馆学了十五年：学习了十五年，馆 is a noun and cannot appear between the verb 学习 and its duration phrase; use the verb 学习 directly before the duration.
- 对他很多好处：对他有很多好处，use 有 in the pattern 对 + person + 有 + 好处 to state that something benefits someone.
- 他是大人的时候：他长大以后，长大以后 is the natural time expression for the period after someone becomes an adult.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 做衣服 -- “不是做衣服的工作”：裁缝工作，names the specific kind of work of making or altering clothes.
- 学习 -- “孟子学习了十五年”：求学，specifically describes pursuing education over a long period.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他成为：成为，after 他 has already identified the subject in the first clause, omit the repeated pronoun in the main clause.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为：成为了，becoming a philosopher is presented as a completed change of state in the narrative, so use 了 after the verb.
- Expressiveness: After the job is found, add Mengzi’s feeling to make the moment more personal: “孟子很高兴，因为他终于找到自己喜欢的工作了。”
- Expressiveness: When describing the long period of study, add one concrete detail about his effort: “他白天工作，晚上常常看书学习。”
- Expressiveness: At the ending, add a reaction from others to show his reputation: “很多人都来请教他问题。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly showed the major stages of Mengzi’s life: finding work, studying for many years, and later becoming well known.
- The contrast structure with 不是……而是…… helps explain that the new job was different from making clothes.
