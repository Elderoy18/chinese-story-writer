## Scene 1

> 古代时候，人不知道他们能吃什么植物，常常一吃就死。但是不知道为什么，没有办法。所以有一天，神农出去试一试很多种植物，告诉人什么植物是安全什么是危险。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代时候：在古代，an era is already a time expression, so adding 时候 is redundant; use the location-style time phrase instead. [teacher rule: gudai_de_shihou_why]
- 人：人们，this refers to a concrete group of people in the story, so use 人们 rather than the generic noun 人. [teacher rule: ren_renmen]
- 什么植物是安全什么是危险：什么植物是安全的，什么植物是危险的，parallel classification clauses need clear separation, and adjectives used after 是 to describe a category take 的 here. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：有一个叫神农的人，this is the character’s first mention, so introduce him with a full noun phrase before later using his name or a pronoun. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Temporality): 出去试一试很多种植物：出去试了很多种植物，this is a completed action on one particular day; in a series of actions, the last verb in the series takes 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉：告诉了，telling the people is presented as a completed narrative action, not quoted speech, so it needs 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Conjunction): 但是不知道为什么，没有办法：因为不知道为什么，所以没有办法，being unable to find a solution results from not knowing the reason, so use the cause-and-effect connector rather than a contrast connector. [rule: none]
- Expressiveness: After the sentence about people dying, add a physical reaction before the result, such as “吃了以后，肚子疼得不得了”, to make the danger more vivid.
- Expressiveness: When Shen Nong decides to test plants, add his determination in a short thought or quotation, such as “我一定要找出能吃的植物”, to show his purpose and character.

### Encouragement
You clearly show the problem first and then introduce Shen Nong’s action as a response. Your use of the 一……就…… pattern also effectively shows how quickly the dangerous result happens.

## Scene 2

> 发现有的植物对身体非常好，特别是茶，让肚子感觉又暖和又清楚。他告诉朋友：“你吃哪种植物，这就要看你的口味。比方说，你爱吃甜的，可能喜欢糖。”他最喜欢吃花，除了夹竹桃以外，什么都花想吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 又暖和又清楚：又暖和又舒服，清楚 describes clarity of thought, information, or vision; it cannot describe a stomach’s physical sensation. [rule: none]
- 你吃哪种植物，这就要看你的口味：你吃哪种植物，要看你的口味，this is a direct statement that the choice depends on taste, so it should not use the fixed flexible-answer pattern 这就要看……了. [rule: zhe_jiu_yao_kan]
- 什么都花想吃：什么花都想吃，when 什么 modifies a noun in this “every/any” pattern, the noun comes directly after 什么, followed by 都 and the verb. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好 -- “有的植物对身体非常好”：有益, states more precisely that the plants benefit health；对身体有好处, clearly explains the kind of “good” meant here

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the tea is mentioned, add one sensory detail about its smell or warmth, such as “茶水香香的，喝下去以后肚子暖乎乎的。”
- Expressiveness: In the quoted speech, add a short reason to make the advice sound more personal, such as “你喜欢甜的植物，就可以试一试甜甜的花。”
- Expressiveness: When describing his interest in flowers, add his feeling or thought, such as “他觉得每一朵花都很特别，很想知道能不能吃。”

### Encouragement
- You used direct speech to give the character a voice, and the exception phrase “除了夹竹桃以外” clearly shows that he treats that plant differently from the others.

## Scene 3

> 他发现这个口味，一百岁时候，不小心吃了毒的花。一吃就开始死。但是生病时候告诉人什么植物是药，什么植物是毒。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一百岁时候：一百岁的时候，A multi-syllable age expression before 时候 needs the particle 的. [teacher rule: de_shihou]
- 毒的花：有毒的花，毒 is a noun here; use 有毒的 to describe a flower that contains poison. [vocab list]
- 开始死：快要死了，死亡 is an endpoint rather than an ongoing action that normally follows 开始; 快要……了 expresses that the character was close to dying. [rule: none]
- 告诉：告诉了，This is a completed action in the past, so the verb needs 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- 人：人们，Use 人们 for the concrete group of people whom he told. [teacher rule: ren_renmen]
- 是毒：有毒，Use 有毒 to describe a plant that contains poison. [vocab list]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 告诉 -- “告诉人什么植物是药，什么植物是毒”：向人们说明，emphasizes explaining this knowledge clearly to other people；告诉大家，makes the audience more specific and natural in this context

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: At the moment he eats the flower, add one visual detail about it, such as “那朵花又黄又大，像星星一样漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: Show his physical pain after eating it, such as “他马上肚子疼了起来，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Add his frightened thought before he explains the plants, such as “他想：我会死吗？”

### Encouragement
- You used the pattern 一……就…… to show that eating the flower quickly led to a serious result. You also included both medicine and poisonous plants, which gives this scene an important contrast.
