## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有很多人不知道什么植物能吃，什么植物不能吃。有很多人吃植物以后就吃坏肚子了，因为他们吃了毒。那个时候，有一个老人叫神农，他什么植物都觉得好吃，不怕生病，不怕毒。他希望别人也可以吃好吃的植物。可是他知道，如果他们还怕吃毒，不会吃他喜欢的植物。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，The two time expressions redundantly mark the same period; use the concise narrative time phrase.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 有很多人吃植物以后就吃坏肚子了：他们吃植物以后就吃坏肚子了，The group was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other group competes with it, so a pronoun is more natural on close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 如果他们还怕吃毒，不会吃他喜欢的植物：如果他们还怕吃毒，就不会吃他喜欢的植物，A conditional clause introduced by 如果 is naturally followed by 就 in the result clause.
- Expressiveness: After the people become ill, add one physical detail to show the consequence, such as “有的人肚子疼得直冒汗。”
- Expressiveness: When introducing 神农, add his purpose or inner thought, such as “他想：我要找出能吃的植物，帮大家少生病。”
- Expressiveness: After his hope for others, add a short line of speech to show his determination, such as “大家别怕，我会找到安全的植物。”

### Encouragement
You clearly introduce the main problem—people cannot tell which plants are safe to eat—and then connect it to 神农’s wish to help others. The final conditional idea also shows a thoughtful attempt to explain why people hesitate to eat unfamiliar plants.

## Scene 2

> 所以，他上山下山找植物，吃了很多样子的植物。他是第一人，找到了茶。他把所有的植物写下来了。他一直都吃了，他连毒都能吃。可是他还没吃了一个叫夹竹桃的植物。他找到了那个像星星的植物，觉得很漂亮，马上就吃下了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很多样子的植物：很多种植物，样子 describes an appearance, while 种 is the classifier for different types of plants.
- 他是第一人，找到了茶：他是第一个找到茶的人，use 第一个 before a verb phrase to identify the person who did an action first.
- 一个叫夹竹桃的植物：一种叫夹竹桃的植物，种 is the suitable classifier when introducing a plant species.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 一直都吃了：一直都在吃，了 marks a completed action, but 一直 describes an ongoing action; use 在 for this progressive action.
- Expressiveness: At the mountain-searching moment, add a small detail about the difficult journey, such as “翻过一座又一座山”, to show persistence.
- Expressiveness: When the plant first appears, describe its colour and shape more vividly, such as “黄色的花，像星星一样”.
- Expressiveness: After the character eats the plant, add an inner feeling such as “肚子有一点疼”, which can make the danger feel more immediate.

### Encouragement
You used completed forms well in “找到了茶” and “吃下了,” so the key discoveries and actions are clear. The description “像星星的植物” also gives the scene an imaginative visual detail.

## Scene 3

> 可是夹竹桃的毒很重，让他肚子很痛。他在地上躺着，觉得他快会死了。可是正好有别人找到他了，给了他一个像伞的植物，叫灵芝。他吃灵芝以后就觉得很好，不再生病了。他测量起来了，像星星的植物是毒，像伞的植物是药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 快会死了：快要死了，Use this imminent-change pattern to say that death seems very near; 会 is for a future prediction and is not natural here with 快.
- 别人：有人，别人 distinguishes other people from a specified person or group, but this sentence introduces an unspecified person who finds him.
- 像星星的植物是毒：像星星的植物有毒，毒 is a noun here; 有毒 describes a plant as poisonous.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很好 -- “他吃灵芝以后就觉得很好”：好多了，shows that his condition improved compared with before and is more precise after the stomach pain.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 觉得他快会死了：觉得自己快会死了，Use 自己 when the person who has the thought and the person who may die are the same; 他 can suggest that he is thinking about someone else.
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃灵芝以后：吃了灵芝以后，The eating is completed before the later feeling of improvement, so use 了 for the completed action.
- Expressiveness: At the moment of pain, add a physical detail to show how severe it is: “他疼得满头大汗，在地上打滚。”
- Expressiveness: Add his worried thought after he lies down: “他想：‘我会死吗？’”
- Expressiveness: When the helper arrives, describe the person briefly to make the moment clearer: “这时，一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人走过来。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the cause-and-effect sequence: the poisonous plant causes pain, and the medicinal plant helps him feel better.
- Your use of the ongoing posture while he is on the ground creates a clear picture of his condition.
