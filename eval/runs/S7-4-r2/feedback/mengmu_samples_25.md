## Scene 1

> 这是古代的故事。孟子小的时候，他的爸爸很早就去世了。从他的家到墓地很近，所以他每天老是从早到晚去墓地跟朋友们玩玩儿。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 从他的家到墓地很近：他的家离墓地很近，Use 离 to express that one place is close to another; 从…到… is normally used for a route or distance measurement.
- 从早到晚去墓地跟朋友们玩玩儿：从早到晚在墓地跟朋友们玩儿，The time span describes the playing activity, so it should modify the location-and-action phrase rather than the single trip to the cemetery.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 朋友们 -- “跟朋友们玩玩儿”：小伙伴们，more specifically suggests companions of the same age, which suits a young child in this scene.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子小的时候，他的爸爸很早就去世了：有一个小男孩叫孟子；他小的时候，他的爸爸很早就去世了，On first mention, introduce the character with a name and a brief description; after that, the pronoun can refer clearly to him.
- Expressiveness: After the visit to the cemetery, add a visual detail about the people there: “孟子看到有人在墓地哭，也有人带着花来祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: Show Mengzi’s reaction to make the scene more personal: “他觉得这些事情很有趣，就想学他们的样子。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish the setting, Mengzi’s family situation, and the reason the cemetery becomes part of his daily life.
- The cause-and-effect connection is easy to follow, and the routine time expressions make the scene feel like part of his childhood.

## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈觉得在墓地玩儿，害得，所以他们搬家。搬家对孟子有好的影响，他才五岁，可是每天不是写字就是帮妈妈的工作。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在墓地玩儿，害得：孟子在墓地玩儿会带来麻烦，The original use of “害得” is incomplete: this structure must be followed by the person affected and a harmful result.
- 帮妈妈的工作：帮妈妈干活，“帮妈妈的工作” incorrectly turns the activity into a possessive noun phrase. Use “帮 + person + verb phrase” to say that someone helps a person do work.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬家：搬家了，This move is a completed event in the narrative, so the verb needs “了” to mark completion.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他才五岁，可是每天不是写字就是：他才五岁，就每天不是写字就是，“才 + age/number + 就 + verb” shows that an action began unusually early; “可是” does not express this early-action relationship.
- Expressiveness: After the mother notices Mengzi’s behaviour, add her feeling to show why she wants to move: “孟母很着急。”
- Expressiveness: Add one concrete detail about what Mengzi does at the cemetery, such as: “孟子一边笑一边学着别人祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: Add the mother’s thought before the move to make her decision more vivid: “孟母心想：‘如果一直住在这儿，麻烦会越来越多。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show a cause-and-effect relationship with “所以,” and you use “不是……就是……” effectively to describe Mengzi’s daily routine.

## Scene 3

> 他很聪明的人，难怪他成为有名的人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他很聪明的人：他是一个很聪明的人，This is a noun phrase; to say that he is such a person, use a copula plus an indefinite classifier phrase.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 人 -- “有名的人”：有名的哲学家，specifies what kind of renowned person he became and makes the achievement more precise in this story.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 难怪他成为有名的人：难怪成为有名的人，After the first clause has established him as the subject, omit the repeated pronoun in the next clause of the same sentence; this uses zero form for a close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为：成为了，Becoming a renowned person is a completed change of state in this retrospective story ending, so add 了.
- Expressiveness: At the final achievement, add a time word to make the ending feel more story-like: “后来，孟子成为了有名的哲学家。”
- Expressiveness: You could show his mother's positive feeling at this point: “孟母看到孟子这么聪明，心里很高兴。”

### Encouragement
You used 难怪 to connect Mencius's intelligence with his later achievement, which gives the sentence a clear sense of cause and result.
