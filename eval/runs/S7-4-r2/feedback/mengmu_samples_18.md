## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个男孩名字叫孟子。孟子和孟子的妈妈两个人住在很穷的农村。他们住的地方旁边是个墓地，墓地老是有很多人祭拜。因为人们在祭拜，他们一直在哭。孟子和孟子的妈妈住在这里才一个星期就觉得受不了了，而且害得孟子的学习。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，The original repeats the time meaning because 古代 already refers to a historical time period.
- 有一个男孩名字叫孟子：有一个叫孟子的男孩，When the name modifies the noun, place the modifier before the noun and link it with 的.
- 而且害得孟子的学习：而且这里的环境害得孟子没法学习，害得 must be followed by the affected person and a harmful result clause; 学习 alone cannot complete this structure.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 一直在 -- “他们一直在哭”：不停地，specifies that the crying continues without stopping.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子的妈妈：孟子和他妈妈，Use a pronoun for the second mention in the same clause; repeating the full name is unnecessary.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子的妈妈住在这里：孟子和他妈妈住在这里，Use a pronoun for the second mention in the same clause.
- Coherence (Temporality): 住在这里才一个星期：在这里才住了一个星期，The one-week stay is completed before the following reaction, so the duration verb needs 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为人们在祭拜，他们一直在哭：因为人们在祭拜，所以他们一直在哭，Use 因为……所以…… to mark the cause-and-effect relationship clearly.
- Expressiveness: When describing the cemetery, add a visual detail about the visitors, such as: “来祭拜的人穿着黑衣服，脸上很伤心。”
- Expressiveness: Show Mengzi’s mother’s feelings at the moment she cannot bear the place anymore, such as: “孟子的妈妈皱着眉头，觉得这里太吵了。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought or line of speech from the mother, such as: “她心想：‘不能再住在这里了。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly establish the setting and explain why the cemetery is unpleasant for the family. Your use of ongoing actions in “在祭拜” and “一直在哭” also helps the scene feel active.

## Scene 2

> 孟子的妈妈觉得孟子取得良好的教育是很重要的，所以孟子的妈妈认为这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好，就打算跟孟子搬家。孟子的妈妈带孟子不是搬到城市，而是搬到了孟子老师的家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 孟子取得良好的教育：孟子接受良好的教育，取得 usually refers to obtaining an achievement or result, while 接受 is the usual verb for receiving education.
- 跟孟子搬家：带着孟子搬家，跟孟子搬家 incorrectly suggests that both people move together as equal participants; 带着孟子搬家 clearly shows that the mother takes her child with her.
- 不是搬到城市，而是搬到了孟子老师的家：搬到了孟子老师的家，the structure 不是……而是…… is used to correct a conventional or previously stated wrong idea; here the sentence simply states the destination of the move.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 地方 -- “这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好”：环境，more specifically refers to the surroundings that affect a child’s education.
- 良好 -- “孟子接受良好的教育”：优质的，more specifically emphasizes the high quality of the education.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 所以孟子的妈妈认为：所以认为，within the same sentence, the mother is already the subject, so Chinese naturally uses zero form instead of repeating her name.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子的妈妈带孟子搬到了孟子老师的家：她带孟子搬到了孟子老师的家，after the mother was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other possible female referent intervenes, use a pronoun for close re-occurrence.
- Expressiveness: After the mother realizes that the place is unsuitable, add her worried feeling: “孟子的妈妈很着急。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short internal thought to show why she wants to move: “她心想：‘我一定要给孟子找一个更好的环境。’”
- Expressiveness: Describe the teacher’s home as a learning-friendly place: “老师的家很安静，书架上有很多书。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the mother’s reason for wanting to move, and the completed move is correctly expressed with 搬到了.
