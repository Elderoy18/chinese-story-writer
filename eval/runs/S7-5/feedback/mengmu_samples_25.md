## Scene 1

> 这是古代的故事。孟子小的时候，他的爸爸很早就去世了。从他的家到墓地很近，所以他每天老是从早到晚去墓地跟朋友们玩玩儿。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 从他的家到墓地很近：他的家离墓地很近，Use 离 to state that one location is close to another; 从……到…… is not the suitable pattern here for expressing distance. [vocab list]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 玩玩儿 -- “去墓地跟朋友们玩玩儿”：嬉戏，adds a more vivid sense of children playing cheerfully together.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子小的时候，他的爸爸：有一个小男孩叫孟子。他小的时候，他的爸爸，On a character’s first mention, introduce the character with a full noun phrase rather than only giving the name inside a time phrase. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Expressiveness: At the cemetery scene, add a small action detail to show what the children do: “他和朋友们在墓地旁边跑来跑去。”
- Expressiveness: Add Mengzi’s feeling about visiting the cemetery: “孟子觉得墓地附近很神秘，也很好玩。”

### Encouragement
- You used the completed-action marker correctly in “去世了,” and the routine expression “每天从早到晚” clearly shows how often Mengzi went there.

## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈觉得在墓地玩儿，害得，所以他们搬家。搬家对孟子有好的影响，他才五岁，可是每天不是写字就是帮妈妈的工作。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 害得：害得自己很担心，The structure requires an affected person followed by a harmful result; here, “自己” clearly refers to the mother, and “很担心” supplies the result. [teacher rule: haide_structure]
- 帮妈妈的工作：帮妈妈做事，The verb “帮” can take a person plus a verb phrase, but it cannot directly take “工作” as the object in this structure. [rule: verb_object_collocation]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好的 -- “搬家对孟子有好的影响”：积极的，specifies that the influence supports Mengzi’s development and learning.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 所以他们搬家：所以他们搬家了，The move is a completed event in the narrative, so the verb needs “了” to mark completion. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: At the mother’s decision, add a short line of speech to show her worry, for example: “妈妈很担心，说：‘我们搬家吧。’”
- Expressiveness: When describing Mengzi’s writing, add one concrete action to make the routine visible, for example: “他握着笔，一笔一画地写字。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show that the move has changed Mengzi’s daily life. The pattern “不是……就是……” is also a strong way to describe his regular activities.

## Scene 3

> 他很聪明的人，难怪他成为有名的人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他很聪明的人：他是一个很聪明的人，A noun phrase such as “a very smart person” needs the copula and a measure word after the subject. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为有名的人：成为了有名的人，This describes a completed result in the story, so use 了 after the verb. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: After “很聪明,” add one concrete action that shows his intelligence, such as “他每天认真读书，遇到问题也会自己思考。”
- Expressiveness: You could make the final achievement more vivid by adding others’ reaction, such as “后来，大家都很佩服他。”

### Encouragement
- You used “难怪” well to connect his intelligence with his later achievement. The sentence also has a clear, positive ending.

- You need to explain that his mother moves because she worries that copying sacrifice rituals will be bad for him.
- Missing scene: After leaving the graveyard, they live near a market, and 孟子 copies people buying and selling every day.
- You need to add that his mother then moves from the market to a school neighborhood, where 孟子 begins studying every day.
- You need to say that 孟子 grows up to become a famous philosopher, not only a famous person.

### Overall Encouragement
