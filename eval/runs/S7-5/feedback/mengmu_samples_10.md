## Scene 1

> 孟子小的时候，从早到晚都跟朋友玩。孟子和他的朋友喜欢玩在墓地里，装祭拜。孟子五岁的时候，跟妈妈搬家。他们搬家不是因为孟子玩得太多，而是因为妈妈认为他们的家离墓地太近。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 喜欢玩在墓地里：喜欢在墓地里玩，A location phrase that sets the place of an action normally comes before the main verb; here 墓地里 modifies 玩.
- 装祭拜：假装祭拜，To express pretending to carry out an action, use 假装 + verb phrase; 装 does not naturally take 祭拜 in this meaning.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 玩 -- “从早到晚都跟朋友玩”：嬉戏，emphasizes the lively, childlike manner of playing with friends.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子小的时候，从早到晚都跟朋友玩：孟子是一个小男孩。小的时候，他从早到晚都跟朋友玩，On first mention, introduce the character with a brief noun phrase before using the name and later pronouns.
- Coherence (Temporality): 孟子五岁的时候，跟妈妈搬家：孟子五岁的时候，跟妈妈搬家了，This is a completed move in the narrative, so the action needs 了.
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery play, add a small action showing how they copy the ceremony, such as “他们一边笑一边祭拜。” This makes the scene easier to picture.
- Expressiveness: At the final sentence, add the mother’s feeling or thought, such as “妈妈觉得很担心。” This can show why the distance from the cemetery matters to her.
- Expressiveness: You could add a short inner thought after the reason for moving: “妈妈心想：‘再住下去，麻烦会越来越多。’” This gives the mother a clearer voice.

### Encouragement
You used “从早到晚都” well to describe a daily routine. The final sentence also clearly contrasts the two reasons for moving with “不是……而是……”.

## Scene 2

> 妈妈认为，住离墓地太近，害得孟子的心气。孟子和孟母搬到子思老师的家，跟子思老师住。孟母帮子思打扫房间和做饭。子思帮孟母教她的儿子。因为孟子现在没有朋友，他老是读书。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 住离墓地太近：住在离墓地太近的地方，The verb for living in a place needs a place phrase introduced by 在.
- 害得孟子的心气：害得孟子心情不好，The structure 害得 needs a person followed by a harmful result clause; a possessive noun phrase alone cannot express that result.
- 老是读书：一直读书，老是 usually conveys a negative, complaining attitude; 一直 describes sustained reading without that complaint.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 跟子思老师住：跟他住，After the teacher has just been named in the same sentence, use a pronoun for the close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟母帮子思打扫房间和做饭：孟母帮他打扫房间和做饭，Use a pronoun when the same person was mentioned recently and no other possible referent competes.
- Coherence (Reference): 子思帮孟母教她的儿子：他帮孟母教她的儿子，Use a pronoun for this close re-occurrence of the teacher’s name.
- Coherence (Temporality): 孟子和孟母搬到子思老师的家：孟子和孟母搬到了子思老师的家，The move is completed before the later actions of living and working there, so the completed action takes 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为孟子现在没有朋友，他老是读书：因为孟子现在没有朋友，所以他老是读书，Use 因为……所以…… to make the cause-and-effect relationship explicit.
- Expressiveness: At the mother’s worry in the first sentence, add her concerned expression or inner thought, for example: 孟母皱着眉头想：“这里不能再住下去了。”
- Expressiveness: When describing Mengzi’s reading, show his quiet action and feeling, for example: 孟子一个人安静地坐在桌子旁边读书，觉得有一点孤单。

### Encouragement
You clearly show a cause for Mengzi’s change in behavior, and the scene gives each character a meaningful action. The sequence from moving to the teacher’s home to Mengzi’s reading is easy to follow.

## Scene 3

> 子思认为，对孩子来说，读书最重要，特别是学哲学。孟子不是练习写文章，就是看哲学书。孟子从五岁一直学哲学。他才五岁就学哲学，难怪他成为很有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 从五岁一直学哲学：从五岁开始一直学哲学，Use 从 + time + 开始 to mark when an action begins; 一直 then shows that the study continued.
- 成为很有名的哲学家：成为了很有名的哲学家，This describes a completed change of identity, so the verb needs 了 to mark the completed result.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子从五岁一直学哲学：他从五岁一直学哲学，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence: 孟子 was the subject of the immediately preceding sentence, and no competing person appears there.
- Expressiveness: After the study routine, add a detail about the child’s attitude toward learning, such as: “他觉得哲学很有意思，每天都想多读一点。”
- Expressiveness: At the final result, add a brief evaluative description of his achievement, such as: “后来，他成了一个受人尊敬的哲学家。”

### Encouragement
- You used both the 不是……就是…… structure and the 才……就…… structure correctly, and they make Mengzi’s daily study and early start easy to understand.
