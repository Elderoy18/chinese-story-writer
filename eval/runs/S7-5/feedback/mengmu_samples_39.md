## Scene 1

> 古代的时候人觉得祭拜非常重要，孟子很小的时候，从早到晚一直祭拜，因为他妈妈告诉他祭拜很重要。虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜。他的同学们老是对他开玩笑了。孟子妈妈看了他的同学们对孟子不好的影响，所以孟子和孟子妈妈搬家了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，this expression redundantly combines a historical time noun with a “time of” phrase.
- 人：人们，use this form when referring to a group of people.
- 看了：看到了，here the mother sees and realizes the classmates’ influence; 看 means “look,” while 看到 means “see/perceive.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不好 -- “他的同学们对孟子不好的影响”：负面，specifies that the influence harms him or has an unfavorable effect.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子很小的时候：一个叫孟子的小男孩很小的时候，on first mention, introduce the character with a full noun phrase rather than only a name.
- Coherence (Reference): 所以孟子和孟子妈妈搬家了：所以她带着他搬家了，after both characters have just been mentioned, use pronouns rather than repeating their names.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他的同学们老是对他开玩笑了：他的同学们老是对他开玩笑，老是 describes a repeated routine, so it takes the zero particle rather than completed-action 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜：他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜，虽然 needs a contrasting second clause with 但是 or 可是; this sentence instead gives a cause with 因为.
- Expressiveness: After “从早到晚一直祭拜,” add Mencius’s feeling to show why he keeps doing it, such as “孟子觉得祭拜很好玩。”
- Expressiveness: At the classmates’ teasing, add a short line of speech to make the scene more lively, such as “他们笑着说：‘你每天都在做什么？’”
- Expressiveness: Before the move, show the mother’s worry through an inner thought, such as “孟子妈妈心想：‘住在这里会影响孩子。’”

### Encouragement
- You use “从早到晚一直” well to show that Mencius repeats the action for a long time. You also include a clear reason for his behavior: his mother has told him that worship is important.

## Scene 2

> 孟子才十一岁，成为一个哲学家。孟子不是祭拜，而是看政治文章，难怪他成为了哲学家。不过，看过很多政治文章对他害得，他有很多问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 孟子才十一岁，成为一个哲学家：孟子才十一岁，就成为了一个哲学家，The pattern with 才 plus an age needs 就 before the main action, and becoming a philosopher is presented as a completed past event, so it takes 了.
- 看过很多政治文章对他害得，他有很多问题：看过很多政治文章害得他有很多问题，害得 must be followed directly by the affected person and then a harmful result; 对他 cannot be placed before 害得.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子不是祭拜：他不是祭拜，孟子 was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other person competes as the referent, so use a pronoun for this close re-occurrence.
- Expressiveness: At the point where he reads political articles, add a small action detail to show his concentration: “他每天坐在窗边，认真地看政治文章。”
- Expressiveness: After “他有很多问题,” show one of his thoughts as a question: “他想：‘为什么有的人能当领导呢？’”
- Expressiveness: When he becomes a philosopher, add others’ reaction to make the achievement feel more vivid: “大家都很佩服他，说他很聪明。”

### Encouragement
You used 不是……而是…… effectively to contrast two different activities. The sentence with 难怪 also clearly shows the connection between reading and becoming a philosopher.

## Scene 3

> 他一直问问题，但是不能解决那些问题。孟子长大，他开始能解决问题。他常常建议你不是学金融，而是学政治，因为如果你懂政治，你会成为高薪。后来孟子成为教授，他教学生很厉害。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不是学金融，而是学政治：不要学金融，而要学政治，This is advice, so use the recommendation pattern with a negative command and an alternative; the original contrast pattern is for correcting or refuting a statement.
- 成为高薪：得到一份高薪的工作，The verb for becoming needs a noun that names an identity or role; a high salary describes a job, not a person’s identity.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 一直 -- “他一直问问题”：不断地，emphasizes that the questions continue one after another without stopping.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子长大：他长大，The name was used in the immediately preceding context and no other male character competes as the referent, so a pronoun is more natural for close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Reference): 你：学生，The narrator has not introduced an addressee, so this pronoun has no clear referent; naming the people receiving the advice makes the reference clear.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他长大，他开始能解决问题：他长大以后，他开始能解决问题，Use a sequence connector to show that being grown up happens before gaining this ability.
- Coherence (Temporality): 孟子成为教授：孟子成为了教授，Becoming a professor is a completed change of state in this past narrative, so it needs 了.
- Expressiveness: At the point where he cannot solve the questions, add an action that shows his frustration, e.g. “他皱着眉头，想了很久也想不明白。”
- Expressiveness: When he gives advice, add a short line of speech to show his voice, e.g. “他说：‘学政治能帮助你了解国家。’”
- Expressiveness: At the professor scene, show the students’ response, e.g. “学生们认真地听他讲课，还常常问他问题。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the character’s development from asking questions to solving them and teaching others. You also use duration words effectively to show repeated actions and habits.
