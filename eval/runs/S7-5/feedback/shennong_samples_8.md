## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个人叫神农。一天，神农正在路上走，一看很多人正在吃一些植物。一些人一吃植物就生病了，神农想帮别人。所以想知道哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，This expression redundantly combines a historical time period with a second “time of” phrase; use the shorter time expression. [vocab list]
- 别人：人们，This scene refers to the group of people Shennong has just observed, not to “other people” contrasted with someone else. [teacher rule: bieren]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 想知道 -- “所以想知道哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃”：弄清楚，emphasizes finding a clear answer about which plants are safe and which are not.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 一天，神农正在路上走，一看很多人正在吃一些植物。一些人一吃植物就生病了：一天，神农正在路上走，一看到很多人正在吃一些植物，就发现一些人一吃植物就生病了，Here the narrator means that Shennong saw the people, so the verb needs the resultative complement 到. The pattern 一……就…… also needs 就 to introduce the immediate following event. [rule: resultative_complement] [teacher rule: yi_jiu]
- Expressiveness: After the people become ill, add a visible detail, such as “有的人捂着肚子，脸色很难看。” This shows illness instead of only stating it.
- Expressiveness: Add Shennong’s concern before his decision, such as “神农很担心，心想：‘大家不知道哪些植物能吃，太危险了！’” This gives the character a feeling and a reason for acting.

### Encouragement
You introduce Shennong clearly at the beginning, and you use “正在” well to show an action in progress. The contrast between plants that are safe to eat and plants that are not safe is also very clear.

## Scene 2

> 然后，神农开始尝很多植物，比方说他吃过生姜、芥兰和菠菜。一些植物吃真好，特别是黄瓜。但是，一些植物毒得很，特别是夹竹桃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 吃真好：吃起来真好，Use the complement 起来 after an action verb to describe the experience or quality of doing that action. [rule: none]
- 毒得很：有毒，毒 is a noun and cannot take the degree complement 得很; use 有毒 to describe a poisonous plant. [teacher rule: du_youdu]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 吃过 -- “他吃过生姜、芥兰和菠菜”：尝过，emphasizes sampling the plants rather than simply eating them；试吃过，highlights that he ate them in order to test them.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After 黄瓜, add a sensory detail to show why it is good to eat, such as “黄瓜又脆又清爽”.
- Expressiveness: After 夹竹桃, describe its appearance before revealing its danger, such as “夹竹桃开着黄色的、像星星一样的花”.
- Expressiveness: At the end, add Shen Nong’s cautious reaction, such as “神农想：这种植物看起来很漂亮，可是不能随便吃。”

### Encouragement
You used 比方说 correctly to introduce clear examples of plants. The contrast between edible plants and dangerous plants also makes this part easy to follow.

## Scene 3

> 一个人对神农说，麻烦我们可以吃什么植物？神农说，这就要看你想不想生病了。以后，神农告诉别人一些植物能吃，一些植物不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 麻烦我们可以吃什么植物：请问，我们可以吃什么植物？，麻烦 means “to trouble/request” and cannot by itself introduce a polite question; 请问 is the appropriate question-opening expression here. [rule: word_choice]
- 这就要看你想不想生病了：这就要看是什么植物了，this pattern should give a flexible answer to the question of which plants are edible; the part after 看 needs to be the relevant question, here what kind of plant it is. [rule: zhe_jiu_yao_kan]
- 以后：后来，when a sentence begins with “afterwards,” use 后来 rather than 以后. [vocab list]
- 别人：人们，别人 distinguishes other people from a person or group already being discussed; here the intended general group is people in general. [teacher rule: bieren]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农告诉：他告诉，because 神农 was the subject in the immediately preceding sentence and no competing character has appeared, a pronoun makes the close re-occurrence smoother. [rule: redundancy_delete]
- Expressiveness: At the first question, add the speaker’s worried expression, such as “一个人皱着眉头问：‘请问，我们可以吃什么植物？’” This shows concern about choosing the wrong plant.
- Expressiveness: When 神农 answers, give one concrete example, such as “神农说：‘像伞一样的植物是灵芝，可以吃。’” This makes his knowledge more vivid and specific.
- Expressiveness: Add the listener’s reaction after receiving the information, such as “那个人听了以后，放心多了。” This gives the scene an emotional result.

### Encouragement
You used the A-not-A question form “想不想” correctly. You also clearly contrasted edible and inedible plants with “一些植物能吃，一些植物不能吃,” which helps the reader understand the main idea.
