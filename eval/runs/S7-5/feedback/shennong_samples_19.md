## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男人觉得植物很有意思。他叫神农。有的植物吃了以后他的肚子疼死了，可是有的吃了以后很舒服。他找很多植物。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 already expresses a time period, so adding 的时候 is redundant.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 找 -- “他找很多植物”：寻找, emphasizes deliberately looking for plants over time；探寻, highlights exploration to discover unknown plants.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一个人男人觉得植物很有意思。他叫神农。→ 一个叫神农的男人觉得植物很有意思。, Introduce the character’s name together with his role at the first mention, rather than naming him only in the following sentence.
- Expressiveness: After the sentence about plants being interesting, add one visual detail about their appearance, such as “有的叶子又圆又绿，有的花像小星星。” This makes the plants feel more real.
- Expressiveness: After the stomachache, show Shennong’s physical feeling with a detail such as “他疼得在地上躺着，难受得不得了。” This helps the reader understand how serious the pain is.
- Expressiveness: After he feels well, add his thought, such as “神农想：原来有的植物能吃，有的植物不能吃！” This shows what he learns from the experience.

### Encouragement
You clearly contrast plants that make Shennong ill with plants that make him feel comfortable. The use of “可是” makes this difference easy for the reader to follow.

## Scene 2

> 凉山很大的山，这也找植物。一天，他找到黄色的花。他听说你跟黄色的花做茶很有用。比方说，你的头发疼死，这冰茶是药。他一喝茶就很不舒服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 凉山很大的山：凉山是一座很大的山，A sentence identifying what a noun is needs the copula 是, and mountains use the measure word 座.
- 这也找植物：他也在这里找植物，A location before an action needs 在 + place; 这里 clearly refers to the mountain, and 他 identifies the person doing the searching.
- 你跟黄色的花做茶：用黄色的花做茶，用 introduces the material used to make something; 你跟 means “you and/with” and cannot introduce the material for making tea.
- 头发疼死：头疼得要死，头发 cannot feel pain; 头疼 describes a headache, and a degree complement needs 得 before 要死.
- 这冰茶：这杯茶，When a demonstrative points to one serving of a countable drink, use 这 + measure word + noun.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 你：他，The narration is about 神农, but 你 suddenly addresses an unintroduced reader. Use 他 to refer back to the already established character.
- Coherence (Temporality): 找到：找到了，Finding the flower is a completed discovery in the past, so the verb needs 了.
- Expressiveness: After describing the mountain, add one sensory detail about the setting, such as “山上空气很新鲜，到处都是绿树。”
- Expressiveness: Make the flower more vivid at “黄色的花,” for example: “那儿开着黄色的像星星一样的花，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: Show 神农’s physical reaction and thoughts after drinking the tea, such as “他肚子疼了起来，躺在地上，心想：‘我会死吗？’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduce a new event with “一天,” and your use of “一喝茶就” effectively shows that the uncomfortable feeling happened immediately after drinking the tea.

## Scene 3

> 糟糕，这黄色的花是夹竹桃，特别是毒。正好在这儿，灵芝药的夹竹桃他吃完了，也写一个课文的植物。谢谢神农。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 这黄色的花：这朵黄色的花，A demonstrative before a noun needs an appropriate measure word here.
- 特别是毒：特别有毒，特别是 introduces a typical example, while 特别 modifies the predicate 有毒; 毒 alone cannot serve as the adjective after it.
- 灵芝药的夹竹桃：灵芝和夹竹桃，的 must link a modifier to a noun; these are two items, so they should be joined with 和.
- 一个课文的植物：一篇关于植物的课文，课文 takes the measure word 篇, and 关于植物 correctly states what the text is about.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 也写一篇关于植物的课文：也写了一篇关于植物的课文，This is a completed writing action, so it needs 了.
- Expressiveness: After the discovery of the flower, add a visual detail to show why it first seemed attractive: “这朵花像星星一样，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: At the moment of danger, add Shennong’s physical reaction and feeling: “神农吓了一跳，肚子疼了起来。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought to make his worry clearer: “神农想：这朵花原来有毒！”

### Encouragement
You clearly identified the yellow flower as 夹竹桃 and showed that it is dangerous. You also used 糟糕 effectively to create an immediate feeling of alarm.
