## Scene 1

> 很久以前，有一个老男人叫神农。神农在古代每一天看到了别的小镇人生病还是不舒服，所以他决定开始找新的药。他下一天开始爬当地的山脉，找了各种各样的植物。神农试一试每个植物，知道有什么好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 有一个老男人叫神农：有一个叫神农的老人，A name used as a modifier should come before the noun with 的; 老人 is also a more neutral term for an elderly man in this story. [rule: attributive_modifier_de]
- 别的小镇人：人们，别的 distinguishes one group from another previously mentioned group, but no comparison group has been established here. [teacher rule: bieren]
- 还是：或者，In this declarative sentence, the two conditions are alternatives, so use 或者 rather than the question-choice connector 还是. [rule: none]
- 下一天：第二天，Use 第二天 to mean “the next day” in a narrative. [rule: time_expression]
- 找了各种各样的植物：找到了各种各样的植物，找 describes searching, while 找到 expresses successfully finding the plants. [vocab list]
- 每个植物：每种植物，种 is the appropriate measure word for kinds of plants. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 试一试 -- “神农试一试每个植物”：尝一尝，specifies that he samples a plant in order to learn about it, which makes his method clearer in this scene.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：他，After the first mention, use a pronoun when the same character is still the clear subject in the next sentence. [rule: redundancy_delete]
- Coherence (Reference): 所以他决定：所以决定，When the subject remains the same within one sentence, use zero form rather than repeating the pronoun. [rule: redundancy_delete]
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：他，The preceding sentence already has only one clear character, so a pronoun is more natural for this close re-occurrence. [rule: redundancy_delete]
- Coherence (Temporality): 每一天看到了：每天看到，This describes a routine, so use the zero particle rather than 了, which marks a completed action. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Conjunction): 神农在古代每一天看到了别的小镇人生病还是不舒服，所以：因为神农在古代每一天看到了别的小镇人生病还是不舒服，所以，Use the paired cause-and-effect connector to show that seeing sick people caused his decision. [rule: none]
- Expressiveness: When describing the sick people, add one concrete symptom to make their suffering visible, such as “有的人肚子疼得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: After his decision, add a short thought to show his motivation, such as “他想：我一定要找到能治病的药！”
- Expressiveness: At the mountain-climbing moment, add a small journey detail, such as “他走啊走啊，爬过了一座又一座高山。”

### Encouragement
You clearly introduce the main character at the beginning and give a logical reason for his decision to search for medicine. The use of “各种各样的植物” is vivid and fits this exploration scene well.

## Scene 2

> 比方说，他一次试一试有些植物的叶，吃得又新鲜又甜。煮这些叶以后，他一喝就知道他发现新的茶叶。虽然他找到了很多的新的植物，但是他的旅程有的时候特别是危险。比方说，他一吃灵芝就开始生病。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一次试一试：有一次，尝了，Use “有一次” to introduce one past event. An action that was actually carried out should use the regular verb with completed aspect, not verb reduplication. [rule: verb_reduplication]
- 吃得又新鲜又甜：吃起来又新鲜又甜，The qualities describe how the leaves taste, so use “吃起来” rather than a 得-complement describing the action of eating. [rule: none]
- 知道他发现新的茶叶：发现了这种叶子可以做茶，“知道” introduces something already known, but this moment describes a new discovery after drinking. Use “发现了” for the completed discovery. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- 特别是危险：特别危险，“特别是” must introduce a typical example after a general statement; it cannot mean “very.” [rule: example_marker_tebie_bifang]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 甜 -- “吃得又新鲜又甜”：清甜，specifies a light, fresh sweetness that suits the taste of leaves or tea.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 比方说：有一次，“比方说” needs to introduce an example of a preceding general statement, but this is the opening event of the scene. “有一次” introduces the event in sequence. [rule: example_marker_tebie_bifang]
- Expressiveness: At the point where the leaves are boiled, add a sensory detail about their smell: “叶子煮开后，飘出一股淡淡的清香。”
- Expressiveness: After the final sentence about becoming ill, show the character’s fear through an inner thought: “他想：‘我会死吗？’”

### Encouragement
You used the pattern “一……就……” clearly to show an immediate result. You also used “虽然……但是……” effectively to contrast the successful discoveries with the danger of the journey.

## Scene 3

> 一个爬山的男人正好看到神农的麻烦，然后给了他一些药。神农最终他的小镇回到，跟别的人分享他的发现。你认为神农的故事真的吗？这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他的小镇回到：回到他的小镇，The destination should follow the verb of return, rather than appearing between the time adverb and the verb. [rule: none]
- 跟别的人分享：跟别人分享，别的人 specifically contrasts people with a previously mentioned person or group; here the general word 别人 is needed. [teacher rule: bieren]
- 神农的故事真的吗？：神农的故事是真的吗？，The adjective predicate 真的 needs 是 in this yes-or-no question pattern. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 麻烦 -- “看到神农的麻烦”：困境，describes a difficult situation more specifically and gives the scene a stronger sense of urgency.
- 药 -- “给了他一些药”：草药，specifies that the medicine comes from plants, which fits this story’s setting.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他的小镇：自己的小镇，Because the town belongs to the subject and another male character was just mentioned, 自己的 clearly shows that the town is Shen Nong’s. [teacher rule: ziji_possessor]
- Coherence (Temporality): 回到：回到了，The return is a completed event in the story, so it needs 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Conjunction): 这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处。：这就要看你相不相信中药对身体有好处了。，The phrase 这就要看……了 requires a question form after 看, such as an A-not-A form, and its final 了 is part of this fixed structure. [rule: zhe_jiu_yao_kan]
- Expressiveness: At the moment the helper appears, add one visible detail to make him easier to imagine, such as “一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人正好看到神农的麻烦。”
- Expressiveness: Show Shen Nong’s physical feeling before the medicine is given, such as “神农躺在地上，肚子疼得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of dialogue when Shen Nong returns, such as “神农对大家说：‘有些植物可以做药，有些植物不能吃。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduce the new helper with a full noun phrase, and you use 看到 and 给了 well to show the two actions in order. Your final question also gives the reader something to think about.
