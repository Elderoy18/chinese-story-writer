## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个男孩名字叫孟子。孟子和孟子的妈妈两个人住在很穷的农村。他们住的地方旁边是个墓地，墓地老是有很多人祭拜。因为人们在祭拜，他们一直在哭。孟子和孟子的妈妈住在这里才一个星期就觉得受不了了，而且害得孟子的学习。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，The original repeats the time meaning because 古代 already refers to a historical time period, while 的时候 also means “at the time of.” [vocab list]
- 而且害得孟子的学习：而且影响了孟子的学习，害得 must be followed by a person and a harmful result clause; it cannot directly take a noun phrase such as 孟子的学习. [teacher rule: haide_structure]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很穷 -- “住在很穷的农村”：贫困的农村地区，identifies the area’s economic condition more precisely while keeping the same meaning.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子的妈妈两个人：孟子和妈妈两个人，Use zero form for the repeated possessor within the same noun phrase; once 孟子 is named, 妈妈 naturally refers to his mother. [rule: none]
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子的妈妈住在这里：孟子和妈妈住在这里，Use zero form for the repeated possessor in the same sentence; repeating 孟子 inside the phrase is unnecessary. [rule: none]
- Coherence (Temporality): 住在这里才一个星期就觉得受不了了：才在这里住了一个星期，就觉得受不了了，In the 才…就… construction, 才 introduces the short duration before the action, and 了 marks the one-week stay as completed. [rule: cai_jiu_construction]
- Expressiveness: At the cemetery description, add a sound detail to make the setting more vivid: “墓地里常常传来伤心的哭声。”
- Expressiveness: When the family can no longer bear the environment, show the mother’s thoughts or speech: “孟子的妈妈心想：‘再住下去，孟子的学习会受影响。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly establish the setting and explain why the cemetery environment troubles the family. Your use of the contrastive timing idea with 才…就… also effectively shows that they became uncomfortable very quickly.

## Scene 2

> 孟子的妈妈觉得孟子取得良好的教育是很重要的，所以孟子的妈妈认为这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好，就打算跟孟子搬家。孟子的妈妈带孟子不是搬到城市，而是搬到了孟子老师的家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 取得良好的教育：接受良好的教育，教育 is something a child receives or accepts; 取得 is more naturally used for achievements, results, or qualifications. [rule: none]
- 不是搬到城市，而是搬到了孟子老师的家：搬到了孟子老师的家，this use of “not X but Y” does not correct a conventional or previously stated mistaken idea; simply state the actual destination. [rule: bushi_ershi_construction]
- 跟孟子搬家：带着孟子搬家，this means that the mother moves while taking her son with her; 跟孟子搬家 suggests that she moves together with him as an equal participant. [vocab list]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 地方 -- “这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好”：环境，specifies that the concern is the child’s learning and living setting rather than only a physical location

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子的妈妈认为：她认为，after the mother was named earlier in the same sentence and no other person competes as the subject, use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子的妈妈带孟子：她带孟子，at the start of the next sentence, the mother is still the recent and only possible referent, so a pronoun avoids unnecessary repetition of the name. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Expressiveness: At the mother’s decision to move, add a brief worried feeling, such as “孟母皱着眉头，心里很着急。”
- Expressiveness: You could add a short line of thought before the move, such as “她心想：‘这里不适合孟子学习。’”
- Expressiveness: When describing the teacher’s home, add one concrete learning-related detail, such as “老师家里有很多书，屋子里很安静。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect the mother’s concern about education to her decision to move, and the completed move is correctly shown with “搬到了.”

## Scene 3

> 孟子跟他的老师从早到晚学习，难怪他的成绩很快就进步了很多。孟子学习的时候，孟子的妈妈不是待在家里就是编织衣服赚钱。以后孟子成为了中国有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 以后孟子成为了中国有名的哲学家：后来孟子成为了中国有名的哲学家，At the beginning of a sentence, “以后” normally follows a stated time point to mean “after”; “后来” introduces a later event in a narrative. [vocab list]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 进步 -- “他的成绩很快就进步了很多”：提高，specifically describes a measurable rise in scores or grades.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the first sentence, add the teacher’s reaction through dialogue, such as “老师高兴地说：‘你的成绩进步得真快！’”
- Expressiveness: In the sentence about the mother, add her feeling to show her effort and concern, such as “孟子的妈妈虽然很累，还是希望孟子认真学习。”
- Expressiveness: At the final sentence, add a brief public reaction to make the ending feel more vivid, such as “人们都很敬佩这位有名的哲学家。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the connection between Mengzi’s hard work and his improved grades. The final sentence also gives the scene a strong sense of achievement.
