## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，孟子和孟母住了在靠墓地的家。孩子才五岁就每天在墓地祭拜了。在那个城市，孟母从早到晚都找工作，可是找不到，害得孟母没有钱。难怪她跟孩子搬家，原来买不了房。他们搬了到别一个城市找工作。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，this time expression is redundant because both parts already refer to a historical time. [vocab list]
- 住了在：住在，an aspect marker cannot be placed between the verb and the location marker; use the location structure without 了 here. [teacher rule: le_placement]
- 跟孩子搬家：带着孟子搬家，this means that the mother takes Mengzi with her when moving; 跟 is not the appropriate verb for this relationship. [vocab list]
- 搬了到：搬到了，put 了 after the resultative/location complement, not between the verb and 到. [teacher rule: le_placement]
- 别一个城市：另一个城市，use 另一个 for one different city; 别的 is used to distinguish other people or things from a previously mentioned one. [teacher rule: bieren]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 找工作 -- “孟母从早到晚都找工作”：求职，specifically names the activity of seeking employment.
- 买不了房 -- “原来买不了房”：买不起房，makes clear that the obstacle is lacking enough money.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟母：一个叫孟子的小男孩和他的母亲，first mention should introduce Mengzi clearly as a character rather than using only his name. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Reference): 孩子：他，Mengzi was mentioned in the immediately preceding sentence, so a pronoun is more natural for a close re-occurrence. [rule: none]
- Coherence (Reference): 那个城市：城里，this is the first mention of a city, so 那个 has no earlier city to refer back to. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Reference): 害得孟母没有钱：害得她没有钱，孟母 is the clear subject in the same sentence, so use a pronoun for the close re-occurrence. [rule: none]
- Coherence (Temporality): 每天在墓地祭拜了：每天在墓地祭拜，daily actions are routines, so they take the zero aspect particle rather than completed-action 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery action, add Mengzi’s feeling to show why he does it. Example: “孟子觉得祭拜很有趣。”
- Expressiveness: When Mengmu cannot find work, add a small physical detail to show her worry. Example: “孟母叹了口气，心里很着急。”

### Encouragement
You used the pattern “才五岁就” correctly to show that Mengzi began the action very young. The contrast between trying to find work and not finding it also gives the scene a clear problem for Mengmu.

## Scene 2

> 这个城市老是很吵，不是人吵架，就是都叫，害得孟子一直不能睡觉。在那儿，孟母也找不到工作。孟子和妈妈要又一个次搬了家。在第三个城市，孟母找到工作，在商店做衣服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 人：人们，This refers to a concrete group making noise in the city, so use the plural noun form. [teacher rule: ren_renmen]
- 要又一个次搬了家：要再搬一次家，For a move that is planned but has not happened yet, use the pattern modal verb + 再 + verb + number + measure word + object. The completed marker 了 does not fit a future plan. [teacher rule: zai_structure]
- 找到工作：找到了工作，Finding the job is a completed action in this past-event narration, so add 了 after the resultative verb. [rule: aspect_le_completed]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After “很吵,” add one sound detail to show why the city is disturbing, for example: “外面吵架的声音一直传进房间。”
- Expressiveness: Show Mengzi’s physical reaction to make his sleeplessness more vivid, for example: “孟子捂着耳朵，觉得很难受。”
- Expressiveness: Add Mengmu’s thought or speech before the move, for example: “孟母说：‘这儿太吵了，我们得再搬一次家。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly show a cause and result with “害得孟子一直不能睡觉,” which makes the problem in the city easy to understand. You also move the scene forward clearly by showing that Mengmu eventually finds work in the third city.

## Scene 3

> 孟子在那个商店也找了到工作，可是不是做衣服的工作，而是喜欢的工作。孟子学习馆学了十五年，他的学习和他的旅行经验很重要，对他很多好处。他是大人的时候，他成为很有名的哲人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 学习馆学：在学校学习，The location needs the preposition and a standard school noun; the original wording does not form a natural place-and-action expression. [rule: word_choice]
- 对他很多好处：对他有很多好处，The fixed collocation is “对 + person + 有好处”; the verb is required before the object phrase. [rule: verb_object_collocation]
- 他是大人的时候：他长大以后，This is the natural expression for the time after someone becomes an adult. [rule: word_choice]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 学习 -- “他的学习和他的旅行经验很重要”：学业，specifies his formal school-based studies rather than learning in a general sense；受教育的经历，emphasizes the experience gained during his years of education

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子：他，Use a pronoun because Mengzi was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other male character competes as the referent. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Reference): 他的旅行经验：旅行经验，Within the same noun phrase, the second possessive pronoun is unnecessary because the first one already applies to both items. [rule: none]
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为：成为了，Use 了 for the completed change of status: he finished becoming a well-known philosopher. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: After the school sentence, add a learning habit to show how he studied, such as “他每天认真地看书，也常常向老师请教问题。”
- Expressiveness: At the final sentence, add Mengmu’s reaction to make the achievement feel more meaningful, such as “孟母听到这个消息，高兴极了。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show a long time span of study before Mengzi’s later achievement. The contrast between the clothing job and the job he likes also gives the first sentence a clear purpose.
