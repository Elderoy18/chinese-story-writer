## Scene 1

> 古代，孟子跟孟母住在墓地的旁边。可是孟母注意，住在墓地的旁边影响孟子。他常常跟朋友们祭拜玩，所以孟母决定他们会搬家。这次，他们住在城市里的商店的旁边，从早到晚很吵。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代：在古代，Use the standard time expression for a historical setting. [rule: time_expression]
- 注意：注意到，Bare 注意 means “pay attention to,” while 注意到 means “notice or realize” a fact. [rule: word_choice]
- 跟朋友们祭拜玩：跟朋友们学着别人祭拜，玩 cannot directly follow 祭拜 to mean treating a ritual as a game; 学着别人祭拜 clearly expresses playful imitation. [rule: word_choice]
- 决定他们会搬家：决定搬家，决定 is followed directly by the action the subject decides to do; do not repeat a future clause after it. [teacher rule: jueding_vp]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很吵 -- “从早到晚很吵”：嘈杂，describes many mixed, loud sounds in the shopping area more precisely.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 可是孟母注意到：孟母注意到，可是 introduces contrast, but living beside a cemetery and noticing its effect are not contrasting ideas; simply state the realization. [rule: none]
- Expressiveness: After “他常常跟朋友们学着别人祭拜,” add an action detail to show the imitation: “他们一边哭，一边跪在地上。”
- Expressiveness: At “从早到晚很吵,” add a sound detail: “商人一直大声叫卖，街上人来人往。”
- Expressiveness: Add Meng Mu’s inner thought before the decision: “孟母心想：‘这儿不适合孟子学习。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly established the setting and introduced both Meng Zi and Meng Mu at the beginning.
- You also used 所以 effectively to connect Meng Zi’s behavior with Meng Mu’s decision.

## Scene 2

> 城市也有点儿危险。害得孟母只好一直陪孟子。孟子也没有朋友，难怪他老是看起来不高兴。住在商店的旁边不是适合孟子，而是让他觉得很伤心。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不是适合孟子：不适合孟子，适合 is a verb and can directly follow the negative adverb 不; it should not follow 是 in this predicate. [rule: word_class_misuse]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 危险 -- “城市也有点儿危险”：不太安全，states more specifically that the city lacks safety while keeping the mild degree of concern.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 而是让他觉得很伤心：因为这让他觉得很伤心，the feeling of sadness gives the cause of why the place is unsuitable; 不是……而是…… is for correcting or contrasting two alternatives, not for this cause relationship. [teacher rule: bushi_ershi]
- Coherence (Conjunction): 孟子也没有朋友，难怪他老是看起来不高兴：难怪他老是看起来不高兴，原来他没有朋友，use the paired pattern 难怪……，原来…… to present the result first and then reveal its reason. [teacher rule: nanguai_yuanlai]
- Expressiveness: After mentioning the city, add a concrete sound detail to show why the environment affects him, such as “从早到晚，商店旁边人来人往，叫卖声不断。”
- Expressiveness: At the final sentence, add Meng Mu’s worried thought or speech, such as “孟母着急地想：‘这儿太吵了，会影响孟子学习。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the consequences of the new environment: Meng Mu has to stay with Meng Zi, and he feels unhappy without friends. The phrase “看起来不高兴” effectively describes what others can observe about his feelings.

## Scene 3

> 所以，孟母和孟子搬到学校的旁边住。才五岁孟子开始学哲学。在学校，孟子不是看书，就是练习写字。将来，他成为中国的一个非常有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 才五岁孟子开始学哲学：才五岁，孟子就开始学哲学，The pattern with 才 plus an age requires 就 before the action to show that it began unusually early. [rule: cai_jiu_construction]
- 将来：后来，This story is recounting events that have already happened, so use the narrative-time expression for “later,” not the future-time expression. [rule: time_expression]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬到学校的旁边住：搬到了学校的旁边住，The move is a completed narrative action, so 了 follows the resultative complement 到. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为中国的一个非常有名的哲学家：成为了中国的一个非常有名的哲学家，Becoming a philosopher is presented as a completed later outcome, so the verb needs 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: After the school setting is introduced, add one concrete detail about the learning environment, such as “学校里很安静，常常能听见学生读书的声音。” This helps the reader understand why this place suits learning.
- Expressiveness: Add Mengmu’s positive feeling after she sees Mengzi studying, such as “孟母看见孟子认真看书，心里很高兴。” This gives the scene an emotional response.
- Expressiveness: A short line of dialogue could make the ending more lively, such as “孟母说：‘这儿真适合你学习！’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the change from an unsuitable neighbourhood to a school setting, and the contrast structure in the third sentence makes Mengzi’s daily activities easy to understand.
