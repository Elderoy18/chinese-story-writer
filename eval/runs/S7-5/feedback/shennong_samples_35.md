## Scene 1

> 在古代，很多医生不知道怎么用药。所以他们找到了很多植物对药。神农先看到很多病人，他们吃很多植物对药。但是我觉得他们的吃的植物，让他们病人。神农觉得这个植物可能特别是毒。所以他想找一个植物对药，他们可以吃对药。正好，他找到了一把花，他想把花放在茶里，对药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很多植物对药：很多可以做药的植物，the phrase “对药” does not mean “to make medicine.” Use “做药,” and place the full modifier before “植物” with 的. [rule: attributive_modifier_de]
- 先看到：先看到了，“看到” shows that the seeing reached a result, and 了 marks this observation as completed. [rule: resultative_complement]
- 吃很多植物：吃了很多植物，these patients’ eating happened before their illness, so the completed action needs 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- 他们的吃的植物：他们吃的植物，the action “吃的” already modifies “植物”; adding 的 after “他们” makes the modifier structure unnatural. [rule: attributive_modifier_de]
- 让他们病人：害得他们生病了，“病人” is a noun meaning an ill person, not a verb or result. Use “害得 + person + harmful result” to show that the plants caused their illness. [teacher rule: haide_structure]
- 是毒：有毒，“毒” cannot be used as an adjective after “是” in this meaning; “有毒” means poisonous. [vocab list]
- 一个植物对药，他们可以吃对药：一种人们可以吃、可以做药的植物，“对药” does not fit either action. This revised noun phrase clearly says that the plant is edible and can be used as medicine. [rule: word_choice]
- 对药：做药，“做药” means to use something as medicine; “对药” does not express this meaning here. [rule: word_choice]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农先看到了很多病人：一个叫神农的人先看到了很多病人，this is the first mention of this character in the scene, so introduce him with a full noun phrase rather than only a name. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Reference): 这个植物可能特别有毒：有些植物可能特别有毒，no single plant has been identified before this sentence, so “这个植物” has no clear referent; use a general noun phrase for some of the plants already mentioned. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Expressiveness: When describing the patients, add one visible sign of illness, such as “很多病人脸色很差，还捂着肚子。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where the dangerous flowers appear, describe their appearance before the character uses them, such as “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花。”
- Expressiveness: Add Shennong’s thought before he tries the flowers, such as “神农想：这种花能不能做药呢？”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the central problem that people become ill after eating unknown plants, and you also give Shennong a reason to investigate them. The scene already has a logical movement from the sick patients to his attempt to find useful plants.

## Scene 2

> 他不知道如果这把花对你的身体好，这就要看如果这把花毒不毒了。神农想找别的植物，但是这很麻烦。正好，他找到了别的花。他一吃花，很快就生病了。他的肚子疼死了。神农不知道他吃不吃灵芝或者夹竹桃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 如果这把花对你的身体好：这朵花对他的身体好不好，After 不知道, an embedded yes/no question uses an A-not-A form such as 好不好, not 如果; 朵 is the suitable measure word for one flower, and the narration should keep the third-person reference. [rule: a_not_a_question]
- 如果这把花毒不毒：这朵花有没有毒，The part after 这就要看 must be a question form; remove 如果 and use 有没有毒. 朵 is the suitable measure word for one flower. [rule: zhe_jiu_yao_kan]
- 吃不吃灵芝或者夹竹桃：吃的是灵芝还是夹竹桃，When the question asks which of two things was involved, use 是……还是…… rather than connecting the two choices with 或者. [rule: a_not_a_question]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 找 -- “神农想找别的植物”：寻找, emphasizes an ongoing search for more plants and fits story narration.
- 麻烦 -- “但是这很麻烦”：费力, makes clear that the search requires much effort; 艰辛, highlights that the process is difficult and demanding.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 花：这朵花，This refers back to the particular flower found in the previous sentence, so use a specific noun phrase rather than the generic noun. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Reference): 他：∅，Within the same sentence, the embedded subject is still 神农, so use zero form instead of repeating a pronoun. [rule: none]
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他一吃花，很快就生病了：他一吃这朵花，就很快生病了，The pattern 一……就…… needs 就 to show that illness followed immediately after the action. [teacher rule: yi_jiu]
- Expressiveness: At the moment he eats the flower, add one visual detail to make the danger clearer, such as: “这朵花又黄又大，像星星一样漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: After the stomach pain, add his physical feeling or worried thought, such as: “他躺在地上，难受得不得了，心想：‘我会不会死？’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the event sequence: searching for a plant, eating it, and then becoming ill. The strong phrase “疼死了” effectively shows how severe the stomach pain feels.

## Scene 3

> 别的人给了神农一个灵芝。这个植物可以让神农更好。比方说，他会觉得更健康。吃灵芝以后，他可以再走。现在他觉得更好。最后，神农找到了很多植物，他可以用对药。这些植物让神农很高兴。神农想跟很多人分享他的新药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别的人给了神农一个灵芝：一个人给了神农一个灵芝，别的人 is used to distinguish other people from a person or group already mentioned; no such contrast has been established here. [teacher rule: bieren]
- 很多植物，他可以用对药：很多可以用来做药的植物，an action phrase modifying 植物 must come before the noun and end with 的; 用来做药 means “use [something] to make medicine,” while 对药 does not fit this meaning. [rule: attributive_modifier_de]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 更好 -- “现在他觉得更好”：好多了，shows that his condition has noticeably improved after being ill.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃灵芝以后：吃了灵芝以后，eating is completed before the later action of walking again, so the earlier action needs 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: After “现在他觉得好多了,” add a physical detail to show his recovery, such as “他的肚子不疼了，也有力气站起来了。”
- Expressiveness: At “这些植物让神农很高兴,” add his inner thought, such as “神农想：我终于找到能帮助病人的药了！”
- Expressiveness: After the final sentence, add a short line of speech showing how he shares the medicine, such as “神农说：‘这种植物可以做药，大家要记下来。’”

### Encouragement
You used several useful story-sequencing expressions, including 吃灵芝以后 and 最后. You also clearly showed that the medicine helped 神农 recover and motivated him to share what he had found.
