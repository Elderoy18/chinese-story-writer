## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有很多人不知道什么植物能吃，什么植物不能吃。有很多人吃植物以后就吃坏肚子了，因为他们吃了毒。那个时候，有一个老人叫神农，他什么植物都觉得好吃，不怕生病，不怕毒。他希望别人也可以吃好吃的植物。可是他知道，如果他们还怕吃毒，不会吃他喜欢的植物。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 already names a time period, so adding 的时候 is redundant. [teacher rule: gudai_de_shihou_why]
- 吃了毒：吃了有毒的植物，毒 is a substance and does not identify the plants that made people ill; 有毒的植物 specifically means poisonous plants. [rule: word_choice]
- 怕吃毒：害怕吃有毒的植物，use 有毒的植物 to describe the poisonous plants being eaten, rather than using 毒 alone after 吃 in this context. [rule: word_choice]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不知道 -- “有很多人不知道什么植物能吃，什么植物不能吃”：分不清，emphasizes that people could not distinguish edible plants from inedible ones.
- 好吃 -- “他什么植物都觉得好吃”：可口，gives a more vivid description of food that tastes pleasant.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the people become ill, add a physical detail such as “他们肚子疼得弯下了腰” to show the consequence more vividly.
- Expressiveness: After introducing 神农, add his inner thought, such as “神农想：我要帮助大家找到能吃的植物。” This can show why he cares about other people.

### Encouragement
You clearly introduce the problem of people not knowing which plants are safe, and you connect their illness to what they ate. You also make 神农’s wish to help others understandable.

## Scene 2

> 所以，他上山下山找植物，吃了很多样子的植物。他是第一人，找到了茶。他把所有的植物写下来了。他一直都吃了，他连毒都能吃。可是他还没吃了一个叫夹竹桃的植物。他找到了那个像星星的植物，觉得很漂亮，马上就吃下了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很多样子的植物：很多种植物，样子 describes an object’s appearance; 种 is the classifier used for different kinds of plants. [rule: word_choice]
- 他是第一人，找到了茶：他是第一个找到茶的人，an ordinal before 人 normally needs the classifier 个, and the relative clause should show what he was the first person to do. [rule: none]
- 一个叫夹竹桃的植物：一种叫夹竹桃的植物，种 is the appropriate classifier for a type or species of plant. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 他一直都吃了：他一直都吃，a habitual or continuing action takes the zero particle, not completed-action 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 还没吃了：还没吃过，after 没, completed-action 了 cannot follow the verb; 没……过 expresses that he has not had this experience before. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: When describing the mountain journey, add a small detail about its difficulty, such as “他走啊走啊，去了很多高山。”
- Expressiveness: After the plant appears, describe its colour as well as its shape to make the image clearer: “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花。”
- Expressiveness: Add Shen Nong’s immediate thought before he eats it, such as “他心想：这种花真漂亮，也许可以吃。”

### Encouragement
You used clear completed-action forms in “找到了茶”, “写下来了”, and “吃下了”. The comparison with a star also gives the final plant a memorable image.

## Scene 3

> 可是夹竹桃的毒很重，让他肚子很痛。他在地上躺着，觉得他快会死了。可是正好有别人找到他了，给了他一个像伞的植物，叫灵芝。他吃灵芝以后就觉得很好，不再生病了。他测量起来了，像星星的植物是毒，像伞的植物是药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别人：有人，别人 distinguishes other people from a previously mentioned person or group; here the rescuer is being introduced for the first time, so an indefinite person is needed. [teacher rule: bieren]
- 像星星的植物是毒：像星星的植物有毒，毒 is a noun here; use 有毒 to describe a plant’s poisonous property. [rule: word_class_misuse]
- 快会死了：快要死了，快要 expresses that an event is about to happen; 会 is not needed for this immediate situation. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 毒很重 -- “夹竹桃的毒很重”：毒性很强，specifies that the plant’s poisonous effect is strong；毒性很大，clearly describes the degree of danger from the poison.
- 很好 -- “就觉得很好”：舒服多了，specifies that his body feels much more comfortable after taking the medicine；好多了，shows a clear improvement from his earlier pain.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 觉得他快会死了：觉得自己快要死了，when the person who is thinking and the person who may die are the same, use 自己 rather than repeating 他 in the same sentence. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃灵芝以后：吃了灵芝以后，taking the medicine is a completed action before the later feeling of recovery, so it needs 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: At the moment of stomach pain, add a physical detail to show how severe it is, such as “肚子疼得像火烧一样”.
- Expressiveness: After he lies down, add his worried thought to reveal his fear, such as “我会死吗？”.
- Expressiveness: When the helper appears, add one visual detail to make the new character easier to imagine, such as “一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人”.

### Encouragement
You clearly show the important sequence of poison, pain, help, and recovery. You also use useful story actions such as “躺着”, “给了”, and “吃……以后” to move the scene forward.
