## Scene 1

> 很久以前，古代的人很不知道什么植物能吃，也什么植物不能吃。所以很多人常常吃不安全的植物，吃不安全的植物对你的身体有坏处，很麻烦的情况。但是一个人，神农，要解决这个问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很不知道：不知道，The negative mental-state verb does not take 很 to show degree; use the plain form for “did not know.” [rule: none]
- ，很麻烦的情况：，这是很麻烦的情况。，A noun phrase cannot stand alone after the previous complete clause; add 这是 to make it a complete judgment. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不安全 -- “很多人常常吃不安全的植物”：有毒的，specifies that the plants are unsafe because they contain poison.
- 有坏处 -- “吃不安全的植物对你的身体有坏处”：会伤害身体，states the physical harm more directly；会损害身体，emphasizes damage to health.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一个 人，神农，要解决这个问题：有一个叫神农的人想要解决这个问题，On a character’s first mention, introduce the name together with a clear noun phrase identifying the character. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Conjunction): 也什么植物不能吃：什么植物不能吃，Also must be followed by a verb phrase, not directly by a noun clause. [teacher rule: ye_vp]
- Expressiveness: After the unsafe-plants sentence, add a concrete consequence such as “很多人吃了以后常常生病。” This makes the danger easier for the reader to picture.
- Expressiveness: At the introduction of 神农, add his determination with an inner thought such as “神农想：我一定要找出能吃的植物。” This gives the character a clearer motivation.

### Encouragement
You clearly establish the main problem early: people cannot tell which plants are safe to eat. Your use of 所以 also successfully connects their lack of knowledge with the danger they face.

## Scene 2

> 他又聪明又努力，旅行很远找到很多植物。比方说，灵芝和夹竹桃。他一吃各种各样的植物就知道能不能吃这些植物。然后，神农写他的主意。他喜欢植物的味道，特别是花。神农遇到了一个黄色的花，一吃就觉得不舒服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 旅行很远：到很远的地方旅行，A destination should be expressed with a location phrase; 很远 alone cannot directly follow 旅行 in this structure. [rule: none]
- 写：写下了，写下 expresses recording an idea and includes the completed result of writing it down. [rule: resultative_complement]
- 一个黄色的花：一朵黄色的花，Flowers use the measure word 朵 rather than the general measure word 个. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不舒服 -- “一吃就觉得不舒服”：肚子疼，identifies the specific physical problem more clearly in this scene；难受得不得了，makes the discomfort feel stronger and more vivid without changing the event.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农写他的主意：他写自己的主意，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when no other person competes as the referent; 自己的 also clearly shows that the idea belongs to the subject. [teacher rule: ziji_possessor]
- Coherence (Reference): 神农遇到了一个黄色的花：他遇到了一朵黄色的花，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence because the previous sentence already clearly refers to him. [rule: redundancy_delete]
- Coherence (Temporality): 找到很多植物：找到了很多植物，The finding is a completed result of the journey, so the last verb in this action sequence takes 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: At the journey, add a small detail about the difficult travel, such as “他走啊走啊，去了很多高山。” This helps the reader picture his effort.
- Expressiveness: When the flower appears, describe its shape and appearance, such as “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花，又大又漂亮。” This makes the discovery more vivid.
- Expressiveness: After he feels ill, add his physical reaction or thought, such as “他在地上躺着，难受得不得了，心想：我会死吗？” This shows his fear instead of only stating the event.

### Encouragement
You use clear plant examples, which helps the reader understand what he is discovering. The final event also has a strong cause-and-result pattern: eating the flower immediately leads to discomfort.

## Scene 3

> 发现了夹竹桃有毒。别人看到他，很快给他吃一个灵芝。神农一吃就觉得比较好。然后，他继续写他发现的信息。会告诉他的朋友哪一个植物能不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别人：一个人，别人 is used to distinguish other people from a person or group already being discussed; this new person should be introduced as an individual. [teacher rule: bieren]
- 看到：看到，use the resultative complement in 看到 because the person successfully notices 神农 rather than merely looking in his direction. [rule: resultative_complement]
- 给他吃一个灵芝：给了他一个灵芝，this is a completed action, so 了 follows 给; the person gives 神农 a lingzhi to eat. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- 觉得比较好：觉得好多了，比较 requires a stated comparison, while 好多了 naturally expresses that his condition improved after eating the lingzhi; 了 marks the change of state. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- 写他发现的信息：写下他发现的信息，写 alone describes the process of writing; 写下 emphasizes recording the information so it is kept. [vocab list]
- 哪一个植物能不能吃：哪一种植物能吃，哪一种植物不能吃，use separate clauses to state which kinds are edible and which kinds are not; 哪一种 is the suitable measure-word phrase for a type of plant. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很快 -- “很快给了他一个灵芝”：赶快，emphasizes that the person acts promptly because 神农 is unwell
- 信息 -- “他发现的信息”：发现，more specifically refers to the knowledge he learned from trying the plants

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: At the rescue, describe the new person briefly so the reader can picture him: “这时，一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人走过来。”
- Expressiveness: After 神农 eats the lingzhi, add a bodily detail to show his recovery: “他的肚子慢慢不疼了。”
- Expressiveness: Before the rescue, add 神农’s worried thought: “我会死吗？”

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect the lingzhi with 神农 feeling better, so the actions in this scene are easy to follow.
- Your use of “一……就……” effectively shows that his reaction happens immediately after he eats the lingzhi.
