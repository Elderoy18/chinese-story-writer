- 糟糕 -- “这个问题神农觉得很糟糕”：严重，makes the harmful consequences of the problem more specific.

- Coherence (Reference): 有的时候，神农：有的时候，他，The character was mentioned very recently and there is no competing character, so a pronoun is the natural close re-occurrence form.
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃了很好吃的植物：吃很好吃的植物，With 有的时候, this describes a repeated habit or condition, so use the zero particle rather than completed-action 了.

- 对我的脑有坏处：对我的脑子有坏处，脑子 is the ordinary expression for a person’s brain in this body-related context; 脑 is more often used inside compounds.
