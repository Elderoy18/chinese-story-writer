## Scene 1

> 古代的时候有一个人叫神农。他需要当上医，一看别的人就想帮助他们。但是，他有一个问题，他不知道什么东西能做药。神农听说一个远远的地方有一种植物，什么病人吃都会觉得身体更好。神农三个月走去，找到有植物的地方，他看到一个很漂亮的植物，又香又好看。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，this expression redundantly combines a historical time period with a second “time of” phrase.
- 医：医生，医 is usually a bound element in modern Chinese, while 医生 is the usual noun for a doctor after 当 or 当上.
- 一看：一看到，here the character sees people rather than merely looks toward them, so 看 needs the resultative complement 到.
- 别的人：别人，use 别人 for people other than the speaker or main character; 别的 is normally used to distinguish a different item or type.
- 听说一个远远的地方有：听说在一个远远的地方有，use 在 before a location when it introduces the place where something exists.
- 什么病人吃都会觉得身体更好：病人吃这种植物都会觉得身体更好，什么病人 does not naturally introduce a general group in this declarative sentence; 病人 gives the generic group, and repeating 这种植物 makes the eaten object clear.
- 一个很漂亮的植物：一株很漂亮的植物，株 is an appropriate measure word for an individual plant; 个 is too general here.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 问题 -- “他有一个问题”：难题，emphasizes that this lack of medical knowledge is a difficult problem to solve
- 东西 -- “他不知道什么东西能做药”：植物，names the specific kind of thing he is trying to identify in this story
- 香 -- “又香又好看”：芬芳，more specifically describes the pleasant scent of a flower or plant

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农听说：他听说，after 神农 has been named and no other character has appeared, use a pronoun for this close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农三个月走去：他走了三个月，after the previous sentence already names 神农, use a pronoun for the close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 三个月走去，找到：走了三个月，找到了，put the duration after the verb, and use 了 to show that the journey and the finding were completed actions.
- Expressiveness: At the final description of the plant, add a concrete visual detail such as “开着黄色的、像星星一样的花” to help the reader picture it.
- Expressiveness: After the sentence about wanting to help others, add a short inner thought, such as “他想：我要学会做药，帮助更多病人。” This shows his motivation directly.
- Expressiveness: When he hears about the plant, add his hopeful reaction, such as “他很兴奋，决定马上去看看。” This makes the transition to his journey more lively.

### Encouragement
You clearly introduce the main character and establish his goal of helping others. The final sentence also uses effective sensory description with “又香又好看,” which begins to make the plant feel vivid.

## Scene 2

> 他觉得，这种一定是做药的花。原来他想错了。吃了以后，神农快生病。 这时一个穿着蓝色衣服的人帮神农，快给他一种红的灵芝。神农吃了以后，他没有病，想起来以前一个人告诉他，灵芝对身体有好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 快：很快就，In a completed past event, “快” alone usually suggests that something is about to happen; “很快就” shows that the illness happened soon afterward.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 帮 -- “一个穿着蓝色衣服的人帮神农”：救治，specifies that the person is giving medical help to an ill character.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他：删去，他 refers to the already named subject in the same sentence, so zero form is more natural: “神农吃了以后，没有病……”.
- Coherence (Temporality): 生病：生病了，The illness begins as a completed change of state in the narrative, so it needs 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 快给他一种红的灵芝：赶快给了他一种红的灵芝，“快 + verb” commonly sounds like an imperative urging someone to act; “赶快” fits the narrator’s description, and the completed giving action needs 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 没有病：病好了，After the earlier illness, this should describe recovery rather than simply the absence of illness; 好了 marks the completed change to a healthy state.
- Expressiveness: At “神农很快就生病了,” add a physical feeling to show how serious the illness is: “他的肚子疼了起来，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: After the blue-clothed person arrives, add a short line of speech to make the rescue more immediate: “快吃这个，它也许能帮你！”
- Expressiveness: At the recovery point, show Shennong’s feeling as well as the event: “神农吃了灵芝以后，觉得好多了，心里非常惊讶。”

### Encouragement
- You used a clear event sequence with “原来,” “吃了以后,” and “这时,” which helps the reader follow the scene. You also connected the lingzhi to its benefit for the body, giving the final sentence a meaningful discovery.

## Scene 3

> 啊，他怎么忘记了？他把灵芝放在包里。一回家就告诉别人。他找到药以后他写下怎么做药在书上。所以人们都知道，那红灵芝对身体好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别人：人们，别人 distinguishes other people from a person already being discussed, while this sentence means the general group of people who would hear the news.
- 写下怎么做药在书上：在书上写下怎么做药，The place phrase should come before the verb in this sentence: subject + place + verb.
- 那红灵芝：那种红灵芝，A demonstrative such as 那 needs a measure word before a noun.
- 对身体好处：对身体有好处，This expression needs 有 between 对身体 and 好处.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 放 -- “他把灵芝放在包里”：收进，emphasizes putting the herb away inside the bag for carrying or keeping safely.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 以后他写下：以后，写下，After the same character has already acted in the first part of the sentence, use zero form rather than repeating the pronoun.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他把灵芝放在包里：他把灵芝放进了包里，This is a completed action in the narrative, so it needs 了; 放进 also clearly shows that the herb goes into the bag.
- Coherence (Temporality): 写下：写下了，Writing the information is a completed narrative action, so it needs 了.
- Expressiveness: After the opening question, add a brief feeling of relief or surprise to show what the character thinks: “他想：我差一点儿忘了！”
- Expressiveness: At the writing moment, add one concrete detail about what he records: “他在书上写下：灵芝可以做药，对身体有好处。”

### Encouragement
- You used a clear sequence from returning home, to sharing the news, to writing it down. The opening rhetorical question also gives the scene an immediate sense of the character’s thought.
