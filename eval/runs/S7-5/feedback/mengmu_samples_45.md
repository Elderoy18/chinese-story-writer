## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子。他们住在墓地的旁边，所以孟子老是祭拜。孟子不是祭拜，就是跟朋友聊天。他很喜欢他的城市，但是城市有才两个学校，都很小。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，the original repeats two time expressions with the same meaning; use one time expression only.
- 城市有才两个学校：城市里只有两所学校，use “只有” to express a limited number, and use the classifier “所” for schools.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子：一个叫孟子的男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国，introduce the character’s name in the first mention instead of using a pronoun and naming him only in the next sentence.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子不是祭拜，就是跟朋友聊天：他不是祭拜，就是跟朋友聊天，after the recent mention of the same character, use a pronoun rather than repeating the name.
- Coherence (Reference): 他很喜欢他的城市：他很喜欢自己住的城市，use “自己的” when the possessor is the same person as the subject, and name the city more clearly on its first mention.
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery sentence, add a concrete detail about what Mengzi sees, such as “孟子常常看见来祭拜的人哭着献花。”
- Expressiveness: Add Mengzi’s feeling about copying the worshippers, such as “他觉得祭拜很有意思。”
- Expressiveness: When describing the city, add a sensory detail to show why he likes it, such as “街上商人从早到晚叫卖，热闹极了。”

### Encouragement
- You used clear cause-and-effect and contrast connectors with “所以” and “但是,” helping the scene move logically.
- The routine actions in the cemetery are easy to understand, and the final sentence gives a clear description of the city’s schools.

## Scene 3

> 他难怪抱怨，他想他的朋友，因为他不要离开他的城市。他的妈妈找到孟子一个老师。跟老师孟子学最重要的文学，所以他成为哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不要：不想，`不要` expresses a prohibition or refusal, while `不想` expresses that he does not want to leave.
- 跟老师孟子学：孟子跟老师学习，The learner’s name should come before `跟老师` so that it is clear that he, rather than a teacher named Mengzi, is doing the learning.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 想 -- “他想他的朋友”：想念，specifically expresses missing friends who are no longer nearby.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他的朋友：自己的朋友，Because the friends belong to the same person who is the subject, `自己的` makes this relationship explicit.
- Coherence (Reference): 他的城市：自己的城市，Because the city is the subject’s own city, `自己的` clearly links the possessor back to him.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他的妈妈找到孟子一个老师：他的妈妈给孟子找到了一个老师，`给孟子` marks Mengzi as the beneficiary, and `了` shows that the successful finding of a teacher was completed.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为哲学家：成为了哲学家，`成为` describes a completed change of state here, so it takes `了`.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他难怪抱怨：难怪他抱怨，原来，Use `难怪……，原来……` to present the complaint first and then reveal its reason.
- Expressiveness: After the complaint, add a small physical detail to show Mengzi’s unhappiness, such as `孟子低着头，一路上都不说话。`
- Expressiveness: Give Mengzi a short line of speech to make his reason more personal, such as `他说：“我舍不得我的朋友，也舍不得这个城市。”`
- Expressiveness: When he studies with the teacher, show one learning action, such as `老师每天教孟子读书，孟子认真地听。`

### Encouragement
- You clearly show Mengzi’s reason for complaining and then move the scene forward by introducing a teacher. The ending also gives the reader a clear sense of his later achievement.
