## Scene 1

> 古代时候，人不知道他们能吃什么植物，常常一吃就死。但是不知道为什么，没有办法。所以有一天，神农出去试一试很多种植物，告诉人什么植物是安全什么是危险。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 人不知道：人们不知道，A concrete group of people should be referred to as 人们 rather than the generic noun 人.
- 他们能吃：能吃，The possessive pronoun is redundant because the people who can eat the plants are already the subject.
- 试一试很多种植物：尝试很多种植物，The reduplicated verb suggests trying something briefly, while trying many kinds of plants calls for the verb 尝试.
- 告诉人：告诉人们，This refers to a concrete group, so 人们 is needed rather than generic 人.
- 什么植物是安全什么是危险：什么植物安全，什么植物危险，Safety and danger can function directly as predicates here; repeating the noun phrase also makes the two categories parallel and clear.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉：告诉了，This is the final completed action in a series of past events, so the last verb takes 了.
- Expressiveness: At the opening, add a concrete result of eating the wrong plants, such as “很多人因为吃了有毒的植物，常常生病。”
- Expressiveness: When introducing the exploration, show Shen Nong’s determination with a thought, such as “神农想：我一定要找出能吃的植物。”
- Expressiveness: Add one small action showing how he tests plants, such as “他看一看植物的颜色，再尝一点儿。”

### Encouragement
You clearly establish the problem that people do not know which plants they can eat, and the transition to Shen Nong’s action is easy to follow. The use of the 一……就…… pattern effectively shows the dangerous result.

## Scene 2

> 发现有的植物对身体非常好，特别是茶，让肚子感觉又暖和又清楚。他告诉朋友：“你吃哪种植物，这就要看你的口味。比方说，你爱吃甜的，可能喜欢糖。”他最喜欢吃花，除了夹竹桃以外，什么都花想吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 又暖和又清楚：又暖和又舒服，清楚 describes clarity of thought or information, not a physical feeling in the stomach; 舒服 describes bodily comfort.
- 什么都花想吃：什么花都想吃，when 什么 modifies the noun 花, the natural pattern is 什么花都 + verb; the original word order is not grammatical.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好 -- “有的植物对身体非常好”：非常有益，states more precisely that the plants benefit health.
- 甜的 -- “你爱吃甜的”：甜食，specifies sweet foods rather than leaving the food noun unstated.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After mentioning tea, add a bodily reaction to show its effect: “喝了茶以后，他觉得肚子暖暖的，很舒服。”
- Expressiveness: In the quoted speech, add a simple comment that shows the character’s discovery: “茶不但好喝，对身体也有好处。”

### Encouragement
You use a concrete example, 茶, to explain which plants are beneficial, and you include direct speech to make the scene more lively. The contrast with 夹竹桃 also helps show that different plants have different effects.

## Scene 3

> 他发现这个口味，一百岁时候，不小心吃了毒的花。一吃就开始死。但是生病时候告诉人什么植物是药，什么植物是毒。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一百岁时候：一百岁的时候，The structure ……的时候 requires 的 after a multi-syllable time expression.
- 毒的花：有毒的花，Use 有毒的 before a noun to describe a poisonous plant or flower; 毒的植物 is not the appropriate form here.
- 开始死：快要死了，Death is a change of state, so it is not normally described with 开始 as an ongoing action in this context; 快要死了 expresses that he was close to dying.
- 什么植物是毒：什么植物有毒，毒 is a noun here; use 有毒 to describe the property of a plant.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 这个口味：一种花的味道，The demonstrative 这个 needs a specific, previously identified taste, but no particular taste has been established. Name the new object with a full noun phrase instead.
- Coherence (Reference): 告诉人：告诉人们，Here the narrator means people as a general group, so 人们 is clearer than the vague singular noun 人.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉：告诉了，This is a completed narrative action, not a speaking verb introducing direct quotation, so it needs 了.
- Expressiveness: When he eats the flower, add a visual detail to make the dangerous plant easier to imagine, such as “那朵花是黄色的，像星星一样。”
- Expressiveness: After “一吃就快要死了,” describe his physical feeling, such as “他的肚子马上疼了起来，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought to show his fear, such as “他想：‘我会死吗？’”

### Encouragement
You used the 一……就…… pattern effectively to show an immediate result after eating the flower. The scene also clearly continues the story of 神农 from the earlier scenes.
