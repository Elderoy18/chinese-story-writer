## Scene 1

> 一天，很久很久以前，孟子和他妈妈住在中国。孟子妈妈的工作是祭拜，孟子不高兴，因为他说我们老是搬家，为什么？他妈妈说，从早到晚，我一直祭拜，很多墓地得我祭拜。所以我们得去很多不同的地方。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一天，很久很久以前：很久很久以前的一天，A long-ago time expression should modify the specific day as one phrase; placing the two time expressions side by side is unnatural here. [rule: time_expression]
- 孟子不高兴，因为他说我们老是搬家，为什么？：孟子不高兴，因为他说：“我们老是搬家，为什么？”，Direct speech after a saying verb should be introduced with a colon and enclosed in quotation marks. [rule: none]
- 很多墓地得我祭拜：我得祭拜很多墓地，The subject comes before the modal verb, and the object comes after the main verb: subject + 得 + verb + object. [rule: adverbial_word_order]
- 他妈妈说，从早到晚，我一直祭拜，很多墓地得我祭拜。所以我们得去很多不同的地方。：他妈妈说：“从早到晚，我一直祭拜，我得祭拜很多墓地。所以我们得去很多不同的地方。”，This is the mother’s direct speech, so it needs a colon before the quotation and quotation marks around the full speech. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不高兴 -- “孟子不高兴”：不满，more specifically shows that he is dissatisfied with having to move repeatedly.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子妈妈的工作：他妈妈的工作，The name was mentioned in the previous sentence and no other mother is competing, so a pronoun is more natural for this close re-occurrence. [rule: none]
- Expressiveness: After “孟子不高兴,” add a small action and a quoted question, such as “孟子皱着眉头说：‘我们又要搬家吗？’” This makes his dissatisfaction easier to picture.
- Expressiveness: When describing the cemetery work, add one sensory detail, such as “墓地里很安静，只有风吹树叶的声音。” This can create a clearer setting for the scene.

### Encouragement
- You clearly show why Mengzi feels unhappy and why his mother needs to travel to different places. Your use of “因为” and “所以” gives the scene a logical cause-and-effect structure.

## Scene 2

> 孟子想，难怪我们常常搬家，原来我妈妈很忙。可是常常搬家害得孟子很难找到朋友。每次搬家孟子也觉得新的地方不是太热就是太冷，不是太大就是太小。在哪的地方，他都不高兴。孟子想帮助妈妈祭拜，可是他六岁，所以不能祭拜。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在哪的地方：不管在哪儿，Use the location word 哪儿 rather than 哪的地方; the pattern 不管……都…… expresses “regardless of where.” [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 找到 -- “常常搬家害得孟子很难找到朋友”：交到，more precisely describes forming friendships rather than locating people
- 不高兴 -- “他都不高兴”：闷闷不乐，shows a quiet, continuing unhappy mood after each move
- 新的 -- “新的地方”：陌生的，emphasizes that each new home feels unfamiliar to Meng子

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子很难找到朋友：他很难找到朋友，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the referent is clear and no other male character competes. [rule: none]
- Coherence (Reference): 每次搬家孟子也觉得：每次搬家他也觉得，Use a pronoun because the same character was named in the immediately preceding sentence. [rule: none]
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子想帮助妈妈祭拜：他想帮助妈妈祭拜，Use a pronoun for the same character when the previous sentence already identifies him clearly. [rule: none]
- Expressiveness: After the sentence about repeated moves, add a feeling of loneliness: “每次到了陌生的地方，他都觉得很孤单。”
- Expressiveness: At the final sentence, add a short line of inner speech to show his wish to help: “他心想：‘我什么时候才能帮妈妈呢？’”
- Expressiveness: You could make the mother’s busyness more concrete near the opening: “妈妈从早到晚都在墓地祭拜，很少有时间陪他。”

### Encouragement
You used the pattern 难怪……，原来…… naturally to connect Meng子的 realization with the reason for the family’s moves. You also expressed a clear cause-and-effect relationship with 害得, showing why frequent moves make friendship difficult.

## Scene 3

> 四年后，孟子开始帮妈妈祭拜。他画画很厉害，他越来越喜欢祭拜，所以他祭拜不是想帮妈妈工作，而是他很喜欢画画。孟子成为很著名的古代画家。今天很多中国人很喜欢孟子。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 祭拜不是想帮妈妈工作，而是他很喜欢画画：祭拜不是为了帮妈妈工作，而是因为很喜欢画画，This sentence corrects the reason for the action, so the two halves should clearly express purpose and cause with matching structures. [teacher rule: bushi_ershi]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很厉害 -- “他画画很厉害”：擅长画画, identifies the specific skill more directly；画得很出色, gives a clearer picture of the quality of his paintings
- 很著名 -- “很著名的古代画家”：享有盛名, shows that many people recognize and admire his reputation

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他越来越喜欢祭拜：越来越喜欢祭拜，Within the same sentence, the subject has already been introduced, so use zero form instead of repeating the pronoun. [rule: redundancy_delete]
- Coherence (Reference): 所以他祭拜：所以祭拜，The subject is already clear from the preceding clauses in the same sentence, so the repeated pronoun should be omitted. [rule: redundancy_delete]
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子成为很著名的古代画家：他成为很著名的古代画家，After a recent mention of the same character and with no competing character, use a pronoun rather than repeating the name. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Reference): 很多中国人很喜欢孟子：很多中国人很喜欢他，The character has just been mentioned and no other possible male referent intervenes, so a pronoun is natural here. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为很著名的古代画家：成为了很著名的古代画家，This describes a completed change of status in the narrative, so use 了 after the verb. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: After the first sentence, add a small action detail showing how he helps, such as “他认真地准备祭拜用的东西。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where he enjoys painting, add an inner thought or a short quotation, such as “孟子想：‘画画真有意思！’”
- Expressiveness: When describing his reputation, add a concrete reaction from others, such as “很多人都来看他的画。”

### Encouragement
- You use “四年后” clearly to move the story forward in time, and “越来越” effectively shows that Mengzi’s interest grows gradually.
