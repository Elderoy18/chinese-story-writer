## Scene 1

> 有一天孟子去了墓地玩儿，但是他妈妈觉得这是不合适的地方让孩子玩，所以她决定了他们会搬家找更好的地方。她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福，而且住在这儿教育不太好。因为她望子成龙，所以她会一直搬家才能找到质量好的教育。然后他们搬到了离城市更近的地方。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不合适的地方让孩子玩：不适合孩子玩的地方，Use 适合 with the person or activity that a place suits; here the activity should modify 地方 directly. [vocab list]
- 决定了：决定，Mental-state verbs such as 决定 normally take the zero aspect particle, not 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- 决定他们会搬家找更好的地方：决定搬家，找更好的地方，决定 is followed directly by a verb phrase, so the repeated subject and future modal clause are unnecessary. [teacher rule: jueding_vp]
- 她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福：她担心儿子的童年不会幸福，恐怕 means “I am afraid that” and cannot use another subject such as 她 in this structure; 担心 expresses that she is worried. [teacher rule: kongpa]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 一直 -- “她会一直搬家才能找到质量好的教育”：不断，specifies repeated moves rather than one uninterrupted action.
- 更好的 -- “找更好的地方”：更适合孩子成长的，explains exactly why the new place would be better in this context.
- 质量好的教育 -- “找到质量好的教育”：优质教育，gives a concise and precise term for education of high quality.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the mother realizes the cemetery is unsuitable, add a small physical reaction to show her concern, for example: 孟母皱着眉头，看着正在玩的孟子。
- Expressiveness: At the point where she makes her decision, add a short line of dialogue to reveal her thoughts, for example: 她说：“我得给孟子找一个更适合学习的地方。”
- Expressiveness: When describing the move closer to the city, add one concrete detail about the new environment, for example: 那里街上有很多商人，声音很热闹。

### Encouragement
You clearly show the mother’s concern for her son and connect her concern to the decision to move. You also use completed-action forms well in 孟子去了墓地 and 他们搬到了离城市更近的地方.

## Scene 2

> 他们住才一月就又搬家。古代的时候，一般人不喜欢卖东西的人。但是孟子老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿，害得妈妈着急得很，因为她恐怕儿子会成为跟他们很像。她告诉了孟子：他们不是朋友，而是不好的人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 住才一月：才住了一个月，Place 才 before the duration, and use a measure word with the number; this pattern is 才 + duration + 就 + action. [rule: cai_jiu_construction]
- 又搬家：又搬家了，The move is a completed past action, so the final verb needs 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- 古代的时候：在古代，The original is redundant because both 古代 and 的时候 express time. [vocab list]
- 老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿：老是跟这些卖东西的人玩儿，老是 and 常常 both mark frequent actions, so using both is redundant. [rule: none]
- 害得妈妈着急得很：让妈妈很着急，害得 requires a clearly harmful result after the affected person; ordinary worry is better expressed with 让. [rule: haide_harmful_result]
- 她恐怕：她担心，恐怕 means “I am afraid that” and normally cannot take another person as its subject; use 担心 for a mother’s worry. [teacher rule: kongpa]
- 成为跟他们很像：变得跟他们很像，成为 is used for becoming an identity or role, while 变得 introduces a change into a described state. [rule: word_choice]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 卖东西的人 -- “一般人不喜欢卖东西的人”：商贩，identifies these people more specifically as sellers or vendors.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉了：告诉，A speaking verb that introduces direct speech takes the zero particle rather than 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: At the description of the sellers, add a sensory detail about the busy street, such as “街上人来人往，叫卖声不断。”
- Expressiveness: Show what Mengzi finds appealing about the sellers’ actions, such as “孟子觉得他们收钱、讨价还价很有意思。”
- Expressiveness: After the mother becomes worried, add her private thought or speech, such as “孟母心想：‘这样下去，他会不会只想做买卖？’”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the mother’s concern and give her a direct line of speech, which makes the scene easy to follow. The use of 才……就…… also shows that you are trying to express the quick sequence of events.

## Scene 3

> 你不是当数学家，而是当哲学家，不能当卖东西的人。所以他们又搬家。在他们第三个家，孟子学了怎么祭拜。妈妈感觉这个地方适合他们，告诉了孟子：我们找到了最后的家。在这儿妈妈让儿子从早到晚学习，他难怪成为有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他们第三个家：他们的第三个家，A possessive modifier such as “their” needs 的 before the noun it modifies. [rule: attributive_modifier_de]
- 他难怪：难怪他，难怪 introduces the whole result clause, so it should come before the person and action being explained. [teacher rule: nanguai_yuanlai]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 找到了 -- “我们找到了最后的家”：选定了，emphasizes that the family has chosen this home as the one where they will stay.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 又搬家：又搬家了，This is a repeated action that has already happened in the narrative, so it needs 了 to mark the completed move. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉了孟子：告诉孟子，A speaking verb that introduces quoted speech normally uses the zero particle rather than 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为有名的哲学家：成为了有名的哲学家，Becoming a philosopher is presented as a completed outcome, so 成为 takes 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: After “妈妈感觉这个地方适合他们,” add the mother’s happy reaction: “妈妈看到孟子认真学习，心里很高兴。”
- Expressiveness: Make the final home more vivid by adding a detail about its learning environment: “他们的新家在学校旁边，周围很安静。”

### Encouragement
- You used the contrast pattern “不是……而是……” clearly to show the mother’s expectations for her son.
- You also connected the family’s moves to Mengzi’s later success, giving the scene a clear direction.
