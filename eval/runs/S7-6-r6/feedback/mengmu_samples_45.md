## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子。他们住在墓地的旁边，所以孟子老是祭拜。孟子不是祭拜，就是跟朋友聊天。他很喜欢他的城市，但是城市有才两个学校，都很小。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 already denotes a time, so adding 的时候 redundantly repeats the time meaning.
- 城市有才两个学校：城市只有两个学校，use 只有 before the quantity to express “only”; 有才 is not the correct order for this meaning.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 聊天 -- “跟朋友聊天”：闲聊，specifies casual, relaxed conversation between friends.
- 个 -- “两个学校”：所，the conventional measure word for schools, making the noun phrase more precise.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子：一个叫孟子的男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国，at first mention, introduce the character with both a name and a full noun phrase rather than giving the name only in the following sentence.
- Expressiveness: At the cemetery sentence, add what Mengzi sees and why the activity interests him, for example: “孟子看到很多人来墓地祭拜，觉得很有趣。”
- Expressiveness: When describing his imitation of the ritual, add an action detail to show his mood, for example: “孟子一边笑，一边学着别人祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: After mentioning the small schools, add one concrete detail about them, for example: “两所学校都很小，教室也不多。”

### Encouragement
You used clear, short sentences and kept the main characters easy to follow. The cause-and-effect connection with 所以 also makes the move from living near the cemetery to Mengzi’s behaviour easy to understand.

## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈不是不喜欢他们的城市，而是想孟子有很好的教育。他们搬家到有很多学校的城市。他们从早到晚走路，孟子一直抱怨。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 想孟子有很好的教育：想让孟子受到很好的教育，The verb phrase needs to express that the mother wants Mengzi to receive an education; “have an education” does not fit this meaning naturally in Chinese.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们搬家到有很多学校的城市：他们搬到了有很多学校的城市，This move is a completed event in the narrative, so the predicate needs 了; 搬到 expresses moving to a destination.
- Coherence (Temporality): 从早到晚走路：走了一整天，This describes one completed stretch of walking during the move, so use 了 to mark the completed action.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他们搬家到有很多学校的城市：所以，他们搬到了有很多学校的城市，The mother’s wish for Mengzi’s education is the cause, and the move is its result, so use 因为……所以…… or add 所以 before the result.
- Expressiveness: At the point where Mengzi complains, add a physical detail and quoted speech to show his tiredness: “孟子皱着眉头说：‘走了这么久，我的脚都酸了！’”
- Expressiveness: After the mother’s reason for moving, add her inner thought to make her motivation more vivid: “妈妈心想：‘这个城市的学校太少了。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the mother’s motivation for moving, and the contrast structure helps the reader understand that she is thinking about Mengzi’s education.
- The phrase “从早到晚” effectively shows that the journey feels long and tiring.

## Scene 3

> 他难怪抱怨，他想他的朋友，因为他不要离开他的城市。他的妈妈找到孟子一个老师。跟老师孟子学最重要的文学，所以他成为哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 找到孟子一个老师：给孟子找到了一个老师，use 给 to introduce the person who benefits from the action; 找到了 shows that the mother successfully found a teacher, and 了 marks the completed action.
- 跟老师孟子学：孟子跟老师学，place the subject before the phrase beginning with 跟老师.
- 不要离开：不想离开，use 不想 to express that a character does not want to do something; 不要 is more often used for a prohibition or instruction.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他的城市：自己的城市，because the city belongs to the subject of this clause, 自己的 makes the possessor clear.
- Coherence (Reference): 他成为哲学家：孟子成为了哲学家，after both 孟子 and 老师 appear in the previous clause, 他 could refer to either person; repeat 孟子 to make the referent unambiguous.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为哲学家：成为了哲学家，this is a completed change of state in the narrative, so becoming a philosopher takes 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他难怪抱怨，他想他的朋友：难怪他抱怨，原来他想自己的朋友，place 难怪 before the result and use 原来 to introduce the reason that explains it.
- Expressiveness: At the complaint, add a visible emotion and a line of speech, such as: 孟子皱着眉头说：“我不想离开这里，我会想念朋友的。”
- Expressiveness: When the teacher appears, add one learning detail to show the new situation, such as: 老师每天教孟子读书，还给他讲很多故事。

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect Mengzi’s complaint to his wish to stay in the city, and you show a later change from travelling to studying with a teacher.
