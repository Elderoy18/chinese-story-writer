## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有很多人不知道什么植物能吃，什么植物不能吃。有很多人吃植物以后就吃坏肚子了，因为他们吃了毒。那个时候，有一个老人叫神农，他什么植物都觉得好吃，不怕生病，不怕毒。他希望别人也可以吃好吃的植物。可是他知道，如果他们还怕吃毒，不会吃他喜欢的植物。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，this time expression is redundant because both parts refer to time; use the shorter form.
- 吃了毒：吃了有毒的植物，毒 by itself means poison and does not identify what the people ate; the revised phrase clearly names poisonous plants.
- 怕吃毒：怕吃到有毒的植物，毒 alone refers to poison, while the scene is about people accidentally eating poisonous plants.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不知道 -- “有很多人不知道什么植物能吃，什么植物不能吃”：分不清，emphasizes that people could not distinguish edible plants from inedible ones.
- 好吃 -- “他希望别人也可以吃好吃的植物”：可口，describes food or plants with a pleasant taste more specifically.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After “吃坏肚子了,” add a physical detail to show the illness, such as “很多人肚子疼得直冒冷汗。”
- Expressiveness: At “有一个老人叫神农,” add a short thought showing his motivation, such as “神农想：我要找出能吃的植物。”
- Expressiveness: After “他希望别人也可以吃好吃的植物,” add an evaluative feeling, such as “他觉得大家不应该因为吃错植物而生病。”

### Encouragement
You clearly establish the problem at the beginning: people cannot tell which plants are safe, and some become ill. You also introduce 神农 with a clear wish to help other people, which gives this scene a strong focus.

## Scene 2

> 所以，他上山下山找植物，吃了很多样子的植物。他是第一人，找到了茶。他把所有的植物写下来了。他一直都吃了，他连毒都能吃。可是他还没吃了一个叫夹竹桃的植物。他找到了那个像星星的植物，觉得很漂亮，马上就吃下了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很多样子的植物：很多种植物，样子 refers to an object’s appearance, while 种 is the measure word for different types of plants.
- 他是第一人，找到了茶：他是第一个找到茶的人，use 第一个……的人 to say that someone was the first person to complete a particular action.

### Vocabulary Suggestions

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 他一直都吃了：他一直都吃，an ongoing or habitual action with 一直 uses the zero particle; 了 marks a completed action and does not fit here.
- Coherence (Temporality): 还没吃了：还没吃过，after 没, do not use 了 for an unfinished action; 吃过 expresses that he has not yet had this experience.
- Expressiveness: At the moment he finds the flower, add its colour and shape to make the setting clearer: “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花。”
- Expressiveness: After he notices the flower, add a brief thought or spoken reaction to show why he eats it so quickly: “这种花真漂亮，我想尝一尝。”
- Expressiveness: When he eats it, add a small physical detail to make the action more vivid: “他摘下一朵花，放进嘴里吃了下去。”

## Scene 3

> 可是夹竹桃的毒很重，让他肚子很痛。他在地上躺着，觉得他快会死了。可是正好有别人找到他了，给了他一个像伞的植物，叫灵芝。他吃灵芝以后就觉得很好，不再生病了。他测量起来了，像星星的植物是毒，像伞的植物是药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- “别人”：“一个人”，the word “别人” distinguishes other people from a person or group already being discussed; this new rescuer should be introduced as “一个人”.
- “是毒”：“有毒”，“毒” is a noun meaning poison, while “有毒” is the adjectival predicate meaning poisonous.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- “毒很重” -- “夹竹桃的毒很重”：毒性很强，more precisely describes the strength of the plant’s poisonous effect.
- “很痛” -- “让他肚子很痛”：疼得厉害，gives a more vivid sense of how severe the pain is.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): “吃灵芝以后”：“吃了灵芝以后”，the eating is completed before the following result, so the completed action needs “了”.
- Expressiveness: At “他在地上躺着”, add a physical detail to show his suffering. Example: “他在地上躺着，肚子疼得不得了，额头上都是汗。”
- Expressiveness: At the rescue, describe the new person’s appearance so the scene is easier to picture. Example: “这时，一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人走过来，给了他灵芝。”
- Expressiveness: After “觉得他快会死了”, add his worried inner thought as quoted speech. Example: “他想：‘我会死吗？’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the cause of the pain, the rescue, and the recovery in a logical order. Your use of “可是” also creates a helpful contrast between the dangerous moment and the arrival of help.
