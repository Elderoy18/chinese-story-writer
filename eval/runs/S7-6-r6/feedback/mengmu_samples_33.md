## Scene 1

> 一天，很久很久以前，孟子和他妈妈住在中国。孟子妈妈的工作是祭拜，孟子不高兴，因为他说我们老是搬家，为什么？他妈妈说，从早到晚，我一直祭拜，很多墓地得我祭拜。所以我们得去很多不同的地方。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一天，很久很久以前：很久很久以前的一天，A broad past-time expression should come before the specific day it modifies.
- 很多墓地得我祭拜：很多墓地需要我祭拜，得 is a modal verb and must be followed directly by a verb phrase; it cannot be followed by a person as its object. 需要 correctly expresses that the graves require the speaker’s worship.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不高兴 -- “孟子不高兴”：不满，more specifically shows that he is dissatisfied with the repeated moves；烦恼，shows that the moves are troubling him.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 孟子不高兴，因为他说我们老是搬家，为什么？：孟子不高兴，问：“我们为什么老是搬家？”，因为 should introduce a cause for the preceding result, but this part is the character’s question instead; use 问 to introduce the quoted question.
- Expressiveness: At the moment of the question, add a small action to show the child’s feelings: “孟子皱着眉头，问：‘我们为什么老是搬家？’”
- Expressiveness: When the mother explains her work, add a detail that shows she is tired from travelling: “妈妈叹了一口气，说：‘从早到晚，我一直祭拜。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the reason for the family’s frequent moves, and the dialogue helps the reader understand both characters’ situation.

## Scene 2

> 孟子想，难怪我们常常搬家，原来我妈妈很忙。可是常常搬家害得孟子很难找到朋友。每次搬家孟子也觉得新的地方不是太热就是太冷，不是太大就是太小。在哪的地方，他都不高兴。孟子想帮助妈妈祭拜，可是他六岁，所以不能祭拜。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在哪的地方，他都不高兴：无论在哪个地方，他都不高兴，The location question word requires the measure word in “哪个地方”, and “无论……都……” expresses that the statement applies to every place.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 新的地方 -- “孟子也觉得新的地方不是太热就是太冷”：陌生的地方，emphasizes that each place feels unfamiliar to him；新的环境，refers more specifically to the overall living surroundings after a move.
- 不高兴 -- “他都不高兴”：闷闷不乐，shows a quiet, lasting unhappy mood rather than a general lack of happiness.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子很难找到朋友：他很难找到朋友，Use a pronoun because the character was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other person competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Reference): 每次搬家孟子也觉得：每次搬家他也觉得，Use a pronoun for the same recently mentioned character.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子想帮助妈妈祭拜：他想帮助妈妈祭拜，Use a pronoun because the previous sentence already identifies him clearly.
- Expressiveness: After the difficulty of making friends, add an internal feeling to show why the moving is painful, such as “他觉得很孤单，也很想念以前的朋友。”
- Expressiveness: At the end, turn his thought into a short line of inner speech, such as “我也想帮妈妈，可是我还太小了。”

### Encouragement
You used the pattern “难怪……，原来……” very effectively to connect Mengzi’s realization with his mother’s busy life. You also used “不是……就是……” correctly to describe the uncomfortable features of each new place.

## Scene 3

> 四年后，孟子开始帮妈妈祭拜。他画画很厉害，他越来越喜欢祭拜，所以他祭拜不是想帮妈妈工作，而是他很喜欢画画。孟子成为很著名的古代画家。今天很多中国人很喜欢孟子。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 所以他祭拜不是想帮妈妈工作，而是他很喜欢画画：所以他祭拜，不是为了帮妈妈的忙，而是因为喜欢画画，The paired contrast should present two parallel motives. The original wording also makes “helping Mother work” sound like working for her, rather than assisting her.
- 孟子成为很著名的古代画家：孟子成为了很著名的古代画家，Use 了 to show that this change of identity was completed in the narrative.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 厉害 -- “他画画很厉害”：很擅长画画，states more precisely that he has skill in drawing；画得很好，focuses directly on the quality of his drawings.
- 祭拜 -- “孟子开始帮妈妈祭拜”：祭奠，more specifically describes paying respects to dead people at a grave.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the first sentence, add a small action detail to show what he does during the ritual, such as “他一边祭拜，一边在纸上画下墓地旁的花。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where he starts enjoying the activity, add his feeling or thought, such as “孟子觉得祭拜很有趣，也喜欢把看到的东西画下来。”
- Expressiveness: After describing his drawing skill, add a short line of dialogue from his mother, such as “妈妈看着他的画，说：‘你画得真好！’”

### Encouragement
You used a clear time expression to move the story forward, and you gave a reason for why Mengzi enjoys the activity. The sentence about his drawing skill also helps show his character’s interest.
