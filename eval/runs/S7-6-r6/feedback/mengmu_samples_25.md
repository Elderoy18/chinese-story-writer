## Scene 1

> 这是古代的故事。孟子小的时候，他的爸爸很早就去世了。从他的家到墓地很近，所以他每天老是从早到晚去墓地跟朋友们玩玩儿。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 从他的家到墓地很近：他的家离墓地很近，Use the pattern A 离 B 很近 to state that two places are close; 从……到…… introduces a route or range rather than this location relationship.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子小的时候：有一个小男孩叫孟子；他小的时候，On a character’s first mention, introduce the name together with who the character is; after that introduction, use a pronoun for the close re-occurrence.
- Expressiveness: At the cemetery scene, add one concrete detail about what Mengzi sees, such as: “他看见有人哭着祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: Add Mengzi’s reaction to make his reason for going there more vivid, such as: “他觉得祭拜很有趣。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish the early setting and use a cause-and-effect connection with “所以”. The time phrase “从早到晚” also gives the scene a strong sense of Mengzi’s daily routine.

## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈觉得在墓地玩儿，害得，所以他们搬家。搬家对孟子有好的影响，他才五岁，可是每天不是写字就是帮妈妈的工作。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 害得：害得她老是道歉，This structure needs a person affected by the action and a harmful result after it.
- 帮妈妈的工作：帮妈妈干活，The verb phrase cannot use “工作” directly as the object after “帮妈妈”; this correction expresses helping with the mother’s tasks.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好 -- “搬家对孟子有好的影响”：积极的，specifies that the influence is beneficial rather than merely generally “good.”

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们搬家：他们搬家了，The move is presented as a completed event, so it needs 了.
- Expressiveness: At the cemetery-playing moment, add a concrete action to show what the child is doing, for example: “孟子学着别人祭拜，还一边笑一边玩儿。”
- Expressiveness: After the mother’s reaction, add her inner worry or quoted speech, for example: “孟母心想：‘如果一直住在这儿，麻烦会越来越多。’”

### Encouragement
- You used the contrast pattern “才……可是……” effectively to show that Mengzi is very young but already has a busy daily routine.
- The “不是……就是……” pattern also gives the final sentence a clear sense of repeated daily activities.

## Scene 3

> 他很聪明的人，难怪他成为有名的人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他很聪明的人：他是一个很聪明的人，A nominal predicate needs the copula “是”; adding “一个” also makes the description of a person natural.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 难怪他成为有名的人：难怪成为有名的人，After “他” has already been named as the subject in this sentence, use zero form rather than repeating the pronoun.
- Expressiveness: At “很聪明”, add a concrete quality that shows his intelligence, such as “爱读书，学得很快”.
- Expressiveness: At “有名的人”, make the reason for his fame more specific with a phrase such as “受到大家尊敬”.

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect the character’s intelligence with his later success by using “难怪”, which creates a logical ending.
