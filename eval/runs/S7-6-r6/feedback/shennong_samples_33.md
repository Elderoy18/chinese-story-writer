## Scene 1

> 古代时候，人不知道他们能吃什么植物，常常一吃就死。但是不知道为什么，没有办法。所以有一天，神农出去试一试很多种植物，告诉人什么植物是安全什么是危险。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代时候：在古代，a time noun such as 古代 does not need the additional 时候 phrase; this also avoids the missing 的 required in a regular ……的时候 structure.
- 人不知道：人们不知道，this refers to a concrete group of people, so 人们 is needed rather than the generic noun 人.
- 他们能吃什么植物：能吃什么植物，the pronoun repeats the same group already named as 人们 and is unnecessary.
- 什么植物是安全什么是危险：哪些植物是安全的，哪些植物是危险的，parallel indirect questions need clear repeated question phrases, and 安全的／危险的 modify the implied noun 植物.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很多种 -- “试一试很多种植物”：各种各样的，emphasizes that the plants differ in type and appearance while keeping the meaning of “many kinds.”

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：有一个叫神农的人，this is the character’s first mention, so introduce the name with a full noun phrase before later using shorter references.
- Coherence (Temporality): 有一天，神农出去试一试很多种植物：有一天，神农出去尝试了很多种植物，the event is presented as completed on one particular day, so use 了; the reduplicated form 试一试 describes a brief trial and does not fit trying many kinds.
- Coherence (Conjunction): ，告诉人什么植物：，然后告诉人们什么植物，add 然后 to show that telling people happened after the plant-testing action.
- Expressiveness: At the point where 神农 decides to act, add his motivation or an inner thought, such as “神农想：我要找出能吃的植物。”
- Expressiveness: When describing the plants he tests, add one simple visual detail, such as “有的叶子又大又绿，有的花是黄色的。”
- Expressiveness: After the explanation of his goal, add a short line of speech to make the scene more lively, such as “神农说：‘我会告诉大家哪些植物有毒。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish the danger that people face before introducing 神农, which gives his action a meaningful purpose. Your use of 一……就…… in “一吃就死” also communicates the immediate result very clearly.

## Scene 2

> 发现有的植物对身体非常好，特别是茶，让肚子感觉又暖和又清楚。他告诉朋友：“你吃哪种植物，这就要看你的口味。比方说，你爱吃甜的，可能喜欢糖。”他最喜欢吃花，除了夹竹桃以外，什么都花想吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 让肚子感觉又暖和又清楚：让人觉得肚子暖和，头脑也清楚，清楚 describes a person’s mind or thinking, not a stomach; this revision gives each description the noun it can correctly modify.
- 你吃哪种植物，这就要看你的口味。：你吃哪种植物？这就要看你的口味了。The first clause is a question, so it needs a question mark; the response pattern ends with the fixed final 了.
- 什么都花想吃：什么花都想吃，when 什么 means “every kind of,” it must directly modify 花 in the pattern 什么花都…… .

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 发现 -- “发现有的植物对身体非常好”：辨认出，emphasizes recognizing which plants have this effect after observing them；了解到，emphasizes gaining this knowledge through trying and learning.
- 非常好 -- “有的植物对身体非常好”：非常有益，states more precisely that the plants bring a benefit to health；有益健康，expresses the health benefit in a compact way.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: At the tea description, add a sensory detail about its taste or warmth: “他喝了一口茶，觉得又香又暖和。”
- Expressiveness: After discovering tea’s benefit, add Shennong’s pleased reaction or thought: “神农高兴地想：这种植物可以帮助生病的人。”
- Expressiveness: In the quoted speech, make the explanation sound more like natural dialogue: “神农说：‘每个人喜欢的味道不一样。你爱吃甜的，可能就喜欢糖。’”

### Encouragement
You used a clear example with tea and connected it to its effect on the body. The final sentence also uses the useful contrast pattern “除了……以外” to show Shennong’s strong interest in flowers.

## Scene 3

> 他发现这个口味，一百岁时候，不小心吃了毒的花。一吃就开始死。但是生病时候告诉人什么植物是药，什么植物是毒。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 毒的花：有毒的花，Use the adjectival phrase “有毒的” to describe a poisonous flower; “毒的” is not the natural form here.
- 一百岁时候：一百岁的时候，A multi-syllable time expression before “时候” needs “的.”
- 什么植物是毒：什么植物有毒，“毒” is a noun, while “有毒” describes that a plant is poisonous.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不小心 -- “不小心吃了毒的花”：误食了，specifically means accidentally eating something, especially food or a plant.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 告诉人：告诉人们，“人” should not refer to a particular audience as a concrete group; “人们” clearly means the people he tells.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉：告诉了，This is one completed telling action in the past, so it needs “了.”
- Expressiveness: At the moment he eats the flower, add one visual detail to make the danger more vivid: “那朵黄色的花又大又漂亮，像星星一样。”
- Expressiveness: After he becomes ill, show a physical feeling rather than only saying that he starts to die: “他的肚子突然疼了起来，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech when he explains his discovery: “这种花有毒，不能吃！”

### Encouragement
- You used the “一……就……” pattern correctly to show that the bad result followed immediately after eating the flower.
- You also kept the same character and the plant-testing topic connected to the earlier scenes.
