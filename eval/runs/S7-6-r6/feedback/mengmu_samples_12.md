## Scene 1

> 古代，孟子跟孟母住在墓地的旁边。可是孟母注意，住在墓地的旁边影响孟子。他常常跟朋友们祭拜玩，所以孟母决定他们会搬家。这次，他们住在城市里的商店的旁边，从早到晚很吵。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 孟母注意：孟母注意到，注意到 means “notice/realize” an observation; 注意 by itself more often means “pay attention” and does not fit before this clause.
- 祭拜玩：玩祭拜的游戏，use 玩 with a noun phrase for an activity; 祭拜 can describe the kind of game.
- 决定他们会搬家：决定搬家，决定 should be followed directly by the verb phrase describing the decision.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 吵 -- “从早到晚很吵”：嘈杂，specifies that many sounds are mixed together；喧闹，suggests lively, loud activity around the shops.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 古代，孟子跟孟母住在墓地的旁边。：在古代，有一个小男孩叫孟子，他和母亲住在墓地旁边。，At their first appearance, introduce the main character with a name and identity; then use a pronoun for the close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 可是孟母注意到：孟母注意到，可是 introduces contrast, but the previous sentence and this observation do not form a contrast.
- Expressiveness: At the cemetery scene, add Mengzi’s feeling to show why he copies the ritual, for example: “孟子觉得祭拜很好玩。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought from Mengmu before the decision, for example: “孟母心想：‘住在这儿会影响孟子。’”
- Expressiveness: Make the noisy setting more concrete by naming sounds, for example: “商人从早到晚都在叫卖，所以外面很嘈杂。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the change from the quiet cemetery setting to the noisy shop area, and the cause-and-effect link with 所以 is well chosen.
- You also use 常常 correctly to describe Mengzi’s repeated action.

## Scene 2

> 城市也有点儿危险。害得孟母只好一直陪孟子。孟子也没有朋友，难怪他老是看起来不高兴。住在商店的旁边不是适合孟子，而是让他觉得很伤心。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 住在商店的旁边不是适合孟子，而是让他觉得很伤心：住在商店的旁边不适合孟子，还让他觉得很伤心，The pattern “不是……而是……” is used to reject one idea and replace it with another; here, both clauses describe negative effects, so “不……还……” links them correctly.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不高兴 -- “难怪他老是看起来不高兴”：闷闷不乐，shows a quiet, lingering unhappiness that fits a child who has no friends.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After “孟子也没有朋友,” add a small action showing his loneliness, such as “他常常一个人站在商店门口，看着别人说话。”
- Expressiveness: At “害得孟母只好一直陪孟子,” add Meng Mu’s worried thought or speech, such as “孟母心想：‘这儿人太多，我不能让他一个人出去。’”
- Expressiveness: Describe the busy setting near the shops to make the contrast with Meng Zi’s loneliness clearer, such as “商店旁边人来人往，声音很吵，可是孟子觉得很孤单。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the result of the setting on both Meng Mu and Meng Zi, and the use of “害得” connects the first two sentences effectively. Your pronouns are also clear because both characters have already been introduced.

## Scene 3

> 所以，孟母和孟子搬到学校的旁边住。才五岁孟子开始学哲学。在学校，孟子不是看书，就是练习写字。将来，他成为中国的一个非常有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 才五岁孟子开始学哲学：才五岁，孟子就开始学哲学，The pattern for an action that begins unusually early is 才 + age/number + 就 + verb.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬到学校的旁边住：搬到了学校的旁边住，The move is a completed event in the story, so it needs 了 after the resultative complement 到.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为：成为了，Becoming a philosopher is presented as a completed later result, so the verb needs 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 将来：后来，The surrounding events are narrated as past events, so 后来 clearly marks the next stage in the story’s sequence rather than a future prediction.
- Expressiveness: At the school scene, add one quiet environmental detail to show why this new place suits studying: “学校旁边很安静，只听见学生读书的声音。”
- Expressiveness: After the study routine, add Mengzi’s feeling or concentration: “孟子觉得看书很有意思，每天都认真学习。”
- Expressiveness: Add Mengmu’s reaction to make the change of setting more vivid: “孟母看到孟子认真读书，心里非常高兴。”

### Encouragement
- You used the pattern “不是……就是……” correctly to show Mengzi’s regular study activities.
- The final sentence gives a clear outcome for Mengzi and connects well to his earlier learning.
