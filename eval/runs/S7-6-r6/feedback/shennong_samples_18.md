## Scene 1

> 神农和他的家人住在古代，所以不知道什么植物能吃。因为他的家人常常生病了，所以神农和他的哥哥一起去旅行找可以吃的植物。他们去很多不一样的地方，比方说高山、山谷和沙漠。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 住在古代：在古代生活，古代 is a time period rather than a place, so it cannot follow 住在; use 在古代 to mark the time of living.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 找 -- “一起去旅行找可以吃的植物”：寻找，emphasizes deliberately searching for plants; 探寻，emphasizes exploring unfamiliar places to find an answer.
- 去旅行 -- “神农和他的哥哥一起去旅行找可以吃的植物”：踏上旅程，emphasizes the beginning of a journey; 启程，highlights setting out for the search.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 常常生病了：常常生病，habitual actions use the zero particle rather than 了, because they are not single completed events.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们去很多不一样的地方：他们去了很多不一样的地方，this is a completed narrative action, so 去 needs 了.
- Expressiveness: At the point where the family is often ill, add Shen Nong’s feeling to show his motivation: “神农看到家人常常生病，心里很担心。”
- Expressiveness: Make the journey more vivid by showing how they move through the places: “他们翻过高山，走过山谷，还穿过了炎热的沙漠。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech before the journey: “神农对哥哥说：‘我们去找能治病的植物吧！’”

### Encouragement
You clearly show why Shen Nong begins his journey: his family’s illness gives the action a strong reason. Your list of mountains, valleys, and deserts also creates a sense that the journey is wide-ranging.
