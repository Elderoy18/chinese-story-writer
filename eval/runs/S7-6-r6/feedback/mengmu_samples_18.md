## Scene 2

> 孟子的妈妈觉得孟子取得良好的教育是很重要的，所以孟子的妈妈认为这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好，就打算跟孟子搬家。孟子的妈妈带孟子不是搬到城市，而是搬到了孟子老师的家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 取得良好的教育：接受良好的教育，教育 is normally something a person receives; 取得 is used for achievements or results that someone obtains.
- 跟孟子搬家：带着孟子搬家，跟孟子搬家 suggests that the mother and child move together as equal participants, while 带着孟子搬家 clearly means that the mother takes her child with her.
- 孟子老师的家：孟子的老师的家，when referring to Mencius’s teacher, the possessive marker 的 is needed after 孟子; without it, 孟子老师 can be read as “Teacher Mencius.”

- 不太好 -- “对儿子的教育不太好”：不太合适，more precisely says that this environment does not suit his education

- Coherence (Reference): 孟子的妈妈（in “孟子的妈妈带孟子不是搬到城市”）：她，her name appeared in the preceding sentence, so 她 is the natural close re-occurrence form.
