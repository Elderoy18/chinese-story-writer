## Scene 1

> 古代的时候人觉得祭拜非常重要，孟子很小的时候，从早到晚一直祭拜，因为他妈妈告诉他祭拜很重要。虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜。他的同学们老是对他开玩笑了。孟子妈妈看了他的同学们对孟子不好的影响，所以孟子和孟子妈妈搬家了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候人觉得：在古代，人们觉得，The time phrase is redundant because the historical period already expresses time, and the people in this setting are a concrete group, so use 人们.
- 看了他的同学们对孟子不好的影响：看到了他的同学们对孟子不好的影响，This means that Mengzi’s mother noticed the influence; 看 expresses looking, while 看到 expresses successfully seeing or noticing a result.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不好 -- “他的同学们对孟子不好的影响”：负面，specifies that the influence harms Mengzi and forms the precise phrase “负面影响”.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子很小的时候：有一个小男孩叫孟子；他很小的时候，Mengzi is appearing for the first time, so introduce him with both his name and who he is before using later reference.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子妈妈搬家了：孟子和妈妈搬家了，Within the same clause, do not repeat the name inside the second reference; the kinship noun already clearly refers to Mengzi’s mother.
- Coherence (Temporality): 老是对他开玩笑了：老是对他开玩笑，A habitual action with 老是 uses the zero particle, not 了, because it is not one completed event.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜：他没有朋友，因为他每天只祭拜，Because the second clause gives the cause, use 因为; 虽然 needs a contrasting result clause with 但是 or 可是.
- Expressiveness: After the description of Mengzi’s worship routine, add one visible action detail to show what he does: “他低着头，认真地鞠躬。”
- Expressiveness: Before the move, add Mengzi’s mother’s worried thought to reveal her feelings: “孟子的妈妈很担心，心想：‘这样下去会影响孟子。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show that worship is an everyday routine by using a concrete time range from morning to night.
- The final sentence also gives a clear reason for the family’s move, which helps the scene progress logically.

## Scene 2

> 孟子才十一岁，成为一个哲学家。孟子不是祭拜，而是看政治文章，难怪他成为了哲学家。不过，看过很多政治文章对他害得，他有很多问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 才十一岁，成为：才十一岁，就成为，The pattern with a young age uses 才 + number + 就 + verb to show that the achievement happened unusually early.
- 不是祭拜，而是看政治文章：不再祭拜，而是看政治文章，The pattern 不是……而是…… is for correcting or refuting an idea; here the intended meaning is that he stopped one activity and did another instead, so 不再 is needed.
- 看过很多政治文章对他害得，他有很多问题：他看过很多政治文章，害得他有很多问题，害得 needs the event as its subject, followed by the person affected and a harmful result; 对他 cannot come before 害得 in this structure.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子：他，After mentioning 孟子 in the immediately preceding sentence, use a pronoun because no other male character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为一个哲学家：成为了一个哲学家，Becoming a philosopher is presented as a completed achievement at age eleven, so the completed action needs 了.
- Expressiveness: At the point where he reads, add a simple detail about his focused action, such as “他每天坐在桌子前，认真地看政治文章。”
- Expressiveness: When he has questions, show one thought directly, such as “他想：‘为什么人们要这样做呢？’”
- Expressiveness: Add a feeling to show his interest in learning, such as “他觉得这些文章很有意思，越看越想知道更多。”

### Encouragement
You used useful academic story vocabulary, including “政治文章” and “哲学家.” You also tried to connect reading with the character’s thoughts, which gives the scene a clear learning focus.

## Scene 3

> 他一直问问题，但是不能解决那些问题。孟子长大，他开始能解决问题。他常常建议你不是学金融，而是学政治，因为如果你懂政治，你会成为高薪。后来孟子成为教授，他教学生很厉害。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不是学金融，而是学政治：不要学金融，而要学政治，This is advice about what someone should and should not study, so the negative-and-alternative pattern fits better than the corrective contrast pattern.
- 成为高薪：有一份高薪的工作，The word 高薪 describes a job with high pay, not a person; a person can have such a job.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 一直 -- “他一直问问题”：不断，emphasizes that he keeps raising questions again and again during this period.
- 很厉害 -- “他教学生很厉害”：很擅长教学生，states more precisely that teaching students is a skill he does especially well；教学生很有办法，suggests that he has effective ways to teach.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 你：学生，The listener has not been introduced, so 你 has no clear referent here; use a full noun for the people receiving his advice.
- Coherence (Temporality): 后来孟子成为教授：后来孟子成为了教授，Use 了 because becoming a professor is a completed change of state before the following teaching action.
- Expressiveness: At the point where he can solve problems, add a feeling or thought, such as “孟子高兴地想：‘我终于明白了！’”
- Expressiveness: Turn his advice into direct speech to show his personality, such as “孟子说：‘不要只学金融，也要学政治。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show Mengzi’s development from asking questions to being able to solve them. Your scene also uses time progression with “后来” effectively to move the narrative forward.
