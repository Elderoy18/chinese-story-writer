## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个人叫神农。一天，神农正在路上走，一看很多人正在吃一些植物。一些人一吃植物就生病了，神农想帮别人。所以想知道哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，The original repeats the idea of “time”: 古代 already names a historical period, while 的时候 also means “at the time of.”
- 一看很多人正在吃一些植物：看到很多人正在吃一些植物，Use 看到 when the meaning is that he successfully sees something; 看 describes the action of looking.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the people begin eating plants, add a small visual detail to make the setting easier to imagine: “路边长着许多绿色的植物。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where Shennong wants to help, add his worried thought: “神农心想：他们这样吃，会不会生病？”
- Expressiveness: When describing the sick people, show their condition with an observable detail: “有的人捂着肚子，脸色很难看。”

### Encouragement
You clearly establish the historical setting and introduce Shennong at the beginning. The sentence using the immediate-result pattern to describe people becoming ill is also effective and easy to understand.

## Scene 2

> 然后，神农开始尝很多植物，比方说他吃过生姜、芥兰和菠菜。一些植物吃真好，特别是黄瓜。但是，一些植物毒得很，特别是夹竹桃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一些植物吃真好：一些植物吃起来真好吃，Use the complement pattern with “吃起来” to describe how food tastes; “吃真好” is not a complete natural predicate.
- 一些植物毒得很：一些植物很有毒，“毒” cannot be intensified with “得很”; “有毒” is the correct predicate for plants that contain poison.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 尝 -- “神农开始尝很多植物”：尝试，emphasizes that he is testing the plants to learn whether they are safe or useful.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After “特别是黄瓜,” add one sensory detail about its taste, such as “黄瓜吃起来清清凉凉的。” This makes the positive example more vivid.
- Expressiveness: At “特别是夹竹桃,” describe the plant’s appearance before revealing its danger, such as “夹竹桃开着黄色的、像星星一样的花。” This creates a stronger contrast between its beauty and its danger.
- Expressiveness: Add a brief evaluative contrast after the poisonous-plant sentence, such as “它看起来很漂亮，可是不能吃。” This gives the reader a clear warning in the narrator’s voice.

### Encouragement
You used “然后” and “但是” effectively to move from the earlier scene into a contrast between safe and dangerous plants. The specific examples of vegetables also make the scene easy to understand.

## Scene 3

> 一个人对神农说，麻烦我们可以吃什么植物？神农说，这就要看你想不想生病了。以后，神农告诉别人一些植物能吃，一些植物不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 麻烦我们可以吃什么植物：请问，我们可以吃什么植物？，麻烦 means “to trouble someone” or “to ask someone for help”; it cannot by itself introduce a direct question. 请问 is the appropriate polite question opener here.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 神农告诉别人一些植物能吃，一些植物不能吃：神农告诉了别人，一些植物能吃，一些植物不能吃，This is a completed event that happens later in the story, so 告诉 takes 了.
- Expressiveness: At the person’s question, add a feeling to show why the question matters: “那个人很担心，因为他不想生病。”
- Expressiveness: In the reply, add a short quoted explanation that gives the answer more detail: “神农说：‘这就要看是哪种植物了。’”
- Expressiveness: After the final sentence, add one concrete danger from the story: “有毒的植物吃了会让人肚子疼。”

### Encouragement
- You used dialogue clearly, and the question-and-answer structure makes this short scene easy to follow. Your final sentence also clearly contrasts edible and inedible plants.
