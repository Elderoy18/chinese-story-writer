## Scene 1

> 有一天孟子去了墓地玩儿，但是他妈妈觉得这是不合适的地方让孩子玩，所以她决定了他们会搬家找更好的地方。她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福，而且住在这儿教育不太好。因为她望子成龙，所以她会一直搬家才能找到质量好的教育。然后他们搬到了离城市更近的地方。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 这是不合适的地方让孩子玩：这不是适合孩子玩儿的地方，To describe whether a place suits an activity, use the structure 适合 + person + VP; the activity cannot be placed after the noun phrase in this way.
- 他们会搬家找：搬家，找，After 决定, use the action phrase directly; repeating a new subject and a future clause is unnecessary here.
- 她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福：她担心儿子的童年不会幸福，恐怕 means “I am afraid that” and cannot follow a subject other than 我; use 担心 when the subject is afraid about something.
- 会一直搬家才能：得不断搬家，才能，才能 marks a result that can happen only after a necessary condition; the required action should come before 才能, with a suitable modal such as 得.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 更好的地方 -- “搬家，找更好的地方”：教育环境更好的地方，specifies that the place is better because its educational setting is better for the child.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 有一天孟子去了墓地玩儿：有一天，一个小男孩叫孟子。他去了墓地玩儿，This is the character’s first mention, so introduce him with a brief noun phrase before using later pronouns.
- Coherence (Temporality): 决定了：决定，决定 is a mental-state verb and takes the zero particle rather than 了 when it introduces a planned action.
- Expressiveness: After the visit to the cemetery, add one observed detail to show why the place affects the child, for example: “孟子看见来祭拜的人，觉得他们的动作很有趣。”
- Expressiveness: At the mother’s decision, add her feeling or a short line of speech, for example: “孟母皱着眉头说：‘这儿不适合孩子成长。’”
- Expressiveness: After the move, describe the new setting with one sensory detail, for example: “街上有很多商人卖东西，从早到晚都很热闹。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the mother’s motivation through the cause-and-effect connection with 因为 and 所以. You also used 搬到了 correctly to show that the move was completed.

## Scene 2

> 他们住才一月就又搬家。古代的时候，一般人不喜欢卖东西的人。但是孟子老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿，害得妈妈着急得很，因为她恐怕儿子会成为跟他们很像。她告诉了孟子：他们不是朋友，而是不好的人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，this expression redundantly combines two time expressions; use the shorter time phrase.
- 老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿：老是跟这些卖东西的人玩儿，both frequency adverbs modify the same verb, so using both together is redundant and unnatural.
- 害得妈妈着急得很：让妈妈很着急，害得 requires a clearly harmful result, while being worried is better expressed with 让.
- 她恐怕儿子会成为跟他们很像：她担心儿子会变得跟他们很像，恐怕 cannot take 她 as its subject in this meaning; use 担心 for a person’s fear, and use 变得 for becoming similar in a quality.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 卖东西的人 -- “这些卖东西的人”：商人，names the people’s job more concisely and specifically in this market setting

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们住才一月就又搬家：他们才住了一个月，就又搬家了，才 should come before the verb phrase; 一个月 is the usual duration expression, and 了 marks both the completed period of living there and the completed move.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉了孟子：告诉孟子，remove 了 because a saying verb that introduces quoted speech normally takes the zero particle rather than marking completion.
- Expressiveness: At “卖东西的人,” add one sensory detail about the market to show why it attracts孟子, such as “街上从早到晚都很热闹，商人不停地叫卖。”
- Expressiveness: At the mother’s worry, show her feeling through an action or thought, such as “妈妈皱着眉头，心里很着急。”
- Expressiveness: Make the mother’s warning more vivid with a direct line of speech, such as “孟母说：‘他们不是你的朋友，你不能老跟着他们玩儿。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the contrast between what other people think and what孟子 does by using 但是. You also give the mother a clear reason for worrying, which helps the scene feel connected.

## Scene 3

> 你不是当数学家，而是当哲学家，不能当卖东西的人。所以他们又搬家。在他们第三个家，孟子学了怎么祭拜。妈妈感觉这个地方适合他们，告诉了孟子：我们找到了最后的家。在这儿妈妈让儿子从早到晚学习，他难怪成为有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在他们第三个家：在他们的第三个家里，A possessive modifier needs 的 before the noun, and 家里 completes the location phrase after 在.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉了孟子：：告诉孟子：，A speaking verb that introduces direct speech normally takes the zero particle; 了 is not needed here.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为：成为了，Becoming a philosopher is presented as a completed change that happened after this period of study, so it needs 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他难怪成为有名的哲学家：难怪他成为了有名的哲学家，Place 难怪 before the clause it introduces to show that the earlier study explains the later result.
- Expressiveness: At the point where the mother judges the home suitable, add one concrete detail about the environment, such as “这儿很安静，旁边就是学校。”
- Expressiveness: Show Mengzi’s response to studying so the reader can see his change, such as “孟子认真地读书，觉得学习很有意思。”
- Expressiveness: Make the mother’s final words warmer and more emotional, such as “妈妈高兴地说：‘我们终于找到合适的家了！’”

### Encouragement
- You used 所以 and 又 effectively to show that the family moved again as a result of the mother’s decision.  
- The ending clearly connects Mengzi’s long period of study with his later success as a philosopher.
