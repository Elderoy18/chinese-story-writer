## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，孟子和孟母住了在靠墓地的家。孩子才五岁就每天在墓地祭拜了。在那个城市，孟母从早到晚都找工作，可是找不到，害得孟母没有钱。难怪她跟孩子搬家，原来买不了房。他们搬了到别一个城市找工作。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，Both parts refer to time, so using them together is redundant; the shorter time expression is natural in narration.
- 住了在靠墓地的家：住在靠近墓地的家里，A continuing living situation does not take completed-action 了; use 住在 before the location, and 靠近 means “near.”
- 跟孩子搬家：带着孩子搬家，跟 expresses doing something together as equal participants, while 带着 shows that the mother takes her child with her.
- 搬了到：搬到了，Put 了 after the directional complement 到: 搬到 + 了 shows that the move was completed.
- 别一个城市：另一个城市，Use 另一个 for one additional city; 别的 is used to distinguish other people or things from the one already discussed.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 买不了 -- “原来买不了房”：买不起，specifies that the reason she cannot buy a home is a lack of money.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孩子：他，Meng子 was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other child has appeared, so use a pronoun for this close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Reference): 害得孟母没有钱：害得没有钱，The same person is already the subject of the sentence, so use zero form rather than repeat the name within the same sentence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 每天在墓地祭拜了：每天在墓地祭拜，Every day describes a routine, so the verb takes the zero particle rather than completed-action 了.
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery activity, add a concrete detail about what Meng子 observes, such as “他每天看到很多人来墓地祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: Show Meng子的 feeling or playful behaviour at the cemetery, such as “他觉得很好玩，常常学别人祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: Add Meng母’s worried thought before the move, such as “孟母心想：‘再住在这儿，孩子会学坏的。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish the main characters and use time expressions such as “从早到晚” and “每天” to show their daily life. The reason for the family’s move is also easy to understand.

## Scene 2

> 这个城市老是很吵，不是人吵架，就是都叫，害得孟子一直不能睡觉。在那儿，孟母也找不到工作。孟子和妈妈要又一个次搬了家。在第三个城市，孟母找到工作，在商店做衣服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不是人吵架，就是都叫：不是有人吵架，就是有人大叫，A bare 人 cannot refer to a specific, indefinite set of people in this situation; 有人 introduces the people clearly, and the two clauses become parallel.
- 又一个次搬：再搬一次，For a move that has not happened yet, use 再 rather than 又; the required order is 再 + 搬 + 一次.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 吵 -- “这个城市老是很吵”：嘈杂，describes many mixed, disturbing sounds in the city more precisely.
- 睡觉 -- “孟子一直不能睡觉”：入睡，specifically emphasizes that the noise makes it difficult for him to fall asleep.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和妈妈：孟子和她，孟母 was named in the immediately previous sentence and no other female character competes with her, so a pronoun is more natural for this close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬了家：搬家，After the modal verb 要, do not use 了 because the move is planned for the future rather than completed.
- Coherence (Temporality): 孟母找到工作：孟母找到了工作，Finding the job is a completed event, so the verb needs 了 before its object.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 找不到工作。孟子：找不到工作，所以孟子，The inability to find work is a cause for the decision to move, so use 所以 to introduce the result.
- Expressiveness: After the description of the noise, add a concrete sound detail to make the setting vivid, such as “从早到晚，外面都是吵架声和叫喊声。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where the family decides to move, add Meng Mu’s worried thought or speech, such as “孟母着急地说：‘这儿太吵了，我们得再搬一次家。’”
- Expressiveness: When Meng Mu finds work, add her feeling to show the change in her situation, such as “孟母终于找到了工作，心里很高兴。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show two believable problems in the city—noise and difficulty finding work—and use 害得 effectively to show how the noise affects Mengzi.
- The final sentence gives Meng Mu a concrete job, which makes the scene feel more specific.

## Scene 3

> 孟子在那个商店也找了到工作，可是不是做衣服的工作，而是喜欢的工作。孟子学习馆学了十五年，他的学习和他的旅行经验很重要，对他很多好处。他是大人的时候，他成为很有名的哲人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 找了到：找到了，The perfective marker must follow the resultative complement; use this form when the search has successfully ended in finding work.
- 学习馆学：在学校学习，`学习馆` is not an appropriate place noun here, and `学习` cannot take it as its object in this construction. Use `在` + place + `学习`.
- 对他很多好处：对他有很多好处，`好处` is a noun, so this predicate needs `有` to state that something brings benefits to him.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 做 -- `做衣服的工作`：`缝制衣服的工作`，specifies that the work involves sewing garments rather than the broader idea of making clothes.
- 很有名 -- `很有名的哲人`：`享有盛名的哲人`，expresses more specifically that he has an established public reputation.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子：他，The previous scene mentioned this character in its final sentence, and no male character competes for the reference, so use a pronoun for this close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子：他，After the first sentence has named the character again, use a pronoun in the next sentence rather than repeating the name.
- Coherence (Reference): 他的旅行经验：旅行经验，Within the same noun phrase, the possessor has already been stated in `他的学习`, so use zero form for the second possession.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为：成为了，This is a completed life event in the past narrative, so use `了` to mark the completed change of state.
- Expressiveness: At the point about his long period of study, add a daily-study detail and his feeling about learning, for example: `他每天认真读书，觉得学到了很多新知识。`
- Expressiveness: At the final achievement, show a family member’s reaction or include brief praise, for example: `孟母听了以后，心里非常高兴。`

### Encouragement
You clearly connect his work, long-term study, travel experience, and later achievement into one chronological scene. You also use the contrast pattern with `不是……而是……` to show that the job is different from making clothes.
