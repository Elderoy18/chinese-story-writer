## Scene 1

> 很久以前，有一个老男人叫神农。神农在古代每一天看到了别的小镇人生病还是不舒服，所以他决定开始找新的药。他下一天开始爬当地的山脉，找了各种各样的植物。神农试一试每个植物，知道有什么好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别的小镇人：别的小镇里的人，People who live in a town need the location phrase with 里 before 人; 小镇人 is not the natural noun phrase here.
- 生病还是不舒服：生病或者感到不舒服，In this declarative sentence, 或者 connects two possible conditions; 还是 is normally used for alternatives in questions.
- 下一天：第二天，第二天 is the usual narrative expression for “the next day”; 下一天 is not idiomatic here.
- 爬当地的山脉：翻越当地的山脉，山脉 is a chain of mountains, so 翻越 is the fitting verb for travelling across it; 爬 is more natural with an individual mountain.
- 找了各种各样的植物：找到了各种各样的植物，找 means “look for,” while 找到 expresses successfully finding the plants.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不舒服 -- “别的小镇里的人生病还是不舒服”：感到身体不适，specifies that the discomfort is physical；身体不舒服，keeps the simple tone while making the bodily meaning explicit

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农在古代每一天看到了：他在古代每一天看到了，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the recently mentioned character has no competing referent.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农试一试每个植物：他试一试每个植物，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence because the same character continues to perform the actions.
- Coherence (Temporality): 每一天看到了：每一天看到，A routine action takes the zero particle; 了 is for a completed action, not something that happens every day.
- Expressiveness: After the decision to search, add a short thought to show Shennong’s determination: “他想：我要找到能治病的药！”
- Expressiveness: When he travels in the mountains, add one concrete detail about the journey: “他走啊走啊，去了很多高山。”
- Expressiveness: When he tests the plants, add a feeling of care or courage: “他虽然有一点害怕，但是还是想帮助生病的人。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduce Shennong at the beginning, and the scene has a logical purpose: he sees people suffering and decides to search for medicine.
- Your use of words such as “当地的山脉” and “各种各样的植物” gives the journey a wider, more adventurous feeling.

## Scene 2

> 比方说，他一次试一试有些植物的叶，吃得又新鲜又甜。煮这些叶以后，他一喝就知道他发现新的茶叶。虽然他找到了很多的新的植物，但是他的旅程有的时候特别是危险。比方说，他一吃灵芝就开始生病。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一次试一试：试吃了一些，The original verb phrase does not naturally express tasting several leaves in one narrative event. “试吃” specifically means to taste food in order to try it.
- 吃得又新鲜又甜：这些叶子吃起来又新鲜又甜，A phrase with “得” describes the manner or degree of an action, but “新鲜” and “甜” describe the leaves’ qualities; “吃起来” introduces how the food tastes.
- 特别是危险：特别危险，“特别是” must introduce a typical example after a general statement, while this sentence needs “特别” as an adverb meaning “very.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 新的 -- “很多的新的植物”：陌生的，emphasizes that these are plants unfamiliar to the character rather than simply recently existing.
- 甜 -- “又新鲜又甜”：清甜，describes a light, fresh sweetness that suits the taste of brewed leaves.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他一喝就知道他发现新的茶叶：他一喝就知道发现新的茶叶，The second pronoun has the same referent as the subject in the same sentence, so Chinese normally uses zero form rather than repeating it.
- Coherence (Temporality): 煮这些叶以后：煮了这些叶以后，The boiling action is completed before the later drinking action, so it needs “了” to mark completion.
- Expressiveness: After the brewing moment, add a sensory detail about the drink, such as “水变成了淡淡的绿色，还闻起来很清香。”
- Expressiveness: At the moment of discovery, add the character’s feeling or thought, such as “神农高兴地想：这可能是一种很有用的植物！”

### Encouragement
- You use the contrast pair well to show that finding plants is helpful but the journey can still be dangerous.
- The immediate-action pattern in the final sentence creates a clear and lively cause-and-result moment.

## Scene 3

> 一个爬山的男人正好看到神农的麻烦，然后给了他一些药。神农最终他的小镇回到，跟别的人分享他的发现。你认为神农的故事真的吗？这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他的小镇回到：回到他的小镇，The destination must follow the directional-result verb phrase, so the place comes after 回到.
- 神农的故事真的吗：神农的故事是真的吗，A yes/no question asking whether something is true needs 是 before the adjective.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 麻烦 -- “看到神农的麻烦”：困境，specifies that Shennong is in a difficult situation rather than referring to a general trouble.
- 给了 -- “给了他一些药”：递给了，shows the concrete action of handing the medicine to Shennong.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 跟别的人分享他的发现：跟别的人分享了他的发现，This is the final completed action in a series of past events, so it takes 了; in a series, only the last verb needs 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处：这就要看你相不相信中药对身体有好处了，This expression gives a conditional response to the preceding question; the part after 看 must use a question form, and the final 了 belongs to the fixed expression.
- Expressiveness: At the first sentence, add a physical detail showing Shennong’s condition, such as “神农躺在地上，肚子疼得很厉害。”
- Expressiveness: Describe the helpful man briefly to make him easier to picture, such as “一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人正好走过来。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech when the medicine is given, such as “男人说：‘快吃这个药吧！’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the ending sequence: someone helps Shennong, and then Shennong returns home to share what he learned. Your final question also gives the scene an interesting reflective ending.
