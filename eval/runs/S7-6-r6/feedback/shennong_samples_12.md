## Scene 1

> 很久以前，古代的人很不知道什么植物能吃，也什么植物不能吃。所以很多人常常吃不安全的植物，吃不安全的植物对你的身体有坏处，很麻烦的情况。但是一个人，神农，要解决这个问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很不知道：不知道，The degree adverb 很 cannot directly modify the negative mental-state verb 知道 in this construction.
- 也什么植物不能吃：什么植物不能吃，After 不知道, this should be a parallel object clause; 也 cannot directly precede a noun phrase or clause and must be followed by a verb phrase.
- ，很麻烦的情况：，这是一种很麻烦的情况，The original ending is only a noun phrase and cannot serve as a complete predicate; add 这 and 是 to form a complete statement.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不安全 -- “吃不安全的植物”：有毒的，identifies the specific danger from poison in these plants.
- 有坏处 -- “对你的身体有坏处”：有害，states the harmful effect more directly and concisely.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 你的身体：他们的身体，The second-person pronoun does not refer back to the group of many people; use a pronoun that clearly refers to them.
- Coherence (Reference): 但是一个人，神农，要解决这个问题：但是有一个叫神农的人要解决这个问题，On first mention, introduce the character clearly with both his name and a full noun phrase describing who he is.
- Expressiveness: After the harmful-plant sentence, add a concrete consequence, such as “很多人常常生病”, to show what the harm looks like.
- Expressiveness: At the point where Shen Nong appears, add his inner thought or a short quote, such as “神农想：我要找出哪些植物能吃。”

### Encouragement
You clearly establish the ancient setting, the problem facing many people, and Shen Nong’s goal. The use of 所以 also gives a clear cause-and-effect link between not knowing plants and eating unsafe ones.

## Scene 2

> 他又聪明又努力，旅行很远找到很多植物。比方说，灵芝和夹竹桃。他一吃各种各样的植物就知道能不能吃这些植物。然后，神农写他的主意。他喜欢植物的味道，特别是花。神农遇到了一个黄色的花，一吃就觉得不舒服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 旅行很远：到很远的地方旅行，use 到 + place to show where the journey goes; 旅行 cannot directly take 很远 to express the destination.
- 找到很多植物：找到了很多植物，the finding is a completed action in this past narrative, so it needs 了.
- 写：写下，写下 shows that the ideas were recorded, rather than merely beginning the act of writing.
- 一个黄色的花：一朵黄色的花，朵 is the appropriate measure word for an individual flower.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不舒服 -- “一吃就觉得不舒服”：肚子疼，specifies that the discomfort is stomach pain and makes the reaction more vivid.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 然后，神农写他的主意：然后，他写下自己的主意，after the immediately preceding reference to the same character, use a pronoun; use 自己的 when the possessor is the subject of the sentence.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农遇到了一个黄色的花：他遇到了一朵黄色的花，use a pronoun when the same character was mentioned in the previous sentence and no other character competes as the referent.
- Expressiveness: At the description of the flower, add one visual detail to show why it catches his attention, such as “花又大又漂亮，像黄色的星星。”
- Expressiveness: After he feels ill, add an inner thought or short speech to show his fear, such as “神农心想：‘我的肚子好疼！’”

### Encouragement
- You use plant examples clearly, and the contrast between edible and inedible plants is easy to understand. Your use of the 一…就… pattern also makes the final event feel immediate.

## Scene 3

> 发现了夹竹桃有毒。别人看到他，很快给他吃一个灵芝。神农一吃就觉得比较好。然后，他继续写他发现的信息。会告诉他的朋友哪一个植物能不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别人：一个人，别人 means all other people in contrast with a specified person; here the rescuer is an unidentified single person.
- 写他发现的信息：写下他发现的信息，写 information down requires 写下 or 记下 because the intended action is recording discoveries.
- 哪一个植物能不能吃：哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃，哪一个 asks the reader to select one item, while 能不能 asks a yes-or-no question; use parallel clauses to distinguish edible and inedible plants.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 比较好 -- “神农一吃就觉得比较好”：好一些了，shows a modest improvement after feeling ill；好多了，more clearly shows that his condition improved compared with before
- 很快 -- “很快给他吃一个灵芝”：立刻，emphasizes that the helper acted immediately；赶紧，adds a sense of urgency because 神农 was unwell

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 给他吃一个灵芝：给他吃了一个灵芝，this is a completed rescue action, so it needs 了 after the verb phrase.
- Expressiveness: After discovering the poisonous plant, add a physical reaction to show why help is urgent, for example: “神农肚子疼了起来，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: At the helper’s arrival, add one visual detail and a short line of speech, for example: “这时，一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人走过来，说：“快吃这个！””
- Expressiveness: After feeling better, let 神农 ask about the medicine, for example: “神农问：“你给我吃的是什么？””

### Encouragement
You clearly connected the poison, the helper’s lingzhi, and 神农’s recovery in a logical sequence. You also used the pattern “一……就……” correctly to show an immediate result.
