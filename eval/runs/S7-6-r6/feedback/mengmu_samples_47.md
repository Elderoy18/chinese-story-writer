## Scene 1

> 这个男孩子叫孟子，孟子是古代的人。孟子的妈妈觉得住在哪儿对童年很重要，所以他们常常搬了家。她望子成龙，所以孟子的妈妈安排他的生活。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- Nothing to flag.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 住在哪儿 -- “住在哪儿对童年很重要”：居住的环境，specifies that the whole living environment, not only the address, affects a child；成长，can replace “童年” to focus more precisely on a child’s development
- 安排 -- “孟子的妈妈安排他的生活”：规划，emphasizes making thoughtful plans for his daily life and future

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 这个男孩子叫孟子：在古代，有一个男孩子叫孟子，A first-mentioned character should be introduced as a new person with an indefinite noun phrase rather than “这个”, which normally points back to someone already mentioned.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子是古代的人：是古代的人，After naming the boy in the same sentence, use zero form rather than repeating his name when no other person competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Reference): 她望子成龙，所以孟子的妈妈安排他的生活：她望子成龙，所以安排他的生活，The mother is already the subject of the first clause, so use zero form in the second clause instead of repeating the noun phrase.
- Coherence (Temporality): 常常搬了家：常常搬家，A habitual action takes the zero particle; “了” marks a completed event, not a repeated routine.
- Expressiveness: After the mother’s thought about where to live, add her inner concern to show why she keeps moving, for example: 孟子的妈妈心想：“住的地方会影响孩子，我一定要小心选择。”
- Expressiveness: Add one concrete detail about how she manages his life, for example: 她每天陪孟子看书，还告诉他要认真学习。

### Encouragement
You clearly introduce the central idea that the mother cares deeply about her son’s upbringing. You also used the cause-and-effect connector “所以” well to connect her concern with the family’s moves.

## Scene 2

> 因为他们常常搬了家，中国人说，孟母三迁。孟子没有爸爸，所以，他的妈妈更重要。从这儿到那儿，妈妈教孟子很多技能。先她让孟子跟朋友玩儿，然后教他怎么买又便宜又好质量的东西。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 常常搬了家：常常搬家，A habitual action uses no 了 because it is not presented as one completed event.
- 先她让孟子跟朋友玩儿：她先让孟子跟朋友玩儿，The subject comes before the time/order adverbial 先.
- 又便宜又好质量的东西：又便宜又质量好的东西，质量好 is the correct adjective phrase; 好质量 cannot directly modify 东西 in this structure.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 技能 -- “妈妈教孟子很多技能”：本领，more specifically refers to practical abilities that a child learns；生活本领，makes clear that these are useful everyday abilities.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 妈妈教孟子很多技能：她教孟子很多技能，妈妈 was just mentioned in the preceding sentence and no other female character competes with the reference, so a pronoun is more natural for a close re-occurrence.
- Expressiveness: After “妈妈教孟子很多技能,” add a feeling to show孟子的 response, such as “孟子觉得很好玩儿，也很想学。”
- Expressiveness: At “又便宜又质量好的东西,” add a concrete example of an item, such as “比如新鲜的水果和结实的布。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech after the mother teaches him, such as “妈妈说：‘买东西的时候，要先看看质量。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the close relationship between孟子 and his mother, and the use of 先……然后…… gives the teaching actions a clear order.
