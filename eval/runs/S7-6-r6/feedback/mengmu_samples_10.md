## Scene 1

> 孟子小的时候，从早到晚都跟朋友玩。孟子和他的朋友喜欢玩在墓地里，装祭拜。孟子五岁的时候，跟妈妈搬家。他们搬家不是因为孟子玩得太多，而是因为妈妈认为他们的家离墓地太近。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 玩在墓地里：在墓地里玩，A location phrase should come before the main action verb.
- 装祭拜：装作祭拜，When meaning “pretend to do an action,” use 装作 before the verb phrase.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 太多 -- “孟子玩得太多”：太久，specifies that the problem is the long amount of time he spends playing.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子小的时候：孟子是一个小男孩。他小的时候，On a character’s first mention, briefly introduce the character with a name and identifying noun phrase before using a pronoun.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和他的朋友：他和朋友，After the name appeared in the immediately preceding sentence and no other main character intervened, use a pronoun for the close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 跟妈妈搬家：跟妈妈搬了家，This is a completed move at age five, so the completed action needs 了.
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery setting, add a small visual detail about the visitors, such as “孟子看到有人穿着黑衣服，在墓碑前哭。”
- Expressiveness: Show why the pretend worship is inappropriate through a visitor’s reaction, such as “来祭拜的人看见了，很不高兴。”
- Expressiveness: Add the mother’s worried thought before the final explanation, such as “妈妈心想：‘这里会不会影响孟子？’”

### Encouragement
- You used clear time information with “从早到晚” and “五岁的时候,” which helps the reader follow the scene.
- Your final contrast clearly explains the reason for the family’s move.

## Scene 2

> 妈妈认为，住离墓地太近，害得孟子的心气。孟子和孟母搬到子思老师的家，跟子思老师住。孟母帮子思打扫房间和做饭。子思帮孟母教她的儿子。因为孟子现在没有朋友，他老是读书。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 住离墓地太近：住在离墓地太近的地方，The verb needs 在 before a location, and 的地方 completes the location phrase after 离墓地太近.
- 害得孟子的心气：害得孟子心情不好，害得 must be followed by the person affected and a harmful result clause; 心气 alone is a noun, not a result.
- 老是：一直，In this neutral description of sustained studying, 一直 expresses continuous action; 老是 often suggests an unwanted or annoying repetition.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 做饭 -- “孟母帮子思打扫房间和做饭”：下厨，specifically emphasizes personally preparing meals at home.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬到：搬到了，The move is a completed event in the narrative, so 到 needs 了 to mark completion.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为孟子现在没有朋友，他：因为孟子现在没有朋友，所以他，Because having no friends is presented as the cause of studying, use 因为……所以…… to show the cause-and-effect relation clearly.
- Expressiveness: After the studying action, add a quiet study detail, such as “房间里很安静，只有翻书的声音。”
- Expressiveness: Show Meng Zi’s feeling about losing his friends, such as “孟子有一点孤单，可是他还是认真读书。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line from the teacher, such as “子思老师说：‘你要认真读书。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the new relationship between Meng Zi, Meng Mu, and the teacher. The final sentence also gives a logical reason for why Meng Zi begins to study more.
