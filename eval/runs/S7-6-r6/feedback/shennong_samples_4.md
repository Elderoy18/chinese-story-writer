- 写了：记下了，This action is recording information about the plant; 记下 means to write information down for later reference.

- 糟糕 -- “这个问题神农觉得很糟糕”：严重，emphasizes that the problem has important harmful consequences；令人担忧，specifies that the problem makes people feel concerned.

- Coherence (Reference): 所以神农：所以，Within the same sentence, the subject has already been established as 神农, so use zero form rather than repeating the name.
- Coherence (Reference): 这个植物对你的身体：它对身体，The plant was introduced in the immediately preceding clause, so use a pronoun for the close re-occurrence; the statement is general, so no reader-directed possessor is needed.
- Coherence (Reference): 有的时候，神农：有的时候，他，Use a pronoun because 神农 is the recent, unambiguous subject.
