## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男人觉得植物很有意思。他叫神农。有的植物吃了以后他的肚子疼死了，可是有的吃了以后很舒服。他找很多植物。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 already names a time period, so adding 的时候 is redundant.
- 他找很多植物：他找到了很多植物，找 means “to look for”; 找到 means that he successfully found the plants, which is the completed result intended in this narrative.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 有意思 -- “植物很有意思”：神奇，emphasizes the surprising qualities of the plants；奇妙，adds a sense of wonder about how different plants affect the body.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After “他的肚子疼死了,” add a short inner thought to show Shen Nong’s fear, such as “他想：‘我会不会生病了？’”
- Expressiveness: At “有的吃了以后很舒服,” add one concrete bodily detail, such as “他觉得身体暖暖的，很有力气,” to make the contrast clearer.
- Expressiveness: Describe one plant before Shen Nong searches for more, for example “有的花又黄又小，像星星一样,” to help the reader picture the scene.

### Encouragement
You clearly show the different effects that plants can have on Shen Nong, using the contrast between pain and comfort. The sentence with “吃了以后” also gives the events a clear time order.

## Scene 2

> 凉山很大的山，这也找植物。一天，他找到黄色的花。他听说你跟黄色的花做茶很有用。比方说，你的头发疼死，这冰茶是药。他一喝茶就很不舒服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 凉山很大的山：凉山是一座很大的山，A linking verb and the matching measure word are needed to identify the mountain.
- 跟黄色的花：用黄色的花，Use 用 to show the material used to make tea; 跟 means “with” as a companion and does not express this relationship.
- 头发疼死：头疼得要命，头发 cannot feel pain; 头疼 names the condition, and 得要命 expresses the very strong degree.
- 这冰茶：这杯茶，A demonstrative before a countable noun needs a measure word; 杯 is the suitable measure word for tea.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 有用 -- “用黄色的花做茶很有用”：有疗效，specifies that the tea has a helpful medical effect in this context.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 这也找植物：他也在这里找植物，The demonstrative 这 cannot clearly refer to the character who searches for plants. Use the pronoun 他 for the recently mentioned 神农 and 这里 for the mountain location.
- Coherence (Reference): 你：删除，There is no introduced listener or second-person character for 你 to refer to; the sentence can use an impersonal statement instead.
- Coherence (Reference): 你的：他的，The story is about 神农, so the possessive pronoun should refer back to him rather than to an unintroduced listener.
- Coherence (Reference): 黄色的花：一朵黄色的花，This flower is being introduced for the first time, so use an indefinite noun phrase with a measure word.
- Coherence (Temporality): 找到：找到了，The discovery happened on that day and is completed, so 找到 needs 了.
- Expressiveness: When the flower first appears, add one visual detail to make it memorable, such as: 黄色的花像星星一样，又大又漂亮。
- Expressiveness: At the moment he feels ill, show his physical reaction more clearly, such as: 他捂着肚子，难受得不得了。
- Expressiveness: You could add a short thought to show his fear, such as: 神农想：“这种茶真的可以喝吗？”

### Encouragement
- You used 一天 to begin a new event clearly, and the pattern 他一喝茶就很不舒服 effectively shows an immediate result.

## Scene 3

> 糟糕，这黄色的花是夹竹桃，特别是毒。正好在这儿，灵芝药的夹竹桃他吃完了，也写一个课文的植物。谢谢神农。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 这黄色的花：这朵黄色的花，A demonstrative such as 这 needs a matching measure word before a countable noun such as 花.
- 特别是毒：特别有毒，特别 is the degree adverb here; 特别是 introduces an example, while 有毒 is the appropriate adjectival form for “poisonous.”
- 灵芝药的：灵芝和，的 marks modification or possession and cannot connect two plant names; 和 correctly coordinates them.
- 也写一个课文的植物：也把这两种植物记下了，Use 记下 for recording plants or plant information. The completed final action in this sequence also needs 了.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 正好在这儿：这时，Use 这时 to introduce the next event in the narrative; 正好在这儿 refers to a location and does not clearly show the time transition.
- Expressiveness: After identifying the flower, add a visual detail to make it memorable: “黄色的花像星星一样，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: When Shennong eats the plant, show his physical reaction: “他一吃，肚子就疼了起来。”
- Expressiveness: Near the final thanks, add a short line of speech to show the character’s feeling: “神农说：‘谢谢你，这是什么药？’”

### Encouragement
- The opening uses “糟糕” effectively to show alarm, and you clearly name the flower as “夹竹桃.”
