## Scene 1

> 古代的时候有一个人叫神农。他需要当上医，一看别的人就想帮助他们。但是，他有一个问题，他不知道什么东西能做药。神农听说一个远远的地方有一种植物，什么病人吃都会觉得身体更好。神农三个月走去，找到有植物的地方，他看到一个很漂亮的植物，又香又好看。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，this phrase redundantly combines a historical time period with a “time of” expression.
- 当上医：当上医生，医 is not normally used alone to mean a doctor; 医生 is the standard noun for this profession.
- 一看：一看到，here the meaning is that he sees someone, so the resultative complement 到 is needed.
- 别的人：别人，别人 refers to other people as a group; 别的 is used to distinguish other items from a particular one.
- 三个月走去：走了三个月，an amount of time follows the verb, and 去 cannot follow it without a destination or a result.
- 一个很漂亮的植物：一株很漂亮的植物，株 is the matching measure word for one individual plant.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 东西 -- “他不知道什么东西能做药”：植物，identifies exactly what kind of thing he is trying to learn about in this scene.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 但是，他有一个问题，他不知道什么东西能做药：但是，他有一个问题，不知道什么东西能做药，after the subject has just been named in the same sentence, use a zero form rather than repeating the pronoun.
- Coherence (Temporality): 找到有植物的地方：找到了有植物的地方，finding the place is a completed event in the narrative, so it needs 了.
- Expressiveness: When the plant first appears, add a concrete visual detail: “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花。”
- Expressiveness: Show Shennong’s reaction to the beautiful plant: “神农觉得很惊讶，想把这种植物带回去研究。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought before he begins his journey: “神农想：‘我要找到能做药的植物。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduce the main character and his goal, and the scene has a logical movement from learning about a plant to travelling to find it. The sensory description “又香又好看” is also a strong start for making the plant memorable.

## Scene 2

> 他觉得，这种一定是做药的花。原来他想错了。吃了以后，神农快生病。 这时一个穿着蓝色衣服的人帮神农，快给他一种红的灵芝。神农吃了以后，他没有病，想起来以前一个人告诉他，灵芝对身体有好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 快给：赶快给了，In this completed narrative action, use 赶快 to mean “rapidly” and add 了 after 给 to mark that the giving was completed.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 觉得 -- “他觉得，这种一定是做药的花”：以为，more precisely shows that he held a belief that later proved mistaken.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他：神农，Two male characters appear in the same sentence, so 他 could refer either to the blue-clothed person or to 神农. Repeat 神农 to make the recipient clear.
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃了以后，神农快生病：吃了以后，神农很快就生病了，快 + verb can suggest that something is about to happen; 很快就 expresses that it happened rapidly, and 了 marks the completed change into being ill.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他没有病：他的病好了，After he has become ill, this needs to describe the completed recovery; 病好了 marks that change of state.
- Coherence (Temporality): 想起来：想起了，This remembering is the final completed action in the sequence, so use 了 after the resultative verb 想起.
- Expressiveness: After 神农 becomes ill, add a bodily feeling to show how serious it is, for example: “他的肚子疼得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: When the blue-clothed person appears, describe the medicine more vividly, for example: “他拿起一种红色的、像伞一样的灵芝。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short worried thought when 神农 is sick, for example: “神农想：‘我会死吗？’”

### Encouragement
You use 原来 and 这时 well to show a mistaken belief and introduce a new event. You also clearly connect the lingzhi with its benefit for the body.

## Scene 3

> 啊，他怎么忘记了？他把灵芝放在包里。一回家就告诉别人。他找到药以后他写下怎么做药在书上。所以人们都知道，那红灵芝对身体好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在书上：在书上写下怎么做药，The locative phrase should come before the verb it modifies; it cannot be placed after the object in this word order.
- 对身体好处：对身体有好处，The fixed expression is 对……有好处, meaning that something benefits the body.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 药 -- “他找到药以后”：药材，identifies the medicine more precisely as a natural material, such as a medicinal plant, used for making medicine.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 那红灵芝：那朵红灵芝，A demonstrative before a concrete countable object needs a measure word; use 朵 for this individual lingzhi.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他把灵芝放在包里：他把灵芝放在包里了，Putting the lingzhi into the bag is a completed narrative action, so it needs 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 写下：写下了，The writing is completed in this part of the narrative, and 了 follows the resultative complement 下.
- Expressiveness: At the opening question, add a small action to show the character’s sudden realization: “他拍了拍自己的额头，心想：‘我怎么把灵芝忘了？’”
- Expressiveness: When he tells other people, add a short line of speech to make the moment more vivid: “他对大家说：‘这种红灵芝对身体有好处。’”
- Expressiveness: When he writes in the book, add one concrete detail about what he records: “他在书上写下了红灵芝的样子和用处。”

### Encouragement
You used clear time order with 一……就…… and 以后, which helps the reader follow the events. You also connected the lingzhi to its benefit for the body, showing that the character has learned something important.
