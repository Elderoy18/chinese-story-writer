## Scene 1

> 古代，孟子跟孟母住在墓地的旁边。可是孟母注意，住在墓地的旁边影响孟子。他常常跟朋友们祭拜玩，所以孟母决定他们会搬家。这次，他们住在城市里的商店的旁边，从早到晚很吵。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 孟母注意：孟母注意到，The result complement 到 is needed here to show that she noticed or became aware of the situation. [rule: none]
- 他常常跟朋友们祭拜玩：他常常跟朋友们学着别人祭拜，祭拜玩 is not a natural verb phrase. 学着别人祭拜 clearly expresses that he and his friends imitate the people who come to worship. [rule: word_choice]
- 孟母决定他们会搬家：孟母决定搬家，决定 should be followed directly by the action being decided; do not repeat the people who will do it or add 会. [teacher rule: jueding_vp]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 可是孟母注意到：孟母注意到，可是 introduces contrast, but the mother noticing the negative effect is not contrary to the previous sentence; it is a consequence of living near the cemetery. [rule: none]
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery is introduced, add one concrete detail about the visitors, such as “每天都有很多人来墓地祭拜，有的人还在哭。”
- Expressiveness: Show Mengmu’s worry after she notices Mengzi’s behaviour, such as “孟母很担心，觉得这儿不适合孩子。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of inner speech before the decision to move, such as “孟母心想：‘我们不能一直住在这儿。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly establish the setting and introduce both Mengzi and Mengmu right away. You also use a logical cause-and-effect idea when Mengmu decides to move after seeing Mengzi’s behaviour.

## Scene 2

> 城市也有点儿危险。害得孟母只好一直陪孟子。孟子也没有朋友，难怪他老是看起来不高兴。住在商店的旁边不是适合孟子，而是让他觉得很伤心。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 害得孟母只好一直陪孟子：让孟母只好一直陪孟子，the result describes an unwanted necessity but does not explicitly state a harmful result; use 害得 only when it is followed by a person and a clearly harmful outcome. [rule: haide_harmful_result]
- 孟子也没有朋友：孟子没有朋友，also is not supported here because no other person has been stated to have no friends. [rule: you_ye_distinction]
- 不是适合孟子：不适合孟子，适合 is a verb and can directly take 孟子 as its object; it should not follow 是 in this predicate. [rule: word_class_misuse]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 而是让他觉得很伤心：而且让他觉得很伤心，the second clause adds another negative effect rather than correcting a mistaken idea, so use an additive connector instead of the paired contrast connector. [teacher rule: bushi_ershi]
- Expressiveness: After the sentence about having no friends, add Mengzi’s inner feeling, such as “孟子心想：‘这里太吵了，我一点儿也不开心。’”
- Expressiveness: After the sentence about Mengzi’s mother accompanying him, show her worry with a detail such as “孟母看着孟子闷闷不乐的样子，很担心。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show how the new environment affects both Mengzi and his mother, and the detail about Mengzi having no friends makes his unhappiness easy to understand.

## Scene 3

> 所以，孟母和孟子搬到学校的旁边住。才五岁孟子开始学哲学。在学校，孟子不是看书，就是练习写字。将来，他成为中国的一个非常有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 才五岁孟子开始学哲学：才五岁，孟子就开始学哲学，The pattern requires 就 after 才 + age/number to show that the action began unusually early. [rule: cai_jiu_construction]
- 将来：后来，This narrative describes an event that happened after the school scene, so use the past-narrative time word rather than a future-time word. [rule: time_expression]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 看书 -- “孟子不是看书，就是练习写字”：读书，emphasizes reading and studying from books rather than simply looking at them
- 非常有名 -- “一个非常有名的哲学家”：举世闻名，specifies that his reputation is known very widely

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬到学校的旁边住：搬到了学校的旁边住，The move is a completed event in the narrative, so 搬到 takes 了 to mark completion; the following 住 describes the resulting state. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 他成为中国的一个非常有名的哲学家：他成为了中国的一个非常有名的哲学家，Becoming a philosopher is presented as a completed later change, so 成为 takes 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: After the move, add Mengmu’s relief or happiness to show why the new place suits Mengzi, for example: 孟母很高兴地说：“这儿的环境很适合你。”
- Expressiveness: At the school, add a quiet detail to contrast with the noisy shops in the previous scene, for example: 学校里很安静，孟子每天认真地看书、写字。
- Expressiveness: Before the final outcome, add a brief evaluative link showing the importance of his early study, for example: 这些学习让孟子慢慢喜欢上了哲学。

### Encouragement
You clearly show the important change from the noisy city area to the school setting. The sentence using “不是……就是……” also gives a vivid picture of Mengzi’s study routine.
