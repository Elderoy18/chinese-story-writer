- 神农和他的家人住在古代，所以不知道什么植物能吃。：“在古代，神农和他的家人不知道什么植物能吃。” Ancient times is a time setting, not a place where someone “lives,” so use the time expression at the beginning of the sentence.

- Coherence (Temporality): 他们去很多不一样的地方：他们去了很多不一样的地方，The trip to many places is presented as a completed event in the narrative, so the verb needs 了.
- Expressiveness: After “常常生病,” add Shen Nong’s concern and purpose, for example: “神农很担心家人，希望找到对身体有好处的植物。”
- Expressiveness: Add one sensory detail to make the journey more vivid after the list of places, for example: “高山又高又冷，沙漠又热又干。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of dialogue before the journey to show determination, for example: “我们一定要找到能吃的植物！”
