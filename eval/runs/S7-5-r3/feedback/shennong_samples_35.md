## Scene 1

> 在古代，很多医生不知道怎么用药。所以他们找到了很多植物对药。神农先看到很多病人，他们吃很多植物对药。但是我觉得他们的吃的植物，让他们病人。神农觉得这个植物可能特别是毒。所以他想找一个植物对药，他们可以吃对药。正好，他找到了一把花，他想把花放在茶里，对药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很多植物对药：很多能做药的植物，The phrase “对药” does not mean “able to be used as medicine.” Use “能做药的” before the noun to describe the plants’ purpose.
- 很多植物对药：很多能做药的植物，The phrase “对药” does not mean “able to be used as medicine.” Use “能做药的” before the noun to describe the plants’ purpose.
- 让他们病人：让他们生病，“病人” is a noun meaning a sick person, so it cannot follow “让” to describe the result. Use the verb “生病” after “让” to show that the plants caused them to become ill.
- 一个植物对药，他们可以吃对药：一种能做药的植物，让病人吃，“对药” cannot mean “serve as medicine.” “能做药的植物” means plants that can be made into medicine, and “让病人吃” clearly states who can take it.
- 把花放在茶里，对药：把花放在茶里做药，“对药” does not express making medicine. Use “做药” for the purpose of using the flower as medicine.

## Scene 2

> 他不知道如果这把花对你的身体好，这就要看如果这把花毒不毒了。神农想找别的植物，但是这很麻烦。正好，他找到了别的花。他一吃花，很快就生病了。他的肚子疼死了。神农不知道他吃不吃灵芝或者夹竹桃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 这把花（两处）：这种花（两处），“把” describes a handful or bunch; the story refers to a type of flower, so “种” is the appropriate measure word.
- 不知道如果这把花对你的身体好：不知道这把花对你的身体好不好，an embedded yes/no question after “不知道” uses an A-not-A form rather than “如果”.
- 看如果：看，after “看” in this pattern, use the embedded A-not-A question directly rather than “如果”.
- 毒不毒：有没有毒，“毒” cannot be used as an adjective in this way; “有毒” expresses that a plant contains poison.
- 吃不吃灵芝或者夹竹桃：吃的是灵芝还是夹竹桃，when choosing between two possibilities, use “是……还是……”, rather than adding “或者” after an A-not-A question.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 麻烦 -- “但是这很麻烦”：费劲, emphasizes that finding the plants takes a lot of effort；艰辛, emphasizes that the task is difficult and tiring.
- 很快 -- “很快就生病了”：没过多久, gives a clearer sense that only a short time passed before he became ill.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 你的身体：他的身体，there is no addressee in the scene, so “你” has no clear referent; the body belongs to the previously mentioned character, 神农.
- Coherence (Reference): 他一吃花：他一吃这种花，there are several flower references in the scene, so repeat the noun with “这种” to make clear which flower caused the illness.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农不知道他吃的是灵芝还是夹竹桃：神农不知道吃的是灵芝还是夹竹桃，when the same character re-occurs within the same sentence and no other person competes as the subject, use the zero form rather than repeating a pronoun.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他一吃花，很快就生病了：他一吃花，就很快生病了，“一……就……” needs “就” to connect an action immediately to its result.
- Expressiveness: At the moment of the stomach pain, add a physical detail to show how serious it feels: “他疼得在地上打滚。”
- Expressiveness: When he finds the flower, describe its appearance so the reader can picture it: “那种花又黄又大，像星星一样漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: After he becomes ill, add an inner thought or short question: “他想：我会不会死？”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the main sequence of finding a flower, eating it, and becoming ill. The completed actions in “找到了” and “生病了” are used well, and “疼死了” vividly shows the character’s pain.

## Scene 3
