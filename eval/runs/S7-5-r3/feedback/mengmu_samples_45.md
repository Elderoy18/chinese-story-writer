## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子。他们住在墓地的旁边，所以孟子老是祭拜。孟子不是祭拜，就是跟朋友聊天。他很喜欢他的城市，但是城市有才两个学校，都很小。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，this phrase redundantly combines a time period with another “time of” expression; use the shorter time expression. [vocab list]
- 有才：才有，才 is an adverb and must come before the verb 有 in an “only has” statement. [rule: none]
- 两个学校：两所学校，schools use the measure word 所 rather than the general measure word 个. [rule: measure_word]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 小 -- “都很小”：规模都很小，specifies that the schools are small in overall scale rather than merely using a general description.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子：一个叫孟子的男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国，at first mention, introduce the character with both the name and a full noun phrase rather than naming him only in the next sentence. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子不是祭拜：他不是祭拜，孟子 was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no competing male character is present, so a pronoun is more natural on close re-occurrence. [rule: redundancy_delete]
- Coherence (Reference): 他的城市：自己的城市，when the possessor is the same person as the subject, 自己的 makes that relationship explicit. [teacher rule: ziji_possessor]
- Expressiveness: After “他们住在墓地的旁边”, add a concrete image of the setting, such as “每天都有不同的人来墓地祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: At “所以孟子老是祭拜”, add Mengzi’s reason or feeling: “他觉得祭拜很有趣。”
- Expressiveness: After describing the small schools, show Mengzi’s reaction with a short thought, such as “孟子觉得这儿有一点儿无聊。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduce the setting and connect Mengzi’s home near the cemetery to his behaviour with “所以.”
- You also use “不是……就是……” effectively to show Mengzi’s repeated activities.

## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈不是不喜欢他们的城市，而是想孟子有很好的教育。他们搬家到有很多学校的城市。他们从早到晚走路，孟子一直抱怨。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 想孟子有很好的教育：想让孟子受到很好的教育，When the person who is expected to act or experience something is different from the subject of 想, use 想让 + person + verb phrase. [rule: none]
- 搬家到有很多学校的城市：搬到了有很多学校的城市，This move is presented as a completed story event, so the verb needs 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很好 -- “孟子有很好的教育”：良好的，specifies that the education has positive quality；优质的，emphasizes high-quality educational resources.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他们：所以他们，Add 所以 to show that the mother’s wish for better education causes the move. [rule: coherence_connectives]
- Expressiveness: After the walking sentence, add a line of quoted speech to show Mengzi’s impatience, such as “妈妈，我们还要走多久？”
- Expressiveness: When they arrive in the new city, describe its school-related atmosphere, such as “街上有很多学生背着书包走来走去。”
- Expressiveness: Show the mother’s hope through an internal thought, such as “她心想：这儿的学校很多，孟子一定能学得更好。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the mother’s reason for moving and Mengzi’s reaction during the journey. The contrast structure in the first sentence gives the scene a useful sense of motivation.

## Scene 3

> 他难怪抱怨，他想他的朋友，因为他不要离开他的城市。他的妈妈找到孟子一个老师。跟老师孟子学最重要的文学，所以他成为哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他难怪：难怪他，The evaluative expression must come before the clause it comments on: it is “no wonder he complains,” not “he no wonder complains.” [teacher rule: nanguai_yuanlai]
- 不要离开：不想离开，For a third-person character’s wish, this needs the mental-state verb meaning “does not want to.” The original form is more often used to prohibit or urge someone not to do something. [rule: none]
- 找到孟子一个老师：给孟子找了一个老师，Use 给 to mark the person who benefits from finding the teacher, and add 了 because the successful finding is completed. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- 成为哲学家：成为了哲学家，Becoming a philosopher is presented as a completed change of state in the narrative, so use 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 想 -- “他想他的朋友”：想念，specifically expresses missing friends who are no longer nearby；惦记，emphasizes continuing to worry about or keep the friends in mind.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他的城市：自己的城市，The subject and the possessor are the same person, so this form makes that relationship clear. [teacher rule: ziji_possessor]
- Coherence (Reference): 跟老师孟子学：他跟老师学，After the immediately preceding mention of the character, use a pronoun; putting it before the phrase with 跟老师 also gives the sentence a clear subject. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Expressiveness: At the complaint, add an internal feeling to show why leaving is difficult: “他很舍不得自己的朋友，心里很难过。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech after the mother finds the teacher: “妈妈说：‘你要好好学习，以后会有更多机会。’”
- Expressiveness: After the final achievement, add a response from other people: “后来，人们都很尊敬这位哲学家。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show a cause for the character’s complaint and give the scene a forward direction through finding a teacher and studying. You also use the important story outcome of becoming a philosopher.
