## Scene 1

> 很久以前，有一个老男人叫神农。神农在古代每一天看到了别的小镇人生病还是不舒服，所以他决定开始找新的药。他下一天开始爬当地的山脉，找了各种各样的植物。神农试一试每个植物，知道有什么好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 有一个老男人叫神农：有一个叫神农的老人，Put the identifying phrase before the noun with 的. “老人” is also a neutral, suitable term for an older man in this story. [rule: attributive_modifier_de]
- 别的小镇人：小镇上的人，别的 distinguishes people or things from a previously stated one; it cannot mean people in general. [teacher rule: bieren]
- 生病还是不舒服：生病或感到不舒服，还是 normally presents alternatives in a question or an uncertain choice; use 或 to list two possibilities in this statement. [rule: none]
- 下一天：第二天，Use 第二天 to mean the next chronological day in a narrative; 下一天 is not the standard expression here. [rule: none]
- 找了各种各样的植物：找到了各种各样的植物，找 means “search for,” while 找到 means that the search successfully resulted in finding something. [vocab list]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好处 -- “知道有什么好处”：药用价值，specifies that the plants’ benefits are connected with treating illness or making medicine；作用，focuses more generally on what each plant can do.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农在古代每一天：他在古代每一天，Use a pronoun when the name appeared in the immediately previous sentence and no other person competes as the referent. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Reference): 神农试一试每个植物：他试一试每个植物，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence of the same character after the previous sentence already used 他. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Temporality): 每一天看到了：每一天看到，A habitual action marked by 每一天 takes the zero particle, not 了, because it is not one completed event. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: After the sick people are introduced, add Shennong’s feeling to show his motivation, such as “他看见人们生病，心里很难过。”
- Expressiveness: When he begins his journey, add a little landscape detail to make the setting vivid, such as “他走过一座又一座高山。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where he tests plants, add a short thought to show his goal, such as “他想：我要找出能治病的植物。”

### Encouragement
You clearly establish Shennong, the problem of sick people, and his decision to search for medicine. The cause-and-effect connection with 因为……所以…… also helps the scene move forward logically.

## Scene 2

> 比方说，他一次试一试有些植物的叶，吃得又新鲜又甜。煮这些叶以后，他一喝就知道他发现新的茶叶。虽然他找到了很多的新的植物，但是他的旅程有的时候特别是危险。比方说，他一吃灵芝就开始生病。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 试一试：尝了，Verb reduplication is for a brief, tentative action or suggestion; this sentence recounts an action that happened, so use a completed verb. “尝” also specifically means tasting food or leaves. [rule: verb_reduplication] [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- 吃得又新鲜又甜：这些叶子吃起来又新鲜又甜，A complement after 得 describes the manner or result of eating, but 新鲜 describes the leaves themselves. Use 吃起来 to describe their taste and quality. [rule: none]
- 很多的新的植物：很多新的植物，A quantity phrase such as 很多 directly modifies the following noun phrase; the extra 的 makes the stacked modifiers unnatural here. [rule: none]
- 特别是危险：特别危险，特别是 must introduce a typical example from a general statement, while 特别 directly intensifies an adjective such as 危险. [rule: example_marker_tebie_bifang]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 新的 -- “很多的新的植物”：陌生的，emphasizes that the plants were unfamiliar to him；未尝过的，specifies that he had not tasted these plants before

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他发现新的茶叶：自己发现了新的茶叶，The second 他 could be read as another male character; 自己 clearly shows that he realizes his own discovery. The discovery is completed at that point, so it also needs 了. [rule: ambiguous_reference] [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: After describing the leaves, add a sensory detail about their appearance or smell, such as “叶子绿绿的，还有一股清香。”
- Expressiveness: At the moment he becomes ill, show his physical feeling, such as “他的肚子突然疼了起来，疼得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought after he gets sick to show his fear, such as “他想：我会不会死？”

### Encouragement
- You used both 虽然……但是…… and 一……就…… effectively to connect ideas and show an immediate result.
- The scene already has a clear sequence: tasting leaves, boiling them, discovering tea, and then facing danger on the journey.

## Scene 3

> 一个爬山的男人正好看到神农的麻烦，然后给了他一些药。神农最终他的小镇回到，跟别的人分享他的发现。你认为神农的故事真的吗？这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 最终他的小镇回到：最终回到他的小镇，The compound verb must come before the place it reaches: the person returns to a place, rather than placing the location before the verb. [rule: none]
- 别的人：别人，This sentence means sharing with other people in general; use this form rather than a phrase that distinguishes a specific different type of person. [teacher rule: bieren]
- 故事真的吗：故事是真的吗，A predicate adjective such as 真 needs the linking verb in this yes/no question pattern. [rule: none]
- 这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处：这就要看你相不相信中药对身体有好处了，The structure needs a question form after 看, such as an A-not-A form, and the final 了 belongs to this fixed structure. [rule: zhe_jiu_yao_kan]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 药 -- “给了他一些药”：草药，specifies that the medicine comes from plants, which fits this story’s setting.
- 给 -- “给了他一些药”：递给了，specifies that the man directly handed the medicine to Shen Nong.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 分享：分享了，This is the last action in a completed sequence, so the final verb takes 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: At the moment the man helps Shen Nong, add a visual detail to make the helper memorable: “一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人赶快递给神农一些草药。”
- Expressiveness: After Shen Nong recovers, show his feeling with a short line of speech: “神农感激地说：‘谢谢你救了我！’”
- Expressiveness: Before the final question, add an inner thought to create a more reflective ending: “神农想：‘这个故事是真的吗？’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the helpful man’s action with “给了他一些药,” and you move the scene forward from being helped to returning home. Your attempt to use “这就要看” also shows that you are trying a useful, more advanced response structure.
