## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个人叫神农。一天，神农正在路上走，一看很多人正在吃一些植物。一些人一吃植物就生病了，神农想帮别人。所以想知道哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，The original repeats the time meaning because 古代 already refers to a historical time period. [vocab list]
- 看：看到，The sentence means that Shennong saw people eating plants, so the perception result needs 看到 rather than 看. [rule: resultative_complement]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 一：delete 一，An immediate sequence beginning with 一V needs a following 就 clause. Since this sentence only states that Shennong saw something, use the direct perception verb 看到 instead. [teacher rule: yi_jiu]
- Expressiveness: After Shennong sees the sick people, add his concern to show his motivation more clearly: “神农很担心，觉得这样吃植物太危险了。”
- Expressiveness: Add one visual detail about the plants beside the road to make the setting easier to imagine: “路边长着各种各样的植物，有的开着小花。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduce the main character and use ongoing-action forms well in “正在路上走” and “正在吃”.
- The contrast between plants that can be eaten and plants that cannot be eaten is clear and fits this story scene well.

## Scene 2

> 然后，神农开始尝很多植物，比方说他吃过生姜、芥兰和菠菜。一些植物吃真好，特别是黄瓜。但是，一些植物毒得很，特别是夹竹桃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一些植物吃真好：一些植物吃起来很好，The verb phrase needs the complement 起来 to describe how something tastes when eaten. [rule: none]
- 毒得很：有毒，毒 is a noun and cannot take the degree complement 得很; use 有毒 to describe a poisonous plant. [teacher rule: du_youdu]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很多 -- “神农开始尝很多植物”：各种各样的，specifies that he tried a wide range of different plants.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：他，The character was named in the immediately preceding scene and no other person competes as the referent, so use a pronoun for this close re-occurrence. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Reference): 比方说他吃过：比方说吃过，After the subject has already been stated in the same sentence, use zero form rather than repeating the pronoun. [rule: none]
- Expressiveness: At the sentence about the poisonous plant, add a visual detail to make the danger more memorable: “夹竹桃开着黄色的、像星星一样的花。”
- Expressiveness: After the examples of good plants, add Shen Nong’s feeling about his discovery: “神农觉得很有希望。”

### Encouragement
You used concrete plant names such as “生姜、芥兰和菠菜,” which makes the scene specific. The contrast between useful plants and dangerous plants is also clearly organized with “但是.”

## Scene 3

> 一个人对神农说，麻烦我们可以吃什么植物？神农说，这就要看你想不想生病了。以后，神农告诉别人一些植物能吃，一些植物不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 麻烦：请问，This polite question needs an inquiry opener such as “请问”; “麻烦” cannot directly introduce a question clause in this way. [rule: word_choice]
- 这就要看你想不想生病了：这就要看是哪种植物了，The phrase “这就要看……了” should answer the uncertain point in the preceding question. Here, the question is which plants are edible, so the part after “看” should ask which plant it is. [rule: zhe_jiu_yao_kan]
- 以后：后来，“以后” does not begin a sentence to mean “afterwards”; use “后来” for a later event in the narrative. [vocab list]
- 别人：人们，“别人” distinguishes other people from a previously mentioned person or group. Since 神农 is giving general information, “人们” is the appropriate general group. [teacher rule: bieren]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农告诉人们：他告诉人们，神农 was named in the immediately preceding sentence, and no other third-person male character competes here, so use the pronoun for a close re-occurrence. [rule: redundancy_delete]
- Expressiveness: At the person’s question, add a feeling to show why the question matters, such as: “那个人很担心，因为他不想再生病。”
- Expressiveness: In 神农’s reply, give one concrete example of a safe and an unsafe plant: “神农说：‘灵芝可以吃，夹竹桃有毒，不能吃。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly connected this scene to the earlier events by asking which plants can be eaten.
- You also tried the useful response pattern “这就要看……了,” which is a good expression to learn.
