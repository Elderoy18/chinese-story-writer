## Scene 1

> 古代时候，人不知道他们能吃什么植物，常常一吃就死。但是不知道为什么，没有办法。所以有一天，神农出去试一试很多种植物，告诉人什么植物是安全什么是危险。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代时候：在古代，Use the established time expression for a historical setting; the original combines two overlapping time expressions.
- 人：人们，This refers to a concrete group of people in the story, so use the plural human-group noun.
- 他们能吃什么植物：什么植物能吃，After the people have been named, the repeated subject is unnecessary in this embedded question.
- 告诉人：告诉人们，The listener is a concrete group of people, so use the plural human-group noun.
- 什么植物是安全什么是危险：什么植物是安全的，什么植物是危险的，When these adjectives follow 是 to describe which plants have each quality, they need 的.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很多种 -- “试一试很多种植物”：各种各样的，emphasizes the wide variety of plants Shennong examines.
- 没有办法 -- “不知道为什么，没有办法”：束手无策，expresses more specifically that the people do not know how to respond to the problem.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：一个叫神农的人，This is the character’s first mention, so introduce him with his name and a full noun phrase rather than using only a name.
- Coherence (Temporality): 出去试一试很多种植物：出去试了很多种植物，This is a completed testing action in a past narrative, so use 了 after 试; the repeated-verb form suggests only a brief trial and does not fit testing many kinds.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉：告诉了，The telling is presented as a completed event after the testing, so it takes 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 但是不知道为什么，没有办法：因为不知道为什么，所以没有办法，The second idea gives the cause of having no solution, not a contrast.
- Expressiveness: When describing the danger to people, add a more specific cause, such as “很多人因为吃了有毒的植物，常常生病。”
- Expressiveness: After introducing Shennong, add his motivation through an inner thought, such as “神农想：‘我一定要找出能吃的植物。’”
- Expressiveness: When Shennong begins his search, add a small action detail, such as “他走进高山，仔细观察各种植物的颜色和形状。”

### Encouragement
You clearly establish the problem that people cannot identify edible plants, and your use of “一吃就死” effectively shows the immediate danger. Introducing Shennong as someone who tests plants gives this scene a clear direction.

## Scene 2

> 发现有的植物对身体非常好，特别是茶，让肚子感觉又暖和又清楚。他告诉朋友：“你吃哪种植物，这就要看你的口味。比方说，你爱吃甜的，可能喜欢糖。”他最喜欢吃花，除了夹竹桃以外，什么都花想吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 让肚子感觉又暖和又清楚：让人觉得肚子暖和，头脑清醒，清楚 describes information or thoughts, not a physical feeling in the stomach; 暖和 can describe the stomach, while 清醒 can describe a person’s mind.
- 告诉了朋友：告诉朋友，A speaking verb that introduces quoted speech normally takes the zero particle rather than 了.
- 这就要看你的口味：这就要看你的口味了，The final 了 is part of the fixed 这就要看……了 structure here; it does not mark a completed action.
- 什么都花想吃：什么花都想吃，什么 modifies the noun 花, so 花 must come before 都 and the verb phrase.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好 -- “有的植物对身体非常好”：很有益，states more precisely that the plants benefit health
- 甜的 -- “你爱吃甜的”：甜食，specifies that the person likes sweet foods rather than anything that is sweet

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 你爱吃甜的，可能喜欢糖：如果你爱吃甜的，你可能喜欢糖，The first idea is a condition for the second, so 如果 makes the conditional relationship clear.
- Expressiveness: At the tea discovery, add what Shennong realizes about its use: “原来茶不但好喝，还能做药！”
- Expressiveness: After the advice about taste, add a short spoken explanation to make the dialogue sound more natural: “每个人的口味都不一样。”
- Expressiveness: At the final sentence, add Shennong’s cautious thought about the dangerous flower: “夹竹桃不能吃，我一定要记住。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect the discovery of plants with Shennong’s advice to his friend, and the quoted speech gives the scene a lively narrative voice.
- The contrast between flowers he wants to try and 夹竹桃 shows that you are beginning to include important safety information in the story.

## Scene 3

> 他发现这个口味，一百岁时候，不小心吃了毒的花。一吃就开始死。但是生病时候告诉人什么植物是药，什么植物是毒。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 毒的花：有毒的花，`毒的` is not the normal adjective form before a noun; `有毒的` describes a flower that contains poison.
- 开始死：肚子就疼了起来，`开始` does not naturally modify the final event `死` here; `疼了起来` describes the symptom beginning after he eats the flower.
- 生病时候：生病的时候，A multi-syllable phrase before `时候` needs `的`.
- 告诉人：告诉人们，`人` is generic, while the people receiving the information are a concrete group, so `人们` is needed.
- 什么植物是毒：什么植物有毒，`毒` is a noun here; use `有毒` to describe a plant that contains poison.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不小心 -- “不小心吃了”：误食了，specifically means accidentally eating something harmful or unsuitable.
- 告诉 -- “告诉人什么植物是药，什么植物是毒”：向人们说明，emphasizes explaining the difference between medicinal and poisonous plants.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他发现这个口味，一百岁时候：他一百岁的时候发现了一种花，`这个` has no specific earlier noun to refer to, and `口味` does not identify the object he later eats. `一种花` introduces the new object clearly; the age phrase comes before the action, and `了` marks the completed discovery.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉：告诉了，This is a completed action in the past story, rather than quoted speech or a routine, so it needs `了`.
- Expressiveness: At the first mention of the flower, add its appearance to make the danger more vivid, for example: “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花。”
- Expressiveness: After he eats it, describe his physical feeling, for example: “他的肚子疼了起来，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: When he explains the plants to others, add a short line of speech, for example: “这种花有毒，不能吃。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the cause-and-effect sequence with `一……就……`, and you include the important contrast between medicinal plants and poisonous plants.
