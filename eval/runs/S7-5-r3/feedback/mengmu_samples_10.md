## Scene 1

> 孟子小的时候，从早到晚都跟朋友玩。孟子和他的朋友喜欢玩在墓地里，装祭拜。孟子五岁的时候，跟妈妈搬家。他们搬家不是因为孟子玩得太多，而是因为妈妈认为他们的家离墓地太近。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 喜欢玩在墓地里：喜欢在墓地里玩，A location phrase such as “在墓地里” normally comes before the action verb it modifies.
- 装祭拜：装作祭拜，“装作” is used before a verb phrase to mean pretending to carry out that action.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 玩 -- “从早到晚都跟朋友玩”：嬉戏，emphasizes the carefree, playful activity of young children with friends.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和他的朋友喜欢在墓地里玩：他和他的朋友喜欢在墓地里玩，Use a pronoun for Mengzi because he was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no competing male character has appeared.
- Coherence (Temporality): 跟妈妈搬家：跟妈妈搬了家，This is a completed move at age five, so the last verb in the action phrase needs 了 to mark completion.
- Expressiveness: At the cemetery-play spot, add a quiet visual detail to contrast with the children’s game, such as “墓地里有人低着头，安静地祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: Show Mengzi’s reaction while he imitates others, for example “孟子觉得很有趣，笑着学别人鞠躬。”
- Expressiveness: At the mother’s reason for moving, add her worried inner thought or speech, such as “妈妈皱着眉头想：‘这儿离墓地太近了。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly establish Mengzi’s childhood habit and then explain the family’s reason for moving. The contrast structure “不是……而是……” is used effectively to make that reason clear.

## Scene 2

> 妈妈认为，住离墓地太近，害得孟子的心气。孟子和孟母搬到子思老师的家，跟子思老师住。孟母帮子思打扫房间和做饭。子思帮孟母教她的儿子。因为孟子现在没有朋友，他老是读书。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 住离墓地太近：住得离墓地太近，Use 得 after 住 to introduce the degree complement describing how near the home is to the cemetery.
- 害得孟子的心气：会影响孟子的心情，害得 must be followed by a person and a harmful result clause; the original ends with a noun phrase and does not state the result.
- 搬到子思老师的家：搬到了子思老师的家，The move is a completed event, so 搬到 needs the completion marker 了.
- 老是读书：一直读书，老是 has a negative, complaining tone; 一直 neutrally describes the child’s continued reading.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟母帮子思打扫房间和做饭：她帮子思打扫房间和做饭，After the mother was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other female character competes, use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Reference): 子思帮孟母教她的儿子：子思帮她教她的儿子，The mother is still the recent, unambiguous referent, so a pronoun is more natural than repeating her name.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为孟子现在没有朋友，他一直读书：因为孟子现在没有朋友，所以他一直读书，Use 因为……所以…… to make the cause-and-effect relationship explicit.
- Expressiveness: After the move, add one detail about the quiet learning environment, such as: 子思老师的家很安静，书也很多。
- Expressiveness: Show the child’s feeling about having no friends before describing the reading: 孟子觉得有一点孤单。
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of dialogue when the mother asks for help: 孟母说：“请您帮我教孟子读书吧。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect the new home with the child’s changed daily routine, and you give both adults helpful roles in the scene.
