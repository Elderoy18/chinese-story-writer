## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个男人，他的名字是神农。他看到了有很多人不知道哪个植物好吃，也不知道什么植物对你的身体有好处。这个问题神农觉得很糟糕，所以神农去了吃每种植物。他吃了一种植物以后，就写了这个植物对你有没有好处。有的时候，神农吃了很好吃的植物。有的时候，他吃了很危险的植物。他吃完了每种植物以后，会来跟他的朋友聊到他吃的植物了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 already expresses a time period, so adding 的时候 is redundant.
- 写了：写下了，写下 records information or a discovery, while 写 alone does not clearly express recording what the plant does.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很糟糕 -- “这个问题神农觉得很糟糕”：令人担忧，specifies that the problem makes him worried rather than simply being bad.
- 很危险的 -- “他吃了很危险的植物”：有毒的，specifies that the danger comes from poison when the plant is eaten.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 所以神农：所以，after 神农 has appeared in the first clause of this linked sentence, use the zero form rather than repeating the name.
- Coherence (Reference): 对你的身体：对身体，your refers to the reader, not to a character in the story; omit the possessor for a general statement about health.
- Coherence (Temporality): 去了吃：去吃了，when 去 is followed by the main action, place 了 after the completed main action.
- Coherence (Temporality): 有的时候，神农吃了：有的时候，神农吃，use the zero particle for a habitual action introduced by 有的时候.
- Coherence (Temporality): 有的时候，他吃了：有的时候，他吃，use the zero particle for a habitual action introduced by 有的时候.
- Coherence (Temporality): 会来跟他的朋友聊到他吃的植物了：会来跟他的朋友聊到他吃的植物，use the zero particle in a modal, habitual clause with 会 rather than marking the action as completed with 了.
- Expressiveness: At the dangerous-plant sentence, add a physical reaction to show the consequence: “他一吃这种植物，肚子就疼了起来。”
- Expressiveness: After the writing sentence, add a feeling about the difficult work: “虽然记下每种植物很麻烦，但是神农还是愿意去做。”
- Expressiveness: At the final conversation with his friends, add a short question and answer: “朋友问：‘这种植物能吃吗？’神农说：‘有毒，不能吃。’”

### Encouragement
You introduce the main character clearly and explain why he begins testing plants. The repeated time-frame structure also makes it easy to compare the safe and dangerous plants.

- 一个别的人问神农：另一个人问神农，别的 is used to distinguish other people from a stated group; for one additional person in the sequence, use 另一个人.
- 一个别的人问神农：另一个人问神农，别的 is used to distinguish other people from a stated group; for one additional person in the sequence, use 另一个人.

### Vocabulary Suggestions

- 坏处 -- “哪个植物对你的身体有坏处”：危害，identifies physical harm to the body more specifically；有害，gives a concise adjective for something that can harm the body.

### Encouragement

## Scene 3

> 神农说，有一天，我吃了一个黄色的花，名字是夹竹桃。我一吃夹竹桃，就觉得这种植物对我的脑有坏处。可是，一个灵芝和一个男人正好在我的旁边，我说，麻烦你把那个植物给我吃一下。我一吃这个灵芝就好了。所以，你得小心吃植物，因为有的植物对你的健康有坏处，特别是夹竹桃。因为夹竹桃有毒。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一个黄色的花：一朵黄色的花，Flowers normally take the measure word 朵 rather than 个.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 吃 -- “你得小心吃植物”：食用, specifically means consuming a plant as food or medicine and fits this health-warning context；谨慎食用, makes the careful manner of eating more explicit.
- 旁边 -- “正好在我的旁边”：身边, emphasizes that the man and the plant are very close to the speaker.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 那个植物：那个灵芝，Both the yellow flower and the lingzhi are plants, so the vague noun could refer to either one; repeat the specific noun for clarity.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 特别是夹竹桃。因为夹竹桃有毒。：特别是夹竹桃，因为它有毒。, The final 因为-clause gives the cause for why this plant is especially dangerous, so it should attach to the preceding statement.
- Expressiveness: When the flower first appears, add a visual detail to make it memorable: “那儿开着黄色的像星星一样的花，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: After the harmful effect begins, show Shennong’s feeling and thought: “我觉得很难受，心想：我会死吗？”
- Expressiveness: Describe the helpful man briefly before the request: “这时，一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人走过来。”

### Encouragement
- You used the 一……就…… pattern effectively to show that Shennong ate the plant and quickly felt its effect.
- Your final warning clearly communicates that some plants can harm people’s health.
