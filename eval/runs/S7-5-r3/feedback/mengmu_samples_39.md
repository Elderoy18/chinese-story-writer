## Scene 1

> 古代的时候人觉得祭拜非常重要，孟子很小的时候，从早到晚一直祭拜，因为他妈妈告诉他祭拜很重要。虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜。他的同学们老是对他开玩笑了。孟子妈妈看了他的同学们对孟子不好的影响，所以孟子和孟子妈妈搬家了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，this expression redundantly combines a historical time period with another “time of” structure.
- 人：人们，this refers to people as a group in ancient times, so the plural collective noun is needed.
- 看了：看到了，the intended meaning is that the mother perceived the classmates’ influence; 看 means “look,” while 看到 means “see” or “notice.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不好的 -- “他的同学们对孟子不好的影响”：负面的，specifies that the influence harmed him in an emotional or behavioural way；不良的，gives the common, precise phrase for an adverse influence.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子很小的时候：有一个小男孩叫孟子。他很小的时候，on a character’s first mention, introduce the character with both a role and a name; after that, use a pronoun for the close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他的同学们老是对他开玩笑了：他的同学们老是对他开玩笑，a habitual action with 老是 uses the zero particle rather than 了, which marks a completed action.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜：因为他每天只祭拜，所以没有朋友，these clauses express cause and effect, not contrast; use the paired connector 因为……所以…… to show the reason and result.
- Expressiveness: After the classmates’ teasing, add Mengzi’s feeling to show how it affects him, for example: “孟子低着头，觉得很难过。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where the mother notices the influence, add her thoughts or speech, for example: “孟子妈妈很着急，心想：‘不能再住在这儿了。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show a cause for Mengzi’s behaviour and connect it to the family’s decision to move. The scene also uses specific characters—the mother and classmates—to make the problem understandable.

## Scene 2

> 孟子才十一岁，成为一个哲学家。孟子不是祭拜，而是看政治文章，难怪他成为了哲学家。不过，看过很多政治文章对他害得，他有很多问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 孟子才十一岁，成为一个哲学家：孟子才十一岁，就成为一个哲学家，The pattern for an unusually early achievement is “才 + age/number + 就 + verb.”
- 不过，看过很多政治文章对他害得，他有很多问题：不过，看了很多政治文章，害得他有很多问题，The structure requires the cause before “害得,” followed by the affected person and a harmful result; “对他害得” cannot form this structure.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子不是祭拜：他不是祭拜，Since the character was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other male character competes as the referent, use a pronoun for this close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为一个哲学家：成为了一个哲学家，Becoming a philosopher is a completed change of state in this narrative, so it needs the completion marker.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 不是祭拜，而是看政治文章，难怪他成为了哲学家：难怪他成为了哲学家，原来他不是祭拜，而是看政治文章，Use the paired pattern with “难怪” followed by “原来” to connect the result with its explanation.
- Expressiveness: At the point where he reads, add a small action and reaction to show concentration: “他皱着眉头，一页一页地看文章。”
- Expressiveness: When he has questions, name one feeling and include a short thought: “他觉得很困惑，心想：‘这句话到底是什么意思？’”

### Encouragement
You clearly state the character’s young age and achievement, which makes the opening easy to understand. You also used a contrast structure to show that his interests changed.

## Scene 3

> 他一直问问题，但是不能解决那些问题。孟子长大，他开始能解决问题。他常常建议你不是学金融，而是学政治，因为如果你懂政治，你会成为高薪。后来孟子成为教授，他教学生很厉害。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不是学金融，而是学政治：不要学金融，而要学政治，With a recommendation, use a negative instruction followed by the preferred action; this does not use the corrective contrast meaning of the original construction.
- 成为高薪：拿到高薪，The verb cannot take this complement: 高薪 describes pay, not a person whom someone can become.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 问问题 -- “他一直问问题”：提出问题, emphasizes formulating questions for discussion or study；追问, emphasizes continuing to ask until he receives an answer
- 厉害 -- “他教学生很厉害”：有办法, shows that he knows effective ways to teach；有经验, specifically shows that he has teaching experience

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 你：人们，The pronoun introduces an unclear addressee in a third-person narrative; use a full noun phrase for the people receiving his advice.
- Coherence (Temporality): 后来孟子成为教授：后来孟子成为了教授，This is a completed change of role, so use 了 after the verb.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 孟子长大，他开始能解决问题：孟子长大以后，他开始能解决问题，Use 以后 to mark the sequence: being grown up happens before his new ability to solve problems.
- Expressiveness: After the first sentence, add an internal reaction to show how the unanswered questions affect him, such as “他越想越糊涂。”
- Expressiveness: When describing his advice, add a short line of speech, such as “他对人们说：‘学政治能帮助你理解国家大事。’”
- Expressiveness: At the end, show the students’ response to make his teaching ability more vivid, such as “学生们都认真地听他讲课。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show a change in the character’s development, from asking questions to being able to solve them. The use of time words such as “后来” also helps the narrative move forward.
