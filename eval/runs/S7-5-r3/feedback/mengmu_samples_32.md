## Scene 1

> 有一天孟子去了墓地玩儿，但是他妈妈觉得这是不合适的地方让孩子玩，所以她决定了他们会搬家找更好的地方。她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福，而且住在这儿教育不太好。因为她望子成龙，所以她会一直搬家才能找到质量好的教育。然后他们搬到了离城市更近的地方。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不合适的地方让孩子玩：不适合孩子玩的地方，Use “适合” to express that a place suits a person for an activity; this noun phrase clearly means a place suitable for children to play.
- 她决定他们会搬家找更好的地方：她决定搬家，找更好的地方，“决定” takes a verb phrase, so the person carrying out the decision does not need to be repeated after it.
- 她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福：她担心儿子的童年不会幸福，“恐怕” means “I am afraid that” and cannot take “她” as its subject; “担心” expresses that she is worried.
- 会一直搬家才能：得一直搬家，才能，“才能” introduces a necessary condition, so use “得” to show that repeated moving is necessary before finding good education.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 更好的 -- “找更好的地方”：更适合孩子成长的，specifies that the new place is better because it supports the child’s development.
- 玩儿 -- “去了墓地玩儿”：嬉戏，emphasizes carefree, childlike play, which makes the mother’s concern more vivid.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 决定了：决定，“决定” is a mental-state verb, so it takes the zero particle rather than “了.”
- Expressiveness: At the mother’s reaction, add a small physical detail to show her concern: “孟母皱着眉头。”
- Expressiveness: After she judges the cemetery unsuitable, add a short line of speech: “这儿不是孩子该玩儿的地方。”
- Expressiveness: At “教育不太好,” show her inner worry more specifically: “她心里很着急，怕孟子学不到东西。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the mother’s reason for moving by using “因为……所以……,” and the final “然后” helps the scene move forward in time.
- Your sentences also keep the focus on the mother’s concern for her son’s childhood and education.

## Scene 2

> 他们住才一月就又搬家。古代的时候，一般人不喜欢卖东西的人。但是孟子老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿，害得妈妈着急得很，因为她恐怕儿子会成为跟他们很像。她告诉了孟子：他们不是朋友，而是不好的人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 住才一月：才住了一个月，Place 才 before the verb to show that the stay lasted only a short time, and use the measure word 个 with 月 when expressing a duration.
- 古代的时候：在古代，The original expression redundantly combines two time expressions; this shorter form introduces the historical time naturally.
- 老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿：老是跟这些卖东西的人玩儿，Do not use two frequency adverbs together when both express a repeated habit; 老是 already conveys that he does this repeatedly.
- 害得妈妈着急得很：让妈妈着急得很，害得 requires a clearly harmful result after the affected person; being worried is not itself a harmful result in this structure.
- 恐怕：担心，When the subject is someone other than the speaker, use 担心 to express that person’s fear or worry; 恐怕 normally means “I am afraid that...” or introduces a whole clause.
- 成为跟他们很像：成为跟他们很像的人，成为 needs a noun phrase after it; adding 人 turns the descriptive phrase into a noun phrase.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 卖东西的人 -- “这些卖东西的人”：商人，identifies their occupation more specifically and makes the sentence less repetitive.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 就又搬家：就又搬家了，Add 了 to show that the repeated move was completed.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉了孟子：告诉孟子，A speaking verb that introduces quoted speech takes the zero particle, rather than 了.
- Expressiveness: At the description of the new neighbourhood, add a sound detail to show why it attracts Mengzi, such as “街上从早到晚都是叫卖声。”
- Expressiveness: Show Mengzi copying the merchants’ actions rather than only saying that he plays with them, such as “孟子学着他们收钱、讨价还价。”
- Expressiveness: Add Mengzi’s reaction after his mother speaks, such as “孟子低下头，小声说：‘我以后不跟他们玩儿了。’”

### Encouragement
You used a clear contrast between ordinary people’s attitude and Mengzi’s behaviour with 但是. You also connected the mother’s worry to Mengzi’s actions, which helps the scene show her reason for speaking to him.

## Scene 3

> 你不是当数学家，而是当哲学家，不能当卖东西的人。所以他们又搬家。在他们第三个家，孟子学了怎么祭拜。妈妈感觉这个地方适合他们，告诉了孟子：我们找到了最后的家。在这儿妈妈让儿子从早到晚学习，他难怪成为有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在他们第三个家：在他们的第三个家里，A modified home needs the location word 里 when it is used as the place where an action happens.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 地方 -- “妈妈感觉这个地方适合他们”：环境，specifies the surrounding living and learning conditions that make the place suitable；居住环境，more specifically refers to the conditions of the family’s home.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 所以他们又搬家：所以他们又搬家了，This is a completed move in the past, so the final action needs 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉了孟子：告诉孟子，A speaking verb that introduces quoted speech uses the zero particle rather than 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他难怪成为有名的哲学家：难怪他后来成为了有名的哲学家，难怪 should introduce the conclusion drawn from the previous reason; becoming a philosopher is a completed later result, so use 后来 and 了.
- Expressiveness: At the mother’s statement about the final home, add her happiness and a more vivid comment: “妈妈高兴地说：‘这儿的环境很适合孩子。’”
- Expressiveness: At the study sentence, add one concrete detail about Mengzi’s effort: “孟子每天坐在窗边，认真地看书学习。”
- Expressiveness: Before the final result, add a transition that shows time passing: “很多年以后，孟子成为了有名的哲学家。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect the family’s new home with Mengzi’s studying and his later achievement. The mother’s quoted speech also helps show her thoughts about the move.
