## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男人觉得植物很有意思。他叫神农。有的植物吃了以后他的肚子疼死了，可是有的吃了以后很舒服。他找很多植物。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，This expression is redundant because both parts indicate time; use the shorter time expression. [teacher rule: gudai_de_shihou_why]
- 可是有的植物吃了以后很舒服：可是他吃了有的植物以后，觉得很舒服，The person who feels comfortable must be the subject; the original wording makes the plants seem to feel comfortable. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一个男人觉得植物很有意思。他叫神农：一个叫神农的男人觉得植物很有意思，Name the main character when he first appears, rather than introducing him first as an unspecified man and giving his name only afterwards. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Expressiveness: After the stomach pain, add a physical reaction to show how serious it feels: “他在地上躺着，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: At the first mention of plants, add one visual detail that explains why he finds them interesting: “有的植物开着黄色的花，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: Add an inner thought after he becomes ill to show his worry: “他想：‘我会不会生病？’”

### Encouragement
You clearly show two different results from eating different plants, and the contrast with “可是” helps the reader understand why Shennong wants to keep exploring. The completed-action phrase with “吃了以后” is also used well.

## Scene 2

> 凉山很大的山，这也找植物。一天，他找到黄色的花。他听说你跟黄色的花做茶很有用。比方说，你的头发疼死，这冰茶是药。他一喝茶就很不舒服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 凉山很大的山：凉山是一座很大的山，A noun phrase needs the copula and an appropriate measure word before the descriptive noun phrase. [rule: none]
- 找植物：在这里找植物，A location phrase needs 在 before the place where the action happens. [rule: location_zai_place]
- 一天：有一天，Use 有一天 to introduce one particular day in a narrative. [rule: none]
- 你跟黄色的花做茶很有用：用黄色的花做的茶很有用，跟 links people or accompanying things, while 用 introduces the material used to make something; 做的 modifies 茶. [rule: none]
- 头发疼死：头疼的时候，头发 does not normally describe the part that feels pain; use 头疼, and add 的 before 时候 in this time expression. [teacher rule: de_shihou]
- 这冰茶：这杯冰茶，This/that plus a countable noun needs a measure word. [teacher rule: zhe_na_mw]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很有用 -- “用黄色的花做的茶很有用”：能治病，specifies the useful medicinal effect in this context

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 这：神农，At the start of this new setting, repeat the character’s name rather than using 这, which would otherwise refer to the mountain instead of the person doing the action. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Temporality): 找到：找到了，This is a completed discovery in the narrative, so add 了 after the resultative verb. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: After describing the mountain, add one sensory detail about the setting, such as “山上空气很新鲜，风轻轻地吹着。”
- Expressiveness: When the flower appears, describe its shape or appearance more vividly, such as “花又大又漂亮，像星星一样。”
- Expressiveness: After the tea makes him feel unwell, show his reaction or thought, such as “他捂着肚子想：‘我会不会生病？’”

### Encouragement
You introduce a clear new setting and use an immediate-action pattern to show that drinking the tea quickly causes a reaction. Your use of an example marker also shows that you are trying to explain why the tea is useful.

## Scene 3

> 糟糕，这黄色的花是夹竹桃，特别是毒。正好在这儿，灵芝药的夹竹桃他吃完了，也写一个课文的植物。谢谢神农。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 这黄色的花：这朵黄色的花，A demonstrative before a countable noun needs an appropriate measure word; 朵 is used for flowers. [teacher rule: zhe_na_mw]
- 特别是毒：特别有毒，特别是 introduces an example, while 特别 directly modifies an adjective or adjective-like phrase; 有毒 describes a poisonous plant. [rule: example_marker_tebie_bifang]
- 灵芝药的夹竹桃：夹竹桃的药是灵芝，The possessive pattern 夹竹桃的药 means “medicine for oleander”; the original modifier order cannot describe the relationship between the two plants. [rule: none]
- 一个课文的植物：一篇关于植物的课文，篇 is the measure word for a text, and 关于植物 correctly shows that the text is about plants. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃完了，也写：吃完，也写了，For a series of completed actions, only the last verb takes 了; the final writing action should carry the completion marker. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Conjunction): 正好在这儿：这时，Use 这时 to introduce a new event interrupting or continuing the scene; 正好在这儿 describes a location and does not provide that time connection. [rule: none]
- Expressiveness: After identifying the flower, add a visual detail to make it memorable, such as `开着像星星一样的黄色花`.
- Expressiveness: Show Shennong’s physical reaction after eating the plant, such as `他的肚子疼得不得了`.
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought to show his fear, such as `“我会死吗？”`

### Encouragement
- You clearly identify the yellow flower as 夹竹桃 and use 糟糕 to create an immediate feeling of danger. You also connect two actions with 也, which shows you are building a sequence of events.
