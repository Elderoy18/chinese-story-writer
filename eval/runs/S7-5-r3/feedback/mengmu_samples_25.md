## Scene 1

> 这是古代的故事。孟子小的时候，他的爸爸很早就去世了。从他的家到墓地很近，所以他每天老是从早到晚去墓地跟朋友们玩玩儿。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 从他的家到墓地很近：他的家离墓地很近，Use 离 to state that two places are close; 从……到…… does not directly take 很近 in this pattern. [vocab list]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子小的时候：有一个小男孩叫孟子，他小的时候，This is Mengzi’s first appearance, so introduce him with a full noun phrase before using a pronoun. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Expressiveness: After 墓地, add a small visual detail about the setting, such as “墓地里常常有人安静地祭拜。” This makes the place easier for the reader to picture.
- Expressiveness: At 跟朋友们玩玩儿, show what the children do or how Mengzi feels, such as “孟子觉得那些仪式很有趣，就和朋友学着别人祭拜。” This adds Mengzi’s motivation and makes the action more vivid.

### Encouragement
You clearly establish Mengzi’s early family situation and connect the nearby cemetery to his daily activity with 所以. The time phrase 从早到晚 also gives the scene a strong sense of routine.

## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈觉得在墓地玩儿，害得，所以他们搬家。搬家对孟子有好的影响，他才五岁，可是每天不是写字就是帮妈妈的工作。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在墓地玩儿，害得：孟子在墓地玩儿，害得她老是道歉，The complement after 害得 must state the affected person and the harmful result; the original leaves this structure unfinished. [teacher rule: haide_structure]
- 帮妈妈的工作：帮妈妈干活，After 帮 + person, the following part needs to be an action that the person is helped to do; 妈妈的工作 is a noun phrase, while 干活 is a verb phrase. [rule: verb_object_collocation]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好的 -- “搬家对孟子有好的影响”：积极的，specifies that the influence is beneficial rather than merely “good.”

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们搬家：他们搬家了，This move is presented as a completed event in the narrative, so it needs 了 to mark completion. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: At the cemetery moment, add a concrete action showing what the child does there, such as “孟子和朋友学着别人祭拜，还一边笑。”
- Expressiveness: After the mother’s concern, add her thought or speech to show her feelings, such as “孟母心想：‘如果一直住在这儿，麻烦会越来越多。’”
- Expressiveness: In the sentence about writing, show the child’s effort with a detail such as “他才五岁，写字写得很认真。”

### Encouragement
- You used “才五岁，可是” effectively to contrast Mengzi’s young age with his daily activities.
- Your use of “不是……就是……” gives the last sentence a clear routine-like pattern.

## Scene 3

> 他很聪明的人，难怪他成为有名的人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他很聪明的人：他是一个很聪明的人，A noun phrase used as the predicate needs the copula and a suitable measure word here. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 人 -- “成为有名的人”：有名的哲学家，identifies the kind of well-known person more specifically and fits this story’s topic.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 难怪他成为：难怪成为，After the same subject has just been mentioned, use zero form rather than repeating the pronoun. [rule: redundancy_delete]
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为有名的人：成为了有名的人，Use 了 because becoming a well-known person is presented as a completed result in the story. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: After the result, add a short comment from other people, such as: “人们都说，他的成功离不开妈妈的三次搬家。” This makes the ending feel more meaningful.

### Encouragement
- You used “难怪” well to show that the character’s intelligence explains his later success. The phrase describing him as a clever person is also clear and natural.
