## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个男孩名字叫孟子。孟子和孟子的妈妈两个人住在很穷的农村。他们住的地方旁边是个墓地，墓地老是有很多人祭拜。因为人们在祭拜，他们一直在哭。孟子和孟子的妈妈住在这里才一个星期就觉得受不了了，而且害得孟子的学习。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，This expression is redundant because 古代 already names a time period, while 的时候 also means “the time of.”
- 而且害得孟子的学习：而且影响了孟子的学习，害得 must be followed by a person and a harmful result clause; it cannot directly take the noun phrase 孟子的学习.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子的妈妈两个人：孟子和他的妈妈两个人，After 孟子 has just been named and no other male character competes with him, use the pronoun 他 rather than repeating 孟子 in the same noun phrase.
- Coherence (Temporality): 住在这里才一个星期就觉得受不了了：才在这里住了一个星期，就觉得受不了了，A completed period of living needs 了 after 住, and the duration goes after the verb. The pattern is 才 + duration + 就 + result.
- Expressiveness: After the description of the cemetery, add a sensory detail about what Mengzi hears, such as “墓地旁边常常传来伤心的哭声。” This makes the setting feel more immediate.
- Expressiveness: When the family cannot bear the place any longer, show the mother’s reaction through a short line of speech, such as “孟子的妈妈叹了口气，说：‘这里太吵了。’” This helps readers understand why the setting troubles them.

## Scene 2

> 孟子的妈妈觉得孟子取得良好的教育是很重要的，所以孟子的妈妈认为这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好，就打算跟孟子搬家。孟子的妈妈带孟子不是搬到城市，而是搬到了孟子老师的家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 孟子取得良好的教育：孟子接受良好的教育，The verb 取得 does not naturally collocate with 教育; 接受 describes receiving an education.
- 跟孟子搬家：带着孟子搬家，跟孟子搬家 suggests that the mother and son move as equal participants, while 带着孟子搬家 correctly shows that the mother takes her child with her.
- 不是搬到城市，而是搬到了：搬到了，The pattern 不是……而是…… should correct a conventional or previously stated mistaken idea; here, a direct statement of the actual destination is more natural.
- 孟子老师的家：孟子的老师的家，Without the first 的, this means the home of a teacher named 孟子; the added 的 shows that the teacher belongs to 孟子.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 地方 -- “这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好”：环境，specifies the living and learning surroundings that affect the child’s education.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子的妈妈认为：认为，The subject has already appeared in the same sentence and no other possible subject intervenes, so use zero form rather than repeat the full noun phrase.
- Expressiveness: After “不太好,” add the mother’s worried feeling and thought: “她很着急，心想：‘住在这儿会影响孟子的学习。’”
- Expressiveness: After the move, describe the new surroundings to show why they suit study: “新家附近很安静，孟子可以专心学习。”

### Encouragement
