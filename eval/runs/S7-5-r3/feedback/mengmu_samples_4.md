## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，孟子和孟母住了在靠墓地的家。孩子才五岁就每天在墓地祭拜了。在那个城市，孟母从早到晚都找工作，可是找不到，害得孟母没有钱。难怪她跟孩子搬家，原来买不了房。他们搬了到别一个城市找工作。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，this time expression is redundant because both parts indicate time; use the concise narrative form. [vocab list]
- 住了在：住在，了 cannot be placed between the verb and the locative phrase; this describes a living situation rather than a completed action, so omit 了. [teacher rule: le_placement]
- 每天在墓地祭拜了：每天在墓地祭拜，actions presented as a daily routine take no 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- 跟孩子搬家：带着孩子搬家，跟 expresses doing an action together, while 带着 shows that the mother takes the child with her when moving. [vocab list]
- 搬了到：搬到了，place 了 after the directional/result complement, as in 搬到 + 了. [teacher rule: le_placement]
- 别一个城市：另一个城市，另一个 refers to one different city; 别的 can modify a noun, but 别一个 is not the standard form. [teacher rule: bieren]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 买不了 -- “原来买不了房”：买不起，specifies that the reason for not being able to buy a home is insufficient money.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟母住在：有一个小男孩叫孟子，他和母亲孟母住在，first mention should introduce the main character with both a name and a clear identity. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Reference): 孩子才五岁：他才五岁，after 孟子 was named in the immediately preceding sentence, use a pronoun for this close re-occurrence. [rule: none]
- Coherence (Reference): 在那个城市：删除，那个城市 refers to a specific city that has not been introduced, so its referent is unclear. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery action, add a small visual detail showing what the child sees and imitates, such as “他看见有人在墓地前哭，就学着他们的样子祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: At “害得孟母没有钱,” add the mother’s feeling or thought, such as “孟母很着急，心想：‘再这样下去可不行。’”
- Expressiveness: At the move, add a short reason in the mother’s voice, such as “孟母说：‘我们去一个更容易找到工作的地方吧。’”

### Encouragement
- You used the pattern “才五岁就” well to show that the child was very young when he began the action.
- You also used “难怪……，原来……” effectively to connect the move with its explanation.

## Scene 2

> 这个城市老是很吵，不是人吵架，就是都叫，害得孟子一直不能睡觉。在那儿，孟母也找不到工作。孟子和妈妈要又一个次搬了家。在第三个城市，孟母找到工作，在商店做衣服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不是人吵架，就是都叫：不是有人吵架，就是有人大叫，The second alternative needs a clear subject, and “有人” naturally refers to unspecified people making the noise. [teacher rule: ren_renmen]
- 孟子和妈妈要又一个次搬了家：孟子和妈妈要再搬一次家，For a future repeated action after a modal verb, use the pattern modal verb + “再” + verb + number + measure word + object; “又” refers to a repetition already completed, and “了” does not fit this future plan. [teacher rule: zai_structure]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 吵 -- “这个城市老是很吵”：嘈杂，describes many mixed, unpleasant sounds in the city；喧闹，suggests a noisy and lively environment.
- 做衣服 -- “在商店做衣服”：缝衣服，specifies that her work is sewing clothes；制作衣服，specifies that she is making garments.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 孟母找到工作：孟母找到了工作，Finding the job is a completed action and a change of state in the narrative, so it needs “了” after the resultative complement. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: After describing the noise, add one concrete sound detail, such as “晚上还有人在外面大声吵架。”
- Expressiveness: Show Mengzi’s feeling about not sleeping, such as “孟子躺在床上，觉得又累又烦。”
- Expressiveness: Add Mengmu’s worried thought before the move, such as “孟母心想：‘这儿太吵了，孩子没办法好好休息。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly show why this city is unsuitable: the noise affects Mengzi, while Mengmu cannot find work. The move to a third city also creates a clear change in the scene.

## Scene 3

> 孟子在那个商店也找了到工作，可是不是做衣服的工作，而是喜欢的工作。孟子学习馆学了十五年，他的学习和他的旅行经验很重要，对他很多好处。他是大人的时候，他成为很有名的哲人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 找了到：找到了，The completion marker follows the resultative complement: use 找到 + 了, not 找 + 了 + 到. [teacher rule: le_placement]
- 学习馆学了：在学校学习了，学校 is the normal place word here, and a location before an action uses 在 + place + verb. [rule: none]
- 对他很多好处：对他有很多好处，The phrase requires 有: something 对 someone 有好处. [rule: verb_object_collocation]
- 他是大人的时候：长大以后，This time expression means “after growing up”; 大人的时候 is not the natural expression for this stage of life. [rule: word_choice]
- 成为很有名：成为了很有名，Becoming a philosopher is a completed change of state in this narrative, so 成为 takes 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 做衣服 -- “不是做衣服的工作”：制衣，specifically names clothing-production work and is more concise in this context.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: At the point where Mengzi finds work, add his feeling to show why this job matters. Example: “孟子很高兴，因为这是自己喜欢的工作。”
- Expressiveness: When describing the long period of study, add one concrete learning activity. Example: “在学校里，他每天认真读书，也常常向老师请教。”
- Expressiveness: After Mengzi becomes a philosopher, add a brief reaction from his mother. Example: “孟母看到他这么有名，心里非常自豪。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show a progression from finding work, to studying for many years, to becoming a philosopher. You also used the contrast pattern “不是……而是……” effectively to distinguish the two kinds of work.
