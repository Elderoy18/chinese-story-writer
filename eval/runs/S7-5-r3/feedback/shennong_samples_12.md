## Scene 1

> 很久以前，古代的人很不知道什么植物能吃，也什么植物不能吃。所以很多人常常吃不安全的植物，吃不安全的植物对你的身体有坏处，很麻烦的情况。但是一个人，神农，要解决这个问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很不知道：不知道，A degree adverb cannot modify a negative knowledge verb in this way; state the lack of knowledge without it. [rule: none]
- 也什么植物不能吃：也不知道什么植物不能吃，The adverb 也 must modify a verb phrase, so it needs the verb that is shared with the previous clause. [teacher rule: ye_vp]
- 很麻烦的情况：这是一种很麻烦的情况，A noun phrase cannot stand alone as the predicate of this sentence; it needs a complete subject–verb structure. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不安全 -- “不安全的植物”：有害的植物，specifies that the plants cause harm to people rather than being generally unsafe.
- 有坏处 -- “对你的身体有坏处”：有害，states more directly that eating the plants damages the body.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 古代的人：人们，Use 人们 for the concrete group of people in this historical setting; 人 by itself is generic. [teacher rule: ren_renmen]
- Coherence (Reference): 一个人，神农：有一个叫神农的人，On first mention, introduce a character with a clear noun phrase that includes both the name and who the person is. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Expressiveness: After the sentence about harmful plants, show the result with a physical detail, such as “很多人吃了以后，肚子疼，还生病了。”
- Expressiveness: When introducing Shennong, add his purpose as an inner thought or quoted speech, such as “神农想：我要找出能吃的植物，帮助大家。”

### Encouragement
You clearly establish the danger that people face and introduce Shennong as someone who wants to solve the problem. Your scene already has a strong beginning-to-problem structure.

## Scene 2

> 他又聪明又努力，旅行很远找到很多植物。比方说，灵芝和夹竹桃。他一吃各种各样的植物就知道能不能吃这些植物。然后，神农写他的主意。他喜欢植物的味道，特别是花。神农遇到了一个黄色的花，一吃就觉得不舒服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 旅行很远：到很远的地方旅行，A destination should be expressed with a place phrase; this makes clear where the travel happened. [rule: none]
- 写他的主意：写下了他的主意，Writing an idea down needs the resultative complement 下 to show that it was recorded, and 了 marks the completed action. [rule: resultative_complement]
- 遇到了一个黄色的花：看到了一朵黄色的花，看到 shows the result of visually noticing the flower, and 朵 is the appropriate measure word for a flower. [rule: resultative_complement]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 聪明 -- “他又聪明又努力”：善于观察，specifies the useful skill of noticing details while searching for plants.
- 不舒服 -- “一吃就觉得不舒服”：肚子疼，gives a more exact physical symptom after eating the flower.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 然后，神农：然后，他，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the same character was named in the previous sentence and no other character competes as the referent. [rule: redundancy_delete]
- Coherence (Reference): 神农遇到了：他遇到了，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence because the previous sentence already clearly refers to the same character. [rule: redundancy_delete]
- Coherence (Temporality): 找到很多植物：找到了很多植物，The finding is a completed event in the past, so the final action in this action sequence needs 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: After the travel sentence, add a small landscape detail to make the journey vivid, for example: “他走过了很多高山和树林。”
- Expressiveness: When the flower appears, describe its shape or appearance more specifically, for example: “那朵花又黄又大，像星星一样。”
- Expressiveness: After the character feels ill, add an inner thought or short quotation to show his worry, for example: “神农想：‘我的肚子怎么这么疼？’”

### Encouragement
You clearly introduce the important plants and use the immediate-result pattern effectively to show that eating the flower quickly causes a reaction. The sequence word also helps the actions move forward in order.

## Scene 3

> 发现了夹竹桃有毒。别人看到他，很快给他吃一个灵芝。神农一吃就觉得比较好。然后，他继续写他发现的信息。会告诉他的朋友哪一个植物能不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 写：写下，The verb needs the resultative complement 下 to show that the information is recorded and kept. [vocab list]
- 哪一个植物能不能吃：哪一种植物能吃，哪一种植物不能吃，哪一个 asks which single item, while 能不能 asks whether it can be eaten; use parallel clauses to explain both categories. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 别人看到他：一个人看到他，别人 distinguishes other people from a previously mentioned person or group; introduce this new, unspecified person with a full noun phrase instead. [teacher rule: bieren]
- Coherence (Reference): 神农一吃就觉得比较好：他一吃就觉得比较好，The name was used in the immediately previous context and no competing male character has intervened, so use a pronoun for this close re-occurrence. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Temporality): 很快给他吃一个灵芝：很快给他吃了一个灵芝，The feeding action is completed, so it needs 了; in a series of actions, the final action takes 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: After the medicine works, add a physical detail such as “他的肚子不那么疼了”, which shows exactly how his condition improves.
- Expressiveness: Before he writes, add an inner reaction such as “神农松了一口气，心想：灵芝真的可以做药”, which makes his discovery feel important.
- Expressiveness: When he tells his friends, add a short warning such as “夹竹桃有毒，千万不能吃”, which makes his advice more vivid and direct.

### Encouragement
You clearly connected the discovery of the poisonous flower, the medicine, and Shennong’s later record-keeping. You also used the useful pattern “一……就……” correctly to show a quick result.
