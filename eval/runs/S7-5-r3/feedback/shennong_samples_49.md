## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有很多人不知道什么植物能吃，什么植物不能吃。有很多人吃植物以后就吃坏肚子了，因为他们吃了毒。那个时候，有一个老人叫神农，他什么植物都觉得好吃，不怕生病，不怕毒。他希望别人也可以吃好吃的植物。可是他知道，如果他们还怕吃毒，不会吃他喜欢的植物。

- 古代的时候：在古代，The original redundantly combines a historical time period with a time-of construction; use the shorter time expression.
- 吃植物以后：吃了植物以后，The eating happens before the later result, so the completed action needs 了 before 以后.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the sentence about people becoming ill, add a physical symptom to make the consequence vivid, for example: “有的人肚子疼得直不起腰。”
- Expressiveness: After introducing 神农, add his inner motivation to show why he is different from other people, for example: “神农心想：我要找出能吃的植物。”
- Expressiveness: At the end, add a short line of quoted speech to show his concern for others, for example: “大家不用害怕，我会先试一试。”

### Encouragement

## Scene 2

> 所以，他上山下山找植物，吃了很多样子的植物。他是第一人，找到了茶。他把所有的植物写下来了。他一直都吃了，他连毒都能吃。可是他还没吃了一个叫夹竹桃的植物。他找到了那个像星星的植物，觉得很漂亮，马上就吃下了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很多样子的植物：很多种植物，样子 describes an appearance, while 种 is the appropriate measure word for different kinds of plants.
- 第一人：第一个人，第一人 is not the usual noun phrase for “the first person”; use the ordinal phrase 第一个 before 人.
- 一个叫夹竹桃的植物：一种叫夹竹桃的植物，夹竹桃 is a type of plant, so 种 is the appropriate measure word for a plant kind.
- 像星星的植物：像星星一样的植物，when a comparison modifies a noun, use 像……一样的 to form the comparison phrase.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他连毒都能吃：连毒都能吃，because the subject is already stated in the preceding clause and no other person competes as the referent, use zero form rather than repeat the pronoun.
- Coherence (Temporality): 一直都吃了：一直都吃，一直都 describes a habitual or continuing action, so it takes the zero particle rather than completed 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 还没吃了：还没吃过，没 cannot be followed by completed 了; 过 marks that he has not had this experience before.
- Expressiveness: At the flower description, add a more concrete visual detail: “黄色的花又大又漂亮，像星星一样。”
