## Scene 1

> 古代的时候有一个人叫神农。他需要当上医，一看别的人就想帮助他们。但是，他有一个问题，他不知道什么东西能做药。神农听说一个远远的地方有一种植物，什么病人吃都会觉得身体更好。神农三个月走去，找到有植物的地方，他看到一个很漂亮的植物，又香又好看。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，The phrase repeats the time meaning because 古代 already names a time period. [teacher rule: gudai_de_shihou_why]
- 别的人：别人，别的人 distinguishes people from a previously mentioned person or group, but no such comparison has been established here; 别人 means other people in general. [teacher rule: bieren]
- 什么病人吃都会觉得身体更好：不管得什么病的人吃了这种植物都会觉得身体更好，什么病人 does not by itself mean “any patient” in this structure; 不管……的人 clearly expresses that all kinds of patients are included. [rule: none]
- 一个很漂亮的植物：一株很漂亮的植物，Plants use a matching measure word such as 株 when referring to one individual plant; 个 is too general here. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 问题 -- “但是，他有一个问题”：难题，specifies that this is a difficult problem to solve, not simply a question.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 有植物的地方：有这种植物的地方，This refers back to the plant just mentioned, so a demonstrative noun phrase makes the connection specific and clear. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Temporality): 神农三个月走去，找到：神农走了三个月，才找到了，A completed duration takes 了 after the verb, and the completed result of finding also takes 了; 才 shows that the finding happened only after the long journey. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: At the point where Shennong decides to help people, add his purpose or determination: “我要找到能治病的植物，帮助大家。”
- Expressiveness: Make the three-month journey more vivid with one travel detail: “他走过了很多高山，也经过了许多小河。”
- Expressiveness: Expand the plant description with a clear visual comparison: “它开着黄色的花，像星星一样，又大又漂亮。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show Shennong’s reason for searching for plants: he wants to help others but does not yet know what can become medicine. The final description, especially “又香又好看,” gives the scene a pleasant and vivid ending.

## Scene 2

> 他觉得，这种一定是做药的花。原来他想错了。吃了以后，神农快生病。 这时一个穿着蓝色衣服的人帮神农，快给他一种红的灵芝。神农吃了以后，他没有病，想起来以前一个人告诉他，灵芝对身体有好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 快生病：很快生病了，In a past narrative, 很快 expresses that the illness happened quickly, and 了 marks the completed change into being ill. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- 快给他一种红的灵芝：赶快给了他一种红的灵芝，In a declarative past event, 快给 can sound like an imperative. Use 赶快 for a quick action and 给了 for a completed act of giving. [rule: aspect_le_completed]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 有好处 -- “灵芝对身体有好处”：有益于身体健康，specifies that the benefit is for physical health.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农吃了以后，他没有病：神农吃了以后，没有病，After the name has appeared in the same sentence and no other person competes as the referent, use zero form rather than repeating the pronoun. [rule: redundancy_delete]
- Expressiveness: At the moment when the flower is identified, add one visual detail to make it memorable, such as “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花。”
- Expressiveness: After the illness begins, show Shen Nong’s physical feeling or inner worry, such as “他肚子疼得不得了，心想：我会不会死？”
- Expressiveness: Make the helper’s action clearer and more vivid by describing the plant, such as “那个人拿起一株像伞一样的植物，给了神农吃。”

### Encouragement
- You used “原来” effectively to show that Shen Nong’s first idea was wrong. You also included a clear time transition with “这时” and gave the new person a useful clothing detail.

## Scene 3

> 啊，他怎么忘记了？他把灵芝放在包里。一回家就告诉别人。他找到药以后他写下怎么做药在书上。所以人们都知道，那红灵芝对身体好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 那红灵芝：那种红灵芝，A demonstrative before a noun needs an appropriate measure word; 种 identifies a type of medicinal fungus. [teacher rule: zhe_na_mw]
- 写下怎么做药在书上：在书上写下怎么做药，The place phrase must come before the verb, not after the verb’s object. [rule: adverbial_word_order]
- 对身体好处：对身体有好处，This expression needs 有 because 好处 is a noun; 对身体有好处 means “be beneficial to the body.” [vocab list]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 放 -- “他把灵芝放在包里”：装进，specifies the movement of putting the medicinal fungus inside the bag, rather than only stating its final location.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 把灵芝放在包里：把灵芝放在包里了，This is a completed action in the narrative, so 了 is needed after the verb phrase. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 一回家就告诉别人：一回家就告诉了别人，The return and telling form a series of actions; the final completed verb takes 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 写下：写下了，Writing the information down is completed in this past event, so 了 is needed after the resultative verb. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: At the opening, add a short inner thought to show his surprise, such as: “他想：我怎么把这么重要的事忘了？”
- Expressiveness: When he writes in the book, add one concrete detail about what he records, such as: “他把灵芝的名字和对身体的好处写在书上。”

### Encouragement
You used the immediate-action structure effectively to show that he told others as soon as he returned home. The final cause-and-effect link also gives the scene a clear ending: his written discovery helps people learn about the medicinal fungus.
