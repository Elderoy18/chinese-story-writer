## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个男孩名字叫孟子。孟子和孟子的妈妈两个人住在很穷的农村。他们住的地方旁边是个墓地，墓地老是有很多人祭拜。因为人们在祭拜，他们一直在哭。孟子和孟子的妈妈住在这里才一个星期就觉得受不了了，而且害得孟子的学习。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，the original is redundant because both parts express a time period. [vocab list]
- 害得孟子的学习：还害得孟子不能好好学习，害得 must be followed by the affected person and a harmful result clause, not only a noun phrase. [teacher rule: haide_structure]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子的妈妈两个人：孟子和他的妈妈两个人，use a pronoun for the close re-occurrence of the same character rather than repeating the name as a possessor. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子的妈妈住在这里：孟子和他的妈妈住在这里，use a pronoun for the close re-occurrence of the same character. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Temporality): 住在这里才一个星期：在这里才住了一个星期，use 了 after 住 to mark the completed duration before the following reaction. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为人们在祭拜，他们一直在哭：因为人们在祭拜，所以他们一直在哭，because this sentence presents a cause and its result, add 所以 to complete the cause-and-effect connector. [rule: none]
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery description, add a sound detail to make the setting clearer, such as “哭声从早到晚都没有停过。”
- Expressiveness: At the final sentence, show Mengzi’s mother’s reaction with a short thought, such as “孟母心想：‘这里太吵了，孟子没法儿好好学习。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly established the setting near a cemetery and connected the worshippers’ crying to why the family found the place difficult to live in. Your use of ongoing actions in “在祭拜” and “一直在哭” helps the scene feel active.

## Scene 2

> 孟子的妈妈觉得孟子取得良好的教育是很重要的，所以孟子的妈妈认为这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好，就打算跟孟子搬家。孟子的妈妈带孟子不是搬到城市，而是搬到了孟子老师的家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 取得良好的教育：接受良好的教育，The verb does not naturally collocate with education in this meaning; this expression means receiving a good education. [rule: none]
- 跟孟子搬家：带着孟子搬家，This wording means that the mother takes her son with her when she moves; 跟 expresses moving together rather than taking someone along. [vocab list]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不太好 -- “这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好”：不利于，states more precisely that the environment has a negative effect on his education

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子的妈妈（sentence opening）：她，The mother was mentioned in the immediately preceding scene, so a pronoun is natural when no other female character competes as the referent. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子（in “觉得孟子取得”）：他，孟子 was also mentioned in the preceding scene, and the pronoun is clear here because it contrasts with his mother. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子的妈妈（after “所以”）：她，Use a pronoun when the same character has just been named and no competing referent has appeared. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子的妈妈带孟子：她带他，Both characters have already been named nearby, so pronouns make this repeated reference smoother and remain clear. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Conjunction): 不是搬到城市，而是搬到了孟子老师的家：搬到了孟子老师的家，This situation does not need to correct or refute an expected destination, so the contrast pair is unnecessary. [rule: bushi_ershi_construction]
- Expressiveness: After the mother realizes the place is unsuitable, add her worried feeling: “她很着急，担心孟子的学习会受到影响。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought or line of speech before the decision to move: “她心想：‘我得给孟子找一个更适合学习的地方。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly explain the mother’s reason for moving, and the cause-and-effect connector links her concern to her decision well.

## Scene 3

> 孟子跟他的老师从早到晚学习，难怪他的成绩很快就进步了很多。孟子学习的时候，孟子的妈妈不是待在家里就是编织衣服赚钱。以后孟子成为了中国有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 以后：后来，At the beginning of this sentence, the word for “afterwards” should be 后来; 以后 is normally used after a time expression or before a later action. [vocab list]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 学习 -- “孟子跟他的老师从早到晚学习”：用功学习，adds that he studies diligently rather than only stating the activity.
- 成绩 -- “他的成绩很快就进步了很多”：学习成绩，makes clear that this is his academic performance.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: In the first sentence, add a small detail about Mengzi’s study habits, such as “认真地看书” or “常常问老师问题”.
- Expressiveness: In the second sentence, add Mengzi’s mother’s feeling about his improvement, such as “很高兴地” or “放心地”.
- Expressiveness: Before the final achievement, add a brief sense of time passing with a phrase such as “长大以后” or “经过多年的学习”.

### Encouragement
You clearly connect Mengzi’s hard work with his improving results. The completed form in “成为了” also works well for his later achievement.
