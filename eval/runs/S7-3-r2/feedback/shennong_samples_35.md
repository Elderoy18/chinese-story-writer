## Scene 1

> 在古代，很多医生不知道怎么用药。所以他们找到了很多植物对药。神农先看到很多病人，他们吃很多植物对药。但是我觉得他们的吃的植物，让他们病人。神农觉得这个植物可能特别是毒。所以他想找一个植物对药，他们可以吃对药。正好，他找到了一把花，他想把花放在茶里，对药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 植物对药：可以做药的植物，`对药` cannot describe a plant’s use here; `可以做药的` states that the plants can be used to make medicine. [rule: none]
- 他们的吃的植物：他们吃的植物，when a pronoun is the subject of a relative clause, it should be directly followed by the verb; the extra possessive `的` incorrectly turns it into a possessive phrase. [rule: none]
- 让他们病人：让他们生病，`让` must be followed by a verb phrase describing the result, while `病人` is a noun meaning a sick person. [rule: none]
- 特别是毒：特别有毒，`毒` cannot be modified by `特别` as an adjective; `有毒` is the adjective phrase meaning “poisonous.” [teacher rule: du_youdu]
- 一个植物：一种植物，`一种` is the appropriate measure-word phrase when referring to one kind of plant. [rule: none]
- 植物对药：可以做药的植物，`对药` does not mean “be used as medicine”; `可以做药的植物` clearly describes the intended kind of plant. [rule: none]
- 吃对药：吃，`对药` cannot follow `吃` with the intended meaning; after the earlier description of the plant, `吃` is sufficient here. [rule: none]
- 对药：做成药，`对药` is not a verb phrase; `做成药` expresses turning the flower into medicine. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 放 -- “他想把花放在茶里”：浸泡，specifies that the flower stays in the tea for a period of time rather than merely being placed there.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：一个叫神农的人，this is the character’s first mention, so introduce the name together with a brief noun phrase rather than assuming the reader already knows who he is. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Reference): 这个植物：这些植物中有些，`这个植物` has no clear single referent because several plants have just been mentioned; this phrase clearly refers back to that group. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Reference): 他们可以吃：人们可以吃，`他们` could refer either to the doctors or to the patients; `人们` clearly names the general group who can eat the plant. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Temporality): 先看到：先看到了，seeing the patients is a completed action before the following description, so use `了`. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们吃很多植物：他们吃了很多植物，these eating actions happened before the patients became ill, so the completed-action marker `了` is needed. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: After the patients are introduced, add a physical detail to show their illness, such as “很多病人捂着肚子，脸色很差。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where the man decides to search for safe plants, add his determination: “神农想：我一定要找出能做药的植物。”
- Expressiveness: When the flower appears, describe its appearance more vividly: “花是黄色的，像星星一样，又大又漂亮。”

### Encouragement
- You used several useful story connectors, including the cause-and-effect connector “所以” and the contrast connector “但是.”
- Your scene has a clear central problem: people become ill after eating plants, and the main character begins looking for a safer solution.

## Scene 2

> 他不知道如果这把花对你的身体好，这就要看如果这把花毒不毒了。神农想找别的植物，但是这很麻烦。正好，他找到了别的花。他一吃花，很快就生病了。他的肚子疼死了。神农不知道他吃不吃灵芝或者夹竹桃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不知道如果：不知道这把花对你的身体好不好，An embedded yes/no question after 不知道 uses an A-not-A form, not 如果. [rule: a_not_a_question]
- 看如果：看这把花毒不毒，After 看 in this pattern, the following part must be a direct A-not-A question; 如果 is not used before it. [rule: zhe_jiu_yao_kan]
- 吃不吃灵芝或者夹竹桃：吃的是灵芝还是夹竹桃，When the uncertainty is between two named alternatives, use 是……还是……; 吃不吃 asks whether he ate at all rather than which plant he ate. [rule: a_not_a_question]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 麻烦 -- “但是这很麻烦”：费劲，emphasizes the sustained effort needed to search for plants；艰难，emphasizes that the task is difficult to carry out.
- 很快 -- “很快就生病了”：不一会儿，gives a more story-like sense that only a short time passed；没过多久，clearly shows that the illness followed soon after eating the flower.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 花：这种花，The bare noun can mean flowers in general rather than the flower he has just found; use a clear noun phrase to refer back to that specific flower. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Reference): 他：∅，The embedded subject is the same person as 神农 in the same sentence, so use zero form rather than repeating the pronoun. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Expressiveness: After “正好，他找到了别的花”, add one visual detail about the flower, such as “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花。”
- Expressiveness: After “他的肚子疼死了”, show his physical state and thoughts, such as “他躺在地上，难受得不得了，心想：‘我会死吗？’”

### Encouragement
You clearly connect eating the flower with becoming ill, so the event sequence is easy to follow. The phrase about his stomach hurting strongly gives the scene emotional force.

## Scene 3

> 别的人给了神农一个灵芝。这个植物可以让神农更好。比方说，他会觉得更健康。吃灵芝以后，他可以再走。现在他觉得更好。最后，神农找到了很多植物，他可以用对药。这些植物让神农很高兴。神农想跟很多人分享他的新药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别的人：一个人，别的人 refers to people other than an already identified person or group; since no such contrast has been introduced here, use an indefinite person for this first appearance. [teacher rule: bieren]
- 很多植物，他可以用对药：很多可以用来做药的植物，use 可以用来做药 to describe plants that can serve as medicine; 对 cannot connect 用 with 药 in this meaning. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 更好 -- “现在他觉得更好”：好多了，shows a clearer degree of recovery after he ate the medicine.
- 高兴 -- “这些植物让神农很高兴”：欣慰，shows that he feels pleased and relieved by finding useful plants.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: When the person gives the medicine, add one visual detail to make the helper memorable: “这时，一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人走过来，给了神农一株像伞一样的灵芝。”
- Expressiveness: Describe Shen Nong’s physical recovery after eating it: “神农吃了以后，肚子不那么疼了，慢慢站了起来。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech when he realizes what the plant can do: “神农高兴地说：‘原来灵芝可以做药！’”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the sequence from receiving the medicine to feeling better and continuing the journey. You also use completed-action marking well in actions such as giving and finding.
