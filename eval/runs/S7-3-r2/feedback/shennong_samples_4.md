## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个男人，他的名字是神农。他看到了有很多人不知道哪个植物好吃，也不知道什么植物对你的身体有好处。这个问题神农觉得很糟糕，所以神农去了吃每种植物。他吃了一种植物以后，就写了这个植物对你有没有好处。有的时候，神农吃了很好吃的植物。有的时候，他吃了很危险的植物。他吃完了每种植物以后，会来跟他的朋友聊到他吃的植物了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，The original repeats the time meaning because 古代 already refers to a historical time period.
- 写了：写下了，写下 means to record information so it can be kept; here it fits recording what the plant does to the body.
- 聊到：聊，聊到 means that a topic comes up during a conversation, while this sentence means that he intentionally discusses the plants with his friends.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 看到了 -- “他看到了有很多人不知道哪个植物好吃”：发现，emphasizes that he realizes people’s lack of knowledge through observation.
- 糟糕 -- “这个问题神农觉得很糟糕”：严重，states more precisely that the problem has important harmful consequences.
- 很好吃 -- “很好吃的植物”：味道鲜美，describes the plants’ pleasant taste more vividly.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 所以神农：所以他，Use a pronoun when the same character was named in the immediately preceding clause and no other character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Temporality): 去了吃每种植物：去吃了每种植物，With a construction beginning with 去, the completion marker follows the main action: 去 + V. + 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 有的时候，神农吃了很好吃的植物：有的时候，神农吃很好吃的植物，A habitual action introduced by 有的时候 takes the zero particle, not 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 有的时候，他吃了很危险的植物：有的时候，他吃很危险的植物，A repeated or habitual action takes the zero particle, not 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他吃的植物了：他吃的植物，After the modal verb 会, the action is not presented as completed, so the predicate takes the zero particle rather than 了.
- Expressiveness: At the point where 神农 decides to act, add an inner thought or a short quotation: “我一定要找出哪些植物可以吃。”
- Expressiveness: After the dangerous plants are mentioned, add a physical reaction to show the risk: “他觉得肚子疼得厉害。”
- Expressiveness: When he records his findings, add one concrete detail: “他把植物的颜色、味道和用处都写下来了。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the two different results of trying plants with the repeated pattern 有的时候, which makes the scene easy to follow.
- You also use 吃完了……以后 well to show the order of actions.

## Scene 3

> 神农说，有一天，我吃了一个黄色的花，名字是夹竹桃。我一吃夹竹桃，就觉得这种植物对我的脑有坏处。可是，一个灵芝和一个男人正好在我的旁边，我说，麻烦你把那个植物给我吃一下。我一吃这个灵芝就好了。所以，你得小心吃植物，因为有的植物对你的健康有坏处，特别是夹竹桃。因为夹竹桃有毒。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一个黄色的花：一朵黄色的花，Flowers take the measure word 朵; 个 is not the appropriate measure word here.
- 对我的脑有坏处：对我的大脑有坏处，脑 is usually used in compounds; 大脑 is the natural noun when referring to the brain as the body part.
- 一个灵芝：一株灵芝，When referring to a growing medicinal plant such as 灵芝, 株 is a suitable measure word rather than the general classifier 个.
- 这个灵芝：这株灵芝，This demonstrative phrase needs a matching measure word before the noun; 株 fits 灵芝 in this context.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 旁边 -- “一个灵芝和一个男人正好在我的旁边”：身边，makes the location feel more directly connected to the narrator and is especially natural for the man standing close by.
- 坏处 -- “有的植物对你的健康有坏处”：有害，more specifically identifies the plants as physically harmful to health.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 那个植物：那株灵芝，The phrase 那个植物 is vague because both 夹竹桃 and 灵芝 are plants in the scene; repeat the specific noun 灵芝 to make the referent clear.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 所以，你得小心吃植物，因为有的植物对你的健康有坏处，特别是夹竹桃。因为夹竹桃有毒。：因为有的植物对你的健康有坏处，特别是夹竹桃有毒，所以你得小心吃植物。，Use 因为……所以…… to connect the cause and result in one clear order; the final 因为 clause cannot stand alone here without its result.
- Expressiveness: When 神农 eats the flower, add a physical reaction to show why he needs help: “我肚子疼了起来，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Describe the yellow flower before he eats it to make the dangerous choice more vivid: “黄色的花像星星一样，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: Give the helpful man a small action and a line of speech: “一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人跑过来，说：‘快吃这个灵芝！’”

### Encouragement
- You used the pattern 一……就…… well to show that the effects happened immediately. You also included useful story vocabulary such as 夹竹桃、灵芝、有毒, and 健康.
