## Scene 1

> 很久以前，古代的人很不知道什么植物能吃，也什么植物不能吃。所以很多人常常吃不安全的植物，吃不安全的植物对你的身体有坏处，很麻烦的情况。但是一个人，神农，要解决这个问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很不知道：根本不知道，Degree adverb 很 cannot directly modify the negative knowledge verb 不知道; 根本 strengthens a complete lack of knowledge.
- 也什么植物不能吃：也不知道什么植物不能吃，Also must modify a verb phrase here, so repeat 不知道 before the embedded question.
- 很麻烦的情况：这是一种很麻烦的情况，A noun phrase cannot stand alone as the final predicate here; add a demonstrative subject and 是 to make a complete clause.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不安全的 -- “吃不安全的植物”：有毒的，specifies that the plants contain poison, which more precisely explains why they harm the body.
- 有坏处 -- “对你的身体有坏处”：有害，states more directly that the effect is harmful to the body.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 但是一个人，神农：但是，一个叫神农的人，On a character’s first mention, introduce the name together with a full noun phrase rather than placing the name after a vague noun.
- Expressiveness: At the consequence of eating unsafe plants, add a concrete physical result: “很多人吃了有毒的植物以后，常常生病。”
- Expressiveness: When introducing Shennong’s goal, add his determination through an inner thought: “神农想：‘我一定要找出能吃的植物。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly establish the historical setting and the people’s problem before introducing Shennong. Your use of the contrast connector shows that you are already linking ideas across sentences.

## Scene 2

> 他又聪明又努力，旅行很远找到很多植物。比方说，灵芝和夹竹桃。他一吃各种各样的植物就知道能不能吃这些植物。然后，神农写他的主意。他喜欢植物的味道，特别是花。神农遇到了一个黄色的花，一吃就觉得不舒服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 旅行很远：到很远的地方旅行，Use a place phrase with “到” to express where someone travels; “旅行” cannot directly take “很远” as its distance complement.
- 一个黄色的花：一朵黄色的花，Flowers use the matching measure word “朵,” not the general measure word “个.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 努力 -- “他又聪明又努力”：勤奋，emphasizes steady, consistent hard work as a personal quality.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 然后，神农写他的主意：然后，他写他的主意，Use a pronoun when the same character was mentioned in the immediately preceding sentence and no other character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农遇到了一个黄色的花：他遇到了一朵黄色的花，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence of the same character; the preceding sentence already makes the referent clear.
- Coherence (Temporality): 找到很多植物：找到了很多植物，The finding is a completed event in this past narrative, so the verb needs “了.”
- Coherence (Temporality): 写他的主意：写了他的主意，Because this is the next completed action after “然后,” add “了” to mark completion.
- Expressiveness: After the travel sentence, add one setting detail to make the journey easier to imagine. Example: “他走过了很多高山，找到了许多植物。”
- Expressiveness: At the moment he feels ill, show a physical reaction as well as the general feeling. Example: “他捂着肚子，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought after he eats the flower to show his worry. Example: “他想：我会不会生病了？”

### Encouragement
You clearly show that the character is intelligent and hardworking, and your use of the “一……就……” pattern effectively shows an immediate result. Naming “灵芝”和“夹竹桃” also gives the scene specific and interesting details.

## Scene 3

> 发现了夹竹桃有毒。别人看到他，很快给他吃一个灵芝。神农一吃就觉得比较好。然后，他继续写他发现的信息。会告诉他的朋友哪一个植物能不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别人看到他：一个人看到他，别人 distinguishes other people from the speaker or a stated person; no such comparison is present here, so introduce the new character as one person.
- 很快给他吃一个灵芝：很快给他吃了一个灵芝，this is a completed rescue action, so the aspect marker belongs after the final verb in the verb sequence.
- 写他发现的信息：写下他发现的信息，写 means to compose written content, while 写下 means to record information or findings.
- 哪一个植物能不能吃：哪一种植物能吃，哪一种植物不能吃，哪一个 asks the reader to select one item, while 能不能 asks a yes-or-no question; use the two parallel categories when explaining which plants are edible.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好 -- “神农一吃就觉得比较好”：好一些了, makes the improvement from his earlier discomfort more specific and natural in this context
- 很快 -- “很快给他吃一个灵芝”：立刻, emphasizes that the helper acted with no delay

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: Before the helper arrives, add a physical detail about Shen Nong’s pain, such as “神农躺在地上，肚子疼得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Describe the new helper briefly at the point of first appearance, such as “这时，一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人走过来。”
- Expressiveness: After recovering, add Shen Nong’s question to show his curiosity, such as “神农问：‘你给我吃的是什么？’”

### Encouragement
You used the pattern “一……就……” successfully to show that Shen Nong felt better immediately after eating the medicine. The scene also has a clear sequence: illness, help, recovery, and recording his findings.
