> 古代的时候，有很多人不知道什么植物能吃，什么植物不能吃。有很多人吃植物以后就吃坏肚子了，因为他们吃了毒。那个时候，有一个老人叫神农，他什么植物都觉得好吃，不怕生病，不怕毒。他希望别人也可以吃好吃的植物。可是他知道，如果他们还怕吃毒，不会吃他喜欢的植物。

## Scene 2

> 所以，他上山下山找植物，吃了很多样子的植物。他是第一人，找到了茶。他把所有的植物写下来了。他一直都吃了，他连毒都能吃。可是他还没吃了一个叫夹竹桃的植物。他找到了那个像星星的植物，觉得很漂亮，马上就吃下了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很多样子的植物：很多种植物，样子 describes an object’s appearance, while 种 is the measure word for different kinds or types of plants.
- 他是第一人，找到了茶：他是第一个找到茶的人，第一人 means a leading or outstanding person; use 第一个……的人 for the first person to complete an action.
- 他一直都吃了：他一直都吃，an action described as continuing with 一直 takes the zero particle, not completed-action 了.
- 还没吃了：还没吃过，negative completed experience is expressed with 没 + verb + 过; 了 cannot follow 没 in this sentence.
- 一个叫夹竹桃的植物：一种叫夹竹桃的植物，种 is the appropriate measure word when referring to a species or type of plant.
- 像星星的植物：像星星一样的植物，use 像……一样 to make a complete comparison.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 找 -- “他上山下山找植物”：寻找, emphasizes actively looking for plants over a period of time；探寻, emphasizes exploring in order to discover unknown plants.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 那个像星星的植物：一个像星星一样的植物，this is the first mention of this particular plant, so use 一个 rather than 那个, which points to a previously identified object.
- Expressiveness: At “觉得很漂亮,” add a visual detail about the flowers: “黄色的花又大又漂亮，像星星一样。”
- Expressiveness: Before he eats the plant, add his thought to show his decision: “他心想：这么漂亮，一定很好吃！”

### Encouragement
- You used clear action order: he searches, finds tea, records plants, and then discovers a new plant. The phrase “上山下山” also gives the reader a strong sense that his search is energetic and difficult.

## Scene 3

> 可是夹竹桃的毒很重，让他肚子很痛。他在地上躺着，觉得他快会死了。可是正好有别人找到他了，给了他一个像伞的植物，叫灵芝。他吃灵芝以后就觉得很好，不再生病了。他测量起来了，像星星的植物是毒，像伞的植物是药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别人：一个男人，This person is appearing without any contrast to the narrator or another named person, so the word for “other people” does not fit; introduce him as an unspecified man instead.
- 测量起来了：看了一下，This verb means to measure size, length, or quantity; it does not mean to identify whether a plant is poisonous or medicinal.
- 是毒：有毒，Use the adjective phrase meaning “poisonous,” rather than saying that the plant “is poison.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很重 -- “夹竹桃的毒很重”：毒性很强，more precisely describes the strength of the plant’s poison.
- 很痛 -- “让他肚子很痛”：疼得厉害，gives a more vivid description of strong physical pain.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 觉得他快会死了：觉得快会死了，When the same character remains the subject within one sentence, use zero form rather than repeating the pronoun.
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃灵芝以后：吃了灵芝以后，The eating is completed before the later feeling of recovery, so use 了 for the completed action.
- Expressiveness: At the moment of pain, add a physical reaction to show how serious it feels, for example: “他疼得直冒冷汗。”
- Expressiveness: Add the character’s frightened thought while he is lying on the ground, for example: “他想：我会死吗？”
- Expressiveness: Make the helper more vivid by adding one clear detail, for example: “一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人走过来。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the cause-and-effect sequence from eating the plant, to pain, to receiving help and feeling better.
- The use of “吃灵芝以后就” gives the recovery event a clear time order.
