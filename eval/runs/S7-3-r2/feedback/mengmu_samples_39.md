## Scene 1

> 古代的时候人觉得祭拜非常重要，孟子很小的时候，从早到晚一直祭拜，因为他妈妈告诉他祭拜很重要。虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜。他的同学们老是对他开玩笑了。孟子妈妈看了他的同学们对孟子不好的影响，所以孟子和孟子妈妈搬家了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，The original repeats the idea of “time”: the noun already names a historical period, so the added time phrase is redundant.
- 人：人们，This refers to a group of people in general, so the plural collective noun is needed.
- 看了：看到了，Here the mother perceives the influence; this meaning requires the resultative verb for “see,” rather than the verb for “look.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不好的 -- “同学们对孟子不好的影响”：不良的，This specifies that the influence is harmful or negative and forms a natural phrase with “影响.”

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子很小的时候：有一个小男孩叫孟子。他很小的时候，This is the character’s first mention, so introduce him with a full noun phrase before using a pronoun.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子妈妈看到了：她看到了，The mother was mentioned in the immediately preceding information and no other mother competes as a referent, so a pronoun is more natural.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子妈妈：孟子和妈妈，After naming Mengzi in the same phrase, do not repeat his name inside the phrase naming his mother.
- Coherence (Temporality): 老是对他开玩笑了：老是对他开玩笑，This describes a repeated habit, so it takes the zero particle rather than completed-action “了.”
- Coherence (Conjunction): 虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜：因为他每天只祭拜，所以他没有朋友，The intended relation is cause and effect; “虽然” needs a contrasting result clause, while the paired cause-and-effect connector clearly links these ideas.
- Expressiveness: At the point where Mengzi worships all day, add an action detail such as “他学着大人的样子，低着头祭拜。” This shows what his worship looks like.
- Expressiveness: When the classmates tease him, add Mengzi’s feeling, for example: “孟子觉得很难过，不明白他们为什么笑他。” This gives the reader his inner response.
- Expressiveness: Before the move, add a short thought from the mother, such as “孟母心想：‘这里会影响孩子。’” This makes her decision more vivid.

### Encouragement
- You clearly show Mengzi’s repeated routine with “从早到晚一直,” and you give a logical reason for the family’s move. The scene also introduces an important conflict between Mengzi and his classmates.

## Scene 2

> 孟子才十一岁，成为一个哲学家。孟子不是祭拜，而是看政治文章，难怪他成为了哲学家。不过，看过很多政治文章对他害得，他有很多问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 才十一岁，成为一个哲学家：才十一岁，就成为了一个哲学家，Use the paired pattern with an age: 才 + number + 就 + verb. The change into this role is completed, so it also needs 了.
- 看过很多政治文章对他害得，他有很多问题：看过很多政治文章害得他有很多问题，The structure is cause + 害得 + person + harmful result; 对他 cannot appear before 害得 in this structure.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子不是祭拜：他不是祭拜，Use a pronoun when the same character was named in the immediately previous sentence and no other character competes as the referent.
- Expressiveness: At the final sentence, show one specific question that troubles him, such as: “他想：‘这些政治文章到底是什么意思？’”
- Expressiveness: When describing his reading, add a small action to show concentration, such as: “他一边看，一边在纸上写下不懂的地方。”

### Encouragement
You used the contrast pattern “不是……而是……” clearly to show how Mengzi’s interests changed. The final sentence also gives a believable consequence of reading difficult material.

## Scene 3

> 他一直问问题，但是不能解决那些问题。孟子长大，他开始能解决问题。他常常建议你不是学金融，而是学政治，因为如果你懂政治，你会成为高薪。后来孟子成为教授，他教学生很厉害。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不是学金融，而是学政治：不要学金融，而要学政治，A recommendation should use a negative instruction followed by the preferred action; the original contrast construction is not the natural way to give this advice.
- 成为高薪：有很高的薪水，The verb used for becoming requires a new identity or role, while salary is income that someone has or receives.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 解决 -- “不能解决那些问题”：解答，more precisely means answering questions rather than dealing with general difficulties.
- 厉害 -- “他教学生很厉害”：很有办法，emphasizes that he has effective ways of teaching students.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 你：人们，This new pronoun has no established listener in a third-person story, so use a full noun phrase for the people receiving the advice.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子：他，The character was named in the immediately previous sentence and no competing male character appears, so a pronoun is more natural for this close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为教授：成为了教授，Becoming a professor is a completed change of state in this later event, so it needs the completion marker.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 长大，他开始能解决问题：长大以后，开始能解决问题，Use the sequence connector to show that the ability began after he grew up.
- Expressiveness: After the difficulty with the questions, add an inner feeling, such as “皱着眉头，心里很着急”, to show why solving them matters to him.
- Expressiveness: At the teaching moment, add a small classroom detail, such as “学生们认真地听他讲课”, to make his teaching ability more vivid.

### Encouragement
You use useful time markers such as “后来” and show a clear development in the character’s abilities. Your attempt to explain his advice with a reason also gives the scene a stronger connection between ideas.
