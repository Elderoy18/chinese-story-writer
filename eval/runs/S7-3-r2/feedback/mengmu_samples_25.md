## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈觉得在墓地玩儿，害得，所以他们搬家。搬家对孟子有好的影响，他才五岁，可是每天不是写字就是帮妈妈的工作。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在墓地玩儿，害得：孟子在墓地玩儿害得自己很担心，害得 must be followed by the person affected and a harmful result; adding 孟子 also makes clear who is playing. [teacher rule: haide_structure]
- 搬家：搬家了，了 marks that the move was completed. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- 帮妈妈的工作：帮妈妈干活，妈妈的工作 is a noun phrase, but this meaning needs a person followed by the action being helped with. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好的 -- “搬家对孟子有好的影响”：有益的，specifies that the effect benefits Mengzi；积极的，makes the beneficial nature of the effect more precise.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他才五岁，可是每天不是写字就是帮妈妈的工作：他才五岁，就每天不是写字就是帮妈妈干活，才……就…… expresses that someone began doing something at an unusually early age; 可是 introduces contrast instead. [rule: cai_jiu_construction]
- Expressiveness: At the part about playing near the cemetery, add one concrete action to show what Mengzi does there, such as “孟子学着别人祭拜，还笑个不停。”
- Expressiveness: After the mother becomes worried, add her inner thought or speech to show her reason for moving, such as “孟母心想：‘住在这儿太麻烦了。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect the move with its effect on Mengzi’s daily life. Your use of the pattern “不是……就是……” also gives the second sentence a lively sense of his routine.

### Vocabulary Suggestions

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 难怪他成为有名的人：难怪成为有名的人，Since the same person was already named in the preceding clause of this sentence, use zero form rather than repeating the pronoun. [rule: none]
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为：成为了，This is a completed later achievement in the narrative, so the verb needs the completion marker. [rule: aspect_le_completed]

- Expressiveness: Add one detail about his learning before the final achievement, such as: “他认真看书学习，后来成为了有名的哲学家。” This gives the reader a clearer sense of how he developed.

### Encouragement
- You used “难怪” effectively to connect his intelligence with his later achievement, creating a clear ending relationship.
