## Scene 1

> 一天，很久很久以前，孟子和他妈妈住在中国。孟子妈妈的工作是祭拜，孟子不高兴，因为他说我们老是搬家，为什么？他妈妈说，从早到晚，我一直祭拜，很多墓地得我祭拜。所以我们得去很多不同的地方。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一天，很久很久以前：很久很久以前的一天，A time expression that modifies “day” should come before it and link with “的.” [rule: time_expression]
- 他说我们老是搬家，为什么？：他说：“我们老是搬家，为什么？”，Direct speech needs a colon after the speaking verb and quotation marks around the exact words spoken. [rule: none]
- 他妈妈说，从早到晚，我一直祭拜，很多墓地得我祭拜。所以我们得去很多不同的地方。：他妈妈说：“从早到晚，我一直祭拜。我得去很多墓地祭拜，所以我们得去很多不同的地方。”，Direct speech needs quotation punctuation; also, the modal verb “得” must follow its subject and come before the action verb, so “我得去……” expresses “I have to go to…”. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 地方 -- “去很多不同的地方”：不同的墓地，identifies the places more exactly as cemeteries, matching the previous sentence；各处墓地，emphasizes that the cemeteries are in many separate locations.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After “孟子不高兴,” add a small physical detail to show his feeling, such as “孟子皱着眉头，觉得很委屈。”
- Expressiveness: Make Mengzi’s question sound more like natural dialogue at “为什么？”, such as “妈妈，我们为什么又要搬家？”
- Expressiveness: At the mother’s explanation, add a detail about her tiredness, such as “妈妈叹了口气，看起来很累。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show why Mengzi feels unhappy and give his mother a reason for moving often. The use of “从早到晚” also gives the mother’s daily routine a clear sense of duration.

## Scene 2

> 孟子想，难怪我们常常搬家，原来我妈妈很忙。可是常常搬家害得孟子很难找到朋友。每次搬家孟子也觉得新的地方不是太热就是太冷，不是太大就是太小。在哪的地方，他都不高兴。孟子想帮助妈妈祭拜，可是他六岁，所以不能祭拜。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在哪的地方：在哪个地方，A location question needs a measure word between the interrogative word and the noun: use the pattern 哪个 + noun. [rule: none]
- 可是他六岁：可是他才六岁，Use 才 before an age to emphasize that the child is still very young, which explains the limitation that follows. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 忙 -- “原来我妈妈很忙”：忙着四处祭拜，specifies what keeps the mother busy in this scene；忙于祭拜，states the activity in a concise narrative style.
- 新的 -- “新的地方不是太热就是太冷”：陌生的，emphasizes that each place is unfamiliar to the child.
- 不高兴 -- “他都不高兴”：闷闷不乐，shows a continuing, subdued kind of unhappiness.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 每次搬家孟子也觉得：每次搬家，他也觉得，孟子 was the clear referent in the preceding sentence, so a pronoun is more natural for a close re-occurrence. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子想帮助妈妈祭拜：他想帮助妈妈祭拜，The previous sentence already clearly refers to 孟子 with 他, so use a pronoun rather than repeating the name. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Expressiveness: After the description of each new place, add a physical reaction to show his discomfort: “他觉得冷得发抖，热得满头大汗。”
- Expressiveness: At the final sentence, add a short line of internal speech to show his wish to help: “要是我能帮妈妈就好了。”

### Encouragement
You used the pattern 难怪……，原来…… naturally to show Mengzi’s realization. You also gave clear reasons for his unhappiness and used contrast words effectively to connect his ideas.

### Encouragement
