## Scene 1

> 孟子小的时候，从早到晚都跟朋友玩。孟子和他的朋友喜欢玩在墓地里，装祭拜。孟子五岁的时候，跟妈妈搬家。他们搬家不是因为孟子玩得太多，而是因为妈妈认为他们的家离墓地太近。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 喜欢玩在墓地里：喜欢在墓地里玩，The place phrase should come before the main verb in this sentence.
- 装祭拜：装作祭拜，装作 means “pretend to do something”; 装 cannot directly take this verb phrase here.
- 跟妈妈搬家：妈妈带着他搬家，The mother is the person who takes the child to move; 带着孟子搬家 expresses this relationship more accurately.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 玩 -- “从早到晚都跟朋友玩”：玩闹，emphasizes active, childlike play with friends.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子小的时候：有一个小男孩叫孟子，他小时候，At a character’s first mention, introduce the character with a full noun phrase; later reference to the same character can use a pronoun.
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬家：搬家了，This move is presented as a completed event, so the final verb needs 了.
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery play, add a detail showing why the behavior is inappropriate: “孟子一边笑一边装作祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: At the mother’s reason for moving, show her worry with an internal thought or quotation: “妈妈很担心，说：‘住在墓地旁边，孟子会学别人祭拜。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show Mengzi’s daily habit with “从早到晚都,” and the final sentence uses the contrast pattern “不是……而是……” effectively to explain the reason for the move.

## Scene 2

> 妈妈认为，住离墓地太近，害得孟子的心气。孟子和孟母搬到子思老师的家，跟子思老师住。孟母帮子思打扫房间和做饭。子思帮孟母教她的儿子。因为孟子现在没有朋友，他老是读书。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 住离墓地太近：住在离墓地太近的地方，A location phrase after a verb of living needs 在, and the place needs a noun such as 地方.
- 孟子的心气：孟子很生气，害得 must be followed by the affected person and a harmful result clause; 心气 is a noun and cannot be the result by itself.
- 搬到：搬到了，The move is completed before the following living situation, so the completed action needs 了.
- 老是：一直，老是 has a complaining or negative tone; 一直 neutrally describes continuing study.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 现在 -- “因为孟子现在没有朋友”：这时，locates the event more clearly at this point in the story rather than in a general present time.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 妈妈认为：她认为，The mother was named in the immediately preceding scene and no other female character competes as the referent, so use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟母搬到了子思老师的家：他们搬到了子思老师的家，Both characters were just mentioned, so the pronoun naturally refers to them as a pair.
- Coherence (Reference): 跟子思老师住：跟他住，子思老师 was named earlier in the same sentence, so a close re-occurrence should use a pronoun.
- Coherence (Reference): 子思帮孟母教她的儿子：他帮孟母教她的儿子，子思老师 is the only recently mentioned male character, so a pronoun is clearer and avoids repeating the name.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为孟子现在没有朋友，他：因为孟子现在没有朋友，所以他，Add 所以 to complete the cause-and-effect connector pair.
- Expressiveness: After the first sentence, add Meng母’s worried reaction, for example: “孟母皱着眉头，心里很担心。”
- Expressiveness: When describing the new home, add one concrete detail about the work she does, for example: “孟母每天早早起来，认真地打扫房间、做饭。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech from 子思老师 to show his kindness, for example: “子思老师说：‘我会认真教孟子读书。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly show how the characters support one another in their new home, especially through the details about cleaning, cooking, and teaching. You also used a sensible cause-and-effect idea to explain why 孟子 studies so much.

## Scene 3

> 子思认为，对孩子来说，读书最重要，特别是学哲学。孟子不是练习写文章，就是看哲学书。孟子从五岁一直学哲学。他才五岁就学哲学，难怪他成为很有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 从五岁：从五岁开始，Use 开始 after 从 + time to mark the starting point of an action.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很有名 -- “他成为很有名的哲学家”：享有盛名，expresses that he became a philosopher widely known and respected.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子从五岁一直学哲学：他从五岁一直学哲学，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the previous sentence has already named the same character and no competing person is present.
- Coherence (Temporality): 一直学哲学：一直在学哲学，Use 一直在 + verb to show that the learning is an ongoing action over a long period.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为很有名的哲学家：成为了很有名的哲学家，This describes a completed change of status, so the verb takes 了.
- Expressiveness: After the first sentence, add a short line of advice from the teacher, such as “子思说：‘读书不只是记住，还要认真思考。’”
- Expressiveness: When describing Mengzi’s study, add a feeling to show his interest, such as “他越学越感兴趣，常常忘了休息。”

### Encouragement
You used the paired structure “不是……就是……” correctly to show Mengzi’s two regular study activities. The sentence with “才五岁就” also effectively emphasizes how early he began studying philosophy.
