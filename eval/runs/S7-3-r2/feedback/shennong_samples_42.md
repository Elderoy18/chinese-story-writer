## Scene 1

> 古代的时候有一个人叫神农。他需要当上医，一看别的人就想帮助他们。但是，他有一个问题，他不知道什么东西能做药。神农听说一个远远的地方有一种植物，什么病人吃都会觉得身体更好。神农三个月走去，找到有植物的地方，他看到一个很漂亮的植物，又香又好看。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，the phrase repeats the idea of “time,” because 古代 already names a time period. [vocab list]
- 医：医生，医 by itself refers to medicine or medical practice; 医生 is the person whose job is treating patients. [rule: none]
- 一看：一看到，here the meaning is “see someone,” so it needs 看到 rather than 看. [vocab list]
- 别的人：别人，别人 refers to other people as a group; 别的 must be followed by a noun. [teacher rule: bieren]
- 什么病人吃都会觉得身体更好：不管什么病人，吃了都会觉得身体更好，use 不管什么……都…… to express that the result applies to every kind of patient. [rule: none]
- 一个很漂亮的植物：一棵很漂亮的植物，棵 is the matching measure word for a plant; 个 is too general here. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 帮助 -- “一看到别人就想帮助他们”：救治，specifies that his help is medical treatment for patients.
- 漂亮 -- “一个很漂亮的植物”：色彩鲜艳，describes more specifically that the plant is visually striking because of its vivid colours.
- 身体更好 -- “都会觉得身体更好”：身体状况有所好转，specifies that the patient’s physical condition improves.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 神农三个月走去，找到有植物的地方：神农走了三个月，找到了有植物的地方，put the duration after the verb, and use 了 for the completed journey and completed result of finding the place. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: After the character learns about the plant, add an internal-state phrase such as “心里很着急” to show why finding medicine matters to him.
- Expressiveness: Make the plant’s appearance more vivid with a detail such as “开着黄色的花” before describing its smell and beauty.
- Expressiveness: Add a short goal-oriented thought, such as “一定要找到能做药的植物”, to show the character’s determination.

### Encouragement
You introduced the main character clearly by naming him at the beginning. The description of the plant as fragrant and attractive also starts to create a sensory setting for the scene.

## Scene 2

> 他觉得，这种一定是做药的花。原来他想错了。吃了以后，神农快生病。 这时一个穿着蓝色衣服的人帮神农，快给他一种红的灵芝。神农吃了以后，他没有病，想起来以前一个人告诉他，灵芝对身体有好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 快：赶快，In this narrative position, “赶快” clearly means doing the next action without delay; “快” alone does not naturally modify this completed giving action. [rule: none]
- 以前一个人：以前有一个人，An indefinite person in this past event needs “有” to introduce that person naturally. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他：delete it，The name was already used in the same sentence, so zero form is more natural than repeating a pronoun. [rule: redundancy_delete]
- Coherence (Temporality): 神农快生病：神农很快就生病了，This is a completed event after eating, so the verb needs “了”; “很快就” also clearly marks that the illness happened soon afterwards. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 给：给了，The blue-clothed person completed the action of giving the medicine, so “给” needs the completion marker “了.” [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 没有病：病就好了，The sentence describes a change from being ill to recovering, so use “好了” with “了” to show the completed change of state. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: When the illness begins, add a physical reaction to show how serious it feels: “神农肚子疼得不得了，躺在地上不能动。”
- Expressiveness: At the moment of help, add urgent speech from the blue-clothed person: “蓝衣人赶快说：‘快吃这个灵芝！’”
- Expressiveness: After recovering, add Shennong’s discovery as an inner thought: “神农想：‘原来灵芝真的可以做药！’”

### Encouragement
You used clear time markers such as “原来”, “吃了以后”, and “这时” to move the action forward. You also included an important change in Shennong’s understanding of the medicinal plant.

## Scene 3

> 啊，他怎么忘记了？他把灵芝放在包里。一回家就告诉别人。他找到药以后他写下怎么做药在书上。所以人们都知道，那红灵芝对身体好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他把灵芝放在包里：他把灵芝放在包里了，This is a completed action in the narrative, so it needs 了 to mark completion. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- 写下怎么做药在书上：在书上写下了做药的方法，The location phrase should come before the verb here, and the resultative verb needs 了 after it; 做药的方法 clearly names the content he wrote. [teacher rule: le_placement]
- 对身体好处：对身体有好处，The noun 好处 needs 有 in this predicate structure; the corrected phrase means “is beneficial to the body.” [vocab list]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 以后他写下：以后写下，After the same person has been named in the earlier clause and no other character competes as the subject, use zero form rather than repeating the pronoun in the same sentence. [rule: none]
- Expressiveness: After the opening question, add a brief thought to show his realization, such as: “他想：原来灵芝对身体有好处！”
- Expressiveness: When he tells others, add a short line of speech to make the moment more lively, such as: “灵芝是药，可以吃！”
- Expressiveness: At the writing moment, add one concrete detail about what he records, such as: “他认真地写下了灵芝的样子和作用。”

### Encouragement
You used the sequence pattern 一……就…… naturally to show that he told others immediately after returning home. The scene also clearly shows how his discovery leads to sharing useful knowledge with people.
