### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 住在古代：在古代生活，An era is expressed with a time phrase, so use this form rather than treating it as the location after the residence verb. [rule: time_expression]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 找 -- “一起去旅行找可以吃的植物”：寻找, emphasizes a continued search during the journey；探寻, emphasizes exploring unknown plants and their uses

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：一个叫神农的人，At the character’s first mention, introduce the person with a fuller noun phrase so the reader knows who this new character is. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Temporality): 家人常常生病了：家人常常生病，This describes a repeated routine, so use the zero particle rather than completed-action 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们去很多不一样的地方：他们去了很多不一样的地方，This journey action is presented as completed, so the verb needs 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: After the family’s illness is mentioned, add the danger more clearly: “很多人因为吃了有毒的植物，常常生病。”
- Expressiveness: After the decision to travel, add Shen Nong’s motivation or thought: “神农想：‘我一定要找到能吃的植物。’”
- Expressiveness: Make the travel locations more vivid with one sensory detail: “高山上空气清新，山谷里开着各种各样的花。”

### Encouragement

## Scene 3

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 是毒的：有毒，毒 is a noun and does not form the natural predicate 是毒的 here; 有毒 means “poisonous.” [vocab list]
- 想知道灵芝会帮他：想知道灵芝会不会帮他，after 想知道, a yes-or-no uncertainty is normally expressed with the A-not-A form 会不会. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 吃完了以后 -- “神农吃完了以后开始痊愈了”：服用灵芝以后，specifies that he took the lingzhi as medicine and fits a recovery context.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农说：他说，use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the previous sentence already names the same character and no other possible speaker has intervened. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Temporality): 哥哥给神农灵芝：哥哥给了神农灵芝，this is a completed giving action, so 给 takes 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: After the recovery sentence, add a concrete physical change, such as “神农觉得肚子慢慢不疼了。” This shows how the medicine helped rather than only stating the result.
- Expressiveness: In the thank-you line, add a feeling to make the dialogue more personal, such as “谢谢你，哥哥！我刚才真害怕。” This lets the reader hear 神农’s relief.

### Encouragement
You clearly connected the lingzhi to 神农’s recovery, and your dialogue gives the scene a warm, thankful ending. The use of 痊愈 is also a strong and precise recovery word.
