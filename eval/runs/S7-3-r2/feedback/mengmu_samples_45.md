## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子。他们住在墓地的旁边，所以孟子老是祭拜。孟子不是祭拜，就是跟朋友聊天。他很喜欢他的城市，但是城市有才两个学校，都很小。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，`古代` already names a time period, so adding `的时候` is redundant.
- 城市有才两个学校：城市里才有两个学校，`才` must come before the verb `有` when it limits the number that exists.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 小 -- “都很小”：规模不大，specifies that the schools are small in scale rather than simply physically small.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子：一个叫孟子的男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国，at first mention, introduce the character with his name inside the noun phrase instead of giving the name only through a following pronoun.
- Coherence (Reference): 他很喜欢他的城市：他很喜欢自己的城市，when the possessor is the same person as the subject, `自己的` makes that relationship clear.
- Expressiveness: At the cemetery scene, add Mengzi’s reaction and an action, such as “孟子觉得祭拜很好玩，学着别人鞠躬。” This helps the reader understand why he often worships.
- Expressiveness: When describing the city, add one sensory detail, such as “街上有很多商人卖东西，从早到晚都很热闹。” This makes the setting more vivid.

### Encouragement
You used `所以` to show a clear cause-and-effect relationship, and the paired pattern `不是……就是……` gives Mengzi’s daily activities a lively rhythm.

## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈不是不喜欢他们的城市，而是想孟子有很好的教育。他们搬家到有很多学校的城市。他们从早到晚走路，孟子一直抱怨。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 想孟子有很好的教育：想让孟子有很好的教育，When a person wants another person to do or have something, use 想让 + person + verb phrase.
- 搬家到：搬到，Use 搬到 + destination for moving to a place; 搬家 cannot directly take 到 + place in this structure.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他们从早到晚走路，孟子一直抱怨：他们从早到晚走路，所以孟子一直抱怨，Add 所以 to show the cause-and-effect relation: the long walk causes Mengzi’s complaining.
- Expressiveness: After the complaint, add Mengzi’s feeling and a short line of speech, such as: 孟子觉得很累，皱着眉头说：“我们还要走多久？”
- Expressiveness: At the new city, add one concrete detail about the schools, such as: 孟子看到街上有很多学生背着书包去上学。

### Encouragement
You clearly show the mother’s positive reason for moving, rather than simply saying that she dislikes the old city. The contrast structure in the first sentence also gives the scene a clear focus.

## Scene 3

> 他难怪抱怨，他想他的朋友，因为他不要离开他的城市。他的妈妈找到孟子一个老师。跟老师孟子学最重要的文学，所以他成为哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他难怪抱怨，他想：难怪他抱怨，原来他想，Place 难怪 before the clause that becomes understandable, and use 原来 to introduce the explanation.
- 他的妈妈找到孟子一个老师：他的妈妈给孟子找到了一个老师，Use 给 to mark Mengzi as the beneficiary of the search, and use 了 because the teacher has been found.
- 跟老师孟子学：孟子跟老师学，Put the subject before the prepositional phrase so that it is clear that Mengzi is learning from the teacher.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 想 -- “他想他的朋友”：想念，specifically expresses missing friends who are no longer nearby；挂念，adds a sense of caring and worrying about friends from afar
- 抱怨 -- “难怪他抱怨”：发牢骚，more specifically describes grumbling aloud about an unhappy situation

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他的朋友：自己的朋友，Because the subject is also the possessor, 自己的 clearly shows that these are Mengzi’s own friends.
- Coherence (Reference): 他的城市：自己的城市，Because Mengzi is both the subject and the possessor, 自己的 makes the reference precise.
- Coherence (Temporality): 所以他成为哲学家：所以他成为了哲学家，Use 了 for the completed change of state: Mengzi eventually became a philosopher.
- Expressiveness: At the opening, add Mengzi’s feelings about leaving his friends, such as: “我真舍不得我的朋友。”
- Expressiveness: When his mother finds a teacher, show Mengzi’s reaction with a short thought or line of speech, such as: “孟子有点不高兴，可是他还是认真听老师讲课。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show Mengzi’s reason for complaining and then move the story forward to his learning with a teacher.
- Using Mengzi’s name in the middle of the scene helps the reader keep track of who is acting.
