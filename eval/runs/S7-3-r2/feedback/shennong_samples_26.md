## Scene 1

> 很久以前，有一个老男人叫神农。神农在古代每一天看到了别的小镇人生病还是不舒服，所以他决定开始找新的药。他下一天开始爬当地的山脉，找了各种各样的植物。神农试一试每个植物，知道有什么好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别的小镇人：小镇里的人，别的 is used to distinguish other people or things from a stated one; no such comparison is established here, and 小镇里的人 is the natural noun phrase for people in the town.
- 下一天：第二天，第二天 is the standard narrative time expression for “the following day”; 下一天 is not natural in this context.
- 找了各种各样的植物：找到了各种各样的植物，找 means “look for,” while 找到 means “find.” The following action shows that the plants were found.
- 每个植物：每一种植物，when referring to each type of plant, use the classifier 种 rather than the general classifier 个.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 所以他决定开始找新的药：所以决定开始找新的药，because 神农 has already been named earlier in the same sentence and no other character competes as the subject, use zero form rather than repeating 他.
- Coherence (Reference): 知道有什么好处：知道它们各有什么好处，adding 它们 makes the referent—the plants—clear, while 各 shows that each plant may have different benefits.
- Coherence (Temporality): 每一天看到了：每一天看到，每一天 describes a routine, so use the zero particle rather than 了, which marks a completed action.
- Coherence (Temporality): 试一试：试了试，this is a completed action in a past narrative, so the action takes 了.
- Expressiveness: At the point where Shennong climbs the mountains, add a small visual detail about the setting, such as “山上长着很多绿色的植物”.
- Expressiveness: When he tests the plants, show his careful feeling or action, such as “他小心地尝了尝，不敢吃太多”.
- Expressiveness: After learning about a plant’s benefit, add a short thought to show his purpose, such as “神农想：这种植物也许能帮助生病的人”.

### Encouragement
- You introduce Shennong clearly at the beginning and keep him as the central character throughout the scene.
- You also use a clear cause-and-effect connection with 所以, and your actions follow a logical order from searching to testing.

## Scene 2

> 比方说，他一次试一试有些植物的叶，吃得又新鲜又甜。煮这些叶以后，他一喝就知道他发现新的茶叶。虽然他找到了很多的新的植物，但是他的旅程有的时候特别是危险。比方说，他一吃灵芝就开始生病。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 试一试：尝了，Verb reduplication is for a light or tentative attempt, but this sentence recounts an action he actually carried out; “尝了” states that he tasted the leaves.
- 吃得又新鲜又甜：这些叶子又新鲜又甜，A complement after “得” should describe the manner or result of the eating action. Here, freshness and sweetness are qualities of the leaves.
- 特别是危险：特别危险，“特别是” introduces a particular item or situation, while “特别” can directly intensify the adjective “危险.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 新 -- “很多的新的植物”：陌生的，emphasizes that these are plants he does not yet know；未尝过的，specifies that he has not tasted these plants before.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他发现：发现，The subject is already “他” earlier in the same sentence, so use zero form rather than repeating the pronoun.
- Coherence (Temporality): 煮这些叶以后：煮了这些叶以后，The boiling must be completed before the later action introduced by “以后,” so use “了” for completion.
- Coherence (Temporality): 发现新的茶叶：发现了新的茶叶，Finding the tea is presented as a completed discovery that he knows about after drinking it.
- Expressiveness: After the leaves are boiled, add a sensory detail about the drink, such as “茶水闻起来很香，喝起来有一点甜。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where he becomes ill, add his physical feeling to show the danger, such as “他马上捂着肚子，疼得站不起来。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought after he gets sick, such as “神农想：这种植物为什么让我这么难受？”

### Encouragement
You used the contrast pair and the immediate-sequence pattern effectively to connect events. Your scene also clearly shows that Shennong is testing plants and learning from the results.

## Scene 3

> 一个爬山的男人正好看到神农的麻烦，然后给了他一些药。神农最终他的小镇回到，跟别的人分享他的发现。你认为神农的故事真的吗？这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 最终他的小镇回到：最终回到他住的小镇，The verb phrase must come before its destination; the destination cannot be placed before the verb in this structure.
- 别的人：别人，Use this word for people other than the person being discussed; the original phrase suggests different people rather than all other people.
- 神农的故事真的吗：神农的故事是真的吗，When asking whether something is true with the predicate “真的”, use “是” before the predicate.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 药 -- “给了他一些药”：草药，identifies the medicine more precisely as plant-based medicine, which fits this story’s setting.
- 麻烦 -- “看到神农的麻烦”：困境，shows more clearly that Shen Nong is in a difficult situation rather than having a small inconvenience.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处：这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处了，This fixed expression ends with “了”; here it is part of the structure rather than a marker of completed action.
- Expressiveness: At the rescue moment, add Shen Nong’s physical feeling to show why he needs help: “神农躺在地上，肚子疼得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: When Shen Nong shares his discovery, add a short line of speech: “他说：‘灵芝可以做药，夹竹桃有毒，不能吃。’”

### Encouragement
- You introduced the new man clearly with a full noun phrase, and the first sentence gives the rescue events in an easy-to-follow order.
- Your final question directly involves the reader, which makes the ending feel engaging.
