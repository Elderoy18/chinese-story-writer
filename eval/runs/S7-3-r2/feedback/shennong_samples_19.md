## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男人觉得植物很有意思。他叫神农。有的植物吃了以后他的肚子疼死了，可是有的吃了以后很舒服。他找很多植物。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 already expresses a time period, so adding 的时候 is redundant.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一个男人觉得植物很有意思。他叫神农：有一个叫神农的男人觉得植物很有意思。，A character’s name should be included when the character is first introduced, rather than added only in the following sentence.

- Expressiveness: Add Shennong’s thought after trying the plants, such as “这些植物到底能不能吃呢？” This shows his curiosity and purpose.

## Scene 2

> 凉山很大的山，这也找植物。一天，他找到黄色的花。他听说你跟黄色的花做茶很有用。比方说，你的头发疼死，这冰茶是药。他一喝茶就很不舒服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 凉山很大的山：凉山是一座很大的山，A noun predicate needs the copula 是 here, and 一座 is the appropriate measure word for a mountain.
- 这也找植物：他也在这里找植物，Here the location needs 在这里, and the action needs a clear subject.
- 找到：找到了，The discovery is a completed event in this scene, so add 了 after the resultative complement.
- 跟黄色的花做茶：用黄色的花做茶，Use 用 to introduce the material used to make something; 跟 does not express that relationship here.
- 头发：头，头发 means “hair”; a headache is expressed with 头疼.
- 疼死：疼得要死，Use 得 to introduce an extreme degree complement after 疼.
- 这冰茶：这杯茶，A demonstrative before a countable noun needs a measure word: 这 + 杯 + 茶.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 有用 -- “用黄色的花做茶很有用”：有药用价值，specifies that the usefulness is medicinal rather than general；能缓解头疼，states the particular benefit suggested by the following example.
- 不舒服 -- “他一喝茶就很不舒服”：难受，more directly describes physical discomfort in this situation；肚子疼得厉害，gives a more specific bodily symptom.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 你（in “他听说你跟黄色的花做茶很有用”）：delete it，No second-person listener has been introduced; the reported general information can begin directly with 用黄色的花做茶.
- Coherence (Reference): 你的：他，The narration has been following 神农, while 你 has no established referent; repeat the recent character with 他.
- Expressiveness: When the yellow flowers first appear, add one visual detail: “黄色的花像星星一样，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: After he feels ill, show his fear with an internal thought: “他肚子疼得不得了，心里想：我会不会死？”
- Expressiveness: Add a short reaction to make the discovery feel important: “神农觉得很奇怪，马上不敢再喝了。”

### Encouragement
- You used the immediate-result pattern with “一……就……”, which clearly shows that the discomfort happened right after drinking the tea.
- You also included key story elements such as the yellow flowers, tea, and the character’s physical reaction.

## Scene 3

> 糟糕，这黄色的花是夹竹桃，特别是毒。正好在这儿，灵芝药的夹竹桃他吃完了，也写一个课文的植物。谢谢神农。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 这黄色的花：这朵黄色的花，A singular flower needs the measure word 朵 after 这.
- 特别是毒：特别有毒，毒 is not an adjective that can follow 特别; 有毒 means “poisonous.”
- 灵芝药的夹竹桃：灵芝是药，夹竹桃有毒，The original noun chain does not create a meaningful modifier relationship with 的; use separate clauses to state what each plant is.
- 也写一个课文的植物：也把这些植物记下来了，To record plants, use 记下 rather than writing “plants” as the object; the result complement 下 shows that the information was recorded.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After identifying the flower, add a visual detail to show why it seemed tempting: “那朵花像星星一样，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: After the eating action, show Shennong’s physical feeling and worry: “他的肚子突然疼了起来，难受得不得了。他想：‘我会死吗？’”

### Encouragement
You clearly identified the important plants, including 夹竹桃 and 灵芝. You also used 了 correctly in 吃完了 to show that the eating action was finished.
