## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，孟子和孟母住了在靠墓地的家。孩子才五岁就每天在墓地祭拜了。在那个城市，孟母从早到晚都找工作，可是找不到，害得孟母没有钱。难怪她跟孩子搬家，原来买不了房。他们搬了到别一个城市找工作。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，the original repeats the time meaning because 古代 already names a time period and 的时候 also means “the time of.” [vocab list]
- 她跟孩子搬家：她带着孟子搬家，跟……搬家 means moving together with someone, while 带着……搬家 expresses that the mother takes her child with her when relocating. [vocab list]
- 别一个城市：另一个城市，别的 distinguishes other people or things from a stated one, while 另一个 means one additional or different city. [teacher rule: bieren]
- 搬了到：搬到了，when a verb is followed by a resultative/directional complement such as 到, 了 comes after that complement. [teacher rule: le_placement]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 买不了 -- “原来买不了房”：买不起，specifies that the house cannot be bought because there is not enough money；负担不起，emphasizes that the cost is beyond what the family can afford.
- 靠 -- “靠墓地的家”：靠近，makes the location relationship with the cemetery more explicit；紧挨着，shows that the home is immediately beside the cemetery.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟母：一个叫孟子的孩子和孟母，孟子 is appearing for the first time, so he should be introduced with a name and a brief identity rather than being assumed known. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Reference): 孩子：他，孟子 was named in the previous sentence and no other child competes as the referent, so a pronoun is natural for this close re-occurrence. [rule: none]
- Coherence (Reference): 害得孟母没有钱：害得她没有钱，孟母 is already the subject of this sentence, so use a pronoun when referring to her again closely. [rule: none]
- Coherence (Temporality): 住了在：住在，住 is a continuing state here, not a completed action, so it does not take 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 每天在墓地祭拜了：每天在墓地祭拜，每天 describes a routine, so the habitual action takes the zero particle rather than 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: After “每天在墓地祭拜,” add a detail showing what Mengzi observes and copies, such as “他看到很多人来祭拜，就学着他们的样子磕头。”
- Expressiveness: At “害得她没有钱,” show Mengmu’s feeling with a short description, such as “孟母每天都很着急，晚上也睡不好。”
- Expressiveness: After “原来买不了房,” add Mengmu’s inner thought or speech, such as “孟母心想：‘这里的生活太难了，我得带孟子搬走。’”

### Encouragement
- You used the pattern 才五岁就 correctly to show that Mengzi was very young when he began the routine.
- Your use of 难怪……，原来…… creates a clear reveal of Mengmu’s reason for moving.

## Scene 2

> 这个城市老是很吵，不是人吵架，就是都叫，害得孟子一直不能睡觉。在那儿，孟母也找不到工作。孟子和妈妈要又一个次搬了家。在第三个城市，孟母找到工作，在商店做衣服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 又一个次搬了家：再搬一次家，After a modal verb, use the pattern modal verb + 再 + verb + number + measure word + object for a move that has not happened yet; the future action does not take 了. [teacher rule: zai_structure]
- 人吵架，就是都叫：有人吵架，就是有人大喊大叫，Use 有人 to introduce an indefinite person in each alternative event; 都 is an adverb and cannot by itself supply the subject. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 吵 -- “这个城市老是很吵”：喧闹，specifies that the city is full of many loud sounds and voices；嘈杂，emphasizes mixed, unpleasant noise.
- 做 -- “在商店做衣服”：缝制，specifies the action of sewing and making clothes.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 孟母找到工作：孟母找到了工作，Finding the job is a completed event in the story, so the verb needs 了 after the resultative complement 到. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Conjunction): 在那儿，孟母也找不到工作。孟子和妈妈要：在那儿，孟母也找不到工作，所以孟子和妈妈要，Add 所以 to show that the city’s noise and the unsuccessful job search cause the family to move again. [rule: coherence_connectives]
- Expressiveness: After describing the noisy city, add a concrete sound detail, such as “外面的人一直大喊大叫，马车也不停地走来走去。”
- Expressiveness: Show Mengzi’s feeling before the move, such as “孟子睡不着，觉得又累又烦。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of dialogue when Mengmu finds work, such as “孟母高兴地说：‘我终于找到工作了！’”

### Encouragement
You clearly show two convincing reasons for the family’s move: the noisy city and Mengmu’s difficulty finding work. The final sentence also moves the story forward effectively by showing that Mengmu finds a job in the third city.

## Scene 3

> 孟子在那个商店也找了到工作，可是不是做衣服的工作，而是喜欢的工作。孟子学习馆学了十五年，他的学习和他的旅行经验很重要，对他很多好处。他是大人的时候，他成为很有名的哲人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 孟子学习馆学了十五年：孟子在学习馆学了十五年，A location used as the setting for an action needs 在 before it in this sentence. [rule: none]
- 对他很多好处：对他有很多好处，This predicate needs 有: the pattern is 对 + person + 有 + 好处, meaning that something benefits that person. [vocab list]
- 他是大人的时候：他长大以后，Use a later-life time expression to show that the next event happened after he grew up. [rule: time_expression]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很有名 -- “他成为很有名的哲人”：声名远扬，shows that his reputation became widely known, making the description more vivid.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): the second 他：∅，After the temporal clause has already named the same person, use zero form rather than repeating the pronoun in the same sentence. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Temporality): 找了到：找到了，The completion marker goes after the resultative complement: V. + 到 + 了. [teacher rule: le_placement]
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为：成为了，This is a completed achievement in the past, so it needs 了 after the verb. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: At the job sentence, add Mengzi’s feeling about finding work, for example: “孟子终于找到了自己喜欢的工作，心里很高兴。”
- Expressiveness: At the fifteen-year study point, add a concrete study detail, for example: “十五年里，他每天认真地看书，常常忘了时间。”
- Expressiveness: After his achievement, add a narrator’s evaluative comment, for example: “难怪人们都说，他的成功离不开多年的学习。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show a long period of learning before Mengzi’s later achievement, which gives this scene a strong sense of progress. The contrast between clothing work and work he likes is also easy to understand.
