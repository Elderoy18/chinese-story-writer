## Scene 1

> 古代，孟子跟孟母住在墓地的旁边。可是孟母注意，住在墓地的旁边影响孟子。他常常跟朋友们祭拜玩，所以孟母决定他们会搬家。这次，他们住在城市里的商店的旁边，从早到晚很吵。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 孟母注意：孟母注意到，The verb needs the result complement 到 when introducing the fact that she noticed.
- 跟朋友们祭拜玩：跟朋友们一起学着别人祭拜，The original verb sequence does not express “playfully imitate worship” naturally; the correction uses 学着 to show imitation.
- 决定他们会搬家：决定搬家，决定 is followed directly by the action that is decided; repeating the people and adding 会 is unnecessary here.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 吵 -- “从早到晚很吵”：吵闹，describes ongoing loud activity in the area；嘈杂，more specifically describes many mixed, unpleasant sounds.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery setting, add a concrete sight that explains why the place affects him, such as “孟子看见有人哭着来祭拜，觉得很好玩。”
- Expressiveness: At the mother’s decision, show her worry through an inner thought or short speech, such as “孟母着急地想：‘不能再让孟子这样下去了。’”
- Expressiveness: In the city scene, add sounds from the shops to make “很吵” vivid, such as “商人从早到晚叫卖，街上到处都是说话声。”

### Encouragement
You clearly establish both settings—the cemetery and the city shops—and the use of 所以 gives the mother’s decision a clear cause-and-effect connection.

## Scene 2

> 城市也有点儿危险。害得孟母只好一直陪孟子。孟子也没有朋友，难怪他老是看起来不高兴。住在商店的旁边不是适合孟子，而是让他觉得很伤心。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不是适合孟子：不适合孟子，An adjectival predicate is negated directly with 不; 不是 cannot be placed before 适合 in this structure.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 陪 -- “害得孟母只好一直陪孟子”：照看，emphasizes that the mother must watch over him because the city feels unsafe.
- 不高兴 -- “难怪他老是看起来不高兴”：闷闷不乐，describes a visibly low and unhappy mood more vividly.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 而是让他觉得很伤心：因为这会让他觉得很伤心，The second clause gives the reason that living there is unsuitable, rather than correcting a mistaken idea; use a cause connector instead of the paired contrast structure.
- Expressiveness: After the opening sentence, add one concrete detail showing why the city feels unsafe, such as: “晚上街上很乱，孟母不敢让孟子一个人出去。”
- Expressiveness: When describing Mengzi’s unhappiness, add an internal thought or action, such as: “他看着外面热闹的商店，心里却觉得很孤单。”
- Expressiveness: You could give Mengmu a short line of speech to show her concern, such as: “这儿不太适合孩子长大，我们得再找一个安静的地方。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect the unsafe environment with Mengmu needing to stay with Mengzi, and the use of 难怪 effectively explains his unhappy mood.

## Scene 3

> 所以，孟母和孟子搬到学校的旁边住。才五岁孟子开始学哲学。在学校，孟子不是看书，就是练习写字。将来，他成为中国的一个非常有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 才五岁孟子开始学哲学：孟子才五岁就开始学哲学，The pattern with a small age uses 才 + number + 就 + verb to show that the action began unusually early.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬到学校的旁边住：搬到了学校的旁边住，The move is a completed event in the narrative, so 了 follows the result complement 到.
- Coherence (Temporality): 将来：后来，This is a later event in a past narrative, so 后来, rather than future-time 将来, marks the sequence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为：成为了，Becoming a philosopher is a completed change of state, so it needs 了.
- Expressiveness: After the school move, add Meng母’s pleased reaction to show why this place suits him: “孟母看到孟子认真学习，心里很高兴。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech from Meng母 after seeing him read and practise writing: “这儿的环境真适合你学习！”
- Expressiveness: Add one detail about Meng子’s focused learning: “孟子每天安静地坐在桌子前看书。”

### Encouragement
You used the contrast pattern “不是……就是……” correctly to show Meng子’s daily study activities. The final sentence also clearly communicates his later success as a philosopher.
