## Scene 1

> 这个男孩子叫孟子，孟子是古代的人。孟子的妈妈觉得住在哪儿对童年很重要，所以他们常常搬了家。她望子成龙，所以孟子的妈妈安排他的生活。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- Nothing to flag.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 生活 -- “孟子的妈妈安排他的生活”：日常生活，specifies that she manages his everyday routines；成长，emphasizes the child’s development, though it would fit best if the sentence focuses on her hopes for him.
- 安排 -- “孟子的妈妈安排他的生活”：规划，emphasizes that she makes deliberate, longer-term plans for him.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 这个男孩子叫孟子，孟子是古代的人：在古代，有一个叫孟子的男孩子。A first-mentioned character needs an unambiguous full introduction; “这个” normally points to someone already introduced, while this is the first mention.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们常常搬了家：他们常常搬家，“常常” describes a routine, so use the zero particle rather than completed-action 了.
- Coherence (Reference): 她望子成龙，所以孟子的妈妈安排他的生活：她望子成龙，所以安排他的生活。The mother was just mentioned as “她,” so use zero form rather than repeating her full noun phrase in the same connected sentence.
- Expressiveness: After the mother’s wish, add a brief inner thought to show why she pays attention to their home: “她心想：‘住的地方会影响孟子的成长。’”
- Expressiveness: Make the frequent moves more concrete with one small detail: “每次搬家，他们都带着简单的行李，去新的地方住。”

### Encouragement
You clearly establish the important relationship between Mengzi and his mother, and “望子成龙” is a strong, fitting expression for her hopes for her son. The use of “所以” also makes the mother’s motivation easy to understand.

## Scene 2

> 因为他们常常搬了家，中国人说，孟母三迁。孟子没有爸爸，所以，他的妈妈更重要。从这儿到那儿，妈妈教孟子很多技能。先她让孟子跟朋友玩儿，然后教他怎么买又便宜又好质量的东西。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 先她让孟子跟朋友玩儿：她先让孟子跟朋友玩儿，A temporal adverb such as “先” normally follows the subject and comes before the predicate.
- 又便宜又好质量的东西：又便宜又质量好的东西，The two parts joined by “又……又……” should have parallel forms; “质量好” is the predicate phrase used to describe an item’s quality.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 从这儿到那儿 -- “从这儿到那儿，妈妈教孟子很多技能”：辗转各地，specifies that the family moved repeatedly among different places.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 妈妈教孟子很多技能：她教孟子很多技能，“妈妈” was mentioned in the immediately preceding sentence, and no other female character competes as the referent, so a pronoun is more natural for a close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 常常搬了家：常常搬家，“常常” describes a habitual action, so it takes the zero aspect particle rather than completed-action “了”.
- Expressiveness: At the point where the mother teaches shopping skills, add a small action to show her patience, such as “妈妈耐心地指着两样东西，让孟子比较。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech after the shopping lesson to make the lesson more vivid, such as “妈妈说：‘买东西不能只看价钱，还要看质量。’”

### Encouragement
You used both cause-and-effect language and sequence words to organize the scene. The detail about learning to choose inexpensive, good-quality items also gives the mother’s teaching a clear, practical focus.
