## Scene 1

> 有一天孟子去了墓地玩儿，但是他妈妈觉得这是不合适的地方让孩子玩，所以她决定了他们会搬家找更好的地方。她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福，而且住在这儿教育不太好。因为她望子成龙，所以她会一直搬家才能找到质量好的教育。然后他们搬到了离城市更近的地方。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不合适的地方让孩子玩：不适合孩子玩的地方，When stating who an activity or place suits, use “适合 + person + verb phrase”; “合适” does not take the person directly in this structure. [vocab list]
- 他们会：删除，“决定” should be followed directly by the action phrase the subject decides to do, so the repeated future clause is unnecessary. [teacher rule: jueding_vp]
- 她恐怕：她担心，“恐怕” means “I am afraid that” and cannot take another subject before it; use “担心” to express that she is afraid about her son’s future. [teacher rule: kongpa]
- 住在这儿教育：这儿的教育，“住在这儿” is an action phrase, while the sentence needs a noun phrase meaning the education in this place. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 更好的 -- “找更好的地方”：更适合孩子成长的，specifies that the new place is better because it supports the child’s development.
- 一直 -- “她会一直搬家”：一次又一次地，makes clear that the family moves repeatedly rather than continuously.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 决定了：决定，“决定” is a mental-state verb, so it normally takes the zero particle rather than “了.” [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: After “孟子去了墓地玩儿,” add a concrete detail about what he sees there, such as “他看见很多人穿着黑衣服，在墓碑前哭。”
- Expressiveness: At “她担心儿子的童年不会幸福,” show the mother’s inner thought or speech, such as “孟母心想：‘这儿不是孩子长大的好地方。’”
- Expressiveness: When the family moves, add a feeling about the new place, such as “孟子看到城里有很多商店，觉得很新鲜。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the mother’s reason for moving and use “因为……所以……” well to connect her hope for her son with her actions.
- The final sentence gives the scene a clear next step by showing where the family moves.

## Scene 2

> 他们住才一月就又搬家。古代的时候，一般人不喜欢卖东西的人。但是孟子老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿，害得妈妈着急得很，因为她恐怕儿子会成为跟他们很像。她告诉了孟子：他们不是朋友，而是不好的人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 住才一月：才住了一个月，In this pattern, 才 comes before the duration; the duration needs the measure word, and the completed stay takes 了. [rule: cai_jiu_construction]
- 古代的时候：在古代，The original repeats the idea of “time”: 古代 is already a time expression, so 的时候 is redundant here. [vocab list]
- 老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿：老是跟这些卖东西的人玩儿，Do not use two frequency adverbs in the same position; 老是 already expresses repeated action. [rule: none]
- 她恐怕：她担心，恐怕 means “I am afraid that” and cannot follow another subject in this way; use 担心 when the mother is afraid about her son’s future. [teacher rule: kongpa]
- 成为跟他们很像：变得跟他们很像，成为 normally introduces an identity or role, while 变得 correctly describes changing into a similar state. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 卖东西的人 -- “这些卖东西的人”：商人，identifies them more precisely as people whose work is selling goods.
- 着急 -- “害得妈妈着急得很”：十分焦虑，emphasizes sustained worry about the child’s future behavior.
- 不好 -- “不好的人”：品行不好，specifies that their character or behavior is bad.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 又搬家：又搬家了，The move is a completed event in the narrative, so the final action needs 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 她告诉了孟子：她告诉孟子，A speaking verb that introduces quoted speech uses the zero particle rather than 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: At the moment when Mengzi plays with the sellers, add a concrete action to show what he copies, for example: “孟子学着商人收钱，还学着讨价还价。”
- Expressiveness: When the mother becomes worried, add her inner thought to make her feeling clearer, for example: “孟母心想：‘如果他一直这样玩儿，会不会影响学习？’”
- Expressiveness: The mother’s warning can sound more natural and personal if it addresses him directly, for example: “他们不是你的朋友，你不能每天都跟着他们玩儿。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show a cause-and-effect relationship with 害得妈妈着急得很, which helps the reader understand why the mother reacts. You also use the contrast pattern 不是……而是…… in a meaningful way to show the mother’s opinion.

## Scene 3

> 你不是当数学家，而是当哲学家，不能当卖东西的人。所以他们又搬家。在他们第三个家，孟子学了怎么祭拜。妈妈感觉这个地方适合他们，告诉了孟子：我们找到了最后的家。在这儿妈妈让儿子从早到晚学习，他难怪成为有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- ：我们找到了最后的家：：“我们找到了最后的家。”，Direct speech should be enclosed in quotation marks, with the full stop placed inside the closing quotation mark. [rule: none]
- 他难怪：难怪他，Place 难怪 before the subject to introduce the conclusion that follows from the preceding reason. [teacher rule: nanguai_yuanlai]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 卖东西的人 -- “不能当卖东西的人”：商人，identifies more precisely a person whose job is buying and selling goods.
- 地方 -- “妈妈感觉这个地方适合他们”：环境，focuses more precisely on the surroundings and atmosphere that make the home suitable.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 又搬家：又搬家了，This is a completed move in the story, so the final verb needs 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉了孟子：告诉孟子，A speaking verb that introduces direct speech takes the zero particle, not 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为有名的哲学家：成为了有名的哲学家，Becoming a famous philosopher is presented as a completed change of state, so use 了 after the verb. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: Around “在他们第三个家”, add a detail about the new setting: “他们的新家在学校旁边，周围很安静。”
- Expressiveness: After the mother decides the place is suitable, add her feeling and a warmer line of speech: “妈妈很高兴，说：‘这儿的环境很适合你学习。’”
- Expressiveness: Make Mengzi’s study routine more vivid after “从早到晚学习”: “孟子每天认真看书，遇到不懂的问题就问妈妈。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect the mother’s decision, the family’s move, and Mengzi’s later success. The contrast between two possible careers in the opening dialogue also gives the scene a clear focus.
