## Scene 1

> 古代时候，人不知道他们能吃什么植物，常常一吃就死。但是不知道为什么，没有办法。所以有一天，神农出去试一试很多种植物，告诉人什么植物是安全什么是危险。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代时候：在古代，use this time expression directly; the original also lacks the required particle in the structure with 时候.
- 人：人们，use 人们 for the concrete group of people in this situation; 人 is a generic noun.
- 告诉人：告诉人们，use 人们 when referring to the group receiving the information.
- 什么植物是安全什么是危险：哪些植物是安全的，哪些植物是危险的，parallel categories need a clear repeated noun phrase, and the adjective predicates after 是 need 的.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 没有办法 -- “但是不知道为什么，没有办法”：束手无策，emphasizes that the people do not know how to solve the problem and feel helpless.
- 常常 -- “常常一吃就死”：屡屡，highlights that this tragic result happens repeatedly.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：有一个叫神农的人，when a character first appears, introduce the name with a full noun phrase so the reader knows who the character is.
- Expressiveness: After “常常一吃就死,” add a physical detail about the consequences, such as “人们吃了有毒的植物以后，肚子疼得很厉害。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where Shennong begins his work, add his determination or thoughts, such as “神农想：我一定要找出能吃的植物。”
- Expressiveness: Describe the plants Shennong examines to make the scene more vivid, such as “他看到山上有很多颜色不一样的花和草。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish the main problem first and then move to Shennong’s action, which gives the scene a logical direction.
- The pattern “一吃就死” effectively shows how immediate and serious the danger is.

## Scene 2

> 发现有的植物对身体非常好，特别是茶，让肚子感觉又暖和又清楚。他告诉朋友：“你吃哪种植物，这就要看你的口味。比方说，你爱吃甜的，可能喜欢糖。”他最喜欢吃花，除了夹竹桃以外，什么都花想吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 让肚子感觉又暖和又清楚：让人觉得肚子又暖和又舒服，A body part cannot normally be the experiencer of a feeling, and 清楚 describes clear understanding rather than a physical stomach sensation; 舒服 fits the intended bodily feeling.
- 什么都花想吃：什么花都想吃，In the “wh-word + 都” pattern, the noun must come before 都: 什么花都 means “every kind of flower.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 非常好 -- “有的植物对身体非常好”：非常有益，specifies that the plants are beneficial to health rather than merely “good” in a general way
- 甜的 -- “你爱吃甜的”：甜食，specifies sweet foods rather than using a broad descriptive phrase

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the discovery about tea, add a concrete detail about its taste or smell, such as “把茶放在水里以后，闻起来很香。”
- Expressiveness: Show the character’s reaction to the discovery after the first sentence, such as “神农高兴地想：原来茶对身体这么好！”
- Expressiveness: Add a reason for the character’s preference for flowers in the final sentence, such as “他觉得花又漂亮又有趣。”

### Encouragement
The scene clearly continues with the established character through the natural use of 他, and the quoted speech gives the narrative a more lively voice. The contrast introduced by 除了夹竹桃以外 also clearly shows that one plant is an exception.

## Scene 3

> 他发现这个口味，一百岁时候，不小心吃了毒的花。一吃就开始死。但是生病时候告诉人什么植物是药，什么植物是毒。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一百岁时候：一百岁的时候，Multi-syllable time expressions before “时候” require the particle “的.”
- 毒的花：有毒的花，Use “有毒的” to describe a poisonous plant; “毒的植物” is not the standard adjective form.
- 开始死：开始死去，“死” is a punctual event and does not naturally follow “开始” here; “死去” can describe the process of dying.
- 生病时候：生病的时候，Multi-syllable verb phrases before “时候” require “的.”
- 人：人们，This refers to a concrete group of people whom he tells, so use “人们” rather than the generic noun “人.”
- 什么植物是毒：什么植物有毒，Use “有毒” to state that a plant is poisonous; “是毒” is not the correct expression here.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不小心 -- “不小心吃了毒的花”：误食了，specifies that the accidental action was eating something unsuitable or dangerous.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 这个口味：一种花的味道，“这个” needs a clear earlier referent, but no particular taste has been introduced; use a full noun phrase for this new object.
- Coherence (Temporality): 发现：发现了，This is a completed discovery in a past narrative, so add “了” after the verb.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉人们：告诉了人们，This tells a completed action in the story, and this indirect-reporting use of “告诉” needs “了.”
- Expressiveness: After the poisonous flower appears, add a visual detail: “那朵花又黄又大，像星星一样。”
- Expressiveness: Show the physical result of eating it: “他捂着肚子，疼得直冒汗。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short warning when he tells people: “这种花有毒，不能吃。”

### Encouragement
- You used the pattern “一……就……” effectively to show a quick result after eating the flower.
- Your scene also clearly contrasts plants that are medicine with plants that are dangerous, which fits the story’s main idea.
