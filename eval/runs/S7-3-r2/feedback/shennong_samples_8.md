## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个人叫神农。一天，神农正在路上走，一看很多人正在吃一些植物。一些人一吃植物就生病了，神农想帮别人。所以想知道哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，this expression repeats the time meaning because both parts refer to a time period.
- 一看：一看到，when the meaning is “see,” use the resultative verb rather than the verb meaning “look.”
- 别人：人们，this refers to the group of people already mentioned, not to “other people” contrasted with a speaker.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 想知道 -- “所以想知道哪些植物能吃”：弄清楚，emphasizes finding a clear answer about which plants are edible and which are not.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一天，神农正在路上走：一天，他正在路上走，after a recent mention of the same character with no competing character, use a pronoun.
- Coherence (Reference): 所以想知道：所以他想知道，after the previous sentence ends, use a pronoun to make the continuing subject clear.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 很多人正在吃一些植物。一些人一吃植物就生病了：很多人正在吃一些植物，就发现一些人一吃植物就生病了，add “就” to complete the “一…就…” pattern: seeing the people leads directly to discovering that some become ill.
- Expressiveness: After the people become ill, add a physical detail to show the consequence, such as “有的人捂着肚子，疼得很厉害。”
- Expressiveness: At “神农想帮人们,” add his feeling or inner thought, such as “神农很担心，心想：我一定要弄清楚！”

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduce the main character and set up the important question of which plants can and cannot be eaten.
- The illness sentence uses the “一…就…” idea effectively to show a quick cause-and-effect relationship.

## Scene 2

> 然后，神农开始尝很多植物，比方说他吃过生姜、芥兰和菠菜。一些植物吃真好，特别是黄瓜。但是，一些植物毒得很，特别是夹竹桃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一些植物吃真好：一些植物很好吃，A noun cannot directly be followed by the verb and adjective pattern used here; use the adjective predicate to describe the plants’ taste.
- 毒得很：有毒，毒 cannot be intensified with 得很; use 有毒 to describe poisonous plants.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 然后，神农开始尝很多植物：然后，他开始尝很多植物，神农 was the active character at the end of the previous scene, so the close re-occurrence rule calls for a pronoun rather than repeating the name.
- Expressiveness: After mentioning the poisonous plant, add a physical reaction to show the danger, such as: “神农吃了夹竹桃以后，肚子疼了起来。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought to show Shen Nong’s purpose and concern, such as: “他想：我一定要找出哪些植物能吃。”
- Expressiveness: Show his careful work with one accompanying action, such as: “他一边尝植物，一边记下它们的用处。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly contrast plants that are good to eat with plants that are dangerous, and the connector 但是 fits that contrast well.
- Your examples of specific plants, such as 生姜、芥兰、菠菜、黄瓜, make the scene more concrete.

## Scene 3

> 一个人对神农说，麻烦我们可以吃什么植物？神农说，这就要看你想不想生病了。以后，神农告诉别人一些植物能吃，一些植物不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 麻烦我们可以吃什么植物？：请问，我们可以吃什么植物？，麻烦 is used when asking someone to do something, usually in a pattern such as 麻烦你……; it cannot introduce a standalone information question.
- 以后：后来，sentence-initial 以后 cannot mean “afterwards” in this context; 后来 introduces a later event in the narration.
- 别人：人们，别人 means other people in contrast with a specified person or group; here Shen Nong is speaking generally to the public.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 告诉 -- “神农告诉别人一些植物能吃”：说明，emphasizes explaining the information clearly rather than only passing it on.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the person’s question, add a feeling to show why the question matters: “那个人担心地问：‘请问，我们可以吃什么植物？’”
- Expressiveness: Shen Nong could give one concrete example in his reply: “神农说：‘像伞一样的灵芝是药，可以吃。’”

### Encouragement
You used dialogue effectively to make this scene feel active. You also used an A-not-A question form well in Shen Nong’s reply.
