## Scene 1

> 有一天孟子去了墓地玩儿，但是他妈妈觉得这是不合适的地方让孩子玩，所以她决定了他们会搬家找更好的地方。她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福，而且住在这儿教育不太好。因为她望子成龙，所以她会一直搬家才能找到质量好的教育。然后他们搬到了离城市更近的地方。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福：她担心儿子的童年不会幸福，恐怕 means “I am afraid that” and cannot follow another subject in this way; 担心 expresses that she is worried about her son’s future.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不合适 -- “这是不合适的地方让孩子玩”：不适合孩子玩的，specifies that the place is unsuitable specifically for a child’s play；不利于孩子成长的，makes the concern about the child’s development more precise.
- 更好 -- “找更好的地方”：更适合孩子成长的，explains exactly in what way the new place would be better.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他们会搬家找更好的地方：搬家，找更好的地方，after 决定, the same subject should continue with a zero form and a direct verb phrase rather than introducing 他们 and 会.
- Coherence (Temporality): 决定了：决定，决定 is a mental-state verb, so it normally takes the zero particle rather than 了 when introducing a decision.
- Expressiveness: At the mother’s worry, add her facial expression and inner thought, for example: 孟母皱着眉头，心想：“不能让孟子在这儿玩下去。”
- Expressiveness: After the move closer to the city, describe what the new area is like, for example: 城市里从早到晚都很热闹，商人卖着各种各样的东西。
- Expressiveness: Show Mengzi’s reaction to the new place with a small action, for example: 孟子好奇地看着来来往往的人群。

### Encouragement
You clearly show the mother’s reason for moving by using a cause-and-effect connection. The completed-action markers in 去了 and 搬到了 are also used well, and 望子成龙 is a strong, fitting expression for the mother’s hope.

## Scene 2

> 他们住才一月就又搬家。古代的时候，一般人不喜欢卖东西的人。但是孟子老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿，害得妈妈着急得很，因为她恐怕儿子会成为跟他们很像。她告诉了孟子：他们不是朋友，而是不好的人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，This expression is redundant because both parts express time; use the shorter time expression.
- 老是跟这些卖东西的人常常：老是跟这些卖东西的人，Do not use two frequency adverbs together when both mean that an action happens repeatedly.
- 她恐怕儿子会：她担心儿子会，恐怕 means “I am afraid that...” and cannot take another subject before it in this structure.
- 成为跟他们很像：变得跟他们很像，成为 needs a noun phrase describing a new role or identity; use 变得 before an adjective phrase describing a change of state.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们住才一月就又搬家。：他们才住了一个月，就又搬家了。Put 才 before the verb, and use 了 to show that the one-month stay and the later move were completed events.
- Coherence (Temporality): 她告诉了孟子：她告诉孟子：A speaking verb that introduces quoted speech takes the zero particle, not 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他们不是朋友，而是不好的人。：他们不是好人，你不能老跟他们玩儿。Use 不是……而是…… when correcting a conventional or mistaken idea; here a direct warning expresses the mother’s meaning more naturally.
- Expressiveness: At the mention of the sellers, add a market detail so the setting feels active, for example: “街上有很多商人吆喝着卖东西，热闹极了。”
- Expressiveness: After the mother becomes worried, add her inner thought to show why she wants to move, for example: “孟母心想：‘这样下去，孩子会不会只学着买卖东西？’”
- Expressiveness: Make the mother’s warning more emotional with a fuller line of dialogue, for example: “孟母说：‘你不能老跟他们玩儿，要好好学习。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the mother’s worry and connect it to her son’s behaviour with a cause-and-effect idea. The dialogue at the end is also a strong way to show what the mother thinks.

## Scene 3

> 你不是当数学家，而是当哲学家，不能当卖东西的人。所以他们又搬家。在他们第三个家，孟子学了怎么祭拜。妈妈感觉这个地方适合他们，告诉了孟子：我们找到了最后的家。在这儿妈妈让儿子从早到晚学习，他难怪成为有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在他们第三个家：在他们的第三个家里，A pronoun used as a possessive modifier normally takes 的 here, and 里 clearly marks the home as the location.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 卖东西的人 -- “不能当卖东西的人”：商人，names the occupation more precisely and concisely in this context.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 又搬家：又搬家了，This move is completed before the following sentence introduces the third home, so the completed action needs 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉了孟子：告诉孟子，A speaking verb that introduces quoted speech takes the zero particle, not 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为：成为了，Becoming a philosopher is presented as a completed result in the narrative, so use 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): ，他难怪：，所以他后来，Use 所以 to connect the mother’s requirement that her son study with the resulting achievement. 难怪 introduces a conclusion and should not be placed after the subject as the connector here.
- Expressiveness: At the final study sentence, add a small action such as “孟子每天坐在桌前认真看书。” This makes his effort easier for the reader to picture.
- Expressiveness: After the mother finds the suitable home, add her happy thought or speech, such as “这儿的环境很适合孩子。” This shows why she feels relieved.

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the sequence of moving, settling into a new home, and studying. You also used direct speech to give the mother a voice in the scene.
