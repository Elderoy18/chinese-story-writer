## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男人觉得植物很有意思。他叫神农。有的植物吃了以后他的肚子疼死了，可是有的吃了以后很舒服。他找很多植物。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 already names a time period, so adding 的时候 is redundant.
- 他找很多植物：他找到了很多植物，找 means “to look for,” while 找到 means “to find”; the completed discovery in this narrative needs 找到了.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 有意思 -- “一个男人觉得植物很有意思”：令人好奇，specifies that the plants make him want to learn more about them.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一个男人觉得植物很有意思。他叫神农：有一个叫神农的男人觉得植物很有意思，Introduce a character with the name and identity together at the first mention, rather than using an unnamed noun first and naming him only in the next sentence.
- Expressiveness: After “他的肚子疼死了,” add a concrete physical detail, such as “他疼得躺在地上，额头上都是汗。”
- Expressiveness: Show Shen Nong’s curiosity with a short thought or question after the first sentence, such as “他想：这些植物到底能不能吃呢？”

### Encouragement
- You used the contrast pattern “有的……可是有的……” effectively to show that different plants have different effects. The phrase “肚子疼死了” is also vivid and clearly shows Shen Nong’s strong discomfort.

## Scene 2

> 凉山很大的山，这也找植物。一天，他找到黄色的花。他听说你跟黄色的花做茶很有用。比方说，你的头发疼死，这冰茶是药。他一喝茶就很不舒服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 凉山很大的山：凉山是一座很大的山，A noun description needs the linking verb and an appropriate measure word before the noun phrase.
- 这也找植物：他在这里找植物，Use 在 + location for an action happening in a place; 也 needs a preceding parallel idea, which is not present here.
- 你跟黄色的花做茶：用黄色的花做的茶，跟 links people or accompanying participants; 用 introduces the material used. The phrase 做的 modifies 茶.
- 你的头发疼死：你的头疼得要命，头发 cannot feel pain; 头疼 expresses a headache, and 疼得要命 uses the verb + 得 + degree-complement structure.
- 这冰茶：这杯冰茶，A demonstrative followed by a countable noun needs a suitable measure word.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不舒服 -- “他一喝茶就很不舒服”：难受，gives a more vivid description of physical discomfort in this situation.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 找到：找到了，This is a completed event in the story, so the verb needs 了 to mark completion.
- Expressiveness: After the yellow flowers appear, add a visual detail to make the setting easier to imagine, such as “黄色的花像星星一样，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: At the moment he feels ill, add his physical reaction or a short thought, such as “他的肚子疼了起来，心里很害怕。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech after he drinks the tea to show his reaction, such as “他说：‘我的肚子好疼！’”

### Encouragement
You used a clear event sequence: finding flowers, hearing about tea, and then drinking it. Your use of the pattern “一……就……” also effectively shows that the discomfort happened immediately after drinking the tea.

## Scene 3

> 糟糕，这黄色的花是夹竹桃，特别是毒。正好在这儿，灵芝药的夹竹桃他吃完了，也写一个课文的植物。谢谢神农。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 这黄色的花：这朵黄色的花，A demonstrative referring to one countable flower needs an appropriate measure word before the noun.
- 特别是毒：特别有毒，Use 有毒 to describe a poisonous plant; 毒 alone cannot serve as the adjective here.
- 灵芝药的夹竹桃他吃完了：他吃了灵芝这种药，The original modifier chain does not show a grammatical relationship between 灵芝 and 夹竹桃. Use 灵芝这种药 to identify what he ate.
- 写一个课文的植物：记下这些植物，When recording information about plants, use 记下 or 写下 rather than 写 directly with the plants as its object.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 也写一个课文的植物：也把这些植物记下来了，Both eating and recording are completed past-story actions here, so the final action needs 了.
- Expressiveness: After the opening exclamation, add a physical reaction to show why the discovery is alarming, such as “神农吓了一跳，肚子又疼了起来。”
- Expressiveness: When he takes the medicine, add a brief change in his feelings, such as “吃了灵芝以后，他觉得舒服多了。”
- Expressiveness: Turn the final thanks into quoted speech to make the ending sound more like a scene, such as “神农说：‘谢谢你救了我！’”

### Encouragement
You clearly identify the yellow flower as 夹竹桃 and use 糟糕 to give the opening an immediate feeling of danger. You also connect the later action with 也, showing that Shennong does more than one thing.
