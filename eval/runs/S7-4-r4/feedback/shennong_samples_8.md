## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个人叫神农。一天，神农正在路上走，一看很多人正在吃一些植物。一些人一吃植物就生病了，神农想帮别人。所以想知道哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，This time expression is redundant because 古代 already names a time period.
- 别人：人们，别人 distinguishes other people from a particular person or group already under discussion; here the meaning is people in general.
- 一看很多人正在吃一些植物：一看到很多人正在吃一些植物，就发现一些人一吃植物就生病了，看到 expresses the result of looking—actually seeing—and the pattern 一V requires 就 in the following result clause.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the people become ill, add Shen Nong’s concern to show his motivation more vividly: “神农很担心，心想：我一定要找到能吃的植物。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where Shen Nong sees the plants, add one simple visual detail: “路边有很多绿色的植物，还有黄色的小花。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought after the final sentence to show his determination: “神农想：我不能让人们再生病了。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduce Shen Nong at the beginning and use ongoing-action markers well in “正在路上走” and “正在吃”. 
- The sentence “一些人一吃植物就生病了” effectively shows the danger and gives a clear reason for Shen Nong’s next action.

## Scene 2

> 然后，神农开始尝很多植物，比方说他吃过生姜、芥兰和菠菜。一些植物吃真好，特别是黄瓜。但是，一些植物毒得很，特别是夹竹桃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一些植物吃真好：一些植物很好吃，Use the adjective phrase after the subject to describe that the plants taste good; “吃真好” is not a natural predicate here.
- 一些植物毒得很：一些植物有毒，The word “毒” cannot be intensified with “得很”; use “有毒” to describe poisonous plants.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After “特别是黄瓜”, add a sensory detail to show why it is good to eat, such as “黄瓜又脆又清爽。”
- Expressiveness: At “特别是夹竹桃”, describe the plant’s appearance before revealing its danger, such as “夹竹桃开着黄色的、像星星一样的花。”

### Encouragement
You used “然后” effectively to continue from the earlier scene, and your contrast between edible plants and poisonous plants is clear. The examples of ginger, kale, spinach, cucumber, and oleander also make the scene concrete.

## Scene 3

> 一个人对神农说，麻烦我们可以吃什么植物？神农说，这就要看你想不想生病了。以后，神农告诉别人一些植物能吃，一些植物不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 麻烦我们可以吃什么植物？：请问，我们可以吃什么植物？，“麻烦” does not introduce a question in this way; “请问” is the polite question-opening expression.
- 以后：后来，“后来” introduces an event that happened afterwards, while sentence-initial “以后” normally follows a stated time or event.
- 别人：人们，“别人” means people other than a specified speaker or group; here the general group being told is “人们”.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 说 -- “一个人对神农说”：问，more precisely shows that the person is asking a question rather than simply speaking.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉：告诉了，This is a completed narrative action that happens later, so it needs 了 to mark completion.
- Expressiveness: At the reply from 神农, add a specific contrast between a helpful and a harmful plant: “像伞一样的灵芝是药，可以吃；夹竹桃有毒，不能吃。”
- Expressiveness: Make 神农’s reply sound patient and helpful by adding an adverb and quotation marks: “神农耐心地说：‘这就要看是哪种植物了。’”

### Encouragement
You used the A-not-A form “想不想” correctly in the reply. You also clearly contrasted plants that can be eaten with plants that cannot, which makes the scene easy to follow.
