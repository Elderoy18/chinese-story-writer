## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个男孩名字叫孟子。孟子和孟子的妈妈两个人住在很穷的农村。他们住的地方旁边是个墓地，墓地老是有很多人祭拜。因为人们在祭拜，他们一直在哭。孟子和孟子的妈妈住在这里才一个星期就觉得受不了了，而且害得孟子的学习。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，this expression redundantly combines two time expressions; use the shorter time phrase.
- 害得孟子的学习：害得孟子不能好好学习，害得 must be followed by the person affected and a harmful result clause, not only a noun phrase.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子的妈妈两个人：孟子和他的妈妈两个人，after naming 孟子, use a pronoun for his mother’s possessor within the same sentence rather than repeating the name.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子的妈妈住在这里：孟子和他的妈妈住在这里，use a pronoun for the already established possessor instead of repeating the name in the same sentence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 住在这里才一个星期：才在这里住了一个星期，this is a completed period of living there, so the action verb needs 了.
- Expressiveness: At the description of the cemetery, add a sensory detail to make the setting feel more real, such as “墓地里很安静，只能听见人们伤心的哭声。”
- Expressiveness: When孟子 cannot tolerate the place, add his feeling or a short thought, such as “孟子觉得这里又冷又可怕。”
- Expressiveness: You could show the mother’s concern with a line of speech after the final sentence, such as “孟子的妈妈说：‘这里不适合你学习。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly establish the cemetery setting and connect the mourners’ worship with their crying. The phrase “才一个星期就觉得受不了了” also effectively shows that the family quickly found the environment difficult.

## Scene 2

> 孟子的妈妈觉得孟子取得良好的教育是很重要的，所以孟子的妈妈认为这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好，就打算跟孟子搬家。孟子的妈妈带孟子不是搬到城市，而是搬到了孟子老师的家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 取得良好的教育：接受良好的教育，取得 is usually used for achievements or results, while 接受教育 describes receiving an education.
- 跟：带着，跟孟子搬家 suggests that the mother moves together with Mengzi as equals; 带着孟子搬家 shows that she takes her child with her.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 地方 -- “这样的地方”：环境，more specifically refers to the surroundings and atmosphere that affect a child’s education.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子的妈妈认为：她认为，after the mother has just been named and no other woman competes as the referent, use a pronoun.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子搬家：他搬家，孟子 has just been mentioned and is the only possible child referent, so a pronoun is more natural.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子的妈妈带孟子：她带他，both characters were named in the immediately previous sentence, so pronouns make the repeated reference smoother.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 孟子的妈妈带孟子不是搬到城市，而是搬到了孟子老师的家：孟子的妈妈带孟子搬到了孟子的老师家，the 不是……而是…… pattern is used to correct a conventional or mistaken idea; here the sentence simply states the destination, so the contrast pair is unnecessary.
- Expressiveness: At “这样的地方”, add one concrete detail about the surroundings and why they affect study, such as “墓地旁边每天都有人哭，环境很吵。”
- Expressiveness: Show the mother’s private worry before her decision, such as “孟母心想：‘这里会影响孟子的学习。’”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech when she decides to move, such as “她说：‘我们得找一个更安静的地方。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly explain the mother’s reason for moving and use 因为……所以…… effectively to connect her concern with her decision.

## Scene 3

> 孟子跟他的老师从早到晚学习，难怪他的成绩很快就进步了很多。孟子学习的时候，孟子的妈妈不是待在家里就是编织衣服赚钱。以后孟子成为了中国有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 以后：后来，Sentence-initial “以后” cannot independently mean “afterwards” in a narrative; “后来” introduces a later event.

### Vocabulary Suggestions

### Coherence & Expressiveness

### Encouragement
