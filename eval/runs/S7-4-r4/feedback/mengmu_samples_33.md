## Scene 1

> 一天，很久很久以前，孟子和他妈妈住在中国。孟子妈妈的工作是祭拜，孟子不高兴，因为他说我们老是搬家，为什么？他妈妈说，从早到晚，我一直祭拜，很多墓地得我祭拜。所以我们得去很多不同的地方。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很多墓地得我祭拜：我得去很多墓地祭拜，The modal verb requires the person who must act as its subject; the place phrase belongs with the action of going there.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不高兴 -- “孟子不高兴”：不满，specifies that he is dissatisfied with the repeated moves rather than generally unhappy.
- 地方 -- “我们得去很多不同的地方”：许多不同的墓地，identifies the particular places being discussed and connects clearly to the earlier mention of worshipping at graves.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和他妈妈住在中国：有一个小男孩叫孟子，他和妈妈住在中国，The first mention should introduce the character with both a name and a brief identity; after that, the pronoun can refer clearly to him.
- Expressiveness: At the question about moving, show Mengzi’s feeling through an action, such as “孟子皱着眉头问：‘我们为什么老是搬家？’”
- Expressiveness: In the mother’s reply, add a tired feeling to make her work more vivid, such as “妈妈叹了口气，说：‘从早到晚，我一直祭拜。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the reason for the family’s repeated moves, and the dialogue helps the reader understand both Mengzi’s question and his mother’s situation.

## Scene 2

> 孟子想，难怪我们常常搬家，原来我妈妈很忙。可是常常搬家害得孟子很难找到朋友。每次搬家孟子也觉得新的地方不是太热就是太冷，不是太大就是太小。在哪的地方，他都不高兴。孟子想帮助妈妈祭拜，可是他六岁，所以不能祭拜。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在哪的地方：不管在哪个地方，The place word needs the classifier in this structure, and adding 不管 clearly expresses “no matter where.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 忙 -- “原来我妈妈很忙”：忙着祭拜，specifies what makes the mother busy in this scene；工作繁忙，emphasizes that her busyness comes from her work.
- 新的 -- “孟子也觉得新的地方”：陌生的，emphasizes that each place feels unfamiliar to him after moving.
- 不高兴 -- “他都不高兴”：闷闷不乐，shows a continuing, outwardly visible unhappy mood.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After “很难找到朋友,” add a small action or feeling to show his loneliness, such as “他看着别的孩子一起玩，心里很孤单。”
- Expressiveness: At “不是太热就是太冷,” add sensory details to make the different places vivid, such as “有的地方晒得他满头大汗，有的地方冷得他直发抖。”
- Expressiveness: After “不能祭拜,” add a short line of speech to show his wish to help, such as “孟子低下头说：‘我也想帮妈妈。’”

### Encouragement
- You used the paired pattern “难怪……原来……” effectively to show Mengzi’s realization.
- Your two “不是……就是……” patterns clearly describe why the new places make him unhappy.

## Scene 3

> 四年后，孟子开始帮妈妈祭拜。他画画很厉害，他越来越喜欢祭拜，所以他祭拜不是想帮妈妈工作，而是他很喜欢画画。孟子成为很著名的古代画家。今天很多中国人很喜欢孟子。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- Nothing to flag.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 厉害 -- “他画画很厉害”：擅长画画，identifies painting as the particular skill he is good at；画得很出色，focuses specifically on the high quality of his paintings
- 很著名 -- “孟子成为很著名的古代画家”：享有盛名的，emphasizes that many people recognize and respect his reputation

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 而是他很喜欢画画：而是很喜欢画画，The subject has not changed within the paired clause, so Chinese normally uses the zero form instead of repeating the pronoun.
- Coherence (Temporality): 孟子成为很著名的古代画家：孟子成为了很著名的古代画家，This is a completed change of state in the narrative, so the verb takes 了.
- Expressiveness: At “他画画很厉害,” add a small visual detail about his paintings, such as: “他画的人又清楚又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: Show why he enjoys the activity through an inner feeling, such as: “孟子一边画画，一边觉得祭拜很有意思。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of dialogue from his mother after noticing his skill, such as: “妈妈说：‘你画得真好！’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show a change in Mengzi’s interests over time, using “越来越喜欢” effectively.
- Your final sentence gives the scene a broad ending by showing that many people still like Mengzi today.
