## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，孟子和孟母住了在靠墓地的家。孩子才五岁就每天在墓地祭拜了。在那个城市，孟母从早到晚都找工作，可是找不到，害得孟母没有钱。难怪她跟孩子搬家，原来买不了房。他们搬了到别一个城市找工作。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，this expression is redundant because 古代 already names a time period; use 在古代 for the narrative setting.
- 住了在靠墓地的家：住在墓地旁边的家里，了 cannot be placed between the verb and its location phrase; this is a continuing living situation, so use 住在 + place without 了.
- 跟：带着，跟孟子搬家 means moving together as companions, while 带着孟子搬家 means that the mother takes her child with her when moving.
- 别一个城市：另一个城市，别 cannot be used directly before 一个 to mean “another”; use 另一个 for one different city.
- 搬了到：搬到了，了 follows the directional/resultative complement, so it comes after 到 rather than before it.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟母：有一个小男孩叫孟子，他和孟母，孟子 is appearing for the first time, so introduce him as a child with his name before using the family label 孟母.
- Coherence (Reference): 在那个城市：在一个城市里，那个 assumes that the city has already been identified, but this is its first mention; introduce it with an indefinite noun phrase.
- Coherence (Reference): 害得孟母没有钱：害得她没有钱，孟母 was just named, and no other female character competes as the referent, so use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Reference): 孩子：他，孟子 has just been referred to as 孩子, so a pronoun is more natural for this close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 每天在墓地祭拜了：每天在墓地祭拜，every day describes a routine, so the verb takes the zero particle rather than 了, which marks a completed action.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 原来买不了房。他们搬到了另一个城市找工作：原来买不了房，所以他们搬到了另一个城市找工作，add 所以 to show the cause-and-effect link between the difficulty and the move.
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery sentence, add the child’s reaction to make his action more vivid, for example: “孟子觉得祭拜很有趣，常常学着别人鞠躬。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where the job search fails, show Meng母’s worry with an action and inner thought, for example: “孟母叹了口气，心想：‘这样下去不行。’”
- Expressiveness: When the family reaches the new city, add one sensory detail about the setting, for example: “新家附近人来人往，从早到晚都很热闹。”

### Encouragement
You used 才五岁就 well to show that Meng子 was very young. The phrase 从早到晚 also gives a clear sense of how hard 孟母 was trying to find work.

## Scene 2

> 这个城市老是很吵，不是人吵架，就是都叫，害得孟子一直不能睡觉。在那儿，孟母也找不到工作。孟子和妈妈要又一个次搬了家。在第三个城市，孟母找到工作，在商店做衣服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 要又一个次搬：要再搬一次，A planned repeat uses the pattern modal verb + 再 + verb + number + measure word; 又 is for a repeat that has already happened, and the measure-word phrase is 一次, not 一个次.
- 不是人吵架，就是都叫：不是有人吵架，就是有人大叫，The two alternatives in this pattern need parallel clauses; 都 also needs a clear plural group to quantify, while 有人 clearly introduces the people making each kind of noise.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 吵 -- “这个城市老是很吵”：嘈杂，specifies that many mixed sounds make the city noisy.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 了：∅，After the modal verb 要, the future action takes the zero particle rather than 了, because it has not been completed.
- Coherence (Temporality): 找到工作：找到了工作，This is a completed discovery in the narrative, so add 了 after the resultative complement 到.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 孟子和妈妈要：所以孟子和妈妈要，The city’s noise and the unsuccessful job search give reasons for moving, so 所以 clearly introduces the result.
- Expressiveness: After the description of the noise, add a concrete sound detail, such as “外面的人一直大声吵架，叫喊声传进了房间。”
- Expressiveness: Show Mengzi’s feeling through a short line of speech, such as “孟子难过地说：‘妈妈，我想睡觉，可是外面太吵了！’”
- Expressiveness: When Mengmu finds work, add her reaction, such as “孟母终于找到了工作，心里很高兴。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show how the noisy environment affects Mengzi, and the use of 害得 effectively connects the noise with his difficulty sleeping. The new location and Mengmu’s job also make the scene easy to follow.

## Scene 3

> 孟子在那个商店也找了到工作，可是不是做衣服的工作，而是喜欢的工作。孟子学习馆学了十五年，他的学习和他的旅行经验很重要，对他很多好处。他是大人的时候，他成为很有名的哲人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 找了到：找到了，The resultative complement 到 must follow the verb directly, and 了 comes after the complete verb-complement phrase.
- 孟子学习馆学了十五年：孟子在学习馆学习了十五年，A location noun needs 在 before it, and the action verb 学习 should follow the location phrase.
- 对他很多好处：对他有很多好处，The structure is 对……有好处; 有 is required to state that something brings benefits.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很有名 -- “他成为很有名的哲人”：远近闻名，shows that his reputation was known in many places；声名远扬，emphasizes that his reputation spread widely.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为很有名的哲人：成为了很有名的哲人，This is a completed change of identity in the narrative, so use 了 after 成为.
- Expressiveness: After the preferred job is mentioned, add a feeling to show why it matters: “他终于找到了自己喜欢的工作，心里很高兴。”
- Expressiveness: When describing the long period of study, add a concrete habit: “这十五年里，他每天认真看书，也常常出去旅行。”
- Expressiveness: At the ending, add the effect of his ideas: “他的想法帮助了很多人，所以大家都很尊敬他。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show a long development from finding work to studying and eventually becoming a respected thinker. The contrast structure “不是……，而是……” is also used effectively to distinguish the two kinds of work.
