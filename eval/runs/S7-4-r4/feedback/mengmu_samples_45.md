## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子。他们住在墓地的旁边，所以孟子老是祭拜。孟子不是祭拜，就是跟朋友聊天。他很喜欢他的城市，但是城市有才两个学校，都很小。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 is already a time expression, so adding 的时候 redundantly says “at the time of ancient times.”
- 城市有才两个学校：城市里只有两所学校，use 只有 before the number to mean “only,” and use 所, the matching measure word for schools.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 小 -- “都很小”：规模很小，specifies that the schools are small in size and capacity rather than simply using a general description.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子：有一个叫孟子的男孩子和他的妈妈住在中国，at first mention, introduce the character with both the name and a full noun phrase rather than naming him only in the following sentence.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子不是祭拜，就是跟朋友聊天：他不是祭拜，就是跟朋友聊天，孟子 was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other male character competes as the referent, so use a pronoun for close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Reference): 他很喜欢他的城市：他很喜欢自己的城市，when the city belongs to the subject of the sentence, 自己的 makes the possessor relationship clear.
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery setting, add a visual detail about visitors, such as “每天都有很多人来墓地祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: Show what Mengzi does while copying the visitors, such as “孟子一边笑，一边学着别人祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: Add Mengzi’s feeling about his new surroundings, such as “他觉得这里很热闹，也很好玩。”

### Encouragement
You clearly introduce Mengzi, his mother, and the two places in this scene. You also use the contrast connector well to show that he likes the city despite its limited schools.

## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈不是不喜欢他们的城市，而是想孟子有很好的教育。他们搬家到有很多学校的城市。他们从早到晚走路，孟子一直抱怨。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 想孟子有：想让孟子接受，When the mother wants another person to receive an education, use 让 to introduce that person and 接受 to describe receiving education.
- 搬家到：搬到了，The move is a completed narrative action, so use 了; 搬到 + place expresses moving to a destination.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他们：所以他们，The move happens because the mother wants better education for Meng子, so add 所以 to show the cause-and-effect relationship.
- Expressiveness: After the long walk, add a physical detail such as “他们走了很久，孟子的腿都酸了” to make the journey feel tiring and real.
- Expressiveness: At “孟子一直抱怨,” add a short line of speech, such as “妈妈，我们还要走多久？” to show his impatience directly.

### Encouragement
- You used the contrast structure “不是……而是……” appropriately to explain the mother’s reason for moving.
- “从早到晚” and “一直抱怨” clearly show the long duration of the journey and Meng子’s ongoing feeling.

## Scene 3

> 他难怪抱怨，他想他的朋友，因为他不要离开他的城市。他的妈妈找到孟子一个老师。跟老师孟子学最重要的文学，所以他成为哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他难怪抱怨，他想他的朋友：难怪他抱怨，原来他想他的朋友，The subject follows 难怪, and the explanation for the surprising result is introduced with 原来.
- 孟子：给孟子，Use 给 before the person who receives the result of finding a teacher.
- 跟老师孟子学：孟子跟老师学，Put the subject before the phrase that states whom he learns from.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 想 -- “他想他的朋友”：想念, specifically expresses missing friends who are not nearby

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他的朋友：自己的朋友，Use 自己的 when the friends belong to the same person as the subject, making the possessor clear.
- Coherence (Reference): 他的城市：自己的城市，Use 自己的 when the city belongs to the same person as the subject, making the possessor clear.
- Coherence (Temporality): 找到：找到了，Finding the teacher is a completed action, so 了 follows the resultative verb.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为：成为了，Becoming a philosopher is a completed change of state in this narration, so use 了.
- Expressiveness: After the complaint, add Mengzi’s feeling or speech, such as: 孟子低着头说：“我舍不得我的朋友。”
- Expressiveness: At the teacher scene, show how the teacher helps him learn, such as: 老师耐心地教孟子读书。
- Expressiveness: Before the final result, add a detail about Mengzi’s effort, such as: 孟子每天认真学习，慢慢对哲学有了兴趣。

### Encouragement
You clearly show Mengzi’s reason for complaining and connect his learning to his later achievement. You also used a teacher and friends to make the scene feel connected to the earlier events.
