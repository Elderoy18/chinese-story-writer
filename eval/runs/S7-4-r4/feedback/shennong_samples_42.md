## Scene 1

> 古代的时候有一个人叫神农。他需要当上医，一看别的人就想帮助他们。但是，他有一个问题，他不知道什么东西能做药。神农听说一个远远的地方有一种植物，什么病人吃都会觉得身体更好。神农三个月走去，找到有植物的地方，他看到一个很漂亮的植物，又香又好看。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，This time expression is redundant because both parts refer to “the time of ancient times.”
- 当上医：当上医生，The noun for a person whose job is treating patients is 医生; 医 alone does not fit naturally here.
- 一看：一看到，The sentence means that he sees someone, so the verb needs the resultative complement 到 rather than only the action of looking.
- 别的人：别人，别人 refers to other people as a group; 别的 is used to distinguish a different item or type.
- 什么病人：病人，什么病人 asks which type of patient, but this sentence makes a general claim about patients who eat the plant.
- 一个很漂亮的植物：一株很漂亮的植物，Plants need a suitable measure word such as 株 rather than the general measure word 个.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 东西 -- “他不知道什么东西能做药”：植物，identifies the relevant category more precisely, since the scene is about finding medicinal plants

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 有植物的地方：有这种植物的地方，Use 这种植物 to refer back clearly to the plant mentioned in the previous sentence.
- Coherence (Reference): 他看到：看到，After 神农 has remained the subject throughout the same sentence, Chinese normally uses zero form rather than repeating the pronoun.
- Coherence (Temporality): 病人吃都会觉得：病人吃了都会觉得，The eating must be completed before the patients feel better, so use 了 for the completed action in this conditional situation.
- Coherence (Temporality): 神农三个月走去：神农走了三个月，Put the duration after the verb and use 了 to show that the three-month journey was completed.
- Coherence (Temporality): 看到：看到了，The sentence narrates a completed moment of seeing the plant, so add 了 after the resultative complement.
- Expressiveness: When describing the plant, add a concrete visual detail, such as “那儿开着黄色的花，像星星一样。”
- Expressiveness: You could show Shennong’s hope after hearing about the plant, such as “神农很希望找到这种植物。”
- Expressiveness: The report he hears could include a short quotation, such as “有人说：‘这种植物可以治病。’”

### Encouragement
- You introduced the main character clearly and gave him a meaningful goal. The paired description pattern “又香又好看” is also a good way to make the plant easier for the reader to imagine.

## Scene 2

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- Nothing to flag.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好处 -- “灵芝对身体有好处”：有益于身体健康，states the health benefit more precisely in this context.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农快生病：他快生病，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the same character was named or clearly referred to in the immediately preceding sentence and no other character competes.
- Coherence (Temporality): 快给他一种红的灵芝：赶快给了他一种红的灵芝，This is a completed action in the narrative, so the verb needs 了; 赶快 clearly means that the person acted quickly.
- Expressiveness: At the moment he becomes ill, add one physical detail to show the effect of the flower, such as “肚子突然疼了起来”.
- Expressiveness: When the blue-clothed person appears, add a quick action to make the rescue more vivid, such as “他赶快走过来”.
- Expressiveness: After recognizing the benefit of the plant, add Shen Nong’s thought, such as “他心想：原来灵芝可以治病！”.

### Encouragement
You used the time sequence with “吃了以后” effectively, and the blue-clothed person is introduced clearly as a new character. The final sentence also connects Shen Nong’s recovery to his earlier knowledge about lingzhi.

## Scene 3

> 啊，他怎么忘记了？他把灵芝放在包里。一回家就告诉别人。他找到药以后他写下怎么做药在书上。所以人们都知道，那红灵芝对身体好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 写下怎么做药在书上：在书上写下怎么做药的方法，The location phrase should come before the verb here, and the object needs a noun phrase meaning “the method of making medicine.”
- 那红灵芝：那种红灵芝，A demonstrative such as 那 needs a measure word before the noun; 种 identifies a type of plant.
- 对身体好处：对身体有好处，The fixed expression is 对……有好处; 有 is required to state that something benefits the body.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 放 -- “他把灵芝放在包里”：装进，emphasizes putting the lingzhi inside the bag.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他找到药以后他写下：他找到药以后，写下，When the same character continues as the subject within one sentence, use zero form instead of repeating the pronoun.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他把灵芝放在包里：他把灵芝放在包里了，This is a completed action in the narrative, so it needs 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉别人：告诉了人们，The telling happens as a completed action after returning home, so add 了; 人们 refers to the concrete group of people he tells.
- Coherence (Temporality): 写下：写下了，The recording is completed, and 了 comes after the resultative complement 下.
- Expressiveness: At the opening question, add an internal thought to show his sudden realization: “啊，我怎么把灵芝忘了？”
- Expressiveness: When he puts the lingzhi away, describe its appearance: “那灵芝像一把红色的小伞。”
- Expressiveness: After writing, add a feeling of relief or pride: “神农看着书上的字，心里很高兴。”

### Encouragement
- You used a clear action sequence with 一……就……, which helps the reader follow what happens after he returns home. Your ending also connects writing down his discovery with other people learning about the lingzhi.
