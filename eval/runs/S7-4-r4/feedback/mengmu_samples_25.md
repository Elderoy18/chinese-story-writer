## Scene 1

> 这是古代的故事。孟子小的时候，他的爸爸很早就去世了。从他的家到墓地很近，所以他每天老是从早到晚去墓地跟朋友们玩玩儿。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 从他的家到墓地很近：他的家离墓地很近，use 离 to express that one place is close to another; 从 marks a starting point for movement, not distance.
- 每天老是从早到晚去墓地跟朋友们玩玩儿：每天从早到晚都在墓地跟朋友们玩儿，老是 describes repeated habitual actions, while 从早到晚 describes a continuous duration; the revised wording shows that the playing continues through the day.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子小的时候：有一个小男孩叫孟子。他小的时候，at a character’s first mention, introduce the character with a full noun phrase; after that, use a pronoun when the reference is close and clear.
- Coherence (Reference): 所以他每天：所以孟子每天，both Mengzi and his father appear in the previous sentence, so 他 could refer to either person; repeat the name to make the referent clear.
- Expressiveness: After the final sentence, add a small cemetery detail to create atmosphere, such as “墓地里常常有人哭泣，四周安安静静的。”
- Expressiveness: Show Mengzi’s reason for going there through his thoughts or actions, such as “孟子觉得祭拜很好玩儿，便学着大人的样子拜来拜去。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of dialogue with his friends, such as “他笑着对朋友说：‘我们也来学一学吧！’”

### Encouragement
You clearly establish the main setting and explain why Mengzi often goes to the cemetery. The cause-and-effect connector 所以 also helps the reader follow the scene.

## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈觉得在墓地玩儿，害得，所以他们搬家。搬家对孟子有好的影响，他才五岁，可是每天不是写字就是帮妈妈的工作。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在墓地玩儿，害得：孟子在墓地玩儿会带来麻烦，The original use of 害得 is incomplete: this structure must be followed by the person affected and a harmful result.
- 他们搬家：他们搬了家，This is a completed move, so the separable verb needs 了 after 搬.
- 帮妈妈的工作：帮妈妈做事，After 帮妈妈, use a verb phrase to state what help is given; 的工作 makes it sound as though the help is directed at “Mother’s work” rather than at Mother.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 影响 -- “搬家对孟子有好的影响”：益处，states more specifically that the move benefits Mengzi；积极的影响，specifies that the effect is beneficial rather than merely “good”

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他才五岁，可是每天不是写字就是帮妈妈的工作：他才五岁，就每天不是写字就是帮妈妈做事，After 才 + age, use 就 to introduce an action that happens at such an early age; 可是 is not the needed connector here.
- Expressiveness: At the mother’s thought about the cemetery, add her worry to show why she decides to move: “孟母很担心，心想：‘孟子每天在这儿玩儿，会学到不好的东西。’”
- Expressiveness: Add a brief detail about what Mengzi does near the cemetery, such as “他学着来祭拜的人哭，还觉得很好玩儿。” This makes the setting and the mother’s concern more vivid.
- Expressiveness: Show Mengzi’s feelings after moving, for example: “孟子觉得新地方很陌生，也有一点想念朋友。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect the move with the mother’s concern and show that the move affects Mengzi’s daily life. Your use of the pattern “不是……就是……” also gives a clear picture of his routine.

## Scene 3

> 他很聪明的人，难怪他成为有名的人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他很聪明的人：他是个很聪明的人，A noun phrase such as “a very intelligent person” needs a linking verb and a measure word after the subject.
- 成为有名的人：成为了有名的人，The becoming is presented as a completed result, so add 了 after the verb.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 难怪他成为了有名的人：难怪成为了有名的人，After the same person has just been named in the preceding clause, omit the repeated pronoun.
- Expressiveness: After “很聪明”, add a brief reason showing his learning, such as “他很爱看书，也很爱思考。”
- Expressiveness: Add a feeling or comment from his mother before the result, such as “孟母看到他认真学习，心里很高兴。”

### Encouragement
- You used “难怪” well to connect his intelligence with his later success. The phrase “有名的人” clearly expresses that he became well known.
