## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有很多人不知道什么植物能吃，什么植物不能吃。有很多人吃植物以后就吃坏肚子了，因为他们吃了毒。那个时候，有一个老人叫神农，他什么植物都觉得好吃，不怕生病，不怕毒。他希望别人也可以吃好吃的植物。可是他知道，如果他们还怕吃毒，不会吃他喜欢的植物。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，The phrase repeats the time meaning because 古代 already names a period; use the shorter time expression.
- 因为他们吃了毒：因为他们吃了有毒的植物，毒 names poison itself, while this sentence refers to the plants people ate; 有毒的植物 identifies plants that contain poison.
- 还怕吃毒：害怕吃到有毒的植物，吃毒 is not natural for accidentally eating a poisonous plant; 吃到 expresses encountering and eating it.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不知道 -- “有很多人不知道什么植物能吃，什么植物不能吃”：分不清，emphasizes that people cannot distinguish edible plants from inedible ones.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After “吃坏肚子了”, add a concrete symptom to show the consequence: “有的人肚子疼得站不起来。”
- Expressiveness: After introducing 神农, add his inner thought or goal: “神农心想：我要找出哪些植物能吃，哪些不能吃。”

### Encouragement
You clearly present the main problem by contrasting plants people can eat with plants they cannot eat. You also use a cause expression with 因为 to explain why people became ill.

## Scene 2

> 所以，他上山下山找植物，吃了很多样子的植物。他是第一人，找到了茶。他把所有的植物写下来了。他一直都吃了，他连毒都能吃。可是他还没吃了一个叫夹竹桃的植物。他找到了那个像星星的植物，觉得很漂亮，马上就吃下了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他是第一人，找到了茶：他是第一个找到茶的人，An ordinal expression here needs the measure word 个, and the relative clause states that he was the first person to find tea.
- 一直都吃了：一直都在吃各种植物，了 marks a completed action, but 一直 describes an ongoing action; use 在 for the progressive action instead.
- 毒：有毒的植物，毒 is a noun meaning poison; this sentence needs a noun phrase naming the plants that are poisonous.
- 还没吃了：还没吃，没 already negates the completed action, so 了 is not used after the verb here.
- 一个叫夹竹桃的植物：一种叫夹竹桃的植物，Use 种 as the measure word for a type of plant.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 写下来 -- “他把所有的植物写下来了”：记下，emphasizes preserving his discoveries in written form；记录下来，emphasizes making a systematic record of the plants he investigated

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: At “上山下山找植物,” add a travel detail to show how hard his search was. For example: “他走啊走啊，翻过了一座又一座高山。”
- Expressiveness: After he finds tea, add what he learns about it. For example: “他发现，茶不但好喝，而且可以做药。”
- Expressiveness: At the description of the oleander, make the flowers more vivid and show why he wants to eat them. For example: “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花，又大又漂亮。”

### Encouragement
- You use completed-action forms well in “找到了茶” and “吃下了,” which makes the sequence of discoveries and actions easy to follow.
- The details “上山下山” and “像星星的植物” already give the scene a clear sense of movement and visual imagination.

## Scene 3

> 可是夹竹桃的毒很重，让他肚子很痛。他在地上躺着，觉得他快会死了。可是正好有别人找到他了，给了他一个像伞的植物，叫灵芝。他吃灵芝以后就觉得很好，不再生病了。他测量起来了，像星星的植物是毒，像伞的植物是药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 快会：快要，Use 快要 for an imminent event; 会 is a future/modal marker and is not naturally combined with 快 in this meaning.
- 别人：一个男人，别人 distinguishes other people from a stated person or group, but no such contrast has been established here; use an indefinite noun phrase to introduce the new character.
- 像伞的植物：像伞一样的植物，Use the comparison pattern 像……一样 to state that the plant has the shape of an umbrella.
- 不再生病了：肚子不再痛了，生病 means “to become ill”; for the current recovery from the poison, refer to the symptom that has stopped.
- 测量起来了：明白了，测量 means physically determining size or length, while this sentence describes realizing the different effects of the two plants.
- 像星星的植物：像星星一样的植物，Use the comparison pattern 像……一样 to describe the plant’s star-like shape.
- 是毒：有毒，毒 is a noun here; 有毒 means “poisonous” and correctly describes the plant’s property.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 重 -- “夹竹桃的毒很重”：毒性很强，states more precisely that the plant’s poisonous effect is strong
- 很痛 -- “让他肚子很痛”：疼得厉害，describes the intensity of the pain more vividly
- 很好 -- “就觉得很好”：好多了，clearly shows that he feels better than before；舒服多了，specifies that his body feels much more comfortable

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 觉得他快会死了：觉得自己快要死了，In a thought about his own condition, 他 could be read as another male character; use 自己 to make the self-reference clear.
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃灵芝以后：吃了灵芝以后，Eating is completed before the later feeling of recovery, so the completed action needs 了.
- Expressiveness: After “他在地上躺着,” add a physical detail to show his suffering, such as “他疼得直冒冷汗，连站起来的力气都没有。”
- Expressiveness: At “觉得自己快要死了,” add an inner thought or short quotation, such as “他心想：‘我会死吗？’”
- Expressiveness: When the new man appears, add one identifying detail, such as “这时，一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人走过来。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the cause, the pain, the helpful plant, and the recovery in a logical order. You also successfully use 着 in “躺着” to describe an ongoing state.
