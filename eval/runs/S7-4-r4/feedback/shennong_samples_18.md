## Scene 1

> 神农和他的家人住在古代，所以不知道什么植物能吃。因为他的家人常常生病了，所以神农和他的哥哥一起去旅行找可以吃的植物。他们去很多不一样的地方，比方说高山、山谷和沙漠。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 住在古代：生活在古代，古代 is a historical time period rather than a physical place; use a verb such as 生活 to describe living in that period.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 找 -- “一起去旅行找可以吃的植物”：寻找, emphasizes a sustained search for plants rather than simply looking once.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：一个叫神农的人，This is the character’s first mention, so introduce him with a name and a brief noun phrase rather than assuming the reader already knows who he is.
- Coherence (Temporality): 常常生病了：常常生病，常常 describes a routine or repeated state, so use the zero particle rather than 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们去很多不一样的地方：他们去了很多不一样的地方，The visits to the places are completed narrative actions, so the final verb needs 了.
- Expressiveness: After the list of places, add one small physical detail to make the journey vivid, such as “高山又高又冷，沙漠里很热。”
- Expressiveness: Add Shen Nong’s purpose or feeling before the journey, such as “神农想：我要找到能治好家人的植物。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the cause of Shen Nong’s journey with 因为……所以……, and your list of mountains, valleys, and deserts gives the journey a broad sense of adventure.

## Scene 2

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在很漂亮的河找：在一条很漂亮的河边找，A river itself is not the place where someone searches; use the bank as the location, and use the measure word for one river.
- 接近知道：想知道，接近 means “approach” and cannot directly modify 知道 in this phrase; 想知道 expresses the intended wish to learn something.
- 了。“：了。”，A quotation must end with the closing quotation mark, not another opening quotation mark.
- 太麻烦了。：太麻烦了。”，Direct speech needs a closing quotation mark after the final punctuation.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the first sentence, describe the flowers so the setting is easier to imagine: “河边开着黄色的、像星星一样的花。”
- Expressiveness: At “神农一吃夹竹桃就生病了,” add a physical feeling to show the effect: “他的肚子疼了起来，难受得不得了。”

### Encouragement

## Scene 3

> 夹竹桃是毒的，我想知道灵芝会帮他。”哥哥给神农灵芝。神农吃完了以后开始痊愈了。神农说：“谢谢哥哥，你帮得真好。灵芝我们可以吃，特别是如果我们已经吃了夹竹桃。“

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 夹竹桃是毒的：夹竹桃是有毒的，毒 is a noun here; use 有毒 to describe a plant’s poisonous property.
- 开始痊愈了：开始好起来了，痊愈 means recovering completely, so it cannot naturally follow 开始; 好起来 expresses that the recovery has begun.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 帮 -- “灵芝会帮他”：帮他解毒，specifies that the help is removing the poison from the earlier plant.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农说：他说，after 神农 was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no new speaker intervened, use a pronoun for close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 哥哥给神农灵芝：哥哥给了神农灵芝，this is a completed action, so 给 needs 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 灵芝我们可以吃，特别是如果我们已经吃了夹竹桃：灵芝可以吃；如果我们已经吃了夹竹桃，就更应该吃灵芝，特别是 introduces a typical example, not a conditional situation; use 如果……就…… for a condition and result.
- Expressiveness: After the recovery, add a physical detail to show the change: “神农慢慢坐起来，觉得肚子没有那么疼了。”
- Expressiveness: Make the thanks more personal by adding a feeling: “谢谢哥哥！刚才我真的很害怕。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short question about the medicine to make the dialogue more lively: “灵芝为什么能让我好起来？”

### Encouragement
- You clearly connected the lingzhi to Shen Nong’s recovery, and you used a direct quotation to let the characters speak. The sequence from eating the plant to feeling better is easy to follow.
