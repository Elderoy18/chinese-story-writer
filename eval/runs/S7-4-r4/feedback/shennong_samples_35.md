## Scene 1

> 在古代，很多医生不知道怎么用药。所以他们找到了很多植物对药。神农先看到很多病人，他们吃很多植物对药。但是我觉得他们的吃的植物，让他们病人。神农觉得这个植物可能特别是毒。所以他想找一个植物对药，他们可以吃对药。正好，他找到了一把花，他想把花放在茶里，对药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很多植物对药：很多可以做药的植物，Use the verb phrase “可以做药” to say that plants can be used to make medicine; “对药” is not a valid phrase here.
- 吃很多植物对药：吃了很多可以做药的植物，“了” marks the completed eating action, and “可以做药的植物” correctly describes plants that can be made into medicine.
- 他们的吃的植物：他们吃的植物，A verb phrase used before a noun takes the pattern subject + verb + 的; the extra possessive “的” after “他们” is not used in this structure.
- 让他们病人：让他们生病了，“病人” is a noun meaning a patient, not a verb meaning to become ill. Use “生病” for the action or state, with “了” for the resulting change of state.
- 特别是毒：特别有毒，“毒” cannot directly function as an adjective after “特别”; “有毒” means toxic and can be modified by “特别.”
- 找一个植物对药：找一个植物做药，“做药” means to use something in making medicine; “对药” does not express this meaning.
- 吃对药：吃，Here the intended meaning is that people can eat the plant. “对药” cannot follow “吃” in this context.
- 对药：用来做药，“用来做药” clearly expresses the purpose of putting the flowers into the tea.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 看到 -- “神农先看到很多病人”：发现，emphasizes noticing the sick people after coming across them.
- 正好 -- “正好，他找到了一把花”：碰巧，more clearly highlights that finding the flowers happened by chance.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农先看到很多病人：一个叫神农的人先看到了很多病人，This is the character’s first mention, so introduce him with a name and a brief noun phrase rather than assuming the reader already knows him.
- Coherence (Reference): 这个植物：这些植物，The previous sentence mentions many plants, not one identified plant, so “这个植物” has no clear singular referent.
- Coherence (Reference): 他们可以吃：病人可以吃，Both doctors and sick people have been mentioned, so “他们” can refer to either group. Repeat “病人” to make the referent clear.
- Coherence (Reference): 他找到了一把花，他想：他找到了一把花，想，After the subject has already been named in the same sentence, use zero form rather than repeating the pronoun.
- Expressiveness: After “很多病人,” add one physical detail to show how ill they are, such as “他们脸色很差，肚子也疼。”
- Expressiveness: After “神农觉得这个植物可能特别有毒,” add Shennong’s worried thought, such as “他想：不能再让大家随便吃植物了。”
- Expressiveness: At “他找到了一把花,” describe the flowers so the discovery feels more vivid, such as “那些花又黄又大，像星星一样。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect the scene to Shennong’s goal of finding plants that can help sick people. You also use several useful story words, including “病人,” “植物,” “茶,” and “可能.”

## Scene 2

> 他不知道如果这把花对你的身体好，这就要看如果这把花毒不毒了。神农想找别的植物，但是这很麻烦。正好，他找到了别的花。他一吃花，很快就生病了。他的肚子疼死了。神农不知道他吃不吃灵芝或者夹竹桃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不知道如果这把花对他的身体好不好：不知道这把花对他的身体好不好，An embedded yes/no question after “不知道” uses an A-not-A form, not “如果,” which introduces a condition.
- 这就要看如果这把花毒不毒了：这就要看这把花毒不毒了，“这就要看……” must be followed by a question form; “毒不毒” is already an A-not-A question, so “如果” should be removed.
- 他吃不吃灵芝或者夹竹桃：自己吃的是灵芝还是夹竹桃，The uncertainty is about which plant he ate, not whether he eats. Two alternative possibilities use “是……还是……”.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 麻烦 -- “但是这很麻烦”：艰辛, emphasizes that searching for plants requires sustained hard work and effort；不容易, clearly emphasizes that the task is difficult to accomplish.
- 很快 -- “很快就生病了”：没过多久, shows that only a short time passed before he became ill；没一会儿, gives a vivid sense that the illness came on soon.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 你的身体：他的身体，The narrator is describing what happened to Shennong, rather than directly addressing the reader, so the possessive pronoun should refer back to him.
- Expressiveness: After “他的肚子疼死了,” add a physical reaction to show how ill he feels: “他疼得躺在地上，站不起来。”
- Expressiveness: Add Shennong’s worried thought after he becomes sick: “他想：我会不会死？”
- Expressiveness: At the end, add a short line of speech that shows his uncertainty: “神农说：‘我吃的到底是什么花？’”

### Encouragement
- You used the useful pattern “一……就……” in “他一吃花，很快就生病了,” which clearly shows that the illness happened soon after he ate the flower.
- Your scene also gives Shennong a clear problem and keeps the focus on his search for useful and dangerous plants.

## Scene 3

> 别的人给了神农一个灵芝。这个植物可以让神农更好。比方说，他会觉得更健康。吃灵芝以后，他可以再走。现在他觉得更好。最后，神农找到了很多植物，他可以用对药。这些植物让神农很高兴。神农想跟很多人分享他的新药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别的人给了神农一个灵芝：一个男人给了神农一个灵芝，别的人 distinguishes other people from a person already under discussion, but no such person has been introduced here. Use an indefinite full noun phrase for this newly appearing helper.
- 最后，神农找到了很多植物，他可以用对药：最后，神农找到了很多可以用来做药的植物，the phrase 用对药 is not grammatical here. Use 用来做药 to say that the plants can serve as medicine.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 再走 -- “他可以再走”：继续走，emphasizes that he resumes his journey after recovering.
- 高兴 -- “这些植物让神农很高兴”：欣慰，expresses happiness mixed with satisfaction after his hard work produces useful results.
- 新药 -- “分享他的新药”：新发现的药，makes clear that these are medicines he has newly discovered.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农想跟很多人分享他的新药：他想跟很多人分享他的新药，after a close mention of the same person and with no competing male character, use a pronoun rather than repeating the name.
- Expressiveness: After the recovery sentence, add a physical detail to show the change, such as “他的肚子不疼了，也觉得全身有力气了。”
- Expressiveness: When the helper gives the medicine, add a short line of speech, such as “快吃这个，也许能帮你！”
- Expressiveness: Show why the discovery matters to him by adding a thought after he finds the plants, such as “神农想：这些植物可以帮助很多病人。”

### Encouragement
You used clear time markers such as “吃灵芝以后” and “最后” to organize the events. You also showed the positive result of the medicine and 神农’s wish to help others.
