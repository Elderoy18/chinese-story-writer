## Scene 1

> 这个男孩子叫孟子，孟子是古代的人。孟子的妈妈觉得住在哪儿对童年很重要，所以他们常常搬了家。她望子成龙，所以孟子的妈妈安排他的生活。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 对童年很重要：对孩子的成长很重要，童年 is a time period, while the sentence means that the living place affects the child’s development.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 安排 -- “安排他的生活”：规划，emphasizes making a considered plan for his daily life；照料，emphasizes taking care of the practical parts of his life.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 这个男孩子叫孟子，孟子是古代的人：在古代，有一个叫孟子的男孩子，At first mention, use an indefinite full noun phrase rather than “这个”, which suggests the boy has already been identified.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子的妈妈安排他的生活：安排他的生活，After “她” has already referred to Mengzi’s mother in the same sentence, use zero form instead of repeating the full noun phrase.
- Coherence (Temporality): 常常搬了家：常常搬家，A habitual action introduced by “常常” takes the zero particle, not completed-action 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 所以他们常常搬家：因为孟子的妈妈觉得住在哪儿对孩子的成长很重要，所以他们常常搬家，Use 因为……所以…… to show the cause-and-effect relationship between the mother’s belief and the repeated moves.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 所以：因为她望子成龙，所以，Use 因为……所以…… to connect her hope for her son with her decision to organize his life.
- Expressiveness: After “望子成龙”, add the mother’s inner thought to show her motivation, such as “她心想：我要让孟子好好学习。”
- Expressiveness: At “常常搬家”, add a small concrete detail about the repeated moves, such as “他们收拾好东西，又搬到新的地方。”
- Expressiveness: Describe the mother’s feeling when she plans Mengzi’s life, such as “孟子的妈妈很着急，也很希望他有一个好的未来。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the mother’s motivation with “望子成龙”, and the use of “所以” shows that you are already connecting reasons and actions in the story.

## Scene 2

> 因为他们常常搬了家，中国人说，孟母三迁。孟子没有爸爸，所以，他的妈妈更重要。从这儿到那儿，妈妈教孟子很多技能。先她让孟子跟朋友玩儿，然后教他怎么买又便宜又好质量的东西。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 常常搬了家：常常搬家，A habitual action takes no 了 because it does not describe one completed move.
- 先她让：她先让，The subject should come before the time-order adverb 先.
- 又便宜又好质量的东西：又便宜、质量又好的东西，质量好 is the correct predicate for “good quality”; 好质量 cannot directly modify a noun in this structure.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 技能 -- “妈妈教孟子很多技能”：生活本领，specifies that these are practical abilities for everyday life, which fits learning to buy things.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 妈妈教孟子很多技能：她教孟子很多技能，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence: 孟子的妈妈 was named in the immediately preceding sentence, and no other female character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Temporality): 然后教他：然后教了他，In a completed series of actions, only the final verb takes 了; here 教 is the final action after 让.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为他们常常搬家，中国人说，孟母三迁：因为他们常常搬家，所以中国人说“孟母三迁”，Use 因为……所以…… to show that the repeated moves are the cause of this saying.
- Expressiveness: After the buying lesson, add a concrete action showing what Mengzi practises, such as “孟子学着跟商人讨价还价。”
- Expressiveness: Add the mother’s goal or feeling when she teaches him, such as “妈妈希望孟子以后能照顾自己。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of dialogue during the lesson, such as “妈妈说：‘买东西的时候，要先看看质量。’”

### Encouragement
You use 先……然后…… to show the order of the mother’s teaching clearly. You also give a concrete example of a useful skill by mentioning how to buy affordable, good-quality things.
