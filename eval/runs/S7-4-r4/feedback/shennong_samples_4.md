## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个男人，他的名字是神农。他看到了有很多人不知道哪个植物好吃，也不知道什么植物对你的身体有好处。这个问题神农觉得很糟糕，所以神农去了吃每种植物。他吃了一种植物以后，就写了这个植物对你有没有好处。有的时候，神农吃了很好吃的植物。有的时候，他吃了很危险的植物。他吃完了每种植物以后，会来跟他的朋友聊到他吃的植物了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，This time expression is redundant because both parts express “in ancient times.”
- 去了吃：去吃了，With a verb followed by another action, place 了 after the action phrase: 去吃了.
- 写了：写下了，Use 写下 to mean recording information so it is kept; 写 alone does not express this “write down” meaning clearly here.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 糟糕 -- “这个问题神农觉得很糟糕”：严重，makes the problem’s harmful consequences more specific in this context；令人担忧，shows that the problem causes concern rather than only being generally bad

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 所以神农：所以他，After the first mention of the character in the same sentence, use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when no other character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Reference): 你的身体：身体，The reader-facing pronoun has no specific referent in this general statement; use a general noun phrase instead.
- Coherence (Reference): 对你有没有好处：对身体有没有好处，The pronoun does not refer to a particular person in the narration, so a general expression makes the reference clear.
- Coherence (Reference): 有的时候，神农：有的时候，他，The name was mentioned recently and no other character is competing, so a pronoun is more natural for this close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃了很好吃的植物：吃很好吃的植物，This describes a repeated habit introduced by 有的时候, so use the zero particle rather than completed-action 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃了很危险的植物：吃很危险的植物，This is also a repeated habit, so it does not take completed-action 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 会来跟他的朋友聊到他吃的植物了：会来跟他的朋友聊到他吃的植物，A routine with 会 uses the zero particle; the final 了 does not mark a completed action here.
- Expressiveness: After the description of the problem, add Shen Nong’s concern or a short thought, such as: 神农想：“要是大家吃错了植物，就会生病。”
- Expressiveness: When describing a dangerous plant, add a physical reaction to show its effect, such as: 他吃了以后，肚子疼了起来，难受得不得了。
- Expressiveness: Make one plant more vivid with a visual detail, such as: 那种植物开着黄色的花，像星星一样漂亮。

### Encouragement
You clearly establish the central problem and show Shen Nong’s systematic habit of eating plants and recording their effects. You also use time phrases such as 有的时候 and 吃了……以后 to organize the events.

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections

- 这就要看你的口味了：这就要看你喜欢什么口味了，The structure 这就要看……了 needs a question form after 看, such as a wh-question.
- 一个别的人：另一个人，别的 distinguishes other people from a stated group; here the speaker means one additional person, so 另一个人 fits.
- 哪个植物：哪种植物，Use 种 when asking which type of plant in a general category.
- 一个别的人：另一个人，别的 distinguishes other people from a stated group; here the speaker means one additional person, so 另一个人 fits.
- 哪个植物：哪种植物，Use 种 when asking which type of plant in a general category.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 甜的 -- “有很多甜的水果”：清甜的，adds the idea of a light, refreshing sweetness that fits apples, watermelon, and pears.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 对你的身体有好处：对身体有好处，This is a general fact about plants, so the addressee pronoun is unnecessary and can make the benefit sound limited to one unidentified person.
- Coherence (Reference): 对你的身体有坏处：对身体有坏处，This is also a general statement, so removing the pronoun keeps the reference broad and clear.
- Expressiveness: After the fruit examples, add one sensory detail to make the list more vivid, such as “西瓜又甜又多汁。”
- Expressiveness: At the first question, show why the person is asking, such as “他摸着肚子，好奇地问神农。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of direct speech after the answer about helpful plants, such as “神农说：‘茶不但好喝，对身体也有好处。’”

### Encouragement
You organize this scene clearly by using several questions and answers from different people. Your use of 比方说 also gives helpful, concrete examples for each answer.

## Scene 3

> 神农说，有一天，我吃了一个黄色的花，名字是夹竹桃。我一吃夹竹桃，就觉得这种植物对我的脑有坏处。可是，一个灵芝和一个男人正好在我的旁边，我说，麻烦你把那个植物给我吃一下。我一吃这个灵芝就好了。所以，你得小心吃植物，因为有的植物对你的健康有坏处，特别是夹竹桃。因为夹竹桃有毒。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一个黄色的花：一朵黄色的花，Flowers take the matching measure word “朵”; “个” is too general here.
- 我的脑：我的身体，The noun “脑” refers specifically to the brain. For general physical harm from a poisonous plant, “身体” fits the intended meaning.
- 一个灵芝：一株灵芝，Use “株” as the measure word for a growing medicinal fungus or plant; “个” is too general in this context.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 吃 -- “我一吃这个灵芝就好了”：服用，identifies the lingzhi more precisely as something taken for healing；服下，emphasizes swallowing it and completing the action.
- 有坏处 -- “有的植物对你的健康有坏处”：有害，states more directly that the plants can harm health.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 那个植物：灵芝，Two plants have already appeared, so “那个植物” could refer to either the oleander or the lingzhi. Repeat the name of the intended plant for clarity.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为夹竹桃有毒。：夹竹桃有毒。，“因为” introduces a cause and normally needs an effect clause. Here it begins a standalone sentence, so remove it and state the supporting fact directly.
- Expressiveness: At the flower’s introduction, add a visual detail to make the setting easier to imagine: “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: After the poisonous plant is eaten, show a physical reaction instead of only saying it is harmful: “我的肚子疼了起来，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Make the request sound more urgent by adding an emotion and direct speech: “我着急地说：‘请给我一点儿灵芝吃吧！’”

### Encouragement
You clearly connect the poisonous plant to the safety warning at the end, so the main message is easy to understand. The recovery event is also presented in a clear order.
