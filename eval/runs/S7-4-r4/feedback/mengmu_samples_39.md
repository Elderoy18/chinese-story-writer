## Scene 1

> 古代的时候人觉得祭拜非常重要，孟子很小的时候，从早到晚一直祭拜，因为他妈妈告诉他祭拜很重要。虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜。他的同学们老是对他开玩笑了。孟子妈妈看了他的同学们对孟子不好的影响，所以孟子和孟子妈妈搬家了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，This phrase redundantly combines two time expressions; use the shorter time expression.
- 人：人们，This refers to a concrete group of people in that historical period, so the plural group noun is needed.
- 看了：看到了，The verb means “look,” while the intended meaning is “see/notice”; the completed resultative form is required here.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不好 -- “他的同学们对孟子不好的影响”：负面，identifies the influence specifically as negative and fits naturally before “影响”.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子很小的时候：有一个叫孟子的小男孩，他很小的时候，This is the character’s first mention, so introduce him with a full noun phrase before using a pronoun.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子妈妈搬家了：孟子和妈妈搬家了，Within the same clause, the second repeated name should be omitted because “妈妈” clearly refers to Mengzi’s mother.
- Coherence (Temporality): 老是对他开玩笑了：老是对他开玩笑，This is a repeated habit, so it takes the zero particle rather than completed-action 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜：因为他每天只祭拜，所以他没有朋友，The relationship is cause and effect, so use the paired connector for cause and result rather than combining contrast and cause connectors.
- Expressiveness: After the first mention of Mengzi’s worship, add a small action detail to show how seriously he does it, such as “他低着头，认真地祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: At the classmates’ teasing scene, add one short line of dialogue to make the moment more vivid, such as “同学们笑着说：‘你怎么每天都在祭拜？’”
- Expressiveness: When his mother notices the influence, add her feeling to explain her response, such as “妈妈很担心孟子会越来越孤单。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show a cause for Mengzi’s behavior and use a time phrase to show that he worships throughout the day. The final sentence also gives the move a clear reason, which helps the scene feel connected.

## Scene 2

> 孟子才十一岁，成为一个哲学家。孟子不是祭拜，而是看政治文章，难怪他成为了哲学家。不过，看过很多政治文章对他害得，他有很多问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 对他害得，他有很多问题：害得他有很多问题，害得 must be followed directly by the person affected and then the harmful result; 对他 cannot be placed before 害得.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 看 -- “而是看政治文章”：阅读，specifies reading written texts carefully rather than simply looking at them.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子不是祭拜：他不是祭拜，孟子 was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other male character competes for the reference, so use the pronoun 他 for this close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 孟子才十一岁，成为一个哲学家：孟子才十一岁，就成为了一个哲学家，becoming a philosopher is a completed change of state, so use 了; 才 with an unexpectedly young age naturally pairs with 就 before the achievement.
- Expressiveness: At “看政治文章,” add a small action to show how seriously he studies: “他坐在桌子前，认真地阅读政治文章。”
- Expressiveness: At “有很多问题,” include one simple thought or question to make his curiosity vivid: “他想：‘为什么有的人很有钱，有的人很穷？’”

### Encouragement
You used the contrast structure 不是……而是…… clearly to show the difference between worshipping and studying. Your final idea also shows that reading leads Mengzi to think and ask questions, which gives the scene a thoughtful direction.

## Scene 3

> 他一直问问题，但是不能解决那些问题。孟子长大，他开始能解决问题。他常常建议你不是学金融，而是学政治，因为如果你懂政治，你会成为高薪。后来孟子成为教授，他教学生很厉害。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 成为高薪：得到高薪，成为 is used for becoming an identity or role, while 高薪 means a high salary; 得到 correctly expresses receiving that salary.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 问问题 -- “他一直问问题”：提出问题，emphasizes actively raising questions for discussion or learning.
- 厉害 -- “他教学生很厉害”：很擅长教学生，states more precisely that he has strong teaching ability.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子长大，他开始能解决问题：他长大了，开始能解决问题，孟子 was named in the immediately previous sentence with no competing character, so use the pronoun for close re-occurrence; within the same sentence, omit the repeated pronoun before the second action.
- Coherence (Reference): 后来孟子成为教授，他教学生很厉害：后来他成为了教授，教学生很厉害，after the same character has remained the topic, use a pronoun for close re-occurrence and use zero form for the second action in the same sentence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 孟子长大：孟子长大了，this growing-up is a completed change of state before he begins solving problems, so it needs 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 孟子成为教授：孟子成为了教授，becoming a professor is a completed event in the later part of the narrative, so it needs 了.
- Expressiveness: After he can solve problems, add an internal feeling to show his growth, for example: “他觉得很有成就感。”
- Expressiveness: At the teaching point, add a short line of speech showing how he helps students, for example: “孟子说：‘别着急，我们一起想办法。’”
- Expressiveness: You could make his teaching skill more concrete with one classroom detail, for example: “学生听了他的解释，马上明白了。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show Mengzi’s development from asking questions to solving them and eventually becoming a professor. The contrast with 但是 also effectively shows that asking questions and answering them are different stages of learning.
