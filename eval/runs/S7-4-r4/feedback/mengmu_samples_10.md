## Scene 1

> 孟子小的时候，从早到晚都跟朋友玩。孟子和他的朋友喜欢玩在墓地里，装祭拜。孟子五岁的时候，跟妈妈搬家。他们搬家不是因为孟子玩得太多，而是因为妈妈认为他们的家离墓地太近。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 喜欢玩在墓地里：喜欢在墓地里玩，The place phrase should come before the verb it modifies.
- 装祭拜：假装祭拜，装 does not naturally introduce the action of performing a ritual; 假装 means “pretend to do” and can be followed by an action.
- 五岁的时候：五岁的时候，The structure ……的时候 requires 的 after a multi-syllable phrase.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和他的朋友喜欢在墓地里玩：他和朋友喜欢在墓地里玩，Use a pronoun for a recently mentioned character when no competing referent has appeared.
- Coherence (Temporality): 跟妈妈搬家：跟妈妈搬了家，This is a completed move at a specific age, so the completed action needs 了.
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery is mentioned, add a small visual or sound detail: “墓地里常常有人哭着祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: Show why the children copy the ritual by adding Mengzi’s thought: “孟子觉得祭拜很有趣。”
- Expressiveness: Show the mother’s concern with a short thought or line of speech: “妈妈想：‘这儿不适合孩子长大。’”

### Encouragement
- You used the contrast pattern “不是……而是……” clearly to explain the reason for the move.
- Your scene also establishes Mengzi’s childhood behaviour and the cemetery setting efficiently.

## Scene 2

> 妈妈认为，住离墓地太近，害得孟子的心气。孟子和孟母搬到子思老师的家，跟子思老师住。孟母帮子思打扫房间和做饭。子思帮孟母教她的儿子。因为孟子现在没有朋友，他老是读书。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 住离墓地太近：住在离墓地太近的地方，A residence verb needs 在 before the place phrase, and 地方 completes the description of the location.
- 害得孟子的心气：害得孟子心情不好，害得 must be followed by the affected person and a harmful result; 心情不好 states that result clearly.
- 老是读书：一直读书，老是 often expresses complaint or an unwanted repeated action, while the intended meaning here is a regular study habit.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 做饭 -- “孟母帮子思打扫房间和做饭”：准备饭菜，specifies preparing a whole meal rather than only cooking rice or one dish.
- 朋友 -- “因为孟子现在没有朋友”：玩伴，more specifically describes friends whom a child plays with.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬到子思老师的家：搬到了子思老师的家，The move is a completed narrative action, so 搬到 takes 了.
- Coherence (Reference): 他老是读书：老是读书，Because 孟子 is already named in the first half of this same sentence, use zero form rather than repeating a pronoun.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为孟子现在没有朋友：因为孟子现在没有朋友，所以，This is a cause-and-effect relationship, so add 所以 to complete the paired connector.
- Expressiveness: After the move, add a small detail showing how busy the mother is, such as “孟母忙了一整天，累得腰酸背痛。”
- Expressiveness: When describing Mengzi’s studying, add his feeling of loneliness: “孟子坐在窗边，觉得很孤单。”
- Expressiveness: You could show the mother’s hope with an inner thought: “孟母心想：‘希望孟子在这里能安心学习。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly show how the family’s new living situation changes Mengzi’s daily life. The use of 因为 also gives the final sentence a clear reason for his studying.

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子从五岁开始一直学哲学：他从五岁开始一直学哲学，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the previously mentioned character is still the only possible referent.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他成为很有名的哲学家：他成为了很有名的哲学家，Becoming a philosopher is a completed change of state in the narrative, so the verb needs 了.
- Expressiveness: After the first sentence, add a short quotation from the teacher to show his teaching idea, such as: 子思说：“学哲学能让你学会思考。”
- Expressiveness: In the sentence about reading and writing, add a concrete study detail to make the action easier to picture, such as: 孟子每天安静地坐在桌前，认真地读哲学书。
- Expressiveness: At the final sentence, add Mengzi’s feeling about his studies or success, such as: 孟子觉得学习虽然很辛苦，但是很有意思。

### Encouragement
You used the paired structure 不是……就是…… correctly to show Mengzi’s two regular study activities. The final sentence also clearly connects his early study of philosophy with his later achievement.
