## Scene 1

> 很久以前，古代的人很不知道什么植物能吃，也什么植物不能吃。所以很多人常常吃不安全的植物，吃不安全的植物对你的身体有坏处，很麻烦的情况。但是一个人，神农，要解决这个问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很不知道：不知道，Degree adverbs such as 很 do not normally modify the negative verb phrase 不知道; use 不知道 to state a lack of knowledge.
- 吃不安全的植物对你的身体有坏处，很麻烦的情况：吃不安全的植物对他们的身体有坏处，是一件很麻烦的事，The original ends with a noun phrase, 很麻烦的情况, without a predicate. Add 是一件……的事 to make it a complete statement.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不安全 -- “很多人常常吃不安全的植物”：有毒的，specifies that the plants contain poison and explains the physical danger more clearly in this story context

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一个人，神农：有一个叫神农的人，A character’s first mention should introduce the person clearly with a full noun phrase that includes the name.
- Coherence (Reference): 你的身体：他们的身体，The previous sentence refers to 很多人, so use a pronoun referring back to that group instead of shifting unexpectedly to the reader.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 吃不安全的植物对他们的身体有坏处，是一件很麻烦的事：所以，吃不安全的植物对他们的身体有坏处，是一件很麻烦的事，Add 所以 to show that the harmful effect is the result of eating the unsafe plants.
- Expressiveness: After “不安全的植物,” add a concrete consequence to show the danger: “很多人吃了以后常常生病。”
- Expressiveness: At the introduction of 神农, show his determination with a short thought or quote: “神农想：‘我一定要找出能吃的植物。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish the central problem—people do not know which plants are safe—and introduce 神农 as the person who will address it. The use of 所以 also shows that you are thinking about cause and effect in the story.

## Scene 2

> 他又聪明又努力，旅行很远找到很多植物。比方说，灵芝和夹竹桃。他一吃各种各样的植物就知道能不能吃这些植物。然后，神农写他的主意。他喜欢植物的味道，特别是花。神农遇到了一个黄色的花，一吃就觉得不舒服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 旅行很远：到很远的地方旅行，A destination should be expressed with a place phrase after 到; 旅行很远 does not clearly show where he travelled.
- 写他的主意：写下了自己的想法，写下 means to record an idea in writing; 写 by itself is not natural for recording a thought in this context.
- 遇到了一个黄色的花：看到了一朵黄色的花，Use the measure word 朵 for an individual flower, and 看到 is the natural verb for noticing a flower during a journey.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 努力 -- “他又聪明又努力”：勤奋，emphasizes that he works hard consistently rather than making only a single effort.
- 很远 -- “到很远的地方旅行”：遥远，emphasizes the great distance of the place he travelled to.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 然后，神农写下了自己的想法：然后，他写下了自己的想法，Use a pronoun because 神农 was the clear subject in the preceding sentence and no other character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农看到了一朵黄色的花：他看到了一朵黄色的花，Use a pronoun for the same character when he was mentioned in the immediately preceding sentence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 找到很多植物：找到了很多植物，The finding is a completed event in the narrative, so the final verb in this action sequence takes 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 一吃就觉得不舒服：一吃这种花，就觉得不舒服了，The final 了 marks the change into an uncomfortable state; it does not attach to the mental-state verb 觉得.
- Expressiveness: At the point where he sees the flower, add a visual detail: “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: When he becomes ill, show a physical reaction: “他肚子疼了起来，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Add his worried thought after the pain begins: “他想：我会死吗？”

### Encouragement
You clearly show that Shen Nong is both intelligent and hardworking, and the sequence from travelling, finding plants, tasting them, and feeling ill is easy to follow. The use of “一……就……” effectively shows that his discomfort happened immediately after eating the flower.

## Scene 3

> 发现了夹竹桃有毒。别人看到他，很快给他吃一个灵芝。神农一吃就觉得比较好。然后，他继续写他发现的信息。会告诉他的朋友哪一个植物能不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 写：记下，In this context, the action is recording information that Shen Nong has discovered. “记下” specifically means to record and keep information, while “写” alone does not express that meaning clearly.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很快 -- “很快给他吃一个灵芝”：立刻，emphasizes that the helper acted without any delay；马上，gives a clear sense of immediate action in the rescue.
- 信息 -- “他发现的信息”：结果，more specifically refers to the results of his plant discoveries；发现的内容，makes clear that he is recording what he learned about the plants.
- 哪一个 -- “哪一个植物能不能吃”：哪一种，more precisely classifies plants by type or species.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 别人看到他：一个人看到他，This is the first mention of this helper, so use a full noun phrase to introduce the person. “别人” means other people in contrast with a particular person or group, but no such contrast has been established here.
- Coherence (Temporality): 给他吃一个灵芝：给他吃了一个灵芝，The helper’s action of giving Shen Nong the lingzhi is completed, so the final verb needs 了.
- Expressiveness: After the discovery that the flower is poisonous, add a physical reaction to show Shen Nong’s suffering, such as “神农肚子疼了起来，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Make the helper more vivid when he appears, such as “一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人走过来，给神农吃了一个灵芝。”
- Expressiveness: At the end, add a short line of speech to show what Shen Nong will tell his friends, such as “灵芝是药，可以吃；夹竹桃有毒，不能吃。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the important change from discovering danger to feeling better after eating lingzhi. You also use “然后” to move the events forward in a logical order.
