## Scene 1

> 古代，孟子跟孟母住在墓地的旁边。可是孟母注意，住在墓地的旁边影响孟子。他常常跟朋友们祭拜玩，所以孟母决定他们会搬家。这次，他们住在城市里的商店的旁边，从早到晚很吵。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 注意：注意到，when the verb means noticing a fact or situation, it needs the result complement 到.
- 祭拜玩：学着祭拜，祭拜 is a verb, so it cannot directly modify 玩 in this order; 学着祭拜 expresses copying the ritual as a game.
- 决定他们会搬家：决定搬家，决定 is followed directly by the action that is decided; do not repeat the people who will perform it.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 吵 -- “从早到晚很吵”：嘈杂，specifies that the shops create a mixture of loud, busy sounds.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子跟孟母：有一个小男孩叫孟子，他和妈妈，at first mention, introduce the main character with a name and a brief identity before using shorter references.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟母：她，when this character was mentioned in the immediately previous sentence and no female character competes, use a pronoun rather than repeating the name.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 可是：—，可是 introduces contrast, but the previous sentence does not set up an expectation that the next sentence opposes; begin the observation directly instead.
- Expressiveness: At the cemetery scene, add a small visual detail about what the children copy, such as: “他和朋友们学着大人低头，还假装哭。”
- Expressiveness: Show Mengmu’s concern with an internal thought after she notices the effect: “孟母心想：‘如果一直住在这儿，孟子会学坏。’”
- Expressiveness: Make the city scene more vivid by describing the shop sounds: “商店里有人叫卖，有人讨价还价，街上嘈杂极了。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the change from the quiet cemetery setting to the noisy city setting, and the reason for the family’s move is easy to understand.

## Scene 2

> 城市也有点儿危险。害得孟母只好一直陪孟子。孟子也没有朋友，难怪他老是看起来不高兴。住在商店的旁边不是适合孟子，而是让他觉得很伤心。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不是适合孟子：不适合孟子，For a negative evaluation with “适合,” use “不适合”; “不是适合” would need a following noun phrase such as a place or situation.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不高兴 -- “难怪他老是看起来不高兴”：闷闷不乐，describes a person who seems quietly unhappy over a period of time.
- 危险 -- “城市也有点儿危险”：不太安全，specifies that the place may not be safe while keeping the mild meaning of “有点儿.”

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 孟子也没有朋友：孟子没有朋友，“也” is additive and needs an earlier comparable statement; no one else has been stated to have no friends.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 而是让他觉得很伤心：因为这让他觉得很伤心，His sadness gives the reason that living there is unsuitable, rather than correcting one idea with another.
- Expressiveness: After the sentence about danger, add one concrete detail about the street: “街上人来人往，声音很吵。”
- Expressiveness: Show Mengzi’s feeling through a small action after “没有朋友”: “他常常一个人站在窗边，看着外面的孩子玩儿。”
- Expressiveness: Add Mengmu’s inner thought or speech after the final sentence: “孟母心想：‘这儿真的不适合孟子。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show Mengzi’s unhappiness and use “难怪” effectively to link his lack of friends to how he appears.
- “只好一直陪孟子” also communicates that Mengmu feels responsible for protecting him.

## Scene 3

> 所以，孟母和孟子搬到学校的旁边住。才五岁孟子开始学哲学。在学校，孟子不是看书，就是练习写字。将来，他成为中国的一个非常有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 才五岁孟子开始学哲学：孟子才五岁就开始学哲学，This construction places the subject before the age phrase and requires 就 after 才 + age to show that the action began unusually early.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬到学校的旁边住：搬到学校的旁边住了，The move and beginning to live there are completed narrative actions; in a series of actions, only the last verb takes 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为：成为了，Becoming a philosopher is presented as a completed change in the past, so it needs 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 将来：后来，This is a past historical narrative, so 后来 introduces the next event in the sequence; 将来 refers to the future.
- Expressiveness: After the move, add one sensory detail about the school environment to show why it is better for studying, such as “学校旁边很安静，常常能听到学生读书的声音。”
- Expressiveness: When Meng Mu sees Meng Zi studying, add her feeling or a short line of speech, such as “孟母很高兴，说：‘这儿的环境很适合学习。’”

### Encouragement
