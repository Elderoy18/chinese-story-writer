## Scene 1

> 古代时候，人不知道他们能吃什么植物，常常一吃就死。但是不知道为什么，没有办法。所以有一天，神农出去试一试很多种植物，告诉人什么植物是安全什么是危险。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代时候：在古代，The original repeats the idea of time because 古代 already names a historical period.
- 人：人们，This refers to the people living in that situation, not people in general.
- 人：人们，This again refers to a concrete group who need the information.
- 什么植物是安全什么是危险：哪些植物安全，哪些植物危险，Use two parallel clauses to show the two categories clearly.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：一个叫神农的男人，This is the character’s first mention, so introduce him with both his name and a short identifying noun phrase.
- Coherence (Temporality): 出去试一试很多种植物：出去试了很多种植物，Trying many kinds of plants is a completed series of actions here, so the final verb takes 了; the reduplicated form suggests one brief tentative try and conflicts with 很多种.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 但是不知道为什么：他们也不知道为什么，The earlier event and not knowing the reason do not form a contrast; 也 adds another related problem instead.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 所以有一天：于是，有一天，Use 于是 to show that Shennong’s action follows from the people’s problem; it links the cause to the response naturally.
- Expressiveness: After 常常一吃就死, add the people’s fear, such as “人们都很害怕，不知道怎么办。” This shows the emotional impact of the danger.
- Expressiveness: Before Shennong acts, add his inner thought, such as “神农想：我一定要找出能吃的植物。” This makes his reason for testing plants more vivid.
- Expressiveness: When he goes out, add one simple setting detail, such as “他走到高山上，看见了很多不同的植物。” This helps the reader picture his search.

### Encouragement
- You clearly explain the main problem first and then introduce Shennong’s response, which gives the scene a strong purpose.
- The pattern 一吃就死 effectively shows the immediate danger of eating unknown plants.

## Scene 2

> 发现有的植物对身体非常好，特别是茶，让肚子感觉又暖和又清楚。他告诉朋友：“你吃哪种植物，这就要看你的口味。比方说，你爱吃甜的，可能喜欢糖。”他最喜欢吃花，除了夹竹桃以外，什么都花想吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 让肚子感觉又暖和又清楚：让人觉得又暖和又清醒，In this causative structure, the affected experiencer should be a person who can “feel”; a stomach can be warm but cannot “feel clear.” “清醒” describes a person’s mental state.
- 什么都花想吃：什么花都想吃，In the “什么……都……” pattern, the noun being referred to must follow “什么,” so “花” belongs directly after it.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好 -- “有的植物对身体非常好”：很有益，states more precisely that the plants benefit health；有助于身体健康，specifies that they support physical health.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After mentioning tea, add a sensory result to make its effect more vivid, for example: “喝了以后，他觉得身体暖暖的，头脑也清醒了。”
- Expressiveness: When he speaks to his friend, add a short reaction or feeling to show why he wants to share his discovery, for example: “他高兴地告诉朋友：‘我发现茶对身体很有益！’”

### Encouragement
- You used direct speech to show the character sharing his ideas, which makes the scene more lively.
- The contrast with “除了夹竹桃以外” clearly shows that the character treats that flower differently from the others.

## Scene 3

> 他发现这个口味，一百岁时候，不小心吃了毒的花。一吃就开始死。但是生病时候告诉人什么植物是药，什么植物是毒。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一百岁时候：一百岁的时候，A time expression followed by 时候 requires 的.
- 毒的花：有毒的花，Use 有毒的 to describe a poisonous plant; 毒的植物 is not the standard form.
- 一吃就开始死：一吃就快要死了，死 describes an endpoint, so it does not normally follow 开始; 快要……了 expresses that the change of state is about to happen.
- 告诉人：告诉人们，Here the narrator means a concrete group of people, so 人们 is needed rather than the generic noun 人.
- 什么植物是毒：什么植物有毒，毒 is a noun here; 有毒 is the adjective phrase meaning “poisonous.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 吃 -- “不小心吃了毒的花”：误食了，specifically means accidentally eating or ingesting something that should not be eaten.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 这个口味：一种花的味道，This is the first mention of the taste, so 这个 does not have a clear earlier noun to refer to; introduce it with a full noun phrase.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉：告诉了，This is a completed action in the narrative, so it needs 了 after the verb.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 但是：删除，But does not express contrast here; the time phrase already connects the action of becoming ill with telling people.
- Expressiveness: After the poisonous flower is eaten, add a physical reaction, such as “他一吃，肚子就疼了起来，疼得直冒冷汗。”
- Expressiveness: Show his fear with an inner thought at the point where he nearly dies: “他想：‘我会不会死？’”
- Expressiveness: Make the information he gives people more lively through quoted speech: “他说：‘这种植物是药，可以吃；那种花有毒，不能吃。’”

### Encouragement
You used the 一……就…… pattern effectively to show that the danger happened immediately after eating. You also clearly tried to distinguish plants that can be used as medicine from plants that are poisonous.
