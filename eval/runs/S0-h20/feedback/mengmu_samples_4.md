## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，孟子和孟母住了在靠墓地的家。孩子才五岁就每天在墓地祭拜了。在那个城市，孟母从早到晚都找工作，可是找不到，害得孟母没有钱。难怪她跟孩子搬家，原来买不了房。他们搬了到别一个城市找工作。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “古代的时候”  
Formula: 在 + historical time period  
Correct: “在古代，孟子和孟母……”

Incorrect phrase: “孟子和孟母住了在靠墓地的家”  
Formula: Subject + 住在 + place; 靠近 + place + 的 + house/place  
Correct: “孟子和孟母住在靠近墓地的房子里。”

Incorrect phrase: “孩子才五岁就每天在墓地祭拜了”  
Formula: Subject + 才 + age + 就 + verb; habitual action + 每天 + verb (no 了); 祭拜 + object  
Correct: “孟子才五岁，就每天在墓地祭拜祖先。”  
“才五岁就……” is used very well here: it emphasizes that he was very young when he began doing this.

Incorrect phrase: “从早到晚都找工作，可是找不到”  
Formula: 从早到晚 + 都 + verb, but use 一直 when emphasizing continuous effort; 找不到 + object  
Correct: “孟母从早到晚一直找工作，可是找不到工作。”  
You can omit the second “工作” in more natural writing: “可是一直找不到。”

Incorrect phrase: “害得孟母没有钱”  
Formula: Result/cause + 害得 + person + result complement  
Correct: “结果孟母没钱了。”  
“害得” is grammatically possible, but it usually suggests that another person or event caused harm. Here, “结果” is more natural.

Incorrect phrase: “他们搬了到别一个城市找工作”  
Formula: 搬到 + place + (去) + verb; 另一个 + noun  
Correct: “他们搬到另一个城市去找工作。”  

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “靠”  
Context: “靠墓地的家”  
Alternatives: “靠近墓地的房子” or “墓地附近的房子.” “靠” is informal and usually needs a clearer location structure. “靠近” means “close to” and is more precise, while “附近” means “nearby” and sounds very natural for describing an area.

Word: “祭拜”  
Context: “每天在墓地祭拜祖先”  
Alternatives: “祭奠祖先” or “扫墓.” “祭拜” is a general word for worshipping or making offerings to ancestors or deities. “祭奠” is more formal and specifically expresses mourning and honoring the dead. “扫墓” refers to visiting and tending a grave, often during Qingming Festival.

Word: “找不到”  
Context: “可是找不到工作”  
Alternatives: “一直没找到工作” or “求职不顺.” “找不到工作” is clear and appropriate. “一直没找到” emphasizes that the unsuccessful search continued for a long time, while “求职不顺” is more advanced and means that the job search has not gone smoothly.

Word: “没有钱”  
Context: “孟母没有钱”  
Alternatives: “手头拮据” or “经济困难.” “没有钱” is simple and direct. “手头拮据” means having little money available at the moment, while “经济困难” is more formal and describes financial hardship more generally.

Word: “搬家”  
Context: “她跟孩子搬家”  
Alternatives: “迁居” or “搬迁.” “搬家” is the everyday word for moving house. “迁居” is more literary and often used in historical narratives, while “搬迁” is more formal and can refer to a family, company, or community moving to a new location.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You used a clear story sequence and correctly used the useful pattern “才五岁就……,” which makes Mengzi’s young age stand out.  
Next time, focus on word order with location phrases, especially “住在……” and “搬到……”.

## Scene 2

> 这个城市老是很吵，不是人吵架，就是都叫，害得孟子一直不能睡觉。在那儿，孟母也找不到工作。孟子和妈妈要又一个次搬了家。在第三个城市，孟母找到工作，在商店做衣服。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “不是人吵架，就是都叫” / Formula: 不是 + A +，就是 + B; the subjects and verbs should be clear and parallel / Correct: “不是有人吵架，就是有人大声叫喊。”  
“都叫” is unclear; “有人大声叫喊” clearly describes the noisy activity.

Incorrect phrase: “孟子和妈妈要又一个次搬了家” / Formula: Subject + 又 + V. + 了 + Num. + MW. + Object; for a completed move, use 搬了……次家 / Correct: “孟子和妈妈又搬了一次家。”  
“要” is unnecessary here because the move has already happened, and “一个次” should be “一次.”

Incorrect phrase: “在第三个城市，孟母找到工作” / Formula: Subject + V. + 了 + Object, when reporting a completed event / Correct: “在第三个城市，孟母找到了工作。”  
Use “找到了” to show that she successfully found a job.

Incorrect phrase: “在商店做衣服” / Formula: 在 + Place + V. + Object; use 做衣服 for making clothes, or 卖衣服 for selling them / Correct: “在一家商店里做衣服。”  
Adding “一家” and “里” makes the location sound more complete and natural.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “老是” / Context: “这个城市老是很吵” / Alternatives: “总是” or “一直” / “老是” often expresses annoyance or complaint, so it is possible here. “总是” is more neutral, while “一直” emphasizes that the noise continues without stopping. For example: “这个城市总是很吵。”

Word: “吵” / Context: “这个城市老是很吵” / Alternatives: “嘈杂” or “喧闹” / “吵” is common and simple. “嘈杂” describes a place with many mixed, unpleasant sounds, while “喧闹” emphasizes a lively, loud atmosphere. “嘈杂” fits this negative context especially well.

Word: “叫” / Context: “就是都叫” / Alternatives: “叫喊” or “吵闹” / “叫” can mean “call” or “shout,” so it is not specific enough here. “叫喊” means shouting loudly, while “吵闹” refers to making noise, often through loud talking or arguing.

Word: “找不到工作” / Context: “孟母也找不到工作” / Alternatives: “一直没找到工作” or “很难找到工作” / “找不到工作” states the result. “一直没找到工作” highlights that she has been searching for some time, and “很难找到工作” emphasizes that finding employment is difficult.

Word: “做衣服” / Context: “在商店做衣服” / Alternatives: “做裁缝” or “缝制衣服” / “做衣服” is understandable and means making clothes. “做裁缝” means working as a tailor, while “缝制衣服” is a more formal word for sewing and producing clothing.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You clearly explained why Mengzi and his mother needed to move again, and the story has a logical sequence from the noisy city to the new job.  
Next time, focus on using “了” for completed events and making the subject of each action clear.

## Scene 3

> 孟子在那个商店也找了到工作，可是不是做衣服的工作，而是喜欢的工作。孟子学习馆学了十五年，他的学习和他的旅行经验很重要，对他很多好处。他是大人的时候，他成为很有名的哲人。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “孟子在那个商店也找了到工作” / Formula: 找到 + object; 在 + place + 找到 + object / Correct: “孟子也在那个商店找到了工作。”  
“找了到” is not a possible verb form; use the result complement 到 after 找.

Incorrect phrase: “可是……而是……” / Formula: 不是 + A + 而是 + B / Correct: “可是他不是做衣服的，而是做自己喜欢的工作。”  
When contrasting two choices, use the full pattern 不是……而是……, not 可是……而是…….

Incorrect phrase: “孟子学习馆学了十五年” / Formula: 在 + place + 学习 + duration; or 在 + place + 学了 + duration / Correct: “孟子在学习馆学习了十五年。”  
You need 在 before the place, and 学习馆 should be the location rather than directly following 孟子.

Incorrect phrase: “他的学习和他的旅行经验很重要，对他很多好处” / Formula: A + 对 + somebody + 很有好处 / Correct: “他的学习和旅行经验对他很有好处。”  
Use 对……很有好处 to say that something benefits someone. Also, repeating 他的 is unnecessary here.

Incorrect phrase: “他是大人的时候” / Formula: 长大以后 / 他成为大人以后 / Correct: “他长大以后……”  
“是大人” is understandable, but 长大以后 is much more natural for “when he became an adult.”

Incorrect phrase: “他成为很有名的哲人” / Formula: 成为 + 一名 + adjective + noun / Correct: “他成为了一名很有名的哲学家。”  
Add 一名 before a profession. 哲学家 is the standard word for “philosopher”; 哲人 is more literary and means a wise person.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “商店” / Context: “在那个商店找到了工作” / Alternatives: “店铺” or “服装店.” 商店 is a general, everyday word for a store. 店铺 is slightly more formal and can sound more descriptive in a story. Since the job involves making clothes, 服装店 is more specific and gives the reader a clearer picture.

Word: “喜欢的工作” / Context: “做自己喜欢的工作” / Alternatives: “自己感兴趣的工作” or “自己热爱的工作.” 喜欢的工作 is simple and correct. 感兴趣的 emphasizes curiosity and interest, while 热爱的 is stronger: it suggests he is deeply passionate about the work.

Word: “学习馆” / Context: “在学习馆学习了十五年” / Alternatives: “书院” or “学堂.” 学习馆 is understandable but not a common name for a place of study. 书院 is especially suitable for an ancient Chinese setting and has a traditional, historical feeling. 学堂 is also an old-fashioned word for a school.

Word: “旅行经验” / Context: “他的学习和旅行经验” / Alternatives: “游历经历” or “阅历.” 旅行经验 refers to practical experience gained while travelling. 游历 is more literary and often used for travelling to learn about different places. 阅历 means broad life experience and fits well when describing why someone became wise.

Word: “很有名” / Context: “一名很有名的哲学家” / Alternatives: “著名的” or “享有盛名的.” 很有名 is common and natural in conversation. 著名的 is more concise and appropriate in writing. 享有盛名的 is more formal and means that someone has an excellent, widely recognized reputation.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You clearly expressed Mengzi’s path from finding meaningful work to gaining learning and travel experience, and then becoming a philosopher.  
Next time, focus on sentence patterns with result complements (找到) and contrasts (不是……而是……).
