## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子。他们住在墓地的旁边，所以孟子老是祭拜。孟子不是祭拜，就是跟朋友聊天。他很喜欢他的城市，但是城市有才两个学校，都很小。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “古代的时候” / Formula: 在 + historical time period / Correct: “在古代”  
“古代的时候” is understandable, but “在古代” is more natural and concise.

Incorrect phrase: “一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子。” / Formula: 有 + 一个 + 叫 + name + 的 + noun / Correct: “在古代，有一个叫孟子的男孩子和他的妈妈住在中国。”  
Introduce the character naturally when he first appears. Also, “和” is more neutral in writing than “跟.”

Incorrect phrase: “住在墓地的旁边” / Formula: 住在 + place / Correct: “住在墓地旁边” or “住在离墓地很近的地方”  
“墓地旁边” does not need 的. The second option emphasizes that their home was close to the cemetery.

Incorrect phrase: “孟子老是祭拜” / Formula: Subject + 常常/经常 + verb + object / Correct: “孟子常常祭拜祖先。”  
“老是” often sounds negative, as if the speaker is complaining. Use “常常” or “经常” for a neutral habit. Also, 祭拜 usually needs an object, such as “祖先.”

Incorrect phrase: “孟子不是祭拜，就是跟朋友聊天。” / Formula: 不是 + verb phrase A +，就是 + verb phrase B / Correct: “孟子不是祭拜祖先，就是跟朋友聊天。”  
The structure is good, but the first verb phrase needs the object “祖先.”

Incorrect phrase: “城市有才两个学校” / Formula: Place + 才 + number + measure word + noun / Correct: “这个城市才有两所学校，而且都很小。”  
“才” should come before “有.” Use 所 as the measure word for schools. “这个城市” is clearer than repeating 城市 without a modifier.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “男孩子” / Context: “一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。” / Alternatives: “小男孩” is more natural for a young child and sounds warm in a story. “少年” refers to an older boy, usually a teenager, and has a slightly more literary feeling. “男孩子” is correct but fairly general.

Word: “墓地的旁边” / Context: “他们住在墓地的旁边。” / Alternatives: “墓地附近” means “near the cemetery” and sounds concise and natural. “墓地边” is especially common in storytelling. Both are smoother than “墓地的旁边.”

Word: “祭拜” / Context: “孟子常常祭拜祖先。” / Alternatives: “祭祀” is more formal and is often used for traditional religious or ancestral ceremonies. “拜祭” is also common and emphasizes paying respects, especially to ancestors or deceased family members. “祭拜” is neutral and commonly used.

Word: “聊天” / Context: “跟朋友聊天。” / Alternatives: “闲聊” emphasizes casual, relaxed conversation without an important purpose. “谈天” is a little more literary and often describes friendly conversation. “聊天” is the most everyday and general word.

Word: “很小” / Context: “两所学校，都很小。” / Alternatives: “规模很小” specifically describes the school’s size, number of students, or facilities, and is more precise. “规模不大” is a softer, more natural way to say that the schools are not large. “很小” is simple but can sound vague for schools.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You used a clear story sequence and included an interesting contrast between Mengzi’s life near the cemetery and the limited number of schools in his city.  
Next time, focus on introducing characters in the first sentence and choosing “常常” instead of “老是” for neutral repeated actions.

## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈不是不喜欢他们的城市，而是想孟子有很好的教育。他们搬家到有很多学校的城市。他们从早到晚走路，孟子一直抱怨。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “而是想孟子有很好的教育” / Formula: Subject + 想让 + Person + 有/得到 + Object; or Subject + 是为了 + Verb phrase / Correct: “而是想让孟子得到良好的教育。”  
“不是……而是……” is possible here because it contrasts the mother’s reason for moving: she does not dislike the city; she wants better education for Mencius.

Incorrect phrase: “他们搬家到有很多学校的城市” / Formula: Subject + 搬到 + Place / Correct: “他们搬到了一座有很多学校的城市。”  
Use 搬到 + place, rather than 搬家到. Adding 一座 makes “city” sound more natural.

Incorrect phrase: “他们从早到晚走路” / Formula: Subject + 从早到晚 + 走路/赶路 / Correct: “他们从早到晚都在赶路。”  
The original is understandable, but 都在赶路 sounds more natural if you mean they spent the whole day travelling on foot.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “很好的教育” / Context: “想让孟子得到很好的教育” / Alternatives: “良好的教育” or “更好的教育.” 很好的 is simple and understandable. 良好的 is more formal and natural in writing, while 更好的 clearly emphasizes that the new city offers an improvement over the previous place.

Word: “有很多学校的城市” / Context: “搬到了一座有很多学校的城市” / Alternatives: “教育资源丰富的城市.” 有很多学校 directly describes the number of schools. 教育资源丰富 means the city has abundant educational resources, such as good schools, teachers, libraries, and learning opportunities.

Word: “走路” / Context: “他们从早到晚走路” / Alternatives: “赶路” or “长途跋涉.” 走路 simply means “walk.” 赶路 emphasizes travelling in order to reach a destination, while 长途跋涉 is more literary and highlights the difficulty of a long journey.

Word: “一直抱怨” / Context: “孟子一直抱怨” / Alternatives: “不停地抱怨” or “叫苦不迭.” 一直抱怨 is natural and clearly shows repeated complaining. 不停地抱怨 is slightly stronger, while 叫苦不迭 is more idiomatic and vivid, suggesting that someone repeatedly complains about hardship.

Word: “妈妈” / Context: “他的妈妈” / Alternatives: “母亲” or “孟母.” 妈妈 is warm and conversational. 母亲 is more formal and suitable for narrative writing, while 孟母 is concise and especially appropriate in a story about Mencius.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You clearly expressed the mother’s motivation for moving and used “不是……而是……” to create a meaningful contrast.  
Next time, focus on using natural verb patterns such as “想让……得到……” and “搬到 + place.”

## Scene 3

> 他难怪抱怨，他想他的朋友，因为他不要离开他的城市。他的妈妈找到孟子一个老师。跟老师孟子学最重要的文学，所以他成为哲学家。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “他难怪抱怨” / Formula: 因为 + reason，(所以) + someone + 难怪 + reaction / Correct: “因为他不想离开自己的城市，所以他抱怨。”  
“难怪” needs a reason that explains why something is unsurprising. Here, simply saying he complained is clearer.

Incorrect phrase: “他想他的朋友” / Formula: 想念 + person / Correct: “他想念他的朋友。”  
想 can mean “to think” or “to miss,” but 想念 is more specific for missing people.

Incorrect phrase: “因为他不要离开他的城市” / Formula: 因为 + subject + 不想 + verb / Correct: “因为他不想离开自己的城市。”  
不要 is usually used for commands (“don’t…”), while 不想 expresses one’s own unwillingness.

Incorrect phrase: “他的妈妈找到孟子一个老师” / Formula: subject + 给 + person + 找到/找了 + object / Correct: “他的妈妈给孟子找了一位老师。”  
Use 给孟子 before the verb, and 一位 is the natural measure word for a teacher.

Incorrect phrase: “跟老师孟子学最重要的文学” / Formula: subject + 跟 + person + 学习 + object / Correct: “孟子跟老师学习重要的文学。”  
The subject 孟子 should come first, and 跟老师 should be placed before 学习.

Incorrect phrase: “所以他成为哲学家” / Formula: subject + 后来 + 成为 + profession / Correct: “后来，他成为了一位哲学家。”  
Becoming a philosopher happened later, so 后来 makes the timeline clearer. Also, studying literature does not directly explain becoming a philosopher, so avoid using 所以 here.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “抱怨” / Context: “他抱怨。” / Alternatives: “埋怨” is stronger and often means blaming someone or something; “表达不满” is more formal and means “to express dissatisfaction.” 抱怨 is a common, neutral word for complaining.

Word: “想念” / Context: “他想念他的朋友。” / Alternatives: “思念” is more literary and emotional, often used when people are separated for a long time; “挂念” emphasizes worrying about and caring for someone. 想念 is the most natural everyday choice.

Word: “离开” / Context: “离开自己的城市。” / Alternatives: “搬离” specifically means moving away from a place, especially a home or city; “告别” is more emotional and means saying goodbye to a person or place. 离开 is general and neutral.

Word: “找到” / Context: “给孟子找了一位老师。” / Alternatives: “请” a teacher means to hire or invite a teacher, which is more precise in this context; “聘请” is formal and usually used for hiring professionals. 找 is general, while 请 better shows that the mother arranged a teacher for him.

Word: “最重要的文学” / Context: “学习重要的文学。” / Alternatives: “经典文学” means classic or canonical literature; “文学作品” means literary works, such as poems, novels, or essays. 最重要的文学 sounds vague, while 经典文学 is more natural if you mean famous and influential texts.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You clearly communicated the main story: Mencius misses his friends, his mother helps him, and he later becomes a philosopher.  
Next time, focus on word order and choosing between 不要 (“don’t”) and 不想 (“don’t want to”).
