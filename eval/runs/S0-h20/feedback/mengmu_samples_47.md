## Scene 1

> 这个男孩子叫孟子，孟子是古代的人。孟子的妈妈觉得住在哪儿对童年很重要，所以他们常常搬了家。她望子成龙，所以孟子的妈妈安排他的生活。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “孟子是古代的人” / Formula: 在 + historical time period，+ person + 是/生活在 + …的人 / Correct: “孟子是古代人” or, more naturally, “孟子生活在古代。”

Incorrect phrase: “觉得住在哪儿对童年很重要” / Formula: 觉得 + clause; for a general statement, use 居住环境/住的地方 + 对 + child’s growth + 很重要 / Correct: “觉得住的地方对孩子的成长很重要。”

Incorrect phrase: “他们常常搬了家” / Formula: 常常/经常 + verb (no 了 for habitual actions) / Correct: “他们常常搬家。”

Incorrect phrase: “她望子成龙，所以孟子的妈妈安排他的生活” / Formula: Subject + 希望/望子成龙，所以 + same subject + 安排 + possessive pronoun + life / Correct: “孟子的妈妈望子成龙，所以她安排孟子的生活。”  
“她” is clearer here because the mother has already been introduced, and “他的生活” could otherwise refer to a different male person.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “古代的人” / Context: “孟子是古代的人。” / Alternatives: “古代人” is the natural everyday term for a person from ancient times. “生活在古代的人” is more descriptive and emphasizes when the person lived.

Word: “住在哪儿” / Context: “住在哪儿对童年很重要。” / Alternatives: “居住环境” is more advanced and refers to the overall living environment, including the neighborhood and surroundings. “住的地方” is simpler and natural, referring specifically to the place where someone lives.

Word: “童年” / Context: “对童年很重要。” / Alternatives: “成长” is more suitable here because it refers to a child’s development, not only the period of childhood. “成长环境” emphasizes how a child’s surroundings influence their development.

Word: “常常” / Context: “他们常常搬家。” / Alternatives: “多次” means “many times” and is useful when you want to stress the number of moves. “频繁地” is more formal and emphasizes that something happened very often in a short period.

Word: “安排他的生活” / Context: “孟子的妈妈安排他的生活。” / Alternatives: “规划孟子的成长” sounds more purposeful and emphasizes planning his education and development. “为孟子选择合适的环境” is more specific, emphasizing the mother’s choice of places to live.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You did especially well in clearly connecting the mother’s belief about a child’s environment with her decision to move often.  
Next time, focus on using **常常/经常 + verb** without **了**, and use pronouns carefully so the reference is clear.

## Scene 2

> 因为他们常常搬了家，中国人说，孟母三迁。孟子没有爸爸，所以，他的妈妈更重要。从这儿到那儿，妈妈教孟子很多技能。先她让孟子跟朋友玩儿，然后教他怎么买又便宜又好质量的东西。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “因为他们常常搬了家，中国人说，孟母三迁。” / Formula: 因为 + specific subject + 为了/原因 + V. 了 + number + measure word + object；人们 + 把 + event + 称为 + name / Correct: “因为孟母为了教育孟子搬了三次家，人们把这件事称为‘孟母三迁’。”  
“他们” does not clearly refer to anyone, and “常常” (a habitual action) should not be used with “搬了家” here. Since 孟母三迁 means that she moved three times, “搬了三次家” is more accurate.

Incorrect phrase: “孟子没有爸爸，所以，他的妈妈更重要。” / Formula: A + 对 + B + 来说 + 更 + adjective / Correct: “孟子没有爸爸，所以妈妈对孟子来说更重要。”  
“更重要” needs a clearer perspective or comparison. Also, do not put a comma directly after “所以”.

Incorrect phrase: “从这儿到那儿，妈妈教孟子很多技能。” / Formula: 在 + event/process + 的过程中，谁 + V. + object / Correct: “在搬家的过程中，孟母教了孟子很多生活技能。”  
“从这儿到那儿” literally means “from here to there,” so it does not clearly express the process of moving several times.

Incorrect phrase: “先她让孟子跟朋友玩儿” / Formula: subject + 先 + V.，然后 + V. / Correct: “她先让孟子跟朋友玩儿，然后教他……”  
The adverb “先” should come after the subject.

Incorrect phrase: “教他怎么买又便宜又好质量的东西。” / Formula: 买 + 又便宜又 + adjective + 的 + noun / Correct: “教他怎么买又便宜又质量好的东西。”  
In Chinese, say “质量好” (“good quality”), not “好质量.”

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “中国人” / Context: “中国人说，孟母三迁。” / Alternatives: “人们” or “后人.” “中国人” means Chinese people in general, which is very broad. “人们” is more natural when referring generally to people who use an expression, while “后人” means later generations and fits a historical story especially well.

Word: “妈妈” / Context: “妈妈教孟子很多技能。” / Alternatives: “孟母.” “妈妈” is warm and natural in everyday speech, but “孟母” is more appropriate in a historical or literary story about Mencius because it is her established name.

Word: “技能” / Context: “教孟子很多技能。” / Alternatives: “生活技能” or “本领.” “技能” often refers to practical or professional skills. “生活技能” specifically means everyday life skills, such as shopping wisely; “本领” is broader and can mean useful abilities.

Word: “便宜” / Context: “又便宜又质量好的东西。” / Alternatives: “实惠.” “便宜” focuses only on low price. “实惠” means good value for the price—reasonably priced and worthwhile—so it sounds especially natural when talking about shopping.

Word: “朋友” / Context: “跟朋友玩儿。” / Alternatives: “小伙伴” or “同伴.” “朋友” is correct and general. “小伙伴” sounds more natural for children playing together, while “同伴” is slightly more formal and emphasizes someone who accompanies you.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You did well connecting the events with “先……然后……,” which gives your short story a clear sequence.  
Next time, focus on making the subject clear and using more precise phrases for historical events, such as “孟母搬了三次家.”

## Scene 3

> 最后，她让孟子学写作。他不是写作就是看书，难怪他很聪明。现在在中国，孟子很有名。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “他不是写作就是看书” / Formula: 不是 + A + 就是 + B means “either A or B”; use it only when presenting two alternatives. For habitual actions, use 一边……一边……, or simply list the actions. / Correct: “他不是写作，就是看书。” This sentence is grammatically correct, but it means he was always doing one of these two things. A more natural version is: “他常常写作、看书。”

Incorrect phrase: “现在在中国，孟子很有名” / Formula: Time expression + Subject + Predicate; avoid placing both 现在 and 在中国 before the subject when the location is not essential. / Correct: “现在，孟子在中国很有名。” More naturally: “如今，孟子在中国非常有名。”

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “最后” / Context: “最后，她让孟子学写作。” / Alternatives: “后来” is better for the next event in a story and makes the narrative flow naturally. “最后” emphasizes the final event or conclusion of a sequence. You could write: “后来，她让孟子学习写作。”

Word: “让” / Context: “她让孟子学写作。” / Alternatives: “鼓励” means “encourage” and sounds more positive, while “教” means “teach” and emphasizes that she personally taught him. “让” can mean “make/ask someone to do something,” so it may sound somewhat commanding. For example: “她鼓励孟子学习写作。”

Word: “学写作” / Context: “她让孟子学写作。” / Alternatives: “学习写作” is more complete and slightly more formal. “练习写作” emphasizes practising writing skills rather than learning the subject generally.

Word: “看书” / Context: “他不是写作就是看书。” / Alternatives: “阅读” is more formal and literary; it focuses on the activity of reading. “博览群书” means reading a wide range of books and suggests that he was very well read, but it is more advanced. For example: “他常常写作、阅读。”

Word: “很有名” / Context: “孟子很有名。” / Alternatives: “著名” is a more formal adjective meaning “famous.” “家喻户晓” means “known to every household” and strongly emphasizes widespread fame. For example: “孟子是中国著名的思想家。”

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You expressed a clear cause-and-effect idea with “难怪,” and your short ending gives the story a logical conclusion.  
Next time, focus on choosing narrative connectors such as “后来” and using more precise words for learning and reading.
