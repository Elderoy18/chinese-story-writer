## Scene 1

> 古代的时候人觉得祭拜非常重要，孟子很小的时候，从早到晚一直祭拜，因为他妈妈告诉他祭拜很重要。虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜。他的同学们老是对他开玩笑了。孟子妈妈看了他的同学们对孟子不好的影响，所以孟子和孟子妈妈搬家了。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “古代的时候人觉得祭拜非常重要”  
Formula: 在 + historical time + ，+ subject + verb phrase  
Correct: “在古代，人们觉得祭拜非常重要。”  
“在古代” is more natural than “古代的时候,” and “人们” is needed as the subject.

Incorrect phrase: “孟子很小的时候，从早到晚一直祭拜”  
Formula: When first introducing a character: 在古代，有一个叫……的人；Subject + 在/从……到…… + verb + object  
Correct: “在古代，有一个叫孟子的孩子。他很小的时候，就从早到晚祭拜祖先。”  
Introduce 孟子 briefly on his first mention. Also, “祭拜” usually needs an object, such as “祖先.”

Incorrect phrase: “虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜。”  
Formula: 虽然 + clause，but/可是 + clause；or 因为 + reason，所以 + result  
Correct: “因为他每天只祭拜，所以他没有朋友。”  
“虽然” cannot be paired with “因为.” Here, the sentence expresses a cause and result, so use 因为……所以…….

Incorrect phrase: “他的同学们老是对他开玩笑了。”  
Formula: Subject + 老是 + verb phrase (for repeated, negative actions)  
Correct: “他的同学们老是拿他开玩笑。”  
“老是” is appropriate here because the classmates’ behavior is negative. However, don’t use “了” for a repeated habitual action. “拿某人开玩笑” is also more natural.

Incorrect phrase: “孟子妈妈看了他的同学们对孟子不好的影响”  
Formula: Subject + 看到/发现 + someone + 对 + someone + 造成的 + negative influence  
Correct: “孟子的妈妈看到了同学们对孟子造成的不良影响。”  
Use “孟子的妈妈,” not “孟子妈妈.” The classmates “造成” an influence; they are not the influence itself.

Incorrect phrase: “所以孟子和孟子妈妈搬家了。”  
Formula: 所以 + subject + 搬家了；Possessive noun + 的 + family member  
Correct: “所以，孟子和他的妈妈搬家了。”  
After introducing 孟子, “他的妈妈” avoids unnecessary repetition and sounds smoother.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “觉得”  
Context: “人们觉得祭拜非常重要。”  
Alternatives: “认为” is more formal and is especially natural when expressing a belief or viewpoint. “觉得” is common in conversation and often sounds more personal; “认为” fits a historical or explanatory narrative better.

Word: “非常重要”  
Context: “祭拜非常重要。”  
Alternatives: “至关重要” means “of crucial importance” and is stronger and more formal. “意义重大” emphasizes that something has great significance, especially in a cultural or historical context.

Word: “只祭拜”  
Context: “他每天只祭拜。”  
Alternatives: “一心祭拜” suggests that he devoted all his attention to worshipping, without necessarily sounding as negative as “只.” “专心祭拜” emphasizes concentration and dedication.

Word: “对他开玩笑”  
Context: “同学们老是对他开玩笑。”  
Alternatives: “拿他开玩笑” is the most natural expression when classmates make someone the target of jokes. “嘲笑他” is stronger and means to mock or ridicule him, so use it if the classmates were being cruel.

Word: “不好的影响”  
Context: “同学们对孟子不好的影响。”  
Alternatives: “不良影响” is more concise and formal, commonly used in writing. “负面影响” emphasizes harmful or unfavorable effects and is also very common in modern Chinese.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You created a clear cause-and-effect story: Mengzi’s daily worship, his lack of friends, the classmates’ behavior, and the family’s decision to move all connect logically.  
Next time, focus on introducing characters naturally and using complete cause-and-effect patterns such as “因为……所以……”.

## Scene 2

> 孟子才十一岁，成为一个哲学家。孟子不是祭拜，而是看政治文章，难怪他成为了哲学家。不过，看过很多政治文章对他害得，他有很多问题。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “孟子才十一岁，成为一个哲学家。”  
Formula: Subject + 才 + age + 就 + verb + 了  
Correct: “孟子才十一岁，就成为哲学家了。”  
“才……就……” emphasizes that he became a philosopher at a very young age.

Incorrect phrase: “孟子不是祭拜，而是看政治文章。”  
Formula: Subject + 不是 + verb phrase +，而是 + verb phrase  
Correct: “孟子不是在祭拜祖先，而是在看政治文章。”  
Adding “在” makes the contrast between two ongoing actions clearer. “祭拜” also usually has an object, such as “祖先.”

Incorrect phrase: “不过，看过很多政治文章对他害得，他有很多问题。”  
Formula: Subject + 看了/阅读了 + object + 以后，+ subject + 产生/有 + 疑问  
Correct: “不过，看了很多政治文章以后，他产生了很多疑问。”  
“害得” must be followed by a result, such as “害得他很担心.” Here, “产生了很多疑问” naturally expresses that reading led him to have many questions.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “看”  
Context: “看政治文章”  
Alternatives: “阅读” or “研读.” “看” is a common, general verb. “阅读” is more appropriate for written materials, while “研读” means to study a text carefully and deeply.

Word: “政治文章”  
Context: “看政治文章”  
Alternatives: “政治方面的文章” or “政论文章.” “政治文章” is understandable but broad. “政治方面的文章” is neutral and clear; “政论文章” refers more specifically to articles that discuss or comment on political issues.

Word: “问题”  
Context: “他有很多问题”  
Alternatives: “疑问” or “思考.” “问题” can mean either “questions” or “problems,” so it is ambiguous. “疑问” clearly means intellectual questions or doubts, while “思考” emphasizes deeper reflection.

Word: “祭拜”  
Context: “孟子不是祭拜”  
Alternatives: “祭祀祖先” or “拜祭祖先.” “祭拜” is natural in everyday Chinese. “祭祀” is more formal and is often used when discussing ancient customs; “拜祭” is also commonly used for paying respects to ancestors.

Word: “不过”  
Context: “不过，看了很多政治文章以后……”  
Alternatives: “然而” or “可是.” “不过” is conversational and introduces a contrast. “然而” is more formal and works well in writing, while “可是” is common and slightly more direct.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You used “才……就……” and “不是……而是……” to build an interesting contrast between Mengzi’s age, actions, and intellectual development.  
Next time, focus on choosing precise words such as “疑问” instead of “问题” and using clear cause-and-effect sentence patterns.

## Scene 3

> 他一直问问题，但是不能解决那些问题。孟子长大，他开始能解决问题。他常常建议你不是学金融，而是学政治，因为如果你懂政治，你会成为高薪。后来孟子成为教授，他教学生很厉害。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “他一直问问题，但是不能解决那些问题。”  
Formula: Subject + 一直 + V；although/contrast relationship: 虽然……，但是…… / or use a result contrast naturally.  
Correct: “孟子一直提出问题，但以前不能解决这些问题。”  
“他” is unclear at the beginning because Mengzi has not been introduced yet.

Incorrect phrase: “孟子长大，他开始能解决问题。”  
Formula: Subject + 长大后 + 就/开始 + 能 + V.  
Correct: “孟子长大后，开始能解决这些问题了。”

Incorrect phrase: “他常常建议你不是学金融，而是学政治”  
Formula: 建议 + someone + 不要 + V1，(而)要 + V2.  
Correct: “他常常建议你不要学金融，而要学政治。”  
Use “不是……而是……” to contrast nouns or statements, but after 建议, “不要……而要……” is more natural.

Incorrect phrase: “因为如果你懂政治，你会成为高薪。”  
Formula: 如果……，就会 + V + Object.  
Correct: “因为如果你懂政治，你就会找到一份高薪工作。”  
“高薪” is an adjective and cannot be used after “成为” by itself.

Incorrect phrase: “后来孟子成为教授，他教学生很厉害。”  
Formula: 后来/成为……后，Subject + 很擅长 + V / 教学能力很强.  
Correct: “后来，孟子成为了一名教授后，很擅长教学生。”  
A smoother version is: “后来，孟子成为了一名教授，教学能力很强。”

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “问问题”  
Context: “孟子一直问问题”  
Alternatives: “提出问题” is more formal and natural in academic contexts. “问问题” simply means to ask questions, while “提出问题” emphasizes raising an issue or intellectual question for discussion.

Word: “解决问题”  
Context: “不能解决那些问题”  
Alternatives: “解答问题” is especially suitable for answering intellectual or academic questions; “处理问题” is better for dealing with practical difficulties or situations. “解决问题” is a broad, neutral expression.

Word: “建议”  
Context: “他常常建议你……”  
Alternatives: “劝” means to persuade someone, often because the speaker thinks it is important; “提倡” means to advocate for an idea or practice publicly. “建议” is a neutral, personal recommendation.

Word: “高薪”  
Context: “成为高薪”  
Alternatives: “高薪工作” means a well-paid job; “收入丰厚” means having a substantial income and sounds more polished. “高薪” usually needs a noun after it, such as 工作, 职位, or 行业.

Word: “厉害”  
Context: “他教学生很厉害”  
Alternatives: “擅长教学” means to be skilled at teaching and is more formal; “教学能力很强” emphasizes strong teaching ability. “厉害” is common in spoken Chinese but is less precise here.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You did well in creating a clear timeline for Mengzi: he asked questions, grew up, gave advice, and eventually became a professor.  
Next time, focus on using “长大后 / 成为……后” to connect events smoothly and on choosing complete noun phrases such as “一份高薪工作.”
