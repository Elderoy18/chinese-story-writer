## Scene 1

> 古代的时候有一个人叫神农。他需要当上医，一看别的人就想帮助他们。但是，他有一个问题，他不知道什么东西能做药。神农听说一个远远的地方有一种植物，什么病人吃都会觉得身体更好。神农三个月走去，找到有植物的地方，他看到一个很漂亮的植物，又香又好看。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “古代的时候有一个人叫神农” / Formula: 在 + historical time + ，+ introduce character (name + role) / Correct: “在古代，有一个人叫神农。”  
“在古代” is more natural than “古代的时候.” You could also introduce him more clearly: “在古代，有一位想成为医生的人，叫神农。”

Incorrect phrase: “他需要当上医” / Formula: 谁 + 想/希望 + 成为 + 职业 / Correct: “他想成为一名医生。”  
“当上” usually emphasizes successfully obtaining a position, while “想成为” fits a personal goal better. “医” should be “医生” in modern Chinese.

Incorrect phrase: “一看别的人就想帮助他们” / Formula: 谁 + 一看到 + 人 + 就 + 想 + 帮助 + 他们 / Correct: “他一看到别人就想帮助他们。”  
Use “一看到” to mean “as soon as he sees.” “别人” is more natural than “别的人” here.

Incorrect phrase: “神农听说一个远远的地方有一种植物” / Formula: 听说 + 在 + place + 有 + noun / Correct: “神农听说在一个很远的地方有一种植物。”  
The place needs “在” before it. “远远的地方” can be replaced with the more natural “很远的地方.”

Incorrect phrase: “什么病人吃都会觉得身体更好” / Formula: 无论/任何 + noun + 吃了 + it + 都会 + result / Correct: “任何病人吃了它以后，身体都会好起来。”  
“什么病人” is not natural here. Also, use “它” to clearly refer to the plant.

Incorrect phrase: “神农三个月走去，找到有植物的地方” / Formula: 谁 + 走了/花了 + duration + ，才 + 找到 + place / Correct: “神农走了三个月，才找到了那种植物生长的地方。”  
“走了三个月” shows the completed duration, and “才” emphasizes that it took a long time. “有植物的地方” is too vague because many places have plants.

Incorrect phrase: “一个很漂亮的植物” / Formula: 一 + classifier + adjective + noun / Correct: “一种很美丽的植物。”  
The classifier for plants is usually “种,” not “个.”

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “帮助” / Context: “他一看到别人就想帮助他们。” / Alternatives: “救治” means to treat and save sick or injured people, so it fits especially well because Shennong wants to become a doctor. “帮助” is a general word for helping someone in any situation.

Word: “什么东西” / Context: “他不知道什么东西能做药。” / Alternatives: “什么植物” is more precise because the story is about medicinal plants. “药材” means medicinal materials or herbs and sounds more advanced; it is especially appropriate for traditional Chinese medicine.

Word: “远远的地方” / Context: “一个远远的地方” / Alternatives: “遥远的地方” is more natural and literary, often used in stories to describe a place far away. “很远的地方” is simpler and more conversational.

Word: “漂亮” / Context: “一个很漂亮的植物” / Alternatives: “美丽” is more suitable for flowers or plants and has a more elegant, descriptive feeling. “艳丽” emphasizes bright, striking colors, especially for flowers. “漂亮” is common but is used more often for people, clothes, or general appearance.

Word: “好看” / Context: “又香又好看” / Alternatives: “美观” means visually pleasing but is more formal. “赏心悦目” means so beautiful that it pleases the eyes; it is more advanced and literary. “好看” is simple and natural, but it is less vivid in a story.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You created a clear story sequence—from Shennong’s goal, to his problem, to his journey—and your description “又香又好看” makes the plant easy to imagine.  
Next time, focus on using precise classifiers and structures for duration, such as “走了三个月，才……”.

## Scene 2

> 他觉得，这种一定是做药的花。原来他想错了。吃了以后，神农快生病。 这时一个穿着蓝色衣服的人帮神农，快给他一种红的灵芝。神农吃了以后，他没有病，想起来以前一个人告诉他，灵芝对身体有好处。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “这种一定是做药的花” / Formula: 这种 + noun + 一定 + 是 + description / Correct: “这种花一定是药材。”  
“这种” needs a noun here, and “做药的花” is unnatural. Use “药材” or “可以入药的花.”

Incorrect phrase: “吃了以后，神农快生病。” / Formula: Subject + 差点儿 + verb phrase + 了 / Correct: “吃了以后，神农差点儿生病了。”  
“快” means “fast”; use “差点儿” for “almost.”

Incorrect phrase: “这时一个穿着蓝色衣服的人帮神农，快给他一种红的灵芝。” / Formula: Subject + 赶快 + 给 + person + object，帮助 + person / Correct: “这时，一个穿着蓝色衣服的人赶快给神农一株红色的灵芝，帮助了他。”  
“帮神农” needs more information, while giving him lingzhi explains how the person helped. Use “赶快” rather than “快,” and “红色的” rather than “红的.”

Incorrect phrase: “神农吃了以后，他没有病” / Formula: Subject + 吃了 + object + 以后，+ 没有 + verb / Correct: “神农吃了灵芝以后，没有生病。”  
“没有病” is understandable, but “没有生病” is more natural for saying that he did not become ill. Removing “他” also avoids an unclear reference to either 神农 or the man in blue.

Incorrect phrase: “想起来以前一个人告诉他” / Formula: Subject + 想起 + time + 有人 + verb + 过 + information / Correct: “他想起以前有人告诉过他，灵芝对身体有好处。”  
Use “想起” for remembering something, and “以前有人告诉过他” is the natural word order.

Incorrect phrase: “原来他想错了。吃了以后，神农快生病。” / Formula: Situation + 可是/没想到 + unexpected result / Correct: “可是，原来他想错了：吃了这种花以后，神农差点儿生病了。”  
Adding “可是” and specifying “这种花” makes the connection between the mistaken belief and the illness clearer.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “做药的花” / Context: “这种一定是做药的花。” / Alternatives: “药材” means medicinal material and is the most natural everyday term here. “可以入药的花” means “a flower that can be used in medicine” and is more precise.

Word: “快生病” / Context: “神农快生病。” / Alternatives: “差点儿生病” means he almost became ill. “险些中毒” means he narrowly avoided poisoning and is especially suitable if the flower was poisonous.

Word: “穿着蓝色衣服的人” / Context: “一个穿着蓝色衣服的人” / Alternatives: “穿蓝衣服的人” is shorter and natural in speech. “身穿蓝衣的人” is more literary and suitable for storytelling.

Word: “红的灵芝” / Context: “一种红的灵芝” / Alternatives: “红色的灵芝” is the standard descriptive phrase. “赤灵芝” is a specific, more formal name for red lingzhi.

Word: “对身体有好处” / Context: “灵芝对身体有好处。” / Alternatives: “对身体有益” is more concise and slightly formal. “有滋补作用” emphasizes that lingzhi is believed to nourish and strengthen the body.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You created a clear mini-story with a problem, help from another character, and a solution involving lingzhi.  
Next time, focus on using “差点儿,” “赶快,” and clear subjects to show actions and relationships more naturally.

## Scene 3

> 啊，他怎么忘记了？他把灵芝放在包里。一回家就告诉别人。他找到药以后他写下怎么做药在书上。所以人们都知道，那红灵芝对身体好处。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “一回家就告诉别人”  
Formula: Subject + 一 + Verb Phrase 1 + 就 + Verb Phrase 2 + Object/Content  
Correct: “他一回家就把这个发现告诉了大家。”  
The subject “他” should be clear, and it is more natural to say what he told people.

Incorrect phrase: “他找到药以后他写下怎么做药在书上”  
Formula: Subject + Time + 以后，Subject + 把 + Content + 写在 + Place  
Correct: “他找到灵芝以后，把灵芝的药用方法写在书上。”  
Use a comma after “以后.” Also, “写下……在书上” has awkward word order; use “把……写在书上.” Since 灵芝 itself is medicinal, “灵芝的药用方法” is clearer than “怎么做药.”

Incorrect phrase: “那红灵芝对身体好处”  
Formula: Something + 对 + Someone/Body + 有 + 好处  
Correct: “那种红灵芝对身体有好处。”  
“有” is required before “好处,” and “那种红灵芝” sounds more natural when referring to a type of mushroom.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “告诉”  
Context: “告诉别人”  
Alternatives: “分享” or “告知.” “告诉” is a basic, neutral word for giving someone information. “分享” emphasizes sharing an interesting discovery with others, while “告知” is more formal.

Word: “别人”  
Context: “告诉别人”  
Alternatives: “大家” or “村民们.” “别人” simply means other people and is vague. “大家” is natural for a general audience, while “村民们” makes the story more specific and vivid.

Word: “找到”  
Context: “他找到药以后”  
Alternatives: “发现” or “寻得.” “找到” means to locate something. “发现” emphasizes making a new discovery, which fits this story well. “寻得” is more literary and suitable for a historical story.

Word: “药”  
Context: “找到药”  
Alternatives: “药材” or “药用植物.” “药” is a general word for medicine. “药材” refers to medicinal materials, especially traditional Chinese medicine ingredients; “药用植物” specifically means a plant that can be used medicinally.

Word: “对身体有好处”  
Context: “红灵芝对身体有好处”  
Alternatives: “有益健康” or “具有药用价值.” “对身体有好处” is simple and conversational. “有益健康” is more concise and natural, while “具有药用价值” emphasizes that lingzhi has medical value.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You clearly showed the sequence of events—from finding lingzhi to telling others about its benefits—which gives your story a good flow.  
Next time, focus on adding clear subjects and using the pattern “对……有好处.”
