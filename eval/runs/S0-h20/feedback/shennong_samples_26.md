## Scene 1

> 很久以前，有一个老男人叫神农。神农在古代每一天看到了别的小镇人生病还是不舒服，所以他决定开始找新的药。他下一天开始爬当地的山脉，找了各种各样的植物。神农试一试每个植物，知道有什么好处。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “有一个老男人叫神农” / Formula: 有一位 + 身份/description + 叫 + 名字 / Correct: “有一位叫神农的老人。”  
“老男人” can sound impolite or overly literal in Chinese; “老人” is more natural. This also introduces the character more smoothly.

Incorrect phrase: “神农在古代每一天看到了别的小镇人生病还是不舒服” / Formula: 在古代，+ 人物 + 每天 + 看到 + 地方 + 的人 + 生病 / Correct: “在古代，神农每天看到镇上的人们生病或身体不舒服。”  
“在古代” should come earlier; “每天” is more natural than “每一天” here. Also, “别的小镇人” is unclear—use “镇上的人们.”

Incorrect phrase: “生病还是不舒服” / Formula: A 或 B / Correct: “生病或身体不舒服。”  
“还是” is usually used in questions or to express a choice. In a statement, use “或/或者.”

Incorrect phrase: “所以他决定开始找新的药” / Formula: 所以 + 人物 + 决定 + 开始 + 寻找 + 能做药的植物 / Correct: “所以他决定开始寻找能做药的植物。”  
“新的药” means new medicines that already exist. Here, Shennong is searching for plants that can be used as medicine.

Incorrect phrase: “他下一天开始爬当地的山脉” / Formula: 第二天，+ 人物 + 开始 + 爬/进入 + 当地的山里 / Correct: “第二天，神农开始爬当地的山。”  
Use “第二天” rather than “他下一天.” “山脉” refers to a mountain range and is less natural in this context. Repeating “神农” also makes the reference clearer.

Incorrect phrase: “找了各种各样的植物” / Formula: 开始 + 寻找 + 各种各样的植物 / Correct: “寻找各种各样的植物。”  
Since this action continues with the next sentence, do not use 了 here. Alternatively: “他找到了各种各样的植物,” if you mean that he successfully found them.

Incorrect phrase: “神农试一试每个植物，知道有什么好处” / Formula: 人物 + 尝试 + 每一种植物，想知道/了解 + 它们 + 有什么作用 / Correct: “神农尝试每一种植物，想了解它们有什么作用。”  
“试一试” is usually for casually trying something once; “尝试” fits systematic testing better. “知道” needs a clearer connection, such as “想了解.”

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “老男人” / Context: “有一个老男人叫神农” / Alternatives: “老人” or “长者.” “老男人” is literal and can sound impolite. “老人” is neutral and natural, while “长者” is respectful and more literary, fitting an ancient legend.

Word: “别的小镇人” / Context: “别的小镇人生病” / Alternatives: “镇上的人们” or “当地的百姓.” “镇上的人们” simply means people living in the town. “当地的百姓” has a historical, story-like tone and is especially suitable for an ancient setting.

Word: “不舒服” / Context: “生病还是不舒服” / Alternatives: “身体不适” or “患病.” “不舒服” is common and conversational. “身体不适” is more formal and describes physical discomfort, while “患病” means to suffer from an illness and sounds more formal.

Word: “找新的药” / Context: “开始找新的药” / Alternatives: “寻找草药” or “寻找药用植物.” “找” is basic and general. “寻找” is more appropriate for a long, purposeful search; “草药” means herbal medicine, and “药用植物” precisely means plants that can be used medicinally.

Word: “有什么好处” / Context: “知道有什么好处” / Alternatives: “有什么作用” or “有哪些药效.” “好处” is broad and informal. “作用” means function or effect, while “药效” specifically refers to medicinal effects, making it ideal for this topic.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You created a clear sequence of events and used “所以” effectively to show why Shennong began his search.  
Next time, focus on time expressions such as “在古代” and “第二天,” and use more precise words for medicinal plants and their effects.

## Scene 2

> 比方说，他一次试一试有些植物的叶，吃得又新鲜又甜。煮这些叶以后，他一喝就知道他发现新的茶叶。虽然他找到了很多的新的植物，但是他的旅程有的时候特别是危险。比方说，他一吃灵芝就开始生病。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “他一次试一试有些植物的叶” / Formula: Subject + 尝试/尝了 + quantity + object; verb reduplication (V一V) is for a light suggestion or intention, not a completed action / Correct: “他一次尝试了几种植物的叶子。”

Incorrect phrase: “吃得又新鲜又甜” / Formula: Food + 吃起来 + adjective / Correct: “这些叶子吃起来又新鲜又甜。”  
“吃得……” usually describes how someone performs the action of eating, while “吃起来……” describes the taste or quality of food.

Incorrect phrase: “煮这些叶以后，他一喝就知道他发现新的茶叶” / Formula: Subject + 煮了 + object + 以后，+ subject + 一 + verb + 就 + result; use 自己 to make the reference clear / Correct: “煮了这些叶子以后，他喝了一口，就知道自己发现了一种新的茶叶了。”

Incorrect phrase: “很多的新的植物” / Formula: 很多 + (种) + adjective + noun / Correct: “很多种新植物。”  
You can also say “很多新的植物,” but “很多种新植物” is more natural here.

Incorrect phrase: “他的旅程有的时候特别是危险” / Formula: Subject + 有时候 + 很/特别 + adjective / Correct: “他的旅程有时候特别危险。”

Incorrect phrase: “他一吃灵芝就开始生病” / Formula: Subject + 一 + verb + 就 + result / Correct: “他一吃夹竹桃就开始生病。”  
This is a content correction: 灵芝 is medicinal, while 夹竹桃 is poisonous.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “比方说” / Context: “比方说，他一次尝试了几种植物的叶子。” / Alternatives: “例如” is more concise and is especially natural in writing. “比方说” is conversational and often introduces an example in speech.

Word: “叶” / Context: “植物的叶” / Alternatives: “叶子” is the usual everyday word for a plant’s leaves. “叶” is shorter and more formal or literary, but “叶子” sounds more natural in this narrative.

Word: “新鲜又甜” / Context: “这些叶子吃起来又新鲜又甜。” / Alternatives: “清香甘甜” describes a pleasant, light fragrance and sweetness, so it suits tea especially well. “新鲜” usually emphasizes that food is recently picked or not spoiled.

Word: “旅程” / Context: “他的旅程有时候特别危险。” / Alternatives: “探索过程” emphasizes Shen Nong’s process of investigating and discovering plants. “旅程” focuses more on the physical trip from place to place.

Word: “危险” / Context: “有时候特别危险。” / Alternatives: “凶险” is stronger and more literary, suggesting serious danger. “危险” is a common, neutral word for something unsafe.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You did well to use connecting words such as “虽然……但是……” and “一……就……,” which give your story a clear flow.  
Next time, focus on using completed-action forms such as “尝试了” or “尝了,” and double-check which plants are poisonous or medicinal in the story.

## Scene 3

> 一个爬山的男人正好看到神农的麻烦，然后给了他一些药。神农最终他的小镇回到，跟别的人分享他的发现。你认为神农的故事真的吗？这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “一个爬山的男人” / Formula: 一个 + 正在 + 动作 + 的 + 人 / Correct: “一个正在爬山的男人”  
“爬山的男人” can describe a man who hikes in general; “正在爬山的男人” clearly shows that he is climbing at that moment.

Incorrect phrase: “看到神农的麻烦” / Formula: 人 + 看到 + 某人 + 遇到了 + 困难/麻烦 / Correct: “看到神农遇到了麻烦”  
“神农的麻烦” is vague. This correction clearly states that Shennong was having a problem.

Incorrect phrase: “神农最终他的小镇回到” / Formula: 谁 + 最终/最后 + 回到 + 地点 / Correct: “神农最终回到了自己的小镇”  
The verb phrase should be “回到 + place,” and the location comes after the verb.

Incorrect phrase: “你认为神农的故事真的吗？” / Formula: 你认为 + 句子 + 吗？ / Correct: “你认为神农的故事是真的吗？”  
Use “是……的吗？” to ask whether something is true.

Incorrect phrase: “这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处。” / Formula: 这要看 + 主语 + 是否 + 动词/句子 / Correct: “这要看你是否相信中药对身体有好处。”  
“是否” means “whether or not” and is especially natural after “要看.”

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “爬山” / Context: “一个正在爬山的男人” / Alternatives: “登山” is a more formal and literary word for climbing mountains, while “爬山” is the common everyday term.

Word: “麻烦” / Context: “神农遇到了麻烦” / Alternatives: “困难” means a difficulty or obstacle and sounds more neutral; “困境” suggests a more serious, difficult situation. “麻烦” is common but slightly vague here.

Word: “药” / Context: “给了他一些药” / Alternatives: “草药” refers specifically to medicinal herbs, which fits Shennong’s story especially well. “中草药” emphasizes traditional Chinese herbal medicine.

Word: “小镇” / Context: “回到了自己的小镇” / Alternatives: “家乡” means one’s hometown and is more natural if this is the place where Shennong grew up. “小镇” simply describes a small town and does not necessarily mean his home.

Word: “真的” / Context: “神农的故事是真的吗？” / Alternatives: “真实的” means real or factual and is more formal; “可信的” means believable or credible. “真的” is very common in conversation, while “真实的” suits historical stories better.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You created a clear mini-story with a logical ending question, and your use of “然后” and “最终” gives the events a good flow.  
Next time, focus on Chinese word order, especially placing locations after verbs such as “回到 + 地点.”
