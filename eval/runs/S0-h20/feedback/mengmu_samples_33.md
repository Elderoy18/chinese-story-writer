## Scene 1

> 一天，很久很久以前，孟子和他妈妈住在中国。孟子妈妈的工作是祭拜，孟子不高兴，因为他说我们老是搬家，为什么？他妈妈说，从早到晚，我一直祭拜，很多墓地得我祭拜。所以我们得去很多不同的地方。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “一天，很久很久以前” / Formula: Time background + 有一天 / Correct: “很久很久以前，有一天，孟子和他的妈妈住在中国。”

Incorrect phrase: “孟子和他妈妈” / Formula: Person + 和 + possessive pronoun + family member / Correct: “孟子和他的妈妈。”

Incorrect phrase: “孟子妈妈的工作是祭拜” / Formula: Person + 的工作是 + Verb + Object / Correct: “孟子的妈妈的工作是祭拜祖先。”  
“祭拜” normally needs an object, such as “祖先.”

Incorrect phrase: “孟子不高兴，因为他说我们老是搬家，为什么？” / Formula: Subject + emotional state + because + direct speech / Correct: “孟子不高兴，因为他说：‘我们为什么老是搬家？’”  
Use a colon and quotation marks when writing direct speech.

Incorrect phrase: “很多墓地得我祭拜” / Formula: Place/thing + 需要 + person + 去 + Verb / Correct: “很多墓地需要我去祭拜。”  
Here, “得” is not natural for saying that many cemeteries need her to perform rituals.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “很久很久以前” / Context: “很久很久以前，有一天……” / Alternatives: “在古代” is more concise and suitable for a historical story about Mencius. “很久很久以前” has a fairy-tale storytelling feeling.

Word: “工作” / Context: “孟子的妈妈的工作是祭拜祖先。” / Alternatives: “职责” means duty or responsibility and sounds more formal. “工作” is a general word for a job, while “职责” better emphasizes an important role she has.

Word: “不高兴” / Context: “孟子不高兴” / Alternatives: “不满” means dissatisfied and is more precise when someone is unhappy about a repeated situation. “不高兴” is simple and general; “不满” focuses on dissatisfaction.

Word: “老是搬家” / Context: “我们为什么老是搬家？” / Alternatives: “总是搬家” is more neutral, while “搬来搬去” vividly emphasizes moving repeatedly from place to place. In this sentence, “老是” works well because Mengzi is complaining.

Word: “很多不同的地方” / Context: “所以我们得去很多不同的地方。” / Alternatives: “各地” means many different places or regions and is concise. “四处” means everywhere/around different places and gives a stronger sense of traveling around.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You did well using dialogue to show Mengzi’s feelings and to explain why his mother needed to move.  
Next time, focus on adding objects after verbs such as “祭拜” and using punctuation for direct speech.

## Scene 2

> 孟子想，难怪我们常常搬家，原来我妈妈很忙。可是常常搬家害得孟子很难找到朋友。每次搬家孟子也觉得新的地方不是太热就是太冷，不是太大就是太小。在哪的地方，他都不高兴。孟子想帮助妈妈祭拜，可是他六岁，所以不能祭拜。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “每次搬家孟子也觉得……”  
Formula: 每次 + Event + 时，Subject + Verb / Feeling  
Correct: “每次搬家时，孟子都觉得……”

Incorrect phrase: “在哪的地方，他都不高兴。”  
Formula: 无论 + 在 + 哪个地方，Subject + 都 + Predicate  
Correct: “无论在哪个地方，他都不高兴。”  
“在哪的地方” is not natural Chinese; use “在哪个地方” or “在哪里.”

Incorrect phrase: “可是他六岁，所以不能祭拜。”  
Formula: Subject + 才 + Age + ，还不能 / 不会 + Verb  
Correct: “可是他才六岁，还不能祭拜。”  
Use “才” to emphasize that he is young. “可是……所以……” is also less natural here; “可是……还……” shows the contrast clearly.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “很忙”  
Context: “原来我妈妈很忙。”  
Alternatives: “工作繁忙” or “忙于工作.” “很忙” is simple and conversational. “工作繁忙” is more formal and clearly specifies that she is busy with work; “忙于工作” emphasizes what occupies most of her time.

Word: “常常搬家”  
Context: “我们常常搬家。”  
Alternatives: “频繁搬家” or “经常搬迁.” “常常搬家” is natural in everyday speech. “频繁搬家” emphasizes that the moves happen many times and can suggest inconvenience; “搬迁” is more formal.

Word: “害得”  
Context: “常常搬家害得孟子很难找到朋友。”  
Alternatives: “导致” or “使.” “害得” emphasizes a negative result and has a slightly emotional tone. “导致” is more formal and objective, while “使” is neutral and works well in writing.

Word: “找到朋友”  
Context: “孟子很难找到朋友。”  
Alternatives: “交到朋友” or “结交朋友.” “找到朋友” can sound as if he is literally looking for a friend. “交到朋友” means successfully making friends and is very natural; “结交朋友” is slightly more formal.

Word: “不高兴”  
Context: “他都不高兴。”  
Alternatives: “不开心” or “不满意.” “不高兴” can mean unhappy or even mildly angry. “不开心” focuses on sadness or a lack of happiness, while “不满意” emphasizes that he is dissatisfied with the new place.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You clearly expressed Mengzi’s feelings and connected moving house to his difficulty making friends, which makes the scene easy to understand.  
Next time, focus on using “无论……都……” for “no matter where/what” situations and “才……还不能……” when explaining that someone is too young to do something.

## Scene 3

> 四年后，孟子开始帮妈妈祭拜。他画画很厉害，他越来越喜欢祭拜，所以他祭拜不是想帮妈妈工作，而是他很喜欢画画。孟子成为很著名的古代画家。今天很多中国人很喜欢孟子。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “四年后，孟子开始帮妈妈祭拜。” / Formula: 谁 + 才 + 年龄 + 就 + 开始 + 动作 / Correct: “孟子才四岁就开始帮妈妈祭拜。”  
“ 四年后” needs a clear starting point. If you mean that he began at a very young age, use **才……就……**.

Incorrect phrase: “他画画很厉害。” / Formula: 谁 + 动词 + 得 + 形容词 / Correct: “他画画画得很好。”  
“很厉害” is understandable here, but **画得很好** is more natural when describing someone’s drawing skill.

Incorrect phrase: “所以他祭拜不是想帮妈妈工作，而是他很喜欢画画。” / Formula: 谁 + 做某事 + 不是为了……，而是因为…… / Correct: “所以他祭拜不是为了帮妈妈，而是因为他喜欢祭拜。”  
The original sentence connects “祭拜” (worshipping/commemorating) with “喜欢画画” (liking painting), so the logic is unclear. Also, 祭拜 is not usually described as “妈妈的工作.”

Incorrect phrase: “孟子成为很著名的古代画家。” / Formula: 谁 + 后来 + 成为 + 一位 + 形容词 + 职业 / Correct: “孟子后来成为一位著名的哲学家。”  
Use **一位** before a person’s profession. Also, historically, Mencius was a famous philosopher, not a painter; “古代” is unnecessary because the narrative already takes place in ancient times.

Incorrect phrase: “今天很多中国人很喜欢孟子。” / Formula: 直到今天，很多人 + 仍然 + 动词 / Correct: “直到今天，很多中国人仍然很尊敬孟子。”  
“今天” can mean literally “today.” **直到今天** means “up to the present day” and fits historical figures better.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “帮” / Context: “帮妈妈祭拜” / Alternatives: **跟妈妈一起祭拜** or **协助妈妈准备祭拜**.  
“帮” is simple and common. “跟妈妈一起” emphasizes that Mencius participated with his mother, while “协助” is more formal and emphasizes providing help with preparations.

Word: “很厉害” / Context: “他画画很厉害” / Alternatives: **画得很好**, **很有天赋**, or **技艺高超**.  
“很厉害” is conversational and broad. “很有天赋” emphasizes natural talent, while “技艺高超” is more formal and praises advanced artistic skill.

Word: “越来越喜欢” / Context: “他越来越喜欢祭拜” / Alternatives: **逐渐对祭拜产生兴趣** or **越来越重视祭拜**.  
“越来越喜欢” is clear but basic. “产生兴趣” means he gradually became interested, while “重视” emphasizes that he considered the ritual important, not simply enjoyable.

Word: “很著名” / Context: “很著名的古代画家” / Alternatives: **著名的**, **有名的**, or **享有盛名的**.  
“很著名” is understandable, but “著名的” is smoother. “享有盛名的” is more literary and means someone has a very strong, widely recognized reputation.

Word: “很喜欢” / Context: “很多中国人很喜欢孟子” / Alternatives: **尊敬**, **敬仰**, or **推崇**.  
“喜欢” means “like,” which is slightly too casual for an important historical philosopher. “尊敬” means respect, “敬仰” means deeply admire, and “推崇” means highly value and praise someone’s ideas.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You did well connecting the sentences with words such as “所以” and showing Mencius’s development over time.  
Next time, focus on making sure the reason and result in each sentence match logically, especially when you use “不是……而是……”.
