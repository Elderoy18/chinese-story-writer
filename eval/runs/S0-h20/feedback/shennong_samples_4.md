## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个男人，他的名字是神农。他看到了有很多人不知道哪个植物好吃，也不知道什么植物对你的身体有好处。这个问题神农觉得很糟糕，所以神农去了吃每种植物。他吃了一种植物以后，就写了这个植物对你有没有好处。有的时候，神农吃了很好吃的植物。有的时候，他吃了很危险的植物。他吃完了每种植物以后，会来跟他的朋友聊到他吃的植物了。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “哪个植物好吃”  
Formula: 哪 + measure word + noun  
Correct: “哪种植物好吃”

Incorrect phrase: “什么植物对你的身体有好处”  
Formula: 人们 + 不知道 + 什么……对 + 自己的/人体 + 有好处  
Correct: “什么植物对人体有好处”  
“你” is unclear here because the sentence is about many people in general.

Incorrect phrase: “这个问题神农觉得很糟糕”  
Formula: Subject + 觉得 + object/problem + adjective  
Correct: “神农觉得这个问题很严重”  
Putting “神农” before the verb makes the sentence clearer and more natural.

Incorrect phrase: “所以神农去了吃每种植物”  
Formula: 所以 + subject + 去 + verb + object  
Correct: “所以他去吃每一种植物”  
Use “去吃,” not “去了吃.” Also, “神农” is repeated unnecessarily; “他” clearly refers to Shennong.

Incorrect phrase: “就写了这个植物对你有没有好处”  
Formula: Subject + 写下/记录 + noun phrase  
Correct: “就写下这种植物对人体有没有好处”  
Use “这种植物” to refer to the plant just mentioned, and use “人体” rather than the unclear “你.”

Incorrect phrase: “有的时候，神农吃了很好吃的植物”  
Formula: 有的时候/有时 + subject + verb (habitual action; no 了)  
Correct: “有的时候，神农会吃到很好吃的植物”  
This describes a repeated habit, so “了” should not be used.

Incorrect phrase: “有的时候，他吃了很危险的植物”  
Formula: 有的时候/有时 + subject + verb (habitual action; no 了)  
Correct: “有的时候，他会吃到很危险的植物”

Incorrect phrase: “他吃完了每种植物以后，会来跟他的朋友聊到他吃的植物了”  
Formula: 每次 + verb-complement + 以后，subject + 就会 + 跟 + person + 聊/讨论 + topic  
Correct: “每次吃完一种植物以后，他就会跟朋友聊一聊这种植物。”  
“聊到” usually emphasizes talking until a certain point; “聊一聊” is more natural here. “每种植物” should be “一种植物” because he eats and discusses them one at a time.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “好吃”  
Context: “哪个植物好吃”  
Alternatives: “美味” emphasizes that food tastes especially delicious and is slightly more descriptive. “可食用” means “edible” and is better when discussing whether a plant can safely be eaten, rather than whether it tastes good.

Word: “糟糕”  
Context: “这个问题神农觉得很糟糕”  
Alternatives: “严重” emphasizes that the problem has important consequences. “令人担忧” means “worrying” and gives the sentence a more thoughtful, formal tone. “糟糕” is common and general, while these alternatives are more precise.

Word: “危险的植物”  
Context: “他吃了很危险的植物”  
Alternatives: “有毒的植物” specifically means plants that are poisonous, which fits this story very well. “危险” is broader and could describe many kinds of danger, not only poisoning.

Word: “写了”  
Context: “就写了这种植物对人体有没有好处”  
Alternatives: “记录下来” means to document information carefully, while “写下” means to write something down. Both are more natural than “写了” when Shennong is making notes about plants.

Word: “聊”  
Context: “跟朋友聊一聊这种植物”  
Alternatives: “讨论” is more formal and emphasizes exchanging ideas about a topic. “分享” emphasizes telling others about one’s own experience. For Shennong explaining what happened after tasting a plant, “分享自己的发现” would be especially vivid.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You did a good job creating a clear story sequence: Shennong notices a problem, tests plants, writes down information, and talks with friends.  
Next time, focus on using no “了” for repeated habits and on keeping pronouns such as “你” and “他” clear.

## Scene 2

> 一个人问神农，哪个植物好吃。神农说，这就要看你的口味了。要是你喜欢吃甜的，有很多甜的水果，比方说苹果、西瓜和梨。一个别的人问神农，哪个植物对你的身体有好处。神农说，有很多种植物对你的身体有好处，比方说小白菜、黄瓜、茶和菠菜。一个别的人问神农，哪个植物对你的身体有坏处。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “哪个植物” (used three times)  
Formula: 哪 + measure word + noun  
Correct: “哪种植物”  
Use 种 for categories of plants: “哪种植物好吃？” / “哪种植物对身体有好处？”

Incorrect phrase: “这就要看你的口味了”  
Formula: 这就要看 + question form (A-not-A / wh-question / A还是B) + 了  
Correct: “这就要看你喜欢吃甜的还是酸的了。”  
After 看, this pattern needs a question-like choice, not only a noun such as “你的口味”.

Incorrect phrase: “要是你喜欢吃甜的，有很多甜的水果”  
Formula: 要是/如果 + condition，(就) + result  
Correct: “要是你喜欢吃甜的，就可以吃很多水果，比如苹果、西瓜和梨。”  
The second part should show the result of the condition.

Incorrect phrase: “一个别的人”  
Formula: 一个 + noun / 另一个 + noun  
Correct: “另一个人问神农……”  
“另一个人” is the natural way to introduce another person in a story.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “好吃”  
Context: “哪种植物好吃？”  
Alternatives: “可口” and “美味”. 好吃 is common and casual, while 可口 sounds a little more descriptive and emphasizes that food suits one’s taste. 美味 is stronger and means delicious.

Word: “对你的身体有好处”  
Context: “哪种植物对你的身体有好处？”  
Alternatives: “有益健康” or “对身体有益”. 有好处 is very general; 有益健康 is more precise and means “beneficial to one’s health.”

Word: “坏处”  
Context: “哪种植物对你的身体有坏处？”  
Alternatives: “有害” or “不利”. 坏处 is understandable but general. 有害 means “harmful,” especially for health, while 不利 means “not beneficial” and sounds more formal.

Word: “很多种”  
Context: “有很多种植物对你的身体有好处”  
Alternatives: “多种” or “各类”. 很多种 is natural in speech; 多种 is more concise, while 各类 emphasizes many different categories.

Word: “比方说”  
Context: “比方说苹果、西瓜和梨”  
Alternatives: “例如” or “比如”. 比方说 is conversational. 比如 is also common and slightly shorter, while 例如 is more formal and often used in writing.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You did well in creating a clear question-and-answer structure and giving specific examples of fruits and vegetables.  

Next time, focus on using the correct measure word in “哪种植物” and completing each conditional sentence with a clear result.

## Scene 3

> 神农说，有一天，我吃了一个黄色的花，名字是夹竹桃。我一吃夹竹桃，就觉得这种植物对我的脑有坏处。可是，一个灵芝和一个男人正好在我的旁边，我说，麻烦你把那个植物给我吃一下。我一吃这个灵芝就好了。所以，你得小心吃植物，因为有的植物对你的健康有坏处，特别是夹竹桃。因为夹竹桃有毒。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “有一天，我吃了一个黄色的花” / Formula: 有一天，+ subject + 吃了 + 一朵 + flower / Correct: “有一天，我吃了一朵黄色的花。”  
“花” uses the measure word “朵,” not “个.”

Incorrect phrase: “名字是夹竹桃” / Formula: Noun + 叫 + name / Correct: “它叫夹竹桃。”  
“叫” is the more natural verb for giving the name of a flower or plant.

Incorrect phrase: “我一吃夹竹桃，就觉得这种植物对我的脑有坏处” / Formula: Subject + 一 + verb + object，+ 就 + result / Correct: “我一吃夹竹桃，肚子就疼。”  
The 一……就…… structure is good, but “对我的脑有坏处” is unnatural here and does not match the story content: oleander causes stomach pain/poisoning.

Incorrect phrase: “一个灵芝和一个男人正好在我的旁边，我说，麻烦你把那个植物给我吃一下” / Formula: Subject + 发现/看到 + object；Subject + 吃了 + medicine / Correct: “正好，我发现了灵芝，就吃了灵芝。”  
This introduces an unexplained man, and “那个植物” is vague: it could refer to oleander or lingzhi. The man is also not part of the story.

Incorrect phrase: “所以，你得小心吃植物，因为有的植物对你的健康有坏处，特别是夹竹桃。因为夹竹桃有毒。” / Formula: General statement，特别是 + example；because-clause + main clause / Correct: “所以，吃植物要小心，因为有些植物有毒，特别是夹竹桃。”  
“特别是夹竹桃” is correctly used to give a specific example, but the final “因为夹竹桃有毒” is an incomplete sentence and repeats the previous idea.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “黄色的花” / Context: “我吃了一朵黄色的花。” / Alternatives: “黄颜色的夹竹桃” or “一朵黄花。” “黄色的花” is understandable, but “黄颜色的夹竹桃” is more specific because you are introducing the important plant in the story. “一朵黄花” is shorter and more natural in simple narration.

Word: “对我的脑有坏处” / Context: “这种植物对我的脑有坏处。” / Alternatives: “让我中毒了,” “让我肚子疼,” or “对身体有害。” “有坏处” is general and informal. “有害” means harmful and is more precise for dangerous plants; “中毒” specifically means to be poisoned.

Word: “正好在我的旁边” / Context: “一个灵芝……正好在我的旁边。” / Alternatives: “我正好发现了灵芝” or “我在附近找到了灵芝。” “在我的旁边” only describes location. “发现” emphasizes that Shennong found something unexpectedly, which fits the story better.

Word: “好了” / Context: “我一吃这个灵芝就好了。” / Alternatives: “病就好了,” “身体恢复了,” or “感觉好多了。” “好了” is common but unclear because it does not say what improved. “恢复了” is more formal and means that one’s health returned to normal.

Word: “小心吃植物” / Context: “你得小心吃植物。” / Alternatives: “不要随便吃野生植物” or “吃不认识的植物要小心。” “小心吃植物” is understandable, but “随便吃” highlights the danger of eating plants without knowing what they are, and “不认识的植物” is especially clear and useful.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You used a clear cause-and-effect structure and successfully used “特别是夹竹桃” to connect the general warning to a specific dangerous example.  
Next time, focus on using precise nouns and pronouns so that the reader always knows exactly which plant or person you mean.
