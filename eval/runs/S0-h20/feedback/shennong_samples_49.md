## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有很多人不知道什么植物能吃，什么植物不能吃。有很多人吃植物以后就吃坏肚子了，因为他们吃了毒。那个时候，有一个老人叫神农，他什么植物都觉得好吃，不怕生病，不怕毒。他希望别人也可以吃好吃的植物。可是他知道，如果他们还怕吃毒，不会吃他喜欢的植物。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “古代的时候” / Formula: 在 + historical time period / Correct: “在古代”  
“在古代” is the more natural way to introduce a historical setting.

Incorrect phrase: “吃植物以后就吃坏肚子了” / Formula: Subject + 吃了 + something + 以后 + 就 + 吃坏了 + body part / Correct: “吃了植物以后就吃坏了肚子。”  
吃坏肚子 is understandable, but “吃坏了肚子” is more grammatically complete and natural.

Incorrect phrase: “因为他们吃了毒” / Formula: 因为 + subject + 吃了 + 有毒的植物 / Correct: “因为他们吃了有毒的植物。”  
“毒” usually refers to poison itself; here, you mean the plants were poisonous.

Incorrect phrase: “如果他们还怕吃毒，不会吃他喜欢的植物” / Formula: 如果 + subject + 害怕 + verb phrase，subject + 就 + result / Correct: “如果人们还害怕吃到有毒的植物，就不会吃他喜欢的植物。”  
Use “害怕吃到有毒的植物” to express “be afraid of accidentally eating poisonous plants.” Adding “就” makes the conditional sentence clearer. “人们” is also clearer than “他们” here.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “老人” / Context: “有一个老人叫神农” / Alternatives: “传说中的神农” or “神农氏”  
“老人” simply means an old man. Since Shennong is an important legendary figure, “传说中的神农” sounds more accurate and introduces him as a figure from Chinese mythology. “神农氏” is more formal and historical.

Word: “吃坏肚子” / Context: “吃坏了肚子” / Alternatives: “肚子疼” or “食物中毒”  
“吃坏肚子” is an everyday expression for getting sick after eating something bad. “肚子疼” is simpler and describes the symptom, while “食物中毒” is more specific and serious: food poisoning.

Word: “毒” / Context: “不怕毒” / Alternatives: “不怕中毒” or “不怕有毒的植物”  
“毒” is a noun meaning poison. “不怕中毒” means “not afraid of being poisoned,” while “不怕有毒的植物” specifically refers to poisonous plants.

Word: “希望” / Context: “他希望别人也可以吃好吃的植物” / Alternatives: “他想让人们也能吃到美味的植物”  
“希望” means “to hope” and is correct here. “想让……” makes Shennong’s intention more active: he wants to help people. “吃到” emphasizes successfully finding and eating the tasty plants.

Word: “可是” / Context: “可是他知道……” / Alternatives: “但是” or “然而”  
“可是” is common in spoken Chinese and creates a clear contrast. “但是” is neutral and works in both speech and writing. “然而” is more formal and literary, so it can make a story sound more like a written narrative.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You clearly explained the problem people faced and Shennong’s motivation, so your paragraph has a logical and easy-to-follow storyline.  

Next time, focus on making pronouns such as “他们” more specific and using complete conditional patterns such as “如果……，就……”。

## Scene 2

> 所以，他上山下山找植物，吃了很多样子的植物。他是第一人，找到了茶。他把所有的植物写下来了。他一直都吃了，他连毒都能吃。可是他还没吃了一个叫夹竹桃的植物。他找到了那个像星星的植物，觉得很漂亮，马上就吃下了。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “吃了很多样子的植物” / Formula: 吃 + 很多 + 种/样 + noun / Correct: “吃了很多种植物。”  
“很多样子的植物” sounds like “plants with many appearances”; use “很多种植物” for many types of plants.

Incorrect phrase: “他是第一人，找到了茶。” / Formula: 谁 + 是/成为 + 第一个 + verb + 的人 / Correct: “他是第一个找到茶的人。”  
“第一人” is not natural here, and the relative clause should come before “的人.”

Incorrect phrase: “他一直都吃了” / Formula: Subject + 一直 + verb; or Subject + 一直 + 在 + verb / Correct: “他一直在尝各种植物。”  
“一直” describes an ongoing or habitual action, so do not use completed-action 了 here. Also, specify what he keeps eating/trying.

Incorrect phrase: “他连毒都能吃” / Formula: 连 + object + 都 + verb / Correct: “他连有毒的植物都敢吃。”  
“毒” alone is a noun meaning poison. “有毒的植物” clearly states that he ate poisonous plants; “敢吃” emphasizes his bravery or risk-taking.

Incorrect phrase: “可是他还没吃了一个叫夹竹桃的植物” / Formula: Subject + 还没 + verb + object / Correct: “可是他还没吃过一种叫夹竹桃的植物。”  
After “还没,” do not use 了. “吃过” is natural for an experience that has not happened yet.

Incorrect phrase: “他找到了那个像星星的植物” / Formula: Subject + 找到 + noun + ，这个/它 + 像…… / Correct: “他找到了一种像星星一样的植物。”  
“那个” has no clear earlier reference, so “一种” introduces the plant clearly. “像星星一样” is the more complete comparison pattern.

Incorrect phrase: “马上就吃下了” / Formula: Subject + 马上/立刻 + 就 + verb + object / Correct: “他马上就把它吃下去了。”  
The object is missing. Add “它” to show that he ate the oleander.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “找” / Context: “他上山下山找植物” / Alternatives: “寻找” or “采集.” “找” is a basic everyday word for looking for something. “寻找” is more formal and suitable for a narrative; “采集” means collecting plants or specimens, so it is especially fitting if he brings the plants back to study.

Word: “很多样子的植物” / Context: “吃了很多样子的植物” / Alternatives: “各种植物” or “许多种植物.” “样子” focuses on appearance or shape. “各种植物” emphasizes a wide range of different plants, while “许多种植物” directly emphasizes many species or types.

Word: “写下来了” / Context: “他把所有的植物写下来了” / Alternatives: “记录下来” or “记载下来.” “写下来” simply means writing something down. “记录下来” means systematically making a record, while “记载下来” is more formal and literary, often used for preserving information for the future.

Word: “漂亮” / Context: “觉得很漂亮” / Alternatives: “美丽” or “鲜艳.” “漂亮” is a common general word for attractive appearance. “美丽” is more literary and expressive; “鲜艳” describes bright, vivid colors, which may be especially appropriate for oleander flowers.

Word: “马上” / Context: “马上就吃下了” / Alternatives: “立刻” or “毫不犹豫地.” “马上” means immediately and is common in speech. “立刻” is slightly more formal and direct; “毫不犹豫地” means “without hesitation,” highlighting that he acted rashly or bravely.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You created a clear sequence of actions and used details such as the star-shaped plant to make the story vivid.  
Next time, focus on choosing “种” for types of plants and using “还没 + verb” without 了.

## Scene 3

> 可是夹竹桃的毒很重，让他肚子很痛。他在地上躺着，觉得他快会死了。可是正好有别人找到他了，给了他一个像伞的植物，叫灵芝。他吃灵芝以后就觉得很好，不再生病了。他测量起来了，像星星的植物是毒，像伞的植物是药。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “夹竹桃的毒很重” / Formula: Noun + 有毒 / Correct: “夹竹桃有毒。”  
“毒很重” is understandable, but 有毒 is the standard and more natural way to say that a plant is poisonous.

Incorrect phrase: “觉得他快会死了” / Formula: Subject + 觉得 + 自己 / Correct: “觉得自己快要死了。”  
When the subject and the person in the embedded clause are the same, 自己 makes the reference clear. 快要 is also more natural than 快会 here.

Incorrect phrase: “正好有别人找到他了” / Formula: 有人 + 找到 + object / Correct: “正好有人找到了他。”  
别人 usually means “other people” in contrast to a known person or group. Here, someone unspecified found him, so 有人 is better.

Incorrect phrase: “他吃灵芝以后就觉得很好” / Formula: Subject + 吃了……以后 + 就 + 觉得/感觉 + adjective / Correct: “他吃了灵芝以后，就觉得好多了。”  
Use 了 after 吃 for a completed action. 觉得很好 is vague; 好多了 clearly means that his condition improved.

Incorrect phrase: “他测量起来了” / Formula: Subject + 发现/总结出 + information / Correct: “他总结出来了：像星星的植物有毒，像伞的植物是药。”  
测量 means “to measure” (for example, length or weight), so it does not fit the meaning here. He is making a conclusion from his experience.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “毒很重” / Context: “夹竹桃的毒很重” / Alternatives: “毒性很强” or “剧毒.” 毒很重 is understandable but less idiomatic. 毒性很强 means “highly toxic” in a neutral, factual way; 剧毒 means “extremely poisonous” and is stronger.

Word: “肚子很痛” / Context: “让他肚子很痛” / Alternatives: “肚子疼得厉害” or “腹痛.” 肚子很痛 is common and simple. 肚子疼得厉害 emphasizes the severity of the pain, while 腹痛 is more formal and medical.

Word: “在地上躺着” / Context: “他在地上躺着” / Alternatives: “倒在地上” or “躺倒在地上.” 躺着 simply describes his position. 倒在地上 suggests that he collapsed because he was weak or ill, which makes the scene more vivid.

Word: “像伞的植物” / Context: “一个像伞的植物” / Alternatives: “形状像伞的植物” or “伞状的植物.” 像伞的植物 is clear but slightly informal. 形状像伞的植物 is more precise, while 伞状的植物 is concise and more descriptive.

Word: “药” / Context: “像伞的植物是药” / Alternatives: “药材” or “草药.” 药 is a general word for medicine. 药材 refers to medicinal materials used to make medicine, while 草药 specifically refers to herbal medicine and fits the story well.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You told the story in a clear sequence—poisoning, help, recovery, and Shennong’s conclusion—and your use of contrasting plant shapes is easy to understand.  
Next time, focus on using more precise verbs, such as 倒下, 总结, or 发现, and on making pronoun references clear with 自己 or specific names.
