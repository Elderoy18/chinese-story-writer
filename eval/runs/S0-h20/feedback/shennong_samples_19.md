## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男人觉得植物很有意思。他叫神农。有的植物吃了以后他的肚子疼死了，可是有的吃了以后很舒服。他找很多植物。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “古代的时候”  
Formula: 在 + historical time period  
Correct: “在古代”

Incorrect phrase: “一个男人觉得植物很有意思。他叫神农。”  
Formula: 在 first mention, introduce a character with + name + identity  
Correct: “在古代，有一个叫神农的人，他觉得植物很有意思。”  
This introduces 神农 more smoothly when he first appears.

Incorrect phrase: “有的植物吃了以后他的肚子疼死了”  
Formula: 人 + 吃了 + 有些植物 + 以后，身体部位 + 疼得 + 程度  
Correct: “他吃了有些植物以后，肚子疼得厉害。”  
The original sentence is unclear because it sounds as if the plants themselves ate something. “疼死了” is understandable but very dramatic; “疼得厉害” is more natural here.

Incorrect phrase: “可是有的吃了以后很舒服”  
Formula: 吃了 + 另一些植物 + 以后，主语 + 感觉/身体 + 很……  
Correct: “可是他吃了另一些植物以后，身体感觉很舒服。”  
Add “他” and “植物” so the meaning is clear.

Incorrect phrase: “他找很多植物。”  
Formula: Subject + verb + 了 + quantity + object  
Correct: “他找了很多植物。”  
Because this is a completed action in a past narrative, use “了.”

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “有意思”  
Context: “植物很有意思”  
Alternatives: “有趣” is a common, natural word for something interesting and enjoyable. “神奇” emphasizes that the plants seem amazing or mysterious, which fits a story about Shennong discovering medicinal plants.

Word: “男人”  
Context: “一个男人”  
Alternatives: “人” is more neutral and natural in “有一个叫神农的人.” “人物” can be used when describing him as an important character in a legend, but it is less natural for direct story narration.

Word: “肚子疼死了”  
Context: “他的肚子疼死了”  
Alternatives: “肚子疼得厉害” means the stomachache was severe, without the exaggerated feeling of “疼死了.” “腹痛” is more formal and is often used in medical contexts.

Word: “舒服”  
Context: “吃了以后很舒服”  
Alternatives: “身体感觉很好” is clearer because it specifically describes physical well-being. “很有精神” emphasizes feeling energetic and refreshed, rather than simply comfortable.

Word: “找”  
Context: “他找了很多植物”  
Alternatives: “寻找” is slightly more formal and emphasizes the process of searching. “采集” means to collect plants from nature, so it is especially suitable if Shennong gathers plants to test them.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You clearly showed the contrast between plants that cause pain and plants that make Shennong feel well, which gives your short story a strong, logical flow.  
Next time, focus on making the subject clear after “吃了……以后,” especially who ate the plants and who feels the result.

## Scene 2

> 凉山很大的山，这也找植物。一天，他找到黄色的花。他听说你跟黄色的花做茶很有用。比方说，你的头发疼死，这冰茶是药。他一喝茶就很不舒服。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “凉山很大的山” / Formula: Place + 是/有 + adjective + 的 + noun / Correct: “凉山是一座很大的山。”  
“凉山” is the name of the mountain, so add 是 and a measure word.

Incorrect phrase: “这也找植物” / Formula: Subject + 也 + verb + object / Correct: “他也在那里找植物。”  
“这” cannot be the subject here; use 他 for the person and 在那里 for the location.

Incorrect phrase: “他听说你跟黄色的花做茶很有用” / Formula: Subject + 听说 + noun + 可以/能 + 用来 + verb / Correct: “他听说黄色的花可以用来泡茶，而且很有用。”  
“跟” means “with” or “and,” but it cannot mean “use something to make tea” here. Also, “你” is an unclear reference; the sentence is about the yellow flowers.

Incorrect phrase: “比方说，你的头发疼死” / Formula: General statement + 比方说 + specific example / Correct: “比方说，如果你的头疼，这种茶可以当药。”  
比方说 must introduce an example after a general statement. “头发疼” means “hair hurts”; use 头疼 for “having a headache.”

Incorrect phrase: “这冰茶是药” / Formula: This/that + measure word + noun + 可以 + 当 + noun / Correct: “这种茶可以当药。”  
“冰茶” means iced tea, which is not the intended meaning. “这种茶” clearly refers to tea made from the yellow flowers.

Incorrect phrase: “他一喝茶就很不舒服” / Formula: Subject + 一 + verb + 就 + result / Correct: “他一喝这种茶就觉得很不舒服。”  
The structure is good, but adding 觉得 makes the feeling more natural, and 这种茶 makes the reference clear.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “很大” / Context: “凉山是一座很大的山。” / Alternatives: “高大” emphasizes that the mountain is tall and imposing; “雄伟” is more literary and describes a grand, majestic mountain. 很大 is simple and understandable, but less vivid.

Word: “找” / Context: “他也在那里找植物。” / Alternatives: “寻找” is more formal and often used in stories or writing; “采集” means collecting plants, herbs, or natural materials, so it is ideal if he takes the plants home.

Word: “黄色的花” / Context: “他找到黄色的花。” / Alternatives: “黄花” is shorter and more natural when the flower type is not important; “金黄色的花” creates a brighter, more vivid image. 黄色的花 is clear but somewhat basic.

Word: “很有用” / Context: “黄色的花可以用来泡茶，而且很有用。” / Alternatives: “对身体有好处” specifically means beneficial for one’s health; “有药用价值” means that it has medicinal value and sounds more advanced. 很有用 is broad and does not explain how the tea helps.

Word: “很不舒服” / Context: “他一喝这种茶就觉得很不舒服。” / Alternatives: “身体不适” is a more formal expression for feeling unwell; “肚子疼” is better if the exact symptom is a stomachache. 很不舒服 is natural but nonspecific.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You did well using a clear sequence of events and the useful pattern “一……就……,” which makes the ending easy to understand.  
Next time, focus on making pronoun references clear and choosing precise words for body symptoms, such as 头疼 or 肚子疼.

## Scene 3

> 糟糕，这黄色的花是夹竹桃，特别是毒。正好在这儿，灵芝药的夹竹桃他吃完了，也写一个课文的植物。谢谢神农。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “特别是毒” / Formula: Subject + 特别 + 有 + adjective/noun property / Correct: “特别有毒。”  
“是毒” is not natural here; use “有毒” to describe a poisonous plant.

Incorrect phrase: “正好在这儿” / Formula: Subject + 正好 + 在 + place / Correct: “正好，神农在这儿。”  
This phrase needs a clear subject. Without one, it is unclear who is “here.”

Incorrect phrase: “灵芝药的夹竹桃” / Formula: A 是 B，C 有/是 D / Correct: “灵芝是药，夹竹桃有毒。”  
This mixes up two different plants. 灵芝 is a medicinal fungus, while 夹竹桃 is poisonous; “灵芝药的夹竹桃” is not a meaningful noun phrase.

Incorrect phrase: “夹竹桃他吃完了” / Formula: Subject + 吃了 + object / Correct: “神农吃了夹竹桃。”  
The subject should be stated clearly. “吃完了” means he finished all of it, which is probably not necessary here.

Incorrect phrase: “也写一个课文的植物” / Formula: Subject + 也 + 写下了 + object + 的用处 / Correct: “他也写下了这些植物的用处。”  
“写一个课文” means “write a text/lesson,” but you seem to mean that Shennong recorded what the plants were used for.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “糟糕” / Context: “糟糕，这黄色的花是夹竹桃。” / Alternatives: “不好了” or “糟了。”  
“糟糕” is natural and expresses alarm. “不好了” sounds more urgent, while “糟了” is a short, conversational reaction to bad news.

Word: “黄色的花” / Context: “这黄色的花是夹竹桃。” / Alternatives: “这朵黄花” or “这朵黄色的夹竹桃。”  
“黄色的花” is understandable but general. “这朵黄花” is more concise, and “这朵黄色的夹竹桃” is more specific and vivid.

Word: “特别” / Context: “特别有毒。” / Alternatives: “非常” or “剧毒。”  
“特别有毒” means “especially poisonous.” “非常有毒” is neutral and common, while “剧毒” means “highly toxic” and is stronger and more precise.

Word: “吃完了” / Context: “夹竹桃他吃完了。” / Alternatives: “吃了” or “误食了。”  
“吃完了” emphasizes finishing all the food. “吃了” simply states that he ate it. “误食了” means “accidentally ate,” which fits a poisonous plant particularly well.

Word: “写” / Context: “也写一个课文的植物。” / Alternatives: “记录” or “写下。”  
“写” is a basic word meaning “to write.” “记录” means “to record/document” and sounds more formal, while “写下” emphasizes writing information down.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You did well to include the important contrast between 夹竹桃 being dangerous and 灵芝 being medicine.  
Next time, focus on stating the subject clearly and using short, complete sentences to make your meaning easier to follow.
