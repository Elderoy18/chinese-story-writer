## Scene 1

> 孟子小的时候，从早到晚都跟朋友玩。孟子和他的朋友喜欢玩在墓地里，装祭拜。孟子五岁的时候，跟妈妈搬家。他们搬家不是因为孟子玩得太多，而是因为妈妈认为他们的家离墓地太近。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “孟子和他的朋友喜欢玩在墓地里，装祭拜。” / Formula: 喜欢 + 在 + place + V.; 装 + V. usually means “pretend to do something,” while “play at doing something” is 玩 + V. / Correct: “孟子和他的朋友喜欢在墓地里玩，玩祭拜的游戏。”  
You can also say: “孟子和他的朋友喜欢在墓地里模仿大人祭拜。”

Incorrect phrase: “孟子五岁的时候，跟妈妈搬家。” / Formula: Subject + 跟/和 + person + 一起 + V. + object; for a completed move, 搬家 + 了 / Correct: “孟子五岁的时候，跟妈妈一起搬家了。”

Incorrect phrase: “他们搬家不是因为孟子玩得太多” / Formula: 搬家 is a completed event, so use 搬了家; 玩得太多 is understandable, but 贪玩/总是玩 is more natural for a child’s habit / Correct: “他们搬家不是因为孟子太贪玩，而是因为妈妈认为他们家离墓地太近。”

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “小的时候” / Context: “孟子小的时候” / Alternatives: “小时候” is more concise and natural in everyday Chinese. “年幼时” is more formal and literary, and it emphasizes that he was very young.

Word: “从早到晚都跟朋友玩” / Context: “从早到晚都跟朋友玩” / Alternatives: “整天和朋友玩” is simpler and very natural. “成天和朋友玩” often suggests that someone does this too much, so it fits well if you want to explain why his mother was concerned.

Word: “玩在墓地里” / Context: “喜欢玩在墓地里” / Alternatives: “在墓地里玩耍” is more natural and slightly more written. “在墓地附近玩” is useful if they played near, rather than inside, the cemetery.

Word: “装祭拜” / Context: “装祭拜” / Alternatives: “模仿大人祭拜” clearly means copying adults’ behavior. “玩祭拜的游戏” sounds natural for children pretending to carry out a ritual.

Word: “认为” / Context: “妈妈认为他们的家离墓地太近” / Alternatives: “觉得” is more common in conversation and sounds less formal. “担心” adds the feeling that his mother was worried: “妈妈担心他们家离墓地太近。”

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You clearly explained the cause-and-effect relationship with “不是……而是……,” and your scene has a logical flow from Mengzi’s play to the family’s move.  
Next time, focus on word order with location phrases: use “在 + place + verb,” such as “在墓地里玩。”

## Scene 2

> 妈妈认为，住离墓地太近，害得孟子的心气。孟子和孟母搬到子思老师的家，跟子思老师住。孟母帮子思打扫房间和做饭。子思帮孟母教她的儿子。因为孟子现在没有朋友，他老是读书。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “住离墓地太近，害得孟子的心气” / Formula: 住在 + 离 + 地点 + 形容词 + 的地方；A + 会影响/损害 + B / Correct: “妈妈认为，住在离墓地太近的地方会影响孟子的心性。”  
“害得” is usually followed by a clear negative result, while “影响孟子的心性” expresses the meaning more naturally here.

Incorrect phrase: “子思帮孟母教她的儿子” / Formula: A + 帮（忙）+ B + 教 + 人；use a specific name when a pronoun could be unclear / Correct: “子思帮孟母教孟子。”  
Using “孟子” instead of “她的儿子” makes the sentence clearer.

Incorrect phrase: “他老是读书” / Formula: Subject + 一直/常常 + verb; 老是 is usually used for repeated actions with a negative or complaining tone / Correct: “因为孟子现在没有朋友，他一直读书。”  
“老是” can sound like a complaint. “一直” is more neutral and fits this context better.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “心气” / Context: “害得孟子的心气” / Alternatives: “心性” or “品性.” “心气” usually refers to one’s spirit, ambition, or emotional drive. “心性” refers to a person’s character and inner nature, which fits the story of Mencius learning from his surroundings. “品性” emphasizes moral character.

Word: “住离墓地太近” / Context: “住离墓地太近” / Alternatives: “住在墓地附近” or “居住在墓地旁边.” “附近” is a very natural everyday word for “nearby.” “居住” is more formal and works well in written storytelling.

Word: “搬到子思老师的家” / Context: “孟子和孟母搬到子思老师的家” / Alternatives: “搬到子思老师家里” or “搬去和子思老师同住.” “家里” sounds more natural in conversation and simple narratives. “同住” means “live together” and is more concise.

Word: “打扫房间” / Context: “孟母帮子思打扫房间和做饭” / Alternatives: “打扫屋子” or “料理家务.” “打扫房间” is correct, but “打扫屋子” can refer to cleaning the whole home. “料理家务” is more advanced and includes cooking, cleaning, and other household tasks.

Word: “没有朋友” / Context: “因为孟子现在没有朋友” / Alternatives: “没有玩伴” or “感到孤单.” “没有朋友” is direct and neutral. “没有玩伴” is especially suitable for a child who has no one to play with. “感到孤单” emphasizes Mencius’s feeling of loneliness.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You told the events in a clear, logical order and showed how Mencius’s environment affected his behavior.  
Next time, focus on using “住在 + place” and choosing “一直” instead of “老是” for neutral repeated actions.

## Scene 3

> 子思认为，对孩子来说，读书最重要，特别是学哲学。孟子不是练习写文章，就是看哲学书。孟子从五岁一直学哲学。他才五岁就学哲学，难怪他成为很有名的哲学家。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “对子思来说，读书最重要”  
Formula: 对 + 人 + 来说，+ statement  
Correct: “子思认为，对孩子来说，读书最重要。”  
“对子思来说” would mean “for Zisi,” but the sentence is about what is important for children.

Incorrect phrase: “孟子不是练习写文章，就是看哲学书。”  
Formula: Subject + 不是 + V1，+ 就是 + V2  
Correct: “孟子不是练习写文章，就是看哲学书。”  
This sentence is grammatically correct: it means he either practised writing or read philosophy books.

Incorrect phrase: “孟子从五岁一直学哲学。”  
Formula: 从 + time + 开始，+ 一直 + V + 到/…  
Correct: “孟子从五岁开始一直学哲学。”  
Add “开始” after the starting age to make the time expression complete and natural.

Incorrect phrase: “他才五岁就学哲学。”  
Formula: 才 + number/age + 就 + V  
Correct: “他才五岁就开始学哲学。”  
The 才……就…… structure is correct here. Adding “开始” makes it clearer that age five was when he began learning philosophy.

Incorrect phrase: “难怪他成为很有名的哲学家。”  
Formula: 成为 + adjective + 的 + noun  
Correct: “难怪他后来成为了一位很有名的哲学家。”  
“后来” makes the timeline clearer, and “一位” is natural before a person’s profession.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “最重要”  
Context: “读书最重要”  
Alternatives: “至关重要” means “of crucial importance” and is more formal and emphatic. “非常重要” is simpler and more neutral. “最重要” is correct, but “至关重要” gives the statement a stronger, more academic tone.

Word: “读书”  
Context: “对孩子来说，读书最重要”  
Alternatives: “学习” is broader and includes all kinds of learning, not only reading books. “接受教育” is more formal and refers to receiving an education. “读书” can mean studying, but it can also literally mean reading books.

Word: “练习写文章”  
Context: “练习写文章”  
Alternatives: “练习写作” is more concise and natural, meaning to practise writing in general. “写文章” specifically focuses on writing essays or articles, while “写作” can include many types of written work.

Word: “看哲学书”  
Context: “看哲学书”  
Alternatives: “读哲学书” is more natural when referring to reading books carefully. “研读哲学著作” is more advanced and means to study philosophical works closely and seriously. “看” is casual and can imply simply looking at something.

Word: “很有名”  
Context: “很有名的哲学家”  
Alternatives: “著名的” is a formal, common word for “famous.” “享有盛名的” is more literary and means “highly renowned.” “很有名” is natural in conversation, while “著名” fits historical or academic writing better.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You used the 才……就…… structure correctly and connected Mengzi’s early study to his later success in a clear, logical way.  
Next time, focus on adding “开始” after a starting age and choosing more precise verbs such as “读” or “研读” for books.
