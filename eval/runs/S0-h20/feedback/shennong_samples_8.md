## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个人叫神农。一天，神农正在路上走，一看很多人正在吃一些植物。一些人一吃植物就生病了，神农想帮别人。所以想知道哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “一天，神农正在路上走，一看很多人正在吃一些植物。”  
Formula: Subject + 在/正在 + place + verb; then use 看到/发现 + clause for something noticed  
Correct: “一天，神农正在路上走，看到很多人在吃一些植物。”  
“一看” usually means “as soon as one looks,” so “看到” is more natural here.

Incorrect phrase: “一些人一吃植物就生病了”  
Formula: 一 + subject + verb + 就 + result; use “有些人” for an unspecified group  
Correct: “有些人一吃这些植物就生病了。”  
Adding “这些” makes it clear that the plants are the ones mentioned in the previous sentence.

Incorrect phrase: “神农想帮别人。”  
Formula: character/name + 想 + 帮助 + specific person/group  
Correct: “神农想帮助这些人。”  
“别人” is vague here; “这些人” clearly refers to the people who became sick.

Incorrect phrase: “所以想知道哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃。”  
Formula: Subject + 所以 + 想知道 + question clause  
Correct: “所以他想知道哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃。”  
The subject should be included after “所以” to make the sentence complete and clear.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “古代的时候”  
Context: “古代的时候，有一个人叫神农。”  
Alternatives: “在古代” is shorter and more natural in narrative writing. “很久以前” has a story-like feeling, similar to “once upon a time.”

Word: “路上走”  
Context: “神农正在路上走。”  
Alternatives: “赶路” means travelling toward a destination and is more concise. “行走” is more formal and literary, often used in written stories.

Word: “一看”  
Context: “一看很多人正在吃一些植物。”  
Alternatives: “看到” simply means “saw” and fits this scene naturally. “发现” means “noticed/discovered” and emphasizes that Shennong noticed something important.

Word: “生病了”  
Context: “一些人一吃植物就生病了。”  
Alternatives: “病倒了” means “became ill and collapsed/fell sick,” so it is stronger. “身体不舒服” means “felt unwell” and is softer than “生病.”

Word: “帮别人”  
Context: “神农想帮别人。”  
Alternatives: “帮助这些人” is clearer because it identifies whom he wants to help. “救治这些人” means “treat and save these people” and is appropriate if he is trying to cure their illness.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You did well introducing 神农 clearly at the beginning and creating a logical cause-and-effect story about why he wanted to learn about plants.  
Next time, focus on making pronoun references specific and including the subject after connectors such as “所以.”

## Scene 2

> 然后，神农开始尝很多植物，比方说他吃过生姜、芥兰和菠菜。一些植物吃真好，特别是黄瓜。但是，一些植物毒得很，特别是夹竹桃。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “神农开始尝很多植物” / Formula: Subject + 开始 + 尝试/品尝 + (various kinds of) + noun / Correct: “神农开始尝试各种植物。”  
“尝” is usually used for tasting food, while “尝试各种植物” means testing different plants, which fits Shennong’s story better.

Incorrect phrase: “一些植物吃真好” / Formula: Subject + 吃起来 + adverb + adjective / Correct: “一些植物吃起来很好。”  
Use “吃起来” to describe how something tastes or feels when eaten. “真好” is possible in speech, but “很好” is more natural in this narrative.

Incorrect phrase: “一些植物毒得很” / Formula: 有些/一些 + noun + 很 + adjective / Correct: “一些植物很有毒。”  
“毒得很” is understandable, but “很有毒” is the standard and more natural way to say that plants are poisonous.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “开始尝很多植物” / Alternatives: “开始尝试各种植物” or “开始辨别各种植物”  
“尝” means to taste something, often food. “尝试各种植物” emphasizes that Shennong tested plants to learn their effects. “辨别各种植物” means to identify and distinguish plants, emphasizing knowledge rather than eating.

Word: “比方说” / Alternatives: “比如” or “例如”  
“比方说” is conversational and natural in speech. “比如” is concise and works well in both speaking and writing. “例如” is more formal and is especially suitable for written narration.

Word: “一些植物” / Alternatives: “有些植物” or “部分植物”  
“Some plants” is correct, but “有些植物” sounds especially natural when contrasting two groups: some are beneficial, while others are poisonous. “部分植物” is more formal and objective.

Word: “吃真好” / Alternatives: “吃起来很好吃” or “味道很好”  
“很好吃” directly describes delicious food. “味道很好” focuses on flavor and sounds slightly more descriptive. However, in this story, “对身体很好” may be better if you mean the plants are healthy rather than tasty.

Word: “毒得很” / Alternatives: “很有毒” or “毒性很强”  
“很有毒” is the most common everyday expression for poisonous plants. “毒性很强” is more formal and emphasizes that the poison is especially powerful or dangerous.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You used clear contrast words such as “但是” and “特别是,” which makes the paragraph easy to follow.  
Next time, focus on using “吃起来” for taste and “有毒” for poisonous things.

## Scene 3

> 一个人对神农说，麻烦我们可以吃什么植物？神农说，这就要看你想不想生病了。以后，神农告诉别人一些植物能吃，一些植物不能吃。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “麻烦我们可以吃什么植物？” / Formula: 请问 + question; “麻烦” is usually used as a verb (“to trouble someone”) or in “麻烦你……”. / Correct: “请问，我们可以吃什么植物？”

Incorrect phrase: “这就要看你想不想生病了。” / Formula: 这就要看 + A-not-A question that is directly relevant to the uncertain result. Here, whether a plant is edible depends on whether it is poisonous, not whether the person wants to get sick. / Correct: “这就要看这种植物有没有毒了。”  
A fuller answer would be: “这种植物能不能吃，这就要看它有没有毒了。”

Incorrect phrase: “以后，神农告诉别人一些植物能吃，一些植物不能吃。” / Formula: Later action + 告诉 + person + information; use 了 for a completed action in a story. / Correct: “后来，神农告诉人们哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃。”

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “一个人” / Context: “一个人对神农说” / Alternatives: “有个人” is more natural when introducing an unspecified person in a story. “一位村民” is more vivid and specific, suggesting an ordinary person from the community.

Word: “麻烦” / Context: “麻烦我们可以吃什么植物？” / Alternatives: “请问” is the standard polite expression for asking a question. “请教一下” is slightly more respectful and means “I would like to ask for your advice.”

Word: “以后” / Context: “以后，神农告诉别人……” / Alternatives: “后来” is more natural for the next event in a past narrative. “从那以后” emphasizes that, starting from that point, something continued to happen.

Word: “别人” / Context: “神农告诉别人……” / Alternatives: “人们” means people in general and fits this context well. “大家” sounds more conversational and emphasizes the group around him.

Word: “能吃” / Context: “一些植物能吃” / Alternatives: “可以食用” is more formal and precise, often used for plants or food. “可食用的” can describe a noun directly, as in “可食用的植物.”

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You clearly expressed a logical story sequence: someone asks Shennong a question, and he later shares what he has learned with others.  
Next time, focus on using **这就要看……了** with a directly relevant A-not-A question, such as “有没有毒” or “能不能吃.”
