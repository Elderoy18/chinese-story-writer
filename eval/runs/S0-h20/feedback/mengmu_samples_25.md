## Scene 1

> 这是古代的故事。孟子小的时候，他的爸爸很早就去世了。从他的家到墓地很近，所以他每天老是从早到晚去墓地跟朋友们玩玩儿。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “这是古代的故事。” / Formula: 在 + historical time / Correct: “这是一个发生在古代的故事。” or “在古代，有一个叫孟子的小孩子。”  
“古代的故事” is understandable, but “发生在古代的故事” is more natural. Also, introduce Mencius briefly when he first appears.

Incorrect phrase: “孟子小的时候，他的爸爸很早就去世了。” / Formula: Character + 小的时候，+ family member + 就 + 去世了 / Correct: “孟子小的时候，他的爸爸就去世了。”  
“很早” is possible, but it is slightly redundant with “孟子小的时候.” Use “很早” only if you want to strongly emphasize that his father died unusually early.

Incorrect phrase: “从他的家到墓地很近” / Formula: 从 + place A + 到 + place B + 很近 / Correct: “从他家到墓地很近。”  
“他的家” is not wrong, but “他家” is more natural in this sentence.

Incorrect phrase: “每天老是从早到晚去墓地跟朋友们玩玩儿” / Formula: Subject + 每天 + 从早到晚 + 去 + place + 跟 + person + 玩 / Correct: “所以他每天从早到晚都去墓地跟朋友们玩。”  
“每天” and “老是” overlap; using both sounds repetitive. “玩玩儿” usually suggests playing briefly or casually, which does not fit “从早到晚.”

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “这是古代的故事” / Context: “这是古代的故事。” / Alternatives: “这是一个发生在古代的故事” sounds more complete and natural. “发生在古代” clearly describes the time setting of the story, while “古代的故事” can also mean a story that was created long ago.

Word: “很早就去世了” / Context: “他的爸爸很早就去世了。” / Alternatives: “早逝” is a more advanced and concise word meaning “to die young or early.” You could write “他的父亲早逝了.” “去世” is a respectful, common word; “早逝” emphasizes that the death happened prematurely.

Word: “他的爸爸” / Context: “他的爸爸很早就去世了。” / Alternatives: “父亲” is more formal and is especially suitable for written storytelling. “爸爸” feels warmer and more conversational, while “父亲” gives the narrative a more literary tone.

Word: “老是” / Context: “他每天老是从早到晚去墓地……” / Alternatives: “常常” or “经常” are more neutral. “老是” often expresses annoyance or criticism, as if the speaker thinks the action happens too much. “常常” simply means “often,” and works better for a neutral story.

Word: “墓地” / Context: “去墓地跟朋友们玩……” / Alternatives: “坟地” is a natural alternative in a story about Mencius living near graves. “墓地” is neutral and modern; “坟地” feels more concrete and traditional, though it can sound slightly more informal.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You clearly explained the cause-and-effect relationship between living near the cemetery and Mencius’s daily behavior, and your sentence flow is easy to follow.  
Next time, focus on introducing characters naturally and avoiding overlapping frequency words such as “每天” and “老是.”

## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈觉得在墓地玩儿，害得，所以他们搬家。搬家对孟子有好的影响，他才五岁，可是每天不是写字就是帮妈妈的工作。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “在墓地玩儿，害得” / Formula: Subject + 觉得/认为 + Clause；“害得” needs a result after it: A + 害得 + B + Result / Correct: “他的妈妈觉得在墓地玩儿不太好，所以他们搬家了。”  
“害得” is incomplete here. You could use “不太好” to explain why his mother wanted to move.

Incorrect phrase: “搬家对孟子有好的影响” / Formula: Subject + 对 + Person + 有 + Adjective + 影响 / Correct: “搬家对孟子有好的影响。”  
This sentence is grammatically correct. For a more natural meaning in this story, however, “有不好的影响” may fit better, because moving often could make life difficult for him.

Incorrect phrase: “他才五岁，可是每天不是写字就是帮妈妈的工作” / Formula: 才 + Number + 就 + Verb; 不是 + Verb phrase + 就是 + Verb phrase / Correct: “他才五岁，就每天不是写字就是帮妈妈工作。”  
Use “才五岁就……” to emphasize that he was very young when he had to do these things. Also, say “帮妈妈工作” or “帮妈妈做家务,” not “帮妈妈的工作.”

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “玩儿” / Context: “在墓地玩儿” / Alternatives: “玩耍” is slightly more formal and works well in writing: “在墓地附近玩耍。” “嬉戏” is more literary and suggests children playing happily and noisily. “玩儿” is natural in speech but more casual.

Word: “害得” / Context: “在墓地玩儿，害得……” / Alternatives: “担心” means “to worry” and clearly expresses the mother’s feeling: “妈妈担心墓地附近的环境不好。” “影响” emphasizes an effect or consequence: “妈妈认为这会影响孟子的成长。” Unlike “害得,” both options can be used easily in this context.

Word: “搬家” / Context: “所以他们搬家” / Alternatives: “搬到别的地方住” is clearer for a beginner and explicitly describes moving residence. “迁居” is a more formal written word, often used in historical or narrative writing.

Word: “好的影响” / Context: “搬家对孟子有好的影响” / Alternatives: “积极的影响” is more formal and commonly used in writing. “好处” is more direct and conversational: “搬家对孟子有好处。” “好的影响” is understandable, but “积极的影响” sounds more natural in an essay.

Word: “帮妈妈工作” / Context: “帮妈妈工作” / Alternatives: “帮妈妈做家务” specifically means helping with household chores, such as cooking or cleaning. “帮妈妈干活儿” is more colloquial and can include both housework and other physical work. Choose “做家务” if that is what you mean.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You clearly expressed the mother’s reason for moving and showed how Mengzi’s young age made his daily responsibilities surprising.  
Next time, focus on completing result expressions such as “害得……” and using the pattern “才……就……” together.

## Scene 3

> 他很聪明的人，难怪他成为有名的人。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “他很聪明的人” / Formula: Subject + 很 + adjective + 的 + noun; or Subject + 很 + adjective / Correct: “他是一个很聪明的人。”  
Use 是 when identifying someone as “a … person.” Alternatively, simply say: “他很聪明。”

Incorrect phrase: “成为有名的人” / Formula: 成为 + adjective + noun (usually a specific role, identity, or profession) / Correct: “他成为了一位有名的哲学家。”  
“有名的人” is grammatically possible, but it is vague. After 成为, a more specific identity is more natural.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “聪明” / Context: “他是一个很聪明的人。” / Alternatives: “智慧” or “睿智.” “聪明” is common and means quick-thinking or intelligent. “智慧” emphasizes deeper wisdom and good judgment, while “睿智” is more formal and describes someone exceptionally wise.

Word: “有名” / Context: “他成为了一位有名的哲学家。” / Alternatives: “著名” or “闻名.” “有名” is a common, neutral word for being well-known. “著名” is more formal and often used for widely recognized people or places. “闻名” suggests being famous for a particular achievement or quality.

Word: “难怪” / Context: “难怪他成为……” / Alternatives: “因此” or “所以.” “难怪” means “no wonder” and expresses that the following result now seems understandable. “因此” and “所以” simply show cause and effect, without the speaker’s feeling of realization.

Word: “人” / Context: “有名的人” / Alternatives: “哲学家,” “学者,” or “思想家.” “人” is very general. “哲学家” specifically means a philosopher; “学者” emphasizes academic learning and research; “思想家” emphasizes influential ideas and thinking.

Word: “成为” / Context: “成为有名的人” / Alternatives: “变得” or “成了.” “成为” is more formal and is especially suitable for becoming a role or identity, such as a 哲学家. “变得” is used for a change in qualities, such as 变得很有名. “成了” is more conversational.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You used “难怪” well to connect his intelligence with his later success, which gives the sentence a clear logical flow.  
Next time, focus on distinguishing adjective sentences (“他很聪明”) from noun descriptions (“他是一个很聪明的人”).
