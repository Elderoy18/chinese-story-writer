## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个男孩名字叫孟子。孟子和孟子的妈妈两个人住在很穷的农村。他们住的地方旁边是个墓地，墓地老是有很多人祭拜。因为人们在祭拜，他们一直在哭。孟子和孟子的妈妈住在这里才一个星期就觉得受不了了，而且害得孟子的学习。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “古代的时候” / Formula: 在 + historical time period / Correct: “在古代”  
“在古代” is the more natural way to begin a historical story.

Incorrect phrase: “有一个男孩名字叫孟子” / Formula: 有一个 + 叫 + name + 的 + noun / Correct: “有一个叫孟子的男孩”  
This introduces the character more naturally.

Incorrect phrase: “孟子和孟子的妈妈两个人住在很穷的农村” / Formula: Subject + 住在 + place；modifier + 的 + noun / Correct: “孟子和妈妈住在一个贫穷的农村”  
You do not need to repeat “孟子的.” Also, “贫穷的农村” is more natural than “很穷的农村.”

Incorrect phrase: “他们住的地方旁边是个墓地” / Formula: Place + 旁边 + 有 + noun / Correct: “他们住的地方旁边有一座墓地”  
Use “有” to say that something exists next to a place. “一座” is a suitable measure word for a cemetery.

Incorrect phrase: “墓地老是有很多人祭拜” / Formula: Place + 总是 + 有人 + 来 + verb / Correct: “墓地旁总是有人来祭拜”  
“老是” can sound impatient or negative. Also, people usually “来祭拜” at a cemetery rather than “祭拜墓地.”

Incorrect phrase: “因为人们在祭拜，他们一直在哭” / Formula: 因为 + specific person/group + verb，+ they + result / Correct: “因为来祭拜的人一直在哭，所以孟子和妈妈觉得很吵。”  
“他们” is unclear: it could refer to the worshippers or to Mencius and his mother. Naming “来祭拜的人” makes the meaning clear.

Incorrect phrase: “孟子和孟子的妈妈住在这里才一个星期就觉得受不了了，而且害得孟子的学习” / Formula: Subject + 才 + duration + 就 + result；影响/害得 + person + verb / Correct: “孟子和妈妈住在这里才一个星期就受不了了，这也影响了孟子的学习。”  
“害得孟子的学习” is incomplete: “学习” needs a verb such as “影响了.”


**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “很穷” / Context: “住在很穷的农村” / Alternatives: “贫穷” or “贫困”  
“很穷” is simple and conversational. “贫穷” is more formal and suitable for narration, while “贫困” often emphasizes difficult economic conditions.

Word: “农村” / Context: “住在一个贫穷的农村” / Alternatives: “乡村” or “村庄”  
“农村” often refers broadly to rural areas, especially in contrast with cities. “乡村” has a more literary and scenic feeling, while “村庄” refers to one specific village.

Word: “老是” / Context: “墓地老是有很多人祭拜” / Alternatives: “总是” or “经常”  
“老是” often suggests annoyance, so it can sound like the narrator is complaining. “总是” means “always” and is more neutral; “经常” means “frequently” and is slightly less absolute.

Word: “哭” / Context: “他们一直在哭” / Alternatives: “哭泣” or “痛哭”  
“哭” is the basic everyday word. “哭泣” is more written and literary, while “痛哭” emphasizes intense, sorrowful crying.

Word: “受不了” / Context: “就觉得受不了了” / Alternatives: “无法忍受” or “难以忍受”  
“受不了” is common in speech and expresses that something has become too difficult to tolerate. “无法忍受” is stronger and more formal, while “难以忍受” sounds more literary and descriptive.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You created a clear cause-and-effect story: the cemetery, the crying visitors, and the effect on Mengzi’s life connect logically.  
Next time, focus on making pronoun references clear and using “有” to describe things that exist in a location.

## Scene 2

> 孟子的妈妈觉得孟子取得良好的教育是很重要的，所以孟子的妈妈认为这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好，就打算跟孟子搬家。孟子的妈妈带孟子不是搬到城市，而是搬到了孟子老师的家。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “孟子取得良好的教育”  
Formula: 人 + 接受/获得 + （形容词）+ 教育  
Correct: “孟子接受良好的教育” or “孟子获得良好的教育”  
“取得” is usually used for achievements or results, such as “取得成功” or “取得进步.”


Incorrect phrase: “孟子的妈妈带孟子不是搬到城市，而是搬到了孟子老师的家。”  
Formula: 主语 + 不是/没有 + 动词短语，而是 + 动词短语  
Correct: “孟子的妈妈没有带孟子搬到城市，而是带孟子搬到了孟子老师家附近。”  
The two parts of “不是……而是……” should have parallel verb phrases. Also, if they moved near the teacher rather than into the teacher’s home, “老师家附近” is clearer and more natural.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “觉得”  
Context: “孟子的妈妈觉得……”  
Alternatives: “认为” or “意识到”  
“觉得” is common and conversational. “认为” sounds more formal and is useful when expressing an opinion or judgment. “意识到” emphasizes that she came to realize the importance of education.

Word: “取得教育”  
Context: “孟子取得良好的教育”  
Alternatives: “接受良好的教育” or “获得良好的教育”  
“接受教育” is the most natural collocation for receiving schooling. “获得教育” emphasizes gaining access to education.

Word: “良好的”  
Context: “良好的教育”  
Alternatives: “优质的教育” or “更好的教育”  
“良好的教育” is correct and neutral. “优质的教育” emphasizes high quality, while “更好的教育” is especially natural when comparing the new place with the old one.

Word: “不太好”  
Context: “对儿子的教育不太好”  
Alternatives: “不利于儿子的教育” or “不适合儿子学习”  
“不太好” is simple and understandable. “不利于……” is more formal and clearly means something has a negative effect. “不适合儿子学习” is direct and natural in this situation.

Word: “打算”  
Context: “就打算跟孟子搬家”  
Alternatives: “决定” or “计划”  
“打算” means she intends to do something. “决定” is stronger: she has made up her mind. “计划” emphasizes an organized plan, perhaps including where and when to move.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You explained Mengzi’s mother’s motivation clearly, and the cause-and-effect flow with “所以” and “就” is very easy to follow.  
Next time, focus on choosing natural verb–noun combinations, especially for ideas such as “receiving an education.”

## Scene 3

> 孟子跟他的老师从早到晚学习，难怪他的成绩很快就进步了很多。孟子学习的时候，孟子的妈妈不是待在家里就是编织衣服赚钱。以后孟子成为了中国有名的哲学家。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “孟子跟他的老师从早到晚学习” / Formula: 谁 + 跟 + 谁 + 一起 + 学习 + 什么；or 谁 + 在 + 地点 + 跟 + 谁 + 学习 / Correct: “孟子从早到晚跟老师一起学习。”  
“跟老师学习” is understandable, but adding “一起” makes it clear that Mencius studied together with his teacher.

Incorrect phrase: “孟子学习的时候，孟子的妈妈不是待在家里就是编织衣服赚钱。” / Formula: 当/在……的时候，谁 + 不是 + V1 + 就是 + V2；the two options should be parallel and logical / Correct: “孟子学习的时候，孟子的妈妈不是在家里做家务，就是编织衣服赚钱。”  
“待在家里” describes a location, while “编织衣服赚钱” is an action. “在家里做家务” makes the two parts more parallel.

Incorrect phrase: “以后孟子成为了中国有名的哲学家。” / Formula: In a narrative, later = 后来 + 谁 + 成为（了）+ …… / Correct: “后来，孟子成为了中国有名的哲学家。”  
“以后” usually means “after a specified time/event” or “in the future.” “后来” is more natural for the next event in a past narrative.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “进步了很多” / Context: “他的成绩很快就进步了很多。” / Alternatives: “提高了很多” or “有了很大的提高.” “进步” is natural and emphasizes overall development; “提高” is especially natural when discussing grades, ability, or a measurable level.

Word: “待在家里” / Context: “孟子的妈妈不是待在家里……” / Alternatives: “做家务” or “操持家务.” “待在家里” only means “stay at home” and does not say what she does there. “做家务” is common and neutral; “操持家务” is more formal and emphasizes managing household responsibilities.

Word: “编织衣服” / Context: “编织衣服赚钱。” / Alternatives: “织布” or “缝制衣服.” “编织” is usually used for weaving items such as baskets, sweaters, or fabric. “织布” specifically means weaving cloth, while “缝制衣服” means sewing clothes.

Word: “有名的” / Context: “中国有名的哲学家。” / Alternatives: “著名的” or “杰出的.” “有名” is common and simple. “著名” is more formal and means widely known; “杰出” emphasizes that he was exceptionally excellent, not merely famous.

Word: “从早到晚” / Context: “从早到晚学习。” / Alternatives: “整天” or “孜孜不倦地.” “从早到晚” vividly emphasizes the long duration of study. “整天” is more casual, while “孜孜不倦地” is literary and praises someone for studying diligently without becoming tired.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You created a clear, logical mini-story that connects Mencius’s hard work, his mother’s effort, and his later success.  
Next time, focus on choosing “后来” versus “以后” and making the two halves of “不是……就是……” parallel.
