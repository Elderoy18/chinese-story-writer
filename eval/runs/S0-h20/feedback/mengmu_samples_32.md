## Scene 1

> 有一天孟子去了墓地玩儿，但是他妈妈觉得这是不合适的地方让孩子玩，所以她决定了他们会搬家找更好的地方。她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福，而且住在这儿教育不太好。因为她望子成龙，所以她会一直搬家才能找到质量好的教育。然后他们搬到了离城市更近的地方。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “她决定了他们会搬家找更好的地方” / Formula: Subject + 决定 + Verb Phrase; for a planned action, use 决定 + 搬到 + Place / Correct: “她决定搬家，到一个更好的地方去住。”

Incorrect phrase: “这是不合适的地方让孩子玩” / Formula: Place + 适合/不适合 + Person + Verb / Correct: “这个地方不适合孩子玩。”

Incorrect phrase: “住在这儿教育不太好” / Formula: 在 + Place + 的 + Noun + 很/不太 + Adjective / Correct: “这里的教育环境不太好。”


Incorrect phrase: “她会一直搬家才能找到质量好的教育” / Formula: 为了 + Goal, Subject + 才/就 + Verb; or Subject + 一直/不断 + Verb, 直到 + Result / Correct: “为了让儿子得到更好的教育，她决定继续搬家。”  
“会一直搬家才能” is not natural here because 搬家 is a completed action, rather than a continuous activity.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “不合适” / Context: “这是不合适的地方让孩子玩” / Alternatives: “不适合” is more natural when talking about whether a place is suitable for an activity: “这个地方不适合孩子玩。” “不妥” is more formal and emphasizes that something is inappropriate or unwise.

Word: “恐怕” / Context: “她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福” / Alternatives: “担心” more directly expresses a mother’s worry: “她担心儿子不会有幸福的童年。” “害怕” is stronger and suggests fear, while “恐怕” often means “I’m afraid that…” or expresses an uncertain prediction.

Word: “教育不太好” / Context: “住在这儿教育不太好” / Alternatives: “教育环境不好” refers to the overall educational setting. “教育资源不足” is more advanced and specifically means that there are not enough schools, teachers, or learning opportunities.

Word: “质量好的教育” / Context: “找到质量好的教育” / Alternatives: “优质教育” is concise and formal, meaning high-quality education. “更好的教育资源” is very natural when referring to schools, teachers, and learning opportunities available in an area.

Word: “离城市更近的地方” / Context: “他们搬到了离城市更近的地方” / Alternatives: “市郊” means the suburbs and is a concise, natural noun. “靠近市中心的地方” specifically emphasizes being closer to the center of the city, where educational resources may be better.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You clearly explained Meng Mu’s motivation for moving and connected her concern for her son with the idea of “望子成龙” very well.  
Next time, focus on using **决定 + verb phrase** and more natural phrases for discussing education, such as “教育环境” and “教育资源.”

## Scene 2

> 他们住才一月就又搬家。古代的时候，一般人不喜欢卖东西的人。但是孟子老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿，害得妈妈着急得很，因为她恐怕儿子会成为跟他们很像。她告诉了孟子：他们不是朋友，而是不好的人。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “他们住才一月就又搬家。” / Formula: Subject + 才 + 在 + place + 住了 + duration + 就 + 又 + verb / Correct: “他们才在那儿住了一个月，就又搬家了。”

Incorrect phrase: “古代的时候” / Formula: 在 + historical time / Correct: “在古代，一般人不喜欢卖东西的人。”

Incorrect phrase: “孟子老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿” / Formula: Subject + 常常/老是 + 跟 + modifier + 的 + noun + 玩儿. Do not use 老是 and 常常 together, because both indicate frequency. / Correct: “但是孟子常常跟这些卖东西的人玩儿。”  
“这些卖东西的人” is understandable, but “这些做买卖的人” is more natural here.

Incorrect phrase: “害得妈妈着急得很” / Formula: Subject + 让/使 + specific person + adjective; use a specific reference rather than “妈妈” when needed. / Correct: “这让孟母非常着急。”

Incorrect phrase: “因为她恐怕儿子会成为跟他们很像。” / Formula: 因为 + subject + 担心/害怕 + noun + 会 + become + 很像 + object / Correct: “因为她担心儿子会变得跟他们很像。”  
“成为跟他们很像” is incomplete; use “变得跟他们很像” or “成为和他们很像的人.”

Incorrect phrase: “她告诉了孟子：他们不是朋友，而是不好的人。” / Formula: Subject + 告诉 + person + (that) + specific noun phrase; modifiers come before the noun with 的. / Correct: “她告诉孟子：他们不是你的朋友，而是一些不好的人。”  
For a general statement, “告诉” does not need 了 here.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “卖东西的人” / Context: “这些卖东西的人” / Alternatives: “做买卖的人” or “商贩.” “卖东西的人” is clear and neutral, but it is quite basic. “做买卖的人” is a natural general expression for people engaged in trade, while “商贩” is more concise and is especially suitable for small-scale street vendors in a historical setting.

Word: “玩儿” / Context: “跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿” / Alternatives: “一起玩耍” or “来往.” “玩儿” means to play and is appropriate for a child. “一起玩耍” is slightly more written and descriptive; “来往” means to associate or have contact with someone, and it better conveys Mengzi repeatedly mixing with these people.

Word: “着急得很” / Context: “害得妈妈着急得很” / Alternatives: “担心” or “忧虑.” “着急” emphasizes an immediate feeling of anxiety or impatience. “担心” is more natural for a mother worrying about her child’s future, while “忧虑” is more formal and literary.

Word: “恐怕” / Context: “她恐怕儿子会……” / Alternatives: “担心” or “害怕.” “恐怕” often introduces an uncertain prediction, such as “恐怕会下雨.” “担心” more directly expresses Meng Mu’s personal worry; “害怕” is stronger and emphasizes fear.

Word: “不好的人” / Context: “而是不好的人” / Alternatives: “品行不好的人” or “不适合交往的人.” “不好的人” is understandable but vague. “品行不好的人” specifically describes people with bad character or conduct, while “不适合交往的人” sounds gentler and emphasizes that they are not good companions for Mengzi.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You clearly expressed the cause-and-effect relationship between Mengzi’s behavior and Meng Mu’s worry, which gives the scene a logical flow.  
Next time, focus on word order with **才…就…** and on choosing one frequency word, such as **常常** or **老是**, rather than using both together.

## Scene 3

> 你不是当数学家，而是当哲学家，不能当卖东西的人。所以他们又搬家。在他们第三个家，孟子学了怎么祭拜。妈妈感觉这个地方适合他们，告诉了孟子：我们找到了最后的家。在这儿妈妈让儿子从早到晚学习，他难怪成为有名的哲学家。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “你不是当数学家，而是当哲学家，不能当卖东西的人。” / Formula: Subject + 不是 + A， 而是 + B; when giving advice or expectations, use 要/应该 + V. / Correct: “孟子，你不是要当数学家，而是要当哲学家，不能当商人。”  
“你” is inconsistent with the later third-person narration (“他们”“孟子”). Also, 商人 is the natural noun for “a person who sells things.”

Incorrect phrase: “所以他们又搬家。” / Formula: Subject + 又 + 搬家 + 了; or Subject + 又 + 搬到 + Place + 去了 / Correct: “所以他们又搬家了。”  
Use 了 to show that this move was completed.

Incorrect phrase: “在他们第三个家” / Formula: 在 + possessive + ordinal number + measure word + noun / Correct: “在他们的第三个家里”  
Add 的 after 他们, and 家里 is more natural when describing an activity inside the home.

Incorrect phrase: “孟子学了怎么祭拜。” / Formula: 学会了 + 怎么 + V.; or 学习 + 怎么 + V. / Correct: “孟子学会了怎么祭拜。”  
学了 can mean “studied,” but 学会了 clearly means that he successfully learned the skill.

Incorrect phrase: “妈妈感觉这个地方适合他们，告诉了孟子” / Formula: Subject + 觉得 + clause, then + 告诉 + person + content / Correct: “妈妈觉得这个地方很适合他们，就告诉孟子：‘我们找到了最后的家。’”  
觉得 is more natural than 感觉 when stating an opinion. 告诉 normally takes the listener and then the content; 了 is not needed before direct speech here.

Incorrect phrase: “在这儿妈妈让儿子从早到晚学习，他难怪成为有名的哲学家。” / Formula: 因为/在 + background, Subject + 从早到晚 + 学习; 难怪 + result / Correct: “在这儿，妈妈让孟子从早到晚学习，难怪他后来成为了一位有名的哲学家。”  
Use 孟子 instead of 儿子 for clarity. 难怪 introduces a result that is understandable because of the previous information, and 后来 fits the later result in a narrative.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “数学家” / Context: “你不是当数学家，而是当哲学家” / Alternatives: “学者” or “读书人.” 数学家 specifically means a mathematician, which does not fit the traditional story of Mencius very well. 学者 means a learned scholar or academic, while 读书人 is a more traditional term for an educated person.

Word: “卖东西的人” / Context: “不能当卖东西的人” / Alternatives: “商人” or “生意人.” 卖东西的人 is understandable but descriptive and basic. 商人 is the standard, neutral word for a merchant or businessperson; 生意人 is slightly more conversational.

Word: “最后的家” / Context: “我们找到了最后的家” / Alternatives: “安定的家” or “合适的住处.” 最后的家 literally means “the final home,” which can sound dramatic or even suggest death. 安定的家 emphasizes a stable home where they will no longer need to move, while 合适的住处 means a suitable place to live.

Word: “感觉” / Context: “妈妈感觉这个地方适合他们” / Alternatives: “觉得” or “认为.” 感觉 often refers to a physical sensation or personal impression. 觉得 is the most natural everyday word for an opinion, while 认为 is more formal and expresses a considered judgment.

Word: “有名” / Context: “有名的哲学家” / Alternatives: “著名的” or “伟大的.” 有名 means well-known and is common in everyday speech. 著名 is more formal and suits historical writing; 伟大 emphasizes Mencius’s exceptional importance and influence, not merely his fame.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You clearly understand the main sequence of the story—moving house, finding a suitable environment, and Mencius becoming a philosopher—and your ideas connect logically.  
Next time, focus on keeping the point of view consistent (“孟子/他” rather than switching to “你”) and using more precise story vocabulary.
