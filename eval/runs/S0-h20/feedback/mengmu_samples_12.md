## Scene 1

> 古代，孟子跟孟母住在墓地的旁边。可是孟母注意，住在墓地的旁边影响孟子。他常常跟朋友们祭拜玩，所以孟母决定他们会搬家。这次，他们住在城市里的商店的旁边，从早到晚很吵。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “古代，孟子跟孟母住在墓地的旁边。”  
Formula: 在 + historical time / time expression, + subject + 在 + place + verb  
Correct: “在古代，孟子和孟母住在墓地旁边。”  
“在古代” is the natural time expression. “墓地旁边” is more concise than “墓地的旁边.”

Incorrect phrase: “可是孟母注意，住在墓地的旁边影响孟子。”  
Formula: Subject + 注意到/发现 + clause; Subject + 对 + person + 有影响  
Correct: “可是孟母注意到，住在墓地旁边对孟子有影响。”  
Use “注意到” when someone notices a fact. “对孟子有影响” is the natural way to say “affects Mencius.”

Incorrect phrase: “他常常跟朋友们祭拜玩”  
Formula: 跟 + person + 一起 + verb; 模仿 + activity / 玩 + game  
Correct: “他常常跟朋友们一起模仿祭拜。”  
“祭拜” is not normally followed by “玩.” In this story, Mencius imitates the funeral rituals, so “模仿祭拜” is clearer.

Incorrect phrase: “所以孟母决定他们会搬家。”  
Formula: Subject + 决定 + verb phrase  
Correct: “所以孟母决定搬家。”  
After “决定,” use the action directly. “会” is unnecessary here.

Incorrect phrase: “这次，他们住在城市里的商店的旁边”  
Formula: 住在 + place; 搬到 + place  
Correct: “这次，他们搬到城市里的商店旁边住。”  
Use “搬到” to show the change of residence. Also, “商店旁边” does not need “的.”

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “跟”  
Context: “孟子跟孟母住在墓地的旁边。”  
Alternatives: “和” is slightly more neutral and common in written narration. “跟” is perfectly correct but sounds a little more conversational. For a story, “孟子和孟母” is a smooth choice.

Word: “注意”  
Context: “孟母注意……”  
Alternatives: “注意到” means “to notice/realize a specific fact,” so it fits best here. “发现” means “to discover” and emphasizes that she came to understand a problem after observing it.

Word: “影响”  
Context: “影响孟子。”  
Alternatives: “有影响” is more natural in this context: “对孟子有影响.” “不利” means harmful or unfavorable and is more formal: “这对孟子的成长不利.”

Word: “吵”  
Context: “从早到晚很吵。”  
Alternatives: “吵闹” emphasizes a noisy, bustling environment with people and activity. “嘈杂” is more formal and describes mixed, unpleasant noise. For this story, “环境很吵闹” works very well.

Word: “商店”  
Context: “城市里的商店的旁边。”  
Alternatives: “集市” refers to a marketplace, often with many vendors and crowds; it fits the traditional Mencius story better. “市场” is a more modern general word for a market. You could write “搬到集市旁边.”

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You clearly organized the story as a sequence—living near the cemetery, noticing its influence, and moving to a new place—and your use of “可是” and “所以” makes the logic easy to follow.  
Next time, focus on using “注意到/发现 + complete idea” and “决定 + verb phrase” to make your sentences sound more natural.

## Scene 2

> 城市也有点儿危险。害得孟母只好一直陪孟子。孟子也没有朋友，难怪他老是看起来不高兴。住在商店的旁边不是适合孟子，而是让他觉得很伤心。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “城市也有点儿危险。害得孟母只好一直陪孟子。”  
Formula: Cause + 害得 + affected person + harmful result  
Correct: “城市也有点儿危险，害得孟母只好一直陪着孟子。”  
“陪着” emphasizes that Meng’s mother keeps accompanying him over time. The two clauses can also be joined for smoother flow.

Incorrect phrase: “孟子也没有朋友，难怪他老是看起来不高兴。”  
Formula: Subject + 看起来 + adjective  
Correct: “孟子也没有朋友，难怪他看起来老是不高兴。”  
“老是” normally comes before the verb phrase “看起来,” rather than between “看起来” and the adjective.

Incorrect phrase: “住在商店的旁边不是适合孟子”  
Formula: Place/Activity + 不适合 + someone + 做/for something  
Correct: “住在商店旁边不适合孟子成长。”  
“适合” needs a person/object after it, and “孟子成长” clearly explains what is unsuitable. “商店旁边” is also more natural than “商店的旁边” here.

Incorrect phrase: “不是适合孟子，而是让他觉得很伤心。”  
Formula: 虽然/…不但…，但是/而且…; or Situation + 让 + someone + feel + adjective  
Correct: “住在商店旁边不但不适合孟子成长，还让他觉得很伤心。”  
“不是……而是……” contrasts two noun-like choices or descriptions, but here you mean that this situation has two negative effects, so “不但……还……” is clearer.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “危险”  
Context: “城市也有点儿危险。”  
Alternatives: “复杂” or “不安全.” “危险” means there is direct danger. “不安全” is more natural when describing a living environment, while “复杂” suggests that the city environment has many troubling influences or situations.

Word: “一直”  
Context: “孟母只好一直陪着孟子。”  
Alternatives: “时常” or “寸步不离地.” “一直” simply means continuously. “时常” means frequently, while “寸步不离地” vividly emphasizes that Meng’s mother cannot leave him even for a moment.

Word: “没有朋友”  
Context: “孟子也没有朋友。”  
Alternatives: “交不到朋友” or “缺少玩伴.” “没有朋友” is direct and understandable. “交不到朋友” emphasizes the difficulty of making friends, and “缺少玩伴” specifically means lacking friends to play with.

Word: “不高兴”  
Context: “他看起来老是不高兴。”  
Alternatives: “闷闷不乐” or “郁闷.” “不高兴” is a general, everyday expression for being unhappy. “闷闷不乐” is more descriptive and literary, suggesting someone is quietly unhappy; “郁闷” suggests feeling depressed, frustrated, or emotionally weighed down.

Word: “伤心”  
Context: “让他觉得很伤心。”  
Alternatives: “难过” or “孤单.” “伤心” often describes sadness caused by a specific painful event. “难过” is a broader, softer word for feeling unhappy, while “孤单” is especially suitable here because Mengzi has no friends and feels lonely.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You clearly explain the emotional consequences of the environment, especially by connecting Mengzi’s lack of friends with his unhappiness.  
Next time, focus on choosing connectives such as “不但……还……” to show the relationship between two ideas more precisely.

## Scene 3

> 所以，孟母和孟子搬到学校的旁边住。才五岁孟子开始学哲学。在学校，孟子不是看书，就是练习写字。将来，他成为中国的一个非常有名的哲学家。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “才五岁孟子开始学哲学”  
Formula: 谁 + 才 + 年龄/数量 + 就 + 动词  
Correct: “孟子才五岁就开始学哲学了。”

Incorrect phrase: “将来，他成为中国的一个非常有名的哲学家”  
Formula: In a past narrative, use 后来 + 主语 + 成为(了) + 身份; 将来 is for the future.  
Correct: “后来，他成为了中国一位非常有名的哲学家。”

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “搬到学校的旁边住”  
Alternative: “搬到学校附近居住” sounds slightly more formal and concise. “学校旁边” emphasizes directly next to the school, while “学校附近” means in the area near the school.

Word: “学哲学”  
Alternative: “学习儒家思想” or “学习孔子的思想.” “哲学” is a broad modern term, while “儒家思想” is more historically specific for Mencius.

Word: “看书”  
Alternative: “阅读.” “看书” is very common in everyday speech; “阅读” is more formal and fits a description of serious study.

Word: “练习写字”  
Alternative: “练字” or “习字.” Both are more concise and natural for practising handwriting; “习字” has a slightly more literary or traditional feeling.

Word: “非常有名”  
Alternative: “著名” or “享有盛名.” “非常有名” is clear but basic; “著名” is more concise and formal, while “享有盛名” emphasizes having a highly respected reputation.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You used a clear chronological structure and the “不是……就是……” pattern correctly to show Mencius’s diligent daily routine.  
Next time, focus on choosing narrative time words carefully, especially the difference between “后来” and “将来.”
