## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有很多人不知道什么植物能吃，什么植物不能吃。有很多人吃植物以后就吃坏肚子了，因为他们吃了毒。那个时候，有一个老人叫神农，他什么植物都觉得好吃，不怕生病，不怕毒。他希望别人也可以吃好吃的植物。可是他知道，如果他们还怕吃毒，不会吃他喜欢的植物。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，This time expression is not idiomatic here; use the fixed narrative expression “在古代”.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好吃 -- “别人也可以吃好吃的植物”：可口，emphasizes that the plants taste pleasant and are suitable to eat；美味，gives a more vivid sense of delicious taste.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 有很多人吃植物以后就吃坏肚子了：他们吃植物以后就吃坏肚子了，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence: “很多人” was introduced in the immediately preceding sentence, with no competing group.
- Coherence (Reference): 有一个老人叫神农，他什么植物都觉得好吃：有一个老人叫神农，什么植物都觉得好吃，Use zero form for the same character within the same sentence after introducing his name.
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃植物以后：吃了植物以后，Use “了” for the completed action that happens before the later result of getting an upset stomach.
- Expressiveness: After the people become ill, add a concrete symptom to make the problem easier to picture, for example: “有的人肚子疼得站不起来。”
- Expressiveness: Show Shennong’s confidence with a short thought or line of speech after he says he is not afraid, for example: “神农想：我一定要找出能吃的植物。”
- Expressiveness: Make the danger in the final sentence more specific, for example: “如果他们害怕吃到有毒的植物，就不敢尝试新的植物。”

### Encouragement
You clearly establish the main problem—people cannot tell which plants are safe—and introduce Shennong’s brave personality. Your use of “可是” also creates a meaningful contrast between his hope and the people’s fear.

## Scene 2

> 所以，他上山下山找植物，吃了很多样子的植物。他是第一人，找到了茶。他把所有的植物写下来了。他一直都吃了，他连毒都能吃。可是他还没吃了一个叫夹竹桃的植物。他找到了那个像星星的植物，觉得很漂亮，马上就吃下了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他是第一人，找到了茶：他是第一个找到茶的人，An ordinal expression for a person needs the measure word in this context, and the action should modify the person to show that he was the first person who found tea.
- 连毒都能吃：连有毒的植物都敢吃，毒 is a noun meaning poison; for plants that contain poison, use 有毒的植物. 敢吃 also clearly shows that he is willing to eat something dangerous.
- 像星星的植物：像星星一样的植物，A comparison used before a noun normally uses 像……一样 to show what the plant resembles.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 找 -- “他上山下山找植物”：探寻，emphasizes searching through many places to discover something unknown；寻找，emphasizes a continued search rather than a single attempt.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 一直都吃了：一直都在吃，Use 在 for an ongoing action. 了 marks a completed action and does not fit with 一直都, which describes an action continuing over time.
- Coherence (Temporality): 还没吃了：还没吃过，Use 没……过 to say that someone has not had an experience before now; 了 cannot follow 没 in this negative experience structure.
- Expressiveness: After finding tea, add a concrete detail about its use, such as “他把茶放在水里，发现茶水很好喝。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where he sees the plant, add visual details from his observation, such as “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: Show his immediate thought before he eats it, such as “他想：这么漂亮的植物，一定很好吃吧！”

### Encouragement
- You used a clear time sequence: he searches, finds tea, records plants, and then discovers another plant. The use of “把……写下来” and “觉得很漂亮” also helps show both his actions and his reaction.

## Scene 3

> 可是夹竹桃的毒很重，让他肚子很痛。他在地上躺着，觉得他快会死了。可是正好有别人找到他了，给了他一个像伞的植物，叫灵芝。他吃灵芝以后就觉得很好，不再生病了。他测量起来了，像星星的植物是毒，像伞的植物是药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 快会死了：快要死了，快要 marks an event that is about to happen; 会 is not needed after it here.
- 测量起来了：发现，测量 means measuring size, length, or amount, so it cannot introduce his conclusion about which plants are poisonous or medicinal.
- 像星星的植物：像星星一样的植物，when a comparison modifies a noun, use the pattern 像……一样的.
- 是毒：有毒，有毒 describes a plant that contains poison; 是毒 would identify the plant itself as poison rather than describe its property.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 毒很重 -- “夹竹桃的毒很重”：毒性很强，states more precisely that the plant’s poisonous effect is strong.
- 很好 -- “他吃灵芝以后就觉得很好”：好多了，shows that his condition improved after eating the medicine.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 给了他一个像伞的植物：给了神农一个像伞的植物，repeat the name after a new male character has appeared, because the pronoun could otherwise refer to either man.
- Coherence (Reference): 他吃灵芝以后：神农吃灵芝以后，repeat the name because the previous sentence contains both the helper and 神农, so the pronoun is ambiguous.
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃灵芝以后：吃了灵芝以后，use 了 for the completed action that happens before the later result.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 可是正好有别人找到他了：这时，正好有一个男人找到了他，use 这时 to introduce a new event interrupting the painful scene; 可是 expresses contrast, which is not the relationship here.
- Expressiveness: At the moment he lies on the ground, add his fearful thought to show his feelings: “我会死吗？”
- Expressiveness: Describe the helper with one visible detail when he arrives: “一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人走过来。”
- Expressiveness: Add a brief reaction after the medicine works: “神农觉得肚子不那么痛了，终于松了一口气。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the cause-and-effect sequence: the poisonous plant hurts 神农, and 灵芝 makes him feel better.
- Your use of concrete comparisons, such as “像伞的植物” and “像星星的植物,” helps the reader distinguish the two plants.
