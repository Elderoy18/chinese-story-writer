## Scene 1

> 在古代，很多医生不知道怎么用药。所以他们找到了很多植物对药。神农先看到很多病人，他们吃很多植物对药。但是我觉得他们的吃的植物，让他们病人。神农觉得这个植物可能特别是毒。所以他想找一个植物对药，他们可以吃对药。正好，他找到了一把花，他想把花放在茶里，对药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很多植物对药：很多可以做药的植物，The phrase “对药” cannot mean “to be used as medicine.” Use “做药,” and place the description before “植物.”
- 植物对药：植物做的药，“对药” is not a valid expression here. “植物做的药” means medicine made from plants.
- 他们的吃的植物：他们吃的植物，Use a verb phrase plus “的” before a noun to describe the noun; the extra possessive “的” does not express “the plants that they eat.”
- 让他们病人：让他们生病了，“病人” is a noun meaning “patient,” not a verb. After “让 + person,” use a verb phrase such as “生病了.”
- 特别是毒：有毒，“毒” is a noun and cannot be modified by “特别” in this way. Use “有毒” to describe a poisonous plant.
- 一个植物对药，他们可以吃对药：一种可以做药、可以让病人吃的植物，Use “一种” when referring to a type of plant. Both descriptions need to come before “植物,” and “对药” must be replaced with “做药.”
- 对药：做成药，“对药” is not a verb phrase. “做成药” expresses turning the flowers into medicine.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农先看到很多病人：一个叫神农的男人先看到很多病人，This is Shen Nong’s first mention, so introduce him with a name and a brief noun phrase rather than using only the name.
- Coherence (Reference): 这个植物：这些植物，“这个植物” has no clear singular referent because the previous sentence mentions many plants. Repeat the noun with “这些” to refer to that group.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们吃很多植物做的药：他们吃了很多植物做的药，Use “了” for the completed eating action that happened before the plants made the patients ill.
- Expressiveness: When Shen Nong sees the patients, add a visible detail about their condition: “病人们捂着肚子，脸色很不好。”
- Expressiveness: After Shen Nong realizes that the plants may be poisonous, add his determination: “神农想：‘我一定要找到能治病的植物。’”
- Expressiveness: Describe the flowers when he finds them: “这些花是黄色的，像星星一样，又大又漂亮。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduced the setting and included several important characters: doctors, patients, and Shen Nong.
- You also tried to show cause and effect with connectors such as “所以” and “但是,” which is a useful storytelling skill.

## Scene 2

> 他不知道如果这把花对你的身体好，这就要看如果这把花毒不毒了。神农想找别的植物，但是这很麻烦。正好，他找到了别的花。他一吃花，很快就生病了。他的肚子疼死了。神农不知道他吃不吃灵芝或者夹竹桃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不知道如果这把花对你的身体好：不知道这把花对他的身体好不好，After 不知道, an embedded yes/no question uses an A-not-A form, not 如果; the narration is about 神农, so the pronoun should be 他的 rather than 你的.
- 要看如果这把花毒不毒：要看这把花毒不毒，The part after 这就要看 must be the question being considered. 毒不毒 already forms an A-not-A question, so 如果 should not be added.
- 吃不吃灵芝或者夹竹桃：吃灵芝还是夹竹桃，When choosing between two items, use a choice question with 还是. 吃不吃 is a yes/no question and does not match the alternative introduced by 或者.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 麻烦 -- “但是这很麻烦”：不容易，states more clearly that finding more plants is difficult；艰辛，emphasizes the effort and hardship involved in the search.
- 很快 -- “很快就生病了”：没过多久，shows that only a short time passed before he became ill.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他：Ø，Use zero form when the same character, 神农, has already been named earlier in the same clause and no other character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他一吃花，很快就生病了：他一吃花，就很快生病了，The paired sequence connector 一……就…… should link the first action directly to its immediate result; 很快 can follow 就.
- Expressiveness: Before the eating action, describe the flower’s appearance to make the danger more vivid: “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花。”
- Expressiveness: After the illness begins, add a physical reaction: “他的肚子疼得不得了，只能躺在地上。”
- Expressiveness: Add 神农’s worried inner thought after he becomes ill: “神农心想：‘我会死吗？’”

### Encouragement
- You correctly use 找到了 to show that the search had a result, and 生病了 clearly shows a change of state. The phrase 疼死了 also naturally communicates very intense pain.

## Scene 3

> 别的人给了神农一个灵芝。这个植物可以让神农更好。比方说，他会觉得更健康。吃灵芝以后，他可以再走。现在他觉得更好。最后，神农找到了很多植物，他可以用对药。这些植物让神农很高兴。神农想跟很多人分享他的新药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别的人：一个人，别的人 means people other than an already identified person or group, so it needs a comparison context; use 一个人 to introduce an unspecified giver for the first time.
- 让神农更好：让神农好一些，better cannot directly describe a person’s physical recovery in this pattern; 好一些 expresses that his condition improved.
- 他可以用对药：可以用来做药的植物，用 cannot be followed by 对药 here; 用来做药 expresses the purpose of the plants, and the whole phrase must come before 植物 as its modifier.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 再走 -- “他可以再走”：继续赶路，specifies that he is able to resume his journey.
- 高兴 -- “这些植物让神农很高兴”：欣慰，shows that he feels pleased and relieved because his search has produced useful results.
- 新药 -- “分享他的新药”：新发现的药材，makes clear that these are medicinal plants he has newly discovered.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农想跟很多人分享他的新药：他想跟很多人分享他的新药，use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when 神农 was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other male character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃灵芝以后，他可以再走：吃了灵芝以后，他可以再走了，use 了 after 吃 for the completed action before 以后, and after 走 to mark the change of state that he is able to walk again.
- Expressiveness: After he eats the medicine, add a physical detail to show his recovery, such as “他的肚子不那么疼了，身体也有力气了。”
- Expressiveness: When he finds the useful plants, add an inner thought to show his motivation, such as “神农想：这些植物也许能帮助生病的人。”
- Expressiveness: You could describe the medicinal plant before it is given to him, such as “那种植物像一把小伞，长在花的旁边。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the change from Shen Nong receiving the medicine to feeling better and continuing his journey. You also use completed-action wording well in “找到了很多植物,” which helps move the narrative forward.

- After 神农 feels better, add that another person in brown comes and talks with him.

### Overall Encouragement
