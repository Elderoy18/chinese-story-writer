## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个男孩名字叫孟子。孟子和孟子的妈妈两个人住在很穷的农村。他们住的地方旁边是个墓地，墓地老是有很多人祭拜。因为人们在祭拜，他们一直在哭。孟子和孟子的妈妈住在这里才一个星期就觉得受不了了，而且害得孟子的学习。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，this time expression is not idiomatic for a historical setting; use the fixed expression “在古代”.
- 有一个男孩名字叫孟子：有一个叫孟子的男孩，use an attributive phrase with “叫……的” before the noun to introduce a named character naturally.
- 墓地老是有很多人祭拜：老是有很多人来墓地祭拜，people perform this action at a place, so “来墓地祭拜” clearly marks the cemetery as the destination of the action.
- 而且害得孟子的学习：还影响了孟子的学习，“害得” must be followed by a result or consequence, such as an action or state; it cannot directly take “孟子的学习” as its object. “影响了” correctly takes the study as its object.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子的妈妈两个人：孟子和妈妈两个人，after “孟子” has already appeared in the same sentence, use the shorter repeated reference “妈妈” rather than repeating the name inside the possessive phrase.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子的妈妈住在这里：孟子和妈妈住在这里，when the two characters are named together, use “妈妈” for the second mention instead of repeating “孟子” within the same clause.
- Coherence (Temporality): 住在这里才一个星期：住在这里才住了一个星期，use “了” after the last verb in this completed duration expression to show that the one-week stay has reached that point.
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery is introduced, add a visual detail about the mourners, such as “来墓地祭拜的人都穿着黑衣服”.
- Expressiveness: At “他们一直在哭”, show what Mengzi feels when he hears them, such as “孟子听见哭声，觉得很害怕”.
- Expressiveness: Before the final sentence, add Mengzi’s mother’s thought or speech, such as “孟子的妈妈说：‘这里太吵了，会影响你学习。’”

### Encouragement
You introduced Mengzi clearly at the beginning and established the cemetery setting early in the scene. You also used “因为” to show a cause-and-effect relationship between the mourners’ ritual and their crying.

## Scene 2

> 孟子的妈妈觉得孟子取得良好的教育是很重要的，所以孟子的妈妈认为这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好，就打算跟孟子搬家。孟子的妈妈带孟子不是搬到城市，而是搬到了孟子老师的家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 取得良好的教育：接受良好的教育，教育 is normally something a person receives, so 接受 is the appropriate verb in this collocation.
- 跟孟子搬家：带着孟子搬家，跟孟子搬家 suggests that both people move together as equal participants; 带着孟子搬家 correctly shows that the mother takes her child with her.
- 带孟子不是搬到城市：不是带孟子搬到城市，when both alternatives have the same subject, the subject should come before the paired structure 不是……而是…….

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 地方 -- “这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好”：环境，more specifically refers to the surroundings and atmosphere that affect a child’s education.
- 不太好 -- “对儿子的教育不太好”：不太合适，more precisely expresses that this setting is not suitable for the child’s education.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 所以孟子的妈妈认为：所以她认为，use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the same character was named earlier in the sentence and no other possible referent competes.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子的妈妈带孟子不是搬到城市：她带孟子不是搬到城市，use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence because the mother was the subject of the immediately preceding sentence.
- Expressiveness: At the point where the mother decides to move, add her feeling to show why she acts: “孟子的妈妈很着急，担心这样的环境会影响孟子的学习。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of inner speech after her judgment of the place: “她心想：‘我得给孟子找一个更适合学习的地方。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the mother’s reason for moving, using 所以 to connect her concern about education with her decision. You also try to contrast two destinations with 不是……而是……, which is a useful structure for narrative writing.

## Scene 3

> 孟子跟他的老师从早到晚学习，难怪他的成绩很快就进步了很多。孟子学习的时候，孟子的妈妈不是待在家里就是编织衣服赚钱。以后孟子成为了中国有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 以后孟子成为了中国有名的哲学家：后来孟子成为了中国有名的哲学家，Sentence-initial “以后” does not mean “afterwards” in a narrative; use “后来” to introduce a later event.

### Encouragement
