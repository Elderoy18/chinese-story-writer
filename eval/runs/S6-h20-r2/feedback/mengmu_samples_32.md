## Scene 1

> 有一天孟子去了墓地玩儿，但是他妈妈觉得这是不合适的地方让孩子玩，所以她决定了他们会搬家找更好的地方。她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福，而且住在这儿教育不太好。因为她望子成龙，所以她会一直搬家才能找到质量好的教育。然后他们搬到了离城市更近的地方。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不合适的地方让孩子玩：不适合让孩子玩儿的地方，When a verb phrase describes a place, it needs to modify the noun with 的; this version clearly means a place that is unsuitable for a child to play in.
- 决定了他们会搬家找更好的地方：决定带着孟子搬家，找更好的地方，决定 is a mental-state verb and normally takes the zero aspect marker, not 了. It is also followed naturally by the action that is decided, rather than by a prediction with 会.
- 她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福：她担心儿子的童年不会幸福，恐怕 means “I am afraid that” and cannot use another subject in this pattern. 担心 correctly expresses that the mother is worried about her son’s future.
- 会一直搬家才能：得一直搬家，才能，Before 才能, use 得 or 要 to express a necessary condition. 会 expresses a future prediction, rather than necessity.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 更好的地方 -- “找更好的地方”：更适合孩子成长的地方，specifies why the new place would be better for the child.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: At the cemetery scene, add one small visual detail and Mengzi’s reaction: “墓地里有人在哭，孟子却觉得很好玩。”
- Expressiveness: After the mother’s concern, show her inner thoughts in quoted speech: “孟母心想：‘住在这儿会影响孩子，我得搬家。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show Mengzi’s mother’s motivation with the fitting idiom “望子成龙,” and the scene has a logical movement from her concern to the family’s move.

## Scene 2

> 他们住才一月就又搬家。古代的时候，一般人不喜欢卖东西的人。但是孟子老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿，害得妈妈着急得很，因为她恐怕儿子会成为跟他们很像。她告诉了孟子：他们不是朋友，而是不好的人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他们住才一月就又搬家：他们才住了一个月，就又搬家了，In a duration construction, 才 comes before the verb, and completed actions take 了. Use a measure word with the duration: 一个月.
- 古代的时候：在古代，This time expression is not used naturally here; use 在古代 or 古代.
- 老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿：老是跟这些卖东西的人玩儿，老是 and 常常 are both frequency adverbs modifying the same verb, so only one is needed.
- 她恐怕儿子会：她担心儿子会，恐怕 means “I am afraid that” and cannot take another subject such as 她 before it. Use 担心 to describe her worry.
- 成为跟他们很像：变得跟他们很像，成为 is used for becoming a person, role, or identity, while 变得 is used for changing into a state or quality.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 玩儿 -- “跟这些卖东西的人玩儿”：来往，more precisely describes regularly spending time and associating with these people in this context.
- 不好 -- “不好的人”：品行不好，specifies that the problem is these people’s behaviour or character rather than something generally “not good.”

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 她告诉了孟子：她告诉孟子，A saying verb that introduces quoted speech uses the zero particle; 了 is not needed because the focus is the speech that follows.
- Coherence (Reference): 他们不是朋友，而是不好的人：这些卖东西的人不是你的朋友，而是不好的人，Use the full noun again when the pronoun could refer to several recently mentioned people, including 孟子, 妈妈, and the sellers.
- Expressiveness: After the sentence about the sellers, add a small detail about the busy street, such as “街上很热闹，商人一直大声叫卖。”
- Expressiveness: Show the mother’s feeling before her speech with an action or thought, such as “妈妈叹了一口气，心里很担心。”
- Expressiveness: Make the mother’s warning more vivid with a reason, such as “你总是跟他们在一起，会学他们卖东西的。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show why the mother is worried and include direct speech, which makes the scene easier to follow.
- Your use of “害得妈妈着急得很” effectively connects Mengzi’s behaviour with his mother’s reaction.

## Scene 3

> 你不是当数学家，而是当哲学家，不能当卖东西的人。所以他们又搬家。在他们第三个家，孟子学了怎么祭拜。妈妈感觉这个地方适合他们，告诉了孟子：我们找到了最后的家。在这儿妈妈让儿子从早到晚学习，他难怪成为有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在他们第三个家：在他们的第三个家里，A possessive modifier before a noun needs 的, and 家 needs the location word 里 when it means “in the home.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 卖东西的人 -- “不能当卖东西的人”：商人，names the occupation more precisely and naturally in this context.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 在这儿妈妈让儿子从早到晚学习：在这儿，她让儿子从早到晚学习，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence: 妈妈 was the subject of the immediately preceding sentence, so repeating the noun is unnecessary.
- Coherence (Temporality): 所以他们又搬家：所以他们又搬家了，Use 了 for the completed action of moving house.
- Coherence (Temporality): 妈妈感觉这个地方适合他们：妈妈感觉这个地方适合他们，Mental-state verbs use the zero particle; do not add 了 to a continuing judgement.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉了孟子：告诉孟子，A saying verb that introduces quoted speech takes the zero particle, not 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为有名的哲学家：成为了有名的哲学家，Use 了 for the completed change of state of becoming a philosopher.
- Expressiveness: Before the mother’s first line, show her worry through an action or feeling: “妈妈皱着眉头，心里很着急。”
- Expressiveness: When describing the third home, add one concrete detail that explains why it is suitable: “这儿很安静，旁边就是学校。”
- Expressiveness: After the final result, add how other people responded to Mengzi: “后来，人们都很敬重他。”

### Encouragement
You used the contrast pattern “不是……而是……” appropriately to show the mother’s hopes for her son. The phrase “从早到晚学习” also gives a clear sense of Mengzi’s diligent routine.
