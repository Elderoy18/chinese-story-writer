## Scene 1

> 古代的时候有一个人叫神农。他需要当上医，一看别的人就想帮助他们。但是，他有一个问题，他不知道什么东西能做药。神农听说一个远远的地方有一种植物，什么病人吃都会觉得身体更好。神农三个月走去，找到有植物的地方，他看到一个很漂亮的植物，又香又好看。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，“古代的时候” is not the standard time expression here; use “在古代” to introduce the historical setting.
- 当上医：当上医生，“医” is not normally used alone after “当上” in this context; “医生” is the ordinary noun for a person who treats patients.
- 别的人：病人，“别的人” means people other than a previously specified person or group. Here the intended meaning is people who need medical treatment, so “病人” fits.
- 一个远远的地方：一个很远的地方，“远远的” is usually used adverbially, while “很远的” naturally describes a distant place.
- 什么病人吃都会觉得身体更好：不管什么病人吃了，都会觉得身体好多了，Use “不管……都……” for a universal condition. “了” marks eating the plant as completed before the result, and “好多了” clearly expresses an improvement in health.
- 三个月走去：走了三个月，A duration phrase follows the verb, as in “走了三个月”; “走去” also normally needs a destination when it means walking somewhere.
- 一个很漂亮的植物：一株很漂亮的植物，“株” is the appropriate measure word for an individual plant.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 帮助 -- “一看到病人就想帮助他们”：医治，specifies that he wants to treat their illnesses rather than help them in a general way.
- 香 -- “又香又好看”：芳香，specifies that the plant has a pleasant fragrance.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 有植物的地方：有这种植物的地方，Use “这种植物” to refer back clearly to the plant mentioned in the previous sentence; “有植物” could mean a place with any kind of plant.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农走了三个月：他走了三个月，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the name was used in the immediately preceding sentence and no other character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Temporality): 找到有这种植物的地方：找到了有这种植物的地方，Use “了” for the completed action of finding the place.
- Expressiveness: After hearing about the plant, add Shen Nong’s determination with quoted speech, for example: “神农想：‘这种植物可能能帮助病人，我一定要去找一找。’”
- Expressiveness: When he arrives, add a sensory detail that shows his reaction to the plant, for example: “神农闻到一阵芳香，觉得这种植物很特别。”
- Expressiveness: Add a feeling during the long journey to make the action more vivid, for example: “他走了很久，虽然很累，但是没有放弃。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduce Shen Nong and explain his wish to help sick people, which gives the scene a strong purpose. You also use descriptive details such as “又香又好看” to help the reader imagine the plant.

### Coherence & Expressiveness

- Coherence (Temporality): “吃了以后，神农快生病”： “吃了以后，神农很快就生病了”，a completed event needs 了; the sequence pattern after ……以后 naturally uses 就 to show what happened next.

- Expressiveness: When the blue-clothed person helps, show the urgency through an action: “他赶快拿起灵芝，给神农吃了。”

## Scene 3

> 啊，他怎么忘记了？他把灵芝放在包里。一回家就告诉别人。他找到药以后他写下怎么做药在书上。所以人们都知道，那红灵芝对身体好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在书上：在书上写下怎么做药，A location phrase such as this should come before the verb when it states where the writing is done.
- 别人：人们，别人 means “other people” in contrast with a specific person or group already mentioned; here the intended general group is 人们.
- 那红灵芝：那种红灵芝，A demonstrative such as 那 must be followed by a measure word before a noun; 种 identifies the type of medicinal fungus.
- 对身体好处：对身体有好处，The fixed expression is 对……有好处; 有 is required to state that something benefits the body.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 药 -- “他找到药以后”：药材，specifies that he found a medicinal material, such as a plant used for medicine.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 他把灵芝放在包里：他把灵芝放进了包里，Use 了 for this completed action in the narrative; 进 also shows the result that the lingzhi ended up inside the bag.
- Coherence (Temporality): 一回家就告诉：一回家就告诉了，Use 了 because telling people is a completed event in this sequence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 写下：写下了，Use 了 for the completed act of recording the information after he found the medicine.
- Expressiveness: At the opening, add a small action to show his sudden memory: “他拍了拍自己的头，突然想起来了。”
- Expressiveness: When he tells people, add a short line of speech: “这种红灵芝对身体有好处，可以做药！”
- Expressiveness: Add his feeling after writing the information down: “他觉得很高兴，因为以后可以帮助更多人。”

### Encouragement
- You created a clear sequence of actions: he puts away the lingzhi, returns home, tells people, and records what he learned. Your use of 所以 also clearly shows the result of his work.
