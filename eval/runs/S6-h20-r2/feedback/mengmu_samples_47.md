### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 安排 -- “孟子的妈妈安排他的生活”：规划，emphasizes that she carefully plans the child’s life and future direction.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 这个男孩子叫孟子，孟子是古代的人：在古代，有一个男孩子叫孟子，The first mention rule requires an unambiguous introduction with an indefinite full noun phrase; a demonstrative is not natural when the reader has not met the boy before.

- Coherence (Temporality): 他们常常搬了家：他们常常搬家，The zero-particle rule applies to habitual actions; the frequency word makes this a routine rather than one completed move.
- Expressiveness: After the mother thinks about the child’s future, add her inner feeling to show her concern, for example: “她很担心孟子的未来。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of inner speech after the final sentence to make her motivation more vivid, for example: “我要让孟子有一个好的成长环境。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the mother’s reason for moving and use the idiom “望子成龙” appropriately to express her hopes for her son.

- 先她让孟子跟朋友玩儿：她先让孟子跟朋友玩儿，A sequence adverb such as “先” normally comes after the subject and before the verb.
- 又便宜又好质量的东西：又便宜又质量好的东西，Items joined by “又……又……” should have parallel forms. “便宜” and “质量好” are both adjective phrases, while “好质量” is not a natural phrase.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 常常搬了家：常常搬家，Use the zero particle for a habitual action; “常常” describes a routine, so it does not take completed-action “了”.

- Expressiveness: After “从这儿到那儿”, add Mengzi’s feeling about moving, such as “孟子觉得搬来搬去很好玩儿。”
- Expressiveness: At the shopping lesson, add a small action showing what his mother teaches: “妈妈指着两样东西，让孟子比较价钱。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech after the shopping lesson, such as “妈妈说：‘买东西要看价钱，也要看质量。’”

### Encouragement
