## Scene 1

> 古代时候，人不知道他们能吃什么植物，常常一吃就死。但是不知道为什么，没有办法。所以有一天，神农出去试一试很多种植物，告诉人什么植物是安全什么是危险。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代时候：古代的时候，The time expression before “时候” needs the particle “的” here; only fixed short forms such as “小时候” omit it.
- 人不知道他们能吃什么植物：人们不知道自己能吃什么植物，Use “人们” for a concrete group of people. With this general group as the subject, “自己” naturally refers back to that group.
- 告诉人：告诉人们，“人” is a generic noun and should not refer to this concrete group; use “人们”.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很多种 -- “试了很多种植物”：各种各样的，emphasizes that the plants belong to many different varieties.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农出去试了很多种植物：有一个叫神农的人出去试了很多种植物，This is the first mention of the character, so use a full noun phrase with the name and identity rather than introducing him only by name.
- Coherence (Temporality): 出去试一试很多种植物：出去试了很多种植物，This is a completed action in the past narrative, so use “了”. The reduplicated form “试一试” suggests a brief tentative try and does not fit testing many kinds of plants.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 但是不知道为什么，没有办法：他们不知道为什么，也没有办法，“但是” introduces contrast, but these two ideas do not contrast. “也” links the two related negative facts.
- Expressiveness: After the deaths, add the people’s fear to show the seriousness of the problem, for example: “人们都很害怕，不知道该吃什么。”
- Expressiveness: Show Shennong’s purpose or determination before he begins, for example: “神农想：‘我一定要找出安全的植物。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish the problem first and then introduce Shennong’s action as a response, which gives this short scene a logical direction. Your use of “一吃就死” also creates a strong sense of danger.

## Scene 2

> 发现有的植物对身体非常好，特别是茶，让肚子感觉又暖和又清楚。他告诉朋友：“你吃哪种植物，这就要看你的口味。比方说，你爱吃甜的，可能喜欢糖。”他最喜欢吃花，除了夹竹桃以外，什么都花想吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 清楚：舒服，清楚 describes whether information or thinking is clear; it cannot describe a physical feeling in the stomach.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好 -- “有的植物对身体非常好”：很有好处，states more precisely that the plants benefit the body.
- 甜的 -- “你爱吃甜的”：甜食，names sweet foods more specifically in this context.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 你吃哪种植物，这就要看你的口味。：你喜欢吃哪种植物，要看你的口味。，the original use of 这就要看……了 does not set up an uncertain condition correctly; use 要看 to show that food preference determines the choice.
- Expressiveness: After mentioning tea, add a concrete action and sensory detail, for example: “他把茶叶放进热水里，喝了一口，觉得肚子暖暖的。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where he wants to eat flowers, add an inner thought to show his curiosity, for example: “他心想：这些花这么漂亮，不知道是什么味道。”

### Encouragement
- You used a direct quotation to show what the character says, and you connected tea with a warm bodily feeling. The contrast with “除了夹竹桃以外” also clearly shows that one flower is an exception.

## Scene 3

> 他发现这个口味，一百岁时候，不小心吃了毒的花。一吃就开始死。但是生病时候告诉人什么植物是药，什么植物是毒。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他发现这个口味，一百岁时候：他一百岁的时候，The phrase “发现这个口味” does not fit the following accidental-eating event: “口味” means a flavour or personal preference, not something that introduces an age. A time expression with “时候” requires “的”.
- 毒的花：有毒的花，“毒” is a noun meaning poison; use “有毒的” to describe a flower that contains poison.
- 开始死：快要死了，“死” is an instantaneous change of state, so it does not naturally follow “开始”. Use “快要……了” for an imminent change.
- 生病时候：生病的时候，Multi-syllable verb phrases before “时候” need the structural particle “的”.
- 告诉人：告诉人们，“人” is a generic noun and should not refer to a specific group of people in this context; use “人们”.
- 什么植物是毒：什么植物有毒，“有毒” describes a plant that contains poison. “是毒” would mean that the plant itself is poison.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不小心 -- “不小心吃了有毒的花”：误食了，specifically means accidentally ate something, especially something unsafe or unsuitable to eat.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉人们：告诉了人们，This is a completed action in a past narrative, so the verb needs the completion marker “了”.
- Expressiveness: After the poisonous flower is eaten, add a visual detail about it, such as “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花。”
- Expressiveness: Show the physical pain and fear at “快要死了”, such as “他捂着肚子，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Turn the information he gives people into direct speech, such as “这种花有毒，不能吃。”

### Encouragement
- You used the “一……就……” pattern well to show that the danger happened immediately after eating the flower. You also clearly included the important contrast between plants that are medicine and plants that are poisonous.
