## Scene 1

> 很久以前，古代的人很不知道什么植物能吃，也什么植物不能吃。所以很多人常常吃不安全的植物，吃不安全的植物对你的身体有坏处，很麻烦的情况。但是一个人，神农，要解决这个问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的人：古代的人们，Use “人们” for the concrete group of people living in ancient times; “人” is more generic.
- 很不知道：不知道，“很” does not normally modify the negative knowledge verb “不知道.”
- 也什么植物不能吃：也不知道什么植物不能吃，Parallel clauses joined by “也” need the repeated predicate “不知道.”
- 很麻烦的情况：是一种很麻烦的情况，A noun phrase cannot stand alone as the predicate here; use “是” to link the situation to its description.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不安全的 -- “很多人常常吃不安全的植物”：有害的，specifies that the plants are harmful to people’s health.
- 有坏处 -- “吃不安全的植物对你的身体有坏处”：会损害健康，states the harmful physical effect more directly and precisely.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 但是一个人，神农，要解决这个问题：但是，有一个叫神农的人要解决这个问题，For a first mention, introduce the character clearly with a full noun phrase rather than naming him after the vague phrase “一个人.”
- Expressiveness: After the sentence about harmful plants, add a physical result to make the danger vivid: “他们吃了以后，常常肚子疼。”
- Expressiveness: At the end, show why the problem matters to the people: “人们很担心，不知道该吃什么。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly set the story in the past and introduced the central problem before bringing in 神农. Your use of “所以” also correctly shows that people’s lack of knowledge leads to harmful choices.

## Scene 2

> 他又聪明又努力，旅行很远找到很多植物。比方说，灵芝和夹竹桃。他一吃各种各样的植物就知道能不能吃这些植物。然后，神农写他的主意。他喜欢植物的味道，特别是花。神农遇到了一个黄色的花，一吃就觉得不舒服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 旅行很远找到很多植物：走了很远的路，找到了很多植物，Distance is naturally expressed with a verb plus a distance object, such as “走了很远的路”; the completed actions in this narrative need “了”.
- 写他的主意：写下了自己的想法，“写下” means to record an idea in writing, and it naturally takes content such as “想法”; “自己的” refers back to the subject.
- 一个黄色的花：一朵黄色的花，Flowers use the measure word “朵”, not the general measure word “个”.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 然后，神农写下了自己的想法：然后，他写下了自己的想法，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence: the previous sentence already refers to the same character as “他”, and no other person competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农遇到了一朵黄色的花：他遇到了一朵黄色的花，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence: the preceding sentence is still about the same character.
- Expressiveness: At the flower’s first appearance, add a visual detail to make it feel tempting or unusual, such as “那儿开着又大又漂亮、像星星一样的黄花”.
- Expressiveness: After the character eats the flower, show the physical feeling more vividly, such as “他肚子疼了起来，在地上躺着”.
- Expressiveness: Add an inner thought at the moment of pain to show fear, such as “他想：我会死吗？”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show Shennong’s actions in sequence, especially with “然后” and the pattern “一……就……”.
- You also chose relevant plants, including “灵芝”和“夹竹桃”, which helps make the scene specific.

## Scene 3

> 发现了夹竹桃有毒。别人看到他，很快给他吃一个灵芝。神农一吃就觉得比较好。然后，他继续写他发现的信息。会告诉他的朋友哪一个植物能不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 比较好：好多了，比较 is used to compare two things or states explicitly; for a character whose condition has improved after being unwell, 好多了 expresses a clear change for the better.
- 写他发现的信息：记下他发现的信息，写 is too general here; 记下 means to record information so that the discovery is kept.
- 哪一个植物能不能吃：哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃，哪一个 asks the reader to choose one item, while 能不能 asks a yes-or-no question; use parallel clauses to explain which plants are edible and which are not.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 发现 -- “发现了夹竹桃有毒”：辨认出，emphasizes identifying the plant’s poisonous nature after observing or testing it.
- 很快 -- “很快给他吃一个灵芝”：赶快，adds the sense that the helper acts urgently because the character is unwell.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 给他吃一个灵芝：给他吃了一个灵芝，了 marks the completed action of giving the lingzhi to him to eat.
- Expressiveness: After the poisonous plant is discovered, add a physical reaction to show how serious the problem is: “他肚子疼了起来，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: When the helper arrives, describe the person’s action more vividly: “一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人赶快走过来，给神农吃了一个灵芝。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short question after he feels better to show curiosity: “神农问：‘你给我吃的是什么？’”

### Encouragement
- You used the pattern “一……就……” effectively to connect eating the lingzhi with feeling better right away.
- Your scene also has a clear time sequence with “然后”, moving from the discovery to recording and sharing the information.
