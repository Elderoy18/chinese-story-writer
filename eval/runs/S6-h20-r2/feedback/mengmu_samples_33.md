## Scene 1

> 一天，很久很久以前，孟子和他妈妈住在中国。孟子妈妈的工作是祭拜，孟子不高兴，因为他说我们老是搬家，为什么？他妈妈说，从早到晚，我一直祭拜，很多墓地得我祭拜。所以我们得去很多不同的地方。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一天，很久很久以前：在很久很久以前的一天，Use a single, complete time phrase; the original places two time expressions together without showing their relationship.
- 很多墓地得我祭拜：我得去很多墓地祭拜，The person who is required to act must be the subject of the modal verb 得, and 得 comes before the action verb phrase.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不高兴 -- “孟子不高兴”：不满，shows that he objects to the repeated moves rather than simply feeling unhappy.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和他妈妈：有一个小男孩叫孟子，他和妈妈，This is the first mention of the main character, so introduce him with his name and role before using a pronoun.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子妈妈的工作：他妈妈的工作，For a close re-occurrence, use a pronoun-based reference rather than repeating the name.
- Coherence (Reference): 他妈妈说：她说，For a close re-occurrence of the same character, a pronoun is more natural than repeating the relationship noun phrase.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 孟子不高兴，因为他说我们老是搬家，为什么？：孟子不高兴，因为他们老是搬家。他说：“为什么我们老是搬家？”，The connector 因为 must introduce the cause of his unhappiness; the quoted question should be a separate sentence introduced by the speaking verb.
- Expressiveness: When Mengzi complains, add a small action showing his feeling: 孟子皱着眉头说：“为什么我们老是搬家？”
- Expressiveness: Before the mother explains, show that her work is tiring: 妈妈叹了一口气，说：“从早到晚，我一直祭拜……”
- Expressiveness: Make the many places more vivid with a detail about travel: 她每天要走很远的路，去不同的墓地祭拜。

### Encouragement
You clearly show both Mengzi’s frustration and his mother’s reason for moving. The use of a quoted question gives the scene a lively beginning.

## Scene 2

> 孟子想，难怪我们常常搬家，原来我妈妈很忙。可是常常搬家害得孟子很难找到朋友。每次搬家孟子也觉得新的地方不是太热就是太冷，不是太大就是太小。在哪的地方，他都不高兴。孟子想帮助妈妈祭拜，可是他六岁，所以不能祭拜。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在哪的地方：不管在哪个地方，The question word needs the classifier in “哪个地方”; “不管……都……” is the correct structure for saying that the result is the same in every place.

### Coherence & Expressiveness

- Expressiveness: At “新的地方不是太热就是太冷,” add one concrete detail about the new homes, for example: “有的房子又小又暗。”
- Expressiveness: At the final sentence, add a short inner thought to show why he cannot help, for example: “他心想：‘我什么时候才能帮妈妈呢？’”

### Encouragement

## Scene 3

> 四年后，孟子开始帮妈妈祭拜。他画画很厉害，他越来越喜欢祭拜，所以他祭拜不是想帮妈妈工作，而是他很喜欢画画。孟子成为很著名的古代画家。今天很多中国人很喜欢孟子。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不是想帮妈妈工作：不是为了帮妈妈工作，Use “为了” before an intended purpose; “不是为了……，而是……” clearly contrasts two reasons for doing an action.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 厉害 -- “他画画很厉害”：擅长画画，identifies painting as a skill he is especially good at.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他画画很厉害，他越来越喜欢祭拜：他画画很厉害，越来越喜欢祭拜，Use zero form when the same subject continues within the same sentence.
- Coherence (Reference): 所以他祭拜：所以祭拜，Use zero form when the subject remains the same as in the preceding clause.
- Coherence (Reference): 而是他很喜欢画画：而是很喜欢画画，Use zero form for the same subject within a paired construction.
- Coherence (Temporality): 孟子成为很著名的古代画家：孟子成为了很著名的古代画家，Use “了” for a completed change of state: he eventually became a famous painter.
- Expressiveness: After “孟子开始帮妈妈祭拜,” add an action detail showing how he behaves during the ritual, such as “他一边祭拜，一边学着妈妈的样子鞠躬。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where he likes painting, add his feeling or a short thought, such as “孟子觉得画画比祭拜有意思多了。”
- Expressiveness: You could show the mother’s reaction with a brief line of speech, such as “妈妈说：‘你要认真祭拜，不要只顾着画画。’”

### Encouragement
- You use time expressions such as “四年后” and “今天” to show that the story moves from childhood to the present. You also clearly show that Mengzi has a strong interest in painting.
