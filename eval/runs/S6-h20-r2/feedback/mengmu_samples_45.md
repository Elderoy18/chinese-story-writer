## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子。他们住在墓地的旁边，所以孟子老是祭拜。孟子不是祭拜，就是跟朋友聊天。他很喜欢他的城市，但是城市有才两个学校，都很小。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，this is the standard time expression for a historical setting; the listed form is not natural here.
- 老是祭拜：经常祭拜，老是 carries a complaining or negative connotation, while this sentence does not yet describe the action as negative.
- 城市有才两个学校：城市里才有两所学校，才 should come before the verb to emphasize the small number, and a numeral with 学校 needs the measure word 所.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 小 -- “都很小”：规模不大，specifies that each school is small in scale rather than physically small.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子。：有一个叫孟子的男孩子和他的妈妈住在中国。，by the first-mention rule, introduce the character with his name and a full noun phrase when he first appears, rather than naming him only in the following sentence.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子不是祭拜，就是跟朋友聊天。：他不是祭拜，就是跟朋友聊天。，by the pronoun rule, use a pronoun when the same character was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no competing character is present.
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery sentence, add the boy’s feeling to explain why he keeps doing this: 孟子觉得祭拜很有趣。
- Expressiveness: At the point where he talks with friends, add a small action or quoted line to make the scene more vivid: 他跟朋友说：“我们也来学一学吧！”
- Expressiveness: When describing the schools, add the mother’s concern: 孟母看到学校这么小，心里很着急。

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish the setting, the family relationship, and the contrast between the cemetery area and the city. Your use of the pattern “不是……，就是……” also gives the character’s daily activities a clear rhythm.

### Encouragement

- “从早到晚” and “一直抱怨” clearly show that the walking and complaining lasted for a long time.
