## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个人叫神农。一天，神农正在路上走，一看很多人正在吃一些植物。一些人一吃植物就生病了，神农想帮别人。所以想知道哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，This is the standard expression for setting a story in an ancient time period.
- 看很多人：看到很多人，Use the result complement in this context: the character notices or sees the people, rather than merely directing his eyes at them.
- 别人：人们，This sentence means that Shennong wants to help people in general; this word is used to distinguish other people from a stated or implied person or group.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 一看很多人正在吃一些植物。一些人一吃植物就生病了：一看到很多人正在吃一些植物，就发现一些人一吃植物就生病了，The pattern beginning with a single action needs the matching connector to show the immediate result or next event.
- Expressiveness: After the people become ill, add Shennong’s reaction to show why he wants to help: “神农看见他们很难受，心里很着急。”
- Expressiveness: You could describe the plants briefly where Shennong sees them: “路边长着各种各样的植物，有的开着小花。”

### Encouragement
- You introduce Shennong clearly at the beginning and use the progressive form well to show actions happening at that moment.  
- The contrast between plants that can be eaten and plants that cannot be eaten gives the scene a clear purpose.

## Scene 2

> 然后，神农开始尝很多植物，比方说他吃过生姜、芥兰和菠菜。一些植物吃真好，特别是黄瓜。但是，一些植物毒得很，特别是夹竹桃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 吃真好：很好吃，Use the adjective “好吃” as the predicate for food or plants that taste good; the degree adverb “很” goes before it.
- 毒得很：有毒，“毒” cannot take degree complements such as “得很” in this meaning. Use “有毒” to describe a poisonous plant.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 尝 -- “神农开始尝很多植物”：尝试，emphasizes that he is testing plants to learn whether they are safe or useful.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农开始尝很多植物：他开始尝很多植物，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the same character was named in the immediately preceding context and no other character competes as the referent.
- Expressiveness: After introducing the poisonous flower, add a visual detail to make it memorable: “黄色的花像星星一样，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where he considers eating it, add a short thought to show his uncertainty: “神农想：‘这种花真漂亮，不知道能不能吃。’”

### Encouragement
- You use a clear sequence marker and give concrete examples of edible plants, which makes the scene easy to follow.
- The contrast between plants that taste good and plants that are poisonous is a strong way to organize this part of the story.

## Scene 3

> 一个人对神农说，麻烦我们可以吃什么植物？神农说，这就要看你想不想生病了。以后，神农告诉别人一些植物能吃，一些植物不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 麻烦：请问，Use “请问” to introduce a polite question. “麻烦” means “trouble” or asks someone to do a favour, so it cannot directly introduce this question.
- 这就要看你想不想生病了：这就要看你吃什么植物了，“这就要看……了” must give the factor that determines the uncertain answer. Wanting or not wanting to be ill does not determine which plants are edible; the kind of plant eaten is the relevant factor.
- 以后：后来，Sentence-initial “后来” means “afterwards” in a narrative. “以后” usually needs a stated time point and means “after that time.”
- 别人：那个人，“别人” distinguishes other people from the speaker or another stated person. Here, Shennong is replying to the person introduced in the previous sentence, so “那个人” is the clear referent.
- 告诉：告诉了，Add “了” because this is a completed telling action in the narrative.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 说 -- “一个人对神农说”：问，more precisely shows that the person is asking a question rather than making a general statement.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: At the question, add the person's worry to show why he asks: “一个人担心地问：‘请问，什么植物可以吃？’”
- Expressiveness: In Shennong's reply, add concrete examples of safe and dangerous plants: “像伞一样的灵芝可以吃，夹竹桃有毒，不能吃。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly set up a conversation between the person and Shennong, which makes the scene easy to follow.
- You also use the contrast between plants that can be eaten and plants that cannot be eaten well.
