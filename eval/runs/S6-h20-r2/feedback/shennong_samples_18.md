## Scene 1

> 神农和他的家人住在古代，所以不知道什么植物能吃。因为他的家人常常生病了，所以神农和他的哥哥一起去旅行找可以吃的植物。他们去很多不一样的地方，比方说高山、山谷和沙漠。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 住在古代：在古代住在一起，Ancient times is a time expression, not a place where someone can live; use the time phrase before the action.
- 因为他的家人常常生病了：因为他的家人常常生病，A habitual action or state with “常常” takes the zero aspect marker, so it does not use 了.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不一样 -- “很多不一样的地方”：各不相同，emphasizes that the mountains, valleys, and deserts differ from one another.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：一个叫神农的男人，This is the character’s first mention, so introduce him with a name and a brief noun phrase rather than using only the name.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们去很多不一样的地方：他们去了很多不一样的地方，Use 了 for the completed action of visiting several places in the narrative.
- Expressiveness: After the list of places, add a detail about the difficult journey, such as “他们走啊走啊，走得很累。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where they search for plants, add Shen Nong’s purpose or feeling, such as “神农想：我一定要找到可以吃的植物。”

### Encouragement
- You used clear cause-and-effect connectors and gave specific examples of the places they visited. The list of “高山、山谷和沙漠” makes the journey feel broad and adventurous.

- 了。“：了。”，The closing quotation mark must be ”, and it comes after the sentence-final punctuation.

- 麻烦 -- “太麻烦了”：棘手，specifies that the situation is difficult to deal with rather than merely inconvenient.

### Encouragement

## Scene 3

> 夹竹桃是毒的，我想知道灵芝会帮他。”哥哥给神农灵芝。神农吃完了以后开始痊愈了。神农说：“谢谢哥哥，你帮得真好。灵芝我们可以吃，特别是如果我们已经吃了夹竹桃。“

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 夹竹桃是毒的：夹竹桃有毒，Use 有毒 to describe that a plant contains poison; 毒 is a noun and should not be used here as an adjective with 的.
- 哥哥给神农灵芝：哥哥给了神农灵芝，Add 了 because giving the medicine is a completed action.
- 灵芝我们可以吃：我们可以吃灵芝，In a regular statement, the modal verb can comes after the subject and before the verb; the object follows the verb.
- 特别是如果我们已经吃了夹竹桃：如果我们已经吃了夹竹桃，特别是 is used to single out an example or a typical item, not to introduce an if-condition.
- 夹竹桃。“：夹竹桃。”，Use the closing quotation mark after the final period of direct speech.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 吃完 -- “神农吃完了以后开始痊愈了”：服下，specifically describes taking medicine and fits the healing context.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农吃完了以后开始痊愈了：他吃完了以后开始痊愈了，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence: 神农 was just named in the previous sentence, and no competing male character appears in between.

- Expressiveness: After the recovery, add a physical feeling to show the change: “他觉得肚子没有那么疼了。”

### Encouragement
