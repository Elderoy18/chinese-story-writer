### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections

- 又一个次搬了家：再搬一次家，Use 再 for a move that has not happened yet; the measure-word phrase is 一次, and a modal verb such as 要 is followed by the verb without 了.
- 找到工作：找到了工作，找到 means “find successfully,” and 了 marks the completed result of finding a job.
