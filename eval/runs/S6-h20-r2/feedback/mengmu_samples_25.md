## Scene 2

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 害得：害得孟子学坏，The verb pattern “害得” must be followed by the affected person and a result; it cannot end without a result complement.

- Expressiveness: At the point where the mother worries about the cemetery, add her inner feeling: “他的妈妈很担心，怕孟子学别人祭拜。”

### Encouragement
