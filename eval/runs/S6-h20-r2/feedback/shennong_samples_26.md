## Scene 1

> 很久以前，有一个老男人叫神农。神农在古代每一天看到了别的小镇人生病还是不舒服，所以他决定开始找新的药。他下一天开始爬当地的山脉，找了各种各样的植物。神农试一试每个植物，知道有什么好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别的小镇人：别的小镇上的人，A location noun such as 小镇 needs 的上 before 人 to mean “the people in another town.”
- 生病还是不舒服：生病或者感到不舒服，In a declarative sentence listing alternatives, use 或者; 还是 is normally used in an alternative question. Add 感到 because 不舒服 describes the people’s physical feeling.
- 他下一天：第二天，Use 第二天 to mean “the next day” in a past narrative; 下一天 is not the usual time expression here.
- 找了各种各样的植物：找到了各种各样的植物，找 means “look for,” while 找到 means “find.” The sentence describes plants that he found after climbing the mountains.
- 每个植物：每种植物，Use 种 as the measure word for types of plants; 个 does not fit plants in this meaning.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农在古代每天看到：他在古代每天看到，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence: the previous sentence has already introduced Shennong, and no other male character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农试一试每种植物：他试一试每种植物，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence: Shennong remains the only active character, so repeating his name is unnecessary.
- Coherence (Temporality): 每一天看到了：每天看到，Use the zero particle for a habitual action. 每天 shows that this was a routine observation, not one completed act.
- Coherence (Temporality): 试一试每种植物：试了试每种植物，Use 了 for the completed testing action in this past narrative before he learned about the plants’ benefits.
- Expressiveness: At the point where he sees ill people, add a concrete sign of illness to make the problem vivid, such as “有的人捂着肚子，有的人脸色很差。”
- Expressiveness: After he decides to search for medicine, add his determination or a short thought, such as “他想：我一定要找到能帮助大家的药。”
- Expressiveness: When he climbs the mountains, add a simple landscape detail, such as “山上长着很多陌生的花草。”

### Encouragement
- You gave the scene a clear historical setting and showed a logical reason for Shennong’s decision. The sequence from seeing sick people to searching in the mountains is easy to follow.

## Scene 2

> 比方说，他一次试一试有些植物的叶，吃得又新鲜又甜。煮这些叶以后，他一喝就知道他发现新的茶叶。虽然他找到了很多的新的植物，但是他的旅程有的时候特别是危险。比方说，他一吃灵芝就开始生病。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一次试一试有些植物的叶，吃得又新鲜又甜：有一次，他尝了一些植物的叶，发现这些叶又新鲜又甜，Use “有一次” to introduce one particular occasion. Verb reduplication such as “试一试” is for a light attempt or suggestion, not an action already carried out; use the completed action “尝了”. Also, “得” introduces a complement describing how an action is performed, while “新鲜、甜” describe the leaves, so use “发现” to introduce those qualities.
- 煮这些叶以后：煮了这些叶以后，The action of boiling is completed before the later action of drinking, so it needs the perfective marker “了”.
- 知道他发现新的茶叶：知道自己发现了新的茶叶，The discovery is a completed event, so “发现” needs “了”. “自己” clearly refers back to the subject of “知道”.
- 特别是危险：特别危险，“特别” is an adverb that directly modifies an adjective; “是” is not used between them.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 新的 -- “找到了很多的新的植物”：未尝过的，specifies that these were plants he had not tasted before, which fits his exploration more precisely.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 就开始生病：就开始生病了，This is a completed change of state in the example event, so the final verb phrase takes “了”.
- Expressiveness: After the tea is boiled, add one sensory detail about it, such as “水变成了淡淡的绿色，还有一股清香”.
- Expressiveness: At the moment he recognises the tea, add Shen Nong’s thought, such as “这可能是一种能喝的植物”.
- Expressiveness: At the dangerous part of the journey, show his physical reaction or feeling, such as “他肚子疼得站不起来了”.

### Encouragement
- You use the paired contrast connector “虽然……但是……” correctly to show that the journey had both success and danger. Your use of “一……就……” also clearly shows a quick result after an action.

## Scene 3

> 一个爬山的男人正好看到神农的麻烦，然后给了他一些药。神农最终他的小镇回到，跟别的人分享他的发现。你认为神农的故事真的吗？这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 看到神农的麻烦：看到了神农的麻烦，看到 means “to see/perceive,” while 看 means “to look”; 了 marks that the seeing was completed.
- 他的小镇回到：回到他的小镇，回到 is a directional verb phrase and must come before its destination.
- 这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处：这就要看你是否相信中药对身体有好处，after 看 in this meaning, use an embedded yes/no question such as 是否 or 相不相信.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 给了他一些药：给了神农一些药，repeat the name when two male characters are present, because the pronoun can otherwise refer to either man.
- Coherence (Temporality): 神农最终回到他的小镇：神农最终回到了他的小镇，use 了 after the directional complement 到 to show that the return was completed.
- Expressiveness: At the rescue moment, add one visual detail about the helper or the medicine, such as: “一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人走过来，拿起像伞一样的灵芝给神农吃。”
- Expressiveness: After the medicine, show Shennong’s physical feeling and a short line of speech, such as: “神农觉得好多了，连忙说：‘谢谢你！’”
- Expressiveness: When he returns, add an emotion to make the ending more vivid, such as: “神农高兴地回到小镇，跟别人分享他的发现。”

### Encouragement
