## Scene 1

> 孟子小的时候，从早到晚都跟朋友玩。孟子和他的朋友喜欢玩在墓地里，装祭拜。孟子五岁的时候，跟妈妈搬家。他们搬家不是因为孟子玩得太多，而是因为妈妈认为他们的家离墓地太近。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 喜欢玩在墓地里，装祭拜：喜欢在墓地里玩，装作祭拜，A location phrase such as “在墓地里” normally comes before the main verb “玩”; “装作 + verb” is the structure for pretending to do an action.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子小的时候：有一个小男孩叫孟子；他小的时候，On first mention, introduce a character with a name and a brief noun phrase rather than assuming the reader already knows who the character is.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和他的朋友：他和朋友，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the previous sentence has already named the character and no competing person is present.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子五岁的时候：他五岁的时候，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence; repeating the name so soon is unnecessary.
- Coherence (Temporality): 跟妈妈搬家：跟妈妈搬了家，Use completed-action “了” because this sentence recounts one finished move.
- Expressiveness: At the cemetery scene, add a small action to show how the children pretend to worship, for example: “他们一边笑，一边学着别人祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: Before the final reason for moving, show the mother’s concern with an internal thought or short quote, for example: “妈妈心想：‘住在这儿会影响孩子。’”

### Encouragement
- You used clear time expressions to organize the scene, especially “从早到晚” and “五岁的时候.”
- The contrast structure “不是……而是……” clearly explains why the family moved.

## Scene 2

> 妈妈认为，住离墓地太近，害得孟子的心气。孟子和孟母搬到子思老师的家，跟子思老师住。孟母帮子思打扫房间和做饭。子思帮孟母教她的儿子。因为孟子现在没有朋友，他老是读书。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 害得孟子的心气：害得孟子心情不好，the pattern after 害得 must state a result for the affected person; 心情不好 is a predicate result, while 心气 alone is only a noun.
- 老是读书：一直读书，老是 usually carries a negative, complaining tone; 一直 neutrally describes that he keeps reading.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 朋友 -- “孟子现在没有朋友”：玩伴，more specifically refers to companions a child plays with, which fits the reason he spends his time reading.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟母搬到子思老师的家：他们搬到了子思老师的家，use a pronoun for close re-occurrence when the two people were named in the immediately preceding sentence and no competing pair has appeared.
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬到子思老师的家：搬到了子思老师的家，use 了 for the completed action of moving to a new home.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为孟子现在没有朋友，他一直读书：因为孟子现在没有朋友，所以他一直读书，use 因为……所以…… to mark the cause-and-effect relation.
- Expressiveness: At the final sentence, add a small physical detail to show how he reads: “孟子坐在窗边，安安静静地读书。”
- Expressiveness: After describing the new study environment, show the mother’s feeling: “孟母看见孟子认真读书，心里很高兴。”

### Encouragement
You clearly connect the new home with changes in each character’s actions. You also use a cause to explain why Mengzi reads, which gives the scene a clear direction.
