## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男人觉得植物很有意思。他叫神农。有的植物吃了以后他的肚子疼死了，可是有的吃了以后很舒服。他找很多植物。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，this time expression is used for a general historical setting; the fixed natural form is “在古代”.
- 他找很多植物：他找到了很多植物，“找” means to look for, while “找到” means to find; “了” marks that the finding was completed.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一个男人觉得植物很有意思。他叫神农：有一个叫神农的男人觉得植物很有意思，first mention should introduce a character with both the name and a clear noun phrase, rather than naming him only in the next sentence.
- Expressiveness: After the stomach pain, add a physical reaction to make the discomfort vivid: “他疼得弯下了腰。”
- Expressiveness: Add an inner thought after he finds a plant that makes him feel well: “神农想：这种植物也许可以帮助生病的人。”

### Encouragement
- You used “有的……可是有的……” effectively to contrast the different effects of the plants. The vivid phrase “疼死了” also clearly conveys how severe the stomach pain feels.

## Scene 2

> 凉山很大的山，这也找植物。一天，他找到黄色的花。他听说你跟黄色的花做茶很有用。比方说，你的头发疼死，这冰茶是药。他一喝茶就很不舒服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 凉山很大的山：凉山是一座很大的山，A noun used to identify or describe another noun needs the linking verb and an appropriate measure word here.
- 这也找植物：他也在这儿找植物，After the sentence shifts to the mountain, the actor must be stated, and a location needs a location expression.
- 你跟黄色的花做茶很有用：用黄色的花做的茶很有用，Use 用 to introduce the material used to make something; 跟 means “with/and” and cannot introduce the material in this structure.
- 头发疼死：头疼得要死，The intended body part is the head, not hair; a degree complement uses 得 before the result or degree phrase.
- 这冰茶：这杯茶，A demonstrative before a countable noun needs a measure word; 杯 is the suitable measure word for tea.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 你的：他的，The pronoun rule requires a pronoun to refer to an established character; there is no addressee introduced as “you,” while the sentence is about the male character.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他一喝茶就很不舒服：可是，他一喝茶就很不舒服，Use a contrast connector because feeling unwell is unexpected after hearing that the tea is useful.
- Expressiveness: When he reaches the mountain, add a simple visual detail: “山上开着很多黄色的小花。”
- Expressiveness: Show his physical feeling after drinking the tea: “他捂着肚子，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought to show his surprise: “他想：‘这种茶为什么让我这么难受？’”

### Encouragement
You used a clear time marker to begin the event and the immediate-result pattern to show what happened after he drank the tea. The scene also introduces the yellow flowers clearly as an important new detail.

## Scene 3

> 糟糕，这黄色的花是夹竹桃，特别是毒。正好在这儿，灵芝药的夹竹桃他吃完了，也写一个课文的植物。谢谢神农。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 这黄色的花：这朵黄色的花，A demonstrative before a countable noun needs a measure word: 这 + measure word + noun.
- 特别是毒：特别有毒，毒 is a noun; use 有毒 to describe a plant’s poisonous property.
- 正好在这儿，灵芝药的夹竹桃他吃完了：正好这儿有灵芝，他吃了灵芝，The original does not form a grammatical noun phrase: 的 links a modifier to a noun, but it cannot connect 灵芝、药、夹竹桃 in this way. Use a complete clause to say that the place has 灵芝, then state what he ate.
- 一个课文的植物：这些植物，课文 is an individual lesson text and cannot be used as the possessor of 植物 here. Use the plants themselves as the direct object of the writing action.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 也写：也写下了，Use 了 for the completed writing action in this past event. 写下 means to record something in writing.
- Expressiveness: After identifying the poisonous flower, add the physical reaction: “他一吃夹竹桃，肚子就疼了起来。”
- Expressiveness: Describe the flower before the danger is revealed: “黄色的花又大又漂亮，像星星一样。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought to show his fear: “他想：‘我会不会死？’”

### Encouragement
- Your opening “糟糕” creates an immediate sense that something dangerous has happened. You also correctly identify the yellow flower as 夹竹桃, which clearly connects this scene to the earlier events.

## End of story

### Content & Completeness
- At the beginning, the story needs to explain that people in ancient times did not know which plants were safe to eat, so they sometimes became sick.
- Before 神农 starts searching, the story needs to say that he decided to test plants to help people know which ones could be eaten safely.
- When 神农 finds 茶, he should recognize it as tea and write it down. In this retelling, the “yellow flower” is later called 夹竹桃, so tea and 夹竹桃 are mixed together.
- After 神农 eats 夹竹桃, he needs to faint, not simply feel uncomfortable.
- After 神农 faints, a person in blue needs to arrive and give him 灵芝. 神农 should eat the 灵芝 and feel better.
- After 神农 feels better, a person in brown needs to come and talk with him.
- At the end, 神农 needs to record both 灵芝 and 夹竹桃, including that 灵芝 helps him and 夹竹桃 is poisonous.

### Overall Encouragement
- You included 神农, plants, tea, 夹竹桃, and 灵芝, so you remembered several important parts of the story. You also showed that some plants can hurt people, which fits the main idea.
