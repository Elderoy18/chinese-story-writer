## Scene 1

> 古代的时候人觉得祭拜非常重要，孟子很小的时候，从早到晚一直祭拜，因为他妈妈告诉他祭拜很重要。虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜。他的同学们老是对他开玩笑了。孟子妈妈看了他的同学们对孟子不好的影响，所以孟子和孟子妈妈搬家了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候人：在古代，人们，Use “在古代” for this historical time setting. “人” is a generic noun; use “人们” for a concrete group of people.
- 看了：看到了，Use “看到” when the meaning is “notice/see.” “看” means “look at,” so it does not fit the intended meaning here.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不好 -- “同学们对孟子不好的影响”：不良影响，specifies that the influence harmed Mengzi’s development or behaviour.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子很小的时候：有一个小男孩叫孟子。他很小的时候，Apply the first-mention rule: introduce Mengzi with a full noun phrase before using his name in the narrative.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子妈妈：孟子和妈妈，Apply the same-clause reference rule: after “孟子” has appeared in the phrase, do not repeat his name inside “孟子妈妈”; “妈妈” is clear here.
- Coherence (Temporality): 同学们老是对他开玩笑了：同学们老是对他开玩笑，Use the zero particle for a repeated routine. “老是” already shows that the teasing happens repeatedly, so completed-action “了” is not needed.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜：因为他每天只祭拜，所以没有朋友，Use the cause-and-effect pair “因为……所以……” because his only worshipping is presented as the reason he has no friends. “虽然” requires a contrasting result clause, not “因为.”
- Expressiveness: At the classmates’ teasing moment, add a short line of speech to make their behaviour clearer, such as “同学们笑着说：‘你怎么每天都在祭拜？’”
- Expressiveness: When his mother notices the influence, add her worried feeling or thought, such as “孟子妈妈很担心，觉得这样下去不行。”
- Expressiveness: Add a small detail about Mengzi’s reaction to show his feelings, such as “孟子听了很难过，只好一个人站在旁边。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show a cause for the move: the mother notices that Mengzi’s classmates are affecting him badly. You also use “从早到晚一直” effectively to show how often Mengzi worships.

## Scene 2

> 孟子才十一岁，成为一个哲学家。孟子不是祭拜，而是看政治文章，难怪他成为了哲学家。不过，看过很多政治文章对他害得，他有很多问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 对他害得：对他有害，"有害" is an adjective meaning harmful; "害得" requires a verb-complement structure and cannot follow "对他" in this way.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 问题 -- “他有很多问题”：疑问，specifies that these are questions he wants to understand after reading, rather than general difficulties.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子不是祭拜：他不是祭拜，use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the previous sentence has already named the same character and no competing character has appeared.
- Coherence (Temporality): 孟子才十一岁，成为一个哲学家：孟子才十一岁，就成为了一个哲学家，use the paired pattern "才 + number + 就 + verb" for an unexpectedly early event; "了" marks the completed change into a philosopher.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 对他有害，他有很多问题：对他有害，所以他有很多问题，add "所以" to show the cause-and-effect relation between the harmful reading and his many questions.
- Expressiveness: After the reading activity, show his thoughtful attitude with a detail such as “他一边读，一边认真地思考。”
- Expressiveness: Make his questions more concrete with an action such as “每读完一篇文章，他都把不懂的地方记下来。”

### Encouragement
- You used the contrast pattern “不是……而是……” appropriately to show how Mengzi’s interests changed. You also connected his reading to his later identity as a philosopher, which gives the scene a clear focus.

## Scene 3

> 他一直问问题，但是不能解决那些问题。孟子长大，他开始能解决问题。他常常建议你不是学金融，而是学政治，因为如果你懂政治，你会成为高薪。后来孟子成为教授，他教学生很厉害。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 成为高薪：拿到高薪，The word “高薪” means a high salary, so it cannot be the role or identity after “成为”; “拿到” correctly expresses receiving that salary.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 问问题 -- “他一直问问题”：提出问题，emphasizes raising particular questions for discussion or investigation.
- 很厉害 -- “他教学生很厉害”：很有一套，specifically suggests that he has effective methods for teaching students.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 你：学生，The pronoun introduces an unclear addressee in a narrative. Use a full noun phrase for the first mention of the people receiving his advice.
- Coherence (Temporality): 后来孟子成为教授：后来孟子成为了教授，Use “了” for the completed change of state: he finished becoming a professor.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 孟子长大，他开始能解决问题：孟子长大后，开始能解决问题，“后” marks the sequence: first he grew up, and after that he began to solve problems.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 如果你懂政治，你会拿到高薪：如果你懂政治，就会拿到高薪，Use “如果……就……” to mark the conditional relationship clearly.
- Expressiveness: After “不能解决那些问题,” add his feeling to show why this was difficult for him: “他觉得很着急，也不明白该问谁。”
- Expressiveness: After “他教学生很厉害,” add a short line of speech showing his teaching style: “他常常对学生说：‘先想一想，为什么会这样。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly show Mengzi’s development from having unanswered questions to becoming a capable teacher. The use of “但是” and “后来” also helps the scene move from one stage of his life to the next.
