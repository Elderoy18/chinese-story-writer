## Scene 1

> 古代的时候有一个人叫神农。他需要当上医，一看别的人就想帮助他们。但是，他有一个问题，他不知道什么东西能做药。神农听说一个远远的地方有一种植物，什么病人吃都会觉得身体更好。神农三个月走去，找到有植物的地方，他看到一个很漂亮的植物，又香又好看。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “古代的时候有一个人叫神农。” / Formula: 在 + historical time, + introduce character (name + identity) / Correct: “在古代，有一个人叫神农，他想成为一名医生。”  
“在古代” is more natural than “古代的时候,” and 神农 should be introduced with a little context.

Incorrect phrase: “他需要当上医” / Formula: 想/希望 + 成为 + 一名 + profession / Correct: “他想成为一名医生。”  
“当上” usually emphasizes successfully gaining a position, while “成为一名医生” is the natural expression for wanting to be a doctor.

Incorrect phrase: “一看别的人就想帮助他们” / Formula: 一看到 + people + 就 + 想 + help + them / Correct: “他一看到别人，就想帮助他们。”  
Use 看到 rather than 看 here, because it means “whenever he sees.” “别人” is more natural than “别的人.”

Incorrect phrase: “神农听说一个远远的地方有一种植物” / Formula: 听说 + 在 + place + 有 + noun / Correct: “神农听说在一个很远的地方有一种植物。”  
“远远的地方” is unnatural; say “很远的地方.” The place should also come after 在.

Incorrect phrase: “什么病人吃都会觉得身体更好” / Formula: 无论什么 + patient / 不管什么 + illness, + 吃了…都 + feel + better / Correct: “无论什么病人吃了这种植物，都会觉得身体好一些。”  
“什么病人” is unclear: it sounds like “which patient.” “无论什么病人” means “no matter what kind of patient.” Add 了 after 吃 to show the completed action.

Incorrect phrase: “神农三个月走去” / Formula: subject + 走了 + duration + 才 + arrive at + place / Correct: “神农走了三个月，才到了那个地方。”  
Duration usually follows the verb: 走了三个月. “才” emphasizes that the journey took a long time.

Incorrect phrase: “找到有植物的地方” / Formula: 找到 + specific place / 找到 + plant / Correct: “终于找到了那种植物生长的地方。”  
“有植物的地方” is too vague because plants grow in many places. “那种植物生长的地方” clearly refers to the special plant mentioned earlier.

Incorrect phrase: “一个很漂亮的植物” / Formula: 一株 + plant / 一种 + plant type / Correct: “一株很漂亮的植物。”  
Use the measure word 株 for an individual plant. Also, plants are often described as “美丽” rather than “漂亮.”

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “需要” / Context: “他需要当上医” / Alternatives: “想” or “希望.” “需要” means “need to” and can sound practical or necessary. “想成为一名医生” expresses a personal wish naturally, while “希望成为一名医生” sounds slightly more formal and aspirational.

Word: “帮助” / Context: “想帮助他们” / Alternatives: “医治” or “救治.” “帮助” is broad and can mean helping in any way. “医治” specifically means treating illness, while “救治” emphasizes urgently saving and treating sick or injured people.

Word: “远远的地方” / Context: “一个远远的地方” / Alternatives: “遥远的地方” or “很远的地方.” “很远” is simple and natural in everyday writing. “遥远” is more literary and creates a stronger sense of distance, which suits an ancient legend.

Word: “身体更好” / Context: “觉得身体更好” / Alternatives: “身体好一些” or “病情好转.” “身体好一些” means one feels somewhat healthier. “病情好转” is more precise and formal: it means a patient’s condition has improved.

Word: “漂亮” / Context: “一个很漂亮的植物” / Alternatives: “美丽的” or “奇异的.” “漂亮” is common for people and general appearance. “美丽的植物” is more natural for describing a plant, while “奇异的植物” suggests that it looks unusual or mysterious—especially suitable for a medicinal plant in a legend.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You created a clear story sequence—from Shennong’s wish to help people, to his journey to find a medicinal plant—and your sensory description “又香又好看” makes the plant vivid.  
Next time, focus on using precise time and duration structures, such as “走了三个月，才到了……,” and on making vague phrases more specific.

## Scene 2

> 他觉得，这种一定是做药的花。原来他想错了。吃了以后，神农快生病。 这时一个穿着蓝色衣服的人帮神农，快给他一种红的灵芝。神农吃了以后，他没有病，想起来以前一个人告诉他，灵芝对身体有好处。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “这种一定是做药的花”  
Formula: 这/那 + 一定是 + 用来 + 动词 + 的 + 名词  
Correct: “这种一定是用来做药的花。”  
“用来做药” makes the purpose clearer and more natural.

Incorrect phrase: “吃了以后，神农快生病。”  
Formula: Subject + 快要/差点儿 + 动词 + 了  
Correct: “吃了以后，神农快要生病了。”  
Use “快要……了” for “is about to…”; alternatively, if he almost became ill, say “神农差点儿生病了。”

Incorrect phrase: “这时一个穿着蓝色衣服的人帮神农，快给他一种红的灵芝。”  
Formula: 这时，+ person + 帮/帮助 + person + 做什么；给 + person + 一种 + adjective + noun  
Correct: “这时，一个穿着蓝色衣服的人帮助神农，赶快给了他一种红色的灵芝。”  
The original sentence needs punctuation, “赶快” (quickly) rather than “快,” and “红色的灵芝” rather than “红的灵芝.”

Incorrect phrase: “神农吃了以后，他没有病”  
Formula: Subject + 吃了……以后，+ 就 + 没有/不再 + 生病  
Correct: “神农吃了以后，就没有生病。”  
“没有病” describes a general state, while “没有生病” clearly means that he did not become ill after eating it. Also, “他” is redundant because the subject is already 神农.

Incorrect phrase: “想起来以前一个人告诉他”  
Formula: Subject + 想起 + 以前 + someone + 告诉 + him/her + 的事  
Correct: “他想起以前有人告诉过他，灵芝对身体有好处。”  
“一个人” is too vague here; “有人” is more natural when the person is unknown or unimportant. “告诉过” shows that the telling happened in the past.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “做药”  
Context: “这种一定是做药的花。”  
Alternatives: “入药” or “药材.” “做药” is understandable but basic. “入药” means “to be used as medicine” and is a more natural, concise written expression: “这种花一定可以入药。” “药材” refers to medicinal materials, such as herbs used in traditional medicine.

Word: “快生病”  
Context: “神农快生病。”  
Alternatives: “快要生病了” or “身体不舒服.” “快要生病了” specifically means he is about to become ill. “身体不舒服” is softer and describes feeling unwell, without necessarily being sick.

Word: “帮”  
Context: “一个穿着蓝色衣服的人帮神农。”  
Alternatives: “帮助” or “救了.” “帮” is common and conversational. “帮助” is slightly more formal and works well in narration. “救了” is stronger: it means the person saved Shennong from danger or illness.

Word: “红的灵芝”  
Context: “一种红的灵芝。”  
Alternatives: “红色的灵芝” or “赤灵芝.” “红色的” is the standard way to describe an object’s color. “赤灵芝” is a specific, more literary or scientific name for a type of red reishi mushroom.

Word: “对身体有好处”  
Context: “灵芝对身体有好处。”  
Alternatives: “有益健康” or “滋补身体.” “对身体有好处” is clear and everyday. “有益健康” is more concise and formal. “滋补身体” emphasizes nourishing and strengthening the body, often in the context of traditional Chinese medicine.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You told a clear mini-story with a logical sequence: Shennong eats something, feels unwell, receives lingzhi, and remembers its benefit.  
Next time, focus on using “快要……了,” “赶快,” and “动词 + 了以后” to make the actions and timing more natural.

## Scene 3

> 啊，他怎么忘记了？他把灵芝放在包里。一回家就告诉别人。他找到药以后他写下怎么做药在书上。所以人们都知道，那红灵芝对身体好处。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “他怎么忘记了？”  
Formula: 谁 + 怎么 + 把 + 什么事情 + 忘了？  
Correct: “他怎么把这件事忘了？”  
“忘记” usually needs an object: what did he forget?

Incorrect phrase: “一回家就告诉别人。”  
Formula: 谁 + 一 + 动作 + 就 + 把 + 事情 + 告诉 + 人  
Correct: “他一回家，就把这件事告诉别人。”  
Add the subject “他” and specify what he told others.

Incorrect phrase: “他找到药以后他写下怎么做药在书上。”  
Formula: 谁 + 找到/发现 + 什么 + 以后，谁 + 把 + 内容 + 写在 + 地方  
Correct: “他找到灵芝以后，把制作药的方法写在书上。”  
Use “把……写在书上” for the correct word order. “灵芝” is more specific than “药.”

Incorrect phrase: “那红灵芝对身体好处。”  
Formula: 什么 + 对 + 什么 + 有好处  
Correct: “那种红灵芝对身体有好处。”  
You need “有” before “好处.” “那种” is more natural when referring to a type of plant.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “找到”  
Context: “他找到灵芝以后……”  
Alternatives: “发现” is a stronger choice when someone discovers something new or realizes its value. “找到” simply means locating something, while “发现” emphasizes the discovery.

Word: “药”  
Context: “他找到药以后……”  
Alternatives: “药材” or “中药材.” “药” usually refers to medicine that is ready to take, while “药材” refers to natural ingredients, such as lingzhi, that can be used to make medicine.

Word: “别人”  
Context: “告诉别人”  
Alternatives: “大家” or “人们.” “别人” means other people in a general, unspecified way. “大家” sounds warmer and more conversational, while “人们” works well when describing how knowledge spreads among the public.

Word: “怎么做药”  
Context: “写下怎么做药……”  
Alternatives: “制作药的方法” or “药用价值.” “制作药的方法” clearly means the method of making medicine. “药用价值” means a plant’s medicinal value and is especially suitable if he wrote about what lingzhi can do for health.

Word: “好处”  
Context: “对身体有好处”  
Alternatives: “益处” or “有益于健康.” “好处” is common and simple; “益处” is more formal, and “有益于健康” is a natural, polished phrase for describing health benefits.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You clearly connected the events in a logical order: finding lingzhi, going home, recording the knowledge, and helping others learn about it.  
Next time, focus on adding objects after verbs such as “忘记” and “告诉,” and use “把” sentences to make your writing clearer.
