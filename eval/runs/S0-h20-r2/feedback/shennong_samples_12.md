## Scene 1

> 很久以前，古代的人很不知道什么植物能吃，也什么植物不能吃。所以很多人常常吃不安全的植物，吃不安全的植物对你的身体有坏处，很麻烦的情况。但是一个人，神农，要解决这个问题。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “古代的人很不知道什么植物能吃，也什么植物不能吃” / Formula: 人们 + 不知道 + 什么……，什么…… / Correct: “古代的人不知道什么植物能吃，什么植物不能吃。”

Incorrect phrase: “吃不安全的植物对你的身体有坏处，很麻烦的情况” / Formula: 做某事 + 对 + 某人的身体 + 有坏处；这/会造成 + 很麻烦的情况 / Correct: “吃不安全的植物对人的身体有坏处，会造成很麻烦的情况。”

Incorrect phrase: “但是一个人，神农，要解决这个问题” / Formula: First mention: 有一个人叫……，他是……；为了……，他决定…… / Correct: “但是，有一个人叫神农，他决定解决这个问题。”  
“一个人，神农” is not a natural way to introduce a character. Introduce him clearly when he first appears.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “很久以前” / Context: “很久以前，古代的人……” / Alternatives: “在古代” is more direct and natural when you are introducing a historical story. “很久以前” means “a long time ago” and has a fairy-tale/storytelling feeling; “在古代” specifically emphasizes the historical period.

Word: “古代的人” / Context: “古代的人很不知道……” / Alternatives: “古人” is a concise, natural word for people in ancient times. “古代的人” is understandable but longer; “古人” sounds more fluent in historical narration.

Word: “不安全的植物” / Context: “很多人常常吃不安全的植物” / Alternatives: “有毒的植物” is more precise if you mean plants that can harm people. “不安全的” is broad and can describe many dangers, while “有毒的” specifically means poisonous.

Word: “有坏处” / Context: “对人的身体有坏处” / Alternatives: “有害” is more concise and slightly more formal: “对身体有害.” “伤害身体” is more direct and active. “有坏处” is common and simple, but less specific.

Word: “解决这个问题” / Context: “神农决定解决这个问题” / Alternatives: “弄清楚哪些植物能吃” makes the goal more specific and vivid. “解决问题” focuses on fixing a problem, while “弄清楚” emphasizes discovering or finding out the answer.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You clearly set up the historical problem and gave Shennong a meaningful purpose, so your story has a strong beginning.  
Next time, focus on using more specific words, such as “有毒的植物,” and introducing new characters with a complete sentence.

## Scene 2

> 他又聪明又努力，旅行很远找到很多植物。比方说，灵芝和夹竹桃。他一吃各种各样的植物就知道能不能吃这些植物。然后，神农写他的主意。他喜欢植物的味道，特别是花。神农遇到了一个黄色的花，一吃就觉得不舒服。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “他又聪明又努力”  
Formula: First mention: character’s name + brief identity/introduction + description  
Correct: “神农是一位又聪明又努力的人。”  
“他” is unclear because Shennong has not been introduced yet.

Incorrect phrase: “旅行很远找到很多植物。比方说，灵芝和夹竹桃。”  
Formula: Subject + 到/去 + 很远的地方 + 旅行，+ 找到/发现 + …；这些植物包括 + …  
Correct: “他到很远的地方旅行，找到了很多植物，这些植物包括灵芝和夹竹桃。”  
“旅行很远” is not natural; use “到很远的地方旅行.” The second sentence is also a fragment, so it should be connected to the previous sentence.

Incorrect phrase: “然后，神农写他的主意。”  
Formula: Subject + 写下/记录 + 自己的 + 想法/发现  
Correct: “然后，神农写下了自己的想法。”  
“他的” is vague here, and “写主意” is not a natural collocation.

Incorrect phrase: “神农遇到了一个黄色的花”  
Formula: Number + measure word + adjective + noun  
Correct: “神农发现了一朵黄色的花。”  
Flowers use the measure word “朵,” not “个.” “发现” is also more natural than “遇到” for finding a plant.

Incorrect phrase: “一吃就觉得不舒服。”  
Formula: Subject + 一 + verb + object，+ 就 + result  
Correct: “他一吃这朵花，就觉得不舒服。”  
Adding “他” and “这朵花” makes it completely clear that Shennong ate the flower, rather than leaving the subject/object implicit.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “很远”  
Context: “到很远的地方旅行”  
Alternatives: “遥远的地方” or “偏远的地方.” “很远” is simple and neutral. “遥远” sounds more literary and emphasizes a great distance, while “偏远” emphasizes that a place is remote and far from cities.

Word: “找到”  
Context: “找到了很多植物”  
Alternatives: “发现了很多植物” or “辨认出很多植物.” “找到” means to locate something one was looking for. “发现” emphasizes making a new discovery, which fits Shennong’s exploration better. “辨认出” means to identify or recognize plants correctly.

Word: “主意”  
Context: “写他的主意”  
Alternatives: “想法,” “发现,” or “观察结果.” “主意” usually means an idea or plan for what to do. “想法” is a general thought or opinion; “发现” is something newly learned; “观察结果” is more formal and emphasizes the results of careful observation.

Word: “遇到”  
Context: “遇到了一个黄色的花”  
Alternatives: “发现” or “看见.” “遇到” is most commonly used for meeting people or encountering situations. “发现” is better when a traveler finds a new plant, while “看见” simply means to see it.

Word: “不舒服”  
Context: “觉得不舒服”  
Alternatives: “感到身体不适,” “肚子疼,” or “中毒了.” “不舒服” is broad and everyday language. “身体不适” is more formal, “肚子疼” gives a specific symptom, and “中毒了” is much stronger and should only be used if the plant is poisonous.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You did well using a clear sequence of events and including specific plants such as 灵芝 and 夹竹桃, which makes the story more interesting.  
Next time, focus on introducing the main character clearly and using measure words such as “一朵花.”

## Scene 3

> 发现了夹竹桃有毒。别人看到他，很快给他吃一个灵芝。神农一吃就觉得比较好。然后，他继续写他发现的信息。会告诉他的朋友哪一个植物能不能吃。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “发现了夹竹桃有毒。” / Formula: Subject + 发现 + object / Correct: “神农发现夹竹桃有毒。”  
The sentence needs a clear subject.

Incorrect phrase: “别人看到他，很快给他吃一个灵芝。” / Formula: Somebody + 给 + somebody + 吃/喝 + object; use a specific person instead of a vague “别人” / Correct: “他的朋友看到神农后，很快给他吃了一个灵芝。”  
“别人” is too vague here, and “吃了” shows a completed action.

Incorrect phrase: “神农一吃就觉得比较好。” / Formula: Subject + 一 + verb phrase + 就 + result; 身体 + 好一些 / Correct: “神农一吃灵芝就觉得身体好一些了。”  
“比较好” needs a clearer reference, such as his health or condition.

Incorrect phrase: “然后，他继续写他发现的信息。” / Formula: Subject + 继续 + verb + object; 记录/写下 + information / Correct: “然后，神农继续记录他发现的信息。”  
“写信息” is understandable, but “记录信息” is more natural.

Incorrect phrase: “会告诉他的朋友哪一个植物能不能吃。” / Formula: Subject + 会 + 告诉 + somebody + 哪些/什么 + noun + 能不能 + verb / Correct: “他会告诉他的朋友哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃。”  
This sentence needs a subject, and “哪一个植物能不能吃” is not natural. Use “哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃” to express the contrast clearly.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “别人” / Context: “别人看到他” / Alternatives: “朋友” or “邻居”  
“别人” simply means “other people,” so it is vague. “朋友” makes the relationship personal and clear, while “邻居” refers specifically to people who live nearby.

Word: “给他吃一个灵芝” / Context: “很快给他吃一个灵芝” / Alternatives: “让他吃了一株灵芝” or “给他服用灵芝”  
“给他吃” is understandable but slightly informal and incomplete in this context. “让他吃了一株灵芝” clearly says someone had Shennong eat it. “服用” is more formal and is especially natural for medicine.

Word: “觉得比较好” / Context: “神农一吃就觉得比较好” / Alternatives: “觉得好多了” or “感觉身体舒服多了”  
“比较好” means “relatively better,” but it does not clearly say what improved. “好多了” is natural for recovery, while “身体舒服多了” vividly describes feeling much more comfortable.

Word: “写” / Context: “继续写他发现的信息” / Alternatives: “记录” or “记下”  
“写” is a basic, general verb. “记录” means to document information systematically and sounds suitable for Shennong’s discoveries. “记下” means to write something down and is more conversational.

Word: “信息” / Context: “他发现的信息” / Alternatives: “发现” or “药草知识”  
“信息” is broad and modern-sounding. “发现” focuses on the things Shennong discovered, while “药草知识” specifically refers to knowledge about medicinal plants and better fits the story.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You did well by presenting the events in a clear chronological order and including the important plot points: oleander is poisonous, lingzhi helps, and Shennong shares his discoveries.  
Next time, focus on adding clear subjects and using specific nouns instead of vague words such as “别人.”
