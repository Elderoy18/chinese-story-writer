## Scene 1

> 很久以前，有一个老男人叫神农。神农在古代每一天看到了别的小镇人生病还是不舒服，所以他决定开始找新的药。他下一天开始爬当地的山脉，找了各种各样的植物。神农试一试每个植物，知道有什么好处。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “神农在古代每一天看到了别的小镇人生病还是不舒服”  
Formula: Subject + 每天 + 都 + verb + Place + 的 + People + 生病或感到不舒服  
Correct: “神农每天都看到小镇上的人生病或感到不舒服。”  
“很久以前” already gives the time setting, so “在古代” is unnecessary here. Use “或” rather than “还是” when listing two alternatives in a statement.

Incorrect phrase: “所以他决定开始找新的药”  
Formula: 因为/所以 + Subject + 决定 + verb phrase  
Correct: “所以他决定寻找能治病的草药。”  
“新的药” means medicine that is newly invented or newly available. Here, Shen Nong is looking for medicinal plants that can treat illnesses.

Incorrect phrase: “他下一天开始爬当地的山脉”  
Formula: 第二天 + Subject + 开始 + verb  
Correct: “第二天，他开始爬附近的山。”  
For “the next day” in a story, use “第二天,” not “下一天.” Also, “爬山” is more natural than “爬山脉.”

Incorrect phrase: “找了各种各样的植物”  
Formula: Subject + 寻找 + various + noun  
Correct: “寻找各种各样的植物。”  
“找到了植物” means he successfully found the plants. Since he is still searching, “寻找” is more accurate.

Incorrect phrase: “神农试一试每个植物，知道有什么好处”  
Formula: Subject + 尝试 + 每一种 + noun + ，想知道/了解 + its + benefits  
Correct: “神农尝试每一种植物，想知道它们有什么好处。”  
Use the measure word “种” for types of plants. “试一试” is usually for a brief or casual attempt; “尝试” fits a narrative better. “想知道” clearly expresses his purpose.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “老男人”  
Context: “有一个老男人叫神农。”  
Alternatives: “老人” or “老者.” “老男人” can sound blunt or slightly negative in Chinese. “老人” is neutral and respectful, while “老者” is more literary and works especially well in a historical legend.

Word: “看到了”  
Context: “神农每天都看到了小镇上的人生病……”  
Alternatives: “发现” or “看见.” “看到” focuses on visually seeing something, while “发现” emphasizes noticing or realizing a problem. In this context, “发现” is more natural because Shen Nong notices that people are ill.

Word: “不舒服”  
Context: “生病或感到不舒服”  
Alternatives: “身体不适” or “患病.” “不舒服” is common and conversational. “身体不适” is more formal and broad, while “患病” specifically means suffering from an illness.

Word: “当地的山脉”  
Context: “爬当地的山脉”  
Alternatives: “附近的山” or “深山.” “当地的山脉” sounds geographical and technical. “附近的山” is natural for a simple story, while “深山” creates a more vivid image of remote, wild mountains.

Word: “好处”  
Context: “知道它们有什么好处”  
Alternatives: “药效” or “功效.” “好处” is a general word for benefits. “药效” means medicinal effect, and “功效” means a specific useful effect; both are more precise for medicinal plants.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You did well by giving the story a clear cause-and-effect flow: Shen Nong sees sick people, so he decides to search for medicine.  

Next time, focus on choosing precise time expressions and using “寻找” versus “找到” to show whether an action is still in progress or already completed.

## Scene 2

> 比方说，他一次试一试有些植物的叶，吃得又新鲜又甜。煮这些叶以后，他一喝就知道他发现新的茶叶。虽然他找到了很多的新的植物，但是他的旅程有的时候特别是危险。比方说，他一吃灵芝就开始生病。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “他一次试一试有些植物的叶” / Formula: 有一次，谁 + 尝了/试了 + 什么；use a completed verb rather than verb reduplication for an action that actually happened / Correct: “有一次，他尝了几种植物的叶子。”

Incorrect phrase: “吃得又新鲜又甜” / Formula: 食物 + 吃起来 + 又……又…… / Correct: “这些叶子吃起来又新鲜又甜。”

Incorrect phrase: “他一喝就知道他发现新的茶叶” / Formula: 谁 + 一 + 动词 + 就 + 知道/发现 + 一种 + 名词；use 了 for a completed discovery / Correct: “煮了这些叶子以后，他一喝就知道自己发现了一种新的茶叶。”

Incorrect phrase: “很多的新的植物” / Formula: 很多 + adjective + noun / Correct: “很多新的植物。”

Incorrect phrase: “他的旅程有的时候特别是危险” / Formula: 谁的 + 名词 + 有时候 + 很 + adjective；特别 directly modifies an adjective, without 是 / Correct: “但是，他的旅程有时候特别危险。”

Incorrect phrase: “他一吃灵芝就开始生病” / Formula: 灵芝 is medicinal, so it should make the person feel better rather than ill / Correct: “他一吃灵芝，身体就开始好起来了。”  
This is also a story-content correction: 灵芝 is medicine, not a poisonous plant.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “比方说” / Context: “比方说，他……” / Alternatives: “例如” is more concise and slightly more formal in writing. “有一次” is best when you are introducing one specific event in a story, rather than giving a general example.

Word: “试一试” / Context: “试一试有些植物的叶” / Alternatives: “尝了” means “tasted” and clearly describes eating a small amount to learn its flavor. “品尝” is more formal and often suggests tasting food or tea carefully.

Word: “叶” / Context: “植物的叶” / Alternatives: “叶子” is the natural everyday word for a plant’s leaves. “嫩叶” means young, tender leaves and creates a more vivid description of leaves suitable for tea.

Word: “新的植物” / Context: “找到了很多新的植物” / Alternatives: “陌生的植物” emphasizes that the plants were unfamiliar to Shennong. “从未见过的植物” is more descriptive and means plants that he had never seen before.

Word: “危险” / Context: “旅程……特别危险” / Alternatives: “凶险” is stronger and more literary, suggesting serious hidden danger. “充满危险” means “full of dangers” and makes the journey sound more dramatic.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You did well using time-order words such as “以后” and “一……就……,” which helps your story move forward clearly.  
Next time, focus on choosing completed verbs such as “尝了” for actions that have already happened, and check whether the story details match the original plot.

## Scene 3

> 一个爬山的男人正好看到神农的麻烦，然后给了他一些药。神农最终他的小镇回到，跟别的人分享他的发现。你认为神农的故事真的吗？这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “一个爬山的男人” / Formula: Verb + object + 的 + noun / Correct: “一个正在爬山的男人” or “一个爬山的男人” is understandable, but “正在爬山的男人” more clearly shows that he is climbing at that moment.

Incorrect phrase: “看到神农的麻烦” / Formula: Subject + 看到 + person + 遇到的/正在经历的 + problem / Correct: “看到神农遇到了麻烦” or “看到神农正遇到麻烦。” “神农的麻烦” sounds incomplete because it does not explain what kind of trouble he has.

Incorrect phrase: “神农最终他的小镇回到” / Formula: Subject + 最终 + 回到 + place / Correct: “神农最终回到了他的小镇。” The place must come after 回到, and 了 shows that the return was completed.

Incorrect phrase: “你认为神农的故事真的吗？” / Formula: Subject + 认为 + clause + 吗？ / Correct: “你认为神农的故事是真的吗？” Put 是 before the adjective 真.

Incorrect phrase: “这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处” / Formula: 这要看 + subject + 是否/是不是 + verb phrase / Correct: “这就要看你是否相信中药对身体有好处。” Adding 是否 makes the indirect question more natural and complete.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “麻烦” / Context: “神农遇到了麻烦” / Alternatives: “困难” means a difficult situation or obstacle and sounds slightly more formal. “危险” is better if Shennong is in physical danger, for example after eating a poisonous plant. “麻烦” is common and conversational, but it is less specific.

Word: “药” / Context: “给了他一些药” / Alternatives: “草药” specifically means medicinal herbs, which fits Shennong’s story especially well. “药材” refers to materials used to make medicine, often dried herbs, rather than ready-to-take medicine. “药” is a broad, general word.

Word: “最终” / Context: “神农最终回到了他的小镇” / Alternatives: “最后” is more common in beginner narratives and simply means “in the end.” “终于” emphasizes that something happened after a long wait, effort, or difficulty. “最终” sounds more formal and emphasizes the final outcome.

Word: “小镇” / Context: “回到了他的小镇” / Alternatives: “村庄” is more suitable if it is a small rural settlement. “家乡” emphasizes Shennong’s hometown and emotional connection to the place. “小镇” means a small town and is neutral.

Word: “有好处” / Context: “中药对身体有好处” / Alternatives: “有益于身体健康” is more formal and means “beneficial to physical health.” “有疗效” means that a medicine has a real therapeutic effect, so it is stronger and more specific than “有好处.” “有好处” is simple and broad.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You created a clear ending by asking the reader a question and connecting Shennong’s story to traditional Chinese medicine.  
Next time, focus on Chinese word order, especially the pattern “回到 + place,” and on using “是……吗？” for yes/no questions.
