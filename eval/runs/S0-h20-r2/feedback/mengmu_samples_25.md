## Scene 1

> 这是古代的故事。孟子小的时候，他的爸爸很早就去世了。从他的家到墓地很近，所以他每天老是从早到晚去墓地跟朋友们玩玩儿。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “这是古代的故事。” / Formula: 在 + historical time; first mention = name + brief introduction / Correct: “这是一个发生在古代的故事，讲的是孟子小时候的事。”  
“古代的故事” is understandable, but “发生在古代的故事” is more natural. It is also helpful to introduce 孟子 when he first appears.

Incorrect phrase: “孟子小的时候” / Formula: Character + 小时候 / Correct: “孟子小时候”  
When talking generally about someone’s childhood, “小时候” is more natural without 的.

Incorrect phrase: “从他的家到墓地很近” / Formula: Place A + 离 + Place B + 很近 / Correct: “他家离墓地很近”  
Use 离 to describe the distance between two places. “他的家” is also slightly unclear on first mention; “孟子家” or “他家” after introducing him is clearer.

Incorrect phrase: “每天老是从早到晚去墓地跟朋友们玩玩儿” / Formula: Subject + 每天 + 从早到晚 + 去 + place + 跟 + people + 玩 / Correct: “所以他每天从早到晚都去墓地跟朋友们玩。”  
“每天” and “老是” overlap: both express repeated actions, and 老是 can sound negative or complaining. Also, add 都 with 从早到晚, and use 玩 rather than 玩玩儿 for a regular daily habit.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “去世” / Context: “他的爸爸很早就去世了。” / Alternatives: “过世” is a gentler, more conversational euphemism for dying. “去世” is already appropriate and respectful, especially in writing; “病逝” can be used only if the person died from illness.

Word: “很早” / Context: “他的爸爸很早就去世了。” / Alternatives: “早早” makes the sentence more emotional and literary: “爸爸早早就去世了.” “在他很小的时候” gives a more specific connection to Mengzi’s childhood.

Word: “墓地” / Context: “他家离墓地很近。” / Alternatives: “坟地” is more colloquial and often refers to a burial ground in the countryside. “墓园” sounds more modern and organized. “墓地” is neutral and accurate.

Word: “玩” / Context: “跟朋友们玩。” / Alternatives: “玩耍” is slightly more formal and suitable for narration about children. “嬉戏” is more literary and vividly suggests children playing happily.

Word: “朋友们” / Context: “跟朋友们玩。” / Alternatives: “小伙伴们” is more natural when referring to a child’s playmates and creates a warmer childhood feeling. “朋友们” is neutral and can refer to people of any age.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You clearly expressed the cause-and-effect relationship between the father’s death, the home’s location, and Mengzi’s daily activities.  
Next time, focus on natural distance patterns such as “A 离 B 很近” and avoid using both “每天” and “老是” together.

## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈觉得在墓地玩儿，害得，所以他们搬家。搬家对孟子有好的影响，他才五岁，可是每天不是写字就是帮妈妈的工作。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “觉得在墓地玩儿，害得” / Formula: Subject + 觉得 + clause; if expressing a negative result, use “害怕/不好” or “害得 + person + result” / Correct: “他的妈妈觉得在墓地玩儿不好，所以他们搬家了。”  
“害得” needs a following result, such as “害得孟子学会了不好的习惯.”

Incorrect phrase: “所以他们搬家” / Formula: Subject + 因为/觉得…，所以 + verb + 了 (for a completed event) / Correct: “所以他们搬家了。”

Incorrect phrase: “搬家对孟子有好的影响” / Formula: Topic + 对 + person + 有 + adjective + 的 + noun / Correct: “搬家对孟子有好的影响。”  
This sentence is grammatically acceptable. A more natural version is: “搬家对孟子有积极的影响。”

Incorrect phrase: “他才五岁，可是每天不是写字就是帮妈妈的工作” / Formula: 才 + number/age + 就 + verb; 不是 + V1 + 就是 + V2; 帮 + person + verb / Correct: “他才五岁，就每天不是写字，就是帮妈妈工作。”  
Use “才五岁就…” to emphasize that he was very young when he began doing these things. “帮妈妈的工作” means “help with Mom’s work” incorrectly; say “帮妈妈工作” or “帮妈妈做家务.”

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “觉得” / Context: “他的妈妈觉得在墓地玩儿不好” / Alternatives: “认为” is more formal and is especially useful for opinions in narrative writing. “担心” emphasizes that his mother was worried about the possible negative effect. For example: “他的妈妈担心在墓地玩儿会影响孟子。”

Word: “玩儿” / Context: “在墓地玩儿” / Alternatives: “玩耍” is slightly more written and narrative-like than “玩儿.” “嬉戏” is more literary and suggests children playing happily and actively. For this story, “在墓地附近玩耍” sounds natural.

Word: “好的影响” / Context: “搬家对孟子有好的影响” / Alternatives: “积极的影响” is a more formal, natural phrase for a beneficial effect. “良好的影响” also means positive influence, with a more polished written tone.

Word: “写字” / Context: “每天不是写字就是…” / Alternatives: “练字” specifically means practising handwriting or calligraphy, while “写字” simply means writing characters. If Mengzi was practising Chinese characters, “每天练字” is more precise.

Word: “帮妈妈工作” / Context: “帮妈妈工作” / Alternatives: “帮妈妈做家务” means helping with household chores and is likely the most natural meaning here. “协助妈妈做事” is more formal and broader, meaning assisting his mother with tasks.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You clearly expressed the cause of the move and connected it to Mengzi’s daily life, which gives your short scene a logical flow.  
Next time, focus on using “才……就……” and completing result expressions such as “害得……”.

## Scene 3

> 他很聪明的人，难怪他成为有名的人。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “他很聪明的人” / Formula: Subject + 很 + adjective; or Subject + 是 + adjective + 的 + noun / Correct: “他很聪明。” If you mean “He is a very smart person,” say: “他是一个很聪明的人。”

Incorrect phrase: “成为有名的人” / Formula: 成为 + specific noun/profession/identity / Correct: “成为一位有名的哲学家。”  
“有名的人” is understandable, but it is vague; *成为* is more natural with a specific identity, such as 哲学家、医生、作家, etc.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “聪明” / Context: “他很聪明” / Alternatives: “智慧” or “有才智.” “聪明” is a common, everyday word for being intelligent. “有智慧” emphasizes deeper wisdom and good judgment, while “有才智” is slightly more formal and highlights intellectual ability.

Word: “有名” / Context: “成为有名的人” / Alternatives: “著名” or “享有盛名.” “有名” is natural and common. “著名” is more formal and is often used for well-known people, places, or works. “享有盛名” is literary and means someone has a highly respected reputation.

Word: “人” / Context: “有名的人” / Alternatives: “哲学家,” “学者,” or “思想家.” “人” is very broad and unspecific. Using a role such as “哲学家” makes the sentence clearer and gives the reader more information about what he became.

Word: “难怪” / Context: “难怪他成为有名的人” / Alternatives: “因此” or “所以.” “难怪” means “no wonder,” and it expresses that the result now seems understandable. “因此” and “所以” simply show cause and effect, so they are better if you do not want to express surprise.

Word: “成为” / Context: “他成为……” / Alternatives: “变成” or “成了.” “成为” is formal and is especially suitable for becoming a profession, status, or identity. “变成” often suggests a noticeable change in state, while “成了” is more conversational.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You used “难怪” well to connect his intelligence with his later success, which shows a clear sense of cause and effect.  
Next time, focus on choosing a specific noun after “成为,” such as “哲学家” or “学者.”
