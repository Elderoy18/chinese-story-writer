## Scene 1

> 一天，很久很久以前，孟子和他妈妈住在中国。孟子妈妈的工作是祭拜，孟子不高兴，因为他说我们老是搬家，为什么？他妈妈说，从早到晚，我一直祭拜，很多墓地得我祭拜。所以我们得去很多不同的地方。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “一天，很久很久以前”  
Formula: 在 + time expression + 的 + noun  
Correct: “在很久很久以前的一天，……”  
“很久很久以前” is a time expression, so it sounds smoother with “在……的一天.”

Incorrect phrase: “孟子和他妈妈住在中国”  
Formula: Name + 和 + 他的/她的 + family member  
Correct: “孟子和他的妈妈住在中国。”  
“He妈妈” is understandable in speech, but “他的妈妈” is clearer and more standard in writing.

Incorrect phrase: “孟子妈妈的工作是祭拜”  
Formula: 某人的职责/工作是 + verb phrase  
Correct: “孟子的妈妈的职责是祭祀。”  
“祭拜” is usually an action done toward ancestors or gods; “祭祀” is more natural for a person’s regular duty or occupation.

Incorrect phrase: “孟子不高兴，因为他说我们老是搬家，为什么？”  
Formula: 谁 + 情绪 +，问 + 谁 + ：“question”  
Correct: “孟子很不高兴，问妈妈：‘我们为什么老是搬家？’”  
Use quotation marks for direct speech. Here, “老是” is appropriate because Mengzi is complaining about moving repeatedly.

Incorrect phrase: “很多墓地得我祭拜”  
Formula: Object + 需要 + person + 去 + verb / 谁 + 得 + verb + object  
Correct: “很多墓地需要我去祭拜。” / “我得祭拜很多墓地。”  
“得” should normally follow the person who must do something, not the object.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “很久很久以前”  
Context: “一天，很久很久以前……”  
Alternatives: “在远古时代” or “在很久以前.” “很久很久以前” is vivid and story-like, while “远古时代” sounds more historical and literary. “很久以前” is simpler and more neutral.

Word: “工作”  
Context: “孟子妈妈的工作是祭拜”  
Alternatives: “职责” or “职业.” “工作” is general and everyday. “职责” emphasizes a duty or responsibility, while “职业” emphasizes a formal profession.

Word: “祭拜”  
Context: “从早到晚，我一直祭拜”  
Alternatives: “祭祀” or “祭奠.” “祭拜” means worshipping ancestors, gods, or sacred figures. “祭祀” is more formal and fits traditional religious ceremonies; “祭奠” specifically means mourning or honoring someone who has died.

Word: “不高兴”  
Context: “孟子不高兴”  
Alternatives: “不满” or “苦恼.” “不高兴” is simple and broad. “不满” emphasizes dissatisfaction with the repeated moving, while “苦恼” suggests that he feels troubled and worried by it.

Word: “很多不同的地方”  
Context: “所以我们得去很多不同的地方”  
Alternatives: “各地” or “四处.” “很多不同的地方” is clear but slightly long. “各地” means various places or regions, while “四处” emphasizes moving around in many directions or places.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You did well using dialogue to show Mengzi’s feelings and his mother’s explanation, which makes the scene easy to follow.  
Next time, focus on word order with “得” and on using quotation marks for direct speech.

## Scene 2

> 孟子想，难怪我们常常搬家，原来我妈妈很忙。可是常常搬家害得孟子很难找到朋友。每次搬家孟子也觉得新的地方不是太热就是太冷，不是太大就是太小。在哪的地方，他都不高兴。孟子想帮助妈妈祭拜，可是他六岁，所以不能祭拜。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “每次搬家孟子也觉得新的地方不是太热就是太冷，不是太大就是太小。” / Formula: 每次 + V. + 的时候，主语 + 都 + 觉得…… / Correct: “每次搬家的时候，孟子都觉得新地方不是太热就是太冷，不是太大就是太小。”

Incorrect phrase: “在哪的地方，他都不高兴。” / Formula: 在 + 疑问词 + 地方，主语 + 都 + V. / Correct: “不管在哪个地方，他都不高兴。”  
“在哪的地方” is not natural Chinese. Use “在哪个地方” or “不管在哪个地方.”

Incorrect phrase: “孟子想帮助妈妈祭拜，可是他六岁，所以不能祭拜。” / Formula: 虽然 + 原因/情况，可是/但是 + 结果；才 + 年龄 + 就 + V. (for something done at a young age) / Correct: “孟子想帮妈妈祭拜，可是他才六岁，还不能参加祭拜。”  
Use “才六岁” to emphasize that he is very young. “参加祭拜” is more natural than simply repeating “祭拜.”

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “常常” / Context: “我们常常搬家” / Alternatives: “经常” and “频繁” are more natural here. “常常” means “often” and is fine in conversation; “经常” is neutral and common for repeated actions, while “频繁” emphasizes that moving happened many times in a short period.

Word: “忙” / Context: “原来我妈妈很忙” / Alternatives: “工作很忙” or “工作繁忙.” “忙” is simple and general; “工作很忙” clearly explains why the mother is busy, while “工作繁忙” is more formal.

Word: “害得” / Context: “常常搬家害得孟子很难找到朋友” / Alternatives: “使” or “让.” “害得” strongly emphasizes a negative result and sounds like a complaint. “使” is more formal and objective; “让” is neutral and natural in everyday writing.

Word: “新的地方” / Context: “新的地方不是太热就是太冷” / Alternatives: “新住处” or “新家.” “新的地方” is broad and can mean any location. “新住处” specifically means a new place to live, while “新家” sounds warmer and more personal.

Word: “帮助妈妈祭拜” / Context: “孟子想帮助妈妈祭拜” / Alternatives: “帮妈妈准备祭拜” or “和妈妈一起祭拜.” “帮助妈妈祭拜” is understandable, but “帮妈妈准备祭拜” is clearer if he helps with preparations; “和妈妈一起祭拜” emphasizes that they do the ritual together.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You expressed Mengzi’s feelings clearly, especially his frustration with constantly moving and not being able to make friends.  
Next time, focus on using “才……就……” for young ages and “不管……都……” for situations that are true everywhere.

## Scene 3

> 四年后，孟子开始帮妈妈祭拜。他画画很厉害，他越来越喜欢祭拜，所以他祭拜不是想帮妈妈工作，而是他很喜欢画画。孟子成为很著名的古代画家。今天很多中国人很喜欢孟子。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “他画画很厉害” / Formula: Subject + 画得 + adjective / Correct: “他画得很好。”  
“很厉害” is understandable, but 画得很好 is the more natural way to describe someone’s painting skill.

Incorrect phrase: “他祭拜不是想帮妈妈工作，而是他很喜欢画画” / Formula: Subject + 做某事，不是为了……，而是因为…… / Correct: “他祭拜不是为了帮妈妈祭拜，而是因为他喜欢画画。”  
However, the meaning is still a little unclear: painting and worshipping are different activities. If he likes painting, you could write: “他帮妈妈祭拜的时候喜欢画画。”

Incorrect phrase: “孟子成为很著名的古代画家” / Formula: Subject + 成为 + 一个/一名 + modifier + noun / Correct: “孟子成为了一名很著名的古代画家。”  
Also, historically, 孟子 was a philosopher, not a painter. A more accurate sentence is: “孟子后来成为了一名著名的哲学家。”

Incorrect phrase: “今天很多中国人很喜欢孟子” / Formula: Time + place/group + subject + verb / Correct: “今天，中国很多人都很喜欢孟子。”  
Putting “中国” before “很多人” sounds more natural here. Adding 都 emphasizes that many people share this opinion.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “开始” / Context: “孟子开始帮妈妈祭拜。” / Alternatives: “开始” simply marks the beginning of an action. “着手” means to begin doing something formally or seriously, but it is less suitable for this simple story. “主动” can be useful if you mean that Mencius chose to help on his own: “孟子主动帮妈妈祭拜。”

Word: “很厉害” / Context: “他画画很厉害。” / Alternatives: “很厉害” is common and conversational, but general. “擅长” means “to be skilled at” and is more precise: “他擅长画画。” “出色” means exceptionally good and is slightly more formal: “他画得很出色。”

Word: “越来越喜欢” / Context: “他越来越喜欢祭拜。” / Alternatives: “越来越喜欢” clearly shows that someone likes something more over time. “逐渐喜欢上” emphasizes a gradual process of beginning to like something: “他逐渐喜欢上了画画。” This may fit better if you want to describe his developing interest in painting.

Word: “工作” / Context: “帮妈妈工作” / Alternatives: “工作” usually refers to a job or general work. For helping with a specific religious or family activity, “帮忙” is more natural: “帮妈妈的忙。” “祭拜” can also be used directly: “帮妈妈祭拜。”

Word: “很著名” / Context: “很著名的古代画家” / Alternatives: “很著名” means widely known and is neutral. “著名” is already strong, so you often do not need 很. “有名” is more conversational and slightly less formal. “杰出” means outstanding because of one’s achievements, so it is a stronger compliment: “一位杰出的哲学家。”

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You did well using time expressions such as “四年后” and “今天” to give your short story a clear timeline.  
Next time, focus on making sure the actions and reasons connect logically, especially when using “不是……而是……”.
