## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子。他们住在墓地的旁边，所以孟子老是祭拜。孟子不是祭拜，就是跟朋友聊天。他很喜欢他的城市，但是城市有才两个学校，都很小。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “古代的时候” / Formula: 在 + historical time period / Correct: “在古代，一个叫孟子的男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。” “在古代” is more natural than “古代的时候.” Also, introduce the character naturally on first mention.

Incorrect phrase: “孟子老是祭拜” / Formula: Subject + 常常/一直 + verb; use 老是 mainly for repeated actions with a negative or complaining tone / Correct: “孟子常常祭拜。” Here, the sentence is neutral, so “常常” is better.

Incorrect phrase: “孟子不是祭拜，就是跟朋友聊天” / Formula: 不是 + Verb phrase A + 就是 + Verb phrase B / Correct: “孟子不是在祭拜，就是在跟朋友聊天。” Add “在” to make the two verb phrases parallel and natural.

Incorrect phrase: “城市有才两个学校” / Formula: Subject + 才 + 有 + number + measure word + noun / Correct: “城市才有两所学校。” “才” should come before “有,” and schools use the measure word “所.”

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “住在墓地的旁边” / Context: “他们住在墓地的旁边” / Alternatives: “住在墓地附近” is more concise and natural. “旁边” means directly next to something, while “附近” means in the surrounding area and sounds smoother in this description.

Word: “祭拜” / Context: “孟子常常祭拜” / Alternatives: “祭拜祖先” or “扫墓” would be more specific. “祭拜” is a formal word for worshipping or making offerings, but it usually needs an object, such as ancestors. “扫墓” specifically means visiting and cleaning ancestors’ graves.

Word: “聊天” / Context: “跟朋友聊天” / Alternatives: “闲聊” emphasizes casual conversation, while “交谈” is more neutral or formal. “聊天” is common and natural in everyday speech.

Word: “喜欢他的城市” / Context: “他很喜欢他的城市” / Alternatives: “喜爱这座城市” sounds more literary and emotionally warm. “喜欢” is a general everyday word, while “喜爱” expresses deeper affection.

Word: “很小” / Context: “两个学校，都很小” / Alternatives: “规模很小” is more precise for schools. “很小” describes physical size generally, whereas “规模很小” refers to the school’s size in terms of student population, buildings, or resources.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You created a clear little story with logical connections, especially using “所以” to explain why Mencius often visited the cemetery.  
Next time, focus on word order with “才” and on choosing neutral frequency words such as “常常” instead of “老是.”

## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈不是不喜欢他们的城市，而是想孟子有很好的教育。他们搬家到有很多学校的城市。他们从早到晚走路，孟子一直抱怨。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “他的妈妈不是不喜欢他们的城市，而是想孟子有很好的教育。”  
Formula: Subject + 不是因为 + reason + 才/而 + action， 而是 + 希望/为了 + someone + 能 + result  
Correct: “他的妈妈不是因为不喜欢他们的城市才搬家，而是希望孟子能接受良好的教育。”  
“想孟子有……” is not natural; use “希望孟子能……” or “想让孟子……”. Adding “才搬家” also makes the contrast clear.

Incorrect phrase: “他们搬家到有很多学校的城市。”  
Formula: Subject + 搬到 + place  
Correct: “他们搬到了一座有很多学校的城市。”  
“搬到” is the verb meaning “move to”; “搬家到” is less natural here. “一座” is a natural measure word for a city.

Incorrect phrase: “他们从早到晚走路，孟子一直抱怨。”  
Formula: 因为/为了 + reason，Subject + action， 所以/因此 + result  
Correct: “为了搬到新城市，他们从早到晚赶路，因此孟子一直抱怨。”  
The original sentences are understandable, but adding a connector makes the cause-and-effect relationship clearer.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “很好的教育”  
Context: “孟子有很好的教育”  
Alternatives: “良好的教育” and “优质的教育.” “很好的” is simple and general. “良好的教育” is natural and neutral, while “优质的教育” emphasizes high quality and good educational resources.

Word: “有很多学校的城市”  
Context: “搬到有很多学校的城市”  
Alternative: “教育资源丰富的城市.” This is a more advanced and natural phrase. It suggests that the city has many schools, good teachers, and other learning opportunities—not merely a large number of schools.

Word: “走路”  
Context: “他们从早到晚走路”  
Alternatives: “步行” and “赶路.” “走路” simply means walking. “步行” is more formal, while “赶路” emphasizes traveling for a long time in order to reach a destination.

Word: “抱怨”  
Context: “孟子一直抱怨”  
Alternatives: “埋怨” and “发牢骚.” “抱怨” is a general word for complaining. “埋怨” often means blaming someone or something, while “发牢骚” is more informal and describes repeatedly expressing dissatisfaction.

Word: “他的妈妈”  
Context: “他的妈妈不是……”  
Alternative: “孟母.” “他的妈妈” is correct, but “孟母” is more concise and is the traditional name used when discussing Mencius’s mother in a historical or literary context.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You expressed the mother’s motivation, the move, and Mencius’s reaction in a clear logical sequence.  
Next time, focus on using “希望让/希望……能……” and connectors such as “因为、为了、因此” to make your sentences flow more smoothly.

## Scene 3

> 他难怪抱怨，他想他的朋友，因为他不要离开他的城市。他的妈妈找到孟子一个老师。跟老师孟子学最重要的文学，所以他成为哲学家。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “他难怪抱怨” / Formula: 难怪 + subject + verb / Correct: “难怪他抱怨。”  
Alternatively: “他抱怨也难怪。” This means it is understandable that he complains.

Incorrect phrase: “他想他的朋友” / Formula: 想念 + person / Correct: “他想念他的朋友。”  
“想” can mean “to think” or “to want”; use “想念” specifically for missing someone.

Incorrect phrase: “因为他不要离开他的城市” / Formula: 因为 + subject + 不想 + verb / Correct: “因为他不想离开他的城市。”  
不要 usually means “don’t!” as a command, while 不想 expresses “does not want to.”

Incorrect phrase: “他的妈妈找到孟子一个老师” / Formula: subject + 给 + person + 找 + 一个 + noun / Correct: “孟子的妈妈给孟子找了一个老师。”  
Use 给孟子 to show who the teacher was found for.

Incorrect phrase: “跟老师孟子学最重要的文学” / Formula: subject + 跟 + person + 学习 + object / Correct: “孟子跟老师学习重要的文学作品。”  
The subject, 孟子, should come before 跟老师.

Incorrect phrase: “所以他成为哲学家” / Formula: 后来 + subject + 成为了 + identity / Correct: “后来，他成为了一位哲学家。”  
后来 is more natural for a later event in a narrative.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “抱怨” / Context: “难怪他抱怨。” / Alternatives: “不满” or “埋怨.” 抱怨 means to complain and is common in daily speech. 不满 means “to be dissatisfied” and sounds more neutral or formal. 埋怨 often means blaming someone or something for a problem.

Word: “想” / Context: “他想他的朋友。” / Alternatives: “想念” or “挂念.” 想 can have several meanings, including “to think” and “to want.” 想念 clearly means to miss someone. 挂念 means to worry about or keep someone in one’s thoughts, with a warmer and more caring tone.

Word: “找到” / Context: “妈妈给孟子找了一个老师。” / Alternatives: “请” or “聘请.” 找 means to look for or find someone. 请一位老师 means to invite or hire a teacher, while 聘请 is more formal and means to employ someone professionally.

Word: “最重要的文学” / Context: “学习重要的文学作品。” / Alternatives: “经典文学作品” or “名著.” 文学 is a broad field—literature in general. 经典文学作品 emphasizes influential, well-known literary works, while 名著 refers to famous classic books.

Word: “哲学家” / Context: “他成为了一位哲学家。” / Alternatives: “思想家” or “儒家学者.” 哲学家 is a general term for a philosopher. 思想家 emphasizes someone known for influential ideas, and 儒家学者 is more specific if you are writing about Confucian learning.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You clearly showed a logical story: he misses his friend, his mother finds a teacher, and he later becomes a philosopher.  
Next time, focus on word order, especially putting the subject before phrases such as “跟老师学习.”
