## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，孟子和孟母住了在靠墓地的家。孩子才五岁就每天在墓地祭拜了。在那个城市，孟母从早到晚都找工作，可是找不到，害得孟母没有钱。难怪她跟孩子搬家，原来买不了房。他们搬了到别一个城市找工作。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “古代的时候，孟子和孟母住了在靠墓地的家。”  
Formula: 在 + time；Subject + 住在 + place；靠近 + place + 的 + noun  
Correct: “在古代，孟子和孟母住在靠近墓地的房子里。”

Incorrect phrase: “孩子才五岁就每天在墓地祭拜了。”  
Formula: Subject + 才 + age + 就 + frequency + 在 + place + verb；“每天” usually does not take 了 for a habitual action.  
Correct: “孟子才五岁就每天在墓地祭拜。”

Incorrect phrase: “可是找不到，害得孟母没有钱。”  
Formula: Subject + 找不到 + object；because of a result, use “所以/就 + result.”  
Correct: “可是找不到工作，所以孟母就没有钱了。”

Incorrect phrase: “难怪她跟孩子搬家，原来买不了房。”  
Formula: 难怪 + result，原来 + reason；带着 + person + 搬家  
Correct: “难怪孟母要带着孩子搬家，原来她买不起房子。”

Incorrect phrase: “他们搬了到别一个城市找工作。”  
Formula: Subject + 搬到 + place + 去 + verb；“另一个” means “another.”  
Correct: “他们搬到另一个城市去找工作。”

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “靠”  
Context: “靠墓地的家”  
Alternatives: “靠近墓地的房子” or “墓地附近的房子.” “靠” is often used in spoken Chinese and can mean “next to,” while “靠近” is clearer and more natural for describing a location. “附近” means “nearby” and is especially useful for places.

Word: “家”  
Context: “住在靠墓地的家”  
Alternatives: “房子” or “住所.” “家” can mean both “home” and “family,” so “房子” is more precise when you mean the building. “住所” is more formal and means “residence.”

Word: “孩子”  
Context: “孩子才五岁”  
Alternatives: “孟子” or “小孟子.” “孩子” is understandable, but using “孟子” makes the reference clearer. “小孟子” sounds more vivid and emphasizes that he was very young.

Word: “没有钱”  
Context: “孟母没有钱”  
Alternatives: “经济困难” or “手头拮据.” “没有钱” is simple and direct. “经济困难” means “to be in financial difficulty,” while “手头拮据” means having very little money available and is more advanced.

Word: “买不了房”  
Context: “原来买不了房”  
Alternatives: “买不起房子” or “负担不起房价.” “买不了” can mean one is unable to buy for various reasons; “买不起” specifically emphasizes that something is too expensive. “负担不起房价” is more formal and stresses that the housing price is unaffordable.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You did well connecting the family’s financial problem, the move, and the job search into a clear sequence of events.  
Next time, focus on word order with location phrases, especially “住在 + place” and “搬到 + place.”

## Scene 2

> 这个城市老是很吵，不是人吵架，就是都叫，害得孟子一直不能睡觉。在那儿，孟母也找不到工作。孟子和妈妈要又一个次搬了家。在第三个城市，孟母找到工作，在商店做衣服。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “不是人吵架，就是都叫” / Formula: 不是 + 有人 + VP， 就是 + 有人 + VP / Correct: “不是有人吵架，就是有人大喊大叫。”  
“都叫” is unclear here; use “有人大喊大叫” to describe noisy people more naturally.

Incorrect phrase: “孟子和妈妈要又一个次搬了家” / Formula: Subject + 又 + V + 了 + Num. + Measure Word + Object / Correct: “孟子和孟母又搬了一次家。”  
For a completed action, use “又搬了一次家.” Do not use “要” or “一个次” here.

Incorrect phrase: “在商店做衣服” / Formula: 在 + Place + 里 + V / Correct: “在商店里做衣服。”  
When describing an action happening inside a place, add “里” after the location.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “老是” / Context: “这个城市老是很吵” / Alternatives: “总是” or “一直”  
“老是” often expresses annoyance or complaint, which is possible here. “总是” is more neutral and natural for a factual description. You could write: “这个城市总是很吵。”

Word: “吵” / Context: “这个城市……很吵” / Alternatives: “嘈杂” or “喧闹”  
“吵” is simple and common. “嘈杂” emphasizes mixed, unpleasant noises, while “喧闹” describes a lively and noisy environment. “这个城市非常嘈杂” is more descriptive.

Word: “叫” / Context: “有人……叫” / Alternatives: “大喊大叫” or “吵闹”  
“叫” alone can mean calling someone or making a sound, so it is not very precise. “大喊大叫” means shouting loudly, while “吵闹” means making noise or causing a disturbance.

Word: “找不到工作” / Context: “孟母也找不到工作” / Alternatives: “找不到合适的工作” or “一直没有找到工作”  
“找不到工作” means she cannot find any job. “找不到合适的工作” is more specific: she cannot find a suitable job. “一直没有找到工作” emphasizes that this situation continued for some time.

Word: “做衣服” / Context: “在商店里做衣服” / Alternatives: “缝衣服” or “缝制衣服”  
“做衣服” is understandable but broad. “缝衣服” is more everyday and means sewing clothes; “缝制衣服” is more formal and emphasizes the process of making clothes.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You clearly showed the cause-and-effect relationship between the noisy city, Mengzi’s sleep problems, and the family’s decision to move again.  
Next time, focus on using “又 + verb + 了” for repeated completed actions and making people’s actions more specific.

## Scene 3

> 孟子在那个商店也找了到工作，可是不是做衣服的工作，而是喜欢的工作。孟子学习馆学了十五年，他的学习和他的旅行经验很重要，对他很多好处。他是大人的时候，他成为很有名的哲人。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “孟子在那个商店也找了到工作”  
Formula: Subject + 在 + Place + 找到 + Object  
Correct: “孟子在那家商店找到了工作。”  
“找到” is the correct result complement; use “也” only when there is a clear earlier action to compare with.

Incorrect phrase: “可是不是做衣服的工作，而是喜欢的工作”  
Formula: Subject + 找到的不是 + A +，而是 + 自己喜欢的 + B  
Correct: “不过，他找到的不是做衣服的工作，而是自己喜欢的工作。”  
Use **不是……而是……** for “not X, but Y,” and add “自己” to make clear who likes the job.

Incorrect phrase: “孟子学习馆学了十五年”  
Formula: Subject + 在 + Place + V. + Duration  
Correct: “孟子在学馆学习了十五年。”  
You need “在” before the place, and “学习” should be used as the verb. “学馆” is a more suitable word for an old-style school.

Incorrect phrase: “他的学习和他的旅行经验很重要，对他很多好处”  
Formula: A + 对 + Person + 很有好处 / 给 + Person + 带来 + 很多好处  
Correct: “他的学习和旅行经验对他很有好处。”  
Alternatively: “他的学习和旅行经验给他带来了很多好处。”

Incorrect phrase: “他是大人的时候，他成为很有名的哲人”  
Formula: Subject + 长大以后/成年后 + 成为（了）+ Noun  
Correct: “他长大以后成为了一位很有名的哲学家。”  
“他是大人的时候” is not natural Chinese; use “长大以后” or “成年后.” “哲学家” is also more common than “哲人” here.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “那个商店”  
Context: “孟子在那个商店……”  
Alternatives: **那家店铺** or **那间铺子**. “商店” is a modern, everyday word. “店铺” is slightly more formal, while “铺子” has a more traditional, historical feeling and suits a story about Mencius better.

Word: “做衣服的工作”  
Context: “不是做衣服的工作……”  
Alternatives: **制衣工作** or **裁缝的工作**. “做衣服的工作” is understandable but general. “制衣工作” sounds more concise and professional; “裁缝的工作” specifically refers to tailoring and making or altering clothes.

Word: “学习馆”  
Context: “孟子学习馆学了十五年”  
Alternatives: **学馆** or **私塾**. “学习馆” is not a standard term. “学馆” means an old-style school, while “私塾” refers to a traditional private school, often with a teacher teaching a small group of students.

Word: “旅行经验”  
Context: “他的学习和他的旅行经验……”  
Alternatives: **游历经历** or **见闻**. “旅行经验” emphasizes practical experience from traveling. “游历经历” has a more literary, historical tone; “见闻” emphasizes the things a person has seen, heard, and learned through travel.

Word: “很有名”  
Context: “成为很有名的哲人”  
Alternatives: **著名的** or **声名远扬的**. “很有名” is simple and common. “著名” is more formal and natural in writing, while “声名远扬” means someone’s reputation is widely known and has a more vivid, literary feeling.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You clearly expressed a logical life story: Mencius found a job, studied for many years, gained experience, and became a philosopher.  
Next time, focus on using place words with “在” and checking result complements such as “找到.”
