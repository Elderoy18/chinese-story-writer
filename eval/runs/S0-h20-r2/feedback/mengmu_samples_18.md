## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个男孩名字叫孟子。孟子和孟子的妈妈两个人住在很穷的农村。他们住的地方旁边是个墓地，墓地老是有很多人祭拜。因为人们在祭拜，他们一直在哭。孟子和孟子的妈妈住在这里才一个星期就觉得受不了了，而且害得孟子的学习。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “古代的时候，有一个男孩名字叫孟子。” / Formula: 在 + historical time；有一个 + 叫 + name + 的 + noun / Correct: “在古代，有一个叫孟子的男孩子。” “在古代” is more natural than “古代的时候,” and the modifier “叫孟子” should come before “男孩子.”

Incorrect phrase: “孟子和孟子的妈妈两个人住在很穷的农村。” / Formula: Person A 和 Person B + 住在 + place；modifier + 的 + noun / Correct: “孟子和他的妈妈住在一个很贫穷的农村。” Repeating “孟子” is unnecessary after he has been introduced. Also, “很穷的农村” is understandable, but “很贫穷的农村” is more natural.

Incorrect phrase: “他们住的地方旁边是个墓地” / Formula: place + 的旁边 + 是 + noun / Correct: “他们住的地方旁边有一个墓地。” Use “有” to introduce a new place or object.

Incorrect phrase: “墓地老是有很多人祭拜。” / Formula: 有很多人 + 在 + place + verb；很多 + noun (no 的) / Correct: “墓地里老是有很多人来祭拜。” “祭拜” needs people as its subject, and “墓地里” makes the location clear.

Incorrect phrase: “因为人们在祭拜，他们一直在哭。” / Formula: 因为 + reason, 所以 + result；来祭拜的人 + 一直 + verb / Correct: “因为来祭拜的人一直在哭，所以孟子和他的妈妈觉得很吵。” “他们” could refer to either the visitors or Mengzi and his mother, so it is clearer to name the people directly.

Incorrect phrase: “孟子和孟子的妈妈住在这里才一个星期就觉得受不了了，而且害得孟子的学习。” / Formula: subject + 在这里住了 + duration + 就 + 受不了了；这 + 影响/耽误 + someone 的 + noun / Correct: “孟子和他的妈妈在这里住了才一个星期就受不了了，而且这里的环境还影响了孟子的学习。” “害得” needs a clear cause before it, such as “这里的环境” or “哭声.”

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “很穷” / Context: “住在很穷的农村” / Alternatives: “贫穷” or “偏僻.” “很穷” is simple and usually describes people or families. “贫穷” is more formal and can describe an area’s economic condition. “偏僻” means remote or far from busy places; it may fit a rural setting even if the village is not poor.

Word: “农村” / Context: “住在一个很贫穷的农村” / Alternatives: “乡村” or “村庄.” “农村” often refers broadly to rural areas, especially in contrast with cities. “乡村” has a more literary and scenic feeling, while “村庄” refers to one specific village.

Word: “旁边” / Context: “住的地方旁边有一个墓地” / Alternatives: “附近” or “紧挨着.” “旁边” is a basic word for “next to.” “附近” means “nearby” but not necessarily directly next to it. “紧挨着” emphasizes that the two places are extremely close.

Word: “老是” / Context: “墓地里老是有很多人来祭拜” / Alternatives: “经常” or “时常.” “老是” often expresses annoyance or complaint, which works well here because the visitors disturb the family. “经常” is more neutral, while “时常” is slightly more written and literary.

Word: “受不了了” / Context: “住了才一个星期就受不了了” / Alternatives: “难以忍受” or “无法忍受.” “受不了了” is natural spoken Chinese and strongly expresses frustration. “难以忍受” is more formal and means “hard to tolerate,” while “无法忍受” is even stronger: “completely unbearable.”

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You created a clear story sequence and gave a convincing reason why Mengzi and his mother could not continue living there.

Next time, focus on introducing new people and places clearly, and make sure every cause-and-effect sentence has a specific subject.

## Scene 2

> 孟子的妈妈觉得孟子取得良好的教育是很重要的，所以孟子的妈妈认为这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好，就打算跟孟子搬家。孟子的妈妈带孟子不是搬到城市，而是搬到了孟子老师的家。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “孟子取得良好的教育是很重要的” / Formula: Subject + 接受/获得 + 教育 / Correct: “孟子接受良好的教育是很重要的。”  
“取得” is usually used for achievements or results, such as “取得成功” or “取得好成绩.” For education, “接受教育” is the most natural expression.

Incorrect phrase: “这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好” / Formula: Place/Environment + 对 + someone’s + education/growth + 不好；or 对……不利 / Correct: “这样的地方不利于儿子的教育。”  
You can also say: “这样的环境对儿子的教育不太好。” “环境” is more natural than “地方” when discussing a child’s education.

Incorrect phrase: “就打算跟孟子搬家” / Formula: Subject + 打算 + 带 + person + 搬家 / Correct: “就打算带孟子搬家。”  
“跟孟子搬家” can mean “move house together with Mencius,” but “带孟子搬家” clearly shows that his mother is taking him to a new home.

Incorrect phrase: “孟子的妈妈带孟子不是搬到城市，而是搬到了孟子老师的家” / Formula: Subject + 不是 + 搬到 + Place A + 而是 + 搬到 + Place B / Correct: “孟子的妈妈没有带孟子搬到城市，而是带他搬到了老师家里。”  
Use “不是……而是……” to contrast the two destinations clearly. “老师家里” is more natural than “孟子老师的家.”

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “良好的教育” / Context: “孟子取得良好的教育” / Alternatives: “优质的教育” or “良好的教育环境.”  
“良好” means generally good and is neutral. “优质” emphasizes high quality, while “良好的教育环境” focuses on the surroundings and conditions that support learning.

Word: “这样的地方” / Context: “这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好” / Alternatives: “这样的环境” or “这样的居住环境.”  
“地方” simply means a place or location. “环境” is more appropriate when discussing the influence of a neighborhood, community, or home setting on a child.

Word: “不太好” / Context: “对儿子的教育不太好” / Alternatives: “不利” or “不合适.”  
“不太好” is simple and somewhat vague. “不利于教育” means it has a negative effect on education, while “不适合孩子成长” means it is not suitable for a child’s development.

Word: “认为” / Context: “孟子的妈妈认为……” / Alternatives: “意识到” or “觉得.”  
“认为” means “to believe” or “to think,” and sounds neutral. “意识到” emphasizes that she came to realize an important fact, while “觉得” is more conversational and personal.

Word: “搬到” / Context: “搬到了孟子老师的家” / Alternatives: “迁居到” or “搬去.”  
“搬到” is the most common everyday word for moving to a place. “迁居到” is more formal and literary, while “搬去” is natural when the destination is already clear from the context.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You expressed the cause-and-effect relationship clearly: Mencius’s mother cared about his education, so she decided to move.  
Next time, focus on using more natural collocations such as “接受教育,” “教育环境,” and “不利于…….”

## Scene 3

> 孟子跟他的老师从早到晚学习，难怪他的成绩很快就进步了很多。孟子学习的时候，孟子的妈妈不是待在家里就是编织衣服赚钱。以后孟子成为了中国有名的哲学家。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “孟子跟他的老师从早到晚学习” / Formula: Subject + 跟 + person + 一起 + verb; or Subject + 在 + place + verb / Correct: “孟子从早到晚跟老师一起学习。”  
“跟老师学习” can mean “learn from the teacher,” but “跟老师一起学习” clearly means that Mencius and his teacher studied together.

Incorrect phrase: “孟子学习的时候，孟子的妈妈不是待在家里就是编织衣服赚钱。” / Formula: 不是 + V1 + 就是 + V2; the two alternatives should be parallel and logically contrasting / Correct: “孟子学习的时候，孟子的妈妈不是在家做家务，就是编织衣服赚钱。”  
“待在家里” is a state, while “编织衣服赚钱” is an action, so they are not fully parallel. Also, being at home does not contrast naturally with weaving clothes, since she could weave at home.

Incorrect phrase: “以后孟子成为了中国有名的哲学家。” / Formula: In a narrative, 后来 + subject + verb; 以后 is usually used after a time expression, such as “长大以后” / Correct: “后来，孟子成为了中国有名的哲学家。”  
You could also say: “长大以后，孟子成为了中国著名的哲学家。”

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “进步了很多” / Context: “他的成绩很快就进步了很多” / Alternatives: “提高了很多” or “显著提高”  
“进步” is natural, but it more often describes a person’s ability or general performance. “提高” is especially natural for grades: “成绩提高了很多.” “显著提高” is more formal and emphasizes a clear, noticeable improvement.

Word: “待在家里” / Context: “妈妈不是待在家里就是……” / Alternatives: “做家务” or “操持家务”  
“待在家里” simply means “stay at home” and does not explain what she is doing. “做家务” is common and neutral. “操持家务” is more literary and emphasizes taking care of household responsibilities.

Word: “编织衣服” / Context: “编织衣服赚钱” / Alternatives: “织布” or “做衣服”  
“编织衣服” is understandable, but it is less idiomatic because people generally weave cloth or knit sweaters, then make clothes. “织布” means weaving fabric, while “做衣服” means making clothes and is broader.

Word: “有名的” / Context: “中国有名的哲学家” / Alternatives: “著名的” or “杰出的”  
“有名的” is common and beginner-friendly, meaning well-known. “著名的” is more formal and natural in historical writing. “杰出的” means outstanding, emphasizing his achievements and excellence, not only his fame.

Word: “难怪” / Context: “难怪他的成绩很快就进步了很多” / Alternatives: “因此” or “所以”  
“难怪” means “no wonder” and shows the speaker’s realization after seeing a result. “因此” is more formal and shows a direct cause-and-effect relationship; it fits well in a short historical description. “所以” is more conversational and also clearly marks the result.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You connected Mencius’s hard work, his mother’s life, and his later success in a clear and logical three-sentence story.  
Next time, focus on choosing narrative time words carefully, especially the difference between “后来” and “以后.”
