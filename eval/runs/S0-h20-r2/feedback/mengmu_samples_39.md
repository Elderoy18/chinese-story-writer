## Scene 1

> 古代的时候人觉得祭拜非常重要，孟子很小的时候，从早到晚一直祭拜，因为他妈妈告诉他祭拜很重要。虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜。他的同学们老是对他开玩笑了。孟子妈妈看了他的同学们对孟子不好的影响，所以孟子和孟子妈妈搬家了。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “古代的时候人觉得祭拜非常重要” / Formula: 在 + historical time, + 人们 + 认为/觉得 + statement / Correct: “在古代，人们认为祭拜非常重要。”

Incorrect phrase: “孟子很小的时候” (first mention of 孟子) / Formula: First mention = 有一个 + identity + 叫 + name; later use the name / Correct: “有一个男孩叫孟子。孟子小时候，……”  
This introduces the character clearly before using his name.

Incorrect phrase: “虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜” / Formula: 因为 + reason， 所以 + result / Correct: “因为他每天只祭拜，所以他没有朋友。”  
“虽然” needs a contrasting result, usually with “但是/可是,” so it does not fit this sentence.

Incorrect phrase: “他的同学们老是对他开玩笑了” / Formula: Subject + 老是 + verb phrase (for repeated negative actions); no 了 for a habitual action / Correct: “他的同学们老是跟他开玩笑。”  
Here, “老是” is appropriate because the classmates’ behavior is negative and repeated.

Incorrect phrase: “孟子妈妈看了他的同学们对孟子不好的影响” / Formula: Subject + 看到 + someone/something + 带来的 + 影响 / Correct: “孟子的妈妈看到了同学们给孟子带来的不良影响。”  
“看了影响” is not natural; she “saw/noticed the negative influence” that the classmates had on him.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “觉得” / Context: “人觉得祭拜非常重要” / Alternatives: “认为” is more formal and natural for stating a general belief held by many people. “觉得” is more conversational and often expresses a personal feeling or opinion.

Word: “祭拜” / Context: “从早到晚一直祭拜” / Alternatives: “祭祀” is more formal and historical, especially for worshipping ancestors or gods in an ancient setting. “祭拜” is still correct, but “祭祀” better matches the historical tone.

Word: “很小的时候” / Context: “孟子很小的时候” / Alternatives: “小时候” is shorter and more natural. “年幼时” is more literary and emphasizes that he was very young.

Word: “对他开玩笑” / Context: “他的同学们老是对他开玩笑” / Alternatives: “取笑他” means to make fun of him and clearly suggests unkind behavior. “嘲笑他” is even stronger and means to ridicule him. “开玩笑” can sometimes be friendly, so it is less clear here.

Word: “不好的影响” / Context: “同学们对孟子不好的影响” / Alternatives: “不良影响” is a formal, concise expression for a harmful effect. “负面影响” is also natural and emphasizes that the effect was negative.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You created a clear cause-and-effect story: Mengzi’s daily behavior leads to his classmates’ reactions and then to the family’s move.  
Next time, focus on introducing characters clearly and using complete linking patterns such as “因为……所以……”.

## Scene 2

> 孟子才十一岁，成为一个哲学家。孟子不是祭拜，而是看政治文章，难怪他成为了哲学家。不过，看过很多政治文章对他害得，他有很多问题。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “孟子才十一岁，成为一个哲学家。” / Formula: 谁 + 才 + 年龄 + 就 + 动词 + 了 / Correct: “孟子才十一岁就成为了一位哲学家。”  
“才……就……” emphasizes that he became a philosopher at a very young age.

Incorrect phrase: “孟子不是祭拜，而是看政治文章” / Formula: 谁 + 不是 + 动作A +，而是 + 动作B / Correct: “孟子不是在祭拜，而是在看政治文章。”  
Use 在 with ongoing actions here. Also, “祭拜” is usually something one does *to* an ancestor, god, or grave, so the meaning should be clear from the context.

Incorrect phrase: “难怪他成为了哲学家。” / Formula: 难怪 + result / Correct: “难怪他后来成为了一位哲学家。”  
Adding “后来” makes the timeline more coherent: first he read political articles, and later he became a philosopher.

Incorrect phrase: “看过很多政治文章对他害得” / Formula: 做某事 + 对 + 某人 + 有害 / Correct: “看了很多政治文章对他有害。”  
“害得” needs a result after it, such as “害得他有很多问题.” However, the intended meaning seems to be that reading gave him many questions, which is not necessarily harmful.

Incorrect phrase: “他有很多问题。” / Formula: 因为/所以 + 谁 + 对 + 什么 + 有很多问题 / Correct: “所以他对政治有很多问题。”  
“有很多问题” can mean “has many problems.” To say that he has many questions about a topic, use “对……有很多问题.”

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “政治文章” / Context: “看政治文章” / Alternatives: “政治方面的文章” is more natural for articles about politics in general. “政论文章” is more advanced and refers specifically to opinion or commentary writing about political or public affairs.

Word: “哲学家” / Context: “成为一个哲学家” / Alternatives: “思想家” is especially suitable for Mencius, because it emphasizes his influential ideas and theories. “哲学家” is still correct, but “思想家” is a broader and more culturally natural description of him.

Word: “看” / Context: “看政治文章” / Alternatives: “阅读” is more formal and fits written articles well. “研读” means to study a text carefully and deeply, which better shows that Mencius was seriously engaging with difficult ideas.

Word: “问题” / Context: “他对政治有很多问题” / Alternatives: “疑问” specifically means questions or uncertainties, so it is clearer here. “思考” emphasizes the intellectual reflection that reading inspired, and it gives the sentence a more positive tone.

Word: “害” / Context: “对他有害” / Alternatives: “影响” is neutral and means “influence.” “启发” is positive and means “inspire” or “enlighten.” Since reading political articles likely helped Mencius think deeply, “给了他很多启发” is probably the best choice.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You used “不是……而是……” correctly to contrast two actions, and your ideas have a clear connection between reading and becoming a philosopher.  
Next time, focus on using “才……就……” together and choosing words that show whether an effect is negative (“有害”) or positive (“启发”).

## Scene 3

> 他一直问问题，但是不能解决那些问题。孟子长大，他开始能解决问题。他常常建议你不是学金融，而是学政治，因为如果你懂政治，你会成为高薪。后来孟子成为教授，他教学生很厉害。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “孟子长大，他开始能解决问题。”  
Formula: Subject + 长大以后/后来 + 就/开始 + 能 + Verb + Object  
Correct: “孟子长大以后，就开始能解决这些问题了。”  
“长大” needs a time connector such as “以后,” and “这些问题” clearly refers back to the earlier problems.

Incorrect phrase: “他常常建议你不是学金融，而是学政治。”  
Formula: Subject + 建议 + Person + 不要/别 + Verb A，(而) + Verb B / 建议 + Person + Verb B  
Correct: “他常常建议你不要学金融，而要学政治。”  
After “建议你,” use “不要” to advise against an action. “不是……而是……” can contrast two nouns or statements, but it is less natural here without “不要.”

Incorrect phrase: “因为如果你懂政治”  
Formula: 如果……，就…… / 因为……，所以……  
Correct: “因为他认为，如果你懂政治，你就会……”  
Do not usually combine “因为” and “如果” directly without completing both relationships. Adding “他认为” also makes the logic clearer.

Incorrect phrase: “你会成为高薪。”  
Formula: Subject + 成为 + Noun/identity; Subject + 获得/有 + 高薪  
Correct: “你会获得高薪。” / “你会成为高薪人才。”  
“高薪” is a salary, not a person or identity, so it cannot directly follow “成为.”

Incorrect phrase: “后来孟子成为教授，他教学生很厉害。”  
Formula: 后来，Subject + 成为 + Profession，(并且/而且) + 很会/擅长 + Verb  
Correct: “后来，孟子成为了一名教授，而且很会教学生。”  
Adding “一名” makes “教授” sound more natural as a profession, and “很会教学生” is a more natural way to say he teaches well.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “问问题”  
Context: “他一直问问题。”  
Alternatives: “提出问题” is more formal and is often used when someone raises thoughtful questions in class or discussion. “不断提问” emphasizes that he keeps asking questions repeatedly. “问问题” is correct but more basic and conversational.

Word: “解决问题”  
Context: “不能解决那些问题。”  
Alternatives: “解答这些问题” is especially suitable for answering questions, while “解决问题” is broader and can include practical difficulties. “攻克难题” is more advanced and emphasizes overcoming particularly difficult problems.

Word: “建议”  
Context: “他常常建议你……”  
Alternatives: “劝你” is more personal and conversational, often used when encouraging someone to make a choice. “主张” is more formal and means to advocate for an idea or position, so it fits well with political or academic discussions.

Word: “懂政治”  
Context: “如果你懂政治……”  
Alternatives: “了解政治” means to have general knowledge of politics and is more natural for beginners. “精通政治” means to have deep, expert-level knowledge, so it is much stronger than “懂政治.”

Word: “教学生很厉害”  
Context: “他教学生很厉害。”  
Alternatives: “教学能力很强” is formal and emphasizes professional teaching ability. “很擅长教书” is natural and means he is skilled at teaching. “很厉害” is common in speech, but it is vague and less precise.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You clearly show Mengzi’s development from someone who asks questions to a successful professor, which gives your short paragraph a strong, logical storyline.  
Next time, focus on using connectors such as “长大以后,” “如果……就……,” and “后来” to make the relationships between ideas even clearer.
