## Scene 1

> 古代，孟子跟孟母住在墓地的旁边。可是孟母注意，住在墓地的旁边影响孟子。他常常跟朋友们祭拜玩，所以孟母决定他们会搬家。这次，他们住在城市里的商店的旁边，从早到晚很吵。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “古代” / Formula: 在 + time period / Correct: “在古代，孟子跟孟母住在墓地旁边。”  
“在古代” sounds more complete and natural when introducing the time of a story.

Incorrect phrase: “孟母注意，住在墓地的旁边影响孟子” / Formula: Subject + 注意到/发现 + clause; Subject + 受 + place/environment + 影响 / Correct: “孟母注意到，住在墓地旁边会影响孟子。”  
“注意” normally needs an object or “到.” Adding “会” makes the cause-and-effect meaning clearer.

Incorrect phrase: “他常常跟朋友们祭拜玩” / Formula: 跟 + person + 一起 + V; 玩 + object / Correct: “他常常跟朋友们一起玩祭拜的游戏。”  
“祭拜” is an action, so it cannot directly be followed by “玩.” If you mean Mencius copied funeral rituals as a game, “玩祭拜的游戏” is clear.

Incorrect phrase: “所以孟母决定他们会搬家” / Formula: Subject + 决定 + verb phrase / Correct: “所以孟母决定搬家。”  
After “决定,” use the action directly. “会” is unnecessary here.

Incorrect phrase: “这次，他们住在城市里的商店的旁边” / Formula: 在 + place + 住; place + 旁边 / Correct: “这次，他们搬到城市里，住在商店旁边。”  
Use “搬到” for moving to a new place. “城市里的商店的旁边” has too many 的’s; “商店旁边” is more natural.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “可是” / Context: “可是孟母注意到……” / Alternatives: “但是” is a neutral and common contrast word. “不过” is slightly softer and is especially natural in conversational storytelling. “可是” is also correct, but it can sound a little stronger and more dramatic.

Word: “影响” / Context: “住在墓地旁边会影响孟子” / Alternatives: “不利于孟子的成长” means “not beneficial to Mencius’s development” and is more specific and formal. “给孟子带来不好的影响” emphasizes the negative result more clearly than the neutral word “影响.”

Word: “祭拜” / Context: “玩祭拜的游戏” / Alternatives: “模仿大人祭拜” means “imitate adults performing worship/rites” and describes the scene more vividly. “模仿办丧事” is even more specific if you mean that he copied funeral ceremonies near the cemetery.

Word: “吵” / Context: “从早到晚很吵” / Alternatives: “嘈杂” describes a noisy, busy environment and is more advanced than “吵.” “喧闹” emphasizes that the place is lively and full of noise, often because many people are talking or moving around.

Word: “从早到晚” / Context: “从早到晚很吵” / Alternatives: “整天” is concise and natural: “商店旁边整天都很嘈杂。” “一天到晚” stresses that something happens continuously and can suggest annoyance, which fits the noisy setting well.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You told the story in a clear chronological order and used the important contrast between the quiet cemetery area and the noisy marketplace well.  
Next time, focus on using “注意到 + clause,” “决定 + verb phrase,” and “搬到 + place” accurately.

## Scene 2

> 城市也有点儿危险。害得孟母只好一直陪孟子。孟子也没有朋友，难怪他老是看起来不高兴。住在商店的旁边不是适合孟子，而是让他觉得很伤心。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “害得孟母只好一直陪孟子” / Formula: 害得 + someone + clearly harmful result / Correct: “这害得孟母不得不一直陪着孟子。”  
“不得不” makes the negative consequence clearer, and “陪着” emphasizes an ongoing action.

Incorrect phrase: “住在商店的旁边不是适合孟子” / Formula: Place/Action + 不适合 + someone + 做某事 / Correct: “住在商店旁边不适合孟子成长。”  
“适合” needs a person or purpose after it; here, “孟子成长” clearly expresses what is unsuitable.

Incorrect phrase: “不是适合孟子，而是让他觉得很伤心” / Formula: 虽然/不但……，但是/而且……; or X 不仅……，还…… / Correct: “住在商店旁边不仅不适合孟子成长，还让他感到很伤心。”  
“不是……而是……” is used to contrast two alternatives, but here you are adding two negative effects, so “不仅……还……” is more coherent.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “危险” / Context: “城市也有点儿危险。” / Alternatives: “复杂” or “不安全.” “危险” means there is direct physical danger. “不安全” is more natural when describing a neighborhood where a child may face risks, while “复杂” can describe a chaotic social environment.

Word: “一直陪孟子” / Context: “孟母只好一直陪孟子。” / Alternatives: “寸步不离地照顾孟子” or “时刻陪伴孟子.” “一直陪” is simple and understandable. “寸步不离” emphasizes that Meng’s mother cannot leave him even briefly, while “时刻陪伴” is more neutral and polished.

Word: “没有朋友” / Context: “孟子也没有朋友。” / Alternatives: “交不到朋友” or “缺少玩伴.” “没有朋友” states the situation. “交不到朋友” emphasizes that he is unable to make friends, while “缺少玩伴” is especially suitable for a child who has nobody to play with.

Word: “看起来不高兴” / Context: “难怪他老是看起来不高兴。” / Alternatives: “闷闷不乐” or “郁郁寡欢.” “看起来不高兴” is a basic description based on appearance. “闷闷不乐” means quietly unhappy or depressed, and “郁郁寡欢” is more literary and describes long-term unhappiness.

Word: “很伤心” / Context: “让他觉得很伤心。” / Alternatives: “感到孤独” or “感到失落.” “伤心” usually suggests sadness caused by a particular upsetting event. “孤独” better fits the situation of having no friends, while “失落” suggests disappointment and emotional emptiness.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You clearly explain the connection between the unsafe environment, Mengzi’s lack of friends, and his unhappiness, so your ideas flow logically.  
Next time, focus on using result structures such as “害得” precisely and linking related negative effects with “不仅……还……”.

## Scene 3

> 所以，孟母和孟子搬到学校的旁边住。才五岁孟子开始学哲学。在学校，孟子不是看书，就是练习写字。将来，他成为中国的一个非常有名的哲学家。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “搬到学校的旁边住” / Formula: 搬到 + place + （去）住; for a general location, “学校附近” is more natural / Correct: “孟母和孟子搬到学校附近住。”

Incorrect phrase: “才五岁孟子开始学哲学” / Formula: Subject + 才 + number/age + 就 + verb / Correct: “孟子才五岁就开始学习哲学。”

Incorrect phrase: “将来，他成为中国的一个非常有名的哲学家” / Formula: In a past narrative, use 后来 + subject + 成为/成为了 + noun / Correct: “后来，他成为了中国一位非常有名的哲学家。”

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “学校的旁边” / Context: “搬到学校的旁边住” / Alternatives: “学校附近” is more natural for “near a school” in everyday Chinese. “学校旁边” means directly next to the school and is more specific.

Word: “学哲学” / Context: “开始学哲学” / Alternatives: “学习哲学” is slightly more formal and fits a written historical story better. “学哲学” is common and natural in conversation.

Word: “看书” / Context: “孟子不是看书，就是练习写字” / Alternatives: “读书” specifically emphasizes reading and studying books, so it is especially suitable for a student’s learning routine. “看书” is broader and can also mean casually looking at a book.

Word: “练习写字” / Context: “就是练习写字” / Alternatives: “练字” is a concise, natural expression meaning to practise handwriting. “练习写字” is also correct, but it is a little longer.

Word: “非常有名” / Context: “一个非常有名的哲学家” / Alternatives: “著名” is more concise and formal, meaning well-known and respected. “伟大” means great or highly influential, so it is stronger than “有名” and fits an important historical philosopher.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You used “不是……就是……” very well to describe Mengzi’s focused daily study activities.  
Next time, focus on choosing narrative time words carefully: use “后来” for later events in a past story, rather than “将来.”
