## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有很多人不知道什么植物能吃，什么植物不能吃。有很多人吃植物以后就吃坏肚子了，因为他们吃了毒。那个时候，有一个老人叫神农，他什么植物都觉得好吃，不怕生病，不怕毒。他希望别人也可以吃好吃的植物。可是他知道，如果他们还怕吃毒，不会吃他喜欢的植物。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “古代的时候” / Formula: 在 + historical time period / Correct: “在古代”

Incorrect phrase: “吃植物以后就吃坏肚子了” / Formula: 谁 + 吃了……以后 + 吃坏了 + 身体部位 / Correct: “吃了植物以后就吃坏了肚子。”  
“吃坏肚子” is understandable, but “吃坏了肚子” is the more natural word order.

Incorrect phrase: “因为他们吃了毒” / Formula: 谁 + 吃了 + 有毒的 + 名词 / Correct: “因为他们吃了有毒的植物。”  
Since the passage is about plants, this is clearer than simply saying “毒.”

Incorrect phrase: “他什么植物都觉得好吃” / Formula: 谁 + 觉得 + 什么 + 名词 + 都 + 形容词 / Correct: “他觉得什么植物都好吃。”

Incorrect phrase: “不怕毒” / Formula: 谁 + 不怕 + 中毒 / 有毒的东西 / Correct: “不怕中毒，也不怕吃有毒的植物。”  
“不怕毒” is understandable, but it is less natural because “毒” is a noun; “中毒” describes the harmful result.

Incorrect phrase: “如果他们还怕吃毒，不会吃他喜欢的植物” / Formula: 如果 + condition，subject + 就 + result / Correct: “可是他知道，如果他们还怕吃有毒的植物，就不会吃他喜欢的植物。”  
Add “就” to make the conditional sentence complete and clear.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “吃坏了肚子” / Context: “有很多人吃植物以后就吃坏了肚子。” / Alternatives: “肚子疼” is a simple, common expression for having a stomachache. “食物中毒” is more precise and formal; it means becoming ill specifically because of contaminated or poisonous food.

Word: “毒” / Context: “因为他们吃了毒。” / Alternatives: “有毒的植物” clearly means “poisonous plants.” “毒性” is more formal and refers to the poisonous properties of something, for example: “这种植物有毒性。”

Word: “好吃” / Context: “他觉得什么植物都好吃。” / Alternatives: “美味” is more literary and positive, often used for food that tastes especially good. “可口” means tasty and pleasant to eat, and is slightly more formal than “好吃.”

Word: “希望” / Context: “他希望别人也可以吃好吃的植物。” / Alternatives: “想让” is more direct and natural when you mean “want someone to do something”: “他想让别人也吃美味的植物。” “盼望” is stronger and more emotional than “希望.”

Word: “可是” / Context: “可是他知道……” / Alternatives: “但是” is a neutral and very common contrast word. “然而” is more formal and is often used in written storytelling. Here, “但是他知道……” would sound especially smooth.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You did a good job creating a clear story situation: people do not know which plants are safe, and Shennong wants to help them.  
Next time, focus on word order with “觉得” and on completing conditional sentences with “如果……就……”.

## Scene 2

> 所以，他上山下山找植物，吃了很多样子的植物。他是第一人，找到了茶。他把所有的植物写下来了。他一直都吃了，他连毒都能吃。可是他还没吃了一个叫夹竹桃的植物。他找到了那个像星星的植物，觉得很漂亮，马上就吃下了。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “他上山下山找植物” / Formula: Subject + 上山下山 + 去 + Verb / Correct: “他上山下山去找植物。”

Incorrect phrase: “吃了很多样子的植物” / Formula: 很多种 + Noun / Correct: “他吃了很多种植物。”

Incorrect phrase: “他是第一人，找到了茶” / Formula: Subject + 是 + 第一个 + Verb + Object + 的人 / Correct: “他是第一个发现茶的人。”

Incorrect phrase: “他一直都吃了” / Formula: 一直 + Verb (for an ongoing/habitual action; do not use 了) / Correct: “他一直都在尝试各种植物。”  
“吃了” describes a completed action, while “一直” describes a continuing action.

Incorrect phrase: “他连毒都能吃” / Formula: 连 + 有毒的 + Noun + 都 + Verb / Correct: “他连有毒的植物都敢吃。”  
“毒” alone is too vague here; the sentence means that he ate poisonous plants.

Incorrect phrase: “他还没吃了一个叫夹竹桃的植物” / Formula: 还没 + Verb + 过 + Object; 一种叫……的植物 / Correct: “他还没吃过一种叫夹竹桃的植物。”

Incorrect phrase: “那个像星星的植物” / Formula: 像……一样的 + Noun / Correct: “那种像星星一样的植物。”


**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “找” / Context: “上山下山去找植物” / Alternatives: “寻找” is more formal and literary than “找,” and it works especially well in a story. “采集” means to collect plants, usually for research, medicine, or use. You could write: “他上山下山寻找植物。”

Word: “很多样子的植物” / Context: “吃了很多样子的植物” / Alternatives: “很多种植物” is the natural way to say “many kinds of plants.” “各种植物” emphasizes a wide variety of different plants. In this story, “他尝试了各种植物” sounds especially natural.

Word: “吃” / Context: “他一直都在尝试各种植物” / Alternatives: “尝试” emphasizes trying something to learn about it, which fits Shennong’s purpose. “品尝” means to taste carefully, often with food or tea, so it sounds less suitable for dangerous plants. “尝试各种植物” is best here.

Word: “写下来” / Context: “他把所有的植物写下来了” / Alternatives: “记录下来” means to make a record and sounds more systematic and scientific. “记载下来” is more formal and is often used for historical records. You could write: “他把各种植物的特点都记录下来了。”

Word: “漂亮” / Context: “觉得很漂亮” / Alternatives: “美丽” is slightly more literary and neutral. “鲜艳” describes bright, striking colors; it can suggest that a flower looks attractive but may be dangerous, so it fits 夹竹桃 well. For example: “他看到夹竹桃的花很鲜艳。”

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You created a clear sequence of events, and the detail about the star-shaped plant makes the story vivid.  
Next time, focus on choosing between **了** for completed actions and **一直/还没** for ongoing or unfinished actions.

## Scene 3

> 可是夹竹桃的毒很重，让他肚子很痛。他在地上躺着，觉得他快会死了。可是正好有别人找到他了，给了他一个像伞的植物，叫灵芝。他吃灵芝以后就觉得很好，不再生病了。他测量起来了，像星星的植物是毒，像伞的植物是药。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “觉得他快会死了” / Formula: Subject + 觉得 + 自己/他人 + 快要/快 + verb / Correct: “觉得自己快要死了。”  
“他” is ambiguous here because it could refer to Shennong or another person; use “自己” to clearly refer back to the subject.

Incorrect phrase: “可是正好有别人找到他了” / Formula: Someone specific + 正好 + 找到 + person / Correct: “可是正好有人找到了他。”  
“别人” is vague in this context, while “有人” naturally introduces an unspecified person. Also, “找到了他” is the more standard word order.

Incorrect phrase: “给了他一个像伞的植物” / Formula: 给 + person + 一种 + description + 的 + noun / Correct: “给了他一种像伞的植物。”  
Use “一种” for an unspecified type of plant. “一个植物” is less natural because 植物 is usually classified with 种.

Incorrect phrase: “他吃灵芝以后就觉得很好” / Formula: Subject + 吃了 + noun + 以后 + 就 + 觉得 + 身体/状态 + 很好 / Correct: “他吃了灵芝以后，就觉得身体好多了。”  
“觉得很好” is understandable, but it is vague. “身体好多了” clearly describes his recovery.

Incorrect phrase: “他测量起来了” / Formula: Subject + 总结/发现 + information / Correct: “他总结出来了” or “他发现了。”  
“测量” means “to measure,” such as measuring length or weight. Here, Shennong is drawing a conclusion from what he experienced, so 总结 or 发现 is appropriate.

Incorrect phrase: “像星星的植物是毒，像伞的植物是药” / Formula: A + 的植物 + 有毒；B + 的植物 + 是药材/可以治病 / Correct: “他总结出来，像星星的植物有毒，像伞的植物是药材。”  
Use 有毒 rather than 是毒. Also, 药材 is more precise for a medicinal plant.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “毒很重” / Context: “夹竹桃的毒很重” / Alternatives: “毒性很强” or “毒性很大.” “毒很重” is understandable but less idiomatic. “毒性很强” is a natural, neutral way to say that a poison is powerful.

Word: “肚子很痛” / Context: “让他肚子很痛” / Alternatives: “肚子剧痛” or “腹痛难忍.” “肚子很痛” is simple and clear. “剧痛” emphasizes severe, sudden pain, while “腹痛难忍” is more literary and means the abdominal pain is almost unbearable.

Word: “在地上躺着” / Context: “他在地上躺着” / Alternatives: “倒在地上” or “躺倒在地上.” “躺着” only describes his position. “倒在地上” suggests that he collapsed because he was weak or in pain, which fits the scene better.

Word: “像伞的植物” / Context: “一个像伞的植物” / Alternatives: “长得像伞的灵芝” or “伞状的灵芝.” “像伞的植物” is understandable, but “长得像伞的” sounds more natural in a story. “伞状的” is more concise and descriptive.

Word: “是药” / Context: “像伞的植物是药” / Alternatives: “是药材” or “有药用价值.” “是药” is basic and can refer to medicine in general. “药材” specifically refers to natural materials used as medicine, while “有药用价值” means it has medicinal value and is more formal.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You clearly described the cause, Shennong’s painful reaction, and the healing effect of lingzhi in a logical sequence.  
Next time, focus on using more precise subjects and verbs, especially “自己,” “有人,” and “总结/发现.”
