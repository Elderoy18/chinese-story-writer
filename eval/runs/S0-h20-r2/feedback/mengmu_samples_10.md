## Scene 1

> 孟子小的时候，从早到晚都跟朋友玩。孟子和他的朋友喜欢玩在墓地里，装祭拜。孟子五岁的时候，跟妈妈搬家。他们搬家不是因为孟子玩得太多，而是因为妈妈认为他们的家离墓地太近。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “孟子和他的朋友喜欢玩在墓地里”  
Formula: Subject + 喜欢 + 在 + Place + Verb  
Correct: “孟子和他的朋友喜欢在墓地里玩。”

Incorrect phrase: “装祭拜”  
Formula: Subject + 装作 + 在 + Verb / Subject + 模仿 + Verb phrase  
Correct: “装作在祭拜。”  
Full sentence: “孟子和他的朋友喜欢在墓地里玩，装作在祭拜。”  
“装” alone usually means “to pack” or “to install”; use “装作” when pretending to do something.

Incorrect phrase: “孟子五岁的时候，跟妈妈搬家。”  
Formula: Time + Subject + 跟 + Person + Verb + 了 + Object  
Correct: “孟子五岁的时候，跟妈妈搬了家。”  
You can also emphasize how young he was: “孟子才五岁就跟妈妈搬了家。” (才 + age + 就 + action)

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “小的时候”  
Context: “孟子小的时候”  
Alternatives: “年幼的时候” or “年幼时.” “小的时候” is very common and natural in conversation. “年幼时” is more formal and literary, so it works well in a historical story.

Word: “从早到晚”  
Context: “从早到晚都跟朋友玩”  
Alternatives: “整天” or “一天到晚.” “从早到晚” emphasizes the time range from morning until evening. “整天” is shorter and natural, while “一天到晚” can suggest that someone does something excessively.

Word: “玩”  
Context: “都跟朋友玩”  
Alternatives: “玩耍” or “嬉戏.” “玩” is simple and everyday. “玩耍” is slightly more written and suitable for children’s activities; “嬉戏” is more literary and creates a lively, playful image.

Word: “装作”  
Context: “装作在祭拜”  
Alternatives: “模仿.” “装作” means pretending to be something or pretending to do something. “模仿祭拜的动作” is especially natural here because the children are copying what adults do at the cemetery.

Word: “太近”  
Context: “他们的家离墓地太近”  
Alternatives: “离墓地很近” or “紧挨着墓地.” “太近” emphasizes that the distance is unacceptably close. “紧挨着” is stronger and means “right next to,” creating a more vivid description.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

Your story has a clear cause-and-effect flow: Mengzi’s behavior at the cemetery helps explain why his mother decides to move.  
Next time, focus on word order with place phrases, especially using “在 + place + verb.”

## Scene 2

> 妈妈认为，住离墓地太近，害得孟子的心气。孟子和孟母搬到子思老师的家，跟子思老师住。孟母帮子思打扫房间和做饭。子思帮孟母教她的儿子。因为孟子现在没有朋友，他老是读书。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “住离墓地太近，害得孟子的心气。” / Formula: 住得 + 离 + 地点 + 太近，+ 会/让/使 + 人 + 失去/受到影响 + 心气 / Correct: “妈妈认为，住得离墓地太近，会影响孟子的心气。”  
“住” needs **得** before a complement such as “离墓地太近.” Also, “害得孟子的心气” is incomplete; add a verb describing what happens to his motivation.

Incorrect phrase: “子思帮孟母教她的儿子。” / Formula: A + 帮 + B + 教 + 人 / Correct: “子思帮孟母教孟子。”  
“她的儿子” can be understood, but it is clearer to name “孟子” directly. “子思” helps Meng’s mother teach Mencius.

Incorrect phrase: “因为孟子现在没有朋友，他老是读书。” / Formula: 因为 + 原因，+ 所以 + 结果；一直 + 做积极/中性的事 / Correct: “因为孟子现在没有朋友，所以他一直读书。”  
“老是” often has a negative or complaining tone. Use “一直” for a neutral or positive habit such as studying.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “心气” / Context: “会影响孟子的心气。” / Alternatives: “学习的积极性” or “上进心.” “心气” can refer to one’s spirit, ambition, or emotional drive, but it is somewhat vague here. “学习的积极性” specifically means motivation to study, while “上进心” emphasizes the desire to improve oneself.

Word: “搬到子思老师的家” / Context: “孟子和孟母搬到子思老师的家。” / Alternatives: “搬去子思老师家里” or “投靠子思老师.” “搬到” is correct and neutral. “搬去……家里” sounds more conversational, while “投靠” suggests that they rely on Zisi for support or guidance.

Word: “跟子思老师住” / Context: “跟子思老师住。” / Alternatives: “与子思老师同住” or “住在子思老师家里.” “跟……住” is natural in speech. “同住” is more concise and written, while “住在……家里” makes the location especially clear.

Word: “打扫房间” / Context: “孟母帮子思打扫房间和做饭。” / Alternatives: “料理家务” or “操持家务.” “打扫房间和做饭” clearly lists two chores. “料理家务” means taking care of household tasks generally, while “操持家务” emphasizes working hard to manage the household.

Word: “读书” / Context: “他一直读书。” / Alternatives: “用功学习” or “勤奋读书.” “读书” can mean reading books or studying. “用功学习” clearly emphasizes studying hard, while “勤奋读书” has a more literary tone and highlights diligence.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You clearly described the family’s move and each person’s role, so the scene has a logical and easy-to-follow sequence.  
Next time, focus on using **得** after verbs for degree complements and choosing “一直” instead of “老是” for positive habits.

## Scene 3

> 子思认为，对孩子来说，读书最重要，特别是学哲学。孟子不是练习写文章，就是看哲学书。孟子从五岁一直学哲学。他才五岁就学哲学，难怪他成为很有名的哲学家。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “子思认为，对孩子来说，读书最重要，特别是学哲学。” / Formula: 对……来说，A 是最……的；“读书” and “学哲学” should be parallel actions / Correct: “子思认为，对孩子来说，学习最重要，特别是学习哲学。”

Incorrect phrase: “孟子不是练习写文章，就是看哲学书。” / Formula: Subject + 不是 + V. + Object，+ 就是 + V. + Object; this pattern means “either…or…” / Correct: “孟子不是练习写文章，就是看哲学书。” This sentence is grammatically correct, but it means he did one activity or the other. If you mean “not X but Y,” say: “孟子不是练习写文章，而是看哲学书。”

Incorrect phrase: “孟子从五岁一直学哲学。” / Formula: Subject + 从 + time + 起/开始 + 一直 + V. / Correct: “孟子从五岁开始一直学习哲学。”

Incorrect phrase: “他才五岁就学哲学。” / Formula: Subject + 才 + age/number + 就 + V. + Object / Correct: “他才五岁就开始学习哲学。”

Incorrect phrase: “难怪他成为很有名的哲学家。” / Formula: 成为 + adjective + noun; for a completed past change, use 了 after 成为 / Correct: “难怪他后来成为了一位很有名的哲学家。”

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “读书” / Context: “读书最重要” / Alternatives: “学习” or “接受教育.” “读书” can specifically mean reading books or studying in general. “学习” is broader and more precise here because it includes learning philosophy, writing, and other subjects. “接受教育” is more formal and emphasizes receiving an education.

Word: “练习写文章” / Context: “孟子不是练习写文章，就是看哲学书” / Alternatives: “练习写作” or “写文章.” “练习写文章” is understandable but slightly repetitive. “练习写作” sounds more natural and refers to practicing writing as a skill. “写文章” simply describes the activity of writing articles.

Word: “看哲学书” / Context: “就是看哲学书” / Alternatives: “阅读哲学著作” or “研读哲学书籍.” “看” is common and casual. “阅读” is more formal and appropriate for books. “研读” suggests careful, serious, in-depth study, so it fits philosophy especially well.

Word: “学哲学” / Context: “一直学哲学” / Alternatives: “学习哲学” or “钻研哲学.” “学哲学” is natural in conversation. “学习哲学” is slightly more formal and suitable for writing. “钻研哲学” emphasizes deeply investigating a difficult academic subject.

Word: “很有名” / Context: “很有名的哲学家” / Alternatives: “著名的” or “卓越的.” “很有名” means widely known and is common in speech. “著名的” is more concise and formal. “卓越的” means outstanding or exceptional; it praises the person’s ability and achievements, not just their fame.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You used the “才五岁就……” structure correctly to show that Mencius began studying philosophy at a very young age, and your ideas flow logically from education to his success.  
Next time, focus on choosing whether “不是……就是……” truly expresses two alternatives, or whether you mean “不是……而是……”.
