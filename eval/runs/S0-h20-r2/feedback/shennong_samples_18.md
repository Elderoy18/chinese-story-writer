## Scene 1

> 神农和他的家人住在古代，所以不知道什么植物能吃。因为他的家人常常生病了，所以神农和他的哥哥一起去旅行找可以吃的植物。他们去很多不一样的地方，比方说高山、山谷和沙漠。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “神农和他的家人住在古代”  
Formula: 在 + time, + character introduction (name + identity/background)  
Correct: “在古代，有一位叫神农的人。”  
“住在古代” is not natural because 古代 is a time period, not a place. Introducing 神农 clearly at his first mention also makes the story easier to follow.

Incorrect phrase: “因为他的家人常常生病了”  
Formula: Subject + 常常 + verb (no 了 for a habitual action)  
Correct: “因为神农的家人常常生病”  
“常常” describes a repeated habit or situation, so do not use 了. Using “神农的家人” is also clearer than “他的家人.”

Incorrect phrase: “他们去很多不一样的地方”  
Formula: Subject + 去了 + quantity + adjective + place  
Correct: “他们去了很多不同的地方”  
Use 了 because this sentence describes completed travel in the story. “不同的地方” is more natural than “不一样的地方” in this context.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “住在古代”  
Context: “神农和他的家人住在古代”  
Alternatives: “生活在古代” or “在古代”  
“住” focuses on living in a physical location, while “生活在古代” means living during an historical period. Simply beginning with “在古代” is the most natural way to set the time of a story.

Word: “不知道什么植物能吃”  
Context: “不知道什么植物能吃”  
Alternatives: “不知道哪些植物可以食用” or “不知道哪些植物能吃”  
“什么植物” is understandable, but “哪些植物” is more specific when talking about a group of plants. “可以食用” is more formal and precise than “能吃.”

Word: “去旅行”  
Context: “一起去旅行找可以吃的植物”  
Alternatives: “到各地寻找” or “四处寻找”  
“去旅行” emphasizes travelling for enjoyment or experience. “到各地寻找” emphasizes going to different places with a clear purpose, while “四处寻找” means searching everywhere.

Word: “找”  
Context: “找可以吃的植物”  
Alternatives: “寻找” or “探索”  
“找” is a common, everyday word. “寻找” is slightly more formal and fits a story well. “探索” emphasizes investigating or discovering something unknown.

Word: “不一样的地方”  
Context: “很多不一样的地方”  
Alternatives: “不同的地方” or “各地”  
“不一样” is conversational and often compares two specific things. “不同的” is more natural for describing many places, while “各地” means “various places” and is especially concise in narratives.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You did well using clear cause-and-effect structure with “因为……所以……” and giving vivid examples of the places they visited.  
Next time, focus on using “了” only for completed events, not for repeated actions such as “常常生病.”

## Scene 2

> 他们在很漂亮的河找夹竹桃。神农问他的哥哥：“我应该吃这种植物吗？”哥哥说：“这就要看你想要安全还是接近知道了。“神农一吃夹竹桃就生病了。哥哥说：“太麻烦了。

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “他们在很漂亮的河找夹竹桃。”  
Formula: Specific people + 在 + 一条/一个 + modifier + 的 + place + verb  
Correct: “神农和他的哥哥在一条很漂亮的河边找夹竹桃。”  
“他们” has no clear referent at the beginning, and we usually say “在河边” rather than “在河找” for an activity next to a river.

Incorrect phrase: “这就要看你想要安全还是接近知道了。”  
Formula: (Uncertain question) + 这就要看 + A-not-A / wh-question / A还是B + 了  
Correct: “这种植物能不能吃？这就要看它有没有毒了。”  
After “这就要看,” the following part needs to be a question form. “接近知道” is not a natural phrase; “有没有毒” directly answers whether Shennong should eat the plant.

Incorrect phrase: “哥哥说：‘太麻烦了。’”  
Formula: Person/situation + specific description or result  
Correct: “哥哥说：‘太糟糕了！你可能中毒了。’”  
“太麻烦了” is vague here: it usually means something is troublesome or inconvenient. Since Shennong becomes ill after eating oleander, “太糟糕了” or “你可能中毒了” is clearer.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “漂亮”  
Context: “很漂亮的河”  
Alternatives: “美丽的河边” or “风景优美的河边.” “漂亮” is common and often describes people or visible objects. “美丽” is more literary and natural for scenery, while “风景优美” emphasizes that the surrounding landscape is attractive.

Word: “找”  
Context: “找夹竹桃”  
Alternatives: “寻找夹竹桃” or “采摘夹竹桃.” “找” simply means “look for.” “寻找” is more formal and fits a story narration; “采摘” means to pick or gather plants, if they are taking the flowers rather than only looking for them.

Word: “应该”  
Context: “我应该吃这种植物吗？”  
Alternatives: “我能吃这种植物吗？” or “这种植物可以吃吗？” “应该” asks whether something is advisable. “能” asks whether it is possible or safe, and “可以” asks whether it is allowed or suitable; these are more natural in this situation.

Word: “安全”  
Context: “你想要安全……”  
Alternatives: “无毒” or “安全无害.” “安全” is a broad word meaning “safe.” “无毒” specifically means “non-toxic,” which is the key issue when discussing unknown plants; “安全无害” emphasizes that something will not cause harm.

Word: “麻烦”  
Context: “太麻烦了。”  
Alternatives: “糟糕” or “危险.” “麻烦” means troublesome, inconvenient, or requiring effort. “糟糕” expresses that a bad situation has happened, while “危险” emphasizes the possible danger of poisoning.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You created a clear mini-story with a question, an answer, and an immediate consequence, which gives the scene a good flow.  
Next time, focus on making pronouns and reactions more specific, especially when describing danger or illness.

## Scene 3

> 夹竹桃是毒的，我想知道灵芝会帮他。”哥哥给神农灵芝。神农吃完了以后开始痊愈了。神农说：“谢谢哥哥，你帮得真好。灵芝我们可以吃，特别是如果我们已经吃了夹竹桃。“

**Grammar Corrections**

Incorrect phrase: “夹竹桃是毒的，我想知道灵芝会帮他。”  
Formula: [植物/东西] + 有毒；[明确的人] + 想知道 + [事物] + 能不能 + 帮助 + [明确对象]  
Correct: “夹竹桃有毒。神农想知道灵芝能不能帮助自己。”  
“他” is unclear here, so name 神农 directly.

Incorrect phrase: “哥哥给神农灵芝。”  
Formula: [明确的人] + 给 + [人] + [东西] + 了  
Correct: “神农的哥哥给了神农一株灵芝。”  
“哥哥” needs a clearer reference because he has not been introduced yet.

Incorrect phrase: “神农吃完了以后开始痊愈了。”  
Formula: [谁] + 吃完 + [东西] + 以后，+ 身体 + 开始 + 好起来  
Correct: “神农吃完灵芝以后，身体开始好起来了。”  
“痊愈” means “to fully recover,” so “开始好起来” is more natural for the beginning of recovery.

Incorrect phrase: “灵芝我们可以吃，特别是如果我们已经吃了夹竹桃。”  
Formula: 如果 + condition，+ result；特别是 + noun/clause that gives a typical example of the previous general statement  
Correct: “灵芝可以吃。如果已经吃了夹竹桃，灵芝可能会有帮助。”  
The first sentence is about whether lingzhi is edible, while the second is about its possible helpful effect after eating oleander. Separating them makes the logic clearer.

**Vocabulary Suggestions**

Word: “毒的”  
Context: “夹竹桃是毒的。”  
Alternative: “有毒” is the standard and most natural way to say that a plant is poisonous: “夹竹桃有毒。” “毒的” is understandable, but it sounds less complete in this context.

Word: “帮”  
Context: “我想知道灵芝会帮他。”  
Alternatives: “帮助” is more neutral and complete in writing; “解毒” is more specific and means “to counteract poison.” For example: “神农想知道灵芝能不能帮助解毒。”

Word: “给”  
Context: “哥哥给神农灵芝。”  
Alternatives: “递给” means “to hand something to someone” and creates a clearer action scene; “送给” means “to give as a gift.” Here, “递给” fits well: “哥哥把灵芝递给神农。”

Word: “痊愈”  
Context: “开始痊愈了。”  
Alternatives: “好起来” means “to begin getting better” and is natural for an early stage of recovery. “恢复” is slightly more formal and can refer to recovering one’s health or strength.

Word: “帮得真好”  
Context: “谢谢哥哥，你帮得真好。”  
Alternatives: “你帮了我大忙” means “you helped me a lot” and sounds very natural and grateful. “多亏了你” means “thanks to you” and emphasizes that the good result happened because of that person.

**Encouragement and Next Steps**

You did well by creating a clear sequence of danger, help, recovery, and gratitude in a short dialogue.  
Next time, focus on making pronouns such as “他” and family words such as “哥哥” clear by introducing exactly who they refer to.
