## Scene 1

> 有一天孟子去了墓地玩儿，但是他妈妈觉得这是不合适的地方让孩子玩，所以她决定了他们会搬家找更好的地方。她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福，而且住在这儿教育不太好。因为她望子成龙，所以她会一直搬家才能找到质量好的教育。然后他们搬到了离城市更近的地方。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 这是不合适的地方让孩子玩：这儿不适合孩子玩，Use “适合 + person + activity” to say that a place is suitable for someone to do something; the original wording does not correctly express this relationship.
- 他们会搬家找：搬家，找，After “决定,” use the action phrase directly; do not repeat the subject before the verb phrase.
- 她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福：她担心儿子的童年不会幸福，“恐怕” means “I am afraid that” and cannot take another subject such as “她”; use “担心” for a character being worried.
- 会一直搬家才能找到：得一直搬家，才能找到，“得……，才能……” expresses that an action is necessary before the result can happen; “会……才能……” does not form this necessary-condition structure naturally.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 更好 -- “找更好的地方”：条件更好的，specifies that the new place has improved living and learning conditions；更适合孩子成长的，makes clear that “better” is measured by the child’s development.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子：一个叫孟子的小男孩，On a first mention, introduce a character with both a name and a brief identifying noun phrase, rather than assuming the reader already knows who the named person is.
- Coherence (Temporality): 决定了：决定，“决定” is a mental-state verb, so it normally takes the zero particle rather than completed-action “了.”
- Expressiveness: At the cemetery, add one concrete sight or sound to show why the mother dislikes the environment, such as “墓地旁有人哭着祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: When the mother makes her decision, add her feeling and a short thought: “她皱着眉头，心想：‘这儿不适合孟子。’”
- Expressiveness: When the family arrives near the city, show the new atmosphere with a detail such as “街上有很多商人卖东西，热闹极了。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the mother’s motivation through the cause-and-effect chain beginning with “因为她望子成龙，所以……”.
- Your use of “然后” and “搬到了” also gives the scene a clear sense of movement to the family’s next home.

## Scene 2

> 他们住才一月就又搬家。古代的时候，一般人不喜欢卖东西的人。但是孟子老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿，害得妈妈着急得很，因为她恐怕儿子会成为跟他们很像。她告诉了孟子：他们不是朋友，而是不好的人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 住才一月：才住了一个月，Duration phrases with 才 come before the verb, and “一个月” is the natural measure-word phrase here.
- 老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿：老是跟这些卖东西的人玩儿，Use only one frequency adverb here; both 老是 and 常常 express repeated action.
- 害得妈妈着急得很：让妈妈很着急，害得 requires a clearly harmful result after the affected person; being worried is better expressed with 让.
- 她恐怕：她担心，恐怕 cannot follow another subject to mean that person is afraid; use 担心 to express the mother’s worry.
- 成为：变得，成为 introduces an identity or role, while 变得 is used for a change into a state such as 很像.
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 already expresses a time period, so adding 的时候 is redundant.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 卖东西的人 -- “一般人不喜欢卖东西的人”：商贩，specifically refers to people who sell goods and is more concise in this market setting；做买卖的人，emphasizes that these people earn their living by trading goods

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 又搬家：又搬家了，The move is a completed event in the narrative, so the final action takes 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉了孟子：告诉孟子，A speaking verb that introduces quoted speech takes the zero particle rather than 了.
- Coherence (Reference): 他们很像：卖东西的人很像，The pronoun could refer either to 一般人 or to the sellers; repeat the noun to make the referent clear.
- Expressiveness: After introducing the sellers, add one sensory detail about the market atmosphere, such as “街上从早到晚都热闹极了，到处有人叫卖。”
- Expressiveness: Show what 孟子 does with the sellers instead of only saying he plays with them, such as “孟子学着商贩收钱，还跟别人讨价还价。”
- Expressiveness: Make the mother’s concern more personal in her quoted speech, such as “孟母说：‘我不想让你每天只学着做买卖。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the connection between the mother’s worry and Mengzi’s behaviour, and you use direct speech to let the mother speak for herself.

## Scene 3

> 你不是当数学家，而是当哲学家，不能当卖东西的人。所以他们又搬家。在他们第三个家，孟子学了怎么祭拜。妈妈感觉这个地方适合他们，告诉了孟子：我们找到了最后的家。在这儿妈妈让儿子从早到晚学习，他难怪成为有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他们第三个家：他们的第三个家，The possessive modifier needs 的 before the ordinal noun phrase.
- 告诉了孟子：告诉孟子，A speaking verb that introduces quoted speech normally takes the zero aspect marker, not 了.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 地方 -- “这个地方适合他们”：环境，specifies the living and learning conditions rather than only the physical place.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为有名的哲学家：成为了有名的哲学家，This is a completed later outcome, so becoming a philosopher takes 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他难怪：所以他，The earlier studying is the cause and becoming a philosopher is the result, so 所以 clearly marks the cause-and-effect relationship.
- Expressiveness: After the third home is introduced, add one setting detail to show why it supports learning, such as “他们的新家在学校旁边。”
- Expressiveness: Show the mother’s positive feeling with an action or a short line of speech at “妈妈感觉这个地方适合他们,” such as “妈妈高兴地说：‘这儿的环境很适合孩子。’”
- Expressiveness: Make the studying scene more vivid by adding a concrete learning action, such as “孟子每天认真读书，还常常问老师问题。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect the family’s move with Mengzi’s learning and later success. You also use the contrast pattern “不是……而是……” to show the mother’s hopes for him.
