### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，The original repeats the time meaning because both parts refer to a historical time period.
- 哪个植物好吃：什么植物能吃，The original asks which plants taste good; this scene needs the meaning of which plants are safe or suitable to eat.
- 去了吃：去吃了，With a purpose verb after 去, the completion marker follows the main action rather than appearing between 去 and that action.
- 写了：写下了，The result complement 下 shows that Shennong recorded the information and kept it in writing.
- 有的时候，神农吃了很好吃的植物：有的时候，神农吃很好吃的植物，A repeated or habitual action introduced by this time expression uses the zero aspect marker, not 了.
- 有的时候，他吃了很危险的植物：有的时候，他吃很危险的植物，A repeated or habitual action introduced by this time expression uses the zero aspect marker, not 了.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 糟糕 -- “这个问题神农觉得很糟糕”：严重，emphasizes that the problem has harmful consequences for many people.
- 危险 -- “很危险的植物”：有毒的，specifies that the plants are dangerous because they contain poison.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 你的身体：人的身体，This is narration rather than quoted speech, so the second-person pronoun incorrectly addresses the reader; use a generic reference for people in general.
- Coherence (Reference): 所以神农：所以他，Shennong was named in the immediately preceding clause and no other character intervenes, so a pronoun is the natural close re-occurrence form.
- Coherence (Reference): 对你有没有好处：对人的身体有没有好处，The narration is not addressing the reader, so the generic reference makes clear that the notes concern people generally.
- Coherence (Temporality): 会来跟他的朋友聊到他吃的植物了：会来跟他的朋友聊到他吃的植物，A modal verb such as 会 normally takes the zero aspect marker here; the final 了 does not express a completed action.
- Expressiveness: After the dangerous plant is eaten, add a physical reaction to show the danger more vividly, such as “他的肚子疼了起来。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where Shennong writes his notes, add his purpose or determination, such as “他想帮助人们找到能吃的植物。”
- Expressiveness: When he talks with his friends, include a short line of dialogue, such as “这种植物不能吃，会让人生病。”

### Encouragement
