## Scene 1

> 一天，很久很久以前，孟子和他妈妈住在中国。孟子妈妈的工作是祭拜，孟子不高兴，因为他说我们老是搬家，为什么？他妈妈说，从早到晚，我一直祭拜，很多墓地得我祭拜。所以我们得去很多不同的地方。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一天，很久很久以前：很久很久以前的一天，When a long time expression modifies a particular day, it should come before the day and connect with 的.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不高兴 -- “孟子不高兴”：不满，shows that he is dissatisfied with moving so often；委屈，shows that he feels wronged or upset by the repeated moves

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和他妈妈住在中国：有一个小男孩叫孟子，他和妈妈住在中国，On a character’s first mention, introduce the character with a name and identifying noun phrase before using a pronoun.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子妈妈的工作：他的妈妈的工作，The mother was mentioned in the immediately previous sentence, so a pronoun is more natural than repeating 孟子’s name.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 孟子不高兴，因为他说我们老是搬家，为什么？：孟子不高兴，问妈妈：“我们老是搬家，为什么？”，Because introduces a cause, but the following words are Mengzi’s question rather than the cause of his unhappiness. Use a speech verb to introduce the question instead.
- Expressiveness: At Mengzi’s question, add a small physical action to show his feeling, such as “孟子皱着眉头问”.
- Expressiveness: When describing the mother’s work, add one sensory detail about the setting, such as “墓地周围很安静，只有风吹树叶的声音”.
- Expressiveness: Show the mother’s emotion before her explanation, such as “妈妈叹了口气，说：‘从早到晚，我一直祭拜。’”

### Encouragement
You used a question to show Mengzi’s confusion, which makes the scene feel more like a real conversation. You also gave a clear reason why the family needs to travel to different places.

## Scene 2

> 孟子想，难怪我们常常搬家，原来我妈妈很忙。可是常常搬家害得孟子很难找到朋友。每次搬家孟子也觉得新的地方不是太热就是太冷，不是太大就是太小。在哪的地方，他都不高兴。孟子想帮助妈妈祭拜，可是他六岁，所以不能祭拜。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在哪的地方：在任何地方，The interrogative word cannot modify this noun phrase with 的 to express “every place”; with 都, use a universal phrase instead.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 常常 -- “我们常常搬家”：频繁，emphasizes that the moves happen repeatedly and often.
- 新的 -- “新的地方”：陌生的，shows that each new home feels unfamiliar from Mengzi’s point of view.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 可是常常搬家害得孟子很难找到朋友：可是常常搬家害得他很难找到朋友，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when no other character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Reference): 每次搬家孟子也觉得：每次搬家他也觉得，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence of the same character.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子想帮助妈妈祭拜：他想帮助妈妈祭拜，Use a pronoun because the previous sentence already clearly refers to Mengzi.
- Expressiveness: After the difficulty with making friends, add a small detail showing loneliness, such as: “他看着别的孩子一起玩，心里很孤单。”
- Expressiveness: At the end, add Mengzi’s own words to make his wish to help his mother more immediate, such as: “妈妈，我也想帮你。”

### Encouragement
You used the opening realization effectively by linking frequent moves to his mother’s busy life. You also used the paired either-or structure clearly to describe why Mengzi dislikes each new place.

## Scene 3

> 四年后，孟子开始帮妈妈祭拜。他画画很厉害，他越来越喜欢祭拜，所以他祭拜不是想帮妈妈工作，而是他很喜欢画画。孟子成为很著名的古代画家。今天很多中国人很喜欢孟子。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 成为：成为了，This is a completed change in the narrative, so add 了 after the verb.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很厉害 -- “他画画很厉害”：擅长, states more precisely that he has a strong skill in drawing；画得很好, directly describes the high quality of his drawings.
- 很著名 -- “孟子成为很著名的古代画家”：享有盛名, gives a more vivid sense that many people knew and respected him.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子成为了很著名的古代画家：他成为了很著名的古代画家，Use a pronoun because the same character was mentioned in the immediately preceding sentences and no other character competes as the referent.
- Expressiveness: At “他画画很厉害,” add one concrete detail about what he draws, such as “他常常画祭拜的人和墓地旁边的花。”
- Expressiveness: After “他越来越喜欢祭拜,” add his inner feeling to show why the activity attracts him, such as “他觉得祭拜的时候很安静，也很特别。”
- Expressiveness: At the ending, add a short evaluative detail about his reputation, such as “人们都觉得他的画很有意思。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show a change over time with “四年后” and “越来越,” which helps the scene move forward. You also use “不是……而是……” to show a contrast between two reasons for the character’s actions.
