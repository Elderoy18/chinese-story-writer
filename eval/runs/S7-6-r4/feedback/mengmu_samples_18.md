## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个男孩名字叫孟子。孟子和孟子的妈妈两个人住在很穷的农村。他们住的地方旁边是个墓地，墓地老是有很多人祭拜。因为人们在祭拜，他们一直在哭。孟子和孟子的妈妈住在这里才一个星期就觉得受不了了，而且害得孟子的学习。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 already refers to a time period, so adding 的时候 is redundant.
- 有一个男孩名字叫孟子：有一个叫孟子的男孩，use 叫孟子 as an attributive phrase before 男孩 to introduce the character naturally.
- 而且害得孟子的学习：而且墓地旁的哭声影响了孟子的学习，害得 must be followed by the person affected and a harmful result; it cannot directly take 学习 as its object.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很多人 -- “墓地老是有很多人祭拜”：来祭拜的人，identifies these people more specifically as visitors who have come to pay their respects.
- 很穷 -- “住在很穷的农村”：贫困，describes the village’s economic condition more precisely.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子的妈妈两个人：孟子和他的妈妈两个人，within one noun phrase, use a pronoun for the repeated possessor after naming 孟子 once.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子的妈妈住在这里：孟子和他的妈妈住在这里，after naming 孟子, use 他的 to avoid unnecessarily repeating his name in the same phrase.
- Coherence (Temporality): 住在这里才一个星期：才在这里住了一个星期，the one-week stay is a completed duration before they reach the point of not being able to bear it, so use 了 with the completed action.
- Expressiveness: At “他们一直在哭,” add a sound detail to show why the cemetery feels upsetting, for example: “哭声听起来很伤心。”
- Expressiveness: At the end, add Mengzi’s feeling or a short line of speech, for example: “孟子说：‘妈妈，我晚上也听见哭声，睡不着。’”
- Expressiveness: Describe Mengzi’s mother’s reaction before the final sentence, for example: “孟子的妈妈看见他没办法认真学习，很着急。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduce the setting, the cemetery, and the reason the family finds the place difficult to live in. Your use of “才一个星期就” also effectively shows that the problem becomes serious very quickly.

## Scene 2

> 孟子的妈妈觉得孟子取得良好的教育是很重要的，所以孟子的妈妈认为这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好，就打算跟孟子搬家。孟子的妈妈带孟子不是搬到城市，而是搬到了孟子老师的家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 取得良好的教育：接受良好的教育，Education is normally something a child receives; 取得 is used for obtaining achievements, results, or qualifications rather than receiving education.
- 跟孟子搬家：带着孟子搬家，跟 means “together with” and does not clearly express that the mother is taking her child to a new home; 带着 shows that she moves with him.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不太好 -- “这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好”：不太合适，states more precisely that this environment does not suit the child’s education；不利于，更 specifically shows that the place may negatively affect his education.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子的妈妈认为：她认为，Use a pronoun when the same character was named in the immediately preceding clause and no other person competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 孟子的妈妈带孟子不是搬到城市，而是搬到了孟子老师的家：她没有带孟子搬到城市，而是带他搬到了孟子老师的家，For a contrast between two destinations, use 没有……而是……; 不是……而是…… is used to correct a mistaken or conventional claim.
- Expressiveness: After the mother decides to move, add her worry to show her reason more vividly, for example: “她担心这样的环境会影响孟子的学习。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of inner speech before the move, for example: “她心想：‘我一定要给孟子找一个更适合学习的地方。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect the mother’s concern about education with her decision to move, so the events are easy to follow.
- The completed-action form in “搬到了” is used well to show that the move has happened.

## Scene 3

> 孟子跟他的老师从早到晚学习，难怪他的成绩很快就进步了很多。孟子学习的时候，孟子的妈妈不是待在家里就是编织衣服赚钱。以后孟子成为了中国有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 以后：后来，For a new sentence meaning “afterwards” in a narrative, use this time adverb rather than 以后.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 成绩 -- “他的成绩很快就进步了很多”：学习成绩，specifies that the improvement is in his school performance.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After “从早到晚学习,” add a concrete study action to show his effort, such as “孟子每天认真地看书、写字，还向老师请教问题。”
- Expressiveness: After his progress, add his mother’s feeling or a short line of speech, such as “孟子的妈妈看到以后很高兴，说：‘你越来越用功了！’”
- Expressiveness: In the final sentence, add an evaluative detail about his later achievement, such as “后来孟子成为了中国有名而受人尊敬的哲学家。”

### Encouragement
You used “难怪” well to connect his long hours of study with his improved results. You also used the paired structure “不是……就是……” clearly to describe his mother’s routine.
