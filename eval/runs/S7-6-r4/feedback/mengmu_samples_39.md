## Scene 1

> 古代的时候人觉得祭拜非常重要，孟子很小的时候，从早到晚一直祭拜，因为他妈妈告诉他祭拜很重要。虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜。他的同学们老是对他开玩笑了。孟子妈妈看了他的同学们对孟子不好的影响，所以孟子和孟子妈妈搬家了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，this phrase redundantly combines a historical time period with another “time of” expression.
- 孟子妈妈：孟子的妈妈，when a name modifies a kinship noun in this narrative style, use 的 to show the possessive relationship.
- 看了：看到了，here the mother notices the classmates’ influence; 看 means “look,” while 看到 expresses successfully seeing or noticing something.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很小 -- “孟子很小的时候”：年幼，more specifically describes a child at an early age.
- 不好 -- “对孟子不好的影响”：负面的，specifies that the influence harms his behaviour or development.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子很小的时候：有一个叫孟子的小男孩；他很小的时候，孟子 is first introduced in this scene, so introduce him with a full noun phrase before using a pronoun.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子妈妈搬家了：孟子和他的妈妈搬家了，within the same noun phrase, repeat the name only once and use a pronoun for the already identified mother.
- Coherence (Temporality): 同学们老是对他开玩笑了：同学们老是对他开玩笑，老是 describes a repeated habit, so it takes the zero particle rather than completed-action 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜：因为他每天只祭拜，所以没有朋友，the relationship is cause and effect, so use the paired connector to link the reason and result.
- Expressiveness: After the description of daily worship, add Mengzi’s feeling to show why he keeps doing it, for example: “孟子觉得祭拜很好玩。”
- Expressiveness: At the classmates’ teasing, add a short line of speech to make the scene more vivid, for example: “同学们笑着说：‘你怎么每天都在祭拜？’”
- Expressiveness: When his mother notices the influence, add her worried thought, for example: “孟子的妈妈很着急，心想：‘不能让他再这样下去了。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly show a cause-and-effect chain: Mengzi worships often, his classmates react, and his mother decides to move. You also use useful story details such as “从早到晚” and “每天,” which make the routine easy to understand.

## Scene 2

> 孟子才十一岁，成为一个哲学家。孟子不是祭拜，而是看政治文章，难怪他成为了哲学家。不过，看过很多政治文章对他害得，他有很多问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 孟子才十一岁，成为一个哲学家：孟子才十一岁，就成为了一个哲学家，An age introduced with 才 needs 就 before the following action, and becoming a philosopher is presented as a completed change, so use 了 after 成为.
- 看过很多政治文章对他害得，他有很多问题：看过很多政治文章害得他有很多问题，害得 needs the pattern “cause + 害得 + person + harmful result”; 对他 cannot appear before 害得 in this structure.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 孟子不是祭拜，而是看政治文章：孟子不再祭拜，而是看政治文章，The pair 不是……而是…… corrects a mistaken or conventional idea; here the intended meaning is a change from an earlier habit, so 不再 expresses it accurately.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 难怪他成为了哲学家：难怪他成为了哲学家，原来他看过很多政治文章，Use 难怪……，原来…… to connect the result with the explanation that makes it understandable.
- Expressiveness: At the point where he reads, add a study detail to show his effort, such as “他读到不懂的地方，会认真地想一想。”
- Expressiveness: After the harmful result, specify what kind of problems he has, such as “有些文章太难了，让他觉得很困惑。”

### Encouragement
You clearly connect Mengzi’s reading with his later career as a philosopher. You also used 看过 well to show that he had already read many articles.

## Scene 3

> 他一直问问题，但是不能解决那些问题。孟子长大，他开始能解决问题。他常常建议你不是学金融，而是学政治，因为如果你懂政治，你会成为高薪。后来孟子成为教授，他教学生很厉害。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 建议你不是学金融，而是学政治：建议你学政治，不要学金融，The pattern “不是……而是……” is for rejecting a stated or conventional idea and replacing it; here the intended meaning is advice about what to study.
- 你会成为高薪：你会拿到高薪，A person cannot “become” a salary; “高薪” is the amount of pay, so “拿到” correctly expresses receiving it.
- 后来孟子成为教授：后来孟子成为了教授，This is a completed change of status in the narrative, so add 了 after the verb.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 问问题 -- “他一直问问题”：不断提出疑问，emphasizes that he keeps raising intellectual questions as he thinks and learns.
- 很厉害 -- “他教学生很厉害”：很会教学生，makes clear that he is skilled at teaching rather than simply sounding generally impressive.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他开始能解决问题：开始能解决问题，After “孟子” appears in the same sentence, use zero form rather than repeating a pronoun.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 孟子长大：孟子长大以后，Use 以后 to show the sequence: his ability to solve problems begins after he grows up.
- Expressiveness: After the opening sentence, add a small detail about his thinking, such as “他常常皱着眉头，想很久。” This shows his effort and frustration.
- Expressiveness: When he becomes a professor, add a student reaction, such as “学生们都认真地听他的课，还常常举手问问题。” This makes his teaching ability more vivid.
- Expressiveness: Turn his advice into quoted speech, such as “他对学生说：‘学政治可以帮助你了解社会。’” This gives his role as a teacher a clearer voice.

### Encouragement
You clearly show Mengzi’s development from someone who cannot solve his questions to someone who becomes a professor. The scene also uses useful academic-topic vocabulary, including “金融”, “政治”, and “教授”.
