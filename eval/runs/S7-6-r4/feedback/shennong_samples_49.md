## Scene 2

> 所以，他上山下山找植物，吃了很多样子的植物。他是第一人，找到了茶。他把所有的植物写下来了。他一直都吃了，他连毒都能吃。可是他还没吃了一个叫夹竹桃的植物。他找到了那个像星星的植物，觉得很漂亮，马上就吃下了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很多样子的植物：很多种植物，样子 describes an object’s outward appearance; 种 is used to count different types of plants.
- 他是第一人，找到了茶：他是第一个找到茶的人，第一人 usually means a leading or foremost person; use 第一个 + verb phrase + 的人 to identify the first person who did an action.
- 吃了：吃过，with 还没, use 过 to describe an experience that has not happened; 了 does not fit an action that is still absent.
- 一个叫夹竹桃的植物：一种叫夹竹桃的植物，种 is the suitable measure word when referring to a type of plant.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他连毒都能吃：连毒都能吃，after 他 has already appeared earlier in the same sentence and no other person competes as the subject, use zero form rather than repeat the pronoun.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他一直都吃了：他一直都在吃，an action described with 一直 is ongoing, so use 在 for the progressive action rather than 了, which marks completion.
- Expressiveness: At the final sentence, add a visual detail about the flower before Shen Nong eats it: “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: Add Shen Nong’s immediate thought to show why he decides to eat the plant: “他想：‘这么漂亮，一定很好吃！’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show a sequence of exploration, discovery, recording, and then trying a new plant. Your use of resultative verbs such as 找到了 and 写下来了 also helps the actions feel complete and easy to follow.

## Scene 3

> 可是夹竹桃的毒很重，让他肚子很痛。他在地上躺着，觉得他快会死了。可是正好有别人找到他了，给了他一个像伞的植物，叫灵芝。他吃灵芝以后就觉得很好，不再生病了。他测量起来了，像星星的植物是毒，像伞的植物是药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 快会：快要，Use “快要” to express that an event is about to happen; “会” expresses prediction or ability and does not naturally form an imminent-event phrase after “快”.
- 别人：一个人，“别人” distinguishes other people from a person or group already being discussed; here the rescuer is a newly introduced, unspecified person.
- 像星星的植物是毒：像星星的植物有毒，“毒” is a noun here and cannot serve directly as the predicate meaning “poisonous”; use “有毒” for a poisonous plant.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很重 -- “夹竹桃的毒很重”：毒性很强，specifies that the plant’s poison has strong harmful effects.
- 很好 -- “他吃灵芝以后就觉得很好”：好多了，shows that he feels better than when his stomach hurt, matching the recovery in this scene.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 觉得他快要死了：觉得自己快要死了，Use “自己” when the person who has the thought is also the person who may die; “他” could otherwise refer to another male character.
- Expressiveness: At “让他肚子很痛,” add a stronger physical reaction, such as “他的肚子疼得不得了，脸都白了。”
- Expressiveness: At “觉得自己快要死了,” add an inner thought or a short question, such as “他想：‘我会死吗？’”
- Expressiveness: At the rescue, describe the rescuer’s action more vividly, such as “一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人赶快跑过来，把灵芝给了他。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the important sequence of poison, pain, rescue, and recovery. You also use “躺着” and “吃灵芝以后” effectively to make the actions easy to follow.
