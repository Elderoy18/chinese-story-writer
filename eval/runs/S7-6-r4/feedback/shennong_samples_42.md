## Scene 1

> 古代的时候有一个人叫神农。他需要当上医，一看别的人就想帮助他们。但是，他有一个问题，他不知道什么东西能做药。神农听说一个远远的地方有一种植物，什么病人吃都会觉得身体更好。神农三个月走去，找到有植物的地方，他看到一个很漂亮的植物，又香又好看。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 already expresses a time period, so adding 的时候 is redundant.
- 当上医：当上医生，医 alone is not the usual noun for the profession in this context; 医生 means “doctor.”
- 一看：一看到，看 describes the process of looking, while 看到 is needed here because the sentence means that he sees someone.
- 别的人：别人，别人 refers to other people generally; 别的 is used to distinguish a different one or type from one already discussed.
- 一个很漂亮的植物：一棵很漂亮的植物，棵 is an appropriate measure word for an individual plant; 个 is too general here.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 做药 -- “他不知道什么东西能做药”：入药，more precisely means “to be used as a medicinal ingredient.”
- 身体更好 -- “什么病人吃都会觉得身体更好”：身体好转了，more specifically describes someone’s health improving after treatment.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他不知道：不知道，within the same sentence, the subject has already been named, so use zero form rather than repeating the pronoun.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农听说：他听说，use a pronoun when the same character was mentioned in the immediately preceding sentence and no competing character is present.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：他，use a pronoun for this close re-occurrence of the same character.
- Coherence (Temporality): 三个月走去，找到：走了三个月，找到了，use 了 to mark the completed three-month journey and the completed result of finding the place; 走去 also normally needs a stated destination, so it should not be used after the duration phrase here.
- Coherence (Temporality): 什么病人吃都会：什么病人吃了都会，eating is completed before the resulting improvement, so use 了 after 吃.
- Expressiveness: At the moment when 神农 hears about the plant, add a brief thought to show his curiosity: “神农想：这种植物真的能治病吗？”
- Expressiveness: When he reaches the place, add a concrete visual detail about the plant: “它开着黄色的、像星星一样的花。”
- Expressiveness: Add a feeling after he sees the plant to make the discovery more vivid: “神农又惊讶又高兴，马上走过去闻了闻。”

### Encouragement
- You introduced the main character clearly with 神农, and the description 又香又好看 gives the plant an appealing sensory detail.
- Your scene also has a clear progression from hearing about a plant to travelling and seeing it.

## Scene 2

> 他觉得，这种一定是做药的花。原来他想错了。吃了以后，神农快生病。 这时一个穿着蓝色衣服的人帮神农，快给他一种红的灵芝。神农吃了以后，他没有病，想起来以前一个人告诉他，灵芝对身体有好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 快：赶快，In this narrative, the adverb should show that the helper acted urgently; 赶快 naturally modifies the action of giving.
- 给：给了，The giving is a completed action in the past, so it needs 了 after the verb.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 做药的 -- “这种一定是做药的花”：可以入药的，specifies that the flower can be used as a medicinal material；能治病的，makes the intended benefit of the flower more concrete.
- 没有病 -- “神农吃了以后，他没有病”：病好了，expresses recovery from the illness more precisely；感觉好多了，shows that his condition improved, without claiming that he was completely cured.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他 -- 神农，Both the blue-clothed person and Shennong are male characters in this sentence, so 他 could refer to either one. Repeat the name to make the recipient clear.
- Coherence (Temporality): 以前一个人告诉他 -- 以前有一个人告诉过他，Use 有 to introduce the previously unnamed person, and use 过 to show that the telling happened before Shennong remembered it.
- Expressiveness: After Shennong becomes ill, add a physical reaction to show how serious it is, such as “他的肚子疼得很厉害，躺在地上动不了。”
- Expressiveness: When the helper arrives, add a short line of speech to make the rescue more vivid, such as “蓝衣人说：‘快吃这个！它可以救你。’”
- Expressiveness: Show Shennong’s relief after eating the lingzhi, such as “神农慢慢坐起来，觉得身体舒服多了。”

### Encouragement
You used “吃了以后” to organize the events in time, and you introduced the new helper with a clear descriptive phrase. The ending also connects Shennong’s recovery to his earlier knowledge about lingzhi.

## Scene 3

> 啊，他怎么忘记了？他把灵芝放在包里。一回家就告诉别人。他找到药以后他写下怎么做药在书上。所以人们都知道，那红灵芝对身体好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 写下怎么做药在书上：在书上写下怎么做药，The location phrase should come before the verb when it states where the writing takes place.
- 对身体好处：对身体有好处，This expression needs 有: it means “be beneficial to the body.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 药 -- “他找到药以后”：能治病的植物，This specifies that the medicine is a plant with a healing effect, matching the story context.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他找到药以后他：他找到药以后，The same person has already been named in this sentence, so use zero form rather than repeating the pronoun in the main clause.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他把灵芝放在包里：他把灵芝放在包里了，This is a completed action in the narrative, so add 了 after the verb phrase.
- Coherence (Reference): 那红灵芝：那种红灵芝，A demonstrative such as 那 needs a measure word or classifier before the noun; 种 identifies it as a type of plant.
- Expressiveness: At the opening realization, add a short inner thought to show his surprise, such as “啊，原来灵芝一直在我的包里！”
- Expressiveness: When he tells others, add a line of speech to make the scene livelier, such as “这是灵芝，对身体有好处！”
- Expressiveness: You could show his care for the medicine with a detail such as “他赶快把灵芝收进包里，生怕弄丢了。”

### Encouragement
- The opening question effectively shows Shen Nong’s sudden realization. You also used the sequence pattern with 一……就…… well to show what he did as soon as he got home.
