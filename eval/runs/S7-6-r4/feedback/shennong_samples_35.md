## Scene 1

> 在古代，很多医生不知道怎么用药。所以他们找到了很多植物对药。神农先看到很多病人，他们吃很多植物对药。但是我觉得他们的吃的植物，让他们病人。神农觉得这个植物可能特别是毒。所以他想找一个植物对药，他们可以吃对药。正好，他找到了一把花，他想把花放在茶里，对药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很多植物对药：很多能做药的植物，A plant that can serve as medicine needs a modifier before the noun; the phrase with 对药 does not express this meaning.
- 对药：来治病，To express the purpose of eating plants, use a purpose phrase meaning “to treat illness”; 对药 is not a valid verb phrase here.
- 病人：生病，病人 is a noun meaning “patient,” while this position needs a verb describing what happens to them.
- 特别是毒：特别有毒，毒 is not used as an adjective after 特别; 有毒 describes something poisonous.
- 一个植物对药：一种能做药的植物，Use 一种 for a type of plant, and use a modifier before 植物 to say that it can be medicine.
- 对药：[delete]，After 吃, 对药 does not form a meaningful phrase; the earlier modifier already states that the plant can be used as medicine.
- 对药：用来做药，Use 用来做药 to state the intended use of the flower; 对药 is not a valid expression for “use as medicine.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 看到 -- “神农先看到很多病人”：发现, emphasizes that he notices an important situation, not only that he sees it.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：一个叫神农的人，This is the character’s first mention, so introduce him with both his name and a brief noun phrase.
- Coherence (Reference): 他们可以吃：人们可以吃，Several groups have appeared, including doctors and patients, so the pronoun is unclear here; repeat a full noun for the general group of people.
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃很多植物：吃了很多植物，The eating happened before Shennong saw that the people were ill, so use 了 for the completed action.
- Expressiveness: When Shennong sees the patients, add a physical detail to show that they are ill, such as: “他们脸色很差，肚子也很疼。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where Shennong thinks about the poisonous plant, add his worried thought: “神农想：这些植物到底能不能吃？”
- Expressiveness: Describe the flower more vividly when it appears, such as: “那把花是黄色的，像小星星一样。”

### Encouragement
You clearly connect Shennong’s observation, his worry, and his plan to search for helpful plants. You also try to use a purpose idea and a 把 sentence, which are useful structures for telling a story.

## Scene 2

> 他不知道如果这把花对你的身体好，这就要看如果这把花毒不毒了。神农想找别的植物，但是这很麻烦。正好，他找到了别的花。他一吃花，很快就生病了。他的肚子疼死了。神农不知道他吃不吃灵芝或者夹竹桃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不知道如果：不知道，After a verb of knowing, introduce the content directly; if you use a conditional clause, it needs a result clause.
- 要看如果这把花毒不毒了：要看这把花毒不毒了，After a verb meaning “see/check,” state the indirect question directly; 如果 incorrectly introduces a conditional clause here.
- 吃不吃灵芝或者夹竹桃：吃的是灵芝还是夹竹桃，When identifying which of two things someone ate, use the pattern 是……还是……; 吃不吃……或者…… instead asks whether someone eats either one.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 找 -- “神农想找别的植物”：寻找, emphasizes an ongoing search for plants; 探寻, emphasizes exploring in order to discover something unknown

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 你的：自己的，The body belongs to the subject of the sentence, so use the reflexive possessive to make that relationship clear.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农不知道他：神农不知道，When the subject remains the same within one sentence, use zero form rather than repeating a pronoun.
- Coherence (Reference): 花：这种花，Use a referring phrase for the flower just found, rather than a bare noun that can sound like flowers in general.
- Expressiveness: After the flower is found, add one visual detail to make it memorable: “那种花是黄色的，像星星一样，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: At the moment of illness, show Shennong’s physical feeling and thought: “神农躺在地上，难受得不得了，心想：我会死吗？”

### Encouragement
You clearly connect eating the flower with becoming ill, and the strong description of the stomach pain makes the danger feel immediate.

## Scene 3

> 别的人给了神农一个灵芝。这个植物可以让神农更好。比方说，他会觉得更健康。吃灵芝以后，他可以再走。现在他觉得更好。最后，神农找到了很多植物，他可以用对药。这些植物让神农很高兴。神农想跟很多人分享他的新药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别的人：有人，The phrase refers to people other than a previously contrasted person or group; no such contrast has been established here, so use an indefinite person reference.
- 这个植物可以让神农更好：这个植物可以让神农的身体好起来，When the intended result is physical recovery, state the body/health explicitly; “更好” alone can describe a person’s overall quality rather than recovery from illness.
- 找到了很多植物，他可以用对药：找到了很多可以用来做药的植物，Use “用来 + verb” to state a plant’s purpose. “对药” cannot follow “用” to mean “as medicine.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 再走 -- “他可以再走”：继续走，emphasizes that he resumes his journey after recovering.
- 新药 -- “神农想跟很多人分享他的新药”：新发现的药，makes clear that this medicine comes from his recent discovery.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the recovery sentence, add a physical detail and a feeling of relief, for example: 神农摸了摸肚子，惊喜地说：“我的肚子不疼了！”
- Expressiveness: At the sharing moment, add a short line of speech to show what he teaches others, for example: 神农说：“这种植物叫灵芝，可以做药。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the sequence from receiving the medicine, feeling healthier, and continuing the journey. You also keep the main character’s goal clear by ending with his wish to share the medicine with others.
