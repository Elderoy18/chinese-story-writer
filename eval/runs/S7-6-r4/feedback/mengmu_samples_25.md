## Scene 1

> 这是古代的故事。孟子小的时候，他的爸爸很早就去世了。从他的家到墓地很近，所以他每天老是从早到晚去墓地跟朋友们玩玩儿。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 从他的家到墓地很近：他的家离墓地很近，Use the pattern A 离 B 很近 to state that two places are close; 从 A 到 B normally introduces a route or distance measurement.

### Vocabulary Suggestions

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: At the cemetery sentence, add a visual detail about the people there, for example: “墓地里常常有人伤心地哭。”
- Expressiveness: You could show Mengzi’s feeling about going there, for example: “孟子觉得墓地很有意思。”

### Encouragement
You clearly introduce Mengzi and explain why he often goes to the cemetery. The cause-and-effect connection with “所以” is also used well.

## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈觉得在墓地玩儿，害得，所以他们搬家。搬家对孟子有好的影响，他才五岁，可是每天不是写字就是帮妈妈的工作。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 害得，所以：会带来麻烦，所以，`害得` must be followed by the person affected and a harmful result; since no result follows here, `会带来麻烦` clearly states the consequence.
- 帮妈妈的工作：帮妈妈干活，`帮` can be followed by a person and an action; `的` incorrectly turns the following words into a noun phrase instead of expressing the action of helping.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好的 -- “搬家对孟子有好的影响”：积极的，specifies that the influence helps his development in a constructive way；有益的，emphasizes that the influence benefits him.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 所以他们搬家：所以他们搬家了，this move is presented as the completed result of the mother’s decision, so the completed action needs `了`.
- Expressiveness: At the cemetery scene, add one concrete action to show what the children do, such as: “孟子觉得祭拜很有趣，就跟朋友学着别人祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: After the mother notices the problem, add her worried thought or speech, such as: “孟子的妈妈很着急，心想：‘不能让孟子一直这样玩下去。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show a cause-and-effect relationship with `所以`, and the contrast in `他才五岁，可是……` helps the reader notice that Mengzi is still very young.
- Your use of `不是……就是……` gives the final sentence a clear description of his daily activities.

## Scene 3

> 他很聪明的人，难怪他成为有名的人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他很聪明的人：他是一个很聪明的人，A noun phrase used to describe the subject needs the copula “是”; “一个” introduces the noun “人.”
- 成为有名的人：成为了有名的人，“成为” describes a completed change of state here, so it takes “了.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 难怪他成为有名的人：难怪成为有名的人，The subject has already appeared in the first clause of the same sentence, so use zero form instead of repeating the pronoun.
- Expressiveness: At “很聪明,” show what his intelligence looks like through an action: “孟子看书很认真，也很快明白书里的意思。”
- Expressiveness: At the end, add a feeling or a short comment from his mother: “孟母看到孟子成为了有名的人，心里很高兴。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect Mengzi’s intelligence with his later success, and “难怪” gives the sentence a useful cause-and-result feeling.
