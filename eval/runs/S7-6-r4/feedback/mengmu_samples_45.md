## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子。他们住在墓地的旁边，所以孟子老是祭拜。孟子不是祭拜，就是跟朋友聊天。他很喜欢他的城市，但是城市有才两个学校，都很小。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，The original repeats the idea of “time”: 古代 already names a historical time period.
- 有才：只有，To express that the city has only two schools, use 只有 before the number; 有才 does not form this “only” meaning here.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 聊天 -- “孟子不是祭拜，就是跟朋友聊天”：闲聊，emphasizes relaxed, casual conversation with friends.
- 都很小 -- “城市只有两个学校，都很小”：规模都不大，specifies that the schools are small in scale rather than merely physically small.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子：一个叫孟子的男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国，Introduce the main character with his name in the first mention rather than naming him only in the following sentence.
- Coherence (Reference): 他很喜欢他的城市：孟子很喜欢自己的城市，Use 自己的 when the city belongs to the same person as the subject, making the possession explicit.
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery sentence, add Mengzi’s feeling to show why he keeps copying the rituals, for example: “孟子觉得祭拜很有趣。”
- Expressiveness: Add one visible detail about the cemetery atmosphere, for example: “墓地里常常有人安静地祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: At the end, add Mengzi’s reaction to the city, for example: “孟子觉得城市很热闹。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduced the setting, Mengzi, and his mother at the beginning of the scene.
- You also used both 所以 and 但是 to show cause and contrast between ideas.

## Scene 2

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 想孟子有很好的教育：想让孟子受到很好的教育，To express a wish for another person’s situation or action, use 想让 + person + verb phrase; 受到很好的教育 clearly expresses receiving an education.

- Coherence (Conjunction): 他们搬家到有很多学校的城市：所以，他们搬家到有很多学校的城市，Add 所以 to show that the mother’s wish for better education causes the move.

### Encouragement

## Scene 3

> 他难怪抱怨，他想他的朋友，因为他不要离开他的城市。他的妈妈找到孟子一个老师。跟老师孟子学最重要的文学，所以他成为哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 难怪：删除，this word introduces a “no wonder” conclusion and normally works with a later explanation introduced by 原来; it does not fit before the direct statement of his complaint here.
- 不要离开：不想离开，the sentence describes his personal wish, so 不想 expresses that he does not want to leave; 不要 is more often used for a prohibition or refusal.
- 找到孟子一个老师：给孟子找了一个老师，use 给 to mark Mengzi as the person receiving the teacher; this structure means that his mother found a teacher for him.
- 跟老师孟子学：孟子跟老师学，put the subject before the phrase beginning with 跟老师.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 想 -- “他想他的朋友”：想念，specifically expresses missing friends who are not nearby.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他的朋友：自己的朋友，when the friends belong to the same person as the subject, 自己的 makes the possessor clearly refer back to him.
- Coherence (Reference): 他的城市：自己的城市，自己的 clearly shows that the city is the one he considers his own, rather than another male person’s city.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他抱怨，他想他的朋友：他抱怨，因为他想他的朋友，use 因为 to connect his missing friends as a reason for his complaint.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为哲学家：成为了哲学家，becoming a philosopher is a completed change of state in the story, so use 了.
- Expressiveness: At the complaint, add a small physical action to show Mengzi’s unhappiness, for example: “孟子一边走一边叹气。”
- Expressiveness: After the mother finds a teacher, add her hopeful thought or speech, for example: “妈妈说：‘我希望你跟老师好好学习。’”
- Expressiveness: Describe Mengzi’s feeling when he learns, for example: “孟子觉得文学很有意思，学习得很认真。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show Mengzi’s reason for being unhappy and connect his learning with his later success. You also use the correct word 哲学家 for his final role.
