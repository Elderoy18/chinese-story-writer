## Scene 1

> 神农和他的家人住在古代，所以不知道什么植物能吃。因为他的家人常常生病了，所以神农和他的哥哥一起去旅行找可以吃的植物。他们去很多不一样的地方，比方说高山、山谷和沙漠。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 神农和他的家人住在古代：在古代，神农和他的家人，Ancient times are a time period, not a place where someone can live; use a time phrase rather than 住在 + location.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不一样 -- “他们去很多不一样的地方”：各不相同，emphasizes that the places differ clearly from one another.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 常常生病了：常常生病，A habitual action takes the zero particle; 了 is not used for a routine or repeated state.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们去很多不一样的地方：他们去了很多不一样的地方，This describes a completed part of the journey, so the completed action needs 了.
- Expressiveness: After the family’s illness, add a physical detail to make the problem more vivid: “他的家人常常肚子疼，只能躺在床上休息。”
- Expressiveness: Show Shen Nong’s motivation with an inner thought before the journey: “神农很担心，心想：‘我一定要找到能治病的植物。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly connect the family’s illness to Shen Nong’s reason for travelling. Your list of mountains, valleys, and deserts also gives the journey a strong sense of variety.

## Scene 2

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在很漂亮的河找夹竹桃：在一条很漂亮的河边找夹竹桃，A river needs the measure word 条 when introduced as one river, and 河边 is needed to show that the characters are searching beside the river rather than inside it.
- “这就要看你想要安全还是接近知道了”： “这就要看这种植物有没有毒了”，The part after 这就要看 must be a question form that states the uncertain condition. “接近知道” does not form a meaningful alternative with “安全”; 有没有毒 clearly gives the condition that determines whether the plant can be eaten.
- “。“：。”，A direct quotation must end with a closing quotation mark, not an opening quotation mark.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 麻烦 -- “太麻烦了”：棘手，emphasizes that the problem is difficult to handle in this situation.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: Add a visual detail about the place where they find the plant, such as “河边开着黄色的花，像星星一样。”
- Expressiveness: Show Shennong’s physical reaction after eating the plant, such as “神农捂着肚子，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Add the brother’s worried feeling to make his speech more vivid, such as “哥哥着急地说：‘太棘手了！’”

### Encouragement
You used dialogue clearly to show Shennong asking his brother for advice. The 一……就…… pattern in the sentence about eating the plant and becoming ill also expresses the immediate result well.

## Scene 3

> 夹竹桃是毒的，我想知道灵芝会帮他。”哥哥给神农灵芝。神农吃完了以后开始痊愈了。神农说：“谢谢哥哥，你帮得真好。灵芝我们可以吃，特别是如果我们已经吃了夹竹桃。“

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 夹竹桃是毒的：夹竹桃是有毒的，Use 有毒 as the adjectival description of a poisonous plant; 毒 alone does not fit this predicate pattern.
- 灵芝会帮他：灵芝会不会帮他，After 想知道, use a yes-or-no form such as 会不会 when the speaker is wondering whether something will happen.
- 夹竹桃。“：夹竹桃。”，The final quotation needs a closing quotation mark after the sentence-final punctuation.
- 开始痊愈了：开始好起来了，痊愈 describes being fully recovered, so it does not fit naturally after 开始; 好起来 describes the beginning of improvement.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 真好 -- “你帮得真好”：很及时，specifies that the help came at the needed moment；帮了我大忙，makes the importance of the brother’s help more concrete

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 我：神农，The narration has used third person, so 我 has no clearly established speaker here; repeat the name to identify whose thought this is.
- Coherence (Reference): 他：自己，After 神农 becomes the subject of the thought, use 自己 to make clear that the lingzhi may help Shen Nong rather than his brother.
- Coherence (Temporality): 哥哥给神农灵芝：哥哥给了神农灵芝，Giving the lingzhi is a completed action in the event sequence, so the verb needs 了.
- Expressiveness: After the recovery begins, add one physical detail to show the change, such as “神农的肚子慢慢不疼了，也能坐起来了。”
- Expressiveness: At the moment Shen Nong wonders about the medicine, add his worried inner thought, such as “神农心想：灵芝真的能帮我吗？”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the helpful action of the brother and Shen Nong’s gratitude. The dialogue also gives the scene a warm, direct feeling.
