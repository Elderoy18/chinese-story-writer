## Scene 1

> 孟子小的时候，从早到晚都跟朋友玩。孟子和他的朋友喜欢玩在墓地里，装祭拜。孟子五岁的时候，跟妈妈搬家。他们搬家不是因为孟子玩得太多，而是因为妈妈认为他们的家离墓地太近。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 喜欢玩在墓地里：喜欢在墓地里玩，A location phrase such as “在墓地里” normally comes before the action verb “玩”.
- 装祭拜：假装祭拜，“装” cannot directly introduce an action in this meaning; “假装 + verb phrase” expresses pretending to do an action.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 朋友 -- “孟子和他的朋友”：小伙伴，more specifically suggests friends of a similar young age in this childhood scene.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子小的时候：孟子是一个小男孩，他小的时候，On a character’s first mention, introduce the character with a fuller noun phrase before using a pronoun.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和他的朋友：他和朋友，Since “孟子” was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other male character competes as the referent, use the pronoun “他”.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子五岁的时候：他五岁的时候，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence of the same character.
- Coherence (Reference): 妈妈：他的妈妈，This is the mother’s first mention, so use a full noun phrase that clearly identifies whose mother she is.
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬家：搬了家，This is a completed move at age five, so use “了” to mark the completed action.
- Expressiveness: After the tomb setting, add a reason that makes the children’s action more vivid, such as “孟子觉得祭拜很有趣。”
- Expressiveness: Show the mother’s feeling before the final explanation, for example “妈妈很担心孟子会学坏。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly state both the children’s behaviour and the reason for the family’s move. The contrast structure “不是……而是……” is used well to distinguish the real reason for moving.

## Scene 2

> 妈妈认为，住离墓地太近，害得孟子的心气。孟子和孟母搬到子思老师的家，跟子思老师住。孟母帮子思打扫房间和做饭。子思帮孟母教她的儿子。因为孟子现在没有朋友，他老是读书。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 住离墓地太近：住在离墓地太近的地方，The verb needs 在 before the location, and the distance phrase needs 的地方 to form a complete place expression.
- 害得孟子的心气：害得孟子心情不好，害得 must be followed by the affected person and a harmful result; 心气 alone does not state the result.
- 老是：一直，老是 often suggests an annoying or unwanted repeated action; for Mencius continuing to study, 一直 is the fitting word.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 跟子思老师住：跟他住，After 子思老师 has just been named in the same sentence and no other person competes as the referent, use a pronoun instead of repeating the name.
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬到子思老师的家：搬到了子思老师的家，The move is presented as a completed event, so 搬到 needs 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为孟子现在没有朋友，他一直读书：因为孟子现在没有朋友，所以他一直读书，Because the lack of friends is given as the cause of his studying, use the paired connector 因为……所以…… to show the cause-and-effect relation clearly.
- Expressiveness: After the move, add a small detail about the new home or study environment, such as “子思老师的家很安静，书架上放着很多书。”
- Expressiveness: Show Mengzi’s feeling about having no friends before he begins studying, such as “孟子觉得有一点孤单，只好一个人读书。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech from Zisi when he teaches Mengzi, such as “子思老师说：‘读书的时候要认真想一想。’”

### Encouragement
