## Scene 1

> 很久以前，古代的人很不知道什么植物能吃，也什么植物不能吃。所以很多人常常吃不安全的植物，吃不安全的植物对你的身体有坏处，很麻烦的情况。但是一个人，神农，要解决这个问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很不知道：不知道，The adverb “很” does not naturally modify the negated mental-state verb “不知道”; use the verb without “很”.
- 也什么植物不能吃：也不知道什么植物不能吃，“也” must be followed by a verb phrase; repeat “不知道” before the second question clause.
- 很麻烦的情况：这是很麻烦的情况，This noun phrase needs a subject–predicate structure to complete the sentence; “这是” makes it a full statement.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不安全 -- “吃不安全的植物”：有害的，specifies that the plants harm the body；有毒的，makes the danger more concrete when the plant causes illness.
- 坏处 -- “对你的身体有坏处”：危害，more precisely describes physical harm in this context.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 你的身体：他们的身体，The preceding subject is “很多人,” so use a third-person plural possessive pronoun rather than shifting to the reader with “你的”.
- Coherence (Reference): 但是一个人，神农：但是，有一个叫神农的人，On first mention, introduce the character clearly with the name and a full noun phrase rather than placing the name after an unclear phrase.
- Expressiveness: After the sentence about harmful plants, add a concrete physical result, such as “很多人吃了以后，常常肚子疼。” This helps readers see why the situation is serious.
- Expressiveness: At the point where Shennong appears, add his determined thought or speech, such as “神农想：我一定要找出能吃的植物。” This shows his motivation more vividly.

### Encouragement
You clearly establish the central problem first and then introduce Shennong as the person who wants to solve it. Your use of “所以” also successfully shows the cause-and-effect link between not knowing plants and people being harmed.

## Scene 2

> 他又聪明又努力，旅行很远找到很多植物。比方说，灵芝和夹竹桃。他一吃各种各样的植物就知道能不能吃这些植物。然后，神农写他的主意。他喜欢植物的味道，特别是花。神农遇到了一个黄色的花，一吃就觉得不舒服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 旅行很远：到很远的地方旅行，Use a destination phrase to show where the travel goes; the verb cannot use this distance phrase directly in this position.
- 写他的主意：写下了自己的主意，Writing an idea down needs the resultative complement in order to show that it was recorded; use the reflexive possessor because the ideas belong to the subject.
- 遇到了一个黄色的花：看到了一朵黄色的花，Use the classifier for flowers, and use the resultative form to show that he saw the flower.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 然后，神农写他的主意：然后，他写下了自己的主意，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the same character was mentioned in the previous sentence and no other character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农遇到了一个黄色的花：他看到了一朵黄色的花，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence of the same character.
- Coherence (Temporality): 找到很多植物：找到了很多植物，The final action in this series is completed, so it needs 了.
- Expressiveness: At the point where the flower appears, add a visual comparison: “那朵花又大又漂亮，像星星一样。”
- Expressiveness: After he eats the flower, make his physical reaction more concrete: “他肚子疼了起来，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short worried thought after he becomes ill: “他想：我会死吗？”

### Encouragement

## Scene 3

> 发现了夹竹桃有毒。别人看到他，很快给他吃一个灵芝。神农一吃就觉得比较好。然后，他继续写他发现的信息。会告诉他的朋友哪一个植物能不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 给他吃一个灵芝：给他吃了一个灵芝，This is a completed rescue action, so the verb phrase needs 了 to mark completion.
- 写他发现的信息：写下他发现的信息，写 means composing text in general, while 写下 means recording information so it is kept.
- 哪一个植物能不能吃：哪一种植物能吃，哪一种植物不能吃，哪一个 asks about the identity of one item, while 能不能 asks a yes-or-no question. Use parallel clauses to distinguish edible and non-edible kinds of plants.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 比较好 -- “神农一吃就觉得比较好”：好一些，shows a modest improvement and sounds more natural for feeling better after taking medicine

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 别人看到他：一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人看到他，别人 needs a contrast with a previously mentioned person; this new character should instead be introduced with a full noun phrase on first mention.
- Expressiveness: At the moment when the medicine helps, add a physical feeling to show the change. Example: “神农的肚子慢慢不疼了。”
- Expressiveness: Describe the helper briefly when introducing him. Example: “一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人跑了过来。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought from 神农 after he feels better. Example: “神农想：‘灵芝真的可以做药！’”

### Encouragement
You used the sequence of discovery, help, recovery, and recording clearly. The pattern 一……就…… in “神农一吃就觉得比较好” is also a good way to show an immediate result.
