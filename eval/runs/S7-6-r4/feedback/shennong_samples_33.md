## Scene 1

> 古代时候，人不知道他们能吃什么植物，常常一吃就死。但是不知道为什么，没有办法。所以有一天，神农出去试一试很多种植物，告诉人什么植物是安全什么是危险。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代时候：在古代，A time noun such as 古代 is normally used directly with 在; adding 时候 is redundant here.
- 人：人们，This refers to a concrete group of people, so use 人们 rather than the generic noun 人.
- 他们：delete，After 人们不知道, the subject of 能吃什么植物 is understood to be the same people, so repeating 他们 is redundant.
- 什么植物是安全什么是危险：什么植物是安全的，什么植物是危险的，With 是 + adjective to classify something, use the adjective form ending in 的; the comma also separates the two parallel questions.
- 人：人们，This is again a concrete group receiving information, so 人们 is needed.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 没有办法 -- “不知道为什么，没有办法”：想不出办法，makes clear that the people cannot think of a solution；束手无策，expresses that they do not know how to respond to the problem.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：一个叫神农的人，This is the character’s first mention, so introduce him with a full noun phrase rather than only using his name.
- Coherence (Temporality): 出去试一试很多种植物：出去尝试了很多种植物，The testing is presented as a completed event in the narrative, so the action needs 了.
- Expressiveness: At “常常一吃就死,” add a feeling of fear among the people, such as “大家都很害怕，不敢随便吃植物。”
- Expressiveness: After introducing 神农, add his purpose in a short thought, such as “神农想：我一定要找出能吃的植物。”
- Expressiveness: At the end, add a brief result of his work, such as “人们听了以后，就知道哪些植物不能吃。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly established the main problem at the beginning: people did not know which plants they could eat.
- Your use of “一吃就死” effectively shows the serious danger and gives the scene a strong opening.

## Scene 2

> 发现有的植物对身体非常好，特别是茶，让肚子感觉又暖和又清楚。他告诉朋友：“你吃哪种植物，这就要看你的口味。比方说，你爱吃甜的，可能喜欢糖。”他最喜欢吃花，除了夹竹桃以外，什么都花想吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 又暖和又清楚：又暖和又舒服，清楚 describes clarity of information or thinking, not a physical sensation in the stomach; 舒服 correctly describes a warm, pleasant bodily feeling.
- 这就要看你的口味：这就要看你的口味怎么样了，after 这就要看……了, the part after 看 needs a question form; 怎么样 asks about the nature of the taste preference.
- 什么都花想吃：什么花都想吃，when 什么 introduces a noun in this “whatever” pattern, the noun comes directly after 什么, followed by 都 and the verb.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好 -- “有的植物对身体非常好”：有益，specifies that the plants benefit health；有好处，clearly states that they bring a health benefit.
- 花 -- “他最喜欢吃花”：花朵，specifies that he likes eating blossoms rather than referring generally to plants.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the tea discovery, add a sensory detail about its smell or taste, such as “茶水香香的，喝下去很舒服。” This helps the reader experience why the tea seems beneficial.
- Expressiveness: At the point where he talks to his friend, add a short thought showing his curiosity, such as “神农想：不同的植物也许有不同的用处。” This gives the character an inner reason for exploring plants.
- Expressiveness: When he mentions flowers, describe their appearance or his reaction, such as “他看着五颜六色的花，觉得很好奇。” This makes the scene more vivid.

### Encouragement
You clearly connect tea with its effect on the body, and the direct speech gives the scene a conversational feeling. You also use the pattern “除了……以外” effectively to show that 夹竹桃 is an exception.

## Scene 3

> 他发现这个口味，一百岁时候，不小心吃了毒的花。一吃就开始死。但是生病时候告诉人什么植物是药，什么植物是毒。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他发现这个口味，一百岁时候：他一百岁的时候发现这个口味，The age expression needs 的 before 时候, and a time adverbial normally comes before the main verb.
- 发现：发现了，This discovery is presented as a completed event in the past, so it needs 了.
- 毒的花：有毒的花，毒 is a noun; use 有毒的 before a noun to describe a poisonous plant or flower.
- 开始死：快要死了，死 is a change of state rather than an action that normally “begins”; 快要……了 expresses that he was close to dying.
- 生病时候：生病的时候，A multi-syllable phrase before 时候 needs 的.
- 人：人们，Use 人们 for the concrete group of people whom he informed.
- 是毒：有毒，Use 有毒 to say that a plant is poisonous.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 吃 -- “不小心吃了毒的花”：误食了，means accidentally ate something and is especially precise for accidentally eating a poisonous flower.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 这个口味：一种花，This is a new object in the scene, but 这个 does not clearly refer to a previously identified taste. Introduce the flower with a full noun phrase on its first mention.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉：告诉了，This is a single completed action in the past, so the verb needs 了.
- Expressiveness: After the poisonous flower appears, add one visual detail, such as “黄色的花像星星一样，又大又漂亮,” to show why he chose to eat it.
- Expressiveness: At “快要死了,” describe his physical feeling with “他的肚子疼得很厉害，躺在地上不能动,” so the danger feels more immediate.
- Expressiveness: When he tells people about the plants, add a short line of speech, such as “这种花有毒，不能吃；这种植物是药，可以吃,” to make his warning clear.

### Encouragement
You clearly show the important contrast between a poisonous flower and a plant that can be used as medicine. You also used the “一……就……” pattern correctly to show a quick result.
