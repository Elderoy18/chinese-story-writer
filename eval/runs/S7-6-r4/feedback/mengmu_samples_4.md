## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，孟子和孟母住了在靠墓地的家。孩子才五岁就每天在墓地祭拜了。在那个城市，孟母从早到晚都找工作，可是找不到，害得孟母没有钱。难怪她跟孩子搬家，原来买不了房。他们搬了到别一个城市找工作。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，This phrase redundantly combines a historical time noun with a second time expression.
- 住了在靠墓地的家：住在靠近墓地的家里，A location follows 住在, and 住 describes a state here rather than a completed action, so 了 should be removed.
- 跟孩子搬家：带着孩子搬家，A parent takes a child when moving; 跟 expresses doing an action together as equals.
- 搬了到：搬到了，With a directional complement, 了 follows the complement: 搬到 + 了.
- 别一个城市：另一个城市，Use 另一个 for one additional or different city; 别的 refers to other people or things as a group.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 找工作 -- “孟母从早到晚都找工作”：四处找工作，emphasizes that she looks in many places for employment.
- 买不了 -- “原来买不了房”：买不起房，more precisely shows that the obstacle is a lack of money.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孩子才五岁：他才五岁，孟子 was named in the immediately preceding sentence, so a pronoun is more natural for a close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Reference): 在那个城市：在他们住的城市，The city has not previously been introduced, so 那个城市 does not have a clear referent; this noun phrase identifies the location clearly.
- Coherence (Reference): 害得孟母没有钱：害得她没有钱，孟母 was already named earlier in the same sentence, and no other female character competes as the referent, so use a pronoun.
- Coherence (Temporality): 每天在墓地祭拜了：每天在墓地祭拜，A daily action is a routine, so it takes the zero aspect particle rather than completed-action 了.
- Expressiveness: At the cemetery scene, add what the child finds interesting: “孟子觉得祭拜很有趣，就学着别人祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: At the job-search scene, show the mother’s worry with an internal thought: “孟母很着急，心想：‘这样下去，我们怎么办呢？’”
- Expressiveness: When the family moves, add a small feeling or physical detail: “孟子抱着自己的小包，安静地跟着妈妈走。”

### Encouragement
You clearly include time details such as the child’s age and the mother’s all-day job search, which helps the reader understand the situation. You also use the cause-and-result pattern with 害得 effectively to show why the mother is worried.

- 又一个次搬：再搬一次，Use the structure modal verb + 再 + verb + number + measure word for an action that will be repeated in the future.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 吵 -- “这个城市老是很吵”：嘈杂，specifies that the city is filled with unpleasant, mixed noise.

- Coherence (Temporality): 了：∅，After the modal verb, this is a planned action rather than a completed one, so use the zero particle.

### Encouragement

## Scene 3

> 孟子在那个商店也找了到工作，可是不是做衣服的工作，而是喜欢的工作。孟子学习馆学了十五年，他的学习和他的旅行经验很重要，对他很多好处。他是大人的时候，他成为很有名的哲人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 找了到：找到了，In a verb-resultative compound, the perfective marker follows the resultative complement; this form means that he successfully found a job.
- 学习馆学了：在学校学习了，学习馆 is not the ordinary word for a school, and the location of an action needs 在 before it.
- 对他很多好处：对他有很多好处，The pattern needs 有: 对 + person + 有 + 好处. Without 有, the predicate is incomplete.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 喜欢 -- “而是喜欢的工作”：心仪的，identifies a job that he especially wants or prefers.
- 很有名 -- “成为很有名的哲人”：享有盛名，shows that his reputation is well established and widely recognized.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他的学习和他的旅行经验：他的学习和旅行经验，Both coordinated nouns belong to the same person, so keep the pronoun once and use zero form before the second noun.
- Expressiveness: After the shop sentence, add a brief scene detail such as “商店里从早到晚很热闹，客人来来往往。” This helps the reader picture Mengzi’s workplace.
- Expressiveness: After the fifteen-year study period, add Mengzi’s attitude toward learning, such as “孟子觉得读书很有意思，每天都认真地看书。”
- Expressiveness: Before the final sentence, add a line of reaction from his mother, such as “孟母高兴地说：‘这儿的环境很适合孟子学习！’”

### Encouragement
You clearly show a long period of study with “十五年” and correctly use 了 to present it as a completed period. Your final use of “成为很有名的哲人” also gives the scene a strong, clear ending.
