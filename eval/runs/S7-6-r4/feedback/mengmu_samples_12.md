## Scene 1

> 古代，孟子跟孟母住在墓地的旁边。可是孟母注意，住在墓地的旁边影响孟子。他常常跟朋友们祭拜玩，所以孟母决定他们会搬家。这次，他们住在城市里的商店的旁边，从早到晚很吵。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 孟母注意：孟母注意到，The result complement “到” is needed here because the verb introduces what she noticed: the following clause is the content of her realization.
- 祭拜玩：玩祭拜的游戏，The verb “玩” should take a noun phrase as its object; “祭拜” alone cannot follow it in this position.
- 决定他们会搬家：决定搬家，“决定” is followed directly by the action phrase being decided; do not repeat the people who will carry out that action.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 古代，孟子跟孟母住在墓地的旁边。：在古代，有一个小男孩叫孟子，他和妈妈住在墓地的旁边。，On first mention, introduce the main character with a name and a brief identifying description before using later references to him and his mother.
- Expressiveness: After the sentence about the cemetery, add Mengmu’s worry to show her inner thoughts, for example: “孟母很担心孟子会学坏。”
- Expressiveness: Make the noise near the shops more concrete with a sound detail, for example: “从早到晚，商人的叫卖声让人觉得很吵。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short action showing what Mengzi does in the new place, for example: “孟子每天都看商人卖东西，觉得很有意思。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the change from the quiet cemetery setting to a noisy place near shops. You also used “所以” well to connect Mengzi’s behavior with Mengmu’s decision.

## Scene 2

> 城市也有点儿危险。害得孟母只好一直陪孟子。孟子也没有朋友，难怪他老是看起来不高兴。住在商店的旁边不是适合孟子，而是让他觉得很伤心。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不是适合孟子：不适合孟子，适合 means “be suitable for” and is directly negated with 不 before it; 不是适合 cannot introduce this negative statement before the object.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 危险 -- “城市也有点儿危险”：不太安全，specifies that the city environment lacks safety while keeping the mild meaning of 有点儿危险.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 而是让他觉得很伤心：因为这让他觉得很伤心，the sadness gives the reason that living there is unsuitable, so a cause connector is needed rather than 不是……而是……, which corrects one claim by contrasting it with another.
- Expressiveness: After “城市也有点儿危险”, add one concrete detail about the surroundings, such as “商店旁边人来人往，声音很大。”, to show what makes the place troubling.
- Expressiveness: After “害得孟母只好一直陪孟子”, show Meng Mu’s worry with an inner thought or short speech, such as “孟母很担心，心想：‘这儿不适合孟子。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect Meng Zi’s lack of friends with his unhappy appearance by using 难怪. Your references to 孟母 and 孟子 are also easy to follow.

## Scene 3

> 所以，孟母和孟子搬到学校的旁边住。才五岁孟子开始学哲学。在学校，孟子不是看书，就是练习写字。将来，他成为中国的一个非常有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 搬到学校的旁边住：搬到了学校的旁边住，The move is a completed event in the narrative, so the resultative verb needs 了 after 到.
- 才五岁孟子开始学哲学：孟子才五岁就开始学哲学，The pattern for an action that starts at a young age is 才 + number + 就 + verb.
- 成为：成为了，Becoming a philosopher is a completed change in the narrated life story, so 成为 takes 了.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 将来：后来，This is a completed historical narrative, so 后来 is the sequence connector that introduces the later event; 将来 points to an unrealized future.
- Expressiveness: After the move, add a quiet school-related detail to make the new setting vivid: “学校旁边很安静，常常能听见学生读书的声音。”
- Expressiveness: At the sentence about studying, show Mengzi’s attitude: “孟子认真地看书，也认真练习写字。”
- Expressiveness: After he begins studying, add Mengmu’s pleased reaction or speech: “孟母高兴地说：‘这儿的环境很适合孩子。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the important change from the noisy city to a school environment, and the study routine in the third sentence is easy to understand.
