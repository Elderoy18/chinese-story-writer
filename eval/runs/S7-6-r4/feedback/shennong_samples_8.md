## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个人叫神农。一天，神农正在路上走，一看很多人正在吃一些植物。一些人一吃植物就生病了，神农想帮别人。所以想知道哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 already names a time period, so adding 的时候 is redundant.
- 一看很多人正在吃一些植物：一看到很多人正在吃一些植物，Here the meaning is that Shennong sees the people; 看 describes looking, while 看到 marks the result of seeing.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 知道 -- “想知道哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃”：弄清楚，emphasizes finding a clear answer after observing and trying different plants

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 。一些人一吃植物就生病了：，就发现一些人一吃植物就生病了，The pattern 一V就 needs 就 to introduce the immediate result; here, seeing people eat leads Shennong to discover that some become ill.
- Expressiveness: After the people become ill, add a visible detail such as “有的人捂着肚子，脸色很难看。” This makes the danger easier for the reader to picture.
- Expressiveness: At the point where Shennong decides to help, add his inner thought or a short quotation, such as “神农想：‘我一定要弄清楚哪些植物能吃。’” This shows his determination.

### Encouragement
You introduce the setting, Shennong, and the main problem clearly. The contrast between plants that can be eaten and plants that cannot be eaten is also easy to understand.

## Scene 2

> 然后，神农开始尝很多植物，比方说他吃过生姜、芥兰和菠菜。一些植物吃真好，特别是黄瓜。但是，一些植物毒得很，特别是夹竹桃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一些植物吃真好：一些植物很好吃，Use the predicate adjective 好吃 to describe food or plants that taste good; 吃真好 is not a natural predicate structure.
- 毒得很：有毒，毒 is a noun and cannot take the degree complement 得很; use 有毒 as the adjective meaning “poisonous.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很多 -- “神农开始尝很多植物”：许多种，specifies that he tried many different kinds of plants rather than only a large quantity.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一些植物：另一些植物，The second group is intended to be different from the plants that taste good, so 另一些 clearly distinguishes this new group of plants.
- Expressiveness: At the mention of the cucumber, add a sensory detail to show why it tastes good: “黄瓜又脆又清甜。”
- Expressiveness: Before naming the poisonous flower, describe its attractive appearance to make the danger more vivid: “夹竹桃开着黄色的、像星星一样的花，看起来又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: Add Shennong’s thought after learning that the flower is dangerous: “神农想：这种漂亮的花不能吃。”

### Encouragement
You clearly contrast helpful, tasty plants with dangerous ones, and your examples make the scene easy to understand. The use of 比方说 and 特别是 is also appropriate here.

## Scene 3

> 一个人对神农说，麻烦我们可以吃什么植物？神农说，这就要看你想不想生病了。以后，神农告诉别人一些植物能吃，一些植物不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 麻烦我们可以吃什么植物？：请问，我们可以吃什么植物？，“麻烦” normally introduces a request to someone to do something, while “请问” is the appropriate polite opener for a question.
- 以后：后来，“以后” does not begin a new sentence to mean “afterwards”; use “后来” to introduce the later event.
- 别人：人们，“别人” distinguishes other people from a particular person already under discussion; here the meaning is people in general, so “人们” fits.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 说 -- “一个人对神农说”：问，shows more precisely that the person is asking for information rather than simply speaking.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: At the first sentence, add a small detail about the visitor’s feeling to make the encounter more vivid, such as “一个着急的人对神农问……”.
- Expressiveness: After the answer about illness, add a concrete warning about the result of eating harmful plants, such as “吃了有毒的植物，肚子会疼起来。”
- Expressiveness: Give Shennong a short, helpful reply that shows his knowledge, such as “我会告诉你哪些植物可以吃，哪些植物有毒。”

### Encouragement
You used direct dialogue clearly, which makes this scene easy to follow. The contrast between edible and inedible plants also matches the main idea of Shennong’s investigation well.
