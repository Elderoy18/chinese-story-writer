## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男人觉得植物很有意思。他叫神农。有的植物吃了以后他的肚子疼死了，可是有的吃了以后很舒服。他找很多植物。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，The original expression redundantly combines a historical time period with a “time of” structure.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一个男人觉得植物很有意思。他叫神农：一个叫神农的男人觉得植物很有意思，Introduce a character with both the name and a full noun phrase at the first mention, rather than naming him only in the following sentence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他找很多植物：他找了很多植物，This is a completed action in the narrative, so the verb needs 了 to mark completion.
- Expressiveness: At the point where the character feels pain, add a physical reaction to show how serious it is: “有的植物吃了以后，他的肚子疼得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: When mentioning the plants he looks for, add one visual detail to make the scene easier to imagine: “他找了很多植物，有的花是黄色的，像星星一样。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show that different plants can have very different effects, and the contrast between pain and comfort makes the scene easy to understand.

## Scene 2

> 凉山很大的山，这也找植物。一天，他找到黄色的花。他听说你跟黄色的花做茶很有用。比方说，你的头发疼死，这冰茶是药。他一喝茶就很不舒服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 凉山很大的山：凉山是一座很大的山，A noun phrase used as the predicate needs the copula and a suitable measure word here.
- 跟黄色的花做茶很有用：用黄色的花泡的茶很有用，Use 用 to show the material used for making the tea; the modifier phrase needs 的 before 茶.
- 头发：头，Hair cannot feel pain; 头 refers to the body part that can ache.
- 这冰茶：这杯冰茶，A demonstrative before a countable noun needs a measure word.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很大 -- “凉山是一座很大的山”：高大，emphasizes the mountain’s impressive size and height in this setting

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 这也找植物：他也找植物，After the mountain is introduced, 这 would refer to the mountain, not to the continuing character; use the pronoun for the previously established person.
- Coherence (Reference): 黄色的花：一朵黄色的花，This is the flower’s first mention, so introduce it with an indefinite noun phrase and measure word.
- Coherence (Temporality): 找到：找到了，Finding the flower is a completed event in the narrative, so add 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 就很不舒服：就很不舒服了，Add 了 to mark the change into an uncomfortable state after drinking the tea.
- Expressiveness: At the flower’s introduction, add a visual detail so the setting is easier to picture: “那儿开着黄色的花，像星星一样，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: When he becomes ill, show his physical feeling more clearly: “他肚子疼了起来，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought to show his fear: “他想：我会不会生病了？”

### Encouragement
- You created a clear sequence from finding a flower to drinking tea and feeling unwell. You also tried to explain an example, which helps the reader understand why the tea matters.

## Scene 3

> 糟糕，这黄色的花是夹竹桃，特别是毒。正好在这儿，灵芝药的夹竹桃他吃完了，也写一个课文的植物。谢谢神农。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 这黄色的花：这朵黄色的花，Demonstratives such as 这 need a measure word before a countable noun; 朵 is the suitable measure word for a flower.
- 特别是毒：特别有毒，特别是 must introduce an example from a preceding general group; it cannot mean “very.” Use 有毒 after 特别 to describe the flower’s poisonous quality.
- 灵芝药的夹竹桃：灵芝是药，夹竹桃有毒，The particle 的 makes a modifier relationship, so this phrase incorrectly makes 夹竹桃 seem to belong to 灵芝药. Separate the two classifications into complete clauses.
- 一个课文：一篇课文，课文 takes the measure word 篇 rather than 个.
- 写一个课文的植物：把这些植物写到了书上，Writing a plant name is not the same as recording information about plants. This revised structure clearly means that the plant information was recorded in a book.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃完了，也把这些植物写到了书上：吃完，也把这些植物写到了书上，For a connected series of completed actions, only the final verb takes 了; 吃完 already shows the result of finishing the eating action.
- Expressiveness: After the realization about the flower, add a physical reaction to show fear, for example: “神农吓了一跳，马上吐了出来。”
- Expressiveness: Describe the flower more vividly at the point where it is identified, for example: “黄色的花像星星一样，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought to show why the discovery matters, for example: “神农想：以后不能随便吃这种花了。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly identify the flower as 夹竹桃 and use 糟糕 to show that the discovery is dangerous. You also try to include both the eating action and the important act of recording plant knowledge.
