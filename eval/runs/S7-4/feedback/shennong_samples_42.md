## Scene 1

> 古代的时候有一个人叫神农。他需要当上医，一看别的人就想帮助他们。但是，他有一个问题，他不知道什么东西能做药。神农听说一个远远的地方有一种植物，什么病人吃都会觉得身体更好。神农三个月走去，找到有植物的地方，他看到一个很漂亮的植物，又香又好看。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，Ancient times are already a time expression, so adding 的时候 is redundant. [rule: time_expression]
- 当上医：当上医生，医 cannot normally stand alone as the job noun after 当上; 医生 means “doctor.” [rule: word_choice]
- 一看：一看到，The verb 看 means “look”; when Shen Nong sees someone, the resultative complement 到 is needed. [rule: resultative_complement]
- 别的人：别人，别人 refers collectively to other people; 别的 needs a following noun to distinguish another kind of thing. [teacher rule: bieren]
- 什么病人吃都会觉得身体更好：任何病人吃了都会觉得身体更好，什么 is an interrogative word asking which patient, while 任何 expresses that all patients are included; 了 marks eating as completed before the resulting feeling. [rule: none]
- 一个很漂亮的植物：一株很漂亮的植物，株 is the appropriate measure word for an individual plant. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 但是，他有一个问题，他不知道什么东西能做药：但是，他有一个问题，不知道什么东西能做药，After the subject has appeared in the same sentence and no other person competes with it, use zero form rather than repeating the pronoun. [rule: redundancy_delete]
- Coherence (Temporality): 神农三个月走去，找到有植物的地方：神农走了三个月，找到了有植物的地方，Both the three-month walk and the discovery are completed past events, so they need 了; 走了三个月 expresses a completed duration naturally. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: When Shen Nong wants to help sick people, add his inner thought: “我要帮助生病的人。”
- Expressiveness: When describing the faraway place, add one visual detail from the setting: “那儿开着黄色的花，像星星一样。”
- Expressiveness: After he notices the beautiful plant, show his curiosity with a short thought: “这种植物能做药吗？”

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduce Shen Nong and explain his goal of helping sick people. The description “又香又好看” also gives the plant two appealing sensory details.

## Scene 2

> 他觉得，这种一定是做药的花。原来他想错了。吃了以后，神农快生病。 这时一个穿着蓝色衣服的人帮神农，快给他一种红的灵芝。神农吃了以后，他没有病，想起来以前一个人告诉他，灵芝对身体有好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- Nothing to flag.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好处 -- “灵芝对身体有好处”：疗效，specifies that the benefit is a medicinal effect in this context；益处，keeps the general meaning but sounds slightly more specific than a broad “good thing.”

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 给他：给神农，Both the blue-clothed person and Shennong are male characters in this sentence, so the pronoun can be unclear; repeat the name for clear reference. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Reference): 神农吃了以后，他没有病：神农吃了以后，没有病，When the same character continues within one sentence and no other character competes, use zero form rather than repeating the pronoun. [rule: redundancy_delete]
- Coherence (Temporality): 神农快生病：神农很快就生病了，The narration describes an illness that happened after eating, so use completed-action 了; 很快就 marks that the change happened quickly. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 快给：赶快给了，The giving is a completed action in the narrative, so it needs 了; 赶快 is the natural adverb for acting quickly. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: At the moment when Shennong becomes ill, add a physical reaction to show the pain: “他的肚子疼了起来，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: After he remembers the medicinal plant, add a short thought to show surprise and relief: “他想：原来灵芝真的可以治病！”
- Expressiveness: You could make the flower more vivid when Shennong first mistakes it for medicine: “那朵花又黄又大，像星星一样漂亮。”

### Encouragement
You use temporal phrases to show the order of events, and the blue-clothed helper gives the scene a clear visual detail. The ending also connects the medicine to Shennong’s earlier knowledge in a meaningful way.

## Scene 3

> 啊，他怎么忘记了？他把灵芝放在包里。一回家就告诉别人。他找到药以后他写下怎么做药在书上。所以人们都知道，那红灵芝对身体好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别人：大家，别人 means people other than a specified person or group; here he is sharing the news generally, so 大家 fits better. [teacher rule: bieren]
- 写下怎么做药在书上：把怎么做药写在书上，use the 把 structure to place the object before the action, and place the location phrase with the verb. [rule: none]
- 对身体好处：对身体有好处，this expression requires 有 in the structure 对……有好处. [vocab list]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 放在 -- “他把灵芝放在包里”：装进，emphasizes putting the lingzhi inside the bag.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他找到药以后他写下：他找到药以后写下，when the same subject continues within one sentence, use zero form rather than repeating the pronoun. [rule: none]
- Expressiveness: After he puts the lingzhi away, add a small action that shows care: “他小心地把灵芝装进包里，生怕弄丢了。”
- Expressiveness: When he tells people, add a short line of speech: “大家看，这种灵芝可以做药，对身体有好处！”

### Encouragement
You included clear actions, such as putting the lingzhi in a bag and writing information down. You also used the connectors 一……就…… and 所以 to show event order and results.
