## Scene 1

> 这是古代的故事。孟子小的时候，他的爸爸很早就去世了。从他的家到墓地很近，所以他每天老是从早到晚去墓地跟朋友们玩玩儿。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 从他的家到墓地很近：他的家离墓地很近，use 离 to state that two places are close; 从……到…… normally introduces a route or distance, such as “the road from A to B.” [vocab list]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the opening, add a brief image of the setting, such as “墓地旁边有很多石碑，风吹过的时候很安静。” This helps the reader picture the place.
- Expressiveness: At the sentence about going to the cemetery, add Mengzi’s reaction to make his actions more personal, such as “孟子觉得那里很神秘，也很好玩。”
- Expressiveness: You could show an interaction with his friends through a short line of speech, such as “他对朋友们说：‘我们去那边看看吧！’”

### Encouragement
You clearly establish the time period and explain why Mengzi often went to the cemetery. The cause-and-effect connection between the nearby cemetery and his daily activity is easy to follow.

## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈觉得在墓地玩儿，害得，所以他们搬家。搬家对孟子有好的影响，他才五岁，可是每天不是写字就是帮妈妈的工作。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在墓地玩儿，害得：他在墓地玩儿会惹麻烦，The structure with “害得” must be followed by the person affected and a harmful result; it cannot stand alone before a comma. [teacher rule: haide_structure]
- 帮妈妈的工作：帮妈妈工作，“帮” can take a person followed by an action; “妈妈的工作” is a noun phrase and does not fit as the object here. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好的 -- “搬家对孟子有好的影响”：积极的，specifies that the influence is beneficial and supports his development.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他的妈妈：孟子的妈妈，This is the mother’s first mention in the story, so introduce her with a full noun phrase rather than a possessive pronoun. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Temporality): 所以他们搬家：所以他们搬家了，This move is a completed event in the narrative, so the final verb takes 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: At the mother’s decision, add her worried thought to show why she wants to move: “孟子的妈妈很担心，心想：‘他每天这样玩儿，会不会学坏？’”
- Expressiveness: Add a small detail about Mengzi’s actions at the cemetery, such as “他学着别人哭，还跟朋友一起笑。”
- Expressiveness: After the move, show the mother’s positive feeling with a short reaction: “孟子的妈妈看到孟子每天写字，心里很高兴。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the contrast between Mengzi being very young and already having a daily routine. The pattern “不是……就是……” is used well to describe the two things he does each day.

## Scene 3

> 他很聪明的人，难怪他成为有名的人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他很聪明的人：他很聪明，An adjective can serve as the predicate by itself; do not add a following noun unless the sentence uses a full noun phrase such as “是一个很聪明的人”. [rule: adjective_predicate]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为有名的人：成为了有名的人，This is a completed achievement in the past, so use 了 after the verb to mark completion. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: After “他很聪明”, show his intelligence through a concrete action, such as “他喜欢看书，也常常思考问题。”
- Expressiveness: Add a reaction from other people after his achievement, such as “大家都很佩服他。”

### Encouragement
- You used “难怪” effectively to connect his intelligence with his later achievement. The sentence also gives a clear positive description of the character.
