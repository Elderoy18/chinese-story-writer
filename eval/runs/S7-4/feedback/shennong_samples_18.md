## Scene 1

> 神农和他的家人住在古代，所以不知道什么植物能吃。因为他的家人常常生病了，所以神农和他的哥哥一起去旅行找可以吃的植物。他们去很多不一样的地方，比方说高山、山谷和沙漠。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 住在古代：生活在古代，The verb for residing normally takes a physical place; a time period needs a verb meaning to live in an era. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 找 -- “去旅行找可以吃的植物”：寻找, emphasizes an ongoing search for an answer and fits this exploration context；探寻, highlights carefully seeking out unknown plants.
- 不一样 -- “很多不一样的地方”：各不相同, more precisely shows that the places differed from one another.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：一个叫神农的人，At first mention, introduce the character with both a name and a brief identifying noun phrase rather than assuming the reader already knows him. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Temporality): 常常生病了：常常生病，A habitual condition uses the zero particle; 了 marks a completed event or change of state. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们去很多不一样的地方：他们去了很多不一样的地方，This is a completed travel event in the narrative, so the verb takes 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: After the family’s illness, add Shen Nong’s concern or determination: “神农很担心家人，决定去找可以治病的植物。”
- Expressiveness: Make the journey more vivid by adding one detail about the difficult places: “高山又高又冷，沙漠里没有水。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of dialogue before the brothers leave: “哥哥说：‘我们一起去找药吧！’”

### Encouragement
You clearly connect the family’s repeated illnesses with Shen Nong’s reason for travelling, which gives the scene a logical motivation. The list of mountains, valleys, and deserts also creates a good sense that their journey covers many different places.

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在很漂亮的河找：在一条很漂亮的河边找，A river is not normally used as the exact location for an action; use 河边 for the place beside it, and 一条 naturally introduces the river. [rule: none]
- 你想要安全还是接近知道：你想要安全还是想知道答案，接近 means “get close to” and cannot modify 知道 in this meaning. After 这就要看, use a clear question or alternative structure such as A还是B. [rule: zhe_jiu_yao_kan]
- 了。“：了。”，The quotation must end with a closing quotation mark rather than another opening quotation mark. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 麻烦 -- “太麻烦了”：严重，specifies that the illness is serious rather than merely inconvenient；糟糕，expresses the brother’s immediate negative reaction to the situation.

- Expressiveness: Before the question, add Shennong’s uncertainty to show his thoughts: “神农有一点担心，不知道这种植物能不能吃。”

### Encouragement

## Scene 3

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 夹竹桃是毒的：夹竹桃是有毒的，Use 有毒 to describe a poisonous plant; 毒 is not used directly as this adjective predicate. [rule: word_class_misuse]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 帮 -- “我想知道灵芝会帮他”：给他解毒，states the specific help that the medicinal plant provides here；让他好转，focuses on the improvement in his condition.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 哥哥给神农灵芝：哥哥给了神农灵芝，This is a completed action in the event sequence, so the verb needs 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: After the recovery begins, add a bodily feeling to show the change rather than only stating it: “神农觉得肚子没有那么疼了，慢慢有了力气。”
- Expressiveness: Make the brother’s concern clearer with a short line of dialogue before he gives the medicine: “哥哥着急地说：‘快吃一点儿灵芝吧！’”

### Encouragement
You used a clear time sequence with “吃完了以后,” and the dialogue between the brothers helps the scene feel active. The word “痊愈” is also a strong, specific word for recovery.
