## Scene 1

> 这个男孩子叫孟子，孟子是古代的人。孟子的妈妈觉得住在哪儿对童年很重要，所以他们常常搬了家。她望子成龙，所以孟子的妈妈安排他的生活。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- Nothing to flag.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 重要 -- “住在哪儿对童年很重要”：影响很大，more specifically shows that the place affects a child’s growth.
- 安排 -- “安排他的生活”：规划，emphasizes making thoughtful plans for his daily life and future.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 这个男孩子叫孟子，孟子是古代的人：在古代，有一个叫孟子的男孩子。A character’s first mention should introduce the character clearly with a full noun phrase; repeating the name again in the same sentence is unnecessary.
- Coherence (Temporality): 常常搬了家：常常搬家，A habitual action uses the zero particle rather than 了, which marks a completed action.
- Coherence (Reference): 她望子成龙，所以孟子的妈妈安排他的生活：她望子成龙，所以安排他的生活。After the mother has been named as the subject, use zero form for the same referent later in the same sentence.
- Expressiveness: At “她望子成龙,” add the mother’s worried inner thought, such as: “住的地方会不会影响孟子的成长呢？”
- Expressiveness: Make the mother’s planning more concrete with one example, such as: “她每天安排孟子读书、写字。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the mother’s motivation with “望子成龙,” and the cause-and-effect link with “所以” helps the ideas connect naturally.

## Scene 2

> 因为他们常常搬了家，中国人说，孟母三迁。孟子没有爸爸，所以，他的妈妈更重要。从这儿到那儿，妈妈教孟子很多技能。先她让孟子跟朋友玩儿，然后教他怎么买又便宜又好质量的东西。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 先她让孟子跟朋友玩儿：她先让孟子跟朋友玩儿，The time adverb should follow the subject and come before the verb it modifies.
- 又便宜又好质量的东西：又便宜又质量好的东西，The phrase for “good quality” is formed with the noun followed by the adjective, so the two descriptions can be parallel.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 技能 -- “妈妈教孟子很多技能”：生活技能，specifies that these are practical abilities for everyday life, such as choosing things to buy.
- 东西 -- “又便宜又好质量的东西”：商品，more precisely identifies the things as goods that people buy and sell.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 常常搬了家：常常搬家，A habitual action takes the zero particle rather than completed-action 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为他们常常搬家，中国人说，孟母三迁：因为他们常常搬家，所以中国人说“孟母三迁”，Add 所以 to complete the cause-and-effect connection introduced by 因为.
- Expressiveness: After explaining why the mother is especially important, add her sense of responsibility: “孟子的妈妈觉得自己要更用心地照顾他。”
- Expressiveness: When the mother teaches shopping, add a small piece of quoted advice: “她告诉孟子：‘买东西要看价钱，也要看质量。’”
- Expressiveness: Show Mengzi’s feeling when he plays with his friends: “孟子跟朋友玩儿的时候，开心得笑起来。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the mother teaching Mengzi different practical things, and the sequence from playing with friends to learning how to shop is easy to follow.
