## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个男孩名字叫孟子。孟子和孟子的妈妈两个人住在很穷的农村。他们住的地方旁边是个墓地，墓地老是有很多人祭拜。因为人们在祭拜，他们一直在哭。孟子和孟子的妈妈住在这里才一个星期就觉得受不了了，而且害得孟子的学习。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，The original expression redundantly combines a historical time period with a “time of” phrase. [vocab list]
- 害得：影响了，害得 must be followed by a person and a harmful result clause; it cannot directly take a noun phrase such as the student’s studies. [teacher rule: haide_structure]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很穷 -- “住在很穷的农村”：贫困，specifies that the rural area has economic hardship.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子的妈妈：孟子和他的妈妈，After 孟子 has just been named, use the pronoun to refer back to him rather than repeating his name in the same noun phrase. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子的学习：他的学习，孟子 has already been named in this sentence, and the male pronoun clearly refers back to him. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Temporality): 住在这里才一个星期：在这里才住了一个星期，The week of living there is presented as a completed duration before the next result, so the action needs 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为人们在祭拜，他们一直在哭：因为人们在祭拜，所以他们一直在哭，Use the paired connector because this sentence presents worship as the cause and crying as the result. [rule: none]
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery is introduced, add a sensory detail about its atmosphere, such as “墓地旁常常传来哭声，气氛有点可怕。”
- Expressiveness: At the final sentence, show Mengzi’s feeling more specifically, such as “孟子觉得每天听到哭声，心里很不舒服。”
- Expressiveness: You could add a short thought from his mother to show why the situation worries her, such as “孟子的妈妈心想：‘这里太吵了，会影响孩子学习。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduce the setting, the cemetery, and the people’s crying, so the reader can understand why the family feels uncomfortable. Your use of “才一个星期就” also effectively shows that the problem appears quickly.

## Scene 2

> 孟子的妈妈觉得孟子取得良好的教育是很重要的，所以孟子的妈妈认为这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好，就打算跟孟子搬家。孟子的妈妈带孟子不是搬到城市，而是搬到了孟子老师的家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 取得良好的教育：接受良好的教育，Education is something a child receives; 取得 is normally used for achievements or results rather than education itself. [rule: none]
- 跟孟子搬家：带着孟子搬家，The mother takes her son with her when moving; 跟孟子搬家 incorrectly makes them sound like equal companions in this action. [vocab list]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 良好 -- “接受良好的教育”：优质，specifies that the education is of high quality.
- 地方 -- “这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好”：学习环境，makes clear that the concern is the environment’s effect on learning.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子的妈妈认为：她认为，Use a pronoun for a recently mentioned character when no other possible referent has appeared. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子的妈妈带孟子：她带孟子，Repeat the name only after intervening information makes a pronoun unclear; here the mother remains the clear subject. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Conjunction): 带孟子不是搬到城市，而是搬到了孟子老师的家：带孟子搬到了孟子老师的家，The pattern 不是……而是…… should correct a conventional or previously stated mistaken idea, rather than simply introduce the destination. [rule: bushi_ershi_construction]
- Expressiveness: At the decision point, add the mother’s worried feeling, such as “很着急” or “担心孟子学不好,” to show why she wants to move.
- Expressiveness: Add a brief thought before the move, using a detail such as “得找一个适合学习的环境,” to make the mother’s decision feel more personal.

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect the importance of education with the mother’s decision to move, so the main reason for her action is easy to understand.

## Scene 3

> 孟子跟他的老师从早到晚学习，难怪他的成绩很快就进步了很多。孟子学习的时候，孟子的妈妈不是待在家里就是编织衣服赚钱。以后孟子成为了中国有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 以后孟子成为了中国有名的哲学家：后来孟子成为了中国有名的哲学家，At the beginning of a new sentence, use 后来 rather than 以后 to mean “afterwards” in a narrative. [vocab list]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 进步 -- “他的成绩很快就进步了很多”：提高，specifically describes a rise in academic results.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the study sentence, add a small detail about Mengzi’s effort, such as “他每天认真地看书，还常常问老师问题。”
- Expressiveness: In the sentence about his mother, add her feeling about his progress, such as “孟子的妈妈看到他这么用功，心里很高兴。”
- Expressiveness: Before the final outcome, add a brief evaluative detail, such as “他长大以后，常常思考怎么做一个好人。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect Mengzi’s long hours of study with his improving grades by using “难怪,” which makes the result easy to understand.
- You also use the “不是……就是……” structure well to show his mother’s two possible daily activities.
