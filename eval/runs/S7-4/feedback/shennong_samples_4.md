## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个男人，他的名字是神农。他看到了有很多人不知道哪个植物好吃，也不知道什么植物对你的身体有好处。这个问题神农觉得很糟糕，所以神农去了吃每种植物。他吃了一种植物以后，就写了这个植物对你有没有好处。有的时候，神农吃了很好吃的植物。有的时候，他吃了很危险的植物。他吃完了每种植物以后，会来跟他的朋友聊到他吃的植物了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，This expression redundantly combines a historical time period with another “time of” phrase; use the shorter time expression.
- 去了吃：去吃了，With an action following 去, place 了 after that action to show the completed action.
- 写了：记下了，For recording information about a plant, this verb means to write down and keep the finding as a record.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 糟糕 -- “这个问题神农觉得很糟糕”：严重，specifies that the problem has serious consequences rather than merely sounding bad.
- 好处 -- “这个植物对你有没有好处”：作用，more precisely refers to the effect that the plant has on the body.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 所以神农：所以他，The name was mentioned in the immediately preceding sentence and no other character competes as the referent, so a pronoun is more natural.
- Coherence (Temporality): 有的时候，神农吃了很好吃的植物：有的时候，神农吃很好吃的植物，A repeated habit uses the zero particle rather than 了, which marks a completed individual action.
- Coherence (Temporality): 有的时候，他吃了很危险的植物：有的时候，他吃很危险的植物，A repeated habit uses the zero particle rather than 了, which marks a completed individual action.
- Coherence (Temporality): 会来跟他的朋友聊到他吃的植物了：会来跟他的朋友聊到他吃的植物，A modal verb such as 会 normally takes the zero particle; the final 了 does not fit a habitual or future action.
- Expressiveness: After the sentence about people not knowing plants, add Shen Nong’s determination with a short thought, such as “神农想：我要找出能吃的植物。”
- Expressiveness: When describing the dangerous plants, add a physical reaction to make the event vivid, such as “他吃了以后，肚子疼了起来。”
- Expressiveness: When he records his discoveries, add a concrete detail about what he notes, such as “他记下了植物的颜色和作用。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show Shen Nong’s reason for testing plants and use time phrases to organize his repeated actions. The contrast between helpful plants and dangerous plants also gives this scene a clear focus.

## Scene 2

> 一个人问神农，哪个植物好吃。神农说，这就要看你的口味了。要是你喜欢吃甜的，有很多甜的水果，比方说苹果、西瓜和梨。一个别的人问神农，哪个植物对你的身体有好处。神农说，有很多种植物对你的身体有好处，比方说小白菜、黄瓜、茶和菠菜。一个别的人问神农，哪个植物对你的身体有坏处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一个别的人：另一个人，别的 is used to distinguish other people from a stated group; when introducing one additional individual, use 另一个人.
- 一个别的人：另一个人，别的 is used to distinguish other people from a stated group; this sentence introduces one additional questioner, so 另一个人 is the natural form.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很多 -- “有很多甜的水果”：多种，makes clear that there are several kinds of sweet fruit rather than simply a large quantity.
- 坏处 -- “哪个植物对你的身体有坏处”：有害，states more directly that the plant is harmful to the body.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 要是你喜欢吃甜的，有很多甜的水果：要是你喜欢吃甜的，可以选择很多甜的水果，比方说苹果、西瓜和梨，a conditional connector needs a result that depends on the condition; 可以选择 gives the reader an appropriate recommendation after 要是.
- Expressiveness: After “苹果、西瓜和梨,” add a small sensory detail, such as “它们又甜又多汁,” to make the food sound more appealing.
- Expressiveness: In the answer about helpful plants, add a simple use for one example, such as “茶可以泡在水里喝,” to make the information more concrete.
- Expressiveness: Add a short reaction from the questioner after an answer, such as “那个人高兴地说：‘谢谢你！’”, to make the dialogue feel more alive.

### Encouragement
- You used clear question-and-answer dialogue, and your examples of fruits and plants make Shennong’s answers easy to understand.

## Scene 3

> 神农说，有一天，我吃了一个黄色的花，名字是夹竹桃。我一吃夹竹桃，就觉得这种植物对我的脑有坏处。可是，一个灵芝和一个男人正好在我的旁边，我说，麻烦你把那个植物给我吃一下。我一吃这个灵芝就好了。所以，你得小心吃植物，因为有的植物对你的健康有坏处，特别是夹竹桃。因为夹竹桃有毒。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一个黄色的花：一朵黄色的花，The measure word for an individual flower should be the matching classifier rather than the general classifier.
- 一个灵芝：一株灵芝，When referring to one individual medicinal plant, the classifier should match the plant rather than use the general classifier.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 旁边 -- “一个灵芝和一个男人正好在我的旁边”：身边，emphasizes that they are very close to the narrator；附近，means they are not far away without requiring them to be immediately beside the narrator
- 好了 -- “我一吃这个灵芝就好了”：恢复过来了，states more specifically that the narrator recovered after feeling unwell

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 那个植物：灵芝，Two plants have been mentioned in the nearby context, so the noun should be repeated to make clear that the request is for the healing plant, not the yellow flower.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 特别是夹竹桃。因为夹竹桃有毒。：特别是夹竹桃，因为它有毒。A cause introduced by “因为” should be attached to the statement it explains; here it explains why this plant needs special caution.
- Expressiveness: When the yellow flower first appears, add a visual detail: “那朵花又黄又大，像星星一样。”
- Expressiveness: After eating the flower, show the narrator’s physical feeling: “我觉得头很疼，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Make the man’s help more lively with a short line of speech: “一个穿蓝色衣服的男人走过来，说：‘快吃这个灵芝！’”

### Encouragement
You use the “一……就……” pattern effectively to show that the narrator felt better immediately after eating the medicine. The final warning also clearly connects the event to the lesson about being careful with plants.
