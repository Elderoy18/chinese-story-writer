## Scene 1

> 古代的时候人觉得祭拜非常重要，孟子很小的时候，从早到晚一直祭拜，因为他妈妈告诉他祭拜很重要。虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜。他的同学们老是对他开玩笑了。孟子妈妈看了他的同学们对孟子不好的影响，所以孟子和孟子妈妈搬家了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，This expression redundantly combines a time period with “the time of”; use the concise time expression instead.
- 人：人们，This refers to a concrete group of people in the past, so use the plural human-group noun.
- 看了：看到了，Here the mother notices an effect; 看 means “look,” while 看到 means “see” or “notice.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不好 -- “他的同学们对孟子不好的影响”：负面，identifies the influence specifically as harmful rather than simply “not good.”

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子很小的时候：有一个小男孩叫孟子。他很小的时候，This is the character’s first mention, so introduce him with a full noun phrase before using a pronoun.
- Coherence (Reference): 所以孟子和孟子妈妈搬家了：所以他们搬家了，Both characters were named in the immediately preceding sentence, so a pronoun avoids unnecessarily repeating their names.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他的同学们老是对他开玩笑了：他的同学们老是对他开玩笑，A habitual action with 老是 takes the zero particle, not completed-action 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜：他没有朋友，因为他每天只祭拜，虽然 needs a contrasting result clause with 但是 or 可是; this sentence expresses a cause, so use 因为 to connect the reason.
- Expressiveness: At the opening, add one visible detail to show how seriously people treat the ritual, such as “人们穿着黑衣服，安静地祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: After the classmates tease him, add Mengzi’s feeling to make the moment more vivid, such as “孟子低下头，觉得很难过。”
- Expressiveness: When his mother notices the influence, add her private thought or speech, such as “孟子妈妈想：‘这样下去会影响孟子。’”

### Encouragement
You use clear time language in “从早到晚,” which helps the reader understand how often Mengzi worships. The scene also has a logical movement from his behavior to his classmates’ reaction and his family’s decision to move.

## Scene 2

> 孟子才十一岁，成为一个哲学家。孟子不是祭拜，而是看政治文章，难怪他成为了哲学家。不过，看过很多政治文章对他害得，他有很多问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不是祭拜，而是看政治文章：不是在祭拜，而是在看政治文章，The two parts of this corrective contrast need parallel verb phrases; adding 在 makes both actions clearly ongoing.
- 对他害得，他有很多问题：害得他有很多问题，The structure is 害得 + person + harmful result, so the affected person must follow 害得 directly.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 问题 -- “他有很多问题”：疑问, specifies that these are unanswered intellectual questions rather than general difficulties；困惑, emphasizes the confusion he feels after reading

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子不是祭拜：他不是在祭拜，Since 孟子 was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other person is competing as the referent, use a pronoun for this close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 孟子才十一岁，成为一个哲学家：孟子才十一岁，就成为了一个哲学家，The pattern 才…就… shows that the result happened unusually early, and 了 marks the completed change of becoming a philosopher.
- Expressiveness: After “看政治文章,” add an internal reaction to show his thinking: “他皱着眉头，心里有很多疑问。”
- Expressiveness: After the statement about becoming a philosopher, add a brief reaction from others: “大家听说以后，都觉得很惊讶。”

### Encouragement
You chose details such as political articles and unanswered questions to show that Mengzi is thinking seriously. You also attempted a useful contrast structure with “不是……而是……,” which is a strong way to connect two actions.

## Scene 3

> 他一直问问题，但是不能解决那些问题。孟子长大，他开始能解决问题。他常常建议你不是学金融，而是学政治，因为如果你懂政治，你会成为高薪。后来孟子成为教授，他教学生很厉害。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 建议你不是学金融，而是学政治：建议你不要学金融，而要学政治，After 建议, use a negative-and-positive recommendation to state the advised choice; 不是……而是…… here sounds like an assertion rather than advice.
- 成为高薪：得到高薪，High salary is pay, not an identity or role that someone can become; 成为 is used with roles such as 教授.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 问 -- “他一直问问题”：提出，emphasizes clearly raising questions for discussion or inquiry.
- 厉害 -- “他教学生很厉害”：很有办法，shows that he has effective ways of teaching students.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子：他，The previous sentence already identifies the only character as 孟子, so use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Reference): 后来孟子：后来他，The character was named or referred to in the immediately preceding sentence, with no competing male character, so a pronoun is more natural here.
- Coherence (Temporality): 长大：长大了，Growing up is a completed change of state here, so add 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为教授：成为了教授，Becoming a professor is a completed event in the later part of the narrative, so add 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为如果：如果，This sentence presents a condition and its result, so use the conditional connector 如果; 因为 is unnecessary before it.
- Expressiveness: At “他一直问问题,” add a small action or feeling to show his determination, such as “皱着眉头，反复思考”.
- Expressiveness: At his advice about politics, add a short line of speech to make his teaching voice clearer, such as “学政治能帮助大家”.
- Expressiveness: At “他教学生很厉害,” show one teaching action, such as “耐心地听学生回答”.

### Encouragement
You use 一直 and 常常 well to show repeated actions and habits. The first sentence also clearly contrasts asking questions with being unable to solve them.
