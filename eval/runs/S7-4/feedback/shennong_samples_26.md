## Scene 1

> 很久以前，有一个老男人叫神农。神农在古代每一天看到了别的小镇人生病还是不舒服，所以他决定开始找新的药。他下一天开始爬当地的山脉，找了各种各样的植物。神农试一试每个植物，知道有什么好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别的小镇人：小镇上的人，别的 is used to distinguish other people or things from one already mentioned; there is no such contrast here.
- 生病还是不舒服：生病或者不舒服，in a declarative sentence, use 或者 to connect two possible conditions; 还是 is normally used for alternatives in a question.
- 下一天：第二天，第二天 is the usual narrative expression for the day after the previous event.
- 爬当地的山脉：爬当地的山，山脉 is a chain of mountains, while 爬 normally takes an individual mountain as its object.
- 找了各种各样的植物：找到了各种各样的植物，找 means “look for,” while 找到 means “find”; the sentence describes plants he successfully found.
- 每个植物：每种植物，种 is the matching measure word for types of plants.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好处 -- “知道有什么好处”：药用价值，specifies that he is learning each plant’s possible medicinal use in this context.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农在古代每一天看到了：他在古代每一天看到了，after the name has appeared in the immediately previous sentence and no other person competes as the referent, use a pronoun.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农试一试每个植物：他试一试每个植物，after the recent reference to the same person, use a pronoun rather than repeating the name.
- Coherence (Temporality): 每一天看到了：每一天看到，every day describes a routine, so it takes the zero particle rather than completed-action 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 试一试：试了试，this is a completed testing action in a past narrative, so the verb needs 了.
- Expressiveness: When describing the ill townspeople, add a visible symptom, such as “有的人脸色很差，一直捂着肚子”.
- Expressiveness: After seeing the sick people, add Shennong’s determination with a thought or short quotation, such as “我一定要找到能治病的植物！”
- Expressiveness: While he searches in the mountains, add one sensory detail about the plants, such as “山上长着绿绿的草和红色的小花”.

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduce Shennong at the beginning and keep the scene focused on his purpose: finding medicine to help sick people.
- The cause-and-effect connection with “因为……所以……” gives the events a logical order.

## Scene 2

> 比方说，他一次试一试有些植物的叶，吃得又新鲜又甜。煮这些叶以后，他一喝就知道他发现新的茶叶。虽然他找到了很多的新的植物，但是他的旅程有的时候特别是危险。比方说，他一吃灵芝就开始生病。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一次：有一次，When introducing one particular past occasion, use the time expression with 有; 一次 alone does not naturally introduce the event here.
- 试一试：尝了，This is an action that was actually carried out, so verb reduplication is not suitable; use the completed verb for tasting food.
- 吃得又新鲜又甜：觉得又新鲜又甜，吃得 describes the manner or result of eating, but 新鲜 and 甜 describe the leaves’ qualities; 觉得 introduces the character’s taste judgment.
- 发现：发现了，The discovery happened after he drank the boiled leaves, so use 了 to mark the completed action.
- 特别是危险：特别危险，特别 is an adverb that directly modifies the adjective 危险; 特别是 is used to single out an example or category, so it does not fit before 危险 here.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 新的 -- “找到了很多的新的植物”：陌生的，emphasizes that these were plants Shen Nong did not know before；未尝过的，more specifically shows that he had not tasted these plants before

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他（在“发现”前）：omit it，The subject is already 他 earlier in the same sentence, so the repeated pronoun should use zero form rather than appear again.
- Coherence (Temporality): 煮这些叶以后：煮了这些叶以后，The boiling happened before the next action of drinking, so 了 marks this earlier action as completed.
- Expressiveness: After the leaves are boiled, add a sensory detail about the drink, such as “水里飘出一股清香。”
- Expressiveness: At the moment he discovers the tea, add Shen Nong’s reaction or a short thought, such as “神农高兴地想：这种叶子真特别！”
- Expressiveness: When describing the dangerous journey, add one concrete danger to show why it was dangerous, such as “有的时候，山路又陡又滑。”

### Encouragement
- You used both the contrast pattern “虽然……但是……” and the sequence pattern “一……就……” effectively to connect ideas.
- Your scene clearly shows Shen Nong testing plants and making a discovery, which gives the writing a strong sense of exploration.

## Scene 3

> 一个爬山的男人正好看到神农的麻烦，然后给了他一些药。神农最终他的小镇回到，跟别的人分享他的发现。你认为神农的故事真的吗？这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他的小镇回到：回到他的小镇，The directional verb phrase must come before its destination; the place cannot be placed before 回到.
- 跟别的人：跟别人，别人 already means people other than the person being discussed; 别的人 requires a specific contrasting group, which is not present here.
- 神农的故事真的吗：神农的故事是真的吗，When asking whether the story is true, use 是 to link the subject with the predicate 真.
- 这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处：这就要看你相不相信中药对身体有没有好处了，This structure needs an alternative question after 看, and 了 is part of the fixed concluding structure.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 麻烦 -- “看到神农的麻烦”：困境，specifies that Shennong is in a difficult situation rather than referring generally to “trouble.”

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他的：自己的，Because the town belongs to the subject of the sentence, 自己的 makes that relationship clear and avoids repeating a pronoun for Shennong.
- Coherence (Temporality): 回到：回到了，Returning to the town is a completed event in this sequence, so use 了 after the resultative verb phrase.
- Expressiveness: At the moment the man helps Shennong, add one visual detail about him or the medicine, such as: “一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人赶快拿起一棵像伞一样的植物。”
- Expressiveness: After Shennong receives the medicine, show his physical change or feeling, such as: “神农吃了以后，觉得好多了。”
- Expressiveness: Before the final question, add an evaluative thought about Shennong’s actions, such as: “神农不怕危险，真了不起。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the helpful man’s action and use 然后 well to connect it to giving medicine.
- Your ending also tries to involve the reader with a question, which is a creative way to close this scene.
