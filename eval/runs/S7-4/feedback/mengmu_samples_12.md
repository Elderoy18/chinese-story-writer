## Scene 1

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 注意：注意到，When introducing a fact that someone came to notice, use the result complement in this verb pattern. [rule: none]
- 祭拜玩：玩祭拜的游戏，The verb for playing an activity comes before the activity noun; it cannot follow the verb describing the activity. [rule: none]
- 决定他们会搬家：决定搬家，After this verb, use the action phrase directly; do not repeat the people who will carry out the action. [teacher rule: jueding_vp]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 吵 -- “从早到晚很吵”：吵闹，describes the continuous bustle and noise of the area more vividly while keeping the same meaning.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子跟孟母：有一个小男孩叫孟子，跟他的妈妈，On first mention, introduce the character with a name and a clear description of who he is rather than assuming the reader already knows the family title. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Reference): 他常常跟朋友们玩祭拜的游戏：孟子常常跟朋友们玩祭拜的游戏，Because both the mother and the child appeared in the previous sentence, repeat the name here so the pronoun is not ambiguous. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Expressiveness: After the first sentence, add a small sensory detail about the cemetery to set the scene, for example: “墓地里常常有人哭，四周很安静。”
- Expressiveness: At the child’s activity, show his attitude through an action, for example: “孟子一边笑，一边学别人祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: Give the mother a short thought or line of speech when she decides to move, for example: “孟母心想：‘住在这儿会影响孩子，我得搬家。’”

### Encouragement

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections

- 将来：后来，Use 后来 for a later event in a past narrative; 将来 refers to the future. [rule: time_expression]
- 成为：成为了，Becoming a philosopher is a completed change of state, so add 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
