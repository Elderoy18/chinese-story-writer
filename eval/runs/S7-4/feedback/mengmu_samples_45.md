## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子。他们住在墓地的旁边，所以孟子老是祭拜。孟子不是祭拜，就是跟朋友聊天。他很喜欢他的城市，但是城市有才两个学校，都很小。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，The original repeats the idea of “time”: 古代 already names a historical time period, so 的时候 is redundant. [vocab list]
- 有才两个学校：才有两个学校，The limiting adverb 才 should come before the verb 有. [rule: none]
- 两个学校：两所学校，所 is the conventional measure word for schools; 个 is too general here. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很小 -- “都很小”：规模不大，specifies that the schools are small in scale rather than merely physically small

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子：一个叫孟子的男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国，Introduce the boy’s name in the first noun phrase, so the reader knows immediately who the main character is. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子不是祭拜：他不是祭拜，Use a pronoun when the same character was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other character competes as the referent. [rule: none]
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery is introduced, add one sensory detail about its atmosphere, such as “墓地旁边很安静，常常有人来哭。”
- Expressiveness: At the ritual sentence, show why the boy does it, such as “孟子觉得祭拜很好玩，常常学着大人鞠躬。”
- Expressiveness: After the small-school detail, add the boy’s reaction or a short thought, such as “他有一点失望：‘学校怎么这么少？’”

### Encouragement
You used a clear either-or pattern to show the boy’s daily activities, and the final sentence creates a meaningful contrast between his liking for the city and its limited schools.

## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈不是不喜欢他们的城市，而是想孟子有很好的教育。他们搬家到有很多学校的城市。他们从早到晚走路，孟子一直抱怨。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 想孟子有很好的教育：想让孟子受到很好的教育，想 cannot directly introduce the person who should receive an action in this pattern; use 让 before the person, and use 受到教育 for receiving education. [rule: none]
- 搬家到有很多学校的城市：搬到有很多学校的城市，when the destination follows, use 搬到 + place; 搬家 does not take 到 in this structure. [rule: preposition_dao_place]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很好 -- “孟子受到很好的教育”：优质的，specifies that the education is high in quality；良好的，describes education that is suitably supportive and beneficial.
- 很多 -- “有很多学校的城市”：多所，gives a more precise way to describe several schools in this noun phrase.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬到有很多学校的城市：搬到了有很多学校的城市，moving to the city is completed before the following events, so the completed action needs 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: At the mother’s motivation, add a brief inner thought to show her concern: “这儿的学校太少了，我得让孟子受到更好的教育。”
- Expressiveness: After the complaint, add a short line of dialogue showing Mengzi’s tiredness: “妈妈，我们还要走多久？我的腿都酸了。”
- Expressiveness: Describe the long walk with one concrete physical detail: “太阳快下山了，他们的脚上全是灰。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the mother’s reason for moving and Mengzi’s unhappy reaction. The phrase “孟子一直抱怨” effectively communicates his feeling during the journey.

## Scene 3

> 他难怪抱怨，他想他的朋友，因为他不要离开他的城市。他的妈妈找到孟子一个老师。跟老师孟子学最重要的文学，所以他成为哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他难怪抱怨：难怪他抱怨，Place the subject after the pattern headed by 难怪; the explanation can then be introduced with 原来. [teacher rule: nanguai_yuanlai]
- 他的妈妈找到孟子一个老师：他的妈妈给孟子找到了一个老师，The person who receives the help needs to be introduced with 给 rather than placed as a second bare object; 找到了 also marks that the finding was completed. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- 跟老师孟子学：孟子跟老师学，Put the subject before the 跟 phrase so that it is clear that Mengzi is learning from the teacher. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 想 -- “他想他的朋友”：想念, specifically expresses missing his friends rather than simply thinking about them.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为哲学家：成为了哲学家，Becoming a philosopher is a completed change of state in this past narrative, so it needs 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: At the complaint, add Mengzi’s feeling through a short line of speech, such as: “我不想离开这里，我会想念朋友的！”
- Expressiveness: When his mother finds a teacher, show her supportive attitude with a brief detail, such as: “妈妈耐心地安慰他。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show both Mengzi’s reason for complaining and the result of his learning.
- The scene also keeps the focus on Mengzi and his mother, which makes the actions easy to follow.
