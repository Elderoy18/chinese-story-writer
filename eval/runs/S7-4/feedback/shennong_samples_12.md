## Scene 1

> 很久以前，古代的人很不知道什么植物能吃，也什么植物不能吃。所以很多人常常吃不安全的植物，吃不安全的植物对你的身体有坏处，很麻烦的情况。但是一个人，神农，要解决这个问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很不知道：不知道，The adverb 很 cannot modify the negated cognitive verb phrase 不知道 in this way. [rule: none]
- 很麻烦的情况：这是很麻烦的情况，A noun phrase cannot stand alone as the final predicate here; adding 这是 makes it a complete clause. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不安全 -- “吃不安全的植物”：有毒的，specifies that the plants harm people because they contain poison and fits this story context.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一个，神农：有一个叫神农的人，At a character’s first mention, introduce the character clearly with both the name and a full noun phrase. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Expressiveness: After “不安全的植物”, add a concrete result such as “很多人吃了以后肚子疼，常常生病。” This shows why the situation is serious.
- Expressiveness: At “神农要解决这个问题”, add his determination or thoughts, such as “他想：我一定要找出能吃的植物。” This gives the character a clearer purpose.

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish the historical setting and the main problem before introducing the character. Your use of “所以” also helps show the connection between people’s lack of knowledge and the danger they face.

## Scene 2

> 他又聪明又努力，旅行很远找到很多植物。比方说，灵芝和夹竹桃。他一吃各种各样的植物就知道能不能吃这些植物。然后，神农写他的主意。他喜欢植物的味道，特别是花。神农遇到了一个黄色的花，一吃就觉得不舒服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 旅行：走，In this context, this verb more naturally expresses covering a long distance on foot while searching for plants. [rule: none]
- 写他的主意：写下自己的想法，The resultative complement 下 shows that the ideas are recorded, and 想法 refers to a person’s thoughts, while 主意 more often means a plan or a proposed solution. [rule: none]
- 一个黄色的花：一朵黄色的花，Flowers use the matching measure word 朵 rather than the general measure word 个. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农写他的主意：他写他的主意，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence: the previous sentence already clearly refers to the same character, with no competing person. [rule: redundancy_delete]
- Coherence (Reference): 神农遇到了一个黄色的花：他遇到了一个黄色的花，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence because the same character was mentioned in the immediately preceding sentence. [rule: redundancy_delete]
- Expressiveness: At the moment the yellow flower appears, add a visual detail to make the setting clearer, for example: “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花。”
- Expressiveness: After the character feels unwell, show the physical feeling more specifically, for example: “他的肚子疼了起来，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Add a brief inner thought after the pain begins, for example: “他想：‘我会不会生病？’”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the character’s hard work through travelling, testing plants, and recording ideas. The cause-and-result pattern in the sentence about eating plants and learning whether they can be eaten is also a strong storytelling choice.

## Scene 3

> 发现了夹竹桃有毒。别人看到他，很快给他吃一个灵芝。神农一吃就觉得比较好。然后，他继续写他发现的信息。会告诉他的朋友哪一个植物能不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别人：一个男人，This word distinguishes other people from a stated person or group, but no such contrast exists here. Use a full noun phrase to introduce the unidentified person. [teacher rule: bieren]
- 写他发现的信息：写下他发现的信息，The verb needs to express recording the information he discovered; the result complement makes clear that he puts the information into writing. [vocab list]
- 哪一个植物能不能吃：哪一种植物能吃，哪一种不能吃，An embedded question should either ask which plants can be eaten or whether a particular plant can be eaten; it should not combine a selection question with a yes-or-no form in this way. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 比较好 -- “神农一吃就觉得比较好”：好一些了，more clearly shows that his condition has improved by a small amount after eating the medicine；好多了，shows a larger, noticeable improvement in how he feels
- 信息 -- “他发现的信息”：发现，refers more precisely to the plant-related knowledge he has learned through his experiments

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 给他吃一个灵芝：给了他一个灵芝，Giving the medicine is a completed action in this event sequence, so use 了 after the verb; the recipient follows 给了 naturally. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Conjunction): 。会告诉：，还会告诉，The second action adds to the recording action, so a connector makes the relationship between the two actions clear. [rule: none]
- Expressiveness: After the poisonous plant is identified, add a physical reaction to make the danger vivid, such as: “他的肚子突然疼了起来。”
- Expressiveness: At “神农一吃就觉得比较好,” add a short thought that shows his relief and curiosity, such as: “神农想：这是什么药？”
- Expressiveness: When he records his discoveries, include one concrete detail about what he writes, such as: “他写下了夹竹桃有毒，灵芝可以做药。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly connected the poisonous plant, the medicine, and Shennong’s later recording activity. The use of “一……就……” in “一吃就觉得” effectively shows an immediate result.
