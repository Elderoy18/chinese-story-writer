## Scene 1

> 一天，很久很久以前，孟子和他妈妈住在中国。孟子妈妈的工作是祭拜，孟子不高兴，因为他说我们老是搬家，为什么？他妈妈说，从早到晚，我一直祭拜，很多墓地得我祭拜。所以我们得去很多不同的地方。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一天，很久很久以前：很久很久以前的一天，A time modifier such as “long ago” should come before the noun it describes; this forms one clear opening time phrase.
- ，因为他说我们老是搬家，为什么？：，问：“我们为什么老是搬家？”，A question about a reason should be introduced with “问,” not “说,” and the question word should come before the action in the quoted question.
- 很多墓地得我祭拜：我得去很多墓地祭拜，Here, the cemeteries are the places where the ritual happens, so use “去 + place + verb” rather than making “墓地” the object of “祭拜.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 祭拜 -- “孟子妈妈的工作是祭拜”：祭奠，specifically describes holding a memorial rite for people who have died, which fits the cemetery setting.
- 很多不同的地方 -- “所以我们得去很多不同的地方”：各地，refers more clearly and concisely to many places in different regions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After “孟子不高兴,” add a small physical detail to show his feeling: “孟子皱着眉头，觉得很烦。”
- Expressiveness: Make Mengzi’s question sound more like spoken dialogue: “妈妈，我们为什么总是搬家呢？”
- Expressiveness: After the mother explains her work, add her tired feeling: “妈妈叹了口气，说：‘我的工作很忙。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly show why the family needs to move by connecting the mother’s work with “所以我们得去很多不同的地方.” The dialogue also gives both Mengzi and his mother a voice in the scene.

## Scene 2

> 孟子想，难怪我们常常搬家，原来我妈妈很忙。可是常常搬家害得孟子很难找到朋友。每次搬家孟子也觉得新的地方不是太热就是太冷，不是太大就是太小。在哪的地方，他都不高兴。孟子想帮助妈妈祭拜，可是他六岁，所以不能祭拜。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 在哪的地方：无论在哪个地方，interrogative 哪 must be followed by the classifier 个 before 地方; 无论……都 expresses that the result is the same in every place.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 新的地方 -- “孟子也觉得新的地方不是太热就是太冷”：陌生的地方，emphasizes that each place feels unfamiliar to Mengzi after a move.
- 不高兴 -- “他都不高兴”：闷闷不乐，shows a continuing, quiet unhappy mood rather than a general feeling of displeasure.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 每次搬家孟子也觉得：每次搬家他也觉得，use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the previous sentence has already identified the same character and no competing character intervenes.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子想帮助妈妈祭拜：他想帮助妈妈祭拜，use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence of the same character.
- Expressiveness: After the difficulty finding friends, add an inner feeling to show why moving is upsetting: “他觉得很孤单，也很想念以前的朋友。”
- Expressiveness: At the final sentence, add a short thought or line of speech that shows Mengzi’s disappointment: “孟子小声地说：‘我什么时候才能帮妈妈呢？’”

### Encouragement
You used the pattern with 难怪 and 原来 effectively to show Mengzi’s realization. You also connected frequent moving to a clear personal consequence: difficulty making friends.

## Scene 3

> 四年后，孟子开始帮妈妈祭拜。他画画很厉害，他越来越喜欢祭拜，所以他祭拜不是想帮妈妈工作，而是他很喜欢画画。孟子成为很著名的古代画家。今天很多中国人很喜欢孟子。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 祭拜不是想帮妈妈工作，而是他很喜欢画画：祭拜不是为了帮妈妈祭拜，而是因为他很喜欢画画，When contrasting two motivations with this paired structure, both halves should clearly state a purpose or reason; the first needs a purpose marker and the second needs a reason marker.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很厉害 -- “他画画很厉害”：画得很出色，describes the quality of his paintings more specifically；技艺高超，emphasizes that his painting skill is highly developed.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子成为很著名的古代画家：他成为了很著名的古代画家，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the previous sentences already clearly refer to the same character.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为很著名的古代画家：成为了很著名的古代画家，Becoming a painter is presented as a completed change of state in the narrative, so it needs 了.
- Expressiveness: After “开始帮妈妈祭拜,” add an inner feeling to show why this new responsibility matters to him: “他觉得自己终于能帮上妈妈了，心里很高兴。”
- Expressiveness: At “他画画很厉害,” add one visual detail about his work: “他画的花和小鸟看起来像真的一样。”
- Expressiveness: Before the final sentence, add a brief evaluative detail about why people remember him: “他的画让很多人觉得又漂亮又有意思。”

### Encouragement
You used a clear time expression to move the story forward and included a contrast structure to explain the character’s motivation. The final sentence also gives the scene a strong sense of outcome by showing that many people still admire Mengzi.
