## Scene 1

> 在古代，很多医生不知道怎么用药。所以他们找到了很多植物对药。神农先看到很多病人，他们吃很多植物对药。但是我觉得他们的吃的植物，让他们病人。神农觉得这个植物可能特别是毒。所以他想找一个植物对药，他们可以吃对药。正好，他找到了一把花，他想把花放在茶里，对药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很多植物对药：很多能做药的植物，The phrase “对药” cannot mean “able to be made into medicine”; “能做药的” correctly modifies the plants. [rule: none]
- 他们吃很多植物对药：他们把很多植物当药吃，“对药” cannot follow “吃” to express using something as medicine; this structure clearly states that they ate the plants as medicine. [rule: none]
- 他们的吃的植物：他们吃的植物，When the person who performs the action is inside a relative clause, place that subject directly before the verb; the extra possessive marker is not used here. [rule: none]
- 病人：生病，“病人” is a noun meaning “patient,” while this position needs a verb meaning “become ill.” [rule: word_class_misuse]
- 特别是毒：特别有毒，“毒” cannot be used as an adjective after an intensifier such as “特别”; “有毒” is the correct adjective phrase. [teacher rule: du_youdu]
- 一个植物对药：一种能做药的植物，“对药” is not a valid way to describe a plant’s medical use; “能做药的” correctly describes a kind of plant that can be used as medicine. [rule: none]
- 可以吃对药：可以吃、能做药，“对药” cannot express either “edible” or “usable as medicine”; these two predicates correctly describe the plant. [rule: none]
- 放在茶里，对药：放在茶里，做成药，“对药” is not a verb phrase; “做成药” expresses making the flower into medicine. [rule: none]

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农先看到很多病人：一个叫神农的人先看到很多病人，This is the first mention of this character, so introduce him with a full noun phrase rather than only a name. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Coherence (Reference): 这个植物可能特别有毒：这些植物可能特别有毒，The earlier sentence refers to many plants and does not identify one specific plant, so the singular reference is unclear. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Reference): 他们可以吃、能做药：人们可以吃、能做药，The pronoun could refer either to the doctors or to the patients; “人们” makes the intended general group clear. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Reference): 他找到了一把花，他想把花放在茶里：他找到了一把花，想把花放在茶里，After the same subject has just been named and no other character intervenes, use zero form instead of repeating the pronoun. [rule: redundancy_delete]
- Expressiveness: When Shennong sees the sick people, add his feeling to show why he wants to help, for example: “神农看到很多病人，心里很着急。”
- Expressiveness: Describe the flowers more vividly when he finds them, for example: “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought before he tries the flower, for example: “这种花能做药吗？”

## Scene 2

> 他不知道如果这把花对你的身体好，这就要看如果这把花毒不毒了。神农想找别的植物，但是这很麻烦。正好，他找到了别的花。他一吃花，很快就生病了。他的肚子疼死了。神农不知道他吃不吃灵芝或者夹竹桃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不知道如果这把花对：不知道这把花对不对，An embedded yes/no question after 不知道 needs an A-not-A form, not 如果. [rule: a_not_a_question]
- 要看如果这把花毒不毒：要看这把花毒不毒，The part after 这就要看……了 must be a question form; 毒不毒 already provides the required A-not-A question, so 如果 should be removed. [rule: zhe_jiu_yao_kan]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 找 -- “神农想找别的植物”：寻找, emphasizes an ongoing search for plants；探寻, emphasizes exploring in order to discover an answer.
- 麻烦 -- “但是这很麻烦”：繁琐, emphasizes that the task involves many complicated steps；艰辛, emphasizes that the search is difficult and tiring.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 你的身体：他的身体，The narration is about 神农, so the second-person pronoun has no established listener; repeat the named character’s third-person reference instead. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Reference): 这很麻烦：这件事很麻烦，The pronoun is too vague because it should clearly refer to the task of looking for other plants. [rule: ambiguous_reference]
- Coherence (Reference): 他吃不吃灵芝或者夹竹桃：吃的是灵芝还是夹竹桃，The subject is already 神农 in the same sentence, so use zero form rather than repeating a pronoun; the two possible plants should be linked as alternatives. [rule: character_introduction_first_mention]
- Expressiveness: When 神农 becomes ill, add a physical reaction to show how severe the pain is: “他在地上躺着，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Before he eats the flower, describe its appearance to make the scene more vivid: “那儿开着黄色的像星星一样的花，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: After his stomach hurts, add his worried thought: “我会死吗？”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the sequence from searching for plants to eating a flower and becoming sick. The strong phrase “疼死了” communicates 神农’s pain vividly.

## Scene 3

> 别的人给了神农一个灵芝。这个植物可以让神农更好。比方说，他会觉得更健康。吃灵芝以后，他可以再走。现在他觉得更好。最后，神农找到了很多植物，他可以用对药。这些植物让神农很高兴。神农想跟很多人分享他的新药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别的人：有人，别的人/别人 should distinguish other people from a person or group already being contrasted; here no such contrast has been introduced, so 有人 naturally introduces the giver. [teacher rule: bieren]
- 神农找到了很多植物，他可以用对药：神农找到了很多可以用来做药的植物， 对药 does not express the purpose of using a plant as medicine; 用来做药 means that the plants can be used to make medicine. [rule: word_choice]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 灵芝 -- “给了神农一个灵芝”：一株灵芝，uses a more precise measure word for an individual medicinal plant.
- 走 -- “他可以再走”：继续赶路，makes clear that he resumes his journey after recovering.
- 高兴 -- “这些植物让神农很高兴”：欣慰，adds the sense that he feels pleased because his efforts have produced useful results.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After “有人给了神农一个灵芝,” add a brief description of the helper so the moment is easier to picture: “这时，一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人走过来，给了神农一株灵芝。”
- Expressiveness: At “现在他觉得更好,” show a physical feeling of recovery: “他的肚子不那么疼了，慢慢站了起来。”
- Expressiveness: After the final sentence, add a short line of speech to show why sharing the medicine matters: “神农说：‘这些植物可以治病，我要告诉大家。’”

### Encouragement
- You used clear time markers such as “吃灵芝以后,” “现在,” and “最后” to show the order of events. You also clearly showed that the lingzhi helps Shennong recover and continue his journey.
