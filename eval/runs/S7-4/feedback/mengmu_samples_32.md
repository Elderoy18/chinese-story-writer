## Scene 1

> 有一天孟子去了墓地玩儿，但是他妈妈觉得这是不合适的地方让孩子玩，所以她决定了他们会搬家找更好的地方。她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福，而且住在这儿教育不太好。因为她望子成龙，所以她会一直搬家才能找到质量好的教育。然后他们搬到了离城市更近的地方。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 这是不合适的地方让孩子玩：这是不适合孩子玩的地方，To describe whether a place is suitable for an activity, use the pattern “适合 + person + verb phrase” before the noun; “让孩子玩” cannot directly modify “地方” here. [rule: none]
- 她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福：她担心儿子的童年不会幸福，“恐怕” expresses the speaker’s own “I am afraid that” and cannot describe her fear in this pattern; “担心” correctly expresses her worry. [teacher rule: kongpa]
- 她会一直搬家才能找到：她要一直搬家，才能找到，“才能” introduces a necessary condition, so the earlier action should be presented as something required with “要”, and the two parts should be separated. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 更好 -- “找更好的地方”：教育条件更好的地方，specifies that the place is better because it offers better educational conditions；更适合孩子成长的地方，makes clear that the place better supports the child’s development.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他们会：，After “决定”, use the verb phrase directly; the subject is already understood as the family, so repeating it is unnecessary. [teacher rule: jueding_vp]
- Coherence (Temporality): 决定了：决定，“决定” is a mental-state verb and takes the zero particle here, not “了”. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: After the mother sees the cemetery, add her visible reaction to show why she wants to move: “孟母皱着眉头，心里很着急。”
- Expressiveness: At the point about the child’s future, add a short internal thought or quotation: “她心想：‘住在这儿会不会影响孩子？’”
- Expressiveness: When describing the cemetery, add one concrete detail to make the setting clearer: “墓地里有人安静地祭拜。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the mother’s reason for moving, and the cause-and-effect links with “所以” and “因为……所以……” help the reader follow her thinking.
- You also use completed-action markers well in “去了” and “搬到了”, which makes the events easy to follow.

## Scene 2

> 他们住才一月就又搬家。古代的时候，一般人不喜欢卖东西的人。但是孟子老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿，害得妈妈着急得很，因为她恐怕儿子会成为跟他们很像。她告诉了孟子：他们不是朋友，而是不好的人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 住才一月：才住了一个月，In a 才……就…… construction, 才 comes before the short duration; a duration of one month needs the classifier 个, and the completed stay takes 了. [rule: cai_jiu_construction]
- 古代的时候：在古代，The original redundantly combines two time expressions meaning “in ancient times.” [vocab list]
- 老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿：老是跟这些卖东西的人玩儿，Using both 老是 and 常常 as frequency adverbs for the same action is redundant; keep one. [rule: none]
- 她恐怕儿子会：她担心儿子会，恐怕 means “I am afraid that” and cannot take another subject before it in this pattern; 担心 expresses that she is worried about her son. [teacher rule: kongpa]
- 成为跟他们很像：变得跟他们很像，成为 is followed by a noun identity or role, while 变得 is used for a change into a state or quality. [rule: none]

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 卖东西的人 -- “一般人不喜欢卖东西的人”：商人，names the occupation more precisely and avoids repeating a long descriptive phrase.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 又搬家：又搬家了，The move is a completed event in the narrative, so the final action needs 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉了孟子：告诉孟子，A speaking verb that introduces quoted speech uses the zero particle rather than 了. [rule: aspect_le_completed]
- Expressiveness: At the point where Mengzi plays with the sellers, add one concrete action showing what he copies, such as “孟子学着商人收钱，还大声叫卖。”
- Expressiveness: After the mother becomes worried, add a brief inner thought to show her concern, such as “妈妈心想：‘再这样下去，孟子会不会只想做买卖？’”
- Expressiveness: Make the sellers’ area more vivid with a sound detail, such as “街上到处都是叫卖声，热闹极了。”

### Encouragement
You clearly showed the contrast between what ordinary people think and what Mengzi does. The mother’s direct speech also helps the reader understand her concern.
