## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，孟子和孟母住了在靠墓地的家。孩子才五岁就每天在墓地祭拜了。在那个城市，孟母从早到晚都找工作，可是找不到，害得孟母没有钱。难怪她跟孩子搬家，原来买不了房。他们搬了到别一个城市找工作。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，this expression is redundant because 古代 already names a time period.
- 跟孩子：带着孩子，this expresses that the mother moves together with and takes the child along; 跟孩子搬家 is not the expected wording here.
- 别一个城市：另一个城市，别的 distinguishes other things from a specifically mentioned one, while 另一个 means one additional/different city.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟母：有一个小男孩叫孟子，他和妈妈，first mentions should introduce the character clearly with a name and identity before using a shorter reference.
- Coherence (Reference): 孩子：他，孟子 was named in the immediately previous sentence and no other male character competes with him, so a pronoun is more natural for a close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Reference): 在那个城市：在一个城市里，this city has not been introduced before, so its first mention needs an indefinite full noun phrase rather than 那个.
- Coherence (Reference): 害得孟母没有钱：害得她没有钱，孟母 is already the subject of this sentence, so a pronoun is the natural close re-occurrence in the result clause.
- Coherence (Temporality): 住了在靠墓地的家：住在靠近墓地的家里，住 is a continuing living state here, so it does not take 了; 在 must come before the location phrase.
- Coherence (Temporality): 每天在墓地祭拜了：每天在墓地祭拜，每天 shows a routine, so the verb takes the zero aspect particle rather than 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬家：搬家了，this move is presented as a completed past event, so it needs 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬了到：搬到了，了 follows the resultative complement 到, not the main verb before it.
- Expressiveness: At the cemetery scene, add an action that shows what the child does: “他一边笑，一边学着别人祭拜。” This makes the action easier to picture and shows his playful attitude.
- Expressiveness: After the unsuccessful job search, add the mother’s feeling or a short thought: “孟母叹了口气，觉得生活越来越困难。” This gives the reader a clearer sense of her worry.

### Encouragement
You used the pattern 才五岁就 correctly to show that the child began doing something at a young age. You also connected the mother’s problem and the move with 难怪……原来……, which gives the scene a clear cause-and-result direction.

## Scene 2

> 这个城市老是很吵，不是人吵架，就是都叫，害得孟子一直不能睡觉。在那儿，孟母也找不到工作。孟子和妈妈要又一个次搬了家。在第三个城市，孟母找到工作，在商店做衣服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 要又一个次搬：要再搬一次，A future repeated action after a modal verb uses the order modal verb + 再 + verb + number + measure word + object; “又” is for a repetition that has already happened, and “一次” is the correct number-measure-word phrase.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 吵 -- “这个城市老是很吵”：嘈杂，describes a place filled with many mixed, disturbing sounds more precisely.
- 做 -- “在商店做衣服”：缝制，specifies that her work is sewing garments.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 人：人们，This refers to a concrete group in the city, so use “人们” rather than the generic noun “人.”
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬了家：搬家，The action is still planned after “要,” not completed, so it takes the zero particle rather than “了.”
- Coherence (Temporality): 找到工作：找到了工作，Finding the job is a completed event in the narrative, so add “了” after the resultative complement “到.”
- Expressiveness: After “这个城市老是很吵,” add a sound detail to show why it is noisy, such as “街上有人大声吵架，还有人不停地喊叫。”
- Expressiveness: After “害得孟子一直不能睡觉,” add Mengzi’s feeling or action, such as “孟子躺在床上，难受得捂住耳朵。”
- Expressiveness: After Mengmu finds work, add her reaction, such as “孟母终于松了一口气，觉得生活会好一点。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show how the noisy environment affects Mengzi and why the family needs to move again. The cause-and-result phrase “害得孟子一直不能睡觉” is especially effective.

## Scene 3

> 孟子在那个商店也找了到工作，可是不是做衣服的工作，而是喜欢的工作。孟子学习馆学了十五年，他的学习和他的旅行经验很重要，对他很多好处。他是大人的时候，他成为很有名的哲人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 找了到：找到了，A resultative complement such as 到 comes directly after the verb, and 了 follows the completed result.
- 孟子学习馆学了十五年：孟子在学习馆学了十五年，A place noun needs 在 before it when it states where an action happened.
- 对他很多好处：对他有很多好处，The pattern is 对 + person + 有 + benefit; 有 is needed before the noun phrase.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 旅行 -- “他的学习和他的旅行经验很重要”：游历，specifies experience gained through travelling to different places and observing them.
- 很有名 -- “他成为很有名的哲人”：享有盛名，expresses that the philosopher has a widely recognised reputation while keeping the strong meaning of “very famous.”

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他的学习和他的旅行经验：自己的学习和旅行经验，Because these experiences belong to the same person introduced as the subject earlier in the sentence, 自己的 makes the possession clear and avoids repeating the pronoun.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他成为很有名的哲人：他成为了很有名的哲人，Becoming a philosopher is a completed change of state in this past narrative, so the verb needs 了.
- Expressiveness: At the point about the long period of study, add a concrete result of his learning: “十五年的学习让孟子懂得了很多人生道理。”
- Expressiveness: Before the final sentence, add Mengzi’s feeling or thought to make his success more personal: “孟子很高兴，心想：‘我终于可以用学到的知识帮助别人了。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly use the contrast between the clothing job and the job he likes, which helps the reader understand the difference. You also connect his study and travel experience to his later success as a philosopher.
