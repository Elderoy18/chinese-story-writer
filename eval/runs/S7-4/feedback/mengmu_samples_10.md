## Scene 1

> 孟子小的时候，从早到晚都跟朋友玩。孟子和他的朋友喜欢玩在墓地里，装祭拜。孟子五岁的时候，跟妈妈搬家。他们搬家不是因为孟子玩得太多，而是因为妈妈认为他们的家离墓地太近。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 小的时候：小时候，A monosyllabic word followed by 时候 forms a fixed time expression without 的.
- 喜欢玩在墓地里，装祭拜：喜欢在墓地里玩，假装祭拜，The location phrase with 在 should come before the action verb, and 假装 can introduce the action that is being imitated.
- 跟妈妈：妈妈带着他，This wording makes Mengzi sound like the person leading the move. For a child moving with his mother, the mother should be the subject and should take the child with her.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 玩 -- “从早到晚都跟朋友玩”：嬉戏，adds a more vivid sense of children playing happily together.
- 认为 -- “妈妈认为他们的家离墓地太近”：觉得，sounds more natural for a mother’s personal feeling or judgment in this situation.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬家：搬家了，This is one completed move at a specific time in the past, so the final action should take 了.
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery-game description, add a concrete action to show what the children imitate, such as “他们学着大人低头、鞠躬。”
- Expressiveness: Add Mengzi’s feeling about the game to make his motivation clearer, such as “孟子觉得这样很好玩，笑个不停。”
- Expressiveness: At the final sentence, show the mother’s concern through a brief thought or quotation, such as “孟母心想：‘这里会影响孟子的学习。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show Mengzi’s daily routine with “从早到晚都,” and the final sentence uses a clear reason contrast with “不是……而是……”.

## Scene 2

> 妈妈认为，住离墓地太近，害得孟子的心气。孟子和孟母搬到子思老师的家，跟子思老师住。孟母帮子思打扫房间和做饭。子思帮孟母教她的儿子。因为孟子现在没有朋友，他老是读书。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 住离墓地太近：住在离墓地太近的地方，The verb “住” needs a location phrase marked with 在, and 的地方 completes the place being lived in.
- 害得孟子的心气：害得孟子心情不好，害得 must be followed by the affected person and a harmful result clause; 心情不好 states the result.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 朋友 -- “孟子现在没有朋友”：玩伴，more precisely describes friends a young child plays with in this context.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 跟子思老师住：跟他住，After 子思老师 has just been named in the same sentence, use a pronoun for the close re-occurrence rather than repeating the full name.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟母帮子思打扫房间：孟母帮他打扫房间，子思 has been mentioned in the preceding sentence, so a pronoun makes the reference smoother.
- Coherence (Reference): 子思帮孟母教她的儿子：他帮孟母教她的儿子，Use a pronoun for the close re-occurrence of 子思 rather than repeating his name.
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬到子思老师的家：搬到了子思老师的家，This move is a completed event in the narrative, so 搬到 takes 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为孟子现在没有朋友，他老是读书：因为孟子现在没有朋友，所以他一直读书，Because the lack of friends is presented as the cause of his reading, add 所以 for the cause-and-effect connection; 一直 is neutral, while 老是 usually carries a complaining or negative tone.
- Expressiveness: After the move, add a small detail about the new home to help the reader picture it, such as “子思老师的家很安静，书架上有很多书。”
- Expressiveness: At the final sentence, show孟子的 feeling about having no friends, such as “孟子觉得有一点孤单，只好坐在桌子前读书。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech from 孟母 when she helps with the housework, such as “孟母说：‘谢谢您教孟子读书。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduce the move, explain how the family helps 子思老师, and show why 孟子 begins reading more. The cause-and-effect idea in the final sentence is a strong way to connect his situation with his action.

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections

### Vocabulary Suggestions
