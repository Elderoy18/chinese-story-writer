## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有很多人不知道什么植物能吃，什么植物不能吃。有很多人吃植物以后就吃坏肚子了，因为他们吃了毒。那个时候，有一个老人叫神农，他什么植物都觉得好吃，不怕生病，不怕毒。他希望别人也可以吃好吃的植物。可是他知道，如果他们还怕吃毒，不会吃他喜欢的植物。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，The time noun and the phrase both express “time of,” so using them together is redundant.
- 吃植物以后：吃了植物以后，The eating is completed before the later result of having an upset stomach, so the earlier action needs the completed-action marker.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好吃 -- “他什么植物都觉得好吃”：可口，describes food as pleasant to eat and gives the taste judgment a more specific word.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the people become ill, add a physical detail to show the result more vividly, such as “他们肚子疼得很厉害。”
- Expressiveness: When introducing Shennong, add his purpose or inner thought to make him feel more active, such as “他想找出哪些植物可以吃。”
- Expressiveness: After he realizes people are afraid, add a short thought or quotation, such as “我一定要告诉大家哪些植物不能吃。”

### Encouragement
You clearly introduce the setting, the problem people face, and Shennong in a logical order. The contrast between his wish for others to eat plants and his understanding of their fear is also well expressed.

## Scene 2

> 所以，他上山下山找植物，吃了很多样子的植物。他是第一人，找到了茶。他把所有的植物写下来了。他一直都吃了，他连毒都能吃。可是他还没吃了一个叫夹竹桃的植物。他找到了那个像星星的植物，觉得很漂亮，马上就吃下了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他是第一人，找到了茶：他是第一个找到茶的人，When referring to the first person to do an action, use the ordinal phrase with the measure word and make the action a modifier of the person.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 找 -- “他上山下山找植物”：寻找, emphasizes the ongoing process of looking for plants during his journey
- 写下来 -- “他把所有的植物写下来了”：记下来, emphasizes recording his discoveries so the information is kept

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 他一直都吃了：他一直都吃，This describes an ongoing habit, so it needs the zero particle rather than 了, which marks a completed action.
- Coherence (Temporality): 可是他还没吃了一个叫夹竹桃的植物：可是他还没吃过一种叫夹竹桃的植物，After 没, do not use completed-action 了; 过 appropriately marks that he has not had this experience before.
- Expressiveness: At the description of the plant, add colour and shape to make it easier to imagine: “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: After he sees the beautiful plant, add his thought to show why he eats it so quickly: “他心想：这么漂亮，一定可以吃。”
- Expressiveness: After he eats it, add a physical reaction to make the moment more dramatic: “没过多久，他的肚子突然疼了起来。”

### Encouragement
You used a clear sequence of actions, from searching for plants to finding tea and then trying the beautiful plant. The phrase “像星星的植物” also gives the reader a vivid visual image.

## Scene 3

> 可是夹竹桃的毒很重，让他肚子很痛。他在地上躺着，觉得他快会死了。可是正好有别人找到他了，给了他一个像伞的植物，叫灵芝。他吃灵芝以后就觉得很好，不再生病了。他测量起来了，像星星的植物是毒，像伞的植物是药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 快会：快要，Use the imminent marker “快要” for something expected to happen very soon; “会” only marks a general future possibility here.
- 别人：一个人，“别人” distinguishes other people from a stated speaker or group, so it does not introduce an unknown helper in this narration; “一个人” introduces the new character clearly.
- 他测量起来了：他明白了， “测量” means to measure physical size, length, or distance, so it cannot introduce his conclusion about which plants are poisonous or medicinal.
- 像星星的植物是毒：像星星的植物有毒，Use “有毒” to describe a poisonous plant; “毒” is a noun and cannot serve directly as the predicate in this meaning.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很痛 -- “让他肚子很痛”：疼得厉害，shows more vividly how severe the stomach pain is in this moment.
- 很好 -- “就觉得很好”：好多了，specifically shows that his condition has improved after eating the medicine.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 觉得他快会死了：觉得自己快要死了，Use “自己” when the person who feels something and the person who may die are the same; this avoids repeating a pronoun for the same character within the sentence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃灵芝以后：吃了灵芝以后，The eating is completed before he feels better, so the earlier action needs completed-action “了”.
- Expressiveness: After “他在地上躺着”, add a physical reaction such as “难受得不得了” to make his pain more vivid.
- Expressiveness: When the helper appears, add one identifying detail, such as “穿着蓝色衣服的男人”, so the new character is easier to picture.
- Expressiveness: Before the helper arrives, add an inner thought such as “会不会死” to show Shen Nong’s fear.

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the cause and result: the poisonous plant causes pain, while the medicinal plant helps him recover. You also use the contrast between star-shaped and umbrella-shaped plants effectively.
