## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有很多人不知道什么植物能吃，什么植物不能吃。有很多人吃植物以后就吃坏肚子了，因为他们吃了毒。那个时候，有一个老人叫神农，他什么植物都觉得好吃，不怕生病，不怕毒。他希望别人也可以吃好吃的植物。可是他知道，如果他们还怕吃毒，不会吃他喜欢的植物。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 already expresses a time period, so adding 的时候 is redundant.
- 吃了毒：吃了有毒的植物，毒 by itself is a noun meaning poison; here the intended meaning is that people ate plants that were poisonous.
- 吃毒：吃到有毒的植物，when referring to the danger of unknowingly consuming a poisonous plant, use 有毒的植物; 吃到 shows the feared accidental result.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 如果他们还怕吃到有毒的植物，不会吃他喜欢的植物：如果他们还怕吃到有毒的植物，就不会吃他喜欢的植物，an if-condition normally uses 就 in the result clause to mark the conditional relation.
- Expressiveness: After the people become ill, add a physical detail to make the consequence clearer, such as `肚子疼得不得了`.
- Expressiveness: When introducing 神农, add a brief thought to show his motivation, such as `他想帮助人们找到能吃的植物`.
- Expressiveness: Add a short question from the worried people before 神农 appears, such as `“什么植物能吃呢？”`

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish the problem of people not knowing which plants are safe, and you introduce 神农 with a clear wish to help others.
- Your use of `什么植物能吃，什么植物不能吃` is an effective and natural way to contrast the two kinds of plants.

## Scene 2

> 所以，他上山下山找植物，吃了很多样子的植物。他是第一人，找到了茶。他把所有的植物写下来了。他一直都吃了，他连毒都能吃。可是他还没吃了一个叫夹竹桃的植物。他找到了那个像星星的植物，觉得很漂亮，马上就吃下了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很多样子的植物：很多种植物，样子 describes an object’s appearance, while 种 is the measure word used for different kinds of plants.
- 他是第一人，找到了茶：他是第一个找到茶的人，use 第一个人 for “the first person,” and place the action that defines this person before 人.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 一直都吃了：一直都吃，use the zero particle with an ongoing or habitual action marked by 一直; 了 cannot mark an action as completed here.
- Coherence (Temporality): 还没吃了：还没吃过，after 还没, do not use completed 了; 过 shows that he has not had the experience of eating it yet.
- Expressiveness: When he finds the plant, add its colour and shape to make the setting easier to picture: “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花。”
- Expressiveness: After he thinks the plant is beautiful, add a short thought to show why he eats it so quickly: “这么漂亮，一定很好吃！”

### Encouragement
- You used a clear sequence of actions, moving from searching for plants to finding tea and then discovering the new plant. You also used completed-action forms effectively in phrases such as “找到了茶” and “吃下了.”

## Scene 3

> 可是夹竹桃的毒很重，让他肚子很痛。他在地上躺着，觉得他快会死了。可是正好有别人找到他了，给了他一个像伞的植物，叫灵芝。他吃灵芝以后就觉得很好，不再生病了。他测量起来了，像星星的植物是毒，像伞的植物是药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别人：有一个人，别人 means “other people” in contrast with a speaker or a known person; use an indefinite person phrase for an unknown rescuer.
- 快会死了：快要死了，快要 + verb expresses that an event is imminent; 会 does not form the usual pattern for an immediate change of state here.
- 像伞的植物：像伞一样的植物，when a comparison modifies a noun, use 像……一样的 + noun.
- 像星星的植物是毒：像星星一样的植物有毒，use 像……一样的 to modify 植物, and use 有毒 to describe the property of being poisonous; 毒 is a noun.
- 像伞的植物是药：像伞一样的植物是药，when a comparison modifies a noun, use 像……一样的 + noun.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很重 -- “夹竹桃的毒很重”：毒性很强, specifies that the plant’s poisonous effect is strong rather than referring to physical weight.
- 很痛 -- “让他肚子很痛”：疼得厉害, gives a more vivid and specific degree of pain in this sentence.
- 很好 -- “觉得很好”：好多了, clearly shows that his condition improved after eating the medicine.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 觉得他快会死了：觉得自己快要死了，when the person who is thinking and the person who may die are the same, use 自己 to make the reference clear.
- Expressiveness: At “他在地上躺着”, add his physical suffering and an inner thought: “他在地上躺着，难受得不得了，心想：‘我会死吗？’”
- Expressiveness: At the rescuer’s arrival, describe the new character so the moment is easier to imagine: “这时，一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人走过来。”
- Expressiveness: At the final classification, use quoted speech to show that he has learned something: “神农说：‘像伞一样的植物是药，可以吃；像星星一样的花有毒，不能吃。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the cause of Shennong’s pain, the help he receives, and his recovery after eating the medicine. The final comparison between the two plants is also a good way to show what he has learned.
