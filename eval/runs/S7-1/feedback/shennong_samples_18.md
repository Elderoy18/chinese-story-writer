## Scene 1

> 神农和他的家人住在古代，所以不知道什么植物能吃。因为他的家人常常生病了，所以神农和他的哥哥一起去旅行找可以吃的植物。他们去很多不一样的地方，比方说高山、山谷和沙漠。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 住在古代：在古代生活，古代 is a historical time period rather than a concrete place to live; use it as a time phrase with a verb such as 生活.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 找 -- “一起去旅行找可以吃的植物”：寻找，emphasizes a continuing, purposeful search during the journey；探寻，highlights exploring in order to discover which plants are edible.
- 不一样 -- “很多不一样的地方”：地形各异，specifies that the places differ in their landforms, which fits the following examples of mountains, valleys, and deserts.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：一个叫神农的男人，by the first-mention rule, introduce a new character with both a name and a brief identifying noun phrase.
- Coherence (Temporality): 常常生病了：常常生病，by the zero-particle rule, habitual actions with 常常 do not take 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们去很多不一样的地方：他们去了很多不一样的地方，by the 了 rule, add 了 to show that visiting these places was completed.
- Expressiveness: After “常常生病,” add one visible symptom to make the problem more concrete, such as “他们常常肚子疼，脸色也很差。”
- Expressiveness: At the start of the journey, add Shennong’s determination through quoted speech, such as “神农说：‘我们一定要找到能吃的植物！’”
- Expressiveness: Describe one feature of the changing landscape after the list of places, such as “高山又高又冷，沙漠里却又干又热。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect the family’s illness with Shennong’s decision to search for edible plants.
- Your examples of “高山、山谷和沙漠” make the journey feel broad and varied.

## Scene 2

> 他们在很漂亮的河找夹竹桃。神农问他的哥哥：“我应该吃这种植物吗？”哥哥说：“这就要看你想要安全还是接近知道了。“神农一吃夹竹桃就生病了。哥哥说：“太麻烦了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他们在很漂亮的河找夹竹桃：他们在一条很漂亮的河边找夹竹桃，河 is the watercourse itself, so it cannot be the location for searching; use 河边 for the bank as the location after 在.
- 这就要看你想要安全还是接近知道了：这就要看你想要安全，还是想更多地了解这种植物了，接近 means “to get close to” a place, person, or target and cannot modify 知道; use 更了解这种植物 to express gaining more knowledge.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 麻烦 -- “太麻烦了”：糟糕，gives the brother’s immediate negative reaction more clearly in this situation.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: At the riverbank, add a visual detail about the plant, such as “河边开着黄色的、像星星一样的花。”
- Expressiveness: After the illness, show Shennong’s physical feeling, such as “他的肚子疼得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Make the brother’s reaction more vivid with a short line of speech, such as “哥哥着急地说：‘你怎么样？’”

### Encouragement
- You used direct speech to show the conversation between the two characters, and the pattern “一……就……” clearly shows that Shennong became ill immediately after eating the plant.

## Scene 3

> 夹竹桃是毒的，我想知道灵芝会帮他。”哥哥给神农灵芝。神农吃完了以后开始痊愈了。神农说：“谢谢哥哥，你帮得真好。灵芝我们可以吃，特别是如果我们已经吃了夹竹桃。“

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 夹竹桃是毒的：夹竹桃有毒，毒 is a noun; use 有毒 to describe a plant’s poisonous property.
- `夹竹桃。“`：`夹竹桃。”`，A spoken quotation must end with the closing quotation mark after the full stop.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 吃完了 -- “神农吃完了以后”：服下灵芝，specifies that Shen Nong takes the lingzhi as medicine rather than simply finishing some food.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农说：他说，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when Shen Nong was the subject of the immediately previous sentence and no competing referent makes 他 unclear.
- Coherence (Temporality): 哥哥给神农灵芝：哥哥给了神农灵芝，Use 了 after 给 to mark the completed action of giving the lingzhi.
- Expressiveness: At the recovery point, add a physical detail to show that the medicine is working: `他的肚子不那么疼了，脸色也好多了。`
- Expressiveness: In Shen Nong’s final speech, explain the medicinal role of lingzhi more specifically: `灵芝是药，可以吃。`
- Expressiveness: The brother could show concern before giving the medicine: `神农会不会有事？快吃一点灵芝吧！`

### Encouragement
- You created a clear sequence of illness, medicine, recovery, and thanks. The phrase `吃完了以后` also orders the events naturally.
