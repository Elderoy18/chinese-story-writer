## Scene 1

> 这是古代的故事。孟子小的时候，他的爸爸很早就去世了。从他的家到墓地很近，所以他每天老是从早到晚去墓地跟朋友们玩玩儿。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 玩玩儿：玩儿，Verb reduplication presents an action as brief or casual, so it clashes with an activity lasting from morning to evening.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很早 -- “他的爸爸很早就去世了”：年幼时就去世了，specifies that the death happened during Mengzi’s childhood rather than simply “early” in an unspecified sense.
- 朋友们 -- “去墓地跟朋友们玩儿”：小伙伴们，more specifically shows that these are companions of a young child.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子小的时候，他的爸爸很早就去世了：有一个小男孩叫孟子。他小的时候，他的爸爸很早就去世了，On first mention, introduce a character with a full noun phrase stating who he is before using pronouns or possessive references.
- Expressiveness: After the father’s death, add Mengzi’s or his mother’s feeling to make this background more personal: “孟子和妈妈都很难过。”
- Expressiveness: At the cemetery scene, add a sensory detail about what Mengzi sees or hears: “墓地里常常有人哭，气氛很安静。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish the time period and explain the reason for Mengzi’s daily visits with a logical cause-and-effect connector. The phrase “从早到晚” also gives the reader a strong sense of how often he went there.

## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈觉得在墓地玩儿，害得，所以他们搬家。搬家对孟子有好的影响，他才五岁，可是每天不是写字就是帮妈妈的工作。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 害得：会带来麻烦，害得 must be followed by a person and a harmful result; it cannot stand alone after the cause.
- 帮妈妈的工作：帮妈妈干活，after 帮 + person, use a verb phrase to state the action being done; 妈妈的工作 is a noun phrase and cannot complete the second verb phrase in 不是……就是…….

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好的 -- “搬家对孟子有好的影响”：积极的，specifies that the move supports Mengzi’s development in a positive way.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们搬家：他们搬家了，use 了 for this completed, one-time action.
- Expressiveness: After “在墓地玩儿”, add the mother’s worry and a concrete reason, such as “孟子每天学着别人祭拜，让孟母很担心。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought or line of speech after “有好的影响”, such as “孟母想：‘孩子还小，应该好好学习。’”
- Expressiveness: Make the new routine more vivid after “每天不是写字就是帮妈妈干活”, such as “他认真地坐在桌子前写字，也会帮妈妈扫地。”

### Encouragement
- You used the contrast pattern “他才五岁，可是……” effectively to show that Mengzi was very young but already had responsibilities.  
- The pattern “每天不是……就是……” also gives the scene a clear sense of his daily routine.

## Scene 3

> 他很聪明的人，难怪他成为有名的人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他很聪明的人：他是一个很聪明的人，Use 是 to link the subject to a noun phrase; 很聪明 modifies 人 and therefore needs 的.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 有名 -- “成为有名的人”：名人，this names the exact category of a person who is widely known and makes the ending more concise.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 难怪他成为有名的人：难怪他成为了有名的人，Use 了 after 成为 to mark the completed change of becoming a well-known person.
- Expressiveness: At the ending, add the mother’s proud feeling to make the result more emotional: 孟母听了以后，非常高兴。
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech after the achievement to show encouragement: 孟母说：“你终于长大了！”

### Encouragement
- You use 难怪 well to show a connection between being intelligent and later success. The final sentence also gives the scene a clear concluding feeling.

## End of story

### Content & Completeness
- After the family lives near the graveyard, add that 孟子 copies the adults by offering sacrifices every day, not only that he plays there.
- After 孟母 decides the graveyard is not a good place for him, include their move to a neighborhood near the market and show 孟子 imitating people buying and selling.
- After the market, add that 孟母 moves again to live near a school; then 孟子 begins studying every day.
- At the end, explain that 孟子 grows up to become a famous philosopher, not only “a famous person.”

### Overall Encouragement
- You included the important beginning near the graveyard and showed that 孟母 cares about her son’s environment. You also gave the story an ending by showing that 孟子 becomes well known.
