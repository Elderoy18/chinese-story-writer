## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男人觉得植物很有意思。他叫神农。有的植物吃了以后他的肚子疼死了，可是有的吃了以后很舒服。他找很多植物。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，the time noun already expresses the period, so adding a second “time of” phrase is redundant.
- 他找很多植物：他找到了很多植物，when the sentence reports that he found plants, use 找到 rather than 找, because 找 means “to look for”; add 了 to mark the completed discovery.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一个男人觉得植物很有意思。他叫神农。：一个叫神农的男人觉得植物很有意思。，Use the character’s name in the first mention so the reader is introduced to him unambiguously; this follows the first-mention reference rule.
- Expressiveness: After the painful result, add Shen Nong’s feeling: “神农很害怕，不知道自己会不会生病。”
- Expressiveness: Make the plants more visual by adding one small detail: “有的植物开着黄色的小花，看起来很漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: Add a brief inner thought after he finds plants: “神农想：我一定要知道什么植物能吃。”

### Encouragement
- You created a clear contrast between plants that make Shen Nong feel pain and plants that make him feel comfortable. This gives the scene a strong connection to his interest in plants.

## Scene 2

> 凉山很大的山，这也找植物。一天，他找到黄色的花。他听说你跟黄色的花做茶很有用。比方说，你的头发疼死，这冰茶是药。他一喝茶就很不舒服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 凉山很大的山：凉山是一座很大的山，A noun phrase cannot directly state what a named place is; use 是 to link the name with its description, and 座 is the matching measure word for a mountain.
- 这也找植物：他也在这里找植物，Use 在这里 for the location. The demonstrative 这 alone cannot serve as the location in this sentence, and 他 clearly refers back to 神农.
- 你跟黄色的花做茶很有用：用黄色的花做的茶很有用，The character 你 should be 用, which introduces the material used to make something. The phrase 用黄色的花做的 modifies 茶, so it needs 的.
- 你的头发疼死：你的头疼得要死，头发 means “hair,” not “head.” A degree/result complement after 疼 uses 得, as in 疼得要死.
- 这冰茶：这杯冰茶，A demonstrative before a counted noun needs a measure word; 杯 is the appropriate measure word for a drink.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很大 -- “凉山是一座很大的山”：高大，describes the mountain’s large and tall physical appearance more specifically；巍峨，creates a stronger image of an imposing mountain in a story.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 找到黄色的花：找到了一朵黄色的花，Use 了 after the resultative verb 找到 to show that this one-time discovery was completed. Adding 一朵 also introduces the flower as a new, indefinite object.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 比方说，：[delete]，比方说 introduces an example of a general statement, but the following sentence is not an example of a general category, so this connector should be removed.
- Expressiveness: When the flower first appears, add a visual detail to make the place easier to imagine: “那儿开着黄色的像星星一样的花，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: At the moment of discomfort, show Shennong’s physical reaction rather than only stating that he feels unwell: “他捂着肚子，疼得直冒汗。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought to reveal his fear: “他想：我会不会生病？”

### Encouragement
- You created a clear event sequence: Shennong finds a flower, learns about tea, and then has a reaction after drinking it.
- The structure “一喝茶就很不舒服” effectively shows that the discomfort happens immediately after drinking the tea.

## Scene 3

> 糟糕，这黄色的花是夹竹桃，特别是毒。正好在这儿，灵芝药的夹竹桃他吃完了，也写一个课文的植物。谢谢神农。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 这黄色的花：这朵黄色的花，A demonstrative such as 这 needs a matching measure word before a countable noun; 朵 is the measure word for flowers.
- 特别是毒：特别有毒，毒 is a noun meaning poison, while 有毒 is the adjective phrase meaning poisonous; 特别 modifies the adjective phrase.
- 正好在这儿，灵芝药的夹竹桃他吃完了：这儿正好有灵芝这种药；他吃完夹竹桃以后，The original strings several nouns together with 的 without showing their relationships. Use 有 to introduce the medicine, and use separate clauses to state that 灵芝 is a medicine and that he finished eating 夹竹桃.
- 一个课文的植物：一篇关于植物的课文，Use the measure word 篇 for a written text, and place 关于植物 before 课文 to show that the text is about plants.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 也写：也写了，Use 了 for the completed action of writing in this past sequence; the action of eating is already marked as completed by 吃完.
- Expressiveness: After the discovery that the flower is poisonous, add a physical reaction to show the danger: 他捂着肚子，疼得直冒冷汗。
- Expressiveness: At the appearance of 灵芝, add a rescue action and a new character: 这时，一个穿蓝衣服的男人走过来，给神农吃灵芝。
- Expressiveness: Add a short question after 神农 feels better: 神农问：“这是什么药？”

### Encouragement
- Your opening with 糟糕 creates an immediate feeling of alarm, and you clearly identify the yellow flower as 夹竹桃.
- Ending with 谢谢神农 shows appreciation for the character and gives the scene a warm tone.
