## Scene 1

> 很久以前，有一个老男人叫神农。神农在古代每一天看到了别的小镇人生病还是不舒服，所以他决定开始找新的药。他下一天开始爬当地的山脉，找了各种各样的植物。神农试一试每个植物，知道有什么好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别的小镇人：小镇里的人，Chinese does not normally form this noun phrase with 小镇人; use 小镇里的人 for people living in a town. Also, 别的 should distinguish places from one already mentioned, but no such town has been established here.
- 生病还是不舒服：生病或者觉得不舒服，还是 is normally used for alternatives in a question; use 或者 for alternatives in this statement. Add 觉得 because 不舒服 describes a person’s feeling or condition.
- 下一天：第二天，第二天 is the standard narrative expression for “the next day.”
- 找了各种各样的植物：找到了各种各样的植物，找 means “to look for,” while 找到 means “to find.” The sentence says that he successfully found the plants, so it needs 找到.
- 每个植物：每种植物，种 is the appropriate measure word when referring to every kind or species of plant; 个 does not fit this general-category meaning.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农在古代每一天：他在古代每一天，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence: 神农 was named in the immediately preceding sentence, and no other male character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Reference): 当地的山脉：小镇附近的山，Repeat the place with a full noun phrase when it has not been introduced before; 当地 has no established location to refer back to.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农试一试：他试一试，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence: the previous sentence already refers clearly to 神农 as 他.
- Coherence (Temporality): 每一天看到了：每一天看到，Use the zero particle for a habitual action. 每一天 shows a routine, so 了 should not mark the seeing action as completed.
- Expressiveness: When describing the sick people, add a concrete symptom to make their condition easier to imagine. Example: 很多人肚子疼，脸色也很不好。
- Expressiveness: At the point where he decides to search, add his inner thought or a short quotation. Example: 他想：“我一定要找到能治病的植物。”
- Expressiveness: When he tests the plants, add one detail about his careful method. Example: 他先尝一小口，再看看自己的身体有什么变化。

### Encouragement
- You introduced 神农 clearly in the opening sentence with both his name and a description. You also used 所以 to show the reason for his decision, which gives the scene a clear cause-and-effect connection.

## Scene 2

> 比方说，他一次试一试有些植物的叶，吃得又新鲜又甜。煮这些叶以后，他一喝就知道他发现新的茶叶。虽然他找到了很多的新的植物，但是他的旅程有的时候特别是危险。比方说，他一吃灵芝就开始生病。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 特别是危险：特别危险，特别是 introduces a specifically singled-out person, thing, or case, while 特别 directly modifies an adjective such as 危险.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 一次试一试有些植物的叶：有一次，他尝了一些植物的叶子，For a completed one-time narrative event, use 有一次 and the perfective form 尝了. Verb reduplication is for a light or tentative attempt, not for an action that was actually carried out.
- Coherence (Temporality): 发现新的茶叶：发现了新的茶叶，This is a completed discovery in the past, so it needs 了 after the verb: V. + 了.
- Expressiveness: After the tasting in the first sentence, add a visual detail about the leaves, such as “这些叶子绿绿的，闻起来还有一股清香。”
- Expressiveness: At the dangerous part of the journey, describe the setting to show why it was dangerous, such as “山路又陡又滑，他走得很小心。”
- Expressiveness: After the final sentence, show the physical feeling of becoming ill, such as “他肚子疼得不得了，只能躺在地上。”

### Encouragement
- You used the contrast pattern 虽然……但是…… appropriately to connect finding plants with the dangers of the journey. You also used 一……就…… clearly to show a quick result after eating.

## Scene 3

> 一个爬山的男人正好看到神农的麻烦，然后给了他一些药。神农最终他的小镇回到，跟别的人分享他的发现。你认为神农的故事真的吗？这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 神农最终他的小镇回到：神农最终回到自己的小镇，The directional structure is “回到 + place,” so the destination follows “回到.” Use “自己的” to clearly show that the town belongs to the same person as the subject.
- 跟别的人分享他的发现：跟小镇的人们分享他的发现，“别人/别的” distinguishes other people from a specified person; here the intended group is the people in the town, so “小镇的人们” is specific and natural.
- 你认为神农的故事真的吗：你认为神农的故事是真的吗，An adjective predicate such as “真的” needs “是” in this embedded yes-or-no question.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 一些药 -- “给了他一些药”：几味药，specifies that the medicine is made up of several traditional remedies or ingredients.
- 有好处 -- “中药对身体有好处”：有益于身体健康，states the specific benefit more clearly: supporting physical health.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 回到：回到了，The return is a completed action, so use “了” after the resultative complement “到”: “回到了.”
- Coherence (Temporality): 这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处：这就要看你相信中药对身体有好处了，“这就要看……了” is a fixed structure; its final “了” belongs to that structure rather than marking a completed action.
- Expressiveness: At the rescue moment, describe the man’s appearance or urgency: “一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人赶快给了神农几味药。”
- Expressiveness: After receiving the medicine, add Shen Nong’s feeling or thought: “神农心里想：‘希望这些药能让我好起来。’”
- Expressiveness: At the ending, add a brief evaluation of Shen Nong: “我觉得神农很了不起，因为他不怕危险。”

### Encouragement
- You used “然后” effectively to connect the man’s help with the medicine he gave Shen Nong.
- Your final question directly involves the reader, which gives the ending an interesting conversational tone.
