## Scene 1

> 这个男孩子叫孟子，孟子是古代的人。孟子的妈妈觉得住在哪儿对童年很重要，所以他们常常搬了家。她望子成龙，所以孟子的妈妈安排他的生活。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- Nothing to flag.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 安排 -- “孟子的妈妈安排他的生活”：规划，emphasizes that she is carefully planning his future and upbringing over time.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): “这个男孩子”：“有一个男孩子”，use an indefinite noun phrase for a first mention; “这个” suggests that the reader already knows which boy is meant.
- Coherence (Reference): “孟子，孟子是”：“孟子，是”，use zero form when the same character continues as the subject in the same sentence.
- Coherence (Temporality): “他们常常搬了家”：“他们常常搬家”，use the zero particle for a habitual action; “常常” describes something that happens repeatedly, rather than one completed move.
- Expressiveness: After “望子成龙,” add the mother’s inner thought to show her motivation more vividly: “她心想：‘我要让孟子好好学习。’”
- Expressiveness: At “常常搬家,” add a brief feeling word to show that the moves are difficult: “孟子的妈妈很着急。”

### Encouragement
- You used “望子成龙” appropriately to show the mother’s hope for her son, and you used “所以” to connect her belief with her actions.

## Scene 2

> 因为他们常常搬了家，中国人说，孟母三迁。孟子没有爸爸，所以，他的妈妈更重要。从这儿到那儿，妈妈教孟子很多技能。先她让孟子跟朋友玩儿，然后教他怎么买又便宜又好质量的东西。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 先她让孟子跟朋友玩儿：她先让孟子跟朋友玩儿，In a sentence with a subject and a sequence adverb, the subject normally comes before the adverb.
- 又便宜又好质量的东西：质量又好又便宜的东西，质量 is a noun, so it cannot directly follow 好 as though it were an adjective; use 质量好 to describe the quality of the items.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 技能 -- “妈妈教孟子很多技能”：生活本领，specifies that these are practical abilities for everyday life.
- 从这儿到那儿 -- “从这儿到那儿，妈妈教孟子很多技能”：搬来搬去，more vividly shows repeated moves between different places.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 常常搬了家：常常搬家，A habitual action takes the zero particle; do not use 了 with 常常.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为他们常常搬家，中国人说：因为他们常常搬家，所以中国人说，Because this sentence presents a cause and its result, use the paired connector 因为……所以…….
- Expressiveness: After “孟子没有爸爸”, add the mother’s inner feeling to show why she takes such an active role, for example: 妈妈心想：“我一定要好好照顾孟子。”
- Expressiveness: At “妈妈教孟子很多技能”, add a small action detail showing her teaching style, for example: 妈妈耐心地一个一个地教他。
- Expressiveness: After the final sentence, show Mengzi’s response to learning, for example: 孟子学得很认真，也觉得很开心。

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the mother’s importance in Mengzi’s life and use a useful sequence pattern with 先……然后…….
- The examples of playing with friends and learning to buy good-value items make the mother’s teaching concrete.
