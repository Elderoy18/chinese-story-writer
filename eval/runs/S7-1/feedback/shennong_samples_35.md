## Scene 1

> 在古代，很多医生不知道怎么用药。所以他们找到了很多植物对药。神农先看到很多病人，他们吃很多植物对药。但是我觉得他们的吃的植物，让他们病人。神农觉得这个植物可能特别是毒。所以他想找一个植物对药，他们可以吃对药。正好，他找到了一把花，他想把花放在茶里，对药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很多植物对药：很多能做药的植物，`对药` does not mean “can be used as medicine”; use `能做药的` to describe plants that can serve as medicine.
- 很多植物对药：很多能做药的植物，`对药` is not the correct expression for a plant’s medicinal use; `能做药的植物` has the needed modifier structure.
- 他们的吃的植物：他们吃的植物，when a verb phrase modifies a noun, use `他们吃的 + noun`; `他们的吃的` incorrectly combines two modifier markers.
- 让他们病人：害得他们生病，`害得` requires a person followed by a harmful result clause; `生病` is the verb phrase expressing that result.
- 特别是毒：有毒，`毒` is not an adjective that can be modified by `特别`; use `有毒` to say that something is poisonous.
- 想找一个植物对药，他们可以吃对药：想找一种能做药、人们可以吃的植物，use `一种` as the measure word for a type of plant, and place the descriptions `能做药` and `人们可以吃` before `植物`.
- 对药：做药，`对药` does not mean “make medicine”; `做药` expresses using the flower as medicine.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不知道 -- “很多医生不知道怎么用药”：不了解，emphasizes that the doctors lack understanding of how to use medicine.
  
### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：一个叫神农的男人，first mention should introduce a new character with a name and a brief noun phrase, rather than using only the name.
- Coherence (Reference): 他们：这些病人，repeat the noun when another character, `神农`, is also present and the pronoun could refer to more than one group.
- Coherence (Reference): 他们：人们，repeat the noun when the pronoun’s referent is unclear; here it is not clear whether `他们` means the doctors or the patients.
- Coherence (Reference): 这个植物：这些植物中有些，use a plural noun phrase because the earlier sentences mention many plants, not one specific plant.
- Coherence (Temporality): 先看到：先看到了，use `了` for the completed event of Shennong seeing the patients.
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃很多：吃了很多，use `了` because the patients’ eating is presented as a completed past action.
- Coherence (Temporality): 生病：生病了，use `了` after a verb to mark the resulting change of state: the patients became ill.
- Expressiveness: When Shennong sees the patients, add a concrete description of their condition, such as `他们脸色很差，肚子疼得站不起来。`
- Expressiveness: When he realizes that some plants are dangerous, show his thought or feeling, such as `神农心想：“这些植物可能有毒，不能随便吃。”`
- Expressiveness: Describe the flower more vividly when he finds it, such as `那是一朵黄色的花，像星星一样漂亮。`

### Encouragement
- You set the scene clearly with `在古代` and introduced the problem of doctors not knowing how to use medicine.
- You also used `所以` to show Shennong’s reason for beginning his search, which gives the events a clear direction.

## Scene 2

> 他不知道如果这把花对你的身体好，这就要看如果这把花毒不毒了。神农想找别的植物，但是这很麻烦。正好，他找到了别的花。他一吃花，很快就生病了。他的肚子疼死了。神农不知道他吃不吃灵芝或者夹竹桃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不知道如果这把花对你的身体好：不知道这把花对他的身体好不好，After 不知道, an embedded yes/no question uses an A-not-A form, not 如果. In third-person narration, 他的 refers back to 神农; 你的 would address an unintroduced listener.
- 要看如果这把花毒不毒：要看这把花毒不毒，After 看, remove 如果 because 毒不毒 already forms the required embedded A-not-A question.
- 吃不吃灵芝或者夹竹桃：吃的是灵芝还是夹竹桃，To ask which of two things was eaten, use 是……还是……; 吃不吃 asks whether someone eats at all, not which plant it was.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好 -- “这把花对他的身体好不好”：有益于他的身体健康，specifies that the benefit is to health rather than using the broad word 好.
- 麻烦 -- “找别的植物，但是这很麻烦”：很费劲，more specifically shows that finding the plants takes considerable effort.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农不知道他吃的是灵芝还是夹竹桃：神农不知道吃的是灵芝还是夹竹桃，Use zero form when the subject remains 神农 within the same sentence; repeating 他 is unnecessary.
- Expressiveness: After “他一吃花，很快就生病了”, add a physical detail to show how ill he feels, such as “他躺在地上，难受得不得了。”
- Expressiveness: Describe the flower when 神农 finds it, such as “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花。”
- Expressiveness: Add 神农’s worried thought after his stomach starts hurting, such as “他想：‘我会不会死？’”

### Encouragement
You clearly show a cause-and-effect sequence: 神农 finds a flower, eats it, and then becomes ill. You also use completed-action forms such as 找到了 and 生病了 effectively to move the scene forward.

## Scene 3

> 别的人给了神农一个灵芝。这个植物可以让神农更好。比方说，他会觉得更健康。吃灵芝以后，他可以再走。现在他觉得更好。最后，神农找到了很多植物，他可以用对药。这些植物让神农很高兴。神农想跟很多人分享他的新药。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 别的人：别人，别人 refers to other people; 别的 is normally used before a noun to modify it.
- 一个灵芝：一株灵芝，株 is the appropriate measure word for an individual medicinal plant such as 灵芝.
- 很多植物，他可以用对药：很多可以用来做药的植物，use 用来 + verb phrase to express purpose. 对药 is not a verb phrase; 做药 means “to make medicine.”

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 走 -- “他可以再走”：继续赶路，shows that he is able to resume his journey rather than simply walk again.
- 很多 -- “找到了很多植物”：很多种，makes clear that he found many different kinds of plants；各种各样的，emphasizes the variety of plants he discovered.
- 很高兴 -- “这些植物让神农很高兴”：很欣慰，shows that he feels pleased because his hard work has produced a useful result.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 这个植物可以让神农更好：这个植物可以让他更好，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when 神农 was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other person competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Reference): 最后，神农找到了很多植物：最后，他找到了很多植物，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence because the previous sentences are still about 神农.
- Coherence (Reference): 这些植物让神农很高兴：这些植物让他很高兴，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the same character remains the clear referent.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农想跟很多人分享他的新药：他想跟很多人分享他的新药，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence because the preceding sentence is still about 神农.
- Coherence (Temporality): 吃灵芝以后：吃了灵芝以后，Use 了 after 吃 because eating the 灵芝 is completed before the later action of walking again.
- Expressiveness: At the first sentence, add one visual detail about the helper so the moment feels clearer: “一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人走过来，给了神农一株灵芝。”
- Expressiveness: After the 灵芝 works, describe Shen Nong’s physical feeling: “神农觉得肚子不那么疼了，舒服多了。”
- Expressiveness: At the ending, add Shen Nong’s thought or speech to show why he wants to share the plants: “我要把这些药用植物告诉大家。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the positive result of the 灵芝: Shen Nong feels healthier and can continue his journey. You also use time words such as “吃灵芝以后” and “最后” to organize the events in a clear order.
