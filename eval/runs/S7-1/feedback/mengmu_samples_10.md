## Scene 1

> 孟子小的时候，从早到晚都跟朋友玩。孟子和他的朋友喜欢玩在墓地里，装祭拜。孟子五岁的时候，跟妈妈搬家。他们搬家不是因为孟子玩得太多，而是因为妈妈认为他们的家离墓地太近。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 喜欢玩在墓地里：喜欢在墓地里玩，With 喜欢 + verb phrase, the location phrase normally comes before the main verb.
- 装祭拜：假装祭拜，假装 + verb means to pretend to perform an action; 装 cannot directly introduce this action in this use.
- 跟妈妈：妈妈带着他，跟妈妈搬家 means that Mengzi moved together with his mother, while 妈妈带着他搬家 correctly makes the mother the person who moved him.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 朋友 -- “从早到晚都跟朋友玩”：小伙伴，specifies that these are playmates of a similar young age and suits the childhood setting.
- 玩 -- “从早到晚都跟朋友玩”：嬉戏，gives a more vivid picture of children playing together.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子小的时候：在古代，有一个小男孩叫孟子。孟子小的时候，Use a name plus a full noun phrase when a character is mentioned for the first time, so the reader knows who 孟子 is.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和他的朋友：他和他的朋友，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the referent is still clear.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子五岁的时候：他五岁的时候，Use a pronoun for this close re-occurrence of the same character.
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬家：搬家了，Use 了 for the completed relocation at a particular time in the past.
- Expressiveness: After the sentence about the cemetery, add a concrete action showing the children’s imitation, such as “他们学着别人低头、鞠躬。”
- Expressiveness: Add Mengzi’s feeling to explain why he plays there, such as “孟子觉得祭拜很好玩。”
- Expressiveness: At the final sentence, show the mother’s concern with a short thought or speech line, such as “妈妈心想：‘住在这儿会影响孟子。’”

### Encouragement
You used “从早到晚都” naturally to show a repeated daily habit. Your “不是……而是……” sentence also clearly contrasts the false reason for the move with the real reason.

## Scene 2

> 妈妈认为，住离墓地太近，害得孟子的心气。孟子和孟母搬到子思老师的家，跟子思老师住。孟母帮子思打扫房间和做饭。子思帮孟母教她的儿子。因为孟子现在没有朋友，他老是读书。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 住离墓地太近：住得离墓地太近，use 得 after 住 to form the degree-complement pattern “住得 + adjective phrase.”
- 害得孟子的心气：会伤害孟子的心气，害得 must be followed by a person and a harmful resulting clause; 伤害 is a transitive verb that can take “孟子的心气” as its object.
- 老是读书：一直读书，老是 usually expresses annoyance or a complaint, while this sentence neutrally describes a continuing study habit.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟母：她，use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the mother was named in the immediately previous sentence and no other female character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬到：搬到了，use 了 with a completed move to a new place.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为孟子现在没有朋友，：因为孟子现在没有朋友，所以，use the paired connector 因为……所以…… to show the cause-and-effect relationship.
- Expressiveness: After the mother’s concern, add her worried thought to show her motivation, for example: “孟母很担心：‘这样会影响孟子的学习。’”
- Expressiveness: At the final sentence, show how Mengzi studies rather than only stating the action, for example: “孟子每天坐在桌前，安安静静地读书。”

## Scene 3

> 子思认为，对孩子来说，读书最重要，特别是学哲学。孟子不是练习写文章，就是看哲学书。孟子从五岁一直学哲学。他才五岁就学哲学，难怪他成为很有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 孟子从五岁一直学哲学：孟子从五岁开始一直学哲学，Use 从 + time + 开始 to mark the starting point of an action that continues afterward.
- 成为很有名的哲学家：成为了很有名的哲学家，Use 了 after 成为 to mark the completed change of state: he eventually became a philosopher.

### Vocabulary Suggestions

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子从五岁一直学哲学。他才五岁就学哲学，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the same character was named in the immediately preceding sentence and no other character competes as the referent.
- Expressiveness: After “他才五岁就学哲学,” add a small detail showing his effort, such as “每天都认真地看书，还会问子思老师问题。”
- Expressiveness: At “难怪他成为了很有名的哲学家,” add an evaluative phrase that connects his childhood study to his later achievement, such as “他小时候打下了很好的基础。”

### Encouragement
