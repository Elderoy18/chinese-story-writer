## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子。他们住在墓地的旁边，所以孟子老是祭拜。孟子不是祭拜，就是跟朋友聊天。他很喜欢他的城市，但是城市有才两个学校，都很小。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 already expresses a time period, so adding 的时候 is redundant.
- 城市有才两个学校：城市里才有两个学校，才 must come before the verb 有 to show that the number is limited; 里 marks the city as the location.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 小 -- “两个学校，都很小”：规模不大，specifies that the schools are not large in scale.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一个男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国。他叫孟子：一个叫孟子的男孩子跟他的妈妈住在中国，at first mention, introduce the character with his name and a full noun phrase instead of naming him only in the following sentence.
- Coherence (Reference): 所以孟子老是祭拜：所以他老是祭拜，after a close mention with no competing male character, use a pronoun.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子不是祭拜：他不是祭拜，after the immediately preceding reference to the same character, use a pronoun rather than repeating the name.
- Coherence (Reference): 他很喜欢他的城市：他很喜欢自己的城市，when the possessor is the same person as the subject, 自己的 makes that relationship clear.
- Expressiveness: After the cemetery setting, add a detail about the people there, such as “每天都有不同的人来墓地祭拜”, to make the place feel more active.
- Expressiveness: At “孟子老是祭拜”, show his reason or feeling, such as “孟子觉得祭拜很有趣，一边笑一边学别人祭拜”, to explain his behaviour more vividly.
- Expressiveness: After the chatting sentence, add the mother’s reaction or speech, such as “孟母很生气，说：‘不要再学别人祭拜了。’”, to show how another character feels.

### Encouragement
You clearly establish the setting and introduce the relationship between Mengzi and his mother. The contrast with 但是 and the pattern 不是……就是…… are both used effectively.

## Scene 2

> 他的妈妈不是不喜欢他们的城市，而是想孟子有很好的教育。他们搬家到有很多学校的城市。他们从早到晚走路，孟子一直抱怨。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 想孟子有很好的教育：想让孟子受到很好的教育，when expressing what someone wants another person to experience or receive, use 想让 + person + verb phrase; 想孟子有 does not express this intended meaning.
- 搬家：搬，use 搬到 + place for moving to a destination; 搬家到 is not the standard verb-direction structure.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很好 -- “孟子有很好的教育”：优质的，specifies that the education is of high quality.
- 很多 -- “有很多学校的城市”：多所，adds the appropriate measure word and makes the number of schools more precise.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬到有很多学校的城市：搬到了有很多学校的城市，use 了 after 搬到 to mark the completed move in this sequence of past events.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 。他们搬家：，所以他们搬家，use 所以 to show the cause-and-effect relation: the mother wants better education, so the family moves.
- Expressiveness: After arriving in the new city, add a concrete atmosphere detail, such as “城市里有很多商人卖东西，从早到晚都很热闹。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where Mengzi complains, include a short line of speech to show his feeling, such as “孟子说：‘走了这么久，我太累了！’”
- Expressiveness: Before the move, show the mother’s thoughts more directly, such as “孟母心想：‘我希望孟子能受到更好的教育。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly explained the mother’s motivation for moving, and the contrast structure helps show that she is acting for Mengzi’s education. The time phrases also make Mengzi’s long walk and continued complaint easy to understand.

## Scene 3

> 他难怪抱怨，他想他的朋友，因为他不要离开他的城市。他的妈妈找到孟子一个老师。跟老师孟子学最重要的文学，所以他成为哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 他难怪抱怨：难怪他抱怨，Place the consequence after 难怪; this construction introduces something that becomes understandable.
- 不要离开：不想离开，Do not use 不要 for a character’s personal unwillingness; 不想 expresses that he does not want to do something.
- 找到孟子一个老师：给孟子找到了一个老师，Use 给 to introduce the person who benefits from the action, and place 了 after the resultative complement 到 to mark the completed search.
- 跟老师孟子学：孟子跟老师学，Put the subject before the prepositional phrase 跟老师.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他的朋友：自己的朋友，Use 自己的 when the possessor is the same person as the subject; this makes the reference clearly point back to him.
- Coherence (Reference): 他的城市：自己的城市，Use 自己的 when the city belongs to the subject of the sentence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为哲学家：成为了哲学家，Use 了 to mark the completed change of state: he became a philosopher.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 因为：原来，After 难怪, use 原来 to introduce the reason that makes the result understandable.
- Expressiveness: At the beginning, show Mengzi’s feelings through a short line of speech: “他低着头说：‘我真舍不得离开这里。’”
- Expressiveness: When Mengzi studies with the teacher, add a concrete learning action: “孟子认真地听老师讲课，还不停地问问题。”

### Encouragement
- You chose useful story words such as 抱怨、老师, and 哲学家, and you tried to show both Mengzi’s reason for complaining and the result of his studies.
