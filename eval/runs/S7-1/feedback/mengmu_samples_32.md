## Scene 1

> 有一天孟子去了墓地玩儿，但是他妈妈觉得这是不合适的地方让孩子玩，所以她决定了他们会搬家找更好的地方。她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福，而且住在这儿教育不太好。因为她望子成龙，所以她会一直搬家才能找到质量好的教育。然后他们搬到了离城市更近的地方。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不合适的地方让孩子玩：不适合让孩子玩的地方，use the pattern “适合 + verb phrase” to describe whether an activity is suitable in a place.
- 她恐怕儿子的童年不会幸福：她担心儿子的童年不会幸福，恐怕 means “I am afraid that” and cannot take another subject such as 她; 担心 expresses that she is worried.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 更好的地方 -- “搬家找更好的地方”：更适合孩子成长的地方，specifies that the new place would be more suitable for the child’s development.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他们会搬家：搬家，after 决定, use the zero form before the verb phrase rather than repeating the people who will carry out the decision.
- Coherence (Temporality): 决定了：决定，决定 is a mental-state verb, so it normally takes the zero particle rather than 了.
- Expressiveness: After the mother’s concern, add an inner thought to show why she wants to move: “她心想：‘我得给孟子找一个更好的地方。’”
- Expressiveness: Describe the cemetery’s atmosphere to make the place feel unsuitable for play: “墓地里很安静，常常有人来祭拜。”
- Expressiveness: Add the mother’s feeling before the move: “孟母很着急，不想让孟子在这里长大。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the mother’s reason for moving and use cause-and-effect linking with 因为……所以…… well.
- Your final sentence moves the story forward naturally with 然后 and 搬到了.

## Scene 2

> 他们住才一月就又搬家。古代的时候，一般人不喜欢卖东西的人。但是孟子老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿，害得妈妈着急得很，因为她恐怕儿子会成为跟他们很像。她告诉了孟子：他们不是朋友，而是不好的人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，this expression is redundant because 古代 already names a time period, while 的时候 also means “at the time of.”
- 老是跟这些卖东西的人常常玩儿：老是跟这些卖东西的人玩儿，老是 and 常常 are both frequency adverbs modifying the same verb, so only one should be used.
- 她恐怕儿子会成为跟他们很像：她担心儿子会变得跟他们很像，恐怕 cannot take 她 as its subject in this structure; use 担心 for her fear. Also, 成为 normally takes a noun phrase, while 变得 can be followed by the adjective phrase 跟他们很像.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 卖东西的人 -- “孟子老是跟这些卖东西的人玩儿”：商贩，specifies that these are people who sell goods in a market-like setting.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 他们住才一月就又搬家：他们才住了一个月，就又搬家了，use 才 before the completed duration phrase, and use 了 after 住 and 搬家 to mark the two completed actions in this sequence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉了孟子：告诉孟子，use the zero particle with a saying verb that introduces quoted speech.
- Expressiveness: When describing the people selling things, add one concrete market detail to show why the setting catches the child’s attention: “街上有很多商贩，他们大声叫卖，热闹极了。”
- Expressiveness: Show what Mengzi does while playing with them, rather than only saying that he plays: “孟子学着商人收钱，还跟客人讨价还价。”
- Expressiveness: Add the mother’s worried inner thought before her speech: “妈妈心想：‘这样下去，孩子会不会只想做买卖？’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the mother’s worry and use the result structure 害得妈妈着急得很 effectively. The final quoted speech also gives the scene a clear sense of conflict.

## Scene 3

> 你不是当数学家，而是当哲学家，不能当卖东西的人。所以他们又搬家。在他们第三个家，孟子学了怎么祭拜。妈妈感觉这个地方适合他们，告诉了孟子：我们找到了最后的家。在这儿妈妈让儿子从早到晚学习，他难怪成为有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- Nothing to flag.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 这个地方 -- “妈妈感觉这个地方适合他们”：这里的环境，specifies that the surrounding environment, rather than just the location in general, suits the family.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬家：搬家了，Use 了 because this move is a completed event in the narrative.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉了孟子：告诉孟子，A speaking verb that introduces direct speech normally takes the zero particle rather than 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为：成为了，Use 了 to mark becoming a philosopher as a completed change of state.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 他难怪：所以他后来，Use 所以 to link the mother's requirement to study with its result; this is a cause-and-effect relation.
- Expressiveness: At “在他们第三个家”, add one concrete detail about the learning environment, such as “新家在学校旁边，每天都能听见读书声。”
- Expressiveness: At the mother’s reaction, show her feeling through speech, such as “妈妈高兴地说：‘这儿的环境很适合你学习。’”
- Expressiveness: At “从早到晚学习”, add Mengzi’s own attitude, such as “孟子觉得读书很有意思，每天都认真看书。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the mother’s goal through direct speech and connect the family’s move to Mengzi’s education. The final sentence also gives the scene a clear result by showing Mengzi’s later achievement.
