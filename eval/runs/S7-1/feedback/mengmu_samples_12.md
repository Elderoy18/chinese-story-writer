## Scene 1

> 古代，孟子跟孟母住在墓地的旁边。可是孟母注意，住在墓地的旁边影响孟子。他常常跟朋友们祭拜玩，所以孟母决定他们会搬家。这次，他们住在城市里的商店的旁边，从早到晚很吵。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 孟母注意：孟母注意到了，In this meaning, 注意到 means “notice” or “realize”; 到 is required, and 了 marks the completed realization.
- 跟朋友们祭拜玩：跟朋友们学别人祭拜，玩 cannot follow 祭拜 as a result complement. 学别人祭拜 expresses that the children imitate the mourning rituals.
- 决定他们会搬家：决定带着孟子搬家，决定 is followed directly by a verb phrase. 带着孟子搬家 clearly expresses that the mother moves together with her son.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the first sentence, add a sensory detail about the cemetery to make the setting vivid, such as “每天都有来祭拜的人在墓地哭。”
- Expressiveness: After the mother realizes the problem, add her inner thought or quoted speech, such as “孟母心想：‘住在这儿会影响孟子的学习。’”
- Expressiveness: At the final sentence, describe the busy commercial area more specifically, such as “商人从早到晚卖各种各样的东西，街上热闹极了。”

### Encouragement
You introduce the two main characters clearly at the beginning, and the pronouns remain easy to understand throughout the scene. The contrast between the quiet cemetery setting and the noisy city setting gives the scene a clear change of place.

## Scene 2

> 城市也有点儿危险。害得孟母只好一直陪孟子。孟子也没有朋友，难怪他老是看起来不高兴。住在商店的旁边不是适合孟子，而是让他觉得很伤心。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不是适合孟子：不适合孟子，Use the negative marker 不 directly before the verb 适合; 不是 does not naturally negate this bare verb phrase.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 危险 -- “城市也有点儿危险”：不太安全，specifies that the concern is safety while keeping the mild degree expressed by 有点儿
- 不高兴 -- “他老是看起来不高兴”：闷闷不乐，gives a more vivid picture of his continuing unhappy mood

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 而是让他觉得很伤心：，因为这让他觉得很伤心，Use 因为 for a cause relation: feeling sad is a reason that living there is unsuitable, rather than a contrast or correction expressed by 不是……而是……
- Expressiveness: After “孟子也没有朋友,” add an observable detail to show his loneliness, such as “他常常一个人坐在窗边，看着外面的商店。”
- Expressiveness: At “孟母只好一直陪孟子,” add Meng Mu’s concern through a short thought or quotation, such as “孟母想：‘这儿不太安全，孟子也不开心。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly connect the noisy, unsafe environment with Meng Zi’s feelings and Meng Mu’s worry. The use of 难怪 also creates a natural link between his lack of friends and his unhappy appearance.

## Scene 3

> 所以，孟母和孟子搬到学校的旁边住。才五岁孟子开始学哲学。在学校，孟子不是看书，就是练习写字。将来，他成为中国的一个非常有名的哲学家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 才五岁孟子开始学哲学：孟子才五岁就开始学哲学，Use the 才…就… construction to show that he began learning at the early age of five.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 练习写字 -- “孟子不是看书，就是练习写字”：练字，this is a more specific everyday expression for practising handwriting.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 搬到学校的旁边住：搬到了学校的旁边住，Use 了 after the resultative complement 到 to mark the completed move in this past narrative.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为：成为了，Use 了 to mark the completed change of state: he eventually became a philosopher.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 将来：后来，Use 后来 to sequence a later event in a past narrative; 将来 refers to the future.
- Expressiveness: After the move, add a quiet learning detail to contrast with the noisy shops: “学校里很安静，孟子可以认真读书。”
- Expressiveness: At the final sentence, add Mengzi’s feeling about learning: “孟子觉得学习很有意思，每天都很高兴。”
- Expressiveness: You could add a short line from Mengmu after she sees him studying: “孟母笑着说：‘这儿真的很适合你。’”

### Encouragement
- You used the paired pattern “不是……就是……” correctly to show Mengzi’s two study activities.
- The activities “看书” and “练习写字” clearly show that the new environment supports his learning.
