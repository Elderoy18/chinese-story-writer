## Scene 1

> 古代的时候有一个人叫神农。他需要当上医，一看别的人就想帮助他们。但是，他有一个问题，他不知道什么东西能做药。神农听说一个远远的地方有一种植物，什么病人吃都会觉得身体更好。神农三个月走去，找到有植物的地方，他看到一个很漂亮的植物，又香又好看。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 already refers to a time period, so adding 的时候 is redundant.
- 医：医生，医 by itself is not the ordinary noun for a doctor; 医生 is the standard word in this context.
- 一看别的人就想帮助他们：一看到别人就想帮助他们，看到 means “see” rather than merely “look,” and 别人 is used for other people in general; 别的人 requires a stated contrast with a particular person or group.
- 神农三个月走去：他走了三个月，duration phrases normally follow the verb, and 走去 needs a destination; 走了三个月 expresses that the journey lasted three months.
- 一个很漂亮的植物：一株很漂亮的植物，株 is the appropriate measure word for an individual plant.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 问题 -- “但是，他有一个问题”：疑问，specifies that his problem is a particular uncertainty about which things can be used as medicine.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农听说：他听说，use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the referent is still clearly 神农 and no competing character has appeared.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农三个月走去：他走了三个月，use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence; 神农 remains the clear subject from the previous sentence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 找到有植物的地方：找到了有植物的地方，use 了 after the resultative verb to mark that the finding was completed.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他看到一个很漂亮的植物：他看到了一个很漂亮的植物，use 了 after the resultative verb to show that this was a completed event in the narrative.
- Expressiveness: At the point where he wants to become a doctor, add his motivation with a short thought or quotation: “我要找到能治病的植物。”
- Expressiveness: When he reaches the plant, add a visual detail to make the setting easier to imagine: “那儿开着黄色的花，像星星一样。”
- Expressiveness: After the long journey, show his physical feeling before he sees the plant: “他走了三个月，又累又渴。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduce 神农 and explain his wish to help sick people, which gives this scene a strong purpose.
- The description “又香又好看” uses two senses to make the plant more vivid.

## Scene 2

> 他觉得，这种一定是做药的花。原来他想错了。吃了以后，神农快生病。 这时一个穿着蓝色衣服的人帮神农，快给他一种红的灵芝。神农吃了以后，他没有病，想起来以前一个人告诉他，灵芝对身体有好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 这种一定是做药的花：这种花一定可以做药，The noun after “这种” should be stated here, and “可以做药” naturally expresses that the flower can be used as medicine.
- 快：赶快，“赶快” is the adverb meaning “in a hurry” before an action; “快” alone does not naturally modify this declarative action.
- 以前一个人：以前有一个人，Use “有” to introduce the existence of a person in this earlier event.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 吃了以后，神农：吃了以后，他，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when no other character has intervened.
- Coherence (Reference): 给他：给神农，The pronoun is ambiguous because it could refer to either the blue-clothed person or Shen Nong; repeat the name for clear reference.
- Coherence (Reference): 他没有病：病好了，Within the same sentence, use zero form rather than repeating the subject pronoun; this also clearly expresses recovery after the earlier illness.
- Coherence (Temporality): 快生病：很快就生病了，Use “了” for the completed change of state in the story: Shen Nong became ill after eating.
- Coherence (Temporality): 给：给了，Use “了” because giving the lingzhi is a completed action in the event sequence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 想起来：想起了，Use “了” for the completed act of remembering; it follows the resultative complement “起”.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 帮神农，赶快给：为了帮神农，赶快给，“为了” introduces the purpose relation: the man hurried to give the lingzhi in order to help Shen Nong.
- Expressiveness: After Shen Nong eats the flower, add a physical reaction to make his illness vivid, for example: “他肚子疼得厉害，只能躺在地上。”
- Expressiveness: When the blue-clothed man appears, add a brief action showing urgency, for example: “他赶快拿起像伞一样的灵芝。”
- Expressiveness: Add Shen Nong’s surprised thought when he remembers the lingzhi’s benefit, for example: “原来灵芝真的可以救人！”

### Encouragement
- You used clear time markers such as “原来”, “吃了以后”, and “这时”, which help the reader follow the order of events.
- You also introduced the blue-clothed helper with a useful visual detail, making the new character easy to picture.

## Scene 3

> 啊，他怎么忘记了？他把灵芝放在包里。一回家就告诉别人。他找到药以后他写下怎么做药在书上。所以人们都知道，那红灵芝对身体好处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 告诉别人：告诉人们，别人 means people other than a specifically contrasted person; here the intended general group is 人们.
- 写下怎么做药在书上：在书上写下怎么做药，when a location is expressed with 在, it should come before the verb in this sentence.
- 那红灵芝：那株红灵芝，use a classifier between a demonstrative and a countable plant noun.
- 对身体好处：对身体有好处，use 有 in the fixed structure 对……有好处.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他找到药以后他写下：他找到药以后，写下，use zero form for the same subject after a time phrase when no competing character has appeared.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他把灵芝放在包里：他把灵芝放在包里了，use 了 for this completed action in the narrative sequence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 一回家就告诉别人：一回家就告诉了人们，use 了 because the telling is a completed event.
- Coherence (Temporality): 写下怎么做药：写下了怎么做药，use 了 after the resultative complement 下 to mark the completed act of recording.
- Expressiveness: At the opening question, make the remembered object explicit as an inner thought: “啊，我怎么把灵芝忘了？”
- Expressiveness: When he puts away the herb, add a detail showing care: “他小心地把灵芝放进包里。”
- Expressiveness: At the final sentence, show the people’s reaction with a short quotation: “人们高兴地说：‘原来红灵芝对身体有好处！’”

### Encouragement
- Your scene has a clear sequence from remembering the herb, returning home, and recording what was learned. The opening rhetorical question also gives the character an immediate inner voice.
