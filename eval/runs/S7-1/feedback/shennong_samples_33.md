## Scene 1

> 古代时候，人不知道他们能吃什么植物，常常一吃就死。但是不知道为什么，没有办法。所以有一天，神农出去试一试很多种植物，告诉人什么植物是安全什么是危险。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代时候：在古代，`古代` already names a time period, so adding `时候` is redundant; `在古代` is the natural time expression.
- 人：人们，`人` is a generic noun and should not refer to the concrete group of people in this situation; use `人们`.
- 告诉人：告诉人们，use `人们` for the group receiving the information, rather than the generic noun `人`.
- 什么植物是安全什么是危险：什么植物安全，什么植物危险，coordinated embedded clauses need clear, parallel predicates; here `安全` and `危险` can directly describe the plants.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 神农：一个叫神农的人，follow the first-mention rule by introducing a new character with a full noun phrase before using the name alone later.
- Coherence (Temporality): 出去试一试：出去试了，this is a completed narrative action, so use `了` after the final verb to mark completed testing.
- Coherence (Temporality): 告诉：告诉了，this is a completed act of informing people, so use `了` to mark completion.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 但是不知道为什么，没有办法：因为不知道为什么，所以没有办法，use `因为……所以……` for the cause-and-effect relation: not knowing the reason causes the lack of a solution.
- Expressiveness: After `常常一吃就死`, add a concrete consequence to make the danger more vivid, such as “很多人因为吃了有毒的植物，常常生病。”
- Expressiveness: When introducing the new character, add a small detail about his purpose or feeling, such as “有一个叫神农的人，他想帮助人们找出能吃的植物。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short thought before the testing action to show motivation, such as “神农想：‘我一定要知道什么植物能吃。’”

### Encouragement
- Your opening clearly establishes that people face a dangerous problem with plants.
- The pattern `一吃就死` creates a strong, immediate sense of cause and result.

## Scene 2

> 发现有的植物对身体非常好，特别是茶，让肚子感觉又暖和又清楚。他告诉朋友：“你吃哪种植物，这就要看你的口味。比方说，你爱吃甜的，可能喜欢糖。”他最喜欢吃花，除了夹竹桃以外，什么都花想吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 让肚子感觉又暖和又清楚：让人觉得肚子暖和，头脑清楚，清楚 describes thinking or awareness, not a physical feeling in the stomach; this correction gives each description an appropriate subject.
- 这就要看你的口味：这就要看你的口味了，this fixed expression ends with 了; the earlier question phrase introduces the uncertain condition.
- 什么都花想吃：什么花都想吃，use the pattern 什么 + noun + 都 + verb when meaning “every kind of” that noun.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 好 -- “有的植物对身体非常好”：有益，specifies that the plants benefit health rather than being generally “good.”

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After mentioning tea, add a concrete detail about how it is used: “把茶放在水里，水闻起来很香。”
- Expressiveness: Show the character’s reaction to the discovery with an inner thought: “他想：原来这种植物不但好喝，还对身体有好处！”
- Expressiveness: Make the dialogue more personal by adding a response from the friend: “朋友说：‘那我以后要小心一点，不随便吃植物。’”

### Encouragement
You connect the discovery about plants to a short conversation, which makes the scene feel more active. The example about sweet food also helps explain the idea of personal taste.

## Scene 3

> 他发现这个口味，一百岁时候，不小心吃了毒的花。一吃就开始死。但是生病时候告诉人什么植物是药，什么植物是毒。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一百岁时候：一百岁的时候，A multi-syllable age phrase before 时候 requires 的; only fixed monosyllabic forms such as 小时候 can omit it.
- 毒的花：有毒的花，毒 is a noun, while 有毒的 describes a plant that contains poison.
- 开始死：快要死了，开始 usually introduces an action that can gradually begin, but 死 is normally a completed change of state; 快要……了 expresses that death is approaching.
- 人：人们，Use 人们 for a concrete group of people whom he tells.
- 是毒：有毒，Use 有毒 to state that a plant is poisonous; 是毒 is not the natural predicate here.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不小心吃了 -- “不小心吃了”：误食了，This compact verb specifically means accidentally eating something and fits naturally before the object.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: At the moment he eats the flower, add a visual detail about it, such as “那朵花是黄色的，像星星一样，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: Show his physical pain and thoughts after eating it, such as “他捂着肚子，疼得不得了，心想：‘我会死吗？’”
- Expressiveness: Turn the information he gives people into direct speech, such as “这种花有毒，不能吃。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show that the dangerous event happens immediately after he eats the flower. The scene also moves from his experience to information that can help other people, which gives it a clear purpose.
