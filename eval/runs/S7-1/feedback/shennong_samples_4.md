## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个男人，他的名字是神农。他看到了有很多人不知道哪个植物好吃，也不知道什么植物对你的身体有好处。这个问题神农觉得很糟糕，所以神农去了吃每种植物。他吃了一种植物以后，就写了这个植物对你有没有好处。有的时候，神农吃了很好吃的植物。有的时候，他吃了很危险的植物。他吃完了每种植物以后，会来跟他的朋友聊到他吃的植物了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 already names a time period, so adding 的时候 is redundant.
- 哪个植物：哪种植物，种 is the appropriate measure word when referring to a category or type of plant rather than one individually identified plant.
- 去了吃：去吃了，when 去 is followed by another verb, the completion marker follows the whole verb phrase: 去吃了.
- 写了：写下了，写下 means to record information so it can be kept; plain 写 only means to write.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 看到了 -- “他看到了有很多人不知道哪个植物好吃”：发现，emphasizes that he came to realize this problem through observation.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 对你的身体有好处：对身体有好处，your body introduces a second-person referent even though the narration has not addressed a reader or listener; use a general expression for a general fact.
- Coherence (Reference): 所以神农去了吃每种植物：所以他去吃了每种植物，use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the named character was just mentioned and no competing character is present.
- Coherence (Temporality): 有的时候，神农吃了很好吃的植物：有的时候，神农吃很好吃的植物，use the zero particle for a habitual action introduced by 有的时候; 了 is not used for routines.
- Coherence (Temporality): 有的时候，他吃了很危险的植物：有的时候，他吃很危险的植物，use the zero particle for another habitual action; 了 would mark one completed event instead.
- Coherence (Temporality): 会来跟他的朋友聊到他吃的植物了：会来跟他的朋友聊到他吃的植物，use the zero particle after the modal verb 会; the sentence describes a habitual or possible action rather than a completed change of state.
- Expressiveness: When introducing the problem, add a concrete consequence to show why it worries him, such as “很多人吃错了植物，常常生病。”
- Expressiveness: After the dangerous plant is mentioned, describe his physical reaction to make the event more vivid, such as “他吃了以后，肚子疼得很厉害。”
- Expressiveness: During the conversation with his friends, add a short line of speech to show what he learned, such as “神农说：‘这种植物对身体有好处。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly introduce the main character and establish the problem that motivates his actions. You also use sequence expressions such as 以后 to show the order of events.

## Scene 2

> 一个人问神农，哪个植物好吃。神农说，这就要看你的口味了。要是你喜欢吃甜的，有很多甜的水果，比方说苹果、西瓜和梨。一个别的人问神农，哪个植物对你的身体有好处。神农说，有很多种植物对你的身体有好处，比方说小白菜、黄瓜、茶和菠菜。一个别的人问神农，哪个植物对你的身体有坏处。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 哪个植物好吃：哪种植物好吃，Use 哪种 when asking about a type or kind of plant; the examples that follow are different kinds of fruit.
- 一个别的人：另一个人，另一个人 introduces one additional person, whereas 别人/别的 refers to people or things other than a specified one and does not fit naturally after 一个.
- 哪个植物对你的身体有好处：哪种植物对你的身体有好处，Use 哪种 when asking which kind of plant has this property.
- 一个别的人：另一个人，另一个人 is the natural form for one more person in the scene.
- 哪个植物对你的身体有坏处：哪种植物对你的身体有坏处，Use 哪种 for a question about a category or type of plant.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 坏处 -- “哪个植物对你的身体有坏处”：有害，this directly describes a plant as harmful to the body and is more concise in this question.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: At the first answer, add a small action to show Shen Nong’s friendly manner: “神农笑着说：‘这就要看你的口味了。’”
- Expressiveness: When listing the fruit, add one visual detail to make the examples more vivid: “苹果红红的，西瓜又大又圆。”

### Encouragement

## Scene 3

> 神农说，有一天，我吃了一个黄色的花，名字是夹竹桃。我一吃夹竹桃，就觉得这种植物对我的脑有坏处。可是，一个灵芝和一个男人正好在我的旁边，我说，麻烦你把那个植物给我吃一下。我一吃这个灵芝就好了。所以，你得小心吃植物，因为有的植物对你的健康有坏处，特别是夹竹桃。因为夹竹桃有毒。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一个黄色的花：一朵黄色的花，朵 is the appropriate measure word for an individual flower; 个 is too general here.
- 我的脑：我的大脑，脑 is usually used in compounds, while 大脑 is the natural noun for the brain in this meaning.
- 一个灵芝：一株灵芝，株 is the appropriate measure word for an individual medicinal plant.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 旁边 -- “一个灵芝和一个男人正好在我的旁边”：身边，more specifically conveys that they are close to the speaker.
- 好了 -- “我一吃这个灵芝就好了”：痊愈了，states more precisely that the illness was fully cured.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Conjunction): 可是，一个灵芝和一个男人正好在我的旁边：这时，一个灵芝和一个男人正好在我的旁边，this is an interruption by a new event, so 这时 is the suitable connector; 可是 introduces contrast, but there is no contrasting idea here.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 所以，你得小心吃植物，因为有的植物对你的健康有坏处，特别是夹竹桃。因为夹竹桃有毒。：所以，你得小心吃植物，因为有的植物对你的健康有坏处，特别是夹竹桃，因为它有毒。，因为 introduces a cause and cannot stand alone as a separate sentence here; attach it to the preceding result and use 它 for the closely repeated noun.
- Expressiveness: When the illness begins, add a physical feeling to show the effect of the plant, such as “我的肚子突然疼了起来，疼得我站不住。”
- Expressiveness: Describe the helpful man at the point when he appears, such as “这时，一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人走过来。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short request in quotation marks to make the moment more vivid, such as “我说：‘麻烦你，把那个灵芝给我吃一下。’”

### Encouragement
- You used the useful pattern 一……就…… correctly to show that the illness and recovery happened quickly.
- Your final warning clearly explains why readers should be careful about eating plants.
