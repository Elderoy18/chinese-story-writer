## Scene 1

> 一天，很久很久以前，孟子和他妈妈住在中国。孟子妈妈的工作是祭拜，孟子不高兴，因为他说我们老是搬家，为什么？他妈妈说，从早到晚，我一直祭拜，很多墓地得我祭拜。所以我们得去很多不同的地方。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一天，很久很久以前：很久很久以前的一天，when “one day” is specified within a distant past time, the past-time phrase should modify it before 的一天.
- 他妈妈说，从早到晚，我一直祭拜，很多墓地得我祭拜。所以我们得去很多不同的地方。：他妈妈说：“从早到晚，我一直祭拜，很多墓地得我祭拜，所以我们得去很多不同的地方。”，direct speech using 我 should be introduced with a colon and enclosed in quotation marks.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不高兴 -- “孟子不高兴”：不满，more specifically shows that he is dissatisfied with the repeated moves.
- 地方 -- “很多不同的地方”：很多不同的墓地，specifies the kind of places being discussed and connects clearly to the earlier mention of 墓地.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和他妈妈住在中国：有一个小男孩叫孟子，他和妈妈住在中国，under the first-mention rule, introduce a new main character with both a name and a brief identity.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子妈妈的工作：他妈妈的工作，under the pronoun rule, use a pronoun for a recently mentioned character when no other possible referent competes.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 孟子不高兴，因为他说我们老是搬家，为什么？：孟子不高兴，问：“我们为什么老是搬家？”，因为 introduces a cause, but the question expresses Mengzi’s dissatisfaction rather than giving its cause; state the question directly.
- Expressiveness: At Mengzi’s question, add a small action showing his feeling, such as “孟子皱着眉头问：‘我们为什么老是搬家？’”
- Expressiveness: At his mother’s reply, add her emotion to make the dialogue more vivid, such as “妈妈叹了口气说：‘从早到晚，我一直祭拜。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly introduce the main situation and use dialogue to show why Mengzi feels unhappy. The time phrase 从早到晚 and the word 一直 effectively show how demanding his mother’s work is.

## Scene 2

### Vocabulary Suggestions

- 新的 -- “新的地方”：新搬去的，specifies that this is the place the family has just moved into.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): “常常搬家害得孟子很难找到朋友”：“常常搬家害得他很难找到朋友”，use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when no other character could be the referent.
- Coherence (Reference): “每次搬家孟子也觉得”：“每次搬家，他也觉得”，use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence of the same character.
- Coherence (Reference): “孟子想帮助妈妈祭拜”：“他想帮助妈妈祭拜”，use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence because the previous sentence already clearly refers to Meng子.
- Coherence (Conjunction): “在哪的地方，他都不高兴”：“不管在哪个地方，他都不高兴”，use the paired connector “不管……都……” to express that the feeling applies regardless of location; “哪” also needs the classifier “个” before “地方”.
- Expressiveness: After the difficulty with friends, add a concrete image of loneliness: “他看见别的孩子一起玩，心里觉得很孤单。”
- Expressiveness: At the description of the new places, add physical details that show the heat and cold: “夏天晒得他满头大汗，冬天又冷得他发抖。”
- Expressiveness: Before the final sentence, add a short line of dialogue to show his wish to help: “妈妈，我也想帮你祭拜。”

### Encouragement
You used the explanatory pattern “难怪……原来……” effectively to show that Meng子 understands why the family moves so often. You also clearly connected frequent moving with Meng子’s difficulty making friends and his unhappiness in each new place.

## Scene 3

> 四年后，孟子开始帮妈妈祭拜。他画画很厉害，他越来越喜欢祭拜，所以他祭拜不是想帮妈妈工作，而是他很喜欢画画。孟子成为很著名的古代画家。今天很多中国人很喜欢孟子。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- Nothing to flag.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很厉害 -- “他画画很厉害”：很擅长画画，states more precisely that he has strong painting skills.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他越来越喜欢祭拜：越来越喜欢祭拜，when the subject remains the same within a closely connected sentence, use the zero form rather than repeating the pronoun.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子成为：他成为，after the recent references to the same character and with no competing character, use a pronoun rather than repeating the name.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为很著名的古代画家：成为了很著名的古代画家，use 了 after 成为 to show that this change of identity was completed.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 祭拜不是想帮妈妈工作，而是他很喜欢画画：祭拜不是为了帮妈妈工作，而是因为喜欢画画，use 不是……而是…… to contrast two motivations; 为了 introduces a purpose, while 因为 introduces the reason.
- Expressiveness: After “他画画很厉害,” add one concrete detail about what he paints, such as “他常常画墓地旁边的树和来祭拜的人。”
- Expressiveness: Show his feeling about painting with a short thought or quotation after “他越来越喜欢祭拜,” such as “他心想：画下这些场景真有意思！”
- Expressiveness: Add a brief description of the setting during the ritual scene, such as “墓地旁很安静，只有风吹树叶的声音。”

### Encouragement
You used “四年后” effectively to move the story forward in time. You also clearly showed that Mengzi’s interest in painting became important to him.
