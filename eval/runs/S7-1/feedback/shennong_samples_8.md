## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个人叫神农。一天，神农正在路上走，一看很多人正在吃一些植物。一些人一吃植物就生病了，神农想帮别人。所以想知道哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，古代 already names a time period, so adding 的时候 is redundant.
- 一看很多人：看到很多人，here 看 is intended to mean “see,” so it needs the resultative form 看到.
- 帮别人：帮人们，别人 distinguishes other people from the speaker or a stated person; for the general group in this scene, 人们 is appropriate.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 知道 -- “想知道哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃”：弄清楚，emphasizes resolving the uncertainty through investigation；辨别出，emphasizes telling edible and inedible plants apart.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 一天，神农正在路上走：一天，他正在路上走，by the close re-occurrence rule, use a pronoun after the recently introduced name when no competing character has appeared.
- Expressiveness: When the people become ill, add a visible detail of their condition, such as `有人捂着肚子，脸色很难看。`
- Expressiveness: At “神农想帮人们,” add his concern and inner thought, such as `神农很担心，心想：“我一定要弄清楚哪些植物能吃。”`
- Expressiveness: You could add a brief line of speech from a sick person to make the scene more immediate, such as `一个人说：“我吃了这种植物以后，肚子很疼！”`

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish the problem that motivates Shennong’s investigation: people eat plants without knowing which ones are safe. You also use `正在` and `一……就……`-style timing effectively to make the actions feel immediate.

## Scene 2

> 然后，神农开始尝很多植物，比方说他吃过生姜、芥兰和菠菜。一些植物吃真好，特别是黄瓜。但是，一些植物毒得很，特别是夹竹桃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 一些植物吃真好：一些植物真好吃，Use the adjective compound “好吃” to describe food that tastes good; “吃真好” does not form a natural predicate here.
- 毒得很：有毒，Use “有毒” to describe a poisonous plant. “毒” cannot take a degree complement such as “得很”.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 很多 -- “神农开始尝很多植物”：各种各样的，emphasizes that he tried many different kinds of plants, not simply a large quantity.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Expressiveness: After the list of plants, add a small action to show careful exploration: “他一边尝，一边记下它们的味道和用处。”
- Expressiveness: At the safe-plant sentence, add a sensory detail: “黄瓜又脆又清凉。”
- Expressiveness: At the poisonous-plant sentence, show the immediate result and the character’s feeling: “神农吃了夹竹桃以后，肚子疼了起来。”

### Encouragement
You use the sequence connector effectively after the earlier scene, and the contrast between tasty plants and poisonous plants makes this part easy to follow.

## Scene 3

> 一个人对神农说，麻烦我们可以吃什么植物？神农说，这就要看你想不想生病了。以后，神农告诉别人一些植物能吃，一些植物不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 麻烦我们可以吃什么植物：请问，我们可以吃什么植物？，麻烦 cannot directly introduce a question in this way; 请问 is the appropriate polite opening for asking a question.
- 这就要看你想不想生病了：这就要看是什么植物了，this structure should respond to the uncertainty in the preceding question. The question after 看 should identify the relevant condition—here, the type of plant—not whether someone wants to become ill.
- 以后，神农告诉别人一些植物能吃：后来，神农告诉别人一些植物能吃，when a sentence begins with a word meaning “afterwards,” use 后来 rather than 以后.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 神农告诉别人一些植物能吃，一些植物不能吃：神农告诉了别人一些植物能吃，一些植物不能吃，this is a completed action in the narrative, so 了 marks completion. 告诉 here does not introduce direct quoted speech, so the zero-particle rule for reporting speech does not apply.
- Expressiveness: At the first question, add the speaker’s feeling to show why the question matters: “一个人着急地问：‘请问，我们可以吃什么植物？’”
- Expressiveness: Let 神农 give a brief, helpful explanation after his answer: “神农说：‘有毒的植物不能吃，吃了会生病。’”

### Encouragement
- You used direct speech well to make the scene feel like a real conversation.
- The contrast between plants that can and cannot be eaten clearly connects to the earlier events in the story.
