## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，有一个男孩名字叫孟子。孟子和孟子的妈妈两个人住在很穷的农村。他们住的地方旁边是个墓地，墓地老是有很多人祭拜。因为人们在祭拜，他们一直在哭。孟子和孟子的妈妈住在这里才一个星期就觉得受不了了，而且害得孟子的学习。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，both parts express time, so using them together is redundant.
- 害得孟子的学习：而且这里的环境影响了孟子的学习，害得 must be followed by a person and a harmful result clause; it cannot directly take a noun phrase such as 孟子的学习.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 哭 -- “他们一直在哭”：哀哭，specifies that the crying is connected with mourning at the cemetery.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子的妈妈住在这里：孟子和他的妈妈住在这里，use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the referent is clear; repeating 孟子 inside the same noun phrase is unnecessary.
- Coherence (Temporality): 住在这里才一个星期就觉得受不了了：才住了一个星期就觉得受不了了，use 了 after 住 to mark the completed period of one week before the later reaction.
- Expressiveness: Add a visual detail about the mourners near “墓地老是有很多人祭拜,” such as “有的人低着头，有的人擦眼泪。”
- Expressiveness: Show the boy’s reaction to the cemetery scene, such as “孟子觉得祭拜很有趣，想学着他们的样子。”
- Expressiveness: Add the mother’s inner thought at the end, such as “孟子的妈妈心想：‘如果一直住在这儿，麻烦会越来越多。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly establish the setting by introducing the boy, his mother, their poor rural home, and the nearby cemetery.
- You also use details such as the mourners’ crying to create an unhappy atmosphere.

## Scene 2

> 孟子的妈妈觉得孟子取得良好的教育是很重要的，所以孟子的妈妈认为这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好，就打算跟孟子搬家。孟子的妈妈带孟子不是搬到城市，而是搬到了孟子老师的家。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 取得良好的教育：接受良好的教育，接受 is the natural verb for receiving an education; 取得 is normally used for obtaining achievements, results, or qualifications.
- 跟孟子搬家：带着孟子搬家，跟孟子搬家 only says that they move together, while 带着孟子搬家 clearly means that the mother takes her son with her when relocating.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 地方 -- “这样的地方对儿子的教育不太好”：环境，specifies that the surroundings and daily atmosphere are unsuitable for education.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 所以孟子的妈妈认为：所以她认为，use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when no other possible female referent has appeared.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子的妈妈带孟子：她带孟子，use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence of the same character at the start of the next sentence.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 不是搬到城市，而是搬到了孟子老师的家：搬到了孟子老师的家，the paired connector is for correcting or contrasting an expected claim; here the sentence simply needs to state the destination.
- Expressiveness: After the mother judges the environment, add her worried inner thought, such as “孟母心想：如果一直住在这儿，孟子的学习会受影响。”
- Expressiveness: Add a short line of speech when she decides to move, such as “我们得找一个更适合学习的地方。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly explain the mother’s reason for wanting to move, connecting her concern about education to her decision. You also use the completed-action marker correctly in 搬到了.
