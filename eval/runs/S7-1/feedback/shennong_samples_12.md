## Scene 1

> 很久以前，古代的人很不知道什么植物能吃，也什么植物不能吃。所以很多人常常吃不安全的植物，吃不安全的植物对你的身体有坏处，很麻烦的情况。但是一个人，神农，要解决这个问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 很不知道：不知道，The negated mental-state verb does not normally take the degree adverb 很; use 不知道 to state a lack of knowledge.
- 也什么植物不能吃：什么植物不能吃，Also, 也 must modify a verb phrase and cannot directly introduce this noun clause; the two embedded questions can be placed side by side.
- 很麻烦的情况：这是一种很麻烦的情况，The original is only a noun phrase and lacks a predicate; adding 是 makes it a complete clause.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不安全的 -- “吃不安全的植物”：有毒的，identifies the specific danger as poison in the plants.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 吃不安全的植物对你的身体有坏处：吃这种植物对身体有坏处，Use a shorter repeated reference such as 这种植物 after the plants have been mentioned; removing 你的 also keeps the general statement focused on the same generic people rather than introducing an unprepared addressee.
- Coherence (Reference): 一个人，神农：一个叫神农的人，On first mention, introduce a character with a full noun phrase that gives both the name and who the person is.
- Expressiveness: After the general problem, show one concrete result with a physical detail: “有人吃了有毒的植物，肚子疼得厉害。”
- Expressiveness: When introducing Shen Nong, add his determined thought: “神农想：‘我一定要找出能吃的植物。’”

### Encouragement
You clearly establish the main problem at the beginning and use 所以 to show why people suffer from it. Introducing Shen Nong as the person who wants to help gives the scene a strong direction.

## Scene 2

> 他又聪明又努力，旅行很远找到很多植物。比方说，灵芝和夹竹桃。他一吃各种各样的植物就知道能不能吃这些植物。然后，神农写他的主意。他喜欢植物的味道，特别是花。神农遇到了一个黄色的花，一吃就觉得不舒服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 旅行很远：到很远的地方旅行，use a location phrase after 到 because 很远 describes a place or distance, not the verb 旅行 directly.
- 写他的主意：写下了自己的想法，写 does not naturally take 主意 as its object in this meaning; 写下 means to record an idea, and 自己的 clearly refers back to the subject. The completed action also needs 了 after the resultative complement 下.
- 特别是花：特别是花的味道，特别是 should refer to the same kind of thing as the previous noun phrase, so it needs to specify the taste of flowers.
- 一个黄色的花：一朵黄色的花，朵 is the appropriate measure word for a flower; 个 is too generic here.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不舒服 -- “一吃就觉得不舒服”：肚子疼，names the specific physical symptom more clearly in this situation；难受得不得了，shows that the discomfort is very strong.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 然后，神农写下了自己的想法：然后，他写下了自己的想法，follow the close re-occurrence rule: 神农 was the clear subject in the previous sentence, so a pronoun is more natural here.
- Coherence (Reference): 神农遇到了一朵黄色的花：他遇到了一朵黄色的花，follow the close re-occurrence rule: the same character continues from the preceding sentence, with no competing person.
- Coherence (Temporality): 找到很多植物：找到了很多植物，use 了 for the completed final action in this sequence of events.
- Coherence (Temporality): 觉得不舒服：觉得不舒服了，use 了 to show the change into a new uncomfortable state after eating the flower.
- Expressiveness: After the travel sentence, add a detail about the difficult journey, such as “他走啊走啊，去了很多高山。”
- Expressiveness: When introducing the flower, describe its appearance more vividly, such as “那儿开着黄色的、像星星一样的花，又大又漂亮。”
- Expressiveness: After the discomfort, add Shen Nong’s worried thought to show his feelings, such as “他心想：“我会死吗？””

### Encouragement
- You clearly show Shen Nong as an active character: he travels, tries plants, records ideas, and reacts to what he eats.
- “又聪明又努力” is a strong, natural description that gives the character a positive personality.

## Scene 3

> 发现了夹竹桃有毒。别人看到他，很快给他吃一个灵芝。神农一吃就觉得比较好。然后，他继续写他发现的信息。会告诉他的朋友哪一个植物能不能吃。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 写他发现的信息：写下他发现的信息，Use 写下 when the meaning is to record information so that it is kept; bare 写 is not the natural verb for recording these discoveries.
- 哪一个植物能不能吃：哪些植物能吃，哪些植物不能吃，哪一个 selects a single item, so it cannot be combined with 能不能 to explain which types are edible and which are not.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 比较好 -- “神农一吃就觉得比较好”：舒服些，specifies that his physical discomfort became less severe.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 别人看到他：一个男人看到他，Use a full noun phrase for this character’s first mention; 别人 means “other people” in contrast with a particular person and does not introduce one specific rescuer clearly.
- Coherence (Temporality): 发现了夹竹桃有毒：发现夹竹桃有毒，Use the zero particle with the mental-state verb 发现 rather than 了.
- Coherence (Temporality): 很快给他吃一个灵芝：很快给他吃了一个灵芝，Use 了 after the completed action of giving him a lingzhi to eat.
- Expressiveness: At the rescue moment, add the man’s appearance and urgency: “一个穿着蓝色衣服的男人赶快走过来，给神农吃了一个灵芝。”
- Expressiveness: Show Shennong’s recovery with a physical detail: “他觉得肚子不那么疼了，慢慢坐起来。”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show the important sequence of discovery, treatment, recovery, recording, and sharing knowledge. The use of “一……就……” effectively makes Shennong’s improvement feel immediate.
