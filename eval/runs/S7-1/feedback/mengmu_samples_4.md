## Scene 1

> 古代的时候，孟子和孟母住了在靠墓地的家。孩子才五岁就每天在墓地祭拜了。在那个城市，孟母从早到晚都找工作，可是找不到，害得孟母没有钱。难怪她跟孩子搬家，原来买不了房。他们搬了到别一个城市找工作。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候：在古代，this expression redundantly combines two time expressions; use the single time expression for a historical setting.
- 住了在靠墓地的家：住在靠近墓地的家里，use a location phrase after the verb with “住在”; the ongoing state of living somewhere does not need “了”.
- 跟孩子搬家：带着孩子搬家，“跟” presents the mother and child as equal co-participants, while “带着” accurately shows that the mother takes her young child with her.
- 搬了到：搬到了，place “了” after the directional/resultative complement “到” to mark the completed move.
- 别一个城市：另一个城市，“另一个” is the standard expression for “another” one; “别一个” does not fit here.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 没有钱 -- “害得孟母没有钱”：身无分文，emphasizes that she has no money at all.
- 买不了 -- “原来买不了房”：买不起，specifies that she cannot afford a house because of insufficient money.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟母：有一个小男孩叫孟子，他和妈妈，first mention should introduce a character with an identifying noun phrase, rather than assuming the reader already knows who the named characters are.
- Coherence (Reference): 孩子：他，use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when “孟子” was named in the previous sentence and no other male character competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Reference): 在那个城市：在一个城市里，first mention of a place should use an indefinite noun phrase; “那个” incorrectly assumes that this city has already been introduced.
- Coherence (Reference): 害得孟母没有钱：害得她没有钱，after “孟母” has just been named and no other woman is present, use a pronoun for the close re-occurrence.
- Coherence (Temporality): 每天在墓地祭拜了：每天在墓地祭拜，remove “了” because “每天” describes a habitual routine, which takes the zero aspect marker.
- Expressiveness: After the child’s ritual action, add his feeling and a visible action: “他觉得祭拜很有趣，脸上带着笑容。”
- Expressiveness: After the unsuccessful job search, show the mother’s worry through an internal thought: “孟母很着急，心想：‘我得带着孟子去别的地方找工作。’”

### Encouragement
- You used “才五岁就” effectively to show that the child was very young when he began copying the ritual behavior.
- The cause-and-result idea is clear: the mother cannot find work, has no money, and decides to move.

## Scene 2

> 这个城市老是很吵，不是人吵架，就是都叫，害得孟子一直不能睡觉。在那儿，孟母也找不到工作。孟子和妈妈要又一个次搬了家。在第三个城市，孟母找到工作，在商店做衣服。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不是人吵架，就是都叫：不是人们在吵架，就是在叫，`人` is a generic noun and should not refer to a concrete group of residents; use `人们` for the people making noise.
- 要又一个次搬了家：要再搬一次家，use `再` for a move that has not happened yet; after a modal verb such as `要`, use no `了`, and the numeral-measure-word order is `一次`.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 吵 -- “这个城市老是很吵”：嘈杂，describes a place filled with mixed, disturbing sounds more precisely.
- 做 -- “在商店做衣服”：缝制，specifies that the work involves sewing clothes.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Temporality): 孟母找到工作：孟母找到了工作，use `了` after the resultative verb `找到` because finding the job is a completed action.
- Expressiveness: After the opening sentence, add a sound detail to show why the city is noisy, for example: “街上传来不停的叫卖声和吵架声。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where Mengzi cannot sleep, add his feeling or speech, for example: “孟子难过地说：‘妈妈，我睡不着。’”
- Expressiveness: When describing Mengmu’s work, add a small physical detail, for example: “孟母每天低头缝衣服，手都酸了。”

### Encouragement
You clearly show the effect of the noisy city on Mengzi by using a cause-and-result structure. You also move the story forward clearly by changing to the third city and giving Mengmu a new job.

## Scene 3

> 孟子在那个商店也找了到工作，可是不是做衣服的工作，而是喜欢的工作。孟子学习馆学了十五年，他的学习和他的旅行经验很重要，对他很多好处。他是大人的时候，他成为很有名的哲人。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 学习馆学：在学校学习，学校 is the standard word for a school, and a location used before an action needs 在, as in 在学校学习.
- 对他很多好处：对他有很多好处，The verb 有 is required in the pattern 对 + person + 有 + 好处.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 做 -- “不是做衣服的工作”：缝制，specifies that the work involves sewing clothes rather than making them in a general way.
- 很重要 -- “他的学习和他的旅行经验很重要”：很宝贵，shows that these experiences have lasting value for him.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 他的学习和他的旅行经验：他的学习和旅行经验，Use zero form for the repeated possessor within the same coordinated phrase; the second 他的 is unnecessary because both kinds of experience belong to the same person.
- Coherence (Temporality): 他成为很有名的哲人：他成为了很有名的哲人，Use 了 after 成为 to mark the completed change of state.
- Expressiveness: After the job is introduced, add a small detail about why he enjoys it, such as “他喜欢跟客人说话，也喜欢帮助他们选衣服。”
- Expressiveness: When describing the long period of study, show his effort or feelings with a detail such as “他每天认真读书，还常常向老师请教问题。”
- Expressiveness: At the final achievement, add an evaluative reaction from him or others, such as “大家都很敬佩他，说他是一个有学问的人。”

### Encouragement
- You use the contrast pattern “不是……而是……” effectively to show that the new job is different from making clothes.
- The fifteen-year time span gives this scene a clear sense of Mengzi’s long-term growth.
