## Scene 1

> 古代的时候人觉得祭拜非常重要，孟子很小的时候，从早到晚一直祭拜，因为他妈妈告诉他祭拜很重要。虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜。他的同学们老是对他开玩笑了。孟子妈妈看了他的同学们对孟子不好的影响，所以孟子和孟子妈妈搬家了。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 古代的时候人：在古代，人们，古代 already functions as a time expression, so adding 的时候 is redundant; 人们 is needed for a concrete group of people rather than generic 人.
- 看了：看到了，when the meaning is “noticed/saw the effect,” use 看到 rather than 看; 到 marks reaching the result of seeing.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 不好 -- “同学们对孟子不好的影响”：负面，identifies the influence specifically as harmful or adverse；不良，gives a concise term for a harmful influence in this context.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子很小的时候：有一个叫孟子的小男孩。他很小的时候，first mention should briefly introduce the character with a full noun phrase before using the name alone.
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子和孟子妈妈：孟子和他的妈妈，when the same character has already been mentioned nearby, use a pronoun rather than repeating the name inside the same phrase.
- Coherence (Temporality): 老是对他开玩笑了：老是对他开玩笑，老是 describes a habitual action, so the zero particle is required rather than completed-action 了.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 虽然他没有朋友，因为每天只祭拜：因为他每天只祭拜，所以没有朋友，虽然 requires a contrasting second clause with 但是 or 可是; this sentence expresses cause and effect, so use 因为……所以……
- Expressiveness: After “祭拜非常重要,” add one visual or sound detail to show the atmosphere, such as “墓地里有人低声哭，也有人安静地鞠躬。”
- Expressiveness: After “他没有朋友,” add Mengzi’s feeling to make the consequence more personal, such as “孟子觉得很孤单。”
- Expressiveness: At the point where his mother notices the influence, add her worried thought or speech, such as “孟子的妈妈很担心，心想：‘这样下去会影响他。’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show Mengzi’s routine with “从早到晚一直,” and the final move gives the scene a clear result. Your use of “因为” and “所以” also shows that you are connecting events by their reasons and outcomes.

## Scene 2

> 孟子才十一岁，成为一个哲学家。孟子不是祭拜，而是看政治文章，难怪他成为了哲学家。不过，看过很多政治文章对他害得，他有很多问题。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 才十一岁，成为：才十一岁，就成为，The paired pattern is 才 + number + 就 + verb to show that the result happened unusually early.
- 对他害得：害得他，The pattern is 害得 + person + harmful result; do not add 对 before the person in this structure.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- No suggestions.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子不是祭拜：他不是祭拜，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence when the same character was named in the immediately previous sentence and no other person competes as the referent.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为一个哲学家：成为了一个哲学家，Use 了 after 成为 to mark the completed change of identity in this past narrative event.
- Expressiveness: At the point where he reads, add an inner question to show what interests him: “他一边看，一边想：‘为什么人们要这样做？’”
- Expressiveness: Make the final “problems” more concrete by naming a type of question: “他有很多关于国家和人们生活的问题。”
- Expressiveness: Add a brief description of his reading habit to make the scene more vivid: “每天晚上，他认真地看政治文章。”

### Encouragement
- You used the contrast pattern “不是……而是……” effectively to show a change in the character’s activity.
- The final sentence also tries to show that reading led to the character thinking deeply, which is a strong connection between actions and ideas.

## Scene 3

> 他一直问问题，但是不能解决那些问题。孟子长大，他开始能解决问题。他常常建议你不是学金融，而是学政治，因为如果你懂政治，你会成为高薪。后来孟子成为教授，他教学生很厉害。

### Grammar & Vocabulary Corrections
- 不是学金融，而是学政治：不要学金融，而要学政治，Use “不要……而要……” when giving advice about what someone should not do and should do; “不是……而是……” is used to correct or contrast statements, not to form this recommendation.

### Vocabulary Suggestions
- 问问题 -- “他一直问问题”：不断提出疑问，specifies that he keeps raising intellectual questions rather than simply asking questions.
- 厉害 -- “他教学生很厉害”：很会教学生，states more precisely that he is skilled at teaching students；教得很好，emphasizes the high quality of his teaching.

### Coherence & Expressiveness
- Coherence (Reference): 孟子长大：他长大，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence: the previous sentence already clearly refers to Meng子.
- Coherence (Reference): 你：人们，The pronoun “你” has no introduced referent here; use a full noun phrase for an unspecified group of people.
- Coherence (Reference): 后来孟子成为教授：后来，他成为教授，Use a pronoun for a close re-occurrence because no other character has intervened.
- Coherence (Temporality): 长大：长大了，Use 了 after a completed change of state: he has grown up.
- Coherence (Temporality): 成为教授：成为了教授，Use 了 to mark the completed achievement of becoming a professor.
- Coherence (Conjunction): 孟子长大，他开始能解决问题：孟子长大了以后，他开始能解决问题，Use 以后 to show the sequence: his ability to solve problems begins after he grows up.
- Expressiveness: After the questions in the first sentence, add an inner feeling to show his persistence, for example: “他觉得这些问题一定有答案。”
- Expressiveness: When he becomes able to solve problems, add a concrete learning detail, for example: “他认真看书，也常常和老师讨论。”
- Expressiveness: After the final sentence, add a short line of praise from a student, for example: “学生说：‘孟子老师讲得真清楚！’”

### Encouragement
- You clearly show Meng子’s development from someone who asks many questions to someone who can solve them and teach others.
- Your use of “后来” helps move the scene forward in time.
