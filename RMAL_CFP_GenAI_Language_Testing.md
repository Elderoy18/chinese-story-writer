# Call for Papers: Generative AI in Language Testing and Assessment

**Journal:** *Research Methods in Applied Linguistics* (Elsevier, ScienceDirect) — supports open access
**Posted:** 20 April 2026
**Submission deadline:** 15 October 2026

## Scope

This special issue invites submissions that advance research methods for integrating generative AI and large language models (LLMs) into language testing and assessment. We welcome studies that examine how GenAI can support, and potentially distort, key stages of the assessment cycle, including construct specification, task and item development, scoring and feedback, and quality assurance. We particularly encourage method-focused contributions that articulate validation strategies for GenAI-enabled assessment, such as prompt and model-version documentation, reliability and validity evidence, fairness analyses, transparency and auditability, and human-in-the-loop workflows.

This hybrid special issue combines invited and open submissions. We look forward to receiving your contribution.
